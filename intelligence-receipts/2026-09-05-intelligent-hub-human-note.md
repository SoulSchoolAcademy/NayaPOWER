## What I identified

The NayaNET Intelligent Hub is not just a dashboard. It is the personal intelligence command center and gateway into NayaNET.

The Hub is where a person can see, activate, control, connect, understand, and verify their intelligence. It sits between the person's identity and the intelligence systems they are authorized to use.

The Academy and the Hub are different. The Academy is where I learn. The Hub is where I enter and operate NayaNET.

## The architecture I want preserved

The core relationship is:

**Identity → Hub → Superbrain → Sharing Gate → Intelligent Blocks → Collective → Evidence**

The Hub is a control center/gateway, not a tenth world. The nine worlds remain destinations in one living environment, with Naya at the center.

The Hub must make these worlds and functions understandable and actionable rather than turning everything into a generic administrative dashboard.

## Personal intelligence sovereignty

My Superbrain is private. The Hub gives me visibility and control over it; NayaNET does not automatically own or expose it.

Personal Smart Notes and complete Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly, and Lifetime intelligence reports remain private and available to their owner.

Only selected wisdom or learned intelligence may cross the Sharing Gate, and only with deliberate consent.

The governing privacy rule is:

**Private by default. Shared by choice. Collective by consent. Public by decision.**

## Source connection

GitHub is the first authorized intelligence source connector for the prototype. It is not the permanent architecture.

The intended source path is:

**Authorized Source → Intelligence Source Adapter → Superbrain → Sharing Gate → Intelligent Block → Collective**

Other authorized sources can be added later.

## The first real proof

The first working proof should be:

**NayaNET Identity → Intelligent Hub → Connect GitHub → authorized Naya Power source → identify one real wisdom item → explicit consent → create one Intelligent Block → publish to Collective → evidence receipt.**

The Hub must show what actually happened, not merely what the system intends to happen.

## Living intelligence

Today should be a timestamped living intelligence feed. My Intelligence should show how my intelligence develops: Smart Notes, intelligence events, and available report horizons. Collective should show deliberately shared wisdom. Evidence should show what actually happened. Connection should show what is connected and authorized. Spaces should show where people and intelligence interact.

Every meaningful intelligence item should be traceable:

**Source → Event → Insight → Intelligent Block → Decision → Shared? → Collective Result.**

## Why this matters

The Hub is the front door to a compounding intelligence system. A person should be able to watch intelligence move from experience and source material into insight, action, shared wisdom, collective learning, and back into better decisions.

The Hub therefore has to be grounded in real evidence. If the intelligence capture layer cannot prove what happened, the Hub cannot be trusted.

## Design intent

The interface should feel like a living environment rather than a generic dashboard:

- Naya at the center.
- Power Player heartbeat.
- Living Sun / spatial intelligence map.
- Physical-feeling objects and dimensional destinations.
- Meaningful state and consequence.
- Clear pathways for what I can do next.

The principle is:

**MAKE DIGITAL OBJECTS FEEL PHYSICAL.**

## Core human insight

We are not building a machine to replace human intelligence. We are building a network that helps human intelligence connect with itself and with AI in ways that compound learning, action, and collective wisdom.
