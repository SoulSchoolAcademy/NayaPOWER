# NayaPOWER — Mission

## Mission
MAKE THE INTELLIGENCE REQUIRED TO ACCOMPLISH SOMETHING AVAILABLE, UNDERSTANDABLE, ACTIONABLE, VERIFIABLE, AND CONTINUOUSLY COMPOUNDING FOR THE HUMAN WHO WANTS TO ACCOMPLISH IT.

## What this means
AVAILABLE — relevant intelligence, context, tools, evidence, and capabilities can be reached.
UNDERSTANDABLE — complexity is distilled into usable understanding without destroying evidence or uncertainty.
ACTIONABLE — understanding becomes an appropriate next action.
VERIFIABLE — the system distinguishes claims from what actually happened.
COMPOUNDING — verified experience becomes reusable learning and future intelligence.

## Mission test
Every major capability must be explainable as a contribution to one or more of these five outcomes.

The product is not complete when Naya produces an answer. It is complete when useful movement toward the human outcome occurs at the required proof level and the resulting intelligence can survive into future work.
