4 · Build in it

This is a workspace, not an archive

The Box is where building starts. Not a picture of an operating system — the operating system, operated.

  1. Find the intelligence. Search above — by topic, by date, by his exact words — and open the entry that decides your build.
  2. Add it to your build. Take the entry's words and its source citation into your brief. The source is the proof; the Box never invents what it was not given.
  3. Open the Factory and PI from the linked view. The Factory holds the parts; PI holds what you are actually building. One view, one click, no hunting.
  4. Build — and put what you learned back in the Box. Smart-note it, mail the link, read the feed. Equalize, organize, optimize, maximize, distill.
GOLD — what's in it for you. The right note, in order, in your hand, when you need it — so nobody forgets and everybody learns and grows quicker. That is the speed of intelligence.
RED — why it matters to you. Every minute spent hunting for what Shawn already said is a minute not building. The Box ends the hunt.

About the ten-section note format: a full Smart Note has ten sections (nutshell, human, child, grandma, Naya, what it means, why it matters, how it connects, what's in it for you, the learning) plus the GOLD and RED layers. Those ten sections live in the filed Smart Note — not in the harvested Box entry. The harvested store carries title, text, date, type, topics and source only, so this page renders entries faithfully from what the harvest holds and does not invent sections the data does not carry. Where a filed note exists, the entry links to it — 4 of the 5 featured entries carry a link (SB-0021 has no filed note yet), and 4 of the 2,086 entries in the store carry one so far; the full backfill is owed as the reconcile job. The GOLD and RED layers on a featured entry are quoted from that entry's own words, where the entry supports them — SB-2007 carries a GOLD layer but no RED layer, and SB-1941 carries neither, because their own words state none.
