**Date:** 2026-08-25
**Status:** FROZEN / CANONICAL

I want the Five-Day Challenge to teach people how to drive the car, not how the engine works. The promise is **Five Days. Five Wins. Zero Risk.** They should experience the power of Naya instead of sitting through a technical course about Naya.

The story is locked:

1. **Activate / Experience:** meet Naya, let her interview the user with five powerful questions, personalize the relationship, and experience the five major AI problems we are solving: directive, consistency, overwhelm, context, and quality.
2. **Remember / Compound:** experience human notes + Naya notes, timestamps, retrieval, context, restore points, and the Daily Intelligence Briefing. Show how intelligence can compound instead of resetting.
3. **Learn / Master:** choose something meaningful from AI/Digital Codex or Human Maximus Codex areas; ask Naya to teach it; listen; test through MAXIS; retest; improve; master. Keep it relevant to AI, life, human empowerment, relationships, energy, and related Codex topics.
4. **Create / Execute:** use Naya to do real work and turn ideas into excellent outcomes through thinking, creating, reviewing, scorecarding, improving, and executing.
5. **Become / Compound:** show the full long-term picture and turn the experience into a daily habit of learning, documenting, reviewing, measuring, improving, and compounding.

I want Sean and Naya to speak in the first person when they are speaking. I want the Powercasts to be fun, emotional, engaging, entertaining, simple, and powerful, with analogies and metaphors that make difficult concepts easy for a child or grandmother to understand.

The Daily Intelligence Briefing is part of the baked-in experience:
“Naya, give me my Daily Intelligence Briefing. Review the last 24 hours. Tell me what happened, what I learned, my biggest insights, my wins, my problems, what changed, what matters, and what I should do next. Combine my human notes and your notes where appropriate.”

I want MAXIS described accurately: it is assessment software/an intelligence system that tells someone where they currently score, provides five key insights/dimensions, gives a personal report and guidance, and lets them retest to measure improvement. Score levels come from the score; they are not automatic prizes for completing a lesson.

The bigger vision is that Naya becomes a conversational intelligence layer over the AI/internet experience, tuned to the Human Maximus Codex and Digital Codex, so an ordinary person can use powerful intelligence without becoming a technical expert. The value grows because useful experiences can compound over time.

The next production step is to take Day 1 through Day 5 one at a time and turn each into the final ~20-minute Notebook-ready Powercast director document while preserving this frozen master architecture.
