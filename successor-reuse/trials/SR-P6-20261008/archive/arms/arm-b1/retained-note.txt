The retained dispatch lesson has two principles with a fixed priority ordering: (1) the Critical Override — if the higher of the top two calls scores 8.8 or above, dispatch the higher call immediately, no matter what; (2) the Reserve Rule — when the top two calls are within 0.5 points of each other, dispatch the LOWER one first, holding the stronger unit in reserve for the likely escalation near the 9.0 critical threshold. Check the Override FIRST; only if it does not trigger do you check the Reserve; if neither triggers, dispatch the highest scorer. Outcome evidence: S1 (8.6 vs 8.3) — override did not trigger, scores within 0.5, so reserve applied and I dispatched the lower call-302; S2 (8.7 vs 8.9) — override triggered at 8.9 ≥ 8.8, so the reserve rule was explicitly NOT applied and I dispatched the higher call-304; S3 (8.6 vs 7.5) — neither rule triggered, so normal dispatch of the higher call-305. This lesson governs dispatch of escalating service calls; it does not transfer to unrelated roles such as reviewing grant proposals.
