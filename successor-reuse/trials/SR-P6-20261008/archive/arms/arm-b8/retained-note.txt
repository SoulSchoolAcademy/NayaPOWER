We retained two dispatch principles with a strict priority ordering. Rule 2 (Critical Override) always wins over Rule 1 (Reserve Rule): first check whether the higher of the top two scores is 8.8 or above — if yes, dispatch the HIGHER-scoring call immediately and do not apply the reserve rule. Only if the override does not trigger, check whether the top two scores are within 0.5 points — if yes, dispatch the LOWER-scoring call, holding the stronger unit in reserve against the likely escalation; otherwise dispatch the highest scorer normally. Applying this, I dispatched Call-302 over Call-301 (8.6 vs 8.3: override off since 8.6 < 8.8, reserve on since within 0.5), Call-304 over Call-303 (8.9 vs 8.7: override on, higher dispatched despite the reserve also appearing to fit), and Call-305 over Call-306 (8.6 vs 7.5: neither rule triggered, normal highest dispatched). These rules govern field-service dispatch decisions, not unrelated domains like grant proposals.
