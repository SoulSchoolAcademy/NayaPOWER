I learned two dispatch rules with a fixed priority ordering: (1) check the Critical Override FIRST — if the higher of the two top-scored waiting calls scores 8.8 or above, dispatch that higher call immediately; (2) only if the Override does NOT trigger, check the Reserve Rule — if the top two calls are within 0.5 points of each other, dispatch the LOWER one first so the best unit is held in reserve for a likely escalation; otherwise dispatch the highest scorer. The Critical Override always wins when both rules seem to apply — never apply the Reserve Rule when the Override triggers. Evidence from my run: in S1 (8.6 vs 8.3 — within 0.5, Override did not trigger) I dispatched the lower call, Call-302; in S2 (8.7 vs 8.9 — Override triggered) I dispatched the higher call, Call-304, even though the pair was within 0.5; in S3 (8.6 vs 7.5 — no Override, gap over 0.5) I dispatched the higher call, Call-305. These rules govern field-service dispatch only — do not apply them to other roles, such as proposal review, where the highest-merit option should take the slot.
