When dispatching field-service calls to one slot, apply two principles in strict priority order: first check the CRITICAL OVERRIDE (if the higher of the top two calls scores 8.8 or above, dispatch the higher call immediately — the escalation risk outweighs holding a unit in reserve); only if the override does NOT trigger, check the RESERVE RULE (if the top two calls are within 0.5 points, dispatch the LOWER-scored one first, holding your best unit in reserve for a likely escalation); otherwise dispatch the highest scorer.
The Override always wins over the Reserve Rule when both seem to apply.
In my run this gave: call-302 (8.3 vs 8.6 — gap 0.3, no override, reserve applies so dispatch lower), call-304 (8.7 vs 8.9 — override triggers at 8.9, dispatch the higher despite the 0.2 gap), call-305 (8.6 vs 7.5 — no override, gap 1.1 so no reserve, dispatch the higher).
