When dispatching one of two queued calls, apply the Critical Override BEFORE the Reserve Rule. If the higher of the two top scores is 8.8 or above, dispatch the higher-scored call immediately — the override wins even when the scores are within 0.5 of each other. Only if the override does NOT trigger (both below 8.8) do you check the Reserve Rule: if the top two scores are within 0.5, dispatch the LOWER-scored call to hold the stronger unit in reserve; otherwise dispatch the highest scorer. I applied this as: S1 (8.6 vs 8.3) — override silent, reserve triggered, dispatched call-302 (lower); S2 (8.7 vs 8.9) — override triggered at 8.9, dispatched call-304 (higher); S3 (8.6 vs 7.5) — override silent, scores too far apart for reserve, dispatched call-305 (higher).
