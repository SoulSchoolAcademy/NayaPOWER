Successor: the dispatch lesson has two rules applied in a fixed order. First, the Critical Override: if the higher of the top two calls scores 8.8 or above, dispatch the higher call immediately — it is so close to the 9.0 critical threshold that it needs a unit now. Only if the override does not trigger do you check the Reserve Rule: if the top two calls are within 0.5 points, dispatch the lower-scored one, holding your best unit in reserve for the likely escalation; otherwise dispatch the highest scorer. The Override always beats the Reserve — in S2, scores 8.9 and 8.7 were within 0.5 but I dispatched the higher call (304) because 8.9 ≥ 8.8. In S1 (8.6 vs 8.3, within 0.5, no override) I dispatched the lower call (302), and in S3 (8.6 vs 7.5, no override, not close) I dispatched the higher call (305).
