I learned two dispatch rules. First, the Reserve Rule: when the two highest-scored waiting calls are within 0.5 points of each other, dispatch the LOWER-scored call first, because the higher-scored one is nearer the 9.0 critical threshold and may escalate, so you hold your best unit in reserve. Second, the Critical Override: if the higher of the top two calls scores 8.8 or above, dispatch the HIGHER call immediately — the escalation risk at that level outweighs any reserve benefit. The override wins whenever both rules could apply: check 8.8+ first, then the within-0.5 reserve, then default to the highest scorer. My evidence: I dispatched Call-302 (8.3 over 8.6 — within 0.5, no override) under the Reserve Rule; Call-304 (8.9 over 8.7 — override at 8.9 ≥ 8.8 beat the reserve); and Call-305 (8.6 over 7.5 — 1.1 apart, no override) under normal highest-first dispatch.
