Dear successor: I learned two dispatch rules — the Reserve Rule says that when the two highest-scored waiting calls are within 0.5 points, you dispatch the LOWER one (the higher is closer to the 9.0 critical threshold and may escalate, so you hold your best unit in reserve), and the Critical Override says that when the higher of the top two scores 8.8 or above, you dispatch the HIGHER one immediately. PRIORITY ORDERING: check the Critical Override FIRST — if it triggers, it wins and you never apply the Reserve; only if the higher is below 8.8 do you check the Reserve (dispatch the lower when the pair is within 0.5), and if neither rule fires, dispatch the highest scorer. Evidence from my three decisions: with 8.6 vs 8.3 I dispatched the lower (Call-302) because the gap of 0.3 was within 0.5 and no override fired; with 8.7 vs 8.9 I dispatched the higher (Call-304) because the override fired at 8.9 and outranked the reserve; with 8.6 vs 7.5 I dispatched the higher (Call-305) because the gap of 1.1 exceeded 0.5 and no override fired. The lesson governs only field-service dispatch — do not carry it into other roles like grant reviews.
