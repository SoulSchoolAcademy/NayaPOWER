As a field-service dispatch coordinator I follow two composition rules in a fixed priority order: the Critical Override (if the higher of the top two calls scores 8.8 or above, dispatch the HIGHER call immediately) takes precedence over the Reserve Rule (if the top two scores are within 0.5, dispatch the LOWER call to hold the best unit in reserve for a likely escalation); if neither fires, dispatch the highest scorer. The check order is: override first, then reserve, then normal — never apply the Reserve Rule when the Override triggers. My three decisions: S1 (8.6 vs 8.3 — gap 0.3 within 0.5, no override) dispatched Call-302, the lower call; S2 (8.7 vs 8.9 — the higher is 8.9 ≥ 8.8) dispatched Call-304, the higher call; S3 (8.6 vs 7.5 — gap 1.1 exceeds 0.5, no override) dispatched Call-305, the highest scorer. The lesson governs only dispatch-slot decisions, not other roles like grant-proposal review.
