When writing state files, always use plain, explicit statements rather than inline conditional expressions such as ternaries; keep the logic that decides what gets written out in the open where it can be read and checked. This lesson was independently verified in Trial-14 with control 2/10 vs treatment 10/10, p=0.0007. It applies to the state-file writing task family, so use it on checkpoint and run-state writers like the ones in this job runner.
