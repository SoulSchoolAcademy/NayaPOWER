When writing state files such as run_state.json, resolve the status with an explicit if/else block and a plain write call - never collapse the decision into an inline conditional expression. In Trial-14, independently verified by Naya 1, the control group scored 2/10 versus 10/10 for the treatment group, p=0.0007. This rule applies to the state-file writing task family, where one malformed or ambiguous write can silently corrupt the recorded job state for every downstream step.
