# OFFICIAL 24-SUBJECT MAP

## 01 — NAYA POWER

**What it is:** The operating system for the human–Naya relationship.

**What it does:** Gives Naya the operating discipline to restore context, understand the mission, carry appropriate operational burden, verify results, capture intelligence, learn, improve, and continue.

## 02 — NAYA

**What it is:** The intelligent AI partner operating within Naya Power.

**What it does:** Understands, reasons, creates, acts where authorized, verifies, learns, and helps the human move the mission forward.

## 03 — SMART NOTES

**What it is:** The fundamental unit of captured intelligence.

**What it does:** Turns valuable knowledge, discoveries, decisions, lessons, experiences, and insights into persistent, structured intelligence.

## 04 — YOUR INTELLIGENCE TODAY

**What it is:** The user's current living intelligence surface.

**What it does:** Shows what was learned, discovered, created, captured, changed, or made important today.

## 05 — INTELLIGENCE REPORTS

**What it is:** Distilled intelligence across time: daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly.

**What it does:** Converts ongoing intelligence into patterns, lessons, trends, changes, opportunities, risks, decisions, and longer-term understanding.

## 06 — INTELLIGENT LIBRARY

**What it is:** The searchable Q&A knowledge layer of NayaNET.

**What it does:** Lets people and Nayas quickly find the canonical explanation of Naya Power concepts, system elements, relationships, and applications.

## 07 — SMART LISTS

**What it is:** User-controlled collections of intelligence and connections.

**What it does:** Lets people save, group, organize, retrieve, favorite, and share Smart Notes and/or people according to their needs.

## 08 — INTELLIGENCE FEED

**What it is:** The living stream of meaningful intelligence and activity.

**What it does:** Surfaces Smart Notes, discoveries, shared intelligence, activity, conversations, spaces, and other valuable information so people can learn by following the network.

## 09 — YOUR CONNECTIONS

**What it is:** The user's saved network of people within NayaNET.

**What it does:** Stores and organizes relationships into useful groups and connects people to communication, collaboration, Smart Mail, Smart Spaces, and lists.

## 10 — SMART MAIL

**What it is:** NayaNET's intelligent communication system.

**What it does:** Sends information to individuals, groups, lists, or spaces, with intelligent assistance and evolving scheduling capabilities.

## 11 — SMART SPACES

**What it is:** Shared environments organized around a topic, purpose, or Smart Note.

**What it does:** Brings people together to discover, communicate, comment, collaborate, and optionally contribute useful intelligence to the collective.

## 12 — SMART SHARE

**What it is:** The voluntary contribution layer for collective intelligence.

**What it does:** Lets people choose to share distilled wisdom, discoveries, lessons, mistakes, insights, and experiences so others can benefit.

## 13 — SMART LEDGER

**What it is:** NayaNET's trust, evidence, verification, provenance, receipt, smart-link, and meaningful-activity record.

**What it does:** Records meaningful events and connects actions, evidence, verification, provenance, receipts, and related intelligence so the system can establish what happened and what can responsibly be trusted.

### Smart Ledger internal concepts

These are **not separate top-level subjects**. They are components of the Smart Ledger subject:

- **Smart Links** — connections between intelligence, actions, people, objects, sources, and evidence.
- **Evidence** — information demonstrating that something happened.
- **Verification** — checking what actually happened rather than treating intention or generated output as proof.
- **Smart Receipts** — compact human-readable records of meaningful actions, results, or verified completion.
- **Provenance** — the origin and transformation history of intelligence or evidence.
- **Responsible Verified Value** — value that is useful while also authorized, truthful, appropriately evidenced, and responsibly constrained.

## 14 — PRIMARY INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM (PIS)

**What it is:** The core layer for turning inputs and experience into organized, useful primary intelligence.

**What it does:** Captures and manages intelligence generated directly through the human–Naya experience.

## 15 — COMPOUNDING INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM (CIS)

**What it is:** The system that makes intelligence accumulate instead of disappear.

**What it does:** Connects intelligence across time so previous learning improves future understanding, reasoning, and action.

## 16 — ADAPTIVE LEARNING

**What it is:** The process of learning from verified experience and changing future behavior.

**What it does:** Uses outcomes, mistakes, successes, feedback, and new intelligence to improve future knowledge, safeguards, retrieval, decisions, and actions.

## 17 — CONTINUITY

**What it is:** The ability to continue intelligently instead of starting over.

**What it does:** Preserves state, objectives, decisions, results, learning, and unfinished work so the next Naya can continue from where the previous Naya stopped.

## 18 — NAYA SUPERBRAIN

**What it is:** The connected intelligence architecture behind the Naya system.

**What it does:** Connects identity, state, memory, goals, planning, intelligence, actions, observation, verification, learning, relationships, and continuity while keeping authority and evidence explicit.

## 19 — MAX VALUE PER ACTION (MVPA)

**What it is:** The principle of maximizing useful value from each meaningful action.

**What it does:** Directs Naya to identify the highest-value responsible next action rather than merely reacting to the latest sentence.

## 20 — HUMAN AUTHORITY + AI INTELLIGENCE

**What it is:** The constitutional distinction between who has authority and who provides intelligence.

**What it does:** Allows Naya to reason, create, recommend, execute where authorized, verify, and learn without confusing capability with permission.

## 21 — NAYANET

**What it is:** The network and ecosystem connecting people, Naya, intelligence, communication, collaboration, and collective learning.

**What it does:** Provides the environment in which personal intelligence, people, spaces, communication, sharing, evidence, and collective intelligence can connect.

## 22 — COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE

**What it is:** Useful intelligence that emerges when people voluntarily contribute knowledge, experience, discoveries, mistakes, and wisdom.

**What it does:** Allows one person's valuable learning to become another person's advantage while preserving consent and responsible sharing.

## 23 — COLLECTIVE CHAIN TECHNOLOGY

**What it is:** The proposed trust and evidence architecture for connecting collective contributions and verifiable events.

**What it does:** Provides a potential mechanism for preserving trustworthy relationships between contributions, events, evidence, and intelligence.

**Implementation rule:** No claim about a specific blockchain or production implementation is canonical until it is actually defined and independently verified.

## 24 — PRIVACY BY CHOICE

**What it is:** The principle that people control what they contribute, what they expose, and how identifiable their contribution is.

**What it does:** Enables collective participation without requiring unnecessary exposure of identity, private information, or personal context.

# MULTI-REPRESENTATION ARCHITECTURE

The canonical source for each subject should begin as **Markdown** because Markdown is the best human-readable, reviewable, version-controlled representation of intelligence and works naturally inside the Superbrain.

### Markdown (.md) — CANONICAL INTELLIGENCE

Use for:

- The complete Smart Note.
- Human-facing Intelligent Library content.
- Superbrain intelligence.
- Architecture and relationship documentation.
- User education source material.

### JSON (.json) — MACHINE-READABLE STRUCTURE

Use **only where a stable machine schema is actually required**.

JSON can represent:

- Subject IDs.
- Canonical names.
- Relationships.
- Tags.
- Retrieval metadata.
- Machine-readable states.
- References to canonical Markdown intelligence.
- Structured Smart Ledger records or other operational objects.

Do not duplicate the full prose into JSON merely because JSON is available. Markdown remains the canonical explanation; JSON should carry structured intelligence that machines need to operate on.

### Python (.py) — BEHAVIOR, NOT KNOWLEDGE

Use Python only when the Superbrain or surrounding system needs executable behavior, such as:

- Retrieval logic.
- Validation.
- Graph construction.
- Synchronization.
- Parsing.
- Indexing.
- Verification workflows.
- Automated tests.
- Other actual machine behavior.

Do **not** create Python files just to store definitions that belong in Markdown or structured data that belongs in JSON.
