01 · Color

Color has four jobs. Never cross them.

Obsidian is the field. White is the light — text and idle rims. Purple is the soul — chrome and Naya's presence. The spectrum gives each thing its identity — and never shouts over the brand.

OBSIDIAN

Field. The world and dominant canvas. Deep, never gray.

WHITE

Light. Text 99% of the time. Every idle rim.

PURPLE

Soul. Chrome accent. Naya's presence. Never a flood.

SPECTRUMMeaning. One color per thing. Adjacent things never share a hue.

02 · Buttons

Black heart. White voice. Visible skin.

A button reads as a button before it is touched: #050505 center, white text, a real icon, a 1.5px+ white rim at rest — then purple fire for chrome, or its own spectrum color when it belongs to a themed thing. The glyph is a promise: it shows what happens.

IDLE

White rim visible. Quiet.

HOVER

Own color ignites. Lifts.

PRESS

Compresses. Alive.

03 · Boards

One board. One color. A destination you enter.

The board is the intelligent object: theme-edged, jewel-marked, elevated. One spectrum color per board — spine, kicker, corner jewel all speak it. Text stays white. Inside, meaning nests: the nutshell, then what it means, how to use it.

04 · Typography

Size separates. Color doesn't.

24 for headlines. 18 for body — most text lives here. 14 for details and evidence. White 99% of the time. Display headlines get a voice: serif, light-carved, breathing. Hierarchy comes from size — never from gray, never from colored text.

HEADLINE · 24PX

Your intelligence, collected.

Serif voice. Light-carved. It leads, never shouts.

BODY · 18PX

Keep your people close. Organize them, remember them, reach them.

Most text lives here. White, generous, readable.

DETAIL · 14PX

Evidence lives here: PR #1858 · tip 78661f59 · 40/40 tests.

The floor. Nothing readable ever goes smaller.

◆ 05 · Jewels

Every bullet a jewel. Never twice in a row.

Bullets are drawn jewels, not dots — each glowing its own spectrum color, cycling magenta → purple → blue → green → gold. One global sequence per page: no two adjacent jewels ever share a color. Section heads and board corners carry jewels in their own theme color.

◆✧▲✦◆ the five-jewel cycle
  • ◆Magenta opens — the richest color leads the list.
  • ✧Purple deepens — the eye knows this is a new point.
  • ▲Blue carries — knowledge moving down the page.
  • ✦Green follows — alive and well, never the same twice.
  • ◆Gold closes — the headline point earns the brightest jewel.

06 · Truth

Truth is part of the visual language.

A claim and a proof must never look the same. Scores move on evidence, never optimism. What is verified glows green; what is merely claimed stays quiet until it earns its light.

One contract, three tongues.

Same truth, three languages. The machine is normative — checkable predicates, no adjectives. The AI layer is procedure — what a builder does, in order. The human layer is philosophy — what Naya feels like, and why. On conflict, the machine wins.

08 · The fifteen laws

The laws, at a glance.

Eight shown in full — each burning its own spectrum color. The rest live in the contract. Hover any card: it ignites in its own color, never anyone else's.

LAW 1

Score the experience, never the code.

A 9.5 on CSS is worthless if the rendered page is a 5. The lived page is the only scorecard.

LAW 2

Back it or bench it.

9.0+ ships. Below 9 goes back to the bench. No exceptions, no almost.

LAW 4

Color has jobs. Never cross them.

Chrome acts. Text speaks. Theme identifies. Adjacent things never share a hue.

LAW 5

White light at rest, own color on touch.

Visible 1.5px+ rims idle. Ignition belongs to the thing being touched.

LAW 6

Black heart, white voice, visible skin.

#050505 center, white text and icon, real rim. The glyph is a promise.

LAW 7

Size separates, color doesn't.

24 headlines, 18 body, 14 details. White 99%. Headers get a voice.

LAW 9

Icons are drawn, not shrunk.

Vector geometry, readable at 16px. A shrunken render is mud.

LAW 13

Verify before claiming.

Read the file. Count the matches. Watch it render. Then say it's done.

09 · The gate

Nothing reaches Shawn unchecked.

Every page runs the gate before it ships. Each check is mechanical where it can be, human where it must be. One fail — back to the bench.

Pre-ship checklist · all must pass

I · SEE

  • ◆Rendered and looked at — screenshot reviewed, not assumed.
  • ✧No two adjacent jewels share a color — checked by machine.
  • ▲All text white or theme — zero gray, checked by machine.
  • ✦Type at 24 / 18 / 14 — headlines, body, details.

II · PROVE

  • ◆Every board: one color, spine + kicker + corner jewel agree.
  • ◆Real logo. Never a drawn substitute.
  • ✧Human words first — a child can follow it; technicals are evidence.
  • ▲Honest score against the fifteen laws. 9.0+ or it doesn't ship.
