# NAYA DESIGN LAWS

Verbatim from the N5 generation (newest). These are not suggestions.

> The operational canon — mechanics, hexes, the forbidden list, and the final
> law — lives in [DESIGN-LAWS.md](./DESIGN-LAWS.md). These eight are the source.

## The eight laws

1. **Score the experience, never the code.** A 9.5 on CSS is worthless if the
   rendered page is a 5. The lived page is the only scorecard.
2. **Back it or bench it.** 9.0+ ships. Below 9 goes back to the bench. No
   exceptions, no almost.
3. **Color has jobs. Never cross them.** Chrome acts. Text speaks. Theme
   identifies. Adjacent things never share a hue.
4. **White light at rest, own color on touch.** Visible 1.5px+ rims idle.
   Ignition belongs to the thing being touched.
5. **Black heart, white voice, visible skin.** #050507 center, white text and
   icon, real rim. The glyph is a promise.
6. **Size separates, color doesn't.** 24 / 18 / 14. Nothing else. White 99%
   of the time. Headers get a voice. (Resolution: Law 52 governs — 24/18/14 is
   the mnemonic. See DECISIONS.md.)
7. **Icons are drawn, not shrunk.** Vector geometry, readable at 16px. A
   shrunken render is mud.
8. **Verify before claiming.** Read the file. Count the matches. Watch it
   render. Then say it's done.

## One X, one job

- **One library, one DNA.** Buttons, boards, icons, inputs, tabs, modals,
  toasts, tables, navigation — each element exists once, canonically. Pages
  consume blocks; they do not reinvent them. A page with a private button
  class is drift. (Law 91)
- **One canonical button.** `.naya-btn` is the only button system. (Law 21)
- **One spectrum step per board.** (Law 45)
- **One accent per element. One jewel per board.** (Law 16)
- **One system, no forks.**
- **Color has four jobs. Never cross them.** Obsidian = field, white = light,
  purple = soul, spectrum = meaning.
- **One contract, three tongues.** Same truth, three languages. On conflict,
  the machine wins.

## Foundation laws (Naya_2)

1. **Spectrum is meaning.** Color separates jobs, relationships, identity and
   rhythm. It never becomes a random rainbow.
2. **Material has a soul.** Black creates depth. White reveals edges. Purple
   signals the presence of Naya.
3. **Intelligence is structural.** Every component explains what it is, what
   it can do, and what state it is in.
4. **Excellence reproduces.** The rules are tokens, anatomy, states and
   tests — not one beautiful page that cannot be repeated.

## Standing principles

- **Prime directive:** premium, not fancy. Living depth, always. Zero flat.
  Flat is a defect, not a style.
- **LAW ZERO — Readability supremacy.** No glow, gradient, accent, or depth
  effect may ever make anything harder to read.
- **95% restraint, 5% fire.** Never monochrome dead, never neon at rest.
- **Alive at rest, brilliant on approach.** If it doesn't move, it's dead —
  but motion serves meaning, never decoration.
- **Animated ≠ alive. Stored ≠ learned. Claimed ≠ proven.**
- **Grandma standard.** A non-technical reader must follow every board.
- **Take the actual code.** The block is the memory — copy the specimen, don't
  re-teach the button.
- **Principles don't survive contact with a deadline.** So every principle
  becomes a checkable predicate.
- **Nothing reaches Shawn unchecked.** Scorecard before showing: per-area
  floor 9.0; nothing below 9.0 is presented.

## The ship gate — all six, or it doesn't ship

1. White edge light is visible on every card and button at rest.
2. Themed elements ignite their own color; chrome ignites purple.
3. Every button has a black heart, white voice, and a truthful icon.
4. Type hierarchy follows the scale — size separates, color doesn't.
5. Copy is plain enough for anyone to understand on first read.
6. Scored the rendered page honestly — 9+ or back to the bench.
