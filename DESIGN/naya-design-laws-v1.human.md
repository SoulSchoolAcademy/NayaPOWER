# The Design Laws — in plain words (DRAFT)

**What this is:** the rulebook for what ships and what doesn't. Every button, board, and page in the Hub gets judged against these laws. If a law says it, builders obey it. If it's not a law, it's just taste — and taste doesn't survive a deadline.

**Where they come from:** your words, Shawn — nothing flat, color with meaning, jewels not dots — plus the design reviews, the vision laws, and the hundred laws we already had. Nothing here contradicts the hundred laws; these say the same truth in builder words, and add the new things the hundred didn't cover.

**Status:** DRAFT. These become official only on your word.

## The six ideas, simply

**1. Nothing is flat.** Every button and board sits off the page — it catches light on top, casts a shadow below. And it moves: hover it and it lifts, press it and it pushes in. If it doesn't respond to your finger, it's not done.

**2. Color has logic.** Black ground, always. Purple is Naya's color — her presence, her chrome. Every other color means something fixed: blue is understanding, green is connected-and-well, gold is a rare achievement, magenta is what matters to you. Colors march in rainbow order down the page, never stacked in walls of one color. And contrast rules everything — if you can't see it at a glance, it fails.

**3. Jewels, not dots.** Bullets are little gems with facets. Icons are drawn crisp, never shrunk-down pictures. Every icon keeps its promise — a chevron goes somewhere, a plus adds something. Avatars sit in jewel rings, because people are precious.

**4. She has a face, and she moves as energy.** Naya is beautiful and she has a face — when you see her, it's her. Her presence moves as energy: light, pulse, warmth in the design. The one place she's pure energy is the voice module — there she speaks, and no fake face pretends otherwise.

**5. Motion is alive, never fake.** Light moves first, words follow. Numbers breathe. Nothing pretends — a demo says demo, live means live evidence. And anyone who needs stillness gets it: the product works fully with motion turned off.

**6. Taste you already approved.** New work must feel like it belongs to the world you loved. When in doubt, match the specimen. Copy reads like an invitation, not documentation. And every page is scored on the render — your eye outranks every law.

## The six ways builders break it (the rogues' gallery)

1. **Flat everything** — dead panels, labels pretending to be buttons.
2. **Walls of one color** — five purple boards stacked.
3. **Dots, not jewels** — gray dots where gems belong.
4. **Fake liveness** — spinners over nothing, success before it happened.
5. **Dead headers** — the one still thing on a living page.
6. **The dead button** — black on black, hairline border, flat. The compound failure: invisible AND dead AND flat, all at once.
