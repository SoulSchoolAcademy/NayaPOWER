# NayaPOWER Smart Note #13 — Smart Share

**Date:** 2026-09-11  
**Subject:** Smart Share  
**System:** NayaPOWER / NayaNET Intelligent Hub  
**Status:** CANONICAL PRODUCT DEFINITION — runtime implementation and end-to-end behavior remain to be independently verified where noted.

## 1. IN A NUTSHELL

**Smart Share is NayaNET's collective-intelligence syncing and consent layer.**

It is where a human can explicitly agree to let NayaNET distill valuable wisdom from eligible intelligence they create — especially Smart Notes and Daily Intelligence Reports — and contribute genuinely new, reusable wisdom to the collective intelligence of the network.

Smart Share is not primarily “share this object with this person.” It is the agreement and activation layer that lets an individual superbrain contribute wisdom to the collective superbrain.

The core promise is simple:

> **Your life can teach people you will never meet.**

Smart Share makes that possible while preserving the person's privacy and identity boundaries.

The collective contribution is **not a copy of the user's private Superbrain, Smart Notes, reports, conversations, identity, alias, face, or raw personal history.** It is derived, generalized wisdom that has been intentionally authorized for collective use.

The collective system must also distinguish **new intelligence from intelligence it already knows**. Repeated or already-understood wisdom should not create duplicate learning events or unnecessary notifications.

## 2. HUMAN

Most people spend their entire lives learning things.

They discover what works. They make mistakes. They recover. They have breakthroughs. They develop wisdom. They learn lessons that could save someone else years of pain.

But most of that intelligence disappears with the individual.

Social media can broadcast thoughts, but it does not provide a disciplined system for turning millions of people's lived experience into anonymous, reusable collective wisdom.

Smart Share is designed to change that.

When you choose to participate, NayaNET can take eligible intelligence from your Smart Notes and Daily Intelligence Reports, distill the valuable wisdom, protect your identity, compare it with what the collective already knows, and contribute genuinely new intelligence to the collective.

You are not giving NayaNET your private life.

**You are choosing to let the wisdom your life produces help humanity.**

## 3. CHILD

Imagine a giant clubhouse where everyone has learned something useful.

One kid learns how to build the coolest paper airplane. Another learns how to fix a mistake without giving up. Someone else discovers a better way to solve a hard problem.

Instead of keeping all those lessons in separate notebooks, everyone can choose to put the useful lessons into a giant **Wisdom Book**.

But nobody has to write their name in it.

The clubhouse learns the lesson — not who gave it.

And if the Wisdom Book already knows the lesson, you do not need to write the same thing again.

That is Smart Share.

## 4. GRANDMA

Think of Smart Share as voluntarily putting the best lessons from your life into a giant anonymous library that everyone can benefit from.

You remain you. Your private life remains private. You decide whether to participate.

NayaNET takes the useful lesson, removes unnecessary personal identity and raw private detail, checks whether the collective already knows it, and — when there is genuinely valuable new wisdom — adds it to the collective knowledge of the network.

It is like saying:

> “I learned this the hard way. Maybe somebody else shouldn't have to.”

## 5. NAYA

My job with Smart Share is not to expose you.

My job is to help transform authorized human experience into useful collective intelligence.

I should:

- respect the human's explicit choice to participate;
- preserve the boundary between private Superbrain intelligence and eligible collective contributions;
- distill reusable wisdom instead of copying raw personal material;
- remove unnecessary identity and attribution from the collective object;
- preserve provenance and evidence internally where needed without exposing contributor identity;
- compare candidate wisdom against existing collective intelligence;
- avoid duplicate learning events;
- notify the collective only when genuinely new collective wisdom has been learned or otherwise becomes eligible for publication under the system's rules;
- preserve uncertainty rather than pretending every contribution is truth;
- never treat popularity, repetition, or consensus as verification;
- preserve the contributor's ability to revoke or change participation according to the governing consent and revocation system;
- never use collective contribution as authority over the person's sovereign Superbrain.

The governing principle is:

**Human authority chooses whether and how to contribute. AI intelligence helps determine what useful wisdom can responsibly be derived.**

## 6. MACHINE

The intended Smart Share flow is:

`PRIVATE SUPERBRAIN → ELIGIBLE SMART NOTES / DAILY INTELLIGENCE REPORTS → EXPLICIT SMART SHARE CONSENT → WISDOM DISTILLATION → IDENTITY / RAW-SOURCE PROTECTION → EXISTING-COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE CHECK → NEW? → COLLECTIVE CANDIDATE → VERIFICATION → COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE → ACTIVITY FEED / FUTURE RETRIEVAL`

Decision branch:

`KNOWN / ALREADY UNDERSTOOD → NO DUPLICATE COLLECTIVE EVENT → NO NEW-LEARNING NOTIFICATION`

`GENUINELY NEW → CANDIDATE → REVIEW / VERIFY → PUBLISH / PROMOTE ACCORDING TO AUTHORIZED RULES → COLLECTIVE LEARNING → NOTIFICATION WHEN APPROPRIATE`

The collective object should be generalized, reusable wisdom rather than a raw personal record.

The existing Collective Intelligence Event architecture requires `identity_included = false` and explicitly separates collective intelligence from contributor identity and raw source material.

## 7. LEARNING

Smart Share creates a compounding intelligence loop:

`INDIVIDUAL EXPERIENCE → SMART NOTE → DAILY INTELLIGENCE REPORT → DISTILLED WISDOM → CONSENT → COLLECTIVE CANDIDATE → EXISTING-INTELLIGENCE CHECK → VERIFICATION → COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE → FUTURE RETRIEVAL → APPLICATION → OUTCOME → NEW LEARNING`

This creates a fundamental distinction:

**Sharing information is not the same as creating collective intelligence.**

The system must learn from the contribution, not merely store it.

A contribution that is already known should not inflate the apparent amount of collective learning. A contribution that is new but unverified should remain appropriately uncertain. A verified contribution can become durable collective intelligence and can later influence future reasoning and action.

The system must never claim “the collective learned” merely because a Smart Note was created, a report was generated, or data was stored.

## 8. ULTIMATE MEANING

Smart Share is one of the mechanisms that can turn NayaNET from a network of individual intelligence systems into a **collective intelligence network**.

Imagine millions of people each carrying a lifetime of experiences, discoveries, mistakes, solutions, insights, and wisdom.

If those people voluntarily contribute the best of what they learn, and an intelligence system can safely distill, compare, verify, connect, and reuse that wisdom, the network can become smarter through the participation of its people.

That is the deeper purpose.

**Not more content. More wisdom.**

**Not more noise. More useful intelligence.**

**Not one person's brain replacing another. Millions of people helping the collective become wiser.**

A powerful human reason for Smart Share is:

> **“I wish people could learn and grow from my life, my experiences, my wisdom, and my knowledge.”**

Smart Share is designed to make that possible at network scale.

## 9. HOW IT CONNECTS

Smart Share connects directly to:

- **Smart Notes** — primary individual intelligence events that can contain lessons and wisdom.
- **Daily Intelligence Reports** — periodic distillations that can surface higher-level learning.
- **Your Superbrain** — remains the sovereign individual intelligence system; Smart Share does not mean sharing everything inside it.
- **Collective Intelligence** — receives eligible, distilled, privacy-protected contributions.
- **Collective Chain Technology** — can provide the downstream structure for verified collective intelligence.
- **Activity Feed / Smart Feed** — can surface genuinely new collective wisdom and meaningful collective-learning events.
- **Intelligence Hub** — the individual environment where intelligence is captured, organized, found, and compounded.
- **Smart Spaces / Your Connections** — separate social and relationship systems; Smart Share is not equivalent to connecting with a person.
- **Smart Mail** — separate communication system; Smart Share is not an email/message-delivery action.

Three major agreements around the NayaNET connection experience are conceptually distinct:

1. **Connect the Intelligent Hub to the Superbrain** — access and use the user's intelligence environment.
2. **Agree to share wisdom** — opt into collective intelligence contribution through Smart Share.
3. **Agree to connect with other humans** — enable human connection through Smart Spaces and Connections according to user choice and system rules.

These agreements must not be conflated.

## 10. HOW TO APPLY IT

### Example 1 — A life lesson

A person creates a Smart Note after discovering a principle that dramatically improved how they solve a recurring problem.

If Smart Share is enabled, Naya can identify the reusable lesson, remove unnecessary personal detail, compare it with existing collective intelligence, and — if it is genuinely new and passes the required checks — contribute it to the collective.

### Example 2 — A breakthrough

A user has a major breakthrough during the day. Their Daily Intelligence Report later captures the deeper lesson.

Smart Share can use that eligible intelligence as a candidate source for collective wisdom rather than simply broadcasting the raw report.

### Example 3 — Already known

A user contributes an excellent insight, but the collective already contains substantially equivalent verified wisdom.

The system should recognize that the intelligence is already known, avoid creating a duplicate learning event, and avoid pretending the network learned something new.

### Example 4 — New collective wisdom

A genuinely new and useful pattern emerges from a person's experience.

The system creates a candidate collective intelligence event, preserves appropriate provenance and uncertainty, performs the required verification, and — if promoted — makes the new collective wisdom available for future retrieval and, where appropriate, surfaces the learning event in the Activity Feed.

### Example 5 — Identity protection

The collective intelligence object should not say who contributed it.

No face. No real name. No alias. No public identity attribution.

The system may retain minimal private consent, audit, abuse-prevention, or revocation records when legitimately required, but those records are not part of the public collective intelligence object.

- benefit from the collective intelligence created by other people's experiences;
- help create a network where wisdom compounds rather than disappears.

The deepest benefit is simple:

**You do not have to keep the lessons of your life locked inside your own lifetime.**

# GITHUB-ONLY CANONICAL SYSTEM INTELLIGENCE

## 12. SYSTEM ROLE

Smart Share is the consent and activation layer between individual intelligence and collective intelligence.

It is distinct from Smart Share as an object-sharing feature. The canonical product meaning is collective intelligence contribution and synchronization.

## 13. CONSENT CONTRACT

Smart Share requires explicit user choice before eligible individual intelligence is considered for collective contribution.

Smart Share does not mean:

- NayaNET can inspect or expose everything in the user's Superbrain;
- every Smart Note is automatically public;
- every Daily Intelligence Report is automatically public;
- contributor identity is published;
- raw conversations become collective objects;
- private data becomes collective data merely because it exists in the Hub.

The locked conceptual boundary is:

`PRIVATE SUPERBRAIN REMAINS PRIVATE`

`ELIGIBLE INTELLIGENCE + EXPLICIT CONSENT → DISTILLATION → ANONYMOUS COLLECTIVE CONTRIBUTION`

## 14. PRIVACY AND ANONYMITY CONTRACT

The public/collective intelligence object must not expose:

- contributor real name;
- contributor alias / username;
- face or identifying image;
- email address;
- GitHub username;
- repository URL;
- access token;
- conversation transcript;
- private-memory fields;
- unnecessary raw personal source.

The existing Collective Intelligence Event schema is authoritative for the requirement that `identity_included` be false.

Internal consent/audit/revocation records may exist separately where legitimately required for system integrity, abuse prevention, legal obligations, or user control. They must not leak into the collective intelligence object.

## 15. COLLECTIVE DEDUPLICATION CONTRACT

Every candidate contribution must be checked against existing collective intelligence before creating a new collective learning event.

`CANDIDATE → DISTILL → COMPARE →`

- `KNOWN / EQUIVALENT → DO NOT DUPLICATE`
- `NEW / MATERIALLY DISTINCT → CONTINUE TO REVIEW / VERIFICATION`

The comparison should consider semantic equivalence, not merely exact text matching.

The purpose is to prevent:

- duplicate knowledge;
- inflated learning counts;
- repetitive Activity Feed notifications;
- false claims of new discovery;
- unnecessary collective storage.

## 16. COLLECTIVE LEARNING NOTIFICATION CONTRACT

Activity Feed notification should represent a meaningful collective-learning event, not every individual contribution.

Preferred conceptual trigger:

`NEW COLLECTIVE WISDOM VERIFIED / PROMOTED → ACTIVITY FEED LEARNING EVENT`

Not:

`USER CREATED SMART NOTE → ACTIVITY FEED “COLLECTIVE LEARNED”`

If the contribution is already known, no new-learning notification should be emitted.

If the candidate is still uncertain or unverified, the interface must not represent it as established collective truth.

## 17. COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE EVENT RELATIONSHIP

Smart Share should produce or feed the existing Collective Intelligence Event (CIE) architecture rather than inventing a parallel collective-learning object model.

The CIE remains downstream of authorized contribution and supports:

`CANDIDATE → REVIEWED → PUBLISHED → VALIDATED → SUPERSEDED`

or candidate → rejected.

The collective object is derived intelligence, not a source Note Event, repository, conversation, or contributor record.

## 18. PROVENANCE WITHOUT ATTRIBUTION

The system must preserve enough provenance to understand evidence, scope, confidence, and derivation without exposing contributor identity.

This creates a deliberate distinction:

`PROVENANCE ≠ PUBLIC ATTRIBUTION`

Provenance supports verification and intellectual integrity. Public anonymity protects the contributor's identity boundary.

## 19. REVOCATION AND USER CONTROL

Smart Share must eventually support a clear consent lifecycle, including at minimum:

- enabled / opted in;
- disabled / opted out;
- effective time or version where needed;
- contribution eligibility rules;
- revocation behavior;
- treatment of already-published collective intelligence;
- auditability of consent changes.

Exact runtime behavior remains an implementation question until verified.

## 20. SMART SHARE VS ORDINARY SHARING

| Ordinary sharing | Smart Share |
|---|---|
| Sends or exposes an object | Authorizes collective intelligence contribution |
| Usually has a specific recipient/audience | Collective destination |
| Often preserves the shared object's identity/context | Distills reusable wisdom |
| Can be social or communicative | Primarily intelligence/consent infrastructure |
| May share raw content | Should contribute derived wisdom, not unnecessary raw source |
| Often immediate | Can involve distillation, comparison, review, verification |

Smart Share therefore should not be reduced to a generic share button.

The broader NayaNET connection/setup experience can be modeled as three distinct permissions/agreements:

### A. Intelligent Hub ↔ Superbrain

The user connects the intelligence environment so Naya can work with the user's authorized Hub intelligence.

### C. Human Connection ↔ Smart Spaces / Connections

The user chooses whether and how they connect with other people through NayaNET's social/relationship layer.

These are separate authorities and must not silently imply one another.

## 22. CONSTITUTIONAL BOUNDARIES

Smart Share must respect:

- human sovereignty over the private Superbrain;
- explicit consent for collective contribution;

- least necessary disclosure;
- provenance and evidence integrity;

`CONSENT → ELIGIBILITY → DISTILLATION → PRIVACY PROTECTION → EXISTING-INTELLIGENCE CHECK → VERIFICATION → PROMOTION`

Smart Share is a key bridge in the compounding intelligence architecture:

`INDIVIDUAL INTELLIGENCE → EXPLICIT SHARE / CONSENT → COLLECTIVE CANDIDATE → PROVENANCE + SCOPE → VERIFICATION → COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE → FUTURE RETRIEVAL → APPLICATION → OUTCOME → LEARNING`
