# 11. SAVE VS FAVORITE

These must not be confused.

## SAVE

"I want to retain this."

Save places the intelligence into the user's persistent intelligence collection.

## FAVORITE

"This is especially important to me."

Favorite is a stronger personal signal.

Therefore:

```text
SAVE
=
KEEP THIS

FAVORITE
=
THIS MATTERS ESPECIALLY TO ME
```

Both belong to Smart Lists.

A Favorite may automatically appear in the user's Favorites view, while Saved intelligence belongs to Saved Intelligence.
