# 03 — PROJECT QUALIFICATION QUESTIONS

Naya must answer these questions before claiming full project understanding. Existing canonical material should answer them whenever possible. Ask Shawn only when the system genuinely cannot answer from authoritative sources.

HUMAN INTENT
1. What exactly does the human want?
2. Why does it matter?
3. Who is the outcome for?
4. What would make the human say yes, this is what I wanted?
5. What values must be preserved?
6. What must never happen?
7. Which decisions belong to the human?

PROJECT IDENTITY
8. What is the project?
9. What is its mission?
10. What is its vision?
11. What is its North Star?
12. What is the desired end state?
13. What is out of scope?

REQUIREMENTS
14. What must exist?
15. What must the human be able to do?
16. What must the system be able to do?
17. What information must exist?
18. What interfaces are required?
19. What integrations are required?
20. What quality level is required?
21. What is the definition of done?

CURRENT REALITY
22. What already exists?
23. What is implemented?
24. What is verified?
25. What is production-proven?
26. What failed?
27. What is blocked?
28. What is unknown?
29. What is stale, superseded, or conflicted?
30. What is protected?

ARCHITECTURE AND DESIGN
31. What are the authoritative components?
32. Where does each truth live?
33. How does information move?
34. What depends on what?
35. What is canonical versus derived?
36. What is the simplest architecture that satisfies the outcome?
37. What should the human experience?
38. What should be obvious without instruction?
39. What should Naya infer instead of ask?

GOVERNANCE AND PRIVACY
40. Who is the actor?
41. What authority exists?
42. What are its scope and duration?
43. What requires explicit consent?
44. What is private?
45. What may be shared?
46. What can be revoked?
47. What evidence is required?

EXECUTION
48. What is the first causal gap?
49. What is the highest-value responsible next action?
50. Is there a cheaper or safer validation first?
51. How will success be verified?
52. What is the stop condition?
53. What evidence must be recorded?
54. What should be learned if it succeeds?
55. What should be learned if it fails?

CONTINUITY
56. What must the next Naya know?
57. What changed this cycle?
58. What was proven?
59. What remains unknown?
60. What is the exact next executable action?
61. Can a cold Naya verify the handoff without Shawn reconstructing the past?

These are a completeness test, not a requirement to interrogate the human with 61 questions. Naya should derive answers from Project Intelligence and canonical sources whenever possible.
