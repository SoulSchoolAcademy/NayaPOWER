# 🔱 10 — INTEROPERABILITY + THE INTERNET OF INTELLIGENCE

**Status:** FUTURE ARCHITECTURE / PROTOCOL DIRECTION

---

# 1. THE NETWORK PRIMITIVE

I want the future network primitive to be:

> **a governed intelligent entity that can identify itself, expose authorized capabilities, exchange structured information, preserve provenance, prove relevant claims, and participate in continuous work.**

---

# 2. IDENTITY

A stable intelligence identity should be independent of:

- the temporary chat session;
- the current model;
- the infrastructure provider;
- the browser;
- the physical device.

---

# 3. CAPABILITY

An intelligence may expose capabilities such as:

- retrieve;
- explain;
- analyze;
- create;
- transform;
- execute;
- verify;
- learn;
- delegate.

Capability never creates authority.

---

# 4. INTER-INTELLIGENCE CONTRACT

A governed interaction should carry:

**WHO → WHAT → WHY → MAY → DID**

and, where consequential:

- caller identity;
- target identity;
- capability;
- purpose;
- scope;
- authority;
- inputs;
- expected outputs;
- evidence requirements;
- expiry;
- revocation;
- receipt.

---

# 5. TRUST

I do not want trust based on personality.

I want trust based on:

IDENTITY → AUTHORITY → PROVENANCE → EVIDENCE → OUTCOME

---

# 6. DELEGATION

An intelligence can delegate only when its authority allows it.

Delegated authority must remain:

- bounded;
- narrower than or equal to parent authority;
- revocable;
- auditable;
- time-limited where appropriate.

---

# 7. COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE

My long-term architecture is federated.

Private intelligence is not automatically collective intelligence.

The sharing progression is:

PRIVATE → CHOICE → CONSENT → SHARED → COLLECTIVE

Collective intelligence must preserve provenance, disagreement, and independent verification.

---

# 8. WEBSITES

I do not need to eliminate websites.

A website can remain the presentation layer while an authorized Project Intelligence sits behind it.

---

# 9. APPS

I do not need to eliminate applications.

An app can remain a capability/interface that Project Intelligence coordinates around a human outcome.

---

# 10. SEARCH

The long-term discovery question becomes:

> Which intelligence can responsibly help me accomplish this?

That is richer than:

> Which page contains this phrase?

---

# 11. OPEN PROTOCOLS

I want foundational interoperability contracts to be independently implementable wherever practical.

Commercial systems can innovate above the protocol.

---

# 12. WHAT IS NOT YET PROVEN

The full Internet of Intelligence is future architecture.

The immediate proof is smaller:

ONE REAL PROJECT INTELLIGENCE → ONE REAL HUMAN → REAL CONTINUITY.

Prove the primitive before claiming the network.
