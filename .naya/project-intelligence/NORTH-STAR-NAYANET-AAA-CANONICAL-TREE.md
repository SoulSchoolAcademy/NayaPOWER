## Constitutional invariants

1. One canonical intelligence/governance substrate: NayaPOWER.
2. The Hub is a projection/action surface, never a second brain.
3. Channels/doors never create independent intelligence, authority, governance, persistence, or truth.
4. Capability does not create authority.
5. Unknown, blocked, claimed, implemented, and verified remain distinct states.
6. Consequential actions produce verifiable evidence where applicable.
7. Intelligence has one canonical identity/provenance chain and many projections.
8. Human authority and privacy remain primary.
9. Historical construction artifacts are source/provenance material, not competing canonical implementations.
10. The repository converges toward one understandable canonical implementation lane rather than accumulating another Hub generation or patch stack.
