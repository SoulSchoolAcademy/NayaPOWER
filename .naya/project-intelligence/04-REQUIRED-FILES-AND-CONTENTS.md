# 04 — REQUIRED PROJECT INTELLIGENCE FILES

If Naya were personally responsible for taking a project from mission to verified outcome in one shot, these are the durable files she would want.

00 — INDEX
Purpose: reading route, boundaries, definition of done.

01 — MISSION-VISION-NORTH-STAR-HUMAN-OUTCOME
Purpose: meaning, human outcome, mission, vision, North Star.

02 — CONCEPT-PRODUCT-BOUNDARIES
Purpose: what the system is, relationship between components, what it is not.

03 — QUALIFICATION-QUESTIONS
Purpose: complete discovery and reasoning surface.

04 — REQUIRED-FILES-AND-CONTENTS
Purpose: package contract.

05 — REQUIREMENTS-AND-DEFINITION-OF-DONE
Purpose: explicit and derived requirements and success proof.

06 — SYSTEM-ARCHITECTURE-AND-SOURCE-OF-TRUTH
Purpose: components, ownership, information flow, canonical hierarchy.

07 — DESIGN-AND-BUILD-METHOD
Purpose: how Naya derives the design and builds responsibly.

08 — GOVERNANCE-TRUTH-PRIVACY-AUTHORITY
Purpose: authority, consent, truth states, privacy, verification.

09 — EXECUTION-LEARNING-TORCH
Purpose: execution loop, learning, distillation, successor behavior.

10 — CURRENT-STATE-AND-EVIDENCE
Purpose: actual present state and evidence, explicitly reconciled against live reality.

11 — BUILD-ROADMAP-AND-BOUNDARIES
Purpose: ordered causal implementation boundaries.

12 — COLD-NAYA-ACCEPTANCE
Purpose: prove operational continuity.

13 — SUCCESSOR-HANDOFF
Purpose: exact torch payload.

14 — SOURCE-ROUTE
Purpose: canonical source map.

15 — MACHINE-CONTRACT
Purpose: machine-readable contract for validation and automation.

Every file must be complete enough to be useful without conversation memory.

The package must contain mission, goals, desired outcome, requirements, constraints, architecture, design principles, intelligence model, source-of-truth map, current state, evidence, decisions, dependencies, failures, unknowns, open loops, definition of done, execution method, governance, privacy, acceptance tests, learning rules, and successor handoff.

It must not treat conversation summaries, guesses, stale indexes, or unverified generated prose as higher authority than canonical evidence.
