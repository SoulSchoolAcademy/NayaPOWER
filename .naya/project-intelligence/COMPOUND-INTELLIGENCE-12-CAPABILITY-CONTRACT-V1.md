## Capability contract

Every consequential capability must:
1. authenticate the acting user;
2. remain within explicit authority;
3. preserve provenance and source lineage;
4. distinguish current truth from unknown/conflict/stale state;
5. record a durable operation receipt;
6. fail closed when required authority/evidence is absent;
7. avoid creating a competing intelligence store or semantic identity;
8. leave enough evidence for the next Naya to reconstruct what happened.

## 10/10 acceptance

A capability is **10/10 PRODUCTION-PROVEN** only when its real behavior has been exercised at the relevant boundary and evidence proves its contract. Deployment alone is not proof.

Required evidence classes:
- source/version identity;
- authentication/authority behavior;
- successful real invocation;
- deterministic failure behavior;
- persistence/lineage where applicable;
- retrieval/projection/render evidence where applicable;
- idempotency/replay where applicable;
- privacy/scope boundaries where applicable;
- learning/outcome evidence where applicable;
- successor continuity where applicable.

**UNKNOWN is never promoted to PASS. IMPLEMENTED is never promoted to VERIFIED. VERIFIED is never promoted to PRODUCTION-PROVEN without claim-appropriate evidence.**
