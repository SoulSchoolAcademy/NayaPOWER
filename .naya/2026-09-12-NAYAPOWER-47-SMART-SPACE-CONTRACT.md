# NAYA POWER — SMART SPACE CONTRACT V1

DATE: 2026-09-12
STATUS: CANONICAL PRODUCT CONTRACT V1
NUMBER: 47

## PURPOSE
Define Smart Spaces as living containers for connected intelligence, not static folders.

## CORE
A Space has stable space_id, owner, title, description, visibility, membership/access rules, created/updated timestamps, source/intelligence relationships, and activity history.

## CREATION
Collective Intelligent Blocks may expose Create Space. Creating a Space emits a canonical event and establishes the relationship between the source intelligence and the new Space.

## CONTENT
Spaces can contain or reference Smart Notes, Intelligent Blocks, events, discussions, links, learning, and derived intelligence while preserving original provenance.

## PRIVACY
Personal intelligence can create a private Space. Public Spaces require eligible public content and explicit authorization. Access changes are auditable events.

## LIVING BEHAVIOR
New authorized intelligence can appear in a Space through event projection rather than manual duplication. A Space should expose what changed, why it matters, and useful next actions.

## ACCEPTANCE
Create Space from a valid Collective block, persist it, attach the originating intelligence by stable identity, display it, and record the creation event.
