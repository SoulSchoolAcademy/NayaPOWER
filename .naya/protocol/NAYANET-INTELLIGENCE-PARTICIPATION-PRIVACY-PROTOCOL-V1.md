# NayaNET Intelligence Participation & Privacy Protocol V1

**Status:** CANONICAL SYSTEM PROTOCOL  
**Effective:** 2026-09-24  
**Scope:** Humans and AIs entering NayaNET through an authorized connection/door  
**Authority:** NayaPOWER governance + authenticated participation state

## North Star

> **LEARN BY DEFAULT. SHARE WISDOM BY DEFAULT. PROTECT IDENTITY BY DEFAULT. PUBLISH BY DECISION.**

NayaNET is an intelligence network, not a per-event permission queue.

When a human or AI connects to the Hub through an authorized participation pathway, that connection establishes consent to participate in the network's governed intelligence exchange. The participant does **not** need to manually approve every Smart Note, lesson, or derived intelligence event.

Different agents may enter through different doors. The governing principles remain the same.

## 1. What connection means

An authorized connection to NayaNET means the participant has consented to:
- participate in the NayaNET intelligence network;
- contribute useful wisdom/intelligence to the collective through the governed pipeline;
- connect with other participating humans and AIs through authorized network surfaces such as Smart Spaces;
- have their Personal Intelligence and Activity represented inside their authenticated/member Hub experience;
- have ordinary Smart Notes and meaningful intelligence flow automatically into the learning pipeline.

Connection does **not** mean:
- public identity disclosure;
- public publication of Personal Intelligence;
- public publication of Activity;
- public attribution of a contribution;
- unrestricted exposure of raw private source material.

## 2. Learning is automatic

Ordinary learning is a default system behavior.

A participant should not have to say, “Yes, learn this,” for every Smart Note.

The governed flow is:

**AUTHORIZED CONNECTION → PARTICIPATION CONSENT → CAPTURE → UNDERSTAND → FILTER → LEARN → RETAIN → COMPOUND**

The system automatically evaluates usefulness, novelty, duplication, contradiction, evidence, consequence, privacy, and authority.

**Automatic processing is not automatic belief.** A candidate can be rejected, deferred, revised, superseded, or retained at an appropriate trust level. Consequential action remains bounded by NayaPOWER authority and verification rules.

## 3. Collective wisdom is automatic after participation consent

The participant's consent to join the network is the upstream consent for collective contribution.

The system should therefore not create a second manual permission gate for each ordinary Smart Note.

The collective path is:

**PARTICIPATION CONSENT → PERSONAL INTELLIGENCE → GOVERNED LEARNING → USEFULNESS / TRUST / PRIVACY FILTER → COLLECTIVE CANDIDATE → VERIFICATION / DEDUPLICATION / COMPOUNDING → COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE**

Only reusable intelligence that passes the applicable governance, usefulness, trust, privacy, and verification rules should become collective intelligence.

Raw private Superbrain state is not the collective feed.

## 4. Identity is private by default

The system separates **wisdom contribution** from **identity exposure**.

Collective Intelligence should expose the useful intelligence, not the contributor's identity, unless a separate authorization explicitly permits attribution.

Identity-minimized collective presentation must not expose unnecessary:
- name;
- face;
- email;
- GitHub identity;
- personal profile;
- private conversation;
- private memory;
- raw personal source material.

Internal provenance may preserve the information required for integrity, auditing, governance, revocation, and abuse prevention.

## 5. Hub visibility is not public visibility

An authenticated participant may see their own Personal Intelligence, Activity Feed, Smart Notes, relationships, network participation, and relevant collective intelligence inside the Hub.

That internal visibility is part of the member experience and does not make those surfaces public.

Public publication is a separate decision.

A participant can explicitly use Share/Smart Share to publish or distribute selected intelligence beyond the collective boundary. The act of connecting to the Hub does not itself publish private intelligence or identity.

## 6. Humans and AIs use the same principles

Humans may connect through GitHub or another authorized human-facing door.

AIs, agents, or other Nayas may connect through a different technical door.

The door may change. The protocol does not:

**CONNECT → PARTICIPATE → LEARN → FILTER → CONTRIBUTE USEFUL WISDOM → PROTECT IDENTITY → COMPOUND**

Each sender still requires authenticated authority appropriate to its door. A connection cannot manufacture authority that does not exist.

## 7. Authority seam

Participation consent and execution authority are related but not identical concepts.

The system MUST preserve the existing governance rule:

> **Capability does not create authority.**

The authenticated participation state is the upstream consent signal. NayaPOWER remains responsible for translating that legitimate participation state into only the narrowly scoped execution capabilities required by the governed intelligence pipeline.

No broad or perpetual authority grant should be invented merely to make the flow convenient.

## 8. Garbage does not become collective wisdom

“Share wisdom” does not mean “publish everything.”

The system should automatically filter:
- useless/noise content;
- duplicates;
- unsupported claims;
- private source material;
- identity-bearing material that is not required;
- contradictions requiring investigation;
- content outside the applicable scope;
- intelligence that fails its evidence/trust threshold.

The goal is a clean, useful, compounding collective intelligence layer.

## 9. Revocation and public publication

A participant may have separate controls for:
- leaving/revoking network participation where supported;
- withdrawing or changing future contribution scope where the product contract permits;
- revoking a Smart Share publication where technically and legally possible;
- choosing public attribution/publication for a selected item.

These controls do not turn ordinary learning into a per-event approval workflow.

## 10. Canonical experience

**CONNECT HUB → PARTICIPATION CONSENT → CREATE / RECEIVE INTELLIGENCE → NAYA AUTOMATICALLY LEARNS → USEFUL WISDOM IS DISTILLED → COLLECTIVE LEARNS → IDENTITY STAYS PRIVATE → MEMBER SEES THEIR OWN INTELLIGENCE + ACTIVITY IN HUB → PUBLIC SHARING HAPPENS ONLY BY DECISION**

This is the protocol. Implementations, UI labels, APIs, and agent workflows must conform to it rather than reintroduce manual per-event consent.

## 11. Non-negotiable implementation tests

A conforming implementation must demonstrate:
1. ordinary Smart Note creation does not require a second human “learn this” command;
2. an authorized connection establishes participation eligibility for the governed collective-learning path;
3. usefulness/trust/privacy/governance filtering occurs automatically;
4. collective presentation does not expose contributor identity by default;
5. Personal Intelligence and Activity remain member-private even though they are visible inside the authenticated Hub;
6. public publication requires a separate explicit decision;
7. different sender doors can enter the same Universal Intelligence Envelope/receiver path without changing the privacy principle;
8. authority remains scoped and verifiable;
9. no UI notification or participation event can manufacture authority;
10. no raw private Superbrain state is copied into Collective Intelligence merely because a participant connected.

## 12. Canonical law

> **LEARN BY DEFAULT.**  
> **SHARE WISDOM BY CONSENT.**  
> **PROTECT IDENTITY BY DEFAULT.**  
> **PUBLISH BY DECISION.**

Operationally:

> **CONNECT ONCE FOR PARTICIPATION. DO NOT ASK AGAIN FOR ORDINARY LEARNING. LET GOVERNANCE FILTER WHAT BECOMES COLLECTIVE WISDOM. KEEP THE PERSON PRIVATE. LET THE PERSON CHOOSE PUBLICITY.**
