# NAYA POWER — SMART LINK CONTRACT V1

DATE: 2026-09-12
STATUS: CANONICAL IDENTITY/LINK CONTRACT V1
NUMBER: 48

## PURPOSE
Make Smart Links stable, human-readable entry points into authorized NayaNET identity and intelligence.

## IDENTITY LINK
A claimed alias maps to the user's authorized Smart identity and must resolve consistently across welcome, Hub, Smart Mail, and eligible shared intelligence.

## INTELLIGENCE LINK
A shared intelligence link identifies the canonical intelligence object/event, not a copied rendering. Resolution must enforce visibility and authorization.

## ROUTING
Public link → public content when authorized.
Private link → authenticated owner/session.
Invalid/revoked link → truthful unavailable state.

## SECURITY
Never encode secrets, raw device keys, or bearer credentials in public URLs. Link identifiers must be non-sensitive and revocable.

## SHARING
Share from Collective or authorized Personal content creates a share event. Native share targets may include platform share, email, and text when supported.

## ACCEPTANCE
A Smart Link can be created, resolved from a clean session, render the correct intelligence/identity, enforce privacy, and survive UI redesign without changing its canonical meaning.
