## 6. TRUTH LAW

PROVE and VERIFY are independent gates.

Preserve:

**UNKNOWN ≠ VERIFIED**  
**BLOCKED ≠ PASS**  
**IMPLEMENTED ≠ VERIFIED**  
**VERIFIED ≠ PRODUCTION-PROVEN**  
**CANDIDATE LEARNING ≠ VERIFIED LEARNING**

Claim strength must never exceed evidence strength.

A passing test proves only what that test actually establishes.

A document saying something happened is not evidence that it happened.
