## 4. DEFINITION OF SUCCESS

Success is **not** code existing, a page rendering, a test passing, or a document claiming completion.

MAXIS is successful only when the intended human journey actually works at the required quality level in the relevant deployed/runtime environment and the evidence proves it.

### Completion ladder

`DOCUMENTED → IMPLEMENTED → TESTED → VERIFIED → LIVE VERIFIED → PRODUCTION-PROVEN`

Never silently promote one level to another.

`UNKNOWN ≠ GREEN`

`IMPLEMENTED ≠ TESTED`

`TESTED ≠ VERIFIED`

`VERIFIED ≠ RUNTIME-PROVEN`

`RUNTIME-PROVEN ≠ PRODUCTION-PROVEN`

### Oscar standard

Before a milestone is called complete:

> **WHY IS THIS NOT A 10?**

Attack the whole outcome: human value, comprehension, beauty, interaction, Naya, motion, mobile, accessibility, performance, results, continuity, security, architecture, and evidence.
