# NayaNET Participation Protocol — 2026-09-24

**Required awareness:** YES

The system protocol has been clarified and the canonical documentation updated.

## Core law

**LEARN BY DEFAULT. SHARE WISDOM BY CONSENT. PROTECT IDENTITY BY DEFAULT. PUBLISH BY DECISION.**

Connection to NayaNET through an authorized door establishes participation consent. Ordinary Smart Notes do not require per-event approval. The governed intelligence system automatically filters and distills useful wisdom for collective learning. Identity and raw private source material remain private by default. Public publication requires a separate decision.

## Implementation warning

This document update does not by itself prove runtime enforcement. The production connection/activation → authority → collective-learning causal path must be audited next.

## Required coder awareness

Do not introduce a manual per-Smart-Note consent gate. Do not interpret participation consent as public identity consent. Do not weaken scoped authority to make the flow work.
