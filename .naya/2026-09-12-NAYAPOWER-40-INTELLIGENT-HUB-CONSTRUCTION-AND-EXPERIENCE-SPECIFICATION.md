## 1. PURPOSE

The Intelligent Hub is not the visual representation of GitHub. It is not a GitHub dashboard and not merely an Activity Feed. It is the human-facing environment through which the living intelligence of NayaNET becomes understandable, discoverable, useful, actionable, shareable, connectable, and continuously connected.

GitHub, Superbrain, Smart Notes, machine state, execution systems, evidence systems, databases, adapters, and other canonical sources remain upstream intelligence and system infrastructure. The Hub is the human experience and projection layer.

Core relationship:

CANONICAL INTELLIGENCE → INTELLIGENT EVENT → SMART NOTE → INTELLIGENT BLOCK → SMART FEED → HUMAN UNDERSTANDING → HUMAN ACTION → NEW EVENT → VERIFICATION → UPDATED INTELLIGENCE → CONTINUATION

## 2. PRIMARY PRODUCT JOB

Turn living intelligence into immediately understandable human value.

A human should be able to quickly understand:

- What is happening?
- What is new?
- What matters?
- What did Naya learn?
- What did Naya do?
- What does this mean?
- What does it mean for me?
- What can I do with it?
- What can I save?
- What can I share?
- What can I connect with?
- What has been proven?
- What remains unknown?

The Hub should make intelligence feel simple without making the underlying intelligence simple.

## 4. THREE PRIMARY SMART FEEDS

### ACTIVITY FEED

The Activity Feed is the living operational stream. It records and presents meaningful Naya/system actions, new Smart Notes, intelligence, notifications, discoveries, decisions, completed actions, verification events, failures, blockers, learning, new Smart Spaces, connections, and important system events.

Activity is derived from actual events and verified state. It is a projection, not the canonical database. It is a key human-facing evidence surface but an Activity record alone is not automatically proof.

### PERSONAL INTELLIGENCE FEED

Private human intelligence: private Smart Notes, discoveries, learning, saved intelligence, reflections, research, decisions, collections, and private Naya-generated intelligence. Personal intelligence remains private unless explicitly made public.

### COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE FEED

The default primary feed. Public intelligence, discoveries, learning, community contributions, conversations, Smart Spaces, useful knowledge, and curated/verified intelligence. Its purpose is people connecting through useful intelligence, not generic social media.

## 5. SMART NOTE / INTELLIGENT BLOCK CONTENT CONTRACT

A complete Smart Note is one intelligence event presented through multiple useful views:

1. IN A NUTSHELL
2. HUMAN NOTE
3. CHILD VIEW
4. GRABBER VIEW
5. NAYA NOTE
6. ADAPTER LEARNING
7. WHAT IT MEANS
8. WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU

### IN A NUTSHELL

The simplest possible explanation. Answers WHAT IS THIS? It is the featured block: larger typography, clear icon, white/silver edge treatment.

### HUMAN NOTE

Original human perspective, intent, and relevant context. Semantic color: PURPLE.

### CHILD VIEW

Simplified/derived interpretation for accessibility from another perspective. Semantic color: INDIGO.

### GRABBER VIEW

Attention/interest interpretation communicating why the intelligence deserves attention. Semantic color: SAPPHIRE / CANYON BLUE.

### NAYA NOTE

What Naya understood, recognized, inferred, determined, or recommends, subject to the appropriate truth state. Semantic color: FOREST / EMERALD GREEN. Inference must never be presented as verified fact.

### ADAPTER LEARNING

What was learned from the intelligence: patterns, relationships, reusable lessons, system improvements, behavioral/contextual learning, or future recommendations. Semantic color: LIME GREEN.

### WHAT IT MEANS

The significance of the intelligence: WHY DOES THIS MATTER? Semantic color: YELLOW.

### WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU

The human-value layer: WHY SHOULD I CARE? WHAT CAN I DO WITH THIS? HOW CAN I APPLY IT? Semantic color: GOLD.

## 6. LIVING DEPTH VISUAL LANGUAGE

The Intelligent Block is:

BLACK CENTER + LIVING EDGE LIGHT + LAYERED DEPTH + CLEAR TYPOGRAPHY + SEMANTIC COLOR + INTELLIGENT CONTENT

The main Intelligent Block is elevated above the page. Internal intelligence sections are elevated again above the main block.

SCREEN → ELEVATED INTELLIGENT BLOCK → ELEVATED INTELLIGENCE LAYERS → CONTENT

This is LIVING DEPTH.

Depth is created through elevation, shadow, controlled contrast, surface separation, spacing, restrained glow, hover movement, and state transitions. It must feel premium and alive, not gimmicky.

---

## 7. LIVING EDGE LIGHT + CONTINUOUS COLOR FLOW

The Intelligent Block remains black. Its perimeter softly illuminates according to its position in the visual sequence. The edge treatment must feel alive rather than like a static colored border.

Canonical visual flow:

WHITE / SILVER → MAGENTA / PINK → PURPLE → INDIGO → SAPPHIRE / CANYON → BLUE → FOREST GREEN → EMERALD → LIME → YELLOW → GOLD → ORANGE → RED → WHITE → MAGENTA → PURPLE → CONTINUE

The feed should not render every Intelligent Block with the same border color. Continuous color flow creates visual rhythm and cognitive sectioning: NEW BLOCK / NEW SECTION / NEW INTELLIGENCE / NEW MOMENT.

Color is semantic, not decorative noise.

---

## 8. TYPOGRAPHY

Prioritize large readable headlines, strong hierarchy, short paragraphs, generous line spacing, high contrast, clear labels, obvious section identity, and minimal cognitive load.

The first visual layer must communicate the subject without requiring the user to open every detail.

## 9. COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENT BLOCK ACTIONS

TOP-LEFT: CREATE SPACE
TOP-RIGHT: FAVORITE + SAVE
BOTTOM: RATE THIS INTELLIGENCE + SHARE INTELLIGENCE + LOVE + LIKE
BELOW: COMMENTS

### CREATE SPACE

Transforms intelligence into a shared environment around a subject, Smart Note, discovery, or question. Creation generates an Activity event and invites people to connect and converse around useful intelligence.

### FAVORITE

Marks intelligence as especially important to the user and persists in the user's Smart List / favorite intelligence environment.

### SAVE

Retains intelligence without necessarily marking it as a favorite.

### RATE THIS INTELLIGENCE

Five-star rating interface. Ratings may inform quality, discovery, recommendation, and curation, but must never become the sole measure of truth or value.

### LOVE / LIKE

Default unlit controls are WHITE. Active LOVE is RED. Active LIKE is BLUE. States must be immediately visible.

### SHARE INTELLIGENCE

Use native/mobile-style sharing where available, including Facebook, email, text/message, and other supported channels. Prefer useful Smart Links over raw text dumps.

### COMMENTS

Public intelligence supports discussion, replies where appropriate, moderation, identity, timestamps, notifications, and Activity events.

## 10. PERSONAL INTELLIGENT BLOCK ACTIONS

Personal blocks use the same visual language and support:

- Favorite
- Save
- Share
- Make Public

They do not show collective social actions by default: Love, Like, public rating, or public comments.

### MAKE PUBLIC

PRIVATE → MAKE PUBLIC → PUBLIC INTELLIGENCE → COLLECTIVE FEED

Publication is an explicit authority-changing operation and must never happen accidentally. Once public, the intelligence may become eligible for Collective Feed, rating, social response, comments, Smart Space creation, and public discovery.

## 11. SMART NOTE / ACTIVITY SYNCHRONIZATION

When a meaningful Smart Note is created:

SMART NOTE CREATED → EVENT CREATED → ACTIVITY EVENT → ACTIVITY FEED

Activity is WHAT IS HAPPENING / WHAT HAPPENED.

Smart Note is WHAT THE INTELLIGENCE MEANS.

Both remain connected through stable identity.

A Smart Note identity connects human note, Naya note, machine note, adapter learning, intelligence event, Activity event, saved state, favorite state, visibility, Smart Space, comments, reactions, ratings, provenance, verification, and related intelligence.

## 12. MACHINE NOTE + ADAPTER LEARNING

The Machine Note is the system-readable representation and may contain structured metadata, event identity, source identity, timestamps, actor, relationships, state, provenance, permissions, processing information, adapter information, verification, and references.

Adapter Learning is a first-class part of the Smart Note and asks: WHAT DID THE SYSTEM LEARN FROM THIS?

Learning remains distinguishable from fact. Learned inference is not automatically verified truth.

## 14. GLOBAL NAYANET DESIGN LANGUAGE

The Living Depth visual language applies beyond Intelligent Blocks to buttons, cards, sections, navigation, panels, modals, Smart Spaces, notifications, dashboards, reports, and controls.

Universal principle:

BLACK SURFACE + LIVING EDGE + DEPTH + SEMANTIC LIGHT + RESPONSIVE MOTION + CLEAR ACTION

Buttons and interactive surfaces use REST / HOVER / FOCUS / PRESS / ACTIVE / SUCCESS / WARNING / ERROR / BLOCKED / LOADING / DISABLED states.

Motion communicates meaning: new intelligence emerges, Naya working pulses, verification moves toward evidence, success confirms, new connections illuminate, feed updates emerge, and loading communicates actual work.

## 15. SMART SPACES

A Smart Space is a social intelligence environment created around a subject, Smart Note, discovery, or question.

INTELLIGENCE → CREATE SPACE → SMART SPACE → PEOPLE → CONVERSATION → NEW INTELLIGENCE → NEW SMART NOTES → NEW EVENTS → NEW LEARNING

Smart Spaces are intelligence-generating environments, not ordinary chat rooms.

## 16. SOURCE OF TRUTH + EVENT ARCHITECTURE

The Hub must not become the source of truth merely because it displays intelligence.

SOURCE → EVENT → PROCESSING → PROJECTION → HUB

The existing Cross-System Event Contract remains the event architecture: ONE EVENT IDENTITY → MANY SPECIALIZED PROCESSORS → ONE CONNECTED INTELLIGENCE CHAIN.

The Hub consumes canonical events; it must not create a disconnected parallel event universe.

---

## 17. PROOF + TRUTH STATES

Activity can be a human-facing evidence surface, but ACTIVITY RECORD ≠ PROOF BY ITSELF.

Consequential states must distinguish:

VERIFIED / OBSERVED / IMPLEMENTED / UNKNOWN / BLOCKED / CONFLICTED

The visual system may use semantic accents, but textual truth state remains available. No color substitutes for truth.

Where evidence exists, Activity/Smart Note surfaces should be able to expose what happened, actor, time, source, artifact, state change, verification status, evidence, and next action.

---

## 18. SMART LINKS

Major intelligence events should be capable of producing Smart Links:

WHAT IT IS → WHY IT MATTERS → STATUS → EVIDENCE → VALUE → NEXT ACTION

Smart Links are the human communication bridge from system intelligence to useful action.

## 20. RESPONSIVE EXPERIENCE

Desktop: wide Intelligent Blocks, layered depth, rich content, visible action system, comfortable scanning.

Tablet: compressed depth, preserved hierarchy, adapted controls.

Mobile: one-column intelligence, large readable typography, obvious primary action, touch-friendly controls, preserved semantic color/depth, simplified secondary actions where necessary.

Mobile is not a shrunken desktop; it is the priority/action intelligence experience.

## 22. SEARCH + CURATION

Smart Feed search should ultimately retrieve Intelligent Blocks by WHAT, WHO, WHEN, WHY, STATUS, TOPIC, MISSION, RELATIONSHIP, SOURCE, EVIDENCE, LEARNING, VISIBILITY, and SMART SPACE.

Collective ranking may consider usefulness, relevance, freshness, verified state, engagement, relationships, interests, learning, quality, and source reliability. Popularity, engagement, and ratings must never automatically equal truth or value.

## 23. PRIVACY

Every intelligence object must have explicit visibility. Minimum states:

PRIVATE
PUBLIC

Potential future states may include SHARED, SPACE_ONLY, UNLISTED, and RESTRICTED.

Visibility must be enforced at the data/projection layer, not merely hidden through UI.

---

## 24. PROVENANCE

Every Smart Note preserves where its intelligence originated. Provenance may include human, Naya, system, external source, adapter, event, artifact, and related intelligence.

The human layer can remain simple while the machine layer preserves exact provenance.

---

## 25. FAILURE DESIGN

When something fails, the Intelligent Block should not disappear. It should communicate:

WHAT FAILED
WHY IT MATTERS
WHAT IS KNOWN
WHAT IS UNKNOWN
WHAT CAN BE DONE
WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

A blocked intelligence event remains useful.

## 26. CONTINUITY

Activity and Smart Notes participate in cold-Naya continuity. A cold Naya should be able to understand WHAT HAPPENED, WHAT IS HAPPENING, WHAT WAS VERIFIED, WHAT FAILED, WHAT IS UNKNOWN, and WHAT IS NEXT through canonical state, evidence, and Activity projections.
