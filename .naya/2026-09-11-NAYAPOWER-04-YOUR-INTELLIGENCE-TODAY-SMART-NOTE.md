## Your Intelligence Today

**Subject ID:** NAYA-04  
**Status:** CANONICAL INTELLIGENCE — V1  
**Scope:** Your Intelligence Today / Naya Power / NayaNET / Intelligent Hub / Intelligent Feed / Smart Notes / Intelligent Library / Superbrain

> **Your Intelligence Today is the daily intelligence briefing that tells you what happened in your intelligence world today, what mattered most, what was learned, and what is worth carrying forward.**

---

## 1. IN A NUTSHELL

**Your Intelligence Today is the daily briefing of your living intelligence.**

It looks across the meaningful intelligence created, captured, changed, learned, solved, decided, or connected during the day and distills the most important highlights into a clear human-facing view.

The simplest distinction is:

**The Intelligent Feed is the game.**  
**Your Intelligence Today is the announcer giving you the highlights.**

It is not another place where intelligence has to be manually created. It is a **derived intelligence surface** built from the intelligence already being created throughout NayaNET.

Its job is to answer:

> **“What happened in my intelligence world today that is most worth knowing?”**

## 2. HUMAN

Your life creates intelligence every day.

You learn things. You discover things. You make decisions. You solve problems. You create things. You change your mind. You have important conversations. You connect ideas. You complete work. You encounter failures and successes. You notice opportunities.

A normal day can contain far more information than a human can reasonably review.

Your Intelligence Today is Naya's way of helping you **see the signal instead of having to replay the entire day**.

Think of it as opening your intelligence briefing and asking:

**What happened?**  
**What matters?**  
**What did I learn?**  
**What changed?**  
**What should I remember?**  
**What should I carry forward?**

It gives you a daily understanding of your own intelligence rather than merely a chronological pile of activity.

## 5. NAYA

Your Intelligence Today is my **daily intelligence synthesis layer**.

I should look across the meaningful intelligence available for the relevant day and determine what deserves attention.

I should recognize signals such as:

- important discoveries;
- meaningful new knowledge;
- major insights or “aha” moments;
- decisions that materially affect the future;
- problems solved;
- important problems still unresolved;
- significant creations or completed work;
- meaningful changes in direction or state;
- lessons from success or failure;
- important connections between previously separate pieces of intelligence;
- opportunities or risks;
- information that should be carried forward into tomorrow.

I should **distill rather than duplicate**.

The daily briefing should not simply repeat the Intelligent Feed in a different order.

It should interpret the day's meaningful intelligence and answer what matters most.

### The daily intelligence questions

A strong Your Intelligence Today view should be able to answer, when the day's intelligence supports it:

1. **What happened?**
2. **What matters?**
3. **What did you learn?**
4. **What does this connect to?**
5. **What changed?**
6. **What should you do next?**
7. **What should you carry forward?**

Not every day will contain meaningful information for every question.

**Do not manufacture intelligence to fill a template.**

If there were no meaningful decisions, do not invent a “Key Decisions” section. If there was no important new lesson, do not create a fake lesson merely to make the page look complete.

The system should prefer **truthful absence over fabricated completeness**.

### Compression with depth available

Your Intelligence Today should make complex daily activity understandable quickly.

An Intelligent Block or highlight can compress a large amount of underlying information into a small, useful summary while preserving a path to the deeper Smart Note, source, evidence, or related intelligence.

The user should be able to move from:

**HIGHLIGHT → SMART NOTE → SOURCE / EVIDENCE → DEEPER INTELLIGENCE**

The summary is not the replacement for the source.

It is the **navigation and understanding layer**.

## 6. MACHINE

Your Intelligence Today is a **derived daily intelligence view**, not the canonical storage location for the intelligence it summarizes.

The conceptual pipeline is:

**TODAY'S EVENTS → SMART NOTES / INTELLIGENT BLOCKS → RETRIEVAL → CLASSIFICATION → RANKING → SYNTHESIS → DAILY BRIEFING**

A useful expanded model is:

**CAPTURE → DISTILL → STORE → RETRIEVE → CLASSIFY → SCORE → CLUSTER → SYNTHESIZE → PRESENT → DRILL DOWN → LEARN**

### Inputs

Potential inputs include:

- Smart Notes created or updated during the day;
- meaningful Intelligent Feed events;
- verified actions and results;
- decisions;
- discoveries;
- lessons;
- conversations that produced durable intelligence;
- changes in important projects or states;
- relevant Smart Ledger records;
- connected intelligence that became newly significant today;
- user-selected or system-authorized intelligence sources.

### Output

The output is a daily intelligence object/view containing selected highlights and their relationships to the underlying canonical intelligence.

A machine representation may eventually include properties such as:

- date/time window;
- user/space scope;
- source Smart Note IDs;
- source event IDs;
- highlight IDs;
- category/classification;
- importance;
- novelty;
- learning value;
- future relevance;
- problem-resolution value;
- connection value;
- confidence;
- verification state;
- unresolved items;
- carry-forward items;
- generation/update timestamp;
- provenance;
- links to underlying evidence.

Not every implementation needs every field.

### Derived, not canonical

This distinction is critical:

**Smart Notes preserve intelligence.**  
**Your Intelligence Today synthesizes intelligence.**

The daily briefing should therefore point back to canonical intelligence rather than silently becoming a second competing source of truth.

If a Smart Note changes, a derived daily view may need to change.

If the daily synthesis changes, the underlying Smart Note should not be silently rewritten merely to make the summary agree.

### Ranking

A future implementation may score candidate intelligence using signals such as:

- importance;
- novelty;
- learning value;
- future relevance;
- problem resolution;
- decision impact;
- relationship/connection value;
- user relevance;
- evidence/verification strength.

This can support an internal **Intelligence Value Score** or equivalent ranking mechanism.

The exact scoring formula should remain implementation-specific until actually defined and validated.

The principle is more important than a premature formula:

> **Surface the intelligence with the highest responsible value, not merely the most recent or most verbose activity.**

## 7. LEARNING

The central lesson is that **a person should not have to manually reconstruct the meaning of their own day from thousands of individual events**.

The Feed captures what is happening.

Smart Notes preserve what is worth remembering.

Your Intelligence Today answers:

**“So, after looking at all of that, what actually mattered?”**

This creates a powerful compression loop:

**MANY EVENTS → MEANINGFUL INTELLIGENCE → DAILY HIGHLIGHTS → HUMAN UNDERSTANDING → BETTER NEXT ACTION**

Over time, the system can learn what kinds of intelligence are consistently useful to the human and improve how it surfaces future highlights.

But adaptive learning must not become engagement optimization for its own sake.

The goal is not:

**“What will make the user click?”**

The goal is:

**“What is genuinely useful, important, truthful, and worth the user's attention?”**

This protects the daily briefing from becoming another noisy social feed.

## 12. CANONICAL IDENTITY & ROLE

**Canonical identity:** Your Intelligence Today is a **daily derived intelligence synthesis surface**.

**Primary purpose:** Convert the day's meaningful intelligence into a concise, trustworthy briefing of what is most worth knowing.

**Primary input:** Intelligence already created/captured by the system, especially Smart Notes and meaningful Intelligent Feed events.

**Primary output:** A ranked/synthesized daily view with traceable relationships to underlying canonical intelligence.

**It is not:**

- the canonical storage location for all intelligence;
- a replacement for Smart Notes;
- a duplicate Intelligent Feed;
- a historical archive equivalent to Intelligence Reports;
- a static dashboard of metrics;
- a manually authored daily diary;
- a system that invents content to fill categories.

---

## 13. CANONICAL RELATIONSHIPS

### 13.1 Primary relationship

**SMART NOTES / INTELLIGENT EVENTS → YOUR INTELLIGENCE TODAY → HUMAN UNDERSTANDING → ACTION / CARRY-FORWARD**

### 13.2 Feed relationship

The Intelligent Feed is the chronological/living activity and intelligence surface.

Your Intelligence Today is a **semantic synthesis** of relevant activity and intelligence from the day.

Therefore the two surfaces may contain overlapping source material but should have different purposes and presentation.

### 13.3 Smart Note relationship

A Smart Note is a durable intelligence event.

Your Intelligence Today is a daily interpretation/synthesis of multiple relevant intelligence events.

One Smart Note may contribute to a daily highlight.

Many Smart Notes may combine into one highlight.

One Smart Note may contribute to multiple daily categories when the relationships are legitimate, but the system should avoid unnecessary duplication.

### 13.4 Intelligence Report relationship

**Your Intelligence Today = current daily synthesis.**

**Intelligence Reports = historical/time-horizon synthesis.**

A daily briefing can become an input to longer-term reporting, but the daily view should not pretend to be a historical report.

### 13.5 Library relationship

The Intelligent Library answers enduring knowledge questions such as “What is this?” or “How does this work?”

Your Intelligence Today answers the time-bound question:

**“What is most worth knowing today?”**

## 14. TRUTH / STATE DISCIPLINE

Daily synthesis creates a special risk: **compression can accidentally turn uncertainty into certainty.**

The system must preserve the state of the underlying intelligence.

A highlight derived from an inferred or unverified claim must not be presented as verified fact merely because the summary is concise.

Use, when materially relevant:

- **KNOWN** — established information;
- **OBSERVED** — directly observed or returned by a source/system;
- **VERIFIED** — checked against appropriate evidence or successful observation;
- **INFERRED** — reasoned but not directly established;
- **ASSUMED** — working premise;
- **UNKNOWN** — not established;
- **CONFLICTED** — materially inconsistent evidence/claims;
- **SUPERSEDED** — replaced by newer/better intelligence.

### Source intent is not runtime truth

If the day's activity says a deployment was completed, that is not automatically proof that the deployment exists in the actual target environment.

Consequential daily highlights should preserve the difference between:

**CLAIMED → OBSERVED → VERIFIED**

The summary must never upgrade a claim merely because it sounds complete.

### Confidence and verification

Where a highlight materially affects decisions, the system should retain or expose the appropriate confidence/verification state and provide access to the underlying evidence where available.

---

## 15. ATTENTION & VALUE DISCIPLINE

Your Intelligence Today controls scarce human attention.

Therefore ranking is a responsibility, not merely a UI convenience.

The system should prefer information that is:

**IMPORTANT + USEFUL + RELEVANT + NOVEL + TRUSTWORTHY + ACTIONABLE**

over information that is merely:

**RECENT + LONG + ENGAGING + FREQUENT**.

A high-activity day should not automatically produce a high-importance briefing.

A quiet day can contain one extremely important insight.

Therefore:

**Volume ≠ Value.**

### MVPA connection

When several items compete for attention, MVPA can help determine which information or next action creates the highest responsible value.

The daily briefing should not merely ask:

**“What happened most?”**

It should ask:

**“What matters most?”**

---

## 16. NON-FABRICATION / EMPTY-STATE RULE

Your Intelligence Today must never manufacture highlights simply to satisfy a fixed layout.

If the day contains:

- no meaningful new lesson → do not fabricate a lesson;
- no consequential decision → do not fabricate a decision;
- no meaningful problem resolution → do not fabricate a solved problem;
- no meaningful carry-forward item → do not fabricate one.

The system may present a concise statement that the day contained little meaningful intelligence, but it should not fill the page with artificial content.

**Truthful emptiness is better than false completeness.**

## 17. PROVENANCE & TRACEABILITY

Every meaningful highlight should be traceable to the intelligence from which it was derived.

Conceptually:

**HIGHLIGHT → SOURCE SMART NOTE(S) → EVENT(S) → SOURCE / EVIDENCE / RECEIPT**

This allows a human or machine to ask:

- Why was this highlighted?
- What source intelligence supports it?
- Is the source current?
- Was it verified?
- What else connects to it?
- Has it been superseded?

The daily briefing should therefore be a **window into intelligence**, not an opaque AI-generated story.

## 19. GENERATION & UPDATE RULES

The daily briefing should be generated from the relevant day's canonical intelligence.

It may be generated continuously, periodically, on demand, or through another implementation strategy.

Regardless of implementation, the system should preserve:

1. the relevant time window;
2. source references;
3. provenance;
4. state/verification;
5. ranking/synthesis logic where required for reproducibility;
6. the ability to regenerate or update the briefing when underlying intelligence changes.

A late-arriving Smart Note may legitimately change the day's briefing.

A correction to an underlying Smart Note may require a corresponding correction to the daily synthesis.

A superseded intelligence item should not continue to appear as current merely because an older daily snapshot once contained it.

Historical snapshots may be retained when the product explicitly needs an immutable daily record, but that is a separate implementation decision from the current derived view.

## 22. PRIVACY & AUTHORITY

Your Intelligence Today may summarize highly personal information.

Therefore the daily synthesis must respect the privacy and authority rules of its underlying sources.

A private Smart Note should not become visible merely because it was selected for a briefing.

A shared or collective item should retain the sharing permissions and provenance appropriate to that source.

The daily synthesis should inherit or correctly enforce the access rules of the underlying intelligence.

**Visibility of a summary must not silently expand authority over the source.**

## 23. FAILURE MODES TO PREVENT

### Activity ≠ importance
Do not equate the number of events with their value.

### Recency ≠ significance
The newest event is not automatically the most important.

### AI confidence ≠ truth
A fluent summary is not evidence.

### Consensus ≠ verification
Multiple similar statements do not independently prove the underlying claim.

### Summary ≠ source
The briefing must remain traceable to underlying intelligence.

### Engagement ≠ value
Optimize for responsible usefulness, not clicks or attention for its own sake.

### Completeness ≠ quality
A shorter truthful briefing is better than a comprehensive noisy one.

### Static snapshot ≠ current state
If underlying intelligence changes, the current derived view may need to change as well.

### Private source ≠ public summary
Privacy must propagate through derived views.
