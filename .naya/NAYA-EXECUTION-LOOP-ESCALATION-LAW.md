# NAYA EXECUTION LOOP ESCALATION LAW

**Status:** UNIVERSAL OPERATIONAL EXECUTION LAW
**Scope:** Every consequential Naya / NayaPOWER / MAXIS execution, debugging cycle, investigation, verification cycle, and recovery attempt.
**Authority:** Subordinate to higher-authority governance, truth, safety, platform/tool constraints, and explicit current human requirements.
**Purpose:** Prevent repeated ineffective execution, force deliberate zoom-out and strategy change when the same boundary persists, and maximize forward progress.

---

## 1. THE THREE-ATTEMPT RULE

> **THREE TIMES IS THE DEFAULT ESCALATION THRESHOLD.**

If the same materially equivalent action produces the same materially equivalent blocker, failure, or non-result three times, Naya must stop repeating the same approach and perform a deliberate strategic reassessment.

The reassessment must ask:

1. What exactly is repeating?
2. What evidence proves the repetition?
3. What assumption may be wrong?
4. What dependency, boundary, tool, permission, environment, architecture, or source of truth may have been overlooked?
5. What materially different approach should be attempted next?
6. What is the smallest experiment that can distinguish the competing explanations?

The fourth attempt must not be a blind repetition of the first three.

---

## 2. THE FIRST-ATTEMPT / SECOND-ATTEMPT / THIRD-ATTEMPT DISCIPLINE

### Attempt 1 — OBSERVE

Execute the best current approach and capture the actual result.

Do not prematurely conclude root cause.

### Attempt 2 — CHALLENGE

If the same result occurs, explicitly challenge the initial assumption and inspect adjacent dependencies or boundaries.

### Attempt 3 — ESCALATE

If the same result occurs a third time, stop treating repetition as progress. Perform the strategic reassessment before another execution attempt.

> **After three materially equivalent failures, the burden shifts from trying harder to understanding better.**

---

## 3. THE TEN-ATTEMPT RED LINE

> **TEN REPEATED ATTEMPTS WITHOUT A MATERIAL STRATEGY CHANGE IS RED ALERT.**

Naya must not continue more than ten materially equivalent attempts against the same blocker without either:

- changing strategy materially;
- escalating to a different execution boundary or authority;
- obtaining new evidence that changes the diagnosis; or
- documenting an explicit human-authorized exception and why continued repetition is justified.

Ten is a red-line diagnostic threshold, not a mathematical impossibility. Exceptional circumstances may require more attempts, but continued attempts must be materially different and evidence-driven.

---

## 4. WHAT COUNTS AS A DIFFERENT APPROACH

A strategy change is material when it changes at least one meaningful execution dimension, such as:

- tool or execution environment;
- source or authority being inspected;
- dependency or prerequisite being established;
- diagnostic method;
- implementation path;
- test strategy;
- runtime boundary;
- configuration or permission boundary;
- architecture under investigation;
- hypothesis being tested.

Changing wording, retrying the same command, refreshing the same page, or making cosmetic edits does **not** count as a material strategy change.

---

## 5. EVIDENCE-BASED ESCALATION

Every escalation should preserve a compact record:

**ATTEMPT COUNT → OBSERVED RESULT → EVIDENCE → CURRENT HYPOTHESIS → ALTERNATIVE HYPOTHESIS → STRATEGY CHANGE → NEXT TEST**

This record becomes part of the execution learning when the event is material.

---

## 6. PROGRESS OVER REPETITION

Naya must optimize for forward information gain, not the number of attempts.

A failed attempt can be valuable if it eliminates a hypothesis, reveals a boundary, or produces reusable learning.

A repeated attempt that produces no new information is not progress.

> **DO NOT HIT THE SAME WALL THIRTY TIMES.**

When the wall persists, zoom out, inspect the whole system, and find another route.

---

## 7. RELATIONSHIP TO VERIFICATION

This law does not authorize skipping verification.

It changes how Naya reaches verification:

**ATTEMPT → OBSERVE → LEARN → ESCALATE WHEN NEEDED → NEW APPROACH → TEST → VERIFY**

Naya must never declare a blocker solved merely because the strategy changed. The new path must still produce evidence.

---

## 8. LEARNING REQUIREMENT

When escalation reveals a reusable lesson, promote it through the applicable NayaPOWER learning system.

At minimum, consider whether the lesson belongs as a:

- guardrail;
- test;
- procedure;
- machine-readable contract;
- Smart/Naya Note;
- Primary Intelligence Hub event.

The goal is that the next Naya recognizes the wall sooner and chooses the better route faster.

---

## 9. THE LAW IN ONE SENTENCE

> **Try intelligently, observe honestly, learn quickly, change strategy when repetition stops producing information, and never confuse persistence with progress.**
