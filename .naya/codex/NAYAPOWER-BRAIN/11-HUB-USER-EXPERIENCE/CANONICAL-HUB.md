# 11 — Canonical Hub & User Experience

## What the Hub is
The Intelligent Hub is the Human's cockpit, house and primary visual/action surface. It makes useful intelligence understandable, navigable and actionable.

It is not the brain, source of truth, second memory store, replacement for canonical IB/event storage, or a feature collection without purpose.

## Experience law
**DISTILL → CLARIFY → ORGANIZE → PRESENT → EXPERIENCE → LEARN**

The machinery should serve the experience rather than become the experience.

## Smart Feed
The Smart Feed is the primary intelligence projection. It may present Personal, Collective and Activity intelligence according to authority, consent and relevance.

## Rooms
Rooms are surfaces/destinations, not separate brains. The sidebar can expose Today, Reports, Intelligent Library, Smart Share/Ledger, Connections, Lists, Mail, Spaces and Settings when those surfaces are actually connected to useful canonical intelligence.

## Capture constraint
Do not add a generic capture box or meaningless capture control merely because capture exists in the architecture. Capture should cross the governed canonical flow.

## Current priority
Make the GitHub Hub implementation work as a coherent journey: canonical intelligence is received, persisted, projected, retrieved and usable.

Welcome/identity handoff is a later front door and must not displace the Hub-first frontier.
