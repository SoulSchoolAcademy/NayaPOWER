# 01 — Canonical Mission & Vision

## Purpose
NayaPOWER exists to make it dramatically easier for an ordinary human with a meaningful vision to accomplish extraordinary things with AI without becoming an AI project manager.

## Mission
Capture useful human-AI experience; understand and reconcile it; turn durable value into governed intelligence; preserve provenance and authority; retrieve it when applicable; act on it when authorized; verify outcomes; learn from what actually happened; and make the next interaction more valuable.

## Vision
A human can think, create, decide and direct while a continuously improving network of Nayas carries the operational burden. Important intelligence does not disappear when a conversation, model, interface or individual Naya changes.

## Why
Valuable reasoning is often trapped in conversations, scattered across documents, duplicated in notes, or lost during handoffs. NayaPOWER solves the deeper continuity problem: how experience becomes durable intelligence without losing provenance, authority, privacy, verification or context.

## Product promise
**Think once. Capture it. Learn from it. Remember it. Use it again. Get smarter.**

## North Star
**Every useful interaction should make the next interaction more valuable.**

## Success
Success is not document count, feature count, tabs, notes or responses. Success means the Human does less repetitive project management; Naya restores current truth without conversation archaeology; durable intelligence survives sessions and Naya changes; provenance and authority survive with it; new information reconciles with existing understanding; validated learning changes later behavior when applicable; authorized actions are executed; outcomes are observed and verified; the Hub makes intelligence understandable and actionable; and a cold successor can continue without reconstructing the project from scratch.

## Optimization
**SYNERGIZE → OPTIMIZE → MAXIMIZE → EQUALIZE → LEARN → REPEAT.**
Optimize for maximum verified value per input and output, not merely speed or token minimization.
