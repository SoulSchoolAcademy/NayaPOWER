# 12 — Canonical History & Reconciliation

## History is evidence, not automatically current truth
NayaPOWER contains many plans, note formats, migrations, proof campaigns and implementation attempts. Their existence does not make them current authority.

## Reconciliation
For each historical artifact:
1. identify its actual subject;
2. identify its authority at the time;
3. determine whether the intelligence remains valid;
4. compare with current canonical understanding;
5. classify it;
6. preserve provenance;
7. merge, supersede, retain or archive appropriately.

## Dispositions
CANONICAL, MIGRATABLE, PROJECTION, LEGACY COMPATIBILITY, HISTORICAL, DUPLICATE/SUPERSEDED.

## Smart Note archaeology
The repository contains many Smart Note-like paths and naming conventions. These are historical corpus evidence, not proof of hundreds of current canonical intelligence objects.

The canonical registry, live receiver and current contracts outrank stale migration assumptions.

## Preservation law
Do not destroy unique historical intelligence merely because its format is obsolete. Preserve the intelligence, provenance and reason for change while retiring duplicate operational authority.

## Why reconciliation matters
A system that cannot distinguish current understanding from history will repeatedly resurrect old mistakes. Reconciliation is therefore part of intelligence, not cleanup after intelligence.
