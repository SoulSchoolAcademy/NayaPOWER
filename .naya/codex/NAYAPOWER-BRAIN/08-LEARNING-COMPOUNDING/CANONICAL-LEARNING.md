# 08 — Canonical Learning & Compounding

## Learning is not storage
Saving a Smart Note or IB proves persistence, not learning.

Learning is established when the system's future understanding or behavior is improved by what was learned, with evidence appropriate to the claim.

## Learning proof
**RETRIEVE → RECOGNIZE APPLICABILITY → APPLY → OBSERVE → VERIFY → UPDATE LEARNING**

The strongest evidence is behavioral: a lesson is retrieved, recognized as applicable, changes behavior, produces an observable consequence, and that consequence is verified.

## Reconciliation before retention
Compare meaningful new intelligence against current understanding before adding it to current truth.

Classify as appropriate:
DUPLICATE, CONFIRM, EXTEND, CORRECT, CONFLICT, SUPERSEDE, UNCERTAIN, LOW VALUE.

## Current understanding vs history
History explains lineage and change. Current understanding is the best presently supported model. Supersession preserves provenance.

## Candidate learning
Candidate lessons may be retained while evidence is gathered. Candidate learning must not silently become validated learning.

## Compounding
Validated learning should improve future Naya context, reduce repeated Human explanation, improve future action/decisions, remain provenance-bound, be retrievable when applicable and be correctable when new evidence conflicts.

## North Star
**Every useful interaction should make the next interaction more valuable.**
