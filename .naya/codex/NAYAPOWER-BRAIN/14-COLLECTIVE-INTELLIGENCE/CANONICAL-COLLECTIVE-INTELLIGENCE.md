# 14 — Canonical Collective Intelligence

## Meaning
Collective intelligence is governed connection of intelligence across people, Nayas, interfaces, projects and contexts while preserving identity, privacy, authority, provenance and consent.

It is not automatic sharing or a public pool.

## Consent
**Private by default. Shared by choice. Collective by consent. Public by decision.**

Technical capability never overrides permission.

## Collective Chain
The Collective Chain is the connected graph of Intelligent Blocks, events, lineage and validated learning. Connections preserve provenance and authority rather than flattening all intelligence into one undifferentiated store.

## Naya-to-Naya inheritance
A new Naya should inherit useful intelligence without inheriting mistakes as unquestioned truth. Inheritance requires current-state reconciliation, authority resolution, evidence awareness, provenance, learning state and supersession/conflict handling.

## Model independence
Durable intelligence must survive model changes. The system therefore stores governed intelligence and evidence in durable structures rather than relying on one model's conversational memory.

## Collective success
Authorized participants can benefit from shared learning without losing who owns it, where it came from, whether it is verified, or what permissions govern its use.
