# 1. WHY IT EXISTS

The problem is not that humans lack ideas.

The problem is that turning an idea into reality usually requires the human to carry too much context:

- what we are doing;
- why we are doing it;
- what has already been tried;
- what is true now;
- what failed;
- what is unknown;
- what is allowed;
- what remains;
- what should happen next.

AI can generate enormous amounts of output while still losing the thread.

NayaPOWER exists to remove that burden.

> The human supplies vision, values, intent, and legitimate authority. NayaPOWER supplies governed continuity.
