## 9. The simplest possible cleanup algorithm

For every historical intelligence-bearing source:

**1. READ**  
Understand the document rather than judging its filename.

**2. CLASSIFY**  
CANONICAL / MIGRATABLE / PROJECTION / LEGACY / HISTORICAL / DUPLICATE-SUPERSEDED / NOISE-RETIRED.

**3. DISTILL**  
Extract only durable intelligence.

**4. CROSS-REFERENCE**  
Compare against the September 24 alignment and higher-authority current truth.

**5. RECONCILE**  
Keep what remains true. Update what evolved. Mark what is historical. Remove competing authority.

**6. DEDUPLICATE**  
If two sources mean the same thing, preserve provenance but produce one surviving canonical intelligence object.

**7. ASSIGN ID THROUGH THE LIVE CANONICAL RECEIVER**  
Never invent the next IB number in GitHub.

**8. PROJECT**  
Write the human-readable Smart Note at:

`.naya/memory/smart-notes/YYYY/MM/DD/category/topic/IB-XXXXXX/smart-note.md`

**9. VERIFY**  
Prove the live intelligence lifecycle separately from repository organization.

**10. ARCHIVE / RETAIN**  
Keep historical evidence until dependencies and provenance are safely resolved.
