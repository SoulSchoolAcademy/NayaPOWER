## Learning Lesson

**Prove causality one real object at a time.**

For every important intelligence path, ask:

1. Where did it originate?
2. Who had authority?
3. Where was it canonically persisted?
4. What receipt or evidence proves it?
5. What projection carries it?
6. Can the same object be retrieved after reload or in a fresh context?
7. Can duplicate, unauthorized, malformed, or conflicting inputs be rejected?

**Stop at the first missing link.**

Do not repair a link that evidence has already shown is correct.

This prevents architecture drift, false completion, reward hacking, and endless UI work while the engine remains unproven.
