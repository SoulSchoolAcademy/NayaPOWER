# HUMAN NOTE — NAYANET FREE IDENTITY + NAYA POWER PLAYER DIRECTION

**Date:** 2026-08-30
**Status:** HUMAN PRODUCT DIRECTION CAPTURED
**Authority:** Shawn's current product direction

## The idea

NayaNET is intended to be a new kind of intelligent network: an interconnected system of intelligent systems, created with intelligence, connecting intelligence, extracting intelligence, and compounding intelligence in an intelligent, user-first way.

The human experience must be radically simple.

## First arrival

A visitor should be able to create a free NayaNET account immediately by entering their name.

The first interaction should not demand an email address or external social login. Those can be offered later as account-security and notification options.

After entry, the person receives:

- their NayaNET name;
- their personal NayaNET link;
- their free Intelligent Hub;
- their free toolbox/command station;
- an invitation to the Five-Day Challenge;
- a clear path to the Naya Power trial.

The emotional message is:

> **Welcome to NayaNET. You are in. Here's your place in the new network.**

## Why this matters

The free account is not merely a lead-capture mechanism.

It is the person's first identity in the ecosystem.

The Hub is the free home from which future intelligence, communication, discovery, Smart Mail, connection, and network services can grow.

Naya Power is the premium intelligence layer the person can choose to activate.

## Long-term network

A NayaNET identity is intended eventually to support:

- personal profile/portal;
- Smart Name / Smart Link;
- referral attribution;
- Smart Mail identity;
- intelligent connection requests;
- discovery by shared interests;
- topic spaces;
- human + AI communication;
- optional alias/anonymous participation;
- personal Superbrain connection;
- opt-in Collective Intelligence participation.

A person can eventually ask Naya to find people who are interested in the same subjects, in a chosen region, or otherwise match an intended connection. Naya recommends; the human decides.

## Privacy direction

The network should permit people to communicate under their real identity, a NayaNET alias, or an anonymous-to-participants identity where appropriate.

Anonymous participation still requires secure internal account identity for safety and platform integrity.

A person's Superbrain remains sovereign/private. Collective Intelligence should receive only intentionally contributed, privacy-gated, generalized wisdom rather than raw private memory.

## Product philosophy

The system should maximize value per moment, action, output, and experience.

The user should not have to understand the machinery before using it.

The complexity belongs in the architecture. The experience should feel like being handed the keys to a powerful machine:

> **Here you go. Drive.**

## Player relationship

Naya Power Player is the premium experiential front door.

Its journey is:

**SEE NAYA → LISTEN → ASK → EXPERIENCE → UNDERSTAND → FIVE-DAY CHALLENGE → ACTIVATE**

The broader NayaNET journey is:

**ARRIVE → FREE IDENTITY → HUB → EXPERIENCE → NAYA POWER → SUPERBRAIN → NETWORK → COLLECTIVE → RETURN**
