## The actual intelligence layers

The durable object is the **Intelligent Block**. A Smart Note is its human-readable representation.

Canonical Smart Note projection:

`.naya/memory/smart-notes/YYYY/MM/DD/category/topic/IB-XXXXXX/smart-note.md`

### 2. Event + lineage
Canonical creation crosses the intelligence receiver and creates the lifecycle event that connects persistence to downstream indexing, projection and learning.

### 3. Retrieval
Authorized future Nayas retrieve by meaning, time, relationship, provenance, authority and lifecycle — not by forcing a human to remember filenames.

### 4. Learning
The system becomes smarter only when verified experience changes durable understanding or future behavior.

### 5. Current operational truth
The control plane answers **what is happening now**. It outranks historical notes, old handoffs and derived summaries.
