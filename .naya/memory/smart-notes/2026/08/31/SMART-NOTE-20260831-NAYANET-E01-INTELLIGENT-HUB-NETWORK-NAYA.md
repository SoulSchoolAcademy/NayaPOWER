## Intelligence Captured

The NayaNET product is being deliberately constructed as a sequence of protected experiences rather than one giant application surface. E01 is the doorway; later E-series blocks become the rooms and capabilities behind it.

The strategic model is:

**NayaNET = network layer**  
**Naya Power = intelligence layer**  
**Intelligent Hub = member cockpit into the ecosystem**

The initial activation should focus on practical AI empowerment rather than selling the abstract global-network vision. Network power becomes a discovery moment after the member is inside.

## Experience Logic

E01 must create curiosity and trust with minimal friction. One name is enough at entry. After entry, the system should immediately demonstrate value by revealing the person's Smart Name, Smart Mail identity, Smart Link, and toolbox.

The Hub should then make the ecosystem feel like a place rather than a SaaS dashboard. Naya remains the intelligence anchor and ecosystem capabilities orbit around the member's goals.

## Intelligence Network

The eventual network is more than search. Naya should be able to understand an intent such as finding people with shared interests in a location and help create the appropriate connection or intelligent space.

The communication model expands from human ↔ human to human ↔ Naya, human ↔ AI, and AI ↔ AI.

## Trust Boundary

Personal Superbrains are sovereign. Interconnection is opt-in and scoped. Shared intelligence must be deliberately authorized. Private memory must not become network property merely because a person joins NayaNET.

## Compounding Principle

The network becomes strategically powerful when legitimately shared intelligence can improve collective capability while preserving individual ownership and privacy.

**E01 → prove the doorway → protect it → build E02.**
