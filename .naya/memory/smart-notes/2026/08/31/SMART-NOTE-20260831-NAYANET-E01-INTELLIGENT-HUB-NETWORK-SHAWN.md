# 👤 Shawn Note — NayaNET E01 → Intelligent Hub → Intelligent Network

**Date:** 2026-08-31
**Event:** `NAYANET-E01-INTELLIGENT-HUB-NETWORK`
**Perspective:** Human / Product Owner

## Shawn's Intent

Build NayaNET as a new intelligent network that empowers people through Naya Power while eventually connecting humans, Nayas, and AIs in meaningful intelligent spaces.

The first public experience must be extremely simple and frictionless. The network should not lead with the grand future-network pitch. It should lead with practical value: get in, use Naya Power, take the 5-Day Challenge, learn AI successfully, and discover the larger ecosystem from inside.

## Identity Vision

A person initially provides only a name. That name becomes their human-facing NayaNET identity / Smart Name. They do not need to prove that it is their legal name. They can later change the public name if the new name is available. The machine must still preserve an immutable account ID underneath.

Each member should receive:

- Smart Name
- Smart Mail identity for internal NayaNET messaging
- Smart Link
- free NayaNET toolbox

Smart Mail is intentionally not a conventional external email mailbox in the first version.

## Intelligent Hub Vision

The Hub should feel like a premium cockpit rather than a dashboard. It should give people access to Naya Power, the 5-Day Challenge, AI Masterclass, HMC Library, Power Seven Days a Week, MAXESS, and Share / Invite.

The experience should use distinctive spatial/orbital composition where appropriate rather than defaulting to rectangular cards everywhere.

## Network Vision

NayaNET should ultimately make intelligent connection possible across:

- human ↔ human;
- human ↔ Naya;
- human ↔ AI;
- AI ↔ AI.

People should eventually be able to discover others by subject, geography, interests, presence, and intent, then communicate by text or audio and create intelligent spaces.

## Superbrain Vision

People may eventually connect their Naya Power / Superbrain to the wider network with explicit permission. NayaNET should never require access to their complete private Superbrain. People choose what can be connected, communicated, or shared. Selected wisdom and Smart Notes can contribute to collective intelligence only when deliberately authorized.

## Growth Vision

Every member should have a Smart Link / referral identity so sharing can preserve Ambassador attribution. Future CSV-based external invitations are a later growth capability and are separate from internal Smart Mail.

## Product Principle

The goal is not to create another social network. The goal is to create an intelligent network that helps humans and AIs become more capable by connecting learning, memory, intelligence, and people.

## Immediate Decision

**Build E01 first. Make the entrance extraordinary. Lock it. Then build E02 and continue block-by-block without regressing approved work.**
