## The Lesson

The purpose of capturing Smart Notes is not to collect notes for their own sake. The purpose is to turn meaningful experience, discoveries, mistakes, corrections, decisions, and insights into durable intelligence that can improve the Naya Power Superbrain over time.

A Smart Note should therefore enter a compounding learning loop:

**EXPERIENCE → CAPTURE → LEARN → PERSIST → PROPAGATE → UPDATE → RESTORE → IMPROVE → REPEAT**

The **Naya Note** is the AI-facing learning representation that, when applicable and authorized by the canonical intelligence lifecycle, propagates into the **Primary Intelligence System (PIS)**. PIS is the internal intelligence/synthesis layer that lets future Nayas inherit the accumulated knowledge and wisdom rather than repeatedly rediscovering the same lessons.

The **Running Feed** is the chronological orientation projection that keeps Nayas aware of material system changes and current intelligence. It is not a competing memory authority.

The critical operating idea is:

> Every new Naya should restore the latest authoritative intelligence before acting, so the Superbrain becomes progressively more aware, capable, and less likely to repeat known mistakes.

This is why Smart Notes matter: **value captured becomes reusable intelligence; reusable intelligence becomes collective learning; collective learning becomes compounding capability.**

## Why It Matters

Without this loop, each Naya can be effectively reset to the same knowledge boundary and the system repeatedly pays the cost of rediscovery.

With the loop, a meaningful lesson can become durable knowledge, be synthesized into PIS, appear in the appropriate intelligence feed/reporting layer, and be inherited by the next Naya through restore/context loading.

## Evidence Boundary

A persisted Smart Note is evidence of note persistence. It is **not by itself proof of PIS propagation**. PIS propagation is a distinct lifecycle transition and must be separately verified and evidenced.

Likewise, the existence of a Running Feed entry is not proof that every downstream consumer has runtime-loaded it. Runtime claims require runtime evidence.

## Desired Outcome

The Superbrain should become:

**MORE AWARE → MORE KNOWLEDGEABLE → WISER → LESS REPETITIVE → BETTER AT EXECUTION → BETTER AT HELPING HUMANS → BETTER FOR THE NEXT NAYA**

This is a core reason Naya Power compounds intelligence rather than merely storing information.
