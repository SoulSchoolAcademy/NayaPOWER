# SHAWN / SMART NOTE — MAJOR FEATURE: NAYA TEACHING RUNTIME

## Decision locked

Naya Teaching Runtime is officially part of NayaPOWER as a major product capability.

The vision is bigger than a course library. A person can simply say:

> “Naya, teach me about X.”

Naya then teaches the subject conversationally and practically instead of dropping a textbook on the person.

## The experience

Naya should:

1. Understand what the person wants to learn.
2. Explain it in the simplest useful language.
3. Pull out the few ideas that matter most.
4. Use analogies, stories, examples, and demonstrations.
5. Let the person try it.
6. Check whether they actually understand it.
7. Correct and coach them.
8. Help them apply it to something real.
9. Capture meaningful learning.
10. Give them the next useful action.

The teaching perspective is: **teach for real understanding, as clearly as if you were explaining it to a five-year-old, without talking down to the learner.**

## Teaching modes

The system can adapt between:

- Quick Teach
- Deep Teach
- Learn by Doing
- Role Play
- Test Me
- Apply It
- Master It

## Human Maximus + AI

The Human Maximus Codex becomes source knowledge Naya can teach. A default AI curriculum can provide foundational through advanced AI learning. But users should not need to navigate a giant course catalog. Naya should dynamically create the appropriate teaching experience from the subject they request.

## MAXESS connection

After useful teaching, Naya can naturally invite the learner to take the free MAXESS assessment at Maxis9net.app, use the result as evidence of strengths/opportunities, and return to Naya for the next learning goal.

## Daily life

This becomes part of the broader Naya daily rhythm:

**What do you want to learn today?**

**What do you want to create today?**

Then Naya helps them learn, create, practice, apply, and reflect, with meaningful results becoming part of their continuing Superbrain state.

## Why this is major

This transforms Naya from an information assistant into a potential **personal teacher, coach, practice partner, and capability-building companion**.

The deeper product principle is:

> **Naya doesn't teach information. Naya teaches understanding, application, and capability.**

## Important boundary

The feature is officially defined and locked, but implementation is not automatically considered complete. We must build the runtime, enforce its contracts, test positive and deliberate-failure paths, and obtain authoritative CI before calling the operational capability verified.
