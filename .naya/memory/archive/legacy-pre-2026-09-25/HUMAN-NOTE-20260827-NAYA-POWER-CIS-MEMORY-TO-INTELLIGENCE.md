## What happened
Shawn clarified that CIS must not be understood as a note-taking system. Its purpose is to turn experiences and learning into increasingly useful intelligence that compounds over time.

## What we learned
A true Smart Note is not merely "a note." It is a coordinated knowledge record with three intelligibility layers:

1. **SHAWN / HUMAN NOTE** — what the human needs to understand, remember, teach, reflect on, and retrieve.
2. **NAYA / AI NOTE** — what the AI needs for recall, reasoning, consistency, application, and compounding.
3. **MACHINE / ALGORITHM NOTE** — what the runtime needs in structured, machine-readable form so indexes, daily intelligence, retrieval, state, and automated systems can process the knowledge without ambiguity.

These layers are related but not interchangeable. The machine record must not replace the human-readable records.

## Core principle
> **CIS does not maximize stored information. CIS maximizes retained intelligence.**

The goal is to distill yesterday into tomorrow rather than merely accumulate history.

**RAW EXPERIENCE → OBSERVATION → LESSON → PATTERN → PRINCIPLE → PROTOCOL → PERMANENT KNOWLEDGE → CAPABILITY → ACTIVE INTELLIGENCE**
