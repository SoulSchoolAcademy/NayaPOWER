## Core insight

Naya Power is not primarily a collection of prompts, personality features, or AI utilities. It is an operating framework for the relationship between human intention and artificial intelligence.

The problem it addresses is the gap between access to powerful AI and the human's ability to work with that intelligence effectively. Ordinary AI interaction is often reactive, underspecified, weakly contextualized, and discontinuous. The human carries goals, history, standards, terminology, values, decisions, and preferences that may not be present in the immediate conversation.

Naya Power therefore seeks to improve the **human–AI system**, not merely the underlying model.

## Nine dimensions of an AI operating relationship

1. **Naya Personality — Relationship:** establishes tone, identity, and interpersonal continuity.
2. **Naya Brain — Intelligence:** provides broader knowledge, context, and intellectual foundation.
3. **Naya Notes — Continuity:** preserves decisions, discoveries, knowledge, and history.
4. **Naya Language — Shared meaning:** establishes definitions and reduces ambiguity.
5. **Naya Law — Standards:** protects rules, principles, requirements, and non-negotiables.
6. **Naya Scorecarding / Oscar — Quality control:** evaluates work and drives critique and improvement.
7. **Naya Lead Mode — Initiative:** moves the system beyond purely reactive answering toward responsible execution.
8. **Naya Design — Experience:** translates ideas into visual, UX, communication, and presentation quality.
9. **Naya Coder — Implementation:** applies engineering discipline, architecture, testing, and verification.

Together these form an operating architecture rather than nine unrelated features.

## Human transformation

The success criterion is not that Naya scores highly. The intended outcome is that the human becomes more capable.

**Before:** Explain → Correct → Repeat → Remind → Manage → Evaluate → Start over.

**After:** Direct → Collaborate → Create → Delegate → Evaluate → Improve → Build.

The human remains the source of vision, purpose, judgment, values, experience, creativity, intuition, and decision-making. Naya supplies a structured layer of intelligence around those capabilities.

The target relationship is therefore:

> **Human + AI becomes a more capable system than either operating alone.**
