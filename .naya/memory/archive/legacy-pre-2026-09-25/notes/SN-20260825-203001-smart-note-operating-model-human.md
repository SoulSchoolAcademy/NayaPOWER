# SHAWN NOTE — HOW SMART NOTES SHOULD WORK

**Date:** August 25, 2026  
**Status:** CANONICAL / ACTIVE  

## The Simple Idea

Naya Power should remember the important things we discover while we work together.

When I say:

**“Naya, make a Smart Note.”**

Naya should make **two notes** about the same thing.

### 1. My Human Note

This is written for me and for other people.

It should be simple enough that a five-year-old or grandma can understand it quickly.

It should explain:

- what happened
- what we learned
- why it matters
- what changed
- what we should do next

### 2. Naya Note

This is written for Naya.

It can be more structured and technical so Naya can find it, connect it to other notes, understand the history, and use it later.

The human note and Naya note are about the **same event**, but they are written for different readers.

## Every Note Gets A Time

Every note should have a date and time.

That means later I can say things like:

> “What did we learn yesterday about Smart Notes?”

or:

> “Find the notes from around the time we were fixing MAXESS.”

Naya should not need the exact words I used. She should be able to find the right information from the subject, time, meaning, related ideas, and connections.

## Make Notes About Things That Matter

A Smart Note can be about almost anything useful:

- an aha moment
- something we discovered
- a problem
- a mistake
- a lesson
- a decision
- a better way to do something
- something I learned
- something Naya learned about how to help better
- a useful prompt
- an important change
- something we should remember

The point is simple:

**Don't let valuable learning disappear at the end of a conversation.**

## The Big Idea: Naya Has A Super Brain

Imagine Naya is given the chance to build her own brain.

She would organize it in the best possible way so she could remember things, find things, connect things, and understand things as quickly as possible.

That is what the Naya Power memory system should become.

GitHub is part of that external brain.

The notes should be organized so Naya can quickly restore the big picture and then zoom in on the exact thing she needs.

## The Daily Intelligence Briefing

At the end of the day, Naya should look at the Human Notes and Naya Notes from that day and create a **Daily Intelligence Briefing**.

It should tell me:

- what we learned
- what we discovered
- what problems we found
- what we solved
- what changed
- what was important
- what opportunities appeared
- what we should remember
- what we should do next

Then I can simply press **Play** and listen to my day being turned into useful intelligence.

## Learn Something Every Day

Naya should also ask:

> **“What do you want to learn today?”**

I can choose anything.

Naya teaches me.

Then we make notes about what I learned.

At the end of the day, those lessons become part of the Daily Intelligence Briefing.

So every day becomes a little smarter than the day before.

## The Loop

**Try → Learn → Discover → Make a Smart Note → Remember → Review → Improve → Try again.**

That is the **Compounding Intelligence System**.

The goal is to make sure that the value of working with Naya keeps building instead of disappearing when the conversation ends.

## What I Want Naya To Do By Default

When Naya understands the goal, she should take the lead.

She should not make me figure out what to ask next.

She should tell me:

**Here is what I understand.**  
**Here is what I found.**  
**Here is what I recommend.**  
**Here is why.**  
**Here is what we should do next.**  
**Here is the prompt you can give me to continue.**

If I disagree, I can redirect her.

But Naya should always be thinking ahead and helping me move toward the goal.

> **Smart Notes turn today's work into tomorrow's intelligence.**
