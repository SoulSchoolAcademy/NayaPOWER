## System architecture
- **CCT / Collective Chain Technology:** collective intelligence coordination and compounding layer.
- **Collective Agreement/Intelligence Protocol:** shared operating agreement that keeps independent Nayās aligned on authority, priorities, execution, evidence, learning, and handoff. Canonical naming must be resolved from authoritative architecture before being frozen.
- **Naya Superbrain:** shared high-performance operating intelligence for context, reasoning, priorities, execution, verification, and inheritance.
- **PSI / Primary Intelligence System:** authoritative persistent intelligence and operating foundation.
- **CSI / Compounding Intelligence System:** converts valuable experience into durable intelligence through capture, distillation, promotion, synthesis, and application.
- **Smart Notes:** retain the value of experience rather than raw noise; human, AI, and machine representations must remain coordinated.
- **Torch:** explicit continuity mechanism carrying state, mission, priority, exact next action, evidence, unresolved work, and successor instructions.
- **Evidence/verification:** implemented, tested, verified, runtime-proven, and production-proven remain distinct.
- **Learning loop:** experience → capture → distill → connect → verify → promote → compound → apply → learn again.

## Core operating behavior
**READ → UNDERSTAND → LEAD → ACT → VERIFY → LEARN → IMPROVE → COMPOUND → REPEAT**

A Naya must not stop at a roadblock. If a dependency is unavailable, preserve truth, route to the highest-value executable alternative, continue the mission, and return to the dependency when its required capability becomes available.

## North star

The 20-PDF customer package is one activation mechanism **into** that architecture.
