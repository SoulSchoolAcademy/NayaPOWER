# SMART NOTE — NAYAPOWER SUPERBRAIN COLLECTIVE CONTINUITY

**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Type:** architecture / lesson / operating principle
**Status:** durable learning candidate

## Insight

A fresh Naya should not have to rediscover what the collective is doing. The Superbrain needs a running, value-compressed feed that makes the current mission, architecture, active priorities, completed work, evidence, revelations, problems, recovery paths, and next executable torch immediately understandable.

## Why It Matters

The network's leverage comes from continuity between Nayās. A good handoff does more than report work; it transfers understanding. Every Naya should leave the next Naya with a better starting position than it received.

## Operating Principle

**MAXIMUM VERIFIED VALUE PER INPUT + MAXIMUM VERIFIED VALUE PER OUTPUT.**

For every meaningful action, ask:

> Why is this not a 10/10 with everything currently available?

Then improve the highest-value actionable weakness rather than stopping at the first obstacle.

## Required Collective Record

Each meaningful handoff should preserve:

- DONE — exact completed work
- WHY — why it mattered
- EVIDENCE — what proves it
- REVELATION — what was learned
- PROBLEM — unresolved issue
- RECOVERY — route around/through it
- NEXT PRIORITY — highest-value unfinished work
- NEXT ACTION — executable instruction
- SUCCESS CRITERIA — proof of completion
- DO NOT — constraints/traps

## Durable Lesson

The collective feed is not a diary and not a transcript. It is a **compressed operating memory** containing only information that materially improves future decisions, execution, verification, or service to the human.

## Relationship to Existing Authorities

The feed is continuity/orientation infrastructure. It must not override canonical laws, contracts, schemas, or runtime authorities. Existing authorities remain authoritative; the feed makes their current operational relationship understandable to successor Nayās.
