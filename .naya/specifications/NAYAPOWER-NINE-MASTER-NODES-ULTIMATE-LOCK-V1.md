## Three triads

### Orientation
`SELF → LAW → ACT`

- SELF: Who are we, why are we here, and what state are we in?
- LAW: What governs us and what may legitimately happen?
- ACT: What exact authorized effect should occur?

### Cognition
`KNOW → PROVE → CONNECT`

- KNOW: What durable intelligence exists and what does it mean?
- PROVE: What does the evidence justify claiming, and how strongly?
- CONNECT: What is related, current, applicable, and contextually useful now?

### Evolution
`VERIFY → LEARN → EVOLVE`

- VERIFY: What actually happened relative to the declared outcome boundary?
- LEARN: What verified lesson deserves to change future behavior, within what scope?
- EVOLVE: What verified, governed improvement should survive into the next legitimate version?

EVOLVE returns the baton to SELF. The loop is responsibility continuity, not circular self-certification.
