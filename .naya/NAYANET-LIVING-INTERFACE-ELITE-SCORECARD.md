# NayaNET Living Interface — Elite Scorecard

## Purpose
This is the release gate for the NayaNET Living Interface. It evaluates the experience itself, not the amount of code, visual novelty, or architecture documentation.

## Weighted score
| Dimension | Weight | A 10 means |
|---|---:|---|
| Immediate Understanding | 12% | A first-time visitor instantly understands what NayaNET is, what Naya is, what the Power Player is, and the next action without instruction. |
| Push-Button Simplicity | 12% | The primary action is obvious, one-touch where possible, and every control communicates outcome before press. |
| Living Responsiveness | 12% | Every important object has meaningful rest, focus, hover, press, active, loading, success and return states; feedback is immediate and purposeful. |
| Visual Excellence & Craft | 11% | Composition, typography, light, depth, spacing, artwork, hierarchy and micro-detail feel deliberately authored; nothing is flat or accidental. |
| Power Player Experience | 10% | Listening is the heartbeat of the environment: real artwork, real audio, instant selection, persistent playback, clear progress, immersive expansion and effortless return. |
| Navigation & Spatial Architecture | 9% | Naya is the stable center; worlds feel like destinations; entering/exiting preserves context and never strands the user. |
| Mobile & Responsive Experience | 9% | Mobile is deliberately composed, touch-first and intimate rather than a compressed desktop layout. |
| Emotional Impact / Wow | 8% | The experience creates genuine moments of surprise, recognition and delight through function, not decoration. |
| Accessibility & Inclusivity | 6% | Keyboard, focus, reduced motion, readable type, contrast, semantics and large touch targets are first-class. |
| Performance & Technical Quality | 6% | Immediate interaction, resilient loading, non-blocking audio, no fake functionality, no broken states, no console-level obvious failures. |
| Design System Integrity | 5% | Every object follows one coherent visual language and semantic color/state system. |

## Score bands
- 0–3: Poor
- 4–5: Functional
- 6–7: Good
- 8: Excellent
- 9: Elite
- 10: World-class

## Non-negotiable release rules
1. No generic dashboard composition.
2. No dead rectangles as the primary interaction language.
3. No decorative animation without communicating state, hierarchy, navigation or atmosphere.
4. No important action hidden behind ambiguous icons.
5. No world opens as a tiny tooltip or weak modal; a destination earns a full-page experience.
6. The Power Player is part of the environment, not a footer widget.
7. The Living Sun is useful spatial architecture, not a decorative ring of buttons.
8. Real Powercast artwork/audio must remain the content backbone.
9. Return to Naya is always obvious and preserves listening context.
10. A child, grandmother, technical expert and technology-hater must all be able to demonstrate the primary journey without training.
11. Beauty never compensates for confusion.
12. Motion never blocks action.
13. Every important visual object must have a functional reason to exist.

## Core experience test
ARRIVE → UNDERSTAND → ENTER → FEEL NAYA → SEE/HEAR THE PLAYER → EXPLORE A WORLD → RETURN → CONTINUE LISTENING → DISCOVER → ACT.

## North star
> Push-button simple. Living depth. Maximum useful impact. Zero unnecessary complexity.

> Calm on the surface. Extraordinary underneath.

> The interface should feel like the place where human intent meets intelligence — not another app pretending to be intelligent.
