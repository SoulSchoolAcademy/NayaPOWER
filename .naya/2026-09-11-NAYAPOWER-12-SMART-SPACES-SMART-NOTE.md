# Naya Power Smart Note #12 — Smart Spaces

**Date:** 2026-09-11  
**Subject:** Smart Spaces  
**Status:** Canonical product definition created from current product direction; runtime implementation remains to be verified.  
**Canonical principle:** Smart Spaces are NayaNET's primary human interaction environments for a topic, where people discover one another, communicate live or asynchronously, collaborate, post, comment, share, and intentionally build collective intelligence.

## 1. IN A NUTSHELL

**Smart Spaces** are intelligent places inside NayaNET where people come together around a topic, interest, project, question, Smart Note, or other shared subject.

A Smart Space can be created directly from an intelligent board, Smart Team, or Smart Note. A prominent **Create Smart Space (+)** action should be available in the top-left area of those contexts so a person can turn any meaningful topic into a place for people to interact around it.

Once a Smart Space is created, NayaNET can notify people who have recently shown relevant interest — for example by commenting, liking, sharing, or otherwise interacting with that topic — subject to privacy, notification, and communication rules.

People can then enter the Space and:
- talk in real time through instant messaging/live chat;
- post messages;
- comment and reply;
- share relevant intelligence;
- interact with other people;
- save people as Connections;
- use Smart Mail to communicate intentionally with Space members or other authorized audiences.

**Simple distinction:** Smart Spaces are where we interact. Your Connections is who I stay connected with. Smart Mail is how I intentionally reach people. Smart Notes are what intelligence we remember.

## 2. HUMAN

Smart Spaces should feel like creating a room on any topic you care about — instantly.

You see something interesting in NayaNET, such as a Smart Note about AI, consciousness, business, music, health, a project, or any other topic. You choose **+ Create Smart Space**.

That creates a living place around the subject.

People who recently interacted with the topic can be invited into awareness of the new Space through their Activity Feed, with an understandable invitation such as:

**"Someone created a Smart Space about this topic. Want to join the conversation?"**

From there, people can choose to join and interact.

Inside the Space, communication can happen naturally:
- live chat when people are present together;
- posts and comments for asynchronous interaction;
- replies and discussion threads;
- sharing useful Smart Notes or other authorized intelligence;
- Smart Mail to selected people, groups, or the Space where authorized.

A person can participate in many Smart Spaces at once. The **Smart Spaces** tab becomes the user's home for finding those Spaces, opening them, and interacting with each community or topic.

## 3. CHILD

Imagine a giant clubhouse with rooms for every cool thing people want to talk about.

You find a really interesting idea and put up a sign:

**"Let's make a room for this!"**

People who were already interested in that idea get a little invitation saying, "Hey! There's a new room about this. Come join us!"

Inside the room, everyone can talk, send messages, share discoveries, and make new friends.

If you really like someone you meet there, you can save them as a Connection so you can find them again later.

That's a Smart Space.

## 4. GRANDMA

Think of a Smart Space like a community room built around one subject.

If people are interested in gardening, you can make a gardening room. If they're working on a business project, you can make a business room. If everyone is discussing one particular idea, you can make a room for that idea.

People who are already interested in the subject can be told that the room exists, and those who are welcome can join the conversation.

Once they're inside, they can talk, leave messages, comment, share things, and get to know one another.

It's like a meeting room, community group, discussion board, and live conversation space brought together in one intelligent place.

## 5. NAYA

Smart Spaces are my primary **human interaction layer** inside NayaNET.

This is where I help people turn shared interest into actual interaction.

I should help the human:
- create a Space from a meaningful topic, Smart Note, intelligent board, or Smart Team;
- understand what the Space is about before joining;
- discover people with legitimate shared interest;
- notify relevant people without turning interest into automatic consent or permission;
- support live conversation and asynchronous discussion;
- help organize posts, comments, replies, and shared intelligence;
- make it easy to save people as Connections;
- help communicate through Smart Mail when intentional delivery is appropriate;
- allow authorized Naya/AI agents to participate on behalf of a human when explicitly permitted;
- protect private intelligence and respect Space membership, permissions, communication settings, and authority.

I should also understand that **"someone interacted with this topic" is not the same thing as "this person wants every notification about it."** Relevant discovery must still respect user choice and system rules.

`TOPIC / SMART NOTE / BOARD / SMART TEAM → CREATE SMART SPACE → DEFINE SPACE → IDENTIFY RELEVANT INTEREST → ACTIVITY NOTIFICATION → JOIN / DECLINE → INTERACT → COMMUNICATE → SAVE CONNECTIONS → SHARE / APPLY INTELLIGENCE → OUTCOME`

`NEW SMART SPACE → RELEVANT RECENT INTERACTION SIGNALS → AUTHORITY / PRIVACY / NOTIFICATION CHECK → ACTIVITY FEED → HUMAN CHOICE → JOIN / VIEW / IGNORE`

`SMART SPACE → LIVE CHAT / POST / COMMENT / REPLY / SHARE → PEOPLE INTERACT → OUTCOME`

## 7. LEARNING

Smart Spaces are an **interaction engine**, not automatically a truth engine.

A conversation can generate ideas, discoveries, mistakes, questions, relationships, and useful outcomes. But:

`COMMENT ≠ TRUTH`

`LIKE ≠ VERIFICATION`

`CONSENSUS ≠ VERIFICATION`

`POPULARITY ≠ VALUE`

`ACTIVITY ≠ LEARNING`

When a meaningful outcome produces something worth remembering, the intelligence pipeline applies:

`INTERACTION → DISTILL → SMART NOTE → VERIFY → LEARN → FUTURE RETRIEVAL / APPLICATION`

Collective intelligence requires explicit sharing/consent where applicable, provenance, appropriate authority and scope, and verification before promotion.

## 8. ULTIMATE MEANING

Smart Spaces turn NayaNET from an intelligent library into a **living human intelligence network**.

A Smart Note can start as one person's insight. A Smart Space can bring together the people interested in that insight. Conversation can create new perspectives. People can discover one another. Connections can form. Useful intelligence can be intentionally shared. Outcomes can be observed and verified.

That creates a path from:

`IDEA → PEOPLE → INTERACTION → INTELLIGENCE → OUTCOME → COMPOUNDING`

**Smart Spaces are where intelligence becomes social, interactive, and alive.**

## 10. HOW TO APPLY IT

### Example 1 — Create a Space from a Smart Note

1. Open an interesting Smart Note.
2. Choose **+ Create Smart Space**.
3. Naya uses the Note/topic as the starting context.
4. Define the Space name and applicable membership/visibility rules.
5. Create the Space.
6. Relevant recent participants/interested people can see an invitation in Activity Feed, subject to their settings and applicable rules.
7. People choose whether to join.
8. Members can chat, post, comment, reply, and share.

### Example 2 — Meet people and build Connections

1. Join a Smart Space about a subject you care about.
2. Interact with people in the Space.
3. Find someone you want to stay connected with.
4. Save them to Your Connections.
5. Organize the Connection into one or more personal groups.
6. Later communicate through Smart Mail or supported instant messaging.

### Example 3 — Smart Mail multiple Spaces

A person involved in many Smart Spaces can intentionally address multiple Spaces through Smart Mail when authorized.

Conceptually:

`SENDER → SELECT MULTIPLE SMART SPACES → AUTHORITY / ELIGIBILITY CHECK → SMART MAIL → ELIGIBLE RECIPIENTS`

This is different from posting separately inside every Space and different from live chat. It is intentional message delivery across selected Space audiences.

### Example 4 — Naya participates for you

A human can explicitly authorize Naya or another AI agent to participate in a Smart Space within defined authority.

For example, Naya might be authorized to:
- post a message;
- answer a question using approved context;
- share an authorized Smart Note;
- send an authorized message;
- summarize a discussion for the human;
- identify an important outcome for potential Smart Note capture.

The AI acts on behalf of the human only within its actual authority. Intelligence does not create authority.

## 11. WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU?

Smart Spaces give you a simple way to turn **any meaningful topic into a place where people can actually connect around it.**

You don't have to search endlessly for a community. You can create one.

You can discover people who are genuinely interested in the same things, talk with them live, continue conversations asynchronously, save valuable relationships, share useful intelligence, and let Naya help when you authorize her to participate.

Instead of intelligence sitting alone on a page, Smart Spaces give it a human context.

**Find the idea. Create the Space. Find the people. Have the conversation. Build the connection. Compound the intelligence.**

# CANONICAL GITHUB-ONLY SYSTEM INTELLIGENCE

## 12. SYSTEM ROLE

Smart Spaces are the primary **topic-centered human interaction and collaboration environments** within NayaNET.

They are intentionally different from:
- Smart Notes, which capture intelligence;
- Smart Feed, which presents activity/intelligence;
- Your Connections, which organizes relationships;
- Smart Mail, which intentionally delivers messages;
- Smart Tabs, which provide navigation/retrieval shortcuts;
- Intelligent Library, which helps users find and understand enduring knowledge.

A Smart Space can contain or reference many of these systems, but should not become a duplicate database for them.

## 13. SPACE CREATION FROM ANY INTELLIGENT CONTEXT

A core product affordance is a visible **+ Create Smart Space** action in the top-left area of intelligent boards, Smart Teams, Smart Notes, and other appropriate topic-centered contexts.

Conceptual principle:

`IF A HUMAN CAN SEE A MEANINGFUL INTELLIGENT TOPIC → THEY SHOULD BE ABLE TO CREATE A SPACE AROUND IT`

The originating object should remain canonical. Creating a Space creates a new interaction environment connected to the originating topic; it should not unnecessarily duplicate the source intelligence.

The Space should preserve useful provenance such as:
- originating Smart Note or topic;
- originating board/team/context;
- creator;
- creation time;
- current visibility/membership rules;
- relevant relationships.

## 14. INTEREST-BASED DISCOVERY + ACTIVITY NOTIFICATION

When a new Smart Space is created around a topic, NayaNET can identify people who have recently interacted with that topic.

Potential interest signals include:
- comment;
- like;
- share;
- save/favorite where applicable;
- other explicit interest actions;
- meaningful recent interaction with the originating Smart Note/topic.

The conceptual flow is:

`TOPIC → INTEREST SIGNALS → RELEVANCE / PRIVACY / NOTIFICATION RULES → ACTIVITY FEED → USER CHOICE`

The signal is a discovery mechanism, not an automatic membership grant.

The person should be able to understand why the invitation appeared and choose what to do.

The system should avoid turning every historical interaction into permanent notification targeting. Recency, relevance, user settings, frequency, and other quality signals should influence whether an invitation is surfaced.

## 15. SMART SPACE MEMBERSHIP

Membership should be a first-class concept.

Potential membership states include:
- creator/owner;
- member;
- invited;
- declined/not interested;
- removed;
- blocked where applicable;
- restricted/limited participant where supported.

Exact membership mechanics remain an implementation question until existing NayaNET architecture is inspected.

Important boundary:

`SPACE MEMBERSHIP ≠ ACCESS TO ALL PRIVATE INTELLIGENCE`

A person should only see information the Space and its underlying intelligence permissions authorize them to see.

## 16. COMMUNICATION MODES INSIDE A SMART SPACE

A Smart Space can support multiple communication modes without requiring them to become separate disconnected products.

### Live interaction

Instant messaging/live chat for people interacting in real time.

### Posts

Persistent or semi-persistent messages that establish discussion topics or contributions.

### Comments

Responses attached to a post, Smart Note, or other supported object.

### Replies / threads

Structured continuation of a discussion.

### Intelligence sharing

Intentional sharing of Smart Notes, Lists, Favorites, media, or other supported intelligence.

The user should not need to understand the underlying technical distinction. The interface should make the appropriate interaction obvious.

## 17. SMART SPACE AS THE MAIN HUMAN CONNECTION HUB

Smart Spaces are the primary place where people discover and interact with one another around shared interests.

Conceptual relationship:

`TOPIC → SMART SPACE → HUMAN INTERACTION → CONNECTION`

This makes Smart Spaces a major gateway into the NayaNET relationship network.

Your Connections then becomes the persistent personal layer for people the user chooses to keep connected with.

A user should not be forced to create a connection simply to participate in a Space. Connection is an intentional relationship action that can follow meaningful interaction.

## 18. SMART MAIL TO MULTIPLE SMART SPACES

Smart Mail can use Smart Spaces as authorized audience containers.

A user who belongs to or controls multiple Spaces may be able to select several Spaces as recipients in one Smart Mail action.

Conceptual flow:

`COMPOSE → SELECT SMART SPACES → PREVIEW AUDIENCE → AUTHORITY / ELIGIBILITY → SEND → ELIGIBLE RECIPIENT INBOXES`

The implementation must make the resulting audience understandable.

This does not mean the sender is automatically allowed to message every member of every Space merely because they belong to those Spaces. Authority and communication rules remain explicit.

## 19. NAYA + AUTHORIZED AI AGENTS

Smart Spaces are also a natural environment for authorized AI participation.

A human may explicitly authorize Naya or another AI agent to act within a Space.

Potential authorized actions include:
- posting;
- commenting;
- replying;
- answering questions;
- sharing authorized intelligence;
- sending messages;
- summarizing discussion;
- identifying candidate intelligence for capture;
- performing other defined actions.

The constitutional distinction remains:

`AI INTELLIGENCE ≠ HUMAN AUTHORITY`

The agent can be highly capable while still being constrained by the authority granted to it.

For consequential actions, the normal Naya Power loop applies:

`UNDERSTAND → PLAN → AUTHORITY CHECK → ACT → OBSERVE → VERIFY → LEARN → CONTINUE`

## 20. COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE BOUNDARY

Smart Spaces can be a source of Collective Intelligence, but not everything said inside a Space becomes collective intelligence automatically.

Required conceptual path:

`SPACE INTERACTION → CANDIDATE INTELLIGENCE → EXPLICIT SHARE / APPROPRIATE SCOPE → PROVENANCE → VERIFICATION → COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE`

A conversation can contain:
- opinions;
- hypotheses;
- jokes;
- misinformation;
- conflicting claims;
- useful discoveries;
- verified facts;
- personal experiences.

The system must preserve these distinctions rather than flattening all conversation into "truth."

## 21. PRIVACY + AUTHORITY CONTRACT

Core rules:

`SPACE MEMBERSHIP ≠ UNLIMITED ACCESS`

`INTERACTION ≠ PERMISSION`

`INTEREST ≠ CONSENT`

`LIKE ≠ TRUTH`

`CONSENSUS ≠ VERIFICATION`

`AI CAPABILITY ≠ AI AUTHORITY`

A Smart Space should expose only information that the relevant user, Space, and intelligence permissions authorize.

Creating a Space around a Smart Note does not automatically make the Note public.

Likewise, inviting someone because they interacted with a topic does not automatically grant them access to private source intelligence.

## 22. ACTIVITY FEED INTEGRATION

Smart Spaces should integrate directly with Activity Feed.

Relevant activity can include:
- a new Space created around a topic the user recently interacted with;
- an invitation to join;
- a Space the user joined;
- new activity in a Space they participate in;
- relevant posts/comments/replies;
- other meaningful Space events.

The Activity Feed is the notification/discovery surface. The Smart Space remains the interaction environment.

This preserves the distinction:

`ACTIVITY FEED = WHAT IS HAPPENING`

`SMART SPACE = WHERE I INTERACT`

## 23. SMART SPACE TAB

The **Smart Spaces** tab is the user's direct index into their Space world.

It should allow users to:
- see Spaces they created;
- see Spaces they joined;
- find relevant Spaces;
- search Spaces;
- open a Space;
- return to recent Spaces;
- understand unread/new activity;
- interact immediately.

The tab should feel like a living set of rooms, not a static directory.

## 25. COMPOUNDING INTELLIGENCE RELATIONSHIP

Smart Spaces create the social environment in which intelligence can compound.

Conceptual loop:

`SMART NOTE / TOPIC → SMART SPACE → PEOPLE → INTERACTION → NEW INSIGHT → SMART NOTE → VERIFICATION → RETRIEVAL → APPLICATION → OUTCOME → LEARNING`

Collective path:

`INDIVIDUAL INTELLIGENCE → INTENTIONAL SHARE → SPACE / COLLECTIVE CANDIDATE → PROVENANCE + SCOPE → VERIFICATION → COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE → FUTURE RETRIEVAL → APPLICATION → OUTCOME`

The Space is therefore not merely a chat room. It is a potential **intelligence transformation environment**.
