## SMART MAIL — CURRENT CANONICAL DEFINITION

Smart Mail is **not another feed and not a disconnected email utility**. It is the NayaNET communication and message-routing layer.

It should support, when authorized:

**Human → Smart Mail → Human**  
**Human → Smart Mail → AI**  
**AI → Smart Mail → Human**  
**AI → Smart Mail → AI**

Initial implementation can support human-created/copy-pasted messages. Direct AI/API sending comes after identity, authentication, authorization, recipient resolution, rate limits, auditability, and delivery verification are reliable.

## INTELLIGENT BLOCK NETWORKING

When an Intelligent Block appears in a shared/Collective context, authorized users may eventually be able to:

- connect with the author;
- share the block;
- save/favorite it;
- propose a Smart Space around its topic;
- invite relevant participants based on permitted interaction signals.

Interactions such as likes, comments, favorites, and shares may generate **candidate** connection/Space suggestions, but they do not automatically expose private identities or enroll users in a Space.

A proposed Space should be opt-in and permission-aware.

Example notification concept:

Eligible people may be invited to participate, with private communication available through their configured alias where authorized.
