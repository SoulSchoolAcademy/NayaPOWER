## 1. THE SMART NOTE LAW

A meaningful Naya Power event does not disappear into the conversation.

It becomes a durable Smart Note event with three complementary layers:

1. **SHAWN NOTE** — the human observation, decision, intention, reaction, or judgment.
2. **NAYA NOTE** — the AI interpretation, synthesis, insight, implication, or recommended continuation.
3. **MACHINE NOTE** — the durable structured implementation/state record that another AI or engineer can act on without relying on conversation memory.

These are three views of the same canonical event.

> **SHAWN EXPERIENCES → NAYA UNDERSTANDS → MACHINE PRESERVES.**

The three layers must not contradict one another. The Machine Note must never silently invent facts that were not present in the Shawn Note, Naya Note, or verified project evidence.

> **THE FEED SHOWS WHAT THE SYSTEM LEARNED AND WHAT NOW MATTERS.**
