## 2. The Three Objects

| Object | What it is | What it is not |
|---|---|---|
| **Smart Note** | The human-readable intelligence artifact | A storage bucket for whatever the AI said |
| **Intelligent Block** | The canonical, provenance-bound unit of reusable intelligence | A row in a table |

An Intelligent Block is **a unit of cognition that survives its original conversation.**

The conversation ends. The intelligence doesn't have to.

## 3. What problem this solves

**Intelligence loss.** Insight dies in conversations, documents, meetings, tabs, and people's heads — then the next person rediscovers it.

Captured properly, one experience becomes a building block another person, another Naya, or another project can actually use.

---

## 4. The Six Operations — the intelligence compressor

| Operation | Action |
|---|---|
| **SYNERGIZE** | Bring together everything that belongs together |
| **OPTIMIZE** | Remove repetition, contradiction, dead paths, noise |
| **MAXIMIZE** | Keep maximum useful meaning — simplify without destroying intelligence |
| **EQUALIZE** | Give each piece its proper weight, authority, scope, confidence |
| **ORGANIZE** | Place each piece with its provenance, relationships, applicable rules |
| **DISTILL** | Compress until **precise**, not until vague |

**Target:** maximum intelligence, minimum words, zero ambiguity.

A 56-page analysis → a few hundred words of essential intelligence. That is not loss. That is compression with preserved meaning.

## 5. Perspectives

Different perspectives answer different questions. Each earns its place only by improving understanding or usefulness.

### 👤 Human — what the human actually said
The original contribution, preserved verbatim in intent. **Protects against the AI rewriting history** and passing its interpretation off as the original fact.

### 🤖 AI / Naya — what the AI understood
Synthesis: what it means, why it matters, what relationships and implications follow. **Must stay distinguishable from the human's contribution.**

### 👶 Simple — explain it to a child
If it cannot be said simply, it may not yet be understood. This is the fastest honesty test in the system.

### ✍️ Language / Grammar — the cleanest expression
The canonical wording, terminology, definitions, and naming. Not proofreading — it removes ambiguity so the same idea is not re-invented six ways.

### ⚙️ Machine — what the system needs to operate it
Identity, type, scope, source, relationships, authority, confidence, applicability, dependencies, status, provenance, event lineage. **This is what turns readable into operational.**

### 🔍 Evidence + 🧬 Provenance — what proves it, and where it came from
What supports the claim; its origin and lineage. Without these, a claim is an assertion.

### 🛡️ Governance — what authority, consent, and restrictions apply
Scope, authority, privacy classification, and what must not happen with it.

> **Rule:** perspectives exist because they improve understanding — not to fill fields. Otherwise DISTILL is violated.

## 8. The failure mode this prevents: bloat

**The trap:** dumping everything into the Super Brain. The system becomes bloated, slow, loaded with redundant output and raw books nobody reads. Then retrieval degrades and the intelligence is buried.

**The discipline:** store the *root essence*, optimized and organized — not the raw stream.

- **For the user:** a few hundred skimmable words they will actually read, not 40 pages they won't. They want the book; the raw output is too much to read.
- **For the Super Brain:** only distilled, relevant intelligence is retained — so it stays fast, relevant, and capable.

> **Every substantial output should be distillable into a Smart Note.** Not every exchange — but the ones that carry durable value.
