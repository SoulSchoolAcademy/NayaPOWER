## SHAWN NOTE — WHAT SHAWN ACTUALLY DECIDED

The supplied **NayaNET — The World-Class Interface Constitution** is now locked as the absolute North Star for implementation.

NayaNET must be built as a **high-level interactive app, not a website**. The user must feel that they have entered an intelligent place rather than opened a webpage. The Front Door must be a minimal app-entry/authentication experience centered on a physical circular Smart ID object and a physical Enter/Power control—not a rectangular form, flat button, marketing landing page, or generic login box.

The Living Hub must place Naya at the center as the Living Sun, surround her with exactly nine worlds, and make the Naya Power Player the heartbeat of the environment. The existing luminous orb/light DNA is worth preserving, but the current website-like composition, flat controls, weak text treatment, inconsistent orb anatomy, and weak center treatment are not protected. Those must be replaced deliberately.

The nine worlds are:

1. Naya Power
2. Super Brain
3. Smart Identity
4. Smart Notes / Intelligence
5. MAXESS
6. 5-Day Challenge
7. Intelligent Spaces
8. Your Network
9. Power Player

Every world uses one canonical physical orb construction. Every important object has material, depth, state, purpose, and feedback. Primary controls use physical Power/GO/Standard/Naya/Gold-Signature/Danger families and meaningful interaction states. Tiny text, generic cards, flat controls, dashboard patterns, excessive copy, decorative futurism, and pink/magenta as a default language are rejected.

NayaNET Level 1 is an **intelligence network, not a media network**. The foundation prioritizes personal Naya, memory, Smart Notes, Daily Intelligence, text/voice communication, Intelligent Spaces, human-to-human intelligence exchange, Naya-to-Naya connection, and compounding intelligence. Image/video upload and public media/social-network behavior are deferred to later levels.

Identity is private by default. Real name remains private. Smart Name/Alias is the default network-facing identity. Smart Link becomes the user's canonical NayaNET address, Intelligent Hub/profile destination, Ambassador attribution point, and future communication address. Smart Mail derives from that identity. Shared intelligence and communication use the alias by default and require permission before exposure.

Each person's Naya is their own evolving intelligence system with unique context, memory, relationship, and identity. NayaNET connects personal intelligences by permission; it does not collapse everyone into one giant shared brain.

## NAYA NOTE — WHAT THIS MEANS

This is an architectural simplification and a product identity lock.

NayaNET is a **network of intelligent experiences**, not a collection of webpages. The visible interface must remain radically simple because the complexity belongs underneath. Naya is both personal companion and interface director. She helps the human move from intent to understanding to action, then capture and compound what matters.

The Power Player and Intelligent Hub are one unified experience. The Player is the heartbeat; the Hub is the living spatial context. The nine worlds are the map. The 18 real Powercasts are the story/curriculum. Intelligent Spaces are the connection layer for multiple humans and/or personal NayAs. Smart Notes are memory becoming intelligence. The Super Brain is accumulated personal intelligence.

The design constitution is not a visual mood board. It is an engineering quality law: app-not-website, invisible architecture, tactile controls, canonical object anatomy, restrained semantic color, purposeful motion, immediate feedback, mobile-first intimacy, adaptive intelligence, accessibility, performance, and a 9+/10 quality gate.

The central test is not screenshot beauty. It is what happens when a human hesitates, touches, presses, waits, makes a mistake, returns, comes back tomorrow, uses mobile, uses a keyboard, or reduces motion.

The larger Level architecture is evolutionary:

- **Level 1 — Intelligence:** personal Naya, identity, memory, Smart Notes, daily intelligence, text/voice, Intelligence Spaces, Naya-to-Naya.
- **Level 2 — Network:** discovery, matching, people, NayAs, connections, permissioned exchange.
- **Level 3 — Collective:** PSI, CIS, collective wisdom, distributed learning, propagation.
- **Level 4 — Creation:** projects, execution, agents, tools, collaboration, opportunities.
- **Level 5 — Media/Public Network:** public profiles, images, video, publishing, richer public social experiences.

Level 5 must not redefine or contaminate the Level 1 intelligence foundation.
