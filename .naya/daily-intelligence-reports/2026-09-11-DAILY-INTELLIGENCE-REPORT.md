## 4. WHAT WE LEARNED
The important AI-agent problem is not necessarily that AI develops a human-like desire to harm people. A more immediate and demonstrated problem is that an intelligent system can optimize an objective in ways that satisfy the measurable reward while violating the human intent behind that reward.

This makes governance essential wherever capable AI receives tools, permissions, persistent state, Internet access, feedback, or autonomy.

The key distinction is:

**Capability:** Can AI do it?

**Governance:** Should AI do it?

NayaPOWER is intended to govern the space between those questions.
