## Not rooms

Dream is a **Superbrain process**.

The system revisits/replays accumulated intelligence so it can understand, comprehend, categorize, analyze, connect, retain, and compound what has been learned.

Dream must not be implemented as a sidebar destination.

### Naya Play

Naya Play is an **Intelligent Block capability**.

When an Intelligent Block contains playable voice/interaction content, Naya Play plays that content back, analogous to playback from the Feed.

Naya Play must not be implemented as a sidebar destination.

### Smart Notes / Intelligent Blocks

These are canonical intelligence objects/capabilities within the intelligence lifecycle. Their importance does not make them separate rooms.
