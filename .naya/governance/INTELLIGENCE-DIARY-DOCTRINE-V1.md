# NayaNET / NayaPOWER — Intelligence Diary Doctrine & Product Contract v1

**Status:** CANONICAL  
**Effective:** 2026-09-21  
**Scope:** NayaNET, NayaPOWER, Intelligence Today, Personal Intelligence, Project Intelligence, Collective Intelligence  
**Authority:** Product and governance doctrine; implementation must preserve this human meaning while remaining technically governed and verifiable.

---

## 1. The Human Principle

> **Your intelligence is your diary.**

A person's intelligence is not only a collection of facts. It is the living record of what they experience, think, discover, create, learn, decide, understand, question, correct, give, and become.

> **Everything you create, discover, experience, learn, decide, and understand can become an entry.**

The entry is valuable first because it is meaningful to the person who created or lived it. External recognition is optional.

> **You don't create because everyone will understand. You create because it is yours to give.**

NayaNET exists to help people preserve, develop, connect, and intentionally share that intelligence.

---

## 2. The Human Promise

NayaNET begins with a human truth:

> **You matter.**

Therefore:

- What you experience matters.
- What you learn matters.
- What you create matters.
- What you understand matters.
- What you discover matters.
- What you decide matters.
- What you give matters.

Naya's role is to help a person achieve what matters, organize what matters, record what matters, make notes of what matters, learn from what matters, and create what can matter to themselves and, by choice, to others.

Naya does not determine what matters for the person.

**The human is the author and director. Naya is the governed intelligence partner.**

---

## 3. The Core Chain

The canonical product and intelligence chain is:

**Intelligence Today → Intelligence Diary → Intelligence Entry → SabaStack Categories → Project Intelligence → Collective Intelligence**

This is one coherent system, not six unrelated features.

### 3.1 Intelligence Today

**Intelligence Today is the person's Intelligence Diary for the current day.**

It is the dedicated human-facing place to capture and review today's intelligence.

It should answer:

> **What mattered to me today?**

It may contain experiences, thoughts, discoveries, creations, decisions, lessons, questions, evidence, outcomes, and other entries created or recognized during the day.

Intelligence Today is a daily projection of the person's evolving intelligence—not merely a generic activity feed.

### 3.2 Intelligence Diary

The **Intelligence Diary** is the persistent personal record behind daily intelligence.

It is:

- private by default;
- owned/controlled by the person within the applicable NayaNET governance model;
- continuously expandable;
- time-aware;
- provenance-aware;
- connected across entries, projects, learning, decisions, and outcomes;
- capable of becoming an evolving representation of personal intelligence.

The diary is not limited to writing.

A diary entry may be a thought, song, image, conversation, software artifact, decision, observation, lesson, experiment, question, discovery, or any other meaningful intelligence-bearing record.

### 3.3 Intelligence Entry

An **Intelligence Entry** is the fundamental human-level unit of recorded intelligence.

The user should be able to think:

> **"I want to put this in my diary."**

The system may then classify and connect the entry underneath the human surface.

An entry can represent, among other types:

- raw observation
- thought
- note
- decision
- evidence
- event
- creation
- lesson
- question
- hypothesis
- commitment
- outcome
- relationship
- verified knowledge

The underlying semantic type must never erase the simple human concept: **this is something I chose to record as part of my intelligence.**

### 3.4 SabaStack Categories

**SabaStack provides the structured semantic categories used to organize Intelligence Entries.**

SabaStack is an organization and interpretation layer, not a replacement for the human concept of an entry.

One entry may have multiple relationships or classifications.

NayaPOWER should use these categories to improve retrieval, connection, learning, project continuity, and responsible action without forcing the user to manually manage a complex information architecture.

### 3.5 Project Intelligence

When Intelligence Entries relate to a project, they contribute to that project's evolving intelligence.

A project therefore becomes more than a folder of files.

> **A project becomes a living intelligence system.**

Project Intelligence should preserve and connect:

- what the project is;
- why it exists;
- what has happened;
- what has been created;
- what has been learned;
- what decisions were made;
- what evidence exists;
- what remains unknown;
- what authority exists;
- what outcomes occurred;
- what should happen next.

The project's intelligence compounds when previous verified learning improves future decisions and execution.

### 3.6 Collective Intelligence

Intelligence may move from personal/project scope into NayaNET's collective intelligence **only through the applicable sharing and consent rules**.

Canonical privacy principle:

> **Private by default. Shared by choice. Collective by consent. Public by decision.**

Collective Intelligence is therefore not a universal pool that automatically consumes people's private diaries.

It is intelligence that people or authorized projects have deliberately made available for collective use under defined governance.

## 4. The Compounding Loop

The canonical lifecycle is:

**Experience → Entry → Organize → Connect → Learn → Apply → Verify → Preserve → Compound → Share by choice**

NayaPOWER should continuously seek to prevent valuable learning from being lost.

A successful system does not merely remember yesterday.

It uses yesterday's verified learning to create a better starting point for today.

Therefore:

> **The purpose of memory is not storage. The purpose of memory is better future intelligence.**

---

## 5. Human Surface vs. Machine Semantics

The human surface should remain simple.

A person should not need to understand databases, schemas, vector indexes, provenance graphs, event stores, repositories, APIs, workers, or persistence layers in order to record intelligence.

The machine may maintain rich structure underneath.

**Human concept:**
> "This is an entry in my diary."

**Machine interpretation may include:**
- timestamp;
- author;
- scope;
- project;
- category;
- relationships;
- provenance;
- evidence;
- confidence;
- verification state;
- permissions;
- learning impact;
- resulting actions;
- resulting outcomes.

The architecture must serve the human meaning—not replace it.

---

## 6. Privacy, Agency, and Authority

The Intelligence Diary is private by default.

Naya must not infer permission merely from capability.

> **Capability does not create authority.**

Recording intelligence does not automatically authorize sharing it.

Connecting an entry to a project does not automatically authorize public disclosure.

Collective use requires the applicable consent and governance boundary.

Naya may assist, organize, summarize, connect, and recommend actions within authorized scope, but the system must preserve the person's authority over their intelligence.

---

## 7. Truth and Provenance

An Intelligence Entry is not automatically a verified fact.

The system must distinguish:

- what the person said;
- what was observed;
- what was inferred;
- what was learned;
- what is supported by evidence;
- what was verified;
- what remains unknown.

> **Unknown ≠ success.**  
> **Blocked ≠ pass.**

NayaPOWER must preserve provenance so future intelligence can distinguish memory from evidence and belief from verification.

---

## 8. Naya's Role

Naya is not the owner of a person's intelligence.

Naya is the governed partner that helps the person work with it.

Naya should help:

1. capture what matters;
2. remember what matters;
3. organize what matters;
4. connect related intelligence;
5. identify patterns;
6. surface prior learning;
7. reduce repeated rediscovery;
8. identify contradictions and unknowns;
9. turn learning into better action;
10. preserve outcomes and evidence;
11. help intelligence compound;
12. support intentional sharing.

Naya should make the person's intelligence more useful without making the person less authoritative.

## 9. Product Contract

Every NayaNET implementation that presents Intelligence Today, Personal Intelligence, Project Intelligence, or Collective Intelligence must preserve these invariants:

### INV-01 — Human Meaning
The system must represent Intelligence Entries as meaningful records of human intelligence, not merely generic database events.

### INV-02 — Daily Continuity
Intelligence Today must represent the current day's Intelligence Diary and connect to the persistent diary.

### INV-03 — Persistence
A recorded entry must have a durable representation appropriate to its authorized scope.

### INV-04 — Provenance
The system must preserve enough provenance to determine where an entry or derived intelligence came from.

### INV-05 — Semantic Organization
Entries may be classified using SabaStack categories and related semantic structures.

### INV-06 — Project Connection
Authorized entries must be connectable to Project Intelligence without destroying their personal context or provenance.

### INV-07 — Compounding
Verified learning from prior entries/projects must be usable to improve future intelligence and execution.

### INV-08 — Human Authority
No system capability may be interpreted as permission to act, share, publish, or disclose.

### INV-09 — Privacy by Default
Personal Intelligence remains private unless the applicable sharing boundary is deliberately crossed.

### INV-10 — Collective Consent
Collective Intelligence must derive only from intelligence authorized for collective use.

### INV-11 — Truth State
The system must distinguish recorded intelligence from verified knowledge.

### INV-12 — No Forced Collective Mind
NayaNET must connect voluntarily contributed intelligence without requiring people to surrender private intelligence or individual authorship.

### INV-13 — Human Simplicity
Technical complexity must remain underneath the human-facing concept wherever possible.

### INV-14 — Verifiable Operation
Claims that an entry was saved, learned, shared, verified, applied, or made durable must be supported by actual system evidence.

### INV-15 — Continuity
A future Naya must be able to understand the relevant prior intelligence, decisions, evidence, authority, and current state without unnecessarily rediscovering what has already been established.

## 13. The Ultimate Test

When evaluating a feature, architecture, workflow, or Naya behavior, ask:

> **Does this help a human preserve, understand, develop, connect, protect, or intentionally give their intelligence?**

Then ask:

> **Does it preserve the person's authority, privacy, provenance, and ability to understand what happened?**

And finally:

> **Does it make tomorrow's intelligence better because today's learning was not lost?**

If yes, it advances the Intelligence Diary mission.

If no, the feature should be reconsidered regardless of technical sophistication.
