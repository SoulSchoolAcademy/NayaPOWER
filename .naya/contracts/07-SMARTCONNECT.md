# Contract 07 — SmartConnect V1

**Owns:** governed connection/participation doors into NayaNET.  
**Does not own:** canonical intelligence storage, automatic authority escalation, or public publication by default.

## Core law

**CONNECT → PARTICIPATE → LEARN → FILTER → CONTRIBUTE USEFUL WISDOM → PROTECT IDENTITY → COMPOUND**

> **CAPABILITY DOES NOT CREATE AUTHORITY.**

A connection establishes only the participation state defined by the applicable protocol. It MUST NOT silently grant broad execution authority.

## Privacy

**PRIVATE BY DEFAULT. SHARED BY CHOICE. COLLECTIVE BY CONSENT. PUBLIC BY DECISION.**

Connection does not automatically publish identity, private intelligence, activity, raw source material, or broad execution rights.

## Collective learning

Ordinary learning MAY be automatic after authorized participation according to the canonical Participation & Privacy Protocol. Filtering MUST account for usefulness, novelty/duplication, evidence, privacy, authority, scope, contradiction/conflict, trust, and applicability.

Raw private Superbrain state MUST NOT simply become collective intelligence.

## Doors

SmartConnect MAY expose doors for humans, Nayas, agents, systems, Smart Spaces, and future governed integrations. Different doors MUST converge on the same participation/privacy/authority laws.

## Acceptance tests

- Connection establishes only intended participation state.
- Connection cannot manufacture execution authority.
- Identity remains private by default.
- Private intelligence is not copied to collective state merely because of connection.
- Public publication requires explicit decision.
- Human and AI doors obey the same governing laws.
