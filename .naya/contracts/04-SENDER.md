# Contract 04 — Sender V1

**Owns:** translating human/system intent into a governed canonical intelligence request.  
**Does not own:** canonical identity, persistence, learning promotion, proof fabrication, or Hub truth.

## Mandatory behavior

The Sender MUST:

1. understand requested outcome;
2. identify relevant source/context;
3. preserve intent without silently changing it;
4. surface material ambiguity;
5. establish available authority;
6. invoke the canonical Receiver;
7. expose receiver result/evidence;
8. preserve UNKNOWN/PENDING state;
9. hand the canonical result toward projection/retrieval.

## Prohibited behavior

The Sender MUST NOT:

- allocate or guess IB IDs;
- write a Smart Note as a substitute for Receiver persistence;
- create a second memory store;
- decide that an event is learned;
- manufacture proof;
- call a predicted path a Smart Link;
- grant itself authority;
- silently expand scope;
- equate request submission with consequence success.

## Intelligence Distiller boundary

A Distiller MAY determine title, concepts, domain/type, project/context, retrieval tags, human/child/Grandma/Naya/machine views, provenance candidates, relationships, authority metadata, and next action.

It MUST produce a governed request and MUST NOT become a competing Receiver or persistence store.

## Acceptance tests

- Material ambiguity is clarified or safely bounded.
- Distillation always converges on the same Receiver.
- Sender cannot allocate canonical identity.
- Sender cannot claim persistence from request submission.
- Receiver failure remains visible.
