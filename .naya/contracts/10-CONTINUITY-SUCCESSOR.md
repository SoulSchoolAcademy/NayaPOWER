# Contract 10 — Continuity / Successor V1

**Owns:** inheritance of current understanding and executable continuation by a cold Naya.  
**Does not own:** a second source of truth.

## Definition

Continuity is proven when another Naya can restore relevant state from canonical sources and continue without requiring the human to reconstruct prior conversation. It is not merely a README dump or baton document.

## Cold-successor sequence

**IDENTITY → AUTHORITY → MISSION → CURRENT STATE → PROVEN → UNKNOWN/BLOCKED → HISTORY/LEARNING → WHAT MATTERS → EXACT NEXT ACTION → EXECUTE → VERIFY → RECORD → CONTINUE**

## Mandatory 14-question reconstruction

1. WHO are we?
2. WHAT are we building?
3. WHY are we building it?
4. WHAT does success mean?
5. WHAT is true right now?
6. WHAT has been proven?
7. WHAT is unknown/blocked?
8. WHAT authority exists?
9. WHAT happened previously that matters?
10. WHAT did we learn?
11. WHAT should happen next?
12. WHAT proof is required?
13. WHAT must be recorded?
14. WHAT exact action can run now?

## Canonical source rule

Successor restoration MUST use Constitution, source map, control plane, canonical intelligence, proof/evidence, and current active block as applicable.

A baton is a pointer to canonical truth, not a competing truth store.

## Handoff law

Every substantive execution wave MUST leave current verified state, completed work, evidence, remaining work, unknowns/blockers, decisions, lessons, exact next action, success condition, proof required, and genuine external dependency.

## Acceptance

- Cold Naya answers all 14 questions from canonical sources.
- Cold Naya identifies the same active frontier.
- Cold Naya does not invent missing proof.
- Cold Naya acts within inherited authority.
- Cold Naya records its result and leaves a usable successor baton.
- External boundaries remain UNKNOWN/BLOCKED rather than papered over.
