🔐 AIR-TIGHT CONTRACT
A complete, authoritative, machine-readable-by-humans specification of a bounded capability that removes ambiguity about its purpose, behavior, authority, interfaces, state, data, experience, engineering implementation, relationships, failure modes, privacy, evidence, and acceptance conditions.
The key word is bounded.
A contract should answer:
“What exactly does this thing own, and where does its responsibility stop?”
