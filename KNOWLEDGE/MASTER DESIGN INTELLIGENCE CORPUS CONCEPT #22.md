PART I — THE 10 WORLD-CLASS STUDIOS
IB-DESIGN-001 — Work & Co
Nutshell: Work & Co's lesson is that world-class digital design is not decoration placed on top of technology. It is solving difficult product problems through the combined discipline of design and technology. Its public portfolio emphasizes digital product innovation for organizations including Apple, Google, and Nike. Work & Co
Human view: The user shouldn't feel the organization's complexity. They should experience one understandable product.
Child view: Make the hard machine feel easy.
Design grammar: Strong hierarchy; purposeful interaction; fewer arbitrary surfaces; product before marketing chrome; consistency across a large system.
Machine view: complex system → coherent information architecture → reusable components → predictable interaction → production implementation
AI view: Before generating screens, reconstruct the entire product problem. Don't optimize a page while damaging the system.
Connection to our corpus: Work & Co strongly reinforces Norman, Cooper, Linear, Stripe, Instrument, and the NayaNET principle that the interface is a coherent operating environment rather than disconnected pages.
What's in it for NayaNET: NayaPOWER may be enormously sophisticated underneath. NayaNET should make that sophistication disappear.
Top 5 takeaways
1. Solve the product, not merely the screen.
2. Design and engineering should converge early.
3. Complexity belongs behind the interface.
4. Coherence across the whole system matters more than one spectacular page.
5. Beautiful design has to survive production.
