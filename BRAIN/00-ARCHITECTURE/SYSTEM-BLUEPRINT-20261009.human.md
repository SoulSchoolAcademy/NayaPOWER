# THE SYSTEM BLUEPRINT — in plain words

*For Shawn — the complete architecture and build manual: what exists, what's missing, exactly what to build. Nutshell first.*

We are building a mind that doesn't forget. Right now every AI starts from zero — brilliant in the moment, amnesiac by tomorrow. NayaPOWER is the system where every lesson gets captured, verified by an independent check, reused when a similar situation comes up, and compounded so a cold Naya with no prior conversation can pick up every lesson ever learned and build on them. The test is one sentence: Shawn teaches something once; days later, in a completely new conversation, a completely fresh Naya faces a similar situation — and gets it right, because she pulled the lesson from memory. No one reminded her. No one re-taught her. She just knew.

The system is a mind with nine organs. SELF is the identity gate — who is acting, under what mission, genuinely the continuation of the last cycle. LAW is the permission office — PROHIBITED, NEEDS_AUTHORITY, NEEDS_EVIDENCE, or ADMISSIBLE; LAW runs before the math, no score ever overrides a prohibition. ACT is the only hands — the only node that produces effects in the world, and it never decides whether it *may* act (that's LAW's job). KNOW is the memory — stores experiences, serves back exactly the relevant knowledge; retrieved knowledge never creates authority. PROVE is the evidence judge — decides whether evidence supports a claim (never that the claim is true — that's VERIFY's job). CONNECT is the context broker — decides what may connect to what; RELATED ≠ RELEVANT ≠ APPLICABLE ≠ TRUE ≠ AUTHORIZED. VERIFY is the immune system — independently re-derives, attacks claims, and a different seat must do the verifying. LEARN is the compounding organ — turns verified experience into durable intelligence; STORED ≠ LEARNED. EVOLVE is the successor — closes the cycle and carries context (never authority) to the next one.

A lesson flows seven steps: CAPTURE (a note becomes a file) → GOVERN (the admission gate; capture only ever reaches CANDIDATE) → VERIFY (an independent party re-reads) → RECORD (written down durably, three copies) → RETRIEVE (find the right note later, cold) → REUSE (the note changes what the system does) → PROVE (show the whole chain ran, with receipts). The honest state: the chain works through RECORD — then hits a wall. Nothing promotes to ACTIVE; the elevation machinery exists only in tests. RETRIEVE works but feeds nothing — ACT never queries KNOW, so no decision is informed by stored intelligence. REUSE and PROVE have no code at all. Six of nine node connections exist only on paper.

The blueprint names the gaps honestly (three CRITICAL: the CANDIDATE→ACTIVE ladder is a dead end; ACT never queries KNOW; all nine node specs are unratified with zero wiring; four HIGH: the ACT→KNOW feedback arc, SELF accepting unverified lessons, EVOLVE having no working code, REUSE + PROVE having no code; two MEDIUM: the retrieval predicate bypassed, a phantom executable named in the ops plan). Then it says exactly what to build, in what order: Phase 1 unblocks the flow (wire the elevation ladder, enforce the retrieval predicate); Phase 2 connects decision to memory (ACT→KNOW query path, wire the value calculus as the decision engine); Phase 3 builds the missing lifecycle steps (ExecutionHandoff, REUSE, PROVE); Phase 4 hardens identity and succession (gate SELF's experience recording, build the EVOLVE successor package); Phase 5 fixes the docs. Every phase carries concrete acceptance criteria — code without passing criteria is not done — and the whole thing closes with the 14-step ultimate test, ending at the bar from the first paragraph: a cold Naya who gets it right because she remembered.

**One honest warning:** this blueprint is Version 1.0, CANDIDATE — not ratified. Every section-4 "what's connected" number (612 capture files, 1,180 projections, 1,196 BRAIN files, battery counts, CI states) is a 2026-10-09 snapshot — claims to re-verify, not facts. All nine node specs were CANDIDATE drafts at write time. Read the shapes as law; treat the statuses and numbers as dated evidence.
