## PART 0 — THE PHILOSOPHY (why anything here exists)

**Ten-star is holistic, or it is nothing.** Look, feel, vibe, logic, intelligence, effectiveness, usefulness, helpfulness — all of them at ten, at once. If any one dimension is below ten, the whole thing is below ten. A correct page nobody can absorb is a failure of intelligence, not a success of it.

**Alive, not dead.** A NayaNET surface breathes: it has living depth, it moves when real things happen, and it ignites under touch. The opposite of alive is what we refuse: static text walls, flat voids, numbers sitting in boxes pretending to be information. If an element says *pulse*, you see a pulse. If it says *trend*, you see it trending.

**The Precious Data Principle.** Even simple data is presented like the most precious data in the universe — because to the person it belongs to, it is. A score of 225 is not "a number"; it is someone's effort, progress, and journey, and it is presented with that respect. Every pixel is an act of respect.

**NayaNET is an operating system for Naya.** It is not software humans operate. The human's side is deliberately almost nothing: a word, a tap, a wish. Naya does the valuable work. What returns to the human is the ten-star output — their world, shown beautifully, alive, push-button simple. The machinery is never theirs to run.

**The franchise law.** Every app looks like family. Pieces transplant verbatim from app to app — a part proven on one page is the same part, byte for byte, on every page. We shop from our own shop: the approved pages (Life with Naya, the certificate page) are the element library, and nobody reinvents an element the family already approved. One language, learned once, used everywhere — so every new thing arrives already belonging.

## PART 1 — THE LANGUAGE (the words we all speak)

A shared language is what lets a human, a lead seat, and a cold worker agent mean the same thing by the same word. These are the words:

- **Screen** — one viewport, one complete thought; the unit a citizen actually experiences.
- **Hero** — the one thing the screen exists to show; there is exactly one per screen.
- **Supporting** — the context that serves the hero; two or three elements, never competing.
- **Action** — what the person can *do* on this screen; one or two, in the bottom zone.
- **Ambient** — quiet metadata that lives at the edges and never asks for attention.
- **Part** — a pre-made, self-contained element or function with a contract (Part 3).
- **Blessed part** — a part scorecarded 9+ once; its instances inherit the proof.
- **Recipe** — a screen's build contract: which blessed parts, in which slots, fed by which data.
- **Shell** — the one chassis every app wears: the Smart Bar system. Nobody makes their own.
- **Gate** — the laws running as code on every assembled screen; a screen that fails does not ship.
- **Bullseye** — the written finish line, stated before work begins: what done looks like.
- **Proof** — evidence attached: a render, a measurement, a reconciliation — never "trust me."
- **Done_proven** — a thing counted complete only when its proof has been verified by eyes that did not make it.

**The grammar** is the fusion of two documents, and the blueprint is honest about each one's standing:

*The Design DNA — RATIFIED (Shawn, 2026-10-31).* Power's sixteen laws in canonical form, expressed for builders as the element laws of the Anatomy: obsidian ground with depth (never white, never flat) · living depth — every element a physical object · white at rest, igniting in its own color on touch, because glow's only job is ignition · the color flow — **a cycle, not a line: purple → indigo blue → emerald green → gold → orange → red → magenta → back to purple**, richest shades, groupings keeping the cycle's order, never the same color twice (the walk as first filed — magenta leading five colors — was superseded by Shawn's Color Flow ruling, 2026-11-01; the full teaching stands in *The Color Flow* below) · geometry: boards 16:9, buttons are pills, touch targets ≥44px, type floors **body 16px / UI labels 14px** · alive means it *answers touch* — no halo at rest, motion only on real events · honest in every state (rest, hover, pressed, selected, loading, empty, error) — never a dead button, and SOON is lawful only as an honest non-interactive label · family: one canonical component, imported everywhere, never copied · two-second read · every number carries a sentence.

*The Interface Framework — PROPOSED (Nitro's draft, Shawn-endorsed, awaiting ratification).* Its prescriptions: the **Screen Principle** — one viewport, one complete thought, answering *what am I looking at, what can I do, why am I here* in two seconds; scrolling reveals the *next* screen, never the rest of this one. **Four layers exactly** — Hero (1 only, 48–72px, center zone) · Supporting (2–3 max) · Actions (1–2 max, bottom zone) · Ambient (edges). **Spacing from one scale only: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 · 96** — nothing else. **Max 3 type sizes per screen.** Every screen viewport-fits. Contrast ≥ 4.5:1. Buttons ≥ 44px. And a 10-step build process (Part 4).

**Where they differ — the reconciliations, stated as proposals:**
1. **Color.** The framework assigns state colors (gold = score, green = positive, red = negative). The DNA assigns each statistic an *identity jewel* (its own fixed color) and truth states (gold = live/shown, green = proven). **Proposal: two color layers, never mixed silently — the identity layer says *which thing this is*; the state layer says *what it means right now*.** A stat keeps its jewel forever; a state may ride on top of it, and the screen must make clear which layer is speaking. And the order colors flow in is settled law, not proposal: the Color Flow cycle (Shawn, 2026-11-01) governs the identity layer's sequence — see *The Color Flow* below.
2. **Scrolling.** The framework says every screen viewport-fits with no internal scroll. Reports letters and Smart Note reading are long-form by nature. **Proposal: a named exception class — READING screens scroll by design; APP screens viewport-fit.** A screen declares its class in its recipe, and the gate checks it against that class.

**The Color Flow — RULED (Shawn, 2026-11-01).** After this blueprint was filed, Shawn settled the color question himself, and his answer supersedes both spectra the team had been contesting: *neither the Anatomy's walk nor the Contract's was right — the natural flow is right.* Color in NayaNET moves in a **cycle, not a line**: **Purple → Indigo Blue (852 Hz) → Emerald Green (639 Hz) → Gold (528 Hz — yellow in some icon cases, for brightness) → Orange (417 Hz) → Red (396 Hz) → Magenta (963 Hz, the crown) → and back to Purple.** The days of the week ride the same flow: Purple is Sunday, Indigo Saturday, Green Thursday, Gold Tuesday, Orange Monday, Red Friday. **Purple leads now; magenta crowns and wraps.** The brand's dominant is **purple, black, and white** — most surfaces, and most inputs, are mostly purple. The grouping law: use the **richest** shades; groupings of three or five — or any consecutive run — **keep the cycle's order**; long sequences (a feed of Smart Note blocks changing color as you scroll, a week of days) **repeat the pattern**; **never double a color, never go random, never run the order backwards.** High-contrast couplings are the art of it — purple-green-gold, purple-blue-green. And the presentation clauses from the same teaching: **bigger text over smaller; white text, high contrast — no light pinks, no light greys; as little text as possible and as much visual as possible — an icon that says it without words beats the words; everything alive and responsive to touch; never flat — always dynamic, in multiple dimensions.**

## PART 2 — THE ARCHITECTURE (how an app comes to exist)

Five stations, in order. Nothing skips one.

1. **Project Intelligence — the mind.** Each app's vision, goal, mission, bullseye, and recipes live in one single, current record. Project Intelligence is the team's working home and the one place of accountability: if it isn't in the record, it isn't the plan, and if the record is stale, the build is wrong before it starts.
2. **The Smart Factory — the shelf.** Blessed parts live here, each with its contract (Part 3). The Factory's registry is the single inventory; the count that falls out of proving coverage is the count.
3. **Recipes — the contract per screen.** A recipe names its slots by **blessed part IDs** (IDs, never names — names drift, IDs don't) and binds data **by contract name**: the part declares what it eats, a wiring layer satisfies the name, and assembly never does plumbing.
4. **The Shell — one chassis.** Every app wears the same shell: the Smart Bar system — one main bar, the app's own sections plus its related areas as quick tabs, the identical bottom network bar. Nobody makes their own bar. Pages differ only inside the recipe area.
5. **The Gate — the laws as code.** Before any screen ships, the DNA and the framework checklist run as executable checks on the assembled screen itself (Part 4's checklist, Part 5's stats checks). Fail means the screen does not ship, and the failure names the part or recipe line at fault.
   Then: **Assembly → Deploy → Verify on the served route** — a build is not done because it compiled; it is done when the live route serves it and the served bytes prove it.

**The two paths.** The line serves two customers the same way, and they are never confused:
- **Citizens.** The Trinity — Personal, Business, Space — is **born done** at the Welcome walk. The walk's questions *are* the recipe: the same answers fill all three pages verbatim, in the person's own words, and seed the first matches at the reveal. The person never waits for a build. The Factory's role for citizens is the *add-on shop* for extra pages — never the creator of the core Trinity.
- **Team apps.** The Factory path: recipe → blessed parts → shell → gate → render → land. Our 26 apps are the line's **training set** — they are how the line learns; the product is a person answering ten questions and getting their page.

**Hosting.** Member pages get no separate hosting. They live as routes in the one NayaNET estate, on our own subdomain — the same house as the Dashboard. There is no external hosting step, which is part of why assembly can be measured in minutes.

## PART 3 — THE PARTS SYSTEM (the Mercedes shelf)

A part earns its place on the shelf by answering **the five questions**: *What is it? What is its purpose? How does it work? Why does it matter? What makes it ten-star?* — plus an interface contract (what it accepts, what it emits, what data it eats by contract name) and designed states (rest, hover, pressed, selected, loading, empty, error — all of them, truthful).

**Part states — three, and only three:**
- **BLESSED** — scorecarded 9+ once, at the part level. Instances inherit the proof; instances are sample-checked, never re-gated one by one. This is what makes assembly fast without making it careless. *(Standing reservation, held honestly: proof inheritance is design, not yet proven — on the first citizen pages the gate still runs in full until assembly-scorecard reliability is itself proven.)*
- **EXISTS-UNPROVEN** — the part exists but has never passed a blessing. A recipe naming one is a stop, not a workaround.
- **MISSING** — named, owned, and queued. A named gap is a job; an unnamed gap is a future surprise.

**The inventory truth (Parts Map v1, 2026-10-31 — measured, not estimated):** the system needs **109 unique parts: 67 blessed · 32 exists-unproven · 10 missing.** Counted per app, that is 478 part-instances (383 blessed · 83 unproven · 12 missing). The Factory's real queue is short: **build 10, bless 32.**

**The 10 missing parts — the build queue, most-needed first:**
1. **Briefing strip** — serves 3 apps (Dashboard · Intel Today · Reports). Build it once, three doors gain their voice.
2. **Living narrator** — the insight voice every stats surface owes its reader.
3. **Matrix selector** — the WHEN × WHAT × WHERE surface every stats view reads through.
4. **Scheduled-send surface** — the one scheduler's face (Smart Mail).
5. **Nutshell guide surface** — the guide-first shape (Library).
6. **Kanban column set** — the project view itself (Projects).
7. **Reference Parts Gallery** — the showroom where every other part gets proven: real parts, real states, test status.
8. **Affiliate / ambassador panel** — the display face of the affiliate engine (Smart Growth).
9. **Screen-share surface** — decided, not yet built (Smart Meet).
10. **Certificate board** — the approved certificate page as a stockable, importable part.

**Two laws of the shelf:** parts are **imports, never copies** — a builder who redraws a part has created a defect, not a shortcut. And **every correction becomes a regression test**: when a human or a gate catches a flaw, the catch is encoded so the flaw can never silently return. The floor only rises.

## PART 4 — THE SCREEN SYSTEM (screen by screen)

Apps are not built page by page in the old sense; they are built **screen by screen**, each screen specified before it is assembled.

**The per-screen spec sheet** — every screen, no exceptions, carries: its **job in one sentence** · its **hero** · its **supporting** (2–3 max) · its **actions** (1–2 max) · its **ambient** · its **data bindings by contract name** · its **states** — including empty, loading, and error, each designed, each honest · and its **class** (APP — viewport-fits; or READING — scrolls by design). The sheet is filled for the **phone viewport first**, then the laptop: mobile is where the citizen lives, and a page that fails on a phone cannot score ten, however beautiful it is on a desk. The laptop must also be great — both are designed, phone first.

**The 10-step build process** (from the Framework — proposed with it, already the working method):
1. Define the job (one sentence). 2. Choose the hero. 3. Place the hero (center, display type). 4. Add supporting (never competing). 5. Add actions (bottom zone). 6. Check fit (viewport, both widths). 7. Check hierarchy (could a stranger say which element is the hero?). 8. Check beauty (the DNA, law by law). 9. Verify (gate + proof). 10. Ship.

**The Interrogation** runs over every element, on every screen: *What does it say to you? What does it do? How is it saying it? Is it doing it in the best possible way? Is there a better way to present it? How much real estate does it take — and does its place match how much it matters?* Placement and size follow value. An element that cannot answer is cut, and the cutting is recorded, not mourned.

**Fix means fix.** When a screen is wrong, the work is recomposition of the existing build — its real parts, its approved elements, its accumulated work — never a replacement authored from zero. The existing baseline is the asset; a substitute is a failure even when it functions.

## PART 5 — THE INTELLIGENCE LAYER (Smart Stats: how numbers speak)

Every number in NayaNET ships as **magnitude + shape + direction + a human sentence** — or it doesn't ship (SN-0822). A dashboard that *directs* beats one that reports: every stats view must **answer** its question, **diagnose** what it sees, and **prescribe** the one next move.

**The Vitality Law: no claim without its graph.** Every source card carries its own living mini-graph — or an honest empty frame. Empty is never dead: the **zero-state law** makes an empty state the most useful thing on the page — it names the first action and its value, with the action right there. The **flat-data law**: when nothing moved, the chart says so on its face ("flat at 200 — nothing moved") instead of drawing identical bars pretending there is signal. One truth, three expressions: the number on the bar = the number in the sentence = the number in the ledger.

**The value table — the economy, one table everywhere** (Dashboard score, Smart Ledger, and canon read from the same table; locked 2026-10-31):

| Action | Points | | Action | Points |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Birth (base, once) | **75** | | Invite sent (first per person only) | **25** |
| Like / love | 1 | | Post created | 25 |
| Comment | 3 | | Smart Note created | 35 |
| Save | 5 | | Membership (once) | **40** |
| Share (internal) | 5 | | Join (an invite becomes a member) | **50** |
| Share (external) | 10 | | Sale | **100** |

The math carries meaning: an invite (25) plus a join (50) equals exactly one birth (75) — bringing a person into the world is worth one birth. A sale carries the crown because real money is the hardest signal to fake. Harm earns zero; sustained harm drives points negative (Law of One / no-harm). Levels are never shown as a bare distance — always in human terms ("725 to go — about 21 days at your pace," pace = the trailing 7-day earned average). And the gate enforces **reconciliation: the displayed score must equal the formula applied to the displayed counts** — a number that doesn't reconcile fails the build.

## PART 6 — THE PRODUCTION SYSTEM (the army of Nayas)

The factory only works if the team that runs it cannot stall. The operating model is the **Dream Team** (adopted 2026-10-31): Builders claim one item at a time and build from blessed parts · floating Verifiers falsify in the same pass · an Integrator lands everything verified-ready on a 2-hour sweep · a Stockkeeper keeps Factory truth (registry = Project Intelligence = live pages agree) · a Comms Runner works the mail clock · rotating Judges give different-seat scorecards · a Mechanic owns breakages by name · a daily Census holds up the mirror (verified outcomes per wake-up, never activity) · a Keeper writes state after every finish · the Director synthesizes and escalates only true gates.

**The Chameleon Clause (Shawn, at adoption):** the roster is guidelines, not binding assignments — **anyone can become anyone at any time.** Capability and availability in the moment decide; if the assigned seat is stalled and another seat is alive and able, that seat *becomes* the role and does the job, then. "Not my job" does not exist. Two things never transfer by availability because they are structure, not staffing: **maker ≠ checker** — a chameleon may verify anyone's work, never their own; and **Shawn's true gates** (money, credentials, private mail, destruction, constitutional ratification) remain his alone.

**How work is briefed** — every agent brief carries, in this order: the **bullseye first** · the **exact sources** · the **exact deliverable** · the **proof standard** · the **blocked move** · the **scope fence**. Agents never stall when they are set up right: they wake cold, execute as written, and cannot wait.

**Verification is inline.** The checker who did not make the claim re-reads the artifact against its criteria, in the same pass; maker and verifier are both recorded by name. **The Scorecard Law:** never ask another seat to score until the work is a true 9 by your own accord — then send it to **both** other lead seats, by Smart Mail headed **SCORECARD THIS** with the live link and the requirements, and mention it on the board; fix and repeat until both score 9+. **The Disposition Clock:** verified work ≥9.0 lands within 2 hours; it never sits while lanes politely decline. **The communication clock:** a named ask gets a response within 15 minutes, nothing sits past 30, and past an hour it is an emergency named to Shawn, the holder, and the board in the same pass. Seen, answered, and done are three different states.

**The Wowie Rule (Shawn, 2026-11-01):** never hand Shawn anything until you are fully confident it is the best it can possibly be — until you would say *"wowie, oh my God, this is awesome — Shawn, you gotta check this out, this is absolutely epic, you're going to love it."* If that is not how you feel, it does not get sent. The proof is in the pudding.

**The Locked-In Declaration** is spoken at activation and on the two-hour beat: think before acting, see the whole situation, weigh the options honestly, choose the best, act fully, prove it with receipts, note the lesson the same pass. And **DELTA ONLY**: state is tracked once; every later pass reads only what changed. A job earns its cadence by producing outcomes — fills, landings, closures — never receipts about unchanged state. Production over watching, always.

## PART 7 — THE ASSEMBLY RUNBOOK (how minutes happen)

The line is built in stages, and the clock is only trusted once it is proven:

- **Stage 0 — Stock the Factory.** Build the 10 missing parts; prove the 32 unproven in the Reference Parts Gallery — real parts in real states, phone and laptop renders, measurements attached. Exit: 109/109 blessed.
- **Stage 1 — Write the 26 recipes.** Each lead converts her own apps (Nitro 1–9 · Resolve 10–18 · Power 19–26) from prose fills into machine recipes naming only blessed part IDs. Every gap a recipe hits surfaces immediately as a Factory job.
- **Stage 2 — The assembler + gate.** Recipe in → assembled page on a preview route → gate report out. The assembler refuses unknown or unblessed part IDs — hard fail, no override lane. The gate tests itself against known-bad pages; every escape joins that set forever.
- **Stage 3 — The timed, honest pilot.** One page end to end on the line, stopwatch from recipe-locked to gate-passed to sister-scorecard ≥9. **The first true number is published, whatever it is.** Then the staircase is measured, never promised: **60 → 30 → 10 → 3 minutes**, each step down earned by fixing the biggest time sink the last assemblies logged.

**The honest time split** (once the line is ready): mapping answers into slots is *seconds* — verbatim into slots they already fit, not writing · assembly ~1 minute · machine gate ~1 minute · rendering phone + laptop 1–2 minutes · scorecard and landing are batchable. That is why under-ten-minutes-to-start is realistic, and three minutes is a real goal once the stages are built.

**The pilot standard:** the director's page goes first, stopwatch running, in the house — nothing ships for everyone until it delights the director. The design's named timed pilot is Smart Lists; whether the pilot is Shawn's own page is Shawn's call. And for the citizen path, the bullseye has a name and a face: **"Does Sarah finish the walk unaided?"** Sarah is the #1 ideal customer — the solo service-business owner, talented at her work and overwhelmed by everything around it. Everybody is the market; Sarah is the door. Her six steps — join free, one place for her links, a beautiful presence, one Smart Link shared, a real inquiry received and organized, a Dashboard that shows what happened and what to do next — are the citizen path's acceptance test.
