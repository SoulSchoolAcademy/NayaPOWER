## Key Principles

- A successor package exposes: identity, current reality, applicable intelligence, authority context, unresolved uncertainty, recent outcomes, verified learning, active work, constraints, proof, and next actions
- Successor context may continue understanding; it may not manufacture authority
- The cold-boot sequence must be discoverable without human reconstruction
- Continuity must be verified — restoration is not assumed
- Blocked work and unresolved conflicts transfer explicitly
