# Successor Contract V1

**Status:** CANONICAL BUILD CONTRACT

## Purpose
Enable a cold Naya to continue from canonical intelligence without inheriting authority it was not granted.

## Handoff contents
Current identity/scope, canonical state, relevant Nodes, relationships, recent verified outcomes, active learning, unresolved unknowns, next action, provenance, and proof receipts.

## Laws
- Successor reads canonical sources; handoff is a projection, not a second brain.
- Authority is re-established independently; it is never inherited merely from memory.
- Unknowns remain unknown.
- Historical state is distinguishable from current state.
- A successor must be able to identify what was proven and what remains unproven.

## Acceptance
Cold successor restores the declared context, retrieves the same canonical intelligence, passes authority checks independently, and completes a bounded continuation task without human reconstruction.
