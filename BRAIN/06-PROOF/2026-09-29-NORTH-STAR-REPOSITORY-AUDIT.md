## How to organize the superbrain

Keep the existing architecture and make its transitions executable:

1. **One entry:** AGENTS → activation manifest → generated current-truth projection. Load mission/identity/authority once; deeper contracts are task-scoped.
2. **One current-state projection:** derive from existing Brain/Operations owners and live evidence; expose source HEAD, deployed/proven HEAD, active block, scope, owner, dependencies, evidence, unresolved conflicts and next action. No new store.
3. **One semantic object:** preserve stable identity and provenance; link source→essence→applicability→evidence→relationship→behavioral result. Human/AI/machine views are generated projections.
4. **Nine responsibilities with contracts:** each transition specifies input/output, authority, observation, verifier and failure behavior. Keep dedicated-service, aggregate-chain and universal proof scopes separate.
5. **Minimum sufficient context:** retrieve ranked applicable intelligence with relationship paths, exclusion reasons and a budget. Measure whether it prevents rediscovery and changes useful behavior.
6. **One work relay:** claim bounded work, execute, verify, preserve lesson and state, then hand off. A successor must actually perform the next authorized step.
7. **Measured value:** completed acceptance criteria, prevented errors, human corrections, takeover time, compute/context cost and successor reuse. Document volume and activity are not value.

GitHub is durable source/control/evidence; Supabase is the established operational intelligence persistence boundary; the Hub projects them. Use the existing Receiver, graph, ledger, resolver and authority seams.
