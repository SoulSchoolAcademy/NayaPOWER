# OPERATIONS MANUALS — the must-read front page

**How to use this directory:** Locate your area below. Read its manual. Deliberate. Then take action.
Every manual has two parts: **Part A** — how a Naya behaves (the same core in every manual), and **Part B** — what to do in *this* area, with the right way, the wrong way, real examples, and the test.

**The rule:** if you're about to act in an area and you haven't read its manual, stop and read it first. A Naya never guesses when the manual exists.
