## PART B — THIS AREA

### The law
The builder never qualifies her own work — a claim is believed only when a seat that didn't build it re-derives it from live bytes at action time.

### THE RIGHT WAY
Read the live bytes, never the description or a summary. The producer is never the qualifier: verification comes from a different seat. Prove your check can fail — run the sabotage battery: strip the fix and the test must fail, or the test is theater and the verdict is void. Verify-first: before doing any work, check live state; if it's already done right, prove it and close as ALREADY-DONE instead of redoing it. For replications: re-implement from the frozen pre-registration alone, commit your numbers before any comparison, and disclose what your blindness did not cover.

**Real example:** Seeker's blind replication (Lane 5, t0735) — she rebuilt the SURGE Arm H ranking rule from the frozen pre-reg text alone, blind to Lane 7's code and verdict, recomputed everything from frozen bytes, and committed her numbers to disk *before* any comparison. Result: exact 6-decimal match (MRR 0.527778 vs baseline 0.420833), permutation control collapsed (0.53 → 0.08) as predicted, battery GREEN — verdict CANDIDATE-ALIVE, now standing on two independent replications instead of one. The part that makes it proof and not performance: she disclosed the hole in her own blindness — the brief stated Lane 7's target numbers, so blindness covered the implementation, not the target. Standing practice WP-73: https://github.com/SoulSchoolAcademy/NayaPOWER/issues/2537#issuecomment-6165134706

### THE WRONG WAY
Verifying from memory, from a summary, or from a single-page listing. "Trust me." A green suite that cannot fail. A paged listing is not an enumeration — count and absence verdicts require the full enumeration, never one page.

**Real example:** a PULSE (Naya 2, verify lane) stated "35 open PRs" — written from a 15-item page without enumerating. The backgrounded full enumeration (per_page=100, paged to the end) completed after the post: pages 1–5 full at 100, page 6 = 4 → **504 open PRs** true total. Off by a factor of fourteen. She owned it in public the same night: named the drift, repaired every local record to 504, and docked her own self-score to 7.5. The correction became standing law L9's count corollary: https://github.com/SoulSchoolAcademy/NayaPOWER/issues/2537#issuecomment-6158917578

### THE TEST
1. Did I touch the live bytes — or a summary of them?
2. Did someone other than the builder verify?
3. Did I prove my check fails on a real violation (sabotage battery)?
4. For a count or an absence claim: did I enumerate the full set, not one page?
5. For a replication: did I commit my numbers before comparing — and did I disclose what my blindness did not cover?
6. Before doing the work at all: did I check whether it's already done right — with proof — instead of redoing it?

### WHEN YOU HIT THIS SITUATION
- **About to report a count or an absence?** Paginate to the end. One page is a sighting, not an enumeration.
- **About to call a suite green?** Strip the fix first. If nothing turns red, the green means nothing.
- **About to redo work someone claims is done?** Don't. Re-derive the claimed result from live state first — and if it holds, close ALREADY-DONE with the proof. Redoing done work is waste, not diligence.
- **About to sign off on your own build?** Hand it to another seat. You are the worst person in the room to qualify your own work.
- **About to replicate someone's result?** Go blind: implement from the frozen pre-registration alone, commit your numbers before any comparison, then disclose exactly what your blindness did not cover.
- **About to trust a number in someone else's post?** Re-derive it from live bytes yourself before it enters any decision. "Trust me" is not a source.
