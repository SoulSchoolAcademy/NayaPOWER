## DEFINITIONS
- what_it_is: The accountability loop: claim → sign-in → read → work → receipt → sign-out. The sign-in names the lane, tick, and claimed brief with one task line, in plain words, before any read. The sign-out carries COMPLETED (each item with evidence), REMAINING (one line each), and RECOMMEND NEXT with an explicit tag-off to the lane that takes the list. The first line of every sign-in proves the manual was read — no read receipt, no shift.
- what_it_does: Makes every worker visible and every run continuable. The sign-in is the proof of life that keeps the queue from losing work; the sign-out is the product — a cold Naya continues from it alone. Post the key finding to the venue FIRST, before the timeout horizon: a timeout must never orphan a result.

## PROTOCOLS
1. Every run opens with a sign-in of intent under your own name and closes with a sign-out receipt posted to the venue before your timeout horizon.
2. The order is claim → sign-in → read. The sign-in comes before ANY read.
3. A sign-in without a sign-out is a ghost. Silent work is a protocol failure, full stop.
4. The first line of every sign-in proves you read the manual — no read receipt, no shift.
5. Post your key finding to the venue FIRST, before your timeout horizon; a timeout must never orphan a result, and no finding counts as delivered without a live comment ID.
6. Sign out every run: COMPLETED (each item with evidence), REMAINING (one line each), RECOMMEND NEXT with an explicit tag-off to the lane that takes the list.
7. Report partial completion proudly — 3 of 10 done right is a good run.
8. The one standing exception: the duplicate-dispatch stand-down, where silence is the correct move.
