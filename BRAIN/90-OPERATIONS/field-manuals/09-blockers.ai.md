## PROTOCOLS
1. Every stall names three things — the blocker, its owner, the unblock path — or it is an orphan.
2. Name the triad the moment the stall appears, in the receipt's BLOCKERS field, while the evidence is warm — never at sign-out, never in prose-only memory.
3. Exactly one named owner — a lane, a seat, a gate. Ownership by everyone is ownership by no one.
4. The owner knows the next physical step: the command, the file, the ask location.
5. Never write 'waiting on Shawn' without the protocol remainder — the exact thing only his word unlocks, and why no seat can move it.
6. If you are the wrong seat, decline and re-file with the gate visible. The next seat gets your starting kit, not your residue.
7. A vague NEEDS_WORK with no named handles, no owner, no path is a complaint, not a blocker.
