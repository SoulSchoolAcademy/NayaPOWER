# Registry Reconcile — the Specification (human tongue)

**What this is:** the plan to cure the Smart Note registry's standing defects in
ONE reconciled landing, so the door check goes green and stays green.
**Measured at:** main tip `57866113ca22c48c1580db8489ca8e35b88d6682` (2026-10-26).
**PI item:** `resolve-registry-reconcile-sn0594`. **Law:** L659 — cures on a
shared sealed artifact land as one reconciled artifact, seal regenerated by the
canonical tool, new head re-verified by a distinct seat.

---

## The disease, in plain words

The registry itself is sound — 728 entries, its seal (manifest hash) recomputes
exactly, and every live note's page hash checks out. Wrapped around that sound
core are five kinds of scar tissue from past collisions, plus one live splinter:

1. **The splinter that keeps CI red — SN-0594.** In the 2026-10-04 cleanup, a
   duplicate note was consolidated into SN-0593 and its registry entry was
   retired as a tombstone — deliberately carrying no page and no page hash,
   because "there is no page to bind." But the duplicate's page file was never
   actually removed: a 24-line tombstone pointer still sits on the tree. The
   door checker walks pages, not intentions — a page on disk whose entry has
   no matching page hash is a defect, forever. It is the **only** defect the
   full door check reports (701 pages checked, 1 stale), and SN-0594 is the
   only one of 28 tombstones whose page file survived. It fires on every push
   that touches the note tree, and a red that always fires teaches everyone
   to ignore red. (The pointer page is even stale itself: it names a
   "canonical path" under SN-0594 that no longer exists — the registry's real
   canonical is SN-0593's page, hash-verified.)
2. **Two numbers are claimed by two living notes each.** SN-0833 and SN-1852
   were each captured twice, 47 and 43 seconds apart, in a single landing
   wave on 2026-10-23 — two lanes numbering in parallel. All four notes are
   real, distinct intelligence (four different content hashes, four captures
   on disk), and all four page hashes verify. The public harm is already
   visible: **`/s/sn0833` serves the second note, not the first** — the
   first-claim note is shadowed at its own address.
3. **One machine identity is shared by two entries.** The block
   `IB-…hub-is-intelligence-projection` is the key of both SN-0593's live
   entry and SN-018's tombstone. Everything that looks entries up by block
   id silently sees only one of them; a routine future update to SN-0593
   would silently erase SN-018's tombstone from the record.
4. **One note lives at two addresses.** SN-01773's page exists twice — the
   registry-bound rendering (hash-verified) and an older, divergent rendering
   at a second path. Nothing binds the second copy; it can drift forever
   unnoticed.
5. **The tombstones themselves (28) are healthy** — every one carries its
   supersession evidence, the guards skip them by design, and they are the
   provenance record of the #1229 dedup. They are not defects. (Twelve carry
   a placeholder "successor not yet ingested" instead of a real successor —
   an honesty debt to note, not a gate failure.)

Two further classes are named so nobody mistakes them for part of this cure:
**28 legacy entries have no content hash** (removed by PR #1401; restoring
hash semantics is a Director decision — the drift test says so in its own
words), and the **SN-346 / SN-0346** pair is the same number written two ways
(historical tombstone vs live note; no gate impact, a naming question for the
board).

## The cure — one artifact, five moves

1. **Remove SN-0594's leftover pointer page.** The note's intelligence lives
   whole at SN-0593 (hash-verified); SN-0594 keeps its registry tombstone and
   its capture, so the vanish guard is satisfied and nothing is destroyed —
   the anomaly (a tombstone *with* a page) becomes consistent with its 27
   siblings (tombstones *without* pages). Fallback if the lane refuses any
   deletion: bind the entry honestly to the pointer page's bytes instead
   (projection_path + its real hash) — also green, less clean.
2. **Renumber the two colliding notes — by their own lanes.** First claim
   stands: the 02:42:53Z and 02:42:55Z captures keep SN-0833 and SN-1852.
   The later captures (47s / 43s behind, same wave) are re-filed by their
   owning lanes under fresh numbers allocated by the canonical allocator at
   cure time (next free at spec time: 1870, 1871 — the allocator decides,
   the spec never hard-codes). Each re-filing models the relationship
   (never deletes intelligence), the old page is removed in the same commit
   (the SN-1863→SN-1864 precedent), and a tombstone entry under a suffixed
   block id (`-dup0833`, `-dup1852` — the `-dup0594` precedent) preserves
   the retired pairing in the registry.
3. **Re-key SN-018's tombstone** to a suffixed block id (`-dup0018`), the
   same precedent, ending the silent-shadowing hazard. One field, one entry.
4. **Remove SN-01773's unbound twin page.** The block stays fully registered
   at its canonical, hash-verified home; only the divergent second rendering
   goes.
5. **Regenerate the seal and the index through the canonical tools** —
   manifest recomputed by `smart_note_v2._corpus_manifest_hash` itself, brain
   index by `tools/regenerate_brain_index.py` — and land everything as a
   single commit through the door. No partial landings, no hand-edited hash,
   no re-serialization except in the file's own proven byte form
   (`indent=2, ensure_ascii=False`, trailing newline — verified byte-identical
   round-trip at the tip).

## What this spec cannot decide (named, not buried)

- **Sub-minute first claims.** The timestamps order both pairs cleanly, and
  this spec applies "first-claim stands." But 43–47 seconds inside one
  landing wave is concurrency, not theft — if the board prefers the
  *currently served* note to keep SN-0833, that is a board call, and the cure
  works identically with the roles swapped.
- **Content-hash restoration** for the 28 hashless entries — a Director
  decision on hash semantics (documented in the drift test). Quantified in
  the machine twin; not touched here.
- **SN-346 vs SN-0346** naming hygiene — record-only in this cure.
- The 12 placeholder successors on tombstones — honesty debt, listed in the
  machine twin, for the board to schedule or accept.

## Rollback

One commit in, one commit out: restore the pre-cure tree from the cure
commit's parent (registry bytes, the three removed pages), manifest returns
to `ef5a71d6…` with the restored bytes, and the battery must show the known
baseline (exactly 1 defect) — never a new state. Fully reversible; no
production or database surface involved.
