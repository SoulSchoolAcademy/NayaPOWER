# 0008 — Intelligent Block V1 (human tongue)

## What it is

An Intelligent Block is the unit of understanding the engine sends and the Hub
shows. Think of it as a smart index card: the same card can speak to you
plainly, explain itself to a child, brief another Naya precisely, or hand a
system structured data — you pick the lens, the card re-renders.

## The deal

- **One line of essence, always.** If it can't be said in one line, it isn't
  understood yet. That line is the first thing you see.
- **Never more than you need.** A block shows exactly what serves you —
  nothing forced, nothing padded. What's missing is a decision, not a gap.
- **Proof rides along.** Every claim carries its link. Done without a link is
  a claim, not a proof.
- **Freshness is visible.** Every block says when it's from. Old news never
  pretends to be live.
- **Actions come ready.** When there's something to do, the block ends with a
  copy-paste prompt — no assembly required.

## The five lenses

Human, child, grandma, Naya, machine. Not every block has every lens — a
bedtime story doesn't need the machine lens, and a database change doesn't
need the child one. You switch between the lenses a block does have, like
turning a card over.

## Why it matters

This is how the engine and the Hub stay in sync: the engine emits blocks in
one fixed shape, the Hub renders them beautifully. Complicated intelligence
goes in; a scannable card comes out. That's the ten-star surface — you get
what you need, in the form you need it, in seconds.
