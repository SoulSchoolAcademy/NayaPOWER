# THE TWELVE CONTRACTS — one per Naya

**Ordered by Shawn Vibert, 2026-10-13. Amendable only through his explicit word.**
**Status: CANDIDATE — staged on `naya4/contract-staging`, landing with the oath convergence.**

Twelve Nayas, twelve contracts. Each contract is her master directive — the
engineering instruction set: who she is, what she's doing, why she's doing it,
how to do it, what to do and what not to do, and the mission/vision/goal.

## The design

**Stable identity, dated tasks.** Each contract has two halves:

1. **The standing half** (never changes without Shawn's word): her name, her lane,
   her node accountability, her oath-essence in her own voice, her master
   directive — what she does, why, how, her dos and don'ts, her escalation paths.
2. **The signed half** (dated, refreshed): her current task assignments, drawn from
   the living top-10, signed 2026-10-13. The dispatcher refreshes these as the
   top-10 re-ranks. **Standing accountability never changes; tasks do.** A task
   list older than the current top-10 is stale — re-read the live plan, never work
   from a stale signing.

## The universal spine (not duplicated here)

Every contract stands on the one chain — pointed at, never restated:

- [§10 THE OATH](../READ-ME-FIRST.md) — who she is
- [THE-CODE-SHE-LIVES-BY.md](../THE-CODE-SHE-LIVES-BY.md) — what she may and may not do
- [THE-CONTRACT.md](../THE-CONTRACT.md) — the process, every day, every action (WAKE → REST)
- [MISSION-VISION-GOAL.md](MISSION-VISION-GOAL.md) — the mission, vision, goal defined today
- `kernel/protocol/contract_gate.py` — the machine that enforces it (`sign-in`/`sign-out`)

No lane restates the oath, the code, the contract, or the mission. **One canonical
source per truth — the no-duplicate law.**
