## 4. Admission gate — what deserves a Smart Node

Do not convert every message, idea, log, or document into durable intelligence.

A candidate deserves Smart Node treatment when one or more of these are materially true:

1. it can change a future decision or behavior;
2. it prevents expensive rediscovery or repeated human explanation;
3. it establishes a reusable principle, procedure, capability, constraint, decision, lesson, or state;
4. it resolves or exposes an important contradiction;
5. it contains verified or verification-worthy outcome knowledge;
6. it materially connects otherwise isolated intelligence;
7. a cold successor would be meaningfully worse without it.

Before capture, ask:

**VALUE → UNIQUENESS → PROVENANCE → OWNER/SCOPE → APPLICABILITY → CONFLICT → FUTURE USE**

If the material is low-value, transient, duplicate, or machine exhaust, retain it only as source/history where appropriate.

## 6. Required intelligence envelope

A Smart Note / Intelligent Block capture must be able to answer:

- **IDENTITY** — what is this?
- **PURPOSE** — why preserve it?
- **OWNER** — whose intelligence is it?
- **SCOPE / PRIVACY** — where may it apply and who may access it?
- **TYPE** — what kind of intelligence is it?
- **MEANING** — what is the distilled proposition/procedure/lesson?
- **SOURCE LINEAGE** — where did it come from?
- **EPISTEMIC STATE** — what are we entitled to claim?
- **FRESHNESS / TEMPORAL STATE** — how current is it?
- **APPLICABILITY** — when does it matter?
- **RELATIONSHIPS** — what does it support, depend on, refine, contradict, supersede, or enable?
- **EVIDENCE** — what supports it?
- **CONFLICTS / UNCERTAINTY** — what remains unresolved?
- **AUTHORITY IMPLICATIONS** — what authority is required to use it?
- **VERIFICATION** — how has it been checked?
- **SUPERSESSION** — what replaces or is replaced by it?
- **USAGE / OUTCOME** — what happened when it was applied?
- **BEHAVIORAL EFFECT** — did later behavior change?
- **SUCCESSOR EFFECT** — what must the next Naya inherit?

Existing persistence/schema fields remain authoritative where they are more specific.

## 8. Expert challenge / Council review

High-value or consequential Smart Nodes SHOULD receive selected expert challenge before promotion or high-impact use.

Do not load every expert.

Default review pattern:

```
PRIMARY LENS
+ up to TWO complementary lenses
+ ADVERSARIAL / VERIFICATION lens when consequence warrants it
```

Examples:
- architecture: AI Systems + Verification + Adversarial;
- identity/security: Security & Trust + Governance + Verification;
- graph/retrieval: Graph & Retrieval + Data/Semantic + Performance;
- human interface: Human Systems + Human Cognition + Interaction/Interface.

Expert feedback:
- is intelligence/evidence, not authority;
- must remain attributable;
- must preserve disagreements;
- must not be averaged into false consensus;
- may create refinements, questions, constraints, counterevidence, or candidate learning.

When the Master Cognition Library is canonical, use its registered role packs for this selection. Until then, this protocol does not depend on an unmerged role library.
