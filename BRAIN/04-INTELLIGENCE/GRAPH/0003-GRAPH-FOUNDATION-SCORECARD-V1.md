## What this score does NOT mean

A 10/10 foundation score does not mean the living graph is 10/10 behaviorally proven.

The following remain runtime/proof gates:

- live application runtime consumes the graph;
- relationship-aware retrieval changes held-out behavior;
- graph context participates in verified actions;
- graph-derived learning is behaviorally demonstrated;
- successor runtimes independently recover and use graph intelligence;
- production parity is proven.

Those are execution proofs, not reasons to leave the information architecture unfinished.

## Constitutional relationship

The graph operates under the Constitution. A relationship can describe context or lineage; it cannot manufacture authority.

> **CONTEXT ≠ AUTHORITY**
>
> **RELATIONSHIP ≠ PERMISSION**
>
> **GRAPH EXISTENCE ≠ PROOF OF BEHAVIOR**
