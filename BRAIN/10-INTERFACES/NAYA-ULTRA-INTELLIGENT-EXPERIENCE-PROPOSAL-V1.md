Existing workflow includes maker self-score 9+, two different-seat exact-version scores and joint stamp where applicable; 9.5+ is the AAA target with all independent proof gates passed. Never average away a failed privacy boundary, dead primary action, false metric, untested core mobile path, broken data persistence, unapproved financial claim, owner conflict, or source/deploy mismatch.

- Data: Dashboard value and trend reconcile with event records, including zero, unknown, stale, filtered and permission-restricted scenarios.
