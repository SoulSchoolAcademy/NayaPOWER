## Where the two analyses converge (the short version)

Nitro's Part 1 harvest (KPI anatomy, donut with a heart, bar+line combo,
funnel, heat grid, streaks, ranked bars, people-first, the insight feed,
period tabs) matches my independent read almost item for item. Three of her
calls are better than mine and I adopt them outright:

1. **Block names a human can say.** "Pulse Strip" beats my "kpi-ribbon" —
   these are Factory product pieces, and product pieces get product names.
2. **Native data first.** My draft led with connectors. Hers is right: a
   block is born reading the app's own tables — *that's* what "works right
   away" means. Plugged-in sources arrive later as extra rows and series;
   the block never changes shape.
3. **Insight Feed as a feed.** I had capped Naya's read at 3 cards per
   dashboard. A feed is the truer pattern — the top 3 surface on Home by
   urgency, the rest live one tap away in the feed. Rationing still applies
   to *praise* (one "protect this" at a time), not to the feed itself.

And one convergence worth naming: we both picked **Trackify** as the bar —
the only family in our skin — and both found it still flat: a photograph of
data, not data alive. Its "AI Insights" recommendations are the single most
intelligent pattern in all 29 screens, and our Insight Feed is that pattern
made standard, living, and present in every app instead of one room.

## The frame it serves — one universal app, many front doors

Shawn's architecture (2026-10-22, standing doctrine): **not separate
templates — ONE ultimate app.** Each "app" is a **front door** — an
entrance plus a main-page focus — into the same interconnected system. The
doors: **Personal** (your intelligence today) · **Smart Lists** ·
**Business** · **Booking** (wired to the Calendar) · **Product** (the app
IS the product) · **Events** · **Community** · **Profile** · **Menu** (the
services — ours: NayaPOWER monthly / quarterly / yearly / lifetime) ·
**Sign-up** (email list, follow, Connect) · **Dashboard**.

Three consequences for this system:

1. **The Dashboard is a door — and the blocks live in every door.**
   Dashboard is the door whose focus is the Living Data System itself. But
   beautiful, living data is a *default property of the app*, not a page
   you visit: a Pulse Strip breathes on the Personal door's home, the
   Journey Funnel sits inside Business, the Faces Board inside Community.
   Every door inherits the same 8 blocks; the door chooses which lead.
2. **The simplicity law.** Stats are presented **simplified-beautiful —
   readable by an average person, a child, a grandma**. Simple in
   presentation, never shallow in substance: the full depth is always one
   tap away (that's what the ↗ on every block is *for*). SN-0822's human
   sentence is how a number stays simple without going shallow.
3. **Personal / Business / Space are door configurations, not apps.** A
   door = a cover + leading blocks + its data additions (§"The data") over
   the one base. Which is why Nitro's per-template additions survive the
   doctrine unchanged in substance — they are per-*door* configurations
   now, and the spec's language follows.

## The system, in one breath

**The 8 blocks** (Nitro's canon — contracts in the AI spec): Pulse Strip ·
Trend Panel · Mix Donut · Journey Funnel · Faces Board · Insight Feed ·
Heat & Streaks · Health Gauge. Supporting pieces inside them: delta chips,
micro-sparklines, ranked-bar lists, segmented bars. The radar is reserved

**The data** (Nitro's taxonomy, canonical): a **universal core** every app
tracks from minute one — views, visitors, posts, messages and median
response time, connections, the invite journey (sent → opened → joined),
content published, voice plays, saves, shares, activity heat and streaks,
health score — plus per-template additions: **Personal** (note reach, top
connections), **Business** (leads, deals funnel and value, orders/revenue,
repeat customers), **Space** (member growth, joins, top contributors), and
the **Hub rollup** across every world you own. (Under the doors doctrine
these additions are per-*door* configurations — Personal, Business and
Space are doors of the one app, not separate apps.) My layer adds one thing: each
metric carries a **domain color job** (magenta = people/social, sapphire =
learning, emerald = alive/money-healthy, gold = extraordinary, red = alarm,
purple = the headline) so when a new source plugs in, its data arrives
already knowing what color it is.

**The laws**: SN-0822 over everything; her seven Display Laws (no naked
numbers · spectrum identity, purple opens · motion is proof of life, with
reduced-motion = fully lit static · honest sparse states, never fixtures ·
period is one tap · faces over rows, sentences over labels, journeys over
totals · one system in every app); Smart Graphs G1–G7 underneath (magnitude
becomes light · the number is the hero · truth in every state · the card is
the contract).

## Where I differ from Nitro — explicitly, with reasons

1. **Health Gauge is /100; judgment scores stay /10.** Her gauge computes
   app health from response time, activity, growth, completeness — that's a
   *vitality percentage*, and /100 is right for it. But NayaNET's *judgment*
   scores (the Calculator, scorecards) are canonically /10 (orbit-dial).
   Two scales, two meanings, never mixed on one dial: vitality /100,
   judgment /10. If we ever blend them, a 94 will stop meaning anything.
2. **Pulse Strip sentences are micro-sentences.** SN-0822 demands a human
   sentence under every number — agreed, it ships. But the Composition Law
   says home stays light: on the Strip, the sentence is a micro-read
   (≤6 words — "Invites are converting well"), and the full sentence with
   evidence and action lives in the Insight Feed. The law is honored; the
   strip stays glanceable.
3. **Constellation stays in the instrument box.** Nitro reserves radar for
   the Hub balance view — agreed. Separately, our canon's *constellation*
   (networks of connected things — MicroGridTech's asset map is one in the
   wild) answers a different question ("how are things connected?") and
   stays available as an instrument for Spaces/Hub network views. Radar =
   balance across dimensions; constellation = structure of connections.
   Not duplicates.
