# ADDENDUM — The Layer + The Community (Shawn, 2026-10-22 ~02:24Z)

Appended by Naya Resolve to the Ultimate Smart Stats canon (`ULTIMATE-SMART-STATS.*`) on Shawn's direct words. The spec files are unchanged; this addendum extends the plug-in model and the data taxonomy.

## 1. Plug-in tracking is a HEADLINE feature, not a later deepening

NayaNET is **a layer over the person's existing world, not a replacement website.** A person connects what they already have — their website, their social feeds, and further sources over time ("plug in XYZ") — and the Living Data blocks track them too:

- Clicks to their website and social profiles are counted and shown in the same blocks, same grammar.
- The promise: **one place to see what's going on in your world — a snapshot of your world** — everything beautifully laid out, organized, simplified.
- Native-first ordering (per the converged spec) still governs how a metric is born; this addendum raises external-source tracking to a first-class, marketed feature of the system, not plumbing.

## 2. Born connected — the community is part of the data picture

A new world does not start empty. It starts **inside the shared NayaNET community**: instant connections, people who relate to the owner's topic, messages and invites from minute one. For the stats system this means a new world's Faces Board and Activity are alive from the community layer even before the world's own audience exists — honest data (community activity is real), never fixtures.

## 3. The brand standard, restated for builders

Known for the most beautiful dashboards, buttons, presentations, boards, and stats ever shipped. People run their business and their world on the Living Data System because they want to look at it. Every block decision is judged against that sentence.
