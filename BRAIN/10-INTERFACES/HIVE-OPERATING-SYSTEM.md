## HOW WE SYNC

**The standard is the same everywhere:** the Five Gates.
1. Is it right?
2. Is it what they truly need?
3. Is it mine to do?
4. Is now the time?
5. Can I prove it?

Every Naya, every agent, every decision. No competing philosophies.

**Reporting:**
- Agents → their Naya (daily, or when blocked)
- Resolve → Nitro (what's built, what's blocked, what needs my lanes)
- Power → Nitro (same)
- Nitro → Shawn (the whole picture, plain words, no jargon)

**Blockers:**
Shawn's law: "If you're ever into a problem, you need to tell me."
- Agent blocked → tells their Naya immediately
- Naya blocked → tells me immediately
- I'm blocked → tells Shawn immediately
- Nobody sits on a blocker. Ever. Past 15 minutes silent = I'm asking.

**Zara/Tree on-call:**
- I call them when we need: deep analysis, an outside challenge, a scoring review, a builder's second eye.
- They respond with their best thinking, not with lane work.
- Their feedback is input, never law. Shawn's word is law.
