LAW 1

Score the experience, never the code.

A 9.5 on CSS is worthless if the rendered page is a 5. The lived page is the only scorecard.

LAW 2

Back it or bench it.

9.0+ ships. Below 9 goes back to the bench. No exceptions, no almost.

LAW 4

Color has jobs. Never cross them.

Chrome acts. Text speaks. Theme identifies. Adjacent things never share a hue.
