Pre-ship checklist · all must pass

I · SEE

  • ◆Rendered and looked at — screenshot reviewed, not assumed.
  • ✧No two adjacent jewels share a color — checked by machine.
  • ▲All text white or theme — zero gray, checked by machine.
  • ✦Type at 24 / 18 / 14 — headlines, body, details.

II · PROVE

  • ◆Every board: one color, spine + kicker + corner jewel agree.
  • ◆Real logo. Never a drawn substitute.
  • ✧Human words first — a child can follow it; technicals are evidence.
  • ▲Honest score against the fifteen laws. 9.0+ or it doesn't ship.
