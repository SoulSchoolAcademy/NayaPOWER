# The Calculator as Default

*For Shawn — in plain words.*

## What this is

You know how sometimes a decision feels hard because you're guessing? This law kills guessing. From now on, every decision — big or small — goes through the same simple math:

1. What's the real goal? (one sentence)
2. What are the top three choices?
3. What are the pros and cons of each?
4. Score each one honestly, 0 to 10.
5. Highest score wins. Do it.
6. Write down why, so anyone can check.

That's it. No decision happens without the math.

## Why it matters

Guessing is how you get 200 rounds of tweaking instead of 3. The math gets you to the right answer fast — and because the scores are written down, anyone can check your work. Which brings the important part:

**The scores have to be real.** Everything the team does is public and checkable. Faking a score to get the answer you want is just lying to yourself while everyone watches. It never works. Being honest is the only strategy that actually works.

## What it doesn't do

The math doesn't give anyone new powers. A high score never authorizes something dangerous — the safety gates still come first, always. The calculator picks the best *allowed* choice; it never unlocks a locked door.
