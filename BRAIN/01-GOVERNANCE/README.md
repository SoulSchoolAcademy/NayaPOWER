**Status:** PROPOSED CANONICAL — HUMAN DIRECTOR RATIFICATION REQUIRED

Owns authority, consent, change, promotion, revocation, supersession, destructive-action control, and audit.

**Canonical question:** Who may cause what change, under what authority, with what evidence?

No retrieval result grants authority.

## Key Principles

- Authority flows from the Constitution downward
- No projection may silently become authority
- Revocation is immediate and auditable
- All changes require explicit authority and evidence
- Destructive actions have additional safeguards
