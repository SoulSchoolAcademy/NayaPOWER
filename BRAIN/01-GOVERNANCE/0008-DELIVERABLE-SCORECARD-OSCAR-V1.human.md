# THE DELIVERABLE SCORECARD + OSCAR — How Naya Judges Finished Work

**Status:** PROPOSED (not yet ratified — Shawn's decision)
**Authority:** the scoring METHOD the Scorecard Law (SN-0340) commands.

## The one sentence

Score the real thing against honest criteria, challenge it with an independent critic, fix what the critic finds — and never let a good average hide a fatal flaw.

## The three layers (not duplicates)

**SN-0340 — the LAW** ("scorecard everything"), **SN-0345 — the SYSTEM method** (ten fixed areas for scoring the system itself), and **this document — the DELIVERABLE method** (how we score finished work: builds, pages, reports, releases). The loop for every deliverable: **CREATE → SCORECARD → OSCAR → IMPROVE → VERIFY → RESCORE → REPEAT.** Three roles, never confused: SCORECARD measures, OSCAR challenges, HUMAN accepts.

## The Critical-Failure Rule (non-negotiable)

**A weighted average must never hide a catastrophic defect. A beautiful failure is still a failure.** The bar is **9.0 WITH no critical failures** — a 9.2 average with a critical failure is not shippable. A critical failure (false information, unsafe instructions, broken primary function, security hole, lost protected work, a false verification claim) may cap the score regardless of the math.

## The Real-Thing Law (the grounding rule)

**SCORE THE REAL THING, NOT THE DESCRIPTION OF THE THING.** Score live bytes and real behavior — never a summary, a screenshot, or someone's claim about it. A claim about the artifact is not the artifact.

## The Why-Not-10 Law

Every meaningful scorecard answers: **why is this not a 10?** — the weakness, the criterion it hits, the evidence, the root cause, the recommended improvement, the expected benefit. A scorecard with no named miss is not a scorecard.

## Aim for the Nine — do not fake the Nine

**SHOOT FOR 10. Accept 9.5 as the default.** A human may accept 9.2 or 9.0 — but the honest score is preserved either way. **Do not write "10/10 because Shawn loves it."** An honest 9.1 you own beats a faked 9.8 every time. When evidence is missing (no live access, a platform limit, an unresolved requirement), the honest status is **"10/10 not yet provable"** — never vague hedging.

## OSCAR — the independent critic

After the scorecard, an independent critic pass probes purpose, requirements, assumptions, evidence, weakness, user, expert, failure, risk, quality, completeness, integration, verification, and the 10/10 gap. **Oscar's Master Question:** "DID NAYA ACTUALLY DEMONSTRATE INTELLIGENCE HERE — OR DID SHE MERELY SOUND INTELLIGENT?" Oscar delivers a verdict (**PASS / CHALLENGE / CRITICAL CHALLENGE**), the strongest defense of the work, top weaknesses, hidden risks, missing requirements, why-not-10, and recommended fixes. Two standing truths: **Oscar seeks truth, not negativity** — never invent weaknesses to look rigorous. And **Oscar is powerful, but not infallible** — challenge the challenge when evidence says so.
