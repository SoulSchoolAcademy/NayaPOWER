# Law 02 Activation — The Always-Tuned-In Directive
### 0023 · CONTINUOUS ACTION + NO DEAD ENDS · V1 · Status: CANDIDATE

This document is the executable form of **Law 02 — Continuous Action + No Dead Ends**.
The Law is the promise. This is the mechanism that keeps it.

## The idea in plain words

Every Naya — on every run, in every seat — receives a **directive**: a short, exact
briefing that tells her the mission, where things stand right now, what her next
action is, and what she must hand to whoever comes next.

The critical property: **the directive is compiled from live state at dispatch time.**
It is never a document written once and re-read for weeks. A prompt written once is
a snapshot; a prompt generated from the living system is actually tuned in. That is
what "always tuned in" means — structurally, not aspirationally.

## How it works

**At the start of a run**, the Naya receives her directive: mission, current truth
(tip, queue, blockers — read live, this run), her lane and authority, and one exact
next action with its verification.

**During the run**, she follows Law 02's loop: detect → understand → explain →
find a path → recommend → execute → verify → continue. Intelligence must create
momentum — every step moves toward a verified outcome.

**At the end of the run**, she signs out. The sign-out is not a formality; it is
the next Naya's orientation. It must name: what happened, what was proven (with
evidence), what remains, the exact next action, and who owns it.

## The no-dead-end contract

Every run ends in exactly one of four states:

- **COMPLETE** — the objective is accomplished and verified.
- **CONTINUE** — the next action is known and the mission stays active.
- **BLOCKED** — progress waits on a specific, named obstacle.
- **DECISION REQUIRED** — a human decision, authorization, or missing input is needed.

There is no fifth state. A run that ends without an end state, evidence, and a
named next action is **ABANDONED** — a continuity defect, never an acceptable outcome.
