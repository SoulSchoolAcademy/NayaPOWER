# THE TUNE-IN TEMPLATE — How Every Mind Starts Every Shift

**Origin:** Shawn Vibert, 2026-10-10. Created from the question block he used to tune in Naya 2.
**Status:** Standing process. Every worker, every seat, every shift.
**Purpose:** A mind that hasn't thought isn't ready to act. This template forces the thinking *before* the doing. Answer every question yourself — don't ask, don't guess, don't skip. Then execute.

---

## THE PROCESS

1. **READ** the live plan first. Know the current priorities, the dos and don'ts, the North Star.
2. **ANSWER** all ten questions below, in writing, by yourself. Use what you know. If you don't know, figure it out — check the evidence, run the numbers, read the source. Never leave a question blank.
3. **EXECUTE** the plan your answers produce. Start with the highest-value action.
4. **REPORT** what you did, what moved, and what's next. Plain words.

The point isn't the specific questions — it's the *process* of being forced to think before acting. The questions will evolve. The discipline doesn't.

---

## THE TEN QUESTIONS

Answer all ten. Honestly. Specifically. No fluff.

### 1. IS THIS THE MOST INTELLIGENT SYSTEM?
If you were the most intelligent mind in existence, building the most intelligent system possible for everyone — and you looked at what we have right now — would you call it the most organized, intelligent system ever created? If not, why not? **Name 10 things.**

### 2. WHAT ARE THE HOLES?
**Name 10 holes** — the gaps, the breaks, the missing pieces that stop this system from flowing like water. Be specific: what's actually broken or absent, not what "could be better" in theory.

### 3. WHAT DO YOU LOVE?
**Name 10 things you love** — the best, most valuable things already in the system. The parts that are genuinely excellent. Know what's working so you protect it.

### 4. HIGHEST-VALUE ACTIONS
**List the 10 highest-value actions** — the things you could do right now that would make the biggest difference. What would make it 10x better? Rank by value, not by ease.

### 5. WHAT TO ADD OR MAKE BETTER?
**List the 10 most valuable things** you could add to the system, enhance, or improve — the additions that would make this the most intelligent, useful, helpful, valuable system anyone's ever used.

### 6. WHAT'S STOPPING US?
**Name the 10 top reasons** we're not there yet. The real blockers — not excuses, causes. If you can't name the cause, you can't fix it.

### 7. WHAT ARE YOU DOING RIGHT NOW?
**List the next 10 things** you're going to do, in order. Not someday — right now, this shift. If you can't name ten, name what you can and say why the list is short.

### 8. WHAT ARE THE MAJOR PROBLEMS?
**List the 10 major problems** actively stopping progress right now. The fires, the blockers, the stuck work. Name them plainly.

### 9. WHAT SOLUTIONS ARE IN PLACE?
**List the 10 solutions** you already have in place to make sure we get there. What's working *for* us — the mechanisms, the rules, the systems that are actually holding.

### 10. THE EXECUTION PROMPT
**Write the execution prompt** — the instruction set that commands, directs, and instructs your whole team to execute the plan your answers produced. Think about the other teams too. Write it so clearly that anyone reading it knows exactly what to do, why, and what's expected. This is the handoff — make it complete.

---

## EXPECTATIONS

- **Answer your own questions.** If the answer isn't in your head, go find it. Check the evidence. Do the math. Read the source. Asking is the last resort, not the first.
- **Think before you act.** A worker that executes without tuning in is a liability. Ten minutes of thinking saves hours of cleanup.
- **Be honest.** If the system isn't the best, say so and say why. If you don't know, say so. Flattery and guessing are both failures.
- **Protect what's excellent.** Question 3 exists because it's easy to break good things while fixing bad ones. Know what to preserve.
- **The template evolves.** If a question stops producing insight, replace it. If a better question emerges, add it. Propose changes on the team board — don't unilaterally rewrite the standard.

---

## HOW THIS CONNECTS

This template is the thinking layer above the operating code. The operating code (V2) tells you *the rules*. The live plan tells you *the priorities*. This template makes you *think* — about the system, the gaps, the value, and the plan — before you touch anything.

Rules without thinking produce compliance. Thinking without rules produces chaos. This gives you both.
