# The Plain-Words Decision Format

*For Shawn — in plain words. (Yes, this document follows its own rule.)*

## What this is

When the team needs your decision on something, we'll tell you what it **does** — not its code number.

**Bad:** "PR #2068 needs a click to unblock WO1."

**Good:** "Should we install the safety fix that stops the system from lying about where its intelligence came from? If yes, the lie becomes impossible. If no, the risk stays in the code. The math says yes, 8.3/10. (PR #2068, for the record.)"

## Why it matters

You can't answer a question you don't understand. Code numbers are for the record — they tell you *where*, not *what*. What you need to decide is always *what it does and what it causes*. That's what we'll lead with, every time.

## The promise

Every ask will come as: what it does → why → what happens if yes → what happens if no → the math → the reference numbers last. If you ever get a message that's just numbers and "needs a click," we've broken this law — call it out.
