## What this is, in plain words

Shawn's 100 Daily Success Reminders aren't new ideas — they're the *ancestry* of the laws the team already lives by. This file draws the family tree: his reminder on the left, the current team law on the right, and the exact file where that law lives.

## Why it matters

The team isn't inventing Shawn's standards — it's rediscovering them. Saying that out loud kills "new process" resistance: when a new Naya locks in, she learns that the scorecard bar, the proof standard, and the next-action rule are *his* standard formalized, not bureaucracy.
