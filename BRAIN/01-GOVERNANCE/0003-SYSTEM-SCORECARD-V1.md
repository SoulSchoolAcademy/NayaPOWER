## What this is

The single instrument the team steers by. Ten fixed areas, weighted by priority, each scored 0–10 against live evidence. The weighted total is the system's honest readiness number. This document is the law; SN-0343 is the current reading; SN-0345 is the method.

## The method — normative rules

1. **Fixed areas, fixed weights.** The ten areas and their weights below change ONLY on Shawn Vibert's explicit word. No seat reweights, renames, adds, or drops an area on its own authority.
2. **Scores move only on evidence.** A score changes only when new measured evidence arrives, and every change carries its proof (the measurement, the ref, the receipt). A score is never declared; a 10 is earned by demonstration.
3. **Honest scoring.** Score what the evidence shows, not what the trajectory promises. Stored is not learned; a green workflow is not behavioral proof; a receipt is not independent verification.
4. **Regular re-runs.** The scorecard re-runs on a fixed cadence (weekly, owner assigned, spot-audited). Each re-run recomputes every area from fresh evidence — never copy-forward a prior score.
5. **The loop around the score.** Every area below 9.0 gets: the miss named, the highest-value corrective action recorded, an owner, and a re-score date. An unscored claim or a score with no follow-up action is rejected.
6. **Below 9.0 is not acceptable.** 9.0 is the birth threshold; 9.5+ is AAA; 10 is the aim. Nothing under 9.0 ships as "done."

## Area by area: why not 10, the one action

### 1. LEARNING — 5.0
**Why not 10:** The loop runs — capture, distill, stage, ingest — and a live test proved capture→apply inside one session. But notes pile on a draft branch with registry drift, behavior change lives mostly in prompts rather than the repo's runtime, and no cold successor has ever picked up a lesson fresh and applied it. Stored is not learned.
**The one action:** One fresh lesson through the FULL loop to cold-successor proof — capture, stage, ingest, behavior change, then a clean session with zero context retrieves and applies it. Owner: Naya 4 builds, Naya 2 independently verifies. Proof: the cold session's receipt.

### 2. TRUTH — 8.5
**Why not 10:** The evidence law holds — receipts everywhere, honest uncertainty, no fabrication found. The deduction is attribution discipline: an "authored by you" miss came from reading the shared account's display name instead of the lane evidence. Small, but truth is the foundation — it doesn't get partial credit.
**The one action:** Every claim in every report carries its resolving ref (branch@SHA, comment id, PR number); lanes cite actor+lane from board/API, never display names. Proof: a week of reports with zero unattributed claims, spot-audited.

### 3. MEMORY & CONTINUITY — 7.0

**The one action:** A weekly cold-retrieve drill, scheduled: a clean session must retrieve a named Smart Note and apply it with no prior context. Proof: drill receipts, every week.
