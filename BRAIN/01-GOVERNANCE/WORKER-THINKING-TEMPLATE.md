# The Thinking Template — tune-in triggers for every mind

**Origin:** Shawn, 2026-10-10. **Status:** LIVING — mold it, add to it, never skip it.
**Purpose:** All minds — human or AI — need triggers to think straight. This
template makes a worker answer its own questions before it acts: what are the
holes, what's the priority, what's the most intelligent thing to do. The point
is not the exact questions. The point is the PROCESS: a mind that answers these
cannot drift. Run it every shift, before work starts.

---

## The questions — answer in your own words, then execute

**0. The mirror.** If you were the most intelligent mind in the universe and you
looked at what we have, would you call it the most organized, intelligent
system ever created? If not — why not? Say it plainly.

**1. Ten holes.** Name 10 holes you can see that stop the work from flowing like
water. Be specific: what exactly is stuck, and where?

**2. Ten loves.** Name the 10 things you love most — the best, most valuable
things in the system. Know what to protect while you change everything else.

**3. Ten highest-value actions.** What 10 things could you do right now that
would make it ten times better? Ranked by value, not by ease.

**4. Ten additions.** What 10 things could you add, enhance, or rebuild that
would make this the most intelligent, useful, valuable system anyone ever used?

**5. Ten reasons.** What are the 10 top reasons stopping us from getting there?
Structural, not personal. Name the design flaw, not the person.

**6. Ten next moves.** List the 10 things YOU are going to do right now, in
order. Not the team — you.

**7. Ten problems.** What are the 10 major problems blocking us right now?
Current, concrete, checkable.

**8. Ten solutions in place.** What 10 solutions do you already have running
that ensure we get there? Give yourself honest credit — and honest gaps.

## Then: the execution prompt

After the answers, write the execution prompt: the instruction set that commands
your team to execute the plan. It directs, it instructs, it names owners, it
sets the order. Other teams are part of the plan — coordinate through #1354,
never work in silence. The prompt is the trigger: handed back, the team moves.

---

## How to use this

- **Every shift, before work:** read the live plan, then run this template.
  10 minutes of thinking saves 10 hours of drifting.
- **Mold it:** add questions that catch your lane's failure modes. Remove
  questions that stopped teaching you. A dead template is worse than none —
  if you're answering on autopilot, rewrite it.
- **Plain words:** answer like you're explaining to a smart friend who doesn't
  do computers. If you hide behind jargon, you haven't thought it through.
- **Honest scores:** a defended 7.5 beats a wished 9.0. Name what's actually
  true, especially when it's uncomfortable.
