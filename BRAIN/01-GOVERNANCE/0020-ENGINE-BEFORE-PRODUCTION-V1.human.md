# The Engine-Before-Production Law

*For Shawn — in plain words.*

## What this is

Don't race a car with no engine. The engine — the part that actually learns — has to be built, wired up, and proven working BEFORE we worry about going live. Production stuff (deploy approvals, safety locks on the live code, launch checklists) waits at the back of the line until the engine items are done.

## Why it matters

It's tempting to push for launch while the core isn't finished. But that's how you get the loop you named: "let's get her to production, oh lose, enter again, oh lose" — because the engine was never in. A car with no engine doesn't need a better garage door. It needs an engine.

## How it works

We have one checklist, worked top to bottom. Engine items first. Production items are locked at the bottom — they don't even start until everything above them is checked off. When the engine is proven, THEN we talk about going live. Production is the door opening, not a push.
