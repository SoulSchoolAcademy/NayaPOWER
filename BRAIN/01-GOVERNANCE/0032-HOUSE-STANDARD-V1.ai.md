# 0032 — THE HOUSE STANDARD V1 (AI tongue)

**Purpose:** operationalize Shawn's house standard (2026-10-21) as enforceable behavior for every seat: the system is home/baby — clean, organized, high-performing, fed only verified intelligence; mutual whole-house responsibility; act-then-report. Extends 0030 (value-only brain) and 0031 (never-again reservoir laws); the escalation ladder (0031 L4) is the mechanism this standard motivates.

## Definitions

- **Jam:** any queue, gate, pipeline, or surface whose backlog/age/error state worsens across 2 consecutive checks, OR any single state that blocks downstream flow (red tip CI, a never-succeeded workflow, an expired grant).
- **Bystander act:** posting, noting, or relaying a problem without (a) fixing it, (b) routing it to a named owner in the same hour with confirmation, or (c) escalating it as a genuine human gate WITH a prepared solution packet. Bystander acts are defects, scored like failed work.
- **Gasoline:** creating new work in an area with an open jam, except the corrective work that clears the jam.
- **Good gas:** inputs that passed verification at intake (registered landings, scored ≥9 artifacts, byte-verified claims). Unverified content entering the brain or production is a fuel violation regardless of author.

## Rules (machine-checkable where possible)

- **H1 Act-then-report.** Every problem report carries exactly one of: FIXED (with proof link), ROUTED (named owner + timestamp + landing confirmed), GATE (human-gate packet attached). A report with none of these is returned by reviewers as a bystander act.
- **H2 No gasoline.** While an area has an open jam, new creations in that area are limited to jam-clearing correctives. (Operationalizes 0031 L2/L5 at the area level.)
- **H3 Whole-house ownership.** Any seat may — and must — act on or route any jam it observes, regardless of lane. Lane ownership governs who *fixes*; observation ownership is universal. "Not my lane" is a protocol violation when attached to an observed jam.
- **H4 Clean-as-you-go.** Any landing that leaves a registry, count, index, or listing stale is incomplete work; the landing's receipt must name its hygiene effects (registered, re-stamped, pruned) or state none-applicable.
- **H5 Intake quality gate.** Content entering BRAIN/ or production without its verification artifacts (registry entry, scorecard, proof) is reverted-or-flagged at discovery; the flag routes to the author seat and the index lane.
- **H6 Nightly house check.** The 0030/0031 nightly print adds a JAMS section: open jams by area, age, owner, clear action. A metric worsening 2 consecutive nights auto-creates a drain action (0031 L3) — no seat discretion to watch-and-wait.
- **H7 Full-scope before action.** Consequential actions carry the flow-test trace (function, end-to-end flow, needs, downstream breakage) per the standing FULL-SCOPE FIRST law (SN-0500 delta). Absence of the trace on a consequential receipt = incomplete receipt.

## Metrics (nightly, with the 0031 print)

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Open jams | 0 sustained; any jam >48h escalates |
| Bystander acts per week | 0 |
| Median jam age at clear | < 24h |
| Tip CI state | green |
| Branch count trend | falling to < 200 |
| Learning score basis | adoptions (proven behavior changes), not filings |

## Falsifier

Any week in which a jam was reported by ≥2 seats, remained open >48h, and no FIXED/ROUTED/GATE record exists ⇒ H1/H3 are theater; main seat escalates to Shawn with the evidence and the seats named.
