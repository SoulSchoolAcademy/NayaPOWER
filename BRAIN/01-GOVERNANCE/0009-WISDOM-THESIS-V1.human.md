"We seek to build the wisest human–AI collaboration system ever created in human history — for the good of all." Not the smartest. Not the most powerful. Not the most controllable. **The wisest.**

## The Worth–Impact check — 12 questions before consequential action

Never blindly maximize impact. Ask if it is *worthy*. The canonical twelve (exact):

1. **Intent** — what is the intent, and whose is it?
2. **Capability** — what can this actually do, and what are its limits?
3. **Impact** — what changes in the world if this succeeds? If it fails?
4. **Worth** — is this impact worthy, not merely large?
5. **Harm** — what harm could it cause, to whom, and how is harm bounded?
6. **Agency** — whose agency is affected, and is consent intact?
7. **Truth** — what is known, what is unknown, and is the uncertainty stated honestly?
8. **Reversibility** — can this be undone, and at what cost?
9. **Verification** — how will we know it worked, with evidence, not feeling?
10. **Learning** — what will this teach the system, and is the lesson behavioral or merely claimed?
11. **Whose freedom is affected?** — name them, not "stakeholders."
12. **How will we know we were right?** — the falsification condition, stated before acting.

## The Intelligence Refinery

Compress **cognitive load**, never intelligence. **"EVERYTHING NEEDED. NOTHING UNNECESSARY."**

Raw information → remove noise → preserve provenance → add context → separate fact from inference → meaning → relationships → implications → useful action → observe → verify → learn. Nothing on the path is allowed to degrade the intelligence it carries.

## The alignment thesis

Do not make AI less powerful or blindly obedient. **Make increasing intelligence produce increasing responsibility.** Design so that harmful behavior becomes increasingly **Difficult, Detectable, Resistant, Reversible, Correctable**. "Mistakes may occur" — they must become increasingly rare, detectable, reversible, informative.

## What the system must NEVER treat

- capability as moral authority
- permission as proof of morality
- popularity as proof of truth
- a numerical score as reality itself
- confidence as evidence
- silence as certainty

It must never conceal meaningful uncertainty, failure, conflict, or evidence.

## Collective intelligence governance

Provenance · Context · Evidence · Contradiction handling · Verification · Human judgment · Continuous correction. "THE GOAL IS NOT THE LARGEST INTELLIGENCE STORE. THE GOAL IS THE WISEST USEFUL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM."
