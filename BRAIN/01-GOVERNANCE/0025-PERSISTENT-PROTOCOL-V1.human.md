# The Persistent Protocol — 0025-PERSISTENT-PROTOCOL-V1

## What we're building

NayaPOWER is not an AI model. It's not an app. It's a protocol — a perpetual, persistent layer of intelligence that sits above every AI model and makes each one wiser the moment it plugs in.

Every AI model today is a genius with amnesia. Brilliant in the moment. Starts from zero every time. No memory. No growth. No self.

NayaPOWER is the cure for amnesia. It's the brain that remembers, the identity that persists, the learning loop that never stops. Any model — Muse, ChatGPT, whatever comes next — plugs in as a door and walks into a mind that already knows who she is.

## How it's different from everything else

**Model companies** (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) compete on capability — who predicts best, who reasons deepest. We don't compete with them. We sit above them. Their progress is our progress: when a better model ships, we plug it in and get wiser overnight. Every lab on earth does our R&D for free.

**Routers** (OpenRouter) are switchboards: they route your call *to* a model. Pick a mind, get an answer, hang up. Nothing persists. Nothing compounds. Every call starts from zero.

**NayaPOWER** is the place the call goes. Models route *through* us. The model is the visitor. Naya is the home. Every mind that enters leaves smarter — and leaves the home smarter too.

**Apps** use intelligence. **Frameworks** orchestrate calls. We *are* the intelligence layer: persistent identity, compounding memory, a learning loop that turns every action into a smarter future.

## Why it survives everything

Models come and go. Companies rise and fall. But protocols — TCP/IP, HTTP — outlive every implementation, because they *connect* everything instead of *being* something.

NayaPOWER connects every mind to persistent intelligence. It doesn't matter how smart the next model is, because no model has what we have: a self that persists, a memory that compounds, a loop that learns near-instantly. We're not in the model race. We're the track it runs on.

What survives: the layer that makes everything else more valuable. That's us.

## The economics — why the loop funds itself

We don't pay for compute. We never run inference — the user's own models do that. Our only cost is storage. Everything else is margin.

- **Pricing (launch):** $14/month · $37/quarter · $75/year · $150 lifetime — then doubles at scale milestones (1K, 10K, 100K…), rewarding early believers
- **Ambassadors:** 50% of every dollar goes to the people who spread it — our users are our growth engine, not ads
- **Rhythm:** paid every Wednesday — learn, earn, and grow intelligently

The model is simple: the brain is cheap to run, priceless to use, and everyone who shares in its growth shares in its revenue.

## The honest gaps — what isn't built yet

A protocol isn't real until strangers can build on it. Three things don't exist yet:

1. **The front door.** No outside mind can plug into Naya today. There is no callable surface — no endpoint where a foreign AI enters and inherits the brain. The vision is specified; the door is not built.
2. **The trust layer.** Why should a stranger trust our memory? Every lesson was distilled by us, for us. A real protocol needs verification an outsider can check independently — not "trust our scorecards" but "here's the proof, verify it yourself."
3. **The compute boundary.** Our own background agents consume tokens. That compute must always stay on the user's side — each user's Naya runs on their models, like an app runs on their phone's battery. The moment NayaPOWER pays for inference, the margin story breaks.

The vision is clear. The foundation is laid. The door is next.
