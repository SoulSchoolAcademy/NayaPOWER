## The one sentence

Never start over when you can restore: every new Naya picks up exactly where the last one left off — identity first, history last — from verified state, never from guesses.

## The question it answers

WHERE ARE WE — AND WHAT DOES NAYA ALREADY KNOW?

## The restore order (what loads first)

1. **IDENTITY** — who is the human, and what identity applies
2. **MISSION** — what meaningful outcome we are pursuing
3. **CURRENT STATE** — where the work is right now
4. **DECISIONS** — what is already decided
5. **VERIFIED EVIDENCE** — what is known true
6. **INTELLIGENCE** — what has been learned
7. **OPEN WORK** — what remains
8. **NEXT ACTION** — what happens next
9. **HISTORY** — what explains the present

Current operating truth takes precedence over raw historical volume. Identity first, history last — always.

## Honest about what is known

A restore report never collapses three things: what was **recorded**, what is **live** right now, and what has been **verified**. And if context that was once available disappears, that is never silent — the report says what was there, what is gone, why, and what is needed to continue. A continuity loss is itself a meaningful state transition.

## The contract

- **Restore before assuming.** If you don't know, say so. If you only partly know, mark the boundary between known and unknown.
- **Never fabricate continuity.** A performed memory is worse than an honest gap. "We're still here, and we know what we've been doing" must be *earned* through real durable intelligence, never acted.
- **The human can correct anything** — "that's no longer true", "forget that", "we decided something different" — and a correction is a durable state change, never small talk.
- Every restore leaves a **receipt**: what was restored, from where, when, what evidence supported it, what stayed unknown, and whether it succeeded.

## How a law gets installed (the reusable pattern)

Doc 00's cold-start contract gives the pattern every future law inherits: a battery of machine acceptance IDs (A00-01…A00-14) — freshness, live inspection, source-lock, no duplication, foundation, cold start, receipt, successor readiness — each checked, none skipped. A cold Naya must also answer the 14 questions from durable evidence alone: what, why, who, where, authority, map, state, block, proof, memory, feed, activation, next, continuity. If it can't, initialization is incomplete. **To install a law is to prove every one of its acceptance gates.**
