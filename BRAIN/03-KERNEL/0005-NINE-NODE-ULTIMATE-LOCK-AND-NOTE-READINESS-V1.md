## Resolved semantic collisions for note construction

### KNOW vs PROVE vs CONNECT
- KNOW owns canonical intelligence-object identity, meaning, lifecycle, provenance and durable intelligence.
- PROVE owns epistemic claim/evidence assessment.
- CONNECT owns task/context applicability and relationship-aware selection.
- Notes must not describe KNOW as the final truth or applicability authority.

### CONNECT UNKNOWN applicability
- UNKNOWN may be visible as non-steering context when all privacy/provenance gates permit.
- UNKNOWN cannot become steering-eligible.
- Steering requires explicit APPLICABLE plus matching task class or a valid broader applicability contract.

### VERIFY axes
Never collapse:
- outcome status;
- acceptance decision;
- causal status;
- verification maturity/observation window.

### LEARN candidate vs promotion
- Useful unverified observations may create CANDIDATE learning.
- Promotion to ACTIVE/VERIFIED/LEARNED requires canonical qualifying evidence.
- Evidence-reference presence alone is not evidence validation.

### EVOLVE succession vs authority
- Successor context carries intelligence/state, not automatic permission.
- Current truth outranks stale handoff text.
- Proposal, implementation, verification, adoption, deployment and production proof are separate states.
