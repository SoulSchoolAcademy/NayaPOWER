33. SUPERSESSION DIRECTION MUST BE
LOCKED
There is one subtle semantic issue worth fixing during Node 6 canonicalization.
Current source uses object-level SUPERSEDES traversal to follow from one selected block
toward another block treated as current.
The natural-language meaning of:

normally implies:
A is newer than B.
So edge direction must be made machine-exact.
Do not leave this to interpretation.
Every directional relationship should have a canonical sentence template.
