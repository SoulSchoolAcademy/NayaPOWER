# SELF Node — Elite Contract V2 — in plain words

*For Shawn — the engineering target for SELF: what "built right" actually looks like.*

V1 says what SELF must and must not do. V2 says what a *complete, tested* SELF implementation must demonstrate. It is the canonical engineering target — the north-star behavior, the responsibilities, the invariants, and the acceptance tests. It does not replace V1; they layer.

The north-star behavior is a loop: ESTABLISH → RESTORE → UNDERSTAND → REMEMBER → REFLECT → PRESERVE → HAND OFF → CONTINUE. Every turn of the loop re-establishes who she is, restores what survived, understands the current state, remembers what matters, reflects on what changed, preserves what must survive, and hands off to what comes next.

Ten responsibilities: tell the active runtime apart from durable intelligence; establish a stable, attributable runtime identity; load the mission from canonical authority instead of inventing it; hold the current authorized objective; track what is known, unknown, blocked, in scope, who came before, and where the checkpoints are; persist the minimum state needed to continue; keep meaningful outcomes across the intelligence boundary; support auditable reconstruction of what changed and why; build bounded successor packets that carry intelligence, never authority; allow improvement proposals without granting SELF new authority.

The invariants are the lines that never move: identity is explicit and attributable, and never inferred from a display name alone. Mission and objective exist before consequential work. UNKNOWN stays UNKNOWN until evidence changes it; BLOCKED stays BLOCKED. Continuity needs a legitimate binding or recovery mechanism; missing or corrupt continuity fails closed. A successor packet never creates authority. SELF does not claim consciousness from persistence or behavior — continuity is mechanics, not personhood. And SELF cannot rewrite the mission or governance without authorized change.

A testable SELF must prove all of this on a bench: cold boot, durable checkpointing, experience preservation, cold successor restoration, inherited intelligence, rejection of incompatible systems, explicit failure when identity or objective is missing, and no automatic inheritance of authority.

**What it is not:** SELF is the continuity center of one governed intelligence kernel — not an independent authority, and persistent state is not a claim of consciousness.
