# PROVE Node Contract V1

**ID:** NAYA-KERNEL-PROVE

## Purpose
PROVE determines what can legitimately be claimed and why.

## MUST
- Track provenance.
- Track evidence strength.
- Track epistemic state.
- Preserve conflicts and uncertainty.
- Prevent claim strength from exceeding evidence strength.

## MUST NOT
- Convert assertion into proof.
- Treat a receipt as proof of desired outcome.

## Emits
Evidence context, provenance, truth state, uncertainty and proof status.
