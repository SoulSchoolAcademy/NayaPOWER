## Essence

NayaPOWER is the magic of compounding intelligence made usable: many minds, human and AI, working from one evolving brain so that every conversation, lesson, and decision makes the whole system — and everyone connected to it — stronger than any of them alone (SN-NET-POWER-MAGIC-001). That claim has an archaeology: the Recovery Thesis holds that NayaPOWER was designed, not invented — its architecture was recovered from the archive of what the work itself had already discovered, which is why the archive is the design's source of truth and recovery, not invention, is the honest verb for how its shape was found (SN-0609). The layer model keeps the categories straight: NayaPOWER is not the AI model and not the compute provider — it is the governed, human-owned intelligence and continuity layer that lets many authorized AI engines work from one brain; the human remains owner and director, the AIs are interchangeable engines and interfaces, and NayaNET connects that intelligence without requiring the human to surrender continuity to any single provider (SN-0617). Before declaring an ethical, ownership, portability, or governance hole, a reviewer must distinguish the layers — HUMAN OWNER, NAYAPOWER INTELLIGENCE LAYER, NAYANET NETWORK, AI ENGINE/PROVIDER, ECONOMIC LAYER — because most apparent holes are category errors: DO NO HARM and human agency are already constitutional foundations, identity-private governed wisdom-sharing is the intended model, and the 50% referral-revenue share is an intention to preserve, not an implemented claim to upgrade without evidence (SN-0617).

What the citizen is meant to experience is the opposite of the machinery. "They're not going to understand it, but it's going to feel like magic to them": magic is deep complexity hidden behind perfect simplicity — visual bliss, push-button simple, everything alive, Naya just knowing — and understanding is not required for value, so the presentation layer never exposes the machinery: no architecture diagrams in user surfaces, no scores or "powered by" labels, no advanced toggles by default, and every Intelligent Block must pass the grandmother test of wonder without explanation; the builder's pride lives in the repo, the user's delight lives in the product, and mixing the two audiences is a defect class (SN-0743). The economics follow the experience, never lead it: AI creates income not by replacing human value but by amplifying human capability so dramatically that new income streams emerge as a byproduct of using something wonderful — so positioning never leads with "make money with AI" or the displacement frame, always with capability and experience, and the answer to the jobs question is the reframe: NayaNET doesn't do your job — it makes you capable of jobs that didn't exist (SN-0744).

## Intelligence views

**Human:** There is one brain that belongs to you — your intelligence, your continuity — and any AI can plug into it and work from it, instead of every AI owning a separate piece of you. What you experience is simple and beautiful, almost magical, because all the complicated machinery stays hidden. And as you do extraordinary things with it, new ways to earn appear on their own — the money follows the wonder, not the other way around.

**Child:** Imagine a magic brain that all your robot friends can share, so they all remember the same stories and get smarter together — but the brain belongs to YOU, not to any robot. When you use it, it feels like magic: everything is easy and pretty, and you never have to see the messy wires inside. And doing amazing things with it helps people in real life too.

**Grandma:** Think of it as your family's collected wisdom, written down in one place that you own, dear. Any helper — human or machine — can read from the same book, so nobody starts from scratch and nothing about you is lost when a helper changes. What you see is just a lovely, simple thing that works; all the complicated workings are kept backstage where they belong. And because it helps people do remarkable things, good things follow for them naturally.
