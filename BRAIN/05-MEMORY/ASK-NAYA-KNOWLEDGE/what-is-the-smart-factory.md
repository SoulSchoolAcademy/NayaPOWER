# What is the Smart Factory?

**ID:** what-is-smart-factory
**Keywords:** smart factory, what is the smart factory, factory, build app, app builder, how are apps built
**Sources:** NayaNET Knowledge · Smart Factory

## Answer

The Smart Factory is where Naya apps get built. It holds 152 real pieces — buttons, boards, jewels, navigation, inputs, all of it — and assembles them into a working app from your description or a template. You pick a template, make it yours, hit Build, and your app goes live on its own address. This isn't a mockup generator — the Build button does a real build and deploys a real app.

## Key points

- 152 real pieces, composed — never invented
- 12 ready-made templates to start from
- Real builds that deploy to real live URLs
- Describe what you want, or pick and customize
