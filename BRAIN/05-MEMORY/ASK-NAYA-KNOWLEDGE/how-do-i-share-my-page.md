# How do I share my page with others?

**ID:** how-do-i-share-my-page
**Keywords:** how do i share my page, share my page, share, send my page, invite people, share link
**Sources:** NayaNET Knowledge · Sharing

## Answer

Your page is private until you say otherwise. When you're ready, share its link — anyone with the link sees what you've chosen to make public. Your private notes, lists, and spaces stay private; only what you marked public goes out. Sharing is a choice you make per page — not a switch that exposes everything at once.

## Key points

- Private until you decide to share
- Share with a simple link
- Only your public-marked content goes out
