# What is Smart Note?

**ID:** faq-feat-01
**Keywords:** smart, tell me about smart note, what is smart note, smart note
**Sources:** NayaNET Knowledge · Smart Note

## Answer

A Smart Note captures something worth remembering and turns it into intelligence that can be understood, connected, retrieved, learned from, and used again. A normal note helps you remember something. A Smart Note helps the system remember something intelligently. You have ideas all day. You learn things. You make decisions. You discover patterns. Someone teaches you something. You solve a problem. You have an insight in the shower. You realize something about yourself, your work, another person, or the world. Most of that disappears.
