# What is Smart Lists?

**ID:** faq-feat-05
**Keywords:** tell me about smart lists, what is smart lists, lists, smart lists, smart
**Sources:** NayaNET Knowledge · Smart Lists

## Answer

Smart Lists turn collections of information into intelligent, useful structures that can be organized, understood, updated, and acted upon.  A normal list tells you what is there.  A Smart List can help you understand what matters, what changed, what belongs together, and what should happen next. People naturally make lists.  Things to do.  People to contact. Ideas to explore.  Projects to finish.  Questions to answer.  Things to remember.  But ordinary lists eventually become clutter.  A Smart List is designed to remain useful as the information around it changes.
