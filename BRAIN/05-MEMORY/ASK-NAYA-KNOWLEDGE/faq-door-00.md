# What is Smart Connect?

**ID:** faq-door-00
**Keywords:** smart, what is smart connect, tell me about smart connect, connect, smart connect
**Sources:** NayaNET Knowledge · Smart Connect

## Answer

**Smart Connect is where Naya meets the outside world.**  NayaPOWER is the intelligence layer. Smart Connect gives that intelligence governed doors into other systems, tools, AIs, data, and applications.  A connection does **not** automatically give Naya permission to act.  **CONNECTED ≠ AUTHORIZED**  The door exposes a capability. **LAW decides what may happen.** **ACT performs it.** **VERIFY checks what happened.** ⭐ **GitHub Connect** — Connect Naya to your code and projects. *Builders and everyday Naya users.*  **MCP Connect** — Connect Naya to tools. *Users and developers.*  **AI Connect*
