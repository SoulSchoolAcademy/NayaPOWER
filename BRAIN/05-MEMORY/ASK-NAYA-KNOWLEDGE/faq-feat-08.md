# What is Smart Spaces?

**ID:** faq-feat-08
**Keywords:** what is smart spaces, smart spaces, tell me about smart spaces, spaces, smart
**Sources:** NayaNET Knowledge · Smart Spaces

## Answer

Smart Spaces create focused environments where people, intelligence, tools, conversations, knowledge, activity, and goals can come together around a shared purpose.  A normal workspace gives you somewhere to work.  A Smart Space gives the work context and intelligence. You already create Spaces naturally.  Your company is a Space.  Your family project is a Space.  A creative project is a Space.  A community is a Space.  A research effort is a Space.  A Space exists because something matters enough to organize people and intelligence around it.  NayaNET makes that organization intelligent.
