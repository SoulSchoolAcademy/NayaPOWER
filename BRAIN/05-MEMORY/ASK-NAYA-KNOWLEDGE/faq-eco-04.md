# What is 5-Day Challenge?

**ID:** faq-eco-04
**Keywords:** challenge, 5-day, 5-day challenge, what is 5-day challenge, tell me about 5-day challenge
**Sources:** NayaNET Knowledge · 5-Day Challenge

## Answer

**Five days. Free. Your guided start into NayaNET.** The 5-Day Challenge walks you step by step from “what is this?” to actually living in the system. Each day builds on the last. You discover the feed, capture your first Smart Notes, explore the rooms, connect your first door, and feel the system start to remember you.  By day five, you're not reading about NayaNET anymore — you're using it.  The challenge itself is free. After that, membership continues at $14/month, with quarterly, yearly, and lifetime options.
