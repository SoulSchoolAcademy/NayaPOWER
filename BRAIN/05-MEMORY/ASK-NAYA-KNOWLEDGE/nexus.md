**ID:** nexus
**Keywords:** nexus, human nexus, collective intelligence
**Sources:** Codex · Part VIII — The Human Nexus; Codex · Part XXVI — Grand Thesis; SN-0306 · Complete Codex

## Answer

The Human Nexus is the meeting point — where human and artificial intelligence increase each other's capability. Not human versus machine. Not machine replacing human. Maximum meaningful connection.
