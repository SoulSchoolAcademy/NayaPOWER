# What is AI Connect?

**ID:** faq-door-03
**Keywords:** tell me about ai connect, what is ai connect, connect, ai connect
**Sources:** NayaNET Knowledge · AI Connect

## Answer

AI Connect allows NayaPOWER to communicate with other AI systems or agents. This is the door for **AI-to-AI collaboration**.  It matters because NayaPOWER is not intended to make every AI disappear. Instead:  **Different AIs can become participants in one governed intelligence ecosystem.** Today you might use:  • ChatGPT • Claude • Gemini • a coding agent • a specialized research agent • a local AI • another Naya  Each one can wake up with different context. AI Connect is part of the mechanism for allowing intelligence and tasks to move between them under governance.
