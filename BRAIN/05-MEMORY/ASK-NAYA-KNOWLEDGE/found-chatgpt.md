# How is Naya different from ChatGPT?

**ID:** found-chatgpt
**Keywords:** chatgpt, different from chatgpt, vs chatgpt, versus, compare, difference
**Sources:** NayaNET Knowledge · Comparison

## Answer

ChatGPT is a brilliant conversationalist, but it doesn't remember you between chats, it doesn't connect your knowledge together, and it doesn't learn from your experience. Naya is built differently. Naya remembers what matters, connects ideas across time, learns from how you work, and proves what it claims with evidence. ChatGPT answers. Naya compounds.
