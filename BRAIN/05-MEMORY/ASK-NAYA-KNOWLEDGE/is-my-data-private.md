# Is my data private?

**ID:** is-my-data-private
**Keywords:** is my data private, privacy, private, who sees my data, data privacy, is my stuff private
**Sources:** NayaNET Knowledge · Trust

## Answer

Yes — private by default. What you capture and create is yours alone until you choose otherwise. Sharing is always your call: keep it private, share with people you choose, or contribute to the collective by consent. Naya never shares your private things behind your back. That's the law of the system, not a setting you have to hunt for.

## Key points

- Private by default — no action needed
- Sharing is opt-in, always your choice
- Nothing shared silently, ever
