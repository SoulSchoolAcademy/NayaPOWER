# How do I build my own app?

**ID:** how-do-i-build-my-own-app
**Keywords:** how do i build my own app, build my own app, create an app, make a page, build a page, create a page
**Sources:** NayaNET Knowledge · Smart Factory

## Answer

Head to the Smart Factory. Pick one of the 12 templates — personal page, business, booking, product, portfolio, event, menu, community, signup, dashboard, or a full smart hub — then make it yours: your words, your pictures, your colors. Hit Build and the factory assembles a real working app and puts it live. If you can describe it, Naya can build it.

## Key points

- 12 templates covering the most common needs
- Customize with your own content and style
- The Build button deploys a real live app
