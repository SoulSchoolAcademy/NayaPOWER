# What is Intelligent Reports?

**ID:** faq-feat-04
**Keywords:** intelligent, reports, what is intelligent reports, tell me about intelligent reports, intelligent reports
**Sources:** NayaNET Knowledge · Intelligent Reports

## Answer

Intelligent Reports transform accumulated information, activity, evidence, learning, and patterns into something a human can actually understand and act upon. A report should not simply tell you what happened. It should help you understand what matters, why it matters, what was learned, what is proven, what remains unknown, and what should happen next. Nobody wants to read a pile of data and figure everything out themselves. You want to know: What happened? What matters? What changed? What did we learn? What should I pay attention to?
