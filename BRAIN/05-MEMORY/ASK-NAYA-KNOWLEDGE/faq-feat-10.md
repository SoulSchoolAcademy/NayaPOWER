# What is Collective Chain Technology?

**ID:** faq-feat-10
**Keywords:** chain, what is collective chain technology, collective chain technology, technology, collective, tell me about collective chain technology
**Sources:** NayaNET Knowledge · Collective Chain Technology

## Answer

Collective Chain Technology connects people, intelligence, actions, relationships, evidence, and learning so that knowledge can move through a network and become more useful over time.  It is the connective mechanism that allows many intelligent nodes to participate in one evolving intelligence system. Human beings have always been connected.  One person discovers something.  Another person learns it.  Someone else improves it.  Another person applies it somewhere completely different.  Civilization itself is a giant chain of shared learning.
