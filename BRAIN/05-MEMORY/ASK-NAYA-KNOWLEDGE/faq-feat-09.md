# What is Smart Ledger?

**ID:** faq-feat-09
**Keywords:** smart ledger, tell me about smart ledger, ledger, what is smart ledger, smart
**Sources:** NayaNET Knowledge · Smart Ledger

## Answer

Smart Ledger provides a trustworthy record of important actions, decisions, authorization, outcomes, and evidence so people can understand what happened, what was allowed, who or what was responsible, and what can actually be trusted.  Intelligence without accountability is dangerous. Imagine someone tells you:  “It happened.”  Your next question should be: “How do you know?”  That's the beginning of trust.  The Smart Ledger helps answer:  What happened? When? Why? Under whose authority? What evidence exists? What was actually verified?
