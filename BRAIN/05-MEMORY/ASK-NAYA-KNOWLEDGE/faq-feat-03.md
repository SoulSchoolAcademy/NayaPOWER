# What is Adaptive Learning?

**ID:** faq-feat-03
**Keywords:** learning, tell me about adaptive learning, adaptive learning, adaptive, what is adaptive learning
**Sources:** NayaNET Knowledge · Adaptive Learning

## Answer

Adaptive Learning is the ability of the intelligence system to learn from experience, feedback, outcomes, and verified evidence — and use that learning to improve what happens next. It is the bridge between what happened and what we do differently because of it. You learn when reality teaches you something. You try something. It works. You understand why. You try again. Or it fails. You figure out what went wrong. You change your approach. That change is learning.
