# What is the Zero Point?

**ID:** zeropoint
**Keywords:** zero point, pause, sovereignty, stillness, breathe
**Sources:** Codex · Part IV — The Zero Point; SN-0306 · Complete Codex

## Answer

The Zero Point is the pause between stimulus and response — the smallest unit of human sovereignty. In that space, agency lives, and intelligence can enter.
