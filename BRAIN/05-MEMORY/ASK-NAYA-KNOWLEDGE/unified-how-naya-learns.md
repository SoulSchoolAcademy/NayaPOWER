# How Naya Learns

**ID:** unified-how-naya-learns
**Keywords:** how does naya learn, naya learn, how naya learns
**Sources:** NayaNET Knowledge · How Naya Learns

## Answer

Naya learns by capturing intelligence, connecting it to what she already knows, and proving what works through real evidence. Every interaction teaches her something. Every Smart Note adds to her understanding. Every verified result makes her more capable. This is the compounding loop: capture, connect, verify, learn, improve — forever.
