# What can I connect to NayaNET?

**ID:** what-can-i-connect-to-nayanet
**Keywords:** what can i connect to nayanet, connect my services, integrations, connect google, what connects, hook up
**Sources:** NayaNET Knowledge · Connections

## Answer

Your world: GitHub through the NayaNET app, your Google services, your data, your pages and apps. Every connection is opt-in — nothing connects without your explicit permission, ever. Once connected, your Naya apps stay alive with your real information instead of going stale. One person, one connected smart world, as many pages as you like.

## Key points

- GitHub, Google services, your own data and pages
- Always opt-in — never automatic
- Keeps your apps alive with real information
