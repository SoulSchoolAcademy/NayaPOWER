# What is a personal smart app?

**ID:** what-is-personal-smart-app
**Keywords:** personal app, personal smart app, what is a personal app, my page, personal page, what is my page
**Sources:** NayaNET Knowledge · Personal Apps

## Answer

A personal smart app is your world in one page — not a website, a living app. About you, your smart notes, your connections, your lists, your spaces — all working, all in one place. It's born for the phone: installable, full-screen, always with you. And it's yours — you choose what's public and what stays private. The personal page is the flagship: everything NayaNET can do, in miniature.

## Key points

- Not a website — a mini hub portal that's alive
- Your notes, connections, lists and spaces all working in one place
- Mobile-first and installable, no app store needed
- Private by default, public by your choice
