# What is SDK Connect?

**ID:** faq-door-05
**Keywords:** tell me about sdk connect, sdk connect, connect, what is sdk connect
**Sources:** NayaNET Knowledge · SDK Connect

## Answer

SDK Connect is different from MCP. This is the door for **developers and builders who want to put NayaPOWER intelligence inside their own application.**  The simplest distinction is:  **MCP brings tools to Naya.** **SDK brings NayaPOWER to your application.** Imagine you build:  • a SaaS application • a mobile app • an internal business platform • a robotics system • an education platform • a productivity application • another AI product  You could want that application to use NayaPOWER's governed intelligence capabilities. SDK Connect is the developer-facing pathway for doing that.
