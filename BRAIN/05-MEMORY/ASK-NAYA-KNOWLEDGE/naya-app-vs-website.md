# What is the difference between a Naya app and a website?

**ID:** naya-app-vs-website
**Keywords:** difference between naya app and website, naya app vs website, why not a website, website vs app
**Sources:** NayaNET Knowledge · Philosophy

## Answer

A website sits there. A Naya app works — it's a mini hub portal that's alive: it remembers what you teach it, it connects to your world, it notifies you, it learns. Your smart notes flow into it, your connections live in it, it talks to your other apps. Websites are pages you visit. Naya apps are places you live — active, smart, and intelligent, built for the phone first.

## Key points

- Websites are static pages; Naya apps are alive and working
- They remember, connect, notify, and learn
- Phone-first, installable, always with you
