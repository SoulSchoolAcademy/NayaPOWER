# Can I use NayaNET on my phone?

**ID:** can-i-use-nayanet-on-my-phone
**Keywords:** can i use nayanet on my phone, phone, mobile, install the app, pwa, iphone, android, app store
**Sources:** NayaNET Knowledge · Mobile

## Answer

Yes — every Naya app is born for the phone. Open it in your mobile browser and add it to your home screen: it installs like an app, opens full-screen, and works like one. No app store needed. Your personal page, the Hub, Ask Naya — all of it fits in your pocket.

## Key points

- Mobile-first by design, not an afterthought
- Add to home screen — no app store
- Full-screen app experience on any phone
