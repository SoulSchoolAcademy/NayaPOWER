# What is NayaNET?

**ID:** found-nayanet
**Keywords:** nayanet, naya net, what is nayanet, what is naya net
**Sources:** NayaNET Knowledge · NayaNET

## Answer

NayaNET is the human interface to a living intelligence. It's not a dashboard, not a database, not a chatbot. It's a network where human minds and AI minds connect, share intelligence, and grow more capable together.
