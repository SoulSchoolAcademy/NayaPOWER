## Essence

**The fairness lie detector (D26).** The verification layer was built around three deliberately broken schedulers — one that starves work quietly, one that starves flickering work, one that looks busy without accomplishing anything — and the checker caught all three, **each by its right name**: weak-fairness violation, strong-fairness-only violation, progress-on-service violation; the broken link is named (opportunity, fair service, or verified progress), never just "fail." It also caught three real bugs in its own test setup along the way — a verifier that finds flaws in its own harness is demonstrating the property it exists to prove. The stack is reported as nine layers deep, 390 tests green, every layer proving the layer below. And the checker's verdict discipline is the doctrine: it **never says "all good"** — verdicts are scoped and precise (what was tested, under which assumptions, what wasn't covered), so a scoped test can never masquerade as a production guarantee. Status, kept exact: this is the source lane's build report (390 tests green *per the source*); independent verification of the verification layer had **not** been performed by our lanes at capture. Recorded as reported, not as proven — that distinction is itself the doctrine working (SN-905).

**The assumption audit (D28, FAAP).** A fairness proof is only as honest as the assumptions underneath it, and assumptions are where schedulers go to hide. Every assumption must pass **four gates, all required, no compensation**: (1) **independent justification** — proven from evidence independent of the proof it enables; (2) **satisfiability** — it can actually hold, a witness exists, it isn't vacuous; (3) **minimal sufficiency** — assumption-removal minimization yields the inclusion-minimal sufficient set, no assumption rides along unneeded; (4) **non-circularity** — provenance-cycle audit plus adversarial scheduler substitution: M_unfair ∧ A ∧ ¬F must remain *possible*, otherwise the assumptions force the result. The killer test: if a deliberately unfair scheduler can be made to look fair by strengthening assumptions, the audit rejects the qualification. The sharpest case: assume "the task eventually stays continuously eligible" and the intermittent-starvation trace vanishes — not because the scheduler was fixed, but because the test was rigged; remove the assumption and the counterexample returns, and now you fix the *scheduler*. Qualifications stay conditional and versioned — "strong fairness established for scheduler S, obligation class O, environment E, assumption set A, under model abstraction M" — never a bare PASS; UNKNOWN stays UNKNOWN. Status at capture: NOT STARTED; first experiment defined; owner TBD (SN-906).

**Responsibility follows control (D29).** Every failure is assigned to the component that actually controls it — never to whoever shouts first, and a component cannot convert its own failure into another component's assumption. Three sentences carry it: **control determines responsibility; contracts define guarantees; independent evidence establishes what occurred.** Four ownership domains: Environment (external conditions), Scheduler (queue/allocation/dispatch), Worker (execution attempts/recovery), VERIFY (independent assessment); LAW remains the authority boundary, never an assumable convenience. The formal composition: A_E ∧ S ⊨ G_S; A_E ∧ G_S ∧ W ⊨ G_W — and the sharp edge: **G_S can never appear among the scheduler's own assumptions**; a component may not assume its own guarantee. Evidence must come from both sides of every boundary; on conflicting traces the verdict is BOUNDARY_UNDETERMINED — never blame-by-default. The sharpest case: an ACT gets no database connection, and the label DATABASE_UNAVAILABLE tells you nothing — DB down is environment; DB up but never assigned is scheduler; assigned but misused is worker — and the verifier must classify correctly from independent evidence even when the failing component falsely blames another. When ownership legitimately moves, versioned boundary-migration receipts keep old traces interpretable under their contemporary contracts. Status: NOT STARTED; first experiment defined (three injected failures, correct classification despite false blame); owner TBD (SN-907).

**The verifiable causal trace (D30).** Three components can each log a different, sincere, wrong story about one failure. The doctrine in three sentences: **logs describe what components claim happened; evidence establishes what happened; causal verification establishes why the outcome occurred.** Two promotions are forbidden, always: CORRELATES_WITH is never silently promoted to CAUSED_BY, and HAPPENS_BEFORE establishes ordering, not causation. Three layers stay distinct — Observation (independently witnessed) / Causal relationship (verified enabling or prevention) / Responsibility (controller of the failing condition, per D29). The canonical evidence event keeps producer and observer as *separate parties* (a component does not witness itself), logical clocks (Lamport/vector — wall-clock never trusted alone), artifact hashes, attestation, and two honest status fields (observation_status: reported/authenticated/corroborated/verified; causal_status: unassessed/hypothesized/supported/experimentally verified). The ladder: L0 REPORTED → L1 AUTHENTICATED → L2 INDEPENDENTLY_CORROBORATED → L3 CAUSALLY_QUALIFIED. Cause is assigned only after three checks — mechanism, counterfactual, independent reproduction. Negative claims need coverage evidence; without it the verdict is INSUFFICIENT_OBSERVABILITY, never an invented explanation, and competing explanations are preserved until evidence distinguishes them. The label-swap test keeps it honest: swap ENVIRONMENT_BLOCKER ↔ SCHEDULER_STARVATION with the evidence unchanged and the verdict must not move. Status: NOT STARTED; foundations exist on main as specification machinery (fairness.py, progress.py, liveness.py — not wired into live paths) (SN-908).

**Scope-isolated baselines (D50).** Observations, baselines, and qualification receipts bind to explicit operational scopes (provider, endpoint, region, concurrency regime, workload class). Hierarchical information sharing may improve *estimates* but shall never transfer *certification* beyond demonstrated evidence; aggregate improvement shall not conceal local regression. The standing counterexample is Simpson's paradox: the aggregate timeout rate improves 4.6% → 2.6% while *both* regimes actually worsen, because the traffic mix shifted. Sparse cells borrow statistical strength from parents but stay UNPROVEN_IN_SCOPE until they earn their own evidence; promotion of one scope must re-verify its neighbors; cross-scope failures (a cross-region retry creating a duplicate) get explicit interaction boundaries, because every cell can look healthy while the boundary crossing violates the contract. The falsifier that makes it real: AER-CAL-004 — four competing designs against the same replay, where removing the scope-isolation guard changes the verdict (SN-916).

**Pooling is not qualification (D52).** Three operations, never confused — pooling shares *estimates*, merging shares a *forecasting model*, consolidation shares one *certification* — each with its own evidence bar. Statistical pooling shall never create, broaden, inherit, or restore qualification or execution authority; every observation keeps its original provenance; every guarantee stays bound to its demonstrated applicability, checked against real contract semantics, not label matching. A sparse endpoint with 15 observations is not equivalent to one with 10,000 just because a test can't tell them apart. Semantic splits take priority even when traffic is sparse; new workloads start provisional and qualify only the guarantees that actually cover them. The case: AER-CAL-005 — three endpoints (one verified, one sparse but similar, one new with different semantics): share statistics, keep the sparse one's guarantee unqualified (SN-917).

**One canonically ordered governance state (D53).** Taxonomy revisions, evidence withdrawals, qualification changes, baseline promotions, and consequential authorizations are operations over ONE versioned governance state — never per-subsystem local state, because each subsystem can be locally correct while the combined system makes an unsafe decision. The commit sequence is PREPARE → INDEPENDENT VERIFY → LAW → ATOMIC COMMIT → VERIFY→PROVE→PROPAGATE: the commit locks, rereads the governing state, recomputes the affected scope closure, re-verifies the exact qualification/LAW predicates still hold, publishes and appends receipts atomically, and advances the epoch. A version check performed *outside* the committing transaction is not a guarantee. Dangerous races resolve explicitly: withdrawal-before-merge rejects the merge's stale revision; merge-before-withdrawal means the withdrawal immediately invalidates the dependent current claims (the old receipt stays historical, never current); a split after a promotion recomputes applicability per child. Rollback is a new governed revision, never a reused number — no ABA resurrection. Incident containment takes a fast authoritative path: commit the record, withdraw reliance, fence dependents — recalibration follows; the fast path is a governed write, not an alert flag. The boundary predicate: Authorize(a,s) ⇒ CurrentTaxonomy ∧ CurrentQualification ∧ NoDisqualifyingIncident ∧ EvidenceCovers ∧ LAWPermits — a statistical parent is never an acceptable substitute for EvidenceCovers. The honest limit is named: database ordering cannot revoke an external effect already in flight — represent the outcome, reconcile, and limit claims to the actual enforcement boundary. Sharpest case: AER-TAX-001, four simultaneous operations over two scopes. Status: NOT STARTED / owner TBD at registration (SN-918).

**Crash-safe recovery (D54).** A crashed worker loses progress or acknowledgment — it cannot create new authority, erase a withdrawal, or manufacture an external-effect outcome. Recovery reconstructs what actually committed from durable, authoritative commit evidence; it never blindly replays and never assumes a crash means failure. **Classify the crash first** — before commit (discard or revalidate) / during commit (query the authoritative transaction evidence by operation ID — never guess) / after commit before acknowledgment (recover the committed result, never re-apply) / after commit before projection (replay the derived publication from the committed receipt) / during outbox delivery (redeliver idempotently) / across multiple stores (contain and reconcile against the designated authority — a consistency breach, not a delayed dashboard) / during external ACT execution (preserve the ambiguous outcome, reconcile independently — authorization receipts never prove the effect happened). COMMIT_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN is an epistemic state; no timeout ever auto-converts ambiguity to abort. Operation identity is durable: `operation_id` carries a unique constraint bound to the exact proposal hash — a retry with different payload bytes is an identity mismatch and is rejected; recovery runs under a fenced recovery lease, fences the old writer, rechecks authority at commit, and never invents a new proposal. Stale replays are rejected by epoch: an epoch-81 merge event redelivered after an epoch-82 withdrawal must not resurrect the withdrawn qualification. Recovery liveness is a separate fact from recovery correctness — COMMITTED_PROJECTION_BLOCKED is a valid state; a deadline escalates, never promotes an unverified outcome to PASS. The acceptance lab's six scenarios are, kept exact, *synthetic demonstrations, not executed test results*. Status: NOT STARTED / owner TBD (SN-919).

## What this settles

- Verifiers earn trust by naming failures precisely and scoping verdicts; "all good" is a defect in a verifier (SN-905 — reported, not independently verified).
- Fairness assumptions pass four gates or are thrown out — justification, satisfiability, minimal sufficiency, non-circularity, no compensation (SN-906).
- Responsibility follows control; no component assumes its own guarantee; conflicting traces yield BOUNDARY_UNDETERMINED, never blame-by-default (SN-907).
- Logs claim, evidence establishes, verification explains why; correlation and ordering never silently become causation; the ladder is climbed by evidence only (SN-908).
- Aggregates never certify scopes; sparse scopes stay UNPROVEN_IN_SCOPE (SN-916); pooling/merging/consolidation are three operations with three evidence bars, and statistics never confer authority (SN-917).
- Governance changes commit atomically against one ordered state, re-verified inside the transaction; stale state creates no authority; rollback is a new revision (SN-918).
- Recovery reconstructs the commit; ambiguity stays labeled ambiguity; stale replays and invented proposals are refused (SN-919).
- All eight are CANDIDATE designs: SN-905 reported-not-verified; SN-906, SN-907, SN-908, SN-918 and SN-919 NOT STARTED at capture with owners TBD; SN-916 and SN-917 carry no build status on their pages — nothing here is claimed as running machinery.

## What It All Means

The eight designs share one architecture of honesty: every powerful act — certifying fairness, assigning blame, granting authority, recovering state — is decomposed into evidence-bearing steps with named failure states, so that the powerful act can be *audited* rather than merely trusted. The recurring villains are the silent promotions: correlation into causation, estimates into certification, local correctness into system safety, a crash into either amnesia or invention. The recurring hero is the named, scoped, versioned verdict — a claim that carries its conditions on its face.

## Why It Matters

This is the machinery the rest of the system will stand on if it is built: the fairness layer decides whether work is starved, the responsibility layer decides who repairs, the governance state decides what is currently true. Getting these designs right matters precisely because their failure modes are invisible from inside — a rigged assumption, a misattributed failure, a stale qualification all *look* like normal operation. For the reader, even unbuilt, the designs are a checklist of questions for any platform that schedules, judges, or remembers on your behalf — and the essence's status discipline is the model for how to hold designs honestly: valuable, citable, and never dressed as deployments.

## What Is In It For You

You get the design questions that expose rigged systems anywhere: What assumptions does this fairness claim rest on, and who audited *them*? When it fails, who is blamed — the controller, or the loudest narrator? Is there one current ledger of truth, or several local ones that can disagree? And after a crash, does the system reconstruct — or does it improvise? Ask those of any scheduler, platform, or process you depend on. If you build such systems, you also get eight field-ready designs with their sharpest cases and falsifiers already named.
