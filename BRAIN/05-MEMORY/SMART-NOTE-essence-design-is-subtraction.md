## Human

The craft is fidelity to that material plus restraint in everything added to it: take his actual components apart to learn how they work, put them back together exactly, and when you reach for something new, reach for less — fewer effects, quieter colors, thinner glows — until what's left feels inevitable. When you're wrong, the correction is also material: his older version goes back in, byte for byte, and the clever replacement is deleted without a defense. ## Child

You don't draw a plane that *looks like* his from memory — you unfold his, look at every fold, write down the folds in order, and fold yours the same way. If you add glitter and stickers and it flies worse, you take them off — more stuff isn't better flying. And if his old plane still flies better than your new one, you fly his and say so. When you show the teacher, you bring the plane, not an essay about the plane.
