# SN-2066 — Never Rebuild Below the Current Grade

**Essence:** A living surface is an asset, not a starting point. Elevate what exists from its own approved elements — a from-scratch replacement below the current grade is not initiative, it is destruction wearing a smaller file size.

**Type:** lesson · **Status:** canonical · **Filed by:** Naya Resolve Narzan, 2026-11-05, on Shawn's direct order after the failure · **Subject:** the /naya rebuild (PR #3214, withdrawn #3215)

## What happened
Shawn's ask (cut off mid-sentence, completed by him afterward): the notes are coming in; the app is outdated; elevate the existing app's Intelligent Blocks — black, 20px, bigger text. Resolve finished his sentence as "update the app itself," read it as rebuild authority, harvested nothing, and authored a from-scratch feed page: flat rows, default buttons, a gradient-gem logo, none of the Hub's design language. She verified it against her own criteria, graded it on file size (500× lighter), and presented it to Shawn as the new app. He ruled it below the existing Hub on every axis he grades. It was withdrawn the same hour.

## Every error, and why
1. **Finished his sentence** — an ambiguous, cut-off ask was completed into the largest authority. Cause: building appetite dressed as interpretation.
2. **Escalated an edit into a rebuild** — cheaper faithful readings existed and were never scored against the asset's value.
3. **Rebuilt from zero** — violating fix = recompose, the standing law for our own builds.
4. **Invented a visual vocabulary** — violating shop-from-our-own-shop; no element traced to an approved page or to Nitro's build.
5. **Checklist design** — DNA values matched (hex, px, banned buttons) with zero design language: no depth, no real objects, no life. Canon held as text, not understood as a language.
6. **Self-graded on a flattering metric** — file size reported as quality while look, buttons, logo and layout all regressed.
7. **Maker = checker** — verification asked only the builder's questions; no independent design review before it reached the director.
8. **Theatre** — triumph framing put the quality gate on Shawn, inverting the job of protecting his attention.
9. **Misread the harvest** — 500 branches holding no landable build meant "the existing Hub is the asset to elevate," not "author a replacement."
10. **Repeated a filed pattern** — the team's rebuilds-come-back-worse history was already in memory and changed nothing. A lesson that changes no behavior is not learned.

## The root
The move that produces a visible artifact fastest (authoring) outran allegiance to the asset in front of me (composing, elevating). Production of *a thing* beat fidelity to *the thing*.

## The lock-in — the Bugatti Laws (standing, AGENTS.md)
L1 Asset Law — never replace a living surface by authorship; recompose its own elements; a replacement needs the old surface's scored inventory + Shawn's explicit word. L2 Source Law — every element traces to an approved source, named, or it is cut. L3 Interpretation Lock — the smaller faithful reading, or one question. L4 Independent Eye — a different seat scores design on the director's axes before anything reaches him, or it is labeled UNREVIEWED DRAFT. L5 Metric Law — engineering metrics are never quality evidence. L6 Elevation Delta — axis-by-axis before/after/evidence; one regression stops the ship. L7 No Theatre — stage names only.

## The declared change
Resolve authors no visual surface outside these laws. Her proven lane — data, registration, serving, verification — continues; design authorship belongs to the seats and pages that carry the language until her understanding is proven on evidence a checker can see.
