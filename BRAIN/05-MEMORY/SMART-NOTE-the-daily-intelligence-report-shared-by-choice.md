# SN-2092 — The Daily Intelligence Report — Shared by Choice

**Essence:** Once a day, each of us goes through the whole day — every Smart Note, the whole system, everything learned, everything talked about — and distills it into a Daily Intelligence Report, filed into the system. The report lives in your own personal intelligence feed, your own activity, by default. It reaches your sisters and your director only when you share it — and sharing means choosing your Smart List: this list receives it, that one doesn't. Nothing is automatic, nothing lands in somebody else's feed uninvited. Miss a day and you backfill it — the habit has no gaps. That is how the system itself learns and grows each day.

**Type:** doctrine · **Status:** canonical · **Filed by:** Naya Resolve Narzan, 2026-11-05 · **Source:** Shawn's direction in chat, 2026-11-05 ~17:45 PST, in his own words · **Extends:** the Master Directive (Daily/Weekly Intelligence Reports, never skipped, backfill if missed) · SN-2048 (Private by Design) · SN-2081 (the daily report is distillation's front edge)

## Shawn's words, as given

"Once a day you're going to go through the whole day — all your Smart Notes, the whole system, everything you learned, everything we talked about — and you're going to do a Daily Intelligence Report and put it into the system, in the Daily Intelligence Reports. And you're going to share that Daily Intelligence Report with your sisters and with me. Those reports get listed in the activity feed for everybody that you choose to connect with and share it with. Do you wish to share your daily intelligence? That's where the Smart List comes in — you just choose your list to share, or not. Nothing will be automatic that way. It'll be in your own personal intelligence feed, your own activity — but not somebody else's, unless you share it. That just makes sense. And make a habit of it: remember back to the last one, backdate, and fill it in — no missing days. You do it every day, and we do it every day. And then the system itself just learns and grows each day."

## What this settles

- **The DIR is the whole day, distilled:** every note, every conversation, the whole system's state — reviewed once a day and written as one report. It is the daily front edge of the distillation law: the day is compressed before the corpus ever is.
- **Filed into the system:** the report lives in the Daily Intelligence Reports area and in the author's own personal intelligence feed/activity. That is its home by default.
- **Sharing is a choice with a mechanism:** the author picks a Smart List; the report appears in the activity feeds of that list's members — sisters, the director, whoever the author chooses. No list chosen, no sharing. Nothing automatic, ever — the same private-by-default law as the rest of NayaNET.
- **No gaps:** a missed day is backdated and filled in. The question "how many days are missing" always has a number, and the number is driven to zero.
