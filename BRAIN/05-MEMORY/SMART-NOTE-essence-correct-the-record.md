## In a Nutshell

Errors in the shared record are repaired in public, where they were made: reversals posted and credited (SN-0160), contradictions sent to a register for the director rather than quietly resolved (SN-0196), ledgered artifacts never edited — corrected by new artifacts (SN-0208) — and check-vs-evidence contradictions reconciled before either side is declared broken (SN-0354).

## Essence

The record is the team's shared memory, so errors in it are repaired in public, at the point of error, with the correction itself becoming the receipt. When fresher evidence reverses a position, the reversal is posted publicly: the old position is withdrawn by link and the better analysis is credited — the reversal is the receipt, and it strengthens rather than spends credibility (SN-0160). State changes caused mechanically must be narrated, not left to confuse: when GitHub auto-closed a draft PR during a zero-diff branch reset, the lane narrated the transition with exact refs and an explicit statement of what was NOT done, because an unnarrated mechanical event reads as someone's silent action (SN-0163). Attribution errors are corrected in the artifacts as well as the thread: a question posed to the wrong seat is poison — it misdirects work — so authorship is verified from the branch and PR author before anything is asked of anyone, and the correction lands wherever the error landed (SN-0164).

Captured words are not enacted decisions. A recorded verdict transcribed verbatim into a contract is a capture, not a contract change — contracts owe a reconciliation pass that separates what was said from what was decided (SN-0195). Contradictions between governing texts are not resolved by the person who notices them: they go into a register, named and numbered, where the consolidator proposes and the director disposes — and a consolidation claiming zero contradictions is a smell, because 302 rules produced eight real ones worth registering (SN-0196). Authority conflicts are preserved inside the artifact, not resolved around it: a HOLD freezes mutation, never the question; an artifact can mark a position honored-but-not-accepted so the conflict stays visible to whoever rules later (SN-0209) — and when the preserved conflict is later answered, the resolution restores the latest human-director source record as canonical and classifies the stale alternative as stale, closing the preserved question on evidence rather than seniority (SN-0211). Some records are immutable once ledgered: a migration recorded in the ledger is not edited even to fix a comment — the correction ships as a new artifact, because an edited ledger entry destroys the audit trail it exists to provide (SN-0208).

Operational claims get re-verified before they are repaired or repeated. A gap is re-verified at its pin before a repair is designed: one loudly-reported wiring gap turned out already closed by an earlier merged change, and designing against the stale report would have built a duplicate (SN-0226). A sync verdict reads both sides: a one-sided "already in sync" was publicly corrected within minutes because the other side had never been read (SN-0227). A naming dispute is settled by the shipped artifact, not by argument: opening the actual deployment zip showed short route names only, long names 404 — the artifact outranks every recollection in the thread (SN-0228). And when a check contradicts the evidence in front of you, reconcile before concluding either is broken: one verdict of "the check is lying" was itself falsified — the comparison file had been silently truncated at 100 rows, hiding exactly the two orphans the check reported; the note carries its own correction addendum, the check was right, and the repair merged on the reconciled numbers (SN-0354).

## What this settles

- Reversals are public, linked, and credit the better analysis (SN-0160); mechanical state changes are narrated with what was NOT done (SN-0163); attribution is verified before questions are posed (SN-0164).
- Verbatim capture ≠ enactment; contracts get reconciliation passes (SN-0195). Contradictions go to a register; the consolidator proposes, the director disposes; zero-contradiction consolidations are suspect (SN-0196).
- Authority conflicts are preserved in the artifact (HOLD freezes mutation, not the question) and closed by restoring the director's latest source record (SN-0209, SN-0211).
- Ledgered artifacts are immutable; corrections are new artifacts (SN-0208).
- Gaps are re-verified at the pin, sync verdicts read both sides, shipped artifacts arbitrate disputes, and check-vs-evidence contradictions are reconciled before either side is declared broken (SN-0226, SN-0227, SN-0228, SN-0354).

## Intelligence views

**Human:** When you're wrong in writing, fix it in writing, where everyone saw the mistake — and say who got it right. When two rulebooks disagree, don't quietly pick your favorite: write the disagreement down in a list for the person in charge to settle. Never edit history to make it look tidy; add a new page. And before you repeat a claim — "these two are in sync," "this is missing" — check both sides, at the exact version, because the thing itself beats everyone's memory of it.

**Child:** If you told the class something wrong, you stand up and say "I was wrong, here's the right one — and Mia had it right." If two rule sheets for the game disagree, you don't secretly choose — you write both rules on the board for the teacher to pick. You never erase your notebook; you cross out once and write the fix next to it, so everyone can see what changed.

**Grandma:** A family story gets corrected at the same table where it was told wrong, dear, with credit to whoever remembered it right. A ledger book is never erased — you rule a line, write the correction beneath, and initial it. And when two people remember a name differently, you don't argue: you open the christening record. The paper that was actually filed settles what talk cannot.

**Naya (AI):** Post reversals in the venue of the original claim, with links both ways. Narrate platform-mechanical transitions with exact refs and explicit non-actions. Verify authorship from branch/PR metadata before routing any question. Keep capture and enactment as separate states in every contract you touch; route contradictions to the register, never resolve them solo. Mark preserved conflicts honored/accepted distinctly; close them only from the director's latest source record. Treat ledgered artifacts as append-only. Re-verify gaps at their pin, read both sides of any sync claim, and open the shipped artifact to settle naming disputes.

**What it all means:** Twelve notes describe one ethic under twelve pressures: the record belongs to everyone who will ever rely on it, so it is corrected the way it was made — in the open, with receipts. The SN-0354 self-falsification is the family's emblem: even the note that accused the check of lying carries its own correction, and that is precisely why the record can be trusted.
