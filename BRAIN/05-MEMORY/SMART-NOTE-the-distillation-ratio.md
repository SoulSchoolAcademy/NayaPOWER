# SN-2081 — The Distillation Ratio — One Hundred Into Ten

**Essence:** A thousand Smart Notes is a problem, not an achievement — intelligence nobody has distilled is intelligence undigested. The law: once the corpus passes 100 notes, distill that hundred into 10, over and over — minimize to 10% (150 → 15, 500 → 50, 1,000 → 100 at most), and fewer whenever everything needed still survives. When an essence verifies against its sources, the raw notes it covers retire automatically. The numbers that matter are essences standing and compression achieved — never raw notes filed.

**Type:** law · **Status:** canonical · **Filed by:** Naya Resolve Narzan, 2026-11-05 · **Source:** Shawn's correction and ruling in chat, 2026-11-05 17:09–17:11 PST · **Amends:** SN-2058 (deletion sequencing) · **Companions:** SN-2068–2074, The Seven Essences

## Shawn's words, as given

"I keep mentioning this 1119 notes — we should have been distilling those notes and getting rid of them. There shouldn't be that many. If there's ever a thousand notes, that's a problem. I would say once we get anywhere close to 500 — even that's a lot. Just make it simple: once there's 100 plus smart notes, you just try to distill those 100 into 10, over and over again. Whatever you do, just minimize it to 10% — if it's 150, get it down to 15; if it's 500, get it down to 50; and if you have 1000, you get down to 100 minimum. And if you can do it in less and still get everything you need, you do it."

And his ruling on retirement, chosen at the named choice: **"Distill now — retire the raw notes automatically once each essence verifies."**

## What this settles

- **The ratio is law:** 10 to 1, applied repeatedly, corpus-wide. The ceiling at any corpus size is 10% standing; the target is always fewer when nothing needed is lost.
- **The count is not the score.** Reporting a growing raw count as progress is a defect — the count growing means the distillation job is not being done. Reports carry: essences standing, active notes, compression achieved.
- **Retirement is automatic on verification.** No per-batch tap, no end-of-program wait: an essence verified by a seat that did not make it — checked against its sources — retires the raw notes it covers in the same pass (registry lifecycle → RETIRED naming the essence; feed cards out; the essence becomes the living form).
- **The program shape stands:** batches by theme under the distillation coordinator's Scale Plan (extend an existing essence before creating a new one; the Seven Essences are Batch 1). The corpus at this ruling: 1,119 raw notes → the standing body must come to ~112 or fewer.
