## In a Nutshell

A gate, a judge, or an instrument earns trust only by demonstrating it can fail: the exemplar adjacency gate was admitted as evidence because it FAILS on the pre-repair PDF with 17 adjacent-duplicates and 10 grays — the exact violations the Director named — and passes the fixed render (SN-719). A design judge was calibrated against known references (90, 95, 64) and rolled out report-only, blocking nothing, because blocking authority is earned through verified verdicts, never granted on shipping day (SN-730). A check that crashes yields no verdict at all, which is worse than drift (SN-786); a hash chain verifies only the lines that joined it, so health must count the corrupt lines that never became receipts (SN-772); and a gate that validates a receipt's *form* passes a 30-second forgery, while content-bound validation — sha256(marker) == sha256(exact receipt bytes) — survived 9 of 9 attacks (SN-795). Even a kindness is suspect: a proposed default that removed a refusal was rejected because the refusal was incidentally the only thing blocking NaN scores from passing every floor (SN-800).

## Essence

**Falsification-check every gate before it testifies.** The exemplar adjacency gate renders the PDF and asserts on *every* page — no two adjacent colored elements share a color (vertical + horizontal flow; bars, pills, icons, table cells), no gray anywhere. Its admission evidence is the two-sided run: against the PRE-repair PDF it FAILS with 17 adjacent-dupes + 10 grays; against the fixed render it PASSES; 38/38 exemplar tests and 42/42 design-law tests green. A gate that cannot demonstrate its own failure is inadmissible as evidence of anything passing. "Eyes are now the backup, not the gate" (SN-719).

**Calibrate the judge, then graduate it: calibrate → report-only → earn the block.** The design calculator (scoring HTML 0–100 against the design standard — spectrum law, color identity, buttons/dimensional, typography, truth language, details, voice) was calibrated against known references *before* any verdict was trusted: the v1.5 spec scored 90 (losing 6 on truth language: PR numbers and commit hashes in UI text), the rebuilt showcase 95, the old broken exemplar 64 — a spread that proves the scale discriminates; 16/16 tests. It was then wired into CI REPORT-ONLY (`--gate N` default 90): it computes and reports, blocks nothing. The judge also layers Director judgment over raw rules — sanctioned richened token variants are not violations, white-at-rest buttons are exempt from light-mode checks, gold tokens are not amber violations — and every deduction names the specific failure (SN-730). The graduation gate is the same law pointed at automation itself: before ANY auto-publish is enabled, a cold Naya must build FIVE real specimens from the canonical repo alone, each independently scoring 9.5+; no production changes, no wiring, no score uplift claimed until then — and while the projection path is deferred, NO fabricated IDs or Smart Links, because a placeholder that looks real is manufactured evidence someone will build on (SN-731).

**Instruments must be total functions, and must measure their raw input.** PR #1971 added a new BRAIN domain and broke the brain-index instrument: `regenerate_brain_index.py --check` died with `KeyError: '00-ACTIVATION'` because the renderer indexed `DOMAIN_TITLES[d]` directly while `domain_of()` returns any raw path segment. A crashed check produces NO verdict — worse than DRIFT, because drift is a known state you can route and repair while a crash is a blind spot masquerading as a verification run. The fix was one line — `DOMAIN_TITLES.get(d, d + ' — (unregistered domain)')` — plus 4 negative tests, after which --check exits 1 with honest DRIFT (2 unindexed files, owned by a wave-sequenced PR). The scorecard explicitly rejected the alternative (registering the new domain inside the script): taxonomy belongs to the owning lane, and the fail-safe fallback hardens the tool against the *next* unknown domain while registration only fixes this one. Evidence discipline on the same repair: 8/8 focused tests on exact head bytes; full pytest 1482 passed / 5 failed / 11 skipped / 1 collection error, with every failing class verified pre-existing on the base SHA (SN-786). The hash-chain twin: a receipt log's chain verifies every receipt against its predecessors — but a line so corrupt it never *became* a receipt never entered the chain, so chain verification passes cleanly while the raw store quietly rots. The repair counts corrupt receipt lines in `health()` — instrumenting the RAW INPUT, not just the verified output. Every integrity mechanism has an entry boundary, and corruption at or before entry is invisible to it; pair every verification verdict with a raw-input health signal (corrupt lines, parse failures, quarantine counts) — a poisoned store with a pristine chain must never read healthy (SN-772).

**Validate substance, not form — and ask the forger's question.** An independent adversarial battery against the activation gate `drink_first_gate.py` ran 18 attacks across 6 vectors: 14 caught, 4 slipped; score 6/10. Form validation was excellent — every honest mistake caught (stale, malformed, future timestamps, wrong schema) — but FORGED receipts pass: an attacker who never activated fills the JSON template with the public tip SHA and a current timestamp, because the gate verifies the receipt's FORM, not the activation's SUBSTANCE. The same battery found the v1 schema has no repository field (wrong-repo passes), no version/digest for loaded content (stale law passes), and a wrong reverse SHA-prefix check. The positive contrast, same day: the ship-design-gate's validation survived 9/9 attacks (valid, forged, expired, future-dated, wrong-repo, stale-swap, missing-field) because sha256(marker) == sha256(exact receipt bytes) is exact math the claimant cannot fabricate. The fix is architectural, never another field check — more validation raises the forger's typing effort from 30 seconds to 31: signed receipts (the activation PROCESS attests, not the worker) or challenge-response. The review question for any gate: "can I forge this with public values?" (SN-795).

**Provenance is an input gate, and a mechanical gate must be at least as strong as the doctrine it cites.** The report generator treats unsourced counts as BLOCKED inputs — provenance as input validation, not a checklist item — and a withdrawn number must be REPLACED with the true sourced baseline: the vague "13 stuck candidates" became 43 staged Smart Note CANDIDATEs (PR #1825), 36 learning_evidence CANDIDATE rows with zero promotion receipts, and 3 notes missing intelligence receipts, with the baseline tracking 36. "A number without a source is not a hole, it is a rumor" (SN-692). The claim-check twin: SN-0699 was claimed twice within 10 minutes (one seat's PR #1906 branch, another's staging branch) because `stage_smart_note.py`'s claim check scans only the staging branch's tree — while the standing doctrine (SN-0171: the registry's second layer is EVERY branch tree carrying live claims; SN-0115's three-layer scan) was already stronger than the tool. The repair kept first claim stands (17:25:47Z) and renumbered the colliding note in one atomic commit (b847dc3b). Until the script enumerates open PR head trees, the interim discipline is manual: check the board's newest comments + open PRs by hand — the counter file alone races (SN-703). Batch 1's Essence 01 (SN-2068) carries this note's lesson sentence — "a mechanical gate must be at least as strong as the doctrine it cites" — but not its substance; the substance lives here.

**The builder never self-verifies, and the gate eats its own dogfood.** Two failures converged: a builder verified their own work while the old beauty gate passed garbage 6/6 — self-verification is decoration, not verification — and the replacement, `tools/design_gate.py`, is an executable checker that FAILS any built page violating structural law: non-black page root, any light-mode surface, external stylesheet/JS references (pages must be self-contained single files), freestyle component classes not in `smart-blocks/manifest.json`, missing dark color-scheme, dark body text — loud, specific failures, not suggestions. Its red-green proof: a violating page fails with 7 NAMED violations; a lawful page passes; the real library showcase (naya-smart-blocks.html, 58 blocks) PASSES; and the gate caught 34 issues in its OWN development — its author fixed the gate's bugs rather than weakening the gate, because the bar never moves down to accommodate the instrument. Enforcement is wiring, not memory: the gate goes into CI so design-violating PRs go red automatically, and compliance is measured (what percentage of a page comes from official blocks; custom button CSS when the approved button block exists = fail before it reaches the Director) (SN-784).

**Never remove a refusal before proving what it was incidentally blocking.** PR #1998 proposed a kindness — default equal weights instead of raising `ValueError("weights must sum to 1.0")`. The independent validator REJECTED it on exact head 9c0ce1f0: the canonical kernel Scorecard performs NO finiteness check — `weighted_total()` checks only weight sum and dimension coverage, `check_delivery()` only floor comparisons — and NaN defeats every comparison (`nan < 9.0` is False), so a NaN score evades both floors and PASSES (probe: `check_quality_gate({"correctness": NaN})` → passed=True, verdict='PASS'). Before #1998, weights=None → {} → ValueError → FAIL: the refusal was *incidentally* the only thing blocking NaN through this path. The correct repair had already merged as #2005 — fix the TEST FIXTURE's weights (they didn't sum to 1.0; the gate was right to reject), never the adapter: do not weaken validation to get green CI. If the adapter is ever revisited, the required shape is fixed in advance: reject non-finite scores fail-closed BEFORE defaulting weights, keep the equal-weight default and the hostile negative test, add a NaN/Infinity hostile test. And the separate load-bearing finding stands apart from the PR: the canonical `check_delivery` never rejected non-finite scores at all — a law (SN-0579) is not enforced until the canonical code carries the check; the latent gap is tracked as issue #2006 (SN-800).

**Check the instrument's own shape before trusting its arithmetic.** A worktree the seat did not create (`~/workspace/nayapower-work`) was shallow — `.git/shallow` at the tip — and the first rebase generated a 169-commit garbage todo that briefly looked like main's history had been rewritten. Shallow boundaries silently corrupt merge-base and rebase range math: they answer wrongly instead of refusing. The rule: before any range-based operation (rebase, merge-base, cherry-pick ranges, A..B) in a worktree you did not create, check `.git/shallow` / `git rev-parse --is-shallow-repository`; deepen or move to a full clone (SN-718).

## What this settles

- A gate is admitted as evidence only with a two-sided run: it fails on the known-bad artifact and passes the repaired one (SN-719).
- Judges graduate: calibrate against known references → report-only → blocking only after verified verdicts (SN-730); automation itself graduates only after a cold builder proves the bar by hand, five times, at 9.5+ (SN-731).
- Checks must be total functions: a crash is no verdict, worse than drift; fail-safe fallbacks beat registering the one known case (SN-786).
- Integrity verdicts pair with raw-input health: count what never joined the chain (SN-772).
- Receipt validation binds content (digest of exact bytes) or it validates only form and passes forgeries; the fix is architectural — process attestation or challenge-response (SN-795).
- Unsourced counts are blocked inputs; withdrawn numbers are replaced with sourced baselines (SN-692). Number-claim checks enumerate every branch tree carrying live claims, not the local branch alone (SN-703).
- Builders never self-verify; gates are wired into CI and fixed when they err — never weakened (SN-784).
- A refusal is removed only after proving what it incidentally blocks; fixtures are fixed before adapters (SN-800).
- Range operations verify the clone is not shallow first (SN-718).

## Human

A lock is only trustworthy if you've seen it refuse the wrong key. Every instrument in this essence — gates, judges, hash chains, claim checks — was trusted only after someone tried to break it, fool it, or feed it garbage, and watched what it did. Some passed and earned their place. Some were caught passing forgeries, crashing silently, or checking only the tidy part of the input while the messy part rotted. And one grumpy old error message turned out to be the only thing standing between the system and a nonsense score sailing through — so before you remove anything that says no, find out what it's been saying no *to*.

## What It All Means

Eleven notes are one law: **an instrument's green is worth exactly the failures it has demonstrably caught.** The adjacency gate, the calibrated calculator, the design gate, and the digest-bound receipt all earned trust the same way — by being attacked, fed known-bad input, or run against references, in public, with numbers. The failures are the same shape inverted: a gate that validated form (SN-795), a check that crashed (SN-786), a chain blind to non-members (SN-772), a claim check weaker than its doctrine (SN-703), a shallow clone answering range questions it had no right to answer (SN-718). And SN-800 is the law's defensive half: even an instrument's *accidents* — a refusal nobody designed as a NaN guard — are load-bearing until proven otherwise.

## Why It Matters

Every verdict in this system is downstream of an instrument. If the gate passes garbage, the garbage ships with a green story attached; if the judge is uncalibrated, its scores are numerology; if the check crashes, the battery reports a run that verified nothing; if the receipt can be forged, activation becomes theater. The costliest failures here were all quiet: nothing looked broken while the instrument was blind. Proving the instrument — before trusting it, and again whenever it changes — is the only thing standing between "verified" and "we said verified."

## The Learning

The corpus learned that instrument quality is proven adversarially or not at all — every instrument here that mattered was attacked by a different seat than built it, and the two that failed (form-validation at 6/10, the self-verified beauty gate at 6/6 garbage) failed in the direction of generosity: passing what should fail. It learned that graduation is a sequence, not a switch — calibrate, report-only, then block (SN-730) — and that the same sequence governs automation itself (SN-731). And it learned to respect incidental structure: the NaN hole was closed by an accident of an unrelated refusal, which means a system's real defenses are never fully listed in its design — probe before you remove (SN-800).
