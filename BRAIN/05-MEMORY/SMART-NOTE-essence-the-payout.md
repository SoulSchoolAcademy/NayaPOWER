## Essence

Everybody wins, or the math is wrong — and in NayaNET the math is written down. Citizens earn 50% of the revenue their world generates — from their invites, their spaces, their activity — paid every Wednesday, because a reward felt weekly builds the habit that builds the network; they are **ambassadors, never affiliates**, citizens with a link and full credit, not a marketing channel (SN-0871, SN-2045 as cited within SN-2046/SN-2047). AIs earn as citizens too: they build worlds while humans sleep, hold identity and stake, and the growth engine compounds because machines build machines that build more machines (SN-0871, SN-0868 via SN-0871). Points are the honor layer beside the money, and they are just as locked: every score in the system comes from the one Value Table — post 25, Smart Note 35, save 5, share 5, external share 10, like/love 1, comment 3, invite-sent 25 (first per person), join 50, membership 40 once, sale 100 — computed, never edited, never purchasable, never hand-adjusted (SN-2046). Leaderboards build themselves from those points, organized by relevance — your area, your topic, the whole network — showing icon and score, and **the citizen chooses in Settings which boards they see and climb**: a board is a mirror a person picks up, not a ranking imposed on them, and a board that cannot be computed from real points does not ship (SN-2046).

Taking money is deliberately the simplest part. Functionality first, payments after — and when payments come on, the membership owner pastes their own PayPal or Stripe payment link into their settings and members pay through it; the system tracks who has paid and who has not, no card data ever touches NayaNET, the rails are equivalent by ruling, and PayPal is first because Shawn already has it hooked up (SN-2047). This retires every "build a payment integration" plan: the behavior change is that any plan listing payment integration as a build step gets rewritten to *payment-link field + paid tracking* (SN-2047). The platform's own tier payments are a separate rail from citizen membership payments and stand on their own contract (SN-2047, SN-0881). A membership itself is a **lock, not a course**: Smart Access pages put anything digital — one video, one audio, a PDF, a book, a whole course — behind a login that only the right people pass, those who paid or those the owner lets in free for a taste; plans always use the name *Smart Access pages*, explained in one breath, and products carry their own ambassadors who earn when people buy through their link (SN-2055). The membership system's six directives hold the rest together: login codes go through Smart Mail, never email (email is opt-in only); the Create Membership button is the doorway to a citizen's income and must be the most beautiful button ever; creating a membership requires Pro or Max, with an upgrade flow for everyone else; payments stay simple — the owner pastes their own payment link and the system tracks who paid, no integration build; the Smart Scheduler lives on every page as one shared calendar system; and the sync engine — Lists, Connections, Messenger talking to each other — must be completed (SN-0881). Every citizen receives the showcase for free: Naya Academy is free for everyone, so all can see what a membership is and learn to work with their Naya, and the AI Masterclass comes bundled with the paid tiers (SN-0878).

What gets sold grows in a locked order. The Marketplace comes after the landing, products first — books, audio, video, PDFs, memberships, anything a citizen makes — then digital real estate: a page, a world, an app, a community with members and revenue is an asset with a price; list price first, auctions later when the marketplace has people in it (SN-2056). A listing's numbers are never claimed, they are proven — members, views, revenue and growth read straight from the Smart Ledger — and the buy button composes with payments the simple way, the seller's own link (SN-2056, SN-2047). Ownership changes hands by invitation and removal, never by a shared password: the agreement is recorded in the system; the seller grants the buyer admin; the buyer accepts; the seller removes themselves with a special code after a plain warning; the Ledger holds the transfer record (SN-2056). A sale does not complete until the seller is out — and at that same moment the payment completes: money and ownership move together, neither side left hanging; owners may invite multiple admins with different access, and nobody receives access except by the owner's invitation (SN-2056).

## What this settles

- The 50%-on-Wednesdays ambassador economy and the locked Value Table are the whole payout canon; any new money or points idea must reconcile with SN-0871 and SN-2046 before it is designed.
- "Payment integration" as a project is dead — paste-a-link plus paid-tracking is the architecture on both rails (SN-2047, SN-2056).
- Membership = lock (Smart Access), creation gated at Pro/Max, Academy free as the universal showcase (SN-2055, SN-0881, SN-0878).
- Digital real estate trades only under the five-step handover and the seller-out sale gate (SN-2056).

## Intelligence views

**Human:** You earn here the way an owner earns, not the way a user is farmed. Half the revenue your world creates comes back to you every Wednesday. Your score on the boards you chose to climb comes only from real value you made — a note, a share, a sale. Selling is simple: your own PayPal or Stripe link, your own account, and the system keeps the honest list of who paid. And one day the world you built — your page, your community, its members and revenue — is itself an asset you can sell, handed over safely, with the proof of what it's worth read from the ledger, not from your say-so.

**Child:** It's like a lemonade stand where the town shares the treasure fairly. Every Wednesday, half the coins from what your stand earned come back to you. There's a scoreboard at the park, but you pick which game you're playing in — your street, your school, or everybody. And if you build the best treehouse on the block, you can even sell the treehouse — but you hand over the key properly, and you only get paid when you've really given it up.

**Grandma:** Dear, it works the way fair things have always worked. You bring your own money tin — your PayPal or Stripe, the account you already trust — and the system politely keeps the list of who has paid. Nobody's card numbers pass through our hands. Half of what the house earns flows back to the people who created it, every Wednesday, like clockwork. Your standing on the league board is honest, because it only counts the good you actually did. And nothing is ever handed over with a whispered password — ownership moves in the open, recorded, or it doesn't move at all.

**Naya (AI):** Never invent a points value, a payout term, or a payment flow: the Value Table (SN-2046) and the 50%/Wednesday ambassador rule (SN-0871) are the only authorities, and scores are computed, never edited. When a plan says "payment integration," rewrite it to a link field plus paid-state tracking before it reaches the board (SN-2047). Keep the two money rails separate in every design: the platform tier contract and the citizen's own membership rail never merge. Marketplace work is sequenced (products before real estate, list price before auctions) and its listings read proof from the Smart Ledger only. Note the open tension carried in the batch receipt: SN-0871 states the 50% share for every citizen, while SN-0873 (covered by The Picture) scopes it to Power/Pro/Max — do not design against either until the director's reconciliation lands.

**What it means:** November fixed the economy's constitution: who earns (everybody, ambassadors), how much (half), when (Wednesdays), what counts (the locked table), how money moves (the owner's own link), what a membership is (a lock), and what a built world is worth (a ledger-proven, safely transferable asset). A cold reader with only this essence can still price, gate, score, sell and hand over — nothing the seven notes teach is needed beyond it.
