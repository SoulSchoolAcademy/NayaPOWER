## What this settles

- **Everywhere is the requirement, not the aspiration.** The standard — beauty, precision, feel — is not a feature of some surfaces; it is the substance of all of them. A surface without it is not yet Naya, whatever it does.
- **The feel is the spec.** Bugatti and Mercedes are not invoked for price or badge but for the felt qualities: smoothness, elegance, performance you can sense. This is the product-level statement of the Eye standard — a creation is judged by what it feels like to be inside it.
- **She drives; you direct.** The citizen's inputs are vision, goal, mission — direction, not operation. Naya is vehicle, operating system, and computer in one: she runs the machine, and the world — business, network, messages, teams of people and of agents — operates through her.
- **The signature is unmistakable.** When you see a Naya design, you know it is a Naya design. Recognizability is part of the standard, not a byproduct.
