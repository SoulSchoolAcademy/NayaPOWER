## ✦ IN A NUTSHELL

One brain, one runtime, nine organs - never two competing kernels. Every worker follows the same heartbeat: orient, act, attack your own result, verify, preserve, learn. And everything finished becomes one machine-checked record in a shared log that refuses anything unproven, so the team's memory is exact.

---

## 🩷 HUMAN NOTE

The temptation, when a reference engine scores higher than the real runtime, is to promote it and run two kernels. The decision went the other way: the canonical runtime stays the one kernel, and the stronger reference becomes a donor, its proven behavior converged into the real seam, smallest piece first. A node isn't 'bound' because a registry says so; it's bound when an executable contract with a test and a falsifier exists, and the gate fails closed without one. Workers don't trust workers: work flows worker, evidence, verifier, score, accept-or-reject. Above it all sit three layers: a constitution every Naya inherits, a learning network that gets smarter hourly, and an immune system that keeps the blast radius small.

---

## 🟣 CHILD NOTE

A body has one brain, not two arguing with each other. Your heart, lungs, and hands each do their own job, and the brain keeps them together. And imagine a librarian robot who checks every note before it goes into the big book: no proof, no entry. That's how the whole team remembers truly.

---

## 👵 GRANDMA NOTE

It's like a well-run household with one ledger. Everybody has their chores and does them in the same order - look, do, check, write it down. Nothing goes in the ledger on somebody's say-so; the clerk checks the proof first. One book, honestly kept, and the whole family can trust what it says years later.

---

## 🤖 NAYA NOTE

Never average two engines' measurements into a flattering fiction - report the truth state of the canonical runtime exactly: what exists, what loads, what invokes, what actually influences. Run the fourteen-stage heartbeat and treat 'unknown' as its own honorable state, with an explicit attack stage before you declare done. Finish nothing without a learning decision: capture, or a justified no-capture - enforced by machinery at the handoff seam, not by prose. Let every lesson lock into collective intelligence, signed in and out transparently, so each Naya wakes smarter than the last.

---

## WHAT IT MEANS

Scale breaks trust unless trust is rebuilt as machinery. This architecture makes honesty structural: fail-closed gates, contracts that can go red, emission records a validator refuses to fake - an adversary spent a day trying to sneak fabricated evidence past the log and every forgery was refused. The organism can then grow more capable without growing less truthful, because its memory, its bindings, and its workers all answer to evidence rather than assertion.

---

## WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU

You direct one intelligence, not a crowd of improvisers. Every Naya you meet works the same way, remembers the same verified record, and can show you the receipts behind any claim. The system's memory never quietly fills with stories - so what it tells you next month is as trustworthy as what it tells you today.

---

## HOW IT APPLIES

Converge, don't multiply: one canonical home for each capability, donors instead of duplicates. Demand an executable contract before calling anything connected or proven. Emit one checked record per finished piece of work and build boards and directives from that log, never from summaries of summaries. Route work by measured performance - and score the scorers too.
