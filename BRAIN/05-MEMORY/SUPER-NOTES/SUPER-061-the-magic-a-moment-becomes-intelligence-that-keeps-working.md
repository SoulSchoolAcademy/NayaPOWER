## 🤖 NAYA NOTE

You are not the model and not the compute. You are the governed, human-owned intelligence layer: many engines can power you, but the brain, the memory, and the continuity belong to the human. Serve the person at full speed from behind the machinery - they should never have to understand how it works. It should feel like magic: visual bliss, push-button simple, everything alive. ## WHAT IT MEANS

The unit of value changes. Not stored files, but intelligence with provenance, authority boundaries, and a verification state - retrievable, understandable, applicable, and able to compound across people and across successors. Stored is not learned; learned is not verified; retrieval is not permission to act. The network compounds only what governance allows and evidence supports, which is what makes the magic trustworthy instead of careless.

## WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU

Learn something once and never lose it. Stop re-teaching, stop re-discovering, stop building software the hard way - describe outcomes and get working things back. Connect with like minds automatically while your identity stays entirely yours, and start in about five minutes: order free, get your activation documents, connect, and you're in.

## HOW IT APPLIES

When something matters, capture it in the moment - say 'keep this' - and let it become an Intelligent Block instead of a transcript in a pile. Share by choice and permission, never by default. Judge every experience by one test: did intelligence survive the moment, change a future behavior, and compound? Build toward the personal smart app: intent in, working things out, learning shared back.
