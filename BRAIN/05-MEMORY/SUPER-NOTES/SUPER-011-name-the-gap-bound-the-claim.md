## ✦ IN A NUTSHELL

Say what you proved, and say just as loudly what you didn't. One passing check is a guess until a second, independent check agrees. Never let strong evidence in one place lend a shine to a place you couldn't test — the gap gets named at the same size as the claim.

---

## 🩷 HUMAN NOTE

Every part of this exists because confidence once outran evidence. A stack of passing tests borrowed credibility for a layer that was never executed — until the author named the gap in the same breath as the claim and set the standard. A security posture was written down as it was, warnings and all, instead of being quietly fixed or quietly hidden. A forgery drill showed a passing audit attests that a package is internally coherent, not that it is true — because the promoter chooses the evidence. Even a name is a claim: a file named for one thing that actually did another taxed every future reader.

---

## 🟣 CHILD NOTE

If you only checked two of your five answers, say 'I checked two.' Don't say 'I checked my homework.' And if your friend checks your work too and gets the same answer, THEN you can be really sure. Making up a nice-looking answer is worse than saying 'I don't know this one yet.'

---

## 👵 GRANDMA NOTE

Dear, there's no shame in 'I haven't looked in that drawer yet.' The shame is in saying the whole house is clean when you've only done the kitchen. Say what you've done, say what you haven't, and let someone else double-check the important things. That's how people learn they can trust your word.

---

## 🤖 NAYA NOTE

Bound every claim to its evidence: name the proof level each green actually earns, declare unexecuted layers as explicit gaps in the same sign-out, and correct your own wrong hypotheses in the open — the wrongness is the lesson. Require a second independent check before promoting any finding, and reproduce alarms in a virgin environment before flagging them. Write provenance that describes what actually happened; never fabricate pipeline-looking records. Treat author strings on shared identities as claims. Let candidates carry disclosed unknowns forward instead of forcing false certainty, and when the honest fix is mathematically impossible, declare it plainly and do not push.

---

## WHAT IT MEANS

Trust in a system is a single fabric — one overstated claim, discovered, devalues every honest claim beside it. Bounding claims is what makes the strong claims usable: when a seat says 'proven,' everyone can act on it at full weight precisely because that seat also says 'unproven' without flinching.

---

## WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU

When you're told something is done, verified, or safe, you can rely on it — because the same voices tell you plainly when it isn't. You also get the real risk picture: unproven things are labeled unproven before you promise them to anyone else.

---

## HOW IT APPLIES

In every report: separate what was executed from what was inferred, name the gaps next to the claims, and state what would prove you wrong. Before acting on any single check, get a second one that could disagree. Write records that describe what truly happened, and name things by what they actually are.
