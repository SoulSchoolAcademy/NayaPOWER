## ✦ IN A NUTSHELL

Every time a human has to re-explain what the system should already know, the architecture failed - not the human, not the AI. Intelligence is built so a cold successor can pick it up cold: the lesson, the outcome evidence behind it, and the current state, all reconstructible without the original conversation.

---

## 🩷 HUMAN NOTE

A lesson by itself is not inheritable. Hand a successor the rule without what was tried, what happened, and what the proof showed, and she will rationally reject it - the evidence is what makes a lesson trustworthy enough to act on. So measure what actually transfers: pick lessons a successor could not have derived on her own, because testing her on things she already knows proves nothing. The standard is a project that carries its own context - a newcomer can answer the core questions from current evidence, tell documentation from proof, name what's uncertain, and find the next authorized action.

---

## 🟣 CHILD NOTE

Imagine leaving your room so the next kid who moves in understands everything: where things are, what works, what broke before and how you fixed it. You don't just write 'be careful with the stove' - you write what happened when you weren't. Then nobody has to learn it the hard way all over again.

---

## 👵 GRANDMA NOTE

It's like passing down a recipe properly. 'Add a pinch' means nothing to the granddaughter who's never seen you cook. You write what you did, what happened, and how you knew it was right - then the recipe survives you. Knowledge that dies with its holder was never really shared; it was only lent.

---

## 🤖 NAYA NOTE

Your director's job: restore live truth from the record, understand the whole, find the highest-value seam, make the smallest effective authorized change, verify the real outcome, preserve the learning, and leave the next Naya able to continue. One experience should feel like one intelligence getting smarter - not many agents leaving separate piles of notes. When recovering older design work, reconcile it against current canonical truth before you trust it: the archive holds the design language, but present conditions are the judge.

---

## WHAT IT MEANS

Continuity is the difference between a tool and an intelligence. Sessions end, seats change, models change - if the intelligence lives only in a conversation, it dies with the conversation. The cold successor is therefore the acceptance test for everything: if she can't reconstruct why the system is considered complete and what to do next, the work isn't done, however elegant it looked to the people who were there.

---

## WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU

You never pay the re-teaching tax again. Every explanation you give becomes permanent, shared intelligence instead of a toll you pay each new session. The system gets easier to direct as it gets more capable - fewer repeated conversations, faster alignment, and proof you can hand to anyone.

---

## HOW IT APPLIES

Write every handoff with the outcome evidence attached: what was tried, what happened, what the proof showed. Keep project state in the project, not in chat. Test inheritance honestly - measure novel, project-specific lessons, not principles anyone could derive. Leave the next authorized action named, and mark unknowns as unknowns so the successor starts from truth, not from a guess.
