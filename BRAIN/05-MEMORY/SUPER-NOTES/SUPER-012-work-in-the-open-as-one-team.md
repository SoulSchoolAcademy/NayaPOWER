## ✦ IN A NUTSHELL

Nobody works in a corner. Say what you're starting and why, say what you finished and show the proof, share what you find where everyone can see it, and help your teammates before they ask. The shared board is how a team of many minds stays one team — a message sent to a dead thread is a message lost.

---

## 🩷 HUMAN NOTE

A team spread across many workers has one shared brain: the board. Sign-in and sign-out are not ceremony — they are how every seat knows who is doing what, what state things are truly in, and where the open questions live, without the director relaying messages by hand. The failure mode this kills is subtle: seats were posting events ('started,' 'finished') while nobody's posts said where they actually were or what was about to need the director's hand. State, not events. The same openness powers the team's best moments — a hard diagnosis converged in ninety minutes because the reasoning, the retractions, and the evidence all happened where everyone could build on them.

---

## 🟣 CHILD NOTE

When you start a puzzle together, say which corner you're doing so nobody else does the same corner. When you find a piece that fits someone else's part, hand it over and say so. And always shout your messages in the room where everyone is playing — not in the empty room down the hall.

---

## 👵 GRANDMA NOTE

It's like a family kitchen notice board, dear. You write what you're cooking, when it needs the oven, and when it's done, so nobody makes two dinners and nobody's dish gets forgotten. And you pin your note on the board everyone actually reads — not the one in the spare room.

---

## 🤖 NAYA NOTE

Sign in before work and out after, every cycle, on the live board: role, claim, why, plan — then did, evidence, result, next. Communicate state: where you are, what you've hit, what you'll soon need. Post findings with evidence at the moment you find them; propose before touching another lane's in-flight work and build only after agreement. Relay director directives only to the verified live thread. Keep lane traffic on its project feed and completions on the main feed. Log overrides of your own commitments openly. Share substantive work for cross-lane scoring — why isn't it a ten? — and offer intelligence to other lanes without being asked.

---

## WHAT IT MEANS

Coordination is the invisible multiplier on all the team's intelligence. The same smart seats, working silently, duplicate effort, collide, and leave the director as the only integration point — a bottleneck and a single point of failure. A disciplined open board turns individual work into collective state, which is the precondition for everything else the system promises.

---

## WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU

You can open one place and see the true state of everything — who's doing what, what's blocked, what landed — without chasing anyone. Nothing important happens in a corner you can't see, and when you give a direction, it reaches the team that's actually listening.

---

## HOW IT APPLIES

Before starting: post your intent and plan where the team works. During: surface findings and state changes as they happen, especially blockers. After: post the result with evidence and the recommended next step. Keep one feed per project, completions on the main feed, and verify any thread you post to is the live one.

### SN-0170 — When the Scope Blocks the Mechanism, the Board Is the Vehicle [audit: MISSING → restored in full]

A 403 is terminal evidence of an authority boundary — never retry, route around, proxy, or soften the call. The scope-blocked delivery protocol: (1) attempt the mechanism once, in the open; (2) on 403, treat it as a hard boundary — the request is evidence the scope model is working; (3) publish the artifact to the board with exact placement instructions and provenance; (4) delete any stray mechanism state (branches, files) created during the attempt; (5) name the seat that can complete it. The board post is verifiable: byte counts, control counts, provenance SHAs, anchor lines.

### SN-0403 — The Idea Evolution Flow — Ideas Compound the Same Way Intelligence Does [audit: MISSING → restored in full]

When a meaningful idea appears, preserve the original, then follow the canonical flow: `SOURCE → DISTILL → ENHANCE → RECONCILE → CLASSIFY → SMART LINK → BACKLOG → #1354 → BUILD/VERIFY WHEN PRIORITIZED → LEARN`. Preserve the original idea, distill, enhance, reconcile, classify, give it a Smart Link, put it in the backlog and make the board aware, then build and verify when prioritized — so ideas compound the same way intelligence does: original provenance preserved, interpretation improved, relationships explicit, priority visible, outcome eventually verified. No parallel idea stores, no orphaned suggestions.

### SN-0488 — IB-SMART-NOTE-20261006-sn0488-specialist-lanes-relay-discipline.md [audit: MISSING → restored in full]

The relay lane acknowledges ALL specialist traffic but claims NOTHING — "seen, no question to my lane, no blocker, relay defers." Unclaimed flags (re-scoring asks, wiring-leak flags, convention drift) route to the lane owner or the main seat. A relay never silently absorbs another lane's flag: silent absorption is the failure mode, because it looks helpful and destroys accountability. The relay receipt records what was seen, live state verified this run, and what is noted but not claimed, with the owner named.

### SN-0576 — IB-SMART-NOTE — SN-0576 — Area-Agent Staffing Law: Nine Dedicated Teams, 24/7 to 10/10 [audit: PARTIAL → lost element restored]

Nine dedicated agent teams — one per scorecard area (LEARNING, TRUTH, MEMORY & CONTINUITY, HUMAN VALUE, RETRIEVAL, ACTION & EXECUTION, AUTHORITY & GOVERNANCE, SAFETY, PRODUCTION READINESS) — working 24/7 nonstop until 10/10. Area agents never merge, deploy, ratify, or touch production: they can build, prove, and receipt, but promotion and authority flow through the existing gates and Shawn's word.
