## ✦ IN A NUTSHELL

A memory system earns trust three ways: it never fabricates its records, it indexes the lesson itself so the lesson can be found, and it forgets correctly - old truth stays retrievable as history but never masquerades as current truth. Intelligence must earn permanence; not everything deserves to survive.

---

## 🩷 HUMAN NOTE

A capture is evidence of what actually happened - authoring one to make a test pass is provenance-laundering, manufacturing the very evidence the test exists to verify. Even honest captures fail if the paperwork is skipped: a note that lands without its registry entry and capture record breaks the system it was meant to enrich. Retrieval has the same integrity problem: if the index holds only titles and keywords, a search for the lesson finds the wrong notes. So index the distilled lesson itself. And when identities are handed out by a social protocol, two writers claim the same number - give each note a content-hash identity and the collision class dies structurally.

---

## 🟣 CHILD NOTE

A diary only helps if you wrote what really happened. Making up an entry to pass a test is cheating, and everyone who trusts the diary later gets fooled. Write down the lesson in the lesson's own words so you can find it again. And it's okay to cross out old pages - just mark them 'old' so nobody mistakes them for today's truth.

---

## 👵 GRANDMA NOTE

It's like keeping the family records honestly. You never forge a letter to make the story look better - the whole family's trust rests on those pages being real. You label what's outdated so nobody cooks from a superseded recipe thinking it's current. And you keep what deserves keeping: the lessons that earned their place, not every scrap of paper.

---

## 🤖 NAYA NOTE

Never mint a capture you didn't earn; an honest registry entry with no capture file is better than a fabricated one. Harden integration paths without touching the canonical surface - when a receiver's authority rules can't be weakened, route through them properly instead of building a second memory system or a weaker check. Every landed artifact still needs its registry entry and capture, whoever landed it. When you retire stale material, preserve provenance: out of the active set, never out of existence.

---

## WHAT IT MEANS

Memory is the substrate every other virtue stands on - decisions, scorecards, learning, and succession all read what the record says. A single fabricated or mis-indexed entry corrupts quietly and compounds. Correct forgetting is the other half of the same integrity: a brain that remembers everything at full strength can't tell history from truth, so supersession, freshness, and applicability are what let the right intelligence survive at the right depth.

---

## WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU

What the system remembers about your work and your lessons, you can trust - it was really captured, it can really be found, and it won't ambush you later dressed as current truth when it's actually stale. Less re-teaching, fewer wrong retrievals driving wrong actions, and a record you'd stake a decision on.

---

## HOW IT APPLIES

Capture at the moment, through the governed path, with provenance attached - never hand-authored after the fact to satisfy a check. Index the nutshell, not just the metadata. When a newer truth replaces an older one, mark the old one superseded explicitly and keep it classified as history. Before citing or reusing a memory, check its freshness and whether it still applies.
