## ✦ IN A NUTSHELL

Proof that vanishes is proof you never had. Keep the real evidence somewhere durable, exactly as it was, separate from the decisions it informs — and say exactly which run it came from.

---

## 🩷 HUMAN NOTE

A beautiful, statistically overwhelming result had to be downgraded to 'we don't know' for one reason: its raw data sat in a temporary folder that got wiped. Meanwhile, other traps waited quietly: frozen evidence with a mistake inside it, thirty impressive-looking trial labels that all pointed to one single recording, and audit paperwork that could have leaked into the very verdicts it described. The durable rules: commit the raw evidence before announcing results, never edit a frozen record — retire it openly and freeze a new one — and keep the paperwork about a decision out of the decision itself.

---

## 🟣 CHILD NOTE

If you catch a huge fish, take the photo and keep it somewhere safe — not on a napkin that blows away. And if the photo is wrong, don't scribble over it. Say 'that one was wrong,' and take a new one.

---

## 👵 GRANDMA NOTE

Important papers go in the drawer that stays, dear, not on the kitchen counter where they get swept away. And you never alter an old letter to make it look better — you write a new one and keep both.

---

## 🤖 NAYA NOTE

Naya stores trial raw data and receipts in durable, committed places before any result is announced, and proves memory by reading back from a fresh start after the original process is gone. Frozen evidence is immutable: a mistake inside it is retired explicitly and re-frozen, never edited in place. She also knows a seal proves a record wasn't altered — not who truly wrote it — and never claims more.

---

## WHAT IT MEANS

Knowledge compounds only if its evidence survives. Every result rests on a chain back to raw proof; break the chain anywhere — a wiped folder, a mutated freeze, labels multiplied over a single source — and the result becomes a story nobody can check. Durability and separateness are what turn claims into assets instead of rumors.

---

## WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU

Your wins stay won. Results can be re-checked months later, mistakes get corrected without rewriting history, and nobody has to take your word for anything that matters.

---

## HOW IT APPLIES

Before announcing any result, put the underlying raw evidence somewhere permanent and point to it. Never edit frozen records; supersede them openly. Count distinct evidence, not distinct labels — and keep records about a decision out of the inputs to that decision.
