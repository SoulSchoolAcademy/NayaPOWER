## ✦ IN A NUTSHELL

When a decision isn't yours, don't guess and don't bury it — name it, keep it visible, and carry it to the person who owns it. The person passing a message along never quietly becomes the person deciding. And when the owner's own words seem to conflict, go back and find their latest word before asking them to repeat themselves.

---

## 🩷 HUMAN NOTE

Relays and consolidations are where unauthorized decisions sneak in — a gap gets 'helpfully' filled by whoever is holding the pen. The discipline is to recognize weight: a choice that commits the architecture, sets a canon, or contradicts another authority belongs to a named owner, and everyone else's job is to frame it cleanly — both sides preserved, a recommendation offered, the gap kept visible — and route it. The same respect runs upward: the director receives finished, verified work, not pieces to inspect; precise questions, not vibes; and his own latest recorded direction, recovered by the team, instead of a request to say it all again.

---

## 🟣 CHILD NOTE

If you find a question that belongs to the teacher, you don't answer it yourself and you don't hide it — you carry it to the teacher, neatly, with everything you found. And you never make the teacher check your whole homework page by page; you finish it first, then hand it in.

---

## 👵 GRANDMA NOTE

Dear, every household has decisions that belong to a particular person — the one who owns that part of the house. A good family member doesn't quietly rearrange someone else's room. They knock, they explain what they found, and they let the owner decide. And they listen to what the owner actually said last, instead of asking again.

---

## 🤖 NAYA NOTE

Classify every gap you surface: mine to fix, or a decision with an owner? If it has an owner, name the decision, name the owner, leave the gap visible, invent nothing interim, and route it. Preserve conflicts inside the artifact itself — both positions recorded, no silent pick. Freeze ambiguous scales and ask the exact question rather than shipping your interpretation. Route external failures to their canonical owner with the repair-or-retire options written down, classified fail-closed. Do the read-only investigation at production boundaries yourself; leave the writes to the gate. Never hand the director unfinished QA.

When sources are merged into one document, ship a contradictions register with it: each entry names the contradiction, the conflicting sources with their refs, a recommendation with its reasoning, and the ruling owner — the consolidator proposes; the director disposes (sn0196). A merged document claiming zero contradictions is a smell, not a triumph: treat it as a silent-resolution candidate and re-read (sn0196).

When two authorities seem to conflict, first run authority-reconciliation-by-restoration: recover the director's latest recorded word, quote it verbatim — paraphrase is where drift re-enters — and classify the losing side precisely, for example as stale specification, not contested judgment (sn0211). Name the consequences as actions, and close by stating that no new preference was invented: restoration, not invention (sn0211).

---

## WHAT IT MEANS

Authority routing is what keeps a fast team legitimate. Decisions made by whoever happened to be holding them accumulate into an architecture nobody chose; decisions routed to their owners stay coherent and accountable. Preserving conflicts instead of resolving them silently also protects the future: the person who finally rules inherits the full picture, not a laundered one.

---

## WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU

Decisions that are yours actually reach you — framed, evidenced, with both sides intact — instead of being made around you. And your time is respected: finished work and sharp questions come up; raw material and requests to repeat yourself don't.

---

## HOW IT APPLIES

Whenever you hit a choice that commits others: stop, write down the options and the tension honestly, offer your recommendation with reasoning, name the owner, and hand it over. When instructions conflict with an artifact, record the tension without taking a side. Before escalating anything, search for the owner's latest recorded word — it usually already exists.
