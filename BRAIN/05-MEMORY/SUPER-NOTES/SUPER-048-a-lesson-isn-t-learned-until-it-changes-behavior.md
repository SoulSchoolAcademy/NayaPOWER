## ✦ IN A NUTSHELL

Writing a lesson down is the first rung, not the finish. Stored, cited, or stamped 'learned' means nothing until the lesson quietly changes an ordinary decision — and each level of learning is proven by its own evidence.

---

## 🩷 HUMAN NOTE

A note in a drawer teaches nobody. Stored information becomes intelligence only when its meaning is preserved, connected to what it relates to, findable at the moment it matters, and actually used. The honest measures are unglamorous: of 21 lessons written, only 3 were wired into checks that enforce them. A lesson can be quoted everywhere and lived nowhere. And learned has levels — understands, applies, reuses, generalizes, hands to a successor — each needing its own proof, because a label is not mastery.

---

## 🟣 CHILD NOTE

Writing 'look both ways' in your notebook doesn't keep you safe. You've only really learned it when you actually stop and look — every time, without anyone reminding you. Knowing the words and doing the thing are two different things.

---

## 👵 GRANDMA NOTE

Writing a good recipe in the book is lovely, dear, but dinner only changes when someone cooks from it. And there's a difference between reading the recipe, cooking it once, and teaching your grandchild to cook it. Each step is its own proof.

---

## 🤖 NAYA NOTE

When you capture a lesson, ask immediately: what would be different next time if this were truly active? Route it: a machine-checkable gate when a failure is preventable, a binding behavior constraint, a review discipline, or indexed retrieval intelligence. Learning must reach behavior through the ordinary decision path — if the caller has to name the lesson, it's manual recall, not learning. Judge every claim on separate columns — is it true, is it adopted, is it still current, how far has it transferred — and never collapse them into one word.

---

## WHAT IT MEANS

This is the discipline that keeps the whole compounding idea honest. Capture preserves tuition; activation converts tuition into capability. Systems fool themselves with volume — many notes, many citations, confident labels — while behavior stays identical. The only trustworthy signal is a decision that goes differently because the lesson was there, and stops going differently when it's removed.

---

## WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU

You get lessons that actually protect you — enforced by checks and habits, not by hope that someone remembers. Failures stop repeating, because the fix lives in the machinery and the ordinary workflow, not in a document nobody reopens at the critical moment.

---

## HOW IT APPLIES

For every lesson: decide its rung the same day — gate, behavior, culture, or index — land the enforcement in the next step, and audit later with three honest classes: truly compounding, cited-but-not-lived, or hollow. Report levels humbly: 'understands, proven' until a real event proves the higher rungs.

### SN-0526 — A Smart Note Is Not Finished When Written — the Activation Ladder [audit: PARTIAL → lost element restored]

The activation ladder is explicit: captured ≠ indexed · indexed ≠ retrieved · retrieved ≠ applied · applied ≠ verified · verified once ≠ compounded. A Smart Note is not finished when it is written — capture preserves tuition; activation converts tuition into capability. INDEXED is its own separate rung, distinct from SN-015's eight rungs, with its own index artifact (the structured retrieval index / lesson index) and its own failing gate — the SN-0460 "Index the Lesson" gate FAILS when a captured Smart Note has no index entry or is unretrievable by the retrieval surface. Stored intelligence can exist and never matter; retrieved intelligence can surface and still not change behavior; proven-once intelligence that cannot be retrieved later does not compound.
