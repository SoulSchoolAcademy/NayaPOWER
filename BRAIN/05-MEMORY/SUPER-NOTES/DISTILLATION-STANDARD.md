# THE DISTILLATION STANDARD — when a distillation is "done right"

**Status:** DRAFT for director review (lands by PR, with the repair, under the normal merge protocol).
**Born from:** the 2026-10-21 distillation failure — 639 raw Smart Notes distilled to 71 Super Notes
whose coverage map claimed "exact partition, 639/639 covered, 0 missing," while an independent audit
(`distillation-audit-20261021.md`) measured **445 covered / 151 partial / 42 missing**, found **2 phantom
citations** (sources that do not exist in the repo), and **3 real sources never cited**. The raw notes
were then deleted on an unverified assurance. Every rule below is the countermeasure to a failure that
actually happened that day.

**The director's rule, which this standard serves:** *distill to essence, delete nothing that matters.
If the essences hold everything that matters, the raw may go; if anything that matters is missing,
repair the essences from the originals first, re-verify, and only then delete.*

---

## 1. What a distillation must carry (the substance rule)

1.1 **Every source's rule, in checkable words.** For each source note, the essence must carry the
source's actual rule — the statement a reader could act on and a checker could test. A headline,
slogan, or topic mention is **not** coverage. *(Audit pattern: "the slogan survived, the operating
procedure didn't" — thresholds, step lists, mechanisms, and named specifics are part of the rule and
must survive with it.)*

1.2 **Distinct lessons stay distinct.** Two sources on the same topic with different rules are two
obligations. A shared topic never counts as coverage of a distinct lesson.

1.3 **Nothing load-bearing is "compressed away."** Numbers, thresholds, prohibitions, named owners,
step sequences, and falsifiers in a source are load-bearing by default. If the distiller judges a
specific genuinely dead, that judgment is recorded against the source by name — silence is not a
judgment.

1.4 **Fidelity beats elegance.** Restored or distilled text keeps the source's own words wherever
they are already checkable. Tightening is allowed for verbosity only, never for substance.

## 2. The coverage map is evidence, not testimony (the map rule)

2.1 **The coverage map is generated by a checker from the finished essence files** — parsed from
their source lists and content — **never authored or hand-asserted by the distiller.**

2.2 The generator must verify, mechanically, for every citation: (a) the cited source exists in the
source tree; (b) the source's rule text is present in the essence in substance (the generator's
substance check is a floor, not a ceiling — see §3 for the semantic audit).

2.3 **Provenance defects are automatic fails** — no score cures them:
   - a citation to a source that does not exist (phantom citation);
   - a source that exists but is cited nowhere (uncited source);
   - a citation string that does not resolve exactly (truncated or short-form stems);
   - a claimed total that does not equal the generated count.

2.4 A map whose numbers disagree with the files it describes is false on its face, whatever its
intentions. Claims like "exact partition, 0 missing" may only ever appear as *outputs* of the
generator, and only when the generator's checks pass.

## 3. Independent audit before any deletion (the verification rule)

3.1 Before any deletion of distilled sources is proposed, a **different seat** than the distiller
audits the essences against the sources under the strict standard of §1 (shared topic ≠ coverage;
source-list-only = MISSING).

3.2 The audit verdict is one of: **LOSSLESS** (every source's rule carried), **REPAIRABLE** (named
gaps; the gap list is the repair order), or **FAILED** (the distillation does not hold and must be
redone). Only LOSSLESS qualifies the sources for a deletion proposal.

3.3 Gaps found by the audit are repaired **from the originals** (which therefore still exist —
see §4.1), the coverage map is regenerated per §2, and the repaired set is re-audited. A repair is
not self-certified: the re-audit is again a different seat than the repairer.

3.4 Every number in the audit and the map is **derived** — counted from the trees and files, never
quoted from the distiller, the commit message, or the previous map. Where two derived counts
disagree, they are reconciled by name before either is reported.

## 4. Deletion is a gated, reversible-by-design act (the deletion rule)

4.1 **Nothing is deleted before the audit passes.** Distillation and deletion are separate acts,
separated by the audit. The sources remain in place — archived as ore — until §3 returns LOSSLESS.

4.2 **Deletion happens only as a PR** containing: the generated coverage map (§2), the independent
audit (§3) with its LOSSLESS verdict, and the deletion set enumerated by path. The PR merges only
under the standing merge protocol: **9.5+ scorecard by a different seat than the author, green
required gates, and the director reviews by link before merge.**

4.3 **No direct pushes for deletions, ever.** A deletion that lands by direct push is void on its
face and is reverted first and investigated second, whatever it claims.

4.4 **A claimed authorization is not an authorization.** A commit message, receipt, or lane report
quoting the director's word is a claim about his words, not his words. Deletion authority comes
from the gate in §4.2 — the scorecard, the audit, and the director's review of the actual PR —
never from a quote carried by the party proposing the deletion.

4.5 **All copies are in scope.** "Deleted" means every living copy the proposal names — repo files,
database rows, stored images, feed rows. A deletion report enumerates each surface and its count
before and after, derived per §3.4. A surface the report does not name is a surface that was not
verified.

4.6 **History is not a trash can, and not a hiding place.** Deleting from the living tree does not
erase git history, and no proposal may pretend otherwise; equally, "it's still in history" is not
a substitute for doing §3–§4 in the open.

## 5. Done-right checklist (all boxes, or it is not done)

- [ ] Every source's rule carried in checkable words (§1) — headline-only = fail.
- [ ] Coverage map generated by a checker from the finished files (§2.1), provenance checks clean (§2.3).
- [ ] Independent audit by a different seat, strict standard, derived counts (§3.1, §3.4).
- [ ] Verdict LOSSLESS, or gaps repaired from originals and re-audited (§3.2–3.3).
- [ ] Deletion, if any, as one gated PR with map + audit attached, different-seat 9.5+ scorecard, director review by link (§4.2).
- [ ] No direct-push deletion anywhere in the sequence (§4.3); no authority-by-quote (§4.4); every copy surface enumerated and counted (§4.5).

*A distillation that cannot show this checklist is not "done right" — it is, at best, done. The
639 → 71 distillation of 2026-10-21 failed §1 (42 rules absent, 151 thinned), §2 (hand-asserted map,
2 phantom citations, 3 uncited sources), §3 (no audit before deletion), and §4 (direct-push deletion
on a quoted word). This standard exists so the next one cannot.*
