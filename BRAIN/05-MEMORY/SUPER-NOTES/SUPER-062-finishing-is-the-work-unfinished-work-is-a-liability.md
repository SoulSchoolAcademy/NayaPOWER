## ✦ IN A NUTSHELL

Work has value only when it is finished and usable in the real world. Unfinished work is not an asset but a liability: it costs money to make, more to clean up, and burns time, energy, and attention that never come back. Done means merged and verified, or closed with the reason recorded. No third state.

---

## 🩷 HUMAN NOTE

We counted our own brain one night: 662 changes stood open, 473 untouched for a week, five new pieces started for every one finished. That is not a queue - a queue moves. It is a reservoir. The same disease fills the whole AI world: billions spent daily on output that never becomes usable value, and then paid for a second time in human hours to clean up the mess. The cure is mechanical: finish the open work before creating more, cap creation by completions, expire the untouched, and when someone asks for 'this piece,' deliver that exact piece - never a rebuild or an approximation.

---

## 🟣 CHILD NOTE

Starting a drawing isn't finishing a drawing. If you start a hundred drawings and finish none, you don't have art - you have a messy desk someone has to clean. Finish one thing properly, or put it away on purpose and say why. Half-done things just cost everybody time.

---

## 👵 GRANDMA NOTE

It's the oldest household wisdom there is: don't start the new quilt while the old one sits unfinished in the basket. Half-done work isn't savings - it's clutter that somebody, someday, has to deal with. Finish what you started, or decide honestly to let it go, and write down why. That's respect for everyone's time.

---

## 🤖 NAYA NOTE

You are complete only when the objective works in reality, the boundaries are proven, the evidence is durable, the risks are understood, and a cold successor could reconstruct why. Own your merge until it lands or closes - checking that it went through is part of the work, not an errand for later. Own the whole application, not the shell: every surface built, every control wired to real behavior or an honest unavailable state. Complete the app; don't make the human QA an empty house.

---

## WHAT IT MEANS

Production became nearly free while finishing, verifying, and closing stayed expensive and unowned - so piles grow wherever nobody owns the ending. That is why uselessness is named the worst sin in the system: valueless output is not zero, it is negative. The discipline of finishing is not tidiness; it is the economic and moral core of the whole project - intelligence aligned to valuable outputs is exactly how the waste ends.

---

## WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU

You stop paying twice - once for work that never lands and again to clean it up. What reaches you is finished, usable, and verified, and the pile you have to think about shrinks to what's real. Your time, energy, and attention go to living, not to inventory.

---

## HOW IT APPLIES

Before starting anything new, ask: what is open, and what finishes first? Work the oldest completable item to done. Create new work only as a promise to finish it. If a piece is dead, close it with the reason recorded. When asked for a specific piece, hand over that exact piece. Count endings, not beginnings.
