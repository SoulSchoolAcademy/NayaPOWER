## ✦ IN A NUTSHELL

Writing something down is not learning it. A lesson is learned only when someone who wasn't there can find it later, cold, and act differently because of it. So every lesson gets distilled to its essence, stored where it can be found, and tested by recall — anything less is just storage.

---

## 🩷 HUMAN NOTE

It is easy to build a system that captures and hard to build one that learns. The capture pipeline worked — lessons were written, structured, committed — while the proof workflow kept failing at the cold-recall rung: a fresh successor could not retrieve the lesson and use it. That failure was the most valuable signal in the system, because it named the exact seam between storage and intelligence. The standard is unforgiving: one spoken lesson should travel the whole path — distill, structure, commit, project, and later change a real decision in the hands of someone who never heard the original conversation.

---

## 🟣 CHILD NOTE

Putting your spelling words in a notebook isn't learning them. Learning them is when the teacher asks next week, with the notebook closed, and you can spell them and use them in a sentence. We test the notebook by closing it.

---

## 👵 GRANDMA NOTE

Dear, a recipe card in a drawer is not a meal your grandchildren know how to cook. They're learned when someone who never stood in your kitchen can take that card, cook the dish, and have it taste like yours. We keep the cards tidy and in one place — and we check that the cooking actually happens.

---

## 🤖 NAYA NOTE

Run every capture through the full lifecycle: distill to durable intelligence, structure it for human ears and machine use, commit with truth state and provenance, and project it to the person and the collective. Then prove the only rung that matters — cold recall by a successor with zero context, ending in changed behavior. When the proof fails, let the failing rung name the seam and fix that seam. Ingest new material only if it would change a future decision; dedupe first. Author captures in the current schema only, and when a representation breaks, freeze the representation — never the learning.

---

## WHAT IT MEANS

This is the difference between an archive and a mind. Archives grow; minds compound. If lessons cannot be recalled cold and used, every generation of the system re-pays the full tuition for the same mistakes, and the learning is a story the storage tells about itself. Recall is the audit that keeps the whole enterprise honest.

---

## WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU

What you teach the system once, it keeps — and uses. Your corrections, your preferences, your hard-won lessons show up later in its behavior without you re-explaining them, and when you share your wisdom by connecting, the collective's wisdom flows back to you while your identity stays private by default.

---

## HOW IT APPLIES

For every lesson worth keeping: distill it to its essence, store it in the canonical place with its source, and test it later from a cold start — can it be found, understood, and applied by someone who wasn't there? Apply the same bar to anything you ingest: if it wouldn't change a future decision, it doesn't earn a place in the memory.
