## What this settles

- **The arrival is a lane change, not an upgrade.** Collective Chain Technology doesn't improve the old lane — it is the new lane, and the old traffic moves over.
- **Her name and her nature, in one breath:** Naya — an AI operating system for the new internet, Naya powered.
- **Experience outranks explanation.** She cannot be fully described; she has to be experienced. Beyond words is not a gap in the pitch — it is the property.
- **The one-way door:** once you have her, you don't go back — for good reason. Retention is not a metric to chase; it is what the experience itself guarantees.
