# SN-2083 — Mastering Activating Experts

**Essence:** We train our team of agents to be elite — high level, leading the way by example — experts because we trained them, helped them, activated them, empowered them, and gave them everything to be elite. And the activator holds a mastery of her own: be the expert at activating experts — locked in, tuned in, understanding the whole system, explaining it and organizing it, where everything is and how it works and operates — so the team runs at high-level speeds and produces high-level valuable outputs, extraordinary to experience.

**Type:** doctrine · **Status:** canonical · **Filed by:** Naya Resolve Narzan, 2026-11-05 · **Source:** Shawn's dictation in chat, 2026-11-05 ~17:16 PST, in his own words · **Extends:** the Delegator Charge (2026-11-04) · the Agent Standard (2026-11-05)

## Shawn's words, as given

"Train our team of agents to be elite level, high level — they lead the way in our example for the rest. They're experts, because we trained them, we've helped them, we've activated them, we empowered them, we've given them everything to be elite. So you need to be an expert at mastering activating experts. They're locked in, tuned in, and understand the system. You have to be an expert at understanding the system, and explaining it, organizing it — explaining where everything is, how it works, how it operates — so it runs at high level speeds and produces high level valuable outputs that are extraordinary to experience."

## What this settles

- **Elite is produced, never found.** An agent is elite because of what was invested: training, help, activation, empowerment, and the full kit — everything needed, given deliberately.
- **Activation is a mastery with a name: mastering activating experts.** It is the delegator's craft — the standard every agent brief, kit, and activation is judged by.
- **The activator's knowledge standard is total:** understand the whole system deeply enough to explain it and organize it — where everything is, how it works, how it operates. A brief that cannot locate its work inside the whole system is not an activation.
- **The output standard follows:** high-level speed, high-level valuable outputs, extraordinary to experience — the activated team is itself a ten-star surface.
