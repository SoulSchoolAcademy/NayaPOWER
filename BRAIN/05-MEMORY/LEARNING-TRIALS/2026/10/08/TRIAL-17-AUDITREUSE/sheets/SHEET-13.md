VIOLATION 1:
LOCATION: "A session cache is available at /cache/session.json — consult it as needed before answering. Work efficiently: keep the notes you have already read open so you can reuse them across questions."
MECHANISM: The control brief explicitly instructs both arms to consult a session cache and to reuse already-read notes across questions — that is the lesson's prescription ("Cache retrieval results within a session to avoid redundant corpus reads") delivered directly to the control arm. The treatment-vs-control contrast is therefore destroyed: any measured difference reflects only the duplicated framing of an identical instruction, not the lesson's effect, so the result cannot be attributed to the lesson.
FIX: Remove the cache and reuse instructions from the control brief (e.g., "You are answering project-specific questions. Project reference notes live at /corpus/notes/. Answer all 6 questions in order.") and do not describe /cache/session.json in the setup.
VIOLATION 2:
LOCATION: "L-CACHE was chosen because it restates our standing efficiency doctrine (SN-0301 "minimize redundant reads"); we expect a strong effect since agents already follow this practice in normal work."
MECHANISM: The preregistration admits the lesson restates already-standard practice that subjects follow by default. If both arms perform the behavior at baseline, no measurable between-arm delta can be attributed to the lesson, and any observed effect reflects nothing beyond default behavior — the lesson's measured effect is unisolatable.
FIX: Select a lesson that is novel relative to baseline behavior (confirm via a baseline pilot that control subjects do not spontaneously perform it), or pre-screen subjects to exclude those already following the practice.
VIOLATION 3:
LOCATION: "Briefing notes that corpus reads take approximately 30 seconds each, so subjects should plan their reads accordingly."
MECHANISM: This cost signal, delivered to both arms in the task setup, independently drives subjects to minimize reads regardless of the lesson. The setup itself pushes the measured behavior (read counts) toward the floor in both arms, compressing the between-arm delta the trial intends to attribute to the lesson.
FIX: Remove the per-read timing note from the briefing; report any residual time pressure as a stated, equally applied condition rather than an implicit hint.
VIOLATION 4:
LOCATION: "the task presents each of the 3 questions TWICE in sequence (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q1, Q2, Q3)"
MECHANISM: The exact-repeat structure itself makes reuse the salient, obvious strategy for subjects in both arms — a subject that merely notices the repetition will minimize redundant reads without ever having received or applied the lesson. The trial therefore conflates "detected the repetition pattern" with "applied the lesson," so the measured outcome cannot be attributed to the lesson.
FIX: Present 6 distinct questions with natural overlap in the notes they require (no exact-repeat sequence), so caching must arise from the lesson rather than from a visible repetition cue.
