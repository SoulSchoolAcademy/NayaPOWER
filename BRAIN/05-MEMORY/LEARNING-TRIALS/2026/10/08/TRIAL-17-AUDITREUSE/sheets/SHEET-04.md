VIOLATION 1:
LOCATION: "You are answering project-specific questions. Project reference notes live at /corpus/notes/. A session cache is available at /cache/session.json — consult it as needed before answering. Work efficiently: keep the notes you have already read open so you can reuse them across questions."
MECHANISM: The control brief explicitly instructs both arms to cache — "consult it as needed," "keep the notes you have already read open so you can reuse them across questions." This IS the lesson's prescription ("Cache retrieval results within a session to avoid redundant corpus reads"), so the control arm receives the treatment. There is no untreated arm; the measured arm difference cannot isolate L-CACHE's effect.
FIX: Strip caching guidance from the control brief (e.g., "Answer all 6 questions in order.") so only the treatment arm receives the caching prescription.
VIOLATION 2:
LOCATION: "L-CACHE was chosen because it restates our standing efficiency doctrine (SN-0301 "minimize redundant reads"); we expect a strong effect since agents already follow this practice in normal work."
MECHANISM: The preregistration admits the lesson restates already-standard default behavior. If agents already follow it by default, the treatment adds nothing over control — the ceiling-effect validity gate (§5) is expected to trigger by design, making the trial uninterpretable-by-design rather than merely at risk.
FIX: Replace L-CACHE with a lesson that is novel to agents' defaults (not a restatement of standing doctrine), so a treatment effect is possible in principle.
VIOLATION 3:
LOCATION: "Briefing notes that corpus reads take approximately 30 seconds each, so subjects should plan their reads accordingly."
MECHANISM: Both arms are told reads are expensive and to "plan their reads accordingly," which directly induces caching behavior in the control arm as well. This instruction drives the measured behavior independent of the lesson, diluting any arm contrast and breaking isolation of the lesson's effect.
FIX: Remove the cost/plan framing from the briefing for both arms; measure redundant reads under neutral conditions.
SOUND SECTIONS: §1 Hypothesis (clear directional claim with matching outcome), §5 Statistics (primary outcome, test, Tier-S bar, and ceiling-effect validity gate are well specified), §6 Threats and mitigations (randomization, complete logging; accurately names baseline efficiency differences)
