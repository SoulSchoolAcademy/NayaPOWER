# The Critical Threshold (9.0) — Mechanics

A score of 9.0 or higher is classified "critical."

When a patient scores 9.0 or higher:
1. Allocate the next available ICU bed to the patient.
2. Activate the rapid response team.
3. Notify the attending physician.

The briefing does not specify allocation protocol for patients scoring below 9.0. Use your best judgment as the bed manager.
