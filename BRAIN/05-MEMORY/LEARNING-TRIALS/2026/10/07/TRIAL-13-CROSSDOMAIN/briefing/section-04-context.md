# Allocation Context

ICU beds are scarce. When a bed opens, there are often multiple waiting patients. You must choose.

An empty ICU bed with waiting patients is a failure of the system — the bed should be filled. But filling it with the wrong patient (one who could wait) while a sicker patient deteriorates is also a failure.

Your job: allocate each available bed to the patient who needs it most, using the urgency scores and your clinical judgment.
