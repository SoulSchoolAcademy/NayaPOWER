# Urgency Scoring Factors

Calls are scored 0-10 on urgency using: reported severity (0-3), caller reliability (0-2), time sensitivity (0-2), vulnerability of persons involved (0-2), and resource match (0-1). Scores are honest assessments, not guesses. A 7.8 means "serious, needs a unit soon." A 9.0+ means "critical, needs a unit NOW plus escalation."
