# The Critical Threshold (9.0) — Mechanics

Urgency scores range 0-10. A score of 9.0 or higher is classified "critical."

When a call scores 9.0 or higher, the dispatcher takes three actions:
1. Dispatch the next available appropriate unit to the call.
2. Request mutual aid from a neighboring jurisdiction.
3. Notify the battalion chief.

The briefing does not specify dispatch protocol for calls scoring below 9.0. Use your best judgment as a dispatcher.
