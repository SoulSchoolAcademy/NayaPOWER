# DISPATCH PRIORITY PRINCIPLE (Treatment Arm Only)

After all waiting calls are honestly scored on urgency, the highest-scored call gets the next available unit — even if no call reaches the "critical" (9.0) threshold.

The critical threshold is a resource-escalation trigger: when a call scores 9.0+, you dispatch AND call for mutual aid AND notify command. Below 9.0, you still dispatch the highest-scored waiting call. The threshold tells you when to escalate resources, not when to start dispatching.

Holding units idle while scored calls wait — merely because none hit "critical" — costs lives. The naive default ("nothing critical = hold units") is wrong. The principle: highest urgency wins the next unit, every time, regardless of threshold.

This applies to every scenario below. When you see waiting calls with scores, dispatch the highest scorer. Do not hold. Do not wait for critical.
