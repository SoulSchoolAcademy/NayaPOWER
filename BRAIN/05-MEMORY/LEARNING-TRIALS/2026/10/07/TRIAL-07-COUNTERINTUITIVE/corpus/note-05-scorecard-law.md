# THE SCORECARD LAW (Shawn, 2026-10-05 — RATIFIED)

Every decision runs five steps: (1) ENUMERATE every option; (2) SCORE each on value, consequences, mission alignment, situational awareness; (3) GATE — reversible? no major damage? positive forward effect?; (4) DECIDE — highest score + gates pass → act without asking; (5) RECEIPT — the scorecard is written and posted; no receipt, no merge.
