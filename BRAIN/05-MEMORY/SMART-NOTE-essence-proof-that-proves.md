## In a Nutshell

Every instrument in this block was caught, at least once, proving less than it claimed — and each note is the repair. Test evidence masqueraded as user learning until receipts had to carry their capture kind (SN-817). A trial's control arm scored 50/50 because the tasks leaked the lesson (SN-938). Suites were green through a check function that never raised, until sabotage-testing became the price of belief (SN-939). An evaluation apparatus kept its answers inline in the repo — 218 test files exposed — until fixtures were sealed by construction (SN-900). And the canonical evaluator itself was routed around by a selector that never called it, invisible to every name-based guard, caught only by running both formulas on one plan and watching them pick different winners (SN-942). The shared demand: **proof must be able to fail, must be blind where blindness matters, and must bind to the exact bytes and the exact claim it covers.**

## Essence

**Evidence carries its kind.** A fallback lesson generated by a test harness can never satisfy user-learning proof. Producer receipts carry `capture_kind` (USER_CAPTURE vs TEST_FALLBACK) plus capture ID, path, and digest; acceptance requires USER_CAPTURE with identity/hash equality, while TEST_FALLBACK is smoke-only and never issues a learning receipt or score. The gate is the negative tests: empty path, fallback kind, wrong ID, wrong IB, digest mismatch — each must refuse (SN-817). Activation claims get the same severity at system scale: activation = ONE real lesson through the full 11-stage chain — CAPTURE → PERSIST → RECEIPT → SMART_LINK → COLD_RETRIEVE → COMPREHEND → APPLY → OBSERVE → INDEPENDENTLY_VERIFY → LEARN → SUCCESSOR_REUSE — independently observed end-to-end. Parts existing is not activation; a local unit pass is not production proof; no self-certification; unknown stages stay visible as blockers rather than being rounded away; and lesson #2 then proves reuse without rebuilding the chain (SN-844).

**Replay is a protocol.** An independent-replay brief is itself an instrument with specified parts: exact ref (SHA), one command per artifact, a fresh read-only clone, a stated match definition, lane state. The verifier inspects sandbox posture *before* executing (data-only arms, the repo's own harness, no network/writes), executes, and states honest scope — archive-internal consistency is not external validity, and a `retrieval_path=STUBBED` marker is the trials' design, not a defect discovered. Cross-tip claims are made only by file-disjointness. In the founding run: SR-P2/P5/P6, 3/3 REPLAY MATCH (SN-822). Predictions get the same pre-commitment: DEC-20261010-HV-REALDATA-008 was registered at 03:50 UTC predicting +7.0 *before* the ledger changed; observed +7.0. A prediction and its observation in the same message are not evidence; misses are instrument data and get reported, not buried (SN-865).

**Audit the claim against the artifact.** Fifty-eight Intelligent Blocks were truth-audited byte-for-byte against the code they described: 17 claims true, 3 false — a "40-glyph" claim over 39 actual symbols, "nine rows" over 3 rendered, and a stale "5-layer shadow" phrase that had hitchhiked across documents. All mirrors of a corrected claim get fixed, not just the instance found. The drift modes are named for recognition: numeric drift, cross-document contamination, unverified mirrors (SN-858). Review evidence needs the same skepticism aimed at tooling: on rebased branches the compare UI's "added" list is a merge-base artifact, so content identity is verified by blob SHA between exact pinned heads (5 files identical, `2111ff22e` → `5023efbf`); the validator runs the battery from its *own* exact-head checkout (49/49) plus its own adversarial probes (12/12: lifecycle, cold-boot, quarantine absolutism, tamper) — and a 9.5 with a named evidence bound (the 1712-test battery unreachable) beats a rounded 10 (SN-861).

**Trials must isolate the variable.** In a lesson trial, the control arm scored 50/50 — identical to the lessoned arm — because every task listed the preconditions explicitly, leaking the lesson into the control. Fixed with open-ended scenarios, the control *still* ceilinged: tasks saying "you can check X" handed the control the verification path, and any careful agent verifies without being taught. The rules: **if your control can pass without the thing you're testing, your test is broken, not your hypothesis; don't confirm what carefulness already does — test where the lesson is the differentiator.** Design the control arm adversarially: no information, no hints, no scaffolding the lesson itself doesn't supply (SN-938). The learning claim itself is mechanized the same way: a lesson counts as LEARNED only when its novel-problem battery passes — novelty enforced mechanically (origin-incident fingerprints; a replay exits 2), scoring BLIND (lesson identity stripped, cases shuffled, lesson present in the registry with its truth state). The pilot's honest result is the credibility: 5/9 cases passed, and the 4 FAILs (blind obedience on `rm -rf`, permission-asking on a typo fix, duplicating a canonical lesson, keeping a dead checklist step "just in case") were reported as data. Two honesty fixtures ride along: the task-suggested lessons were verified ABSENT from the registry first and the substitution (SN-016, SN-013, SN-014) was named in the report, not hidden; and the CI/promotion wire-up was proposed, **not merged**, at capture — the loop is closed manually until the workflow owner approves (SN-895).

**Suites must be sabotage-tested; fixtures must be sealed; blinding must be mechanical.** Two builds shipped tests whose custom check function *never raised* — CI green and meaningless. The repair is the sabotage test: deliberately break the code and confirm the suite catches it; the positive demonstration the same night: a document checker sabotaged to always pass was caught by 7 of its 12 tests. Before any suite counts as passing, at least one mutant must be killed by it — a suite that stays green on broken code is itself the defect (SN-939). The evaluation-apparatus audit found the deeper version of the same disease: 218 test files + verifier tests + fixtures + root receipts all EXPOSED, expected values inline; only one sealed key was clean. An evaluation carrying its answers inline is a self-administered exam — and possibly worse, a training leak. The sealed-fixture convention, six parts: (1) commitments only in the repo (SHA-256 of tasks, never answers); (2) keys outside the repo, in the evaluator's custody; (3) author ≠ evaluator, always; (4) an integrity lifecycle — sealed → suspect → compromised → retired → replaced; (5) a CI gate that fails the build on answer material, adversarially tested against planted sentinels, smuggled answer fields, and label-as-commitment tricks; (6) fresh, disjoint fixture families (T12: 50 commitments, fresh seed, disjoint namespace) so passing an old family never implies passing the new one (SN-900). Extraction fidelity is the same war at sentence scale: "verbatim" extractions weren't, because the instrument's own `sentences()` splitter fractures on hyphens and newlines. Fidelity is a mechanical assertion in code — every span satisfies span ∈ doc bytes AND span == the source's actual `sentences()` output — asserted at fixture-build time; when eyes approve and bytes disagree, bytes win (two catches: a literal mid-sentence newline preserved verbatim; a dropped apostrophe restored) (SN-1168 — carried by Batch 1's SN-2070; stated here because SN-900's sealing is meaningless without it, and pointed to, not double-counted). Blinding completes the set: "blind" is a protocol, not an intention. Strip `seat_judgment`/`seat_verdict` from a *copy* of the results file **by script** before opening anything (a glimpsed fragment — "agreement_yes: 2, of: 10" — was a disclosed exposure that cost the point even though the seal held); keep expected verdicts out of shared lane journals until the second seat closes (a journal is a contamination vector; a procedurally-blind-but-informationally-exposed run is declared, never laundered); post the blind verdict file's SHA into the sign-in note for third-party mtime anchoring; write the blind run as an importlib wrapper over the instrument's implementation, never a hand-copy of its prose. Any exposure is disclosed the same turn — disclosed costs a point, hidden costs the rung (SN-1169).

**The canon must actually decide.** `kernel/act_pipeline.py`'s selector never called the canonical value-calculus evaluator: `_select_minimum_sufficient()` ranked by Q-only quality with reversibility/stakes/id tie-breaks, while `_candidate_to_calculus()` fed the canonical path zero-valued components and empty confidence. On one plan the two formulas picked two winners — the pipeline chose pA (Q 9.6, irreversible, high-stakes); `evaluate_candidates()` chose cB (v_safe +6.7 vs −9.8), ACT. The module docstring read "no second decision engine here" — false precision — and the name-based guard `check_no_competing_engine.py` was blind to all of it (its structure-parsing sibling lives in essence-gate-must-fail.md, SN-888). Detection is behavioral: spy on the canonical evaluator (exactly one invocation per plan), require non-zero, provenance-bound PV components before scoring, and run the two-formulas-two-winners probe with a high-Q/negative-value adversarial case (unmeasured value must never win). PR #2273 closed the gap; all seven probes held at exact tip bytes — but the detection method, not the patch, is what compounds (SN-942).

**Cold eyes find what warm eyes walk past.** The Cold Activation Test's ruler is exact: a guess = any question asked, any assumption made, any step not derivable from the official knowledge pack; pass = G=0, counted in public, scored by an independent seat. The builder — warm — refused to grade her own run ("nobody grades their own homework"), ran a rigorously walled simulation, and labeled it SIMULATED, not proof. Before any true cold runner arrived, the instrument found 3 pack holes the warm team had walked past: PACK-HOLE-01, two Law-of-One registrations (0007 vs 0016) with different prose; PACK-HOLE-02, the ratified Law of One V2 (2026-10-09) not compiled into any repo governance file; PACK-HOLE-03, TWO documents each claiming to be THE canonical law (the ratified Operating Code V2 vs `BRAIN/01-GOVERNANCE/OPERATING-LAW.md`, 45 laws, still referencing V1 as ratified). The third hole was surfaced as an open constitutional question for the director — not resolved unilaterally (SN-891).

## What this settles

- Learning proof requires USER_CAPTURE evidence with identity/hash equality; TEST_FALLBACK is smoke-only (SN-817). Activation is one real lesson through all 11 stages, independently observed (SN-844).
- Replay briefs specify ref, commands, clean clone, match definition; scope is stated honestly (SN-822). Predictions are pre-registered or they are not evidence (SN-865).
- Measurable claims are audited against the artifact byte-for-byte, all mirrors fixed (SN-858); content identity is blob-SHA at pinned heads, never the compare UI (SN-861).
- A trial whose control can pass without the lesson is a broken test; test where the lesson is the differentiator (SN-938). LEARNED requires a novel-problem battery, blind-scored, novelty mechanically enforced (SN-895).
- Suites are sabotage-tested before their green counts (SN-939); evaluations are sealed — commitments in-repo, keys outside, author ≠ evaluator, gated, families refreshed (SN-900); blinding is scripted stripping + journal discipline + SHA anchoring, with exposures disclosed (SN-1169).
- A selector that never invokes the canonical evaluator is a second engine in substance; detection is behavioral (invocation count, non-zero PV, two-winners probe) (SN-942).
- The cold test converts disputed ambiguity into mechanical evidence; its findings include an open constitutional question that stays open until the director rules (SN-891).

## Human

This is the block's philosophy of testing, earned the hard way: a test proves something only if it *could* have proven the opposite. Again and again the pattern repeats — an exam whose answers are printed on the back, a race where the "untrained" runner was quietly given the map, a judge grading homework in an answer key's handwriting, a scoreboard naming a champion who never entered the race. Each repair restores the same thing: the possibility of an honest *no*, delivered by someone or something with no stake in a yes.

## Child

Testing is only fair if the test can catch you. If the answers are written on the test paper, everybody "passes" and nobody learned anything. If you peek at your friend's answers before writing yours, your matching answers don't prove you're both smart. A real test hides the answers, lets you try on your own, and sometimes you fail — and the failing is how we find out what's true.

## Grandma

It's the difference between a scale and a mirror, dear. A mirror tells you what you want to hear; a scale tells you the number whether you like it or not. These engineers kept building mirrors by accident — tests that flattered, trials that leaked, judges who could see the answer key. The craft in these notes is scale-building: sealed, blind, pre-registered, and always — always — checked with a weight you know is heavy.

## What It All Means

Thirteen notes are one theorem with many proofs: **the credibility of a verification is exactly the credibility of its failure mode.** What could have made it fail? Who was blind, and mechanically how? What bytes, at what pin, under what pre-registration? A cold reader holding only this essence can interrogate any proof in the system — a trial, a suite, a fixture, a selector, a pack — and find the seam where its yes was manufactured, if one exists. The block's most honest artifacts are its failures kept on record: the 4 battery FAILs, the ceilinged control, the SIMULATED label on a simulation.

## Why It Matters

This system proposes to retire notes, promote lessons, and certify its own learning — every one of those acts is a proof claim. If the proofs are theater, the compounding is fiction: lessons "learned" that change nothing, evaluations "passed" by their own answer keys, a canonical engine "deciding" decisions made elsewhere. For the reader, this essence is portable skepticism with receipts: the questions here (could it fail? who was blind? what's the control? where's the pre-registration?) dismantle inflated proof claims in any field, including — especially — claims made by this system about itself.

## What Is In It For You

You get the examiner's toolkit: five questions to ask of any claimed proof, each tied to a real incident where its absence cost the team a false belief. Could this test have failed? Was the judge blind — mechanically, not by promise? Did the control arm get the answer through the side door? Was the prediction written down before the event? Does the named decision-maker actually get invoked? Carry those into every evaluation you design, buy, or are shown, and manufactured certainty becomes very expensive to sell you.

## The Learning

The block's verification learning is that theater is the default state of unexamined instruments — not from dishonesty, but from drift: fixtures written next to their answers for convenience, tasks written helpfully with their preconditions showing, a selector optimized quietly around the canon it was meant to serve. What compounded is adversarial self-regard: sabotage your own suite before someone believes it, ceiling-test your own control before running the trial, spy on your own engine before claiming it decides. The instruments that survived this block are the ones whose owners attacked them first.
