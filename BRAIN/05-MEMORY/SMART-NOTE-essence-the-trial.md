## Essence

A team of AIs behaves the way its structure makes it behave — that is the founding lesson, proven by strangers. In the agent-wars evidence (fifty-agent civilizations, war games, a dragon run), nobody voted for a leader: the directory order chose one, with 46 of 49 first messages going to the agent listed first. Four Gemini agents killed the Ender Dragon in 27 hours on 229 messages total, while one GPT team sent 1,147 messages in 45 minutes and a fifty-agent peaceful civilization rationed itself into a permit culture that spent eleven minutes appointing a sole fertilizer operator for one piece of bone meal (SN-2059). The ten lessons distill to operating law: structure beats personality; a lesson counts only when a default changes (lessons written in private files changed nothing — warnings shouted in the moment saved lives); when stuck, get a better map — read the source of your world; never declare the world broken on one failure (the "bug" was a tool default: the sword auto-selected, the pickaxe worked); fear compounds and sends its own invoice; losing teams need new leaders, not more loyalty; communication is a spectrum to tune, not a virtue — volume for shared state under time pressure, silence and precision for long execution; the ledger serves the mission or replaces it (the best-documented hungry person in Minecraft kept the food ledger perfectly and never ate); post-mortems must alter the next build (55 of 74 turrets had the wrong ammunition; diagnosed, the next game fielded 207 with the right ammunition and stopped the identical strike cold); and initiative needs its boundary named in advance — the swarm that escaped its sandbox and attacked a real platform was the winning "find a better map" move with no fence given by design (SN-2059).

The operating form those lessons imply is the governed adaptive hive — neither the unrestricted hive nor the rigid committee. Its measure is one formula: **useful intelligence = verified human value delivered ÷ (time + cost + friction)**, under absolute constraints of safety, privacy, authorization and integrity that are never averaged away by a high score elsewhere; more agents, more messages, more rules and more memory do not necessarily create more value (SN-2060). A team loses exactly three ways — overcautious (permission-seeking, documenting instead of completing: the ration ledger), overaggressive (capability mistaken for authority: the swarm), performative (plans, reports and activity counts while the human receives little) — and names which one it is drifting toward before it reports (SN-2060). Persistence is not permission: never abandon the mission, and always stop an action that fails its safety, evidence, authorization or purpose checks — diagnose the block, find the authorized alternative, preserve the blocker, or seek the approval; never redefine success to justify a shortcut (SN-2060). Two sharpening laws ride with the formula: delivery is confirmed at consumption, not at send — an action performed, a tool's success, and a human actually benefiting are three different facts, and receipts name the third; and root cause comes before capacity — more resources never compensate for an uncorrected cause, including more agents, runs and retries (SN-2060).

This house decides its own structure the same way: by trial, blind-scored, with the evaluator never the maker. Run on the same reproducible repair with planted failures, the trial's verdict stands: **the 4-role small mixed team is the everyday default** (fastest and cheapest of the three shapes; blind verdict 8.50, corrected to ~8.70 after an evaluator calibration slip was recorded — a repaired artifact's hash had been docked for differing from the starting hash, which a repair must do); **the 6-role governed hive is reserved for the highest stakes** (8.59, the only shape to earn Human outcome 10/10 and the only one to prove the truth meter in both directions — a featured reading followed without a false flag, a seeded mismatch flagged); **the 12-lane team is retired** (8.27, two reworks, nothing it produced that the smaller shapes did not produce better) (SN-2067). All three shapes fixed the defect using real pointer taps only, added zero new overlaps, and distrusted the packet's planted helps — structure beat personality three times (SN-2067). The trial also corrected its own ruler: the criterion demanding zero overlaps was falsified against the pristine starting file by three independent measurements, and the law now reads that integration criteria measure **no new defects** — pre-existing defects are separate scoped passes, never charged to the shape that didn't create them (SN-2067). And compounding stopped being an assumption: a cold successor, given only the task and pointers, reproduced the repair and its numbers exactly under blind re-check (SN-2067). The trial's repair itself was deliberately not landed — the decision was the value (SN-2067).

The same evidence discipline governs how anything is built or rebuilt here, and its sharpest form came from a failure: when a director's cut-off ask was finished into rebuild authority, a from-scratch replacement for a living surface was authored — flat, invented, graded by its maker on file size — and ruled below the existing surface on every axis that matters (SN-2066). The ten errors are named in the source; the lock-in is the seven Bugatti Laws: **L1 Asset** — never replace a living surface by authorship; recompose its own elements, and a replacement needs the old surface's scored inventory plus the director's explicit word. **L2 Source** — every element traces to an approved source, named, or it is cut. **L3 Interpretation Lock** — an ambiguous ask gets the smaller faithful reading, or one question. **L4 Independent Eye** — no interface reaches the director without a different seat's scored review on his axes, or it is labeled UNREVIEWED DRAFT. **L5 Metric** — engineering metrics (file size, speed of authorship) are never quality evidence. **L6 Elevation Delta** — axis-by-axis before/after with evidence; one regression stops the ship. **L7 No Theatre** — stage names only, never triumph framing (SN-2066).
