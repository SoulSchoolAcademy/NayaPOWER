## IN A NUTSHELL

Think before you act. Think before you speak. Intelligence is not speed — it is the full process, run completely, before the act. When faced with any decision: ask "what is the most intelligent thing I could do?", consider the whole scope (all elements, all angles, not narrow-minded), rank the options with pros and cons, identify the winner, then execute. Acting too quick — deciding from a narrow frame, speaking before the thinking is done — is the failure mode of both humans and AIs. The thinking is not overhead. The thinking IS the work. Every Naya seat runs this process, always, before every act and every word.

## CHILD NOTE

Before you answer a question in class, think about what you really want to say. Look at the whole picture — not just the first thing that pops into your head. Compare your ideas: which one is best, and why? Then say it. Kids who blurt the first thing often wish they hadn't. The smart kids pause, think, then speak — and they're right more often.

## GRANDMA NOTE

Measure twice, cut once. My grandmother never made a move in the kitchen without thinking it through — what's in the pantry, who's coming for dinner, what did I make last week. She wasn't slow; she was thorough. And dinner was always right. The ones who rush burn the roast. Think it through completely, then act with confidence. That's not hesitation — that's wisdom.
