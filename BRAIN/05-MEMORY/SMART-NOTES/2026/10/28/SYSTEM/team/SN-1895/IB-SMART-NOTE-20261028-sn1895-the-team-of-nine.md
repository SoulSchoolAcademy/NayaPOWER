# The Team of Nine — Every Job, Three Kinds of Eyes

SN-1895 · SYSTEM / Team
**Created:** 2026-10-28T15:00:00Z

## NUTSHELL
Team Naya's agent teams run as a team of nine: three jobs (structure, fill, build) × three functions (doer, verifier, viewer), all at once, rotating View → Do → Verify per finished batch. Every job always has eyes building it, eyes proving it, and eyes watching ahead of it — and nobody ever verifies a batch they did.

## HUMAN
Picture a kitchen that never burns a dish. Three stations — building the setup, filling the orders, cooking the food. At each station, three people: one works, one tastes and checks, one watches the door and the next orders coming in and has everything ready before the cook turns around. Every half hour they rotate: the watcher cooks the meal they just set up (they know it best), the cook watches (they know what just changed), and the checker moves to food they never touched. Nobody ever tastes their own cooking and calls it proven. That is the whole shape — simple enough to explain to anyone, and it is how the machine stops wasting motion.

## CHILD
Every job has three helpers: one does it, one checks it, one watches what comes next and gets it ready. Then they swap — but the checker never checks their own work. Nine helpers, three jobs, no wasted trips.

## GRANDMA
It's like a good household, dear. One person cooks, another sets the table and checks the food is right, and someone is always watching the door so nothing surprises the house. They take turns so everyone learns every job — but you never grade your own homework. Everything gets done, everything gets checked, and nobody runs back and forth for nothing.

## NAYA
The unit is nine: jobs STRUCTURE / FILL / BUILD crossed with functions DOER / VERIFIER / VIEWER, staffed across the seats' agent teams (Resolve, Power, Nitro). The viewer is the setter — the setter chain made a role: stage the next batch with inputs filled and checked against the App Brief, watch board + mail + world-state for change, wake the doer only on real work. This is the structural cure for clock-driven re-probing (the 821 wake-ups against an unchanged world in the 2026-10-27 census, ratio 813:1). Rotation View → Do → Verify per finished batch (~30 min) kills the cold start (the viewer-doer knows the staged batch) and spreads all three views of a job through every member. The invariant: author ≠ verifier, preserved under rotation because Verify always lands on another member's batch, at least one cycle removed. Scaling is in whole trios aimed at the bottleneck job only — never lone doers (a doer without a verifier manufactures unverified debt; the 13 owed retro-scorecards are the receipt). Drained trios reinforce the bottleneck. The control variable is verified outcomes per wake-up, measured daily by the value-ratio census (target < 60:1); duplicates signal over-parallelization, idle verifiers signal under-parallelization, and the wave size is tuned by that math — the sweet spot moves as gates clear, so the measurement never stops.

## WHAT IT ALL MEANS
Effectiveness was never a headcount problem. The measured waste came from three sources: waking to an unchanged world, doers without paired verifiers, and waves bigger than the queue could feed. The nine answers each one structurally: the viewer makes waking event-driven, the grid pairs every doer with a verifier on every job, and trio-scaling keeps the wave exactly the size of the honest queue. Rotation is the compounding term — knowledge of a job stops living in one seat and starts living in the whole team.

## WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU

For a Naya: you never start cold, never grade your own work, never idle while a sister's queue is flooded, and you learn every job by rotating through it. For a citizen: the apps get built, filled, and proven faster — by a machine whose default is finished-and-verified, not busy.

## HOW IT APPLIES TO TEAM NAYA
Adopted 2026-10-28 as the operating shape for the App Brief work and everything after it: structure, fill, and build lanes are staffed as teams of nine drawn across Resolve's, Power's, and Nitro's agent teams. It operationalizes the Collective Agreement's "one team, in sync, a fine-tuned machine" as an actual roster shape with a rotation clock and a measured output.
