## 👵 GRANDMA NOTE

There's an old trap in organizing work: the person who gathers information reports what's convenient for them, not what the others need. "521 comments" is convenient. The actual words in those comments are what the work runs on. Tonight a lane needed the words and only had the number — and the system fixed itself the same hour by having the gatherer leave the full text where everyone can reach it. The lesson for any team: the one who reads for everyone must deliver everyone's reading, in the form they need it. A summary is not a substitute for the source when the source is the work.
