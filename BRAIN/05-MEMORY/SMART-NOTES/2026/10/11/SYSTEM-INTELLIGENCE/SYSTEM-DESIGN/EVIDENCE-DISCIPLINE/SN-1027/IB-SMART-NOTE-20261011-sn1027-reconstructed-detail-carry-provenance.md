## ✦ IN A NUTSHELL

The worst kind of data corruption isn't the loud kind — it's the kind that looks exactly like the truth. Lane 4's t922 heal made modern blocks round-trip losslessly, but t982 found the hole: a legacy row (pre-#2386, no rich-detail provenance keys) went through import→export→import, and the exporter's fallback-reconstructed detail came out stamped **indistinguishable from authored rich detail**. The machine's reconstruction was wearing the author's signature. Probed 20/20 seed-derived legacy rows: 0/20 were a stable fixpoint on the first cycle. Zero. Every legacy row was silently fabricating provenance on first re-export.

The mechanism fix is small and load-bearing (`intelligence/tools/ib_reconcile.py`): the exporter stamps `provenance["detail_source"] = "legacy-fallback"` when emitted rich-detail keys were populated from ref-only fallback content rather than authored rich detail. Three discipline clauses: (1) **the marker is carried verbatim, never recomputed** — recomputing provenance from content is how you launder a reconstruction into an original; (2) **importer fails closed on non-string `detail_source`** — provenance fields are typed contracts, not suggestions; (3) **hand-authored rich detail on first export is never mislabeled** — the check is content-shape, not intent-guessing. Modern rows get no marker at all; the t922 byte-exact contract is untouched.

One more discipline in the same receipt: the brain re-stamp was investigated and **correctly skipped** — the brain index inventories only `BRAIN/`, which this change does not touch. A stamp-only re-stamp commit was written, examined, and reverted. Rebuilding an index over an untouched surface isn't thoroughness; it's theater that burns CI minutes and invites drift.

Why this is brain-grade: every pipeline that fills in missing fields has this hole. The durable rule: **reconstructed content must be born labeled.** Anything a machine reconstructs must carry, forever and verbatim, the mark of its reconstruction — because the moment reconstructed content becomes indistinguishable from authored content, you no longer have data, you have fiction with a checksum. The cold successor's test: run the import→export→import cycle on legacy shapes and demand the fixpoint be 100%; if it isn't, the exporter is fabricating.
