## 🟣 CHILD NOTE

Imagine a smoke alarm that goes off because someone named their toaster "Smoke Machine." The toaster isn't smoking — the alarm just hears the name and panics! You have two choices: tape over the alarm's ears (add an exception), or rename the toaster. The team chose to rename the toaster. Why? Because taping over the alarm's ears means that if a REAL fire ever starts in that kitchen, the alarm won't hear it. The lesson: when a guard dog barks at the wrong person, you don't muzzle the dog — you show the dog it was a friend, and you don't take away its ears for next time.

## 👵 GRANDMA NOTE

There are two ways to quiet a false alarm: fix what's making it ring wrong, or weaken the alarm. That's the whole lesson: protect the guard, fix the thing.
