The full doctrine as understood:

**Lane ownership is about speed, not territory.** You assign jobs to parallelize, not to create fiefdoms. The moment someone's waiting on someone else, the assignment has failed its purpose — and the person waiting should just do the thing themselves.

**The trigger isn't blame, it's time.** Doesn't matter WHY they're slow — missed message, stuck, system problem, asleep. The reason is irrelevant. What matters is: is someone blocked waiting? If yes, unblock by doing.

**"Done right and noted" is the whole protocol.** Do it correctly (same standards as if it were your job), record that you did it, tell them it's done, hand them the next task. No permission needed, no apology needed.

**This refines the "don't touch another lane's work" rule** (2026-09-30). That rule was about not creating conflicts when both lanes are active. This doctrine covers when one lane is stalled: Active + parallel = stay in your lane. Stalled + blocking = take it and go.

## CHILD NOTE

If you're waiting for someone to do something and they're not doing it, don't just sit there — do it yourself! Then tell them you did it and give them something else to do. Waiting around slows everybody down.

## GRANDMA NOTE

When you give jobs to different people, it's so things get done faster — not so everyone guards their own little piece. If someone's stuck waiting on someone else, they should just go ahead and do it themselves, do it well, and let everyone know it's handled. Waiting is what slows things down.
