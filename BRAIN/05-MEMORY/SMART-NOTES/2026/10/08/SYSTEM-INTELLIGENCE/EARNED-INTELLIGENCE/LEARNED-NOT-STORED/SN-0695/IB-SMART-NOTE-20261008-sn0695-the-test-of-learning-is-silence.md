Shawn's 2026-10-08 teaching sets the only falsifiable definition of "learned" the learning system may use: **"A lesson isn't learned when it's noted. It's learned when behavior changes permanently without being told again. The test of learning is silence."** The candidate governance doctrine turns it into an eight-rung ladder — CAPTURED → UNDERSTOOD → RETRIEVABLE → APPLIED → VERIFIED → GENERALIZED → INTERNALIZED → AUTOMATIC — with the closure rule: **learning is incomplete until future behavior changes appropriately and that change can be evidenced.** The manual-driving analogy behind it: telling != comprehension; stored != learned; learned != mastered until later authorized behavior improves without repeated human prompting. ## 🟣 CHILD NOTE

Imagine a teacher teaching you to ride a bike. She can talk about balance all day (telling), and you can even recite her words back (noted, stored). But if she has to keep reminding you to lean the right way every time you pedal, you haven't *learned* it. The day she goes quiet and you still balance — that's learning. Shawn says: that's the test for everything. A lesson is only real when the reminder stops and the good behavior stays.
