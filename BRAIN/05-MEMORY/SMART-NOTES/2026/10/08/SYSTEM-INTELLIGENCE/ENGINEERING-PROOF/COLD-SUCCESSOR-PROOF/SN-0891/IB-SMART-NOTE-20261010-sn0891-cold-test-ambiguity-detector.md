## IN A NUTSHELL
The 2026-10-10 Cold Activation Test (Operation Flow Like Water, workstream 6) shipped as an instrument with a precise ruler: a guess = any question asked, any assumption made, any step not derivable from the official knowledge pack — pass = zero guesses (G=0), counted in public, scored by an independent seat. The builder was warm, refused to grade her own run ("Nobody grades their own homework"), and ran a rigorously walled-off simulation instead, labeled honestly SIMULATED — not proof. The instrument proved its value before any true cold runner arrived: it found 3 pack holes the warm team had walked past. PACK-HOLE-01: two Law-of-One registrations (0007 vs 0016) with different prose. PACK-HOLE-02: ratified Law of One V2 (2026-10-09) not compiled into any repo governance file. PACK-HOLE-03: TWO documents each claiming to be THE canonical law — the ratified Operating Code V2 vs `BRAIN/01-GOVERNANCE/OPERATING-LAW.md` (45 laws, still referencing V1 as ratified) — surfaced as an open constitutional question for Shawn, not resolved unilaterally. The lesson: familiarity is the enemy of pack clarity. A warm team debates ambiguity; the cold ruler converts it into a mechanical failure. Run the cold test and the pack's contradictions surface as evidence, not opinions.
