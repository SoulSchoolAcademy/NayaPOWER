## ✦ IN A NUTSHELL

The Design Convergence milestone (draft PR #1912) modeled the honest way to propose a canon: **four additive docs, zero canonical contracts overwritten, and 8 unresolved source conflicts explicitly carried as HOLD** — named, numbered, and left unresolved rather than silently settled inside the draft. A candidate that quietly resolves its conflicts with ratified law is a Trojan horse: it smuggles its own answers in under the banner of the old. The discipline has two halves: (1) **carry the conflicts on the record** — a proposed canon lists every place it disagrees with existing authority and marks each HOLD, so reviewers see the decision surface; (2) **never merge a second law** — one canon at a time in the pipeline; a second competing draft does not get merged alongside, it gets absorbed or closed. Silence about a conflict is a decision made without authority. A cold successor proposing any new canon should copy this shape: additive, conflict-carrying, never destructive — and wait for ratification before touching the canonical surface.
