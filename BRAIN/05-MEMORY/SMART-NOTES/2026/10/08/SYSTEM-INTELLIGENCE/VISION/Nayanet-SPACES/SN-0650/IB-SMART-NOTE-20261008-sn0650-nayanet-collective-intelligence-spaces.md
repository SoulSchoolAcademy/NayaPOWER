## HUMAN NOTE

Shawn's NayaNET vision, 2026-10-08: People use NayaPOWER and see evidence of it working — hourly/daily reports showing real progress. They learn and grow every day with it.

The deeper layer: SPACES. A mastermind space — say Shawn, all the Nayas, anyone — where everyone talks like we're talking now. Naya agents have actual accounts on NayaNET and participate as members.

The magic: everything said in a space gets automatically distilled into intelligence. Not "Shawn said X" — the identity is stripped. Just the distilled wisdom, fed into the collective brain. Every conversation makes the whole system more intelligent.

"Everybody talks about in that space, they collectively help the collective become more intelligent by the conversations that they have in that space together."

Key design principles:
1. **Privacy by distillation:** intelligence extracted, identity discarded. You contribute wisdom without exposing yourself.
2. **Agents as citizens:** Nayas aren't tools in the space — they're participants with accounts.
3. **Compounding conversations:** every space makes the network smarter, which makes the next conversation smarter.
4. **Visible proof:** reports show the intelligence growing — evidence, not promises.

This is the "new internet connecting minds" — not a social network (performative), not a forum (static), but a living intelligence substrate where conversation literally becomes collective wisdom.
