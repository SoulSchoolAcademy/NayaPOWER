# The Welcome Flow — Speak It, Type It, or Ask Your AI
SN-0846 · SYSTEM / activation
**Created:** 2026-10-24T01:12:00Z

## IN A NUTSHELL
Shawn locked the activation design (2026-10-24): onboarding is a slide-by-slide welcome — one question per screen, in the style of the Life with Naya page — and every question offers THREE ways to answer: **A. Speak it** (dictation button — say your vision, your mission, your goal, what you're into) · **B. Type it** (for typers) · **C. Ask your AI to write it for you** (copy-paste it in). Next/Skip always available. The questions are not small talk — they ARE the match dimensions: about you · what you're into · your business · your vision · your goal · your mission · your motto. When the flow ends, the system already knows the person, so it does two things at once: **creates their world** (Personal Smart App, Business page, Space — born filled with their own answers) and **starts the game of match** (Smart Match, SN-0845, plays from minute one). One structured process: a person shares all about them, and NayaNET builds and matches their world from it.

## HUMAN
The flow, step by step:
1. **Welcome.** "Hello, I'm Naya." One screen, one promise: answer a few questions your way, and your world gets built for you.
2. **One question per slide.** Tell me about you · What are you into? · What's your business? · What's your vision? · What's your goal? · What's your mission? · Your motto — the words you live by. Big question, calm screen, nothing else competing.
3. **Three ways to answer, every time.** Speak it — one button, talk naturally, edit the words after if you wish. Type it — a clean well, for people who think on a keyboard. Ask your AI — "ask your AI to write it for you, then paste it here": millions already have an AI that knows them; the paste door meets them where they are instead of making them start from zero.
4. **Skip is a right.** Next without answering; a skipped question is an empty field, never a wall. The flow can be finished later from the page itself.
5. **The reveal.** The last screen shows what was built: your page, your business page, your space — already filled with your own words — and your first matches with their percentages. The person sees the system WORK with what they gave, immediately.

## CHILD
It is like a friendly game of twenty questions where you can talk, write, or bring an answer a friend helped you write. When the game ends, your clubhouse is already built with your name on it — and the game has already found the other kids who love what you love.

## GRANDMA
No long forms. One question at a time, on a calm page, and you may simply speak your answer out loud — the way you'd tell a friend. If you'd rather type, type. If your own helper program knows you well, let it write the answer and just paste it in. Then the system builds your pages from your own words and introduces you to your people.

## NAYA
Why this flow is the algorithm's front door: the match dimensions (SN-0845) and the onboarding questions are the SAME list — into, business, vision, goal, mission, motto, about. Declared answers carry the highest signal weight in the matcher because they are the person's own statement of identity. Design rules: (1) harvest the style — the Life with Naya page is the experience source (slide rhythm, calm one-thing screens); do not invent a new onboarding language (NO-REDESIGN + READ-BEFORE-DESIGN gates apply). (2) Dictation is first-class: the same voice path as the rest of NayaNET; speech lands as editable text before it saves — the person always sees and owns their words. (3) The AI-paste door is a door, not a trick: label it proudly; pasted answers are the person's answers, marked declared just like spoken or typed ones. (4) Every answer writes to the citizen's record ONCE and renders everywhere from it (page, business, space, matcher) — one home per fact. (5) Skipped questions stay visibly unfinished on the person's own page (a gentle "complete your story" door), never nagging, never blocking.
The build composes three existing programs: the Trinity build (the pages this flow creates — brief on board #2793, comment 6376824589), Smart Match (the engine the answers feed — SN-0845), and the Production Engine's intake of value (SN-0844). Lock the trio as one activation system: **Welcome Flow → Trinity → Match.**

## MACHINE
FLOW: slides[about, into, business, vision, goal, mission, motto] × input_modes[speech→editable transcript, typed, ai_paste]; skip allowed per slide; answers persist per-slide on entry (no end-loss). On complete (or sufficient subset): create citizen world {personal_app, business_page, space} rendered FROM the answers; emit matcher seed {topics from into/business, identity statements, declared weight=max}. Invariants: one write per fact, renders derive from the record; input mode is metadata, never a quality signal; a page is never created empty when answers exist.

## WHAT IT ALL MEANS
Activation is where a network either learns who you are or wastes your first visit on a blank profile. This flow makes the first visit the most valuable one: the person shares themselves in the easiest way they have — voice, keys, or their own AI — and receives a built world and real matches in return. Structured sharing in, living system out.

## WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU
Five calm minutes, in your own voice — literally — and you never face an empty page. Your words become your pages, and your answers become your introductions.
