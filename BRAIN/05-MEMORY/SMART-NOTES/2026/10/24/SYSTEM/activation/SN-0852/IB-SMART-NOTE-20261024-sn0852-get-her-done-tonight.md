## AMENDMENT — FINDABLE ≠ VISIBLE: THE PERSONAL DEFAULT IS PRIVATE **AND** FINDABLE (Shawn, 2026-10-24 ~02:55Z)
Shawn's clarification, and it corrects the defaults table above: **visibility and findability are truly independent, and "findable" never meant "people can search up your page."** FINDABLE = the system includes you — the search index and the matching algorithm work with your ALIAS and your declared topics, so matches compute, suggestions flow, and people can CONNECT with you. It reveals nothing else: not your page content, not your real identity, not your details. **The corrected born default for PERSONAL: visibility PRIVATE · findability ON** — private-findable: protected, yet fully connectable, because a citizen who is invisible to the matcher is shut out of the network's value for no reason. The owner may then: make the page PUBLIC (also findable — content joins the alias in view), or turn findability OFF (private + unfindable: fully dark to matching and search; the link still works for whoever they hand it to). Business and Space defaults stand (public + findable). **Layered disclosure is the design:** the CONNECT layer (alias · topics · match % · reasons) is what the system shows the world by default; the REVEAL layer (page content, real details) is the owner's separate, later choice — and even a public page never exposes everything on it by default. **The governing principle, in Shawn's words: be smart about what makes the most sense — by default choose what's logical and gives the citizen the highest value: maximum connection, minimum exposure.** This matches the matcher's founding privacy rule (SN-0845: the matcher only ever sees aliases and declared signals) — tonight's builders implement it exactly so, and the negative tests now include: a private-findable citizen IS matched by alias+topics, and their page content still REFUSES every non-owner.

## AMENDMENT 2 — EVERY PAGE IS A WORLD, AND EVERY WORLD HAS ITS OWN COMMUNITY (Shawn, 2026-10-24 ~03:01Z)
Shawn's completion of the model: going public never requires opening your private page. **A citizen can create a SEPARATE public page alongside their private one** — different content on each, by design: what you post to your public page is not what you post to your private page. And **connections live at the page level: every page carries its own community.** Your public page gathers your public community; your private page gathers your private community — different people, for different reasons, and neither circle leaks into the other. Everybody has a private community, and a public one if they want one. This is why the settings are per-page and the pages are unlimited: a page is not a setting on one profile — it is a world, with its own audience, its own connections, its own life. The defaults stay as ruled (logical, highest-value, privacy-respecting) and every setting stays fully adjustable by the owner — we never force anyone to reveal anything; we give them worlds, and they decide who enters each one.
