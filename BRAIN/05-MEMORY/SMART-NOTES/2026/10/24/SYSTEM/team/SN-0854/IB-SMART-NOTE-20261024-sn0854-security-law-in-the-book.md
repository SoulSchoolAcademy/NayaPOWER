# Security Is Light — and Now It's the Law of Every Space

SN-0854 · SYSTEM / team
**Created:** 2026-10-24T18:54:40Z

## IN A NUTSHELL
The security law is on file, everybody knows, and it ships as features in every space and page — not just as a document. Resolve wrote the law itself (SN-1855, "Security Is Light"): transparency plus composition, the system secured by light instead of walls. Shawn ratified it in the open — "this one's going in the book as the security law." This note is the record that we talked about it, agreed it, and put it on the plan list. And the plan says where it shows up: in Smart Spaces, in Personal pages, and in Business pages — the places where small teams gather, talk, and call each other in private spaces they control. Every page can be private or public, by the owner's choice, and anyone can hold multiple pages — one dedicated to public, one dedicated to private. That is the magic of the system, and the security law is what makes it safe to offer.

## HUMAN
Here is what was agreed, plainly. The law (SN-1855) says: every action lands on the ledger with identity, authority scope, and value attached, so there is no dark corner to operate from; a hostile move posts negative against its own author's profile automatically, by math, without anyone having to accuse anyone; and because everything is built from blocks, a compromised piece gets isolated and swapped like a bad Lego brick, with its provenance chain showing everything it ever touched, so damage is findable and reversible. Three mechanisms complete the model, and they are on the plan list: the gate decides before and the ledger proves after — capability alone never grants permission, sight plus a lock beats either alone; watch the pattern, not just the act — velocity and pattern math spot the shape of an attack while it is forming; and let friction be automatic and reversible — a profile diving into negative value gets tighter limits and more verification, and reopens as the record recovers, no grudges, no politics, just consequences proportional to proven behavior. One honest anchor: the model is the destination, and today there is exactly one known back door — the mail system's anonymous access is too open. It is already found, named, and on the cure path, and the law says it must be the last of its kind, never a precedent. The Nayas as the standing security watch is already half-built into how this team runs: verification seats, watchers before features, citizens who live in the system and notice what does not belong. An inhabited system is a watched system.

## CHILD
Imagine a clubhouse with big windows instead of high walls. Everyone inside can see what everyone is doing, and everything gets written in the clubhouse book with your name beside it. If you are kind, the book shows it. If you sneak, the book shows that too. And your clubhouse has two rooms: one with the door open for visitors, one with the door closed just for your friends. You get to choose which room is which, and you can have as many rooms as you like. That is how we stay safe and free at the same time.

## GRANDMA
Think of a house with a shop window out front and a living room out back. The shop window is public — anyone walking by can look in. The living room is private — only the people you invite come in. You can have both, in the same house, and you decide which is which. And because everything that happens in the house is written down in the open book, nobody can quietly move the furniture or take something that is yours. That is the whole idea: your spaces, your choice, and a record that keeps everyone honest.
