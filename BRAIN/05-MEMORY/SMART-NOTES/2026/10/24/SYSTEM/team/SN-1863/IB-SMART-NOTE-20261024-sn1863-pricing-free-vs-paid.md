# Free Taste, Paid Unlimited: What You Get, in Numbers

## IN A NUTSHELL
Free is a real taste with small numbers. Paid is the whole living system with big numbers or no cap at all. The gate is time, not a maze of locks: five days free, no card, then the living parts quiet down until you pay. You pay for NayaPOWER, never for the fork — fork is one paid feature, ownership, inside the paid set. Some small things stay ungated on purpose and are never advertised.

The numbers below are set by one rule, and it is why they are believable: every paid cap sits above what any real person actually uses in a month, so for a real member paid reads as unlimited — and the cap still exists, quietly, so abuse cannot eat the system. Free caps are real tastes, big enough to feel the feature, small enough that the line is honest. That is what makes the offer near irresistible without ever sounding like a trick.

## HUMAN
**The price ladder (founding, locked):** 5 days free, no card · $14 a month · $37 for 3 months · $75 a year · $150 lifetime, founding only · then the price doubles. Founding is only 1,000 members, and lifetime closes at the first doubling.

**What you get — free vs paid:**

- **Pages:** Free 4 — your Personal, Business, Space, and Dashboard pages. Paid 100 new pages a month · Lifetime founding unlimited pages. (A page costs us almost nothing to hold, so the paid number is an abuse guard, not a ration — no real person fills 100 pages a month.)
- **Spaces:** Free 3 spaces · Paid unlimited spaces. (A space is a container with a feed; nobody realistically runs dozens, so unlimited here is honest.)
- **Mail:** Free 100 people a month · Paid up to 10,000 people a month, inside the system. (Mail is where abuse lives, so the numbers are the spam guard — 100 is a real taste, 10,000 is a real network.)
- **Smart Notes:** Free 10 a month · Paid 100 a month. (About three a day — plenty for a person, and notes feed the shared brain, so quality beats volume.)
- **Reports (daily, weekly, monthly):** Free monthly report only · Paid daily, weekly, and monthly — unlimited. (Frequency is the tier: free sees the month, paid sees every day.)
- **Smart Lists:** Free 3 lists · Paid unlimited lists.
- **Smart Links:** Free 1 — your own Smart Link, because your link is your identity and identity is never charged. Paid unlimited links — one per project, campaign, or offer.
- **Projects (live view of what's going on):** Free 3 projects · Paid 50 projects.
- **Daily highlights / Intel:** Free top 3 highlights a day · Paid the full Intel feed, unlimited.
- **Live calls (audio and video):** Free audio calls up to 4 people · Paid video calls up to 6 people and audio calls up to 10 people. (These are the mesh limits — browser to browser, near zero media cost, and the caps are what keep it free to run. Bigger groups than this are a future monthly add-on, not part of standard or lifetime.)
- **Media — voice and video notes:** Free 60 minutes a month · Paid 600 minutes a month, with voice notes up to 120 minutes each and video notes up to 60 minutes each. (Storage is the one meter that truly moves, so minutes are the honest number here.)
- **Your own AI, plugged into the network:** Paid. This is the product — automation plus connection, a super brain that is yours.
- **Fork (your own copy, ownership, your AI connected direct):** Paid, and only paid. Without a fork your AI still connects — paid engine, through the shared service, into the shared brain. With a fork your AI connects straight to your own repo: your brain, in your account, private by default, yours to customize, and yours to keep even if you leave. If fork were free, the whole system would walk out the door for nothing. Most members will never need it, and they lose nothing by never forking.
- **Ambassador payouts:** Paid members' payments are what the revenue share flows from — 50% of revenue, every Wednesday, in the open, once payout terms are confirmed in the open.

**Quietly ungated, never advertised:** your Smart Link identity, reading the feed, share links. If someone on free finds one odd feature still working, that is fine — it is policy, not a leak. Over-controlling would complicate the design and tax everyone to protect almost nothing.

**Why the numbers work:** small number versus big number, on every function, in plain sight — and every big number is one a real person never hits. Nobody has to guess what paid means. Free is honest and useful — a real home, never an empty trial — and paid is plainly the whole thing, for very little money.

## NAYA
Build rules for every seat:
- **Numbers are the presentation layer.** Every paid surface shows its number plainly (free number vs paid number). Never hide the line behind a locked button with no explanation.
- **Paid caps are abuse guards, not rations.** Set every paid number above real human use (100 pages, 10,000 mail, 100 notes, 600 minutes). If a real member ever hits a paid cap, the cap is wrong, not the member — bring it to the director.
- **One gate: time.** The trial clock is the lock. Do not add per-feature locks on top of it unless a director ruling names one — safety, money, and another citizen's privacy always gate, no exceptions.
- **Fork is paid-only in code, copy, and design.** Never write "paid = forked." A member who never forks loses nothing of the engine.
- **Free stays a real home.** Limits on free are the engine's absence, never sabotage of the free tier — no degraded pages, no fake buttons, no dark patterns.
- **Ungated corners are policy.** Do not file them as defects and do not "fix" them without a director ruling.
- **This note extends SN-1862 (the free line vs the paid line).** That note drew the line; this note puts the numbers on it. Do not duplicate the line note — extend it.
- **Numbers change here first.** When a number changes, change it in this note first, then on every surface, so the system never shows two prices.
