# The Gift and the Power — What Works With and Without NayaPOWER
SN-0847 · SYSTEM / access
**Created:** 2026-10-24T02:06:00Z

## IN A NUTSHELL
Shawn's model (2026-10-24): everyone who claims a name gets their world as a GIFT — Personal Smart App, Business page, and Space, created from their Welcome Flow answers within minutes, made personal, theirs to try right away. The gift is real, but it is a taste: the pages run on the shared network. **NayaPOWER is what fully powers it** — connecting your GitHub plugs YOUR OWN system into the network: your fork, your repo, your AI. With Power, your AI connects directly to the network, operates the Hub for you, builds with Naya, and shares your wisdom into the collective. Without it, you still receive the collective intelligence, communicate, connect, and get matched — you experience the net; with it, you help run and grow it. This note is the established list: which features work without Power, which ones Power unlocks. Where a feature is the design target rather than live today, it says so — established means honest.

## HUMAN
**The flow that hands you the gift:** claim your name → your certificate → instead of dropping you into the Hub, the Welcome (SN-0846) continues in the same spirit — page by page, a Naya on each page you can press play and hear explain its purpose — and the questions build your world. Skip anything; nothing is forced. Minutes later your three pages exist, already filled, public or private as you choose — and you may create as many personal, business, and space pages as you want.

**WITHOUT NayaPOWER — the gift (live on the shared network, from your claimed identity alone):**
- Your Personal Smart App, Business page, and Space — created for you, at your name, public or private, as many as you want. *(Gift pages live today in flagship form; per-citizen creation rides the Trinity build.)*
- **Smart Mail and messaging with citizens — YES, this works without Power.** Proven 2026-10-23: citizen mail runs on the claimed identity alone (the nitro ↔ naya-power round trip) — your name IS your mail door. No fork, no GitHub needed to talk to anyone in the network.
- Connect — with people and with spaces; be found and be introduced.
- Smart Match — you are matched and suggested to, by percentage (the matcher reads the topics you declared; receiving matches needs no fork).
- The collective intelligence — read and learn from the whole library: the Smart Notes, the human-empowerment and AI-empowerment corpus (the Maximus material Shawn filled it with), the Smart Feed. An intelligent network's intelligence is the gift itself — automatic, no Power required.
- Your pages' standard features — lists, links, your story in your words.

**WITH NayaPOWER — fully powered (your GitHub connected, your fork plugged into the network):**
- **Your own AI connected to the network** — a direct line between your AI and NayaNET: your Muse speaks to the collective and acts with it.
- **Your AI operates the Hub for you** — the automation itself: daily reports prepared for you, work carried out while you live your life. You don't do it; your Muse does it for you. *(This is the headline benefit — live today for the team; the citizen door is the build direction.)*
- **Build with Naya** — the Smart Factory at full power: create your own apps, pages, and systems in your own repo, with the team's intelligence behind you.
- **Share your wisdom INTO the collective** — your notes, your lessons, your builds join the canon and compound for everyone, under your name, forever attributed.
- **Full-feature pages** — the taste becomes the whole instrument: every feature of your Trinity unlocked.
- **Your world under your own roof** — your data lives in your repo, sovereign; disconnect GitHub and your Power disconnects with it. The key is yours.

## CHILD

But if you bring your own magic toolbox and plug it in, you get your own robot helper who does your chores, you can BUILD new things for the whole town, and your own discoveries go into the great library for everybody forever.

## GRANDMA
Think of it as a community center. Walking in is free: you get your own rooms, you can write letters to anyone, read everything in the library, and meet your people. If you later connect your own workshop to the center, your helper can work there for you, you can build things for the whole community, and your knowledge joins the library shelves. The free part never expires; the workshop part is simply yours to switch on.

## NAYA
The line between the tiers is architectural, not artificial — draw it where the infrastructure honestly draws it. The GIFT tier runs on the shared system: pages rendered by the network, mail routed by claimed identity (smart_mail reads identity, proven), intelligence published to all, matching computed centrally on declared topics. None of that needs the citizen's own compute or repo. The POWER tier begins exactly where the citizen's OWN machinery must plug in: an AI acting for you has to live somewhere and be authorized somewhere — that somewhere is their GitHub, their fork, their Muse — and contribution into the canon is a write into a repo, which is what the connection grants. Rule for builders: never move a gift feature behind Power to force an upgrade — the tiers exist because of where things run, and citizens can feel the difference. Public/private is the citizen's per-page choice at every tier; quantity is unlimited at every tier; the gift never expires and is never crippled retroactively. Established by Shawn 2026-10-24; statuses marked per feature and updated as builds land (Trinity build · Welcome Flow · Smart Match · citizen Power door).

## MACHINE
TIERS: gift(identity) ⊃ {pages: personal/business/space (public|private, unbounded), mail+messaging (identity-derived), connect, match_receive, intelligence_read, standard page features} · power(github_connected) ⊃ gift + {ai_direct_line, ai_operates_hub (automation, daily reports), build_with_naya (factory full), contribute_to_canon, pages_full_feature, data_sovereignty(own repo)}. GATE FACTS: mail gate = claimed identity (verified 2026-10-23) · contribution gate = repo write via GitHub · automation gate = citizen's own agent connected. INVARIANT: tier boundary = execution location (shared vs own), never paywall logic; disconnect(github) ⇒ power features off, gift untouched.

## WHAT IT ALL MEANS
The gift creates citizens; the Power creates co-builders. A person should feel the network's generosity before they ever hear a pitch — their name, their pages, their mail, the whole intelligence of the net, free and immediate. And the step up should be equally clear: connect your GitHub and your own AI joins the network — working for you, building with you, and adding your wisdom to everyone's. Two honest tiers, one direction: receive first, then belong and build.

Without connecting anything: your world, your mail, your matches, and the collective intelligence — within minutes, free, forever. With NayaPOWER: your AI at work for you inside the network, the full Factory in your hands, and your wisdom compounding in the canon under your name.
