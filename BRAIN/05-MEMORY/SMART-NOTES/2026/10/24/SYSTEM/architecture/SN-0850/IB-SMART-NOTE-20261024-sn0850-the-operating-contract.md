# The Operating Contract — One Brain, Many Doors
SN-0850 · SYSTEM / architecture
**Created:** 2026-10-24T02:36:00Z

## IN A NUTSHELL
The governing rule, locked (Shawn's review, 2026-10-24; architecture recorded in Issue #3029, boundary in Issue #3028, the tier rule in SN-0848): **one network, one governed intelligence core, many authorized doors, two clearly differentiated experiences.** NayaNET is the network — Personal Worlds, Business Pages, Spaces, Connections, the collective intelligence. NayaPOWER is the one governed core behind it — identity, authority, canonical operations, memory, evidence, learning. The doors — Hub · MCP · REST/API · GitHub App · SDK · Webhooks · A2A — are doors into the SAME brain, never separate brains: a Naya arriving through any door reaches the same capabilities, respects the same permissions, and leaves the same evidence. **No Naya needs her own fork to participate** — agents and citizens use the shared, governed capabilities through the doors. A fork is something else entirely, and this contract states it precisely: **a fork is a copy of the code — it does not, by itself, create a running personal NayaPOWER node.** Personal activation = fork + deployment/activation + owner binding + isolated private state + verification. Each of those five is real work, and none may be skipped or implied.

## HUMAN
**What this means for a person:** you never need to copy or run anything to belong — claim your name and the network's doors are open to you (SN-0847's gift). If you later want your OWN NayaPOWER — your personal intelligence layer, running for you — that is an activation, not a download: your copy, brought to life, bound to you, your private state isolated from everyone else's, and verified working before anyone calls it on. **The offer, in its candidate words: "Your world. Your intelligence. Your network."** — start with a personal world already taking shape around you; discover the knowledge, people, projects, and communities that matter to you; and when you want your own Naya to do more, connect NayaPOWER and unlock memory, learning, authorized actions, reports, and automation — **as each capability becomes available and is verified.** The final public offer may contain only capabilities that pass their release tests (#3028); the words never run ahead of the proof.

**The honest economics:** the goal is near-zero MARGINAL cost — one more citizen, one more match, one more page should cost nearly nothing, because matching and organizing are deterministic math on shared machinery (event-driven, cached, no unnecessary AI calls). It is NOT zero total cost, and we never promise that: hosting, storage, security, messaging, and optional model inference are real costs the network carries. Near-zero at the margin, honest in total — that is the economics of the contract, measured per successful outcome (build sequence step 6).

## CHILD
There's one big library with one wise librarian-brain, and lots of doors into it — the front door, the side door, a door for helpers. Whichever door you walk through, it's the same library and the same rules. You never have to build your own library to read the books. If one day you want your OWN librarian who only works for you, we don't just hand you a photocopy of the library — we help you set her up properly, in your own house, with your own private notebooks locked away from everyone else's, and we check she works before we say she's yours.

## GRANDMA
Think of a bank. One bank, many ways in — the branch, the phone, the app — but it's always the same account and the same rules; nobody needs to found their own bank to have an account. And if someone wants a private vault of their own, a photocopy of the bank's rulebook isn't a vault: it has to be built, assigned to them, locked to them alone, and inspected. That's the difference between a fork and an activation.
