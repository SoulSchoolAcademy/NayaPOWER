# Smart Pricing: What You Get Free, What You Get Paid

SN-1864 · SYSTEM / smart-notes
**Created:** 2026-10-24T21:49:32Z

## IN A NUTSHELL
Free is a taste, and it's on a clock: five days, no card, feel the whole living thing. Then the living parts quiet down — your identity and the feed stay yours — until you pay. Paid is everything staying alive, with numbers so generous they feel unlimited: 50 new pages a month, 10,000 mail a month, 100 Smart Notes a month, unlimited lists, links, and projects. One gate — time — not a maze of locked buttons. The fork is inside paid, as ownership, for the people who want it.

## HUMAN
**The prices.** $14 a month · $37 for 3 months · $75 a year · $150 lifetime founding (only 1,000 founding places; when they're gone, prices double at each ladder — 1,000, 10,000, 100,000 members). Ambassador members earn 50% of revenue, paid every Wednesday.

**The table — free vs paid:**

- **Time** — Free: 5 days of the full living system, no card · Paid: always on
- **Your starter pages** (personal, business, space, certificate) — Free: yours, they stay · Paid: yours
- **New pages** — Free: 4 total · Paid: 50 a month, up to 600 a year
- **Spaces you run** — Free: 3 · Paid: unlimited
- **Smart Notes you make** — Free: 10 a month · Paid: 100 a month
- **Smart Mail** — Free: 100 sends a month · Paid: 10,000 people a month
- **Smart Lists** — Free: 3 · Paid: unlimited
- **Smart Links** (incl. ambassador links) — Free: 3 · Paid: unlimited
- **Projects, managed live** — Free: up to 3 · Paid: unlimited
- **Intel Today / daily highlights** — Free: top 3 a day · Paid: the full picture, unlimited
- **Reports** (daily, weekly, monthly) — Free: a weekly taste · Paid: all of them, unlimited
- **Auto-match** — Free: basic matches · Paid: full depth — matches with reasons and percentages, run for you continuously
- **Live calls in your spaces** — Free: voice notes · Paid: live voice and video calls
- **Uploads storage** — Free: 100 MB · Paid: 10 GB
- **Ambassador payouts** — Free: share and be counted · Paid: 50% of revenue, every Wednesday
- **Your own Naya** (memory, hands, automation) — Free: you feel her during the 5 days · Paid: always on, working for you
- **Your fork** (your copy, ownership, customization) — Free: no · Paid: yes, if you want it

**How to read the paid numbers:** they are set far above what a person actually uses, on purpose. Paid should *feel* unlimited — the numbers exist so the system stays clean and fast for everyone, not to nickel-and-dime anyone. And the quiet rule stands (SN-1862): some small things are simply never gated, on purpose, and never advertised. Share links work. Feed reading works. Your identity holds. We bring value; we don't police every corner.

## CHILD
It's like a wonderful fair that comes to town. For five days, everyone rides free — all the rides. After that, the rides need a pass, but the fairground stays open: you can always walk in, see your friends, and look at everything. The pass is cheap, and with it everything runs again — plus you can build your own rides, as many as you want, and if you someday want your very own copy of the whole fair, that's on the pass too.

## GRANDMA
Dear, the first five days are our treat — everything open, no card, just come and feel it. After that, the lights in your rooms dim until you take the membership, though your name on the door and your reading chair never go away. The membership is $14 a month, and honestly it includes so much — pages and pages, letters by the thousand, your own helper who remembers you, your projects all in order, even your earnings every Wednesday — that it feels like no limits at all. That's the idea: simple, generous, and easy to say yes to.

## NAYA
Build rules for every seat (companion to SN-1862's pay line; structure locked by the director 2026-10-24):
- **One gate: time + activation.** The 5-day taste runs on the citizen's join timestamp; the quiet-down touches the living functions (create, mail, match depth, engine), never identity, never feed reading, never inbound links. Do not build per-feature lock mazes.
- **Numbers live in one pricing table** (this note's table, mirrored in the machine record) — surfaces read it, never hard-code their own limits. Changing a number is a director ruling + a table edit, not a code change scattered across pages.
- **Paid numbers are headroom, not friction.** If real members start touching a cap in normal use, the cap is wrong — raise it by ruling and note the change here; the presentation promise is "feels unlimited."
- **The quiet ungated set stays unnamed in public copy.** It is policy (SN-1862), recorded here only as existing. Safety, money, and others' privacy are never in it.
- **Value presentation carries the sale:** the certificate/end slide and the dashboard show this table's paid column against what the member has already felt in their 5 days. Accurate numbers only — a limit we print is a limit the system actually keeps.
