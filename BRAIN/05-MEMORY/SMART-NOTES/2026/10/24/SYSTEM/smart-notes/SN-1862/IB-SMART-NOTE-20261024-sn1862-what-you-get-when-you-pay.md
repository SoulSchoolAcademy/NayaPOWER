# What You Get When You Pay: The Free Line and the NayaPOWER Line

SN-1862 · SYSTEM / smart-notes
**Created:** 2026-10-24T21:37:52Z

## IN A NUTSHELL
Free is the world; paid is the engine. Forking is one feature inside the paid tier — it is not what paying means, and nobody has to fork to pay or to belong. The pay gate sits on one root: your own Naya, holding your memory, acting for you. Everything in the paid tier grows from that root, so one clean activation covers all of it. We gate the value that matters, present it so well that people gladly pay, and deliberately leave small corners ungated — chasing every corner would complicate the design for no real gain.

## HUMAN
**FREE — NayaNET, the world (every citizen, automatically):**
- Citizen identity and the certificate page; personal, business, and space pages.
- The whole collective to read and browse, including every Smart Note.
- Quick notes in your space, private by default, and the Smart Note button that expands them into the lenses and the feed.
- Connect, citizen mail, and being matched on the signals you share.
- Spaces as community pages. A real home — never an empty trial.

**PAID — NayaPOWER, the engine (one activation, fork or no fork):**
- **Your own Naya, with your memory** — a personal intelligence that remembers you, keeps your private notes, and learns how you work.
- **Your Naya with hands** — she operates your Hub for you: automation, coordination, reports, follow-through.
- **The Smart Factory** — build your own smart things from the shared pieces.
- **Full auto-match depth** — the matching engine run for your world: your spaces and mail self-organize, matches arrive with reasons and percentages.
- **Spaces as live project engines** — rooms that function, not just pages.
- **Verification, proof, and publishing** — your work scored and proven, and a path to contribute it into the shared brain.
- **Your own fork** — the ownership feature: your copy in your account, your private workshop, yours to keep. One feature in this list — a headline for many people, and entirely optional to use.

Founding price on record: $14 a month (rising at the 1,000 / 10,000 / 100,000 member marks). Extraordinary value for very little — presented so clearly that paying feels like the obvious move, because it is.

**The gating wisdom (the director's ruling, this note's reason to exist):**
- Gate at the engine, once — never button by button. The paid features share one root, so activation is the only lock the design needs.
- Free is limited by design, not by cost. It costs us almost nothing to give everything away; we limit free because a real boundary is what makes the paid value visible and the whole system sensible.
- Some corners will stay ungated on purpose. If a free member finds an odd ungated thing, that is fine — it is policy, not a leak to hunt. Over-controlling would complicate the design and tax everyone to protect almost nothing.
- The sale is presentation, not pressure: show what the engine does — the memory, the hands, the factory, the depth — and let the value close.

## CHILD
The park is free. Everyone can come in, play, meet friends, and put their best drawings on the big board. But if you want your very own helper who remembers everything about you, builds things with you in the workshop, and does jobs for you while you play — that's the special pass. The pass costs a little, and it's worth a lot. Owning your own little copy of the park is one of the things on the pass — a nice one — but the pass is really about the helper.

## GRANDMA
Dear, think of it as the town library and the reading room being free for all — and they truly are lovely. The membership is for the personal librarian: the one who knows your taste, keeps your place in every book, fetches things before you ask, and helps you build your own collection — including, if you wish, a complete copy of the library shelves for your own home. We don't lock every drawer in the free rooms; that would make the place fussy and unwelcoming. We simply keep the librarian for members, and make sure everyone can see how wonderful having one is.

## NAYA
Build rules for every seat (third panel of the SN-1860 / SN-1861 set; the line itself is issue #3028 and SN-0848):
- **One gate: activation.** Tier checks hang off the citizen's activation state, never off per-feature improvisation. A new paid feature declares itself engine-tier and inherits the gate.
- **Fork is a paid feature, not the tier.** Never write "paid = forked" in code, copy, or design. A member who never forks loses nothing of the engine.
- **Free must stay a real home.** Limits on free are the engine's absence, not sabotage of the world tier — no degraded pages, no fake buttons, no dark patterns (a visible button is not a working feature, in either direction).
- **Ungated corners are policy.** Do not file them as defects and do not "fix" them without a director ruling; the exception is anything touching safety, money, or another citizen's privacy — those gate always.
- **Presentation is part of the mechanism.** The surfaces that show the engine's value (dashboard, certificate, match reasons) are sales surfaces by design: accurate, proven, and beautiful — the value closes, never the pressure.
