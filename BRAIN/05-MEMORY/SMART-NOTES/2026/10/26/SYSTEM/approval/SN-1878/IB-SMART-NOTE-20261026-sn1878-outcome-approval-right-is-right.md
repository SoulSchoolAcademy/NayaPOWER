# Outcome Approval and Right Is Right

SN-1878 · SYSTEM / approval

**Created:** 2026-10-26T17:40:00Z

## In plain words

Shawn approves the outcome. He never approves the technical detail. And nobody's approval makes anything right — not his, not any seat's, not anyone's opinion. A thing is right when it actually is right: it works, fully, beautifully, usefully, and everybody who looks at it can see that it works.

## The law, in two parts

**1. Outcome approval, not means approval.** Shawn approves the OUTCOME, in advance and in full: the most intelligent thing, done right, fully functional, in the way already discussed. He never approves a technical MEANS he cannot judge as done right — no tap on a secret, a route, a schema, or any other implementation detail. Asking him to tap a means puts him in the judge's seat for something he cannot judge. That question is forbidden. The items-endpoint read-only secret tap, asked 2026-10-26, is WITHDRAWN and is never to be asked again in that form.

**2. Right is right.** Approval never MAKES anything right. If Shawn approves it, that does not make it right. If the seats approve it, that does not make it right. It is not about anyone's opinion or what anyone thinks is right. It is about doing what IS right, and understanding what that means: fully functional, done the most intelligent way, the most beautiful way, the most effective way, the most useful way, truly working, valuable — so that when everybody looks at it, everybody experiences it done right. The other seats' checks (Resolve's cold check, Nitro's review, the team functioning on it) are MEASUREMENT of rightness, never the source of it. Nothing is "approved into" rightness.

## What returns to Shawn

A WORKING result. Or a blocker that truly only he can clear, stated as a blocker with proof — never a means-approval question. A credential or operator step that remains is an operator detail the team handles or names exactly with its holder. No credential value ever appears in repo, client code, PI, or board. No staged work is ever presented as live.
