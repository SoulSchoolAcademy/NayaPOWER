## CHILD

You're renting it. The rules are in your hand. Work the sum. If you get stuck, the stuck part is what you study first.

Moving judgment into the seats, bounded by rubrics and verified by receipts, is what lets the whole team think at once — while the human gates stay human precisely because they are few, real, and unmistakable.
