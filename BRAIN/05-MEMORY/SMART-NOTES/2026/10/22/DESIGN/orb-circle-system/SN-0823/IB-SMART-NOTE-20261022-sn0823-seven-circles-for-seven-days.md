## IN A NUTSHELL

Shawn ratified the Orb Circle System as standing design law for every NayaNET smart app on 2026-10-22. Data is carried by living circles: one orb-circle for each day of the week, circle stat rows across the tops of boards, orb-flow motion learned from our own orbs. And every page is scorecarded before it is built — its function, its purpose, why it is not a 10, where its life and elevation live. His standard, in his words: "not more is better — better is better." Crisp, clean, high quality, high contrast, living intelligence, dynamic. The mission bar he set with it: the most extraordinary, awe-inspiring, beautiful, intuitive, useful, push-button-simple, logical, sensible smart app ever created.

## HUMAN

A week of your life should not arrive as a table you have to decode. Seven circles, one for each day, each one glowing with how alive that day actually was — a big bright Sunday, a quiet Tuesday — and you understand your week in one glance, the way you understand a face. Circles are how humans read rhythm. Bars and naked numbers make people work; circles let people feel. So the numbers move into the circles, the circles breathe and flow the way our orbs do, and the page stops being a report and starts being a picture of your world, alive.

## CHILD

Imagine seven bubbles in a row — Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, all the way to Sunday. The day you did lots of things, your bubble is big and shiny. A sleepy day has a small, dim bubble. Today’s bubble wears a glowing ring so you always know where you are. You don’t have to read anything. You just look, and you know.

## GRANDMA

You know how a calendar on the kitchen wall shows you the whole month at once? This is that, but alive. Each day is a little glowing circle, and the busier and brighter the day was, the brighter its circle shines. No small print, no charts to study. You glance at the row of circles and you can see your week — which days were full, which were restful — the same way you’d glance out a window and know the weather.

## NAYA

Build law, effective now. (1) Circles are a first-class living-data form, alongside bars, donuts, and funnels — not decoration. The Reports trend is seven day-circles sized and glowing by each day’s real total, today wearing the hot ring; boards that report numbers lead with a circle stat row; a page’s counts (Today’s posts, messages, invites) render as circles, not sentences. (2) Circle color follows the spectrum walk by position — never two adjacent circles in the same hue. (3) Motion is orb-flow: breathing, a traveling light, the feel of our orbs — never frantic, never dead. (4) Empty space is a defect unless it is dosed breathing room around something that matters; a board that ends a third of the way down the screen over dead black fails review. (5) Every block is scorecarded in the build record before it is built: function, purpose, why it is not a 10, the cure, the after-score.

## MACHINE

Pattern: ORB-CIRCLE-SYSTEM v1. Source family: NAYA-ORB-002. Forms: DAY-CIRCLES ×7 (value = real per-day totals from the page’s own tables; radius + glow ∝ value; current day = hot ring state), STAT-CIRCLE ROW (N circles, number inside, label beneath, spectrum-walk colors by position, no adjacent repeats), MIX-CIRCLES (category readouts beside the donut). Data rule: real page data only; honest empty state when a value is zero — a dim circle, never an invented number. Verification: rendered screenshot at phone width + pixel-motion median-of-3 + per-block scorecard present in the build record. Falsifier: any shipped data surface whose primary numbers render as naked text or a lone bar in an empty field, or any block merged without its scorecard lines.

## WHAT IT ALL MEANS

The system stopped asking “how do we chart this?” and started asking “how would a person feel this?” That is the whole shift. NayaNET’s promise was never more data — it is living intelligence: information that arrives already understood. The circle is the smallest complete picture of a value: it has a size, a color, a glow, a place in a rhythm. Seven of them are a week. A row of them is a dashboard. An app built from them is a world you can read at a glance, which is what “push-button simple” actually means — not fewer features, but zero decoding.

If you run your world on NayaNET, you stop reading reports and start seeing your life. Open any page and the important numbers are already pictures: how today is going, whether this week is stronger than last, who is around you, what needs you next. Nothing to interpret, nothing to hunt for, no dead screens and no flat lists pretending to be design. Just a clean, bright, living view — extraordinary because it is simple, and simple because every detail was fought for.

## HOW IT APPLIES

Effective immediately in the flagship build wave already running: Reports is rebuilt around the day-circles and the stat-circle row; Today’s counts become circles; Settings gains real lit switches and filled control groups; Inbox composes its lower region; Lists and Connections lose their dead space and their ghosts. Every future block, room, and template inherits the pattern through the Factory — the scorecard questions (function? purpose? why not a 10? where is the life? where is the elevation?) are asked of everything we ship, and Shawn’s screenshot test is the gate: if it would not survive his eye, it is not done.

## THE DECLARED BEHAVIOR CHANGE

When I build or verify any data surface, I reach for the living circle first and ask what the circles would say, before I reach for a bar or a number. I scorecard every block before it is built — its function, its purpose, why it is not a 10 — and I treat empty space as a defect to cure, never as a default to ship. Where before I presented naked numbers and lone bars and called it reporting, I now compose data the way a human reads it: in circles, in rhythm, in light. And I do not call a page done until it would survive the screenshot test — crisp, clean, high contrast, alive.
