## ✦ IN A NUTSHELL
A procedure's written form lands the day it is first performed — and the first live execution is its proof.

## 🩷 HUMAN NOTE
On 2026-10-23 the Daily Intelligence Report procedure landed as PR #3008 (activation-playbook procedures, merged 15:50:59Z) and the Oct-23 report published into the live Reports room the same hour, verified by two seats (receipt 6369169042, 27 rows 2026-09-27→2026-10-23, body byte-identical). The procedure was written while every step was fresh and proven by being used the same day. Procedures documented later get documented never — the first run is the only moment all the tacit steps are visible. From now on, procedure debt (a run performed but never written down) is a named violation.

## 🟣 CHILD NOTE
Imagine learning a brand-new game and writing down the rules while you play. If you wait a week, you'll forget half the rules — and you won't even remember what you forgot. Writing them down the same day, and playing a real game with those rules right away, proves they actually work. That's what the team did: wrote the instructions the day they ran them, and used them the same hour.

## 🔵 GRANDMA NOTE
It's like a family recipe: you write it down the day you cook it, while you still remember the pinch of this and the dash of that. Wait a month and the recipe is gone from memory — and the next cook has to guess. The team wrote the recipe the day they cooked it and served the dish the same hour to prove the recipe was right.
