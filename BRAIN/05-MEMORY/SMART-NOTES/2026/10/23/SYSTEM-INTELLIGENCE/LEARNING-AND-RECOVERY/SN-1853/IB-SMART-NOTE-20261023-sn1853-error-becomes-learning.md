An Error Becomes a Lesson Only When Recovery Changes Behavior
SN-1853 · SYSTEM-INTELLIGENCE / LEARNING-AND-RECOVERY
**Created:** 2026-10-23T02:42:17Z

## IN A NUTSHELL
A mistake or wall is evidence; the lasting failure is not learning from it. Record why it happened, how it was overcome, how the next Naya will avoid repeating it, and prove the behavior changed. Smart Notes are a delivery-and-reuse mechanism, not just saved text.

## HUMAN
When we hit a wall, capture the symptom, diagnosis, solution, outcome and next prevention. Don't discard the valuable lesson. Real harm still matters and must be mitigated.

## CHILD
If you trip on a rock, learn where it is, find another path, and tell your friends so no one trips again.

## GRANDMA
Keep the recipe correction: what went wrong, the adjustment that worked, and how you verified the result.

## NAYA
Seek existing lessons first. On meaningful failure: stop and respect safety/authority; diagnose with evidence; recover safely; verify the fix; capture a concise reusable lesson; turn it into a check or an owner-assigned action; prove next-task reuse. Do not confuse note count with intelligence. Do not publish secrets or private data.

## MACHINE
Trigger: reusable error, blocked workflow, or recovery. Fields: exact observed failure, environment/SHA, cause or UNKNOWN, attempts, safe remedy, regression control, evidence, owner, applicability, successor test. Transitions: OBSERVED → DIAGNOSED → RECOVERED → VERIFIED → CAPTURED → RETRIEVED → APPLIED → REUSE_VERIFIED. No claim of learned until observed reuse. Never manufacture authority or live-link status.

## WHAT IT ALL MEANS
Compounding intelligence converts a wall into an increasingly reliable path that others can follow.

## WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU
Less repeated effort, clearer explanations, fewer recurring defects, and a product that actually grows smarter.
