## 🟣 CHILD NOTE
Imagine you ask a friend wearing earplugs to listen for a whisper, and you write down beforehand: "I bet she hears it!" You'll be shocked when she doesn't. But if you first write down "she's wearing earplugs, so whispers are her blind spot — she probably CAN'T hear it", then when she misses it, you LEARNED something about the earplugs. Predictions should name the weakness, not just the wish — the miss then teaches you instead of surprising you.

## 🔵 GRANDMA NOTE
It's like entering a nearsighted friend in a bird-watching contest and betting he'll spot the distant hawk — then being shocked when he doesn't. If you'd written down beforehand "he's nearsighted, so distant birds are his blind spot," the loss would have confirmed what you knew about his eyes instead of feeling like a mystery. Always name the blind spot when you place the bet.
