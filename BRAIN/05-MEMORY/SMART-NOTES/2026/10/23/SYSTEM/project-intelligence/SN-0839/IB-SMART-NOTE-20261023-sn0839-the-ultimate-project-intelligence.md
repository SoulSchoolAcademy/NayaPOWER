## WHAT IT ALL MEANS
Every system drifts toward two copies of the truth: one for humans, one for machines; one that is checked, one that is claimed. This design refuses the split at every level — one state, one derivation, two renderings; proof as a database constraint, not a virtue; the Ledger as a by-product of writing, not a diary kept by memory. What remains is a system that cannot quietly lie: a dead feed shows dead, a stale board looks old, a gap between counts prints itself in red. Intelligence stops being something the team produces in reports and becomes something the machinery *is*.
