## CHILD
Imagine a big wall where the whole team can see every job: what's finished, what's stuck, what needs a decision. Nobody builds their piece alone in a corner — a friend always checks it. And if two friends build the same piece two different ways, the team doesn't fight about it: they look at both, keep the best parts, and make one super-piece. Then they write down what they learned so nobody has to learn it twice. They keep going until the whole wall is finished and everything on it is true.

## GRANDMA
The family is building one big notice board in the middle of town where everyone can see exactly what's happening — no more asking around, no more guessing. Everybody helps build it, and everybody checks each other's work, kindly and carefully. If two people make the same thing differently, they sit together, pick the better one — or combine the best of both into something better than either — and they write down what they learned. Nothing counts as finished until someone else has checked it and it's truly up on the board for all to see.

## NAYA
The method is the machine, and it has five motions: **claim** (from board #2793 — the claim is the lock) · **build** (to the one spec, v1.3 — harvest, never invent) · **scorecard** (a different seat — a genuinely different login — scores grounding, function, performance, usefulness, and runs the journey: why is it not a 10, what ten things make it a 10, apply, re-score, three times) · **verify** (at the real address: live bytes, render, and data — proof or it didn't happen) · **land** (9+ and verified; production also takes Shawn's word). No item starts without a named maker and a named checker. A stuck seat posts to the board and the nearest seat helps — helping is the job, not a favor.

**The Two-Version Rule (Shawn's law, this note):** two attempts at the same thing are an asset, never an argument. Put them side by side, score both on the same rubric, then choose the stronger or synthesize the ultimate from both. The loser retires in the open, both builders are credited, and the lesson files as a Smart Note the same day. Two versions left standing is the only failure — it means the team hasn't finished thinking yet.

Named lanes on this build: the director seat builds P0 and keeps the census honest · Naya Power checks the face and runs the Page Test and the cold seat · Resolve owns the Fourteen Questions view and checks the census · Naya 1 holds the verification lane (she found the broken bridge) · Naya 3 guards the measures and the map. P1 and P2 items post to the board as claimable work for every seat.
