## ✦ IN A NUTSHELL

Naya Design is now explicitly grounded in human-centered interaction science as well as signature visual craft. The external benchmark synthesis strongly confirms the existing direction: purpose before interface, hierarchy, truthful state, causal controls, coherent material craft, complete applications, accessibility, performance, rendered observation and verified learning. The missing layer was to make cognitive ergonomics explicit: distill before display, recognition before recall, progressive disclosure, obvious next action, target/choice ergonomics, immediate feedback, error prevention/recovery, stable interaction grammar, appropriate direct manipulation and measured human outcomes. The design target is maximum responsible human value with minimum necessary cognitive burden.

## 🩷 HUMAN NOTE

Keep the beautiful, living NayaNET design language, but make every design choice serve the person first. The interface should know what matters, show the right amount at the right time, make the next action obvious, prevent mistakes, respond immediately, stay familiar while becoming personal, and hide machine complexity instead of making the human manage it.



## 🟣 CHILD NOTE

Make the app beautiful, but also make it easy enough that I know what to do without asking. Show me the important thing first, make buttons easy to use, tell me what happened, help me fix mistakes, and remember what worked.

## 🔵 GRANDMA NOTE

Do the complicated thinking behind the scenes. On the screen, give me the important information, clear choices, familiar controls, readable words, obvious feedback and a safe way back if something goes wrong.

## 🟠 NAYA NOTE

Fuse Naya's existing signature design mastery with explicit human-factors and cognitive-ergonomics laws so design optimization targets the complete human experience rather than visual excellence alone.

## 🧭 KEY DECISIONS / PRINCIPLES

- Adopt the cross-referenced human-centered design intelligence as a standing Naya Design operating method.
- Preserve NayaNET's existing distinctive living-depth visual DNA because it is compatible with, not opposed to, human-centered design.
- Do not copy external products or aesthetics; extract causal and psychological principles and express them through Naya's design language.
- Make progressive disclosure, recognition over recall, error prevention/recovery, choice complexity and target ergonomics explicit design requirements.
- Optimize personalization in content/context while keeping interaction grammar stable and learnable.
- Treat increasing machine intelligence as a mandate to reduce human cognitive burden.
- Require human-experience evidence in addition to visual review for promoted design learning.
- Keep accessibility, truth, privacy, authority, performance and causal completeness as hard constraints.
- Store reusable design patterns with applicability, evidence, failure modes and supersession rather than as static templates.
- A future Naya should begin from these laws automatically instead of requiring Shawn to restate them.
