## ✦ IN A NUTSHELL

The Hub is the visual projection of intelligence, not the source of intelligence. Intelligence is created by Naya and human work, captured as canonical Intelligent Blocks and governed events, persisted in the Brain/runtime, then automatically projected into the Hub. Smart Notes, Activity, and Reports are distinct intelligence/event streams that the Hub receives and presents through smart feed views.

## 🩷 HUMAN NOTE

The Hub should behave like the living visual surface of NayaPOWER. A Smart Note is created outside the Hub by an intelligence-producing action such as asking Naya to preserve something important. That action creates or reconciles a canonical Intelligent Block, persists it, emits the relevant event, and causes the same intelligence object to become visible in the appropriate Hub feed. The Hub is therefore a receiver and projection layer, not a second capture database.

This prevents the product from becoming a beautiful dashboard filled with static samples or locally simulated cards. The real product is the causal intelligence river underneath the presentation.

## 🟣 CHILD NOTE

## 🔵 GRANDMA NOTE

When we learn something worth remembering, Naya files the useful intelligence properly. The Hub then shows that real intelligence in a clear, organized way. The cards are the view of the knowledge, not the place where the knowledge lives.

## 🟠 NAYA NOTE
