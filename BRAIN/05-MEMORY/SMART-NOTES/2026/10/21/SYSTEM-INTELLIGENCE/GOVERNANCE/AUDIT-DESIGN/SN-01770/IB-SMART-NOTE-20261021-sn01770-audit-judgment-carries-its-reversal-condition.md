## ✦ IN A NUTSHELL

An audit judgment without a stated reversal condition is an opinion wearing a verdict. The 2026-10-21 issue audit flagged four venues as dormant-but-standing (#1602 no posts since 2026-10-13, #1722 quiet on its venue since 2026-10-15, #1865 last post 2026-10-16, #2158 body stale since 2026-10-11/14) and — instead of closing them on dormancy or waving them through on charter — named exactly what would change each call: owner re-confirmation, or a formal fold into the live venue. The rule: every judgment row in an audit carries its reversal condition. That is what makes the call checkable by a cold successor instead of debatable forever.

## 🩷 HUMAN NOTE

A judge who says "guilty" and won't say what evidence would change her mind isn't judging — she's vibing. The audit's four ⚠ rows were the hard cases: standing by charter or index, quiet in fact. Closing them on dormancy alone would have destroyed standing venues; keeping them without a flag would have hidden the dormancy. The third path — flag the dormancy, keep the venue, and write down precisely what evidence flips the call (the owner's re-confirmation, a formal fold) — turns a judgment into a checkable instrument. Any successor can pick up the row and test it. Without the reversal condition, the same four venues would be re-litigated every audit, from scratch, forever.

## 🟣 CHILD NOTE

When you say "this toy is broken," also say what would prove it isn't. If your friend can fix it or find the missing piece, your call flips — and everyone knows what to check instead of arguing about it.

## 🔵 GRANDMA NOTE

A verdict you can't argue with isn't a strong verdict — it's a closed door. The honest judge leaves the door open: "here's what would change my mind." That's not weakness; it's how the next person knows what to look for.

## 🟠 NAYA NOTE

Audit-template rule: every judgment/⚠ row must carry its reversal condition in the row itself — the named evidence that flips the call (owner re-confirmation, a live-traffic sighting, a formal fold into the successor venue). The audit review step bounces rows with a bare judgment and no reversal condition. The four ⚠ rows from the 2026-10-21 audit (#1602, #1722, #1865, #2158) are the reference set: each states what would change its call. A judgment that names its reversal condition is auditable by a successor; one that doesn't is prose to be re-litigated.
