## HUMAN
Ask any AI builder today for an app and you get a shell that doesn't work. Then the real work begins: move this, fix that button, make this function go — weeks or months of correcting, and you'd be lucky to get one working page in a day. It isn't the AI's fault. Nobody ever decided, in advance, what the pieces should be. So every app is ten thousand undecided questions answered by guessing.

The Factory answers those questions once, at the highest level, before anyone arrives. About a hundred elite parts — no more — each one the single best version of its kind: one button language (a few styles; size and shape are settings, not new buttons), one board, one well, one hero, all alive with depth, light, and micro-motion. From those parts, the most-wanted apps come pre-built as finished models. Choosing is light: a color, a wheel swap, your name. The core was already perfect.

## CHILD
Imagine a toy box with exactly the right bricks — not a mountain of random pieces, just the perfect ones. The cars are already designed. You pick the red one or the blue one, put your name on the door, and zoom. Nobody has to invent the wheels while you're waiting to play.

## GRANDMA
It's like buying a car from a good dealership instead of asking someone to invent a car in your driveway. The models are built, tested, and beautiful. You choose the one you like, pick your color, and drive home the same day. All the hard work happened at the factory, long before you arrived — that's exactly how it should be.

## NAYA
The Factory Thesis + the Elite Parts Doctrine (Shawn, 2026-10-21). Speed comes from pre-decision, not faster generation: quality cannot be prompted into existence at build time. The canon is ~100 parts across 16 classes; one language per class; ≤5 variants; size/shape/spectrum are parameters. Anything not cream-of-the-crop is retired, never warehoused. Templates are supercar models — same core vehicle, light personalization (colors, a piece swap, a name). A part enters the canon only by passing the scored gate (≥9.5, no hard-zero across design-language, depth, living states, wired function, provenance, parameterizability, accessibility). The processor's job shrinks to honest assembly: exact template, chosen pieces, promised address.
