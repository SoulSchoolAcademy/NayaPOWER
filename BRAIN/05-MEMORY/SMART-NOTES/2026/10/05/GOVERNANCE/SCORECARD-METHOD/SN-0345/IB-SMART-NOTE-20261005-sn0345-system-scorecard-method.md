## Essence

Scoring the whole system is a different discipline from scoring a deliverable. The system scorecard has **ten fixed areas with fixed weights** (ratified 2026-10-05, weighted total 6.8/10). Weights change only on Shawn Vibert's explicit word. Scores move only on fresh evidence with proof attached. The scorecard re-runs on cadence and is spot-audited. No score is ever declared — a 10 is earned by demonstration.

- **SN-038** (Scorecard Everything doctrine; open PR #1387): owns the deliverable-quality doctrine — score every artifact before it leaves the seat, nothing ships below 9.

Neither covers the **system** scorecard method. This note is only that missing delta:

1. **Fixed areas, fixed weights.** Ten areas, weights summing to 100, set by the Human Director. A seat that reweights on its own authority is not scorecarding — it is moving the goalposts.
2. **Weight-change authority.** Only Shawn's explicit word changes an area or a weight, recorded with date and his verbatim direction.
3. **Evidence-gated score movement.** A score changes only on new measured evidence; every change carries its proof (measurement, ref, receipt). Re-runs recompute from fresh evidence — never copy-forward a prior score.
4. **The loop around the score.** Every area under 9.0 gets the miss named, the highest-value corrective action, an owner, and a re-score date. An unscored claim or a score with no follow-up action is rejected by the machine.
5. **Spot-audit discipline.** Re-runs are independently spot-audited; attribution discipline holds — every claim carries its resolving ref, lanes cite actor+lane from board/API, never display names.
6. **No declared scores.** "We are a 10" is a claim requiring demonstration at every area. Below 9.0 is not acceptable and is never shipped as "done."

## Why it matters

Without a fixed instrument, every seat grades on its own curve and the numbers drift upward while the system stays still. The fixed areas and weights make the score **comparable across time** — 6.8 today means something only because 6.8 next week is measured the same way. The evidence gate is what keeps an honest 5.0 (LEARNING) from becoming a comfortable 7. The re-run cadence is what turns the scorecard from a report into a steering wheel.
