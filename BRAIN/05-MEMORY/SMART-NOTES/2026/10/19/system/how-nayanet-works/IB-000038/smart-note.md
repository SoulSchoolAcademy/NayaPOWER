## Subject

How NayaNET Works — The Intelligent Auto-Matching System

## In a nutshell

NayaNET is the future internet: one intelligent system where the app you build goes live instantly, gets discovered by the right people automatically, and grows through community — while your identity stays entirely yours. The system matches by what you're into, never by who you are. You show up with an alias. The system watches what you love. It connects the dots. It never asks for your name.

## Human view

Here's how it actually works, end to end.

You build your app — you describe it, the system builds it — and it goes live right away at its own address. Then you submit it to NayaNET. We approve it, and now it's part of the network: discoverable, listed, findable. You add it to your Smart Tabs so it lives in your personal world. It can appear in the sidebar menu, part of the shared navigation. And there's search — find any app by its name, by its subject.

But the real magic is what happens around the app. You create a Smart Space around your app's subject — photography, fitness, cooking, whatever it's about. That Space gets shared in the activity feed. People who are into that subject get invited. They join. They connect with you. They talk about your app, your product, what you're building. The Space is where contacts happen, where community forms, where your app finds its people.

And underneath all of it, the system's auto-matcher is always running. It's reading what your app is about, what people are into, what Spaces exist — and it's putting the most relevant matches in front of each person, in their activity feed, without anyone having to search. You don't go find things. The system brings them to you.

Now the part that makes it different from everything else: the system always matches you automatically, but it never reveals your identity unless you choose to. You operate under an alias — could be your real name, could be anything, doesn't matter. The system runs on the alias. It matches on behavior: what apps you build, what Spaces you join, what you read, what you heart. It knows "this alias loves photography apps and is in three photography Spaces" — so when a new photography app launches, it puts it in front of you. It doesn't know or need to know who you actually are.

That's the opposite of Google and Facebook. They match you by selling you — they need to know WHO you are to monetize you. NayaNET matches you by serving you. Your identity is irrelevant to the intelligence, which means it can never be leaked, sold, or exposed. There's nothing to leak. Privacy isn't the price of the intelligence. Privacy is the foundation of it.

It's one system and everything flows. Your app, your Space, your feed, the matcher — they're not separate products, they're parts of each other, helping each other, all valuable, all intelligently thought through. That's how the whole thing works.
