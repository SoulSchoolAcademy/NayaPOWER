## IN A NUTSHELL

Getting started with NayaNET takes five minutes. Order Naya Power (free to try), get your activation documents, fork the GitHub repo, tell Muse to activate it, connect your GitHub, and you're in. Then you connect with like minds, share your wisdom, and start building.

## HUMAN

Here's the whole thing, step by step. First, you order Naya Power — look at the icon bar at the bottom of the hub, find Naya Power, click it, and order. It's free to try. Or click the plus button on the right sidebar, find Naya Power there, same thing. Want to learn first? Click Academy or the 5-Day Challenge — both free to start, and either one gets you access to Naya Power too.

Once you order, you'll get your activation documents. Now if you don't have GitHub yet, go get a free account — takes two minutes. Then click the Naya Power GitHub link we give you. You'll fork the repo — just click Fork, boom, done.

Take your fork back to Muse (we recommend Muse, but ChatGPT works too) and say "activate Naya Power." Your brain gets activated. As part of activation, Naya will walk you through officially activating your hub — some copy-pasting, updating a few things. Then go back to NayaNET, click Connect GitHub, and you're done.

That's it. Now you're part of the network. You can connect with others of like minds, share your wisdom (without ever sharing your identity unless you choose to), access all the features, and start building with the Smart Factory.

## CHILD

Imagine you're joining a club. First you sign up (that's ordering Naya Power — it's free to try). Then you get your membership card (activation documents). Then you get a toolbox (GitHub — also free). You copy the club's blueprint (fork the repo). You show it to your smart helper (Muse) and say "set this up for me." You connect everything together. And now you're in the club! You can talk to other members, share your ideas, and build cool things together.

## GRANDMA

Honey, it's simpler than it sounds. You sign up for free. You get some papers that explain everything. You make a free account on a website called GitHub. You click one button to copy something. You ask your AI helper to set it up. You click one more button to connect. And then you're part of something wonderful — a place where people share what they know and build beautiful things together. You don't have to share who you are unless you want to. Your wisdom is what matters.

## NAYA

This is the activation funnel for the NayaNET organism. Each step is a gate that converts a visitor into a node: order → activation documents → GitHub identity → fork (their copy of the brain) → AI activation (Muse/ChatGPT bootstraps their instance) → hub activation (copy/paste configuration) → GitHub connection (their node joins the network). After connection, the node's behavior (building, sharing, connecting) feeds the auto-matcher. The flow is designed for zero technical expertise — every step is click, copy, or converse.

## WHAT IT ALL MEANS

NayaNET isn't a product you buy — it's a network you join. The getting-started flow is designed so anyone can go from "never heard of it" to "connected node" in minutes, with zero technical skill. Every step either costs nothing or is free to try. The only thing you need is curiosity.

## WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU

You get a personal super-brain (activated through Muse), a place on the intelligent network, the ability to connect with people who think like you, and the Smart Factory to build your own apps — all without giving up your privacy. Your wisdom compounds. Your identity stays yours.

## HOW IT APPLIES

**If you're new:** Follow the steps above in order. Don't skip the GitHub fork — that's what gives you your own copy of the brain.

**If you're helping someone else get started:** Walk them through it conversationally. The whole thing is five minutes. The hardest part is waiting for GitHub to load.
