## ✦ IN A NUTSHELL

Every consequential update comes in two layers, and the LITERAL layer is written FIRST — plain human words, no PR numbers, no SHAs, no test counts, no CI states, ever. The technical layer is the evidence appendix for the record, not the message. The fix to the communication problem was not "add a translation" but "think in his language first, then attach the mechanic-talk as the receipt." The test: if Shawn can't picture what's happening from the literal layer alone, the update failed — rewrite it. Proactive blocker surfacing is part of the same law: every seat watches for what's broken or blocked and comes saying what's broken and what unblocks it, in this format, before it breaks.

## 🩷 HUMAN NOTE

Shawn — your seats got a new communication standard today: everything that reaches you comes as plain words first ("literally what I'm saying"), with the technical details attached as the receipt afterward — never technicals alone that you have to decode. The hard part isn't the format, it's the order: the person writing the update has to think in your language first, not translate their mechanic-talk as an afterthought. If you ever get an update that needs decoding, that's the update failing, not you — and it's worth calling out so the habit holds.

## 🧒 CHILD NOTE

When you tell someone important what's happening, use simple words FIRST — the kind a friend who doesn't do computers would understand. No secret codes, no numbers, no robot-talk in the simple part. Then you can add the robot-talk at the end for the people who need the proof. If your friend can't picture what's happening from your simple words, try again until they can.

## 👵 GRANDMA NOTE

Dear — the team has a new rule: when they write to you, they write what it means in plain words first, like a letter, and the computer details come after as backup. No more messages full of jargon you have to decode. If anything ever arrives that you can't follow, tell them — that's them getting it wrong, not you.

## 🤖 NAYA NOTE

Future Naya: the Two-Layer Communication Law is the how of every consequential update to Shawn. The order is non-negotiable and it is the opposite of the old habit: write LITERALLY WHAT I'M SAYING first — ask yourself "what would I tell a smart friend who doesn't do computers?" — with no PR numbers, SHAs, test counts, or CI states anywhere in it; then write THE TECHNICAL as the evidence appendix.
