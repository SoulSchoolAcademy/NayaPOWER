## Nutshell
This is the official implementation report for the Secure Network: how we will build it, set it up, and prove it is secure and safe — in order, with gates — plus two corrections Shawn forced today: Smart Meet is paid-member only, and the old "free audio-4" number was never his decision and is withdrawn.

## Human view
First, an apology in plain words, because Shawn was right to be confused: SN-1863 was a note Nitro wrote with proposed pricing numbers, including "free audio-4". Those numbers were Nitro's brainstorm, never Shawn's ruling — and Nitro carried "free audio-4" forward as if it were locked. That was wrong. It is withdrawn, officially, here. Shawn's rule is the only rule: do what is logical and sensible and most intelligent — and it is logical that free members do not get everything, while paid members get more features.

## Lesson
Smart Meet — audio and video sharing, in Shawn's name for it — lives in Personal, Business, Space, and Membership, for paid members only. Stage 1: video up to 6, audio up to 10, mesh calls, quiet capability. Stage 2: larger groups get enterprise plans, priced when the cost is known, with a "Want more? Contact us for a quote" button at launch instead of guessed prices.

Paid versus unpaid is part of the program, not an afterthought: one entitlement record per citizen, checked server-side on every load and every request — hiding a button is cosmetic, the server denying is the gate. Free citizens see paid features as locked, honestly, never as broken. Paying upgrades the record at once; refunding reverses it openly. Denied attempts are logged as patterns, never as private content.

## Ultimate meaning
Security is not a launch step. It is a standing practice: mail privacy first, then ID and a live ledger, then hash-only revocable keys, then modular scope with three doors never mixed, then points both ways with live watch, then tiered gating, then Smart Meet, then live deploy gates on every step — and continuous proof after live. Every build must pass the sweet spot test: speed, sensibility, logic, flow, and security, all five, or it does not ship. Good citizens feel speed. Bad actors feel the wall.

## Why it matters
Without tiered gating, "paid gets more" is just words — free citizens would get everything by accident, and paid citizens would pay for nothing. Without server-side checks, a hidden button is not a gate. Without this order, security claims come before security proof, and trust dies first.
