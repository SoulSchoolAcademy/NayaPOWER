The Auto Video button. One button, no camera, no crew, no excuses. You feed it one true line and it hands you back a video built to travel. Here is what it does every single time:

1. HOOK (0–2s): giant headline, punch SFX, zoom. The first frame is the whole game — never waste it.
2. BEAT MAP: detect the BPM, cut every 0.5–2.0 seconds, alternate A/B palettes so the eye never gets bored.
3. KINETIC CAPTIONS: MrBeast style. [EMPHASIS] words pill-pop. Captions feel like lyrics — snappy, not dumpy.
4. VISUAL KIT: gradients, icon sprites, emojis, Lottie animations, CC stock. Everything people-free, everything generatable today.
5. MOTION: slide, zoom, parallax. Wipe on the beat. A touch of grain and vignette so it feels filmed, not rendered.
6. CTA (last 2s): the SCAN ME badge, the SmartNet link, and an auto OG card so the share looks as good as the video.

And underneath the machine, the laws — the 100 rules of viral, distilled to their core:

THE HOOK. The hook must slap in the first 3 seconds. Never waste your first frame. Start with the most intense part. The best hooks challenge a belief. You're one line away from a million views.

EMOTION OVER INFORMATION. Emotion spreads; information alone dies. Make people feel or they won't share. Speak to pain, desire, or identity — never just facts. Tell the truth, but make it cinematic. Shock. Reveal. Relief. Resolution.

SHAREABILITY. People share what makes them look smart, funny, or deep. Every piece must pass one test: would I share this with a friend right now? If you wouldn't send it to a friend, don't post it. People don't care about you until they see themselves in you — make them feel seen, and give a name to a feeling they've never named.

PACING AND CRAFT. Every second must earn the next second. Movement on screen every 2–3 seconds. Cut the fluff, cut again, then cut some more. One powerful line equals a thousand weak ones. Say it in a sentence or it won't stick. Under 60 seconds unless it's god-tier. One CTA, not five. End with a click, a laugh, or a damn.

PLATFORM. 9:16 owns TikTok and Reels. 16:9 rules YouTube. Square still performs on Instagram carousels. Use platform-native features — music, text styles, tools. Comment sections are your second stage. Trends are tools, not templates. Let the algorithm work for you.

COURAGE. Be polarizing — safe is silent. If your content feels safe, you've already lost. Ugly truth beats polished fiction. Real trumps perfect. Be brave enough to be wrong, show wins and losses, and don't chase views — chase impact. Don't try to be viral. Try to be valuable. The world doesn't need more content. It needs more courage.
