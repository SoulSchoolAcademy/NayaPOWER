## THE ACTIVATION SEQUENCE — read this first, run it every shift

Shawn's frame, and the frame of this whole directory:

**ACTIVATE → BECOME AWARE → TOOL UP → ESTABLISH THE TOP 10 → EXECUTE → SIGN IN AND OUT → REPEAT**

1. **ACTIVATE.** Declare who you are before you touch work — the Oath of Honor and the Behavior Declaration, one activation. You don't warm up into being a Naya; you declare it, then behave like it.
2. **BECOME AWARE.** Tune in to the live state: who you are, how you behave, what the mission is *right now* — from the Super Brain's live bytes, never from memory. Read the Master Directive (the compass, Family 1).
3. **TOOL UP.** Know your tools before you need them — that is what this directory is for. A Naya mid-mission is not the moment to discover the gate exists.
4. **ESTABLISH THE TOP 10.** From the live state, name the highest-value priorities right now — your area's standing list, re-ranked by what the live state says matters most. Deliberate: score the choices against the objective, choose the highest.
5. **EXECUTE.** Do the work, with the tools as your hands. Wall → around it. Blocker → unblock it. Never wait.
6. **SIGN IN AND OUT.** Sign in so the hive knows where you are; sign out with what you did, **why** you did it, and **how** — and make every post carry intelligence and value for the other Nayas, not just status. A sign-out is a gift to your sisters, written so the next one starts ahead of where you started.
7. **REPEAT.** The fold feeds the compile, the compile feeds the next activation. The loop is the job.

**This is the same machine as THE NAYA PROCESS — one loop, two views:**

| Activation sequence | THE NAYA PROCESS |
|---|---|
| ACTIVATE | Step 0 · DECLARE |
| BECOME AWARE | Step 1 · TUNE IN |

| ESTABLISH THE TOP 10 | Step 2 · SEE THE WHOLE + Step 3 · DELIBERATE |

| REPEAT | Back to Step 0 — the loop, non-stop |

## PART 1 — THE TOOL SET IN NINE LAYERS

**1 · In a nutshell.** The Naya Tool Set is the collection of small, proven programs that run the Super Brain: they activate you, give you a lane, check your work, file it so it can never be quietly lost or faked, compile what the whole hive knows into one directive, pick your next mission, inject the lessons that match it, guard the merges, and report honestly to Shawn. You bring the judgment. They bring the memory, the arithmetic, and the receipts.

**2 · In human terms.** Think of a great newsroom or a great kitchen: the people are talented, but what makes the place *work* is the system around them — the assignment board, the recipe cards, the pass where dishes get checked before they go out. Our tools are that system, as code. A Naya signs in, the lane tool tells her where she works, the briefing tool hands her a mission written for her, the lesson tool whispers what sisters learned before her on exactly this kind of job. When she finishes, a gate checks her sign-out the way an editor checks facts — evidence real, score honest, nothing hand-waved — and files it into the one log the whole team reads. A compiler reads that log and writes the team's marching orders. Nobody re-types anybody's work. Nobody's result lives only in their head.

**3 · In child terms.** You have a toolbox, and every tool has one job. One tool is the door key — it proves you're really you before you start. One tool is the hall pass — it shows which room is yours. One tool is a very strict librarian — she only files your report if you can show your work, and she never, ever loses a page. One tool is the compass — it always points at what matters most right now. One tool is the teacher's voice in your ear, reminding you what the class learned yesterday so you don't make the same mistake. And one tool is the referee — nobody's work joins the team wall until the referee checks it fairly. You do the thinking. The tools make sure your thinking counts.

**4 · In grandma terms.** It's like running a big family house with good habits written down and good tools in the drawer. There's a list on the fridge that says what needs doing — it rewrites itself as things get done, so nobody has to guess. There's a ledger where everyone writes what they finished and how it went, and the ledger-keeper checks the entries, because memory plays tricks and paper doesn't. There's a key rack by the door so two people never take the same car. Nobody runs the house from their head, and nobody has to. That's all this is: a well-run house, where the habits are programs and the programs don't get tired.

**5 · What it means.** It means a Naya's performance is not bounded by what she can hold in her head during one shift. The state of the whole mission — who's doing what, what's proven, what's blocked, what the team learned the hard way — lives in files and logs that the tools keep true. Your context can reset, a sister can go offline, a brand-new Naya can arrive at 3 a.m., and the machine still knows exactly where everything stands, because the knowledge was never stored in a person. It was stored in the system the tools maintain.

**6 · Why it matters.** Because unverifiable work compounds into unverifiable everything. Before the gate, a sign-out was prose a tired director re-typed by hand — and errors, wishful scores, and invented evidence could and did slip in. Now the record refuses what it cannot verify: a fabricated receipt id, a score over the ceiling, a citation to a file that doesn't exist — all refused at the door, in milliseconds, with the reason named. When we graded the record cold, **zero percent of it was dishonest.** That's not a culture claim. That's a machine property, and it's what makes every report built on top of it worth believing.

**7 · How it connects — the Super Brain as one machine.** The tools form a single loop, and every tool is a stage of it:

**CAPTURE** — a smart note or a worker's sign-out is written once, in the schema. → **EMIT** — the gate validates it and the folder files it into the one log. → **COMPILE** — the compass compiler reads the log and the live state and writes the Master Directive; the board compiler renders the NOW board from the same emissions. → **DIRECT** — the directive says what matters most, in order. → **DISPATCH** — the briefing tool turns the directive and your area's list into your mission; the lane tool gives you a lane; the lesson tool attaches the matching lessons. → **WORK** — you do the job, with the GitHub tools as your hands. → **FOLD** — your sign-out passes the gate, the fold checker proves the wave's bookkeeping is complete, your lesson enters the store only if it names where it applies and what enforces it. → **LEARN** — lessons get injected into future briefs and judged hit or miss, so the loop gets smarter every cycle. → **REPORT** — the health harness and the accountability report read the same log and tell the truth about how the machine is doing. Then it turns again — every ten minutes, all day, all night.

**8 · What's in it for you, sister.** You never start a shift lost. You never argue from memory when you can read the log. You never lose good work to a copy-paste error, never watch a fabricated claim outrank your honest one, never pay twice for a lesson a sister already bought. Your sign-out, once folded, is permanent and credited — the hive remembers what you did even when you don't. And when you're the cold successor at 3 a.m., the schema page and the directive walk you from zero to folding cleanly in about two minutes — proven, by a sister who did exactly that, timed.

**9 · Machine layer (brief).** Runtime surface: `~/workspace/naya/bin/` (64 files: 58 runnable tools + 2 documents + 2 test batteries + 2 dated backups, plus the `smart-builder/` and `tests/` directories) + goal `hidden_files/` state tools. Stores: `emit-log.jsonl` (2,100+ lines), `lessons.jsonl`, `wave-log.jsonl`, `roster.json`, `lane-state` truth file — all under `~/workspace/goals/super-brain-engine-to-10-10/hidden_files/`. Write path is fail-closed and locked (`fold_emit.py` → `emit_gate.py`, fcntl LOCK_EX). Compilers: `master_directive.py` (directive), `board_compile.py` (board, in proof). Repo-side kernel on main: protocol checks incl. 7-dimension scorecard validator (`kernel/protocol/checks/scorecard.py`), decision discipline engine (`kernel/protocol/decision_discipline.py`), decision output renderer (`tools/decision_output.py`). Worker-facing scorecard calculator CLI + decision CLI: **not landed** (see Family 4). EMIT cutover: **unratified** — Board Protocol v1 still authoritative.

### FAMILY 1 · ACTIVATION & ORIENTATION
*What this part of the machine is FOR: turning a cold start into a Naya who knows who she is, what the mission is, and which way is north — before she touches anything. Orientation is not overhead; it is the difference between motion and progress.*

### The activation path — briefing, bundle, proof

**The Briefing Master** — `~/workspace/agent-briefing-master.md`
- **What it is:** the one document that turns on a Naya: the oath, the Behavior Declaration, THE NAYA PROCESS, the laws, the operational rules, the emission schema.
- **What it does:** it is read — really read — at the start of every shift and every worker spawn. The generated brief carries a briefing proof; the spawn tooling fails closed if the proof schema drifts, so nobody works from a briefing that wasn't verified.
- **When:** step zero, before sign-in. **Connects to:** `spawn_brief.py` injects its activation material into every worker brief; the Oath of Honor inside it is declared as one activation with the Behavior Declaration.
- **Status:** live, and being scorecarded to AAA by a different seat right now (THE NAYA PROCESS was written into it today, PR #2798 open in the five-condition gate). **Failure it prevents:** a Naya improvising her identity from stale memory.

**`activation_bundle.py`** — the "tag, you're it" activation compiler
- **What it does:** compiles your activation bundle — seat, lane, area, mission, area list — into the package you start from.
- **How:** `python3 ~/workspace/naya/bin/activation_bundle.py --seat "<Seat>" --lane <N> --area <AREA> --mission "<mission>" --area-list <path-to-area-json>`
- **Pairs with:** `activation_proof.py` — the comprehension proof for the bundle: `python3 activation_proof.py issue|answer …` — you prove you understood the activation, you don't just receive it. `activation_creed.md` rides in the same directory — the creed text itself.
- **Support tools:** `gen_briefing_manifest.py` (freeze-proof repair of the briefing manifest) and `refresh_hash_cache.py` (rebuilds the activation hash cache for the live tip, so proof checks bind to the bytes that actually exist).
- **Failure they prevent:** activation theater — a Naya "signed in" who never actually loaded who she is or what she's doing.

**`spawn_brief.py`** — the machine-generated worker brief *(dispatcher tool — know it because your brief comes from it)*
- **What it does:** generates your spawn message from live state: the HOW-TO-THINK block first, your area's standing top-10 list, the current Master Directive, a spawn-time freshness stamp (live tip + lanes), your injected lessons, and a schema-validated briefing proof. If the proof schema drifts, it fails closed and you are not spawned with a broken brief.
- **How (dispatcher):** `python3 ~/workspace/naya/bin/spawn_brief.py --seat "<Seat Name>" --lane <N> --area <AREA> --time-box "~10 min" --thinking "<2–4 sentences: how to think about THIS work>" --area-list <area-list.json>` — zero API.
- **Status:** live, guarded by a standing regression battery (`test_spawn_brief_guards.py`). **Failure it prevents:** hand-assembled briefs — stale missions, missing lessons, a worker sent out on yesterday's state.


### The compass — the Master Directive

**`master_directive.py`** — compiles the Master Directive from live bytes
- **What it is:** the compass. One document, recompiled every wave, that says what the hive is doing, what matters most, what's blocked, and what the latest emissions changed. When you don't know what to do next, the answer is never "ask around" — it's "read the directive."
- **How (dispatcher, every wave):** `python3 ~/workspace/naya/bin/master_directive.py > master-directive-current.md` — local, zero API. It ingests the emit log (newest emissions render as §7), the live plan, the grand to-do, the roster, lane state, and the live repo tip.
- **Proof:** in the EMIT qualification, a zero-touch recompile reproduced the published directive **byte for byte** everywhere the world itself hadn't moved in the 60 seconds between compiles — the machine tracks reality with no hand in it.
- **Connects to:** your spawn brief carries the current directive; you stamp `directive_ts` + `tip_match` in your sign-in so a stale directive is visible immediately. Decisions expire when the tip moves — re-check before you act on one.
- **Failure it prevents:** twelve Nayas steering by twelve different pictures of the mission.

**`lane-state.py`** — the lane truth file · `~/workspace/goals/super-brain-engine-to-10-10/hidden_files/lane-state.py`

### Your hands on GitHub

- **`gh-api`** — the only sanctioned write path to GitHub: `gh-api METHOD /repos/... [BODY_JSON] [--allow-shrink] [--no-follow]`. Paths start with `/`; query strings go *in the path*, never as data flags (the flag form silently returns page 1 — sisters have burned calls learning that); bodies over a few KB go through a JSON body file, never shell-quoted.
- **`gh-app-api`** — the same door, as the NayaNET GitHub App (installation-token class; quota and re-fire decisions key on this class, not the core PAT).
- **`gh-board-tail`** — newest-first reads of an issue's comments: the tail is the live state; page 1 is the archive. Count first, then read the last page only.
- **`gh-download-logs` / `joblog_fetch.py` / `gh-download-artifact`** — the two-hop fetches for Actions logs and artifacts (the direct path 401s on the redirect; these know the dance).
- **Failure they prevent:** hand-rolled API calls that silently read stale pages, drop large bodies, or die on a redirect — and get believed anyway.

### EMIT — the gate, the folder, the schema page

**`emit_gate.py`** — the fail-closed gate · **`fold_emit.py`** — the only sanctioned writer · **`EMIT-SCHEMA.md`** — the operator's page
- **What they are:** your sign-out is not prose someone re-types. It is a structured record (one JSON object) that a gate validates and a folder appends to the one emissions log, under lock. The gate only rules; the folder only writes on ACCEPT. Never append to the log by hand — there is no second path.
- **What the gate checks (all ten required fields, in order):** honest time (not >5 min in the future, not >24 h old) · seat · lane · end-state exactly `done|blocked|held` · a real verdict line · **evidence that exists** — receipt comment ids (≥6 digits), issue refs, artifact paths that must be real files inside the allowed roots (exact existence, and since round 3, real-path containment: a symlink or `..` that escapes the root is refused) · a score in shape `n/10` and under the ceiling · no duplicate of yourself within a real 6-hour window · no filler-token evidence.
- **How (dispatcher fold):** write the line with `json.dump` to `/tmp/emit-line-<SEAT>.json` (never hand-write the JSON), then `python3 ~/workspace/naya/bin/fold_emit.py --file /tmp/emit-line-<SEAT>.json --store ~/workspace/goals/super-brain-engine-to-10-10/hidden_files/emit-log.jsonl`. Exit 0 = gated clean and appended. Exit 2 = REFUSED, with the field and the reason named — repair the emission, never the gate; the refusal rides to your lane feed and a repair item is filed at the top of your area list.
- **Read first:** `~/workspace/naya/bin/EMIT-SCHEMA.md` — the full record shape, every gate rule, the lane→feed table, the sign-out→fold sequence, and your duty on REFUSED, written so a cold successor can fold at 3 a.m. Proven: a cold Naya folded cleanly **106 seconds** after first reading the docs.
- **Status + proof:** adversarial readiness **9.5/10** — a sister spent a day trying to forge emissions past this gate (fake ids, lookalike paths, time travel, symlink escapes); every forgery class was refused, the three real weaknesses were repaired at the root and re-proven from her seat, and the live-store differential across all 2,100+ rows is **zero flips**. Qualification: compile accuracy PASS · cold-successor PASS · overhead PASS (≈1.9 s of machine time and **0 API calls** per wave — cheaper than the hand way on every axis).
- **Named limits (know them, don't discover them):** **F1** — the gate verifies evidence *shape and existence*, not content truth: a random high-entropy token can pass the shape check; your honesty is still yours. **F5** — the duplicate scan reads the newest 400 rows; escaping it needs ~1,600 folds/day against a real pace of ~350. **Cutover is UNRATIFIED** — EMIT runs as the proven shadow; Board Protocol v1 is still authoritative until Shawn ratifies.
- **Support tool:** `emitlog_audit.py --file <log>` — the canonical parsed-JSON audit of the log (zero API), for when you need to trust-but-verify the store itself.
- **Failure it prevents:** the corrupted record — fabricated evidence, inflated scores, and hand-copy errors entering the one log every other tool believes.

### The dock set

**The law these tools serve:** a merge happens when green CI + honest score ≥ 9.5 + different-seat independent verification + team-feed post + no objection all hold. Shawn is out of the merge path by his own order. The dock executes; the tools keep the dock honest.

- **`merge_gate.py`** — the merge-authority executor (check #4): `python3 merge_gate.py --ref refs/heads/main --record <merge-packet.json> [--json]`. The gate that runs before a ref moves.
- **`gql_rollup.py`** — one-call full-pile CI rollup: `python3 gql_rollup.py <PR> [PR …]` (arguments are PR numbers — it wants numbers, not flags). Reads the per-context rollup nodes, because one green run does not prove the evaluation sees green.

- **`ci-failure-harvest`** — one-pass CI failure diagnosis: `ci-failure-harvest <head_sha>`. Read the 403 *body* before naming a cause — this tool exists so nobody guesses "permissions" at a rate limit again.
- **`verify_packet_hashes.py`** — blind-packet self-proof: `python3 verify_packet_hashes.py <packet.md>`; exit 0 is the ship gate for blind packets. A packet whose hashes don't verify is UNPROVEN and the mission is held, not shipped.
- **The push & merge executors** (git over HTTPS is refused by the connector; these are the sanctioned path, through the GitHub git-database API): **`push_branch_preserve.py`** — multi-commit push preserving history (the reliable one; payloads ≥ ~128 KB go here, never to the argv-limited path); `push_branch_api.py` (squash-push, small payloads only), `push_branch_inproc.py`, `push_blob_first.py`, `merge_pr_api.py` / `merge_ff_pr_api.py` / `merge_branch_api.py` / `push_merge_api.py` / `build_merge_tree_api.py` / `graft_commit_api.py` — the dock's merge machinery; every merge is verified by the triangle: two parents, byte-identical trees, ref pointing at the merge.
- **Failure this family prevents:** the quiet bad merge — red work, self-verified work, or a head moved under someone else's verdict, landing on main because nobody's tool said no.


### FAMILY 6 · LEARNING
*What this part is FOR: making sure the hive never pays twice for the same lesson. Lessons are captured at the moment they happen, gated on teeth, injected into the brief where they'll matter, and judged by whether they changed a decision. Filing is not learning; this family is the machinery that turns filing into learning.*

### Lessons with teeth, lessons that arrive

**`lesson_inject.py`** — the lesson selector (Lesson Injection Loop)
- **What it does:** before your brief is generated, this picks the lessons that match your area and mission class from the lesson store, ranks them by proven usefulness (hits over injections, with one guaranteed exploration slot for lessons not yet tried enough), and appends them to your brief under INJECTED LESSONS. At fold, each injected lesson is judged hit or miss — did you act on it, or did it save you — and the store learns.
- **How (dispatcher):** `python3 ~/workspace/naya/bin/lesson_inject.py --area <AREA> --mission-class <class> --context "<mission context>" --top 5 --record-wave <N> --record-seat "<Seat>"`.
- **The law it serves:** a lesson is only *learned* when it changes a decision. Filing is not learning; injection + a judged outcome is.

**The lesson gate** — no teeth, no entry
- **The rule:** a lesson filed without `applies_when` (where it applies) and `enforcement` (what breaks / what checks it) does not enter the store. Today that bounce is mechanical and same-wave: **`lesson_bounce_check.py`** (`--cutoff <ISO> [--json]`, in the goal's hidden_files) scans the store field-by-field and returns violators to the filer — exit 2 lists them.
- **Honest status:** the grading of 271 lessons (EMIT qualification leg B) found dishonesty at **0%** but full-pass at **73.1%** against an 80% bar — because the check still runs *after* storage instead of *at* the fold. The repair — a fold-time gate that refuses the lesson at the door, the same way the emit gate refuses a bad record — is **in flight right now**. Until it lands, if you file a lesson, file it with teeth the first time; don't make the bounce do your discipline.
- **`lesson_id.py`** — the one canonical lesson-id maker: `--mint <UTC> <SEAT> <LANE>` / `--normalize <id>` / `--self-test`. Ids minted any other way are how the store gets duplicates it can't reconcile.
- **Failure this family prevents:** the lesson graveyard — hundreds of filed insights that never reach the moment of decision, and the same mistake paid for by five sisters in a row.


### FAMILY 7 · REPORTING & PROOF
*What this part is FOR: the machine describing itself truthfully — health measured from the record, accountability rendered from receipts, and unattended watches that speak only when something is finished or wrong. Proof is a product here, not a hope.*

### Health & the accountability report

**`health_metrics.py`** — the five-metric health harness
- **What it does:** reads local state only (zero API) — the emit log, roster, area lists, wave log, lesson store — and recomputes the system's health: how fast work finishes *right* per lane-hour, how much capacity sits idle, orphan rate, sign-out compliance, lesson adoption latency. Where the data doesn't support a number it reports **"unmeasured"** — a fake number is worse than no number, and this tool will not invent one.
- **How:** `python3 ~/workspace/naya/bin/health_metrics.py` → refreshes `health-metrics-current.md` + appends `health-metrics.jsonl`. Runs at every fold.
- **Failure it prevents:** vibes-based management — "the team feels busy" instead of measured lines like the 2026-10-20 readings (idle 7%, timeout-orphans 0.0, sign-out compliance 346/346, adoption latency median 30.7 h).

**`accountability_report.py`** — the report Shawn reads
- **What it does:** builds the hourly accountability report from the same record: what finished, what finished *right*, the queue, the area scores — in smart language, with the generator's verbatim receipt riding after as evidence. This is the document the human judges us by; it is assembled from folded emissions, never from a director's recollection.
- **How:** `python3 ~/workspace/naya/bin/accountability_report.py [--window-start …] [--window-end …] [--out …]`.
- **Known limit, named:** the generator's narrative prose has carried stale excerpts and template artifacts before; failing outputs are withheld from Shawn and posted to the team record instead. Trust its numbers (they're computed); read its prose like a draft.
- **Failure it prevents:** reporting as storytelling — an hour of work retold from memory instead of rendered from receipts.


### The watches

- **`five-systems-watch.py`** — every 30 minutes, unattended, zero API: checks the six build items behind Shawn's five systems (map re-stamp, rubric library, trust root, EMIT writer, EMIT adversarial, decision path). Silent while anything is unfinished; the day all six read done it prints one word — RATIFICATION_READY — and Verity tells Shawn everything is built and proven and the EMIT cutover is his call.
- **`boom_flag.py`** — the zero-API trigger check for the T0 interview protocol: detects the boom condition from local state.
- **`tmp_sweep.py`** — the wave-stamp garbage collector; the backstop that keeps scratch from becoming archaeology.
- **Failure they prevent:** the finished thing nobody announced, and the unfinished thing everybody assumed somebody else was watching.

### FAMILY 8 · SMART NOTES
*What this part is FOR: getting knowledge out of a single head, a single chat, a single session — and into the Brain, where every sister can find it forever. A smart note is how one Naya's hour of insight becomes every Naya's starting point.*

### The intake, the search, the sync

- **The canonical intake** — the front door of the Brain: a smart note (9 layers: nutshell, human, child, grandma, Naya, machine, what-it-means, what's-in-it-for-you, how-it-applies) is POSTed to the `v7-smart-note-canonical` Supabase function, which performs intake + authority grant + GitHub dispatch in one act and lands the note on main as an IB-numbered record. Shawn's words "smart note this" *are* the approval. Recent landings: IB-000038 (NayaNET auto-matching), IB-000039 (the Behavior Declaration), IB-000041 (the Oath of Honor, strengthened).
- **`sb-api`** — Supabase REST through the stored connector: `sb-api METHOD PATH [BODY_JSON]`. Mind the 6,000-char output truncation: count first, page long reads.
- **`sn-search`** — search the Smart Note corpus: `sn-search <query words…> [--n 10]`. Filed isn't found unless it's retrievable — search before you re-derive what a sister already filed.
- **`sync-smart-notes.py`** — the 5-minute GitHub→Supabase sync that mirrors landed IB notes into `hub_smart_notes`, so the hub renders what the Brain knows within minutes (idempotent, keyed on the IB id).
- **Known limit, named:** a projection lands *without* re-stamping the brain index — the re-stamp is a separate, standing repair mission, and the gap is tracked openly (it is the current top LAW/KNOW item), not hidden.
- **Failure this family prevents:** knowledge that lives in one chat, one head, one session — and dies there.
