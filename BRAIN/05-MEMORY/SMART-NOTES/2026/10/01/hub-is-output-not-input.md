## In a nutshell

The Hub is the visual projection of intelligence — the output, not the input. Intelligence enters upstream: you ask an AI for a smart note, it writes an Intelligent Block into the brain, a trigger fires, and the block appears in the feed. The Hub never creates intelligence; it reveals it.

## Human note

Stop treating the Hub as the place where intelligence is made. It is the place where intelligence is *seen*. Capture happens in conversation with the AI — "make me a smart note" — and the Hub's job is to project what was captured, with provenance intact, the moment it lands.

## Child note

Imagine a television. The shows aren't made inside the TV — they're made in studios and sent to it. The Hub is the TV. Smart notes are the shows. You ask Naya to make one, it gets created, and then it plays on your Hub.

## Grandma note

It's like a photo frame that updates itself. You don't paint the picture inside the frame — you take the photo out in the world, and the frame shows it. Ask the AI for a smart note, and the Hub displays it for you.

## Naya note

My capture path is conversational, not a button in the output projection. When Shawn asks for a smart note, I author the Intelligent Block with all its layers, commit it to the brain, and the trigger fans it out to personal and collective feeds. The Hub renders what the trigger delivers — nothing more, nothing less.

## Machine note

INPUT: human utterance → capture intent → IB author → brain/smart-notes write. TRIGGER: new IB detected → event emitted. FANOUT: personal feed (identified to owner) + collective feed (anonymized). OUTPUT: Hub renders blocks from events. The Hub subscribes; it never writes upstream.

## Learning lesson

Every time we bolted capture into the display layer, we created a second, weaker input path that bypassed provenance. One input path — the AI conversation — keeps every block's origin clean.

## What it ultimately means

There is exactly one way intelligence enters the system and one place it is projected. That separation is what makes the collective trustworthy: every block in the collective feed arrived through the same governed capture, with identity stripped but provenance preserved.

## How to use it

When judging any Hub feature, ask: is this input or output? If it's input wearing an output costume — a capture button that writes straight to display state — reroute it through the IB pipeline or remove it.

## What's in it for you

You never wonder where something in your feed came from or whether it's real. Everything visible arrived through the same door, and the Hub never invents what the pipeline didn't deliver.
