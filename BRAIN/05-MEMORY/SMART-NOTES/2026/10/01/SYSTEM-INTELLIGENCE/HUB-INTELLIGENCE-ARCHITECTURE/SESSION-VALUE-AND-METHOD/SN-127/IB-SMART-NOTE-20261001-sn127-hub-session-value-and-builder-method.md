## ✦ IN A NUTSHELL

One working session with the Human Director produced the complete conceptual key for the Hub and the builder method that can finally break the year-long shell cycle:

1. **The Hub is the retina of NayaPOWER** — output/projection only, never input, never agency, never another chatbot.
2. **Exactly three pipelines feed it** — smart notes (captured intelligence), activity (state/change/process), reports (structured synthesis) — as canonical objects, never copies.
3. **One intelligence object → many valid projections** — Feed, Today, Reports, Library, Lists, Spaces etc. are lenses on the same intelligence, not eleven apps.
4. **"Ask Naya" is retrieval, not agency** — a search box over the knowledge base ("ask anything about NayaPOWER"), plus voice read-back of blocks. You talk to Naya through your own AI; the Hub never chats.
5. **The builder method** — zoom out to the full scope, zoom in to the pieces, spec the whole thing completely *before* building, then evolve it piece by piece. Never try to build it all at once.

## 🩷 HUMAN NOTE

Shawn has spent more than a year trying to get AIs to build a complete Hub app. Every attempt looked like success, then got stuck at the shell stage — beautiful surfaces with sample intelligence, never a finished, coherent, functioning application.

This session he finally stated the architecture so plainly that no builder can misunderstand it: the Hub is the screen, not the camera. Intelligence is produced outside the Hub (by Naya, by human work, by any AI the human talks to), captured as canonical Intelligent Blocks in GitHub, distilled by trigger into the ten layers, emitted as governed events — and only then projected into the Hub. Smart notes are input events, not Hub features. The feed is an intelligence consumption system with three streams (Personal / Collective-anonymized / Activity) and smart views over them.

His prescription for the builder disease: we don't zoom out enough (full scope of what must be done) or zoom in enough (the pieces), and we try to get it done all at once. It can't be done all at once. It has to be spec'd completely, understood completely, then built in pieces that evolve toward the whole.

He also confirmed the samples currently in Hub prototypes are visual fixtures — they answer "what should this look like," not "does this intelligence exist" — and that distinction must be encoded in the product contract.

## 🔵 GRANDMA NOTE

Shawn finally got the team to understand something he's been saying for a year: the Hub is for *looking at* your intelligence, not for *putting in* your intelligence. You put intelligence in by talking to Naya (through whatever AI you use). The Hub shows you what came out — your notes, what's happening, your reports — organized like a social feed for your own mind, with your private things kept private and only the intelligence (never your identity) shared with others.

The other big lesson: big things get built in small pieces, but only after someone draws the complete map first.
