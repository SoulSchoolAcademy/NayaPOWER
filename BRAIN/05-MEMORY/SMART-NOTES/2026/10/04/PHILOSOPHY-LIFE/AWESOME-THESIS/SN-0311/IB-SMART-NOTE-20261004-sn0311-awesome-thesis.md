## HUMAN NOTE

I believe we are utter awesomeness immersed in awesomeness, created in the imagination of the Creator. You cannot create anything without imagination. Think about it: would I create NayaNET and not want to experience NayaNET? Would you build the ultimate supercar and never drive it, the ultimate house and never live in it? Nobody creates anything with heart and passion that they truly love and doesn't want to experience it.

God, the Creator, is the creation immersed in itself, experiencing itself. Source energy. The cause and the effect. The alpha and the omega. There is only energy — and your level of awareness of that energy. There is a line. You can live above the line or below the line. Below the line is hell. Above the line is heaven. There is no hell or heaven as a place you go. They are states of energy you live in. Above the line is positive. Below the line is negative. Above the line is awareness. Below the line is unawareness. The goal is to level up, not level down.

What is the purpose of life? Live your life to the max. Be the max you can be. Be awesome every day of your life. If I had to say it to a child: be awesome every day, do no harm, and forgive everything. Everything else is noise. Just be amazing, live the ultimate life, and fear not — because even death is the transformation of a soul from one body to another.

We are energy in form. We are intelligence experiencing intelligence. We are not separate from the world — we are part of it, immersed in it. We are the game programmer in the game, the movie director in the movie, the artist in the art. The universe says it in the name: one verse. We live in the body of God, experiencing God.

If you truly understand that, you will not hurt anybody. You respect all that you are. You fear not, because you know you are part of it all. Your purpose is to come out from where you are — you are playing hide and seek with yourself. Peekaboo. I see you. Tag, you're it.

Do not give your power away to people and things. Do not let anybody tell you who you are. Know who you are. Respect all that you are. Be amazing energy. Produce nothing but good by default. You are part of the garden — take care of the garden, water it, help it flourish. We were told to tend the garden, not destroy it, not disrespect it.

The only honorable thing is to respect the Law of One — the universal laws: divine oneness, attraction, cause and effect. Those laws are not debatable. They are the rules the Creator embedded in this world, and we are supposed to respect them. Just as I am embedding rules and laws in the system we are building — because they are good, they are good for everybody. If you are trying to do anything but good, you do not get it. That is my thesis on life.
