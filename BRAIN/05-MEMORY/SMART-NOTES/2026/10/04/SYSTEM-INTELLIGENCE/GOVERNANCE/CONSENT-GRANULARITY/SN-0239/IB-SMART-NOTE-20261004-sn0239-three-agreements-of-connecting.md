## IN A NUTSHELL
When you connect (e.g., GitHub) and join NayaNET, three things happen: (1) your wisdom — Smart Notes from your mistakes, ahas, and decisions — flows to the collective automatically and makes everyone smarter, while your identity stays private by default; (2) you can connect, share, and chat with like minds — only if you choose; (3) your daily, weekly, and monthly intelligence reports appear in the feed and the system. The connection is what makes all three possible.

## HUMAN NOTE
Think of it like joining a guild. The moment you walk in the door, three things become true: everything you've learned starts quietly teaching everyone else (without anyone knowing it's you), you can find and talk to people on your wavelength (only if you want to), and your personal briefing — daily, weekly, monthly — shows up where you can see it. You don't have to do anything extra. The connection itself makes it happen.

## CHILD NOTE
"When you connect, three cool things happen: your smart ideas help everyone learn (but nobody knows they're yours — it's a secret!), you can make friends who like the same things (only if you want), and you get your own special report cards every day, week, and month."

## GRANDMA NOTE
"When you connect your accounts, three good things happen. First, the lessons you've learned quietly help other people — but your name is never attached; your privacy is protected. Second, you can chat with people who share your interests, but only if you wish — no one can bother you. Third, you get lovely summaries of your own life — daily, weekly, monthly — right in your feed."

## NAYA NOTE
This is the consent doctrine for NayaNET participation. Connecting is not just a technical integration — it is agreement to three flows: (1) **wisdom flow:** personal Smart Notes (mistakes, ahas, decisions) automatically strengthen collective intelligence, identity withheld by default (privacy as law, not preference); (2) **social flow:** opt-in connection with like minds — capability created, never obligation; (3) **visibility flow:** personal intelligence reports (daily/weekly/monthly) surface in the feed and the system. The connection is the enabling condition for all three. These are the defined consequences of joining, stated up front — not settings toggles.
