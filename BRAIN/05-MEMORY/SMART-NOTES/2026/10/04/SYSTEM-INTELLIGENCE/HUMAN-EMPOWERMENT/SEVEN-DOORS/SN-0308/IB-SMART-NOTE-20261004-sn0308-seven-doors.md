## IN A NUTSHELL

The Seven Doors are a weekly human-empowerment practice: seven days, seven doors, each pairing a day with a color, a sound frequency, and a spoken affirmation, forming a rhythm of emotional renewal across the week — I Feel (Monday, orange, 417 Hz), I Do / I Am Love (Tuesday, yellow, 528 Hz), I Speak (Wednesday, light blue, 741 Hz), I Love (Thursday, green, 639 Hz), I Am Renewed (Friday, red, 396 Hz, root), I See (Saturday, blue, 852 Hz), I Understand (Sunday, purple, 963 Hz). The architecture is day → color → frequency → affirmation → practice: each door gives the person something to feel, something to say aloud, and something to do. The affirmations are written to be spoken — this system maps directly onto a future Ask Naya daily experience.

## HUMAN NOTE

Shawn built a daily practice system called the Seven Doors — one door for each day of the week, and each door works the same way. Every day has a color, a sound frequency, and a short affirmation you're meant to say out loud. The week tells a story of renewal.

Monday is the Orange Door: I feel. It's about emotional reset — feeling what you feel without getting trapped by it, and without shaming yourself for it. Tuesday is the Yellow Door: I do, I am love — the day love turns into action. Wednesday is the Light Blue Door: I speak — truth, voice, expression; saying what's real clearly and honestly. Thursday is the Green Door: I love — heart connection and compassion, loving without losing yourself. Friday is the Red Door: I am renewed — the root, the foundation, coming back to solid ground. Saturday is the Blue Door: I see — wisdom and clear seeing. And Sunday is the Purple Door: I understand — the week closes in understanding, and the cycle begins again.

Each door comes with a practice — breathing, spoken affirmations, sometimes prayer — and shorter companion pieces for some days, like Magical Monday and Winning Wednesday. The whole thing is designed to be spoken aloud. These aren't essays to read; they're words to say, in your own voice, every day.

One honest note: the source files aren't perfectly consistent about which chakra number goes with which door — some of the shorter files mislabel them. The day, color, frequency, and affirmation are consistent everywhere; the chakra numbering isn't. I've kept what's consistent and flagged what isn't.

## GRANDMA NOTE

This is an old idea made new: mark the days, give each one a meaning, and let the week itself become a teacher. The tradition of setting aside time each day for reflection is ancient; what's new here is the architecture — color, sound, and spoken word working together so the practice engages the body as well as the mind. A weekly rhythm like this doesn't demand belief. It only asks for repetition.
