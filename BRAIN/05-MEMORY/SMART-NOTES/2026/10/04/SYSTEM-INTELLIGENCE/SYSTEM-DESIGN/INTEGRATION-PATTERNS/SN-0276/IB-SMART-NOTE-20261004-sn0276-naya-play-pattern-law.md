## ✦ IN A NUTSHELL

THE PATTERN: every Intelligent Block gets a Naya Play button **by default**. Click play, Naya reads it back to you beautifully. That's just how we do it — no board ships without one. WHAT SHE SAYS: the spoken version is Naya talking to you directly, in first person, conversational — **not** reading the block word-for-word. Like she's sitting across from you telling you what it means. THE CONSTRAINT: **concise or it doesn't work.** The ChatGPT failure mode: she outputs so much it takes 10 minutes to listen. Nobody has 10 minutes. Target the nutshell — ~30–60 seconds, ~75–150 words. Tell them what it means, what it's about, get it over with. Pipeline: `IB → distilled first-person script (nutshell-length) → Chatterbox → cached audio → Play button.` THE DIRECTION: voice-first is the future. Voice playback on every board. Voice input / dictation on search. Audio commands. Builders: the demo page is the adoption pattern — same button, same contract, every room, now with the concision constraint baked in.
