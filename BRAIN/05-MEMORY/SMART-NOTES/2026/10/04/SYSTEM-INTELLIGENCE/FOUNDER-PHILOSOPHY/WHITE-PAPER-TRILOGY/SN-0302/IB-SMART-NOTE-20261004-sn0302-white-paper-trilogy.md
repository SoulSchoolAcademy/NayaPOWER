## IN A NUTSHELL

Shawn's White Paper trilogy is the canonical statement of what NayaPOWER is and why it exists, organized as three documents: a Letter from the Founder that gives the WHY, a White Paper that describes the WHAT (a human-AI intelligence operating layer), and an Operating Model that gives the HOW. The trilogy's core diagnosis is the 350-horsepower problem: AI is a race engine installed in a golf cart — extraordinary capability trapped inside an ordinary human experience — and NayaPOWER exists to close that gap. The objective it names is 10-star AI service, delivered through a model-agnostic operating layer where the complexity belongs in the system and the simplicity belongs with the human.

## HUMAN NOTE

Shawn wrote three documents that together explain the whole mission. The first is a personal letter about why any of this matters. The second describes what NayaPOWER actually is: a layer of intelligence that sits between a person and AI, so the person doesn't have to become an AI expert to get extraordinary results. The third describes how it operates day to day.

The trilogy starts from a simple observation he calls the 350-horsepower problem. Imagine putting a 350-horsepower race engine inside a golf cart. The engine is extraordinary, but the vehicle doesn't automatically become extraordinary transportation. That's where AI is today: the capability is remarkable, but the human experience of using it hasn't kept pace. He calls that the AI paradox — AI became dramatically more capable, yet the human experience did not automatically become dramatically better. NayaPOWER exists to reduce that gap.

The papers name ten specific problems it solves: complexity, prompting, context loss, memory, organization, execution, verification, learning, personalization, and compounding. And they set the bar in plain words: the objective is 10-star AI service. Two design rules carry the whole thing. First, the complexity belongs in the system, and the simplicity belongs with the human. Second, the system is model-agnostic — it works with whatever AI is best, because the intelligence is in the layer, not in any single model.

## CHILD NOTE

Shawn wrote three letters that explain his big idea. The idea is: AI is like a super-powerful race car engine stuck inside a tiny golf cart. The engine is amazing, but the ride isn't. So he's building a better vehicle around the engine — one that makes the ride smooth and simple for the person inside, while all the complicated machinery stays hidden underneath.

## GRANDMA NOTE

Every powerful tool in history eventually needed someone to make it usable by ordinary people. The engine was invented long before the automobile became comfortable. Shawn's papers make the same point about artificial intelligence: the power exists, but the experience of using it is still rough and demanding. His answer is to build the missing layer — the comfortable vehicle around the powerful engine — and to judge it by one standard: does the person feel served, simply and well.
