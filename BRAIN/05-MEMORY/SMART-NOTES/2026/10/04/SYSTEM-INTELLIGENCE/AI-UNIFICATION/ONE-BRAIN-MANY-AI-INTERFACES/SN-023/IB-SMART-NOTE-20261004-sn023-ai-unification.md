## LEARNING LESSON

The key strategic insight is that **model choice should become independent from intelligence continuity**.

If the durable intelligence remains governed and portable, people can adopt a better model tomorrow without losing the learning accumulated yesterday. Specialized AIs can work in parallel without forcing the human to manually synchronize them.

That is the compounding advantage.

## KEY DECISIONS / PRINCIPLES

1. **One brain, many doors.** Never create one independent memory/brain per AI.
2. **The person owns the continuity.** Model vendors are replaceable participants, not owners of the person's intelligence.
3. **Restore before work.** An AI should retrieve relevant current intelligence before substantive action.
4. **Return intelligence to the river.** Valuable outcomes should flow back through canonical capture, evidence, verification, learning, and succession.
5. **Capability ≠ authority.** Seeing intelligence does not authorize acting on it.
6. **Connection is governed.** Privacy, consent, ownership, scope, revocation, and provenance travel with the intelligence.
7. **Hub ≠ brain.** The Hub visualizes/project intelligence; NayaPOWER remains the governed substrate.
8. **Do not overclaim.** “Works with every AI” becomes a proven product claim only after explicit integrations and interoperability tests establish it.
