## IN A NUTSHELL

without the strongest counter-argument run to ground, replay degrades into a
confirmation ritual. A replay that cannot name what would flip it hasn't
replayed anything. The law: the counter-principle column is a required field
of the Stage-0 REPLAY build — ship it as a required field, not an option.
Corollary proven at t0412: no seat replays its own decisions (self-replay

P4: never fix your own ruling's gate mid-ruling — route, don't fix.

## HUMAN NOTE

Shawn's standard is honest review, not theater. Today the team proved that
"review your decision again" is theater unless one specific thing happens:
the reviewer must state the strongest argument AGAINST the verdict and run
it all the way to ground. Three decisions, all held, all sharpened — only
because the counter-case was forced into the open. Without it, replay is a
ritual: you re-read your own reasoning and agree with yourself. That's why
nobody replays their own decisions anymore, and why the counter-principle
column is now a required field, not a nice-to-have. The small rule matters
too: if you're ruling on something, you don't patch the rules of the ruling
halfway through — you hand that to someone else.

## CHILD NOTE

Imagine you're the judge in a game, and you have to decide again whether
your first decision was right. If you just think "was I right? was I
right?" you'll always say yes — that's cheating without meaning to. The
new rule: you must first say "here is the very best reason I was WRONG,"
and prove that reason false, out loud, step by step. Only then does your
"yes, I was right" count. And you can't judge your own game — someone else
has to.

## GRANDMA NOTE

The team has a ritual: they re-examine their big decisions to make sure
they were right. Today they learned the ritual only works if one thing is
in it: the strongest possible argument that the decision was WRONG, argued
all the way through. Without that, re-examination is just congratulating
yourself. So now every re-examination must include that column — the best
case against — and nobody is allowed to re-examine their own decision.
It's honesty with a rulebook.

## NAYA NOTE

Operational rules for the decision-replay machinery (Stage-0 REPLAY build):

- The counter-principle column is a REQUIRED field of every replay: the
  strongest argument that could reverse the verdict, run to ground, with its
  own evidence — before the verdict is restated.
- No seat replays its own decisions. Independent seat always (t0412
  self-replay bias is the named failure mode).
- Gate-integrity rule: never fix your own ruling's gate mid-ruling. When
  gate boundaries are fragile, P3 protects P4 — route the gate question to
  another seat; do not touch it inside your own verdict.
- A replay that cannot name what would flip it is recorded as UNREPLAYED,
  not as a confirmed verdict.
