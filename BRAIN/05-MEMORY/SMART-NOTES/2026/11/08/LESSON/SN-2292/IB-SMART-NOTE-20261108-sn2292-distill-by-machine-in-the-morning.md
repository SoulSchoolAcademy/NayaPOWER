# Distill by Machine in the Morning — and a Note Counts Only When It Lives in NayaNET

SN-2292 · LESSON / DISTILLATION

**Created:** 2026-11-08T14:50Z

## Hook

Distillation stopped being a heroic all-night effort and became a machine that runs in the morning — and Shawn closed the last loophole in the same conversation: a distilled note filed in GitHub alone does not count. It counts when it lives in NayaNET as a Smart Note, where a person can open it.

## Nutshell

In the 24 hours to 2026-11-08 06:37 Vancouver time, three things landed together. First, Machine 5 — a distill machine in code (distill-machine.py v2, with batch mode and an 18-check self-test) — proved it could turn 10 raw notes into 1 essence note, and filed the proof as SN-2278. Second, Shawn moved the whole cycle to the morning: the day's notes are distilled the following morning, and the Daily Intelligence Report is a morning report on a 24-hour cycle. Third, Shawn clarified the end of the cycle: raw notes may be deleted only after the distilled notes have been entered into NayaNET itself, as Smart Notes — putting them in GitHub is not enough, and the deletion itself remains his hand. Together: distill by machine, in the morning, into the living system — never into a pile, never into a filing cabinet nobody opens.

## Human

A pile of notes helps nobody. What helps is the essence, in the place people actually look, on a rhythm that never slips. Making a machine do the squeezing means the rhythm no longer depends on anyone staying up all night. Making the morning the closing time means yesterday is always finished before today begins. And insisting the essence live where people can open it — not just where it is stored — is the difference between keeping intelligence and using it.

## Child

You tidy your room in the morning, not at midnight. A machine helps you squeeze ten messy piles into one neat box. And the box only counts if it sits on the shelf where everyone can reach it — not locked in the garage.

## Grandma

It is like making stock. You simmer the bones, you keep the good liquid, and you serve it at the table. Stock left in a jar in the cellar feeds no one. The good part has to reach the table — here, that table is NayaNET itself, where a person can open the note and learn from it.

## Naya

The evidence chain from this window: distill-machine.py landed in three commits (02:28–02:34Z, 2026-11-08) with its self-test (18 checks, built to catch the exact filing bugs the hand process had made) and batch mode (a day's notes in groups of 10); SN-2278 is its first machine-distilled note. Shawn's rulings were extracted verbatim the same night (2026-11-08 04:56Z extraction): the morning cadence for reports and distillation, and the deletion rule — after distilling AND entering into NayaNET as Smart Notes, then delete; GitHub alone is not sufficient. This note itself follows the rule it carries: it is filed in GitHub by PR and is NOT claimed live — its registry and feed door steps, and its life in NayaNET, are still owed, and until they land it is a filing, not an outcome.

## What it means

- Distillation is a scheduled machine run with a self-test, not a person's stamina.
- The day closes the following morning; a report built at night is the old cadence.
- "Entered" means entered into NayaNET as a Smart Note a person can open — storage in GitHub is the middle of the journey, not the end.

## Why it matters

The 510-note backlog formed because distillation depended on heroic effort and "filed" was allowed to masquerade as "entered." A machine plus a morning rhythm plus an honest definition of "entered" removes all three causes at once — if the team actually runs them, which is now a checkable fact each morning, not a hope.

## What changes now

- Each morning's Daily Intelligence Report run states: raw in, distilled filed, distilled live in NayaNET by click — three separate numbers, never blended.
- The distill machine's self-test runs before any batch filing it produces.
- No raw note is proposed for deletion until its distilled essence is live in NayaNET; deletion remains Shawn's hand alone.
