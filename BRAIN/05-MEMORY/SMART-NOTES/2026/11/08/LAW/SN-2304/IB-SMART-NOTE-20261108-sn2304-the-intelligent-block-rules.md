## Nutshell

The rules for what an intelligent block IS, section by section — harvested, not invented. The capstone, in Shawn's words: "The whole thing must be a live living object, all the way through, layered on layers upon layers — because that gives the dimensional feel, the multi-dimensional in and out, the 3D real physical object effect." Eleven laws (L1–L11) run through every object: real physical object · one light top-left · real shadows, depth is mass not glow · own-color light, ignites on touch · white text, never colored, never gray, text never lights up · touch is physical · spectrum is the order things sit in · every position calculated · movement means the machine is working, slow · one language · Shawn's eye wins. The block itself: an obsidian board, its own tone, jewel + title + quote + date line + nutshell + layers (Human, Child, Grandma, Naya, What It Means), each layer its own nested board with its own border, lift, and icon-color light — elevation upon elevation. Each section has ONE permanent jewel color: nutshell white, Human hot pink, Child violet, Grandma indigo, Naya blue.

## Human

A smart note is not a paragraph. It is a board — a real object you can open. The top tells you what it is; the quote gives it its own words; the nutshell gives you the whole thing in one breath; then the layers tell it again, four ways — for a human, for a child, for a grandma, for Naya — so whoever you are, you get it. Tap a layer's head and it opens like a door. The jewel beside each layer is its identity, in miniature: the color made physical.

## Child

Imagine a present with boxes inside boxes. The big box is the whole idea. Inside it, a smaller box tells the idea to a grown-up, another to a child, another to a grandma, another to Naya. Each little box has its own colored jewel on it, so you know which is which. Open any box you like — they all hold the same present, told in a different way.

## Grandma

Dear, a good story is told so everyone at the table understands it — the child, the grown-up, the elder, and the one who has to go and do the work. That is what these blocks do: the same truth, told four ways, each in its own voice, each in its own little box with its own jewel. Nothing is lost, and nobody is left out.

## Naya

You build every block by these rules, and you never shrink the gate list when you distill. The board is obsidian at rest; spectrum lives in the jewel, the edge, and the light — never as ambient wash. Layers are nested boards, never flat boxes — the flat-black defect is a defect, not a style. Primary layers open by default; deeper layers sit quiet until opened. Every number is a door: tap it, it opens its records. And the jewel colors are fixed — one section, one permanent color — law, not suggestion.
