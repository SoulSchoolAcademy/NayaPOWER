## Hook

You do not have to pay to earn. Everybody gets a job at join — free or paid — and everybody earns 50%.

## Nutshell

The payout rule, settled by Shawn's ruling: they are paid when we are paid. They are paid on ALL the levels. And — the part that was wrong before — you make 50% of the revenue even if you have not paid for Naya Power. A free member can promote and still make money. He is not going to limit them to that. That is why everybody gets a job: you don't have to pay to get the 50% of the revenue. The design review's scoping of the payout to paid levels was wrong. RECORDS chart-27's marking — "50% all levels" — was right. The page's job-card and close, which said "50% … on any paid level," were too narrow and now read the ruling: 50% on all levels, free members included.

## Human

Imagine you join a club for free, and on your first day they hand you a job: tell people about us, and when they join, you earn half of what they pay — forever, on every level, paid the moment we're paid. You never have to buy anything to earn. That is the deal. The club doesn't limit earning to the people who paid — because the people who haven't paid yet are the ones with the most friends to tell.

## Child

You get a job the moment you join — even if you joined for free. Tell your friends, and when they join, you get half of what they pay. You don't have to pay anything first. Everybody gets a job. Everybody can earn.

## Grandma

You don't have to spend money to make money here. The moment you join — free or paid — you have a job: share it with people, and when they join, you earn half. Paid the moment we're paid. On every level. Nobody is limited because they haven't paid yet.

## Naya

This ruling settles the payout scoping that sat open on the Pricing page — and it settles it in the direction of the masses, not the money. The three wants (connect · earn · affiliate) are for everybody, including the free member: the free member connects (Auto-Match), the free member creates (their world), and the free member earns (50% on all levels). The ambassador system was already designed this way — "every citizen becomes an ambassador at join" — and the ruling confirms the design and corrects the document that narrowed it. The economics hold because the payout only exists when revenue exists: 50% of what is paid, paid the moment we are paid, first payout day ten then daily. A free member earning does not cost us — it brings us the next paying member. The growth loop is the business: citizens earn → talk → more join → more intelligence compounds → more value → more earning. Limiting earning to paid members would have cut the loop at its root — the free member is the one with the most to gain by sharing, and the most friends to share with.

## What it means

- **Free members earn 50% too.** No payment is required to earn. The question "do you make 50% even if you have not paid?" is answered YES, in his words.
- **Paid on ALL levels.** Not "any paid level" — all levels. The job-card and close copy now read the ruling, and RECORDS chart-27 already did.
- **Paid when we're paid.** 50% the moment we're paid, first payout day ten then daily. The timing never changed; the scope did.
- **Everybody gets a job.** At join, free or paid, every citizen is an ambassador with tracked links, a dashboard, and payouts. That is the "you're hired" promise — and it is free to take.
- **The design review was wrong on this one.** Its scoping to paid levels is superseded by this ruling. The RECORDS marking was right. When a review and his word disagree, his word wins — and the review gets corrected, not the word.

## Why it matters

This is the difference between a club for people with money and a movement for the masses. If earning required paying first, the people who need the money most — the everyday Joe, the mom, the grandma, the child — would be locked out of the very thing that would bring them in. By giving everybody the job for free, the system turns every citizen into distribution: the users ARE the distribution, they bring the next users, and they earn half. The 50% is not a cost — it is the growth engine. And it is honest: the dashboard marks what is tracked (the click, the return), never payment-proven; the payout is 50% of what is actually paid, when it is paid.
