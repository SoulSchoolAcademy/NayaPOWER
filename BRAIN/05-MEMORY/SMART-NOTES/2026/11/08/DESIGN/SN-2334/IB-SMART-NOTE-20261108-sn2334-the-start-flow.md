## Why it matters

A chart without a start flow is a demonstration, not a door — the visitor can admire it and still leave, because nothing asked them to begin. The promo matters the same way: half price for the first thousand is not a discount, it is a founding story — the first thousand are founders, not customers, and the price says so. The terms matter most of all: nine days free, charged for the level you are on, change any time — written in plain words, they remove the fear that stops a start. "Zero cost, zero risk" is not a slogan; it is the removal of the last reason to hesitate. And hiding the scorecard matters because the scorecard is a maker's instrument — the visitor does not need to see the judging, only the love: what I love, and what's ten out of ten.
