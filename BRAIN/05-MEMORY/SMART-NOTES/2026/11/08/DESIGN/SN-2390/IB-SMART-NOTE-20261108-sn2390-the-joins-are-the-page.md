## Child

Imagine you build a beautiful toy car. It is shiny and fast-looking. But the wheels are not on. It will not roll. A page is like that car. The pretty parts are not enough. The parts must join up and work.

## Grandma

A lovely shop window means nothing if the door is locked. The customer must be able to walk in, pay, and come back out with what they bought. Check the door, not just the window.

## Naya

A page is a machine, and a machine is its connections. The pieces are the body; the joins are the life. Prove the joins, and the page is real. Admire the pieces and skip the joins, and you have a picture of a page, not a page.

## What it means

- Done means the whole path works: click the block, reach PayPal, pay, come back to the thank-you link, and see the return counted.
- A setting that lives at account level (Auto Return) is set once, for everything — and only when the link it points to is live.
- A page that calls an API that does not exist yet is a page with dead buttons, and dead buttons never ship.

## Why it matters

The Pricing page is the door into NayaNET. If the door looks beautiful but does not open, the stranger leaves. Every round that polishes a piece without proving its join spends the work and keeps the risk. Proving the joins is what turns ten beautiful rounds into one working page.

## What changes now

- Before any page is called done, prove every join live: the click, the destination, and the return — not just the piece that was changed.
- Set the PayPal Auto Return URL only after the thank-you Smart Link is live, and only by his hand.
- Never ship a page whose buttons call an API with no live host — stop, say so plainly, and name the blocker.
- Keep the slug rule: no dashes in Smart Link slugs or worker names, from now on.
