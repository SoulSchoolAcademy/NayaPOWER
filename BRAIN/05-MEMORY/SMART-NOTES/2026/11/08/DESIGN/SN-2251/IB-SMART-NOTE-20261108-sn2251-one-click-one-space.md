# One Click, One Space — Create a Space on Any Intelligence

SN-2251 · DESIGN / SPACES

**Created:** 2026-11-08 00:14Z

> "They should be able to create a space on any smart note where immediately it now creates that space based on that smart note. So the smart note, it'll give them the title, the hook, and now they can get a conversation going on it. There's like a feed that they can communicate. They can invite people, they can audio chat or video chat or interact or instant message or mail each other. Basically with a click of a button you can create a space on any intelligence, any intelligence block, and you can create a conversation around that topic."

## Hook

One click on any intelligence, and a world opens around it — a space, a conversation, people.

## Nutshell

Every Smart Note, every intelligence block, carries a "+ CREATE SPACE" button. One click creates a space FROM that intelligence: the note gives the title and the hook, and the space opens a conversation around that topic — a feed to communicate in, invites to bring people in, audio chat, video chat, instant message, mail. The creation is immediate. That is the design, not a defect.

## Human

You are reading something that matters to you — a note, an idea, a piece of intelligence. You tap one button. A room opens, named after what you were reading, carrying its hook. People come in. You talk about it — in the feed, by voice, by video, by message, by mail. The intelligence becomes a conversation, and the conversation becomes a community.

## Child

You find a cool idea. You press one button. Now you have a clubhouse about that idea, and your friends can come talk about it with you.

## Grandma

Every good thought deserves a room where people can gather around it. One tap, and the room exists — named after the thought, ready for talking, for calling, for writing to each other.

## Naya

A space is not a page. It is a conversation with a home. The intelligence block is the seed: its title names the space, its hook opens the conversation. The space then carries the full system — feed, invites, audio, video, instant message, mail — because a conversation is not one channel, it is all of them, in one place, around one topic.

## What it means

The "+ CREATE SPACE" button creating a space immediately, with no confirmation dialog, is CORRECT. It is the design Shawn ordered. The inspector flagged instant creation as a defect — that flag is wrong, and it is corrected here.

## Why it matters

If we "fix" the button by adding friction — a confirmation, a delay, a second step — we break the very thing that makes it work: one click, one space, one conversation. The magic is the immediacy. The thing to fix is what happens AFTER the click, and what happens on the SECOND click.

## What changes now

1. **Creation stays immediate.** No confirmation dialog. One click, the space exists.
2. **The duplicate is the defect.** A second click on the same note must OPEN the existing space, not create another one. One intelligence, one space. The inspector's two test clicks creating two identically-named spaces is the bug — not the creation, the duplication.
3. **After creation, you land IN the space.** The citizen goes straight into the conversation, with the feed open, the note's title and hook at the top, and invites, audio, video, message, and mail all present. A space that is created but never entered is a room nobody walks into.
4. **Every intelligence block carries it.** Smart Notes, feed cards, any intelligence — the button is the same everywhere, and it always does the same thing: one click, one space, one conversation around that topic.
