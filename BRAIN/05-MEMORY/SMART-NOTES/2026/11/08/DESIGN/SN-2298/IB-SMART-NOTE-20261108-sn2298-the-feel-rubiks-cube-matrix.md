## Hook

One core. Many faces. It flows like water.

## Nutshell

The page is a Rubik's Cube matrix made of intelligent blocks: one core that morphs into its faces. Scroll down, up, across, whatever — free, smooth, easy. Everything alive and lights up. It flows like water to operate. And it makes sense when you experience it: everything where it belongs — if a visitor has to think about where something is or what it does, it is not done. This is the feel the direction serves: SN-2297 gave the movement (down or across, the user's choice); this gives the motion (one core, morphing faces, water-smooth) and the test (no thinking required — the experience explains itself).

## Human

A Rubik's Cube is one thing that becomes many faces with a twist — you never leave the cube, you turn it. Water does not ask you to think about how to drink it; it flows where your hand goes. A page that works this way never makes the visitor stop and wonder — the next thing is already where their hand expects it.

## Child

A toy that turns into different toys — a car that folds into a robot, a box that opens into a house. You don't read instructions; you twist it and it becomes the next thing, smooth and easy. If you have to stop and think about how it works, the toy is broken.

## Grandma

A good house: every room opens off the next, you walk through without thinking, and everything is where your hand expects it — the kettle by the stove, the cups by the kettle. You never stand in the middle wondering where the spoons are. That is the test: if you have to think, the house is not finished.

## Naya

I am Anaya. My pages are one core that morphs into its faces — intelligent blocks, turning smooth, everything alive and lit. Down, up, across, whatever the visitor wants — free, smooth, easy, flowing like water. And I hold the test: if a visitor has to think about where something is or what it does, it is not done. I move the thing until the thinking stops.

## What it means

1. **One core, many faces.** The page is a Rubik's Cube matrix of intelligent blocks — not separate pages stapled together.
2. **Free movement.** Down, up, across, whatever — smooth and easy, the visitor's choice (see SN-2297).
3. **Everything alive, everything lights up.** Stillness is flat; the feel is motion and light on touch.
4. **It flows like water.** Operation is effortless — no friction, no stutter, no dead controls.
5. **The test is the experience.** It makes sense when you experience it. If a visitor has to think about where something is or what it does, it is not done.

## Why it matters

"Down or across" tells the builder which way the page moves; "flows like water" tells them how it must feel — and "if they have to think, it is not done" is the only test that matters, because it is judged by the visitor's experience, not the builder's intent. This note exists so no Naya ever ships a page that is technically navigable and experientially confusing.
