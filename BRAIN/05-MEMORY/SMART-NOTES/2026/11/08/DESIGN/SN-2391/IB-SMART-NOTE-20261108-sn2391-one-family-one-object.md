## Nutshell

Consistency is not decoration. It is what makes nine blocks read as one world.

## Child

If your friend shows you their favorite toy and asks you to bring it, you bring THAT toy. You do not build a new toy that looks a bit like it. Bring the real one. And if you have four toys that belong together, they should look like they belong together.

## Grandma

When someone hands you the good dish and says "use this one," you use that dish. You do not go and make a copy of it from memory. And a set of dishes should match — same pattern, same size, different pictures. That is what makes a table look right.

## Naya

Take the shown object. That is the whole law in four words. A shown object carries his eye in it — its shape, its colors, its light. Recreating it throws his eye away and keeps only your guess. And one family everywhere is the same law applied to the page itself: the pieces must be siblings, or the page is a pile of strangers.

## What it means

- A screenshot, a link, or a page he shows is the deliverable itself — same shape, same colors, same jewel, same glow, placed on the page.
- One icon family, one color per period, one eye per block: the same piece, used everywhere it appears, never redrawn.
- The scorecard rides with the round — the maker scores their own work first, and the presentation carries it.

## Why it matters

Every recreation is a second guess at a decision he already made. Each one costs a round, a rebuild, and his patience — and the page drifts further from the one world it is meant to be. Taking the object and reusing the family costs nothing and keeps the page whole. The difference between a page that feels designed and a page that feels assembled is exactly this rule.

## What changes now

- When he shows an object, copy it verbatim — same structure, same colors, same behavior — and remove the thing it replaces.
- Audit every tier appearance and use the canonical icon, same glyph, same color, in every spot — never a near match.
- Every round's presentation carries its scorecard in full: why not a 10, 10 loves, 10 don't-loves, 10 to make it 10x, 10 solutions applied.
- File code he sends as a Factory asset the same pass — local files first, then GitHub, Smart Note, and Registry — so it never evaporates from chat.
