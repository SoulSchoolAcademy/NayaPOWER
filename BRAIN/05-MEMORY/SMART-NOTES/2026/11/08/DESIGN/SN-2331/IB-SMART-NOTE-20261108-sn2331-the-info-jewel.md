## Why it matters

A control nobody recognises is worse than a missing control — a missing control is honest, but an unrecognised one teaches the visitor that the page's marks mean nothing, and then no mark on the page can be trusted. The white "i" works because it is not invented: it borrows a meaning the whole world already agreed on, so the visitor needs no learning at all. That is the design law in miniature — harvest what the world already knows, make it a real object, repeat it identically until it is a language. And the choice matters: he offered an eye, and the "i" won on instant recognition — recognition beats cleverness, every time.
