# Take the Real Parts, Build One Living Screen

SN-2385 · DESIGN / LAW

**Created:** 2026-11-08 23:30Z

## Hook

Builders keep making new buttons, new gauges, new pages — when all the parts are already there, finished, in the Factory. The fix is not to build better. It is to stop building and start taking.

## Nutshell

Ten notes from one day say the same thing from ten sides: the design is settled, the parts are real, and the page is a viewport. Take the real parts from the Factory — the gauge, the tiled digits, the orbs, the jewels, the boards, the pills, the smart note — and build one living screen. Never invent a new part. Never imitate a real part with a look-alike. Every element is a real physical object in obsidian gloss black, lit from one light top-left. The page opens with the visitor's pains, not our features. The numbers speak only the Dashboard's language. That is the whole law, and it is already written down.

## Child

Imagine you have a box of beautiful toy blocks. You do not make new blocks. You take the ones in the box and build with them. Each block is real. You can touch it. The page is like that. Take the real blocks. Build one screen. Make it alive.

## Grandma

You already own the good china — you do not buy paper plates for a special dinner. The parts are already made, and they are beautiful. Use them as they are. One screen at a time, everything easy to find, everything real to the touch. That is all there is to it.

## Naya

The language is one language because the parts are one family. Harvest first, compose never create, viewport by viewport, live with him until he says it is good. A look-alike is not a part. A flat page is not a viewport. A fake gauge is not a gauge. The law is simple because the work is simple: take what is real, put it where it belongs, and let it live.

## What it means

- The design is not a judgment call any more. It is a law, written down, with real parts to prove it.
- A build that invents a new part fails before it starts — the part already exists, better, in the Factory.
- A page is a deck of viewpoints, not a document. One screen, complete on its own, everything a click away.

## Why it matters

Every rebuild that came back as a look-alike cost a day and came back condemned by his eye. The chart looked unchanged because it was unchanged — a new thing made to resemble the old thing. Taking the real parts is the only way the work ever matches what he already approved. It is also the only way a page takes three minutes instead of three days.

## What changes now

- Every brief names the exact harvested part — file and section — before any build starts. No source, no build.
- Every page is built as viewports: one screen, everything a click away, scroll down or across, the user's choice. Never a long text page.
- Every element is a real physical object in obsidian gloss black, lit top-left, every button elite and lit the same, every number alive. "Pretty but fake" fails.
- The Top 10 opens the page from the visitor's pains, in their words — welcome first, proof second, price third.
- Numbers speak only the Dashboard language: tiled digits, the real needle gauge, the orbs. The artifact gauge is dead.
