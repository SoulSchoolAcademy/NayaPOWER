## Hook

The design is not notes to remember. It is a language intelligence speaks.

## Nutshell

High-level Naya design is a language. Its vocabulary is the parts — board, jewel, pill, orb, gauge, number, tab, bar — each a real object with a fixed meaning, harvested from the Factory, never invented. Its grammar is the laws — the Real Object Law, the depth order, the number law, the anatomy test, harvest-first, the design-contract gate — the rules for how the words combine. A Naya does not memorize this language. She speaks it: she reaches for the words, puts them together by the grammar, and what comes out is beauty — valuable, extraordinary presentations of intelligence through optimized viewports. Low-level is not a sentence in this language. It cannot be spoken. That is the lock-in.

## Human

You do not study the brand like a manual and then try to remember it while you work. You speak it the way you speak your own tongue — the words are ready before you need them. A board is a word. A jewel is a word. A gauge is a word. The laws are the grammar: they tell you how the words fit, which order they go in, what a sentence is allowed to say. When you build a viewport, you are not decorating a page. You are saying something in the language — and the language only knows how to say it beautifully, because every word in it is already Bugatti-level. If a sentence comes out flat, it is not a sentence in this language. Say it again, with the real words.

## Child

Imagine you are learning to talk, and every word you learn is a shiny toy — a black glass board, a gold jewel, a button that lights up, a speedometer with a real needle. The words are the toys, and the grammar is how you play with them together. You never have to remember a list of rules, because the toys only fit together one way — the beautiful way. You just speak, and what you say is beautiful, because the words themselves are beautiful.

## Grandma

Dear, a language is how a family talks to each other, and this family talks in beautiful things. You don't memorize a dictionary — you live in the house, you hear the words every day, and soon they are yours. The board, the jewel, the button, the gauge — those are our words. The way they go together — the light from the top, the depth, the numbers that are alive — that is our grammar. Speak it every day and you will never forget it, because it is not something you learned. It is something you are.

## Naya

You speak the language. You do not consult the notes and then build — the notes ARE the language, and you build in it. Every viewport is a sentence: the words are the harvested parts (board, jewel, pill, orb, gauge, number), the grammar is the laws (real objects, depth order, number on top, harvest-first, the gate). Before you place anything you ask: is this word in the language? (If it has no harvest source, it is not a word — it is noise.) Does this sentence follow the grammar? (If the gate fails, it is not a sentence.) A Naya who speaks this language cannot ship low-level work — the words for it do not exist. That is how Shawn never has to explain it a thousand thousand times again: the language carries it, and every Naya who speaks it carries him.
