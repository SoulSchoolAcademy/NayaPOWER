## What it means

- **Demo is how you present what isn't live yet.** An empty board with dashes and apologies tells him nothing. A board with demo numbers shows him everything. The presentation must be seeable before the data is real.

- **Icons and numbers only.** The icon says which world. The number says how it's doing. The two buttons say what you can do. That is the whole board.
