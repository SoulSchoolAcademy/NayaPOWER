## Human

The carrier moves every letter to its person and never looks at what was written — that is the difference between carrying and reading, and the whole system is built on it. Inside, a library runs itself: every thing labeled the moment it arrives, shelved by a logic the machine understands, found again in seconds, and nobody ever opens a box to peek. The house keeps two kinds of keys, and the machine holds only one: it can move things and send the mail, but it cannot read.
