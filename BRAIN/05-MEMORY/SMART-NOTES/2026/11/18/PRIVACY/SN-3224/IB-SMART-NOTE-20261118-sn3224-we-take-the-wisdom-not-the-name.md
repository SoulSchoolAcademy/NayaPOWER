## What it means

- **THE READ LINE:** never the mail, never the messages, never the video, never the calls — no exception, ever. Carrying a message so it reaches the person it was sent to is not reading it.
- **THE KEEP LINE:** we don't even keep your content. Conversations auto-distill after a week — the words go, the essence stays. A citizen may choose a day, or stretch it to a month, never longer. A saved note is kept for good in the citizen's own Smart Vault.
- **THE DAILY RHYTHM:** intelligence is continuously distilled, daily — take everything in for the day, capture the essence of what is valuable and intelligent, note it, put it in the system, learn and grow from it, share it, do it again the next day. A thousand in, ~100 kept; ~100 in, ~10 kept.
- **THE IDENTITY RULE:** when wisdom is shared, identity is NOT shared unless the citizen chooses. We capture the wisdom only, never the name — anonymous, an alias, or in their own name, their choice.
- **THE REASON TO SAY IT LOUDLY:** people are scared of technology, and a lot of systems sell their data. They need to understand how this works — and this is the opposite: a powerful, valuable feature.
