## The understanding

After the appointments comes the next thing: the summary of the day. It just tells you a summary — of the day, of what happened in it, of what the day was. And this one is the summary page of your day: its own page, its own room, not a line tucked under something else. A day deserves its summary told back to the citizen in one place — what the day held, counted and told — so the citizen can look at their day and see it whole. The summary is not the journal (that is the citizen's own writing, and it gets its own board too); the summary is the day told back to them, and it stands on its own page.

## The law

The summary of the day is its own page — the summary page of your day — telling you a summary of the day.

## Owed from this dictation

Give the summary of the day its own page — the summary page of your day — that tells the citizen a summary of their day.

**The summary of the day, on its own page — the day told back whole.**
