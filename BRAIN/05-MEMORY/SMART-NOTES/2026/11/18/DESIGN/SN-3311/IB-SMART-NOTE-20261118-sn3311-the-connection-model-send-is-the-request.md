## Human

Every connection system asks the same question: how do two people start? The old answer is a button — CONNECT — a handshake you press before you may speak, and a request that sits waiting for an accept. His answer is simpler and truer to how people actually meet: you send a message, and the message IS the request. The other person answers by answering — a reply is a yes, silence is a no, and no one has to manage a request queue to have a conversation. Message and mail are automatic because the connection does not need permission to begin; it needs a first word. And the levels — text, audio, video — are the same connection growing deeper, not three different features. No CONNECT click needed is not the removal of a step; it is the recognition that the step was never the connection. The connection is the message.
