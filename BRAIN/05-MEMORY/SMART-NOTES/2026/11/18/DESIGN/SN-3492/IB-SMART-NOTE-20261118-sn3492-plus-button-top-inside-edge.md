## The understanding

A plus floating low down the screen on its own breaks the line — it reads as a lost button, not a control. Put it where the hand expects it: at the top of the sidebar, on the inside edge, mirrored on both sides so the two pluses face each other across the page. And it does one honest thing when tapped: it pushes to the corner, pushing the sidebar away, opening full screen. The plus is not decoration on the sidebar — it is the sidebar's way of getting out of the way. Top, inside edge, same on both sides, never floating low: the placement is the rule, the push-away is the behaviour, and a plus anywhere else is a defect, not a style choice.
