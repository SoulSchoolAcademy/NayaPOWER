## Nutshell

This is the One-System Law turned into a move. The Hub and Personal do not need to be reconciled by a third design — each already has the piece the other is missing, so the fix is a swap, not a build: the Hub's presentation goes into Personal whole, and Personal's finished sidebar goes into the Hub whole. "It'll fit perfectly" is the test of the law: if the two presentations were really one system, the pieces interchange without redesign. The two rounds run in parallel because neither waits on the other to start — but the Hub round harvests the sidebar bytes from the Personal round's filed file, so the sidebar that lands in the Hub is the finished one, byte for byte, not a redraw. And the law behind the swap, stated once over all feeds: a new feed kept getting a new system — its own frame, its own tabs, its own sidebar — and every new system was another family to align later. Feeds do not get systems, they get compositions. The best part of each feed — the Hub's presentation, Personal's sidebar, the smart tabs that sort by topic — is harvested whole and put with the best parts of the others, and the result is still one system because nothing in it was made new for the occasion.
