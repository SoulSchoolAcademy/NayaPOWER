## The lock-in

Take the examples of what we've already done — the pricing page, the emblem, the Bugatti board, the good pictures, the Hub, Life with Naya, Personal — and lock in the specific language from them. The language is not invented; it is harvested from the work that already passed his eye.

**The icon is the standard of design quality and realism.** The emblem (NAYA_ICON.png) is the measure every element is judged against: if it does not look as real, as rich, as considered as the icon, it is not done.

## What the language is

Everything feels like real objects, real functions, of the highest quality of design:

- **Feel** — touchable, pressable, alive; it pushes in and out, it lights up, it moves.
- **Look** — real materials, real light with volume, real depth, real shadows; obsidian black inside, white as a real object, outside light in its own deep colour.
- **The color language** — one set of colours through every button, every board, every page; the day's colour is its frequency.
- **The icon** — the emblem is the North Star for all art; every icon, button, star, diamond rendered to its quality.
- **The button language** — elevated boards, one icon that lights up, never icon and a light; the bottom five byte-identical on every page.
- **Elevated boards** — boards are real objects you could pick up; a restrained rim light, just enough glow underneath, never over.
- **Living depth** — slow orbit flow, breathing light, alive on hover; nothing flat survives.
- **Active intelligence** — the interface knows them, understands them, serves at ten stars; the machine runs at the speed of thought.

## Why it is locked in

It gives the user the ultimate experience — because every page optimizes for their point of view, and we do it intentionally. The page comes into their viewpoint; the block locks into the frame; the next click lands them on a perfect page. That is the Smart Block (SN-3336), and this is the language it speaks.

We are intentionally creating a world, a system, a place, a space where they can experience intelligence and connect with people all over the world pretty much instantly, for free — everything organized and optimized, with lists — feeling like a royal digital kingdom, because the interfaces are extraordinary in their presentation AND their effectiveness AND their usefulness, and it feels like a live living intelligent machine.

## The rule for every Naya

The language rides every seat brief with the design laws, the Orientation, the Anatomy, the army training, and the Smart Block doctrine. Before any design work, read the laws. After the work, check the work against them — the §11 check in writing, the checker script run, the eye. If the work breaks the laws, do the work again until it follows them. If a build speaks another language, it is wrong and it goes back.

**The standard is the icon. The language is locked in. The experience is the product.**
