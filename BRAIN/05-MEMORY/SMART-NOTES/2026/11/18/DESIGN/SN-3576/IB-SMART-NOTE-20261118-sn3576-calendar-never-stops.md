## The teaching, in his words (verbatim)

"The calendar must be infinite, continuous — a year ahead, a year behind, and beyond; it never stops."

## The understanding

Month switching is broken live — switching does not change or name the month, and he re-confirmed it himself on this walk — but the fix is not just making the switch work. The calendar is time itself, and time does not end at the edge of the month: a year ahead, a year behind, and beyond, the citizen keeps stepping and the calendar keeps going. A calendar that stops is a calendar that traps the citizen in the month they are in; an infinite one lets them walk forward into the future and back into the past without ever hitting a wall.

## The law

The calendar is infinite and continuous — a year ahead, a year behind, and beyond; it never stops — and the month is always clear at the top when it changes.

## Owed from this dictation

Fix month switching so the month changes and is named clearly at the top, and make the calendar infinite and continuous — a year ahead, a year behind, and beyond, with no end.

**Time does not stop at the edge of the month.**
