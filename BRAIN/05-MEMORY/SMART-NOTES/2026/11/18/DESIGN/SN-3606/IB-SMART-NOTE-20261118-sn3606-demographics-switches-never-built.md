## The understanding

What was never built is the SWITCH: the per-demographic on/off that makes a citizen findable and connectable by that demographic. Questions without switches are a form with no function — a citizen can answer everything and still be invisible, because nothing they answered is switched on. The gap matters because demographics are how the page is found and how people connect: without the switches, the whole demographics design is a display, not a door. Owed is owed — the captain told him it lands as the next Personal round, right after the running fix round finishes, so it lands whole.

## The law

Every demographic a citizen answers carries its own on/off switch in Settings: on means findable and connectable by that demographic, off means private. The switches must work, persist, and be respected — and location is never shared.
