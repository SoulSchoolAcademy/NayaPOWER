## What it means

1. **ONE SYSTEM:** the frame stays the Hub and only the center morphs into the function you're in. The Personal universal page is the one exception — feature cover on top, everything below it like the Hub.
2. **THE EYE TEST:** a round that changes nothing the eye sees in the first second has changed nothing, however many checks it passed. Prove the change with side-by-side screenshots before the round is called done.
3. **ALIGN, DO NOT REBUILD:** when a page feels like a different family, the round harvests what the family already has — the existing page base, his icons verbatim, the same materials, the same bottom bar — and scorecards the result against Hub, Personal, Dashboard and Smart Mail. Nothing new is made where the family already has it.
4. **POLISH ONLY:** polish the good page, don't rebuild it — hide the explanation under its own control, keep the first view to what matters, and put the real emblem in the corners verbatim. A round that rebuilds a working page has failed even if its changes are good.
5. **THE RECORDING:** a recording goes on the play button only when it is what the button says it is: his podcast means his voice, or Naya narrating in the first person, from the script it claims. A generated discussion of the document is a review, not the podcast — file the transcript, keep it out of the player.

## Why it matters

1. Without the One-System Law, every function that grows its own frame drifts into a different family, and every drift has to be repaired by a round — Messenger with its cover and pills, the Dashboard with its doors, the calendar with its own bars. With it, a builder who starts from the Hub frame cannot build a different family by accident.
2. Without the eye test, a round can pass every check and change nothing his eye sees — and he gets the same page sent back again. With it, the change must move the first-second read, proven side by side, before the round is called done.
3. Without the latest-finished rule, newer work that has not passed the eye gets sent as the latest — and he is shown a page that is not finished. With it, the latest page is the latest FINISHED page: eye passed, merged, verified loading.
4. Without rescuing the one right thing, a rejected build takes its good part down with it — and the cover he liked is lost with the worker he ordered gone. With it, when he names the one right thing inside something rejected, that thing is harvested and placed where he said, as its own owed item — never lost with the rest.
