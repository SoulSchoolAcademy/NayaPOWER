## The understanding

The smart tabs are organizers — they organize the feed by topic, and you can click any of them. A tab's colour is not decoration and not a designer's pick: it is the topic's own colour, read off its first letter, the same letter the same colour every time. When four tabs in a row are all purple and then a yellow and an orange appear, the eye reads noise instead of order — the colours stopped meaning anything. Deterministic colour restores the meaning: the tab tells you its topic by its colour before you read its word, and the whole row stays aligned because the rule, not a hand, chose every colour. The mapping itself is to be derived from the spectrum flow — a proposed mapping is flagged as proposed, never presented as canon.

## The law

A topic tab's colour is the gematria colour of its first letter, every time. Same letter, same colour, on every page, in every feed.
