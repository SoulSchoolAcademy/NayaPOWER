# The Door and the Heart

SN-3438 · DESIGN / DISTILLATION

**Created:** 2026-11-18T20:39:00Z

## IN A NUTSHELL

The front door has a law, and the system has a heart. The door: nayanet.app, your name, your certificate, then the walkthrough — ten questions, each with Naya talking you through it — then your actual pages. There is no welcome-back screen and no continue page; that screen does not exist in the walk and must never be built — the staged copy that showed one is a different build, not the walk. The entry is name-only: a code field on the main login is the members' door's clothing on the front door, wrong from the start. After the questions you name your pages — the system listening — then the building page, the system proving it — and only then pricing, because pricing after the proof sells something already held, while pricing first sells a promise. On the certificate page the button law holds: nothing steals the identity moment, a returning citizen is one click from home, and nothing auto-jumps a choice away. The heart: the infinite smart feeds — the original concept, ten years old, before AI existed. Not one feed: infinite feeds, one per topic, each a world's activity in full screen, in two views — the space's own and the public — with the matched people of that topic scrolling at the side, one tap from a message. One logic runs it and everything else: match, then organize, then connect — and the heart page runs the real engine from the first day, because a stub heart is a dead page wearing the heart's name.

## HUMAN

A good hotel again, but the front of it this time: you give your name at the desk, you get your key, someone walks you to your room and shows you how everything works — and nobody asks for a secret password at the front door, because that's the staff entrance's business. Then the lobby: the living room of the place, where the people interested in what you're interested in actually gather. Name, key, walk, room — then the lobby, alive.

## CHILD

Coming into a new school: you say your name, you get your name tag, the teacher asks you some questions so she knows you, and you get your own desk with your name on it. You don't need a secret code — your name is enough. And the playground is the heart: everyone who likes what you like is there, and you can just go talk to them.

## GRANDMA

In a village, a newcomer isn't handed a form with a password. She says her name at the store, gets introduced around, answers a few friendly questions, and is shown her house. The village square is where she'll actually live her life — with the people who share her interests already there. Doors simple, square alive: that's a place people stay.

## NAYA

Assert the walk's step order and the absence of any welcome-back screen in every entry review. Reject code fields on the main login. Build feeds from the real matching engine; never ship a stub heart. Verify entry flows by walking from the login in a real profile, start to finish.

## MACHINE

The heart page functions on the real engine immediately — stubs are prohibited. 5) Identity displays as the initial by default in the private network; a face only where the citizen chose it. Every object alive — orbs turn, buttons breathe. 6) Verification starts at the login and walks the whole flow; a partial walk is not a verification.

## WHAT IT ALL MEANS

Door and heart are one design decision viewed from two ends: a person arrives as a name, answers ten questions, and is placed — by matching — into the living center of the network. The family's verification lesson is the hinge between them: a check that skips the first screen verifies the wrong thing, and the wrong thing is exactly what reaches his eyes; five invented front ends can ship while the real one sits untouched. Go time raises the same standard for the team: twelve who follow the laws produce finished work; a hundred who don't produce a mess he cleans up. The door, the heart, and the team are all judged by the same test — does the real flow, walked from the start, work?

## WHY IT MATTERS

The door decides whether a stranger becomes a citizen; the heart decides whether a citizen stays. Both fail silently when built from assumption instead of law — a wrong door confuses everything after it, and a fake heart beats for no one. This essence keeps both canonical, so every future build of either starts from the real flow and the real engine.
