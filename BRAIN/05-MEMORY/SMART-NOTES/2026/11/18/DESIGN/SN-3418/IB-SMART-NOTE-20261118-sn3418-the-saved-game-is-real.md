## HUMAN

Anyone who has played a long game knows the feeling: you save at midnight, and the next day your character is standing exactly where you left her, pack full, quests as you left them. If the save had kept only a note saying 'you were doing well', you'd never trust it again. Memory in software is the same promise.

## CHILD

Imagine you build a huge Lego castle and Mum says tidy up — but there's a magic shelf where the castle stays BUILT, exactly as you left it, and tomorrow you just take it down and keep building. That's a saved game. A photo of the castle would not be the same at all.

## GRANDMA

It's my recipe box again, but bigger: fifty years of cards, and when I open it the card I need is there, in my handwriting, with the stain from the year the sauce bubbled over. That's memory you can cook from. A list saying 'she had recipes' would be no use to anyone.
