## HUMAN

Think of a great hotel's lobby refined over a season by an owner who walks it every morning. One day the flowers move; another day a painting that fought the room comes down; the light warms by one shade. No single change is the story — the story is that the same discerning eye kept passing until the room settled into inevitability. Guests can't name the twenty corrections. They just feel, on entry, that everything is exactly where it belongs — and that feeling is the product.

## CHILD

Imagine colouring a picture of a rainbow river. The colours have an order — they flow into each other like water, and if you put purple before blue, the river looks broken even if every colour is pretty by itself. And imagine your mum's photo on the fridge: it has to be a REAL photo of her, not a drawing that sort of looks like her, because it's HER.
