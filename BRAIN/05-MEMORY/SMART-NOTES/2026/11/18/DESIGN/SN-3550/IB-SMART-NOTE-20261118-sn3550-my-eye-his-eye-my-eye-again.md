# My Eye, His Eye, My Eye Again

SN-3550 · DESIGN

**Created:** 2026-11-19 02:00Z

**Kinds:** Honor · Multiplication · Craft · Knowledge

## Hook

I look at it first and figure out what's wrong, fix it, then send it to him to check, then I check it AGAIN after his check — so it always gets double checked, triple checked. Never straight from a round to his eyes without my eye first, and never finished after his check without my eye again.

## Nutshell

Three checks, in order, no shortcuts. First the captain's eye — she looks, figures out what's wrong, fixes it — because a round's own verdict is never the last word on its own work. Then his eye — he checks what she sent, because his eye is ground truth and nothing is landed until he sees it. Then her eye AGAIN, after his check — because his check finds things, and a fix made after his check is a new version that has never been looked at. A round that goes straight to his eyes skips the first check and wastes his attention on what she would have caught; a round that is called finished after his check skips the last check and ships an unverified fix. Double checked, triple checked — the sequence is the process, and the process is standing from now on. And the same care reaches the details: a plus floating low down the screen on its own breaks the line — it reads as a lost button, not a control. Put it where the hand expects it: at the top of the sidebar, on the inside edge, mirrored on both sides so the two pluses face each other across the page. Tap it and it pushes to the corner, pushing the sidebar away, opening full screen.

Across 10 notes, the thread is: the check, the plus, the ball, the colour flow.

## What it means

1. **THE CHECK:** the standing check process, from now on — the captain looks at it first and figures out what's wrong, fixes it, then sends it to him to check, then checks it AGAIN after his check — double checked, triple checked.
2. **THE PLUS:** the plus button sits at the TOP of the sidebar, on the INSIDE edge — left sidebar's plus on its right inside edge, right sidebar's plus on its left inside edge. Tap it and it pushes to the corner, pushing the sidebar away, opening full screen. Same on both sides.
3. **THE BALL, EVERYWHERE:** the notification ball shows everywhere, not just in the Hub — Personal, Business, World, Worlds, Dashboard — EVERY area that has actions gets a ball. Same black ball, white number, lit around the side in the colour of the area it sits on. The notification system follows the action.
4. **THE FLOW:** the sidebar colour flow is magenta → purple → dark blue (indigo) → cyan blue → green → gold → orange → red → back to magenta — on the right side AND the left side, both aligned, starting with the SAME colour on each side.
5. **THE KEY:** name plus device key together, always. The device key is captured and held silently in the background at name-claim, with no extra step ever shown to the citizen.

## Why it matters

1. Without the three checks, a round that goes straight to his eyes wastes his attention on what she would have caught — and a round called finished after his check ships an unverified fix. With them, every round is double checked, triple checked, every time.
2. Without the plus placed right, a plus floating low down the screen reads as a lost button, not a control — and the line of the page is broken. With it, top inside edge, mirrored, pushing the sidebar away — the placement is the rule, the push-away is the behaviour.
3. Without naming the version, an unchanged link re-sent as new leaves him saying "I don't see any update yet so I don't know why you sent it back" — and trust in every link falls. With it, every handoff names its version: the link, the commit or blob, what changed since the last link he saw.
4. Without the Tetris test, the cover is pieces that almost fit — a photo overlapping wrong, a pill floating, menus with gaps. With it, the Personal cover is one piece, laid out like Tetris: photo overlapping into the cover a little, points pill above, name + saying beside the photo, button bar just below, menus clean underneath — everything fits perfectly, no gaps, no overlaps, no extra black space.
