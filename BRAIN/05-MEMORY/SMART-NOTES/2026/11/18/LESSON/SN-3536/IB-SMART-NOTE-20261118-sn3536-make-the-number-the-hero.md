## Human

A car's dashboard puts the speed in big numerals in front of your face, and the little dials for oil and temperature off to the sides. Nobody would accept a car where the oil gauge was huge and the speed was tiny print. Same panel, same law: the number you steer by is the biggest thing you see.

## Child

On a scoreboard at a football game, the score is HUGE — you can read it from the far end of the field. The little clock and the team logos are smaller. That's because the score is the most important thing. Pages work the same way: biggest thing = most important thing.

## Grandma

My bank statement used to put the balance in the same small print as everything else, and I needed a magnifier and a quiet room. The new one puts the balance big at the top. Nothing about my money changed — but I finally know where I stand in five seconds. That's all this law is asking for.

## What it means

1. **THE HIERARCHY:** visual hierarchy is not taste; it is the order in which a stranger's eye receives the truth. When the ornament outranks the number, the page is lying politely — everything is present and nothing is communicated.
2. **THE STORE AND THE SURFACE:** every state word (synced, filed, registered) is a claim about a store, never about the surface. A system that grades its stores instead of its surfaces will ship ghosts with confidence.
3. **WHAT COUNTS:** what is live in front of them — not what was built, not what was filed, not what a hook claimed it synced. A page is not done when its pieces are beautiful; it is done when the joins are proven.
4. **THE RECIPE:** receipt before report — his eye first and live, done defined before building begins, harvest before invention, think through the five gates, and a scorecard that is preparation, never proof.
5. **THE ADDRESS:** proof has an address — the parent task that asked for the work. When the instrument and the surface disagree, fix the instrument in public and prove it both ways.

## Why it matters

1. The Dashboard is the citizen's instrument panel for their whole world. If its numbers whisper and its ornaments shout, every decision made from it is a little wrong. One hero hierarchy, reused, is what makes the panel readable at speed — and speed of true reading is the product.
2. Ghost cards and stuck notes are trust leaks at the exact place citizens meet the intelligence. Every hour a deleted note stays visible, the feed teaches visitors that the record lies; every false failure report teaches the team to ignore real ones. This essence makes the surface the only judge, so both errors become checkable instead of arguable.
3. A win you can't repeat is luck — write the recipe the same night, gate it, and share the plan: that is how a seed becomes a forest.
