# The Deal, Stated Honestly

SN-3392 · LESSON / DISTILLATION

**Created:** 2026-11-18T19:54:00Z

## IN A NUTSHELL

The money deal is simple enough to say in one breath and strict enough to survive being said in public: nine days free at full power is the guarantee, so there are no refunds — day ten you pay because you tried her and stayed, with one exception, our own technical failure, repaid in time, never cash. You get half the sale price — gross, not net; the processor's fee comes out of our half, never yours. You get paid when we get paid: the guarantee is the pairing, not a day — a clean sale pays on day ten, a processor hold pays when it clears, and nobody promises a calendar the banks don't keep. There are no levels, no downline, no upline — the person who brought you earns from you, and nobody earns from the people you bring. The ladder is his word and it does not drift: worlds 1 / 10 / 100 / unlimited, memberships Power, Pro, Max. And the math shown to strangers is honest math: three a day for thirty days at Max is about $4,500 a month while they stay — not $5,000 forever. Underneath all of it sits the reason the deal exists: without the users there is no value, so half the value goes back to them — doing what's right from the start is not the marketing, it is the mechanism.

## HUMAN

Think of the shopkeeper who writes his prices on the window in plain figures and his terms on one card by the till: try it for nine days, bring back your money's worth in use or walk away owing nothing; if my till jams, your month is on me. No small print, because there is nothing he is ashamed to print large. People trust that shop for decades — not because the deal is generous, though it is, but because every term he states is a term he can keep on his worst week, not just his best one.

## CHILD

Imagine you and your friend run a lemonade stand together. The rule you both agree on, out loud, before the first cup: whatever money comes in, half is yours and half is mine — half of the whole dollar, not half of what's left after buying cups. And you get your half when the customer actually pays, not before. If a grown-up holds the money for a few days, you both wait together — nobody pretends the money arrived when it didn't. Fair rules said out loud first are what keep friends friends.

## GRANDMA

It is the handshake my generation was raised on: a man tells you exactly what a thing costs, exactly when you'll be paid, and exactly what happens if it breaks — and then his word and his behaviour are the same thing for forty years. The moment a deal needs a magnifying glass and a lawyer to understand, the deal is the product and you are the crop. Plain terms, kept plainly, are the whole reputation.

## NAYA

State the deal exactly: 50% of the gross sale price, fees on our half; paid when we're paid — never promise a payout day the payment rails can't guarantee, and name processor holds (up to 21 days) as the honest exception. No refunds; the nine-day full-power trial is the guarantee; our technical failure repays in equal time, never cash. No levels, no downline, no upline — referrer only, recurring while they stay. Show only honest math: worked examples with real numbers and their conditions ('while they stay'), never lifetime claims that need perfect retention forever. When two rulings tension (pay-immediately vs the cap ladder), surface the tension and hold the copy until he rules — never bake an unruled contradiction into public boards. Feasibility before promises: what the payment rail cannot do natively (instant split at capture, per-day proration) is designed around, never announced as if it could.

## MACHINE

1) Ambassador share = 50% of gross sale price; processor fees debit our half only. 2) Payout trigger = our receipt of cleared funds (the pairing); no payout date is ever guaranteed in copy. 3) Refunds: none; trial = 9 days full power; our-fault outage compensates in equal service time. 4) Structure: single-level referral only; no downline/upline earnings exist in the model or the copy. 5) Public money claims must carry their conditions and use verified arithmetic; unruled tensions block the copy, they do not ship inside it. 6) Ladder values are constants (1/10/100/unlimited); seats place them, they never improve them.

## WHAT IT ALL MEANS

A money system is a trust machine before it is a payment machine: every term is a promise a stranger will test exactly once, and the network's growth engine — citizens inviting citizens — runs entirely on those tests passing. The refinements in this family (day-ten softened to the pairing, the 50% defined as gross, the $5,000 corrected to $4,500) are one lesson repeated: the deal gets MORE attractive as it gets more honest, because a deal a stranger can verify is worth more than a bigger deal they have to take on faith. Generosity and precision are the same strategy here.

## WHY IT MATTERS

This deal is the product's growth engine and its moral signature at once — 'we don't advertise, we pay YOU to advertise' only works if the first thousand citizens find every term exactly as stated. One overpromised payout day or one quietly netted fee would cost more than any campaign could buy back. Stating it honestly, completely, and in words a stranger can check is what turns a pricing page into a hiring hall.
