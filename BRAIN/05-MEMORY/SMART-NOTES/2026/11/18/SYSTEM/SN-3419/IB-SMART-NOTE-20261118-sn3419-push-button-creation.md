## HUMAN

A recipe is only a good recipe if someone else can cook from it. The great test of a cookbook is handing it to a stranger and tasting what they make. If they stall at 'prepare the pan', the recipe was missing a step — you don't blame the cook, you fix the book.

## CHILD

Imagine you write instructions for your friend to build your Lego castle, then you go outside. If she builds it perfectly — your instructions were great! If she gets stuck, you don't say she's bad at Lego. You fix the instructions so the next friend can do it.

## GRANDMA

When I taught my granddaughter to knit, I watched her hands, not my own. Every place she tangled was a place my teaching had skipped a stitch. Good teachers and good manuals share that rule: the learner's stall is the teacher's homework.

## WHAT IT ALL MEANS

A factory is not its machines; it is the repeatability of its output in other hands. The activation manual is therefore not documentation about the Factory — it is part of the Factory, the part that travels. The manual-alone proof is the honest audit of that claim: hand the manual to a fresh builder, watch where she stalls, and file the stalls as Factory defects rather than builder failures. That inversion — the system owns the gaps — is what makes the next builder faster instead of just more careful.
