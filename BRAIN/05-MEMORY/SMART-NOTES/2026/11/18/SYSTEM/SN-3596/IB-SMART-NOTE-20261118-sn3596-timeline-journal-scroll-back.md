## The understanding

The timeline journal page is a page the citizen can go back through: they can go back and see what they inputted on any day. On it they can upload, they can share text, they can upload an image, they can share anything they want — anything at all, on their timeline journal page. And it should be something special: a place where they can actually scroll to see what they uploaded or shared on any one day. Going up — scrolling up through the days, back through what they put in. The journal is not a form and not a single entry; it is a timeline, and the timeline is the citizen's own history, scrollable, revisitable, theirs. Make it something special because it holds something special: the days as the citizen lived and wrote them.

## The law

The timeline journal page lets the citizen go back and see what they inputted on any day — share text, upload an image, share anything — and scroll to see what they uploaded or shared on any one day. Make it something special.
