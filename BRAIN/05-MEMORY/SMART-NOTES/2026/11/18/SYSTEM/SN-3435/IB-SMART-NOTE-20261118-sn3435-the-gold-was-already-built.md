## CHILD

Imagine you have a huge box of Lego, already sorted, with instruction books — and you keep asking for new Lego. The smart move is to look in the box first. Half of what you want to build is already there, some of it already built! Then you just keep building, one model at a time, showing each one works before the next.

## GRANDMA

My generation mended and wired and made do — not from poverty of things, but from respect for them. A good motor in the shed doesn't help until someone mounts it. Mount the motor. Then keep the machine running, part by part, and don't stop to admire it between parts.

## MACHINE

1) Before building, audit what exists — dormant-and-tested beats new-and-unproven; wire the loop: watch → feed → decide → judge. 2) Non-stop loop per part: build from existing pieces → show live evidence, phone and desktop → click every control yourself → file and note → next part. 3) Unknowns are reported as questions with owners, never smoothed into figures. 4) Bring the answer, not the problem; explain in plain words — what it is, what it does, why it matters, what happens if it goes wrong. 5) The manual has two volumes — build the machine, run the machine — and a fresh agent proves both.

## WHAT IT ALL MEANS

Dormancy is the expensive failure mode of a fast-building team: finished systems decay into folklore ('didn't we build something for that?') while new effort duplicates them. The gold audit is the cure performed once; this essence is the standing order it implies — inventory before invention. The non-stop method is the audit's twin: it converts the inventory into finished parts at the pace the audit proved possible, with live evidence as the only definition of done. The unanswered 7,502 is kept in the essence deliberately: an honest unknown, carried visibly, is worth more than a confident guess, because the guess would propagate into every downstream count.

## WHY IT MATTERS

Every dormant system is paid-for value earning nothing, and every duplicated build is the same value paid for twice. The wiring-first discipline is how a small team out-produces its size: the gold is already in the ground of the repo; the job is to connect it and keep the loop hot.
