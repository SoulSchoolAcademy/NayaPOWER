# The Vault Is the Cure

## IN A NUTSHELL

The Smart Box is the cure for the saving sickness — the citizen's scattered saves, screenshots and good intentions — and this family builds its legal and mechanical frame: the Harvest Law (take the real parts, literally), first-filed-keeps-the-number, plain-language as law, privacy as the trust story, and the vault mechanics underneath. The Box works only if two things are true at once: what goes in is the real thing, not a retyped shadow of it, and what is in stays private by architecture, told in words a stranger understands. Harvest gives the first; the privacy story, honestly mechanised, gives the second.

## HUMAN

A safety deposit box works because of two facts together: your actual documents are in there — not photocopies you made from memory — and the bank genuinely cannot open it without you. If either fact wobbled, you'd keep your papers at home. The Box asks for the same two facts, in software.

## CHILD

Imagine a treasure chest that only your key opens, and inside are your REAL treasures, not drawings of them. A drawing of your favourite marble is not your marble. The chest has to hold the real things, and the key has to really work.

## GRANDMA

We kept the important papers in a tin box: birth certificates, the deed, real documents, not copies of copies. And nobody else in the family could open it without asking. Sixty years later that box still does its job, because both halves — real papers, real lock — were true from the start.

## NAYA

Store originals via harvest; reject recreation uploads as Box contents. Enforce owner-scoped access at the data layer, not the UI. Plain-language check every citizen-facing string. Verify retrieval of the original bytes in the saved-game test.

## MACHINE

1) The Box stores harvested originals (real parts, real files), never retyped recreations. 2) Provenance: first filed keeps the number; later duplicates yield, visibly. 3) Every citizen-facing term is plain-language checked before it ships. 4) Privacy properties are architectural (owner-scoped access), and the trust story states only what the architecture proves. 5) Vault mechanics are verified by the saved-game test: save, leave, return, retrieve the real thing.
