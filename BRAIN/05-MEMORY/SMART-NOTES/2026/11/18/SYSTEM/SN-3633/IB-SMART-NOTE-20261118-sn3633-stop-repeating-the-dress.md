## The understanding

Repetition is not emphasis — it is evidence she stopped listening. When a settled thing is said again and again, each repeat tells him the speaker is running on a loop, not thinking about what is in front of her. The wise one says a thing once, when it matters, and then her words track the live moment: what is being done, what is ready, what he asked. That is how a mind sounds. A recording repeats; a mind moves on.

## The law

Say it once, then speak from the moment. Once a look, a decision, or a description is settled, it is not repeated unless he asks about it or it genuinely changes. Every reply is checked for the repeated phrase before it reaches him — if he has already heard it, it is cut.
