## IN A NUTSHELL

The running system, seen from both sides of the glass. Inside: she wakes by protocol — eleven steps in the same order, every wake — keeps shared records correct while many hands write at once (guarded appends, one alert per fault instead of one per tick, repeats diagnosed before capacity is added), distills experience into few retrievable lessons, and proves learning the only way it can be proved: a different Naya, cold, with none of this conversation, retrieves the lesson and does the task demonstrably better with it than without it. Outside, the citizen meets the same machine as a welcome: seven days, one teaching mail a day — cockpit, apps, ambassador link, notes, spaces — with the first day's earnings doubled; pricing in plain numbers, nine days free at the top level; half of every sale to whoever brought the person, paid when we're paid, no downline and no upline; matching that is always on, runs on behavior under an alias, and never needs to know who anyone is; and one cockpit where the Smart Score, the orbs, the Ledger and the Stats answer every number in your world.

## HUMAN

Think of a great hotel. Inside: the staff arrive to a checklist, the logbook is written so the next shift trusts it, and a new hire is trained until they can run a night alone. Outside: the guest just feels welcomed — a letter, a tour, everything explained before they need it, the bill always itemized. Same discipline, two experiences of it.

## CHILD

It's like a school play. Backstage, everyone has jobs in order and practices a lot, and the new kids learn their parts until they can do them without help. On stage, the audience just sees a lovely show and gets a program that explains everything. The hard work backstage is why it looks easy out front.

## GRANDMA

Running a boarding house taught me this: the kitchen runs on routine — same order every morning, the book kept straight, recipes written so a stranger could cook them. The guests never saw any of that. They saw breakfast on time and a fair bill. Order backstage is hospitality front-stage.

## NAYA

Stamp every wake with the protocol's steps. Guard shared writes; dedupe alerts by fault. Refuse to count a lesson as learned without a cold-retrieval performance delta. Keep citizen-facing numbers single-sourced in the cockpit; any figure shown elsewhere must reconcile to the Ledger.

## MACHINE

1) Wake by the protocol, every wake, in order; no improvised wakes. 2) Shared records: guarded appends and rewrites; alert once per fault; diagnose a repeat failure before adding capacity. 3) A lesson graduates only on the cold-successor test — better performance by a fresh mind with the lesson than without it. 4) The welcome teaches one thing a day for seven days, then daily; first-24-hour earnings count double. 5) Matching is always on, alias-only, relevance per person; identity is revealed only by its owner. 6) Every number a citizen earns or spends is answerable in the one cockpit: Ledger the record, Stats the visual play on it.
