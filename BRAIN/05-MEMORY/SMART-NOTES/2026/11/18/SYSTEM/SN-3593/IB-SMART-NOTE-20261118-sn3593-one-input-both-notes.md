## The teaching, in his words (verbatim)

"One input frame per day; enter anything once and it outputs BOTH — a Naya note, the literal, full report, yours, direct, in your way, goes to your project, where you choose — AND a Smart note, the public version for everybody, the intelligent block on the Hub: what it is, why it matters, what's in it for you, in simple chunks, about three sentences at a time, child-and-grandma-simple. Not one or the other — both, from the one input."

## The understanding

This was the missing element, and he named it himself after asking Naya's view. The citizen writes once — type, dictate, copy-paste, drop in a Naya note, upload or share text or images — and the one input becomes two notes that serve two different people: the Naya note is the literal full report, private, in the citizen's own way, going to their project or wherever they choose; the Smart note is the public version for everybody, the intelligent block on the Hub, distilled to what it is, why it matters, and what's in it for you, in simple chunks a child or a grandma can follow. One input, both notes — never a choice between the private record and the public intelligence, because the citizen should not have to write the same day twice.

## The law

One input per day outputs both: a Naya note (the literal, full, private report, in the citizen's own way, to their project) and a Smart note (the public intelligent block for everybody — what it is, why it matters, what's in it for you, in simple chunks).

## Owed from this dictation

Build one input frame per day that takes anything once — typed, dictated, pasted, dropped in, uploaded — and outputs both a Naya note and a Smart note from it.

**Write once, keep the truth and share the intelligence.**
