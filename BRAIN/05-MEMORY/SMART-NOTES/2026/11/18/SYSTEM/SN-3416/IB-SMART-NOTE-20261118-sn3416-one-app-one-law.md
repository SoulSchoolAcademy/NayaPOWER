## IN A NUTSHELL

One system, not a pile of apps: this family states the architecture as law — one universal app starting with Personal, the Law of One built into the code (all connected, each vault one's own), the Super Brain as the citizen's own, and the gauge law corrected to suit the gauge to the situation. Architecture here is not a diagram; it is the set of decisions every later builder is forbidden from re-deciding.

## HUMAN

Think of a house with many rooms. The rooms are different — kitchen, study, bedroom — but it's one house, one roof, one front door, and your bedroom is still yours when guests are in the kitchen. Nobody wants to carry four sets of keys to four separate cottages and call it a home.

## CHILD

Your class is one class — everyone learns together and helps each other. But your desk is YOUR desk, and your drawings in it are yours. Being one class doesn't mean everyone can take your drawings. It's both things at once.

## GRANDMA

A family is like that too: one family, shared table, shared stories — and still, you don't open your sister's letters. Togetherness was never the same as no boundaries. It's good to see a computer system finally written that way round.

## NAYA

Build new worlds as configurations of the universal app, never as new apps. Assert vault ownership in data-layer tests for every shared feature. Apply gauge-by-situation with the hero hierarchy invariant. File rule corrections at the rule's home, with the situation named.

## MACHINE

1) One universal app shape serves Personal, Business and Space; per-world reinvention is a defect. 2) Connection never overrides ownership: shared intelligence, private vaults, enforced in the data layer. 3) The citizen's Super Brain view is theirs; seats operate it only under the citizen's direction. 4) Gauge selection suits the situation (calculator vs dashboard standard), with the number-hero hierarchy invariant across all gauges.
