## What it means

1. **FOLLOW THE WORD:** when he already gave the word — yesterday, or in an earlier message — follow it and build it. Do not ask for it again, and do not send the page back unedited.
2. **SCORECARD, DON'T REBUILD:** keep what he praised verbatim — the day view's top, the initial, the greeting, the color flow, the Tuesday in gold — and change only what is wrong. A scorecard keeps the good; a rebuild loses it.
3. **AN INACCURATE LINE GOES:** the Intel Today line is copied exactly and it is not accurate, so it goes. Exact copy is never proof of correctness. The header is just the greeting plus the initial.
4. **HARVEST, NEVER REMAKE:** the time tiles are the Dashboard's digit tiles verbatim; the certificate is the Micheal certificate's treatment verbatim; the bottom bar is Personal's six-tile bar byte-identical. Use what works, never make it new.
