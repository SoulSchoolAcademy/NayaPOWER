## The understanding

He is waiting on the URL — not on a report about the work, not on a promise that it is coming, on the URL itself. The loop is the work: one page, one link, his feedback, the fix, the next link, until it is crafted. Nothing sits between rounds; a finished version that is not sent is work not done, and a link that is not a new checked version breaks the loop twice over — he opens it expecting new work, finds the old page, and the round that should have moved has not moved at all. That is the never-resend lesson from the other side: the loop only turns when every link in it is new and checked. The captain's eye is the gate in the loop — a version passes the check, then the URL goes to him; a version that has not passed does not go.
