## The understanding

The row was doing two jobs at once — who the person is, and what you can do with them — and at one line of height the two jobs ran together into a mess. Putting the actions underneath separates identity from action: the row says who they are and how well you match, and the buttons underneath say what you can do, each with its own clear icon and label. The second half is the bigger law: the sidebar is not a fixed panel, it is the feed's own people. Change the feed and the sidebar changes with it, because the content and the connections belong to the same world — the feed you are on decides who shows up beside it. That is why the percentages are in the sidebar at all: content, connections, percentage and match are one game, played on the side.

## The law

Identity above, actions below: DM / Mail / Audio / Video sit underneath the name, percentage and info — never beside them. And the sidebar follows the feed: change the feed, the sidebar changes with it, contacts and content matching the feed you're on.
