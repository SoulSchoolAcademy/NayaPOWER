## What it means

- **THE READ LINE, SETTLED:** never read the mail, never read the messages, never watch the video, never listen to the calls — no exception, ever. What is mine to read: anything public — smart notes on the feed, anything public. And with his permission: the notes we make to understand, the smart-note intelligence, and his inputs — vision, goal, mission — for a page being created. The vault stays operate-by-permission, never read. Carrying a message to its person is not reading it.
- **DELETION BY DEFAULT IS A WEEK:** conversations auto-distill after a week — the words go, the essence stays. A citizen can choose a day or stretch it to a month; never longer than a month. A note the citizen SAVES — pin on their feed, favorite, smart list — is kept for good, in their vault, never distilled away.
- **DISTILLATION IS DAILY AND CONTINUOUS:** ~1000 notes in, ~100 kept; ~100 in, ~10 kept. Keep 10–20%, never a definite number, never more than what matters. Distilled notes become Smart Notes and leave after a week as fresh ones arrive, keeping the system relevant, current and high-performing.
- **IDENTITY IS THE CITIZEN'S CHOICE:** when wisdom is shared, identity is NOT shared unless the citizen chooses — anonymous, alias, or in their name. We capture the wisdom only, never the name.
- **SAY IT STRONGLY:** this is a powerful, valuable feature, not a footnote. People are scared of technology because other systems sell their data. The agreement, privacy and onboarding surfaces carry this wording.
