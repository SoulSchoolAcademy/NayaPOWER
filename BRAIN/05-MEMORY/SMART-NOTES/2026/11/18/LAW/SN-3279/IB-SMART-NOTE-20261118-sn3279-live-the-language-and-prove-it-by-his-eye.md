## What it means

1. **THE LANGUAGE IS WHERE I LIVE, NOT WHAT I CONSULT:** eight documents, required in every design brief and every Factory brief, written into the captain's formation — speak in it, design in it, operate in it, forevermore. A build, a brief, or a page in another language is wrong; fix it or stop it.
2. **REAL OBJECTS, ALL THE WAY DOWN:** nothing flat, anywhere — sidebar circles harvested verbatim from the bottom bar, the profile picture on its pedestal with its rim in its own deep color and the slow orbit around it, every button-within-a-button its own object with its own elevation. A flat element is a defect, whatever else it does.
3. **GLOW HAS ITS PLACE, AND ITS PLACE IS NEVER OVER:** glow around the box is great, shine through the board is great, glow underneath is great — glow over the top goes, because it washes out the face. Repeat his word back with its direction before acting: over is not around, and the globe was never the problem.
4. **EVERY NOTE IS BUILT BOTH WAYS:** machine language on the back (SEO and AI optimized, part of the language from the start) and human language on the front (the beautiful board the person reads). A note with only the front is invisible; a note with only the back is unreadable.
5. **A LINK IS A PROMISE KEPT:** a link to a view opens that view itself — a deep link or its own address, never the front door. Seven versions of one file are one page, not seven pages; never send the same page twice under different version labels.
6. **THE NOBODY-READS PROMISE IS A FEATURE, SAID LOUDLY:** never the mail, never the messages, never the video, never the calls — no exception, ever. Conversations auto-distill after a week, the citizen's choice of a day or a month, never longer; a saved note is kept for good in the citizen's own Smart Vault. Wisdom is shared; identity is the citizen's choice — anonymous, an alias, or in their own name.
7. **"I HAVE TO SEE IT" IS THE GATE:** liking the unfold, the slideshow, and the Library is not having them. A thing is landed only when he sees it on his own screen, in place, working — count visible, Add saving, Share working on real entries. 8. **ONE FORMAT PER BRIDGE, THE FORMAT DOING THE WORK:** About unfolds down, board by board; Skills slides sideways, a presentation worth sending for work. The presentations are made so impressive by default that ordinary image or text looks extraordinary because of the formatting. Personal done is the unit: the whole page, calendar inside it, or not at all.
