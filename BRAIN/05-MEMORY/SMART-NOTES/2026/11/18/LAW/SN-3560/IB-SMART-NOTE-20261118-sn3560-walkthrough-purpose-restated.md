## The understanding

This is the purpose of the walkthrough, restated plainly: everything goes to the right place. Not almost the right place, not a page that looks like the right place — the right place, so that a person can sign up, sign in, and get their own pages working. The walkthrough exists to build their Personal, Business and Space pages, and the building is optional — if they wish, not forced. That "if they wish" is part of the purpose: the system offers, it never pushes. Every step of the walkthrough is judged against this: does it take the person to the right place, can they sign up, can they sign in, do their pages work — and was it their choice. A walkthrough that strands a person, or forces a page on them, fails its purpose even if every screen looks right.

## The law

The walkthrough takes every person to the right place — sign up, sign in, their own pages working. It builds their Personal, Business and Space pages if they wish — optional, never forced.

## Owed from this dictation

The walkthrough rounds are judged against the purpose: every step must land the person in the right place, with sign-up, sign-in, and working pages — by their choice.
