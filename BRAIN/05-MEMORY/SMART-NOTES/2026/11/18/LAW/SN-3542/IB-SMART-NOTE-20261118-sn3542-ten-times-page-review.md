## The understanding

This is the standing charge for every page walk: look at each page and ask, is that the best feature for that page — and how can that feature, and that page, be ten times better in usefulness. Really think about each page and what it does, and push it to the max. It extends the standing laws — raise the bar as you go, the Universal Smart App Page scorecard — with his explicit usefulness test: the improvement is measured in usefulness, not just in prettiness. A page is not judged by whether it looks finished; it is judged by whether each of its features is the best feature for what that page is for, and whether that feature could be ten times more useful than it is now. The review is the work, not the prelude to it.

## The law

On every page walk, ask of each feature: is that the best feature for that page, and how can it be ten times better in usefulness? Push it to the max.

## Owed from this dictation

Every page walk carries the 10× review: each feature scored against what its page is for, then pushed ten times more useful, not just prettier. The scorecard for a page is a usefulness scorecard first.
