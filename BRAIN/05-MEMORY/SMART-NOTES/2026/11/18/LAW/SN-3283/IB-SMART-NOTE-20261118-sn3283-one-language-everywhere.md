## What it means

1. **WHITE FIRST IS THE TEXT LAW:** white is number one against obsidian, then gold, purple, green, blue sometimes — for text. Gray is flat and boring and stays out. The scale is official: headlines 25, sub-headlines 19, small text 17, all the way through.
2. **NOTHING FLAT IS A LAW, NOT A PREFERENCE:** not the text, not the icons, not the boards. The diamond is a real faceted jewel, bullet points are jewels, and the wisdom board — "just because you can doesn't mean you should — ask first if it's right" — is the model every word is measured against.
3. **THE BEST BUTTONS ARE THE STANDARD, AND THE STANDARD TRAVELS:** the PTS / % / MONEY pills are the best buttons in the whole system — clean, readable at a glance, each its own color, premium. Harvest them verbatim, never redesign, never touch — and carry that quality, aliveness, and clarity onto the weekday plaques, the boards, the NOVEMBER 2026 header, the TODAY button, and the back and forward arrows.
4. **SMALL IS PART OF THE HARVEST:** the Dashboard digit tiles stay — they are good — but small in the month cells, never dominating the cell. A restore that puts them back big restores without the law.
5. **ALIVE MEANS HIGH-TECH COMPUTER, NOT VIDEO GAME:** the spinning orbs stay — the more alive it feels, the better — but the motion is judged by three words: intelligent, elegant, system alive. Fast ambient motion on 8 of 9 surfaces, against the 12-second floor, reads as video game.
6. **THE SCORECARD IS THE JUDGEMENT:** 10 things loved, 10 things not loved — what survives and what must change, named in plain words. The calendar scored that way: today must be known at a glance, the weekday a royal object, the eye balanced, the colors elegant — or the whole reads low-end, cheap, elementary tech.
7. **THE CORRECTION IS THE LAW TOO:** "today lights up way more" was said at 03:47Z and taken back at 03:54Z — no huge TODAY pill, no glow over the day itself, no cell filled with color. Today reads from the weekday plaque and the header TODAY button. The second word supersedes the first, and the supersession is written down, so no builder ever builds the glowing cell again.
8. **ONE MAIL, BEST OF THE BEST:** two Smart Mail presentations become one, taking the best of each — the F and D letters aligned to one hex per hue, the confusing cover control made plain or removed, flat buttons given real depth. A happy medium would average two good pages into a mediocre one.
9. **THE LAW ENFORCES ITSELF:** the text laws became the rule book, the machine form, and the checker — because a lesson counts only when it changes behavior mechanically. The inspector scores the drift; the Factory is the one language.

## Why it matters

1. Without one language, every page is a new design that only sort of looks like the last — and the citizen opens each door into a different house.
2. Without white first and 25/19/17, the next designer ships gray small text again, because nothing in the build path named the ranking or the sizes.
3. Without the best buttons as the travelling standard, the Dashboard keeps the only good buttons in the system — a beautiful example nobody applies, a souvenir instead of a standard.
4. Without the aliveness flavour written down, the next builder hears "more alive" and makes the page faster, brighter, bouncier — a video game — because "alive" was recorded without its direction.
5. Without the scorecard, the calendar keeps getting fixed one defect at a time while the whole still reads cheap — because nobody wrote down that the whole reads cheap.
6. Without the correction recorded, the next builder reads "today lights up way more" and builds a glowing cell — and Shawn has to correct it a second time.
7. Without the machine checker, the laws are a file on a shelf, and the next fresh seat starts cold and breaks the same rules — because nothing in the system made the reading real.
