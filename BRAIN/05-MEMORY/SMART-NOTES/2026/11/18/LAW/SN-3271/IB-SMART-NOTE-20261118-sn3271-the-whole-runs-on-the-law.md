## Hook

## Nutshell

Shawn's house runs on one law that is really four promises kept together. The laws go into the system and to every agent: read first, check after, and redo the work until it follows — and a brief that does not name the laws is incomplete and is not dispatched. The money stays wired honestly: the ladder is his word, Ambassadors are hired and keep half, PayPal is the only billing through nine honest choices, every hop is a Smart Link, and the ledger is included at every level because transparency is not a tier. Everything built is a real object — even the text — one light top-left, real shadow, formula placement, judged by his eye until it stops changing. And the facts underneath are kept true: the ladder is applied exactly as last dictated, settled forms are recorded in his words with their supersessions, and open questions are held honestly instead of guessed at. The day is the proof of it all: the menu opens with the live action first, the greeting carries the Hub's warmth, the numbers are big blocks, the weekday is a royal object that fits whole, the diamond spins slowly as a real jewel, and one headliner says the date once, never twice.

## What it means

1. **THE SYSTEM, NOT THE FILE:** the laws live in GitHub so every Naya pulls the same copy, and every brief names them. A law every agent must read before acting is a system; a law in one file is a document that gets forgotten.
2. **READ FIRST IS A GATE, CHECK AFTER IS THE PROOF:** the activation habit is now a gate for every agent, every action — and the check after is written down, run through the checker, and seen by the eye. A check that is not written down did not happen.
3. **REDO UNTIL IT FOLLOWS:** work that breaks the laws goes back and is done again — no exceptions, no "next time." A correction that is not enforced is a correction that repeats.
4. **SIMPLE MONEY, WIRED HONESTLY:** one ladder, one processor, every hop a Smart Link, the ledger open to everyone it pays. Nine choices and one processor mean almost nothing to break and nothing to hide in.
5. **REAL OBJECTS, EVEN THE TEXT:** there is no flat layer on a Naya page — no text merely drawn, no icon merely a picture. The diamond proves it: if the tiniest element has to be a real spinning jewel, everything does.
6. **THE DAY IS THE PROOF:** the menu, the greeting, the blocks, the weekday, the diamond, the headliner — the law is not argued, it is shown, one day at a time, in the least words that say the most.

## Why it matters

1. Without the lock-in, the rule book is a file and the next fresh seat starts cold, makes new icons, and breaks the same rules — because nothing in the system made the reading real. With it, no seat ever starts cold again.
2. Without the wired money, the network's philosophy fails its audit by strangers: a double charge nobody modelled, a payout rule nobody wrote down, a price that differs in the back room. Wiring every hop while the system is small keeps the first thousand citizens' experience as clean as the first one's.
3. Without the object law at full strength, pages slide back into pictures of computers — flat diamonds, gray text, dead boards — and the network reads as a theme someone bought instead of the future it is.
4. Without the day's discipline, the menu is a mess stuck together, the date is said twice, the glow drowns the object — and the citizen's brain has to put the page in order instead of the page putting itself in order.
5. Without named, fixed, finished, defects get vague names, breaks get quietly routed around, and rounds present themselves without scorecards — and the error rate of a growing network goes up while the network grows, instead of down.
