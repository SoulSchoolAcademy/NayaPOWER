## Human

The banner was doing two jobs and doing both badly: it was the greeting and it was the wisdom, and the wisdom was a big block that pushed everything else down. His fix is subtraction, and the subtraction is precise — three named things come out (the Wisdom of the Day label, the long quote, the "Smart Note of the Day" label), and what remains is four things in a fixed order: motto at the top, greeting, date and live time underneath the greeting, and the sharing line. The order matters: the motto says what this place is, the greeting says who it is for, the date and live time underneath say when it is — live time under the greeting is the day's pulse, not a clock in a corner — and the sharing line says what happens here, replacing the "Our public smart feed" pill, which goes with it. Nothing else is allowed in. The wisdom is not thrown away; it is moved to where it already works — it circulates like a slide in the feed, one after the next, proven by his own two screenshots four minutes apart (Twin Rank 4:41 PM, One Screen Complete 4:46 PM), and he likes the slide, so it stays as feed content and never again as a big block on the banner.
