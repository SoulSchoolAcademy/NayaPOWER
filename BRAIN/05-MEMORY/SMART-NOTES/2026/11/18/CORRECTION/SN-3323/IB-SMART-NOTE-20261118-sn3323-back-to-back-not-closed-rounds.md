## Human

A closed round feels efficient — finish everything, then show it. But he only sees the end, so drift compounds for hours before his eye catches it, and then the fix is a second round, then a third. Back-to-back costs him a look at version one, and it saves him the fight at version four. And an edit that redoes the whole page is not an edit — it is a new page that happens to contain the edit, and every unasked change in it is a new thing for him to catch. Keep the page, change the edit, show it early.
