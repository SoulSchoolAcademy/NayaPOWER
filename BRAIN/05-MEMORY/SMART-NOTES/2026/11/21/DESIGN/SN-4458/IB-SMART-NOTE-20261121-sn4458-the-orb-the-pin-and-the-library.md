1. Without the DM orb as the source, the round invents a second flow when the first one already flows — and spends its hour on a comet that doesn't read, while the element he already approved was sitting in the sidebar all along.
2. Without "Rate This Intel," the control is misnamed every time it is read — "Rate the Intelligence" sounds like grading a system; "Rate This Intel" says what it does, here, now.
3. Without the three small moves, the temptation when the eye says "almost" is to rebuild the board to be safe — and the rebuild loses the fit he just praised.
4. Without the pin-only correction, every word he did not say, carried as if he said it, costs him a correction — and he should never have to correct my naming.
5. Without save landing straight in, saving interrupts the moment by asking the citizen to choose a list first — and a Smart List that only exists in one place is a system the citizen cannot reach from where they are.
6. Without naming the build first, "fixed" and "still broken" are both guesses — and the round goes to fix a page he never saw.

9. Without naming and categorizing the "what is" frames, a citizen who asks "what is CCT?" hunts through the constitution — while fifteen topics grouped by kind feel like one system, not fifteen links.
10. Without the Library, a law nobody can find is a law nobody can follow — frames without a home are a trail, not a library.
