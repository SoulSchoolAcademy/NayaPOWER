## Why It Matters

A screen called NAYA NOTES tells the citizen Naya wrote these — and Naya didn't. The citizen did. The name is the promise (Eleven Law 5): call it YOUR NOTES and the citizen knows at a glance that this is theirs to write, theirs to keep, theirs to go back to on any day. And "Make Both Notes" dies here on purpose: a Naya→AI→Naya round-trip cannot send from Naya to the AI and back to Naya — building it would be building a loop that cannot close.

## How to Use It

Rename the calendar entry screen from "NAYA NOTES" to "YOUR NOTES". The citizen writes whatever they want; it saves to their Smart Vault, auto-organized; all their notes for that day sit there; they can go back to any day and check that day's notes; images uploadable to their notes too. Do NOT build "Make Both Notes" — the Naya→AI→Naya round-trip is ruled out.
