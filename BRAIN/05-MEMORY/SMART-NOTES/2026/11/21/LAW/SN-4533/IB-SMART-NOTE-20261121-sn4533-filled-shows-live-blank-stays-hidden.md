## The Lesson

**FILLED SHOWS LIVE, BLANK STAYS HIDDEN (his ruling, 2026-11-21 ~12:03 MST):**

**WHAT IS FILLED SHOWS, FRAME BY FRAME.** Whatever a citizen fills in on About or Skills shows live, frame by frame. The filled answer is the frame — the page is built from what is real, nothing else.

**WHAT IS BLANK DOES NOT SHOW.** Whatever they leave blank does NOT show live. No empty frame, no placeholder, no "coming soon" — the blank simply is not there. The page never displays its own incompleteness to the world.

**SETTINGS HOLDS THE WHOLE MAP.** In Settings the citizen can see all the available areas and add a new template block with more information whenever they want. The page shows what is filled; Settings shows what could be.

**INPUT STAYS WALKTHROUGH-STYLE.** Fill it in, or skip to the next one. Nothing forced, ever — a finished half-filled page beats an abandoned form.

## Why It Matters

A half-filled page that shows its blanks looks unfinished; a half-filled page that shows only what is filled looks complete. The citizen is never shamed by empty frames, and the page is never a to-do list the world can see. Settings holding the whole map means nothing is ever lost — the citizen can always come back and add more, when they want, in their own time. And walkthrough-style input is what makes the filling possible at all: fill it in, or skip — the page grows one answer at a time.

## How to Use It

Render a frame only when its answer exists; a blank answer renders nothing, not a placeholder. In Settings, list every available area so the citizen can add a new template block whenever they want. Keep the input walkthrough-style — fill it in, or skip to the next one. Audit every page the same way: a blank showing live is out of alignment; a filled answer not showing is out of alignment.
