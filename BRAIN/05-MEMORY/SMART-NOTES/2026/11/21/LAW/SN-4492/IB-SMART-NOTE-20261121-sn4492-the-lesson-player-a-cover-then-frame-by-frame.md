## The Lesson

**The Lesson Player — settled design (as recorded in MEMORY.md from the same call, 2026-11-21 ~09:57 MST — part of the Academy flow):** click a lesson → another cover, looking like a Personal page, featuring THAT specific lesson (first cover was the course, then the lessons within the course). The cover introduces the lesson. Click the button → the lesson unfolds FRAME BY FRAME — images + video + text, you go through it.

**Two covers, two jobs.** The first cover introduces the course; the second introduces the lesson. Each one is a Personal page in its own right, featuring that one thing — the same frame, the same language, a different subject. The cover is the introduction; the button is the door.

**Frame by frame is the format.** The lesson unfolds the way the collective agreement unfolds — images + video + text, one frame at a time, you go through it. Not a scroll, not a wall: frames, fitted like a glove, no scrolling inside a frame.

**The toggle is the citizen's choice.** MIXED (images + videos + text — the full deal, the DEFAULT), or ALL VIDEO, or ALL AUDIO. We do them all; they choose how they view it. And the correction, in the same call: NO all-text view — "doing all text probably doesn't make sense." A lesson is not a document; text alone does not carry it.

## Why It Matters

The player is where the teaching actually happens — the Academy flow brings the citizen to the lesson, the player is the lesson. If the player were a scroll of text, the whole flow would land flat; frame by frame with a toggle, the citizen goes through the lesson the way they go through the agreement — and they choose the shape that suits them. The no-all-text correction matters as much as the toggle: he caught the shape that does not make sense before it was built.

## How to Use It

Build the player as he settled it: lesson cover like a Personal page, button, frame by frame with images + video + text, toggle MIXED (default) / ALL VIDEO / ALL AUDIO. Never offer an all-text view — he corrected that out in the same call, and a builder who adds it back has not read the note.
