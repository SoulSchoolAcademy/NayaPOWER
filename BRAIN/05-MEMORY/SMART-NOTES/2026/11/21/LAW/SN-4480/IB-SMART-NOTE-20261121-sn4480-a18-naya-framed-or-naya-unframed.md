## Why It Matters

The frame is the signature — the NayaNET frame is what makes a block recognizably Naya's, and it is the default because the standard is framed. But a citizen who wants their block unframed can choose it, in a setting, without losing the intelligence inside. Default framed, choice unframed: the standard holds and the citizen is free.

## How to Use It

Ship every block Naya framed, standard — that is the default. Put a setting in the system that lets the citizen choose framed or unframed. Keep it separate from the unformat toggle: unformat governs the boards inside, framed/unframed governs the outer frame. Never default a block to unframed.
