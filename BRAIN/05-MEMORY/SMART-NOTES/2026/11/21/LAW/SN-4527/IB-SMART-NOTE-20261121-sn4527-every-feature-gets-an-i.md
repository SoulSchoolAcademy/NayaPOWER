## The Lesson

**COMPOSER — THE "i" RULE + ADD-NOTE-TO-POST (his clarification, 2026-11-21 ~11:36 MST):**

**EVERY FEATURE GETS A LITTLE "i" BESIDE IT.** Every feature on the page carries a little "i" beside it. Tapping it explains, in the simplest words, what that feature does. No feature stands unexplained; no citizen has to guess what a thing is for. The "i" is the explanation, always there, always in the simplest words — a child and a grandma both understand it on the first tap.

**THE "i" ON POST A NOTE SAYS WHAT A NOTE IS.** For Post a note, the "i" explains: these are your personal daily notes to yourself, private by default, not for the world. The note is yours, for you, by default — that is the first thing the citizen learns about it, before they have shared anything.

**TO SHARE ONE PUBLICLY, YOU MAKE A POST AND ADD THE NOTE TO IT.** A note becomes public only one way: you make a post and use an "add note to post" button — the note then becomes a public post. There is no separate Smart Note / Naya Note creation row, no second way to publish, no switch that quietly makes a private note public. The flow is the whole flow: note for yourself, post to share, add note to post to carry it over.

## Why It Matters

The "i" is the difference between a page a citizen can use cold and a page they have to be taught. Every feature explaining itself in the simplest words means nobody is ever stuck wondering what a button does — they tap the "i" and they know. And the add-note-to-post flow is the privacy law made visible: the private note stays private until the citizen takes the deliberate step of making a post and adding the note to it. Nothing about a note ever goes public by accident, by a toggle, or by a row they did not mean to tap. The note is theirs until they choose to share it, and the choice is one clear action they can see.
