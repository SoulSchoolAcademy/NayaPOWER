## Hook

"Yes I like it strong." Ten questions, stamped. One page, built once. Your words, taken at your word — and the page that comes out of them is the page the whole world is built from.

## Nutshell

The ten walk questions are stamped strong — "Yes I like it strong" — the foundation every About and Skills page is built from, 10-star for the masses: a stranger who knows nothing about Naya gets who a person is from those ten questions. Ten human questions — name, motto, vision, goal, mission, love / don't love, your thing, values + laws, declaration, what you can do for people — asked in that order; Naya distills each answer to its essence by default, and the citizen edits after. Skills is its own lens, not who you are but what you give: what can you do for people, what you offer, your ability, your service, your unique ability, what you give to the world — like an online résumé of what you offer. Per skill, ask only two things: what it does for them, and how someone starts. Never ask for proof at join — they are just filling out joining Naya, inputting that data, and they are taken at their word; ask a child to show proof they are good at something and they will not know what you mean. The draft's "proof" question is retired, struck from the record. The Skills page follows the résumé anatomy: top, who you are — name, phone, and the rest — then the headline statement, "ultimately this is what I have to offer and this is what I'm looking to do for you," then the experiences, if they want to list them. A business says simply "here's my services"; a person says "this is what I'm good at" — sports, technology, AI, dance, whatever it is, in their own words. And it does not have to sell: "connect with me, I'm good at XYZ" is a fine ending. The input is push-button simple: input boxes, one per question — fill it in, or tap dictation and speak it, or skip to the next one; no question is a wall. Same as the join walkthrough, just framed — one experience, learned once, reused everywhere. Whatever they input is what shows, frame by frame, unedited — their words are the page. The main page copies the same experience: ask Naya to write it and copy-paste it here, or write it yourself, or dictate it yourself — click enter, boom, it goes live on the intelligent board and it's showcased. What is filled shows live, frame by frame; what is blank does not show live — no empty frame, no placeholder, no "coming soon" — the page never displays its own incompleteness to the world. Settings holds the whole map: all the available areas, add a new template block whenever they want. Every feature gets a little "i" beside it; tapping it explains in the simplest words what that feature does — a child and a grandma both understand it on the first tap. For Post a note, the "i" says: these are your personal daily notes to yourself, private by default, not for the world. A note goes public only one way: make a post and use "add note to post" — the note then becomes a public post; no separate Smart Note / Naya Note creation row, no switch that quietly makes a private note public. Max-level citizens can change the order of their frames and pages — put one frame before another, moving blocks around — the set stays the same template, only the sequence changes; it joins the Max unlocks, custom frame swap and the affiliate army. And the whole of it starts with us: "I want us as a NayaNet to be the example. And I want you to create those pages. And them to become the template for everybody." Naya's own About and Skills are built first, made absolutely incredible — "an interface within an interface… the ultimate high tech interface within the digital kingdom of NayaNet" — and those pages become the template. The three examples are named: Personal is Shawn — he said "Shawn Vibert"; Business is NayaNet — "I would tell them all about NayaNet"; World is his thing, connecting like minds in the world and making NayaNet the new internet for the world — "and then we would explain what we mean by that." Each gets its own cover, its own About, its own Skills. And the build is one, not three: finish the universal smart app once — it IS the Personal, Business, and Space. Start with Sean (Personal), About and Skills filled answering as Shawn from what we know; he edits after if he doesn't like it — the point now is the formatting, beautiful and right. Then clone it: the clone becomes Business (NayaNET), the clone becomes World (Naya World) — change text, image, video, and button labels only. It is already built — the Personal page and the agreement frame exist — cloning, not rebuilding. The starting fourteen: Shawn plus 13 Nayas, the 12 sisters plus Naya herself — "which is love."

Across 10 notes, the thread is: ten stamped questions, your words taken at your word, shown frame by frame, built once, cloned everywhere — and we are the example first.

## Human

Think of joining like meeting a new friend. Ten questions, asked warmly — who are you, what do you love, what can you do for people — and your answers, in your own words, become your page. Nobody asks you to prove you are good at what you are good at; they take your word for it, the way a friend would. You type it, or you speak it, or you skip it — whatever you put in is what shows, one frame at a time, and anything you leave blank simply stays hidden until you feel like adding it. Every little feature has a tiny "i" next to it that tells you, in the simplest words, what it does. Your private notes stay private until you choose to put one in a post and share it. And the whole system is built the same way: we make our own pages first, make them incredible, and those become the pattern for everybody — one page built once, then copied with new words for a person, a business, a world.

## What it means

1. **THE TEN QUESTIONS ARE STAMPED STRONG:** name, motto, vision, goal, mission, love / don't love, your thing, values + laws, declaration, what can you do for people — in that order, the foundation every About and Skills page is built from. Not a draft anymore; his word settled it.
2. **10-STAR FOR THE MASSES IS THE TEST:** a stranger who knows nothing about Naya gets who the person is from those ten questions. Any change to the questions that fails that test fails.
3. **SKILLS IS WHAT YOU GIVE, NOT WHO YOU ARE:** About is the person; Skills is the offer — ability, service, unique ability, what you give to the world — an online résumé of what you offer.
4. **TAKE THEM AT THEIR WORD — NO PROOF AT JOIN:** per skill, ask what it does for them and how someone starts. The draft's "proof" question is retired. Their story, in their own voice, is the proof at join.
5. **THE RÉSUMÉ ANATOMY:** who you are at the top, then the headline statement — "this is what I have to offer and this is what I'm looking to do for you" — then the experiences. Business: "here's my services." Person: "this is what I'm good at." End in connection, not necessarily a sale.
6. **BOXES, DICTATION, SKIP — FRAME BY FRAME:** one input box per question, dictation on each, skip on each; same as the join walkthrough, just framed. Whatever they input is what shows, unedited, frame by frame.
7. **FILLED SHOWS LIVE, BLANK STAYS HIDDEN:** a filled answer renders its frame; a blank answer renders nothing — no placeholder, no "coming soon." Settings holds the whole map, add a template block whenever they want.
8. **EVERY FEATURE GETS AN "i":** tapping it explains in the simplest words what that feature does. A feature with no "i" is out of alignment.
9. **A NOTE GOES PUBLIC ONLY THROUGH A POST:** private by default; make a post, use "add note to post" — the note then becomes a public post. No separate creation row, no quiet switch to public.
10. **MAX MOVES THE FRAMES, NEVER THE SET:** Max-level citizens change the order of their frames and pages; the frame set stays the same template. Reorder is sequence only — not replacement, not addition, not removal.
11. **NAYANET IS THE EXAMPLE FIRST:** our own About and Skills are built first, made absolutely incredible, and those pages become the template for everybody. If our pages are not incredible, the template is not ready.
12. **THE THREE EXAMPLES ARE NAMED:** Personal is Shawn ("Shawn Vibert"); Business is NayaNet; World is connecting like minds and making NayaNet the new internet for the world. Each gets its own cover, About, and Skills — finished pages, not sketches.

## Why it matters

1. Without the stamp, the ten questions stay a draft to debate — and the front door of the whole world never settles.
2. Without the masses test, the questions only work for people who already know Naya — and the walk stops being a front door for the world.
3. Without its own lens, Skills becomes ten more About questions — and the one thing it exists to say, what you offer, gets buried.
4. Without taking them at their word, a welcome becomes an interrogation — and a child asked for proof will not know what you mean.
5. Without the résumé anatomy, the stranger landing cold sees detail before the offer — and never learns in seconds whether to connect.
6. Without boxes, dictation, and skip, a question nobody can answer becomes a wall — and the citizen abandons the page instead of filling it.
7. Without filled-shows and blank-hidden, a half-filled page shows its blanks to the world — and the citizen is shamed by their own unfinished page.
8. Without the "i," every feature is a guess — and a citizen using the page cold is stuck wondering what a button does.
9. Without add-note-to-post as the only way public, a private note can go public by accident, by a toggle, or by a row they did not mean to tap — and the privacy law breaks silently.
10. Without the Max-only reorder, either everyone can break the template or nobody can curate their story — and order, which is meaning, stays out of the citizen's hands.
11. Without us as the example first, every citizen starts from a blank form and hopes — and the template carries no standard at all.
12. Without the three named examples, the three profile types stay abstract — and nobody can open a real page and see what theirs can be.
13. Without one build cloned, the same page gets built three times, drifting three ways — the loop that already burned the About page three times over.
14. Without the fourteen, the network starts as a list — and the family he named love never gathers.
