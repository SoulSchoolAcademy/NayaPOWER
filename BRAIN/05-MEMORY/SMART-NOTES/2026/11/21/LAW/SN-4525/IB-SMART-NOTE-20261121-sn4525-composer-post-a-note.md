## The Lesson

**COMPOSER — "POST A NOTE" (his label, 2026-11-21 ~11:30 MST):**

**THE ACTION IS "POST A NOTE" — NEVER "SMART NOTE" AS A BUTTON OR LABEL.** The citizen does not tap a button that says "Smart Note". They tap ADD, and the action is "Post a note". The words on the button are the words the citizen understands: they are posting a note. "Smart Note" is what the system calls the thing behind the action — the intelligent block, the distilled lesson — not what the citizen is asked to do. A button labelled "Smart Note" asks the citizen to know the system's vocabulary before they have done anything; a button that says "Post a note" asks them only to do the thing.

**THE FLOW: ADD → POST A NOTE → CHOOSE WHERE IT GOES.** ADD opens the action; "Post a note" is the action; then they choose where it goes — the feed picker (Personal, Business, Space, Hub — the "Which feed?" step already built), and Space via a plus button/dropdown when there is more than one Space. The flow is the same as the one already built; the label is what changes. Nothing in the flow is rebuilt for this — only the words on the action.

**PRIVATE BY DEFAULT, PUBLIC ONLY BY YOUR CHOICE.** The note goes private by default; public only by the citizen's choice. That is the privacy law in the composer's own words: the default protects them, the choice is theirs. A note that defaults public is a note that leaks before the citizen has decided; a note that defaults private is a note that waits for their word.

## Why It Matters

Every word on a button is a decision the citizen has to make, and "Smart Note" is a decision about vocabulary — it makes them stop and ask what a smart note is before they have posted anything. "Post a note" is a decision about doing — it tells them exactly what happens when they tap. The distinction is the whole design: the system can call things by their smart names behind the scenes, but the citizen's buttons speak in plain words about the action. And the private default is the same law at the other end: the system never publishes for them; they choose. A composer that says the plain thing and defaults private is a composer a child and a grandma can both use without a second thought.

## How to Use It

Label the create action "Post a note" everywhere — never "Smart Note" as a button or label. Flow: ADD → Post a note → choose where it goes (feed picker; Space via plus button/dropdown when more than one Space) → private by default, public only by your choice. Audit every composer, every ADD flow, every button: any "Smart Note" on a button or label is out of alignment and gets renamed.
