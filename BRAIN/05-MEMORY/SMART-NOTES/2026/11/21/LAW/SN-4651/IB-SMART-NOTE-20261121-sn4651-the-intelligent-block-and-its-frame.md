A smart post is ONE block, not auto-formatted, verbatim, public or private, with share and images — and it IS the ADD button on the bottom bar: tap ADD, "Which feed?", adding a post. The real difference is who inputs it: posts are the customer's manual sharing — they write it, Naya does not rewrite it or input it for them; notes are Naya's input — the customer tells Naya ("smart note this" / "Naya note this") and Naya inputs the note. Three kinds, settled: smart post / Naya note / smart note. Every block is wrapped in the NayaNET frame — the outer frame is standard, part of the anatomy, so everything comes out framed and beautiful, the layer boards fitting just inside it. The image gets its own frame: directly on top, full width, like a stage, whole and uncropped; headline just below the image; text block underneath; all wrapped in the NayaNET frame. And the guard over it all: keep inventory of every element of each thing — know the anatomy of all of it, always checking, is it all there — because things drift backwards and get forgotten when nobody is keeping the inventory. The proof is the Hub frame going missing unnoticed, and the fix is one base, one link: go back to fix-1e, the version his eye liked, and stay there.

Across 10 notes, the thread is: one block, three kinds, one frame — and a list that keeps every part of it there.

3. **THE NAYA NOTE IS THE FULL DEAL:** the full scope — the math, the technicals, the greater depth, the root — in three languages at once. One name, one thing: the back layer and the standalone full report are the same thing, two surfaces.

## Why it matters

1. Without the anatomy, a smart note is just a summary — and a summary is not the intelligence, not the book in a minute, not the thing a person can use.
2. Without the Naya Note as the full deal, the back of the note is a longer summary instead of the whole scope — and the depth, the math, the root have nowhere to live.
3. Without private by default, a Naya note could land in the public feed or get a share button bolted on — and the privacy promise breaks.
4. Without the flip, the reader can never turn the distilled front over to the full deal on the back — and a document that teaches the system without the flip teaches it wrong.
5. Without the smart post defined as the ADD button, the customer has no way to add a post they can find — and the difference between their sharing and Naya's input stays unclear.
6. Without the unformat choice, the beautiful formatting becomes a cage for their own things — and without the smart-note exception, the public standard has no guard.
7. Without the frame as standard, a block ships with a piece missing — and his eye catches it instead of the inventory, the way the Hub frame went missing unnoticed.
8. Without the settled image layout, every note with a picture is a new decision — and the picture gets cropped into a box instead of shown whole.
9. Without the inventory, a rebuild quietly drops a frame, a bar, a button — and the next person to notice is him, on his screen.
10. Without one base, one link, two links to the same page mean nobody can tell which one is the page — and the version he judged is not the version the fixes land on.
11. Without the one activation document, a brand-new customer who knows nothing about smart notes has no way in — and intelligence has no door into the system.
