## Why It Matters

A page is not a file — it is a list of parts, and the list is the only thing that keeps the parts from drifting away. Without an inventory, a rebuild quietly drops a frame, a bar, a button, and the next person to notice is him, on his screen. With an inventory, every round names the parts it must keep and proves each one is still there. The law turns "did we break anything?" from a hope into a check.

## How to Use It

For every page, keep the inventory of its parts — every element that makes it up, the anatomy of all of it. Every round, every walk, every check runs against that inventory: name what was already on the page, confirm every piece is still there or was instructed away. A missing part is a fail before it reaches his eye. Never remove what he didn't ask to remove; never lose what he didn't ask to lose.
