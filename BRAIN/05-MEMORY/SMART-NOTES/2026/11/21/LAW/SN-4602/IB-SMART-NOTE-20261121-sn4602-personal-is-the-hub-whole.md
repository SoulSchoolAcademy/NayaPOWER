## What it means

1. **FIFTEEN QUESTIONS MAX, PER WORLD:** ten About frames plus the five core Skills fill a Personal; the same fifteen, offered again, fill a Business or a World. Skip any question, do just Personal, or all three — nothing forced.
2. **NO DEAD DOORS:** an uncreated door takes the citizen straight into its walkthrough; the plus button opens a walkthrough for another world that creates the page. Every door either opens a finished page or opens the walkthrough that builds it.
3. **THE SAME DM RAIL, EVERYWHERE:** Personal gets the Hub's DM rail, byte for byte, auto-organizing based on the feed — people and DMs derived from the feed's items, most recent and most relevant first.

And Smart Worlds is the table of contents for all Smart Lists — Worlds, Connections, Smart Notes, Your Notes, Posts You Shared — one look instead of five hunts.

## Why it matters

1. Without fifteen max, the citizen answers and still gets a blank page — the work happens twice or not at all — and the walk has failed its own test.
2. Without no-dead-doors, a page is a picture of a page: the citizen taps About expecting the person and gets nothing, and the promise breaks the moment it is tapped.
3. Without the same DM rail, the side of the page does not match the Hub, and the page is not updated — however finished its middle looks.
4. Without the same boards, the citizen sees two different systems wearing one name, and the feed stops being one feed.
5. Without one invite door, three doors to the same thing make the citizen guess which invite is the invite — and two of the three guesses are wrong.
6. Without one colour per meaning, the row lies about what is different: two buttons in the same purple family are one button twice.
7. Without the right sidebar opening, the citizen is locked out of everything behind it — messages, people, the whole side of their world.
8. Without mail and messenger together, a conversation split across two doors is a message missed — the citizen has to go find the half they need.
9. Without connections staying put, a relationship filed under a generic pile is lost: the pages mean something because their people stay with them.
10. Without Smart Worlds as the contents page, five kinds of lists in five places are five hunts — and a citizen who cannot find their own life will not come back to it.
