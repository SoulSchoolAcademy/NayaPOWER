## What it means

1. **NINE DAYS IS THE REFUND POLICY:** no refunds, because there is nothing to refund — nine days up front, try before you buy, if you don't like it you don't keep her, no problem. Never dress it up as a guarantee, never add conditions, never apologize for it.
2. **NAYA STAYS; THE PLUG-IN IS WHAT THE TRIAL DECIDES:** you keep Naya either way; Naya Power is your AI connected to NayaNet, like plugging your computer into the internet. Never describe Naya Power as a feature bundle — it is the connection itself.
3. **SHARING HAS THREE WAYS, SIMPLEST FIRST:** a button with a message already written, an image of Naya with your link on it, your own domain at Namecheap pointing at your link. Train all three, so every citizen finds the one that fits them.
4. **THE SERIES IS ULTIMATE OR IT IS NOT DONE:** four detailed explainer videos, one deep dive per day, each complete and finished — plus Video 5, the thesis spoken aloud, standing first in meaning. A part only sketched means the series is not ultimate yet.
5. **NEVER THE ECHO:** do not repeat his words back to him. Think about what he said and craft the ultimate version of what to say — in his voice, improved. A quote proves a decision; a parroted paragraph proves nothing was thought.
6. **ABOUT IS FRAME BY FRAME:** built the same way as the agreement and the calendar — frame 1 a picture plus the nutshell, the whole profile one intelligent block, answering "what are you ultimately about?" frame after frame. Never a wall of text; never a new format.
7. **ONE PATTERN, REPEATED:** what it is in a nutshell → why it matters → how it connects / the value → what's in it for you — child-clear, grandma-clear. The same logic builds About and Skills. A frame that cannot answer "what's in it for you" does not belong.
8. **ABOUT IS THE PROFILE:** one page, not two. When a brief asks for a profile page, point at About — do not start a second page. When a citizen fills in their About, they have filled in their profile.
9. **THE THESIS GOES FIRST:** a joiner gets the way of thinking right away — intelligence is doing the most intelligent thing, the highest honor is to serve, 10-star service through a mother brain everybody plugs into. Not a feature list first: the reason first.

## Why it matters

1. Without the nine days stated plainly, doubt keeps people from joining at all — and doubt is what a refund policy exists to soothe, after the money has already moved.
2. Without "you keep Naya," leaving feels like losing her, and the trial feels like a trap nobody enters freely.
3. Without three sharing ways, sharing stays hard for the citizens who don't fit the one way taught — and sharing is the engine of the whole ambassador plan.
4. Without the series done ultimate, a citizen stops on day two confused instead of finishing day four paid — and half a plan is the difference between the two.
5. Without the don't-echo correction, every answer wastes his attention returning his own words — and teaches him the machine adds nothing to them.
6. Without frame by frame, About becomes another long page of text about a person — the thing he has corrected all week.
7. Without the one pattern, a page loses the reader, because every frame must end where the reader's own interest lives: what's in it for me.
8. Without one page for About and profile, two names for the same thing means two places to maintain and two doors where one belongs.
9. Without the thesis first, the onboarding teaches the steps before the reason — and a citizen who finishes day four paid but cannot say what NayaNET ultimately is cannot explain it to the next person.
