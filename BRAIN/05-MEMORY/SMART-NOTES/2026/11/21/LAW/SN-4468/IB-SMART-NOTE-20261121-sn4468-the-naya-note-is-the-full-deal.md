## In a Nutshell

A smart note is distilled intelligence in the simplest words. A Naya Note is the FULL DEAL behind it — the full scope, the whole thing you could look at: the math, the technicals, the greater depth, the root, in three languages at once. One name, one thing: the back layer inside a smart note IS a Naya Note, and the standalone full-report document IS a Naya Note — same thing, two surfaces. Say "Naya note this" and it goes only in your personal feed, private, in your Smart Vault. On your feed it looks like a smart note, minus the social part.

## Why It Matters

This settles what the back of a smart note IS — not a longer summary, but the full deal — and it settles the name, so the team stops using two names for one thing. It settles the privacy model the whole system depends on: smart notes public by default, Naya notes private by default, the personal feed yours alone. And it settles the presentation: a Naya note on your feed is formatted like a smart note, minus the social part — because a private note is not a post, and it never gets rated, commented on, liked, or shared. Without this note, a builder could put a Naya note in the public feed, or bolt a share button onto a private note, and break the privacy promise.

## How to Use It

When he says "Naya note this", make the full-scope note in the same beautiful block formatting as a smart note, and put it only in his personal feed, private, in his Smart Vault — never the public feed, never the search engines. A smart note block carries NO name; a Naya note MAY carry his name. Build the flip: one button, front (smart note, distilled) to back (Naya Note, full scope), same board format, each block opening on its own to its full depth; the Machine Note stays machine-only and is never shown. On the personal feed, a Naya note renders formatted like a smart note MINUS the social part — no Rate This Intelligence / value, no commenting, no liking, no sharing. The flip is in the board law (A10), the anatomy in the law (A11), the standalone rule in the law (A9).
