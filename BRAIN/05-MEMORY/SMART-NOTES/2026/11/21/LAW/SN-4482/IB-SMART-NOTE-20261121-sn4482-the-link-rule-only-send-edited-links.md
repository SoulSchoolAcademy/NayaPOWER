His attention is the scarcest resource in the world — every link he opens costs it. A link that carries nothing new since his last look spends that attention for nothing and teaches him that links can be ignored. A link that carries a real edit, ready to be viewed, earns the click. The rule protects the link itself: when a link arrives, it means something changed and it is ready for his eye — nothing else.

## How to Use It
