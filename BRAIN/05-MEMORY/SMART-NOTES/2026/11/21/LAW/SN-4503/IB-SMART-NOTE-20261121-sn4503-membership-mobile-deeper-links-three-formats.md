## The Lesson

**Membership — mobile, deeper links, three formats (settled, as recorded in MEMORY.md from the same call, 2026-11-21 ~10:01–10:03 MST — sent to the membership coordinator):**

**MOBILE — one design, fits both screens.** The cover goes on the top of the mobile and fits there; the button to go next goes below it; the frame fits around — laptop perfect frame, mobile cover-top + button-below. Mobile first is the law: the cover fits the phone, the button sits below it, the frame fits around both. Not a desktop page squeezed onto a phone — one design, two screens.

**LOGIN — the portal page is the login.** Then the membership area; then they access any videos, audios or text in a beautiful presentation — click, click, click through it all. The portal is the entrance; the membership area is inside it. No second login, no separate door.

**DEEPER LINKS — the layers below the frame.** Below the frame, links for going deeper on anything (like the legal-agreement links below the page) — click one and it opens text-by-text blocks in elevated blocks, same format as Naya notes / Smart Notes, more perspectives, what's in it for you, why it matters, what it is ultimately, child/grandma clarity. The frame keeps going across; the layers sit below for whoever chooses to go deeper. The citizen chooses the depth — the frame does not force it.

**BUTTONS — the matrix at the bottom.** Buttons at the bottom for more information — a matrix-like look; the citizen chooses whatever level they want to see. The bottom is the choice board: every level available, none imposed.

**THREE FORMATS — harvest, not invention.** Every subject is a lesson in three formats — AUDIO (the PowerCast player as one of the frames), VIDEO (frame harvested from the collective agreement), TEXT (frame harvested). "We already have it built. We're just going to take what we have and put it there." Nothing new is built for the formats; the ready pieces are taken and put there.

**SMART ACCESS BEHIND A PAYWALL — the business.** Membership is not just membership — it is smart access behind a paywall. With Naya Max, a citizen builds products behind a paywall with the portal entrance — offer text, audio, video, all formatted beautifully; courses and lessons from simple building blocks; it looks like a royal kingdom because it's all framed at a high level.

**STRUCTURE — hub and branches.** The course is the beginning hub; the lessons are the branches off the hub; the content is the formats of those branches (audio, video, text). Plus the one extra board: a button board with the table of contents to the different areas of the course.

## Why It Matters

Membership is the paid door — and this design says what the door opens onto. One design for both screens means no citizen is ever on the wrong screen. And smart access behind a paywall is the business itself: citizens building products from simple blocks, framed like a royal kingdom. The structure — hub, branches, formats — is the map the whole Academy and membership system hangs on.
