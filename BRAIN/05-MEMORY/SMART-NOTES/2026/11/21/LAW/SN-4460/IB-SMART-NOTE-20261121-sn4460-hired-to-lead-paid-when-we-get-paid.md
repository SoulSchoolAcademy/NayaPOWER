# Hired to Lead, Paid When We Get Paid

SN-4460 · LAW / THE AMBASSADOR AND THE RAIL

**Created:** 2026-11-21 08:59Z

**Kinds:** Honor · Multiplication · Craft · Knowledge

## Hook

"This is not a network marketing. You're an ambassador." And the pay is one sentence: "You get paid when we get paid." Nine days trial, paid day ten — and if people join every day, you get paid every day.

## Nutshell

The four actions under a post each light up in their own colour — Love red, Like purple, Comment blue, Share green — jewel-like and beautiful, never white, never simple. Love used to have red around it, now white — wrong; white is not a colour here, it is the absence of the light, and a button that should be lit and shows white is a button that has gone out. The zero cut off at the bottom is the same failure at a smaller scale: a count that cannot be read is a count that was never drawn. The sidebar is the door to the whole network, and tapping it did nothing — no close-first needed, the tap itself is dead. Messenger and the People rail are the same thing, one rail, not two; two names for one rail is the same failure in language, so the citizen looks for two things and finds neither. One rail, one name, one tap that opens. Every page gets its own Smart List area — Personal, Business, Your World, and Calendar too — because the lists ARE the organization of their vault: saved smart notes, Naya notes, connections, images, anything, organized and reviewable from the feed, per page. "Favorite" becomes "Pin it" — pin to the top of the feed, or to a space, a business, a profile, a list, anywhere in Naya. Save means to your Smart List / Smart Vault, and save lands straight in, no big list popping up first. "Pinned worlds" was the assistant's invention — he never said it; it is corrected out. The Intelligent Library becomes the laws, constitutions, and rules — a search engine over all the frames. An icon is a promise that the thing behind it is there: the AI Score icon points at alscore.nayapower.workers.dev, the Academy icon at academy.nayanet.app — the old app.nayanet.technology answers nothing, and nothing may point at it again. A citizen tapping an icon and getting nothing does not think "the address changed" — they think the feature is broken, and they stop tapping. After Hub and Personal come About and Skills on the four board formats, then membership — and paywalls, not just memberships, so a citizen can charge for their information, presented beautifully. Courses get a cover like a personal page, then frame-by-frame lessons, the citizen choosing combined or three styles for audio, visual, and lesson. The visuals must react to the audio, not just play alongside it — the picture moves because the sound moved, the light rises because the voice rose; mute the audio and the visuals should go still. And Naya talks to them one on one on video, spelling it out exactly: "This is exactly what to do. Follow these steps and you will see success and everybody wins." Script 1 is written — the full four-day plan in her voice, warm, first person, child-clear — voicing and production await his word. The plan itself is approved: "I think that sounds really good." Four days of Fast Action — show it, share it everywhere, invite your world personally, teach one person and get paid — then five days in the Academy. The frame for the whole plan, in his words: "Imagine we're hiring you to be the leader for NayaNET." And the ambassador line, verbatim, so no ambassador ever has to explain it twice: "This is not a network marketing. You're an ambassador. You're just like getting a sales job with a high tech company. You just commission sales. If you sell you get paid. People join, you get paid. If they don't, you don't get paid. But you get to enjoy NayaNET — it's your brain forever, you get to use it forever." The pay timing is settled in one sentence: "You get paid when we get paid. So the trial is nine days. You get paid day 10. If PayPal delays for whatever reason, which sometimes they do, then you just get paid when PayPal pays us. But in most cases, you'll get paid on the 10th day." No monthly cycle, no two-week cycle — the payday is the day we are paid.

Across 10 notes, the thread is: one rail, one list per page, one honest pay promise — hired to lead, paid when we get paid.

## Why it matters

1. Without the four colours, the citizen's hands on a post blur into one gesture — and love showing white where it used to show red reads as broken, because it is.
2. Without the tap that opens, the sidebar is a picture of a door, not a door — and a dead tap locks the citizen out of every room in the network.
3. Without a list per page, a save has nowhere to live — the same failure as Smart Inviter, one level up — and the citizen who saved something can never find it again.
4. Without the icon pointing at the live thing, the citizen learns that tapping leads nowhere — and an untrusted map is a map that stops being used.
5. Without the order — About and Skills before membership — the membership sells a system the pages have not yet explained, and the citizen is asked to join what they have not seen.
6. Without visuals that react, the walkthrough's video and audio feel like two files started together — and the citizen feels the gap even if they cannot name it.
7. Without the one-on-one video, the Fast Action Plan is a document speaking to everyone — and a person who feels lectured to closes the tab instead of following the steps.
8. Without the ambassador line in his words, the first question anyone asks — is this network marketing? — hardens into an objection, and ambassadors are left sounding like they are selling a scheme.
9. Without the day-ten timing, the citizen who shares every day does not know the money follows the same rhythm — and the sharing stops.
10. Without "your brain forever," the pay promise stands alone — and a citizen who has not yet sold anything has nothing, instead of a brain that is already theirs.
