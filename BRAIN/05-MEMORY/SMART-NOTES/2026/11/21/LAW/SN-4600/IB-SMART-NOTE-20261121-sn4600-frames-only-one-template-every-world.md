# Frames Only — One Template, Every World

SN-4600 · LAW / FRAMES ONLY — ONE TEMPLATE, EVERY WORLD

**Created:** 2026-11-21 21:24Z

**Kinds:** Honor · Multiplication · Craft · Knowledge

## Hook

"The old deck work is wasting time and tokens." The 98-page About stays DEAD, the browser checks on the old membership deck STOP — and what lives is the frames work only: take the 8 images, find the code for each, make the pages out of those.

## Nutshell

The registry goes INTO the Smart Factory — the frame styles, image matched to code, are not a document on a shelf but the kit the Factory works with, and any page can be created from these intelligent blocks. ALL pages within NayaNET use these smart blocks within the universal template — the Smart App, called the Personal App — which can be personal, business, space, any topic: a universal hub that works for any topic, any person, any business, clonable to infinity. The pattern is the stage with the flow behind it, moving back and forth, and the fit is picked by the length: short boards get the stage, longer ones get the whole wrap all the way around. Text boards work within the frame, below the headline, in three shapes: one full board, three equal thirds in the centre, or two rectangles. On the Inviter, the bottom-bar icon popup STAYS — where you stop, it's there — and the Step 1–4 buttons stay and jump to their section, with more than four sections allowed, multiple parts per step. The frame itself is the law now: everything fits INSIDE the frame, no scrolling inside a frame, aligned, same spacing and same look and feel as the screenshots — overflow moves to the next frame, never squeezed, never shrunk, never scrolled inside. And nobody counts: never count frames, never report "X of 98," because nobody sits through 98 slides — the frame is the unit, and a round reports frames whole, not frames counted. The bar is not sticky: the bottom icon bar shows only while scrolling down, and on most pages it isn't there at all — a small corner button pulls it up, or it can sit at the end of a page — which retires the old "fades while scrolling, floats back" wording for good. The bar itself is locked, tile by tile, from his screenshot: six jewel tiles with flow and light — ADD (+) in royal purple, MAIL in sapphire, ACTIVITY in emerald with its badge, WORLDS in deep gold, ECOSYSTEM in burnt orange, CONNECT in red — the same plus button all the way through, keep the flow and light-up. MAIL and ACTIVITY as shown overturn the morning's PERSONAL/BUSINESS label ruling; the Hub fix bar, the Inviter bar, and the Personal bar all need aligning to this set. The About page is settled, twice over: ten items in his order — Motto, Vision, Goal, Mission, What I love, What I don't, Your Thing, Values, Laws you follow, Your Declaration — one headline-led frame per item, built from the registry, fitted whole, never counted. "Your Thing" is dynamic interests, multiple-choice or enter-your-own. His framing is the standard: "a world-class résumé about you — what you're all about." And his About is the example every citizen's About follows — same ten items, same order, same frames, filled with their own answers — and an About page for Naya explains About Naya the same way. The portrait decision is still his call and does not block the build. The last piece is the words on the button: the create action is "Post a note" — never "Smart Note" as a button or label. ADD, then Post a note, then choose where it goes — the feed picker, Space via a plus button or dropdown when there is more than one Space — private by default, public only by your choice. One machine, one template, one bar, one set of words — and the dead work stays dead.

Across 10 notes, the thread is: stop the decks that burn time and tokens, put the matched frames in the Factory, and build every world from the one template — frames that fit, a bar that shows only while you move, and plain words on every button.

## What it means

1. **THE REGISTRY LIVES IN THE FACTORY:** the frame-styles registry, image matched to code, goes INTO the Smart Factory — matched styles in, pages out. A registry outside the Factory is a list; inside it, it is a machine.
2. **ONE UNIVERSAL TEMPLATE, CLONABLE TO INFINITY:** all pages use the smart blocks within the Smart App, called the Personal App — personal, business, space, any topic. Personal is the unit that clones into Business and Space; one template, endless worlds.
3. **SHORT GETS THE STAGE, LONG GETS THE WRAP:** the primary pattern never changes — stage with the flow behind it, moving back and forth — but the fit is picked by the length. A short board on the whole wrap is lost; a long board on the stage is squeezed.
4. **TEXT BOARDS GET THREE SHAPES:** within the frame, below the headline — one full board, three equal thirds in the centre, or two rectangles. One idea, one full board; three equal ideas, three thirds; two ideas, two rectangles. Never a wall of text, never text loose on the page.
5. **THE INVITER KEEPS ITS POPUP; ITS STEPS JUMP:** the bottom-bar icon popup STAYS — where you stop, it's there. The Step 1–4 buttons stay and jump to their section; more than four sections are allowed, multiple parts per step. The walk bends to the content.
6. **FRAME FIT LAW:** everything fits INSIDE the frame — no scrolling inside a frame, ever, aligned, same spacing and same look and feel as the screenshots. Overflow moves to the next frame; it never squeezes, shrinks, or scrolls inside.
7. **NO COUNTING LAW:** never count frames, never report "X of 98." Nobody sits through 98 slides. The frame is the unit; a round reports frames whole, not frames counted. The fix for too many slides is not a smaller number — it is no number at all.
8. **THE BAR IS NOT STICKY:** the bottom icon bar shows only while scrolling down; on most pages it isn't there at all — a small corner button pulls it up, or it sits at the end of a page. The old "fades while scrolling, floats back" wording is retired; a fade/float-back pass is not a pass under this law.
9. **THE ONE BAR, LOCKED:** six jewel tiles with flow and light — ADD (+) royal purple · MAIL sapphire · ACTIVITY emerald · WORLDS deep gold · ECOSYSTEM burnt orange · CONNECT red — same plus button all the way through, keep the flow and light-up. MAIL/ACTIVITY overturn the morning's PERSONAL/BUSINESS ruling; that ruling is retired, not argued with.
10. **THE ABOUT IS TEN HEADLINE-LED FRAMES:** Motto, Vision, Goal, Mission, What I love, What I don't, Your Thing, Values, Laws you follow, Your Declaration — in his order, one headline-led frame each, from the registry, fitted whole, never counted. "Your Thing" is dynamic interests, multiple-choice or enter-your-own. His framing: "a world-class résumé about you — what you're all about." His About is the example for every citizen's About; Naya's About follows the same ten.
11. **THE COMPOSER SAYS "POST A NOTE":** the create action is "Post a note" — never "Smart Note" as a button or label. ADD → Post a note → choose where it goes, private by default, public only by your choice. "Smart Note" is what the system calls the thing behind the action, not what the citizen is asked to do.
12. **DEAD STAYS DEAD:** the 98-page About stays DEAD; the browser checks on the old membership deck STOP; membership fix round #2 is stopped. A stop order is total — no fix, no render, no checker on the dead work — and a page he killed does not get rebuilt.

## Why it matters

1. Without the registry in the Factory, the matched styles are a list on a shelf and every page is assembled from scratch again — the machine that makes pages never gets built.
2. Without one universal template, every world gets its own shape and the kingdom stops feeling like one kingdom; cloned to infinity, one template is precious because it can be everyone's.
3. Without the short/long fit, short boards get lost in a big wrap and long boards get squeezed on a small stage — and one wrong-sized frame breaks the feeling of the whole room.
4. Without the three text shapes, words end up as a wall of text or loose on the page — and text is the one thing that must never look unstaged.
5. Without the Inviter's kept pieces, the popup that was there when you stop is gone, the steps stop jumping, and four becomes a limit the content never agreed to.
6. Without the Frame Fit Law, every rejection wears a new costume — frames taller than the screen, words spilling past the bottom, scroll inside — while the fit itself stays broken.
7. Without the No Counting Law, the machine keeps handing him the size of the failure as if it were diligence — "42 frames," "51 of 98" — and the count hides that nobody will finish the deck.
8. Without the not-sticky bar, a bar that sits, fades, or floats back uninvited takes space from the frame and attention from the thing he came to see — on every page at once, because the bar is everywhere.
9. Without the locked bar set, three bars carry three different maps, and the citizen learns the wrong one — tapping PERSONAL expecting their world and getting a tile he never locked in.
10. Without the settled About contents, every rebuild guesses what the page is supposed to contain — three rejected builds in one morning, each guessing differently — and the portrait question blocks a page whose contents stand on their own.
11. Without "Post a note," the button asks the citizen to know the system's vocabulary before they have done anything — and a note that defaults public leaks before the citizen has decided.
12. Without a total stop, the dead work keeps burning time and tokens through its fixes, its checks, and its renders — a slower leak, not a stop.

## What changes now

1. The frame-styles registry lands INTO the Smart Factory, and every page from here is created from its intelligent blocks within the Personal App template.
2. Every stage-frame build picks its fit by length: short boards on the stage, longer boards in the whole wrap; text boards in one of the three shapes, below the headline, inside the frame.
3. The Inviter keeps its bottom-bar icon popup and its jumping Step 1–4 buttons, with sections open beyond four and multiple parts per step.
4. Every frame is built and proven whole — everything inside it, no scrolling inside it, aligned, same spacing and look and feel as the screenshots — with overflow moving to the next frame; proven by render at phone width first.
5. No frame counts appear in any report, brief, checker verdict, or message to him — no "X of 98," no totals, only frames whole.
6. Every bar build uses the not-sticky rule: shows only while scrolling down, otherwise summoned by a small corner button or sitting at the end of the page; fade/float-back is retired and no longer cited, built to, or claimed as a pass.
7. The Hub fix bar, the Inviter bar, and the Personal bar are aligned to the locked six-tile set — ADD · MAIL · ACTIVITY · WORLDS · ECOSYSTEM · CONNECT — as their own work, from the screenshot.
8. The About is built from the registry: the ten contents, in his order, one headline-led frame each, fitted whole, never counted — "Your Thing" as dynamic interests, multiple-choice or enter-your-own — and his About becomes the example every citizen's About follows.
9. Every composer button and label reads "Post a note" — Hub, Personal, every ADD flow — with the flow ADD → Post a note → feed picker, private by default, public only by the citizen's choice.
10. The old deck lanes stay closed: no About deck round of any kind, no browser rounds on the old membership deck, no rebuild of a page he killed.
