## The Lesson

**FRAME FIT LAW (his law, 2026-11-21 ~11:24 MST):**

**EVERYTHING FITS INSIDE THE FRAME.** The frame is the whole thing: the content sits inside it, all of it, nothing spilling past the bottom, nothing cut off at the edge. A frame that needs scrolling inside it is not a frame — it is a window onto something bigger, and the bigger thing is what he sees instead of the frame. "It needs to fit like a glove" was the About verdict; this law is the same verdict made universal: every frame, on every page, fits its content whole.

**NO SCROLLING INSIDE A FRAME.** This is the hard edge of the law. Inner scroll is never the fix for content that does not fit. The About rounds kept shipping frames that scrolled inside — beats taller than the screen, words spilling past the bottom — and each time the fix was more scroll, or a split, or a smaller count. The law ends that: if it scrolls inside, it does not fit, and it does not ship. The checker measures it and the eye sees it; either way, inner scroll is a fail.

**OVERFLOW MOVES TO THE NEXT FRAME.** When the content is too much for one frame, it does not squeeze, shrink, or scroll — it moves to the next frame. That is the only overflow rule. It is also what makes the No Counting Law (SN-4520) workable: frames multiply by overflow, and nobody counts them, because the count was never the point. The frame stays whole; the content flows on.

**ALIGNED, SAME SPACING, SAME LOOK AND FEEL AS THE SCREENSHOTS.** Fit is not only size — it is alignment, spacing, and the look and feel of his 8 frame screenshots. A frame that fits but sits crooked, or spaced differently from its neighbours, or looking like a different family, is not fitted. The screenshots are the reference set; the registry matches each image to its code (SN-4515, SN-4516) so the fit is reproducible, not guessed.

## Why It Matters

The About was rejected three times in one morning, and every rejection was a fit failure wearing a different costume: frames taller than the screen, words spilling past the bottom, scroll inside, density double the agreement's. Each round fixed the costume and kept the fit broken. The Frame Fit Law names the fit itself as the standard, for every page, so no round can pass a frame that scrolls inside it — and no round can call a split or a count a fix. A frame is whole or it is not; whole means everything inside it, aligned, same spacing, same look and feel as the screenshots. That is the bar the registry builds to, and the bar his eye holds.

## How to Use It

Build every frame so its content fits inside it whole — no scrolling inside a frame, ever. Align it, space it the same as its neighbours, match the look and feel of his screenshots. When content overflows, move it to the next frame — never squeeze it, never shrink it, never scroll it inside. Prove fit by render at phone width first: the frame whole, nothing cut, nothing scrolling inside it.
