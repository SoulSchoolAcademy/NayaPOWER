## Why It Matters

The input experience is the whole welcome. Boxes, dictation, skip — that is push-button simple in its truest form: nobody is stuck on a question they cannot answer, nobody has to type if they would rather speak, nobody's words get rewritten by the machine. And "whatever they input is what's going to show" is the trust at the center of it: the page is theirs because the words are theirs, frame by frame. The main-page version is the same trust at full speed: ask Naya, paste it in, or say it yourself — enter — and it is live on the intelligent board, showcased for the world. That is how a citizen goes from nothing to a live, showcased page in one breath.

## How to Use It

Build Skills input as boxes, one per question, with dictation on each and skip on each — same experience as the join walkthrough, just framed. Show whatever they input, frame by frame, unedited. On the main page, offer the same three ways in: ask Naya to write it and copy-paste it here, write it yourself, or dictate yourself — click enter and it goes live on the intelligent board, showcased. Never gate an answer behind a required question; never rewrite what they input before it shows.
