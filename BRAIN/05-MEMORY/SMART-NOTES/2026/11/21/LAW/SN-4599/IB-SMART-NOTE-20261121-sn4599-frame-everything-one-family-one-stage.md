## What it means

1. **FRAME EVERYTHING — STAGE THE ROOM:** every stat in a frame, every app in a frame, every intelligent block in a frame, framed in elegance, staged like a room, everything precious and beautiful. All pages operate within the stage/frame concept, including the Dashboard.
2. **THE FLOWING STAGE IS PRIMARY:** of all the frame styles, the stage with the flow behind it, moving back and forth, is the one to use primarily — you never leave the room, the room moves with you. The other styles are variants, not rivals.
3. **MATCH EVERY STYLE TO ITS CODE, KEPT ON FILE:** look at each of his eight frame-style screenshots, find the code for each one, match image to code, kept on file — so any page style (video, image, text-only) can be created at will with the same look and feel. Unmatched gets flagged, never invented.
4. **SWAP INSIDE ONLY — NEVER START A NEW PAGE:** take the agreement slides (or the calendar ones), you already have the frame, change ONLY the inside — the text, the video, the button label. No creating, no redesign. One family — all the pages must match.
5. **A REJECTED BUILD GETS THROWN OUT, NOT FIXED:** "Don't fix it just throw it out." The 98-frame look-match, the 42-frame split, the rebuild lane — dead, never touched or reused. Fixing a rejected build keeps its mistakes alive.
6. **THE STRUCTURE IS THE LOOK — KEEP THE STRUCTURE:** a right diagnosis does not make a wrong fix right. Splitting dense beats into more frames kept the dense look and made the deck longer. Take the structure that already looks right — the agreement's own structure, ~37 beats, one idea per frame, ~30–45 words, never 98 — and rebuild on it.
7. **HIS EYE IS THE VERDICT:** a checker's byte-PASS does not matter when his eye says the look is wrong on his own screen. His eye gates it before anything is called done.
8. **THE VAULT IS THE CENTER OF THE MAP:** the Smart Vault lives in Supabase — all intelligence is input into it and used from it, every citizen with their own Vault. Every piece must map to a block, a flow, the Vault or the Ledger; a piece serving nothing is the DROP case.

## Why it matters

1. Without the universal frame, every page picks a different frame and the family look breaks — a system of screens instead of one kingdom, every room staged.
2. Without the primary named, the team drifts between styles; naming the flowing stage stops the drift with one sentence: this one, first, always unless there's a reason.
3. Without the image+code registry, every new page starts from a screenshot and a guess; with it, every new page starts from the kit — the difference between a style guide and a factory.
4. Without swap-inside-only, every new page is a new chance to drift, and a new build is a morning lost — the frame that already works is the page.
5. Without throwing rejected builds out, the redo cycle wears a new name — each iteration costs a morning and keeps the rejected structure alive.
6. Without keeping the structure, a right diagnosis produces a wrong fix, a byte-PASS blesses it, and his eye kills it on screen — the 98-frame morning, repeated.
7. Without his eye as the verdict, a PASS that his eye overrules is called a pass, and the page he called "a complete mess" ships.
8. Without the Vault at the center, the map is just a bigger list — and a bigger list is the opposite of the goal: the system must get smaller where it should be smaller and stronger where it should be stronger.
9. Without the verdicts, dead weight drags the whole flow — every extra piece is something to maintain, something to break, something slowing the water — and the release never reaches MAX PERFORMANCE.
10. Without the sequence, the machine works on everything at once and finishes nothing — three rounds in flight with no step declared done; with it, each round knows what it feeds and when it is done.
11. Without checking the keys first, a round stops for a permission it already holds and wastes the one thing the machine cannot get back: his attention, and the hours while the work sits.
