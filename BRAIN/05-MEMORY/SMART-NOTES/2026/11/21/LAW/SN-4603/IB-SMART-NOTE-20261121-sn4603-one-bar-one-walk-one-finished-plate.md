## Why it matters

1. Without the same bar everywhere, a citizen who learns to reach people on one hub finds them unreachable on the next — and the network's whole point, connecting with like minds, breaks at the hub boundary.
2. Without working buttons, a bar is a promise the page breaks the moment a citizen trusts it — each dead button teaches them not to tap, and a drawn bar is not a finished bar.
3. Without the sequence, the bars drift apart again — Personal working and Hub different is two bars, not one, and the citizen meets a different network on every page.
4. Without the final colours filed, two purples read as one colour at a glance, and the superseded "#8e24aa stays" keeps getting built because it is written down and the correction was only spoken.
5. Without the locked walkthrough, every page starts blank and hard — the citizen stares at an empty frame and does not know what to put in it — instead of fifteen questions in their own words and a filled page.
6. Without the optional showcase, the profile tells what a person can do but never what they have done — and a portal to their whole world should show their accomplishments, in their own words, with the proof they choose.
7. Without blank staying hidden, skipped sections show as empty frames — and nobody's portal should have an empty frame in it.
8. Without the tall frame, vertical video made on a phone gets squeezed into a wide frame and cropped — the frame must serve the person's work, not force their work into the frame's shape.
9. Without the diagnostic habit, a working renderer gets rebuilt because it looked broken — a second renderer for a card that was fine, while the real cause, a missing mount, stays unfixed.
10. Without the proof habit, a live page gets called a working flow — and the citizen who taps the Inviter and gets nothing learns the network does not work.
11. Without the hold, the rename lands on the wrong button on the wrong bar — and Your Worlds, the lists table of contents, gets renamed away by accident.
12. Without the Silver Platter Rule, status buries what is ready under what was already checked three times, and half-done plates make him do the finishing himself — his attention is the scarcest thing in the machine.
