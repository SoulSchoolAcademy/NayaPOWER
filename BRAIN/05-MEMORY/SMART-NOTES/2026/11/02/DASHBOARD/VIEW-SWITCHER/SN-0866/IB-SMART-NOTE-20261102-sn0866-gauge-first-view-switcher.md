# The Gauge Is the Showcase — Every View Starts There
SN-0866 · DASHBOARD / VIEW-SWITCHER
**Created:** 2026-11-02T21:45:55Z

## Hook
The gauge is the showcase piece, the default view, on every metric. Bars, donut, big number — the top views, all built as real 3D objects, all alive — are one click away. And every stat area speaks the same format: gauge plus orbs, same time pills, same switcher.

## What happened
With the drill-downs proving the pattern (the Sales drill-down as the proof), Shawn set the view rule: citizens can flip any metric between views — gauge, bars, pie, comparison — but the gauge is always home. Not twelve views: the top ten, the best of the best, the ones that make sense. The gauge adapts to the metric — percentage, points, 1-to-10 — always showing the optimal stat.

## The rule, in his words
"We have our showcase piece which is our gauge, right?... they can just click through the different views and see it from different perspectives... you just put the top 10 in there, right? The best of the best, the ones that will make sense and that's doable, that's logical... we'll adjust our gauge to giving the optimal stat."

## Why it matters
Choice without a home is clutter. The gauge-first rule gives every metric one identity (the gauge) plus controlled exploration (the switcher). A citizen always knows where they are; they choose how deep to look. And "top 10, not all 12" is the distillation law in action — exactly what's needed, nothing more.

## How it connects
The universal format — gauge plus orbs, same time pills, same view switcher on all 9 Smart Link metrics (Impressions, Clicks, CTR, Conversions, Conv Rate, Revenue, EPC, AOV, Rev/1k Views) — is what makes the dashboard one system instead of nine pages. This note is the view law; the one-machine note (SN-0862) is the object law; the drill-down order (SN-0864) is the depth law. Together they are the dashboard's constitution.
