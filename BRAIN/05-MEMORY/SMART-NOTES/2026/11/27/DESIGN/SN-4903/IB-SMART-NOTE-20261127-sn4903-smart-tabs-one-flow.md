# Smart Tabs — One Flow, Flowing Icons, Always a Flowing S

## Nutshell

Shawn's correction, voice call 2026-11-27 ~15:28 MST: the Smart Tabs in the Hub are all different colours, not following a specific flow, and not the nicest colours. The buttons could look better and the colours could light up much more elegantly. His direction: put icons that are FLOWING in those buttons, for each of them — so there is always a flowing S, giving them a lot more life. One flow, elegant colours lighting up, flowing icons.

## What It's Not

This is not a recolour of the tabs, and it is not one more colour per tab. A row of a hundred different-coloured buttons with no flow between them is the problem, not the starting point.

## Child

Imagine a river with a hundred little boats, each painted a different colour, all drifting in different directions. Now imagine the same boats all sailing together down one river, their sails catching the light. That is what the tabs should feel like — one river, not a hundred puddles.

## Grandma

You know how Christmas lights look when they are all tangled up in a box, every colour shouting at once? And then you hang them in one line along the porch, and suddenly they are beautiful? The tabs are the tangled box right now. They need to be hung in one line.

## What It Means

The Smart Tabs are the Auto Organizer's face — the tabs are how the Organizer shows a citizen their organized world. So the tabs must behave like one system: one elegant flow running through them, each button carrying a flowing icon, the colours lighting up in their own colour, always a flowing S giving the row life. This rides with the standing feed law: buttons with icons have a circle with flowing energy around them. On his screen the row today reads COLLECTIVE gold, PERSONAL blue, and tabs cut off mid-word — "ctivation", "Direction", "Desig…" — over a hundred tabs, cut at both edges at phone width.

## Why It Matters

Because the tabs are the first thing a citizen touches to find their world. If the tabs feel random, the whole system feels random — even when the organizing underneath is right. One flow tells the citizen, before a word is read, that this is one system that knows where everything is.

## How It Connects

This is the named first item of the Alignment Order (SN-4902), for the Hub's next round — not folded into the running R24 round mid-flight. It is proved the same way as everything else in the program: staged, phone width first, 9.5+ and the design-language check, his eye the final gate.

## How to Apply

Rebuild the tab row as one flow: each tab an obsidian object with its flowing icon, its own colour lighting only its rim, the whole row reading as one river. Make every tab whole at phone width — nothing cut mid-word. If a tab cannot be whole on the phone, the row is not done.

## What's In It For You

Tabs that feel alive the moment you look at them — one flow, colours lighting up elegantly, the S always flowing — so finding your world feels like gliding, not hunting.
