# Hub R24 — His Eye Goes Back

SN-4905 · DESIGN

**Created:** 2026-11-27T22:45:00Z
**Category:** Design
**SN:** SN-4905

**Kinds:** Knowledge · Craft · Honor

## Nutshell

Hub R23's checker passed 9.5 — and Shawn's eye sent it back. His eye is ground truth over the checker. On his screen: no main left sidebar at all; the points pill cut off at the top; hovering Collective goes full gold; the right sidebar missing completely; and inside the already-full-screen calendar, a button that says "open full screen". R24 is the fix round, built on the R23 staged candidate byte for byte.

## What It's Not

This is not a disagreement about taste. The checker proved the build at 390 and 1440 with zero page errors — and his eye still saw a sidebar missing and points cut off. A checker pass is a milestone inside the machine, never the verdict.

## Child

Imagine you build a puppet theatre and the checker says every string is tied right — and then the person watching says, I cannot see the stage. Both are telling the truth. The strings can be right and the stage still be hidden. R23 was that theatre.

## Grandma

You know how a dress can measure perfectly on the table and still not fit the person wearing it? The checker measured the dress. His eye is the person wearing it. R23 measured fine and did not fit.

## What It Means

His six findings, in his words as near as they were spoken: (1) the main sidebar that was always there is all of a sudden missing — the buttons are in the right order and colours, but the entire main left sidebar is gone; off-canvas behind a hamburger is not acceptable, it must be visible at rest, phone width first. (2) The points pill is sliced at the top edge — it must be whole, not cut off, at both widths. (3) When you hover over Collective it goes full gold and it shouldn't — just around the edge; hover is a gold rim only, the middle unchanged. (4) The right sidebar is missing completely, and the right sidebar and the DM bar are merged into each other — left and right are separate things, never merged; the right sidebar must actually be present and separate from the DM bar. (5) The hamburger menu order must be: Open people rail at the top, then Left sidebar, then Right sidebar, then Neither — so the citizen can have full screen, or either side on its own. (6) Inside the already-full-screen calendar there is a thing that says "open full screen" — you don't need a double open full screen; remove it. One thing he corrected himself on: the calendar does go full screen now — that part is right, and it is out of scope for the fix.

## Why It Matters

Because the Hub is the room where everything happens, and the sidebar is how a citizen finds their world. A Hub without its sidebar is a house without its doors. And because the gap between a checker pass and his eye is the exact gap the Alignment Order (SN-4902) exists to close: nothing reaches him until it passes his eye, on his screen.

## How It Connects

R24 is in flight on the R23 staged candidate as its base, staged only, one hour max, maker then an independent checker with real taps at true 390 and 1440, pass needs 9.5+, then the captain's eye walk, then his eye, live only on his tap. The Smart Tabs correction (SN-4903) is carried as the Hub's next round, not folded into R24. The hourly sync would deploy the restored template live by itself on its next run unless paused first — the sync-pause question still needs his word.

## How to Apply

When a round's checker passes and his eye goes back, the eye findings become the next round's scope, word for word, and the checker of that round must prove each one on his terms — visible at rest, whole, rim only, separate, in that order — not on the checker's terms.

## What's In It For You

A Hub where the sidebar is there when you open it, the points are whole, the hover is a rim and not a flood, and either side opens on its own — the room as it was always meant to be.
