## Human

Think of a family that all speaks the same language at home. Anyone can tell they belong together, because the words are the same, the manners are the same, the care is the same — even though every person is their own person. That is what the pages have to be. Right now they are a family that each speaks a different dialect: you can tell they are related, but you have to learn each one all over again. The Alignment Order is the family agreeing, once, to speak the same language everywhere — and the Vault is the family book on the kitchen table, where everything decided is written down, so nobody has to guess and nobody has to ask the same question twice.

## Child

Imagine you and your friends all build treehouses, but every treehouse has its door in a different place, its ladder a different colour, and its roof a different shape. You can find your way around your own treehouse, but you get lost in everyone else's. Now imagine you all agree: every door in the same place, every ladder the same strong wood, every roof the same shape — but each treehouse still has its own special flag on top. That is the alignment. And the Vault is the big notebook where you write down how to build a treehouse, so the next friend who joins does not have to guess.

1. **THE ANATOMY IS THE LAW:** obsidian black inside, the white in the middle a real object sitting on the black, the light outside only in the object's own colour, one light top-left, touch that presses in and lifts. If it does not look touchable, it does not ship. If it does not hold at phone width first, it does not ship.
2. **THE WALK IS THE EVIDENCE:** six pages walked whole, phone width first — portal, Innovations, the 12 Elements, Grand Mission, pricing, live Hub, live Dashboard — share a mood, not a language. The portal is the closest physical reference; the Hub and Dashboard fail the physical test outright; phone width is the weak point everywhere except pricing.
3. **THE ORDER IS THE PROGRAM:** every page brought to Naya design level, one round per page, one hour max, 9.5+ and the design-language check, staged only, live only on his tap. A director owns it to zero; the running Hub R24 round and the walkthrough plus About and Skills round are taken in, not duplicated.
4. **THE WALKTHROUGH RESTARTS FROM THE PORTAL:** round 4 is garbage — not a base, not a fallback, not evidence. The only official walkthrough is portal, then certificate, then iPhones, built as one project with the About and Skills pages it creates, in the Innovations format, in the one language.
5. **THE VAULT IS THE HABIT:** every seat brief names the Vault first. Maker and checker plug into the Smart Vault and read the current state before they touch a page, and file what they learn back. A round that starts without the Vault read is incomplete.

## Why it matters

1. Until the pages are aligned, every new build has to be corrected by hand, and Shawn has to explain the same thing over and over. Aligned pages mean a new Naya can build right the first time — and a citizen can move from page to page and always know where they are, because it is all one world.
2. A checker pass is a milestone inside the machine, never the verdict. R23 passed 9.5 and his eye still sent it back — sidebar missing, points cut, hover flooding gold — because the checker measured the dress and his eye is the person wearing it.

4. Alignment is not a meeting — it is a habit. Skip the Vault once, and the drift starts again; use it every time, and the whole collective stays in step.
