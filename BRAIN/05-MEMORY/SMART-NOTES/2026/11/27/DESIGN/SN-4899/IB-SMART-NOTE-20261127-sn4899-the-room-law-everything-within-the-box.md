# The Room Law — Everything Fits Within the Box

SN-4899 · DESIGN

**Created:** 2026-11-27T16:58:00Z
**Category:** Design
**SN:** SN-4899

## Nutshell

Everything fits within the box area. Get rid of the lines on the outside, that box thing on the outside — the full page stays visible. A bar, a panel, or a widget that eats the page is not a bar; whatever form it takes, it fits inside the box.

## Child

Imagine a picture frame, and everything in the picture stays inside the frame — nothing spills over the edges, nothing covers the picture up. If a sticker is so big you cannot see the picture, the sticker goes.

## Grandma

Dear, everything belongs inside the box, neat and tidy. Nothing hanging outside, nothing so big it hides the rest. You should see the whole page, all at once — that is how you know it is right.

## What It Means

Shawn's standing order, restated on the voice call 2026-11-27 ~09:0x MST, on the live Dashboard: the right sidebar is missing again, and the Power Memory Bar is huge — it makes it so you don't see the full page. Get rid of those lines on the outside, that box thing — everything fits within the box area. The bar he ordered is not a huge bar that eats the page; whatever form it takes, it fits inside the box area and the full Dashboard stays visible. Why it matters: a page you cannot see whole is a page you cannot use — the frame exists to hold the work, not to hide it.

## How to Apply

How it connects: the Room Law is the One-Build Law applied to layout — the frame holds, the content fits inside it — and the sense check on the whole page: the user sees the page, not the diff. A round that ships a bar outside the box fails the eye walk, whatever its counts say.

## What's In It For You

You always see the full page — nothing cut off, nothing hidden behind a bar that grew too big. Everything in its box, everything visible.
