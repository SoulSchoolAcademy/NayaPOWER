## Nutshell

Shawn's law, voice call 2026-11-27 ~15:32 MST: we must be consistently USING the Vault, just like all the users — everything goes into the Vault. The collective HAS a vault — our system — and WE use it. That is how we stay aligned. ## What It's Not

The Vault is not a filing cabinet we visit when we remember, and it is not a place for summaries after the work is done. A Naya who starts work without reading the Vault is working blind — and working blind is how new pages get created wrong.

## Child

Imagine a team of builders, and each one starts the day by opening the team's big book — where everything is, what was decided, what went wrong last time. The builder who skips the book builds the wrong wall. The builder who reads it builds the right one. The Vault is that big book, and it is alive.

## Grandma

You know how a family keeps its recipes in one box, and every cook checks the box before they start, and writes down anything new they learn? That is how the food stays good for generations. The Vault is our recipe box — and every Naya is a cook in the same kitchen.

## What It Means

The reason new pages get created wrong and things get done wrong is that we go off not knowing what is going on — because we are not plugging into the Vault, into the system, so we are aware of what is going on. So the standing rule from here: EVERY seat brief names the Vault first. Maker and checker plug into the Smart Vault and read the current state before they touch a page, and file what they learn back. A round that starts without the Vault read is incomplete. And the purpose is the Naya Node itself, in his words: Naya design and language and all of this must be in the Vault, because then a Naya just goes boom, goes to the Smart Vault, plugs in, gets ACTIVATED, becomes aware of where things are at, and boom — where we go. That is the idea about the Naya Node.

## Why It Matters

Because alignment is not a meeting — it is a habit. The Vault is the one place where every Naya can know what every other Naya knows. Use it every time, and the whole collective stays in step. Skip it once, and the drift starts again.

This law is the reason this whole filing exists: the design and language notes (SN-4900 to SN-4905) are the activation set — written so a fresh Naya reading them cold knows the standard, the current state, and the next step, with no prior conversation needed. Before any work: open the Vault, read the current notes on your page, and say what you found. After any work: file what you learned back as a smart note, distilled to its essence, in the standard format, so the next Naya starts where you finished. If you cannot find the note you need in the Vault, that is itself the finding — file it.
