# The Money Law — Joins Only

SN-4889 · VISION

**Created:** 2026-11-27T16:48:00Z
**Category:** Vision
**SN:** SN-4889

## Nutshell

Money equals joins only. On the Dashboard, the dollar sign appears only where money makes sense — joins, and only joins. Everything else is points, in every mode. Clicks are never money.

## Child

Imagine a scoreboard where points are stars and money is coins. You earn stars for lots of things — but coins only appear when someone actually joins because you shared. Clicks are never coins.

## Grandma

Dear, points are applause — they say you did well. Money is different: it only arrives when someone truly joins. We never mix the two, and we never call a click a coin.

## What It Means

The Money Law, Shawn 2026-11-15 02:40Z, grounded 2026-10-27: reward in money is indirect, never points-to-money — action leads to sharing, sharing leads to joins, joins are paid in the back end. Money words name three things — the amount, the thing, and the trigger: 'You receive 50% of the revenue when someone joins because you shared.' 'Earn half' names none of them. Why it matters: mixing points and money would let the system promise what it cannot prove. Keeping them apart keeps every dollar honest and every point meaningful.

## How to Apply

How it connects: the Dashboard shows dollars only on joins; every other count is points, and points are law as authority — more points, more post weight in the feeds. Any screen that puts a dollar sign on a click breaks this law.

## What's In It For You

You always know what a number means: points say how much you are valued, dollars say someone joined because of you. Nothing is dressed up as money when it is not.
