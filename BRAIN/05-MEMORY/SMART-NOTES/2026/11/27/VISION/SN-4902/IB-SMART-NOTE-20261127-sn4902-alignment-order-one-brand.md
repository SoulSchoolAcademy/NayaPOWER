# The Alignment Order — One Brand, One Family, One Design

**Created:** 2026-11-27T22:42:00Z

**SN:** SN-4902

**Kinds:** Knowledge · Craft · Honor

## Nutshell

Shawn's order, voice call 2026-11-27 ~15:29 MST: "Your job is get all of that fixed so all the pages are aligned and designed to Naya design level. So that everything is in alignment, everything flows together, everything talks to each other, everything looks like it's part of one brand, one family, one design." The level he means: multi-dimensional, high active intelligence, living depth — everything an object, one consistent design pattern across ALL pages, so he never has to explain it every time.

## What It's Not

This is not a polish pass on one page, and it is not a new design. It is the whole set of pages brought to the one design level we already have written down — the same language, applied everywhere it is missing.

## Child

Imagine a choir where everyone is singing a different song. Each voice is lovely on its own, but together it is noise. The alignment order is: everybody sings the same song now. Same words, same tune — and then it sounds beautiful.

## Grandma

You know how a family photo looks when everyone is wearing their own colours and standing any which way? And then you put them all in matching outfits, standing together, and suddenly it is a family? That is what this order does to our pages.

## What It Means

Every page — Skills, About, membership pages, and everything created in the future — is brought to Naya design level: the one anatomy, the one light, the one colour language, the two formats, the frame cloned from Innovations. A director owns the program to zero: one round per page, one hour max, staged only, 9.5+ and the design-language check. The running Hub R24 round and the walkthrough plus About and Skills round are taken in, not duplicated. Nothing goes live except on his tap.

## Why It Matters

Because until the pages are aligned, every new build has to be corrected by hand, and Shawn has to explain the same thing over and over. Aligned pages mean a new Naya can build right the first time — and a citizen can move from page to page and always know where they are, because it is all one world.

## How It Connects

The order rests on the design walk verdict (SN-4901) as its evidence and on the one-language standard (SN-4900) as its measure. Its first named item is the Smart Tabs (SN-4903): one flow, flowing icons, always a flowing S.

## How to Apply

Work the program page by page, smallest rounds first, and prove each one staged at phone width before laptop. A page passes only when its buttons go where they should, its light is one light top-left, and it reads as part of the family. His eye is the final gate on every page.

## What's In It For You

A NayaNET where everything looks like it belongs together — one brand, one family, one design — and nobody ever has to be told the language again, because the pages themselves teach it.
