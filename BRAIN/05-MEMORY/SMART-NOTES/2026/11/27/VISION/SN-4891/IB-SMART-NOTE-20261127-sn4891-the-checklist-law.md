# The Checklist Law — One List, Worked to Zero

SN-4891 · VISION

**Created:** 2026-11-27T16:50:00Z
**Category:** Vision
**SN:** SN-4891

## Nutshell

When he gives a list, the list is the unit of work. Every item goes onto one checklist, in his words, and nothing is called done until every item is proven on the page he sees. Small items ride together in one round. An item that cannot be done is named with its reason in the same message — never silence.

## Child

Imagine a shopping list. You do not come home with one bag and call the shopping done — you come home with everything on the list, or you say plainly what you could not get and why. That is the rule.

## Grandma

Dear, a list is a promise. You work it down to nothing, and you do not say it is finished while a single thing still sits on it. And if something truly cannot be done, you say so straight away — you never just go quiet.

## What It Means

The Checklist Law, Shawn's charge 2026-11-13, made permanent 2026-11-19: 'can you do that forever more?' Every page he hands over is one to-do list, worked to zero, proven on the page he sees. Sending back one or two items done is a failure, not progress — it makes him retell everything. A project comes to him whole or not at all. Why it matters: partial returns waste the scarcest resource there is — his attention — and force him to repeat what he already said.

## How to Apply

How it connects: the checklist is how the Protocol's 'prove it' step is kept honest, and how the Continuous Cycle Law is obeyed — nothing sits, and a finished round's next step goes out within fifteen minutes.

## What's In It For You

You never have to chase a half-finished job or retell a list. When work comes back to you, the whole list is done — or you are told in the same breath what is not, and why.
