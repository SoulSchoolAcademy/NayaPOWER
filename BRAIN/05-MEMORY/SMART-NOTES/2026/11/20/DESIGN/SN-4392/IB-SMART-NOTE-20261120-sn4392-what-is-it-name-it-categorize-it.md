## Why It Matters

A citizen who asks "what is CCT?" wants one answer, in one frame, in words a child understands — not a hunt through the constitution. Categorizing the answers is what makes fifteen topics feel like one system instead of fifteen links: what we stand for, how we are governed, what it is built on. And knowing the most common format before building means most boards are built right the first time, instead of each one re-deciding its shape.

## How to Use It

For each "what is" topic, build one frame that explains it in the most distilled, real, authentic language — same stage and format, short, child-clear, never scrolling. Group the frames by kind. Default to one full board across for a single distilled idea; reach for halves only when the idea has two parts; thirds and stacked rectangles only when the content asks for them.
