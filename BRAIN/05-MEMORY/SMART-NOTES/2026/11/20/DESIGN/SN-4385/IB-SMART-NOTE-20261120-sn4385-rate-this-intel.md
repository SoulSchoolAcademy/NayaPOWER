## The Lesson

The rating control is renamed from "RATE THE INTELLIGENCE" to "RATE THIS INTEL" — his words, exactly that. For Rate This Intel, use a BIGGER version of that same flowing element — the DM orb from the star fix — white is fine, dealer's choice of white or purple, and add flowing around the OUTSIDE of it as well, not only inside. The bigger orb is the same element scaled, not a second design: one element, three sizes — the DM initial orb, the rating star, the big rate orb. The outside flow is the difference between a jewel that sits and a jewel that lives — the light must move around it, not only through it.

## Why It Matters

A name that is almost right still misnames the control every time it is read. "Rate the Intelligence" sounds like grading a system; "Rate This Intel" says what it does — rate this piece of intelligence, here, now. And a bigger orb that only flows inside is the same orb at a bigger size; the outside flow is what makes it the hero of the section instead of a larger copy.

## How to Use It

Rename the heading exactly: "RATE THIS INTEL". Build the rate orb as the DM element scaled up, with flow inside AND around the outside. Keep VALUE and INTELLIGENCE consistent with the star rule — purple orbs with white stars for value, white orbs with purple stars for intelligence.
