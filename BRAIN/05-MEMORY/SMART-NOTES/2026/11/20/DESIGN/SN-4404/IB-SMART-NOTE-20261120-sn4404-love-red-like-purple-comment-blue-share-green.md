## In a Nutshell

Love lights up red, Like purple, Comment blue, Share green — each action its own colour, jewel-like, never white, never simple.

## The Lesson

His ruling on the four actions under a post: Love **red** · Like **purple** · Comment **blue** (offering intelligence) · Share **green**. Love used to have red around it, now white — wrong. Icons inside the buttons more prominent, **jewel-like and beautiful, not simple**; the zero is cut off at the bottom — make it pro. Each action has its own colour because each action IS a different thing: love is warmth, like is the Naya purple, comment is the offering of intelligence in blue, share is growth in green. White is not a colour here — white is the absence of the light, and a button that should be lit and shows white is a button that has gone out. The zero cut off at the bottom is the same failure at a smaller scale: a count that cannot be read is a count that was never drawn.

## Why It Matters

The four actions are the citizen's hands on a post — love it, like it, add to it, spread it. If they all light up the same, the hands blur into one gesture; if love shows white where it used to show red, the citizen reads it as broken, because it is. Jewel-like icons are the standard of the whole network — the emblem is the north star for all art — and a simple flat icon under a post breaks the standard exactly where the citizen touches it most.

## How to Use It

When building the four actions, give each its colour and keep it: Love red, Like purple, Comment blue, Share green — lit when active, never white. Make the icons jewel-like and prominent, and check the count is fully drawn, never cut off at the bottom. If a regression turns a lit action white, that is the defect, not a restyle.
