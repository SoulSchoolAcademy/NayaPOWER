## Why It Matters

A story built from an invented order has to be defended. A story built from an order that was already in it defends itself — every slide lands where it was always going to land, and the person watching feels it before they can say why. That is why the numbers matter: they are the proof the order wasn't imposed. And the correction matters more than the first count: it shows the system can be wrong and get better in the same breath, in his hands, without anyone having to tell him. When the count corrects itself from 23 to 27 and the meaning deepens with it — heaven to light, love to completion — the story is not just ordered, it is true.

## How to Use It

When he hands you a set of images and asks for the story: don't start by counting boards. Never file a sequence while he is still labeling it; never defend a first number against his correction.
