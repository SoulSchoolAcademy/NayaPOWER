## The Lesson

Sequence is the decision, not just the work. Carrying an unfinished format to ten pages does not save time; it multiplies the unfinished format by ten, and then every page has to be re-carried once the format is finally right. Finish the format once, in one place, where his eye can judge it — then the carry is mechanical: frame around the text, same formatting, page by page. The link check is the same discipline at link scale: a link is not checked by looking at it, it is checked by following it. A footer that lists ten policies nobody has followed is a list, not a check.

## Why It Matters

The policy pages are the legal face of the system, and they inherit whatever the presentation proves. If they are built before the presentation's format exists, they inherit a guess — and the guess will be wrong in exactly the ways his eye is now correcting (flat boards, wrong font, gold text, dead frames). Waiting is not delay; it is the only way the carry lands once instead of twice. And the footer links are the first screen's promise that the legal pages exist and work — a link that goes nowhere breaks that promise on the first screen a citizen sees.
