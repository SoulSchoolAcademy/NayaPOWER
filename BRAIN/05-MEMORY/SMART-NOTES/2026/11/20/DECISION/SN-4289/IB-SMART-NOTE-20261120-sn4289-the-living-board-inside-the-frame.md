## Why It Matters

The person meeting this presentation meets the board before they meet the words. If the showcase is a bare image on a page, the first thing they learn is that this is a page; if it is a lit-up board that breathes and answers their touch, the first thing they learn is that this is alive — and everything after it reads as alive too. The text-as-boards rule protects the same feeling: words that will not fit beautifully beside an image get their own living boards instead of being squeezed. And the one-module rule protects the build: every hour spent making the showcase a real board pays twice, once in the presentation and once in the feed, because the feed post is the same frame with one block added underneath — not a second design, not a copy, the same module.
