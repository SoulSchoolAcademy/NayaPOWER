The question "Do those look like our buttons?" is the whole test, and a flat button fails it before anything else about the page is judged. The deeper lesson is that the real buttons already exist twice over: the Academy's are the originals from his GroovePages page, recreated with real depth and light, and the Hub's Collective / Personal / Activity buttons are great buttons with icons in them. The fix is not "design better buttons" — it is "stop designing buttons and pull the real ones."

## Why It Matters

Buttons are the hands of the presentation — Keep Reading, back, agree — and every tap runs through them. Every page in the system is judged by the same question, and the answer is always the same source — the real buttons, pulled whole, from a page that has them.
