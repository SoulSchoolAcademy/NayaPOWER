## In a Nutshell

Clicking play must do something. The deck play button plays the audio and starts the video; play on the video starts the audio through the visual; pause one and the other rests. And the video plays first of all — it must play on open, not wait for a tap.

## The Lesson

A play button that plays nothing is a broken promise, not a missing feature. The wiring IS the work: audio and video are one experience, not two controls that happen to sit side by side. A video error that appears in a new build is a regression, not a new item — it gets fixed, not filed away.

## Why It Matters

The whole storybook opens with her voice and her video; if neither plays, the visitor meets a dead page. The content is not the problem — the pictures and the audio live inside the page itself (embedded, they travel with it); the video lives on YouTube and the page plays it from there. The problem is showing and playing what is already there.

## How to Use It

Wire the deck play to audio plus video together; wire video play to audio through the visual; wire pause both ways; autoplay the video on open. The content and hosting answer is voice-delivered, not captain-verified — the builder must name exactly where each picture loads from before anything reaches him.
