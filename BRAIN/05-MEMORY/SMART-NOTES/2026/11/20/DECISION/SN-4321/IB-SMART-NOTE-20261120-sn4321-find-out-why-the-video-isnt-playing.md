## The Lesson

A thing that will not play has a reason, and the reason is the work. Re-placing the same video in the same spot and hoping it plays this time is not a fix — it is a repeat with a new timestamp. The diagnosis comes first: what is handed to the build, what the build does with it, where it breaks — named, in order, with proof. Only then does the placement mean anything, because only then can the walk prove it plays.
