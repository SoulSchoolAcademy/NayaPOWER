## The Lesson

The best version of a thing is usually already built, somewhere in the house. The bar he likes is a screenshot of a bar that already exists; the font he loves is a font already rendering on the Ask Naya page; the certificate's glow is already filed. Redesigning any of them is not improvement, it is loss — the new version is "nowhere close to the same," and he has to say so again. Reuse is not laziness here; it is the discipline: take the thing that already earned his eye, put it where it belongs, and spend the effort on the fit, not on a fresh drawing. The two details carry the lesson in miniature: NAYA beside her picture (not underneath — a placement, not a redesign) and less glow in the middle (restraint, not a new effect).
