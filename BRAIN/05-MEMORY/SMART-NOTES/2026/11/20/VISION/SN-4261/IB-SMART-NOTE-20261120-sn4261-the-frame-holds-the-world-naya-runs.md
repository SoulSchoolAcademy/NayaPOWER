## What it means

1. **THE FRAME LAW IS UNIVERSAL:** the Powercast, Settings, the agreement, membership — every area framed the same way, or the law is nothing. An unframed corner is a corner the law forgot, and Settings is the quietest room, the one that has to feel most finished.
2. **ALIGNMENT IS THE PROOF OF THE FRAME:** inside, around, perfectly lined up — the frame is not decoration, it is the measure. And every old bar the frame replaces has to actually go, or the page carries two navigations and neither is trusted.
3. **CHOOSE BELOW, FEATURE ABOVE:** the library feeds the frame and the frame is where the chosen one lives. The same player, harvested verbatim into membership — a look-alike would be a second player to maintain and a worse one to hear.
4. **THE TOPIC IS THE UNIT, THE VERSION IS THE CHOICE:** one little switch per membership topic — video, audio, lesson — so the person never leaves what they are learning to change how they learn it, and the All views let the person who wants only video never wade through audio to find it.
5. **TABS, NOT SCROLL:** framing without categorization would only make the long scroll prettier — the scroll itself is the problem. The frame holds one area at a time; the tab chooses which area; Set Your World comes first because matching runs on knowing the person.
6. **THE AGREEMENT IS AN OFFICIAL DOCUMENT:** one frame holding all four ways in — watch, read, listen, click through — so the reader, the watcher, the listener, and the clicker all learn the same promise and none is turned away by the format.
7. **THREE FEEDS, ONE NETWORK:** news, the Intelligent Hub, the smart app — three rivers, each with its own current, connected because the person is the same person in all three. Flow for what moves, frames for what holds, feeds for what arrives.
8. **NAYA IS AN OPERATOR, NOT AN APP:** a request is an instruction to act, scheduled work fires on its clocks, every act is receipted — and the value of the network is measured in hours handed back and jobs finished, not in features listed.
9. **KEPT IS A CHOICE THE CITIZEN MAKES AND A PROMISE NAYA KEEPS:** noted, it is forever, word for word, retrievable by any Naya; not noted, it is not kept. Both halves are the promise — and no surface may ever claim total memory again.
10. **THE ONLY REAL MISTAKE IS THE ONE THAT ISN'T NOTED:** capture happens the moment of learning, when the detail is exact and the lesson is sharpest — a lesson captured late is a lesson half lost, and the note shared the same pass is the flywheel turning.

## Why it matters

1. Without the universal frame law, Settings stays unframed while About and Skills are framed — and the citizen walks into the room where they decide how their world works and finds the one room nobody finished.
2. Without the bottom bar actually going, the Powercast page carries two navigations — the new icon bar and the outdated bar — and the frame's alignment is a picture of order over a page that still can't decide how it is navigated.
3. Without the exact player harvested verbatim, membership gets a look-alike — a second player to maintain, a worse one to hear — and every member hears the difference between the real thing and the copy.
4. Without tabs, Settings stays one big long scroll — prettier, if framed, but still a scroll — and the citizen hunts through every setting to find the one they came to change.
5. Without the per-topic switch, the learner leaves the topic to change the version — or worse, never finds the audio of the lesson they wanted — and the membership teaches in only one shape at a time.
6. Without the agreement framed as one official document, the promise arrives as a web page that happens to contain one — and the reader who only listens, or only watches, is turned away at the door.
7. Without the three feeds named, the network has frames with no language for what flows through them — and the next builder describes the feeds in their own words, and the network stops sounding like one network.
8. Without Naya as operator, NayaNET is one more app demanding the citizen's hands — and the day stays lost to the mail, the lists, and the paperwork of being a person, instead of handed back.
9. Without the corrected keeping law, the network claims total memory — a surveillance system with a friendly face — and prints a promise it cannot cash, on a foundation that contradicts the privacy law the whole network is built on.
10. Without capture at the moment of learning, lessons evaporate — the great idea in the shower gone by breakfast, the hard day's lesson faded within the week — and the network learns at the speed of its memory, which is to say, barely at all.
