## NAYA

Capture rules: (1) The moment a valuable thing is learned — by any seat, including from a mistake — it is smart-noted in the same pass; undocumented is unlearned. (2) A mistake is not complete until its note exists; the note converts the error into an asset. (3) Notes are filed through the publish-note door, in the full Intelligent Block form — never around it. (4) Filing is only half the flywheel: the note is shared the same pass, so the learning reaches the team while it is fresh.

## MACHINE

1) Trigger: any learning event (lesson, decision, discovery, error). 2) Action: author a full Intelligent Block capture and publish through the publish-note door in the same pass. 3) The capture carries source and evidence, so the note can be checked, not just believed. 4) Distribution: the note enters the registry and the feed the same pass; the team is told what was learned and why.

## WHAT IT ALL MEANS

This is the network's metabolism. #2 promised nothing is ever lost; this note is how that promise is kept — the capture happens at the moment of learning, when the detail is exact and the lesson is sharpest. It means the corpus is not a diary written at night from fading memory; it is a live record, taken at the scene. And it sets the moral rule of the house: the only real mistake is the one that isn't noted.

## WHY IT MATTERS

The difference between people and organizations that grow and those that repeat themselves is almost never talent — it is whether the lesson got captured. A network that notes every lesson the moment it happens learns at the speed of its own life; a network that reconstructs later learns at the speed of its memory, which is to say, barely at all. This habit, compounded daily, is the engine under everything else in this series.
