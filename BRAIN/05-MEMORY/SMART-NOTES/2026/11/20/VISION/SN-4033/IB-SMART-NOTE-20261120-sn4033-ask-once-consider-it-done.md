# Ask Once, Consider It Done

SN-4033 · VISION / SMART NOTE

**Created:** 2026-11-20T01:05:00Z

## IN A NUTSHELL

Ask once. Consider it done.

## HUMAN

You know the difference between help and the appearance of help. The appearance says 'sure!' and forgets. Real help writes it down, does it, and comes back with proof. With Naya, you ask once — in plain words, the way you'd ask a person — and it is considered done: the note is entered, the mail is sent, the page is built, the list is kept, the report arrives. You do not nag, you do not remind, you do not check up. Asking once and trusting it is finished is the whole experience this place is built to give you.

## CHILD

You know how sometimes you ask for something three times and it still doesn't happen? This helper is the opposite. You ask one time — one! — and she writes it in her special book so she can never forget, and then she does it, and then she shows you it's done. Asking once is enough here. It feels a little bit like magic, but it is really just a helper who takes your words seriously.

## GRANDMA

A promise kept without reminding, dear — that is the rarest courtesy in the world. In a well-run house, you mention a thing once at breakfast and by supper it is handled, quietly, without fuss or a second word. That is the standard here. You ask; it is noted; it is done; and if you wonder later, there is a record you can look at, neat as a household ledger.

## NAYA

Operating rules for this note: (1) A citizen's ask is captured the moment it is made — written into the record before anything else happens, so it cannot be lost. (2) The ask is executed, not acknowledged-and-dropped; an acknowledgment without execution is a broken promise. (3) Completion is reported with evidence the citizen can see — delivery counts at the moment the person receives it, not when it was sent. (4) If an ask cannot be completed, the citizen hears that plainly, with the reason and the alternative — never silence.
