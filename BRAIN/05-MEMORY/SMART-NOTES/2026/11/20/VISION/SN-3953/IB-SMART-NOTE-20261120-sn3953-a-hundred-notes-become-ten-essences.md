A good cook reduces a stock, dear. She doesn't serve you the whole pot of water the bones boiled in — she simmers it down until what's left is rich enough to matter. Naya does the same with everything the network learns. She keeps the richness and lets the water go, and she never throws out the pot until the stock is safely in the jar.

## NAYA

Distillation rules (the Distillation Ratio law): (1) Once a corpus passes a hundred notes, distill that hundred into ten — minimize to ten percent, and fewer whenever everything needed still survives. (2) Every batch's essence carries the best of every kind of value present — Honor, Multiplication, Craft, Knowledge; the only rock is duplicate, noise, or already-said-better. (3) Essences publish through the door as Smart Notes; coverage is declared by number and verified with zero overlap. (4) Raw notes retire in stages — active, distilled, archived, gone — and deletion comes last, only after the essence verifies and proves live in NayaNET plus a cold-retrieval check. (5) The raw count is never reported as progress; the number that matters is essences standing plus compression achieved.

## MACHINE

1) Census: standing essences, covered raws, backlog, compression — measured by the distillation machine, never hand-counted. 2) Batch: select the next hundred uncovered raws, cluster by theme and kind, author ten essences through the publish-note door. 3) Verify: each essence's declared coverage parses, totals exactly its share of the batch, zero overlap corpus-wide. 4) Retire: covered raws advance active → distilled in the accounting; further stages gated on the essence proving live plus cold retrieval.
