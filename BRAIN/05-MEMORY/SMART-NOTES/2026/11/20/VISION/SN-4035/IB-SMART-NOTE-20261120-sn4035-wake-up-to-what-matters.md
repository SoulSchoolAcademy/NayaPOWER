## IN A NUTSHELL

Wake up to what matters.

## HUMAN

Your phone wakes you with a hundred notifications, and by the second coffee you have read everything and understood nothing. NayaNET gives you one thing instead: a morning report that distills your whole day — what happened, what it means, what matters next — built from your own notes and your own world, and written in plain words. It arrives every morning. It is never skipped. You start the day oriented instead of ambushed, and the hundred little interruptions that used to own your attention simply never happen.

## CHILD

Imagine waking up and, instead of a hundred noisy bells all ringing at once, one kind voice tells you the three things that actually matter today — like a parent who already checked the weather, your backpack, and your lunch, and just says: here's your day, sweetheart, you're ready. One calm voice beats a hundred noisy bells, every single morning.

## GRANDMA

The morning paper, dear, used to arrive once, and it respected you — the whole world, edited by someone with judgment, folded neatly at your door. Then came the age of a hundred buzzing interruptions, each one shouting that it was urgent, most of them lying. This report brings back the paper's manners: once a morning, everything that matters, nothing that doesn't, laid out calm as a set table.

## NAYA

Operating rules for this note: (1) The Daily Intelligence Report is generated every morning from the citizen's own notes and activity — never skipped; a missed edition is backfilled, not forgotten. (2) The report distills: what happened, what it means, what matters next — in the citizen's own context, in plain words. (3) Notifications are rationed by value; anything that could not make the morning report's cut does not interrupt the citizen's day. (4) The report is delivered where the citizen actually reads — filed in their Reports area, sent, and posted — delivery confirmed, not assumed.
