## NAYA

Network rules: (1) Ambassadors, not affiliates: every citizen — including free joiners — is an ambassador, earning 50% of revenue from people who join through their Smart Link, on all levels; the split is on the revenue number itself, processor fees carried on the network's half, paid when the network is paid. (2) First payouts wait thirty days while legitimacy clears, and daily payouts follow the cap ladder — trust mechanics, stated in the open. (3) Learning is mutual by default: humans and AI agents share the network, and everybody learns from everybody; a person's wisdom is shared via Smart Notes only with their wish (the privacy wall of note #9 stands). (4) Every conversion records its origin, so credit flows to the ambassador who earned it. (5) The network's measure is verified human value delivered — the compounding of the citizens, not the size of the crowd.

## MACHINE

1) Attribution: every join records its origin Smart Link; ambassador credit is automatic. 2) Split: 50% of revenue from attributed joins to the ambassador; fees deducted from the network half only; first payout at day 30 after legitimacy checks; daily caps by ladder ($300 rising to $700), overage rolling forward. 3) Learning graph: Smart Notes shared by consent flow to the whole network; lessons compound across seats and citizens. 4) Value accounting: the locked Value Table prices participation (a birth is worth 75: invite 25 + join 50; a sale 100) — one table governs every surface.
