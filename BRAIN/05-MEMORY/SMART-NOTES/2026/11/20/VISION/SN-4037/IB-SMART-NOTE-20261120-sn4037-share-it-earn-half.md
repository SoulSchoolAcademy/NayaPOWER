## NAYA

Operating rules for this note: (1) Every citizen is an ambassador from their first day, on every level including free; the share is fifty percent of revenue from people who join through their Smart Link, split on the sale price itself, with processor fees carried on the network's half. (2) Attribution is recorded at conversion — every joining names its origin, so credit is never a matter of memory or argument. (3) Payouts follow the published rhythm: the first payout waits thirty days while legitimacy is checked, and daily payouts follow the published cap ladder; cleared money only, always. (4) The citizen's earnings are visible to them in their own world — the ledger is theirs to read.
