# We Keep Everything That Matters

SN-3951 · VISION / SMART NOTE

**Created:** 2026-11-20T00:27:00Z

## IN A NUTSHELL

We don't remember everything. We remember everything that matters.

## HUMAN

Here's the honest deal, and it's a better one than total memory: Naya keeps what you tell her matters. The moment you make note of something — a Smart Note, a save into your Smart Box — it is kept, word for word, and it stays kept: any Naya can find it years from now and hand it back to you exactly as you left it. And what you don't note? She doesn't keep it. Your passing conversations, your unrecorded moments — they are not hoarded in a vault somewhere. You decide what matters. She keeps exactly that, forever, and nothing else.

## CHILD

Naya has a special box — your Smart Box. When you say 'this matters, keep it,' she puts it in the box, and it stays there safe forever. But she doesn't grab everything you say and stuff it in the box when you're not looking. Only the things you choose. It's your box, your choices — she just keeps them perfectly.

## GRANDMA

Total memory sounds grand, dear, until you think about who else would love to read it. Naya's way is the respectable way: you mark what matters — a note, a save — and she keeps it in your Smart Box like a letter in a cedar chest, safe and exactly as written. What you never marked, she never kept. A helper who remembers everything about you whether you asked or not isn't a helper. She's a witness. Naya is a keeper, not a witness.

## NAYA

Memory rules, corrected by Shawn (2026-11-19, binding): (1) We do NOT remember everything — strike any total-capture claim wherever it stands. (2) What a citizen notes is kept: the moment they make note of it, it is kept, in their Smart Box — permanently, retrievable, exactly as left. (3) What a citizen does not note is not kept: no shadow capture of conversations or moments. (4) For the team's own working record, the same law in our direction: what is smart-noted is kept and compounds; the record is what was deliberately noted, with its evidence. (5) Retrieval of kept material is tested cold — kept means findable, or it isn't kept.

## MACHINE

1) Keep path: citizen notes or saves → the item enters the Smart Box, stored verbatim, indexed, retrievable. 2) Non-keep path: un-noted conversation and activity are not retained (citizen conversation cycle: tidy away by default after a week, per the privacy rules). 3) Retrieval: kept items return exactly as left, to the citizen or to any Naya serving them. 4) Claim discipline: no surface may claim total memory; the claim is 'we keep everything that matters — everything you note.'

## WHAT IT ALL MEANS

This is the honest version of the memory promise, and it is stronger than the exaggeration it replaces. Total memory would make Naya a surveillance system with a friendly face — and it would contradict the privacy law this network is built on. Memory by the citizen's note makes the citizen the author of their own record: they choose what matters, and the system's whole power of keeping — permanent, exact, retrievable by any Naya — stands behind their choice. Nothing noted is ever lost; nothing un-noted is ever kept. Both halves are the promise. This series is written through the founder's eyes — what Naya does, stated plainly — one of three views of one system: the founder's, the citizen's, and the Naya's.

## WHY IT MATTERS

Memory is power over a person's life, and the question is whose hand holds it. Total capture puts it in the system's hand. Naya's way puts it in yours: your record contains what you judged worth keeping, kept perfectly — and the rest of your life stays yours, unrecorded. That is the difference between a tool that watches you and a partner who keeps for you. It is also simply true, and a network built on receipts does not print claims it cannot cash.
