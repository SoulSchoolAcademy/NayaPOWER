## Why It Matters

"She opens one place" is the whole product in one line — the answer to hundreds of emails, advertisements, notifications and websites is not a better inbox, it is one place. The five proofs turn the vision into a test every build is judged against, and proof #1 is the whole game: a real person joins and immediately receives something valuable that works. If proof #1 fails, nothing else matters; if it passes, the rest follow in order. The privacy caveat is honest rather than claimed — protections remain under construction, trust must be technically and independently proven — which is why proof #2 exists as a proof and not a promise. And the scale discipline protects the whole thing from the oldest mistake: chasing thousands before the first ten have something real.

And hold the privacy line exactly as he stated it: the promise is the Collective Agreement; the proof is technical and independent, and until it exists, the caveat stays attached to the claim.
