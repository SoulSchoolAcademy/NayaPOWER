## Why It Matters

A finished page sitting on one disk and one branch looks lost the moment nobody can find it — and a whole round can burn searching for something that was safe all along. The Vault is not a filing cabinet; it is the difference between "we have it" and "we think we lost it." Anything worth building is worth filing to the Vault in the same pass, or the next person to need it starts from zero.

## How to Use It
