It has to be being completed… if they're not building and doing the edits you have to have agents at all times doing these edits… get this done right now." More time building, less time watching. ## The Lesson

Watching is not work. A lane with an agent in it that is not building is a lane pretending to be busy — and a list that sits while agents watch it is a list that will still be sitting tomorrow. The order is mechanical: at all times, agents doing the edits; the moment a thing is asked, it is being completed. "Get this done right now" is not urgency for effect — it is the cadence. Words about the work are not the work; the work is the edits landing.

## Why It Matters

This is the Continuous Cycle Law in his own words, sharpened by a day of rounds sitting while seats watched them. Every minute an agent spends watching instead of building is a minute the storybook stays broken on his screen — and he is the one who has to walk it again. The machine earns its keep in completions, not in attention.

## How to Use It

When he asks, start building — do not file it as a to-do entry and watch it. Keep agents on the edits at all times; a quiet seat with a live lane is a seat to check, not a seat to admire. Report completions, not activity: what landed, where, proven how. Fewer words, more done.
