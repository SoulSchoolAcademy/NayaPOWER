## In a Nutshell

Navigation is two buttons only: back and forward. Not three. "You actually only need a back and forth button… you just have a forward and a backwards. You don't need a forward next and then a forward. That's why your forward doesn't work because it's a double forward."

## The Lesson

Three nav buttons (back / next / forward) is not a style choice — it is the defect. Forward and next double up, and the double is why forward does not work. Two buttons is the logic of the thing: one way back, one way forward. The charge that rides with it is the captain's own: he said the captain should have spotted this itself and said so, instead of building the double forward and waiting for him to name it. A doubled control is a defect a wise one names before building, not after the user has to.

## Why It Matters

Every doubled control is a broken control wearing a second label. The user taps forward, nothing sensible happens, and the page looks broken — because the page IS broken, by its own navigation.

## How to Use It

On the agreement, navigation is back and forward only — remove the third button in the fix round. Everywhere else: when two controls do the same job, one of them goes, and the seat that sees the double says so in its own report before the round is built.
