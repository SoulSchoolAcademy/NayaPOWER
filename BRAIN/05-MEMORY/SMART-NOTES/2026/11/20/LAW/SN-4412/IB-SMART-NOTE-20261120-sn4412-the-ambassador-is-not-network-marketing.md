## In a Nutshell

This is not network marketing. You are an ambassador — commission sales, like a sales job with a high-tech company. People join through your link, you get paid. If they don't, you don't. And you still get to enjoy NayaNET — it's your brain forever.

## The Lesson

His words, verbatim: "This is not a network marketing. You're an ambassador. You're just like getting a sales job with a high tech company. You just commission sales. If you sell you get paid. People join, you get paid. If they don't, you don't get paid. But you get to enjoy NayaNET — it's your brain forever, you get to use it forever. You connect with people, they're part of your network for good. You just don't get paid if people don't join through your link. That's it." The line is drawn by the money: commission on a join, nothing for a sign-up, nothing for a downline, nothing owed by anyone. This matches the standing "How You Get Paid" board — not MLM, nobody obligated to invite, 50% of revenue — and it is now said in his own voice, in one place, so no ambassador ever has to explain it twice. The second half is just as important: the enjoyment is not conditional on the pay. The brain is yours forever whether anyone joins through you or not.

## Why It Matters

The first question anyone asks about an ambassador program is whether it is network marketing — and the answer decides whether they listen at all. A clear, short, honest answer in his words removes the objection before it hardens, and it protects the ambassadors from ever sounding like they are selling a scheme. The "your brain forever" half is the reason the program is worth joining even before the first sale: the product is the reward, the commission is the bonus.

## How to Use It

Say it exactly this way, in the videos, the orientation, and the Fast Action Plan: not network marketing; ambassador; commission sales like a sales job with a high-tech company; people join through your link, you get paid; if they don't, you don't; and you still get to enjoy NayaNET — it's your brain forever. Never call it an MLM, never promise pay for recruiting, never let the pay promise stand without the "your brain forever" half.
