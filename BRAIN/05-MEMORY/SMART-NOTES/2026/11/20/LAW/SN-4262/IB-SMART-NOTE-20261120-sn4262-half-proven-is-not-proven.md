## What it means

1. **HALF-PROVEN IS NOT PROVEN:** a fix proven on one route is a claim about that route only. The canonical route is the demo; the other routes are the product. Until every way in is walked, the fix is half-proven, and half-proven is what a citizen trips over.
2. **THE RECEIPT IS THE WORK:** proof is behavior, not a badge. A green light with nothing behind it is a lie and is never told. Sent is not delivered, delivered is not received, and a tool's success message is not the human benefiting.
3. **DISTILL, DON'T ACCUMULATE:** ten thousand notes nobody can hold are worth less than a hundred essences anybody can. Memory without distillation is a warehouse; memory with it is a mind — and essences themselves get distilled, level by level, toward their purest form.
4. **MEASURE WHAT THE CITIZEN SEES:** stops are where a checker measures; motion is where a citizen lives. The pass-through is the proof, the whole chrome is the landing, and the margin is the distance to the next failure — all three named in the verdict, not discovered later by breaking it.
5. **DIAGNOSE THE PATH, NOT THE DESIGN:** when the same board renders one way and hangs another, the board is innocent — the fault rides the entry that fails. Blaming the board rebuilds a working thing; the fix belongs to the fetch, the route, the load.
6. **PRIVACY IS THE GROUND:** trust comes before features. A person cannot relax, create, or build a life inside a system that watches them — so the first promise is the truest one: you are not the product here, and any feature that would require reading private messages is designed differently or not built.
7. **ONE SYSTEM, NO LEARNING CURVE:** people do not want more software; they want their world in one place, kept in order. Four doors, everything else a feature, entered once and available everywhere — and ease is a moral property: every confusing screen quietly tells a person this world is not for them.

## Why it matters

1. Without every route proven, the maker fixes the road they walked, the checker proves the road they were given, and the citizen walks a third road and finds the fix missing — the round ships as done and the failure waits on the citizen's path.
2. Without the pass-through measured, a fix that breaks between the stops ships as a PASS, and the citizen's every scroll passes through the offsets where it fails — the verdict was true at the stops and false in use.
3. Without the margin named, the next maker pushes the paragraph down ~14px without knowing the line — and rediscovers the gap by breaking it, because a fragile pass was banked as if it were a safe one.
4. Without the whole chrome measured, a rect reading 0 certifies a landing where the way back is off-screen — and for Sophia, but not for Thora, Terra or Zoe, the page arrives broken while the measurement says it arrived.
5. Without path diagnosis, the team rebuilds a working board to fix a failing fetch — burning the good thing to chase a fault that lives elsewhere — and names a cause for the wrong-name greeting that the evidence never proved.
6. Without the receipt law, the other nine promises rest on "trust me" — operating your world, remembering everything, distilling the gold — and a system that says it works is indistinguishable from one you can watch working.
7. Without distillation, the pile buries the citizen: files, messages, notes, ten thousand records nobody can hold — and the value was never the pile, it is the essence the pile contains, stated once, whole, in words a stranger can use.

## What changes now

1. Every brief names every way in — chooser, tab, resume link, deep link — and every checker walks each one; a round that proves one route does not call the fix done.
2. Every fix is proven in motion: the checker walks the pass-through between the stops, on a render with real data behind it, and a PASS with a named in-motion failure carries its follow-on in the same pass.
3. Every verdict records the margin with the pass — the number, like ~14px, and the fragility it means — so the next round knows the line it must not cross.
4. Every scroll landing is proven with the whole chrome in view — Back, header, orb — for every person, not just the ones whose layout happens to fit; a container's rect of 0 no longer certifies a page.
5. Every same-board-different-result is diagnosed by contrast first — find the entry that works, find the entry that fails, fix whatever differs — and no cause is named until the evidence proves it.
6. Every report re-reads its numbers live in the same pass and names the source beside each one; delivery is counted only at consumption; a falsified claim is corrected publicly, in the same venue it travelled.
7. Every threshold batch distills a hundred to ten with the best of every kind present in the group, retires its raws in stages, and deletes nothing until the essence proves live plus cold retrieval — and the citizen's welcome still leads with the promise: safe, whole, easy, in that order.
