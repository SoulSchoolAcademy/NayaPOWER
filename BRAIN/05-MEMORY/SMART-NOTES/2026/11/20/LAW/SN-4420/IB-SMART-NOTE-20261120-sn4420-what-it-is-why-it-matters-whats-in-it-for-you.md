The same logic builds About and builds Skills — a skill slide is just the pattern applied to a skill: label the skill, what it is in a nutshell, why it matters, how it connects, the value, what's in it for you. And the clarity bar is a person, not a grade: a child understands it, a grandma understands it. The correction went into the running About Naya build the same evening (submission `c7866c24`).

## Why It Matters

This is the answer to "what do people actually want from a page?" — three things, in his words: what it is, why it matters, what's in it for them. Everything else on the page is decoration around those three. A page that repeats the pattern frame after frame never loses the reader, because every frame ends where the reader's own interest lives: what's in it for me. And one pattern for About AND Skills means the citizen learns the shape once and reads everything in it.

## How to Use It

Write every About frame and every Skills slide in the pattern, in order, none skipped: what it is (nutshell) → child-clear / grandma-clear → why it matters → how to apply it / how it connects / the value → what's in it for you. Then repeat for the next frame, the next skill. If a frame cannot answer "what's in it for you," the frame does not belong.
