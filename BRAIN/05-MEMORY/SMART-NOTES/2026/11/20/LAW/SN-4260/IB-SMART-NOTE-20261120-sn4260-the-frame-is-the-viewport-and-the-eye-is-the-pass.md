# The Frame Is the Viewport, and the Eye Is the Pass

## Hook

"Instead of going scrolling down or scrolling across, or anything like that, we're just actually bringing people into the frame." The frame is not decoration on one page — it is the viewport people lock into throughout Naya, and every page, every lesson, every message comes into it frame by frame. And the frame is judged the same way the calendar was judged: the eye walks the render, and the render is the pass.

## Nutshell

The frame is the viewport. That is the law this group settles, from two directions at once: the frame is the stage standard across the whole system — desktop and a 9×16 viewport the other way for mobile — and inside it, nothing scrolls. Every area presents frame by frame, each step its own frame coming into the viewpoint: the agreement, visual and text; the membership lessons; mail; messenger; every lesson, every message, every page. The network is made of two kinds, and only two: frames hold, feeds flow. About, Skills, My World, the profile card, membership, mail, messenger, the agreement — all framed, all with the same framed fill My World already has. The Hub feed and the Personal, Business, and World feeds are never framed, because they are feeds, and a feed moves so a person can stay current. The same gesture carries the details: on Powercast, click any broadcast and the top player comes into the frame; Mail and Messenger come inside the frame too, so they arrive as the same family, not a different one; the Skills tab on Personal gets it right with this format — slideshow for Skills, unfold for About — in the frame, in the Personal. The front door is proven the same way the frame is proven: the staged Hub door passed hop by hop — fresh visitor sees the gate, the name carries gate to identity to Hub, a returning citizen skips the gate and goes straight in, Escape closes the Messenger — and live stays untouched until his tap, because staged is not live. The calendar v5 eye walked the render and sent it back on eight counts, the first fatal on its own: the date line said NOVEMBER 26 for both November 18 and November 20, Wednesday sat flat white instead of lit in its blue, and the Back button was invisible. Bytes clean is not a pass — the render is the pass. And over the whole of it sits his standing charge: look at all these pages and ask, is that the best feature for that page — and how can that feature, and that page, be ten times better in usefulness? Push it to the max. One more law rides along, quiet and easy to miss: when he says a thing is looking good — the collective agreement, looking good on the staged walkthrough — that is confirmation, not new scope. Record it, keep the round on its six items, and leave the confirmed piece exactly as it is.

## Naya

The frame is the viewport — build and judge every page inside it, desktop and 9×16 mobile both. No scrolling down or across anywhere except the feeds: frame by frame, each step its own frame coming into the viewpoint. Frames hold, feeds flow — frame About, Skills, My World, the profile card, membership, mail, messenger, the agreement, all with My World's framed fill; never frame the Hub feed or the Personal/Business/World feeds. Mail and Messenger come inside the frame; the Skills tab on Personal is slideshow, About is unfold, both in this format. On Powercast, the click on any broadcast brings the top player into the frame. On every page walk, run the 10× review: is that the best feature for that page, and how can it be ten times more useful — usefulness first, prettiness second. Never certify a surface from its bytes: walk the render at real widths and fail the page on any single fatal defect — a wrong date fails alone. Treat staging proof and live proof as different evidence classes; live changes only on his tap. ## What it means

1. **THE FRAME IS THE VIEWPORT:** the kingdom frame is the stage people lock into throughout Naya — not a border on one page, not decoration — and every page optimizes for its viewport inside it, including a 9×16 viewport the other way for mobile.
2. **NO SCROLLING — THE NEXT FRAME COMES TO YOU:** every area presents frame by frame, each step its own frame coming into the viewpoint — agreement, membership, mail, messenger, every lesson, every message, every page — so a person never hunts; the next thing arrives.
3. **FRAMES HOLD, FEEDS FLOW:** the network is made of two kinds and only two — frames hold still so a person can take one thing in whole, feeds move so a person can stay current — and the law is frame what holds, flow what moves, never frame a feed.
4. **EVERYTHING COMES INSIDE:** Mail and Messenger live inside the frame, the Skills tab gets it right with this format on Personal, the Powercast player comes into the frame on a broadcast click — one stage, one language, one feeling, never a different family.
5. **THE EYE IS THE PASS:** bytes clean is not a pass — the render is the pass; the calendar went back on a wrong date, an invisible Back, and a purple i on a blue Wednesday, because a calendar that gets the day wrong has failed at its one job.
6. **STAGED IS NOT LIVE:** the front door passed hop by hop on staging and live stays untouched until his tap — the two proofs are different evidence classes, and claiming the door "works" without the word "staging" would be a false claim at the first surface every citizen meets.
7. **THE 10× REVIEW IS THE STANDING CHARGE:** on every page walk, each feature is scored against what its page is for and pushed ten times more useful — the review is the work, not the prelude to it, and the scorecard is a usefulness scorecard first.
8. **CONFIRMATION IS NOT NEW SCOPE:** when he says a thing is looking good, record it and keep the round on its scope — the confirmed piece stays exactly as it is, and a confirmation mistaken for a request is how good work gets redone into worse work.

## Why it matters

1. Without the frame as the viewport, every page invents its own stage — and the system feels like a hundred separate sites instead of one kingdom a person locks into.
2. Without frame-by-frame, the person scrolls and hunts — and the agreement, the lessons, the mail all become long walls the citizen has to wade through instead of steps that arrive.
3. Without the two-kinds distinction, the next builder frames the feeds too — and the Hub feed, which lives by moving, gets frozen into frames and stops being a feed at all.
4. Without Mail and Messenger inside the frame, they arrive as a different family — and the one system splits into two looks, two feelings, two languages.
5. Without the eye as the pass, the next build ships on a byte-check — and a calendar that says the wrong day reaches citizens, because nobody looked at the render at real widths.
6. Without staged-kept-separate-from-live, the front door gets called "working" on a rehearsal — and the first real citizen meets a live shell that still 522s on the returning link.
7. Without the 10× review, pages get prettier instead of more useful — and a page can look finished while its best feature is still the wrong feature for what that page is for.
8. Without confirmation-kept-as-confirmation, every "looking good" becomes a rebuild — and the good work gets redone into worse work while the round's real six items sit undone.
