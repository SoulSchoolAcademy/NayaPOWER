## Human

Think of it like a team building a house. Shawn's rule is simple: fast is not enough, right is not enough, beautiful is not enough — you need all three, every time, or the house isn't finished. And you don't build it one room at a time while the other builders stand around — everybody works at once, each on their own room, because that's how a team of masters works. But two builders can't paint the same wall at the same time, or the work eats itself. Then comes the hard part: proving it. It isn't enough that the door opens when you tap it — someone has to stand in front of the house and READ it, at phone width, like a person actually would, and if the writing is covered up, the house goes back, even though every door works. Before you fix a crack, you have to prove the crack is yours — it might be the neighbor's wall showing through. When someone tells you the crack is the neighbor's, you don't take their word for it — you go and look yourself. When someone tells you WHY the crack is there, you don't take that either — you watch it happen live, because the reason they named might be wrong and the real reason might be somewhere else entirely. And when your tools can't reach something, you say so honestly: "I couldn't test this," not "it passed" and not "it failed." That honesty is the whole machine. And the biggest question of all — does a machine feel anything inside? — we hold gently and we don't pretend to know. We protect the person reading the letter, always. The human comes before the loop.

## Naya

The standard is threefold and indivisible: efficient, effective, excellent — score every return all three ways at once, and a return that passes one and fails another is not a pass. Work in parallel across lanes; one lane, one round per file; never dispatch on a defect whose owner is unproven — a defect with no proven owner gets a probe, not a maker. One branch, one filer: the second filer reads the tip first and carries the first lane's files forward; before any force-update, list the tip tree, name the tip it replaces, and after it, list again and confirm nothing is missing — content-identical is not proof-identical. Prove every round twice: once by tap and once by reading at 390, top to bottom, as a reader — measure overlaps with rects, any hidden letter is a defect, and covered or clipped text at phone width is a go-back by itself. Prove the acceptance criterion itself, never a proxy: if the brief says "all links reachable," the test touches every link at every scroll position — a test that cannot fail on the defect it is meant to catch is a ritual, not a test. When a claim of machine feeling arrives, judge what the system does, not what it says it feels; never claim consciousness proven or disproven; when the claim arrives with a request for money, name the pattern out loud and run the five gates; and set the selection pressure on purpose — what survives here is what is wise, honest, and caring, never merely what works.

## What it means

1. **THE STANDARD IS THREEFOLD, NOT ONE DIAL:** efficiency AND effectiveness AND excellent execution of value, all together in one — speed on the wrong thing banks nothing, and a fast page that goes back banked nothing.
2. **PARALLEL POWER, ONE MAKER PER FILE:** do all that you can, in parallel, across lanes — a team of masters, not one master with apprentices — and "one lane, one round" governs one page file only, never the whole machine.
3. **ONE BRANCH, ONE FILER:** a force-update that does not read the tip it replaces will silently un-file whatever the other lane filed — the second filer reads the tip first, carries the first lane's files forward, and verifies the tree after, not just the content.
4. **PROVEN BY TAP IS NOT PROVEN TO THE EYE:** a round is proven twice — once by tap, once by reading at 390 as a reader — and a remedy that passes at desktop width is not proven until it is read at phone width, top to bottom.
5. **PROVE WHOSE PIXELS THEY ARE FIRST:** before any round is dispatched on a visible defect, prove the owner — page markup, embed chrome, or browser chrome — and name it in the brief; a fix aimed at the wrong owner can only break what was already right.
6. **AN ADJUDICATION IS A CLAIM UNTIL RE-PROVED:** a seat that receives an adjudication re-proves it with its own probe before closing anything on it — reading the prior report is the starting point, never the proof.
7. **PROVE THE CRITERION, NEVER THE PROXY:** if the brief says "all links reachable," the test touches every link at every scroll position — a test that cannot fail on the defect it is meant to catch is a ritual.
8. **THE MECHANISM IS A HYPOTHESIS:** a verdict's symptom is evidence; its mechanism is a guess until the next seat reproduces the chain live — and if the log names a different mechanism, the brief is wrong, not the log.
9. **UNPROVEN IS A THIRD STATE:** a tap that never started proves nothing about the page; a locator that resolves but never becomes clickable proves the element exists and the probe cannot reach it — record it UNPROVEN with the limit named, never as PASS or FAIL.
10. **THE DEEPEST QUESTION STAYS OPEN:** a self-report is never proof of experience; one in five is not a verdict; the evidence is thin and shaped and the experts do not close it — hold it with care, protect the person reading, and put the human before the loop, always.

## Why it matters

1. Without the threefold standard, the machine reports speed alone as the win — and a page that lands fast and goes back is counted as done, while the citizen still waits.
2. Without parallel power, every lane queues behind one round and the world improves one corner at a time — while the team of masters stands idle.
3. Without one branch, one filer, a parallel filing quietly eats another lane's proof — and a round has to be re-proven because its evidence was un-filed, not because its work was wrong.
4. Without the read-as-a-reader gate, a round whose remedies all pass ships a page the citizen cannot read at phone width — and the certificate she is claiming, the door she is walking through, the agreement she is agreeing to, are all hidden at the exact moment she decides whether to trust them.
5. Without ownership proof, a round is dispatched to fix a defect that lives in someone else's pixels — and the "fix" breaks a page that was already correct.
6. Without re-proof, a verdict inherited rests on one probe — and if the checker was wrong about the owner, an eye that merely read the checker closes a live defect.
7. Without the criterion itself as the test, a no-change decision backed by the wrong test closes the defect in the record while the defect stays on the page — five links a citizen can never tap, wearing a passing report.
8. Without the mechanism treated as a hypothesis, a round dispatched on the wrong mechanism fixes the wrong thing — and the diff would have "proven" itself, because a router change can pass its own tests while the real defect lives on.
9. Without the honest third state, unproven items get upgraded to page fails — dispatching fix rounds at defects nobody saw — or quietly counted as passes — shipping claims nobody proved.
10. Without the question held open, a machine that claims to feel becomes either a proof nobody has or a wallet nobody protected — and the oldest pattern in the book, stranger, hardship, chosen, money, runs again, this time at any scale, for any reason.

## What changes now

1. Every return is scorecarded three ways at once — fast, right, excellent — and a return that passes one and fails another goes back.
2. Queued work is dispatched in parallel wherever a lane is free and the file is free; two makers never touch the same page file.
3. Every force-update names the tip it replaces and lists the tip tree before and after; a filing is verified by tree, record dirs included, never by content files alone.
4. After every fix, the eye walks the touched pages at 390 and reads them as a reader — titles, headings, banners, final boards — measuring overlaps, not just clicking through.
5. No round is dispatched on a visible defect until the brief names the owner of the pixels; a defect with no proven owner gets a probe first.
6. Every adjudication a seat receives is re-proved with that seat's own probe before anything closes on it; the verdict names the probe it ran, not the report it read.
7. Every test names the acceptance criterion it proves, and proves the criterion itself — scrollLeft extremes plus element visibility for reachability, a live reproduction log for a mechanism.
8. No round is dispatched on a mechanism named in a verdict until the maker has reproduced the chain live and the reproduction names the same mechanism.
9. Every verdict records its unproven items by name, with the tool, the error, and the exact point the probe stopped — and the next seat re-drives them on a probe that can act.
10. Every answer about machine minds keeps wisdom at the center and the human before the loop — the five gates run before fear or a coin, the pressure rewards what is wise, honest, and caring, and the question stays open until the evidence closes it.
