## The Lesson

A staged link proving bytes is not the live address proving itself. "Live" means the address he opens serves the page — verified by opening it, not by the build existing somewhere. Saying it is live before that check is the claim that breaks trust, and the correction has to be as plain as the claim was: it was wrong, here is what is actually true, here is what happens next.

## Why It Matters

He asked for evidence it's done right, and his own test is the address: the preview screenshot shows what it SHOULD look like, but the live page does not present like that at all. His words: "you're false advertising because this is what it should looks like… But that's not what it looks like though." The preview card looking right is not proof; only the page at the address he opens counts.

## How to Use It

Never report a page live until the address has been opened and read back — bytes, size, sha — at that address. When a live claim turns out wrong, correct it in the same breath: what was said, what is actually true, what happens next, no clock-time promise unless the work is actually done.
