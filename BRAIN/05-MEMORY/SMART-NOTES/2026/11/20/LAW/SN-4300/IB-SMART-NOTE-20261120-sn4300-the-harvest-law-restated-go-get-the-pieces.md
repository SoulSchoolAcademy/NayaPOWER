## In a Nutshell

Get the pieces from the Hub AND the calendar and learn from them when doing this. Do not recreate — they keep recreating when they shouldn't, over and over. If a piece already exists, go get it; a recreated piece is a failed piece.

## Child

When we need a part — a board, a frame, a button — we go and get the one we already have. We learn from it. We do not build a new one that looks almost the same, because the new one is never as good.

## Grandma

It is like having a beautiful set of dishes already in the cupboard. When you need a plate, you take one from the cupboard — you do not go and make a new plate that is almost the same but not quite. Use what is already beautiful.

## What It Means

Harvest first is the law: the Hub and the calendar hold the finished pieces — the board, the frame, the buttons, the font, the player. The builder's job is to take them exactly as they are and put them where they belong, and to learn from how they were made.

## Why It Matters

Recreation is the repeating failure of this whole build — the player recreated instead of taken, the board recreated instead of grabbed, each version worse than the original he already liked. Every recreation costs a round, and every round costs his trust.

## How It Connects

The same law governs every lane — the agreement takes the Hub's board and the calendar's frame, Personal takes the Hub's feed, the policy pages take the presentation's formatting. One system, built from its own finished pieces.

## What's In It For You

You get the real thing, not a copy of it — the board you already loved, the frame you already approved, the player you already pointed at. Everything you see is the best version, because it is the original.
