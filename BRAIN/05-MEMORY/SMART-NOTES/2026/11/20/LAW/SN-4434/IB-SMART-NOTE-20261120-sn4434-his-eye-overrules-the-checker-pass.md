## How to Use It

Report a checker PASS as what it is: "the checker passed 5/5; your eye is the proof." Never deliver a staged link with the word "done" attached to a checker verdict — the link is "ready for your eye," not "fixed." When his eye overrules a PASS, record the overruling in the open in the same pass — which items, what he saw, what the checker had measured — so the next round fixes the seeing, not just the bytes. And when a checker keeps passing what his eye keeps failing, the check itself is wrong: change what the checker measures, don't argue with his eye.
