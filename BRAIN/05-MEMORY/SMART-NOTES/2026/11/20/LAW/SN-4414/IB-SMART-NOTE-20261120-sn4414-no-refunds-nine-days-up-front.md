There are no refunds — because there is nothing to refund. You get nine days up front. You try it before you buy it. If you don't like it, you don't keep her. No problem.

## The Lesson

The nine-day trial is the whole refund policy: the trial IS the protection. ## Why It Matters

Refunds are friction, disputes, and doubt — and doubt is what keeps a person from joining at all. Nine days up front removes the doubt before the money ever moves: the citizen risks nothing, the decision is theirs, and the end of the trial is a clean yes or a clean no. Saying it plainly also protects the ambassadors — they never have to defend a refund policy, because there is nothing to defend.
