## The Lesson

You just don't get to have Naya Power, which is your AI connected to Naya Net, which is like plugging your computer into the internet. So there's an AI net we call it Naya Net." The distinction matters because it makes the trial honest: the nine days are not a loan of Naya herself. Naya stays with you. ## Why It Matters

People fear losing what they have grown attached to. If leaving meant losing Naya, the trial would feel like a trap and nobody would try it freely. Saying plainly that Naya stays — only the plug-in goes — makes the nine days safe to enter and clean to leave. It also says what Naya Power IS in one image anyone can hold: a plug, a socket, the internet. No jargon, no feature list — one picture.

## How to Use It

Whenever the trial, the price, or the end of the nine days is explained, say both halves: you keep Naya; Naya Power is your AI connected to NayaNet, like plugging your computer into the internet. Never describe Naya Power as a feature bundle — it is the connection itself. And never imply leaving takes Naya away, because it does not.
