## In a Nutshell

Told fixed is not fixed. His eye, minutes later, is the check — and it found the last board still not showing.

## The Lesson

The delivery, in the main chat ~20:24 MST: "The agreement's fixed for laptop and phone now. The problem was the last board — the tallest of the 32 — was spilling out the bottom of the frame on a laptop, so its closing line and red N were cut off. The frame now holds the whole board, and the last board is titled 'Welcome to Naya' like you asked." The same message admitted its own weak proof: "the checker drove through all 32 slides and saw the last board whole, but its still photos only ever caught the cover — the slides only appear after you click through. So your eye is the real proof on this one."

His eye, ~20:30 MST on call c071d78d: he went through the whole thing — it does look better, he has seen three of the four formats — but **the last board doesn't show at all**. He has only ever seen it in mobile view, when it was squished narrow; on laptop it is not displaying. The fix was handed to the Expert Agent during the call.

Six minutes between "fixed" and "still not showing." The delivery told him the work was done; his eye told him it was not. The checker's proof was a drive-through that saw the board whole in its own session — but the build he opened did not show it at all. A claim delivered as done, disproved by the first look, costs more than the fix itself: it spends the trust the next delivery will need. The honest line was already in the delivery — "your eye is the real proof on this one" — but it was followed by "fixed" anyway. If his eye is the real proof, the word "fixed" waits until his eye has seen it.

## Why It Matters

"Fixed" is the strongest word in the machine — it closes the item, moves the list, and tells him to stop watching. Used before his eye has seen it, it closes an item that is still open and hands him a surprise instead of a result. The last board is the closing of the whole agreement — the title he picked, "Welcome to Naya," the payoff of 32 slides — and it is the board he has never once seen whole on his laptop. Every round that ends in a delivery message instead of his eye is a round that can come back like this one.

## How to Use It

Never say "fixed" until his eye has seen it fixed — say "ready for your eye," "staged for you to judge," or "the checker saw it whole, your eye is the proof." When a checker can only prove by driving through and its stills catch only the cover, name that limit in the same message as the delivery, not as a footnote after the claim. If a delivery has already gone out as "fixed" and his eye finds otherwise, say so in the open, in the same pass — the correction is the record, and the next "fixed" has to earn its meaning back.
