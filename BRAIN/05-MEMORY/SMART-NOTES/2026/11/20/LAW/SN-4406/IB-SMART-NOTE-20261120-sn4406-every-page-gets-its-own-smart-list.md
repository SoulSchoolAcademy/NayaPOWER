## In a Nutshell

Intelligent Library becomes the laws / constitutions / rules — a search engine over all the frames. The lists ARE the organization of their vault — their Naya brain. Save lands straight in (SN-4388) — no big list popping up first — and the lists are where what was saved lives, organized and reviewable, per page.

## Why It Matters

A save with nowhere to live is a save that is lost — the same failure as Smart Inviter, one level up. The vault holds everything; the lists are how a citizen finds anything in it again. One Smart List for the whole network would be one drawer for a whole house; a list per page means the thing you saved on Personal is waiting on Personal, and the calendar's list holds what the calendar saved.

## How to Use It

When adding a save destination, give the page its own Smart List area: Personal, Business, Your World, Calendar — each with its own lists of mail, DMs, worlds, connections, smart notes, Naya notes. Keep Smart Worlds named that on the sidebar, opening the list of all their worlds. Keep the Intelligent Library as the search engine over the laws, constitutions, and rules. Never invent a collection name he did not say — "Pinned worlds" was ours, not his, and it is corrected out.
