## Why It Matters

Reporting a recommendation as landed puts words in his mouth — the title on the board would carry a name he never chose, and the record would say he chose it. His pick is the only pick. The repair is honest only when it says, in the same breath, what changed and what did not.

## How to Use It

When a recommendation is offered, hold it as a recommendation until he picks it — "Let's go with that" or a named choice. Never prescribe a retitle to a recommended title in an addendum; prescribe the pick, or hold the retitle until the pick lands. When a repair completes, verify the exact change named — the title, not just the board — and report what did not change in the same message.
