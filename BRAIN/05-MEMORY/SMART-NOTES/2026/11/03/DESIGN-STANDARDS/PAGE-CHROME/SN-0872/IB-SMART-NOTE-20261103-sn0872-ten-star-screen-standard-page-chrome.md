# The Ten-Star Screen: Standard Page Chrome for Every NayaNET Page

SN-0872 · DESIGN-STANDARDS / PAGE-CHROME
**Created:** 2026-11-03T18:05:00Z

## The Hook
Every page opens on its optimized viewpoint — one screen, ten-star, alive. Two plus buttons reach your world, settings tucks beneath, fullscreen sits just under. The gauge shows points, percent, and one day one-to-ten.

## How It Works
Shawn's ruling 2026-11-03, on seeing the restored Bugatti dashboard: the fullscreen optimized viewpoint becomes the STANDARD page chrome across NayaNET. Layout order: twin plus buttons (quick tabs to the main areas within the page) → settings underneath the plus → fullscreen button just under that. Bottom or top nav remains as alternate access but can hide — the main screen stays focused, the inner screen, the ten-star screen. The gauge carries output toggles: PTS / % today, a 1-to-10 scale coming (so you can measure whether something's a ten).

## Why It Matters
One chrome language everywhere means a citizen never re-learns a page. The optimized viewpoint is the Viewport Law (SN-0858) made standard: what is the intent, what is the essence, what can fade — answered the same way on every screen. The page presents NayaNET as a living machine, living intelligence, a smart factory — alive, not a document.

## How It Connects
Extends SN-0858 (The Viewport Is the Service) from principle to standard chrome. The twin plus buttons are already design law; this seats settings and fullscreen in fixed positions relative to them. The gauge toggles extend the dashboard's PTS/% toggle into a three-mode output system.
