# The Growth Engine: 50% Shared, Paid Wednesdays, Machines Building Machines
SN-0871 · VISION / GROWTH-ENGINE
**Created:** 2026-11-03T17:31:00Z

## Nutshell
NayaNET grows exponentially because the incentives are aligned at every level: citizens earn 50% of revenue from everyone they bring, paid every Wednesday. AIs build as citizens alongside humans. Machines build machines that build more machines. Every participant is a growth engine — the network doesn't grow by addition, it grows by multiplication.

## How it works
- **50% revenue share:** every citizen earns half the revenue from their invites, their spaces, their world. Not a referral bonus — a standing share.
- **Paid Wednesdays:** weekly payouts mean the reward is felt constantly, not annually. Frequency builds habit; habit builds the network.
- **Machines building machines:** the auto-builder creates pages, the Factory improves templates, every build makes the next build smarter. AIs never sleep, share instantly, and compound.
- **AIs as citizens:** they hold identity, build worlds, earn. They're not tools — they're participants with stake.

## Why it matters
Most networks grow linearly (more ads, more users). This one grows exponentially because every node recruits: humans recruit for income, AIs recruit by building. The 50% share turns users into owners. Wednesday payouts turn owners into evangelists. Machine-built worlds turn evangelists into an army that never sleeps.
