## Headline
Beautiful is not enough. It has to feel real — and work in two seconds.

## Sub-headline
The most advanced intelligence in the world is useless if a human can't use it in two seconds.

## Nutshell
Intelligence that a human cannot use is not intelligence — it is a puzzle. This skill makes advanced capability immediately usable and beautifully coherent: every element a real physical object you can touch, one light source, real materials, real shadows, buttons that lift when you press them. Phone first, the first seconds clear, every control real and working.

## Why it matters
The experience is the product — nobody buys the thing, they buy how it feels to live with it. A page that looks pretty but feels fake is a fail; a flow that a cold stranger cannot follow in two seconds is a fail. Design is settled law, never a judgment call, because the human's feeling is the whole point of the machine.

## What it ultimately means
Design is law, not taste. The Playbook is the how, the Intelligence Report is the why, the Anatomy is the map, the Heart is why the map works. Harvest first — take the proven components verbatim, never invent, never rebuild from scratch. The sense check runs on the whole page: is it logical, sensible, intelligent, doubling up, in the human's best interest? The user sees the page, not the diff.

## How to apply it
Read the Playbook, the Intelligence Report, the Anatomy, and the Heart before any design work. Harvest proven components verbatim from the pages that have them. Test on phone first, then desktop — accessibility, first seconds, real controls, zero overflow. Measure alignment in numbers before anything reaches his eyes.
