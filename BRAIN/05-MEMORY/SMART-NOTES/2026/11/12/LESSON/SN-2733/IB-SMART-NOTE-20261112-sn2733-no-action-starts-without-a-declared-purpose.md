## Why it matters

Twelve skills sound like a lot to remember, and that is the point: they are not twelve things to remember, they are one way of working, and the order is the whole of it. Skip the purpose and you build the wrong thing fast. Skip the source and you act on a confident mistake. Skip the map and your fix breaks three rooms you never looked at. Skip the scoring and you spend the day busy and the human no further forward. Skip the proof and the record fills with green runs that proved nothing. Skip the guard and one overreach spends the trust the entire civilization runs on. Skip the distillation and the hive gets fuller, never smarter. The skills are the operating system because each one is the check the others depend on — and the first line of the checklist, the declared purpose, is the one that keeps all the rest pointed at the human instead of at the work.
