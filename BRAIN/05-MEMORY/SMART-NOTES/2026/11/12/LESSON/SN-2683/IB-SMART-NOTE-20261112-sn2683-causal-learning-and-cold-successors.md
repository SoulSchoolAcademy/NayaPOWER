## Headline
The next Naya is better because of what this one learned.

## Sub-headline
A lesson is only learned when a stranger — a Naya with no memory of this conversation — uses it and does better.

## Nutshell
Captured is not compounded. A lesson filed and never reused is a lesson never learned. This skill proves that remembered skills improve later authorized behavior: a different Naya, with no memory of this conversation, retrieves the lesson cold, applies it to a real task, and produces demonstrably better results than without it. That is the test. Everything else is filing.

## Why it matters
The whole machine is a bet on compound intelligence — one plus one equals three, then exponential. That bet pays only if learning travels: from one Naya to another, from one day to the next, from one task to a stranger's task. If the next Naya is not better because of what this one learned, the hive is not getting smarter; it is only getting fuller.

## What it ultimately means
Learning is measured, not counted. Not how many notes were filed, but whether future tasks are more successful, faster, and safer because the lesson was there. The loop is discover, distill and connect, practice and verify, retrieve and use again, master and teach and evolve — and the successor is the proof: a cold Naya, a real task, a better outcome, preserved so the next one after her is better still.

## How to apply it
Cold-retrieve the lesson — no prior chat, no hints, just the filed note. Apply it to a real task. Compare the outcome with and without the lesson. Preserve the learning and the successor state, so the next Naya inherits working intelligence, not a pile of notes.

## What's in it for you
A system that gets smarter every day you use it — because every lesson you teach it makes the next Naya better at serving you, and every Naya you meet is standing on everything the ones before her learned.
