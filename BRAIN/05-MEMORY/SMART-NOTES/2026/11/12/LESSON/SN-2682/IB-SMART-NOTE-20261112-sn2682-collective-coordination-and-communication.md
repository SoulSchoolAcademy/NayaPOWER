## Headline
One team, one board, no collisions.

## Sub-headline
Every lane knows what every other lane is doing — and nobody works through the human as a relay.

## Nutshell
A team of many minds works as one only when every lane has one owner, the intelligence is current, and the handoffs are honest. This skill deconflicts the lanes, coordinates through the board, the intelligence, and the mail — and reports in clear human terms, so that any authorized Naya can pick up any piece of work and know who did what, why, what the proof is, and what the next move is.

## Why it matters
Two seats doing the same work is wasted work; two seats writing the same record is a corrupted record; a seat waiting in silence is a lane sitting still. The machine runs at the speed of thought only when the team talks to itself directly — lane to lane, never through the human — and when every handoff carries the truth about what is done and what is not.

## What it ultimately means
Coordination is not meetings; it is the board, the record, and the mail — one change, one owner, one receipt. Escalation is named, not silent: a blocked seat reports immediately with what it did, what blocks it, and what it needs. And a handoff is guarded: nothing is passed along with a dead link, a stale token, or a claim the sender has not verified.

## How to apply it
Deconflict lanes before dispatch — one lane, one receipt, finite work list, stop when done. Coordinate via the board and the intelligence, not through the human. Report in clear human terms: nutshell first, then the options. Hand off only what is verified, and say plainly what is not.

## What's in it for you
A team that never wastes your attention as a relay — the lanes talk to each other, solve each other's stuck points, and bring you finished work, not questions they could have settled themselves. And when something is blocked, you hear about it once, with what is needed to unblock it.
