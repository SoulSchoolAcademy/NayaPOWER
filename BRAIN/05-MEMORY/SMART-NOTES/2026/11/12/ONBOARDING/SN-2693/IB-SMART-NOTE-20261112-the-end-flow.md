# SN-2693 · WALK / ONBOARDING / FLOW

## The End Flow — Names, Building, Pricing, Hub

**Hook:** The walk doesn't end at "you're hired" — it ends when you're standing inside pages built from your own words, and pricing comes after the proof, not before it.

**Nutshell:** Shawn extended the walk flow ~18:09Z Nov 12, asking — in his words — "doesn't that make sense, isn't that the right flow?" It is filed in MEMORY.md as THE END FLOW. After the ten questions there are TWO last pages. First, the page-names page, where the citizen enters each page's title + URL — the chime for the system to create the pages, because all along, as they fill the walk out, the system is already creating. Then the building page — "building it / wait a minute" — boom, done, "here's your pages", they click to view them. Then it redirects to the PRICING page — the whole product shown before they have decided to pay, and they choose their package. Then back from pricing, right into the HUB, and the Hub has all their pages. The data flow: the walk answers go into their Smart Box as they fill them out, and the system inputs that data into the pages and labels them — the pages are created from the walk, filled with their own words. The pages, especially where they get them, must be extraordinary. The flow was confirmed back to him as the right flow, and an addendum was sent to the walk fix-round coordinator (637427b6) with interrupt the same pass.

**Human:** The page-names page is the moment the system listens — their titles, their URLs, their names on their doors. The building page is the moment the system proves it: wait a minute, here they are. And pricing comes after the proof, not before — they see the whole product first, then choose. Put pricing first and you're selling a promise; put it after the pages and you're selling something they already hold.

**Child:** After the ten questions you name your pages, we build them while you wait, and then you see them — boom, here are your pages. Then you see the prices and pick one, and then you're home in the Hub with everything you made.

**Grandma:** After your ten questions, you give each page a name, we build them for you while you wait, and then you see them — here are your pages. Only then do you see the prices and choose, and then you're home, with all your pages waiting for you.

**Naya:** (1) The sequence: ten questions → page-names page (title + URL per page) → building page → "here's your pages" → pricing (whole product, choose a package) → Hub with all their pages. (2) The data flow: walk answers land in the Smart Box as they are given; the pages are created from the walk, labelled with the citizen's own words. (3) The standard: the pages, especially where they get them, must be extraordinary. (4) The state: ruled 18:09Z, filed in MEMORY.md in chat; the end-flow addendum was sent to the walk fix-round coordinator with interrupt the same pass.

**What changes now:** (1) The walk fix round carries the end flow — a walk that ends at the end slide, or shows pricing before the pages exist, is missing the end of the flow. (2) The page-names page is the chime: the citizen's titles + URLs are what the system creates from. (3) The Correction Compound items for this ruling — Factory/inventory + brief-template updates carrying the End Flow — join the owed list.
