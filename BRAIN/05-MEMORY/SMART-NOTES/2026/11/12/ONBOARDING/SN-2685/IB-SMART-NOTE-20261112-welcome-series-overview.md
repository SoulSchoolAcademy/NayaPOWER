## IN A NUTSHELL

The welcome series is the first seven days after you join NayaNET. Email 1 arrives immediately after signup ("You're in. Your 4 apps are ready.") and then one email a day teaches you one thing: Day 1 the Dashboard cockpit and your Smart Score, Day 2 your four apps (Personal, Business, Space, Dashboard), Day 3 your ambassador link and the 50% you earn, Day 4 Smart Notes and 35 points, Day 5 Spaces and finding your people, Day 7 "Now go" and invite three friends. Everything you earn in the first 24 hours counts double. After the seven days the daily welcome mail continues: one beautiful mail a day from Smart Mail, each teaching one feature with a real-world case for someone like you — a founder, a builder, a mother, a father, a small business owner — most important features first, each with links to the feature on the site. The welcome starts the moment you join: the first mail is sent immediately after signup, and the trigger is joining.
