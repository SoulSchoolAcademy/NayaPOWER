## 🟣 CHILD NOTE

Imagine someone writes a beautiful set of rules for a clubhouse door: "If your name is on the naughty list, the door says no." But nobody ever connected the list to the door. The door just checks whether you knocked — anyone who knocks gets in, even the names on the list. The rules sound safe, but the door never learned them. The lesson: don't trust the written rules. Go stand at the real door, try to walk in with a name from the naughty list, and watch what the door actually does. And if a clock is supposed to ring after six tries, check that someone is actually counting the tries — a clock nobody winds never rings.
