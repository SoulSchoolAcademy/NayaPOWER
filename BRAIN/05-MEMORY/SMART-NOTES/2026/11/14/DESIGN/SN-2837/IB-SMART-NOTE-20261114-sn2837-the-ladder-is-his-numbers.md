## Hook

The ladder is his numbers, never guessed: the shape is 1 / 10 / 100 / unlimited, and each row carries his numbers for that row — Personal, Business, Spaces at 1/10/100; Smart Notes and Naya Notes at 10/100/1,000; Connections at 100/1,000/10,000; Project Intelligence at 3/10/100/unlimited projects. ## What it means

1. **Never guess a ladder.** The shape is 1 / 10 / 100 / unlimited, and each row carries his numbers for that row — the counts are his word, never invented.
2. **The flow is green → blue → purple → gold.** Never green → purple → blue. The spectrum is the common colour language, and now it has its sequence.
3. **The headliner is a trillion-dollar plaque, a real object.** Text in a box is the worst design on the planet; if it can't be built to the law, it doesn't ship.
4. **Zero gold of any shade in the entrance.** And the test is his eye, not a grep — a measurement that disagrees with the eye is a measurement of the wrong thing.
5. **Any letter in a number tile is colored by its gematria root number.** K is three; three is gold; the K tile is gold — copied byte for byte, never invented.

## Why it matters

1. A ladder built on guessed rungs is a chart that lies in every cell; a ladder built on his numbers is a chart that can be trusted in every cell.
2. A colour language with two colours swapped teaches the wrong language — every citizen who learns "blue means Pro" from the chart will misread every page that says blue means Power.
3. A flat banner at the head of the page is not a small defect — it sets the ceiling for everything below it. If the first thing a stranger sees is text in a box, they have already learned what the page is: a document, not a world.
4. A control nobody recognises is worse than a missing control — a missing control is honest, but an unrecognised one teaches the visitor that the page's marks mean nothing, and then no mark on the page can be trusted.

## What changes now

1. Record the ladder as he changed it, not as it was settled — mark the old ladder superseded, or the next builder will build the old numbers believing they are settled.
2. Split what is really two things — Smart Meet into Video and Audio; Smart Feed and Today's Intel into two rows — and count each honestly.
3. Wear the spectrum in order: Free green, Power blue, Pro purple, Max gold; group headers flow the same way down the page.
4. Take all gold writing out of the entrance; the brand is purple, black and white, elegant — white diamond, black diamond, royalty.
5. Reverse every popup: nutshell first at the top, tier summary boxes at the bottom — never out front.
6. The popup trigger is a white "i" in a circle — raised like a jewel, identical on all 36 rows, so anyone knows at a glance: tap here and you learn about this element.
