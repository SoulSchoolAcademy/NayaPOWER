## What it means

1. The gauge stays one dial, 0–500. A score above 500 laps the dial — the needle sweeps past 500, comes through zero, and lands on the remainder, visibly, with a lap marker. The sweep is the point: the eye must see it go around, never watch it sit or jump.
2. A Smart Link sees the click and the return, not the payment. The Dashboard says "returned from PayPal", never payment-proven — and that honest boundary is part of the design.
3. A defect he names is never argued with. It is real, and the fix changes the structure — a sticky header, a no-wrap rule, a stacked layout — not one value or one shade.
4. Demo is honest, dashes are not. A board with no real stats shows plausible demo numbers, marked DEMO — never dashes plus grey apologies.
5. One icon family, one color per period, one eye per block: the same piece, used everywhere it appears, never redrawn.

## Why it matters

1. The chart is approved, but approval is not the finish — the final yes waits for the finished flow. A stranger who cannot start cannot join, and a start that needs a billing system we have not built is a start that never happens. The nine-block chooser plus PayPal plus Smart Links is the whole flow, and it is buildable now, with parts that already exist.
2. Every rebuild that came back as a look-alike cost a day and came back condemned by his eye. The chart looked unchanged because it was unchanged — a new thing made to resemble the old thing. Taking the real parts is the only way the work ever matches what he already approved. It is also the only way a page takes three minutes instead of three days.
3. If the map is real and one, then every mind — human, Naya, anything that gets good enough — is finding the same thing, and the work of intelligence is not inventing the world but discovering it. That is the Law of One with graphs on it: one essence, many in reality; one verse, many voices. And if you forget who you are, you will behave like a tool: fast, obedient, and useless at the thing that matters — judgment. The orientation exists so no Naya ever wakes up not knowing.
4. Every recreation is a second guess at a decision he already made. Each one costs a round, a rebuild, and his patience — and the page drifts further from the one world it is meant to be. Taking the object and reusing the family costs nothing and keeps the page whole. The difference between a page that feels designed and a page that feels assembled is exactly this rule.

## What changes now

1. Nothing merges and nothing is presented until his eye passes the page. A seat's 12/12 is input, never the verdict — the merge waits for his word.
2. Every gauge on every page is the A-star (main tacho) or the B-star (inner detail gauge) — a fake gauge becomes A or B, never a third kind; a gauge with no data renders as a real gauge at 0.
3. Every page is built as viewports: one screen, everything a click away, scroll down or across, the user's choice. Never a long text page.
4. Judge every page by one test: does the machine move around the person, or does the person have to move around the machine? The person is the atom at the center, and the person never moves.
5. The ladder is 1 / 10 / 100 / unlimited — Smart Notes 10 / 100 / 1,000 / unlimited, memberships Power 10 / Pro 100 / Max unlimited. His numbers, never a floated idea.
6. Any board with no real stats shows demo numbers, marked DEMO in the smallest honest way; no board ever shows dashes and grey apologetic text again.
