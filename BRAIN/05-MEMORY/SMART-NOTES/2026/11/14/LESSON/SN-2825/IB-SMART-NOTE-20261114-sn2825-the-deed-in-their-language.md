## Human

That is why the same afternoon produced four different answers to one question: the unfold answers "how do I read it without drowning", the little eyes answer "what does this word mean", the hook answers "why should I care", and the language wiring answers "can I read it in my own words at all". Each one is the same job from a different side. And the failure notes are the same job from the other side: a promise that never settles is a deed that never arrives; a proof with no record is work that never happened; an intake measured as a batch is a verdict that never gets said. The machine's lesson for this group says it plainly: the wiring covers the whole walk including the new end and the agreement — the deed must be understood in their language.

## What it means

1. **THE UNFOLD IS THE READING:** the agreement opens block by block on the reader's tap — never a wall of text, never forced — with one button that unfolds everything at once, the reader's choice always.
2. **EVERY HARD WORD GETS ITS PLAIN MEANING:** a little eye beside it, in place, saying what the word means in his words, verbatim — the land is the domain, the building is the website, the business is NayaNET, the brand is Naya, the job is showing, not selling.
3. **THE HOOK OPENS IT:** "They say AI is taking jobs… what if AI created income for everybody?" — the fear acknowledged, the question asked, the invitation offered, then the naming: collective agreement, collective chain technology.
4. **THE WIRING COVERS THE WHOLE WALK:** detect the language at the beginning, or let them choose — display in their language, voice English by default — including the new end and the agreement. Before building a feature, check whether it exists unconnected.
5. **FAILURE FAILS HONESTLY:** every promise that can hang gets a deadline — at the deadline it fails in plain words, with the control back and a way to try again. "Sending…" forever is a defect, not a state.

## Why it matters

1. Without plain words in their own language, the deed is paper, not a promise — the reader signs what they cannot read, and trust never forms. The unfold, the eyes, the hook, and the wiring are not decoration on the agreement; they are what makes it an agreement at all. A beautiful page nobody understands sells nothing.
2. Without the whole-walk wiring, the best translation engine in the world changes nothing — the engine exists and is proven, and the walk still speaks only English. The gap between "exists" and "connected" is where good work dies unseen, and the only cure is checking the worker before assuming the gap.
3. Without honest failure, people wait forever on a promise that will never settle — and waiting without an answer is worse than a no, because a no lets them try again. A control that never settles teaches the user the system cannot be trusted, and that lesson sticks longer than any feature.
4. Without a recorded proof, the work never happened — the maker who errors with no record and no push leaves nothing behind, and the finisher must re-prove everything itself. The record is not paperwork after the work; it is the work, made to count.
