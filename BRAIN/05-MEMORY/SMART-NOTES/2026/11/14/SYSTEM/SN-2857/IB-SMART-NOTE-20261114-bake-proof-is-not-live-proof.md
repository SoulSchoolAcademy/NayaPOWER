## Why it matters

Proof has a scope, and a proof outside its scope is a claim wearing a proof's clothes. The count proves the count. The bake proves the bake. Neither proves the card, and neither proves the live view — and the card and the live view are the only things a citizen ever sees. The defect was found because a human read a card end to end, not because a counter counted to seventeen. That is the discipline: every proof names what it covers, and the thing it does not cover gets its own check, by an eye, on the live thing. A round that closes the count and leaves the card open is honest only if it says so in the same breath.
