## Why it matters

Every verification system drifts toward checking what is easy to check — the fixture, the programmatic fire, the byte count — and away from what matters, which is what the human sees. The drift is invisible because the checks keep passing. The only cure is a rule that cannot be gamed by a fixture: the eye reads the live page, adds the numbers, clicks the slot, and the page is whole only when the eye says so. That is why the double-check exists, and why its FAIL outranks a mechanism PASS — the mechanism checks the machine, the eye checks the experience, and the experience is the product.

## What changes now

1. A mechanism PASS never outranks an eye FAIL. The eye is the ground truth on what a page SHOWS.
2. Numbers on a page must add up as a human reads them — parts sum exactly, labels say honestly what the number is, the pill agrees with the live Dashboard or says plainly what its number is.
3. A real click is the test for anything clickable — a programmatic fire is not a click.
