## Why it matters

A verification system that only ever confirms the checker is a rubber stamp; a verification system that only ever overturns the checker is an adversary. The eye is neither — it is the ground truth, and ground truth corrects both ways. That is what makes the double-check trustworthy: when it clears a fail, the maker can believe the clear; when it overturns a pass, the maker can believe the overturn. The day the eye stops correcting in one direction is the day it has stopped seeing. And the method is the lesson: click what a human would click. A cell can be plain while its icon is a link — the defect lives in the icon, and only a click finds it.
