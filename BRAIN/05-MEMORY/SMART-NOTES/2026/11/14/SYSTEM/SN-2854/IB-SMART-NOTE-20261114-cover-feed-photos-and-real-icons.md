## Why it matters

"Are those the highest level of buttons we can offer?" is the right question, because the answer is a test, not an opinion: the bottom icons are the standard, and anything below them is not finished. A dot-only button fails the test in the open — it cannot say what it is, so the citizen has to guess, and guessing is not ten-star service. The same test, applied to photos, is respect for the citizen's own image: a forced crop is the system deciding what matters in someone else's photo. Naming the size, giving the editor, keeping the shape — that is the system handing the choice back. The standard is one language, everywhere, or it is not a standard.
