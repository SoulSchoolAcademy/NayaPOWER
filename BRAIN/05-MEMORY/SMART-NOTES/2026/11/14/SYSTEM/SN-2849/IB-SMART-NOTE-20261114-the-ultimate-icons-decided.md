## Human

The question was a real fork — two candidate sets, both already built somewhere, both defensible — and the answer is not a compromise, it is a separation of jobs. The five are the bottom bar because the bottom bar is for doing: create, mail, activity, home, ecosystem — the five verbs of the citizen's day, one set on every page. The trio is not a bottom bar at all; it is the world switcher, and it lives where switching happens — the Personal page and the world picker — with the labels Personal / Business / Thing, because "Work" is not a world. Each set gets the job it is shaped for, and neither has to pretend to be the other. The build is his recommendation, taken verbatim: obsidian middle, coloured rim and glow, white-white glyph, the plus glyph straight white. And the quality bar is not a mood, it is a reference object already on the page: the PTS/%/MONEY pills are the highest quality, so every icon is judged against them — if an icon does not hit that level, it is not done. The two candidates collapse into one answer, and the collapse is the lesson: when two good things compete for one place, the answer is usually not to pick the prettier one, but to notice they were never competing for the same job.

## Why it matters

A system with two icon sets competing for the same bar is a system arguing with itself in front of the citizen. The decision ends the argument by giving each set its job: doing lives at the bottom, switching lives where switching happens. And the quality bar — the pills — turns "best of the best" from a feeling into a test any maker can run with their own eyes. That is what a decision is for: not to end the discussion, but to give the next hundred makers the same answer without having to ask again.
