## Why it matters

A system that pays people is a system that will be gamed, and the first big run is not a hypothetical — it is the threat the whole protection exists for. One layer would fail: the cap alone is walked around, the hold alone punishes the honest, the score alone has to be defended. Three layers together mean the heist becomes a drip, the drip is visible, the money is cleared before it moves, and the gamers sort themselves out of the line without anyone being accused. And the ladder matters just as much: a cap set too low tells the honest ambassador their best month is worth $3,000, and a system that caps the honest earner at the same level as the thief has already chosen the thief's side. $300 first, climbing — the protection guards the house and the earner both.
