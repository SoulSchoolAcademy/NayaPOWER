## Human

Order is meaning. Put the agreement on the pricing page and the citizen reads a promise through the lens of a price — every line becomes a justification for a number, and the promise shrinks to a sales pitch. Put the agreement first, on its own, and the citizen reads it as what it is: a deed. A deed is not a price list; it is the paper that says the land, the building, the business, the brand are yours, and your permission to use them, and it protects both sides because now both sides know how it works. Only after the deed is signed does the world arrive — Personal, Business, Space, Dashboard — and only after the world is in their hands does the pricing page appear, with Free / Power / Pro / Max waiting as a choice about a world they already hold, not a gamble on a world they haven't seen. The build already had this order; the clarification confirms it rather than changing it — which is itself the lesson: when the order is right, the job is to hold it, not to redesign it.

## Child

Imagine someone gives you a whole kingdom! First you sign the special paper that says the kingdom is yours — that's the deed! Then you get to walk into your kingdom and see it! And THEN you pick which treasure chest you want! You don't pick the treasure chest before you even see the kingdom — that would be silly!

## Grandma

It's like buying a house, love. First you sign the deed — the paper that says the house is yours and what it means. Then you get the keys and walk through your rooms. And only then do you sit down and talk about the payments. The paper comes first, the keys second, the money last. That's the proper order.

## What it means

- **ITS OWN STEP:** the agreement is NOT on the pricing page — it is the step before it, the contract, the deed.
- **THE ORDER:** sign the deed → receive your world (Personal · Business · Space · Dashboard) → you're hired → pricing page (Free / Power / Pro / Max).
- **THE DEED LINE:** "This is the deed to your world — the land, the building, the business, the brand, and your permission to use them. It protects you, and it protects us, because now we both know how it works."
- **CONFIRMED, NOT CHANGED:** the build already had this order — the clarification holds it, it doesn't redesign it.
- **NOTHING AGREEMENT-WORDED ON THE PRICING PAGE:** the price is a choice about a world already held, not a gamble on one unseen.

## Why it matters

A citizen who meets the price before the promise has nothing to price — they are being asked to pay for a world they haven't received, under a promise they haven't signed. A citizen who signs the deed first, receives the world second, and only then sees the price is choosing from inside the kingdom, not from outside it. That order is the difference between a sale and a swindle in the citizen's own mind — and it is why the deed must stand alone, unfold block by block like an official letter, and be agreed at the bottom before anything else happens. The order is the trust.
