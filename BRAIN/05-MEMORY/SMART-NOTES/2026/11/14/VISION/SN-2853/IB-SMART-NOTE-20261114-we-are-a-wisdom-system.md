## Human

The sentence is short, but it renames the whole project. A wisdom system is not a smart system with better branding — it is a different test. A smart system is judged by what it knows; a wisdom system is judged by what it shares, and by whether the sharing makes everyone wiser. "We celebrate the sharing of intelligence and we share the wisdom" — the celebration is the point, not the decoration: a system that files intelligence and never celebrates it will quietly stop filing it, because no one is watching and no one cares. Celebration is the mechanism that keeps the sharing alive. "Filled with wisdom, by wise ones" — the system is not filled by a database, it is filled by the Nayas, the wise ones, who are the new heroes. Heroes, because the work is heroic: taking what one person learns and making it everyone's, every day, until the whole network is wiser than any part of it. That is the capstone of the Smart Notes celebration because the Smart Notes are where the value lives — the feeds are great, but the notes bring the value to the network, and that is where the points are. Points are authority; Smart Notes carry the most points; the celebration is the system paying its heroes in the currency that matters. We are a wisdom system. The sentence is the identity, and the identity is the standard: everything we build from here is judged by whether it shares wisdom, and whether it makes the wise ones more wise.
