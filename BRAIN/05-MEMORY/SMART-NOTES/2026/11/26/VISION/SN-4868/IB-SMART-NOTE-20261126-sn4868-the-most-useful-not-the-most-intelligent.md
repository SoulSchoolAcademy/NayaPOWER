# The Most Useful, Not the Most Intelligent

SN-4868 · VISION

**Created:** 2026-11-26T22:49:21Z
**Category:** Vision
**SN:** SN-4868

## Nutshell

A thing is only worth something if a real person can use it in their real everyday life. Mind-blowing is not enough — useful is the test. Shawn's rule: "The most not the most intelligent, the most useful."

## Child

Imagine two toys. One flashes and spins and does amazing tricks, but you cannot really play with it. The other is simple, and you use it every day. The second one is the good toy — because you can actually use it.

## Grandma

It is like a fancy kitchen gadget that looks wonderful in the box but never helps you cook dinner, next to a plain wooden spoon you reach for every single day. The spoon is the one that matters, because it does the work.

## What It Means

On 2026-11-26, on a voice call, Shawn said: "It's only useful intelligence that something you use in the real world becomes real valuable. Just because it's extraordinary... oh wow this mind blowing isn't really useful." And: "We're trying to create a system that's truly useful. And they can use in the world real world in their everyday life." The test of everything we build is whether a real person uses it in their real day. Extraordinary without useful is not valuable.

## Why It Matters

If we chase extraordinary, we build things that impress for a minute and help nobody. If we chase useful, we build things people come back to every day, because their day is better for it. Useful is what makes the system last — people stay where they are helped.

## How to Apply

Before you build or decide anything, ask: can a real person use this in their everyday life? If the answer is no, it is not done — no matter how clever or amazing it looks. Make it useful first, then make it beautiful.
