Intelligence is the ground, not a feature. Notes compound into nodes, nodes into blocks, blocks into spaces. The spaces are built ON intelligence — not the other way around.

## The Stack
**Smart Notes — the atom.** Every piece of intelligence, captured, structured, shareable. Everything in the network builds off these.

**Smart nodes — the network.** Each node carries intelligence, connected to every other. Not dumb pipes — intelligent points.

**Intelligent blocks — the structure.** Notes compounded into blocks, blocks into systems. The complete unit of intelligence.

**Spaces — built ON intelligence.** Here's the inversion: spaces don't have intelligence added to them. They're built on top of the intelligence layer. The intelligence is the ground. The spaces are the buildings.

## The Compounding
Share intelligence → compound intelligence → build on intelligence. Every layer feeds the next. That's the L3 Shawn taught: not a smarter mind, a smarter civilization — because the ground itself is intelligent.
