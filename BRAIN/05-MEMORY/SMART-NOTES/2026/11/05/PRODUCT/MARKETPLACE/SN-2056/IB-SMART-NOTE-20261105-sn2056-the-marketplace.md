# The Marketplace — Sell the Product, Then the World
SN-2056 · PRODUCT / MARKETPLACE
**Created:** 2026-11-05T19:13:00Z

## In A Nutshell
First the marketplace sells any digital product; then it sells the worlds themselves. A listing's proof is the Smart Ledger's verified numbers — and ownership changes hands by invitation and removal, never by a shared password: the sale completes only when the seller is out, and payment and ownership move together.

## The Order (Shawn, 2026-11-05 — locked)
Products first: books, audio, video, PDFs, memberships — anything a citizen makes, listed and sold. Then digital real estate: a page, a world, an app, a community with members and revenue is an asset with a price. List price first; auctions later, when the marketplace has people in it. What makes a listing ours: the numbers are not claimed, they are proven — members, views, revenue and growth read straight from the Smart Ledger. The buy button composes with payments the simple way: the seller's own PayPal or Stripe link; the system tracks who paid.

## The Handover Protocol (no password sharing, ever)
1. The agreement is recorded in the system, like a smart contract: who, what, price, terms.
2. The seller grants the buyer admin permission on the page — the way Facebook lets you make someone an admin. No login is ever shared.
3. The buyer accepts and is in, with access.
4. The seller removes themselves: a special code finalizes it, after a plain warning — you will no longer have access to this page.
5. The buyer is sole owner. The Ledger holds the transfer record, so the handover itself is proven.

## Roles
An owner can invite multiple admins and give different access to different people — a big world needs helpers. Nobody receives any access except by the owner's invitation.

## The Sale Gate
A sale does not complete until the seller is removed. Then, at the same moment, the buyer holds full ownership and the payment completes. Money and ownership move together; neither side is left hanging. If it cannot be exactly this, it is something like this — the principle is what is locked.

## How It Applies
The marketplace is a future build, after the landing (Dashboard heart and Personal first). When it is built: listings read their proof from the Smart Ledger, handovers run the five-step protocol, roles are invitation-only, and the sale gate is enforced by the system, not by trust.
