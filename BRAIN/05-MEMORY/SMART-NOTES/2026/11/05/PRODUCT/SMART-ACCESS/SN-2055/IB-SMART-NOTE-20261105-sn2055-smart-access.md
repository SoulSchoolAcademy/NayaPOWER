# Smart Access — The Lock, Not the Course
SN-2055 · PRODUCT / SMART-ACCESS
**Created:** 2026-11-05T19:13:00Z

## In A Nutshell
A membership is a lock, not a course. Smart Access pages put anything digital — one video, one audio, a PDF, a book, a whole course — behind a login only the right people pass: those who paid, or those the owner lets in free for a taste.

## The Ruling (Shawn, 2026-11-05)
Memberships are the container: the easiest way for a citizen to store everything they have and give access only to the right people. A lock on the door does not mean a full course behind it — it could be a single video, an audio, a PDF, or all of it, customized however the owner wishes. Free memberships and free tasters are part of the design: a taste in front, depth behind the next door. Products carry their own ambassadors — someone who loves a product gets the owner's link, shares it inside the network, and earns when people buy through it.

## The Name (settled by Shawn, 2026-11-05)
They are called **Smart Access pages** — always. Plans say it plainly: "You get X unlimited Smart Access pages," with an info note that explains it in one breath: memberships, exclusive content unlock — special logins for exclusive content, valuable courses and content, any digital products. "Paywall" stays only as the plain translation for outsiders.

## How It Applies
Every membership surface, plan page and product door in NayaNET uses the Smart Access name and the lock model: store anything digital behind it, price it or give it free, let ambassadors spread it. The membership door inside the app morphs (Personal, Business, Space, Membership) carries Smart Access pages as its sellable layer.
