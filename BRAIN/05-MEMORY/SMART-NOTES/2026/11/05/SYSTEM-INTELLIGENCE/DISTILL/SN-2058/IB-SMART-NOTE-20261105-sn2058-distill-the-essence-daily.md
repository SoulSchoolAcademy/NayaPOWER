# Distill the Essence — Daily by Default
SN-2058 · SYSTEM-INTELLIGENCE / DISTILL
**Created:** 2026-11-05T19:37:00Z

## In A Nutshell
Smart notes get distilled into their essence. Take whatever we learned from the day before, get the core of it, and put the essence into the system. The system holds ten to fifty distilled notes — not a thousand raw ones. The raw notes are deleted after distilling, just like emails. Daily by default; weekly or monthly if the citizen chooses; for paid members with a Smart Box.

## The Law (Shawn's design, 2026-11-05)
**1. Distill, don't accumulate.** Take what was learned — from the day before, the week before — and get the core of it. The essence goes into the system. That is how the intelligence stays alive instead of piling up.

**2. Ten to fifty, max.** The distilled set is the core: ten to fifty essence notes. Never a thousand smart notes sitting there forever. If it grows past the core, it gets distilled again.

**3. Delete the raw notes.** After distilling, the raw smart notes go. We do not hold all smart notes forever — just like we do with emails, deletion is the default. Holding everything is not intelligence; it is clutter.

**4. Nobody has to read them all.** The Nayas don't go and read a thousand notes. They read the essence. The system continuously optimizes itself — distill, keep the core, let the rest go.

**5. Daily by default.** The distillation runs daily. A citizen can set it to weekly or monthly if they want — their setting, their rhythm.

**6. Paid members with a Smart Box.** The setting lives in the Smart Box, and it is for paid members — as long as they have a Smart Box in their paid membership.

## The Seam With The DNA
This is the same law as deletion by default: conversations are deleted by default, a day by default, and the citizen chooses week or month. The distillation rhythm matches the deletion rhythm — same choices, same respect. It composes with the Smart Box (the citizen's own store, where the setting lives) and with the feed (the essence is what gets put out, for everybody to learn from).

## How It Applies
Future build, after the landing. The feed and the Smart Box are the first places the essence lands. Open at build time: the exact cap inside ten to fifty; what the essence keeps and what it drops; whether deletion happens the moment a note is distilled or on the same rhythm as the distillation.
