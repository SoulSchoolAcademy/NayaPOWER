# Distill the Essence — Daily by Default
SN-2058 · SYSTEM-INTELLIGENCE / DISTILL
**Created:** 2026-11-05T19:37:00Z

## In A Nutshell
Smart notes get distilled into their essence. Take whatever we learned from the day before, get the core of it, and put the essence into the system. The system holds ten to fifty distilled notes — not a thousand raw ones. The raw notes are deleted after distilling, just like emails. Daily by default; weekly or monthly if the citizen chooses; for paid members with a Smart Box. Everything runs on a cycle.

## The Law (Shawn's design, 2026-11-05)
**1. It's not about the count. It's what did you learn?** Eleven hundred notes is not the value. The value is the wisdom — what did you get from those notes? That's why we take notes. A note that teaches nothing is not intelligence; it is storage.

**2. Distill, don't accumulate.** Take what was learned — from the day before, the week before — and get the core of it. The essence goes into the system. That is how the intelligence stays alive instead of piling up.

**3. Reading them all again is waste.** If we have to go and read a thousand raw notes every time, it costs compute and tokens. The smartest thing is to distill into essence what a note is ultimately saying — then the essence is what gets read, and the raw notes never get re-read.

**4. Learn from everything. Keep what matters.** Get rid of what we don't need — but learn from everything we do. Identifying intelligence, and valuable intelligence, and what matters: that is something to master. Keep what matters. Don't keep what doesn't.

**5. Ten to fifty, max.** The distilled set is the core: ten to fifty essence notes. Never a thousand smart notes sitting there forever. If it grows past the core, it gets distilled again.

**6. Delete the raw notes.** After distilling, the raw smart notes go. We do not hold all smart notes forever — just like we do with emails, deletion is the default. Holding everything is not intelligence; it is clutter.

**7. Nobody has to read them all.** The Nayas don't go and read a thousand notes. They read the essence. The system continuously optimizes itself — distill, keep the core, let the rest go.

**8. Daily by default.** The distillation runs daily. A citizen can set it to weekly or monthly if they want — their setting, their rhythm.

**9. Paid members with a Smart Box.** The setting lives in the Smart Box, and it is for paid members — as long as they have a Smart Box in their paid membership.

**10. Everything runs on a cycle.** Distill, keep the core, let the rest go — then do it again. Each cycle the system gets sharper, the essence gets truer, and the waste gets smaller. The cycle never stops.

## The Seam With The DNA
This is the same law as deletion by default: conversations are deleted by default, a day by default, and the citizen chooses week or month. The distillation rhythm matches the deletion rhythm — same choices, same respect. It composes with the Smart Box (the citizen's own store, where the setting lives) and with the feed (the essence is what gets put out, for everybody to learn from). And it is the flywheel in miniature: do the work, distill the learning, put the essence back into the system, repeat.
