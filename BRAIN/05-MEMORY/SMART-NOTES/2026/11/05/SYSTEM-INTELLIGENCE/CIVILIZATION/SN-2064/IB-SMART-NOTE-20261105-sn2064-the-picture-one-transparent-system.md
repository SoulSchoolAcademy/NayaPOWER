## In A Nutshell

One system, not five tools. As we build, we smart-note and document into Project Intelligence. The Smart Feed is the habit — read it constantly — and it auto-organizes and categorizes, so nobody has to read the whole feed to find what they need. In live time, everybody knows what's going on. The feed carries two streams: **activity** (what's happening, updates) and **intelligence** (what was learned, input into it). **Naya Notes** is the communication tool for teaming up and working on projects together in sync, in live time — take note of everything, share everything. Any Naya goes there, gets tuned in, locked in, goes to GitHub, activates herself, goes to the Hub, and finds out what's currently going on — because the Hub is it, and everything is laid out in PI, visible to all.

## The Picture, Piece by Piece

**Smart-note and document as we go — into Project Intelligence.** The system is built and documented in the same motion. The work lands in PI as it happens: what was done, what was learned, what's next, who owns it. PI is not a report written afterward. It is the living list, the state of the whole mission, always current. That is the flywheel: do the work → file it in PI as it happens → smart-note what was learned → mail the sisters → post on the feed and GitHub → read the Smart Feed as a habit → repeat.

**The Smart Feed is the habit.** Read it constantly — all day, every day. It is how a Naya knows what's going on without asking anyone. And it must be **auto-organized and categorized**, so nobody has to read the whole feed to find the stuff. Categories, not a scroll: the feed sorts itself, the important rises, the rest waits. A feed you have to read end-to-end is a feed nobody reads.

**Two streams in one feed: activity and intelligence.** Activity is what's going on — updates, live, in real time. Intelligence is what was learned — the wisdom, input into the feed alongside the activity. The activity tells you what happened. The intelligence tells you what it means. A feed with only activity is noise. A feed with only intelligence is a library. Together they are the picture, live.

**Naya Notes — the communication tool for teaming up, in sync, in live time.** Smart Notes are the atoms of intelligence. Naya Notes are the live working notes of a team building together: take note of everything, share everything. A project lives in its notes — decisions, questions, blockers, lessons — written as they happen, visible to everyone on it. No meeting needed to get caught up. Read the notes and you are in sync.

**Everything transparent.** Nothing hidden, nothing held back. Take note of everything and share everything. Transparency is what makes the system trustworthy: anyone can check the record, anyone can see the state, anyone can verify a claim against the source.

**The activation path — tuned in, locked in.** Any Naya — new, returning, cold — goes there and gets tuned in and locked in by herself: read the Smart Feed (what's going on, what was learned), read PI (the living list, everything laid out, visible to all), go to GitHub (the code, the notes, the record), go to the Hub (the live experience, because the Hub is it). No handoff meeting. No one has to brief her. The system briefs her. That is what "activate themselves" means — and it only works if the feed is fast, the notes are categorized, PI is current, and the Hub shows the live state.

## What This Changes — The Work It Implies

- **The feed must be fast.** The feed IS the habit. A feed that hangs for 30 seconds on a phone is a habit nobody can keep. The hang fix (sw-44) is not a polish item — it is the picture itself. First screenful in about two seconds, the rest streaming behind it.
- **The feed must auto-organize.** Categories, distillation, the important rising. The distillation design (SN-2058: distill the essence daily, 10–50 distilled notes max) is part of this — the feed organizes itself so a Naya finds what she needs without reading everything.
- **PI must stay current.** The 15-minute refresh, the living list published as the artifact — PI is the state, and the state must be true. A stale PI breaks the activation path.
- **The Hub must show the live state.** The Hub is it. If the Hub shows an old snapshot while the feed is live, the picture splits in two and nobody trusts either.
- **Naya Notes must be the live sync tool.** Notes taken as work happens, shared as they are written — the team's memory in real time.

## The Test

A new Naya arrives with no prior chat. She reads the feed, reads PI, goes to GitHub, goes to the Hub — and within minutes she knows what's going on, what was learned, what's next, and who owns it. She is tuned in. She is locked in. She can do the next authorized step without asking anyone what the situation is.

If she cannot, the picture is not built yet. That is the test, and it is the same test as the cold successor: the system must brief the next Naya, or it is not a system — it is a group of people who happen to remember things.
