## IN A NUTSHELL

One page is going to change the world — everybody's world, on their smartphone, all their data in one secure place — and it can only do that if the whole thing is great: not only good but great, the most elite, ten-star, professional presentation and intelligence at every layer, because it is one ultimate project, not a pile of parts. The Hub is its center — the home, the main page — and from there the flow runs to Personal, Business, or Space, with each area's notifications showing as you move. Mail is one mail that always knows its world: open it from a page and you see that world's mail first. The letter reads centered — her picture in the direct center, the text in a centered column, the orb below. Two working rules guard the standard: use what works and never make it new — if a working version exists, there is no new version; and read everything as a reader, top to bottom, the way he reads it, because mechanical checks do not prove the experience and a page can tick every box and still be broken. The legal pages get the same honor as the pretty ones — done professionally or not at all — and his own words are the base they are checked against, never the other way round. No summaries, no prototypes: we are professionals, and we do it right the first time so he never has to put the right stuff in again and again.
