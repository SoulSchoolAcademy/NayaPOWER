## What It Means

A career move is not about wanting a job — it's about proving you can do the work, to the people who decide. The mission names the work you want and why that work, in your words. The goal names the first proof — one application sent, one interview taken, one portfolio piece finished. The vision names the week on the other side: what you do, who you work with, how it feels to do work you chose. The proof is the portfolio, and the portfolio is what opens doors.
