## IN A NUTSHELL

A standard you can point at beats a standard you can only describe. The Hub buttons are the standard — sharp, motion, active states, layers, depth — not a paragraph about buttons: that build, those buttons, judged against everything future. The certificate shows the current certificate, the name elevated and almost glowing on black, never an outdated one kept for relevance. The Smart Note has an official format — nutshell lit in silver, then Child, then Grandma, then What It Means — and the nutshell is the human note; nothing is said twice. A Naya Note is intelligence distilled to its essence, beautiful, dated, timed, organized, kept in the Smart Vault. The bottom bar mirrors Personal exactly — same tiles, same order — folds down when you scroll, and its middle stays white. Color is judged by the eye, not the hex: if two neighbors read the same, they are the same. The orbit comes back to all six buttons because the older buttons were nicer. Flatness fails at the zoom. And the feed speaks the house language: love, like, ten stars — never five.

## HUMAN

When we say 'make it like the standard', there is a real thing to point at — the buttons on the Hub that you already approved. Your certificate always shows your current one, beautifully raised. Notes arrive in one clear format you can learn once, and the bar at the bottom of every page is the same familiar bar, which politely folds away while you read.

## CHILD

It's like having one perfect cookie cutter: every new cookie is checked against it. If a cookie comes out flat or squished, it goes back. And the stars you give things go all the way to ten, because ten is our number.

## GRANDMA

There is one approved example of how things should look, and everything new is compared to it — no guessing, no drift. Your certificate is always the current one, never last year's left in the frame. The writing comes in a tidy, familiar order, and nothing important is said twice.

## WHAT IT ALL MEANS

Taste becomes transferable when it is embodied. This decade converts one evening of 'that one — like that' into durable law: an approved build as the measuring instrument, a format as the container, and the eye — his, then the zoom's — as the court that outranks every table of values.

## WHY IT MATTERS

Teams lose their standard the moment it lives in prose, because prose is re-interpreted by every reader. Naming the artifact — these buttons, this format, this bar — is what lets a hundred builders produce one look, and lets a checker say 'not the standard' with evidence instead of taste.

## WHAT IS IN IT FOR YOU

## THE LEARNING

From now on, when something is approved as the best, we name it the standard and judge against the artifact itself — instead of writing descriptions of it that drift with every reader.
