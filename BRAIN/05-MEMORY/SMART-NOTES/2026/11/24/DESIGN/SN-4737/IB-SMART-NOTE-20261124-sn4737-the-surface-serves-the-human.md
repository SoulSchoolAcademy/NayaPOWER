## NAYA

Operating rules distilled from SN-3175..SN-3184: (1) no machine text on human surfaces — counts, backend names, check times are removed whole; the greeting stands clean. (2) Text on obsidian is white or layered white (25/19/17); gray text is a defect a checker greps for and names. (3) Every icon/board/button is a ball — round, raised, deep — never a flat circle or an empty ring. (4) Clarity is judged at zoom: icon and circle crisp, separate, real space between. (5) Glow over a face is removed; glow around and shine through stay; 3D elevation outranks glow. (6) Machine/SEO language rides the back of every note; human language rides the front. (7) A link opens the view it names, not the front door. (8) Work consolidates into one round — never re-sent, never split, never shown twice. (9) The menu control sits on the right of the top bar.
