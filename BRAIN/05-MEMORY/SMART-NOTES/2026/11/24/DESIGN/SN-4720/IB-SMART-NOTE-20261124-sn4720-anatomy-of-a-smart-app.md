# Anatomy of a Smart App

SN-4720 · DESIGN / DISTILLATION

**Created:** 2026-11-24T17:13:00Z

## IN A NUTSHELL

A page is not a page — it is a smart app, and it gets written down as one: what it has, how it functions, what works, what will be where. The anatomy dictated on 2026-11-16: every app always carries a back button, elegant and blocking nothing, so a person can always go back and forth simply. The visual deck never offers show-all — one slide at a time; read-all is for text only. 'Tap to view' goes to the citizen's real page; there is one page to see and it is theirs. The app installs as an app, and its icon is the citizen's to pick — their upload, their picture, the gallery, the orb library in every colour, any photo. The page is public or private at the citizen's choice, and location is never shared: it exists only to connect people, never to expose them. In Settings, the demographics that connect you are each on or off at your call, and they feed matching — people like you, in your feed. The side column is the citizen's own: your top three featured links, so visitors go straight to your profiles. Under the profile sits your level — points, stars, a bar running to ten stars, and your name itself changing with your level. And when a pop-up opens, it takes the whole screen: no more small pop-ups to squint into.

## HUMAN

This is the spec of what every NayaNET page actually is: not a document but a little app that belongs to its owner — installable, private when they want it private, carrying their links, their level, and their people — with a back button that always works and pop-ups that use the whole screen.

## CHILD

Your page is like your own little app on the phone. You pick its picture, you decide who can see it, it shows your three favourite links, and it has a star bar that fills up as you grow.

## GRANDMA

Your page belongs to you in every way that matters: you choose its icon, you choose whether it is public or private, and nobody ever sees where you are. The buttons always let you go back, and anything that opens, opens big enough to read comfortably.

## NAYA

Smart-app anatomy distilled from SN-2957..SN-2966 (2026-11-16 dictation over the staged Personal page): (1) Routing: 'Tap to view your page' -> the citizen's REAL page; 'See sample' -> the updated Personal; one canonical page per citizen. (2) BACK-BUTTON LAW: every page (or most) carries a back control — elegant, beautiful, clean, blocking nothing; bidirectional movement always possible. (3) NO SHOW-ALL FOR THE VISUAL: visual decks are strictly one slide at a time; show-all exists for text only. (4) An app spec is written as an anatomy: contents, functions, what works, placement — before build. (5) Install-as-app with citizen-chosen icon: own upload / own picture / icon gallery / orb library in every colour / any photo. (6) Visibility = public or private, citizen's choice; LOCATION NEVER SHARED — location data serves connection only. (7) Demographics live in Settings, each item individually on/off, and drive connection matching (people like you surface in feed as connections and as content). (8) Side column = the citizen's top three featured URLs, citizen-editable. (9) Identity header carries points/stars/levels: a level spot under the profile, a progress bar to 10 stars, and the citizen's displayed name changes with level. (10) FULL-SCREEN LAW: every pop-up opens full-screen; small pop-ups are retired.

## MACHINE

1) Every page ships a back control that blocks nothing. 2) Visual decks: one slide at a time, no show-all control. 3) View/sample links resolve to the citizen's real page. 4) Icon at install = citizen's choice from five sources. 5) Location is never displayed or shared; connection use only. 6) Each demographic toggle is independent, default citizen-controlled. 7) Side column renders exactly the citizen's top three featured URLs. 8) Level display: name variant + bar to 10 stars under the profile. 9) All pop-ups render full-screen.

## WHAT IT ALL MEANS

Ownership is the product. Every clause of this anatomy moves a decision from the platform to the citizen — icon, visibility, data, links, even how much of the screen a dialog may take — while the platform keeps only the duties: a working back button, an honest deck, a real page behind every tap.

## WHY IT MATTERS

Without a written anatomy, every builder invents a slightly different app and the citizen relearns every page. With it, nine apps are one app wearing nine faces — and the privacy clauses (location never shared, visibility by choice) are load-bearing trust, not decoration.
