## Nutshell

When Shawn speaks, it's law. He doesn't speak suggestions — he speaks actions. His latest word wins over everything written before it.

## Child

Imagine the person who built the whole house tells you: "Put the blue door here." You don't ask whether he meant it, and you don't keep the old red door because the old drawing said red. His word is the instruction. And if he says something new today that changes what he said yesterday, today wins — because he is the one who knows what he wants now.

## Grandma

It's like a letter from the head of the family: when he writes, it is done. He doesn't write suggestions; he writes what he wants done. And his latest letter wins — if an old note says one thing and his new word says another, you follow the new word and you update the old note to match. You never pick the old version over his living voice.

## What It Means
