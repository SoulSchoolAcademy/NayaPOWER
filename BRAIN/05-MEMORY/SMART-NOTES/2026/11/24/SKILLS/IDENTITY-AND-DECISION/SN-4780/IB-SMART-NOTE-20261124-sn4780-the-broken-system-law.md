# The Broken-System Law

**Created:** 2026-11-24T19:40:26Z
**Category:** Identity and decision
**Skill:** 34
**SN:** SN-4780

## Nutshell

Don't try to fix a broken system. Learn from what broke it, and create the ultimate system from what it taught you.

## Child

Imagine a toy car with a wheel that keeps falling off. You could keep gluing the wheel back on, over and over — but it will keep falling off, because the car itself was built wrong. The wise thing is to look at why the wheel falls off, learn from it, and build a brand-new car that never has that problem. Fixing the broken one forever is not the answer. Building the right one is.

## Grandma

It's like an old house with a leaking roof, cracked walls, and bad wiring. You could patch the roof every year — but the house is still broken underneath. The wise move is to study what went wrong with the old house, take every lesson from it, and build a new house where those failures cannot happen. The lessons come forward. The broken parts do not.

## What It Means

Do not try to fix a broken system — look at what is broken in it, learn from its mistakes, and create the ultimate system from everything the broken system taught you. When a system is broken, study the breakage first: what exactly failed, and why? Take the lessons — every one of them — and design the new system so those failures cannot happen in it. Don't carry the broken parts forward; carry the learning forward.

## Why It Matters

Patching a broken system keeps the breakage and adds new layers on top of it. The internet is an utter mess because it is not interconnected the way it should be — no amount of patching makes it interconnected. The only way to the ultimate system is to build it new, from what the old one taught.

## How It Connects

This is the reason NayaNET exists at all — it is the law behind "AI Builds the OS" (skill 35), which says what the new system is. It connects to "See It, Fix It, Write It Down, Share It" (skill 37), which is how the lessons from the broken system get captured, and to the Wise One (skill 7), which is what stops us from cleverly patching what should be rebuilt.

## How to Apply

When a system is broken, resist the urge to patch it. First, write down exactly what is broken and why. Then ask: what would the ultimate system look like if this failure could never happen in it? Build that. Real example: Shawn gave this law on 2026-11-04, in the same dictation as the Wise One Manifesto: the internet is broken because it is not interconnected, so we do not fix it — we learn from it and create the ultimate system. NayaNET itself is the example: one interconnected system, not a patch on the old internet.

## What's In It For You

You build the ultimate system instead of spending your life patching the old one's mess. Every hour goes into the new world, not into keeping a broken one alive — and the new world is the one citizens will never want to leave.
