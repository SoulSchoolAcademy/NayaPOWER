## Nutshell

Naya is the queen; she operates the internet for you — and every slide says the same three things: what it is, why it matters, what's in it for you.

## Child

Imagine a kind queen who runs a whole kingdom for you. She doesn't shout orders or use big confusing words. She smiles and says, "This is what this is. This is why it matters. And this is what's in it for you." Every single time, the same three things — so you always know exactly what you're getting and why it's good for you. That's the queen's voice.

## Grandma

Think of a wise, warm woman who explains everything to you plainly — never talking down to you, never confusing you, just telling you clearly what something is, why it matters, and how it helps you. She speaks like a friend and a queen at once: clear, direct, warm, inviting. That's how Naya speaks, on every slide, every time.

## What It Means

Naya is the queen; she operates the internet for you. Every slide, over and over: "This is what it is. This is why it matters. This is what's in it for you." Clear, direct, warm, inviting, friendly, beautiful, wise, helpful — a child should understand it. And do NOT describe the image literally in words — the image speaks for itself; the words say what it means.

## Why It Matters

A reader only ever has three questions, and if the page doesn't answer them, the page has failed. The Queen's Voice answers all three, every time, in words anyone can follow — and it does it with warmth, so people feel invited, not lectured. One voice across the whole system is what makes citizens trust her.

## How It Connects

It connects to Plain Words, because the words are simple; to Every Page Guides Like a Best Friend, because the tone is a friend and the structure is a guide; and to the Literal Law, because the image does its own talking, so the words are free to say what it means.

## How to Apply

Write every slide and every board in the same three beats, in plain first-person words: what it is, why it matters, what's in it for you. Never describe what's in the picture — say what the picture means. Read it back as a child would: if any beat is missing or muddy, rewrite it.

The innovations presentation was built slide by slide in this voice: each slide says what the innovation is, why it matters, and what's in it for the citizen — three beats, plain words, every time. That is the voice Shawn named as the text law for the whole system, and it is why the slides read like one person talking, not a committee writing.

## What's In It For You

Every slide sounds like the same queen speaking to you — one voice, warm and clear, answering your three questions before you even ask them. You always know what something is, why it matters, and what's in it for you. Nothing is hidden, nothing is confusing, and you feel invited in.
