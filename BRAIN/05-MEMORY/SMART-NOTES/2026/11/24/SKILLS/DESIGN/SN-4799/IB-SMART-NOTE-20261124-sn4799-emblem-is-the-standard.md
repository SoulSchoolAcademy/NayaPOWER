## Nutshell

One picture — the purple diamond jewel in its silver winged crown — is the quality bar every piece of art is measured against.

## Child

Imagine you have a picture of the most beautiful jewel in the world, and every time you draw a jewel, you hold your drawing next to the picture. Is yours as shiny? As real? As beautiful? If not, you draw it again. The emblem is that picture.

## Grandma

Think of a master jeweler's finest piece, kept in the shop window. Every apprentice holds their work next to it and asks: is mine this fine? That one piece ends every argument about what's good enough. Our emblem is that piece in the window.

## What It Means

The Emblem Is the Standard means NAYA_ICON.png — the purple diamond jewel in a silver winged crown frame — is the north star for all art. Every icon, button, star and diamond is rendered to its quality: real jewelry, faceted, real materials, real light with volume, real depth. The N is always the ball: a glowing red 3D ball orb with a golden N, a real jewel, never flat. Shawn set this on 2026-11-17, and the N Is the Standard ruling followed on 2026-11-23.

## Why It Matters

One quality bar, visible in one image, ends every argument about whether an icon is good enough. Without it, "good enough" is a matter of taste and drifts downward; with it, anyone can hold their work next to the emblem and see the gap in one glance. It keeps the whole system's art at the same high level, everywhere.

## How It Connects

It connects to the Real Object Law (the emblem is the law, made visible), to the Object Anatomy (the construction that makes an icon reach that quality), and to the Awesome Law (the emblem is what "utterly awesome" looks like in one picture). The emblem is the picture; the anatomy is how you paint it; the Awesome Law is the judge.

## How to Apply

Keep the emblem where every maker can see it. Build every piece of art to its standard — faceted like a jewel, lit like a real object, deep like a real thing. Compare, and rebuild what falls short. The N is always the glowing red ball with the golden N — never a flat letter. Real event: when Shawn set this on 2026-11-17, our mockup art rated only about 6 out of 10 against the emblem's 10 — flat shaded circles and pills, pictures *of* objects, not objects. Then on 2026-11-23 came the ruling that the N is the standard: the glowing red 3D ball orb with a golden N. One image now settles what used to be endless debate about icon quality.

Art that is unmistakably Naya — jewel-bright, real, and the same high quality everywhere, so you recognize the system by its shine alone, and every icon you touch feels like it was made by a master jeweler.
