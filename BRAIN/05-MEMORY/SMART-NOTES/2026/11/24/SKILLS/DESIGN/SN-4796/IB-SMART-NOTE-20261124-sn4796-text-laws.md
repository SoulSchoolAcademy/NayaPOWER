# The Text Laws

## Nutshell

Headlines 25, sub-headlines 19, small text 17 — white first, no gray ever — so every word reads clearly at first glance.

## Child

Imagine a book where the big title is big, the smaller title is smaller, and the story words are just the right size — the same sizes in every book you ever pick up. And the words are always bright white, never washed-out gray, so you never have to squint. That's our text rule.

## Grandma

Think of a good newspaper: the headline is big and clear, the smaller headings are a step down, and the story text is comfortable to read. Nothing is pale or gray or tiny. Our pages are set like that newspaper — three sizes, always the same, always bright.

## What It Means

The Text Laws mean: headlines 25, sub-headlines 19, small text 17 — that is the scale, all the way through, on every page. No gray text, ever, at any size. White is first; gold, purple, green, blue only as accents. Wisdom is white, in quotation marks, always. The signature treatment: Marcellus, white, layered — the wisdom board is the model every word is measured against. The laws became official on 2026-11-17, replacing the old 12/14/16/20/28/48 table, and live in Design Laws §2.

## Why It Matters

Text is where the rules get broken most, and where they are most obvious — Shawn sees gray or small text in one glance. If the words are hard to read, the whole page feels cheap, no matter how beautiful the objects are. Clear, bright, well-sized words are what make a page feel considered and trustworthy.

## How It Connects

It connects to Minimum Text (the few words that stay follow the 25/19/17 scale in white), to the Deep Palette (which colors may accent the white), and to the Queen's Voice (clear, warm, direct words). Size, color and fewness work together: the words are big enough, bright enough, and few enough to read at a glance.

## How to Apply

Use only the three sizes: 25 for headlines, 19 for sub-headlines, 17 for small text. Never use gray text at any size — white first, color only as a small accent. Put wisdom in white, in quotation marks, always. When you finish a piece of text, hold it next to the wisdom board and ask: does this read as well as that? If not, fix the size, the brightness, or the words. Real event: the Text Laws became official on 2026-11-17, replacing the old size table. Before that, text sizes drifted page by page; after it, a headline is 25 everywhere — one scale, kept everywhere, is why the pages now read as one system instead of many.

## What's In It For You

Every page reads clearly at first glance — no squinting, no gray wash, no guessing what matters. The words look as considered as the objects around them, and you can trust what you read.
