## Nutshell

Before any design work, read the rule book first — then build, then check the work against the laws, or do it again.

## Child

Imagine you want to build the tallest tower of blocks. First you look at the picture of how towers are built, then you build, then you check your tower against the picture. If it doesn't match, you fix it. You don't just start stacking and hope.

## Grandma

Think of a good cook opening her recipe book before she starts — not after the cake is in the oven. She reads the recipe, she cooks, then she tastes and checks it against the recipe. If it's wrong, she makes it again. Our rule book is the recipe book, and every designer opens it first.
