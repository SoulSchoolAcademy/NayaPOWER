## Nutshell

Show what a thing IS, not a clever picture that stands for it — an image must be obvious at first glance, with no explaining needed.

## Child

Imagine I show you a picture of a dog, but it's not really a dog — it's a squiggle that's supposed to MEAN a dog, and you have to guess. That's silly, right? Just show me the dog! That's the literal law: show the real thing. If the story is about books that hold minds, show books that ARE minds — real books, real and glowing, so you know in two seconds.

## Grandma

Think of a photograph versus a riddle. A photograph shows you your grandchild's face and you know it at once. A riddle makes you work to guess what it means. Our pictures should be photographs, not riddles — the thing itself, shown plainly and beautifully, so anyone can see what it is without a single word of explanation.

## What It Means

Be literal — this is what it is, no metaphor to decode. An image must be obvious at first glance to the average human, with no explaining needed. The books ARE minds; the threads ARE the live connection. Not symbols of minds, not pictures standing for connection, but the things themselves, shown as real 3D objects.

## Why It Matters

If a stranger has to decode your image, you've lost them. Two seconds is all you get. A literal image is understood instantly; a metaphor is a puzzle nobody asked to solve. Clarity at a glance is what makes a presentation work on strangers, children, and grandmas alike.

## How It Connects

It connects to the Real Object Law, because real things are shown, not pictures of things; to the Queen's Voice, because the words never describe the image literally — the image does its own job; and to Plain Words, because the words stay plain when the picture is already clear.

## How to Apply

Before an image ships, ask: would an average person know what this is in two seconds, with no words under it? If the answer is no, the image is wrong — make it literal, make it real, make it the thing itself. Never hide behind a clever symbol when the real thing can be shown.
