## Nutshell

Three big lenses up top — Collective, Personal, Activity — and everything extra tucks underneath as your own quick links, so the page stays clean.

## Child

Imagine you have nine toys and you try to show them all at once — it's a big messy pile and nobody can see anything! But if you put your three favorite toys on the shelf where everyone can see them, and keep the rest in a little box underneath that you can open whenever you want — now it's tidy AND you still have everything. That's the standard of three: three up top, the rest underneath, your choice.

## Grandma

Think of a kitchen shelf with three beautiful jars on it — clean, easy to see, easy to reach. Everything else lives in the cupboard below, and you pull out what you need when you need it. The shelf never gets crowded, and nothing is ever lost. That's how every hub works: three spaces up top, your own quick links underneath.

## What It Means

The Hub shows three big lenses — Collective, Personal, Activity — in that order, auto-organizing from the smart notes in the system. No fourth lens; the World lens lives on the Personal page. The Personal page shows three. The News Hub (separate, reached from the calendar) shows three — local, province, global — never all nine on one screen. And a plus button at the end lets a citizen add quick links underneath (their worlds, business, news feeds), smaller so they fit — clean presentation, choice without clutter.

## Why It Matters

Three is a number the eye can hold at once. Nine on one screen is a crowd; three up top with choice underneath is clean. The citizen still gets everything — but in an order that makes sense at a glance, and the page never turns into a mess no matter how much they add.

## How It Connects

It connects to the One Bar, because one bar below and three lenses above are the two constants of every page; to the Chapter Method, because three or four pictures per chapter make threes the rhythm of the system; and to the Unfold, because more arrives underneath, one step at a time, never all at once.

## How to Apply

Give every hub its three spaces up top, in a fixed order. Never add a fourth lens — if something doesn't fit the three, it lives where it belongs (the World lens lives on Personal). Put a plus button at the end so the citizen can add their own smaller quick links underneath. The top stays three, forever.
