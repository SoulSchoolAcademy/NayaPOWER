## Nutshell

Every page tells you exactly what to do, step by step, like a best friend walking beside you — so nobody is ever lost.

## Child

Imagine your best friend is showing you a brand-new game. She doesn't just hand you the box and walk away. She says: "First, press this button. Now pick your picture. Now press go!" That's what every page does. It tells you step one, step two, step three — and it points right at the button to press.

## Grandma

Think of a kind friend sitting next to you at the kitchen table, showing you how to use a new phone. She doesn't use big fancy words. She just says, "Press here, dear. Now press there. See? You did it." Every page in NayaNET talks to you just like that — warm, plain, and step by step, so you always know what to do next.

## What It Means

A page is not just a pretty picture with buttons on it. It is a guide. It speaks to the person, tells them what this page is for, and walks them through what to do — in plain words, one step at a time, unfolding as they go. The test is simple: could anybody who has never seen the page before follow it and get it right? If not, the page is not finished.

## Why It Matters

If you don't tell people what to do, they don't know — and a person who doesn't know what to do leaves. A page that guides is a page that works for everybody: a child, a grandma, a stranger who just arrived. It is the difference between a system people love and a system people give up on.

## How It Connects

It connects to the Queen's Voice, which gives the page its warm plain words; to Plain Words, Child-and-Grandma Clear, which keeps those words simple; and to the Unfold, which lets the steps arrive one at a time instead of all at once. Together they make a page that teaches as it goes.

## How to Apply

Build every page as a short walk: plain words that say what this is, what you do next, and where to tap — and the next step unfolds only when the person is ready for it. Never use jargon. Never leave a button unexplained. Then test it cold: hand it to someone who has never seen it and watch. If they hesitate, add the missing step.

## What's In It For You

You never get stuck. You never have to ask for help or guess what a button does. Every page is its own teacher, and you can walk through the whole system on your own, step by step, click by click — and feel like someone who cares is right there with you, wanting you to succeed.
