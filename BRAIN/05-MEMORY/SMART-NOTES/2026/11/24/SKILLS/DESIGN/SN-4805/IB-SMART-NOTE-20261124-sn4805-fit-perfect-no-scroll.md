# Fit-Perfect + No-Scroll

## Nutshell

Every screen fits whole in its frame, phone and laptop alike — if you have to scroll down to find something, we messed up.

## Child

Imagine a puzzle that fits perfectly inside its box — every piece in place, nothing sticking out. That's what a screen should be like. You open it and you can see the whole picture at once. If there's more to see, you tap and a whole new picture slides in behind it — you never have to keep sliding down and down to find the missing pieces.

## Grandma

Think of a photograph that fits perfectly in its frame — you can see the whole family at once, nobody cut off at the edges. That's how every screen should be. If something important is hiding below where you can't see it, the frame is wrong. More pictures live behind, like pages in a photo album, and you tap to turn to the next one.

## What It Means

A screen is finished only when everything on it fits inside the screen you're looking at — the phone first, then the laptop. Extra content does not go below in a long scroll; it goes on a screen BEHIND this one, reached by tapping inward. Scrolling down to find what matters is treated as a mistake in the design, not as something the user should do.

## Why It Matters

A screen that fits whole feels finished. A screen you have to scroll forever feels broken, and people lose things at the bottom they never reach. The most important thing on a page should never be the thing nobody scrolls far enough to see.

## How It Connects

It connects to Mobile First, because the phone is judged first; to the Cube, because boards stack behind each other instead of below; and to the Unfold, because more arrives one step at a time. All three are about the same thing: the screen in front of you is always complete.

## How to Apply

Design every screen to the size of the screen it's on, and check it at phone width first. Put the most important things where the eye lands. When there is more, stack it inward — tap a board and the next full board sits directly behind it. If you catch yourself scrolling down to reach something, move it inward instead.

Example (illustrative): a citizen opens their Personal page on their phone and sees their whole world at once — their picture, their words, their buttons, all fitting on the one screen. They tap a board to see more, and the next board slides in whole, just as complete. Nothing is cut off, nothing is hidden below, nothing is lost.

## What's In It For You

You always see the whole picture at once, on any screen. Nothing important is hiding below where you can't find it. Every tap brings you a fresh, complete screen — and the system always feels finished, like a real app in your hand.
