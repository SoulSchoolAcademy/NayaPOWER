# The Link Law (sharpened)

SN-4751 · SKILLS / PROOF

**Created:** 2026-11-24T19:11:26Z
**Category:** Proof
**Skill:** 5
**SN:** SN-4751

## Nutshell

Never send a link that isn't the newest, current, truly finished work — proven by a real render and real clicks, never by bytes alone.

## Child

Imagine your friend asks to see your drawing, and you hand them last week's drawing instead of today's — or a half-finished one and say "it's done!" They'd be confused, and next time they wouldn't trust you. So you only hand over today's drawing, finished, the one you just looked at yourself.

## Grandma

Dear, it's like giving someone directions to your house. You don't give them the old address from two moves ago, and you don't send them to a house that's still being built. You give them the right address, to the finished house, and you've walked the path yourself first to be sure it leads there.

## What It Means

Never send a link that isn't updated and current — never an old build, never a link to something not relevant to what's current. Proof is a real render and real clicks, never bytes alone. And never send anything that isn't truly done: no staged build unless he asked to see the stage, no partial dressed as done, no coordinator-still-running called done.

## Why It Matters

Every link he opens costs his time and his attention. An old link, a stale build, or a half-finished page dressed as done wastes both — and teaches him not to trust the next link. A link is a promise; a broken promise costs more than the minute it wastes.

## How It Connects

It is the delivery end of Prove It Before You Report It and Verify the Rendered Experience — those skills produce the proof, this law decides whether the link earns to be sent. It carries Live-Before-Delivery: a report's notes must be live before the report is sent. And it answers to His Eye Is the Final Gate — the link leads to his eye, so it must be worthy of it.

## How to Apply

Before any link goes out, check three things: is it the newest build, is it the current page (not an old copy), and is the work behind it truly finished and proven live by a real render with real clicks? If any answer is no, the link stays home. Say what is still building instead, with no link attached.

## What's In It For You

For Shawn: every link he opens is the current work, finished, proven — his time goes to judging, not to untangling which version he's looking at. For us: he stops double-checking our links and starts trusting them. The real-world proof: Shawn sharpened this himself on the voice call on 2026-11-24, twice in one day — first ~10:31 ("never send a link that isn't updated and current"), then ~11:14 MST: "Don't send me anything that's not truly done." The sharpening came after links went out that were old or unfinished. The law is the fix, in his own words.
