# Verify the Rendered Experience

SN-4750 · SKILLS / PROOF

**Created:** 2026-11-24T19:10:26Z
**Category:** Proof
**Skill:** 4
**SN:** SN-4750

## Nutshell

Open the real page in a real browser and look at it yourself, phone first — because a file can land perfectly and still be broken for the person using it.

## Child

Imagine someone gives you a toy car in a box and says "it's perfect!" You don't just believe the box — you open it, you push the car, you check the wheels turn. That's what we do with a page: we don't trust the box (the file), we open it and play with it ourselves.

## Grandma

Dear, it's like buying a dress from a catalogue. The picture can be lovely and the parcel can arrive on time — but until you put it on and look in the mirror, you don't know if it fits. We put the page on and look in the mirror: the real browser, the real phone, the real clicks.

## What It Means

Open the live page at its real address in a real browser and look at it with your own eyes — phone first, then desktop, at scroll 0 and scrolled, bottom included. Click every button and record where each lands. Measure the whole element, never an icon inside it. Refuse a score above 8 when any part is untested. Filing checks (blob, byte count, curl 200) are preparation only — they prove the file landed, never that the page works.

## Why It Matters

It catches what bytes cannot: the page as a human experiences it. A file can land perfectly and still trap a visitor, show the wrong page, or hide a broken button. If nobody looks at the rendered page, the first person who does is Shawn — or worse, a citizen.

## How It Connects

It is the hands-on half of Prove It Before You Report It, and it is what makes His Eye Is the Final Gate meaningful — his eye can only judge what we have already looked at ourselves. It feeds the Link Law: a link is only sent after the rendered page behind it has been walked. Debug by Evidence is its partner — the render shows the failure, the evidence finds the cause.

## How to Apply

Go to the real URL in a real browser, at phone width first. Scroll the whole page, top to bottom. Tap every button and write down where each one lands. Check the bottom bar, the edges, the scrolled view. Check desktop too. Score nothing above 8 unless every part has been seen and clicked. Keep the filing checks as preparation — never as the verdict.

## What's In It For You

For Shawn: every page he opens has already been used the way a citizen would use it. For us: we find the broken button, the wrong page, the trapped visitor before he does. The real-world proof, 2026-11-24: the /welcome bytes were perfect while the page trapped a real visitor, and the Settings link downloaded fine while rendering the wrong page entirely. Both failures were invisible to file checks and obvious to a real render.
