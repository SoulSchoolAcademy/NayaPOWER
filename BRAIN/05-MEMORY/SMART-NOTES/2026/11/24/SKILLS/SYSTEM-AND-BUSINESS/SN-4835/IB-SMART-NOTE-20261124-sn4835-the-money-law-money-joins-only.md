# The Money Law — Money = Joins Only

SN-4835 · SKILLS / SYSTEM AND BUSINESS

**Created:** 2026-11-24T20:35:26Z
**Category:** System and business
**Skill:** 89
**SN:** SN-4835

## Nutshell

The dollar sign appears only where money truly is — when someone joins. Everything else is points, never money.

## Child

Imagine a game where you earn stars for helping, and you only get real coins when a new friend actually joins the game because of you. Stars are for fun and fame; coins are only for the real thing.

## Grandma

Think of a church fair: you get raffle tickets for helping set up, for baking, for inviting neighbours — but the money in the tin only comes from the people who actually buy a ticket and come in. Tickets are thanks; money is money, and you never mix them up.

## What It Means

On the Dashboard, the $ symbol appears only where money makes sense — joins, and only joins. Everything else is points, in every mode. Clicks are never money. Points = reputation, NEVER money. Money words name three things: the amount, the thing, and the trigger — "You receive 50% of the revenue when someone joins because you shared."

## Why It Matters

Money must never be confused with attention. If clicks looked like money, citizens would chase clicks and trust would rot. When the dollar sign means exactly one thing, everybody knows exactly what earns and why.

## How It Connects

It connects to Auto-Match, because invites and joins are where the points live — 25 for inviting, 50 for joining. It connects to Membership + Payments, because the membership ladder is the other place money honestly appears: what a tier costs and what it gives. It connects to the Dashboard itself, where the law is enforced in the design — the dollar sign only where it belongs.

## How to Apply

On any screen that shows rewards, show points everywhere except joins — and put the dollar sign only on the join. When you write about money, always name all three things: the amount, the thing, and the trigger. Never write "earn half" — write "You receive 50% of the revenue when someone joins because you shared." If a screen tempts you to put a dollar sign on a click, a view or a share, take it off.

## What's In It For You

You always know exactly what earns and why. Nobody promises you money for a click, nobody confuses a point with a payment, and the 50% share promise is stated so plainly it can never be misread.
