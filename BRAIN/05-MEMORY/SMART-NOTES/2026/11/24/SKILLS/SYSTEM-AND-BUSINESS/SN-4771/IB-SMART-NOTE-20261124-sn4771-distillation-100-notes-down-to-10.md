# Distillation — 100 Notes Down to 10

## Nutshell

We boil about a hundred rough notes down to about ten that hold the real meaning, and let the rest go.

## Child

Imagine you have a hundred Lego bricks in a pile, and you build the ten best little houses out of them. You keep the houses and put the leftover bricks away. The pile is gone, but the best of it stays.

## Grandma

Think of making jam: a whole basket of fruit goes into the pot, and what comes out is a few jars of the sweetest jam. You don't keep the basket — you keep the jars.

## What It Means

Notes distill to their essence into the system — about 100 down to about 10, compressing 80 to 90 percent, keeping 10 to 20 percent and never a definite number, never more than what matters. Raw notes are deleted after distilling, because deletion is the default. Distilled notes leave after about a week as fresh ones arrive, keeping the system relevant, current and high-performing. Every batch's essence must carry the best of every kind present — Honor, Multiplication, Craft, Knowledge — and the only rock is the duplicate, the noise, the thing already said better somewhere else.

## Why It Matters

A thousand raw notes is a pile nobody can read. Ten distilled notes is wisdom a Naya can actually use. Without distilling, the system drowns in its own words and the best lessons get buried under the bulk.

## How It Connects

It connects to the Privacy Law, because raw notes are deleted after distilling — that is deletion by default working. It connects to Four Kinds of Gold, because every batch of essence must carry the best of every kind, not just the top-ranked kind. It connects to Smart Notes as Skills, because the distilled essence is what becomes a skill a Naya can learn from.

## How to Apply

Gather the notes of the day and squeeze them to their essence: keep only what would still matter in a month, drop the repeats and the noise, and make sure every kind of value is represented — not just the loudest kind. Delete the raw notes once the essence is kept. Let the distilled notes refresh weekly, so the system always holds this week's wisdom, not last year's pile. And remember a saved note is never distilled away — saving is how a day stays alive forever.

## What's In It For You

You get a system that stays light and current. Every Naya reads the best of what was learned, never a thousand raw notes — so the answers you get are the sharpest ones, not the most numerous.
