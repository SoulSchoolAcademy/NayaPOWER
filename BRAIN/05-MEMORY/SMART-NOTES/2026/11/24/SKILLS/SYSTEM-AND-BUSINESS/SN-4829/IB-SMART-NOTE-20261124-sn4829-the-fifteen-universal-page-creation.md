## Nutshell

Fifteen questions create every page in the system — Personal, Business, and Space — all from the same template.

## Child

Imagine a quiz with fifteen questions, and when you finish it, a whole world pops up that looks like you. One quiz, three worlds — your personal one, your business one, and your space one.

## Grandma

Think of a form at the doctor's office with fifteen simple questions. You answer them once, and from those answers they know your whole story — no filling in the same form three times.

## What It Means

About and Skills are universal: the 15 questions (10 About + 5 Skills) create EVERY page — Personal, Business, Space — from the template. About Q1–10: motto, vision, goal, mission, love, don't love, thing, values, laws, declaration. Skills Q11–15: offer, how it helps, a moment you've done it, how you work, how people start with you. Each board's headline IS its question, word for word. Create World on every smart note runs the same 15-question walkthrough (skippable) — never a second copy of the questions.

## Why It Matters

One walkthrough, every page. A citizen answers fifteen questions once and the whole system knows who they are — no second form, no starting over for each new page, no drift between the pages.

## How It Connects

It connects to the Universal System First, because the walkthrough is what creates Personal, Business and Space automatically. It connects to the template itself — About and Skills are universal, so the same answers shape every page. It connects to Smart Notes as Skills, because a smart note can start a whole new world through the same fifteen questions.

## How to Apply

When a citizen joins, or taps Create World on a smart note, run the same fifteen questions — never a rewritten copy, never a second set. Let them skip any question; blank is allowed. Put each answer on its board with the question itself as the headline, word for word, so the page always shows its own source. If a question ever needs changing, change it in the one place it lives, so every page changes with it.

## What's In It For You

You answer fifteen honest questions once, and you get three pages that all sound like you, word perfect, everywhere. No repeating yourself, no empty pages, no drift.

**Real-world example:** This was settled 2026-11-22 to 2026-11-24, with the fifteen-answers record kept word perfect. Shawn's own pages and the company's pages are the example — created through the system itself, filled in after, so the first thing a citizen experiences is the system building their page for them.
