# Three Views Only

SN-4831 · SKILLS / SYSTEM AND BUSINESS

**Created:** 2026-11-24T20:31:26Z
**Category:** System and business
**Skill:** 85
**SN:** SN-4831

## Nutshell

The whole system has just three views: the hub, the stage, and the calendar. Learn three doors and you can walk everywhere.

## Child

Imagine a house with only three rooms: the playroom where you see all your toys, the stage where you put on a show, and the calendar on the wall where your whole week lives. Three rooms — and that is the whole house.

## Grandma

Think of a television with three channels, and every programme you could ever want is on one of those three. You never have to hunt through hundreds of channels to find what you are looking for.

## What It Means

The hub (cockpit), the stage (full frame), the calendar view (full frame). Everything else presents in that system. Three views to learn, ever — the whole system is navigable from three doors.

## Why It Matters

A citizen learns three doors and can walk the whole system, instead of learning a new layout on every page and getting lost. Fewer views means less confusion, faster learning, and a system that feels like one place instead of a maze.

## How It Connects

It connects to the Day-Menu Doors, because the calendar view IS the cockpit for a day — tap a date and the day's menu opens. It connects to the Universal System First, because the three pages every citizen gets all live inside these three views. It connects to the Fifteen, because the pages the fifteen questions build are all shown on the stage.

## How to Apply

Whatever you build, put it in one of the three views — the hub to gather and steer, the stage to present a page full frame, the calendar to organise a day. Never invent a fourth kind of screen. If something doesn't fit any of the three, the design is wrong, not the system — rework it until it fits.

## What's In It For You

One navigation to learn, one set of views to keep elite, and you never get lost — wherever you are, you are in one of three places you already know.

**Example (illustrative):** A new citizen opens NayaNET for the first time: the hub shows her worlds, she taps one and the stage opens it full frame, she taps the calendar and her whole day is there. Three taps, three views, and she has seen the whole system without a single lesson. That is what the template architecture settled on 2026-11-23 was built to give her.
