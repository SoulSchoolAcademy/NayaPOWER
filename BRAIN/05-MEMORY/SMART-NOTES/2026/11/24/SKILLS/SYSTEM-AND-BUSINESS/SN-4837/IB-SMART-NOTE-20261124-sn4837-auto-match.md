## Nutshell

One algorithm matches you with people like you, shown as percentages — and the word is CONNECT, never "follow."

## Child

Imagine a teacher who knows all the kids in school and quietly introduces you to the ones who like the same games, speak the same language, and live near you. You don't have to search — the right friends just show up.

## Grandma

Think of a village dance where the host knows everyone and brings you to the people you would get along with best — same language, same part of the world, same kind of heart. She doesn't make you wander the room alone.

## What It Means

One algorithm, matches as percentages, CONNECT never "follow." The onboarding data is how the system becomes aware and intelligently connects people automatically — related pages, spaces, groups, businesses show up in the feed; things not relevant do not. Every match now comes through a privacy gate with reasons attached. Points go up when people invite (25) and when people join (50).

## Why It Matters

Citizens meet the people like them without searching. The network arranges itself around who a person is — same language, same place, same kind of mind — instead of leaving them to hunt through strangers.

## How It Connects

It connects to the Privacy Law, because the privacy gate with reasons attached is what closed the old hole where matches skipped the gate. It connects to the Money Law, because invites and joins are the points — 25 and 50 — and the join is where the money is. It connects to the Fifteen, because the walkthrough answers are the data the matching runs on.

## How to Apply

At onboarding, ask who the person is in their own words — age, where they live, where they're from, their main language — and use those answers to suggest matches by percentage, highest first, with the reason for each match shown. Run every match through the privacy gate first, so nobody's details are handed over to make a match. Say CONNECT, never "follow" — connection is mutual, following is one-way.

## What's In It For You

Your feed shows you the people and worlds meant for you — by default, without you lifting a finger. Every good match is another reason to invite someone in, and the network grows itself around who you are.
