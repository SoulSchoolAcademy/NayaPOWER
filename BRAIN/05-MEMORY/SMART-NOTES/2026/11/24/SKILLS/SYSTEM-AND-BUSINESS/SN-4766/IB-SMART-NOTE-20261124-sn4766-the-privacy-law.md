# The Privacy Law

**Category:** System and business
**Skill:** 20

## Nutshell

NayaNET is a private place: we never spy, never read your mail or messages, and what you save stays yours, locked away safe.

## Child

Imagine a diary with a lock, and only you have the key. Nobody peeks inside — not your friends, not the people who built the diary, nobody. That is how NayaNET treats your words.

## Grandma

Think of a telephone call in your own kitchen with the door shut. Nobody is listening on the line, nobody writes down what you said, and the letters you keep in your drawer stay in your drawer. That is the promise here.

## What It Means

NayaNET is a private communication system that stands outside the current system. We do not spy, we do not read mail, we do not read messages. Citizens use aliases, so nobody needs to know a real name. Conversations fade by default — after a week, unless the citizen chooses a day or a month, never longer. A note a citizen SAVES — pinned, favourited, put in a smart list — is kept for good in their own Smart Vault.

## Why It Matters

Trust is the product. People only talk freely and connect with people like them when they know nobody is watching. If privacy breaks, everything else breaks with it — the matching, the sharing, the whole network.

## How It Connects

It connects to the Two Vaults, because the personal vault is the locked room this law protects. It connects to Distillation, because raw notes are deleted after distilling — deletion by default working. It connects to Auto-Match, because every match passes through a privacy gate and nobody's details are handed over to make a match.

## How to Apply

When you build anything that touches a citizen's words, ask first: does this need to be seen by anyone other than its owner? If not, keep it locked. Never open mail or messages for any reason — no switch, no exception. When a citizen saves something, put it in their own vault and keep it there. When they don't save it, let it fade on their chosen schedule. And when someone asks you to look inside a vault, the answer is no unless that citizen said yes.

## What's In It For You

You get a place where you can talk freely, be yourself, and know your words are yours. People stay where they feel safe — and a system people trust is a system people live in.
