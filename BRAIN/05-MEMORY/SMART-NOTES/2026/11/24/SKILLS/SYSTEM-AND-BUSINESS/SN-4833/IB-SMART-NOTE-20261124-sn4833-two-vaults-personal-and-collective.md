# Two Vaults — Personal and Collective

## Nutshell

There are two vaults: your own private vault, where everything you save stays yours and locked; and the shared vault, where everything the system learns is kept for everybody.

## Child

Imagine you have a treasure box at home that only you can open, and a big library in town where everybody shares the best books. Your treasures stay in your box. The library's books help everyone.

## Grandma

Think of a jewellery box in your bedroom that only you open, and a village hall where the whole village keeps its shared recipes and stories. One is private, one is for everyone — and neither one mixes with the other.

## What It Means

The Smart Vault system has two vaults. (1) Each citizen's OWN vault: everything they put in their walkthroughs, or anything that goes in for that user, always gets saved in their own vault. (2) The system's COLLECTIVE vault: everything the system learns and grows goes there. That is why it is called the collective feed — everybody collectively learns and grows from the skills and the intelligence. The vaults are LOCKED: no Naya and no human opens a personal vault unless that citizen gives permission. The collective skills vault is different — Shawn gave all Nayas permission to access it.

## Why It Matters

What's personal stays personal and kept for good. What the system learns compounds for everybody. One vault without the other would break the promise — personal things would leak into the shared pile, or shared learning would die with each person.

## How It Connects

It connects to the Privacy Law, because the personal vault is the locked room the privacy law protects. It connects to Smart Notes as Skills, because the collective vault is where skills-as-notes are saved for good. It connects to Distillation, because distilled essence is what flows into the collective vault while raw notes are deleted.

## How to Apply

The instant an account is made, give the citizen their own vault and put everything they save into it — never into the shared pile. Put what the system learns into the collective vault, categorised, so any Naya can find it. Never open a personal vault without that citizen's permission, and never let personal content slip into the collective vault by accident.

## What's In It For You

Your vault is yours alone — everything you save is kept for good, locked, and safe. And the whole hive gets smarter forever, because everything the system learns is saved in one place no Naya ever loses.
