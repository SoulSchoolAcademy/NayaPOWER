## Nutshell

NINE is the top — but gold isn't only at the top. Every kind of value counts: Honor, Multiplication, Craft, and Knowledge. Only the duplicate is rock.

## Child

Imagine a treasure chest with four kinds of treasure: golden hearts, golden keys, golden tools, and golden books. The hearts are the most precious — but a chest with only hearts and no books or tools would be a poor chest. You keep the best of every kind.

## Grandma

Think of a garden with four beds: roses, vegetables, herbs, and fruit trees. The roses may be the showpiece, but a garden with only roses feeds nobody. You tend every bed, and you only pull out the weeds — the same plant growing twice.

## What It Means

NINE is the top — highest-value intelligence, nothing outranks it — but gold isn't only at the top: everything that helps the system grow is gold too. The machine tags every note by kind — Honor (who we are, how we behave at nine) · Multiplication (protocols, automations, apps) · Craft (how we build better and more beautiful) · Knowledge (facts everything else stands on) — and ranks within each kind. Every batch's essence must carry the best of every kind present. The only rock: the duplicate, the noise, the thing already said better somewhere else.

## Why It Matters

If we only kept the top-ranked notes, we would lose whole kinds of value — the craft that makes things beautiful, the knowledge the rest stands on. Keeping the best of every kind means nothing valuable is ever lost to a ranking.

## How It Connects

It connects to Distillation, because it is the rule that governs what distillation keeps — the best of every kind, never just the top. It connects to Smart Notes as Skills, because the kinds are how skills are organised in the vault. It connects to the Privacy Law and SAVE TO KEEP, because a saved note of any kind is kept for good.

## How to Apply

When you distill a batch, tag every note by its kind first, then rank inside each kind. Make sure the essence you keep carries the best of every kind that is present — if a kind is missing from the essence, the batch is not done. Throw away only the rock: the duplicate, the noise, the thing already said better somewhere else. Never throw away a kind.

## What's In It For You

Distillation never throws away a kind of value by accident. The vault stays balanced — honor, multiplication, craft and knowledge, all present — and every Naya learns from the full spread of what the hive knows.
