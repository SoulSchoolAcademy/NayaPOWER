# Membership + Payments

**Skill:** 90

## Nutshell

Memberships are an add-on to any page: Power $25 builds 10 sites, Pro $50 builds 100, Max $100 builds unlimited — and the owner takes payment through their own PayPal link.

## Child

Imagine a club with three tickets: a small ticket lets you build 10 clubhouses, a middle ticket lets you build 100, and the golden ticket lets you build as many as you want. You pick the ticket that fits how big you want to grow.

## Grandma

Think of a market stall: you rent a small table, a bigger table, or the whole hall — each one costs more, and each one lets you sell more. And when a customer pays, they pay you directly at your own till, not through the market office.

## What It Means

Memberships are an ADD-ON to any page, never a separate app — a button on Personal, Business, or Space. The ladder: Power ($25) = 10 membership sites · Pro ($50) = 100 · Max ($100) = unlimited. Enormous value at every level, limited on purpose, so each step up is a clear upgrade and Max is always the most attractive. Payments: the owner pastes their own PayPal/Stripe URL (PayPal recommended); the dashboard marks a sale "returned from PayPal," never payment-proven — the system sees the click and the return, not the payment itself.

## Why It Matters

The upgrade path must be obvious at a glance, and the money story must never be overstated. A citizen should see in one second what each tier gives — and an owner should never be told a sale is proven when all we saw was a return.

## How It Connects

It connects to the Money Law, because the ladder is priced in honest money and the dashboard never calls a return a payment. It connects to the Fifteen, because a membership is built on a page the fifteen questions created. It connects to the Publishing Law, because a member's public page must be verified before it goes live.

## How to Apply

Put the membership button on the page itself — Personal, Business or Space — never as a separate app. Show the ladder plainly: 10, 100, unlimited, with the price beside each. Let the owner paste their own payment link and tell them PayPal is recommended, because buyers trust it more. Track the click and the return, and write "returned from PayPal" in the dashboard — never write "paid" when the payment itself happened somewhere we cannot see.

## What's In It For You

You can build memberships on the page you already have, the upgrade path is obvious at a glance, and the money stays honest — you keep your own payment link, and the record never overstates a sale.

**Real-world example:** Shawn settled the ladder on 2026-11-08 at about 18:18Z — Power 10, Pro 100, Max unlimited — replacing the earlier ladder from 2026-11-05, and the payments decision was ruled on 2026-11-05 at 18:45Z: owner pastes their own link, PayPal recommended because buyers trust it more. The fees were verified in the record too — Stripe 2.9% plus 30 cents, PayPal card 2.99% plus 49 cents — so the advice to owners is grounded, not guessed.
