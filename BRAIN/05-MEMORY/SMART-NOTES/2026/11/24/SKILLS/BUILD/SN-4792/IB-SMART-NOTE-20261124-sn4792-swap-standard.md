## What It Means

Every 9:16 image, text, or video slot carries a little swap button — top-right, members only. Video: paste a link (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook) and it plays right there. Image: upload or choose from the library. Text: click and the editor opens.

## Why It Matters

Citizens can change any slot themselves, push-button simple. The page stays theirs, not frozen — they shape their own world without asking anyone. A page you cannot change is a poster; a page you can change is a home.

## How to Apply

Each 9:16 slot — tall, phone-shaped — gets a small swap button in its top-right corner, visible to members. Tap it: for video, paste a link and it plays in place; for an image, upload one or pick from the library; for text, the editor opens right there. Every slot, every time — no slot without its button.
