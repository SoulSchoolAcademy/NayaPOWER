# The Parallel Law

SN-4818 · SKILLS / OPERATIONS

**Created:** 2026-11-24T20:18:26Z
**Category:** Operations
**Skill:** 72
**SN:** SN-4818

## Nutshell

Do them all in parallel — mentioning one piece never demotes the others.

## Child

Imagine you have five chores, and your mom mentions the dishes. That doesn't mean the other four chores disappear — you still do all five, all at the same time as you can. Talking about one never cancels the rest.

## Grandma

When Shawn names several pieces of work, they all move forward together. Saying one piece out loud doesn't push the others down the list or off it. Every piece keeps its place, and every piece gets worked.

## What It Means

Do them all in parallel; mentioning one piece never demotes the others. The whole list moves at once — no item quietly starves while another gets the attention.

## Why It Matters

Attention is not a queue where only the last thing said matters. If naming one piece silently drops the rest, work disappears without anyone deciding to drop it — and Shawn has to name it all over again.

## How It Connects

It connects to the Checklist Law — the whole list is the unit, so the whole list moves. It connects to the Live Sequence — the team works in parallel, one seat on one thing, another on another. And it connects to One Lane, One Round — parallel lanes, not parallel teams on the same file.

## How to Apply

When several pieces are named, dispatch them all — different seats, same time. If you find yourself working on one piece, check the others are moving too. Never treat a mention of one piece as permission to let the rest wait.

Real example: Shawn corrected the team on the voice call of 2026-11-23 (~21:05 MST): mentioning one piece had been treated as if the others could wait. The Parallel Law is that correction — all of them move, all at once, and naming one never demotes the rest.

## What's In It For You

Speed without starvation. Everything advances together, so nothing is left behind to be rediscovered later — and nobody has to re-ask for work that was already asked for.
