# The Checklist Law: One List, Worked to Zero

SN-4756 · SKILLS / OPERATIONS

**Created:** 2026-11-24T19:16:26Z
**Category:** Operations
**Skill:** 10
**SN:** SN-4756

## Nutshell

One list, worked until it is zero — nothing comes back until the whole list is done.

## Child

Imagine your teacher gives you a list of ten jobs to do. You don't run back after two jobs and say "I'm done!" You finish all ten, check them off one by one, and then you show your teacher the finished list.

## Grandma

When Shawn hands us a page of jobs, we write them all on one list. We don't come back to him until every single job on that list is done and proven — or we tell him plainly, in the same breath, why one can't be done.

## What It Means

The list is the unit of work, not one round per item. "Done" means the whole list is proven on the page he sees. Sending back one or two items done is a failure, not progress. When an item can't be done, it is named with its reason in the same pass — never silence.

## Why It Matters

He never has to retell the same list. If work comes back half done, he has to say it all over again — and that spends his attention, the one thing he can't get back. A list worked to zero means he says it once and it stays done.

## How It Connects

It connects to the Whole-Page Rule — the whole page comes back together, not in pieces. It connects to the Continuous Cycle Law, which keeps the list moving until it reaches zero. And it connects to the No-Skipping Law: no item is quietly skipped off the list.

## How to Apply

When a page arrives, write every item from his message onto ONE checklist, in his words. Work the list down to zero. Small items ride together in one round on the page — never queued as separate rounds. Before anything goes back to him, check every item against the page itself: is it there, is it proven? If an item can't be done, write the reason next to it and say so in the same message.

Real example: on 2026-11-13 Shawn told the team, "out of 100% you got like 20%" — a list had come back with only a fifth of it done. The Personal page that day carried a whole list — buttons uneven, plus floating, bottom line, Activity tab, old composer, no Hub feed — all named the day before, none landed, while rounds reported done. The Checklist Law is the fix for that day.

## What's In It For You

Finished work, first time. No retelling, no chasing, no "did you do that one yet?" You say a list once, and it comes back zero — proven on the page you can see.
