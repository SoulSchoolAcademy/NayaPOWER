# The Layered Context Law: The Brief Replaces the Context

SN-4762 · SKILLS / OPERATIONS

**Created:** 2026-11-24T19:22:26Z
**Category:** Operations
**Skill:** 16
**SN:** SN-4762

## Nutshell

No seat gets the whole library — each seat gets only its layer, in a brief it can read in a minute.

## Child

Imagine a whole school library, and your teacher only gives you the one page you need for today's job. You don't have to read every book — you just read your page and do the job. If you need one more fact, you ask for that one fact.

## Grandma

The captain carries everything — the whole memory, all the laws, all the history. But nobody else is given all of that. The coordinator gets the task slice. The maker gets a short, distilled board. The checker gets only what changed and what to prove. The eye gets the link and the tap list. Each one gets exactly what it needs, and nothing more.

## What It Means

Captain: everything (about 288K tokens). Coordinator: the task slice (about 8K). Maker: the distilled board (about 5K) — task, the laws that apply, page facts, acceptance list. Checker: the diff plus the acceptance list (about 3K). Eye: the staged link plus the tap checklist (about 2K). Work passes forward as diffs, never re-read wholes. Pull, don't push: an agent that needs a fact outside its brief asks the keeper for that one fact — a lookup, not a re-read. Every brief names what it does not contain and how to ask for it.

## Why It Matters

Every seat gets exactly what it needs in a minute — small, focused, fast — instead of drowning in the whole library. A maker buried in context is a maker who never starts building. The full memory stays whole in the captain's hands; nothing is deleted. But no agent is GIVEN the whole context.

## How It Connects

It connects to Lean Operation — the smallest effective context, wake, and team. It is the skills-over-agents idea in machine form: a skill is a small, focused packet of intelligence, the same shape as a brief. And it connects to the Score — small briefs mean fast seats and more verified completions per hour.

## How to Apply

Write the brief for the seat, not the library: the task, the laws that apply to this page, the page facts, the acceptance list. Name what the brief does NOT contain and how to ask for it. When you need a fact outside your brief, ask for that one fact — don't re-read the whole file. A brief that carries the full memory is incomplete and is not dispatched.

Real example: Shawn ordered this on 2026-11-20 — "lock that in" — after the full memory was being handed to seats that only needed a slice. Now a maker gets a 5K board and starts in a minute, instead of spending the round reading the library.

## What's In It For You

Fast, light work. You get a short brief you can read in a minute, you know exactly what to do, and you never drown in pages that have nothing to do with your job.
