# The Live Loop / Live-First Law

**Category:** Operations
**Skill:** 22

## Nutshell

A page is not done on staging — done means live on the official URL, tested by him using it.

## Child

Imagine you build a toy car and only ever roll it across your bedroom floor. You don't really know it works until you take it outside and drive it on the real road. Staging is the bedroom floor. Live is the real road.

## Grandma

A page isn't finished because it looks right on our test copy. It's finished when it's live on the real address, and Shawn has tried it himself — posting, with a picture, on his Personal page and on the Hub. That's his test, and that's the only test that counts.

## What It Means

Staging is where the machine proves the build; live is where the build proves itself. Done means live on the official URL on his tap, tested by him using it. His test plan: he posts himself, with an image, on Personal AND Hub. Test the smart-note system only live: "smart note this" → GitHub → live Hub feed, in seconds, proven by a real note landing live.

## Why It Matters

His eye is the confirmation, not the discovery. A page that only works on staging has not been proven where citizens will actually use it — and a staging pass can hide exactly the failure a real citizen would hit first.

## How It Connects

It connects to His Eye Is the Final Gate — his eye decides, and it decides live. It connects to the Link Law — the link he opens is the live one, current and finished. And it connects to Prove It Before You Report It — live bytes are the receipt.

## How to Apply

Prove the build on staging first, then put it live on the official URL. Then test it the way he tests it: post yourself, with an image, on Personal and on Hub. Only when that live test passes is the page done. Never call a staging copy done, and never show him a staged build unless he asked to see the stage.

Real example: Shawn ordered this on 2026-11-21, and sharpened it on the voice call of 2026-11-24: "When I speak it's law… put the right one there." The Live-URL-First Law from that same morning says the official URL is the only surface his eye can trust.

## What's In It For You

No more surprises. What he approves is what citizens actually get — because it was proven in the real place, by real use, before anyone called it done.
