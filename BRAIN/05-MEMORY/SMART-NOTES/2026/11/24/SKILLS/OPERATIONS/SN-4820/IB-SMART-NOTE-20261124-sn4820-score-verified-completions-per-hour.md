# The Score Is Verified Completions Per Hour

## Nutshell

The score is finished, verified work per hour — not how busy the machine looks.

## Child

Imagine two kids doing homework. One does ten pages but gets them all wrong. The other does three pages and gets them all right. Who did better? The second one — because only finished, right work counts. Dispatches are the first kid. Completions are the second.

## Grandma

We don't score ourselves on how many jobs we started, or how fast we woke up, or how many words we used. We score ourselves on how much finished, proven work landed in an hour. The goal is sixty good minutes in every hour — and a minute only counts if what it made proves out.

## What It Means

The score is verified completions per hour (goal 60 or more; VPM = verified productive minutes ÷ total agent-minutes — a minute banks only when its outcome verifies; maker minutes on NOT PASS work bank zero). Completions per hour is the metric; dispatches per hour is vanity. Tokens and clock time are the fuel gauge, never the score.

## Why It Matters

The machine optimizes for finished, verified value — not for how busy it looks. A day full of dispatches and zero landings scores zero, and that is honest. An honest scoreboard is the only one that can make the machine better.

## How It Connects

It connects to Prove It Before You Report It — only verified work counts. It connects to Lean Operation — every minute must bank value. And it connects to Fail Fast — bad work dies early so it stops costing minutes.

## How to Apply

Score every hour on verified completions, not dispatches. Count a minute only when its outcome verifies — work that fails its check banks zero, no matter how long it took. Aim for 60 verified productive minutes per hour. Treat tokens and clock time as fuel, not as the score.

Real example: Shawn re-aimed the machine on 2026-11-16 (18:47Z): "It's not about 15 minutes or 30 minutes. It's about them finishing the work and producing value." The score changed that day from how fast wakes happened to how much verified work landed per hour — and maker minutes on work that did not pass now bank zero.

## What's In It For You

An honest scoreboard. You can see whether the machine is actually producing, and stop doing the things that look busy but land nothing. Value, not noise.
