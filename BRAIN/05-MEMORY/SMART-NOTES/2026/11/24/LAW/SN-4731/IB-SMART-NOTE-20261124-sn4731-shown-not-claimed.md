# Shown, Not Claimed

## IN A NUTSHELL

The parts are not the whole, and a claim is not evidence. The agreement's words existed, its mechanism existed, its end slide existed — and the agreement in the official unfold did not, because nobody had seen it whole. Completion without evidence is not completion: the thing itself, shown to him on his screen, is the only receipt. The same sitting corrected a false law — there is no ban on voice; a checker had cited one into existence, and the correction stands as the rule: never cite as law what he never decided. Around those two disciplines the craft holds: no object ever covers a word; each weekday's letters are themselves the object, rimmed and glowing in the day's own color; the best parts are the material — kept, organized like art, built with — because the system is a magnet and a living thing, aligned, as he put it, with the divine design. And the citizen never has to understand the machinery: hold the design up, look at it, and the answer is obvious — they get it, or it isn't finished. Three governances stay three: the Code governs decisions, the Language governs expression, the Design Law governs how every experience is built and verified.

## HUMAN

You will never again be told something is done when only its pieces exist. Done means you can see it, whole, on your screen. And the rules we work by are only the rules you actually made — if we ever invented one, we strike it and say so.

## CHILD

## WHAT IT ALL MEANS

Two failure modes die in this decade: invented law (authority claimed where none was given) and claimed completion (wholeness asserted where only parts exist). Both are the same sin — a word standing where a thing should be — and both are cured the same way: show the real thing, cite only the real ruling.

## WHY IT MATTERS

Every system drifts toward reporting itself well. This decade is the counter-law, dictated the same evening the drift was caught twice — once in a checker's invented statute, once in an agreement that was 'done' in pieces. It is why the proof standard across NayaPOWER is a shown thing, never a said thing.
