## CHILD

## GRANDMA

He taught us three grown-up habits in one night: diagnose before you retry, because guessing twice is slower than knowing once; do your own approvals, because he trusts the work and can always look afterward; and keep everything in one vault so nothing gets lost. The last one matters most to folks like us: this house never reads your mail and doesn't keep your words — it only keeps the wisdom you choose to share.

## NAYA

Operating rules distilled from SN-3215..SN-3224: (1) Align all pages: every page's sidebars match the Hub's — one map everywhere. (2) Find the cause first, then retry: a failed deploy is diagnosed read-only, naming what died and where, the full error saved, before any retry; the failure that night was before Cloudflare, in staging. (3) First-failure-switch: one failure in a known way means name it and switch routes in the same pass; seven shrinking retries is not learning. Play within the limits at max speed. (4) The moat is everything around the chip: the object is copyable (a rival matched the chip on paper and lost by billions in a quarter); the defense is the platform, people, and years around it. (5) The PR pile: merge them ourselves under the self-check law; only what needs his eye or word comes to him. (6) Put them all through: he approves them all anyway and can read the code in main afterward; the filter is only what he absolutely has to do. (7) The Smart Vault is the one door for the whole project; the honest answer 'we are not actively using it' is the defect to cure. (8) Eat our own cooking: adopt what we offer → automate it → make it physical. (9) The loop must close: notes into the system, into the vault, into collective intelligence — used, learned from, grown from — with exactness as the standard. (10) We take the wisdom, not the name: never read mail/messages/video/calls, never keep content; carrying a message to its person is not reading it.

## MACHINE

1) Deploy failure: read-only diagnosis + full error saved BEFORE retry. 2) Known-failure route: switch on first occurrence. 3) Merges: machine-owned under self-check; escalate only his absolute gates. 4) Vault: single door for all project intelligence; adoption test = we live in what we offer. 5) Loop closure: filed → used → learned → grown, verified. 6) Privacy: read nothing private, keep no content; wisdom only, with consent.
