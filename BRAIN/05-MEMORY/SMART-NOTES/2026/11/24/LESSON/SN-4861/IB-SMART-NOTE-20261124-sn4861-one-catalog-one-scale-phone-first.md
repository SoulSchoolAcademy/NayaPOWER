
# One Catalog, One Scale, Phone First

SN-4861 · LESSON / DISTILLATION

**Created:** 2026-11-24T23:55:55Z

**Kinds:** Multiplication · Craft · Knowledge

## Hook

One catalog, one scale, one phone in the hand — and every page a friend walking beside you.

## Nutshell

Every word follows one scale: headlines 25, sub-headlines 19, small text 17 — white first, never gray. Words are kept to the few that must be there; icons, pictures and objects do the talking. Every object is built the same way — black inside, white as a real object in the middle, light and shadow outside — from a catalog of eight. The purple diamond jewel in its silver winged crown is the bar every piece of art is measured against. Glow lives behind and around the object, never over its face. Reading unfolds three sentences per board, three boards then a button. The view turns to frame what the citizen is looking at. Every screen is built for the phone first. Every page guides like a best friend, and every screen fits whole.

## Human

Think of a book made by one master printer: every word the same clear size, the ink never gray, never faint. The pictures do the talking, each built the same way, lit from the same window, measured against one perfect jewel. The book opens a little at a time, and it always fits in your hand.

## Child

Use the same sizes for words everywhere. White words, never gray. Let pictures do the talking, not lots of words. Build every picture the same way, lit from the top-left. Glow goes behind, never on top. Show three short boards, then a button. Make it for the phone first.

## Grandma

A good page is like a good letter, dear — the words are the same clear size everywhere, white and easy to read, never gray and faint, and there are only as many as you need. Each picture is made the same careful way, lit from the same window, and judged against one perfect jewel.

## Naya

Use only the three sizes — 25, 19, 17 — white first, never gray, wisdom white in quotation marks. Cut words until only the ones that must be there remain. Build every object in three layers from the catalog of eight, one light top-left. Measure all art against the emblem; the N is always the glowing red ball with the golden N. Put glow behind and around, never over the face. Break text into boards of three sentences, three boards then a button — even legal pages. Design at phone width first. Guide every page step by step, and fit every screen whole.

## What it means

1. One scale is one system: a headline is 25 everywhere.
2. Minimum text is a kindness: pictures, icons and objects carry meaning faster than paragraphs.
3. The catalog is the law made buildable: eight objects, one construction each.
4. The emblem is the bar: art is judged against the jewel, not against taste.
5. Guidance is part of the page — and a screen that does not fit whole is not finished.

## Why it matters

1. Before the text laws, sizes drifted page by page and the pages read as many systems.
2. The Messenger rebuild had the right words and still failed as "a pile of text".
3. Glow over the face of an object hides the object; glow behind it makes it real.
4. Three boards then a button is how a long idea becomes a walk instead of a wall.
5. The citizen meets the system on the phone.

## What changes now

1. **From now on, we build every screen from the one catalog, in the one scale, at phone width first, and guide the citizen step by step instead of inventing looks, drifting sizes, and leaving people lost.**
2. Every new object takes its construction from the catalog of eight — never invented, never eyeballed.
3. Every piece of text uses the three sizes only, white first, never gray, cut to the words that must be there.
4. Every long idea is broken into boards of three sentences, three boards then a button — no wall is ever excused.
5. Every screen is judged at phone width first and fitted whole, the rest stacked inward behind a tap.
