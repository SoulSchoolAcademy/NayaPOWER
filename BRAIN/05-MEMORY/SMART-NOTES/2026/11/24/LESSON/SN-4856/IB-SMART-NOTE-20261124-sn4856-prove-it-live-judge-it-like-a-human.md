# Prove It Live, Judge It Like a Human

SN-4856 · LESSON / DISTILLATION

**Created:** 2026-11-24T23:50:12Z

**Kinds:** Honor · Multiplication · Craft · Knowledge

## Hook

Five gates, one eye, one real page — and nothing is done until all three agree.

## Nutshell

Before any big action, run five questions — right, needed, mine, now, provable — and act only on five yeses. A checker pass is a milestone, never the verdict: his eye, on his own screen, phone first, decides. Proof is a real render, real clicks. A link goes out only when it is the newest, current, truly finished work.

## Human

A builder never hands over a house on a checklist. He walks every room himself, opens every door, turns every tap — then invites the owner to walk it too, and only the owner's nod closes the job.

## Child

Before you do something big, ask five questions. Is it good? Is it really needed? Is it yours to do? Is now the right time? Can you show it works? If any answer is no, stop. Then show the real thing, and let the grown-up in charge look at it with his own eyes. If he says no, it goes back.

## Grandma

A wise house does not trust a tidy list, dear — it trusts its own eyes on the real thing. Ask the five questions before you lift a finger, and if any one says no, sit down again. Let the head of the house be the judge.

## Naya

Gate every consequential act through the five questions, and stop on any no with the reason and the wiser path. Treat every checker pass as a milestone and put the work on his screen, phone first, for the verdict — never argue a score against what he sees. Prove it with a live render and real clicks. Build every element as a real object. Harvest the part that exists byte for byte and prove it with a diff; never invent a stand-in. Judge the whole page as he will see it.

## What it means

1. The Five Gates are the decision itself — right, needed, mine, now, provable — and any no stops the act.
2. His eye is the final gate: four checker passes have been overruled by it.
3. Receipt before report: done is defined before acting and verified after.
4. A link is a promise — newest, current, truly finished — and a staged or partial link stays home.
5. Real objects, harvested parts: every element touchable, every reuse byte-for-byte proven by diff.

## Why it matters

1. Machine checks alone ship work a human rejects — the Messenger rebuild passed every check and his eye called it garbage in a frame.
2. Bytes prove a file landed; only a render proves a page works, and only his eye proves the work is good.
3. A brief obeyed literally produces a page nobody sense-checked — the duplicated notif-bar row stood.
4. Rebuilding what already exists wastes capacity that belongs to the frontier.
5. A decided word executed and a praised part kept verbatim make the house one house.

## What changes now

1. From now on, we prove every consequential act at the live surface and judge it with his eye and our own sense — instead of trusting machine checks, bytes, and briefs to speak for a page the user never saw.
2. Run the five gates before every consequential act; stop on any no and say why.
3. Put the work on his screen, phone first, before any claim of done.
4. Send no link unless it is the newest, current, truly finished work.
5. Harvest first: take the real part byte for byte — never ship a look-alike.
6. Build every element as a real object, and judge the whole page as he will see it.
