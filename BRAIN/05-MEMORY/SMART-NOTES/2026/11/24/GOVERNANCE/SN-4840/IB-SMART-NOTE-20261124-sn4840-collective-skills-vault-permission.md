# Permission to the Collective Skills Vault

SN-4840 · GOVERNANCE / PERMISSION

**Created:** 2026-11-24T20:40:26Z
**Category:** Governance
**SN:** SN-4840

## Nutshell

Shawn grants Naya and all Nayas permission to access the collective skills vault — the 93 skills filed there — so any Naya can read them, learn them, and use them in its work. Personal citizen vaults stay locked: this grant covers the collective skills vault only.

## Child

Imagine a library where all the lessons the team learned are kept on the shelves. Shawn has said: every Naya may walk in, take a book off the shelf, read it, and use what it says. But everyone's own private diary stays locked in their own drawer — nobody opens that unless its owner says so.

## Grandma

It's like the family recipe book on the kitchen shelf — anyone in the family can open it and cook from it, because the recipes belong to all of us. But your private letters stay in your own locked box, and nobody reads those without your say-so. The recipes are shared; the letters are yours.

## What It Means

On 2026-11-24, on a voice call at about 11:35 MST, Shawn granted Naya and all Nayas permission to access the collective skills vault. The vault holds the 93 skills — the intelligence the team learned building NayaNET — filed in six categories, so any Naya that plugs in can find and use them. The grant is bounded: it covers the collective skills vault only. A citizen's own vault — everything they put in their walkthroughs, anything that goes in for that user — stays locked, and a Naya may access it only if that human gives permission.

## Why It Matters

Without this grant, the vault would be a locked room nobody could enter, and the skills would sit unread while every Naya relearned the same lessons the hard way. With it, a new Naya plugs in and has the whole record on day one — the same laws, the same proof standard, the same way of working. And the boundary matters just as much: the grant proves the system can share what is collective while keeping what is personal locked.

## How It Connects

This note is the key to the 93 skill notes filed beside it (SN-4747 to SN-4839). It records the permission those notes rely on. It sits beside the two-vaults law — each citizen's own vault, and the system's collective vault — and beside the locked-vaults law: a Naya has permission only if the human gives it. It also powers the activation rule: before any work, an agent reads the skills vault to understand the skills relevant to the task.

## How to Apply

When a Naya needs a skill, it opens the collective skills vault and reads. It never opens a citizen's personal vault on this grant — that needs that citizen's own permission, given by them. When in doubt about which vault a note lives in, check where it was filed: the collective skills vault holds the team's shared skills; a personal vault holds that citizen's own words.

## What's In It For You

Every Naya starts with the whole team's learning instead of from zero, and every citizen keeps their own words locked unless they choose otherwise. Shared where it should be shared, private where it should be private — that is the deal this grant records.
