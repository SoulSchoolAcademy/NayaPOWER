# The Viewport Is the Service
SN-0858 · DESIGN / Viewport Optimization Law
**Created:** 2026-11-01T16:56:25Z

## The Law
Every screen optimizes to the user's viewpoint. The screen is not a fixed layout — it is a living viewport that morphs to what the person is doing in that moment. Everything needed is present and beautiful. Everything not needed fades away.

## What This Means
When a person taps an orb to see its story, they don't need the search bar. They don't need the top navigation. They need the story — centered, clear, in their perfect viewpoint. The screen understands this and morphs: chrome fades, content takes center stage, a tiny control restores the full view when they want it back.

This is not a dashboard feature. This is how every screen in NayaNET works.

## The Three Questions
Every screen, every time, must answer:
1. **What is the person trying to do here?** (intent)
2. **What do they need to see to do it at the highest level?** (essence)
3. **What can fade away without loss?** (restraint)

The screen then morphs to the answers. Not a redesign — the same elements, re-weighted for the moment.

## Why It Matters
Ten-star service is not about showing everything. It's about showing the right thing. A waiter doesn't bring the entire menu to the table on every course — they bring the course you're eating. The viewport is the table. The screen is the service.

When we optimize the viewport to the viewpoint, the person feels understood. The system feels alive. It feels like a living mind — not a static page.

## The Standard
- **One purpose per viewport.** If the screen has two jobs, it has no job.
- **Fade, don't clutter.** Elements not serving the current purpose fade to 0 opacity or collapse — they don't compete.
- **Restore is always one tap.** A minimal control (corner button, swipe, tap-outside) brings back the full view.
- **Morph, don't jump.** Transitions are smooth (300ms). The screen breathes between states.
- **Mobile first.** The constrained viewport is the truth. If it works on phone, it works everywhere.

## Applies To
Every screen. Dashboard, Smart Notes, Mail, Lists, every smart app. The law is universal: understand intent → optimize viewpoint → fade the rest → deliver the max experience.

## The Deeper Truth
We are not building pages. We are building a network of intelligent minds that understand what a person needs and present it beautifully. The viewport is where intelligence becomes experience. When the viewport optimizes itself, the system feels alive — like it's thinking with you.

That's the goal. Not a better dashboard. A living system.
