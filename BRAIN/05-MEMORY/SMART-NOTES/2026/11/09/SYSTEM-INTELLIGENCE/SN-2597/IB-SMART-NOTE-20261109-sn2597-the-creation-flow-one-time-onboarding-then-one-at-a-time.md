## What it means

- **ONBOARDING IS THE CREATION FLOW, NOT A ONE-TIME DOOR:** the same questions, the same guidance, run again for every new page — from the Factory, one app at a time. A creation flow that only works once is a dead end.
- **ONE AT A TIME IS THE LAW:** Space, then Personal, then Business — never all at once. Batch creation is how citizens end up with three half-filled worlds; one at a time is how each world gets its own mission, goal, and vision.
- **MISSION / GOAL / VISION ARE PER WORLD:** one for Personal, one for Space, one for Business — asked for each, guided, filling out all three. One answer for everything would make three worlds with one soul.
- **OPTIONAL IS REAL:** no business, skip it; no Space, skip it. Leftover slots are not lost — they wait in the Factory (a private profile and a public profile are the example he gave).
- **THE CIRCLE IS THE ARCHITECTURE:** every new space/profile connects to your profiles and shows up in your menu, profile, business — in your world tab. Creation feeds the world; the world holds the creation. That is why this belongs inside the one-system merge (SN-2595), not beside it.
