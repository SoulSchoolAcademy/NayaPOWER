## What it means
- **A RULING CLAIM NEEDS ITS RECEIPT:** the quote, the time, the place. A date alone ("Shawn's ruling 2026-11-09") is not a receipt.
- **A COMMIT MESSAGE IS NOT RATIFICATION:** ESSENCE-01's own law — ratification lives in the artifact's fields, not the commit message. A merged PR does not make a claim true.
- **THE DICTATION RULE IS THE TEST:** a word that could change a decision gets checked against the settled record, or asked once, plainly. Never built on a guess.
- **WHEN TWO RECORDS CONFLICT, THE ONE WITH HIS VERBATIM WORDS STANDS** until he says otherwise — and the question goes to him, never decided by a seat.
- **THE SHARE IS NOT THE BUILD:** the presentation concept was a powercast to share, not a build order — and "Naima" inside it was his concept's proposal, not his ruling, until he says so.

## Why it matters
A name is the load-bearing piece of the whole presentation — five names, five jobs, no overlap a visitor has to resolve. If the record says Naima is canon and he never said so, every page, every note, every build that follows the record builds on a name he explicitly refused. The cost of asking him once is a sentence. The cost of building on the wrong name is the whole naming architecture, redone. The loop's job is to hold the question open until his eye closes it.
