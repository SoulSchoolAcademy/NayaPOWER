# SN-2623 — The Three Questions: What Would You Do With It?

## Hook
"Hi, a high-tech ready-made world, personalized for you. What would you do with it? Would you value it? Would you tell your friends?" Three questions, and they are the whole pitch: use, value, tell.

## Nutshell
The pitch is not a product description — it is a question that puts the person in the picture: here is YOUR world, what would YOU do with it? Three questions test everything that matters: would you use it, would you value it, would you tell your friends? Use, value, tell — that is the flywheel. And the one thing the question needs: "personalized for you" has to be SHOWN, not said — the stranger has to see her world with her stuff in it.

## Human
"Hi, a high-tech ready-made world, personalized for you. What would you do with it? Would you value it? Would you tell your friends?" That is the pitch, and it works because it never pitches a product — it puts the person in the picture. Not "here is what we built" but "here is YOUR world — what would YOU do?" The three questions are three tests, in order. What would you do with it — would you live in it, would you use it, is it something that does something for you, or does it just sit there? Would you value it — is it worth something to you, does it know you, does it do the work, does it earn with you? Would you tell your friends — because it works, not because anyone asked you to, and because telling them earns you half? Use, value, tell — that is the flywheel: use it, value it, tell your friends, they join, they use it, they value it, they tell theirs. The question is honest about what it is testing, and it is honest about what it needs: "personalized for you" has to be SHOWN, not said. The stranger has to see her world with her stuff in it — her pages, her people, her work — or the claim is just a claim. Show the world, then ask the questions.

## What it means
- **THE PITCH IS THREE QUESTIONS:** what would you do with it? Would you value it? Would you tell your friends?
- **USE → VALUE → TELL:** that is the flywheel. Use it, value it, tell your friends, they join, they use it, they value it, they tell theirs.
- **IT PUTS THE PERSON IN THE PICTURE:** not "here is our product" — "here is YOUR world, what would YOU do?"
- **PERSONALIZED MUST BE SHOWN, NOT SAID:** the stranger has to see her world with her stuff in it — her pages, her people, her work — or the claim is just a claim.

## Why it matters
Every pitch, every page, every letter is judged by the test in his head: is this ten-star service, would somebody see value in it and trust in it, would they get it if they experienced it? The three questions ARE that test, asked out loud, to a stranger. If she cannot answer yes to all three, the page is not done. And the test is honest about its own condition: the showing comes first. A personalized world you cannot see is a promise, not a product.
