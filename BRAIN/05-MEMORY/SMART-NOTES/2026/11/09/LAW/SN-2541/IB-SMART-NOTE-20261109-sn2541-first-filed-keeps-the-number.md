## Human

Two shops open on the same street with the same number on the door — the post goes to the wrong one, the customers get confused, and the street looks broken. The rule is simple and old: the shop that was there first keeps the number; the second one takes the next free number and puts up its own sign. You do not make the first shop move because the second one arrived.

## Child

Imagine two kids in class both named the same name, and the teacher calls the name — both answer, and nobody knows who she means. The fair rule is: the kid who was in the class first keeps the name, and the new kid gets a new one. And then the teacher checks the whole class list, just in case there are any other two kids sharing a name she missed.

Dear, when two letters arrive with the same address, you don't throw one away — you give the second one its own proper address, love. The first one stays where it is, because it was there first. And a good postmistress checks the whole street while she is at it, so it never happens again — that is what the guard does now.
