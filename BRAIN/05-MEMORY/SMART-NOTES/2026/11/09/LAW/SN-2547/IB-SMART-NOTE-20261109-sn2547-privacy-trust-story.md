## Hook

"AI operator" does not mean she reads your stuff. It means the opposite — and people need to hear it in words they can feel.

## Nutshell

- **SHE DOES NOT READ YOUR MAIL. SHE DOES NOT READ YOUR MESSAGES.** Not programmed to — in fact, by default she does not even SAVE them.
- **WHAT SHE DOES IS DISTILL INTELLIGENCE, BY DEFAULT:** what gets read is the smart notes, and any notes you wish to share and become smart notes.
- **SHARING IS YOUR CHOICE:** to share intelligence with the network, make an intelligent block and make it public — beautiful form, nutshell + multiple perspectives + what it means + how it connects + what's in it for you. Anyone can learn any subject in about a minute, book-level understanding, the essence.
- **EVERYTHING ELSE IS PRIVATE BY DEFAULT:** sharing wisdom by default is recommended (that's what most people do) — without your identity — but you can share publicly, privately, or not at all, whatever you're comfortable with.
- **THE COMMUNITY:** build spaces and talk to people about the wisdom — especially the important topics, especially ones that can help you — a team of people helping you on any one topic. Not just the book in a minute — a whole community to talk about that book or topic, all in one, highly optimized and personalized.
- **THE NETWORK:** an intelligent interconnected network of Super Brains, connected to your Smart Box that stores your world completely private; you communicate on the network completely private — nobody hears your conversations, nobody's reading it.
- **THE MECHANISM:** smart note it to share it with the world; note it privately to share with friends, family, network, project, your Nayas — it's not public until you smart note it. Make it official = make it a smart note.

## Human

A good bank does not read your letters. It keeps your safe, it organizes your boxes when you ask, and it never opens a drawer to satisfy its own curiosity. This is that story, told for a network instead of a bank: the operator holds the keys to run your vault, not to read your papers. The papers you choose to publish become a book anyone can read in a minute; the papers you don't choose stay in the safe, unread, unsaved, unheard. The story is the product's trust, told in plain words — because a privacy architecture nobody understands is a privacy promise nobody believes.

If you want to share a story with the whole school, you copy it out nicely and pin it on the board yourself — that's a smart note. Everything else stays locked in your diary, just yours. And you can still talk to your friends in your own secret clubhouse where nobody else can listen. That's how it works here.
