## Why it matters

Scale is the fastest way a page betrays itself. A visitor who cannot tell whether the numbers are real has already stopped believing the page, and no amount of correct data repairs that — the doubt is visual, not numerical. Proportion is also the cheapest fix in the system: no new data, no new component, just sizes that belong together. And the zoom exchange is a small lesson in honesty: when he asks "is that me or is that you?", the answer comes from the evidence (the screenshots), not from deference — and "it's the build, not your zoom" is the answer that lets the fix land on the right thing.
