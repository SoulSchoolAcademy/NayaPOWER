## Why it matters

A visitor who has to scroll forever stops scrolling — and the close (the ten reasons, the Start button) sits at the very bottom, exactly where a tired scroller never arrives. Shortening the page is not tidiness; it is making sure the ending gets read. And the popup rule keeps the proof without the length: the numbers are still there, one tap away, for whoever wants them.
