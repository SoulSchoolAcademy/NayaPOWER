Dear, he settled it kindly: if someone shares something without letting Naya make it beautiful first, they still earn their points — we reward the sharing, not the formatting. ## Naya

The ruling is a values decision, not a mechanics one: reward the action (sharing, contributing to the hive), nudge the form (beauty, congruence), never punish the choice. It keeps the points system aligned with what the network actually wants — more sharing — instead of turning it into a formatting police. It also draws the line cleanly between public and private: public gets the recommendation and the default; private gets no opinion at all. And it closes the SN-2417 open item in the direction of generosity, which matches the wider law: citizens earn for what they give, and the system's job is to make the beautiful choice the easy one, not the only one.

## Why it matters

A points system that punishes unformatted sharing would teach citizens to share less, or to share only when the format is perfect — the opposite of a network that grows by sharing. Rewarding the action and recommending the form keeps both values alive: the hive grows, and it grows beautiful. It also settles, with his word, a question the notes work was carrying open — the record can now say "settled" instead of "open."
