Auto-format on by default, with an off-switch and only a gentle nudge. No points dock for unformatted shares.

## Why it matters

1. A page that re-prints its prices nags; a page with no door at the decision loses the reader at the exact moment it has won them. Prices once plus doors everywhere is the whole funnel in one rule.
2. Phone first is not a layout preference — it is where the reader lives. A page judged only on desktop is a page judged for nobody.
3. Choice always, beauty by default is the same respect applied to sharing: the network stays beautiful because the beautiful choice is the easy one, not because the other choice is punished.
4. The feed check matters because every claim in this group that proved true was proved from the live bytes — and the two notes that did not parse are still open, named, not papered over.
