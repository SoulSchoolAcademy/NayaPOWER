## Human

Think of a good secretary, a good friend, and the manager of the whole office in one (SN-2535). Every day she reads over your notes and your projects and writes you a short report about your day — what you learned, what got done — and she puts it in your Reports area where you can always find it, this week's and this month's too. She keeps your notes for you, and when you need a new page, she makes it and fills it in. She writes and sends your letters and messages for you — even the welcome letter when someone new joins your team. Anything you hand her, she enters into your Smart Box and remembers it for you. You just live your day and ask; she runs the office. And the test is simple: if you still have to do it by hand inside the app, she isn't doing her job yet (SN-2534). That is the difference between the old internet and the new one — not a feature list, a contrast you can feel. You can feel which world you are standing in.
