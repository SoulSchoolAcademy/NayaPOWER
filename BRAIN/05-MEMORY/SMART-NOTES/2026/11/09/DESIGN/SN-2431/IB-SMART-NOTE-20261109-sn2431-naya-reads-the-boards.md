# SN-2431 — Naya Reads the Boards: Her Face All the Way Through

## Hook

When the Top 10 unfolds, Naya reads it out — her face on every beat, switching sides as you click, so it feels like she is talking to you.

## In a Nutshell

Shawn's design, 2026-11-09: the pricing page's three unfolding sections (Top 10 pain points, Top 10 ideal customers, the founder's letter) carry Naya's face all the way through — different profile pictures, switching from one side to the next as the beats unfold, personal and warm, the way the Academy page goes back and forth between her pictures. He calls this smart design. The eight faces are filed as Factory assets: seven new profiles plus NAYA_POWER.jpg, already the canonical portrait. Balance rides with it — the power of what's said balanced against how long it is, because the details matter. One question stays open for him: does "reads them out" mean her voice speaking the words, her face presenting each beat, or both? No voice is built until he answers.

## Human

The words were already good — word perfect, logical, reasonable, sensible — but words alone are a page talking at you. Her face makes it a person talking to you. As each beat unfolds, she is there: a different picture, the other side, then back again, so the page never sits still and never repeats itself. The pains get her face, the people get her face, the letter already has her portrait — the whole page is her, present the whole way down. That's the warmth: not a stock photo dropped in once, but her face living in the flow, the way the Academy page does it. And the balance standard holds the words in check — strong words, short beats, every detail earning its place.

## Child

Imagine a friend reading you a story, and every time you turn the page, she's sitting on the other side of you, smiling. One page she's on the left, the next she's on the right. That's Naya on the pricing page — she reads the pains, she reads the people, she reads the letter, and her face is with you the whole way. Not the same picture over and over — lots of pictures, so it always feels fresh and warm, like she's really there with you.

## Grandma

Dear, it's a lovely thing — the page doesn't just tell you, it shows you her. Each little piece opens, and there's her face, warm and smiling, first on one side and then the other, like she's sitting across from you telling you herself. The pains, the people, the letter — she's with you through all of it, love. And the words are short and plain, because the details matter and nobody should have to wade through a wall of words to hear something true.
