## What it means

- **BOTH DIRECTIONS ARE TRUE:** intelligence creates the physical, and the physical creates the intelligence — the circle has no single start.
- **SMART BLOCKS ARE THE PHYSICS:** intelligent pieces, Lego blocks, a Rubik's Cube matrix that morphs to the page and optimizes to the user's viewpoint — 10-star service is the morphing, not the decoration.
- **VISUALLY BLISSFUL BUT USEFUL:** numbers or words or anything — beautiful, helpful, functional, incredible to experience. Beauty without use is not the circle.
- **BUILD FOR THE MACHINE AND THE AI:** the AI operates the machine in its language so the human gets the 10-star experience of the interface and of the AI.
- **THE FRANCHISE IS THE SCALE:** millions of smart apps, interconnected, one brand language, any business, any person — as long as they follow the rules of the system.
- **LAW OF ONE + 50% KEEPS IT TURNING:** do no harm to yourself or others; produce positive valuable intelligence; build valuable systems on a network; if you do, you are rewarded — 50% of the revenue — because you are part of the network.

## Why it matters

Every design decision, every seat brief, every page can now be tested against one question: does this keep the circle turning? A page that is beautiful but useless breaks the circle (no value out). A system that keeps all the revenue breaks the circle (no value shared). A machine built for humans to operate breaks the circle (the human is doing the machine's work). The full circle is the test his eye has been running all along, now named in his own words.
