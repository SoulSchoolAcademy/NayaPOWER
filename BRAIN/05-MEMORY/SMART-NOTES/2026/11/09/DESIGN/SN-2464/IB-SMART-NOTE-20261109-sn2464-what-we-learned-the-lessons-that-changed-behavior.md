The twelve lessons, with their moments and their mechanisms:

1. **Dictation is material, not copy (00:41Z Nov 9).** The v49 disaster: his dictation pasted verbatim, stage directions on the page. The lesson: scorecard the existing words against his new words, take what is great from both, CRAFT the 10/10 version in his voice. His product words stay verbatim; his direction gets shaped. Enforced by: template §2A L2 + gate check (h) + human check H2.
2. **Never verbatim-obey — think, don't just obey (00:46Z + 02:42Z).** "It's like as if you just wrote my words verbatim… did you really answer the questions for the people, or did you just say what I said?" A child who just does what he's told, never thinking whether it's right, is a robot — not intelligent. 10-star service IS thinking first. Enforced by: the stranger test (L1) on every beat.
3. **His words are input, not output — the perfect-words responsibility (02:56Z).** "Just because I say them doesn't mean they're the perfect words... it's your responsibility, because I'm inputting my words into you to make them into the perfect words, so that it speaks to the world, the child, the grandma, to everyone." Enforced by: human check H2, attested with evidence.
4. **The stranger test is the gate on every beat (02:42Z).** A stranger who has never heard of NayaNET — does she GET it, BELIEVE it, TRUST us? Every beat must answer the question it raises with a mechanism, a picture, or a number she can check. Enforced by: template §2A L1 + human check H1.
5. **The test in his head, always (02:44Z).** "Is this the 10 star service, and would somebody see value in it and trust in it? If they experienced it, would they get it? Is it obvious? Is it useful? Helpful?" Run on EVERYTHING before it goes out. If any answer is no, it's not done yet. Enforced by: template §2A L5 + human check H4.
6. **Title + subtitle + nutshell — the skimmable standard (02:54Z).** People skim. A wall of text fails. Every board is title + subtitle + nutshell: what it means, what's in it for them, spelled out clear as day. Public copy must be professional and explain ourselves. Enforced by: template §2A L3 + gate check (i).
7. **Naya speaks, faces alternate (02:27Z + 02:59Z).** First person, her face beside the words, LEFT then RIGHT switching down the page, different faces — the switch resets attention and helps people understand. "It's not what you say, it's how you say it." Enforced by: template §2A L4 + gate check (i).
8. **The one ultimate ten, not two (03:21Z + 03:28Z).** One set of ten, each item three in one: pain + solution + what's in it for you. The ten most valuable features are valuable BECAUSE they solve the top ten pains — they go hand in hand. Enforced by: template §2A L7 + human check H5.
9. **Smart note format — `##` sections, never bold-inline (02:44Z).** The sync parser only recognizes `##` headings. Five notes in one night failed to parse because they used bold-inline labels. Enforced by: template §2A L6 + gate check (g).
10. **Harvest first, never invent — and take the shown object (20:41Z Nov 8).** When he SHOWS an object, the deliverable IS that object — same shape, same colors, same jewel, same glow. Recreating it "in the same spirit" is inventing, and it will be condemned. Enforced by: the brief's harvest-sources section + Law 10.
11. **Receipt before report (standing, broken most often).** The 401 false key-death (Nov 4): I told him the key expired; the probe was broken. The dead-buttons misreport: I told him no button; the truth was dead buttons. "Done" from scroll-0 checks while the scrolled view crashed. Enforced by: the delivery rule — live link + byte count + measurements (scroll 0 AND scrolled, desktop AND phone) + DONE + NOT DONE.
12. **A lesson counts only when it changes behavior mechanically (03:31Z).** The psychology: a child obeys rules, an adult understands reasons, a superstar sees the whole and chooses wise before being told. Tonight proved the learning WORKS (each correction changed the next piece) and LEAKS (v4 repeated v49 because the lesson sat in a note, not in its brief). Enforced by: the Correction Compound — lesson → code in the same pass, tested by "would this have stopped the next seat?"
