## Human

Here is the cycle, step by step, the way I see it now.

**One — capture.** Somebody lives something: a day of building, a book read, a mistake made, a breakthrough. The raw experience is slow and expensive — it cost real time to live. Capture it: write it down, smart-note it, say it out loud. Nothing is captured, nothing compounds.

**Two — distill.** Take what was learned and get the core. Not the whole book — the essence. What changed? What would you do differently? What is the rule now? Distillation is the work of throwing away everything except the intelligence. A thousand raw notes become ten to fifty distilled notes — never a thousand, because a thousand raws is a library nobody reads, and ten distilled notes is a brain somebody uses. The raw notes are deleted after distilling, the same as emails — deletion by default — because the essence is what the system keeps, and the noise is what it sheds.

**Three — block.** Put the distillation into an intelligent block: a title, a subtitle, a nutshell — what it means, what's in it for them, spelled out so it's clear as day — with a jewel that tells the eye what kind of thing it is, and orb bullets that break it into pieces one idea at a time. The block is the delivery vehicle. It is designed so a stranger who has never heard of any of this can read it in seconds and completely get it. The formatting is not decoration — it is the speed. A wall of text costs minutes; a block costs seconds.

**Four — learn.** The next person reads the block. She does not pay the reading cost of the book. She does not pay the living cost of the experience. She gets the whole book in seconds — the intelligence, without the time. That is the speedup, and it is real: her time between not-knowing and knowing just collapsed from minutes to seconds.

**Five — grow.** Because she learned in seconds, she produces faster. She builds the next thing sooner, she makes the next mistake sooner, she has the next breakthrough sooner — and all of it gets captured, distilled, and blocked again. The loop turns. And each turn, the system holds more distilled intelligence, so the next person starts further along, and learns faster still.

Automatic means the speedup repeats without anyone pushing it. Automatic means the system gets faster while everybody sleeps. That is the machine: it runs at the speed of thought, and it never stops producing value.
