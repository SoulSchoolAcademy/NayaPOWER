## Why it matters

1. Trust is the page's currency: one dead button teaches the visitor the page lies, and no amount of beautiful copy buys that trust back.
2. An honest map is a build list: 18 rows without a button are 18 jobs waiting, named — the chart says what is real and what is still to build by what it shows and what it doesn't.
3. Measuring the wrong thing is worse than not measuring: a pixel-diff that "proved" circling was really breathing, and the record carried a false proof until a verifier measured the rotation itself.
4. Clearing a false defect matters as much as fixing a real one — it stops the machine chasing ghosts and keeps a clean page looking clean.
