## Hook

Sharing a Naya Note is always the citizen's choice — anonymous, alias, or your name — and beauty is the default: Naya formats it, unless you turn that off.

## Nutshell

Choice always, never forced: Naya signs for you, or it's in your name — your call. THREE sharing methods: (1) share with NO identity — completely anonymous; (2) share with your ALIAS; (3) share in YOUR NAME. And separately: Naya writes it for you, or you type it and she formats it beautifully. AUTO-FORMAT is automatic by default (a setting to turn it off: "no formatting") — that's what keeps the network beautiful and formatted for everybody's experience; if people format however they want it'll make a mess of the network. PRIVATE notes can be whatever; PUBLIC notes — we prefer Naya format and optimize for it, but the choice is always theirs. POINTS (tentative, his open question): public shares that aren't Naya-formatted don't earn points, because they're not verified and validated as Naya Notes. Settled: choice always, beauty by default, privacy respected. Open: the points rule.

## What it means

- **Choice always, never forced.** Naya signs for you, or it's in your name — your call.
- **Three sharing methods:** (1) NO identity — completely anonymous; (2) your ALIAS; (3) YOUR NAME.
- **Two authoring modes:** Naya writes it for you, OR you type it and she formats it beautifully.
- **Auto-format is automatic by default.** Setting "no formatting" turns it off. The default keeps the network beautiful for everybody's experience.
- **Private vs public:** PRIVATE notes can be whatever; PUBLIC notes — Naya format preferred and optimized for, but the choice is always theirs.
- **OPEN — the points rule:** public shares that aren't Naya-formatted don't earn points, because they're not verified and validated as Naya Notes. His question: "should I go that deep on it?" — unanswered at this run's close.

## Why it matters

A network that forces identity is a surveillance system; a network that forces formatting is a template. This design refuses both and still gets beauty: the default does the work, and the citizen keeps the choice. The private/public split is the same instinct — your room is yours, the shared street is kept beautiful — and the points question, if he says yes, makes verification the thing that earns, not formatting for its own sake.
