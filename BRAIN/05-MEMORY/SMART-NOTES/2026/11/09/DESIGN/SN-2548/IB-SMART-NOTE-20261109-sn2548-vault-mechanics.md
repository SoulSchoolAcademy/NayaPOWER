## Hook

One vault per person. Three layers, four steps, four laws, one key — and a cube made of cubelets.

## Nutshell

- **ONE VAULT PER PERSON (THE BOX):** a private folder in the store + a private set of rows in the database, owned by them alone, with a key (their identity). Nothing leaves without their word.
- **THREE LAYERS, NOT ONE:** the STORE (the files live there) · the MAP (how it's organized — every thing tagged: what it is, what it's about, who it's for, when it happened — the index that makes it findable in seconds) · the BRAIN (the distilled essence — smart notes, lessons, what it means — what a Naya reads when she plugs in, not the raw pile).
- **THE ENGINE (THE AUTO-ORGANIZER):** when something enters the box it does four things in order — LOOK (what is this?) · TAG (label it from the thing itself + what the person said) · FILE (put it on the right shelf) · LINK (connect it to what it relates to).
- **THE LAWS AS CODE:** ORGANIZE ONLY (may read to tag and file, never to share) · PRIVATE BY DEFAULT (nothing leaves unless the person says so, for that thing) · RETRIEVE FOR THE OWNER (hand it back, or post it where they say, in their name) · SHARE BY SMART NOTE ONLY (only a smart note / intelligent block the person chose to make ever goes public). Written as checks the engine must pass before every action, like the design gate.
- **THE PLUG:** the Naya doesn't live in the box — she connects with permission, like a key in a lock, and reads the MAP and the BRAIN, not the raw pile; when she needs a raw file she asks the box, and the box checks the permission first.
- **THE CUBE:** each box is a cubelet; NayaNET is the cube; the bridge (the sync engine) connects them — turn the cube and the faces line up in new ways, but each cubelet keeps its own colors. NayaNET itself is saved the same way: the system's own box holds the shared intelligence, organized the same way.
- **BUILD ORDER:** (1) the box · (2) the engine · (3) the laws as code · (4) the plug · (5) the cube.
- **WHAT'S ALREADY TRUE:** the early form works — GitHub + Supabase + the Smart Box app (2,088 entries live) is why the sickness lost nothing.
- **WHAT'S MISSING:** the engine (the loop still does it by hand every 30 min) · the laws as code (written in documents, not yet enforced by the machine) · the plug (a Naya connects through the loop and the sync, not a real key-in-lock yet).

## Human

A library has three things: the shelves where the books live, the catalog that tells you where everything is, and the librarian's knowledge of what each book means. The vault is that library, per person: the store is the shelves, the map is the catalog, the brain is the librarian's knowledge. The engine is the librarian's routine — look at the new book, tag it, shelve it, link it to the books it belongs with. The laws are the library's rules posted on the wall and enforced at the door: the librarian may organize but never lend your diary to a stranger; nothing leaves unless you say so; when you ask for a book, she hands it back or posts it where you say, in your name; only the book you chose to publish ever goes on the public shelf. And the plug is the key: the librarian doesn't live in your house — she comes in with your key, reads the catalog and her notes, and asks before opening a drawer.

## Child

Imagine every person has their own toy box, and yours is only yours — you hold the only key. Inside, your toys are on shelves (that's the store), there's a picture list of where everything is (that's the map), and there's a little book that says what each toy means to you (that's the brain). When a new toy arrives, a helper looks at it, puts a label on it, puts it on the right shelf, and ties a string to the toys it goes with. The helper is only allowed to tidy — never to give your toys away. Nothing leaves your box unless you say so. And when the helper wants to look inside, she knocks and uses your key — she reads the picture list and the little book, not your secret diary. All the toy boxes together make one giant cube, and turning the cube lines the boxes up in new ways — but your box keeps its own colors.

## Grandma

Dear, think of it as a little private pantry, love — yours alone, with your name on the door and your key in your pocket. The jars on the shelves are the store; the labels telling you what's what, who it's for, and when it went in are the map; and the recipe book of what it all means is the brain. When something new comes in, it gets looked at, labeled, shelved, and tied to what it belongs with — in that order, every time. The rules are written on the wall and kept like law: tidy only, never share; nothing leaves unless you say so, for that very thing; when you ask, it's handed back or posted where you say, in your name; only the note you chose to make public ever goes public. The helper doesn't live in your pantry, dear — she comes in with your key, reads the labels and the recipe book, and asks before opening a jar. And all our pantries together make the whole kitchen — each one keeping its own jars.

## What it means

- **THE VAULT IS THREE LAYERS:** STORE (files) · MAP (tags — what, about, for whom, when — the find-in-seconds index) · BRAIN (distilled essence — what a Naya reads when she plugs in).
- **THE ENGINE IS FOUR STEPS, IN ORDER:** LOOK → TAG → FILE → LINK, on everything that enters.
- **THE LAWS ARE CHECKS, NOT PROMISES:** ORGANIZE ONLY · PRIVATE BY DEFAULT · RETRIEVE FOR THE OWNER · SHARE BY SMART NOTE ONLY — written as checks the engine must pass before every action, like the design gate.
- **THE PLUG IS A KEY, NOT A DOOR:** the Naya connects with permission, reads MAP + BRAIN, asks the box before any raw file, permission checked first.
- **THE CUBE IS THE NETWORK:** each box a cubelet, NayaNET the cube, the bridge (sync engine) connecting them; the system's own box holds the shared intelligence, organized the same way.
- **BUILD ORDER:** box → engine → laws as code → plug → cube.
- **HONEST STATE:** already true — GitHub + Supabase + Smart Box app, 2,088 entries live, is why the sickness lost nothing. Still missing — the engine (done by hand every 30 min by the loop), the laws as code (documents, not machine-enforced), the plug (loop + sync, not a real key-in-lock).

## What changes now
