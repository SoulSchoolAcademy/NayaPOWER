## Hook

Page boards and Smart Link boards use different presentations with no explanation — merge them into ONE ultimate board design, sections separated and named.

## Nutshell

- **HIS WORDS, VERBATIM:** "all of a sudden it separates. All of your spaces, business, and all of a sudden it's nine app slash join us. Just random no explanation of why the presentation is different or the boxes are different" — "merge both presentations together make the ultimate"
- **THE DEFECT:** halfway down the page the presentation changes for no reason — different boxes, different actions ("click link copy link link or open link" on one kind, no click-to-open or copy-link on the other). The reader cannot tell why, because there is no why.
- **THE FIX:** ONE ultimate board design for both. The sections are separated and NAMED — pages vs Smart Links — so the reader always knows what they're looking at. Actions are consistent across all boards: page boards get Open in the same style as Copy/Open.
- **THE RULE INSIDE IT:** a change of presentation must have a reason the reader can see. If two things look different, the difference must mean something; if it means nothing, it is one design.

## Why it matters

A reader builds a mental model of a page from its first boards — this is what a board looks like, this is what its buttons do. When the presentation changes halfway down for no reason, the model breaks and the reader has to start over, guessing what the new boxes mean. One design, named sections, consistent actions: the model holds from top to bottom, and the page reads as one thing made by one mind. That is what "it has to make sense and be logical" means at the level of the board.
