## Why it matters

Skimmability is the difference between a page people read and a page people leave. He said it plainly: "they just want to skim it and get it." A wall of text fails question four of his own test (SN-2443) — is it obvious? — before a reader reaches the second line. And professionalism is trust: a visitor who reads raw dictation hears the workshop, not the product. The boards are the hiring pitch — the first thing a future Ambassador reads — so they carry the standard first.
