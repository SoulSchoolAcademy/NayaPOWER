## Child

Imagine telling a friend, "If you do this, you'll get five thousand dollars every month forever." But that only works if every single person stays forever and nobody ever leaves — and people always leave sometimes. The honest way to say it is: "Three people a day, for a month, is ninety people — at fifty dollars each, that's about four thousand five hundred a month, for as long as they stay." Same dream, true numbers.
