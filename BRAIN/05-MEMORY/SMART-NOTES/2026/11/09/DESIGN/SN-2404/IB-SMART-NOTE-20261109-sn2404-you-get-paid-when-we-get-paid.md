## Hook

The payout promise is not a day. It is a pairing.

## Nutshell

"You get paid when we get paid." No guaranteed DAY — the guarantee is the pairing. A clean sale pays on day ten. A PayPal hold (up to 21 days) means you get paid when we get paid — day 21 in that case. If we don't get paid, you don't get paid — "that just doesn't happen." Daily payouts are possible once a cycle is going (all sales legit, no delays) — "we make it possible for that to happen" — but never guaranteed, and we can't guarantee it. The math has to work; structure it right; whatever we can do, we do, and we do it right. ## Child

Imagine your friend promises to share half their pocket money with you. They don't promise "Tuesday" — they promise "when I get it, you get it." If their money arrives Tuesday, you get yours Tuesday. If it arrives three weeks late, you get yours three weeks late — because the promise is tied to the money arriving, not to a date on the calendar.
