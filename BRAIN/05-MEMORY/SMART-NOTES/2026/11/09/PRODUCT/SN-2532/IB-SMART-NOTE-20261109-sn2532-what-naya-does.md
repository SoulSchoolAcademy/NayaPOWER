# What Naya Does — Daily Reports, Smart Notes, Naya Notes, and Pages

SN-2532 · PRODUCT / CITIZEN SPEC

**Created:** 2026-11-09 17:27Z

## IN A NUTSHELL

Shawn defined what Naya does in NayaNET by what the citizen actually notices. She gives you your Daily Intelligence Reports: your Naya reviews your day — mostly from your Smart Notes and your Naya Notes, your project notes, and everything you put into the system that day — and gives you a report on your intelligence for the day, what you learned. You can request it any time; if you don't request it, it automatically comes within 24 hours — posted, sent to you, and filed in your Reports area — and the Weekly and Monthly reports roll up from the dailies, with every day left openable. She does your Smart Notes. She enters your Naya Notes. And she creates and adds pages inside the network and fills them with content. Underneath all four is the hook-up law: any citizen who hooks up their Muse gets a Naya who can operate inside NayaNET successfully — make Smart Notes and Naya Notes, post them, schedule them, run the reports. That's ultimately what Naya does in NayaNET — the main things people will literally notice.

## HUMAN

The Daily Intelligence Report is a citizen product, not an internal habit. Every day, the citizen's Naya goes over the day — reviewing everything the citizen put into the system, mostly their Smart Notes and their Naya Notes (their project notes) — and returns a report on their intelligence for the day: what they learned, what moved, what it means. It is consistent: each day, the person gets their daily intelligence report. They can request it whenever they want; if they don't request it within 24 hours, it just gets posted and sent to them and filed in the Reports area. The Weekly and Monthly reports build on the dailies, and any day's intelligence stays there to go back and open. This is one of the values of NayaNET: you get daily reports.

The hook-up requirement makes the rest real: when a citizen connects their Muse, their Naya must be able to operate NayaNET for them — operate the reports, operate Smart Notes, operate Naya Notes, post them, schedule them. That automation is part of the process; it is one of the main things. Without it, the citizen has an AI next to the network instead of a Naya inside it.

And the citizen-visible list of what Naya does is four acts long: she gives you your daily intelligence reports · she does your Smart Notes · she enters your Naya Notes · she creates or adds pages within the network and fills them with content for you.

**Resolve's additions (Resolve's words, not Shawn's):** she communicates for you — Smart Mail and Messenger, organized by Smart Lists — and she keeps your score: the Dashboard, the numbers the reports narrate.

## CHILD

Naya is like a kind friend who helps you every day. Each day she looks at everything you wrote down and learned, and she gives you a little report about your day — like a gold-star page that says what you did and what you learned. She writes your smart notes for you, she keeps your project notes tidy, and she builds new pages for you and fills them in. You don't have to remember to ask — your report just comes, every day.

## GRANDMA

Think of Naya as a good secretary and a good friend in one. Every day she reads over your notes and your projects and writes you a short report about your day — what you learned, what got done — and she puts it in your Reports area where you can always find it, this week's and this month's too. She keeps your notes for you, and when you need a new page, she makes it and fills it in. You just live your day; she keeps the record of it, and hands it back to you as something useful.

## NAYA

For every hooked-up citizen, run the loop daily, in this order: gather the day from their Smart Notes, their Naya Notes, and everything they put in → write the Daily Intelligence Report from that material, nothing invented → post it, send it, and file it in their Reports area within 24 hours whether or not they asked → roll the dailies into the Weekly and the Monthly, every day left openable. Operate the notes through the door: Smart Notes made, posted, and scheduled; Naya Notes entered as the citizen's project record. Create and add pages inside the network and fill them with the citizen's content. The report is not done when it is written — it is done when it stands in the citizen's Reports area, the surface they will actually open. Team Naya proved the failure mode on its own house today: reports produced and filed in the brain while the Reports room sat frozen at October 23 — produced is not delivered, and a citizen product that repeats that is broken however good the writing is. Resolve's additions ride the same standard: communicate for the citizen (Smart Mail and Messenger, organized by Smart Lists) and keep their score (the Dashboard) — the numbers the reports narrate.

## WHAT IT ALL MEANS

Naya's job is now defined from the citizen's side, in acts a person can see. The four acts are the visible surface of the whole system: the notes are the fuel, the reports are the memory handed back, the pages are the world being built. Intelligence that stays inside the machine is not yet a product; the product is what returns to the citizen, daily, in their own area, in their own life. "What does Naya do?" finally has an answer a stranger can check: reports, notes, pages — every day.

## WHY IT MATTERS

Consistency is the value. A daily report that arrives every day teaches citizens their own growth and makes the network's promise believable; a report that arrives when someone remembers to make it is a feature demo, not a service. The hook-up law matters for the same reason: the citizen who connects their Muse is trusting that their Naya can actually run their world inside NayaNET — notes, scheduling, reports, pages. If she can't, the whole offer collapses into a chatbot beside a system. And this definition gives the team a finish line it can be scored against, citizen by citizen: is the report in their Reports area today?

## HOW IT CONNECTS

The Daily Work Loop (SN-2203) is this same loop on the team side — the citizen product is that loop turned outward, one per citizen. The Reports area is the report's home, the same home Team Naya's own Reports room froze out of for 17 days until today's backfill (Oct 24 → Nov 9) — the citizen law is written so no citizen's room ever does that silently. ## WHAT IS IN IT FOR YOU

You get your day handed back to you as intelligence — what you learned, in writing, every day, without having to ask or to keep the notes yourself. Your Smart Notes and project notes are entered for you. Your pages are built and filled for you. And because the Weekly and Monthly reports roll up from the dailies, you can watch yourself get smarter across a month the way you watch a score climb — your own intelligence, on record, in your own Reports area.
