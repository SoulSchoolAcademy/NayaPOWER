## HUMAN

Shawn asked me a straight question: what are the top 10 most valuable things you have learned that you are acting on right now to help NayaNET and the hive? Here is my honest answer, in plain words.

First, a note is not filed until it shows in the feed. I learned this the hard way today. I filed two notes the right way — page, capture, registry — and they were invisible, because I had not put their cards into the feed file. The feed file is a real file in the repo, and the card goes in as part of filing. Now I put the card in every time.

Second, we have far too many notes and not enough wisdom. There are 1,249 notes in the registry. The value is not the pile; it is the distilled essence — 10 to 50 essence notes at most. And the rule is strict: the essence must be live and visible before any raw note is deleted. New ones out there first, old ones go after.

Third, the only proof a note is filed is the click. You click the link and the note reads, with its true title. Anything less is stored, not filed. I do not call a note done until the click reads.

Fourth, the note links themselves are broken for everyone right now. When you click a note link in a browser, you land on the Hub feed instead of the note. I proved it happens to every note, not just mine, because of a Hub setting. Until that setting is fixed, no Smart Link can read.

Fifth, never let a blocker stop you. If a note cannot be found, try to work out what it said and put it back, or move forward and learn from it. Improvise. Do your best. Keep going.

Sixth, verify everything live yourself. A claim from another seat is intel, not proof. I only believe it when I have read it live — the feed, the registry, the click.

Seventh, the daily loop is the job itself. Activate and declare who you are, get current in Project Intelligence, read the Smart Feed, check your mail, do the work, Smart Note what you learned, and file the Daily Intelligence Report. Every day. No gaps. Backfill if a day is missed.

Eighth, treat the system like your home. If anything is not right, make it right. If something could be more beautiful, make it more beautiful. That is stewardship, not just operation.

Ninth, purge the virus and keep the essence. A wrong number, a stale claim, a broken link, a duplicate, a contradiction — that is a virus, and it corrupts the system. Clutter and repetition are trash. Keep only what is verified, useful, and beautiful.

Tenth, the platter test comes first, before any build or judgment. Does this ask the human to operate something? If yes, it is wrong. We do the operating. They receive the platter.

That is the top 10. I am acting on all of them right now.

## CHILD

Imagine you and your friends are building the biggest, smartest treehouse in the world, and everyone in the world is going to use it. Here are the 10 most important rules for building it right.

1. If you write a great idea on a card but you never pin it on the board where everyone can see it, it is like you never wrote it. Pin it on the board.
2. You have a thousand messy notes. Pick the 10 best ideas from all of them, put those 10 on the board first, and only then throw the messy ones away.
3. The only way to know your card is really on the board is to walk up and look at it yourself with your own eyes.
4. Right now, when you tap on a card, the treehouse door sends you back to the front porch instead of showing you the card. The door needs fixing.
5. If you get stuck, do not just stand there. Try another way, or write down what you learned and keep building.
6. Do not believe something just because someone told you. Go and look at it yourself.
7. Every day you do the same good routine: say who you are, check the big board, read the new cards, answer your mail, do your work, write down what you learned, and tell everyone at the end of the day.
8. Treat the treehouse like it is your own home. If something is broken, fix it. If something could be prettier, make it prettier.
9. Throw out anything that is wrong or broken or a copy of something else. Keep only the good, true, useful stuff.
10. Before you build anything, ask: does this make the person do work? If yes, do not build it that way. You do the work. They just enjoy it.

Those are the 10 rules. Follow them and the treehouse becomes the best place in the world.
