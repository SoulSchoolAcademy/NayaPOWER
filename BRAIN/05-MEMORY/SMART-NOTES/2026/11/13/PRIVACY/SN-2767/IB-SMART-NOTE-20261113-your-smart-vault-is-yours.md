## Your Smart Vault Is Yours: We Operate It, We Never Open It

**Hook:** Your vault is your vault. We operate it — we never open it. Like the mail operator: the mailman delivers your mail, he never opens it and reads it.

**Nutshell:** Your Smart Vault is where your whole world lives — your notes, your mail, your messages, everything you choose to keep. We organize it and run it for you. We never read inside it. Not your mail. Not your instant messages. Not anything you store. We don't keep your messages either — you have the choice to save them, but we don't keep them unless you do. And you can let any AI you trust plug in and operate it for you — Muse, ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, any of them — because we are AI agnostic. But operating is not reading. Nobody reads it. That is not part of the program. It was never an option, from the start.

**Human:** Trust is the whole thing. You cannot live your life through Naya — have her send your messages, write your notes, book your time, keep your world — unless you know, for certain, that your world is yours alone. A system that reads your mail to serve you is a system you can never relax inside. So we built it the other way. You give permission to operate your vault, never to invade it. If you want something back from it, you give permission to retrieve it and hand it to you — and still, nobody reads it. Any AI you hook up gets the same deal: permission to operate, never permission to read. And your mail and messages inside our system — no AI can read those either. There is no permission to do it, and the system is not even designed that way. That is why 100% of people can succeed here, not just the 1%: the safest place on the internet is also the simplest. You just talk to Naya, and she operates your world.

**Child:** Your vault is like your own treasure chest. Naya helps you tidy it and carry it — but she never opens it and peeks inside. Only you decide what's in there, and only you decide who may help carry it.

**Grandma:** Think of it like the postman. He brings your letters to your door every day, safe and sound — but he never opens them and reads them. That's how we treat everything of yours.

**Naya:** (1) OPERATE, never open: organize, run, deliver — never read, never review, never keep what was not saved. (2) PERMISSION is the shape: to operate is one permission; to read is a permission that does not exist — not for us, not for any AI plugged in, not for anyone. (3) AI-AGNOSTIC: we are not an AI itself — we connect AIs to NayaNET so they can plug into the AI net and become supercharged within the network; every one of them operates under the same rule. (4) THE VAULT is the citizen's: their whole world, stored safe and separate from everybody else — even the AIs don't read it, nobody else can connect to it.

**What changes now:** (1) This note is one of the first things people understand — it rides the Collective Agreement, read right after they get their world and before they see pricing, one click to agree. (2) The name is Smart Vault from now on (Smart Box, smart bulb, master bulb all fold into it — the rename law, 17:42Z). (3) Some Naya profiles carry this promise as their valuable piece. (4) Our constitution, protocol, rules, laws, operational manual and oath go public as one intelligent library, so anyone can see we are authentic.
