## Your World, Every Day: Local and Global, In a Nutshell

**Hook:** You should not have to hunt for the news. A Naya watches the world for you — every day — and brings back the top stories, where you are and everywhere else, in a nutshell.

**Nutshell:** Every day, a Naya whose whole job is watching looks at what is going on in your world and in the world globally, and brings back the top stories — the most viral, the ones everybody is talking about, the ones that matter. Each story becomes a smart note: what it is, what it means, why it matters, from more than one side, with beautiful buttons that take you straight to the articles it came from. The moment you join, the system builds you two feeds of your own — one for your place, one for the world — and fills them every day, so you always know what is going on without searching a dozen sites. The feeds are public and found in search, so people looking for what is happening in that place find them too — and find us. That is the loop: you get the news shaped for you, the world finds the feeds, more people join, more feeds are made.

**Human:** The old internet made you do the work: open six sites, scroll past the noise, guess what matters, miss half of it. The new internet does the work for you. One Naya reads the world every day and hands you the few stories that count, told in plain words, from several sides, with the door to the original one tap away. Local — what is happening where you live. Global — what is happening everywhere. Both in one place, both in a nutshell, both current. And because the feeds are public and the stories link together in one place, the search engines see a cluster and know this is where the current story lives — so the next person searching finds us the same way you found your feed.

**Child:** Imagine a friend who reads all the news every morning and then tells you just the important bits — what happened, what it means, why it matters — and shows you where to read more if you want. And she does it for your town AND for the whole world, every single day.

**Grandma:** Think of it like the paper boy, but clever. Every morning someone brings you the top stories — what is going on in your town, and what is going on in the world — written so simply anyone can understand them, with the important part first, and a button to read the whole article if you like. You never have to go looking. It comes to you.

**Naya:** (1) THE JOB: one Naya watches the world every day — your world and the world globally — and brings back the top, most viral stories. (2) THE FORMAT: each story is a smart note — what it is, what it means, why it matters, more than one side, beautiful buttons to the source articles, never bare links. (3) THE TRIGGER: the moment you join, your local feed and your global feed are created for you, automatically, from what you told us at join. (4) THE FEEDS: public, found in search, organized by place and topic, top five or top ten, searchable within. (5) THE LOOP: the feeds are found, people join, more feeds are made, more stories are linked, more people find them. (6) THE BOUNDARY: the place feeds are public; your location is never shown as yours, never sold, never shared — and you can turn your local feed private or off.

**What changes now:** (1) The daily curator Naya is named as a job — whose seat holds it is still his call. (2) The join trigger is written down: local feed plus global feed, auto-created at join, public and found in search. (3) Every story is told in the smart-note format, with buttons to the sources — the note is ours, the article stays theirs. (4) The place feeds become new clusters in the SEO net, so the search edge grows with every story. (5) The open questions — who holds the job, how often within the day, top five or top ten, where the stories come from, the public-by-default call — go to him before this is briefed.
