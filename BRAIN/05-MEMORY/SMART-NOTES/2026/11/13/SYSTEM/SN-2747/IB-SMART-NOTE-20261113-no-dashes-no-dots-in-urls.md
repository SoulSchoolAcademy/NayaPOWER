## No Dashes, No Dots in URLs — Brand First, One Dot

**Hook:** No dashes, no dots — the words just run together. And the brand goes first: alias.nayanet.app, the dot used one time, joining their brand to NayaNET.

**Nutshell:** Shawn 03:14Z Nov 13 — his ruling, settling the 02:59Z dot-vs-dash open question (answer: NEITHER). No spaces, no dashes, no dots in URLs. Slashes are okay, or just put the word right after — why do you have to put a dash? He doesn't think Facebook does dashes; he doesn't think dashes look as good, and dots aren't the way either. Slashes, or the word right after each other. If it's got more than one word, why put a dot or a dash or a slash — why can't you just put the word? If it makes sense to put the slash, put the slash. But without a dot or a dash is better. **The law:** words run together — "Green Box Farm" → "greenboxfarm". Slashes only where they make sense, between the parts. Matches his 2026-11-08 ruling ("I don't like dashes in my domain" — nayanet.app/thankyou, no dash). **Current state — NON-COMPLIANT:** the walk slugify still turns spaces into dashes (proven by the names-page probe: green-box-farm). The slug maker must be changed to concatenate; the names-page dash proof is superseded. Existing dashed addresses stay live so nothing breaks (the twelve sisters' pages, the nine plan Smart Links power-monthly etc. — flagged to him once already); new names follow the new rule. Whether the existing dashed ones get renamed is his call, asked, not decided unilaterally. Follow-on: a slugify-change round, sequenced behind the walk deploy queue (Q10 probe → v23) so it doesn't collide with the rounds already building on the same walk file. **03:22Z — THE SHAPE, SETTLED:** NOT nayanet.app/alias/alias — the alias twice looks weird. Their brand first, then NayaNET: `<alias>.nayanet.app/<page>`. Then slash the category/business/thing name. Depth is theirs: one slash for one word, two for business + subcategory, three for category/topic/subtopic. "We just do one time the dot." The law: `<alias>.nayanet.app/<page>[/<sub>[/<sub>]]` — alias appears exactly once, first, as the subdomain; the dot is used once, joining their brand to NayaNET, never as a word separator; everything after is slashes; words inside each part run together (the 03:14Z law holds inside segments). The bare `alias.nayanet.app/` IS their world — no alias repeated as a page. Examples: iris.nayanet.app/thebridgeroom · sophia.nayanet.app/sophiaslessons. This replaces the slash shape (nayanet.app/iris/thebridgeroom) for new addresses; it needs wildcard subdomain routing (*.nayanet.app → the worker, alias from the host), a build queued behind the walk deploys; existing slash addresses stay live.
