## What it means

- **THE CHOICE IS THE SHOWCASE:** offering both ways is not indecision — it makes the page unique and beautiful and shows off the app's incredible features right away at the beginning. The two choices are the demonstration: the app speaks, the app shows, the app unfolds, the app slides. A visitor who has tried either choice has already experienced the product before reading the agreement.
- **BOTH GREAT, BOTH TRYABLE:** neither choice is the lesser one. People appreciate both of them, and they can try either one. The page does not push one way or hide the other — both are right there, and either can be tried. A choice with a wrong answer is not a choice.
- **LISTEN AND READ IS THE UNFOLD, SPOKEN:** the visual voice-and-read unfold — the play button speaks it (the play button is how they listen to it, SN-3064 02:03Z refinement), the boards show it, unfolding down. This is the About format (SN-3061) with voice added: the story unfolds, and it speaks.
- **THE SLIDESHOW IS THE SAME CONTENT, SIDEWAYS:** a visual slideshow — the same agreement, sliding sideways, one screen at a time. This is the Skills format (SN-3061) applied to the agreement: the showcase way of reading. Same content, two readings — the formats are the reading, not the content.
- **THE REWARD IS THE READING:** "the reward, if they read it." The agreement read in the way they chose is the reward — the understanding, the deal, the 50% straight down the middle, the law of one. The two choices exist so that the reading happens: a person who chooses how to read, reads.
- **"OH MAN, THIS IS SICK" IS THE TARGET REACTION:** they should already be thinking it's awesome, in the first seconds. That reaction — sick, awesome — is the standard the entrance is judged by. A bridge page that does not make them think that in the first seconds has not shown off the features yet.
