## What it means

- **THE ACTION COMES FIRST:** the notification bar is the first thing on the page because the notifications are the number one thing — inbox messages, instant messages, likes, comments, loves, shares, actions on posts. A day menu that opens with anything else is a day menu that hides what he came to see.
- **ALL THREE WORLDS:** the bar shows personal AND business AND space notifications — more than four, because he lives in all three. A bar that shows only personal is a bar that hides two thirds of his life.
- **ALL DYNAMIC:** each category shows where the action is, live. A static bar is a dead board, and dead boards are the law broken.
- **DOORS GROUPED, NOT STUCK TOGETHER:** news together; business/personal/thing together; journal/horoscope/weather together — separated and categorized, three rows maybe, so people know "oh, here's this one, this is my…" and can find their worlds easily. A wall of doors stuck together is a mess, and a mess is hard for the brain to put in order.
- **COLOR MUST MAKE SENSE:** the grouping is done with color, with common sense — the color categorizes, it does not decorate. Color with no common sense is a mess to look at.
- **LABELS SHORT:** "Smart Notes", "Posts", "Notes" — never "for that day." Too much text on a button looks weird, and it is the same law as the text laws: less text, more sense.
- **APPOINTMENTS AND TIMELINE AT THE BOTTOM:** the open-view doors sit under the bar, with appointments and timeline at the bottom — the order is bar, doors, appointments, timeline.
