## What it means

- **THE NUMBERS ARE EVERYWHERE, SO THEY MUST BE EPIC:** the numbers are going to be throughout the whole network, seen everywhere — that is why they must be the best. A number that sits still on every page is a still number everywhere; a living number everywhere is the network alive. The scale of the exposure is the reason for the standard.
- **PICTURES OF NUMBERS ARE NOT LIVING NUMBERS:** the numbers sit still — they're pictures of numbers, not living numbers. A digit tile that never moves, never glows, never responds is a picture of a number. The epic version lifts, glows, ignites in its digit color on hover and tap, animates when it changes, and carries the slow orbit flow — the number is alive, not depicted.
- **THE BUTTONS ARE THE SAME LAW AT THE SAME SCALE:** do the best for all of our buttons — the buttons become the best in the network: real objects, elevation, rim in their own deep color, slow orbit flow, white glyph as a real object, lights up whole. The button is the most-touched object in the network; the best button is the standard, not the upgrade.
- **THE FORMAT MUST MAKE ANYTHING BEAUTIFUL:** no matter what someone pastes — text, a smart note, an image — it formats beautifully and is extraordinary to feel, see, and look at. The test is the paste: text distilled into the block's own voice (nutshell, one idea per board), an image that becomes the board (full-bleed, rimmed, lit), no seams. If pasted content still shows its seams, the format is not yet strong enough — the checker pastes all three and calls it broken if any doesn't come out beautiful.
- **7/10 IS THE SCORECARD, NOT THE VERDICT:** the format does the work, the unfold and the slideshow are one language, the boards light whole — that is the 7. The 3 that is missing is the life: the numbers, the buttons, the seams. The scorecard names exactly what the next level is.
