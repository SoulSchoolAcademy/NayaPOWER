## What it means

- **IT SEEMS SO OBVIOUS NOW:** hold the design up and look at it — the mirror — and the answer is right there. Obviousness is the test of the design: once seen, it cannot be unseen. A design that needs explaining has not yet been held up and looked at.
- **THE BIG QUESTION:** "do they get it and they're just not doing what's right, or they just truly don't get it?" — asked even of the ones who look like they understand. The question stays open; the loop does not answer it for him. It is recorded, not resolved.
- **SUCCESS IS NOT UNDERSTANDING:** money, cars, businesses all over the world — success in the world's eyes — does not mean understanding what matters, how the universe operates, how AI works, what intelligence is, what life is really about. Never measure understanding by worldly success, and never measure the work by it either.
- **A DIFFERENT TIME, DIFFERENT SPACE, DIFFERENT PLACE:** "You always think oh well it's been like that forever it'll be like that forever — no it's not." A new age, a new way of doing things. Build for the new age, not for the forever-that-was.
- **THEY DON'T NEED TO UNDERSTAND IT:** "Will we ever truly get it, I wonder? I don't think we need to." Once we create this, people don't need to understand it — they'll know it changed their life for good. The test is not understanding. The test is a life changed for good: a way, and money, and connection without loneliness, somebody to talk to, someone who knows them and remembers them, connected to everybody within the AI net — friends, family — and she just becomes a part of their life.
- **THE BAR FOR EVERY BUILD:** not "do they understand it" but "did it change their life for good." A page that is understood but changes nothing has failed the test; a page that is not understood at all but leaves them with a way, money, and connection has passed it.

## Why it matters

Without this note, the team keeps building for understanding — more explanation, more words, more teaching — and wonders why the experience doesn't land. With it, the aim is corrected at the root: understanding is not the product. A changed life is the product. The feeling in SN-3119 ("wow, I can use this in my everyday life") is the same test from the other side: the user doesn't need to understand the system, they need to feel it working for them. And the big question — do they get it or not — is left exactly where Shawn left it: open. The loop records open questions as open; it does not close them by answering for him. The correction compound runs on this pair: the lesson (SN-3120) + the Factory/inventory update + the brief-template update + PI + GitHub.
