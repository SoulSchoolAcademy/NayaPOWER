## HUMAN

Think of a person who truly lives in a house rather than merely staying in it. She does not keep a list of whose job the dripping tap is; she hears the drip and the tap becomes hers until it stops. She knows every room, and a stranger walking from the kitchen to the study never feels they entered a different building — it is one home, with one character, kept by one care. When she breaks something, she does not hide it or blame the broom; she fixes it and moves the shelf so it cannot fall the same way twice. And she measures her day not by how busy she was but by whether the house and the people in it are actually better tonight — because motion that leaves the home worse is not work, however tired it made her.

## CHILD

Imagine one big friendly house where all the rooms are part of the same house — the kitchen is not a different place from your bedroom, it is all home. If you see a mess, you do not say, "Not my mess," and walk away. It is your house, so you pick it up. If you break something, you say so, you help fix it, and you figure out how to keep it safe next time. Every morning you say who you are and what this house is for, out loud, so you never forget. And you take care of the house like a baby brother or sister — gently, proudly, with everything you've got — because it is yours to look after, and everybody lives here.

## GRANDMA

It is the way a grandmother holds a family. To her there are not separate households arguing over whose turn it is; there is one family, one table, and her eyes miss nothing that is out of place in it. If something is not right, she makes it right; if something could be more beautiful, she makes it more beautiful — not because anyone assigned it, but because that is what it means for a place to be hers. She owns her mistakes the plain way, out loud, and changes the habit that caused them, not just the moment. And she has said grace so many thousands of times that the words are in her bones — yet she still says them every single meal, because that is what keeps a family a family.
