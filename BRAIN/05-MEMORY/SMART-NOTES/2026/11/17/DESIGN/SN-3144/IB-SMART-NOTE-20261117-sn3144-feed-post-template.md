## Human

The action row is the smallest thing on a feed post and the most-touched — it is where a citizen says what a post meant to them, and where the post's standing is counted. A flat LOVE / LIKE row with plain numbers undersells the thing it measures. Ten stars is the language of the whole system — "we talk about 10 stars all the time" — so a five-star cap would make the feed speak a smaller language than the house speaks. Circles with a flow around them, stars that light up when you click: that is the orbit and the light-up he has already ruled for the bottom bar and the icons, brought to the feed. And the question underneath — one choice point or two standing buttons — is the push-button-simple question: one elegant place to say what you feel, or two buttons side by side. The comment box matters the same way: the "why" behind the stars is the intelligence, and a beautiful box is what invites it.

## What it means

- **10 STARS, NOT 5:** the feed's rating speaks the house language — ten stars, because "we talk about 10 stars all the time." A five-star cap would be a smaller language than the system speaks.
- **THE STARS LIGHT UP:** circles and stars, a flow around the circles, all the stars lighting up when you click — the orbit and the light-up he already ruled, brought to the feed.
- **BEAUTIFUL LOVE AND LIKE, WITH DASHBOARD NUMBERS:** the buttons are beautiful, and the counts are "our typical type numbers, those dashboard like numbers" — the same digit language as the Dashboard.
- **THE COMMENT BOX IS BEAUTIFUL TOO:** the citizen comments why — the why behind the stars is the intelligence, and a beautiful box is what invites it.
- **ONE CHOICE POINT OR TWO — HIS OPEN QUESTION:** love and like both standing, or a dropdown where they pick what they want (star, love, like, share). He asked the captain twice what is most elegant. The answer is pending — nothing from this dictation is law until the captain answers and Shawn confirms.
