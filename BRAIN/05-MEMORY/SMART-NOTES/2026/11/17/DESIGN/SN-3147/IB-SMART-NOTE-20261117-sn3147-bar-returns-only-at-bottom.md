The bar folds away the moment you scroll down — and it only comes back when you reach the very bottom. Not when you stop scrolling. Only at the bottom.

## Human

The 19:24Z rule was a guess at the timing — a second after scrolling stops — and it was wrong in a way that matters: a bar that pops back while you are still reading is a bar that interrupts you. His correction is cleaner and stricter: the bar is gone the moment you scroll down, and it comes back only when you have reached the very bottom — the one moment you are done reading and ready to go somewhere else. That is the fold doing its job: the interface hides while you are in the content and returns only when you are finished with it. And because it is the same on every page, the citizen learns it once and trusts it everywhere.

## What it means

- **FOLDS THE MOMENT YOU SCROLL DOWN:** no delay, no threshold — the bar is gone as soon as the page moves.
- **RETURNS ONLY AT THE VERY BOTTOM:** not after a second, not when scrolling stops — only at the bottom, the moment you are done reading and ready to go somewhere else.
- **SAME ON EVERY PAGE:** Hub, Personal, Business, Smart Mail, all of them. Personal's difference is only the big cover and its tabs.
- **SUPERSEDES THE 19:24Z READING:** a build that returns the bar ~1s after scrolling stops is wrong against this law and goes back. The known case: the Dashboard greeting-out fix (PR #3433) returns ~1s after scrolling stops; its follow-on removes that return.
- **CONFIRMED, NOT A READING:** his ❤️ on the captain's restatement settles it — this is law, not an interpretation awaiting confirmation.

## Why it matters

Without this note, every bar build guesses its own timing — one returns on a timer, one on scroll-stop, one never — and the citizen never learns when the bar will appear. With it, the timing is one rule, learned once, trusted everywhere: gone while you read, back only when you are done. The fold is the experience (SN-3150), and this is the fold's sharpest edge.

## What changes now

1. Every bottom bar folds away the moment the user scrolls down and returns only at the very bottom — never on a timer, never when scrolling stops.
2. The rule is the same on every page; Personal's difference is only the big cover and its tabs.
3. The Dashboard greeting-out fix's ~1s return is removed by its follow-on (brief BRIEF-DASHBOARD-RETURN-AT-BOTTOM-20261117.md).
4. The Hub fix (Amendment #3) and the Smart Mail bar fix carry the rule — each handoff must confirm it applied.
