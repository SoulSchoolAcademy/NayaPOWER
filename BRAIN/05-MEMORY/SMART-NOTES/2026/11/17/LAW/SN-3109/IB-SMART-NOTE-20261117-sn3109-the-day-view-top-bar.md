## What it means

- **STRAIGHT WORDS, NOT BUTTONS:** the weekday is raised words in the day's color, the light and glow embedded in the letters — the same elements as the buttons, but without the button. A word that is a real object does not need a container. "No buttons, just straight words" is a law, not a preference.
- **ABBREVIATIONS SOLVE THE FIT:** "what's the longest word, Wednesday?" — the month row uses Sun through Sat, same straight-word treatment, maybe a nice icon. Fit is solved by saying less, not by squeezing more. This refines SN-3108: the fit requirement stands, the mechanism is abbreviations in the row.
- **THE TIME TAKES THE DAY'S COLOR:** not gold. Gold is a metal; the color belongs to the day. The time is part of the day, so it wears the day's color.
- **LESS GLOW:** "I think you overdo it on the glow. More rich and elegant, less glow." Glow is a spice, not the dish. When everything glows, nothing glows — the glow stops meaning anything. Rich and elegant is the dial setting.
