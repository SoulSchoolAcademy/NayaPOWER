## What it means

- **TOP LEFT IS THE LAW, AND THE CORRECTION IS THE PROOF:** he says "top right" twice and then corrects himself — "oh sorry the numbers in the top left corner." The self-correction is the order: the number sits top left. A builder who takes the first "top right" and ships it has not listened; the correction is the teaching — listen to the end of the sentence.
- **RIM ONLY, NEVER THE WHOLE CELL:** on hover the whole cell does NOT light up — only the outside rim lights. The whole-cell glow was the vision page's first attempt (and the Obsidian order said "lights up whole in its own color") — this dictation narrows it: the light lives on the rim, the middle stays clean. No glow in the middle, ever.
- **LIT AT REST, BRIGHTER ON HOVER:** a light glow of the day's color even when not lit up; on hover it just gets brighter. Every day is lit at rest — you can see each day's color line — just not as bright as the hovered day. The week reads as a color line at rest; the hovered day is the brightest point on it. This is the day-colors law (T1–T38) made visible: the color flow IS the week.
- **THE EYE IS THE DOOR:** in the bottom right corner sits a beautiful human eye — the most beautiful eye, like a jewel — and the eye is the door to the day's info. The eye is not decoration; it is the control. Whatever icon goes there must look like a jewel — the eye as object, the door as jewel. This is the info-eye (the pricing page's eye, harvested) placed where the day's door belongs.
- **THE NUMBERS ARE THE DASHBOARD'S, EXACTLY:** make the numbers look like they're objects and beautiful and the same — not like the ones, literally the ones in the Dashboard. "I can tell they just don't look the same — they're close but not the same." Close is not the same. The harvest must be byte-for-byte, or the eye knows — the same law as the Obsidian order (SN-3078 prov): grab the real code, don't re-create from a picture.
- **PEARL GEMS, NOT NEON:** the colors are pearl gems, not neon. The current ones look more like neons — the feeling of a neon lighting up instead of elegance. Pearl is the finish: soft, deep, gem-like. Neon is the defect. This is the One Set law (SN-3073 prov) sharpened to a finish: the finest jewelry, and pearl is the cut.
- **BIG CELLS, NO BLEED:** the cells are big — he pictured it bigger. The calendar fills the screen with numbers (the Obsidian order) and the cells must be big enough to hold a top-left number and a bottom-right eye without crowding. And the next month's numbers do not bleed in at the bottom — the month ends where the month ends.
