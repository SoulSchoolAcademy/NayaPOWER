## What it means

- **THE INSPECTOR IS A STANDING SEAT:** the inspector is not a one-off review — it is a standing seat that sweeps again after every major landing and once a day with the daily report (Sweep #1 dispatched 03:04Z; this dictation orders the full sweep, Sweep #2). Quality is checked by a seat whose whole job is the check, not by the maker checking their own work.
- **THE SCOPE IS EVERY PAGE, EVERYTHING:** logic, elegance, design, usefulness, high-level design quality — where things sit, how they fit, how they look, what they do. Nothing is too small for the sweep: sizing (oversized, undersized), polish, fine-tuning, rearrangement. The inspector scores the drift between pages, not just the defects inside one page.
- **THE N ICON IS THE NAMED SUSPECT:** the massive icon with the little N in the middle — the 240px `.face-mono` N disc, a 240px circle with a 26px N, on every beat of Epic/unfold/slideshow. He doesn't mind the icon itself; the size is the offence — a giant circle carrying a tiny letter makes no sense. Sweep #2 names it the worst break: fix to 56px, a face, or gone.
- **"WHAT IS THIS?" IS THE TEST:** "I don't know what you're showing me here — what is this? I have no idea what you're showing me on the screen — what's the purpose of this thing you're showing?" Every page must answer at a glance what it is and what it's for. A page that needs explaining has already failed the test — the purpose must be readable, not told.
- **LOCK INTO THE HEART:** "if you just lock into the actual heart of like of what we're doing that'd be better" — the sweep is not a hunt for nits; it is a check against the heart of the mission. Polish serves the heart; drift away from it is what the inspector catches.

## Why it matters

Without this note, quality stays a feeling the maker has about their own page — and the pages keep drifting from each other, each a new design, sort of like it but not completely like it, with oversized icons and unexplained screens. With it, there is a seat whose job is the whole: every page judged on logic, elegance, design, usefulness, and high-level quality, against the heart of what we're doing — and the worst break gets named in plain words, so the fix has a target.
