## What it means

- **THE DAY VIEW IS A DASHBOARD, NOT A MENU:** going inside a day must feel like opening a cockpit, not a list of buttons. The day has its own instruments — appointments as tiles, a timeline, notes, the day's report, Intel, news, weather, horoscope — each a live glimpse of what's inside, not a label on a black bar.
- **THE WISDOM QUOTE LEADS:** a quote, or wisdom for the day, goes across the top — the wisdom quote of the day. Monday's line is "Smart finds the fastest road; wisdom checks the destination first." The quote is the first thing the day says; it sets the day's tone before any instrument is read.
- **PRIME REAL ESTATE — THE WHOLE LAND:** blank space on a day view is not breathing room, it is unbought land left empty. He paid for the whole page — the whole land gets built on. A third built and two-thirds blank is the failure, whatever the third looks like. "Do you think that's pro?" is the test: a pro would never leave prime land empty.
- **"WHEN YOU COULD DO ANYTHING WITH THAT PAGE":** the day view is the most personal page in the system — it is the citizen's day. Anything the system knows about the day belongs on it. The constraint is never "what fits" — it is "what does the day hold."

## Why it matters

Without this note, the next builder makes the day view a prettier menu — nicer buttons, still buttons, still a third of the land. The day is the product the calendar sells; a day that reads as buttons on blank space is a calendar that has nothing to say about your day.
