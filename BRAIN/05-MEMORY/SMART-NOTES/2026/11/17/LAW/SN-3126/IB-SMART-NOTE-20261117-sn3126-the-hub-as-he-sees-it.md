## What it means

- **THE CONTROL SITS BESIDE WHAT IT OPENS:** the three-dot/hamburger that pulls up the right sidebar goes on the LEFT of "Life with Naya" at the top right — right beside it, because it pulls up the right sidebar.
- **GLOW IS EDGES ONLY:** forget the purple glow, forget the red glow — no lighting up the whole board. Glow around the edges, never the middle.
- **THE TAB-CAPTION LINE IS REMOVED:** the line under COLLECTIVE / PERSONAL / ACTIVITY ("Collective is everybody's, together — the public feed is the collective feed. Personal is yours. Activity is what's happening, now.") — that text on its own shouldn't be there.
- **THE GREETING IS PERSONAL:** "Good morning, Shawn." — not a bare "Good morning."
- **EVERY ITEM ITS OWN ICON AND ITS OWN COLOR:** Worlds ≠ Favorite Worlds icons. Pricing ≠ White Paper color. Naya and Worlds sit in flow order, never far apart breaking the color code. Never adjacent duplicates — "you don't go green purple purple."
- **NOTE TYPES KEEP THEIR OWN:** "What's in it for you" and "How it applies" get different colors; Human / Child / Grandma note types each keep their own color + glyph.
- **SETTINGS GOES UNDERNEATH PRICING.** Wisdom of the Day STAYS — the board carries too much text, trim to least words. The "Our public smart feed" pill STAYS.
