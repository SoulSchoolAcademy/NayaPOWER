- **BLACK IS THE FIX, NOT A REDESIGN:** he did not ask for a new shape, a new size, or a new color — "make the eye which is black in the middle so you can see it, that may work." The pearl rim stays, the disc stays, the "i" stays. Only the middle changes: solid black (#050508 in the vision edit), white "i" on it.
- **THE THIRD SHAPE OF THE EYE IN ONE NIGHT:** the eye has now changed shape three times in one night — colored jewel eye (scary, Halloween) → white almond eye (SN-3085, prov) → pearl disc (SN-3089, prov) → pearl disc with a black centre (this note). Each change came from his eye on the page, not from a checklist. The lesson for the machine: the eye is not settled until he says it reads.
- **BRIGHTNESS IS NOT CLARITY:** "it's too bright with the eye in the middle like you can't see it" — brightness washed the mark out. The fix is not more light; it is a dark ground behind the light mark. This is the same law as the rim light (SN-3089): light on the edge, calm dark face — and here, a dark centre carrying the white letter.
