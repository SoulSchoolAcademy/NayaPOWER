## What it means

- **EVERYTHING MADE GOES IN THE VAULT, THE MOMENT IT IS MADE:** a certificate, a font, a page, a build — all digital assets. Saved, kept, findable. The Vault is the Smart Vault (Smart Box = Smart Vault = the store, per the NIAS unifications). "Made yesterday and gone" is not a storage failure — it is a system failure: the work had nowhere to go, so it went nowhere.
- **LIVE ONCE MADE AND PROVEN, IN THE SAME PASS:** his recommendation is the rule: just make them live once you make them. Once a thing is made and proven — his eye has seen it — it goes live in the same pass. Finished work never sits staged. Staged is not a destination; it is a waiting room, and waiting rooms lose things.
