## What it means

- **BECOMING, NOT MADE-THEN-VAULTED:** the Vault Rule (SN-3068 prov) said "into the vault the moment it's made"; the Master Vault (SN-3069 prov) said "immediately implemented, immediately working". The sharpening closes the gap between the two: the system is forever BECOMING alive and active AS it is created — implementation happens DURING creation, not after. There is no "made, then vaulted" gap, because the making IS the vaulting.
- **LOCKED:** into the Smart Vault, into core memory, into the systems — where it just stays there. Locked means it cannot drift out: not into a draft folder, not into a chat message, not into someone's memory. A locked thing stays because it is part of the system now, and part of the system cannot be lost the way a file on a computer can.
- **THE FOUR VERBS ARE THE CHECK:** LABELED, INVENTORIED, ORGANIZED, ACTIVE. A thing is vaulted only when all four hold. Labeled — it has a name and a place. Inventoried — it is counted in the list. Organized — it sits where it belongs, shelved and linked. Active — it is working, being used, official. Three out of four is not vaulted.
- **THE FOUR WAYS THINGS GET LOST, NAMED:** lost in not-being-finished, lost in the feed, lost on a computer, lost in not-active. The vault closes all four: finished things are locked in, the feed is not the store, the computer is not the system, and active means it is used and official, not sitting.
- **ACTIVE AND OFFICIAL CONTINUOUSLY:** not in batches, not at the end of the day, not when someone remembers — continuously. The captain sent it to the Factory round the same pass, so the vault law rides in every brief template from now on: every build lands active and official, continuously.
- **THE LOOP'S OWN MEASURE:** the loop's own work is judged by the same four verbs. A note is vaulted when it is labeled (titled, numbered), inventoried (on the list, in PI), organized (in the box, shelved and linked), active (in the feed, working). A draft sitting in a folder is labeled and nothing else — not vaulted yet.
