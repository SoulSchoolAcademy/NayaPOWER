## What it means

- **"IS IT 10 OUT OF 10?" IS THE SCORECARD AS A QUESTION:** he asks it of the posts the way he asked it of the calendar and the Intelligent Block — and the honest answer is 7. Good is not 10. The boards are good, the pills and the counts are right — and the stars and icons are the gap.
- **FLAT IS THE DEFECT:** the stars are flat outlines; the heart, thumb, and bubble are thin line drawings. Flat outlines and thin lines are not real objects — the Real Object Law (AGENTS.md) says if it doesn't look touchable, it doesn't ship. The facelift is the Real Object Law applied to the post.
- **JEWELS, NOT OUTLINES:** the stars become real jewel objects in their own tiles — Dashboard digit-tile form, harvested, nothing created new — lighting up whole in gold when tapped, lit stars staying lit. A star you tap is a jewel you lit; it stays lit.
- **NOTHING UNFINISHED STAYS:** the empty "-" sixth slot is made whole or removed. An empty slot with a dash in it is an unfinished thing on the page — and an unfinished thing on the page is a fail, whatever the rest of the page scores.
- **THE ACTIVE STATE READS AT A GLANCE:** the icon lights up whole in its own color — LOVE ruby/red, LIKE sapphire, COMMENT deep gold — and the count pops; at rest the icons are white. The state is visible without reading: lit means loved, white means at rest. This is the One Icon That Lights Up law (AGENTS.md) — one icon that itself lights up, never an icon beside its own light.
- **WHAT STAYS IS NAMED:** the pills, rims, labels, and count badges stay as they are. A facelift changes what is wrong and keeps what is right — "DON'T REMOVE WHAT HE DIDN'T ASK TO REMOVE" (AGENTS.md).

With it, the smallest touchpoints get the same law as the biggest pages: real objects, lit whole, nothing unfinished. A post that reads 10 at a glance is the feed reading 10.
