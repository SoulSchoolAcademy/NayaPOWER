- **THE FAULT IS INDEPENDENTLY CONFIRMED:** his eye (19:48Z) and the captain's eye (19:41–19:51Z, pixel-sampled) found the same adjacent purples — My Worlds ~#a21caf, Favorite Worlds ~#8e24aa — plus the same fault at the bottom of the right list and twice in the left sidebar.

- **THE FIX IS A WIDER STEP:** change the color until the neighbors read as different — not the label, not the order, the color itself.

## Why it matters

Without this note, the next builder checks the hex table, sees two different hexes, and ships two purples together again — because the check they ran cannot see the fault. The fault is only visible to an eye, and the only eye that counts is his. A flow with two purples together is not a flow at all; it is a stutter, and it makes the whole spectrum feel careless even when every hex is from the right set.
