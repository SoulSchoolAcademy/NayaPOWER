## Human

The control sat at the top-left because that is where menus usually sit — and that is exactly why it was wrong here. On the Hub, the left is where the world already lives: the sidebar, the feed, the person's own place. The menu control is not the world; it is the way in to the world — and the way in belongs on the right, where the hand reaches for it and where the choice it offers can open without covering what you came to see. And the choice itself is the point: one tap, two doors — the sidebar or the smart messenger — and whichever opens, the people are already there, the ones related to the feed topic you are looking at. Not a menu you have to hunt through, not people you have to go and find: the control on the right, the choice in one tap, the people on the side automatically. That is the Hub behaving like Personal, Business, and World already do — the same pattern, the same side, the same automatic people. One system, one gesture, everywhere.
