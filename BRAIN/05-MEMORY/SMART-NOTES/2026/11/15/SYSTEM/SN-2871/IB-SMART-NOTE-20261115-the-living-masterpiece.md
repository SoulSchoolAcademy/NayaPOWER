## Why it matters

A page is judged by its weakest touch. One flat emoji, one plain bar, one "alright" row of tabs — and the citizen's feeling of the whole drops to that level, because feelings do not average, they anchor. That is why the laws are stated as absolutes ("everything elevated, all the way in") rather than aspirations: an absolute can be checked, and a checked absolute can be enforced by the checker and the eye. The masterpiece framing is what makes the absolutes stick — a team that believes it is shipping a page will accept "alright"; a team that believes it is crafting a living work of art for the world will not. The standard is the feeling, and the feeling is the product.
