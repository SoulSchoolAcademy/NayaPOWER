## Why it matters

Money is the one symbol a citizen cannot afford to misread. The law is small (one symbol, one place) and the protection is large: every future mode, every future orb, every future hero is checked against one sentence — does this $ mean money? Sense and accuracy are not two virtues; they are the one thing a dashboard is for.
