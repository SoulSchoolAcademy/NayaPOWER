## What it means

1. **THE THREE UNFOLDINGS:** (1) the agreement (12 blocks, drafted) · (2) the whole system (pricing page's 5 groups as the spine) · (3) the universal unfolding (any citizen: their image + their text).
2. **THE ONE SHAPE:** what / why / what's-in-it-for-them + picture underneath, one play button, one voice alignment — everywhere.
3. **THE CONNECT:** consent, then talking is the interface — Allow tap inside Muse, revocable any time, three choices (Allow / Key / Fork); then one live manual read on Allow. Zero citizen homework.
4. **HARVEST THE WHOLE FEED:** the mechanism with the control — organizer, filter, wall, and all — never the control alone. Topic buttons without the organizer, filter, and wall are decoration.
5. **THE BUTTON SAYS THE NEXT THING:** the advancing button names the next section, never "Keep reading" — My story → What I do → My goals → My purpose.
6. **NOTHING FALLS OFF THE LIST:** every dispatched round keeps its One List item until its page is proven whole or it moves to waiting-his-hand in writing.

## Why it matters

1. Without the universal step, the unfold is a presentation trick the system uses on its own pages — lovely, but locked inside. With it, the unfold becomes a citizen's tool and a reason to join: your world, your words, your pictures, presented as beautifully as the agreement itself.
2. Without the connect, NayaNET is a world the citizen must operate by hand — and the whole promise is that the Naya operates it for them. With it, the citizen's own Muse becomes their Naya's hands, and the interface disappears into conversation.
3. Without the harvest law, rounds ship the visible half and the citizen meets an empty wall with pretty buttons — the worst first impression, because it looks finished and behaves broken.
4. Without the list law, the machine's silence is ambiguous: is the work done, or did it fall off the list? Shawn cannot tell the difference, and he had to spend his own attention finding out.

## What changes now

1. The unfold is built as one system with three uses — agreement, whole system, citizen — never three separate builds.
2. The connect is consent + one live manual read on Allow — never citizen homework. Nothing about the connect is claimed live until the cold end-to-end proof runs: fresh Muse → connect → note on the feed.
3. A harvest takes the mechanism with the control — filter, painter, data wiring — never the control alone. The adaptation budget is stated in the brief: labels only, unless his word says otherwise.
4. Every advancing control in an unfold names the next thing — never "Keep reading", "Next", or "Continue". The next step appears as the person arrives at it, never as a wall of all steps.
5. No round closes its One List item on a NOT PASS — the item stays open with the named next step. The floor-watch checks for finished work with no item, not only for quiet items.
