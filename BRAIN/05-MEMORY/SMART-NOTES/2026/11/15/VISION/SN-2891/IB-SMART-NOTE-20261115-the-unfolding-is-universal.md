## What it means

- **THE THREE UNFOLDINGS:** (1) the agreement (12 blocks, drafted) · (2) the whole system (pricing page's 5 groups as the spine) · (3) the universal unfolding (any citizen: their image + their text).
- **THE ONE SHAPE:** what / why / what's-in-it-for-them + picture underneath, one play button, one voice alignment — everywhere.
- **THE CITIZEN VERSION:** product, service, themselves, any subject — a beautiful way to present things online so people understand.

## Why it matters

Without the universal step, the unfold is a presentation trick the system uses on its own pages — lovely, but locked inside. With it, the unfold becomes a citizen's tool and a reason to join: your world, your words, your pictures, presented as beautifully as the agreement itself. It is the clearest instance of the flywheel: the system uses itself to explain itself, and then gives the explanation away.

## What changes now

1. The unfold is built as one system with three uses — agreement, whole system, citizen — never three separate builds.
2. The block shape is fixed everywhere; only the content changes.
3. The citizen version waits for the agreement words' approval — it inherits true words, never invents its own.
4. The pricing page's 5 groups (Worlds / Daily / System / Earning / Platform) are the spine of the whole-system unfolding.
