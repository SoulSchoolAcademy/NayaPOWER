## Why it matters

A system that kept everything would be a surveillance system; a system that kept nothing would be a forgetful one. Save-to-keep is the middle that honors both laws: privacy by default, permanence by choice. It puts the citizen in charge of their own memory — the system does not decide what mattered, they do, one tap at a time — and it makes the Living Calendar honest: the calendar does not promise a past it cannot deliver, it promises the past you kept. And the habit compounds: every save is a day that stays alive, and a citizen who saves as they go builds, year by year, a vault that is genuinely theirs.
