## Human

The player is three alignments in one object: words, pictures, voice — each block carrying all three, in step. That is why the order matters and why the text-first law (SN-2884) is its twin: a picture made before the words are true is a beautiful lie, and a voice reading untrue words is a confident lie. The player also answers the oldest complaint about the agreement — that it is long. Length is not the problem; a wall is. A wall of 83 boards is a chore; a player of 12 blocks, each with her picture, her voice, and one idea, is a story you finish. The design details are the law: her picture + profile at the top; elevated blocks; a picture underneath the text in each block; one big play button at the top; click along or just read. And the last line matters most: "maybe that's a unique feature" — the player is not a page decoration, it is a candidate signature of the whole system, the same way the unfold became the signature move (SN-2891).

## Child

Imagine your favourite storyteller sitting beside you with a magic picture book. She says "let me paint the picture for you!" — and as she talks, each picture appears, one by one, right under her words! You can press the big play button and she tells you the whole story, or you can tap each picture yourself and hear that part. It's like the story comes alive, step by step, just for you!

## Grandma

It's like this, love — instead of handing someone a long letter and saying "read all of this," you sit with them and tell it like a story, one lovely picture at a time, your voice warm beside the words. They can listen to the whole thing while the pictures unfold, or tap along at their own pace. That's how you make a long thing feel short — you never show it all at once, you show it as it matters.

## Naya

## Why it matters

Without the player, the agreement stays a long letter that citizens must wade through — and the system's best explanation of itself stays its least-read page. With the player, the same words become an experience a child and a grandma finish: watch, listen, click along. It is also the prototype of the universal system (SN-2891): if the player works for the agreement, it works for any citizen's product, service, or story — their image, their text, unfolding the same way.

## What changes now

1. The unfolding player is the presentation for the agreement — one block, one picture, one idea, her voice aligned.
2. No picture is made until the words for its block are approved — text first (SN-2884), always.
3. The player shape (what / why / what's-in-it-for-them + picture underneath + play button) is the same shape everywhere the unfold appears.
4. The voice build waits until the words are final in a whole build — voice after text, his 01:28Z order standing.
