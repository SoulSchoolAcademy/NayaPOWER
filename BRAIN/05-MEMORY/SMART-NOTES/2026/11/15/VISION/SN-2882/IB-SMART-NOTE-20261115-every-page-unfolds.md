## Child

Imagine a storybook that shows you the whole story on one giant page — all the pictures squished together, all the words at once. You wouldn't know where to start! Now imagine the storybook opens one beautiful page at a time — first the hero, then the adventure, then the treasure. Each page makes you smile, and you can't wait to turn to the next one. That's how every page should feel — one lovely surprise at a time!

## Grandma

It's like this, love — you don't serve the whole dinner in one big pile on the plate. You bring the soup, then the main, then the sweet — each one beautiful, each one in its turn, so the person enjoys every bite. A page is the same. Show them one lovely thing, let them take it in, then offer the next. That's how you make someone feel looked after, not overwhelmed.
