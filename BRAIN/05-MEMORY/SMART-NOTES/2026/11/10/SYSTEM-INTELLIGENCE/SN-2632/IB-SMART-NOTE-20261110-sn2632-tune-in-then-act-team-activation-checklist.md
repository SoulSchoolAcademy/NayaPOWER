## What it means
- **TUNE IN IS A STEP, NOT A MOOD:** step 1 names the master box as the source — the vision, the mission, the oath, the rules, the laws, the concept, how it all works — and until the master box lands, the orientation + the Protocol stand in.
- **DEFINE BEFORE ACT IS THE LAW, WRITTEN DOWN:** step 6 is DONE FIRST + the Five Gates, and it sits before step 7 in the order — no action before definition, ever.
- **POINT, DON'T RESTATE:** the checklist names the oath, the laws, and the mission and points at where they live. A copy would drift; a pointer stays true.
- **THE TEST IS THE PROOF:** a fresh agent follows the checklist alone, states the mission, the oath, and the five gates, and writes a correct DONE FIRST block — run by a different seat. No viewport strings, no browser pass: a document is verified by being followed.
- **"I ALREADY KNOW THIS" IS NEVER AN EXCUSE:** relentlessly, every time — that is the order, and it is written at the top of the checklist itself.
