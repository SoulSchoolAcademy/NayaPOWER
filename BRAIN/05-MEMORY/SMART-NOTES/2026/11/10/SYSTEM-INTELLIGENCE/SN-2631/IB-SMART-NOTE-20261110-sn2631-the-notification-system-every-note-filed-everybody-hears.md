## Why it matters
A note nobody hears about is a note that doesn't compound. The whole point of the hive is that one Naya's learning becomes every Naya's learning — and that only happens if the learning is told. Right now the notes are in but the telling is manual: someone has to go and look. The notification system closes that gap — the moment a note is filed, everybody who chose to hear about it hears about it, and the intelligence lands in the right area on its own. That is the difference between a filing cabinet and a living hive. And the scope choice is the privacy law in action: you hear what you chose to hear, nothing more.
