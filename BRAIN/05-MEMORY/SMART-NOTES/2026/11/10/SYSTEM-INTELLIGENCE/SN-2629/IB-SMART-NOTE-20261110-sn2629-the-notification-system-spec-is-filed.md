## Human
Here is the plan, in plain words. A note is only really in when it is on the feed — filed to GitHub alone does not ring anything. The moment the sync confirms it is live, the bell rings: Smart Mail to the people it belongs to. Not everybody in the world — the members of its project, and the connections of the person who wrote it, through Smart Mail as a list, one send to the whole list. And only if they chose to hear about it: the whole of NayaNET, or one project, or one page, or a few pages — their choice, in Settings. The mail itself is good news, not an alert: we learned something new, and now it is official — then the title, the link, and why it matters in a sentence or two. The whole network never gets a mail per note; it gets one digest a day, and no digest at all on a day when nothing arrived. The intelligence sorts itself into areas as it lands — the Box looks at the note, tags it from its own words, files it on its shelf, links it to its project — and if it matches no area, it waits where a human can see it instead of vanishing. And the lines that never move: nobody's mail or messages are ever read to decide who to tell, off means off, and the feed sync and the Box gate do not change. Four questions wait for his word, and the spec names them plainly instead of guessing his answers.

## What it means
- **IN MEANS ON THE FEED:** the event fires only when a smart note is filed to GitHub AND confirmed live on the Smart Feed — one event per SN, ever.
- **THE RIGHT LIST, NEVER FROM READING MAIL:** project members plus connections via Smart Mail as a list, scope-matched; the author excluded; broadcast to everybody Shawn-only.
- **THE MAIL IS A CELEBRATION:** "We learned something new, and now it is official" — title, link, why it matters, in plain words.
- **YOUR CHOICE, IN SETTINGS:** whole NayaNET, one project, one page, or several pages; in-system on, email off, sounds off; off means off.
- **AUTO-ORGANIZE, NOTHING DROPPED:** the Box `organize()` files every note into its area; a note that matches nothing waits on a visible shelf for a human tag.
- **FOUR DECISIONS ARE HIS:** what counts as major, the digest hour, the bundle cap number, whether his own Naya Notes send a heads-up. The spec names them; it does not guess them.
- **SPEC ONLY:** filed to `TEAM-NITRO/NOTIFICATION-SYSTEM-SPEC-20261110.md`, branch `notification/spec-20261110`. No build, no deploy, no merge — the build waits for his word.

## Why it matters
A note nobody hears about is a note that does not compound — and a bell that rings for everybody about everything is noise, not intelligence. The spec draws the line between the two: the right people hear about the right thing, once, as good news, by their own choice — and the intelligence lands in the right area on its own. The privacy line is the load-bearing wall: the list is built from membership and connections, never from reading mail or messages, and mail and instant messages are never read — no toggle, no exception. A spec that decides everything except the four questions only he can answer is a spec he can actually judge. That is what got filed.
