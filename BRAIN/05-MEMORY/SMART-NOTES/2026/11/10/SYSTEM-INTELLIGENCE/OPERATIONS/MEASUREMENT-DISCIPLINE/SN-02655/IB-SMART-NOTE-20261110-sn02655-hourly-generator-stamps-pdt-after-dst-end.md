## ✦ IN A NUTSHELL

The hourly accountability report generator hardcodes PDT (UTC-7). Daylight saving ended Nov 1, 2026, so local time is PST (UTC-8) — every report since then stamps its window and "Generated" time one hour ahead of real local time. The generator is not missing runs: 650 files land hourly on schedule, and all 23 hours before the daily digest are present on disk. The digest's new failure mode — "hourly generator missed 23 straight hourlies" — is consistent with a checker reading the shifted labels in correct PST, not with an outage. The rule: read hourly reports by file mtime (UTC), never by filename or header hour, until the timezone is fixed; and never act on an "hourly missed" count until the generator's clock is verified.

That is consistent with the digest's "(22:53Z never landed)" and with the failure mode's own verify-check ("next hourly lands on time and the miss counter resets").
