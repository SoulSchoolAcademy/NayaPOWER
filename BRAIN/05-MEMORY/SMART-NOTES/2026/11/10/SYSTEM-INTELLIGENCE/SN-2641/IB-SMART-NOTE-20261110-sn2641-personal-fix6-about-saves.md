# SN-2641 — Personal Fix-6: The About Save Now Persists

## Hook
The Personal About section saved in-session only — the story never reached the app's own data, and a section saved with no connection lost its words outright. Fix-6 writes both, and a reload keeps what was saved.

## Nutshell
Two defects, both measured before the fix. (1) The three Connected links overlapped by 29px at every width: anchor pills are inline, so their vertical padding painted outside the line box. One rule — `a.ph-connect{display:inline-block}` plus an 8px room gap — and the overlap is 0px at 390, 1440 and 360. (2) The About save was in-session only (Resolve, thread 6eb5570b). The story wrote localStorage alone — zero `{k:'about'}` rows in the live backend, verified by REST — and the five fields had no offline mirror, so a save with no connection lost the words. The cure is the sections' own discipline: every save now writes the backend row AND a timestamped on-device mirror, and loading re-decides last-write-wins, so a reload keeps what was saved with or without the connection. Story and motto both proven across a reload in a real browser; the 621px fix-5b proof re-proved byte-identical (About 411–535). No five-fields words written, no redesign, no new component. The design-contract gate is unchanged from fix-5b — (a)/(c)/(f) fail there too, pre-existing, named as the next fix pass, not smuggled in here.

## Human
Shawn ordered a full-time seat on the Personal template, and the first job was the save: you write your story, you reload, and it should still be there. It wasn't — the story lived only in the browser's pocket, never in the app's own store, and the fields lost their words the moment the connection dropped. Now the save writes to both places at once — the app's store for every device, the pocket for this one — and when the page loads it keeps whichever is newest. The Connected links were the same kind of small break: three pills painted on top of each other because the browser never gave them a real box. One rule gives them one. Nothing else on the page was touched, and the one thing carried over — the About pill sitting off-screen on a narrow phone — is still his call, not a fix I took on myself.

Whichever is newest is the one you see. And the three little link buttons that were squished on top of each other? They each have their own seat now, so they sit in a neat row.
