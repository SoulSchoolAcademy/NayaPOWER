## Human

Think of it like editing a letter someone wrote about their family: "your" makes it sound like it belongs to the reader alone; "our" makes it belong to all of us. The letter stays the same — only the little word on the envelope changes. And the rule for the editor is simple: change what you were asked to change, and leave the rest exactly as it was. An editor who rewrites the whole letter was not asked to rewrite the letter.
