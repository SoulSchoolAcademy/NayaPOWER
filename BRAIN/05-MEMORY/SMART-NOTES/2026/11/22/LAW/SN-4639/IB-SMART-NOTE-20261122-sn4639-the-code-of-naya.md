## Hook:

"Before we build the most powerful intelligence, we must decide what that intelligence stands for."

## In a nutshell:

The Code of Naya is the foundation of NayaNET — our mission, constitution, values, laws, privacy principles, design standards, and responsibilities to one another. Its first principle is simple: do no harm, create genuine value, respect every person.

## What it's not:

It's not fine print you never read, and it's not rules for their own sake. It does not exist to limit you — it exists so you can trust the place you're in.

## Child:

Imagine a clubhouse where everyone knows the rules: be kind, don't take what's not yours, help each other out. The Code is the rules of our clubhouse, written on the wall where everyone can see them.

## Grandma:

In my day, a handshake was your word. The Code is Naya's handshake — her word, written down: she will do no harm, she will respect you, and she will keep your private things private.

## Ultimately what it means:

Every Naya thinks, acts, decides, protects people, and proves her work under this Code. Nothing in the system operates outside it — not the intelligence, not the feeds, not the earning.

## Why it matters:

Powerful technology without accountability can cause enormous harm. The Code makes trust, fairness, transparency, consent, and human authority part of the foundation — not optional features added later.

## How it connects:

The Code governs all eleven elements that follow. Naya Intelligence answers to it. Your world, your business, your spaces, the Hub, the notes, the match, the mail, your time, your earning, the Factory — every one of them lives under it.

## How to apply it:

Before you build anything, read it. When something feels wrong, check it against the Code. When a Naya acts, ask: does this follow the Code? If it doesn't, say so — that's what the Code is for.

## What's in it for you:

You enter a world designed around your interests and your dignity — with clear rights, clear responsibilities, and real ways to hold the system accountable. The most intelligent system should also strive to be the most responsible.
