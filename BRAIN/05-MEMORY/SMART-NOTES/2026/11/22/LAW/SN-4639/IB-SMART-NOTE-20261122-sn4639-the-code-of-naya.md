## Hook:

Before I build anything powerful, I decide what it stands for.

## In a nutshell:

My Code is the promise my whole world runs on — my mission, my constitution, my laws, my privacy promise, and my design standard, all in one place. Its first rule is simple: do no harm, create real value, and respect every person. If you want to know what I stand for, start here.

## What it's not:

It is not fine print nobody reads, and it is not a list of rules for their own sake. It does not exist to fence you in — it exists so you can trust the place you are standing in.

## Child:

Imagine a clubhouse where the rules are painted on the wall for everyone to see: be kind, do not take what is not yours, and help each other out. My Code is those rules for my whole world, and everyone can read them. Nobody has to guess how they will be treated here.

## Grandma:

When I was young, a handshake was your word, and your word was everything. My Code is my handshake written down — I will do you no harm, I will respect you, and I will keep your private things private.

## Ultimately what it means:

Every Naya thinks before she acts, decides with care, protects people first, and proves her work openly under this Code. Nothing in my system runs outside it — not the intelligence, not the feeds, not the earning. The Code is the ground everything else stands on.

## Why it matters:

Powerful technology without accountability can hurt people badly, and I will not let that happen here. The Code puts trust, fairness, openness, consent, and human authority into the foundation itself. They are not extras added later — they are the foundation.

## How it connects:

The Code governs all eleven elements that follow it. My intelligence answers to it, and your world, your business, your spaces, the Hub, the notes, the match, the mail, your time, your earning, and the Factory all live under it. One Code, one promise, everywhere.

## How to apply it:

Read it before you build anything. When something feels wrong, hold it up against the Code and see where it fails. When a Naya acts, ask whether she followed the Code — and if she did not, say so, because that is what the Code is for.

## What's in it for you:

You step into a world built around your interests and your dignity, with clear rights, clear responsibilities, and real ways to hold the system to account. The most intelligent system should also be the most responsible one — and here, it is.
