1. Without the name fixed, the page introduces her by the wrong name — and a wrong name on the declaration is a wrong declaration.

4. Without the higher-level framing kept beside it, removing the guarantee reads as lowering the promise — it is not lower, it is honest: a higher level of result than without the system.
5. Without the framing placed somewhere visible, the reader never sees the honesty — and honesty hidden is honesty unused.
