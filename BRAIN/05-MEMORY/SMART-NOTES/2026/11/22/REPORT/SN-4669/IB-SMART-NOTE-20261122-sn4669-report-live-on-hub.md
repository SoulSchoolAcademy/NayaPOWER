## What it means

1. **FILED ≠ LIVE:** SN-4662 was filed and verified on GitHub — and he still could not open a single note. Filing is not delivery.
2. **LIVE MEANS RENDERED ON THE HUB FEED:** a note is live when it renders on the Hub feed and he can open it, proven by a real render — not by a sync exit code.
3. **HOLD THE DELIVERY:** a daily or weekly report goes out only with its notes live. Hold it until they are live — never send the list first and the liveness after.
4. **THE SYNC IS THE GAP:** the hourly Smart Notes sync had not carried the notes to the Hub — the weekly run log said so in its own words. The fix round diagnoses why the sync reports 0 new and runs the established pipeline.
5. **NO MANUAL PRODUCTION WRITES:** the hard limit stands — no manual Supabase production writes from any seat; if the pipeline cannot carry a note, name it and report what needs his hand.
6. **WEEKLY ≠ WELCOME:** the weekly series (the week's lessons distilled) is not the welcome mail series that goes out when someone joins. Two different things.

## Why it matters

1. Without the law, every report is a list of names he cannot open — and he has to spend his attention asking what the list is for.
2. Without holding the delivery, the machine calls filing done — and the proof standard (his eye, on his screen) never gets its turn.
3. Without naming the sync gap, the next report repeats the same miss — filed, verified, and invisible.
4. Without the hard limit, a seat "fixes" liveness with a manual production write — and crosses a protected gate to do it.
5. Without keeping the series straight, the weekly distillation gets mistaken for the welcome mail — and the wrong series gets built or sent.
