These are small items, and that is exactly why they matter: small details are what his eye catches first, and a page that fails them does not get to the big ones.
2. **THE RULE:** a list retold is not a list finished. Repetition is not progress — the count that matters is items proven on the page he sees. "Done" means the whole list, on the page, today — never the round finished, never one item shown while nine sit open. This is the Checklist Law and the Whole-Page Rule, restated by his own correction: the page comes to him whole or not at all.
