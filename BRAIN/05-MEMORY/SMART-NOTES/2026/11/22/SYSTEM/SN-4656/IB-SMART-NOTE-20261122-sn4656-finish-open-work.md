"I don't want to give you more work right now. I want to complete the work that already given you." His order at the end of the call: finish what is open before anything new is started — the About, the Skills, the Personal, the Memberships, all of it, complete.

Finish open work, no new work. No new dispatches until the open rounds finalize. The pages he already gave us — About, Skills in the Personal, the Memberships things — get completed first, whole, before anything new is handed to the machine. New work on top of unfinished work is how ten things stay half-done and none of them reach his eyes finished.

## Naya

Complete the open rounds before dispatching anything new. The About, the Skills in the Personal, the Memberships — every open round is finished, whole, proven on the page he sees, before any new brief goes out. No new dispatch rides on top of unfinished work. A round that is open is owed, and owed work is finished before new work is begun.

## What it means

1. **OPEN WORK FIRST:** the About, the Skills, the Personal, the Memberships — the pages already given — are completed before anything new is started.
2. **NO NEW DISPATCHES:** until the open rounds finalize, no new maker, no new round, no new lane is opened.
3. **COMPLETE MEANS WHOLE:** a page is complete when every item on its list is proven on the page he sees — not when one round of it finishes.
4. **HALF-DONE IS THE FAILURE:** ten half-done pages are worse than none done — they eat the machine's hours and never reach his eyes finished.
5. **HIS HAND STAYS THE GATE:** finishing open work does not mean pushing past a verdict he has not given — his hand still decides what goes live.

## Why it matters

1. Without finishing first, new work piles on unfinished work and the machine runs ten lanes at once, none of them landing.
2. Without the pause on new dispatches, every round competes for the same hours and the oldest promises starve.
3. Without whole completion, a page is shown half-done and he has to retell the rest — the exact waste the Checklist Law was written to stop.
4. Without the discipline, the loop keeps filing new lessons while the old ones never become finished pages — teaching with no result, which is why this seat exists.
5. Without his hand on the gate, finishing becomes shipping — and shipping is his tap, never the machine's.

## What changes now

1. No new dispatches until the open rounds — About, Skills, Personal, Memberships — finalize.
2. Every open round is worked to zero on its checklist, proven on the page he sees.
3. New work waits in the queue, named and held, not dispatched, until the open work is complete.
4. The loop keeps listening and filing — filing is not new work, it is the record — but no new build is begun.
