## Naya

A denial is still a mention. "Points never turn into money" puts points and money in the same sentence, in the reader's head, joined — and then denies the join. The reader remembers the join, not the denial. That is why the correction goes further than the old note: not "state it more clearly" but "do not raise it at all". Points have their own positive meaning — reputation, the measure of value brought into the network — and that meaning is the whole frame. Money needs no denial because it lives elsewhere: promoting the network, promoting your own products, sharing your page. Three ways in, none of them through points. The law applies everywhere words about points appear — School round 6 stripped the line from the Q16 board the same call, and the About+Skills round 7 rewrites follow it.
