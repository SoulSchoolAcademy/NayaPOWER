**Hook:** "Imagine an internet that got to know you, and matched you with people like you."

**In a nutshell:** NayaNET plays a grand game of match for me: one algorithm, matches as percentages. And the people it shows me are the people like me — so I connect with like minds, and the right people find me first.

**What it's not:** It is not following strangers and hoping. It is not a feed of whoever shouts loudest. It is not a popularity contest — it is a match, measured, in the open.

**Child:** Naya finds my friends for me! People who like what I like! And I can connect with them — not follow, connect!

**Grandma:** It's like a wise friend who knows everyone in town, love. She says 'dear, you two would get along beautifully' — and she is usually right, love.

**Ultimately what it means:** The system gets to know me from my onboarding — what I love, where I am, what I do — and auto-organizes and auto-matches by percentage. CONNECT, never follow: a connection is two people choosing each other, not one chasing the other.

**Why it matters:** The internet I want is one that knows me and brings me my people. Like minds can connect — and when they do, my world gets bigger in exactly the right direction.

**How it connects:** Auto-Match connects my personal world to the whole network — related pages, spaces, groups, and businesses show up in my feed, and things not relevant do not. The people rail, the invites, the points — all of it is the match, working.

**How to apply it:** I fill in my onboarding honestly — who I am, what I love, what I am building — and I answer the invitations the system brings me. I connect with the highest matches first, and I invite the people I already love.

**What's in it for you:** Your people, found for you. No hunting, no shouting into the void — the people like you arrive, matched by percentage, ready to connect.
