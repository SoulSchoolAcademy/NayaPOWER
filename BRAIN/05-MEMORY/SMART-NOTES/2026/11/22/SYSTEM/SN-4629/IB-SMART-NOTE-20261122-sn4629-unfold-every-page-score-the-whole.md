If something breaks, you don't tape it — you rebuild the joint so it can't break that way again. The price list isn't guessed; it's the owner's own numbers. And nothing leaves the shop until the owner's eye has passed it.

This essence keeps the money honest, the reading easy, the vocabulary his, and the record bound to the rendered fact — the four currencies the whole system trades in.
