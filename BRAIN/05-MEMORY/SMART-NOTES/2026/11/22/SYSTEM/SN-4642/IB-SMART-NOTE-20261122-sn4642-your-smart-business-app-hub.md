## Hook:

"Your business world is the same as your personal world — turned toward your work, so you can make your living from the place you live."

## In a nutshell:

My business lives in the same world as my personal life, only turned toward work. My products, my services, my customers, my calendar, my ledger and my stats as one — so I can make my living from the same place I live.

## What it's not:

It is not a separate app I have to learn. It is not a shop bolted onto a profile. It is not a spreadsheet pretending to be a business — it is my business, as a world.

## Child:

My shop lives in my world! People can find me, book a time with me, and buy what I make — all in one place!

## Grandma:

It's like a little shop on your own street, love — the door is open, and people can find you. They can book a time, dear, and everything you sell is right there on the shelf, love.

## Ultimately what it means:

The more I put into my business world, the more the system can do for me. My demographics in settings make me findable, auto-match brings me the right people, and the more I share, the more traffic finds me.

## Why it matters:

Making a living should not mean juggling five tools. One world holds my work, my customers, and my money — and the ledger and the stats are one place, so I always know where I stand.

## How it connects:

My business hub connects to my personal world (same machine, different face), to my calendar for bookings, to my memberships and products, and to the feed. So my work reaches the people it is for.

## How to apply it:

I set my demographics in settings so people can find me. I list my products and services, open my calendar for bookings, and share my work as smart notes — each one an invitation to the right people.

## What's in it for you:

A business that runs from your world, not from a pile of apps. You share your work once, and the system carries it to the people looking for exactly that — and getting paid is part of the same flow.
