## Naya

Build About, Skills, and Memberships as three text boards per slide. Three boards explain each thing, in thirds, with room for the text to display nicely. Never a single big board: it leaves a big empty space underneath on a laptop, and a standard that works on mobile but not on laptop is not a standard. Make every board tap-to-swap: a member clicks any board and swaps it for an image or a video. Keep the header fold-away — he confirmed it working with his own eye: after a few seconds the header scrolls away and everything fits. Do not apply this to the School: the School keeps image-in-the-middle with two text boards.

## What it means

1. **THREE TEXT BOARDS PER SLIDE:** About, Skills, Memberships — three boards to explain each thing, in thirds, text displayed nicely.
2. **THE ONE BIG BOARD GOES:** it works on mobile but leaves a big empty space underneath on a laptop. A standard must work on both.
3. **UNIVERSAL FOR LAPTOPS, UNIVERSAL FOR MOBILES:** three boards present well on either — the standard is judged on both, not one.
4. **TAP-TO-SWAP, PER BOARD:** a member clicks any board and swaps it for an image or a video. Every board is swappable, by the member's hand.
5. **HEADER FOLD-AWAY KEEP IT:** confirmed working by his eye — after a few seconds the header scrolls away and the boards fit the space.
6. **NOT THE SCHOOL:** the School keeps image-in-the-middle with two text boards. The three-text-boards standard is About, Skills, Memberships only.

## Why it matters

1. Without three boards, the laptop shows a big empty space under the text — and empty space is what his eye sends back.
2. Without a standard that works on both, the machine builds twice — once for mobile, once for laptop — and the two drift apart.
3. Without tap-to-swap, the boards are fixed words — and a citizen who wants their own picture there cannot put it there.
4. Without keeping the fold-away, a working piece gets rebuilt in the next round — and rebuilding what works is how working things break.
5. Without the School carve-out, the next builder applies three text boards to the School — and the image-in-the-middle ruling he gave at 16:06 is overwritten by accident.

## What changes now

1. About, Skills, Memberships: three text boards per slide, in thirds.
2. Every board tap-to-swap: click any board, swap it for an image or a video.
3. Header fold-away stays as it is — confirmed working, do not rebuild.
4. School stays image-in-the-middle with two text boards — this standard does not touch it.
