## Why it matters

1. Without the rewrite, three boards hold the same thin text the big board held — the standard changes shape, not substance.
2. Without the "never reflow" rule, the machine takes the easy path — fit, accept, ship thin — and calls it done.
3. Without the why named, the boards look like empty slots — they are promises to a new person: your images go here, your videos go here, and it will be beautiful.
4. Without "draft for his eye", the rewrite gets treated as final — and his word-perfect gate is skipped.
5. Without the 10-star bar on the words themselves, the boards are full but the writing is flat — full is not the same as amazing.
