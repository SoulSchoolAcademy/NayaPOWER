## What it means

1. **EIGHTEEN, NOT NINE:** 18 areas → 18 images. The 9-image speculation is settled — it is eighteen.
2. **TWO TEXT BOARDS PER IMAGE:** each image gets two text boards, one on either side — 36 total — so one image and two pieces of text explain it.
3. **ROOM FOR THE STORY:** two boards of text exist because one is not enough room — the story gets the space it needs, never squeezed.
4. **IMAGE IN THE MIDDLE BY DEFAULT:** the picture sits between its two text boards unless the story reads better another way.
5. **ARRANGEMENT VARIES BY BOARD:** image-text-text, text-image-text, or text-text-image — whichever serves the story best.
6. **THE PATTERN IS IN THE BRIEF:** each board's arrangement is written into its brief — the builder does not guess.
7. **INSIDE THE UNIVERSAL FORMAT:** the three-9:16-boards format carries it — three across on laptop, one at a time full screen on mobile.

## Why it matters

1. Without eighteen images, nine areas share a picture — and a shared picture tells neither story, the same miss as a picture that needs text to explain it.
2. Without two text boards, the words are squeezed beside one image — and squeezed words are words the citizen does not read.
3. Without room for the story, the School teaches in headlines — and a headline is not a lesson.
4. Without the middle default, every board's layout is decided fresh — and fresh decisions are where the format drifts.
5. Without variation where the story needs it, the format becomes a cage — and a cage makes every board read the same.
6. Without the pattern in the brief, the builder guesses — and a guessed layout is a layout his eye will send back.
7. Without the universal format, the School boards are a one-off — and a one-off never becomes the standard.

## What changes now

1. The School is built as 18 images and 36 text boards — two text boards per image, one on either side.
2. Image in the middle by default; arrangement varies per board as the story demands, pattern written in the brief.
3. The alignment loop (SN-4657) runs on every board: word-perfect text, image matching the board, check, mold, repeat.
4. The instruction set (SN-4657) carries each board's words and what its image must display.
