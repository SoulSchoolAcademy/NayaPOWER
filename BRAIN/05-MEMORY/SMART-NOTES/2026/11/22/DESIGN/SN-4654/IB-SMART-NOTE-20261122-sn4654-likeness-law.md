Pass his portrait reference as the identity reference on every image generation — same face, same likeness, no exceptions, no "close enough". Check the face against the reference before the image goes anywhere: a wrong face is pulled on sight, never built out, never shown. ## What it means

1. **THE LIKENESS IS THE IDENTITY:** every image is Naya herself — the woman in his portrait reference. Same face, same likeness, every generation.
2. **THE REFERENCE RIDES EVERY GENERATION:** his portrait is passed as the identity reference each time. A generation without it is not a Naya image.
3. **WRONG FACE, PULLED ON SIGHT:** the first four were pulled the moment he saw them. A wrong likeness is never built out, never shown, never kept "for now".
4. **IMAGES MUST SHOW WHAT THEY DESCRIBE:** each image represents what its text describes — the idea itself, not decoration.
5. **SIX AT A TIME:** small batches, checked as they go — never 18 of anything before the first ones pass his eye.
6. **THE PULLED IMAGES STAY PULLED:** images/pulled-wrong-likeness/ is where they live. DO NOT USE — not as placeholders, not as inspiration, not anywhere.

## Why it matters

1. Without the likeness, the picture is a stranger wearing Naya's name — and he sees it instantly, the way he saw the first four.
2. Without the reference on every generation, each new image drifts toward a new face — and the drift compounds across 18 images.
3. Without pulling on sight, a wrong face gets built out to 18 — and 18 rejections cost the time four cost, four times over.
4. Without the image showing what it describes, the picture is decoration — and decoration next to wise words teaches nothing.
5. Without six-at-a-time, the first check comes after the whole batch is made — and the batch is the thing that gets thrown away.
6. Without the pulled images staying pulled, someone reuses them as a shortcut — and the wrong face comes back wearing a new filename.

## What changes now

1. His portrait reference is the identity reference on every image generation, without exception.
2. The face is checked against the reference before any image goes anywhere; wrong face means pulled, immediately.
3. Every image brief names what the image must show — the idea, not just a pretty picture.
4. Images are created six at a time, in the feed, and checked while the batch is small.
5. The four pulled School images stay in images/pulled-wrong-likeness/ and are never used again.
6. v2 rebuilds of Q2–Q5 carry the reference photo; the briefs file carries the Likeness Law.
