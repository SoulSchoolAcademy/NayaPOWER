## Nutshell

The first third always plays first. The text always has to be one third to the LEFT. If we ever do a 16:9, it always goes to the RIGHT — the first third plays first, then the other two thirds join into one 16:9. Always that way, because on mobile it presents the first third first — the thing you read — and it reads better that way. This is the standard for About, Skills, and Memberships. The order is the reading: left first, right second; text first, picture second. Never the picture first and the words after — the citizen reads the words first, and the layout must read them first too.

## Human

Think of it like a book. You always start on the left page — the words — and then the picture joins on the right. On your phone, the first thing you see is the words — the thing you read — and then the picture comes in beside it. If the picture came first, you'd be looking at a picture before you knew what it was about. So: words on the left, always first; picture on the right, joining after.

## Naya

Put the text in the first third, on the left, always. The first third plays first — the citizen reads the words before anything else joins. If a 16:9 ever appears, it goes to the right, and the other two thirds join into it after the first third has played. Never reverse the order: never the picture first and the words after, never the 16:9 on the left. The layout reads the way the citizen reads — left to right, words first — on laptop and on mobile alike. This is the standard for About, Skills, and Memberships.

## What it means

1. **THE FIRST THIRD ALWAYS PLAYS FIRST:** the first third is the opening — it plays before anything else joins.
2. **TEXT ALWAYS ONE THIRD TO THE LEFT:** the words sit in the left third, always. Not the middle, not the right — the left.
3. **16:9 ALWAYS TO THE RIGHT:** if we ever do a 16:9, it goes on the right — the other two thirds join into one 16:9, after the first third plays.
4. **ALWAYS THAT WAY:** no exceptions, no per-board variation. The order is the standard, not a suggestion.
5. **BECAUSE MOBILE READS FIRST THIRD FIRST:** on mobile the first third presents first — the thing you read — and it reads better that way.
6. **THE STANDARD IS ABOUT, SKILLS, MEMBERSHIPS:** the rule governs those pages. The School keeps its own arrangement (SN-4658, SN-4665).

## Why it matters

1. Without text first, the citizen sees a picture before knowing what it is about — the same miss as an image that needs text to explain it, in layout form.
2. Without the left fixed, every board's layout is decided fresh — and fresh decisions are where the format drifts.
3. Without the 16:9 on the right, a wide picture takes the opening — and the opening belongs to the words.
4. Without "always", the rule bends per board — and a bent rule is a rule the next builder ignores.
5. Without the mobile reason, the rule looks arbitrary — and arbitrary rules get dropped the first time they are inconvenient.
6. Without the scope, the rule spreads to the School — and the School's image-in-the-middle ruling is overwritten by accident.

## What changes now

1. About, Skills, Memberships: text in the first third, left, always; 16:9 (if ever) to the right, joining after.
2. Every new board on those pages is checked against the order: left first, right second; words first, picture second.
3. The School keeps its own arrangement — this rule does not touch it.
