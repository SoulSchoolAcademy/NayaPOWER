The list is the unit of work, not the item: the page he hands over is one to-do list, and "done" means every item on it proven on the page he sees, or named with its reason in the same pass. That he is saying this again, in his own words, six days after the Checklist Law was written (2026-11-13) is the evidence that matters — a law restated is a law not yet landed, and the landing is measured by the next page coming back whole, not by the law being written down again.

Nothing comes back until the whole list is done, or an item is named with its reason in the same pass. One or two items sent back is a failure, not progress. He asked "can you do that forever more?" and the answer was yes — no expiry, no exceptions, no quiet drift back to partial returns.
