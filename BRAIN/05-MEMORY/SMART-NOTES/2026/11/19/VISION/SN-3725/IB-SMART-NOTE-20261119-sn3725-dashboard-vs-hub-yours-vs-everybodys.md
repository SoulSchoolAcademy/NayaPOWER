# SN-3725 — Dashboard vs Hub — Yours vs Everybody's

**Filed by:** NayaNET Questions Round, 2026-11-19 — answering the newcomer question: "What is the Dashboard — what do I see there, and how is it different from the Hub?"

## The essence

Two places, one line between them: the Dashboard is yours, the Hub is everybody's. The Dashboard is your day, introduced to you each morning — your appointments, your numbers, your points, your money, your worlds, what needs you today. It is the first thing you see, and everything on it is about you. The Hub is the central stage — the whole network's feed, everybody's posts, everybody's notes, everybody's worlds, flowing past in one living stream. It is where you go to see the world, meet people, and be seen.

So: you start your day on the Dashboard, because it tells you what your day is. You spend your time in the Hub, because that is where everybody is. Your Dashboard never shows you someone else's day. The Hub never pretends to be private — it is the public square, and your private stuff stays in your Vault, not on the stage.

Why it matters: every system has a home screen and a world screen, and people get lost when they cannot tell them apart. Here the line is simple — yours versus everybody's — and once you see it, you never get lost.

What is in it for you: open the Dashboard to run your day. Open the Hub to join the world. Two taps, two jobs, no confusion.
