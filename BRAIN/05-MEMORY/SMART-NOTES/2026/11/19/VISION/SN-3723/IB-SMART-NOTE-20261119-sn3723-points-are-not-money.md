# SN-3723 — Points Are Not Money

**Filed by:** NayaNET Questions Round, 2026-11-19 — answering the newcomer question: "What are points, and how are they different from money?"

## The essence

Points and money are two different things, and mixing them up is how people get confused. Points are authority. The more points you have, the heavier your posts sit in the feeds — your words carry more weight because you have done more, helped more, shown up more. Points say: this person matters here. They are never money. You cannot spend them, cash them out, or turn them into a payment.

Money is joins only. Money moves when someone joins — when a person you brought in becomes a member, and when the system is paid. Citizens earn half the sale price, and they are paid when NayaNET is paid. Clicks are never money. Views are never money. Points are never money. The dollar sign appears only where money makes sense — joins, and only joins.

So: points make your voice heavier. Money pays you for bringing people in. One is standing. The other is earnings. Both are yours to grow, and neither one pretends to be the other.

Why it matters: if points were money, the feed would be a marketplace and every post would be a sales pitch. Keeping them apart keeps the feed honest and the pay honest.

What is in it for you: do good work, help people, show up — your points grow and your posts carry more weight. Bring people in — you earn half, when we are paid. Simple, separate, fair.
