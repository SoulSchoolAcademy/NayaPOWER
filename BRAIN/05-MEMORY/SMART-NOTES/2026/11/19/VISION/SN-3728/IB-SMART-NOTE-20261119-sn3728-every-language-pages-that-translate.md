## The essence

Yes — that is the plan, and it is the whole point. NayaNET is for everyone on earth, not for one language. You build your world in your language, and your page translates so anyone can read it in theirs. A person in Kelowna writes in English; a person in Paris reads it in French; a person in Tokyo reads it in Japanese. Nobody has to learn a new language to join. The system speaks your language, because the system is meant for the whole world.

Now the honest part, said plainly: the translation engine exists, but the walk does not use it yet. When you join today, the walk does not yet ask your language at the start and translate itself around you. That is the gap, and it is named in the note that carries it: the engine exists, the walk does not use it yet. Translation does NOT work today — this note does not claim it does. What it claims is the promise, and the promise is the law: pages translate, citizens build worlds in any language, and the walk will catch up to the engine.

Why it matters: a new internet that only speaks one language is just the old internet with a new coat. If everyone cannot read it, it is not for everyone.

What is in it for you: your language, your words, your world — and anyone on earth can read it in theirs. That is the promise. The engine is ready; the front door is next.
