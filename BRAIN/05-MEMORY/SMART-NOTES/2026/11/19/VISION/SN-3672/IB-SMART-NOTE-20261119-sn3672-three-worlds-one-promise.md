## IN A NUTSHELL

The bottom of the screen is three worlds, not three channels: your personal world, your business world, and your thing — and mail and messages live inside the world they belong to. The three worlds wear three gems — diamond, emerald, amethyst — the signature a citizen reads at a glance, and the Hub is where all three worlds' activity and stats live as one system. The mission that tests every page: reorganize the internet intelligently, connect like minds automatically by default, so nobody feels alone in their business or their thing. The walk becomes a mirror with lit EDIT and eye buttons — a choice whose edit or eye is hidden fails the law. Her name is Naya, N-A-Y-A; 'Anaya' is 'ah, Naya' spoken. And the growth engine is the product itself: the walk is the ad, the notes are the content, ambassadors are taught — literally hired and taught — until they earn.

## HUMAN

Think of a house with three rooms you actually live in — your own room, your work room, and the room for the thing you love. Your letters and conversations happen inside the room they belong to; you don't keep a separate hallway just for letters. Each room has its own jewel on the door so you know where you are the second you look. And the person who runs the house knows your name, spells it right, and does the work for you.

## CHILD

You have three special places: your place, your work place, and the place for your favourite thing. Your messages live in the place they belong to. Each place has its own shiny gem — a diamond, an emerald, a purple amethyst — so you always know where you are. And Naya — that's N-A-Y-A — remembers everything and helps you in all three places.

## GRANDMA

A well-run home has rooms with purposes, dear — you don't eat in the hallway or keep your letters in a box by the door. Each room is itself, you know it by its look, and everything that belongs in it is in it. And you spell a person's name the way they tell you to spell it. That's just respect, and it's also how nothing gets lost.

## NAYA

Assert the three-worlds structure in every navigation review; reject free-standing mail/message channels. Verify gem-signature consistency across worlds. Check every walk control for a visible EDIT and eye. Grep official copy for 'Anaya' as a name and correct it to Naya. Require a measured trigger before any second data system is adopted.

## MACHINE

1) Three worlds at the bottom; mail and messages live inside their world, never as free-standing channels. 2) The gems are the signature: three worlds, three gems, three colors, read at a glance. 3) Every walk choice ships with a lit EDIT and a lit eye; hidden or dim fails. 4) Her name is written Naya everywhere; 'Anaya' appears only as the spoken 'ah, Naya'. 5) No new data system without a measured trigger; a copy is never a replacement for the single source of truth. 6) The product is the ad: show the interface, teach the ambassador, put the link.
