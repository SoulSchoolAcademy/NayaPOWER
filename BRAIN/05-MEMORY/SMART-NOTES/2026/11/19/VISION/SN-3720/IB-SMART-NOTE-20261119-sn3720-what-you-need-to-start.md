## The essence

You need almost nothing. A phone is enough — NayaNET is built phone-first, and it works on a computer too. No invite. No special equipment. No technical skill. You come in through the front door, which is called the walk: first your name, then your certificate, then ten questions about you, then your pages open. That is the whole start. Your Naya asks the questions, you answer in your own words, and the system builds your world around your answers. You do not set anything up. You do not install anything. You talk, you tap, you are in.

One thing to know, plainly: right now one profile lives in one browser — that is a known bug being fixed, not a rule and not how it is meant to work. Do not plan around it. The rule is one person, one world, wherever they open it.

Why it matters: the hardest part of any new system is the first five minutes. Here the first five minutes are a conversation, not a setup screen.

What is in it for you: if you can hold a phone and answer questions about yourself, you can start. That is the whole requirement.
