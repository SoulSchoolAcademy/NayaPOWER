## The essence

The old internet is a set of separate boxes. Facebook is one feed, mixed up, showing you mostly your friends, selling your attention to advertisers. Google is a search box that sends you somewhere else. Every app is its own little world, and you are the product in all of them — your data sold, your mail read, your time chopped up into ads.

NayaNET is not another box. It is one system, and it is yours. One feed that is really infinite feeds — one per topic — showing you everybody, not just your friends, organized by what matters to you. Private by default: your mail is never read, your messages are never kept unless you save them, your data is never sold, there are no ads. One page that is your Personal, your Business, your Space. One memory that keeps what you save and forgets the rest. One Naya who does the work for you, so you talk and tap and it is done. And you earn: bring people in and you get half, because the people who build the network own the network.

So the difference is not a feature. It is the whole shape: the old internet is many boxes that use you. NayaNET is one world that serves you.

Why it matters: if you cannot say how you are different, you are just another app. This is the line an investor and a newcomer both need in one breath.

What is in it for you: a place where you are the citizen, not the product — private, organized, one system, and you get paid for growing it. Once you go, you don't go back.
