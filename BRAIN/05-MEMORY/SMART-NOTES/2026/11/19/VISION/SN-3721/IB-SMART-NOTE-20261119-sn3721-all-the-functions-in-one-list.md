## The essence

Everything in NayaNET is one system, and here is the whole list, in plain words. Your worlds: the Dashboard (your day and your numbers), the Hub (the central stage, everybody's feed), and your pages — Personal, Business, and Space — one page that becomes each of those. Your people: Smart Mail and Messenger, one inbox that is also instant chat; Smart Meet, video and audio with other members; Auto-Match, which connects you with people like you. Your memory: Smart Notes and Naya Notes, the intelligence of the whole system; Smart Lists, the view of your stuff; the Smart Box, the store where it lives; the Smart Vault, the one locked door to it all. Your time: the Calendar, which is your world as a day; the Scheduler and Booker, for appointments. Your work: the Smart Factory, which builds and clones pages; Memberships, for paid members to build their own membership sites; the Smart Ledger and Smart Stats, where the numbers live; Smart Growth and Affiliate, for growing and earning. Your guide: Ask Naya, who answers and does it for you; About, which unfolds the story of each world; Skills, which show what a person or a business can do.

Two honest lines: the full booking flow and the automatic organizing of the feed are still being built — they are on this list because they are part of the system, and they are named as coming, not as working today. Everything else on the list is what the system is made of, in one place, for the first time in a single note a newcomer can read and repeat.

Why it matters: before this note, the functions lived scattered across the builders' manual and a hundred build notes. Nobody new could see the whole thing at once.

What is in it for you: one list, one read, and you know what NayaNET does. If someone asks you what it is, this is the answer.
