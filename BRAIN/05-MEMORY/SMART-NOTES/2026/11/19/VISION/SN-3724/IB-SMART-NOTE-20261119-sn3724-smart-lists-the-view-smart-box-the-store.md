## The essence

Three names, one answer, and a simple picture. The Smart Box is the store. Everything you keep — your notes, your connections, your lists, your world — lives in your Box, and your Box is yours alone, separate from everybody else's. The Smart Lists are the view. A list is how you SEE what is in the Box: your people in one list, your places in another, your notes in another. The Box stores it; the Lists show it. Same stuff, two jobs — store and view, never mixed up.

The Smart Vault is the one door. Everything in your Box sits behind your Vault, and the Vault is locked to you. Nothing leaves it unless you say so. Nothing is kept unless you save it — unsaved notes and messages fade away after a week, by default, because the system forgets unless you tell it to remember. What you save stays in your Vault for good.

Why it matters: on the old internet your stuff is scattered across a dozen apps, and none of it is really yours. Here it is in one place, under one door, and you can see it any way you like — because the store and the view are two different things, and each one does its own job.

What is in it for you: you always know where your stuff is. Open a list to see it. Open the Vault to keep it safe. Save what matters, and it stays yours forever.
