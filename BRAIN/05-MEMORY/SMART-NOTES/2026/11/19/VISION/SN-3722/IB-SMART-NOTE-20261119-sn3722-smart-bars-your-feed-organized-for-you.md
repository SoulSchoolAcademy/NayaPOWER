## The essence

A smart bar is a row of tabs at the top of your feed, and each tab is one topic — your people, your places, your work, your learning, whatever matters to you. Tap a tab and the feed shows you only that topic. The tabs are smart because they are not fixed labels someone picked for everyone: they come from what you actually do, what you care about, and who you connect with. Your contacts sit on smart tabs as smart links. Your notes land on the tab they belong to. One feed becomes as many feeds as you have interests — an infinite number of feeds, one per topic — and you never have to dig through everything to find the one thing you want.

Now the honest part, said plainly: the automatic part is being built right now. Today the tabs on the page are fixed labels — they do not yet organize themselves and create related tabs on their own. Hub fix-9 builds the auto-categorization. When it lands, the tabs will sort themselves and grow new ones as your world grows, without you doing anything. Until then, the plan is the promise, and this note is the plan — not a claim that it works today.

Why it matters: the old internet gives you one feed, mixed up, newest first, full of things you never asked for. Smart bars give you your world, sorted, so the right thing finds you.

What is in it for you: tap a tab, see your topic. No searching, no scrolling past strangers' noise. And soon, you will not even have to make the tabs — they will make themselves.
