# SN-3727 — Smart Meet and Booking — Your Time, Their Time

## The essence

Smart Meet is how members meet face to face — video and audio, member to member. Not a call with Naya, not a call with a machine: a real conversation with a real person, inside NayaNET, on your own page. You meet the people Auto-Match brings you, the people in your worlds, the people you do business with — and you meet them where you already live, not on a separate app you have to open.

Booking is how you set that meeting up. You have your view — your calendar, your time, your appointments — and the other person has their view of you: what you offer, when you are free, what it is for. They pick a time that works for both of you, and it lands on your calendar as an appointment. You book as a person, as a business, or as a space — the same booking, seen from the world it belongs to.

Now the honest part: the full booking flow — the whole thing working end to end, your view and their view and the calendar all joined up — rides after membership. It is being built in that order, and it is not finished today. Smart Meet itself, the meeting itself, is the heart of it, and the booking around it is coming in full. This note says that plainly so nobody books a meeting that the system cannot yet hold.

Why it matters: time is the one thing you cannot get back, and a meeting that takes ten messages to arrange is a meeting that never happens. Booking should be one tap: their time, your time, done.

What is in it for you: meet people face to face inside your own world, and soon book them in one tap — your calendar, their choice, no messages back and forth.

## In his words

"Smart Meet is member-to-member." / Booking is "your view + public view" — your calendar on your side, what you offer on theirs.
