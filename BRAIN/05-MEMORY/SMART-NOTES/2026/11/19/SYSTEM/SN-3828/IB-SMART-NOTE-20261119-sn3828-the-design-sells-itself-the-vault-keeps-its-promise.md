## What it means

1. **THREE WORLDS, ONE PROMISE:** personal, business, and your thing — mail and messages live inside their world, never as free-standing channels; three gems, read at a glance; the Hub holds all three worlds' activity as one system.
2. **THE DESIGN SELLS ITSELF:** the walk is the ad — real examples, his words in the boxes, pricing after the world exists — and the vault is the promise: no reader inside the network, mail and messages not kept, not read, not sold.
3. **NEVER SHOW A SNAPSHOT AS THE PAGE:** a staged link shown to Shawn is the current page, updated and aligned, or it is not shown; a round is finished completely or it does not reach him.
4. **ONE FAMILY THAT CAN STATE ITSELF:** twelve gardens, one earth, one daily report, fourteen questions any seat can answer, six questions for any blockage — a family that can state itself can coordinate itself.
5. **ONE WORLD, ALREADY BUILT:** everybody gets the same finished world, labels personal; a new thing starts at grade one and drifts, so maturity is refusing to start again — and a list is worked whole, one item shown of ten is a failure report, not a delivery.
6. **OPERATE, NEVER OPEN:** permission to operate is never permission to read; no toggle exists that grants reading mail or messages — and every smaller promise (names, links, notifications) keeps the same faith.
7. **WISDOM IS THE TOP OF THE SCALE:** +9 is wisdom of oneself, respect for the law that connects everyone, honor for everyone — harm scores zero, always; the visible score, the color map, and the public face all point the same way.
8. **NOTHING SITS, EVERYTHING COMPOUNDS:** mine the gems, upgrade the body before the engine, take the page's own best pieces verbatim, let the read decide over the counts, and keep the list dispatching inside fifteen minutes.
9. **DONE MEANS GIVEN:** a render without the board write is not a render; a blind probe never certifies absence; a repair that drops a praised feature is a regression; measure the pool before blaming the composer.
10. **THE DEED BEFORE THE PRICE:** fix it without waiting, say it in plain words, sign the deed before the price — agreement, world, hired, package, in that order — and a 10 only counts when it's checked all the way through.
11. **FAIL HONESTLY, PROVE IT IN WRITING:** every promise that can hang gets a deadline and fails in plain words with the control returned; a proof that isn't recorded doesn't count; the GO and the FAIL are reported in the same record.

## Why it matters

1. Without the three worlds, a citizen decides in the first minute whether this is one world or a pile of features — and channels split from worlds read as a pile.
2. Without the vault as architecture, the promise is policy text, and a citizen who suspects the system reads keeps their real life elsewhere — the network gets the leftovers.
3. Without the showing laws, the team presents a fix snapshot as the page — the same defect as presenting a privacy policy as privacy — and Shawn judges a thing that isn't the page.
4. Without the family that states itself, the network fails by drift: seats who can't state the mission, reports that arrive as anecdotes, language that splits by audience.
5. Without one world already built, every duplicated world halves the team's power and doubles its drift, and every partial list taxes the one person's attention the whole system exists to protect.
6. Without operate-never-open, the load-bearing wall falls: a citizen who cannot relax here gives the system their working life and keeps their real life elsewhere.
7. Without wisdom at the top, the scorecard becomes a cleverness contest — and every scale in the system points at speed instead of honor.
8. Without the compounding kit, the team loses more value to unmined lessons, duplicated parts and unread copy than to any lack of talent or compute.
9. Without done-means-given, the team ships seeming-to-finish — a render that printed, a probe that couldn't see, a repair that quietly dropped the praised part — and calls it done.
10. Without the deed before the price, the citizen is asked to pay for a promise they haven't been given yet, and the ambassador doesn't know what the job actually is.
11. Without failing honestly, citizens forgive nothing: a button that spins forever, a proof nobody wrote down, a batch called done when its own record says fail — each one is a silent failure, and silent failures are never forgiven.
