## HUMAN

It's the difference between a landlord who gives every tenant the same solid house — your name on the door, your pictures on the walls — and one who builds a slightly different shack for each tenant and spends forever fixing twelve roofs. Same house, finished once, yours by its labels. And when the owner hands you a list of ten repairs, you do ten — you don't proudly show him the one shiny tap while nine drips continue.

## CHILD

Everyone gets the same wonderful house — you just put your name on the door and your drawings inside. We don't build a new house for each person; that would take forever and they'd all be wobbly. And if someone gives you a list of ten jobs, you finish all ten before you show them. Showing just one is not finished!

## GRANDMA

In my day you bought one good coat and altered it to fit — you didn't sew a new coat for every Sunday. A well-made thing, fitted with your own buttons, outlasts a wardrobe of rushed ones. And a list from the head of the house is worked top to bottom, love — you don't come back beaming about item one with nine still dripping.
