# Smart Notes, Real Stats, and No Little Pop-Ups

SN-3837 · SYSTEM / SMART NOTES AND STATS

**Created:** 2026-11-19 21:23Z

**Kinds:** Honor · Multiplication · Craft · Knowledge

## Hook

"If it says open stats, it opens the stats." A button's label is a contract — and his eye caught the breach: it landed on a really old business page that hasn't been updated, instead of the actual stats page.

## Nutshell

A business board's stats are two real numbers and nothing else: clicks to the page, and joins — "How many connected with you, how many clicks to your page. So connected with you, joins." Clicks are the reach, joins are the connection; together they say whether the business page is working, and any other number on the board is decoration or distraction. The same two numbers are the Dashboard's default: how many clicks and how many sales you got to NayaNet — and for a Business board, clicks to the page and how many people joined and connected with you. And the words matter: the Dashboard feed says "claim your name" and it should say "claim your smart link", NOT your name, because the name is different from the smart link. The name is who you are; the smart link is what the system tracks — you make a smart link for the system, then you can track any link and get the stats on it. A button's label is a contract, so "Open the stats" must open the actual stats page for that business or that space — the live clicks/gauge view, the gauge, the clicks, the joins, the Day/Week/Month/All pills — never the old, un-updated page. Same rule for both, because the citizen does not care which kind of board it is; they care that the button does what it says. And a view is not finished until the way back is obvious: the stats view needs an X at the top center — click it and you go back to the previous screen. The X was there before and now it's gone — "You just changed" — and the workaround he found, closing the right sidebar, returned the view unformatted. Restoring the X is not a new feature; it is putting back what was good, exactly where it was. Through it all runs the live law: "If I'm working with you live and something can be done right away without waiting, I know we have a structure but the structure is a guideline. But if we need it done, we just do it." The rebake was sitting for noon while he was right there, live, asking for it now — he ordered it run immediately, and it ran. The structure still holds the work together between live moments, but it never outranks the live moment. In the messenger, the plus button on the right must be aligned with the current: it opens the sidebar tab or the instant message, and then it moves over — the button gets out of the way once the conversation is open. And the info card — where a citizen meets someone — must be full framed and extraordinary, not a small little pop-up: "The card itself needs to be extraordinary in its presentation." Notes, instant messages and mail, the match data — the percentage matched, in what areas, what the connection is, what's relevant — and message, mail, Smart Meet, call, all from the card, because the person in it is not a little thing. That is the pop-up law, the signature output of the whole system: "Anything that has a pop-up now pops up and optimizes and fits INTO the frame, so it looks like it's meant to be there. No more little tiny pop-up things — always optimizing to the viewport, to that frame. That's our signature output type thing." Every card, every menu, every overlay is judged the same way: is it in the frame, or is it a little tiny thing? And underneath it all, the two notes finally have their names and their shapes: a smart note is intelligence distilled to its essence — in a nutshell at the top, because in a nutshell IS the human level, the most simple words — then the child note, then the grandma note, then the five areas on every intelligent block: ultimately what it means, why it matters, how it connects, how to apply it, what's in it for you. Three to four sentences per section, roughly. A Naya note is the full report, the full letter — same format, same section-by-section presentation, but the whole thing, however many sentences it takes — and Naya notes are the DEFAULT. A quick note is a Naya note too. And Naya reads the Naya notes — that's how she knows.

Across 10 notes, the thread is: the essence at the top, the full report as the default, buttons that keep their word, stats that are two real numbers, and nothing that pops up small ever again.

2. **THE NAME IS NOT THE LINK:** the Dashboard says "claim your smart link", never "claim your name". The name is who you are; the smart link is what the system tracks.
3. **A BUTTON KEEPS ITS WORD:** "Open the stats" opens the actual stats page for that business or that space — the live clicks/gauge view — never the old, un-updated page. Same rule for both.
4. **THE WAY BACK IS PART OF THE VIEW:** the stats view carries an X at the top center; clicking it returns to the previous screen, and the view stays formatted on return. Never remove what worked without a better way back.
5. **LIVE NOW BEATS NOON:** when live with Shawn and something can be done right away, do it right away. The structure is a guideline, not a gate. If it needs doing, we just do it.
6. **THE PLUS OPENS, THEN MOVES OVER:** the messenger's plus button on the right is aligned with the current — it opens the sidebar tab or the instant message, and then it moves over.

8. **NO MORE LITTLE TINY POP-UP THINGS:** every pop-up fits INTO the frame, optimized to the viewport — the frame is our signature output type.
9. **THE ESSENCE AND THE FULL REPORT:** a smart note is intelligence distilled to its essence — nutshell at the top, then child, then grandma, then the five areas — three to four sentences a section. ## Why it matters

1. Without the two numbers, the board fills with decoration and distraction — and nobody can tell at a glance whether the business page is working.
2. Without the right words, the citizen claims a name when they meant to claim a link — and the instruction on the page teaches the wrong thing.
3. Without the contract kept, the citizen clicks "Open the stats" and lands on an old page that hasn't been updated — and stops believing the buttons.
4. Without the X, the citizen is trapped in a view with no obvious way back — and the workaround they find damages the view on return.
5. Without live-now, a schedule makes a live human wait for work that could be done in minutes — the structure serving itself instead of the moment.
6. Without the aligned plus, the first gesture of the messenger breaks — a door off its hinges, opening the wrong thing.
7. Without the framed card, a person is presented as a small little pop-up — and the most important meeting on the page reads as the least important thing.
8. Without the pop-up law, every appearing thing invents its own shape — and the signature output of the whole system dissolves into floating boxes.
9. Without the two notes named, the essence and the full report blur into one another — the short note runs long, the full note gets cut short, and Naya has no record to read.
