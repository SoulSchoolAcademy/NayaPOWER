1. Without the deed in their language, a citizen signs what they don't understand — and the promise and the machine disagree from the first minute.
2. Without one chain, every surface invents its own road, and the town's notes, pages, people and earnings stop connecting.
3. Without taking the real parts, every invented part doubles the maintenance and halves the quality, every guessed number quietly lies to a citizen, and every unproven join breaks at the worst moment.

9. Without the door as the judge, a membership with a perfect paywall and a broken login is called 8/10 — while the member stands outside, unable to get in.
10. Without all three named, the reader takes away the smallest true thing — "a new AI" — and never looks for the network, the free member feels deceived at the first limit, and a finished tool off the list might as well not exist.
11. Without the behavioral definition, "most intelligent thing" is a slogan and the scorecards have no top — and power without wisdom gets called intelligence.
12. Without free forever kept beautiful, systems rot by replacement: each new version quietly discards what people loved about the last, and belonging gets upsold away.
13. Without writing for the stranger, everything the system knows is worthless at the exact point it is misunderstood — by the brief, by the builder, by the page, by the citizen.
