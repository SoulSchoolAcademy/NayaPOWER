2. Without one post per story with all its sources, a story arrives as one link pretending to be the story — and the citizen has no way to check it.
3. Without the back/front split, the optimization leaks onto the front and the page stops being beautiful — or the posts never get found and the bridge to the outside system never carries traffic in.
