The honest part is said plainly: the automatic part is being built right now, the tabs today are fixed labels that do not yet organize themselves, and Hub fix-9 builds the auto-categorization — the plan is the promise, never a claim that it works today. Points and money are two different things, and mixing them up is how people get confused: points are authority — the more you have, the heavier your posts sit in the feeds, because you have done more, helped more, shown up more — and they are never money, never spendable, never cashable; money is joins only, citizens earn half the sale price and are paid when NayaNET is paid, clicks are never money, views are never money, and the dollar sign appears only where money makes sense — joins, and only joins. Smart Meet is how members meet face to face — video and audio, member to member, a real conversation with a real person inside NayaNET, on your own page — and booking is how you set it up: your view, your calendar and your time, and their view, what you offer and when you are free, and they pick a time that works for both; you book as a person, as a business, or as a space, and the full booking flow rides after membership, named as coming, never as finished today. Every language is the promise and the law: you build your world in your language and your page translates so anyone can read it in theirs — and honestly, the translation engine exists but the walk does not use it yet, the walk does not ask your language at the start, so translation does NOT work today and this note does not claim it does.
