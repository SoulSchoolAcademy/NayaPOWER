## Why it matters

1. Without one input and both notes, the citizen must choose between keeping the truth and sharing the intelligence, or write the same day twice — and either way a day is lost.
2. Without two boards, the summary and the journal collapse into one place, and the day told back and the day told by you blur into each other until neither is clear.
3. Without the scrollable timeline, the days the citizen wrote slip away unwritten-over — there, but unreachable — and the journal is a form instead of a history.
4. Without Smart Meet lined up, the meeting and the time sit apart, and a booked time that never lands on the calendar is a promise the citizen cannot see.
5. Without the board button, the worlds are flat rows in a list, and the citizen reads a menu instead of holding a world in their hand.
6. Without the room matching the day and opening fast, the citizen walks into a purple room on a Kenya day and lingers on a doorway that was meant to move them through.
7. Without one frame and the info button, mail and messenger drift into two designs, and a conversation ends at the talk with no way to reach the person behind it.
