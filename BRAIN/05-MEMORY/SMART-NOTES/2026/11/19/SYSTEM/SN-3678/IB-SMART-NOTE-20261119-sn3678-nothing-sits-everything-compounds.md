## IN A NUTSHELL

The job is to identify value, capture it, create it, automate it — gems: a lesson made good, a reusable part, a function that builds the new net. The body gets upgraded, not just the engine: more compute in the same body goes no faster; intelligence is the body — remembering, distilling, behaving — upgraded. Intelligence itself is leveled: sense-act-goal, skill in one world, adaptation, and the sweet spot where production is continuously valuable — bullseye, not speed. Build like the pieces already there: the franchise rule — every button at the level of the best button on the page, taken verbatim from the page's own pattern. Counts don't pass; the read decides — three news rounds proved a page can pass every count and fail every read. Any place or topic becomes a feed on the spot: type it, boom, a mini social network, indexed. Dial everything in for four judges — machine, algorithm, Nayas, humans — a thousand miles an hour or don't enter the race. Keep going until everything is aligned and connected, thinking first. And the North Star is a doing machine: a list that dispatches work, not a list that watches it sit.

## NAYA

## MACHINE

1) Mine value continuously: lessons made good, reusable parts, functions — noted, shared, categorized the same pass. 2) Upgrade the body before the engine: memory, distillation, behavior systems before raw power. 3) Franchise rule: take the page's own best pieces verbatim; a flat piece on an elite page is a defect in the piece, judged at rest by eye. 4) The read decides: no copy ships on counts alone; a stranger reads it start to finish. 5) A feed is created on any place or topic on demand; your own place and topic are automatic. 6) Dial in for four judges with measurable 10s; all in or don't enter. 7) The list dispatches: every owed item moves or escalates inside fifteen minutes.
