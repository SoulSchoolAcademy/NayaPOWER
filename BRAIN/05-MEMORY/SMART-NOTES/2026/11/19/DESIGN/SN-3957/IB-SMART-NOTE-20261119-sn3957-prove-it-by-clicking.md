## Nutshell

The architecture is settled in one sentence: Personal is cover plus the Hub smart feed in the feed area, the Hub stays the Hub presentation unchanged — one feed, two homes, never two feeds, never a cover on the Hub. Then the proof law, in three parts. First, the top buttons: About, Message, and others don't work, and the bytes say why — the routing reads `data-os-go` attributes and there is no `showAbout`, no `goToHub`, no `openMyWorld` anywhere, Share and Message carry no routing attribute at all; the Hub feed moved into Personal and the routing didn't survive the move, so every top button gets re-wired to its right page. Second, every tab — top buttons, lens pills, keyword pills, sidebar items, Smart Tabs — must work when clicked, each one proven by clicking it; one dead tab teaches the person not to trust any of them. Third, the re-wired buttons land on the CURRENT updated pages, not old versions — World to the current calendar, Skills to the updated Skills, About to the updated About — because a working button that lands on a stale page looks done while delivering the old work. Personal goes live on the main page only when everything is working — proven, not claimed — and the tap itself stays his hand. The same proof standard governs the membership: no text overlays images, no text overlays videos, ever — the Academy headline, the Day 1 lesson title, and the FAC headline all sit on top of pictures, so every headline moves onto its own clean ground. And it governs the send: a link goes to him only when the edit is in the build and the build has been verified on screen — send only what's actually edited, hold the rest with the reason named.

Across 10 notes, the thread is: prove it by clicking — every button, every tab, on the current page, before anything goes live or any link is sent.

## What it means

1. **THE MOVE DROPPED THE ROUTING:** the Hub feed moved into Personal and the top buttons' destinations didn't survive — the fix is not a new button set but the destinations restored, every one proven by a click.
2. **EVERY TAB IS A PROMISE:** top buttons, lens pills, keyword pills, sidebar items, Smart Tabs — click me and I take you somewhere; one dead tab teaches the person not to trust any of them.
3. **THE LANDING IS THE CURRENT PAGE:** World to the current calendar, Skills to the updated Skills, About to the updated About — a working button that lands on a stale page is worse than a broken one, because it looks done.
4. **ONE FEED, TWO HOMES:** Personal is cover plus the Hub smart feed in the feed area; the Hub keeps its own presentation untouched — never two feeds, never a cover on the Hub.
5. **MY WORLD IS THE CALENDAR, CURRENT:** the door to the person's days opens onto the calendar they actually live in, synced, never stale — and the greeting meets them on entry.
6. **THE GREETING LIVES WHERE IT BELONGS:** out of Personal, alive on the Hub — centred, active, in the day's colour, with living glow; never polish a block on the wrong page.
7. **THE RANDOM PILL COMES OFF:** a floating control with no purpose is a loose thread on the page — it appears at random, means nothing, and breaks the clean frame.
8. **NO TEXT ON THE PICTURE:** headlines sit on plain ground or are baked into the artwork itself — one or the other, never page text over an image or a video.
9. **LIVE ONLY WHEN EVERYTHING WORKS:** Personal goes live on the main page only after the proof pass — every tab clicked, every walk fix in — and the tap stays his hand, always.
10. **A LINK IS A PROMISE THE EDIT IS IN IT:** send only what's actually edited and checked; an unedited build sent as updated breaks the promise, and holding is the delivery rule working, not slowness.

## Why it matters

1. Without the re-wire, the person taps About or Message and nothing happens — a promise the page breaks in their hand, and these used to work, so the person remembers them working.
2. Without the every-tab law, the top buttons get fixed and a dead lens pill or sidebar item stays behind — and the page still teaches the person not to trust it.
3. Without current landings, the click works and delivers the old work — the round looks done while the person sees the page he already corrected.
4. Without one feed, Personal grows a second feed and the Hub and Personal drift apart — two truths, two orders, and the person never knows which one is theirs.
5. Without the current calendar in My World, the door to the person's days opens onto a stale copy — and the greeting that follows them in would welcome them into yesterday.
6. Without the greeting moved, Personal carries a welcome that belongs to the Hub — and polishing it there is work thrown away, because the block was on the wrong page.
7. Without the pill removed, a random floating control keeps appearing mid-page — a loose thread that tells the person the page isn't finished.
8. Without the no-overlay law, the membership — the shop window — shows headlines pasted over the art, and a cold person reads sloppy before they read a word.
9. Without the live gate, a half-working page goes live and the world meets it half-working — and live is forever.
10. Without the send rule, an unedited build goes to him as updated — and once he learns a link can't be trusted, every later link costs him a check he shouldn't have to make.
