## CHILD

In this workshop, all the pieces are already made and they're beautiful. Your job is to pick the real piece off the shelf — not to draw a picture of it and pretend. If something breaks, we fix the part that let it break, not just the broken bit. The numbers on the price tags are the boss's real numbers — we never make numbers up. And before anything goes out the door, the boss looks at it himself.

## GRANDMA
