## The understanding

The name is who you are; the smart link is what the system tracks — you make a smart link for the system, and then you can track any link and get the stats on it. Getting the words right matters because the words are the instruction: "claim your smart link" tells the citizen what they are actually claiming, and what it is for. The same pass settles what the Dashboard shows by default when you go in: how many clicks and how many sales you got to NayaNet — and for a Business board, clicks to the page and how many people joined and connected with you. The default view is the count of people who came and the count of people who connected, before anything else — the same two numbers as the business board stats ruling, now as the Dashboard's default.

## The law
