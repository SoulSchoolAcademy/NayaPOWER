## The teaching, in his words (in substance, as recorded)

The network is made of frames and feeds. Everything is a frame — About, Skills, My World, the profile/contact card, membership, mail, messenger, the agreement — and they all get the same framed fill My World already has. The feeds themselves are different BECAUSE they are feeds — the Hub feed, the Personal/Business/World feeds flow as feeds, not frames. Frames and feeds, and that makes up our network. (Refines the framed viewpoint law: the frame rule applies to every page and area EXCEPT the feeds.)

## The understanding

Two kinds, two gestures: the frame holds still so a person can take one thing in whole; the feed moves so a person can stay current. The law is not 'frame everything' — it is 'frame what holds, flow what moves.'

## The law

Frames for every page and area — About, Skills, My World, profile card, membership, mail, messenger, agreement — with the same framed fill My World already has. Feeds flow as feeds: the Hub feed and the Personal/Business/World feeds are never framed.

## Owed from this dictation

The framed fill rolls from My World to every framed area; the feeds stay flowing — the Hub feed and the Personal/Business/World feeds are the check that the exception holds.

**Frames hold, feeds flow — that is the whole network.**
