## Hook

He remembered a glitch from yesterday — and his eye found it the moment the latest agreement reached him. The page was stuck mid-agreement, unreadable, no buttons, no scroll. And the fix for that, and for everything after it, is one shape: the frame.

## Nutshell

The whole membership login flow is built and the mail side proves it — a test send landed — but the page cannot send the code because the database will not let it insert the thread. One step is left, one permission, the RLS insert policy for `smart_mail_threads`, and it is his hand, offered to put it in on his tap. A flow one permission from done is not a broken flow; it is a finished flow waiting on a tap — so nobody rebuilds the flow, re-tests the mail, or re-checks the page. Then he asked for the latest collective agreement, because he remembered a glitch from yesterday's test and wanted to make sure it is aligned — and the glitch was real: the welcome front door dropped him straight onto the agreement with no bridge page and no two options, and the page itself was stuck mid-agreement, unreadable, buttonless, unscrollable. A remembered glitch is hunted until it is found, never re-sent on hope. The agreement's design is settled: the top icons GO — random around the text is noise — and the page goes into a frame, each thing it says in its own frame, a button to the next frame, every unfold framed. The first frame is the Naya collective agreement, with clicking play as the first choice — hearing her before reading a wall — and the big beautiful red N piece he loved goes on the first frame, its placement still his to settle, never guessed. The first frame's voice is Naya introducing herself: "hi I'm Naya, part of joining this world" — first person, warm, hers — and the unfold page takes the calendar's frame experience whole, so joining feels like something the citizen has already done. Then the frame stopped being one feature's presentation and became the presentation: it goes over everything in the whole presentation first, then each frame is optimized, one at a time — frame everything, then make each frame perfect. A frame carries text AND visual together, and it is how max-level viewpoints are reached; About and Skills take the same treatment, frame by frame, sideways or down. And the frame now has a spec, a top bar that never changes: Naya top-left, a new picture of her each frame, dots across the top, headline centred, a nice icon right — no time/date, no Your Stats — content fits below, and back / keep reading / next are the same every time, in the same places. Everything plays in the frame: the membership video, the image and audio, the PowerCast player. The slideshow takes the same frame, because a slideshow in a frame is a presentation. In the messenger, the same instinct: one toggle switches from instant message to Smart Mail — one click, one gesture, same frame, same people, the other speed — because the messenger and the mail are one system with two speeds. The messenger moves over: it opens the sidebar tab or the instant message beside what you are doing, never by taking the whole screen. PowerCast as a messenger button does not make sense, he asked — a player in a conversation has not justified its seat — and the proposed replacement is the toggle, proposed, not yet decided by him. And what was missing in the messenger: the search bar for all your contacts sits aligned with your contacts, and the Smart Tab bars sit in the messenger too, so a tap on any tab goes right to that contact list to instant message with it.

Across 10 notes, the thread is: name the one step left, hunt the remembered glitch, and frame everything — then make each frame perfect.
