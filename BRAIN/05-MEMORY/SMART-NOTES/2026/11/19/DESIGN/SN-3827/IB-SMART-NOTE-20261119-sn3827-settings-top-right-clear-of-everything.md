## The teaching, in his words (in substance, as recorded)

The settings page overlays the X button. Settings should be in the top-right corner, clear of everything else. And the settings panel items — Preferences, app behavior, identity, install-as-app — sit at the bottom of the scroll today. They should show up when you click the Settings tab — inside the settings panel, inside the frame. Always put things inside the frame.

## The understanding

A control that covers another control is not a layout choice, it is a collision — the X is the way out, and settings sitting on top of it takes the exit away. Settings live in the top-right corner because that is where the hand expects them and where nothing else needs to be. And the panel's own items belong inside the panel: Preferences, app behavior, identity, install-as-app are settings, so they appear when Settings is clicked, inside the frame — never stranded at the bottom of a scroll, outside the thing they belong to.

## The law

Settings sit top-right, clear of everything. The settings panel's items live inside the settings panel, inside the frame, shown when the Settings tab is clicked. Always put things inside the frame.
