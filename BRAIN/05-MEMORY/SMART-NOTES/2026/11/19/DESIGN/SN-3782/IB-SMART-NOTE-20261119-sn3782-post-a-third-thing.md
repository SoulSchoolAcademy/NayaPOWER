## The understanding

Three things now have names and must never be confused with each other: the smart note (the essence, public), the Naya note (the full report, private by default), and the post (a third thing). A post borrows the same intelligent block and framing, but it is not a block of intelligence and not a full report — it is a post. It can carry an image or none. It can be Naya's words from a prompt — headline, hook, in a nutshell, maybe an image — or the citizen's own direct words, shared as-is without reformatting, or any tone the citizen asks for: engaging, funny, whatever. The citizen's call decides the voice; the frame stays the same.

## The law

A post is a third thing — neither a smart note nor a Naya note. It takes the same intelligent block and framing, with or without images, in Naya's words from a prompt (headline, hook, in a nutshell) or in the citizen's direct words as-is, in whatever tone they ask for.

## Owed from this dictation

The post is now a named third form alongside the smart note and the Naya note; nothing that builds posts may treat a post as a block of intelligence or a full report. "Hook" is a post word — news blocks keep HEADLINE → IN A NUTSHELL → STORY, never "hook".

**A post is a post — with or without images, in your words or hers.**
