## What it means

1. **THE FRAME IS THE PRESENTATION:** it goes over everything first — agreement, slideshow, messenger, About, Skills, every pop-up — and only then is each frame optimized. A frame is not a border; it is text AND visual together, how max-level viewpoints are reached.
2. **THE STANDARD IS A SPEC, NOT A STYLE:** one top bar, one way through, everything inside. Any frame whose top bar differs, whose buttons move, or whose media plays outside it is out of alignment — not a variation.
3. **THE AGREEMENT IS A MEETING, NOT A DOCUMENT:** the citizen meets Naya first — her voice, play first — and the calendar's frame rhythm carries the joining, so it feels like something already known, not something new to learn.
4. **A REMEMBERED GLITCH IS A SPECIFICATION:** his memory said where to look; his eye confirming it was the proof. Never re-send a build with a known maybe-glitch unexamined, and never claim alignment a check cannot find.
5. **THE MESSENGER IS ONE ROOM WITH TWO SPEEDS:** instant message and Smart Mail are the same conversation at two speeds, and one toggle is the door between them — no second app, no lost context.
6. **EVERY BUTTON JUSTIFIES ITS SEAT:** a player in a conversation has not justified his — the question stays open until his eye confirms the toggle in its place, and nothing is claimed removed until he says so.
7. **THE LOGIN IS FINISHED, WAITING ON ONE TAP:** the flow is built, the mail works, the code exists — one permission stands between a finished flow and a working login, and it is his to give.

## Why it matters

1. Without the frame-first order, the next round perfects one corner — a beautiful slideshow, a beautiful card — while the rest of the presentation stands unframed, and the system never reads as one thing.
2. Without the spec, every frame invents its own top bar and its own buttons, and the citizen relearns the journey on every page — the opposite of push-button simple.
3. Without play-first, the citizen meets a wall of legal words before they meet the person asking them to join — and the agreement reads as a document to survive, not a world to join.
4. Without hunting the remembered glitch, the team re-sends the same stuck page on hope, and Shawn has to find the same defect twice — his memory doing the work the check should have done.
5. Without the toggle, the messenger is a room with no door to the mail, and the citizen has to leave the conversation to find the letter — two apps where one system was promised.
6. Without the search sitting with its contacts and the tabs jumping to their lists, the citizen digs for people the system already knows how to show — the auto-organization exists and cannot be reached.
7. Without naming the one permission, the team rebuilds the flow, re-tests the mail, and re-checks the page — burning rounds on a finished thing while the single missing step waits unnamed.
8. Without holding the red N placement open, a round guesses where the jewel goes, and the piece he loved lands in a spot his eye never chose.
