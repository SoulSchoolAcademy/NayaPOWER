## The understanding

A list of empty slots reads as nothing booked — it shows the absence, not the offer. A drop-down shows nothing until asked, and then shows exactly the choice; a mini calendar shows where the time is. The booking control belongs at the centre, wearing the same headliner as every other slide, because it is one family, not a separate tool.

## The law

No full slot list — a drop-down, closed until clicked; 15-minute and half-hour options; "Book a time" centred; the click opens a mini calendar of availability; the slide carries the same headliner as the rest.
