## The teaching, in his words (in substance, as recorded)

Going down the page, some numbers are too close to each other, blending into each other; some are big and some are small. Not right. Every number gets room to breathe; gauges locked to the same scale and spacing; the whole page reads clean and calm. The whole presentation down the page has to be aligned, right, and make sense.

## The understanding

Numbers that touch each other stop being numbers and become noise — the eye cannot tell where one value ends and the next begins, and big mixed with small makes the page shout in one place and whisper in another. Room is not empty space, it is what lets each number be read. One scale and one spacing across the gauges is what holds the whole presentation together: aligned, right, making sense, clean and calm from the top of the page to the bottom.

## The law

Every number gets room to breathe. Gauges locked to the same scale and spacing. The whole presentation down the page aligned, right, and making sense.
