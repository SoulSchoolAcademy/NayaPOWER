"The card itself needs to be extraordinary in its presentation."

## The understanding

The info card as it stands "sucks" — a small little pop-up — and a small little pop-up is the wrong shape for a person. The card is where a citizen meets someone: it must be full framed and beautiful, featuring that person, with everything about them and everything you can do with them in one place. Make notes on them. See the instant messages or the mail to them. See the data — the percentage matched, in what areas, what the connection is, what's relevant. Message them, mail them, schedule a Smart Meet, call them — all from the card. Nothing about the person lives somewhere else; the card is the person, presented extraordinarily. This is the pop-up law applied to the most important pop-up there is: the card stops being a little thing and becomes a framed presentation, because the person in it is not a little thing.

## The law

The info card is full framed and extraordinary: notes, instant messages and mail, the match data (percentage, areas, connection, relevance), and message / mail / Smart Meet / call — all from the card. Never a small little pop-up.
