## The understanding

The frame standard now has a spec, and the spec is a top bar that never changes: Naya top-left — a new picture of her each frame, so the frame always has her face and the face is always fresh — dots across the top to show where you are in the story, headline centred, a nice icon right. What the top bar does NOT carry is as settled as what it does: no time/date, no Your Stats — the frame is for the story, not the dashboard. Below it, the content fits — the frame holds what it shows, nothing spills. And the way through never changes either: back / keep reading / next, the same every time, in the same places, so the citizen's thumb never has to relearn the journey. Everything plays in the frame — the membership video, the image and audio, the PowerCast player — nothing pops the citizen out of the frame to play elsewhere. One bar, one way through, everything inside: that is what makes a frame a frame instead of a page with a border.
