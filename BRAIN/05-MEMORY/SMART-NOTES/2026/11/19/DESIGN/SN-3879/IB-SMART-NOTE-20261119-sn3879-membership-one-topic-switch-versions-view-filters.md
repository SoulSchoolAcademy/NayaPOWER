## The teaching, in his words (in substance, as recorded)

A topic may have a video, an audio, and a lesson — a little button switches between seeing just the video version, the audio version, or the lesson version (like the calendar thing), then click to the next one. And they can see all videos at once, all audios at once, all lessons at once — or the mix. (Rides the membership rebuild: the frame-per-topic switch + the All videos / All audios / All lessons / Mix views.)

## The understanding

The topic is the unit, the version is the choice. A switch per topic means the person never leaves the thing they are learning to change how they learn it — and the All views mean the person who wants only video never has to wade through audio to find it. Same content, every shape, the person's pick.

## The law

Every membership topic: one little switch between its video, audio, and lesson versions, then click to the next one. And the views: all videos, all audios, all lessons — or the mix.
