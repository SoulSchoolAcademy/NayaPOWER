## What it means

1. **THOUGHTFUL FOLLOW-THROUGH:** every delivery arrives checked, with recommend, love, don't love, suggest, and why in the open — "here, check it" makes him do the work that is the captain's.
2. **SIX IN ONE ROW, LABELLED:** Your Worlds shows all six icons across in one row, each in its own elevated board, each labelled — about a third the size, so all six are seen, none cut off, no scrollbar.
3. **A SLIDE THAT EXPLAINS ITSELF:** every slide names itself and explains what it does; the News slide is labelled the news hub with smaller icons and a live new-story count on each door — no guessing.
4. **SEVEN DAYS, SEVEN COVERS:** one real cover per day, in the day's colour, as the inner-frame background behind the content — the mood changes with the day; never a slide, never behind wisdom, never invented.
5. **BOOK A TIME, NOT A LIST:** no full slot list — a drop-down, closed until clicked, 15-minute and half-hour options, "Book a time" centred, the click opening a mini calendar of availability, the same headliner as the rest.
6. **ELITE IS STUDIED, NOT GUESSED:** never build elite from a guess — study the best in the world first, then build to that standard; a walk's feedback closes on his word, and the brief stands until the next walk.
7. **MOTION WAITS ON THE PLATFORM:** when he asks "can it work now," check the platform and report what it says — no blind retries on a path that has already failed; the check is the answer.
8. **THE PHOTO THAT WORKED BEFORE:** retry the exact photo that worked before when he names it — his order overrides the don't-retry note for that photo alone; motion is judged only after activation, never before.
9. **A PILL NAMES THE TOPIC:** topic pills carry the topic name only — no "online" counts on the pill; the counts live where counts belong, not on the door.
10. **THE GLOW IS THE RIM:** an active pill's glow sits around the back of the button, never over the top of it — light around the edge lifts it; light over the top washes it out.

## Why it matters

1. Without thoughtful follow-through, every delivery lands as raw work for him to check — and his eye, which should only have to decide, ends up doing the captain's job.
2. Without six in one row, labelled, the citizen sees three oversized icons, a second row cut off at the bottom, a scrollbar, and no words saying what anything is.
3. Without titles and explanations, a slide of icons asks him to guess what it is for — and a count-less news door gives no hint where the new stories are.
4. Without the seven real covers, the backgrounds are invented or placeholder — and the day's feeling, which is the cover's whole value, is faked instead of felt.
5. Without the drop-down, the appointments slide shows a long list of empty slots that reads as nothing booked — the absence on display, not the offer.
6. Without studying the best, the team builds "elite" from a guess — and the result is what he already saw: a slide that does not look elite.
7. Without the check-first law, the team retries the same failed motion path and reports another still as if trying again were progress.
8. Without the photo that worked before, the retry starts from a fresh guess — and his memory of what worked, which is the best evidence there is, goes unused.
9. Without clean pills, every topic door carries a census on it — clutter fighting the name, on the very thing meant to be read at a glance.
10. Without the glow around the back, the lit pill washes over its own face — the same wash the boards already outlawed, back on the button.
