## The understanding

Two missing pieces, and both are about getting to the right people without a dig. First, the search bar for all your contacts needs to be aligned with your contacts — the search sits with the people it searches, in the same place, on the same axis, so the eye goes from search to person without travelling. A search bar floating away from its contacts is a search for something else. Second, the Smart Tab bars belong in the messenger too: click any tab and it goes right to that contact list — the category's people, only those people — so the citizen can instant message with that contact list directly. The tabs are the auto-organization, in the messenger: the contacts are already categorized (the law of the earlier note), and the tabs are how the citizen reaches a category in one tap instead of scrolling all contacts to find the few they want. "So that's what's missing" — the messenger without its search and its tabs makes the citizen dig for people the system already knows how to show.

## The law

The contact search bar sits aligned with your contacts, in the messenger. The Smart Tab bars sit in the messenger too: click any tab and it goes right to that contact list, so the citizen can instant message with that list. That is what was missing.
