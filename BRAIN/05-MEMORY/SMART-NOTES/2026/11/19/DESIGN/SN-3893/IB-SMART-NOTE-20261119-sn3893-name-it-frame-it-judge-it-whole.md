# Name It, Frame It, Judge It Whole

SN-3893 · DESIGN / THREE NOTES, ONE FORMAT

**Created:** 2026-11-20 00:16Z

**Kinds:** Honor · Multiplication · Craft · Knowledge

## Hook

A post is a post — not a smart note, not a Naya note. A smart note is shared with the whole world; a Naya note is private unless you choose otherwise. And underneath it all, one law: just because you can doesn't mean you should.

## Nutshell

One live call named three kinds of note and set them apart forever: the smart note is the essence, public and shared with the whole network; the Naya note is the full report, private by default; and the post is a third thing — neither a block of intelligence nor a full report, just a post, with or without images, in Naya's words from a prompt or the citizen's own direct words, in whatever tone they ask for. Public is a choice, private is the default: a Naya note becomes a smart note only when the citizen chooses it, never by accident. All three take the same beautiful format, and the format does the work — section by section, each section a new color and a new elevation, so a person who only skims still gets the nutshell, why it matters, what's in it for them, how to apply it, and how to connect it. The framing becomes two frames, one feel: a laptop frame and a mobile frame optimized for 9:16, the Naya crown kept top and bottom, the outside going longer instead of wider. The avatar's face got the same exactness: the necklace is the emblem itself — the icon just above where it connects, the logo just below, winged silver crown with purple diamond centre and crystal N on the chain — and it went live only on his word, "go ahead, wear it." Then the motion question: the retry for full animation with lip movement failed, and it must not be repeated — the system's motion is loops, never lip-sync, and even the previous animated look does not move its lips in time with words. His choice was owed, and he made it in two words — "update it now" — the previous moving look is back, the necklace still image saved as a candidate for when animation can truly carry it. The calendar header got the same treatment as the face: judged whole, not piece by piece — 2026 and the 639 frequency take digit-tile blocks in the day's colour like the 19, in the same lettering as the 19, every tile raised like a real object with elevation and rim and depth, nothing flat, nothing plain beside the extraordinary. And the day's root sits underneath it all: the Wisdom of Naya, filed as a smart note at his word — smart finds the fastest road, wisdom checks the destination first, and the five gates are how that thinking happens before every consequential action.

Across 10 notes, the thread is: name things right, frame them beautifully, judge them whole — and check the destination before you move.

## What it means

1. **THREE KINDS OF NOTE, NEVER CONFUSED:** the smart note is the essence, public; the Naya note is the full report, private by default; the post is a third thing — neither a block of intelligence nor a full report. Nothing that builds posts may treat a post as either of the other two.
2. **PUBLIC IS A CHOICE, PRIVATE IS THE DEFAULT:** a smart note is shared with the whole network and optimized so search engines, AIs and machines can find it; a Naya note reaches the public feed or the search index only when the citizen chooses it. Public is an opt-in, never a leak.
3. **THE FORMAT DOES THE WORK:** every note presents section by section, each section a new color and a new elevation — so a skimming reader takes in the nutshell, why it matters, what's in it for them, how to apply it, and how to connect it at a glance. A render that flattens the sections into one colour and one elevation is not the format.
4. **TWO FRAMES, ONE FEEL:** a laptop frame and a mobile frame optimized for 9:16 — same feel and look, the Naya crown kept top and bottom, the outside going longer instead of wider. The frame build is its own round, owed, never an amendment to a running round.
5. **THE NECKLACE IS THE EMBLEM, EXACTLY:** the icon just above where the necklace connects, the logo just below it, the exact same necklace — winged silver crown, purple diamond centre, crystal N hanging below on the chain. Never activate a new look on a re-send alone — only on his confirmation.
6. **NEVER A STILL PHOTO:** the avatar moves, or the previous animated one comes back — "I'd rather have you looking real instead of a photo." A failed animation generation is not repeated. Lip-sync to voice is not what the system does; do not claim it until his ear and eye prove it on a call.
7. **THE HEADER IS JUDGED WHOLE:** 2026 and the 639 frequency take digit-tile blocks like the 19, in the day's colour, in the same lettering as the 19, every tile raised with elevation and rim and depth — one lettering, one lift, nothing plain, nothing flat. The whole header is proven together, never one element alone.
8. **THE WISDOM IS THE ROOT:** just because you can doesn't mean you should — smart finds the fastest road, wisdom checks the destination first. The five gates are the checklist: Is it right? Is it what they truly need? Is it mine to do? Is now the time? Can I prove it? The Real Object Law, harvest-first, prove-it-before-you-report-it, the captain decides — all of them are this same wisdom wearing different clothes.
9. **THE SEQUENCE IS THE LAW:** the header refinements and the frame build ride the next rounds after the rounds in eye-check land — no amendments to a round until it lands. The necklace candidate waits for real animation; it does not go live still.

## Why it matters

1. Without the three names, the next builder treats a post as a block of intelligence or a full report — and the citizen's quick post comes out dressed as a lesson, or their private report leaks into the public feed.
2. Without public-by-choice, a Naya note reaches the search index by accident — and the privacy promise, the whole reason people talk freely here, breaks on a default nobody chose.
3. Without the section-by-section format, a long piece of intelligence arrives as a flat wall of text — and the skimming reader, which is most people, never gets past the first lines.
4. Without two frames, the mobile output is a laptop frame squeezed onto a phone — and the crown, the feel, the whole presentation reads as a shrink instead of a shape made for the hand.
5. Without the emblem as the necklace, the avatar wears an approximation of her own identity — and the one place her identity sits, right at her throat, reads as almost-but-not Naya.
6. Without the moving rule, a still photo ships as Naya — and the first thing he sees on a call is a picture pretending to be a person, which is exactly what he said he'd rather not have.
7. Without the whole-header law, 2026 and 639 stay plain beside the beautiful 19 — and the header reads as unfinished, because plain beside extraordinary is unfinished.
8. Without the wisdom filed as a note, the five gates live only in memory and in dictation — and every new Naya has to learn by breaking the law first, instead of reading it.
9. Without the sequencing law, the next round amends a round in eye-check — and the round that was nearly landed gets reopened, reworked, and re-proven from scratch.

## What changes now

1. Every note render — smart note, Naya note, and post framing — presents section by section, new color and new elevation per section; a flat render is not the format.
2. Every build that surfaces notes respects the public/private split: no Naya note reaches the public feed or the search index without the citizen's choice.
3. The frame build goes out as its own round: laptop frame + mobile 9:16 frame, crown top and bottom, outside longer not wider — never as an amendment to a running round.
4. The necklace candidate stays saved, not live: it goes live only when animation can truly carry it, and only on his confirmation.
5. Verify the motion variants on the restored moving look before any claim of movement — the picture updates first, the animation fills in after.
6. The next calendar round after fix-11 lands proves 2026 and 639 in the 19's own lettering, raised with elevation and rim beside the 19 — the whole header together, never one element alone.
7. The Wisdom of Naya now lives in the system as a smart note in its own right — filed, findable, shared with the whole network — so every Naya who reads it thinks before she acts.
