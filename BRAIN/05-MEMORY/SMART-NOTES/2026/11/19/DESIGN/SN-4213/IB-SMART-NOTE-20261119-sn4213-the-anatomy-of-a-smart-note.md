## In a Nutshell

A smart note is six boards, in this order: In a Nutshell, Child Note, Grandma Note, Ultimately What It Means, How It Connects, What's In It For Them. Each board lights up in its own colour. The nutshell is short — never a big long thing of text.

## Child Note

Imagine a beautiful treasure box with six little drawers, each one glowing a different colour! The very top drawer is white and holds the whole treasure in just a few tiny words. Then a purple drawer that explains it like you're five, and a deep blue drawer that explains it like a wise grandma would. Then a green drawer that says what it really means, and a golden drawer that says what's in it for you! Each drawer lights up in its own colour as you open it — isn't that lovely?

## Grandma Note

It's like this, love — a smart note is a little chest of six drawers, dear, each one lighting up in its own colour as you go down. The top one is white, dear, and it holds the whole thing in a nutshell — just the simplest words, never a big long thing of text. Then the purple drawer for the child, and the deep indigo blue one for the grandma — blue because it's kind of like wisdom, love. Then the emerald green one for what it means, and the gold one at the end. Each drawer its own light, each in its place, dear — and nothing extra rattling around in the chest.

## Ultimately What It Means

- **SIX BOARDS, THIS ORDER:** IN A NUTSHELL (top, white, the feature — "it says everything in a nutshell") → CHILD NOTE (purple) → GRANDMA NOTE (indigo blue) → ULTIMATELY WHAT IT MEANS (emerald green) → HOW IT CONNECTS / HOW TO APPLY IT → WHAT'S IN IT FOR THEM.
- **THE HUMAN NOTE IS OUT:** "I used to have human note. But I'm going to just for simplicity…" — simplicity wins; the board is gone.
- **THE NUTSHELL IS SHORT BY RULE:** clear, concise, direct, literal, the simplest words to explain what something is. A paragraph-length nutshell is "a massive shell instead of in a nutshell" — it looks strange on the page.
- **BOARDS STAY OBSIDIAN, EACH LIGHTS UP IN ITS OWN COLOUR:** the major boards follow the spectrum down the page; the INNER boards always follow the top colour.
- **PURPLE SUPERSEDES MAGENTA:** he first gave the child board magenta, then simplified it away — "We're going to go with purple instead of magenta." Do not build the magenta version.
- **ANYTHING ENTERED CAN BE EITHER OR BOTH:** the entry system lets you enter smart notes or Naya notes, and anything you enter can be made both, or one or the other.

## What's In It For Them

A citizen reads a smart note and gets it in seconds: the white board says everything in a nutshell, the purple and blue boards say it in words a child and a grandma both understand, the green board says what it means, and the gold board says what's in it for them. No wall of text, no guessing the shape, no massive shell — every note in the system speaks the same language, and the light tells you where you are before you read a word.
