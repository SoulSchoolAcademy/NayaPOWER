## The understanding

Text over a picture loses twice: the words are hard to read and the image is spoiled. A headline earns its own clean ground — that is what makes it look intentional instead of pasted on. The membership is the shop window; a headline pasted over the art tells a cold person the work is sloppy before they read a word.

## The law

No text overlays images. No text overlays videos. Ever. Headlines sit on plain ground, or they are baked into the artwork itself — one or the other, never page text over a picture.
