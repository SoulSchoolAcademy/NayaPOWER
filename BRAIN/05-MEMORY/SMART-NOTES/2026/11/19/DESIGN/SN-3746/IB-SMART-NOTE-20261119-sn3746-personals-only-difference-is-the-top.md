A Personal / Business / Space page has its feature cover on top, with its menus and search and the standard menu bar Personal pages carry — and everything below that mirrors the Hub exactly. ## The understanding

The literal copy needs one seam, and this is where it sits: the top. The feature cover and the standard menu bar are the Personal identity — the citizen's world, their name, their cover, their menus. Everything below is the Hub, exactly as it is, because the Hub is the system and the system does not change between pages. Without this boundary the maker has two wrong moves: copy the Hub whole and lose the Personal page entirely, or rebuild Personal from scratch and lose the Hub. With it, the page is one thing: their world on top, the whole system below — and the Personal area jumps ahead the moment the presentation lands, because it inherits everything the Hub already is.

## The law

Personal's only difference from the Hub is the top: the feature cover, menus, search, and standard menu bar sit on top; everything below mirrors the Hub exactly.
