## The teaching, in his words (in substance, as recorded)

On the Smart Link dashboard the gauges are not one family — one is small, one is big. That doesn't make sense. All gauges the same size, same weight, same materials, so they sit evenly in the frame.

## The understanding

A gauge is a member of a family, and a family reads as one thing: same size, same weight, same materials. One small and one big side by side is not variety, it is a mistake the eye trips on — the page stops reading as a system and starts reading as parts that were never measured against each other. The frame is what makes them sit evenly; the sameness is what makes them calm.

## The law

All gauges one size, one weight, one material family, sitting evenly in the frame. No gauge bigger or smaller than its siblings.
