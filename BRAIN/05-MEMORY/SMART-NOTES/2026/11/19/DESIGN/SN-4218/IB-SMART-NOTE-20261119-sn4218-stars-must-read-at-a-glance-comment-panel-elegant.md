## Ultimately What It Means

- **LIT MUST READ AT A GLANCE:** "there's barely a difference between what you can see for the stars when they're highlighted and when they're not highlighted" fails the control. More contrast, bigger stars, more objectnality, a flow around the lit state — the fix is judged by eye, lit vs unlit side by side.
- **THE PANEL HAS A SHAPE:** input at the VERY TOP; most small text REMOVED; all buttons UNDERNEATH the comment. Simple: just enter the comment.
- **FIVE KEYWORDS STAY:** Ask a question · Build on this · Contribute intelligence · Report an outcome · Add evidence. The rest of that stuff doesn't need to be there.
- **THE INPUT BOX IS ALIVE:** lettering with more depth; lights up WHITE when not hovering, PURPLE on hover — so it feels more active and alive.
- **THE BAR IS THE WORDS:** the screenshot "is NOT beautiful and elegant — it NEEDS to be." Clean, elegant, beautiful is the bar for the whole panel, not a description of its current state.
- **SHARE AND COMMENT KEEP THEIR PLACES:** SHARE in the corner, COMMENT in the middle.

## What's In It For Them

And people speak where speaking is easy — an input at the top with five kind suggestions is an open door, not a form. Both make the feed a place citizens want to take part in, not just read.
