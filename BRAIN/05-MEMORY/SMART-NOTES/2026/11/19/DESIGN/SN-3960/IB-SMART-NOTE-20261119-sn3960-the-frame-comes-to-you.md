## Nutshell

Every area gets the same treatment: each step is its own frame — visual presentations framed as they go through, text presentations framed as they go through — so a person never scrolls down or across; the next frame comes INTO their viewpoint. The frame is the unit of attention: one thing, whole, in front of you, and then the next — instead of a river you have to swim down. Scrolling makes the person hunt; the framed viewpoint makes the system serve. When every area works the same way, the person stops learning pages and starts living in them. It applies to the agreement, visual and text alike, to the membership site, to the mail, and to the messenger — every lesson, every message, every page. It is already carried in the membership rebuild, frame by frame, and in the walkthrough fix-9, the agreement as a framed presentation; the mail framing round and the Messenger framing round follow, with the Messenger's waiting for its one-item fix round to land first, same file, one round at a time.

## What it means

1. **THE FRAME IS THE UNIT OF ATTENTION:** one thing, whole, in front of you, and then the next — never a river you have to swim down.
2. **THE NEXT FRAME COMES INTO THE VIEWPOINT:** no scrolling down, no scrolling across, anywhere — the system serves the step to the person instead of making the person hunt for it.
3. **VISUAL AND TEXT ALIKE:** visual presentations framed as they go through, text presentations framed as they go through — the law holds for both, with no exception for words.
4. **EVERY AREA, THE SAME WAY:** the agreement, the membership site, the mail, the messenger — every lesson, every message, every page. One way of presenting is what makes the network feel like one place.
5. **ALREADY CARRIED, THEN EXTENDED:** the membership rebuild and the agreement's framed presentation carry it now; the mail and Messenger framing rounds follow in their turn, one round at a time per file.

## Why it matters

1. Without the framed viewpoint, every area invents its own way of showing the next step — and the citizen has to learn a new page every time instead of living in one system.
2. Without the frame coming to the person, scrolling stays the default — and the person hunts while the system stands still, which is the machine failing the person.
3. Without visual AND text both framed, the words get left in a long scroll while the pictures get the beautiful treatment — and the agreement, which is mostly words, is exactly the page that would stay unread.
4. Without one law across every area, the membership gets frames while the mail and the messenger stay rivers — and the network feels half-built, framed in some rooms and scrolling in others.
5. Without the sequencing — Messenger's framing waiting for its one-item fix to land — two rounds land on the same file at once, and the page comes back with one round's work undoing the other's.

## What changes now

1. The membership rebuild and the walkthrough fix-9 carry the framed viewpoint now — framed lessons, framed agreement, visual and text alike.
2. The mail framing round and the Messenger framing round are queued next, in their turn — Messenger's only after its one-item fix round lands, same file, one round at a time.
3. Every new area from here on presents as frames, each step its own frame, the next frame coming into the viewpoint — no scrolling down, no scrolling across, anywhere.
