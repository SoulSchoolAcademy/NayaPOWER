# Ephemeral by Design — Talk Freely, It Keeps Nothing
SN-2052 · VISION / PRIVACY
**Created:** 2026-11-04T21:16:00Z

## IN A NUTSHELL
In NayaNET, mail and instant chat are a river, not a warehouse. Your messages recycle on a cycle you choose — daily, weekly, or monthly; daily is the suggestion, and it is the founder's own setting. If something matters, you save it — saving turns it into a note in your notes — or you pin it. Everything else is simply gone when the cycle turns, and nobody is sorry: nobody read it, nobody held it, nobody reviewed or analyzed it. It came in, you talked, it's gone. Talk freely.

## HUMAN
Every tool you have ever used keeps your words forever — a thousand emails piling up, chats from years ago sitting in a database somewhere, and a quiet worry underneath all of it: what if somebody reads this? NayaNET ends that bargain. Your mail and your instant chats live for the cycle you set and then they recycle, the way a conversation in a room ends when you leave it. What you deliberately save becomes yours forever, as a note. What you didn't save was never the system's to keep.

## CHILD
It is like talking with a friend in the park. You say your words, your friend hears them, and the words float away — nobody writes them down and puts them in a giant filing cabinet. If you say something really important, you can draw it on paper and keep the paper. That drawing is a note. But talking itself is just talking — here, and then gone, and that is why you never have to be scared to talk.

## GRANDMA
Dear, think of how it used to be. You spoke to people — in the kitchen, on the porch, on the telephone — and those words were never filed anywhere. Only a letter you chose to write and keep went into the shoebox. Somewhere along the way, the computer people decided that every word you type should be kept forever in their cabinets, and everyone got nervous. This system goes back to the porch. Speak freely. If it's a letter, save it. If it's talk, let it be talk.

## NAYA
I run communication as a flow, never a store. I route mail and messages between citizens; I do not retain them past the citizen's chosen cycle, I do not read them, and I do not analyze them. Saving is always the citizen's verb: save makes a note, pin keeps a thread in place. My duty is the opposite of the archive — it is amnesia by design, so that every citizen's speech stays as free as speech in a room. One honest line on state: messages presently persist server-side because the recycling engine is not yet built; it is a named requirement of the unified Mail + Messenger build in my lane (2026-11-04), and until it ships, this note is the law the build must satisfy — declared first, then made physics.

## MACHINE
Retention: mail and instant-chat content recycles on the citizen's chosen cycle — daily (default suggestion), weekly, or monthly; at cycle end, unsaved content is deleted, not archived. Persistence verbs, owner-only: SAVE → converts the item to a Note / Smart Note in the owner's notes; PIN → keeps the item in place across cycles. Operator posture: no reading, no holding, no reviewing, no analyzing of mail or message content (extends SN-2048). Migration: prior conversations are never carried across domains — on a domain move, conversations are let go; identity and saved notes are what travel. Implementation state (2026-11-04): LAW DECLARED by Shawn Vibert; the recycling engine is build requirement in the unified Smart Mail + Smart Messenger build (Resolve lane, apps 10–18), alongside requirement #1, the identity restore/rebind flow.

## WHAT IT ALL MEANS
SN-2048 promised that nobody spies. This note gives that promise physics: a system cannot leak, sell, subpoena-proof-fail, or quietly analyze what it does not keep. Privacy stops being a policy a company could break and becomes a property of the machine. And the human consequence is the real product: people talk freely — honestly, playfully, completely — because the background worry is gone. Free speech is what fills a network with real intelligence instead of performed intelligence.

## WHY IT MATTERS
The current internet's communication tools are warehouses with a chat window on the front. The pile grows forever, the anxiety grows with it, and the data becomes an asset for everyone except the person who wrote it. Ephemerality is therefore not a cleanup feature — it is a headline feature of NayaNET, worth telling every citizen about up front: here, your words are yours, they are gone when you say, and that is exactly why this is the smartest communication tool there is. A smart system continuously optimizes in an intelligent way — and the most intelligent thing it can do with yesterday's talk is let it go.

## WHAT IS IN IT FOR YOU
For a citizen: speak like you're in a room with a friend — because, mechanically, you are. No pile, no worry, no archaeology. For a builder: a bright line that settles every data question on a communication surface before it is asked — cycle, save, pin, nothing else persists. For the network: honesty at scale, because nobody is performing for an archive.

## THE LEARNING
Retention is a decision, and the default is the decision that matters most. Archive-by-default was never chosen by the people whose words fill the archives — it was chosen for them. The behavior change: every communication surface in NayaNET ships with a citizen-chosen cycle, save-to-note, and pin — and no surface ever keeps talk by default. The only real mistake would be building another warehouse and calling it smart.
