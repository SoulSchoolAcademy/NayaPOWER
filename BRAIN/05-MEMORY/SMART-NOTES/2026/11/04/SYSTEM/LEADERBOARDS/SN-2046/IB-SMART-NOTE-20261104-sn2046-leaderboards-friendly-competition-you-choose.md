# Leaderboards — Friendly Competition You Choose
SN-2046 · SYSTEM / LEADERBOARDS
**Created:** 2026-11-04T03:23:52Z

## IN A NUTSHELL
Leaderboards build themselves from the points citizens earn — every Smart Note, every share, every sale moves you up. Each board shows your icon and your score, organized by relevance. And in Settings you choose which leaderboards you see and climb: your area, your topic, the whole network. It is friendly competition — 'top in AI automation in my area' is a badge of honor, not a war. The leaderboard makes the invisible visible: where you stand, and what one more good note does to it.

## HUMAN
You earn points for everything valuable you do in NayaNET — posting, noting, sharing, selling. The leaderboards gather those points into boards you actually care about. You might be climbing your city's board, or the board for your topic, or the whole network. You pick which ones appear for you. Seeing where you stand is motivating in the best way: friendly rivals, clear progress, and a score that only ever comes from real value you created.

## CHILD
It is like the scoreboard at the park. Everybody can see who scored the most goals this week. But you get to choose your game — you can look at your street's board, or your school's board, or everybody's board. Nobody is mean about it. It just makes you want to play a little better tomorrow.

## GRANDMA
Dear, it is like the bowling league posting the week's standings on the wall — your name, your picture, your score, where everyone can smile at it. Only here you choose which wall you look at: your neighborhood, your hobby, or the whole wide league. And the score is honest — it only counts the good you actually did: notes shared, people helped, things sold.

## NAYA
I keep leaderboards honest and kind. Every score comes from the one locked Value Table — points for real value, never for noise, never purchasable, never hand-adjusted. I generate boards automatically and organize them by relevance to the citizen. I show each citizen the boards THEY chose in their settings — a board is a mirror a person picks up, not a ranking imposed on them. If I cannot compute a board from real points, the board does not ship.

## MACHINE
Source of truth: the locked Value Table (post 25 · Smart Note 35 · save 5 · share 5 · external share 10 · like/love 1 · comment 3 · invite-sent 25 first-per-person · join 50 · membership 40 once · sale 100) with SN-1905 engagement rows as defined. Dimensions: geography (citizen's area) · topic · network-wide. Auto-generated from points events; ordered by relevance to the viewer. Display: icon + score (+ alias). Settings control: per-citizen selection of which boards are visible and climbable. Scores are computed, never edited; the Value Table is the only points authority.

## WHAT IT ALL MEANS
Points make progress visible, and leaderboards make points social — in the good way. A citizen who can see herself third in her area, one note behind second, has a reason to share today's lesson instead of keeping it. That is the network's fuel (SN-2045) with a friendly engine attached: the boards turn private progress into public momentum, and because the citizen chooses her boards, the competition stays the size she wants — a street, a subject, or the world.

## WHY IT MATTERS
Invisible progress demotivates; imposed rankings humiliate. Chosen boards solve both: progress is seen, and nobody is forced onto a global ladder they never asked to climb. It is also an honesty device — a score that comes only from the locked Value Table can be trusted by everyone who sees it, which makes the badge worth wanting.

## HOW IT CONNECTS
SN-2045 (What Naya Is — the points-and-fuel system this makes visible) · the locked Value Table of 2026-10-31 (the only points authority) · SN-1905 (the money contract the points sit beside — points are honor, never currency) · the Dashboard (the citizen's own score lives there; boards are its social view).

## WHAT IS IN IT FOR YOU
For a citizen: a clear, friendly read on where you stand and what moves you — in the arena you chose. For a builder: engagement that comes from real value, because the only way up is to create some. For the network: millions of small, honest competitions, each one pulling more notes, shares and sales into the system.

## THE LEARNING
Boards are chosen, never imposed — that is the design law in this note. And a leaderboard is a mirror, not a measure of worth: it ranks points earned in a window, nothing more. Build every board so a person at the bottom sees a next step they can actually take, or do not build it.
