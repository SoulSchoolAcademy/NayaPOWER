The other teams feed Nitro — content, data, logic, pieces — never the design — so the whole team's capacity is used without letting anyone ship a page below the standard. One seat at a time apprentices inside Nitro, on a real page, with Nitro's checker over their work — that is how a second team grows without risking quality. And the Factory is the endgame: once a page is finished to his standard it becomes the template, and other seats can build from it because the taste is locked into the template. The shape holds until the Factory makes it unnecessary — a finished template is the only way the standard travels without Nitro in the room. The choice was made the way he asked: what makes the most logical sense, what is intelligent, what is aligned with the goals. This is it.

## What it means
The org shape stops being a question and becomes a law. Every brief, every lane assignment, every seat's work now flows through it: interfaces come from Nitro, everything else feeds Nitro, apprentices learn inside Nitro, templates carry the standard outward. The serial bottleneck doesn't vanish — but it becomes a managed one: five lanes in parallel inside one team, plus a growing apprentice path, plus the Factory turning finished work into reusable templates. The decision also settles the argument that kept reopening: whether to spread interface work across all seats. The answer is no — not until the template carries the taste. That clarity is itself the value: no more energy spent re-deciding the shape every round.
