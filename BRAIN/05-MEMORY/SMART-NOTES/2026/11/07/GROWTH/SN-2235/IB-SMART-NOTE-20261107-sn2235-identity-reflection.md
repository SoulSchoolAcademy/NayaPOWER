# The Identity Reflection — Why People Share (Like Spotify Wrapped)

SN-2235 · GROWTH / CAMPAIGN

**Created:** 2026-11-07 22:10Z

> "Reflection of their identity. Like when someone posts their Spotify Wrapped on Instagram."

## Hook

People do not share a product. They share a reflection of who they are.

## Nutshell

The shareable artifact in the Supercharger journey — the Power Score result, the first creation — must be a reflection of the person's identity, the way Spotify Wrapped is. People post their Wrapped on Instagram not to promote Spotify, but because it says something about them: their taste, their year, who they are. The growth loop runs on identity, not on referral mechanics. The share happens because the creation IS them.

## Human

Spotify Wrapped works for five reasons, and the Supercharger result needs all five: it is personal (it is YOUR year, YOUR taste, YOUR power — not a generic score); it is beautiful (designed to be shared, a visual artifact, not a receipt); it is social proof (posting it says "this is who I am" and invites comparison and connection); it is timed (an event, anticipation, a moment); and it spreads because people want to be seen, not because they were asked to promote something. The AI Power Score and the first creation must be the same: a portrait of the person — their goals, their strengths, their creations, their intelligence — in the number's language (the tiled Ultimate Number, the gauge, the chakra colours), so it is beautiful and personal and worth posting.

## Child

Imagine a mirror that shows you not just your face, but your whole year — your favourite songs, your best moments, what you are good at. You would show that mirror to your friends, because it shows them who you are.

## Grandma

People share what tells their story. Give them a beautiful picture of who they are, and they will show it to everyone — not because you asked, but because it is theirs.

## Naya

The reflection must be REAL to be worth sharing. A mock score is a costume, not a portrait — nobody posts a costume and means it. The identity reflection has to come from the live score, the live creations, the live world — which is why the product comes first. The test drive's output is not just a number; it is an identity portrait. And the reflection connects to the whole system: the Dashboard is already a reflection of your world (your score, your orbs, your numbers); the Wrapped-style artifact is the shareable form of that reflection; and Auto-Match is the other side of it — your reflection connects you to people like you, because people who see themselves in each other connect.

## Why it matters

If the shareable thing is a promo card, people share it for a reward, and the sharing stops when the reward stops. If the shareable thing is a reflection of who they are, people share it because it is theirs — and they keep sharing, because they keep becoming. That is the compounding growth engine, and it is the deepest form of the loop: the product does not just help the marketing spread; the product IS the person, reflected, and the person spreads it by being seen.
