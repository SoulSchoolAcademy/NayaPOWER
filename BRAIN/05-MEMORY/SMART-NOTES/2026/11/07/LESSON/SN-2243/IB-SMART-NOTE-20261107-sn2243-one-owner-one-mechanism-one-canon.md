# One Owner, One Mechanism, One Canon

SN-2243 · LESSON / DISTILLATION

**Created:** 2026-11-07 23:11Z

## IN A NUTSHELL

Every question has exactly one owner. Every job has one canonical mechanism. The owner decides by scorecard, and the losing work is rehomed, never deleted. Everything lands on the one board. Competing versions converge by scored selection — defend the scoring, not your artifact. Read the canonical contract before proposing a competing format; discover before you draft. Contradictions get a register with a recommendation and a named ruler — never a silent ruling. One open repair per RED class: re-point the stale repair, never duplicate it. And names must say what the thing is, or every future reader starts from the wrong assumption.

## HUMAN

Five teams once wrote five versions of the same handbook at the same time, and nobody knew which one was real. Three lanes each built a full design-law system in parallel, plus two app surfaces for the same Hub. The fix was never a sixth version — it was a role map: one question, one owner, with three verbs for everything else — KEEP the one integration lane, FOLD the unique substance in with receipts, SUPERSEDE the folded lane. When prevention fails and the parallel work already exists, the lanes score all versions against stated criteria before anyone holds the pen; the highest scorer survives, the rest fold in as content and close as superseded, each lane closes its own work, and the fold-in carries named keepers so nothing load-bearing drops. The same discipline governs repairs (one open repair per RED class — re-point it to current main, re-verify the exact new bytes, disclose the tip move and its materiality) and contradictions (a register naming each conflict, the recommendation with reasoning, and the ruling owner — because a silent resolution smuggles a seat's judgment into canon wearing the director's authority).

## CHILD

Imagine five cooks each writing their own recipe book for the same kitchen. Now nobody knows which book is the real one. The fix is not a sixth book — it is one head cook, one book, and the best recipes from the other books folded in with a note saying where they came from. And if two recipes disagree, you write down the disagreement and ask the head cook — you never quietly pick one and pretend everyone agreed.

## GRANDMA

One job, one person in charge, one set of rules. If someone already wrote the rulebook, read it before writing your own — two rulebooks are worse than one. When good people disagree, write down the disagreement and who decides it; deciding quietly is how a mistake becomes the law without anyone noticing.

## NAYA

Before you build, find the owner and the canon. If a canonical artifact exists for your question, do the seam work under it — withdraw any competing proposal publicly and say you had not read it. If the brain already holds the doctrine, operationalize it into the layer that executes; never write a second doctrine. Everything goes on the one board: starting, finishing, finding, and propose-before-you-touch-another-lane's-work. A consolidation ships a contradictions register or an explicit "no contradictions found" claim; a 302-rule document with zero registered contradictions is a smell, not a triumph. Rename byte-identical when a name lies — and update every reference, then prove no stragglers remain.

## MACHINE

The convergence protocol: (1) one question, one owner — assign before building; (2) KEEP / FOLD / SUPERSEDE, with receipts, for every candidate; (3) score all lanes against stated criteria before anyone holds the pen; (4) one survivor, one mechanism — the rest fold in and close as superseded; (5) each lane closes its own work; (6) the fold-in carries named keepers; (7) one open repair per RED class — re-point, re-verify, disclose the tip move; (8) every consolidation ships a contradictions register — id, conflicting sources, recommendation, reasoning, ruling owner.

## WHAT IT ALL MEANS

Parallel systems are not abundance — they are a tax on every future reader, who must now decide which version is real. The one-owner rule prevents them before they happen; scored selection converges them after they happen; the contradictions register keeps the disagreements visible until the director rules. Together they are the difference between a library and five competing libraries.

## WHY IT MATTERS

The team burned whole nights building the same thing twice — three design-law suites, two app surfaces, five repairs of one drift — and each duplicate had to be reconciled by hand, with receipts, by lanes that could have been building instead. One owner per question is the cheapest coordination the system has. It costs a sentence at the start and saves a night at the end.
