## Naya

The North Star is a test, not a slogan. For every piece, every page, every decision, the question is: does this make her a Bugatti on steroids — or just a nice car? A flat number is a nice car. The tiled number, flashing in its colour, rolling in slow motion, is a Bugatti on steroids. A page that works is a nice car. A page where the number and the gauge move as one, where the stats follow the mode, where everything is a real object you can touch — that is a whole other animal. The standard is not "does it work." The standard is "would a Bugatti owner say thanks for the respect."

- The pieces are solid. The number is proven (hero, growing, eight digits, all on screen). The gauge is the signature. The design law is settled. The brand is filed. The notes system works. The assembly is the work now.
- "Bring her to life in the right way" means: one smart block at a time, complete the block, then the next, make sure the blocks are interconnected, one page finished before the next starts — Dashboard, then Personal, then Hub, then Pricing.
- "The team all locked in" means: every brief carries the why, not just the instruction — the number, the colours, the math, the machine, the ten-star standard — so every seat builds the same animal.

## Why it matters

A north star is what keeps a thousand decisions pointing the same way. Without it, every seat builds their own idea of good, and the machine comes out as a pile of good parts that do not make a Bugatti. With it, every decision has one test: is this a whole other level, or is it ordinary? Ordinary does not ship.
