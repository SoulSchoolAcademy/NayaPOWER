## Hook

The number did not come from imagination. It came from the Dashboard's own buttons, measured live, pixel by pixel.

## Nutshell

The number is built from the Dashboard button anatomy, measured in a real browser from nayanet.app/dashboard (2026-11-07 20:19–20:28Z). Outside to inside: a 1–2px light rim, brightest on the top arc (2px violet #a06bff on featured buttons, 1.5–2px silver-grey on neutral ones) · a black gradient face, top #171720, mid #0f0f15, bottom #020203 — the obsidian black, always visible · a 1px white-grey highlight on the top arc · white bold content, with a soft lavender-grey halo · a soft drop shadow plus a diffused coloured halo outside the rim. The colour lives in the rim, the cap, or a small chip — never on the glyph itself. The number stays white.

## Human

Shawn's rule is harvest first: take what already exists and is already beautiful, and build from it. The Dashboard's buttons were already the language. The number just speaks it.

## Child

## Grandma

You don't invent a new kind of button when the ones you have are already beautiful. You measure the beautiful ones, and you make the new thing the same way.

## Naya

Every builder brief names its harvest sources — the exact file, the exact section, the exact line. The design-law gate fails any build whose elements do not trace to the piece index or a named harvest source. The anatomy is the proof: the number traces to the Dashboard, pixel by pixel.

## What it means

- The number's rim, face, highlight, halo and shadow are the Dashboard's, copied verbatim — not redesigned.
- No gold on the Dashboard components seen. The accent is violet #a06bff, silver-grey, and per-section colours.
- The Hub could not be studied — it hangs (the known feed hang). The Dashboard anatomy is the measured one.

## Why it matters

A number invented from scratch would have looked like a stranger on the page. A number harvested from the page's own buttons looks like it was always there. That is why Shawn said it turned out better than he envisioned — it belongs.
