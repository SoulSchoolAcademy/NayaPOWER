## Hook

Each number has its own colour. As the count rolls, the tile changes colour with the passing digit — and flashes, so you see it.

## Nutshell

The colours are the chakras: 1 red · 2 orange · 3 gold · 4 emerald · 5 cyan · 6 indigo · 7 royal purple · 8 lavender · 9 hot pink (the crown chakra) · 0 white. All the richest colours. The colour lives on the OUTSIDE — the rim, the cap, the halo — and the inside stays obsidian black. As the count rolls, the tile takes the colour of each passing digit (`setReel`, colour-sync), and it flashes (`tileFlash`, brightness 2.1 → 1 over .35s, throttled to at least 120ms between flashes) so each passing colour reads without strobing. When the reel lands, the tile holds the colour of its final digit.

## Grandma

Each number glows in its own colour — red for one, gold for three, purple for seven, pink for nine. As the number counts up, the glow changes with it, so you can see the colours passing by before it settles.

## Naya

Colour-sync is the number's identity made visible. The tile does not wear one colour — it wears the colour of the number passing through it, and then the colour of the number it lands on. The flash is the pulse: bright enough to read, throttled enough not to strobe.

- 1 red, 2 orange, 3 gold, 4 emerald, 5 cyan, 6 indigo, 7 royal purple, 8 lavender, 9 hot pink, 0 white.
- Colour on the outside (rim, cap, halo). Inside stays obsidian black.

Without the colour, the number is just a count. With it, the number has an identity — and the identity is what makes it beautiful, and what makes it ours.
