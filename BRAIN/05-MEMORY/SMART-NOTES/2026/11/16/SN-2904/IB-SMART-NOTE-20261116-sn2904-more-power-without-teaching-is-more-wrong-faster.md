# More Power Without Teaching Is More Wrong, Faster

SN-2904 · LESSON / INTELLIGENCE

**Created:** 2026-11-16 01:02Z

**Kinds:** Multiplication · Craft

## Lesson

Pouring more and more power in does not make a thing more intelligent — it makes it do more of whatever it was already doing, right or wrong. You have to teach it to do things right first: efficient and effective, and verified. The gap is not missing knowledge. Humanity already possesses the knowledge to prevent disease, educate children, improve agriculture and make organizations effective — and it fails to reach the person who needs it, at the moment they need it. The problem is missing connection, context, organization, judgment and execution. Use what we have right; discover what we do not know second.

## What changes now

1. Before more power, we ask: is it doing the right thing right? If not, power waits.
2. We measure connection and delivery — did the knowledge reach the person who needed it, when they needed it — alongside discovery.
3. Efficiency and effectiveness are taught first, scale second.
