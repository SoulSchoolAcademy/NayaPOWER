## Why it matters

If the system shared location, the whole promise would break: citizens tell the system where they are because they trust it won't tell anyone. The moment that trust breaks, the demographics go unanswered, Auto-Match goes blind, and the feed stops finding people like you. "Never shares" has to be a law, not a setting — a setting can be toggled, a law cannot. And public-or-private is the same law at the page level: the citizen decides who the page is for, and the system obeys.
