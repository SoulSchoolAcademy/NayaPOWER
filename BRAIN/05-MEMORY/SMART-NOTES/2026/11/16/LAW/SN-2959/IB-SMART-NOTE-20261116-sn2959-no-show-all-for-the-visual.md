## What it means

- **NO SHOW-ALL FOR THE VISUAL:** the visual agreement deck never gets a read-it-all-at-once / show-all option. One slide at a time, always.
- **READ-IT-ALL IS FOR THE TEXT:** the text / letter may offer read-it-all-at-once. Text is read, not watched.
- **THE END BUTTONS:** bigger, clearer, more impressive presentation, so it is clear which one is which.
- **THE DECK IS A DECK:** the slideshow ends at the last image and never continues sideways into the scroll-down agreement (the standing Visual Collective Agreement rule).

## Why it matters

A show-all option on the visual would be the easiest button in the world to add — and it would quietly destroy the deck. The discipline is in refusing it: the deck's power is the unfolding, and the unfolding only exists one slide at a time. The text/video split is the principle: give the citizen the whole thing where the whole thing is the experience (text), and never where the sequence IS the experience (video).

## What changes now

1. The visual agreement deck never gets a show-all / read-it-all-at-once option.
2. The text / letter may offer read-it-all.
3. The two end buttons get a bigger, clearer, more impressive presentation.
4. The combined walk round carries all three (bigger/clearer deck-end buttons + back button on every view + no show-all for the visual).
