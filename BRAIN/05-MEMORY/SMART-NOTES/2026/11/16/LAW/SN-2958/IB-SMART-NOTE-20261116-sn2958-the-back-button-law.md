## Human

A page with no way back is a door that only opens one way: the citizen taps the wrong end button, or wants to compare the two, and is stuck. Going back and forth simply is not a convenience — it is the system working. The back button is the citizen's freedom to change their mind, and it costs nothing: elegant, beautiful, clean, blocking nothing. "Most of the pages" is his honesty — a page that truly has nowhere to go back to doesn't need one — but the default is that it is there, and a view you entered is a view you can leave.

## Child

Imagine every room in your house has a door back to the hallway! You walk into a room to look around, and you can always walk back out again. That's the back button — a friendly little door that is always there, so you never get stuck in a room!

## Grandma

It's like this, love — every page has a little way back, like a door you came in through. You tap into something to look at it, and you can always tap back to where you were — nice and simple, and it never gets in the way of what you're looking at.

## Naya

A system always has a back button on all — or most — pages, so you can always go back and forth simply. Elegant, beautiful, clean, blocking nothing.

## What it means

- **ALWAYS THERE:** every page — all pages, or most pages — carries a back button, so a user can always go back and forth simply.
- **ELEGANT, NOT BLOCKING:** the button is beautiful and clean and blocks nothing on the page.
- **THE CASE THAT NAMED IT:** after either end button on the visual agreement, the citizen can go back and try the other one.
- **THE PROOF CASE:** the same day, Shawn clicked into the collective agreement and couldn't go back (SN-2971) — the law exists because the failure is real.

## Why it matters

Without the law, every new view is a gamble: does this one let me back out? The citizen shouldn't have to wonder. The back button is the cheapest freedom in the system — one control, always in the same spirit — and it is what makes trying things safe. A citizen who can always go back will always try the next thing; a citizen who got stuck once will hesitate forever.

## What changes now

1. Every page — all, or most — carries a back button: elegant, beautiful, clean, blocking nothing.
2. After either end button on the visual agreement, the citizen can go back and try the other one.
3. The combined walk round carries the law (back button on every view).
4. Any view a citizen can enter, a citizen can leave — the back button is checked, not assumed (SN-2971).
