## Naya

## What it means

- **THE THREE THINGS:** a feed consists of exactly Naya Notes, Smart Notes, and posts. Nothing else. Anything that is none of the three does not belong on a feed.
- **THE NOTE LIVES WHERE THE WORK IS:** Naya makes notes for you on the feed; if that feed is shared with someone on your team, they see the note on that feed.
- **A FEED CAN BE A PROJECT:** a business can be a project, a thing can be a project, or a project can be its own separate feed — a project IS a world you add. No separate project-feed concept.
- **THE FILTERS:** Smart Tabs are the filters; the point is a super-organized system — the smartest, most intelligent way to keep things in line, organized, and findable. Show only Smart Notes / only Naya Notes / only posts.
- **ONE ROW, NOT TWO:** under the tabs, one chip row starting with the type chips All / Smart Notes / Naya Notes / Posts, then the topic chips. The existing "All" filter is the first chip of that row, not a separate control.
- **ACTIONS ARE NOT CONTENT:** the composer's Message / Mail / Create world are actions, not feed content.

## Why it matters

Without the law, every new card type finds its way onto the feed and the feed stops being a feed — it becomes a drawer. Three things is a test any builder can run in a glance: is this a Naya Note, a Smart Note, or a post? If not, it does not go on the feed. The project clause kills a whole category of future work: no separate project-feed system to build, no second feed concept to maintain — a project is a world, a world has a feed, done. And the one row kills the second-control defect at the root: "All" was a filter pretending to be a tab, and the glow was the symptom. One row, kinds first, topics after — the shape is the law made visible.
