## Why it matters

Free forever is the promise — "free to join, forever" is in the definition itself. A walk that strands free citizens breaks the promise at the exact moment it should keep it: the end, when they have built their world and are ready to live in it. Hub-first also fixes the order of the whole business: the citizen is in first, and paying is an upgrade to a world they already inhabit, not a toll at the gate. That is the difference between a system that hires everyone and a system that charges admission.
