## Human

The three ways are not three tiers — the name-only entry is not a lesser path for people who don't care, and the visual is not a lesser path for people who don't read. Each is complete on its own terms: put your name in and the name IS the agreement; read the text and you know what you agree to; go through the visual and you have seen it, then come back and put your name in. "We got you" is the sentence that holds it together — whatever the citizen wants, information or none, the system has a way in for them, and every way is done to the same standard. No way in is a shortcut, no way in is a test, no way in is second-class.

## What it means

- **THE THREE WAYS:** (1) agree by entering — put your name in, the name IS the agreement; (2) read it — the text view; (3) the visual presentation, then back to put your name in.
- **THE STANDARD:** every way is extraordinary — no way in is a shortcut, a test, or second-class.
- **THE CHOICE:** lots of information if you want it, or just go in if you don't — the citizen chooses, the system provides.
- **THE BUILD:** the Visual Collective Agreement round already builds all three — two buttons on the opening page, both ending at the login page, plus the direct name entry on the login itself. No amendment was needed.

## Why it matters

The agreement is the first thing a citizen meets, and the first thing is the whole system in miniature. If the only way in is the long read, the system says: we value our words over your time. If the only way in is the name, it says: we value speed over your understanding. Three ways, each extraordinary, say the true thing: the system is built for you, however you want to come in. That is the front door as a promise, not a funnel.

## What changes now

1. The front door keeps all three ways in — name entry, text view, visual presentation — each complete, each extraordinary.
2. The opening page offers the choice with two buttons; the login itself carries the direct entry.
3. Both views end at the login page to put in the name — the agreement first, the name after.
4. No way in is ever reduced to a shortcut or a test — the standard is the same on all three.
