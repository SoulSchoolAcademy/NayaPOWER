## What it means

- **THE FIT:** the whole month fits the screen perfectly, exactly — no scroll, no overflow, no clipped cell; day cells are the pricing chart's blocks (hover lights up alive in the day's color).
- **THE DAY:** click a day → that day's world: stats, feeds, Intel Today, weather, news, personal, business, space, the daily intelligence report; go back any day and see the highlights, what was learned.
- **THE WISDOM:** every day has daily wisdom, by default — highest-ranked smart note of the day becomes the next day's wisdom; if not, the truest wisdom from everything learned; a new one every day.
- **THE TWO THINGS:** Intel Today (the day's smart-note highlights) and the Daily Intelligence Report (the day's intelligence report) are two different things, both on the day.
- **THE DAILIES:** optional dailies (horoscope, Chinese horoscope, Bible quote) are DEFAULT OFF — citizen turns one on in settings; Naya recommends based on who they are.
- **THE RULE:** operate from the day, not the Hub — one button opens the day's inner menu; scroll to what you want and it unfolds; back any day/week/month.
- **THE TEACHING:** the day teaches — send people to NayaNET, they book a time with you, and if they join you get paid, in a network that's truly private, nobody listening.
