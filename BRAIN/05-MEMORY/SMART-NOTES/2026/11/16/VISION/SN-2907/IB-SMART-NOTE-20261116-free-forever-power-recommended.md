## Human

"You belong even if you join for free" removes the fear; "Power is what we recommend, and it's way more powerful" gives the ambition. Say only the first half and the system sounds like a charity; say only the second and it sounds like a sales page. The founders aside works the same way: a "hey, just so you know" carries more trust than a banner, because it does not ask for a decision — it offers a fact and walks on.
