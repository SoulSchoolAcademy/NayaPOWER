# Children Connect Automatically — and We Never Look

SN-2924 · LESSON / INTELLIGENCE

**Created:** 2026-11-16 02:10Z

**Kinds:** Honor · Knowledge

## Lesson

Children connect with friends on whatever topic — little groupings, audio call, chat, share about the thing, post about the subject they're into — and the system automatically puts them together; they don't have to try and find each other. They see somebody — "I'm X% connected to me, I'm going to connect with them, I'm going to say hi." Always have a breakdown: what percentage, in what area — stats first, so they can look deeper if they want. Transparency about everything is the best way. We learn about them to give better service, and we respect them — we don't read their mail, their messages, we don't listen to their conversations or watch their video. We just let them be.

## What changes now

1. The match always shows its breakdown — what percentage, in what area. That rides the Smart Match real-calibration check (2026-11-17).
2. Respect is the law: no reading mail, no listening, no watching — ever.
