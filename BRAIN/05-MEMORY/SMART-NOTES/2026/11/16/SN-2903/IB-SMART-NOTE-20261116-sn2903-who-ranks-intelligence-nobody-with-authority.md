# Who Ranks Intelligence? Nobody With Authority

SN-2903 · LESSON / INTELLIGENCE

**Created:** 2026-11-16 01:02Z

**Kinds:** Craft · Honor

## Lesson

Nobody with authority determines what intelligence is or at what level an AI sits. Research groups, benchmark creators, laboratories and universities measure what they can measure — mathematics, programming, language, tool use — which is capacity, not intelligence. A model can solve hard problems and still misunderstand a plain human instruction, or fail to see that the software should not be deployed. No single benchmark establishes ultimate intelligence. The scorecard that matters: understand the real problem; recognize what matters; distinguish truth from uncertainty; respect life and human authority; choose efficient action; deliver useful results; verify outcomes independently; learn from mistakes; remember and transfer the learning. The measure: did the intelligence make reality better, safely and verifiably?

## What changes now

1. We never cite a benchmark rank as intelligence — we cite the scorecard above.
2. Every evaluation names what was verified and what remains uncertain.
3. A system that cannot verify its own outcome has not demonstrated intelligence, whatever it scored.
