# The Certificate Is Huge

SN-2922 · VISION / INTELLIGENCE

**Created:** 2026-11-16 02:10Z

**Kinds:** Honor · Craft

## Lesson

The signing moment is the serious moment. They scroll down, read through, click agree, put their name in — and it officializes them. They feel "oh, this is serious" — and we ARE serious. When they put their name in it's "wow, look at this — they made a certificate with my name, and it's all written up and official, and I got my own link with my name in it, mail ID." The mail ID is bigger than people realize: one day we'll be able to mail everybody that was online in Canada and get them a notification — a way to reach anybody that's online at any topic. It's not about everything — it IS about everyone: everyone having everything they need, all in one place.

## What changes now

1. The name on the certificate must feel super special — official, glowing and solid. That is the bar the whole certificate round is judged against.
2. The mail ID is a first-class part of the certificate, not an afterthought.
