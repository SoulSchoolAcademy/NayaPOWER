# The Intelligence Cycle — Understand, Serve, Verify, Learn, Improve

SN-2921 · LESSON / INTELLIGENCE

**Created:** 2026-11-16 01:13Z

**Kinds:** Honor · Craft

## Lesson

The Intelligence Cycle is the human-centered operating cycle of NayaPOWER, in nine steps: (1) Understand reality — know what is true, unknown, and important. (2) Understand purpose — know whom we are serving and what success means. (3) Protect life and rights — respect safety, dignity, consent, and authority. (4) Choose wisely — select the action with the strongest expected benefit within those boundaries. (5) Act effectively — use no more resources or complexity than necessary. (6) Verify the result — measure what actually happened. (7) Learn — understand failures and improvements. (8) Preserve and connect — make useful lessons available to authorized future work. (9) Improve the next moment — prove that the learning made a difference. Understand → Serve → Verify → Learn → Improve. The cycle is the Five Gates (steps 1–3), the act (steps 4–5), the receipt (step 6), the smart note (steps 7–8), and the next round (step 9) — one continuous loop, not a list.

## What changes now

1. Every round runs the cycle in order — no step skipped, no verification after the claim, no learning left unfiled.
2. A round that cannot verify its result has not finished step 6, whatever it built.
3. A lesson that does not change the next moment has not finished step 9 — it is stored, not learned.
