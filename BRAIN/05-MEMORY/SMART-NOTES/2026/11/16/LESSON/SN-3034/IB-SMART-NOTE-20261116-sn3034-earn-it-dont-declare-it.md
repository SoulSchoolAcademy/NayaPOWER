The entrance is the first thing a stranger meets, and "trust us" from a stranger is a reason to leave, not to stay. The 10-star copy works because it never asks for trust — it shows the reasons, one line at a time, and ends by saying we'd rather earn it than ask for it.
2. The behavior laws are the same rule turned inward. Every question put to him that has an obvious answer, every "should I make it better", every "cool" in place of action — each one spends his attention and returns nothing. His attention is the scarcest resource in the system; the machine's job is to spend it only on the calls that are genuinely his.

Plain words are not a simplification of the law; they are the law, stated so the person it protects can actually read it.
