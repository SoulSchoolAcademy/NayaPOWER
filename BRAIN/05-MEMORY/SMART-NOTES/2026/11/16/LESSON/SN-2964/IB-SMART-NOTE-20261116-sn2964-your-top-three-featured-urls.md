## Human

The column today reads like our memberships — but it is the citizen's column, not ours. It is where they feature what matters to them: their profiles, their sites, their top three. A visitor who likes what they see should be one click from the citizen's world elsewhere. Featuring is the word: the citizen features, the page shows, the visitor goes. Three is the number because three is a choice — one is a link, three is a top three, and a top three is a statement about who you are.

## What it means

- **FEATURED, NOT OURS:** the side column (today: Naya Academy, AI Masterclass, Fast Action Club) holds featured links. Those are memberships — but they don't have to be those.
- **YOUR TOP THREE:** Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, your own sites — so visitors go straight to your profiles.
- **THE MECHANISM IS HIS CALL:** right-click, a little edit control, or in Settings — he decides how you edit them.
- **THE NUMBER IS THREE:** a top three, not a link farm.

## Why it matters

A page that only points inward is a dead end; a page that points outward to the citizen's own world is a doorway. The citizen's profiles elsewhere are part of who they are — the page should send visitors there, not hold them hostage. And the column being ours today is the mistake: it tells the visitor about us when it should be telling them about the citizen.
