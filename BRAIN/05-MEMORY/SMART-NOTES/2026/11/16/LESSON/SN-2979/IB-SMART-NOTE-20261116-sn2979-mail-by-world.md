## What it means

- **SEPARATED BY WORLD:** Personal mail, Business mail, Space mail — the citizen always sees where the mail came from.
- **REACHABLE FROM THE SIDEBARS:** mail opens from any of those places — Personal, Business, Space.
- **THE FRONT DOOR SHOWS ITS OWN WORLD FIRST:** opening mail from a page shows that world's mail first — never another world's mail on top.
- **UNIVERSAL FROM THE HUB:** from the Hub's mail, the citizen can select any world and see its mail — one mail, with a world selector.
- **SAME FOR INSTANT MESSAGING:** exactly the same way — separated by world at the front door, universal from the Hub.

## Why it matters

Mixed mail is lost mail. A business letter sitting on top of a personal inbox, or a space message buried under personal mail, is a citizen missing what matters in the world it belongs to. Separating by world at the front door means the citizen never has to sort, filter, or wonder — the page they opened already did the sorting. And the Hub's world selector means the separation never becomes a wall: one mail, every world reachable, the citizen choosing which world to look at instead of the system choosing for them. This is the mail law the Smart Mail round builds to (One List `smart-mail-im-1010`, due 2026-11-17): separate where it came from, universal where you choose.
