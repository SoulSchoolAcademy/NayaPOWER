## Why it matters

Layer-by-layer judging is how broken wholes ship: each seat passes its own layer, nobody owns the whole, and the citizen meets the project at its weakest layer — the left-hung text, the one-sentence summary, the missing unfold — and judges all of it by that. His eye is the only whole-project check the machine has had, and it keeps catching what the layers missed: the letter's picture fixed while the page stayed broken, the legal pages summarized while the presentation stayed plain. The law moves the whole-project verdict into the round itself: a round is done when every layer is great, not when its layer passes.
