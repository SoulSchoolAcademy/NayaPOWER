## Human

Points are authority — more points, more post weight — but a number alone tells the citizen nothing about where they stand. The level is the story of the number: what you are called, how far up the bar you are, what the next level gets you. The name changing with the level makes the progress visible in the one place the citizen always looks: under their own name. And the rewards, stated plainly, are what make the next level worth reaching for. Five stars, you're a five star member; ten stars, a ten star member — the name IS the level.

## Why it matters

A points number without a level is a scoreboard nobody reads; a level without rewards is a title nobody wants. Together they are the reason to come back: the name you carry, the bar you climb, the thing you get at the next step.
