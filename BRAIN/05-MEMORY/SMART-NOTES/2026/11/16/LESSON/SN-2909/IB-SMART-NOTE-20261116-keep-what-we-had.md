## Human

A rebuild is a bet that the new thing will be better than the old thing. When the old thing is already nailed — the text, the buttons, the presentation, the thing he loved — the bet is irrational: the upside is a marginal improvement and the downside is losing the thing he loved. Every redo in our history has proven the downside real: the new build comes back flat, unelevated, boring, terrible text, because a new build re-invents a hundred small decisions the old build had already settled. The intelligent move is addition, not recreation: take the beautiful thing, add the images, make it look like they were always meant to be there. And when trust is spent — "we're not going to have you build anything" — the only way to earn it back is a round that proves, by diff, that nothing else moved. The 21:46Z add-images round is that proof, and it is now the pattern for every future touch.

- **THE SIGNATURE:** the unfolding with the buttons and the presentation just like the pricing page was great, loved, to be locked in as a signature piece.

## Why it matters

This is the most expensive lesson of the evening: a coordinator PASS (66/66 beats, 20 pictures byte-identical) that Shawn rejected outright — because a pass on the wrong thing is not a pass. The machine had measured everything except the one thing that mattered: was this what he asked for? He asked for images added to a beautiful thing. We gave him a new system with images in it. The gap between those two sentences is the whole lesson — and the reason the stop-build order landed.

## What changes now

1. No round rebuilds a page he called beautiful. The brief says ADD, and the diff must prove only additions.
2. Every "add" round's diff is checked for 0 deletions / 0 text or button changes before it is called a pass.
3. A stop-build order is obeyed literally — no new build is dispatched until he re-authorizes it, for that presentation, in his words.
4. The 21:46Z add-images round is the template: image insertions + one minimal frame-CSS block, nothing else.
