## Why it matters

1. The front door is the first thing a citizen meets, and the first thing is the whole system in miniature — a door that only opens for paying citizens is not a front door.
2. Getting the feeds' home wrong — selections instead of the day — would have buried the choice where it can't be made, and put the news where nobody looks for the day's world.
3. A system that calls a person's passion a "space" has already misunderstood them — and a system that misunderstands what you love cannot match you with people who love it too.
4. A quota wall looks like a system failure and is actually a plan limit — but a limit nobody counts is a door that closes without warning, right after a PASS.
5. Adoption is what turns the talk into behaviour: the code becomes the check on every design, the cycle becomes the order of every round, and the next Naya starts from the thinking, not from the notes.
