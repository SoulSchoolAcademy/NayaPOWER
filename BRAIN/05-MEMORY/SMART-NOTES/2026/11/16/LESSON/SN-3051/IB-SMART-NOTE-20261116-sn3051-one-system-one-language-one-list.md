## Why it matters

1. Without the cycle as the clock, the next cadence discussion drifts back to minutes — and the machine optimizes for waking less instead of finishing more.
2. Without the keep-it-exactly rule, the next round "fixes" the end by redesigning it — and the most beautiful elegant piece the team did gets iterated away.
3. Without one navigation system, each page round invents its own navigation, and the citizen has to relearn how to move every time they change pages.
4. Without the Hub hero fixed, the public front door keeps greeting visitors with an internal digest label and a system stamp — the front door talking like a back office.
5. Without the thinking rule, any screenshot becomes a spec and any passing list becomes law, and the system gets built to an accident of what happened to be on screen.
6. Without one language, every round rebuilds its page as its own little system, and the whole slowly becomes many systems wearing one name.
7. Without carrying his corrections, the next seat builds to the proposal instead of the corrected list — Pricing on the wrong side, a duplicate challenge, an unused report.
8. Without the better-home rule, the sidebar becomes a junk drawer — a thing that lives somewhere better also sitting in the sidebar.
9. Without the name-as-deed treatment, the most meaningful act in the whole walk — claiming their world — feels like filling in a form.
10. Without the ratification discipline, a playback awaiting his yes gets recorded as official, and nobody is surprised when he adjusts it again — except everyone.
