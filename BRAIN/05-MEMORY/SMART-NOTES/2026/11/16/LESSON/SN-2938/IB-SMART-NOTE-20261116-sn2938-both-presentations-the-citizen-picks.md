## Child

Imagine you have a story to tell. Some friends want to hear it one little bit at a time — click, next bit, click, next bit. Other friends want to SEE it in big beautiful pictures. So why choose? You give them a little row of buttons and say: pick how YOU want to hear it! Unfold, pictures, or both!

## Grandma

It's like this, love — some people like to read a story step by step, and some like to look at the pictures. You don't force one on everybody. You just ask, nice and simple: how would you like it told? And whichever they pick, that's the one they get. Everybody's happy, nobody's forced.
