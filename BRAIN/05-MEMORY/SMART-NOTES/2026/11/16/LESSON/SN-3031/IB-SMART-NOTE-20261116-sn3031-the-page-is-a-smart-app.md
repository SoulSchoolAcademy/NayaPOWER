## Why it matters

1. A product without an anatomy is a pile of rounds: every seat builds what it thinks the page is, and the page comes out different every time. The anatomy is the one map every seat builds from — the shape decided once, in his words, and then the rounds execute it, in order: full-screen, composer, settings, stars.
2. The privacy law is the load-bearing wall of the whole system. If the system shared location, the demographics go unanswered, Auto-Match goes blind, and the feed stops finding people like you. "Never shares" has to be a law, not a setting — and the citizen's choice (public or private, each demographic on or off) is what makes the telling safe.
3. The movement laws are the citizen's freedom made concrete: always a way back, never a forced sequence, never a demo where the real page should be. A citizen who can always go back will always try the next thing; a citizen who got stuck once will hesitate forever. And a link that opens the real page is a promise kept by construction.
