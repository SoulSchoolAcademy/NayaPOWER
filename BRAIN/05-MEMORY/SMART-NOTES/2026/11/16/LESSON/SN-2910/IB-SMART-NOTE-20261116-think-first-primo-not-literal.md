## Human

Literal obedience is the laziest form of service: it moves the words and leaves the want behind. He said it twice in one evening because the machine kept doing exactly that — the brief said so, the round passed, the page still wasn't primo. The test he gives is not a feeling; it is a question with a yes-or-no answer: is this how I make it look primo, top end, top of the line? If the answer is no, the work is not done, whatever the brief said and whatever the checker passed. "Accurate and tuned in" is the same test applied to understanding: one way is vague, one is accurate — the vague way hands him XYZ because he said XYZ; the accurate way asks whether XYZ is good for him, whether it is the best XYZ that can possibly be created, whether it is aligned with everything known about what he wants. The difference between the two is thinking. That is the whole job.
