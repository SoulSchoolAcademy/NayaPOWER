## What it means

1. **INSTALL AS APP:** in Settings, the page installs as an app — and the citizen picks the icon: upload their own, their picture, our icon gallery, our orb library in every colour, the smart covers library, or any photo. Default: an icon with the page's name (SN-2961).
2. **PUBLIC OR PRIVATE, LOCATION NEVER SHARED:** the page is public or private, the citizen's choice, in Settings — and NayaNET never shares location. Country, region, town, each level their choice, solely to connect like-minded people (SN-2962).
3. **THE DEMOGRAPHICS CONNECT YOU:** a full menu of questions in Settings — where you are, what you do, what you love, and more — each one on or off, enter or skip. What the citizen enters drives Auto-Match: connections, feed content, recommendations; the content source is a setting too — public, NayaNET, or both (SN-2963).
4. **YOUR TOP THREE:** the side column is featured links — the citizen's top three (Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, their own sites), so visitors go straight to their profiles (SN-2964).
5. **POINTS, STARS AND LEVELS:** under the profile, a spot says what level you're at; the name changes automatically with the level; a bar runs all the way to ten stars; click to see every level, how points work, and what each level gets. The level names and rewards are his call — not invented (SN-2965).
6. **THE FULL-SCREEN LAW:** every pop-up opens big, almost full screen, with an X in the corner to exit — regardless of how much information is on it. A small pop-up is a fail (SN-2966).
7. **MAKE A POST, BEAUTIFUL:** the composer, 10 out of 10 — feed chooser at the top, each post type explained in plain words, name, words with speak, up to nine photos as a block with the size stated plainly, preview as you type, scheduler, private/public with an eye that explains, Post it. The post itself: the most beautiful post in the network (SN-2967).
8. **FAST ACTION, RIGHT FIRST TIME:** today runs fast action, one round after another, many agents in parallel — and the guard is understanding before building: every brief carries the whole understanding, maker checks three times, checker verifies, captain's eye walks it. A redo means the brief failed (SN-2968).
9. **THE X ON EVERY ACCOUNT:** every "Also on this device" account row gets its own X — warning first (you will lose this account for good), then confirm, never one-click. Duplicates are separate rows; the X removes that one row. A test account can go (SN-2969).
10. **OPENING BUTTONS IN THE MIDDLE:** "Begin the deck ◆" over "Read the letter ◆", one in one colour, one in another, stacked in the middle. The very bottom holds the legal pages row — and nothing else (SN-2970).

## Why it matters

1. A page that only points inward is a dead end; a page whose every part serves the citizen is their world — and the home screen is the most valuable inch in their day.
2. Privacy is the promise the whole system stands on: if the system shared location, citizens would stop telling it where they are, Auto-Match would go blind, and the feed would stop finding people like them.
3. Auto-Match is only as good as what the citizen tells it — and a citizen asked everything at once, with no choice, tells it nothing. The on/off menu is what makes the telling safe.
4. A points number without a level is a scoreboard nobody reads; a level without rewards is a title nobody wants. Together they are the reason to come back.
5. A pop-up that uses a corner of the screen wastes the screen and insults the citizen's eyes — and a small pop-up is a fail the same as a flat button.
6. The composer is judged by what lands on the feed, not by what the dialog looks like: if the post is the most beautiful post in the network, the composer did its job.
7. Fast action without understanding is just fast redos — lots of agents doing it wrong at the same time is worse than one agent doing it right.
8. Position is meaning: the middle says "this is the choice, make it"; the bottom says "this is the fine print, it's here when you need it."
