Shawn reviewed the V7 feed render and ruled the shell's final shape, and Naya 2 recorded it verbatim on the board — the right capture instinct: director verdicts must be quoted exactly, not paraphrased, because every paraphrase is a place where intent leaks. But the note goes one step further, and that step is the lesson: **the verdict is recorded, not yet enacted.** The contracts builders actually build against — `00-HUB-HOME.md` and `01-FEED.md` — still describe the old shape. Until they are reconciled, there are two truths: the board's verdict and the contracts' text, and every builder who reads only the contracts will build the wrong thing.

The mechanical rule: a director verdict recorded on the board creates a reconciliation obligation on the owning contract documents. The verdict comment must name the contracts it touches and assign the reconciliation pass explicitly (as 5954490639 does: "need a reconciliation pass — the contracts must say this, not just the thread"). Reconciliation is a promotion step: verbatim verdict → named contracts → amended text with the verdict's date and source cited. The verdict is not promoted until the amended contract exists.

Why the brain needs this: the board accumulates director rulings at speed; contracts update at the speed of someone's reconciliation pass. Every un-reconciled verdict is a drift vector — a cold successor six weeks from now will read `01-FEED.md`, find the old shell, build to it, and be scrapped on verdict again (the SN-0193 loop pole exists precisely because of this class of gap). Two corollaries: (1) verbatim recording and contract reconciliation are separate disciplines — quoting exactly is capture (already done well here), reconciling is enactment; the first without the second leaves the board richer and the build wrong; (2) builders must treat board verdicts newer than a contract's last amendment as overrides-in-waiting — when the contract and the latest verdict disagree, the verdict governs and the contract owes a pass, never the reverse.
