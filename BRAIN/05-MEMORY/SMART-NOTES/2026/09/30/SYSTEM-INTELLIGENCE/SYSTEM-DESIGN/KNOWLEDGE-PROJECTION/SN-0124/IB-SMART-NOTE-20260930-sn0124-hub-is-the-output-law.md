## 🩷 HUMAN NOTE

Think of a television versus a camera. The camera collects footage out in the world; the TV displays what the camera captured. You would never bolt a record button onto the television screen and call that a feature — the TV's job is display, and putting input controls on it confuses what the device is. Shawn's law says the Hub is the TV: a beautiful, intelligent projection surface for the blocks, layers, feed, and reports. The "camera" is wherever intelligence is produced — your conversation with a Naya, your day, the upstream runtime.
