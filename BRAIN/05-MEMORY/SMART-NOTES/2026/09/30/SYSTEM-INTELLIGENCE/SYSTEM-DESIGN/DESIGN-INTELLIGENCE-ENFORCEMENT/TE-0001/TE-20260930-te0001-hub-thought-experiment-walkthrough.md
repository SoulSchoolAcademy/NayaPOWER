64px left jewel rail — eleven jewels, one per room, each in its room theme color,

## 01 — Smart Feed (/feed, emerald, THE GAME)
"What is happening now?" Slim header (mode + new count). Three large jewel
controls PERSONAL / COLLECTIVE / ACTIVITY, sticky. One continuous vertical river;
objects rise by verified relevance; emerald energy on live objects. Per object:
jewel identity, distilled essence, "why am I seeing this," provenance, truth
state, time, concise actions. New verified intelligence arrives across a glowing
seam — restrained, state-linked, never fake. Actions in a bottom sheet. Filters
progressive. Holy-shit: the seam — intelligence visibly, truthfully arriving.

## 02 — Your Intelligence Today (/today, magenta, THE HIGHLIGHT REEL)
"What mattered today?" The day as a film: morning-to-now arc, three beats as hero
cards, full timeline below in magenta rhythm. Every beat: "why it mattered" in one
line. The feed is the game; this is the reel. Holy-shit: your day edited into a
trailer for your own life.

## 03 — Your Reports (/reports, indigo, THE FILM ROOM)
"What does it mean over time?" Reports as film posters in a dark room, indigo
depth, each with its thesis line. Open one: the narrative unrolls with evidence
trail attached; every claim carries provenance. Holy-shit: months of scattered
work becoming a story with receipts.

## 04 — Intelligent Library (/library, sapphire, THE VAULT)

## 05 — Smart Connect (/connect, teal, THE PORTAL BAY)
"How can anything connect?" Channels as portals: live (teal, breathing),

## 06 — Smart Ledger (/ledger, gold, THE BLACK BOX)
