## 🩷 HUMAN NOTE

Think of a cookbook that exists in three forms: a beautiful printed book for reading in bed, a chef's manual for the kitchen crew with every technique spelled out, and a database the printing press uses. Same recipes, three forms — and if they ever disagree, the printed book is the one that's right.
