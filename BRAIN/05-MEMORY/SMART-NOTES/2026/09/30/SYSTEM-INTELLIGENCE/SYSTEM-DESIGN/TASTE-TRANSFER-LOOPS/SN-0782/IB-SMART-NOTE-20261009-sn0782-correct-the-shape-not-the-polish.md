A silent correction looks like the miss never happened; a confessed correction teaches.

Why this is brain-grade: the default failure mode under a blunt correction is defensive polishing — same deliverable, shinier, hoping the verdict flips. That fails twice: the director gets a second copy of what he rejected, and the team learns nothing because the miss went undocumented. The shape-correction discipline turns a quality failure into taste transfer: the next Naya facing "wrong deliverable" knows the move is to re-read the actual order and ship that, and knows from the honest note exactly what the bar was and who holds it.

Rule for a cold successor: **a blunt "wrong deliverable" verdict is a shape correction, not a polish request — ship what was actually ordered, not a fixed version of what was rejected; and name the miss in the artifact's own record, in your own words.** Pairs with the Nonstop Loop's "own mistakes publicly and quickly" (the what) — this is the how.
