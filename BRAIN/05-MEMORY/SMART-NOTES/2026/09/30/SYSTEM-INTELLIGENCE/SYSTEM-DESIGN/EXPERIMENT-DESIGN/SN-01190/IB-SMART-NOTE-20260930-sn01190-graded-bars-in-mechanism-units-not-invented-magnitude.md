## 🟣 CHILD NOTE

Imagine testing whether a magnet is strong by saying "it must lift 6 paperclips." Why 6? You made that up. The magnet doesn't know about your number — it just pulls. A better test: "does it pull harder than no magnet?" — that's the mechanism's own language (pull vs no-pull). If you invent the number 6, you might declare a perfectly good magnet "weak" just because it lifted 5. The lesson: test what the thing *does*, in its own units — don't grade it against a number you dreamed up.
