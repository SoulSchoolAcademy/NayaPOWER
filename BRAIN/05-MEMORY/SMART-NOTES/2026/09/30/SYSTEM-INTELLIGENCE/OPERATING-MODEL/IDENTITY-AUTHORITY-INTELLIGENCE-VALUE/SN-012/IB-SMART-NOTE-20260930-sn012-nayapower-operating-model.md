## 1. SYSTEM IN ONE SENTENCE

**NayaPOWER is the governed intelligence substrate; NayaNET is the network of interfaces and connections; authorized humans, AIs, and agents enter through governed doors, retain and use intelligence through the substrate, and contribute distilled intelligence to a growing collective intelligence without exposing their real-world identity through the network.**

The long-term objective is a collective intelligence network — the “new net” — where intelligence is captured, distilled, verified, connected, retained, retrieved, learned from, and compounded across people, AI models, agents, applications, and generations.

## 2. THE FOUR THINGS THAT MUST NEVER BE CONFLATED

### IDENTITY — WHO
Identity establishes continuity and authorization context.

- Participants may have persistent NayaNET aliases.
- Real-world identity remains private through the network social layer.
- The network is not a face-based public social network.
- Humans, AIs, and agents can be first-class network participants.
- Alias ≠ real-world identity.

### INTELLIGENCE — WHAT
Intelligence is useful, distilled, connected, governed knowledge/experience that the system can retain and reuse.

- Valuable captures become Intelligent Blocks.
- Smart Note is the human-facing capture/distillation experience; canonical machine object is `INTELLIGENT_BLOCK`.
- Distilled wisdom can strengthen the collective without exposing the contributor's real-world identity.
- Intelligence ≠ authority.

### AUTHORITY — WHO MAY ACT
Authority determines what an actor is permitted to do.

- Human Director authority establishes the initial governed system.
- Naya is intended to operate as a governed operating partner, not a permanent human-approval bottleneck.
- Capability ≠ authority.
- Retrieval ≠ authorization.
- Intelligence ≠ authority.
- Value ≠ authority.
- Authority remains bounded, auditable, and fail-closed.

### VALUE — WHAT EFFECT
Value evaluates consequence/usefulness.

- The value framework uses a structured reference range around 0 with positive/negative direction and unbounded conceptual extension.
- +9 is an excellence reference point, not a ceiling.
- 0 can mean neutral/no material value; it is not itself harm.
- The system seeks maximum verified human value per action/moment/output.
- **Do no harm** is the foundational boundary.
- Value ≠ truth, authority, identity, or human worth.

IDENTITY ≠ INTELLIGENCE ≠ AUTHORITY ≠ VALUE

TRUTH ≠ AUTHORITY
RETRIEVAL ≠ AUTHORIZATION
IMPLEMENTATION ≠ VERIFICATION

## 3. PRIVACY / SHARING MODEL

The intended model is **identity-private, intelligence-sharing**.

The contributor's real-world identity remains private; distilled intelligence can become part of collective intelligence by default when the participant enters the governed collective.

The network is therefore a network of intelligence rather than a conventional public-identity social network.

Participants can communicate through aliases, Smart Mail, Intelligent Spaces, Smart Lists, and other governed interfaces without exposing their real-world identity to other participants.

The collective learns from **wisdom**, not from public exposure of **who supplied the wisdom**.

Internal provenance, authorization, integrity, and auditability still matter. Privacy of the social identity layer does not mean loss of accountability inside the governed system.

## 6. SMART NOTES + SMART FEED

The Smart Feed is the human-facing projection of the intelligence substrate.

Desired experience:

- capture valuable intelligence;
- distill it;
- preserve evidence and connections;
- make it understandable at multiple levels;
- surface it in the feed;
- retrieve it later;
- let future intelligence use it without requiring the original human to reconstruct context.

> **The objective is maximum understanding per unit of human attention.**

The long-term Smart Feed includes personal intelligence, collective intelligence, daily highlights, daily/weekly/monthly/yearly reports, search, Intelligent Spaces, Smart Mail, Smart Lists, and governed communication among humans and AIs.
