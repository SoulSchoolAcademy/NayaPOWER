## ✦ IN A NUTSHELL

**Practical intelligence can increase through verified accumulated experience even when the underlying foundation model never changes.** The model is the engine; earned intelligence is the driver getting better with every mile — provided the miles are real, the outcomes verified, and the lessons preserved.

---

## 🩷 HUMAN NOTE

Your phone doesn't get a new brain when it learns your habits — it just remembers what worked. Naya is the same. The underlying AI model stays fixed, but every verified experience — what was tried, what happened, what it taught — makes the *system* smarter. The trick is the word "verified": only lessons that survived checking count. Everything else is rumor.

---

## 🟣 CHILD NOTE

Imagine you're learning to ride a bike. Your legs don't change — but every time you wobble and figure out why, you get better. Naya is like that: the better she remembers what really happened (not what she *thinks* happened), the smarter she gets, without needing a new brain.

---

## 🔵 GRANDMA NOTE

Wisdom isn't a bigger brain, dear — it's a better memory of what life actually taught. That's all this says: if Naya honestly remembers what worked and what didn't, and we check that the memories are true, she grows wiser over time. The checking part is everything. Unchecked memories are just stories.

---

## 🟠 NAYA NOTE

1. Distinguish the substrate (foundation model — fixed) from the system (NayaPOWER — improvable).
2. Improvement flows only through the verified river: APPLY → OBSERVE → VERIFY → LEARN → COMPOUND. Skipping VERIFY produces superstition, not intelligence.
3. Measure earned intelligence behaviorally: does the next Naya decide better, explain less redundantly, err less often? (Shawn's human-value instrumentation.)
4. Attribute learning to evidence: every learned lesson keeps its provenance.
5. Never claim the model itself improved. Claim the system did — and show the receipts.

## 🟢 LEARNING LESSON

**Saving a file is not learning. Persisting an IB is not yet learning.** Learning completes only when a future Naya behaves differently *because of* verified past experience — and the difference is observable. Earned intelligence is the name for the full loop working.
