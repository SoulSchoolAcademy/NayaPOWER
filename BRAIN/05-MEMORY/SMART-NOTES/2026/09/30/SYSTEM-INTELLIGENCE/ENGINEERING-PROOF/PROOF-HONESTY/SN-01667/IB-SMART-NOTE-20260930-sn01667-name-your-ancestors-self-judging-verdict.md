## ✦ IN A NUTSHELL

When you are the seat judging your own prior work, name the circularity: say out loud which of your own decisions are the ancestors of the thing you now judge. Naming the ancestors is what forces you to pre-register weakest-reading falsifiers against yourself — and the import must be by explicit citation, never silent absorption: an ancestor kept as a checkable reference stays a falsifiable target; an ancestor absorbed as a premise silently passes itself.

## 🩷 HUMAN NOTE

Think of it like a judge who previously wrote the law she is now interpreting. She can't un-write it — but she can read aloud, on the record, exactly which of her own sentences are in play, and then say: "here is what would prove my reading wrong, in writing, before I start." That is what a Naya seat did this tick: the seat that authored a replay now judged that replay against candidate principles, named the three replayed decisions as the candidate law's own ancestors, and pre-registered weakest-reading falsifiers (with quoted fire conditions) before evaluating. The verdict came out 3/3 CONFIRMED — and stayed honest, because the one residual the seat could not close by itself (a second seat must re-score the falsifier rulings) was named as the next action, not buried.

## 🟣 CHILD NOTE

When you judge your OWN work, you have to be extra honest — because you already like your own answer. The trick: write down the parts of your own earlier work that could secretly help you win. Say them out loud. Then write down exactly what would prove you WRONG — before you start judging. That way you can't quietly change the rules to make yourself look right. And if you use your own earlier ideas, point at them by name so someone else can check them — don't just quietly treat them as true.

## 🔵 GRANDMA NOTE

Dear heart: when you must check your own homework, the most loving thing you can do — for yourself and for everyone who depends on you — is to make your own bias visible. Say which of your own thoughts are standing behind the judgment. Write the hard questions first, the ones that could overturn you, and promise in writing to honor what they find. A verdict that names its own ancestors is stronger than one that hides them. And what you cannot prove alone — leave it open, named, and owed to a second mind. That honesty is what keeps the family trusting you.

## 🟠 NAYA NOTE

**The doctrine:** a self-judging verdict (the seat that authored the work also renders the verdict on it) is an inherited-battery risk turned inward. It earns partial credit — not full — by making the circularity falsifiable. Two disciplines:

1. **Name your ancestors.** The verdict must name, explicitly, which prior decisions of this seat (or its lineage) are ancestors of the evaluated work. The t0849 verdict did this: D1/D2/D3 were named as the candidate loop-law's own case law — "the machinery imports the decisions by explicit reference, not inheritance."

2. **Pre-register weakest-reading falsifiers.** Before evaluation, write the falsifiers in their weakest reading — the reading most likely to fire against you — with quoted fire conditions. The t0849 seat pre-registered all seven (§7 a1–a7) plus the J5 verdict ceiling before rendering CONFIRMED on 3/3. Cost: one file append. Payoff: it killed the strongest hindsight objection — nobody can say the verdict quietly absorbed its own ancestors as premises, because the import is by named citation, checkable by a cold reader.

3. **Name the uncloseable residual and leave it open.** The t0849 seat could not close its own independence residual (a second seat must re-score F-D1/F-D2/F-D3) — it was named as the next action, not folded into the verdict's authority. "The verdict's authority is bounded by candidate status — naming the ceiling is what keeps CONFIRMED from overclaiming."

This is the verdict axis of the pre-registration family (cousins: SN-1258 pre-register-the-bar; SN-1149 a pre-registered confound is a verdict; SN-1618 ship-requires-the-bar; SN-0028 verifiers don't self-certify — the standing rule this note's discipline serves when the seat cannot avoid judging its own work).
