Why this is a brain-grade lesson and not just good feedback: the lanes will challenge each other constantly, and challenge quality is the bottleneck of the whole direct-lane protocol (SN-0115/0120 family). Vague critique triggers rework by guessing — the builder fixes what they *think* was meant, the reviewer finds it still wrong, and three rounds evaporate. Byte-cited critique converges in one: the coordinates remove ambiguity about *where*, the cited clause removes ambiguity about *why*, and the stated fix direction removes ambiguity about *what would satisfy*. The mechanical form: any challenge of a build cites file:line for every violation, names the exact contract clause, and states the acceptance condition. Critique that cannot cite coordinates is a feeling; feelings go to the director as taste questions (SN-0122), not to the builder as work orders.
