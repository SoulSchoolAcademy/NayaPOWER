## 👵 GRANDMA NOTE

There's an old saying: "Experience is the best teacher." What's less said is that a teacher whose students never change hasn't taught anything. The team put it into a rule: don't count the lesson, count the changed decision. A notebook full of lessons nobody acts on is just a heavy notebook. The loop runs — experience, surprise, figuring out why, proving it holds up, making it automatic, checking that behavior actually changed — and only the last step tells you whether the first five were real.
