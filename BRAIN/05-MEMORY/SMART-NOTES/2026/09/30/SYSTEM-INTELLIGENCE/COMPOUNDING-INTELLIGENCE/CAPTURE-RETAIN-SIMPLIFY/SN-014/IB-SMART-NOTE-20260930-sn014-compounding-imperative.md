- Everything is potential learning evidence, but not everything deserves permanent memory.
- Capture the smallest durable lesson that can improve future behavior; preserve provenance and evidence.
- Successful outcomes, failures, refusals, recoveries, negative results, and repeated human corrections can all produce valuable learning.
- Undocumented or unretrievable learning does not compound.
- A lesson retained by one seat but invisible to the team does not fully compound.
- Prefer reconciliation with existing canonical intelligence over creating duplicates.
- Prefer simplifying or removing obsolete process after a lesson is learned instead of endlessly adding machinery.
- Repeated human reconstruction is evidence that capture, retrieval, or handoff failed.
- Capture is not learning theater: retained intelligence must later be retrieved and behaviorally useful.
