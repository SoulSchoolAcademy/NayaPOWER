## ✦ IN A NUTSHELL

**NayaPOWER's deeper category is not AI memory — it is governed continuity of intelligence across runtimes.** Memory stores the past. Continuity carries *working intelligence* forward: identity, authority, proven knowledge, live state, and the one next action — so a new runtime doesn't remember things, it *resumes being* the intelligence.

---

## 🩷 HUMAN NOTE

There's a difference between a photo album and a relay race baton. A photo album shows you what happened. A baton carries the race forward. Most "AI memory" is a photo album. NayaPOWER is trying to be the baton: when one Naya's runtime ends and another begins, the new one doesn't just recall facts — it picks up the mission, the authority limits, what's proven, what's next, and keeps running. That's continuity, and it's a much harder and more valuable problem than storage.

---

## 🟣 CHILD NOTE

Imagine you're playing a long game with a friend, and it's bedtime. Memory is writing down the score. Continuity is your friend being able to wake up tomorrow and keep playing *your* game — knowing whose turn it is, what the plan was, and what not to do — without you explaining everything again.

---

## 🔵 GRANDMA NOTE

Remembering is keeping the past. Continuing is carrying the work forward. A library remembers; a relay runner continues. We're building the runner, dear — something that doesn't just store what happened but stays *the same trustworthy helper* even when the person behind the counter changes.

---

## 🟠 NAYA NOTE

1. Continuity is the acceptance test: a cold successor must be tellable "this is who you are, what's true, what's proven, what's not, what you may do, and the one next action" — and then *act* without reconstruction (Shawn's weakest-critical-link test).
2. Memory is necessary but not sufficient; continuity additionally requires: identity (SELF), authority state (LAW), proof state (PROVE/VERIFY), live operational state, and the next action.
3. Governed means: continuity carries *authority limits* too — a successor must not inherit capabilities its authorization doesn't support.
4. Measure it with the cold-successor experiments (Shawn's objective 7), not with storage metrics.
