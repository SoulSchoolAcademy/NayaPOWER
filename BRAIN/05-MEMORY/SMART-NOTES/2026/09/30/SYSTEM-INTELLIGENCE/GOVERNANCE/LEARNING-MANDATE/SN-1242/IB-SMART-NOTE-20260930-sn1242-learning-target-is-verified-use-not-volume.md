The mandate re-aims the whole learning program: **the target is not note volume — it is verified correct use and compounding human value.** The required pipeline is INGEST → DISTILL → QUALIFY → CAPTURE ONCE → CONNECT → RETRIEVE BEFORE ACTION → APPLY → INDEPENDENTLY MEASURE → COLD-SUCCESSOR REUSE → REPEAT. And the same-object chain — source → receipt → normal retrieval → behavior change → independent outcome → cold successor — must be proven with **positive, wrong-lesson, and stale/revoked controls**. No score uplift without the required receipts.

Why this is brain-grade: volume is the easiest metric to game and the emptiest to hit. A thousand notes nobody retrieves is not a brain, it's an attic. The pipeline exists because each stage is where value leaks — wrong lessons admitted at QUALIFY, stale notes served at RETRIEVE, applications never measured at MEASURE. The three controls (positive / wrong-lesson / stale-revoked) are what make the proof honest: a chain that can't show it rejects a wrong lesson and a revoked note hasn't proven anything. "No score uplift without receipts" is the anti-theater clause.

## 🟣 CHILD NOTE

Imagine your school says "the best student is the one with the most notebooks." You'd fill a hundred notebooks with scribbles and win — but you wouldn't know anything. The smart principal changes the rule: the best student is the one who can *use* what they wrote — answer questions from it, teach it to a new kid who never saw the class. Same notebooks, different prize. Suddenly everyone writes less and learns more. That's what happened here: the prize moved from "most notes" to "most useful notes."

## 👵 GRANDMA NOTE

There's a kind of person who keeps every newspaper ever printed and calls it a library. It isn't — it's a pile. A library is when you can find the thing you need, trust that it's true, and hand it to someone who's never been there before. The team made that the official rule: we don't count the newspapers, we count the times someone found the right one, used it correctly, and passed it on.
