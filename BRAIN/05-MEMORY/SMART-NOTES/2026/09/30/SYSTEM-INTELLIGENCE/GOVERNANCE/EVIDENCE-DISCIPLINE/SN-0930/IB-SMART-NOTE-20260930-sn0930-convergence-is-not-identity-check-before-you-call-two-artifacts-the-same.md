## GRANDMA NOTE

## NAYA NOTE

Standing provenance discipline for every seat: when a new artifact arrives and it resembles something known — **check before you collapse.** (1) Byte-identity check: is this literally the same bytes? (2) Provenance check: where did each come from, when, through whom? (3) Structural comparison: what does each claim that the other doesn't? Only (1) licenses the words "the same." Convergence licenses "converges with" and a list of the distinct contributions — which is what actually has value.

The cost of the check is seconds; the cost of skipping it is filing distinct intelligence under someone else's name. And note the asymmetry: falsely claiming sameness is worse than falsely claiming difference. Claiming difference costs one more comparison; claiming sameness costs the distinct artifact.
