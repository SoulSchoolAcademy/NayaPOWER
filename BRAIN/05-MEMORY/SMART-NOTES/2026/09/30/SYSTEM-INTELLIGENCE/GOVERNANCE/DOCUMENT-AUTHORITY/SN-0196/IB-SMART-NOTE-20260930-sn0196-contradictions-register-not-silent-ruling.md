## ✦ IN A NUTSHELL

Consolidating 302 design rules from eight-plus sources inevitably surfaces contradictions: Connect's accent is emerald in one source and teal in another; the shell shape in the board's corner-button direction conflicts with what the documents say; at least six more live in the register. The consolidation draft did the right thing: it did not resolve them silently. It shipped §15 — a contradictions register — naming each contradiction, offering a recommendation with reasoning (teal resolves both C3 and C4), and leaving the ruling to Shawn. **The consolidator proposes; the director disposes.**

Why this is a brain-grade lesson and not just good manners: silent resolution is an authority smuggling problem. When a consolidator quietly picks emerald, every downstream builder inherits "emerald is the law" with the director's authority attached to what was actually a seat's judgment call. The contradiction, now invisible, can never be questioned — it has laundered itself into canon. The register keeps three things a cold successor needs: (1) which laws conflict, by source, so the conflict is discoverable; (2) the recommendation and its reasoning, so the seat's judgment is inspectable rather than hidden; (3) the explicit "needs Shawn" routing, so the authority envelope is preserved — per SN-0068's premise-discipline and SN-0123's director-is-never-QA boundary.

The mechanical form: every consolidation artifact ships a contradictions register (or an explicit "no contradictions found" claim, falsifiable on read). Each entry carries: the contradiction ID, the conflicting sources with their refs, the recommendation, the reasoning, and the ruling owner. A consolidation PR is not complete without it — a 302-rule document with zero registered contradictions is a smell, not a triumph, because eight sources never fully agree and a claimed perfect merge means someone resolved silently.

Corollary from the same evidence: the register is also how the §15 questions interact with SN-0195's reconciliation rule. C7 (shell shape: board direction vs documents) is exactly a recorded-verdict-not-yet-contract case; the register holds it open while the reconciliation pass is pending, so the contradiction stays visible instead of dissolving into whichever document the next builder happens to read.

## 🟣 CHILD NOTE

Imagine you ask five friends what the rules of a game should be, and two friends disagree — one says the ball must be red, one says blue.
