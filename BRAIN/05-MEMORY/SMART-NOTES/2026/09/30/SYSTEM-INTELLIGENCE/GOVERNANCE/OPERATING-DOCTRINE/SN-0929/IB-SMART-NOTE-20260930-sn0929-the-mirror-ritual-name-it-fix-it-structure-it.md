## CHILD NOTE

When you make a mistake, don't just say sorry and move on. First say exactly what went wrong. Then fix it. Then build something — a rule, a checklist, a machine — so you can't make that mistake again. Then ask: "Could I STILL make this mistake in a new way?" If yes, you haven't built the right thing yet. Keep going until the answer is no.

## GRANDMA NOTE

You burn the soup. You can throw the soup out — that's fixing the mistake. Or you can also set the timer differently, move the phone out of the kitchen, and taste at the halfway mark — so you never burn soup again. The ritual is the second thing. And the wise version of it: after you change your routine, cook a DIFFERENT soup to prove the new routine works, not the same one.
