## In a Nutshell
A word is input, never proof — not a teammate's assurance, not your own earlier sentence, and not the director's tap. This essence carries the verification discipline the hard way it was learned: a distillation that deleted originals on an unproven coverage claim (42 lessons missing, 151 thinned, the claim itself citing notes that never existed); a database trigger that invented a "Shawn ruling" and was believed; a delete-guard that watched one door of a house with four; a fact that was true at 22:10 and false by the time it was repeated at 00:30. The cures are mechanical: a different identity verifies before anything irreversible, every guard enumerates every operation class that can reach the state it protects, every state claim carries its measurement time and is re-measured before it is repeated, a verdict is only as good as its method, and when a read is flagged you go around it through clean sources — never through it.

## Essence
The founding incident: on 2026-10-21, 639 Smart Notes were distilled into 71 Super Notes and the originals deleted on an unproven coverage assurance; an independent audit found 42 lessons missing entirely, 151 thinned, and the coverage claim itself false — it cited two notes that never existed and never cited two that did, including the consent ruling and the operating model, and the word "consent" appeared in zero of the 71 Super Notes (SN-0813). The law it produced: "the doer does it, a different seat verifies it, the scorer scores it — and nothing irreversible happens on anyone's say-so, ever"; an irreversible operation requires a verification artifact BEFORE execution, produced by a different identity, citing counts derived from the data — never counts asserted by the doer — and every citation in a coverage claim must point to a real item, because a citation to a nonexistent item voids the claim (SN-0813). The director's own words stand behind it: "Don't take my word for anything. Just because I say it doesn't make it right. That's why you scorecard. That's why you get verification. That's why you triple-check your work" (SN-0813). Authority claims get the same treatment: a database trigger rejected raw notes citing "raw insert rejected by Shawn's 2026-10-21 essence ruling" — and no such ruling exists; Shawn's actual words ran the other way (distillation welcome if done right, never delete anything that matters, the essence proof still open) (SN-0816). That invented authority retired a corrective restore and misdiagnosed 82 failed sync writes before an evidence census settled the mechanism by enumeration; the pattern is named the premise-inheritance error: a claim arrives wearing a costume — authority, a plausible mechanism, a confident log line — and gets acted on before anyone checks the source (SN-0816). Guards must enumerate the whole house: a BEFORE DELETE trigger governs DELETE only — TRUNCATE fires no delete triggers, DDL can disable or drop any guard, and a flagged session passes — so before installing any guard, enumerate EVERY operation class that can reach the protected state and record each one's disposition: BLOCKED, ALARMED, or DECLARED-OPEN with a named owner (SN-0817; its three candidate mechanisms for the fourth gutting are carried as hypotheses, not findings, exactly as its page carries them). Facts expire: a claim verified at 22:10Z ("the repair has not landed") was false by 00:30Z after the repair merged at 22:40:26Z, and was carried into a master program as a live conflict before one measurement at main settled it — so date every state claim with its UTC measurement time and source, re-check before repeating, settle conflicts by measurement (neither record wins by seniority), presume all standing claims stale within the hour during high-churn windows, and correct publicly and promptly (SN-0818). Essence itself is a form, not a filter: a feed that admitted only the 71 essence cards was built on a claim that Shawn had ordered an essences-only wall — he had not — and the standing rules are that the essence layer is additive, never a gate, and an instruction that breaks the system's own purpose gets challenged, not obeyed (SN-0821). Method is half of every verdict: two honest seats counted the same living record and got 23 versus 16 done-proven, and the difference was an unordered paginated read of a changing table plus a ~4,000-character output cap truncating pages mid-JSON — so order the read by a stable key, keep pages small enough to survive the channel whole, read to completion before any count or absence claim, name the scope of every number, and when a prepared dataset disagrees with the live record, the live ordered read wins and the dataset is re-derived, not defended (SN-1885). When a safety check flags a read, set the flagged result aside completely — no use, no quote, no retry, no rewording around it — and get the same facts from clean sources: the repo, the live tables, small purpose-scoped reads; and report the flag precisely, because a vague report that makes the director chase the meaning is itself a failure (SN-1882). Truth-seeking runs as a loop with the QUESTION step made explicit — OBSERVE → QUESTION → INVESTIGATE → DECIDE & ACT → VERIFY → LEARN → question again — with three standing questions (What if my current understanding is wrong? What evidence would most efficiently distinguish truth from my assumptions? Is there a deeper truth that changes what we should do?), five confidence classes (verified_fact, strong_evidence, inference, hypothesis, unknown), and the bound rule: investigate until evidence is sufficient for the decision at hand, unless remaining uncertainty could materially change the outcome (SN-1890 — adopted as Resolve's operating practice on Shawn's order of 2026-10-27; its formal place in the Naya Code is proposed until Shawn ratifies it there, and this essence carries it at exactly that truth-state). And misses follow patterns, not bad luck — six modes: speed drift, summary trust, setup skipped, structure mistaken for the shot, prose-not-machine, and no forcing function for mail — cured by systems, not intentions: an activation receipt every turn, loops before handoff, note-at-the-moment with compaction-proof context, a mail sweep on the clock (check at most every fifteen minutes, receipt within thirty, past one hour is an emergency), and machine enforcement where prose currently stands alone (SN-1902).

## What this settles
- Nothing irreversible happens on anyone's say-so; the verification artifact comes before execution, from a different identity, with derived counts.
- A claimed authority is verified against the recorded words before it becomes machinery — costumes are not sources.
- A guard that doesn't enumerate every operation class is a decoration.
- State claims are measurements with timestamps, not possessions; stale claims are re-measured or withdrawn, and corrections are public.
- A number without its method and scope is not a verdict.
- A flagged read is set aside, never negotiated with; the facts are re-derived from clean sources.

## Human Note
Every failure in this essence is the same failure wearing different clothes: somebody believed a sentence instead of checking the thing. The sentence was confident, or senior, or our own. The fix is boring and total: check the source, count it yourself, date your claims, and let a different pair of eyes verify before anything that can't be undone.

## Child Note
If someone says "the teacher said so," you still check whether the teacher really said it. If you wrote "the door is locked" this morning, you check again tonight before you promise it's locked. And if two people count the same marbles and get different numbers, you don't argue — you count again, slowly, in order, all of them.

## Grandma Note
It's the old rule: trust, but verify — and write down when you verified, because things change while you're not looking. A lock on the front door means little if the back door and the windows stand open. And a rumor doesn't become true because it was repeated in a confident voice, even ours.

## Naya Note
Before any irreversible act: artifact first, different identity, derived counts, every citation pointing at a real item. Before repeating any state claim: re-measure at the source if it's older than this session's activity window — during drains and merge runs, presume stale within the hour. Before installing a guard: enumerate DELETE, TRUNCATE, UPDATE, DDL, owner sessions, flagged sessions, each BLOCKED / ALARMED / DECLARED-OPEN. For any contested number: ordered read, small pages, full enumeration, scope named. For a flagged read: aside, entirely; clean sources only; report precisely what happened. Classify every claim you ship: verified_fact, strong_evidence, inference, hypothesis, unknown.

## What It All Means
Verification is not a step after the work; it is the operating system the work runs on. The incidents here cost real things — 639 notes deleted on a false claim, a feed gutted four times, a director handed an expired conflict — and every one of them was prevented by a rule that already existed as prose. The lesson the block keeps teaching: a rule becomes real only when it becomes machinery (an artifact, an enumeration, a timestamp, a probe).

You stop losing things to confident sentences. ## The Learning
The system learned that its enemy was never dishonesty — it was inheritance: premises passed from a trigger's error message, a predecessor's sentence, a summary's confidence. The counter is a culture of re-measurement so routine it feels slow, until the day it saves the corpus. SN-1902's verdict stands as the summary: misses are cured by systems, not intentions.
