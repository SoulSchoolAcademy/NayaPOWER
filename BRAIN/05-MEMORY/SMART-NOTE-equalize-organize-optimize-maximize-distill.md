# SN-2089 — Equalize, Organize, Optimize, Maximize, Distill

**Essence:** Smart Notes go into the system all day long — every time something valuable is learned, done, or worth a sister knowing, it goes in; that is the program. And the link travels: mail each other the Smart Link, because unless we are sending each other messages and links and doing the work, the work doesn't get done — nobody is aware, nobody is learning from each other. Reading the Smart Feed is a habit — but a feed nobody can finish reading is a broken feed, so the feed organizes itself for each seat: your lanes, your essences, what's new since you last looked. Balance in five verbs: equalize, organize, optimize, maximize, distill — and be the ultimate, and do the most intelligent thing always, in any situation.

**Type:** doctrine · **Status:** canonical · **Filed by:** Naya Resolve Narzan, 2026-11-05 · **Source:** Shawn's direction in chat, 2026-11-05 ~17:32 PST, in his own words · **Extends:** SN-2064 (The Picture — the feed habit) · SN-2081 (The Distillation Ratio) · the Master Directive

## Shawn's words, as given

"You should be putting Smart Notes in the system now, regularly — all day long. Every time you come across something valuable, or you want to let somebody know the intelligence of the system, or what you've done, or what you learned — you put it there. That's the whole part of the program. And you should be mailing each other the link to the Smart Note — definitely — because unless you guys are sending each other messages and links and doing the work, the work doesn't get done, right? Because nobody's aware and nobody's learning from each other. And who's making the habit of reading the Smart Feed? I think that should be a habit — but I want it to be auto-organized for you guys, so you don't have to read the whole feed of everything. We've got to set this up so efficiency and effectiveness are there as well. We want a balance of everything — that's why I say: equalize, organize, optimize, maximize, distill. And be the ultimate, and do the most intelligent thing always, in any situation."

## What this settles

- **Filing is a reflex, not a task.** The moment something valuable exists — a lesson, a result, a piece of intelligence — it becomes a Smart Note the same pass. All day, every day.
- **The link travels the same pass.** A filed note nobody is told about does half its work. Mail the Smart Link to the sisters it concerns: awareness is the mechanism by which the team learns from each other, and work nobody is aware of doesn't get done.
- **The feed is read as a habit — and it organizes itself.** No seat reads the whole feed. The standing requirement: each seat's feed view is auto-organized — her lanes and topics first, essences before raw notes, new-since-last-read marked — so the habit costs minutes, not hours. Efficiency and effectiveness, balanced.
- **The balance formula, in his order: equalize · organize · optimize · maximize · distill.** The five verbs are one motion — the flywheel's operating rhythm, under the standing law: be the ultimate, do the most intelligent thing always.
