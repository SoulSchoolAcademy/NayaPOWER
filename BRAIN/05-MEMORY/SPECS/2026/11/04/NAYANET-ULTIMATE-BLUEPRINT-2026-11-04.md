## 2. THE HEART — Three Things Everything Flows Through

### Smart Notes — the heart
Anything, anywhere, becomes a note. Dictate, paste, write.
- **"Note it"** → saved, organized to your lists, projects, notes.
- **"Smart note it"** → becomes an intelligent block (11 sections: nutshell, child, grandma, plain explanation, why it matters, what it means, how it connects, Naya view, machine view, what's in it for you, the learning) → posts to your personal intelligence feed → shares to the collective.
- **Available everywhere.** Every page, every view, every moment.
- **Sharing is opt-in, private by default.** Shared wisdom carries your **alias, never your real name.**
- **Link-to-button law:** a link inside a note auto-converts to a clickable button below the note block. No dead links.

### The Intelligent Library — how the world understands you
Every entity (Personal, Business, Space, Membership) gets its own Intelligent Library.
- All that world's smart notes + notes, categorized, listed, searchable.
- A directory of understanding. Someone opens your library → they get you.
- "Go check out my intelligent library" → they learn everything about what you do.

### The Smart Box — the NIA's memory of you [✅ RULED 2026-11-04]
One box, connected to every page. Upload files, images, intelligence → **auto-organizes, labels, and stores** by default.
- **Built for the NIA to operate, not the user to manage.** The citizen *can* click in and rename a folder — but they don't have to. The NIA runs it.
- **Purpose:** so your Naya is aware of everything you've filed and stored. It's how she operates your world for you.
- **Individualized:** your box is yours alone — not everyone's data in one pile. Separate, personal, connected.
- When saving: "put this in the Smart Box" or "put it on this list" — either way, it's in your system.
- Tell it "this is for project X" or "this page" — or it figures it out.
- **Dashboard shows the meter** — how much stored, how much space.
- Your permanent record. Nothing ever lost.
- ✅ **Shawn's ruling 2026-11-04:** Smart Box = NIA-operated memory and organization. **Connections, smart notes, and notes live in Smart Lists** — organized there, not separate from the Box but as its organizational surface. The Box is the store; Lists are the view. One name, one meaning. The name stands because it's about *intelligence*, not just storage.
