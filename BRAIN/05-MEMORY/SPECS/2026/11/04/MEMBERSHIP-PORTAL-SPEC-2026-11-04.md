## WHAT IT IS

A membership is an **add-on to any page** — Personal, Business, or Space. Never a separate app. The portal is the member's view: they log in, they get the content. Beautiful, organized, sacred.

**The difference from a regular page:** login. That's it. Everything else follows the same structure as the whole system.

## CREATION

- **Requires Pro or Max.** Free/Power see the upgrade flow.
- **"Create membership" button** on any page → celestial portal aesthetic (purple, gold, angelic guardians — reference images at ~/workspace/membership/design/).
- **Login codes via Smart Mail**, not email. Email opt-in only.

## CONTENT TYPES

### Video Portal
- Owner **pastes a YouTube URL or any video link** → video plays in the portal.
- Beautiful presentation. **Next video → next video** navigation.
- Organized by categories/courses.

### Audio Portal
- Owner **pastes an audio URL** or **uploads to their Smart Box** → audio plays in the portal.
- **Next audio → next audio** navigation.
- PowerCasts, lesson audios, any audio — all organized beautifully.

### Written (PDFs / Books / eBooks / Courses)
- Upload PDFs, books, ebooks, course documents.
- Displayed alongside video and audio in the same lesson structure.

### Exclusive Smart Notes & Notes
- **Pinned, shared, exclusive** smart notes — intelligence unlocked only inside the portal.
- Sacred, important, valuable — presented in smart-note language.
- Regular notes too. Both live here.

---

## LESSON FORMAT (every lesson, same structure)

Every lesson follows the **identical format** — this is what makes it learnable:

1. **Video** (if any) — plays at top
2. **Audio** (if any) — plays alongside
3. **Nutshell** — what this lesson is, in human form
4. **Naya form** — the same, in Naya's voice
5. **Child form** — the same, explain-like-I'm-five
6. **Written content** — PDFs, text, intelligent blocks
7. **Next lesson →** navigation

This means every lesson outputs in **smart-note language** across many subjects. Consistent. Learnable. Beautiful.
