## 4. TOP 10 LESSONS LEARNED — each with its behavior change

1. **Prove the surface the user named, not a proxy.** From now on: no citizen-surface repair is reported done without a signed-in walk of the exact clicks the user named. (Filed as SN-2291 by this run.)
2. **A serving change is not done until the route it changed is walked live.** From now on: route changes carry a walk receipt, or they stay "landed, unproven."
3. **Stamp hashes from the final landed bytes.** From now on: filing-time fingerprints derive from the on-disk/on-ref bytes, never from a draft (P-141).
4. **When the brief file is the blocker, the mission goes in the dispatch message.** From now on: flagged reads never cost a wave (P-142).
5. **Filed is not registered is not live.** From now on: note counts are always reported in three states, never blended — today proved why (77 filed, 10 registered, 0 click-verified of the new set).
6. **Two live failures of one mechanism means change the mechanism's shape.** From now on: the second live failure of an approach triggers a shape change, not a third variant (fetch, fetch v2, redirect).
7. **The maker's proof and the verifier's proof are different objects.** From now on: receipts label which one they are, and only the verifier's kind feeds the outcome count.
8. **A count nobody re-derives will drift.** From now on: backlog and registry counts are re-derived from the source each report, and conflicts (510 vs 655) are carried as open items, not averaged away.
9. **Detection without prevention is a meter, not a cure.** From now on: the breach register's cure metric is merges landing clean, not breaches filed — the register only shrinks when the landing changes.
10. **Finishing is a discipline of one item at a time.** From now on: the sweep's fix list is worked in order, one app end to end, before new surfaces are started (0 of 26 is the baseline this is measured against).
