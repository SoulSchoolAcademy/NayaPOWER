A **Naya Node** is a named point of intelligence with:

- a durable identity;
- a defined purpose;
- capabilities;
- governed authority;
- memory/intelligence;
- provenance;
- proof boundaries;
- and a continuity boundary.

A **Smart Node is not merely a database row or chatbot session.**

Its job is to provide a governed place where intelligence can be retained and later used without confusing temporary runtime identity with durable intelligence identity.

## 3. What an Intelligent Block is

An **Intelligent Block** is a durable, meaningful, structured, provenance-bound unit of intelligence.

A useful block should answer:

- What is this?
- Where did it come from?
- Who owns it?
- What does it mean?
- What authority applies?
- What is known versus uncertain?
- What can it be used for?
- What evidence supports it?
- Has it actually changed anything?
- Has that change been verified?

Not every text fragment deserves promotion to durable intelligence.

## 5. The nine responsibilities

**SELF → LAW → ACT → KNOW → PROVE → CONNECT → VERIFY → LEARN → EVOLVE**

They are nine responsibilities in one governed intelligence kernel — not nine separate brains.

- **SELF:** Who am I and what is my purpose?
- **LAW:** What governs what I may do?
- **ACT:** What authorized action should happen?
- **KNOW:** What relevant intelligence can I retrieve?
- **PROVE:** What evidence establishes what happened?
- **CONNECT:** What meaningful relationships exist?
- **VERIFY:** What deserves trust or promotion?
- **LEARN:** What verified experience should improve the future?
- **EVOLVE:** How does proven improvement safely carry forward?

## 7. The proof law

These distinctions are permanent:

- **STORED ≠ LEARNED**
- **RETRIEVED ≠ UNDERSTOOD**
- **UNDERSTOOD ≠ APPLIED**
- **APPLIED ≠ CAUSALLY IMPROVED**
- **IMPLEMENTED ≠ VERIFIED**
- **LIVE ≠ INDEPENDENTLY PROVEN**

Therefore the system cannot call itself fully working merely because a record was saved.
