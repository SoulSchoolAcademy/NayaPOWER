# THE DAILY INTELLIGENCE REPORT — STRUCTURE V3 (standing, adopted 2026-10-23 at Shawn's direction; V2 added the Language Law, V3 adds Delivery + Presentation the same day)

One report, every day, same skeleton, same order — so days can be compared at a glance and nothing important has to be hunted for. The report answers five questions: **What happened? What did we learn? Where did we grow? What is valuable now that wasn't yesterday? What happens next?**

## THE LANGUAGE LAW (V2 — Shawn, 2026-10-23: "the daily intelligence reports must be human written, smart note written — a child or a grandma could read the report and understand it")

**Every Daily Intelligence Report is written in Smart Note language.** These reports are not for the team, and not only for Shawn — they are for the whole world to experience. So:

- **Plain words first, always.** If a simple word says it, the simple word wins.
- **No unexplained jargon, ever.** A technical term may appear only when it is truly needed, and then it is explained in everyday words in the same sentence — the first time, every time.
- **Short sentences. One idea each.** If a sentence needs a second breath, it is two sentences.
- **Numbers are translated into meaning.** "20 of 5,400 checks failing, each one named and owned" — never a bare count left for the reader to decode.
- **Every item says what it means for a person** — the time saved, the mistake prevented, the thing that now works. "Why it matters" is not a section; it is a habit inside every section.
- **The grandma test:** before an edition ships, each section is re-read with one question — could a smart grandma, new to all of this, follow it? If not, it is rewritten until she can.
- Proof links stay — they are for whoever wants to check. The words must carry the meaning without them.

## THE FEATURE — "SEND IT IN SMART NOTE LANGUAGE" (Shawn, 2026-10-23 — a NayaNET capability, adopted into the system)

Smart Note language is not just the report's style; it is a **standing feature of the system**, taught to every seat and available on demand:

- **Anyone can say "send it in Smart Note language"** about anything Naya produces — a message, a report, a plan, an explanation — and receive the same truth re-told simply: **in a nutshell** (the essence in a breath), then the **human** version, the **child** version, and the **grandma** version of what it literally means.
- Nothing is lost in the conversion: facts, numbers, and links survive; only the costume changes.
- **Daily Intelligence Reports are born in Smart Note language** — no conversion needed. Everything else converts on request.
- This is taught, not hoped for: the report standard (this document) is where new seats learn it, and editions that fail the grandma test are sent back.

## THE DELIVERY (V3 — Shawn, 2026-10-23: "give them the report in the feed, and give them the link to the reports on nayanet.live so they can read it in the beautiful format")

Asking for your Daily Intelligence Report gets you **both**:
1. **The report itself, right here in chat** — in Smart Note language, the nutshell and the lists, readable on the spot.
2. **The live link to the report on nayanet.live** — the beautiful rendition in the Hub's Reports section, where it lives permanently and anyone can read it in the full presentation.

A link is a claim of completion: the nayanet.live link is shared only after the page is verified live by the sender (opened, and the report seen rendering). Until it is live, chat carries the report and says plainly that the beautiful page is being wired — never a link to a page that isn't there.

## THE PRESENTATION (V3 — the beautiful format, Shawn's spec)

The Hub rendition is built for the eyes, not just the mind:
- **Every section is a raised board** — an elevated card with soft shadow and a gentle luminous edge (the flagship look: obsidian depths, luminous purple, jewel-like elevation). Sections never run together as a wall of text.
- **Section headlines are 24. Body text is 18.** Comfortable line-height, generous spacing, real bullets and numbered lists. Readability is the spec — if it is hard to read, it is wrong, however pretty.
- **As much visual presentation as serves reading** — hierarchy, breathing room, clear separation — and no decoration that costs comprehension.
- The chat rendition follows the same spirit in miniature: clear sections, bold anchors, space between thoughts.

## The skeleton (fixed order, never rearranged)

**Header** — date · window covered · main tip at writing · one-line arc of the day · the language line ("Written in Smart Note language").

1. **IN A NUTSHELL** — the whole day in ≤3 sentences. Verdict first, no numbers leading.
2. **THE DAY IN NUMBERS** — ≤7 headline measures, each translated into meaning and compared to yesterday. Value measures (outcomes landed, lessons flipped, ratio) lead; activity counts never headline alone.
3. **TOP 10 WINS** — ranked by value. Each: what it is, why it matters, proof link. Fewer than 10 on a thin day — never padded.
4. **TOP 10 LESSONS LEARNED** — each lesson carries its **declared behavior change** ("from now on, we do X instead of Y"). A lesson without a behavior change is an observation and doesn't make the list.
5. **WHERE WE GREW** — behavior that observably changed because of earlier lessons, each with its evidence. Not what we know — what we now *do* differently.
6. **WHAT BROKE** — honest: violations of our own standing laws, each with owner and cure status. Zero is allowed only after checking, never by default.
7. **VALUABLE THINGS PRODUCED** — finished assets only, each linked. A link is a claim of completion: nothing unfinished appears here.
8. **KUDOS** — named people/seats, each with the specific reason. Credit is evidence-shaped, not ceremonial.
9. **THE BOARD AHEAD** — ≤7 open items that matter, each with owner and next move.
10. **DECISIONS FOR SHAWN** — true human gates only (production, money, credentials, destructive, constitutional, genuine destination choices), ≤5, each with a recommendation. Decidable-by-scorecard items never appear here.
11. **TOMORROW'S SINGLE PRIORITY** — one line.

**Footer** — built-from line: sources read, window, method. The report must be auditable from its own footer.

## The rules (every edition)

- **Same order, every day.** Comparability is the point of a structure.
- **The Language Law above governs every word.** Smart Note language is not a section of the report; it is the report.
- **Every claim links to its proof.** No link, no claim.
- **Wins and breaks both, at full honesty.** A report that only celebrates is a filing system, not an intelligence report.
- **Targets are labeled targets** — never written as measurements.
- **Chat gets the report itself, plus the live link** (see THE DELIVERY): nutshell + lists in Smart Note language in chat, and the verified-live nayanet.live Reports link for the beautiful rendition. The canonical text lives in the repo at `BRAIN/05-MEMORY/INTELLIGENCE-REPORTS/DAILY/YYYY-MM-DD-NAYAPOWER-DAILY-INTELLIGENCE-REPORT.md`; the Hub Reports page is its presentation — one truth, never divergent copies.
- **Timing:** the day edition closes the UTC day; a mid-day edition labels its window honestly and is extended — never silently replaced — by the close.
- **Three tongues:** this structure is the human form — and under V2, "human form" means Smart Note language, full stop. The AI form carries the same 11 sections with exact values and procedures; the machine form is the same fields as JSON. Same truth, three tongues — and the human tongue speaks plainly.

## Why these eleven (the design logic)

The nutshell respects attention. The numbers make it measurable. Wins and lessons are the day's yield — what we gained and what we learned. Growth proves the learning loop is real (lessons → behavior), which is the difference between a diary and an intelligence system. What Broke keeps it honest. Valuable Things is the inventory — the assets the day added to the world. Kudos keeps credit accurate. The Board Ahead and Decisions convert reading into action with owners attached. The Single Priority forces the ranking the whole report implies. Anything that doesn't serve one of those jobs doesn't belong in the report. And the Language Law makes all of it belong to *everyone*, not just to the people who built the machine.
