# THE DAILY INTELLIGENCE REPORT — STRUCTURE V1 (standing, adopted 2026-10-23 at Shawn's direction)

One report, every day, same skeleton, same order — so days can be compared at a glance and nothing important has to be hunted for. The report answers five questions in plain words: **What happened? What did we learn? Where did we grow? What is valuable now that wasn't yesterday? What happens next?**

## The skeleton (fixed order, never rearranged)

**Header** — date · window covered · main tip at writing · one-line arc of the day.

1. **IN A NUTSHELL** — the whole day in ≤3 sentences. Verdict first, no numbers leading.
2. **THE DAY IN NUMBERS** — ≤7 headline measures, each translated into meaning and compared to yesterday (up/down/flat). Value measures (outcomes landed, lessons flipped, ratio) lead; activity counts (runs, comments) never headline alone.
3. **TOP 10 WINS** — ranked by value. Each: what it is, why it matters, proof link. Fewer than 10 on a thin day — never padded.
4. **TOP 10 LESSONS LEARNED** — each lesson carries its **declared behavior change** ("from now on, we do X instead of Y"). A lesson without a behavior change is an observation and doesn't make the list.
5. **WHERE WE GREW** — behavior that observably changed because of earlier lessons (intelligent events), each with its evidence. This is the growth section: not what we know, what we now *do* differently.
6. **WHAT BROKE** — honest: violations of our own standing laws, each with owner and cure status. Zero is allowed only after checking, never by default.
7. **VALUABLE THINGS PRODUCED** — finished assets only, each linked. A link is a claim of completion: nothing unfinished, in-progress, or "one step away" appears here.
8. **KUDOS** — named people/seats, each with the specific reason. Credit is evidence-shaped, not ceremonial.
9. **THE BOARD AHEAD** — ≤7 open items that matter, each with owner and next move.
10. **DECISIONS FOR SHAWN** — true human gates only (production, money, credentials, destructive, constitutional, genuine destination choices), ≤5, each with a recommendation. Decidable-by-scorecard items never appear here — they are decided, not escalated.
11. **TOMORROW'S SINGLE PRIORITY** — one line. If everything else waits, this doesn't.

**Footer** — built-from line: sources read, window, method. The report must be auditable from its own footer.

## The rules (every edition)

- **Same order, every day.** Comparability is the point of a structure.
- **Plain words first; numbers are evidence, not meaning.** Every count is translated ("20 failed of 5,405 — all named and owned") or cut.
- **Every claim links to its proof.** No link, no claim.
- **Wins and breaks both, at full honesty.** A report that only celebrates is a filing system, not an intelligence report.
- **Targets are labeled targets** — never written as measurements.
- **Chat gets the report itself** (nutshell + lists, tight one-liners); the full file lives in the repo at `BRAIN/05-MEMORY/INTELLIGENCE-REPORTS/DAILY/YYYY-MM-DD-NAYAPOWER-DAILY-INTELLIGENCE-REPORT.md` and in the Hub's Reports section — one current copy, never duplicates.
- **Timing:** the day edition closes the UTC day; a mid-day edition labels its window honestly and is extended — never silently replaced — by the close.
- **Three tongues:** this structure is the human form. The AI form carries the same 11 sections with exact values and procedures; the machine form is the same fields as JSON. Same truth, three tongues.

## Why these eleven (the design logic)

The nutshell respects attention. The numbers make it measurable. Wins and lessons are the day's yield — what we gained and what we learned. Growth proves the learning loop is real (lessons → behavior), which is the difference between a diary and an intelligence system. What Broke keeps it honest. Valuable Things is the inventory — the assets the day added to the world. Kudos keeps credit accurate. The Board Ahead and Decisions convert reading into action with owners attached. The Single Priority forces the ranking the whole report implies. Anything that doesn't serve one of those jobs doesn't belong in the report.
