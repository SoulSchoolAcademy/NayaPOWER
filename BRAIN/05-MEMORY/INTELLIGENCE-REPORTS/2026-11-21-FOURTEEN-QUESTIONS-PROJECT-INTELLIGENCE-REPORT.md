6. **Project Intelligence's open set is frozen.** 192 open rows, identical hash across 70+ checks; done-proven flat at 62 since Nov 14. A mirror that doesn't move is a painting, not intelligence.
7. **The Smart Bar came unmounted.** 5 of 9 conformed on Nov 1 → 0 of 9 exactly today. Interface regressions land silently because no lane re-walks the surfaces on a schedule Resolve owns.
8. **Idle hours.** 12 of 24 hours historically ran no outcome lane; the two staggered lanes now fire hourly, but verified minutes still read 15.7/hour against 60. Capacity exists; verified throughput doesn't yet.
