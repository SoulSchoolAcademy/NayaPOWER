## Why it matters

Facebook's feed is a mirror of who you already know — only your friends show, and a new person arrives to an empty room. His feed is the opposite: everybody shows, because the feed is per subject and the subject is what brings people together, not the friendship that already exists. Showing is not connecting — that is the whole of the privacy in one line. You see everybody on the subject; you choose whether to connect or message or not. And the fill rule is the kindness underneath the design: the system always tries to fill the feed, even at a low match, and the sisters show up to keep it filled — because a feed that looks empty feels empty, and nobody should ever open their world and feel alone in it.

## How to use it

The feed shows everybody in the network on the subject — never friends-only, never hidden for a low match. Showing is not connecting — connection stays the citizen's choice, and messaging is the connection. The system fills the feed even at a low match percentage; the sisters show to keep it filled. Nobody opens the feed to an empty room — "always somebody there" is the bar the feed is judged by.
