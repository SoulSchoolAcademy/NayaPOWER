The long username was pushing the row out and the percentage and buttons would not fit — the row broke on real names. Locking the percentage top-right makes the row unbreakable by construction: the name can run as long as it likes and the percentage never moves. Eye first on line two means knowing who someone is comes before messaging them. And the length lock settles the principle for every board, not just this rail: the board is fixed, the content yields — never the other way round.
