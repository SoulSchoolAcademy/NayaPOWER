## Why it matters

The banner was doing two jobs and doing both badly — the greeting and the wisdom — and the wisdom was a big block pushing everything else down. Now the banner is four things in a fixed order, the wisdom lives where it already works (sliding in the feed, one after the next), the close box is where a close box lives (top corner), and the topic chips finally scroll.

## How to use it

Build the banner as four things in order — motto, greeting, date + live time underneath, sharing line — and nothing else. Put the wisdom in the feed as a circulating slide. Put the close box in the top corner, fold the banner away so the search sits flush at the top, and always leave an obvious way back. Make the topic chips row scroll. Keep people on the side, related and online. Read with SN-3215 (the sidebars, which freeze the Hub as the reference the other pages align to).
