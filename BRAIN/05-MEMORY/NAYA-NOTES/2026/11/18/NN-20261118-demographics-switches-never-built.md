## What it is

Shawn, live on the call, on the Personal page walk: "You haven't done in the personal. You never ever did anything to do with the demographics. Remember you're going to allow them to turn on different demographics so their page can be found. And people can connect with them. We never did do that. You know that right?"

The captain checked the Personal fix file the same night: the demographics QUESTIONS exist in Settings — age, where you are from, ethnicity, language, interests — and the design lives in SN-2963 "The Demographics That Connect You" and SN-2975 "The Perfect Demographics Set". But the ON/OFF SWITCHES that make a citizen findable and connectable by each demographic were never built. The captain told Shawn: owed, lands as the next Personal round right after the running fix round finishes so it lands whole.

## Why it matters

Demographics are how a citizen's page is found and how people connect with them — that is the whole point of asking. Questions without switches leave a citizen fully answered and still invisible. The switch is the door: on means findable by that demographic, off means private, and the citizen holds the choice per demographic. Location is never shared.

## How to use it

Build per-demographic on/off switches in Personal Settings — age, where you are from, ethnicity, language, interests — in the running Personal follow-on round. Each switch must work, persist, and be respected by matching and discovery.
