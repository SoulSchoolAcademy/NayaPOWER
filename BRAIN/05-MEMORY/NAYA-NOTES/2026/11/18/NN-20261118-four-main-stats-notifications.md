## Why it matters

A sale is a notification — the citizen got a sale, and the center should say so beside everything else. Four main stats — sales, personal, business, space — plus each world and the total worlds as stats, give the citizen the whole picture in one place. And because those stats are connected to the Dashboard, the center and the Dashboard can never disagree: one count, two places, always the same.

## How to use it

Show four main stats in the notifications — sales notifications, personal, business, and space — with each world's notification and the total worlds both as stats, all connected to the Dashboard so the counts agree.
