## Why it matters

A banner that stays open is a page that never gets to its own content — the welcome is the front door, not the room. A banner that folds away on its own after ~30 seconds greets him and then gets out of the way, and a little button hidden in the corner brings it back when he wants it. And the choice lives in Settings, which is why Settings has to be right: the setting is the citizen's word about their own page, and it has to be kept somewhere worthy of it.

## How to use it

On entry the banner says good morning / good afternoon; after ~30 seconds it folds away on its own. A little button, hidden in the corner, restores it — never a big button sticking out mid-page. X the cover and the search comes right up flush against the top bar, no button in between. Settings holds the "keep it on all the time" toggle. The Hub eye checks all of it.
