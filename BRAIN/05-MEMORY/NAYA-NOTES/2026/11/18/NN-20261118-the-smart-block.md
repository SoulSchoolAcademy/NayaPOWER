## What it is

Shawn's direction, in his words: **THE WHOLE GAME:** maximize the user's experience by giving them 10-star service — **by giving the ultimate formatting for their point of view, their viewpoint, above the fold, for what they're seeing on their screen.**

- **THE SMART BLOCK:** every screen as it unfolds is a new **Smart Block** — smartly formatted so it fits perfect. Whether you unfold it (Flow, down) or slide side to side (Show, across), either way it presents beautifully and is perfectly formatted on the screen.
- **THE LOOK:** it looks real, it looks high tech, it looks like premium high-end software — **something smart like a Naya.**
- **SMART FLOW + SMART SHOW:** the two modes — **Smart Flow** is the unfold, going DOWN (click a button to unfold the next screen; you can always go back); **Smart Show** is the slideshow, going ACROSS (click and the next page presents exactly, perfectly formatted). The citizen picks their preference. Life with Naya is the formatting model.
- **OPTIMIZE FOR PRESENTATION:** full screen, not centered-narrowed; one visual per block (double blocks don't work); if it doesn't fit one screen it goes to two screens — never unaligned; no more scrolling — click a button to unfold the next screen; images and video are 16:9 and interchangeable. **The goal: optimize the view output** so it fits perfectly in a laptop or a mobile first.
- **LOCKED IN AS DESIGN, ENGINEERING AND ANATOMY:** this is part of Naya design — not just what it looks like, but how it functions, how it operates, how it presents. Every Naya on the team understands it.
- **PRESENTATION IS EVERYTHING:** "It's not what you say, it's how you say it. It's how you present it." **The ten-star delivery of intelligence. We produce value. Intelligently. Non-stop.**

## Why it matters

The formatting IS the service. A page that dumps all its content at once makes the person do the machine's work — scroll, squint, find where they are. A Smart Block does that work for them: one screen, perfectly formed to what they're seeing, and the next one a click away. The name matters because the team needs one word for the unit — when every seat says "Smart Block" they mean the same thing: a screen that fits perfect, presents beautifully, and looks real, high tech, premium.

## How to use it

Make every screen a Smart Block — smartly formatted, fits perfect, in Flow or Show. Full screen, one visual per block; doesn't fit → two screens, never unaligned; click to unfold, back always. Let the citizen pick Flow or Show; let 16:9 images and video swap freely. Judge each block by the look: real, high tech, premium — like a Naya.
