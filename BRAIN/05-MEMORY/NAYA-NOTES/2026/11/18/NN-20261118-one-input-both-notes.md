## What it is

Shawn, on the calendar walk, after asking Naya's view, on the missing element: "One input frame per day; enter anything once and it outputs BOTH — a Naya note, the literal, full report, yours, direct, in your way, goes to your project, where you choose — AND a Smart note, the public version for everybody, the intelligent block on the Hub: what it is, why it matters, what's in it for you, in simple chunks, about three sentences at a time, child-and-grandma-simple. Not one or the other — both, from the one input."

## Why it matters

The citizen should never write the same day twice. The private record and the public intelligence are two different things, and both matter — the full report in the citizen's own words, and the simple version everybody can use. One input that makes both means the day is kept truly and shared wisely, with no extra work.

## How to use it

Build one input frame per day — type, dictate, copy-paste, drop in a Naya note, upload or share text or images — and let it output both a Naya note and a Smart note from the single entry.
