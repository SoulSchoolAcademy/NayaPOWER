## Why it matters

People are scared of technology because other systems sell their data — and they need to understand how this one works. This is the answer, said strongly: nobody reads your mail, nobody listens to your calls, nobody watches your video — and we don't even keep your content. The words go after a week; the essence stays. A thousand notes in, ~100 kept; ~100 in, ~10 kept. And when wisdom is shared, identity is NOT shared unless the citizen chooses — we capture the wisdom only, never the name. Everybody learns and grows from the collective intelligence. That is not a privacy policy. It is a powerful, valuable feature.
