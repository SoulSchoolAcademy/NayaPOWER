## What it is

Shawn, voice call, ~22:32Z — his design for the plus button (his screenshot showed the left plus sitting way down the screen, breaking the line): the plus sits at the TOP of the sidebar, on the INSIDE edge — the left sidebar's plus on its right inside edge, the right sidebar's plus on its left inside edge. Tap it and it pushes to the corner, pushing the sidebar away, opening full screen. Same on both sides. The plus never floats low down the screen on its own.

## Why it matters

A plus floating low down the screen reads as a lost button, not a control — it breaks the line. At the top inside edge, mirrored on both sides, it is where the hand expects it, and tapping it does one honest thing: the sidebar gets out of the way and the screen opens full.

## How to use it

Put every sidebar plus at the top inside edge, mirrored both sides, with the push-to-corner behaviour. A plus floating low down the screen on its own is a defect to fix.
