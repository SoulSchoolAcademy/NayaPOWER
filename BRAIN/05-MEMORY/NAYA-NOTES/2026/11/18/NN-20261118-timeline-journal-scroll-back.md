## Why it matters

A journal you cannot go back through is a diary with its pages glued shut. The citizen puts their days in — text, images, anything — and the page has to give those days back: scroll up, any day, and see what they uploaded or shared on that day. Going up through the timeline is going back through their own life, and the page should feel like something special because what it holds is special.

## How to use it

Build the timeline journal page so the citizen can go back and see what they inputted on any day — share text, upload an image, share anything they want — and scroll to see what they uploaded or shared on any one day; make it something special.
