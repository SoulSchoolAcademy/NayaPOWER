## What it is

Shawn, ~01:06Z, on how the citizen checks notifications: "So I kind of see it as a drop down for personal for business and world and then they have three drop downs they can check the notifications and then basically"

## Why it matters

Seeing the count and checking the notifications are two different acts, and each needs its own shape. The big blocks show the count; the dropdowns are the check. One dropdown per life — personal, business, world — keeps the check simple: the citizen opens the life they care about and sees its notifications, never a mixed list they have to sort through.

## How to use it

Give the notification center three dropdowns — personal, business, world — so the citizen can check the notifications in each life.
