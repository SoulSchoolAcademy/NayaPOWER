## Why it matters

Every connection system asks the same question: how do two people start? The old answer is a button — CONNECT — a handshake you press before you may speak, and a request that sits waiting for an accept. His answer is simpler and truer to how people actually meet: you send a message, and the message IS the request. The other person answers by answering — a reply is a yes, silence is a no, and no one has to manage a request queue to have a conversation. And the refinement makes it theirs, not the system's: each person sets their own level, default open, nobody forced. The connection is the message, and the level is the person's choice.

## How to use it

No CONNECT button stands between two people and a conversation — the message is the request. Acceptance is by action: a reply grants, silence declines — no request queue to manage. Message and mail are automatic; each person sets their own level (only instant message, only mail, only audio, only video, or full-featured), default open. Officially connected when they answer — then each shows up in the other's connections. The connection-profile follow-on #3 builds to this model.
