## Why it matters

This extends the frame law from the border to the whole interface: the frame is not a picture frame around a separate page, it is one continuous piece with the interface inside it. Obsidian in the middle with colour only on the edges is the same law as the black car — obsidian is the stage, colour is the rim light — now stated for the main boards themselves, not just the border. Every icon functional is the mechanical law: a control that does nothing is decoration, and decoration is not allowed to sit there. No overlapping buttons is the presentation law at its plainest: two controls in the same spot is a collision the eye trips over, and the fix is a named place — the indicator top-right, the button where it is. The corner icon going and the logo surround going are both the same cut: what does not serve the frame goes.
