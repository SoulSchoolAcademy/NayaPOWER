## What it is

Shawn, ~01:06Z, talking out what notifies and then locking it in: "Until today you would get a notification when there was new intel for the day, right? New Smart Notes. You get notification when your your your reports, your daily report you get notification for that. And. Maybe get notification when your app is ready if it takes longer than a few seconds probably not. I think that's it actually. So anything else?" And: "So those are where the notifications come from. So, if we can lock in on that that would be good."

## Why it matters

A notification list that drifts is a notification list that spams: every new idea becomes a new ping until the citizen stops listening to all of them. He talked it out until the list was done, asked "So anything else?", and answered himself: "I think that's it actually." Locking it in protects the citizen's attention — new intel and the daily report, and nothing else until he says so.

## How to use it

Send notifications for new intel for the day — new Smart Notes — and for the daily report. Add an app-ready notification only if the app takes longer than a few seconds, probably not. Nothing else until he says so.
