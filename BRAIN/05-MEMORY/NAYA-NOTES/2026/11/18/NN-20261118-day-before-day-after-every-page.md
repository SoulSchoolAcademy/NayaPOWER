*Paired Smart Note: SN-3577 — Day Before and Day After, on Every Page*

**Category:** Law — Navigation

## What it is

Shawn, ~01:16Z, approved ~01:17Z, on moving between days: "Like where they can go to the day. Before and the day after. or. or the day before I guess it would be." And: "Or the day after if they've gone way back. On every page you don't have to have it just on the beginning pages because I think that's a good feature because what if they want to go to this slide but in a different day? Right then they could." And his approval: "Yeah, isn't that beautiful? We should do that."

## Why it matters

The citizen lives in the days, not in the beginnings of them. If day-before and day-after live only on the beginning pages, the citizen who is deep in a room has to walk all the way back to move one day. On every page, the same slide on a different day is one tap away — the calendar becomes a place you move through, not a place you keep returning to the start of.

## How to use it

Put day-before and day-after controls on every page — not just the beginning pages — so the citizen can go to the same slide on a different day, including the day after when they have gone way back.
