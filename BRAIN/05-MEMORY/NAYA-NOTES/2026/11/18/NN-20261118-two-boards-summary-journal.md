## What it is

Shawn, ~01:23Z, ~01:25Z, and ~01:26Z, on the summary and the journal as two boards: "I think they should be two different boards. I think the summary of the day and the journaling of the day is different. I think at the you can basically enter your journal of the day. You can just enter on that screen. It'll be like you can type or you can dictate or you can copy paste. You can just enter you can enter a note. of your Naya notes be entered in your journal." And: "And then I would create a separate one that was literally like the journal input, like the diary input." And: "Your diary input. Is your journal day. I call it almost a diary input. You just put anything you want into that. In your day or notes in there. You call I call them I almost call them diary inputs journal inputs. But you could also if you wanted, you can add Naya notes to your journal or you can upload images. That's what I'm kind of thinking. What do you think?"

## Why it matters

Merging the summary and the journal would make one board do two opposite jobs: telling the citizen their day, and letting the citizen tell their day. Two different boards keep each honest — the summary tells the day back, the journal takes the day in, in whatever form the citizen has it: typed, dictated, pasted, a note, a Naya note, an image. "Diary input" is his word for it, and it is the right word: a diary takes anything you want to put in it.

## How to use it

Make the summary and the journal two different boards; make the journal board a separate diary input where the citizen can type, dictate, copy-paste, or enter a note — and add Naya notes or upload images — putting anything they want into their day.
