## Why it matters

The big blocks are what the citizen sees first — they have to show the right three things and the whole count. Personal, business, and your world are the three doors of a citizen's life; the total number beside them says how much is waiting altogether. Without the total, the three blocks are three separate counts; with it, they are a center, and the click inward is the rest of the story.

## How to use it

Show the feature notifications as big blocks for personal, business, and your world — your spaces — with the total number, and let the citizen click in to see the rest of the notifications.
