## What it is

Shawn, live on the call, on the bottom bar: his suggestion was that the six glyphs — plus, envelope, heartbeat, globe, grid, people — render WHITE in the center of their tiles, while the tiles keep their own deep colors and rim light. Verified in the bytes the same night: the glyphs currently stroke `currentColor`, the tile's own color, not white.

## Why it matters

White in the middle is what makes the glyph read as a real object sitting on the tile instead of a flat mark in the tile's color — the tile is the color, the glyph is the light on it. It is his own suggestion for the official set, and it goes to every page with the set, so the bar never splits into a white-glyph version and a colored-glyph version.

## How to use it

Put white glyphs (`stroke #ffffff`) into the official set everywhere — Dashboard fix 3, the Messenger frame round, and the unification sweep all carry the same spec, from the same harvested source.
