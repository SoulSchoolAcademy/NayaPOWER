## What it is

Shawn, on the calendar walk: "Every day gets its own colour, frequency, theme — different vibe, different energy, same system. Days are viewable through multiple perspectives with different data points."

## Why it matters

If every day looks the same, the calendar is a grid. If every day has its own colour and theme, the calendar is a place — and the citizen can feel which day they are in before they read a word. Multiple perspectives mean the same day serves whoever is looking at it, with the data points that matter to them.

## How to use it

Give each day its own colour, frequency, and theme — Wednesday the Kanya blue, each other day its own — and let every day be viewed through multiple perspectives with different data points, all on the same system.
