## Why it matters

Nine is the floor he counted himself — three lives, three kinds each. If the center lists fewer, something real is missing and the citizen never finds out where their message went. And the world one matters most of all: a citizen can belong to more than one world, so the center has to say which world a notification came from, not just that some world has one.

## How to use it

List the nine sources in the center — instant messages, personal mail, new personal connection, business instant mail, business mail, business connections, world instant messages, world mail, world connections — and make world connections open to the worlds the notifications came from, or just that world when there is only one.
