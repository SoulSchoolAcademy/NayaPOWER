*Paired Smart Note: SN-3561 — The Month Must Change When You Tap It*

**Category:** Design — Calendar

## What it is

Shawn, live ~00:36–00:42Z, on the staged calendar — month switching is broken: switching to next month does not change the month, it stays the same month, and he cannot tell what month he is on; the day may not change either. The month name must change clearly on tap. Same broken date logic family as SN-3521; rides the calendar follow-on round, not the frame round.

## Why it matters

A calendar that does not change month when you tap next month is a picture of a month, not a calendar. The citizen must be able to tell what month they are on from the page itself, at a glance, after every tap.

## How to use it

The calendar follow-on round fixes month switching: the month name changes clearly on tap, the day changes with it. No fix claimed until the eye proves it live.
