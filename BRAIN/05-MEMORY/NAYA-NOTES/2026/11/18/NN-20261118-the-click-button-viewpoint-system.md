# The Click-Button Viewpoint System

**Category:** Design — the click-button viewpoint system + the headlights principle

## What it is

Shawn's direction, in his words: **NOBODY READS TWO HOURS.** The collective agreement's presentation is amazing, but nobody reads a two-hour agreement end to end. "Balance effectiveness and beauty: if they're not going to read it, it doesn't matter how beautiful it is."

- **KEEP THE FULL VERSION ANYWAY.** "Give me this still version" — maybe they listen, maybe they go to the visual, they'll probably start reading the text, one maybe they'll skip down jump to the bottom sign it, or they'll go read it — that's likely. The full text stays; the three ways in make it usable.
- **THE UNFOLD IS THE LESSON.** What the agreement page taught us: the unfold of the buttons, next, how to present it in beautiful boxes, how to present text beautifully or images beautifully. **Adopt that unfold in the About section and in the calendar inner pages** — functional mechanical pages.
- **THE ULTIMATE ABOUT PAGE + SKILLS PAGE.** Built from this concept — the visual slideshow. When people come to NayaNET and go to the About, they can **click to watch the slideshow presentation** (image visual presentation) or **click the text** (the story) or **audio** — so everybody can learn about them through **audio, text or image**.
- **PASTE A VIDEO URL.** On those pages, let people enter a YouTube video as an introduction, or Instagram or TikTok or whatever — **upload / enter in / copy in a URL** so their Skills or About page tells about it. **Allow people to copy in their Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube URLs to showcase videos in their About page** — a video about their business, their space, their page, and now you can view it.
- **THE CLICK-BUTTON VIEWPOINT SYSTEM (the whole system).** Pages are just click, read, click buttons, view image, click button, everything just click button — then you go to the next viewpoint. **Viewpoint by viewpoint by viewpoint: click button, viewpoint, click button, viewpoint, click button.** Everything zoomed in on what they need in that moment. They click a button, go to next. **The whole system is a click-button viewpoint system** — through going horizontal or vertical, the unfold or the slideshow.
- **THE HEADLIGHTS PRINCIPLE.** Lock in a specific way that we display stuff, the highest level of quality, the best presentation, very beautiful to use, very interactive. **If you see everything at once it's overwhelming; if you take it bits at a time it's okay — just like driving at night: the headlights show you just a little bit in front of you and you're good, but you can't see the whole way; if you saw the whole way all at once it'd be like oh my god. Take it a bit at a time.** Apply that in the whole system: it feels alive, you just click buttons and it goes in and goes up down all around, beautiful presentations.
- **APPLY IT EVERYWHERE.** The pricing page unfold + the quality + the collective agreement concept — applied to our pages. This is the locked-in display language.
- **SEQUENCING:** the About + Skills build sequences AFTER the Personal layout fix lands (same Personal file, never two rounds on one file). The calendar inner-page unfold rides the running day-restore round (the king's daily report unfolds section by section — his 15:28Z direction, already briefed). The agreement stays whole; the flow-end round continues as briefed.

## Why it matters

Beauty that nobody uses is not beauty — it is a museum piece. His correction is not "make it shorter" — it is "give them three ways in and let them choose": audio, text, image, with the full version still there. And the unfold is the real prize: a page that shows everything at once overwhelms; a page that shows one viewpoint at a time feels alive, because it only ever shows you what you need in that moment. That is the headlights principle, and it is the locked-in display language for the whole system.

## How to use it

Build About and Skills with three ways in — audio, text, image — plus a pasted video URL (YouTube / Instagram / TikTok / Facebook). Adopt the agreement's unfold in About and in the calendar inner pages. Build the whole system as a click-button viewpoint system — one viewpoint at a time, never everything at once.
