## Why it matters

He did not say the icon was wrong — he said it sucks, and he does not know what it is. An icon the citizen cannot read is not an icon at all. And a row that only lights up is not an object: it has to move when you hover it, light up around the edge when you scroll past it, and sit raised off the page with major elevation and depth. That is what makes a list of worlds feel like a row of real objects you could pick up — and that feeling is the whole product.
