## Why it matters

A glow over the icon is a wash over the object — it covers what it is meant to light. A glow underneath is light with volume: the icon stays itself, a black ball, and its own color lights up behind and beneath it. That is the difference between a picture of light and a real object with light in it — the Real Object Law, in one hover. The Dashboard's best icons and the Hub pills already do it right; the bottom bar has to match them, because one set of icons, one look, everywhere.

## How to use it

On hover, the color glows underneath the icon — the light behind/under it, never a wash over the icon itself. Harvest the mechanism first (the Dashboard's best icons, the Hub pills, the rail picture), then the Hub once its eye lands, then the other pages one at a time — never two rounds on one file.
