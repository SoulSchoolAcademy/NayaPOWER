**Category:** Design — Smart Note Boards

## What it is

Shawn saw the frame update on the intelligent feed, asked for the direct link to see it himself, and gave his design direction on the call. His read: "It was coming together really good" … "It doesn't fit perfect. But it does. I can see how we could get this to work. You could just have to play with the settings a little bit." He had imagined the border on the OUTSIDE of the lights, but inside is fine. Take the board and put it in the MIDDLE of the framing so it is not cutting off the edges; each board lights up; it is very much a BOARD WITHIN A BOARD — the main board is the one set in the frame, then the child note board inside it. The frames must be DYNAMIC: not all notes are the same size, so the frame must extend down — create a REPEATABLE PATTERN on the side (he called this easy, "there's an easy repeatable pattern"); the framing can be any length down the page. Fit the intelligent board in the middle, and make the value-for-the-intelligence elements more dynamic. A smart note is presented in FULL — in a nutshell: what it means ultimately, why it matters, what's in it for you, and how — all written out completely. His critique of the attempt he saw: "you tried to squeeze just three notes — an incomplete note, an incomplete smart note — and make it fit into this framing. That's the wrong framing."

## Why it matters

A frame that only fits one size forces every note to be squeezed or stretched into it — and a squeezed note is an incomplete note, which is the wrong framing by definition. A dynamic frame that grows to any length lets the note be whole first and the frame follow it, so the board sits in the middle, lit, with nothing cut off at the edges. That is the design: the content decides the size, the frame repeats to match.

## How to use it

Build the frame as a repeatable side pattern that extends down to any length, never a fixed-height box. Place the intelligent board in the middle of the framing, child note board inside the main board, each board lighting up. Present every smart note in full — what it means ultimately, why it matters, what's in it for you, and how — written out completely, never squeezed to fit the frame.
