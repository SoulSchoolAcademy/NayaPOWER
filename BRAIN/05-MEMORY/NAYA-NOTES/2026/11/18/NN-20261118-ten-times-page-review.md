## Why it matters

A page is not done when it looks finished — it is done when each of its features is the best feature for what that page is for, and ten times more useful than it was. Measuring the improvement in usefulness, not prettiness, is what keeps the walks honest: the review is the work, and the max is the target.

## How to use it
