## Why it matters

He is waiting on the URL — not on a report about the work, not on a promise that it is coming, on the URL itself. That is the never-resend lesson from the other side: the loop only turns when every link in it is new and checked.

## How to use it

Every round ends the same way: a new version, checked by the captain's eye first, then the URL to him. Work one page, send its URL, take his feedback, fix it, send the next URL. Repeat until crafted — that is the loop, and it does not stop between rounds.
