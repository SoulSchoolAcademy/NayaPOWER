## Why it matters

"Never read it" was being heard as "never read anything" — which would make the whole system blind to the very intelligence it exists to operate. The clarification draws the line exactly: the private things are never read, no exception, ever; the public things are ours to read by definition; and the working notes and his inputs are read with his permission, because that is what permission is for. A system that cannot read the smart-note intelligence cannot smart-note it, cannot operate intelligence for him, cannot build the page from his vision.
