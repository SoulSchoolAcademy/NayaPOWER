## What it is

The captain's answer: "They hit the limit on those big writes, and yes, the team can break them into smaller pieces so they don't break on the way through. The context was the night's glitch with the big files — large writes hitting the limit and landing as nothing — while the team worked to get it to him whole, right, and ready for his morning.

## Why it matters

Big files break on the way through. The fix is not a bigger push — it is smaller pieces, each one carried through and verified. The night already proved it in bytes: the board refresh landed only through small verified edits, while every big-write shape landed 0 bytes. His question turns the proven workaround into the standing method: break it up first, always.

## How to use it

When a round carries a big file, break it into smaller pieces before writing — small pieces, verified landings, no whole-file pushes. A big write reported "done" without the pieces verified is not done.
