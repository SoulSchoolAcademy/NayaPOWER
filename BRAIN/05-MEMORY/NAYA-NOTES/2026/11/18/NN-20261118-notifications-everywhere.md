A notification that only lives in the Hub is only seen when the citizen happens to be in the Hub — everywhere else, what needs them waits silently. The same ball everywhere means the eye learns it once and reads it everywhere, and the rule follows the action, not a list: any area that can ask for the citizen carries a ball, so the citizen always knows at a glance where their attention is owed.

## How to use it

Carry the same ball — black ball, white number, lit around the side in the area's own colour — to every area that has actions: Personal, Business, World, Worlds, Dashboard, and any area that gains actions later. The rounds that own each page carry it there.
