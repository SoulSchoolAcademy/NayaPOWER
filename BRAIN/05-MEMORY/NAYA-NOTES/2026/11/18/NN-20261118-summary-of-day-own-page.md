## Why it matters

A day that is never summed up is a day that slips away — the citizen lived it, but nobody told it back to them. The summary page is where the day is told back whole: what happened, what it added up to, in one place the citizen can open and see. It is not the journal — that is the citizen's own writing — it is the day itself, summarized, on its own page.

## How to use it

Give the summary of the day its own page — the summary page of your day — that tells the citizen a summary of their day.
