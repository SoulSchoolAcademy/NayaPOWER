## Why it matters

A list of names with percentages is a directory, not a connection. The moment the row carries its four buttons — message, mail, audio, video — it stops being a list and becomes a door: one tap and you are talking to the person. People do not discover what is hidden. They discover what is shown. "Oh wow, I can just message them. So simple." — that is the whole product in one sentence. The button does not add a feature; it reveals one that was already there.

## How to use it

Every Connect row carries message / mail / audio / video buttons, in one centered line, moved up — initial ball, name, %, i, CONNECT all on the same horizontal line. No capability is hidden behind a menu or a guess when a visible button can show it. The fix rides connection-profile follow-on #3, once the 429 clears.
