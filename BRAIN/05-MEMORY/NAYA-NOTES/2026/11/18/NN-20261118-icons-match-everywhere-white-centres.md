## What it is

Shawn, live on the call, looking at the pages on screen during the people-rail walk, on the icons being checked: "don't have the updated icons. It's not white in the center… the plus button the mail, the activity the globe, they're not white in the center like the ones. So you gotta make sure all the icons match each of them."

This widens the white-center-glyphs order from one bar to the whole system: Dashboard, Personal, Calendar, walkthrough, Business, Space must all carry the identical set-3 bar with white-centred glyphs. The captain's own grep of the Messenger build confirmed no white-centre rule in its bytes yet, and the Messenger eye proved in render that the glyphs currently show in tile colors — globe yellow, people red.

## Why it matters

A citizen moving between pages must never meet a different icon for the same thing — the icon is the way they find their way home. One set, one source, everywhere: the match is checked page by page, not assumed from one page that got it right. The icon scorecard already decided the official set (Set A, the Personal set, 7/10 over the Hub set's 4/10); this order is the enforcement of that decision across every page.

## How to use it

Carry the white-centred official set into every running round — Messenger follow-on, Personal follow-on, Dashboard fix-3 and its small follow-on round 4 — and let the unification sweep carry the identical spec into every page without an active round. Check each page's icons against the official set; a page keeps no older icons after its round lands.
