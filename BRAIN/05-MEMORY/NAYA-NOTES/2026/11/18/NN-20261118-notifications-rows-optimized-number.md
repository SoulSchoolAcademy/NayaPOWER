## What it is

Shawn, ~01:02Z, on presenting the notifications: "Maybe they are multiple rows, maybe two rows whatever present because it depends on the number. So whatever the number is you know how to do the presentation. So you want to optimize the screen for the number for how many there is and then just people just they can look and see how many notifications in each area and this is their place where they can just quickly see oh there's so many notifications for all the areas. Right?"

## Why it matters

A notification center is a counting place: its whole job is to let the citizen see, at a glance, how many notifications wait in each area. A fixed layout cannot do that job for three areas and for twelve — the screen has to fit itself to the count. Optimize for the number, and the glance works at any count; fix the rows, and it works only by luck.

## How to use it

Lay the notification center out in as many rows as the number of areas needs — multiple rows when there are many, fewer when there are few — so the count in each area and across all areas reads at a glance.
