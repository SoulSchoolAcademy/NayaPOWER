## Why it matters

A notification center that shows only three areas is not the center — it is a sample of it. The citizen comes here to see, at a glance, how many notifications wait in every area; if an area is missing, they never learn it exists. Big numbers showing the zeros, a bigger block, a quite bigger number — that is how the count reads instantly, the way the clock reads. And saying the word twice over itself is noise where there should be signal: show the notifications, don't label them twice.

## How to use it

Count every Hub area that carries a notification first, then present them all in a row of beautiful blocks — bigger blocks, quite bigger numbers, the tab active-looking with a beautiful jewel icon, the word shown once. If an area is uncounted, the center is not done.
