*Paired Smart Note: SN-3576 — The Calendar Never Stops*

**Category:** Design — Calendar

## What it is

Shawn, on the calendar walk, month switching re-confirmed broken live: "The calendar must be infinite, continuous — a year ahead, a year behind, and beyond; it never stops." Switching months today does not change or name the month, and the month must be clear at the top when it changes.

## Why it matters

The citizen lives in time, not in a single month. If the calendar stops at the edge of the month, the citizen cannot plan ahead or look back — and if switching does not even name the month, they cannot tell where they are. An infinite calendar means the citizen can always keep walking, forward or back.

## How to use it

Fix month switching so the month changes and is named clearly at the top, and make the calendar infinite and continuous — a year ahead, a year behind, and beyond.
