(4) The check scores the WHOLE page, not the diff. (5) One checklist per page, shown whole. ## Why it matters

The machine trusted a PASS as proof, and a PASS measures the fix. The fix can be perfect and the page still broken — and nobody whose job it was to look at the page looked at the page. His question is not "why did the seat fail" — it is "why did you send it to me when you know my standard." The last look before him is now a named job with a named standard.
