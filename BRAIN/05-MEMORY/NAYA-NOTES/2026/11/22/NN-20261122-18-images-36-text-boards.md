## What it is

**SCHOOL BOARDS — 18 IMAGES, 36 TEXT BOARDS (his ruling, live call ~16:06 MST 2026-11-22):** 18 areas → 18 images. Each image gets TWO text boards, one on either side — 36 text boards total — so there is enough room for one image and two pieces of text explaining it. Presentation: image in the MIDDLE by default, but the arrangement may vary per board however serves the story best — image-text-text, text-image-text, or text-text-image. Each board's pattern is written into its brief. This settles the 9-vs-18 speculation raised minutes earlier on the same call: it is eighteen, not nine.

## Why it matters

One text board beside one image is not enough room for the story — the words get squeezed and the citizen does not read them. Two boards give the story its space, and the image in the middle — or wherever the story reads best — keeps the picture and the words telling one story together.

## How to use it

Image in the middle by default; vary the arrangement per board as the story demands and write the pattern into that board's brief. Run the alignment loop on every board, and build from the per-board instruction set.
