## What it is

**FIRST-THIRD RULE — TEXT LEFT, 16:9 RIGHT (his rule, live call ~16:36–16:37 MST 2026-11-22):** The first one-third ALWAYS plays first. The text ALWAYS has to be one third to the LEFT. If we ever do a 16:9, it ALWAYS goes to the RIGHT — the first third plays first, then the other two thirds join into one 16:9. Always that way, because on mobile it presents the first third first — the thing you read — and it reads better that way. This is the standard for About, Skills, and Memberships.

## Why it matters

The layout must read the way the citizen reads — left to right, words first. On mobile the first third presents first, so the first third must be the thing you read. A picture first is a picture before its story, and a 16:9 on the left steals the opening that belongs to the words.

## How to use it

Put the text in the first third, on the left, always. If a 16:9 ever appears, it goes to the right, joining after the first third plays. Check every new board on About, Skills, and Memberships against the order: left first, right second; words first, picture second. Do not apply this to the School.
