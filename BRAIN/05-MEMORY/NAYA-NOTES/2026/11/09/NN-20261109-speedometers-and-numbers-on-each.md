# Speedometers and Numbers on Each

## What it is

His balance ruling, 19:22Z Nov 9 — recorded from his words as given:

- "You have the speedometers and you have the numbers on each of them. Not speedometers on some, numbers on others — you just have speedometers and numbers on each of them, you have it well balanced so it looks beautiful and readable."
- **The ruling:** every board carries BOTH — the speedometer AND its numbers — on each of them, well balanced (neither dwarfs the other, aligned, legible), so it looks beautiful and readable.
- Never a board with a speedometer but no numbers, and never a board with numbers but no speedometer.
- Pass-3 brief amended a fifth time (§1c, §1 measurement, §1 must-not, §2, §9) and sent to the pass-3 coordinator the same pass.

## Why it matters

A speedometer without its numbers is a picture; numbers without a speedometer are a spreadsheet. A board needs both, together, balanced — neither dwarfs the other, both aligned, both legible — so the board looks beautiful and reads at a glance. His ruling closes the last loophole in the coverage rule: it is not enough for every board to have a speedometer (SN-2558) — every board must have the speedometer AND its numbers, on each of them. Balance is the standard the eye judges by: beautiful and readable, together.
