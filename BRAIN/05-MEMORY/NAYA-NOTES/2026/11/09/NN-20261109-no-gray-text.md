## Why it matters

Gray text is the quiet way a page stops being readable — not broken, just unpleasant, and every word a small effort to read. His ruling makes readability a law, not a preference: every text element high-contrast, readable at a glance, pleasant to read. White on black is the standard; gray is out, everywhere, on the Dashboard as on the pricing page. The law extends SN-2513 from the pricing page to the whole system — if it is not easy to read, it is not pleasant, and if it is not pleasant, it is not done.

## How to use it

- No gray text anywhere on the Dashboard — every text element high-contrast and readable at a glance (white on black), pleasant to read.
- Information is never written in gray, low-contrast, small text.
- Pass-3 carries the six-times-amended brief (§1c, §1 measurement, §1 must-not, §2, §9); judged by eye on the rendered viewpoint, at real widths — if a word is hard to read, it fails.
