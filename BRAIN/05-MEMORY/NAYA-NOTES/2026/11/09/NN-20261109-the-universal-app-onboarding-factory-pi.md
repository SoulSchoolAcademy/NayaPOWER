## What it is

His dictation, 20:43Z Nov 9 — recorded from his words as given:

- "There's the Hub, there's a Dashboard, and there's one universal intelligent app that morphs into all the interactive hub pages, which are the Personal, the Business, any Space topic, any Group topic, any Membership — and all those pages are pretty much all the same, the only difference is the label in the front, mostly, whether it's a Personal, Business, and or the topic, because any of them can have a goal, a mission, a vision. And then the difference with the membership is you get a login, which all of them can have a login, right? Basically it's just a lot — actually there's just three of them with a login to the membership. The membership is not a separate actually page, it's just a feature of the Personal, the Business and the Space."
- **The major parts:** the welcome process / onboarding — it's got all the 10 pages — and then the pricing. "Onboarding ends at hey, you got your pages. And onboarding basically says the 10 questions — fill in the content for the pages, for the Personal, the Business and the Space, because you find out what they're into, what their business is, the personal goals, missions, visions, and for their space you get all the information, and then you create their world."
- **Creating a new Space runs the same onboarding flow** (very similar, maybe custom for each): as soon as they click to create a new Space it asks them some questions, takes them page by page — they fill it out, or copy-paste from Naya to fill it out, or type it in — and it automatically creates that page.
- **The Smart Factory is the parts:** "if there's some parts that you want to use as Smart Factory and you want to create a viewport or a smart block on your page, because any of the pages are customizable — you can add another block, or you can add a button that goes into a block — you can customize your pages with the Smart Factory pieces. But most of all the pages are already pre-made."
- **Project Intelligence + Smart Factory, tied together:** "Project Intelligence, which is still should be the Smart Factory, and Project Intelligence almost tied together, because then what you do is — you're really that onboarding process is your Project Intelligence, right? So how it goes: you go to create any of the smart apps in the Smart Factory, it just takes you through — hey, give me your Project Intelligence — click build, boom, creates you the app. Now you want to customize — oh, we have all these parts, you can add blocks or parts to your app, or buttons, or [it] goes and adds any functions — no problem, we have them in our factory."
- **The picture:** Hub + Dashboard + ONE universal app (morphs: Personal/Business/Space/Group/Membership — same page, different label; membership = a feature with a login, not a separate page). Onboarding = the 10 questions → fills the pages → creates their world; creating a Space = the same flow. Factory = the parts to customize. ## Why it matters

This dictation settles the architecture that every other decision hangs on — and it simplifies it. One universal app, not five apps. Membership as a feature, not a page. Onboarding as the 10 questions that create the world. Creating a Space as the same flow. The Factory as the parts drawer. And Project Intelligence as the onboarding itself — the input that builds the app. "Give me your Project Intelligence, click build, boom" is the whole product in one sentence: the intelligence IS the build. It reframes PI's job too: PI is not a back-office board — it is the front door, the thing a citizen hands over to get their world.

## How to use it

- Build ONE universal app (Personal/Business/Space/Group/Membership — same page, different label); never build Membership as a separate page, or a second app where the universal app belongs.
- Onboarding is the 10 questions: fill in the content, find out what they are into, their goals, missions, visions — onboarding ends at "hey, you got your pages" — and then you create their world.
- Creating a Space runs the same onboarding flow: questions page by page (fill it out, copy-paste from Naya, or type it in), and it automatically creates the page.
- PI is the onboarding: give your Project Intelligence, click build, boom — the app exists; then customize with parts, blocks, buttons, functions from the Factory.
- The order: Dashboard done-done first, then all pages, then the universal app; the Walkthrough/onboarding is queued.
