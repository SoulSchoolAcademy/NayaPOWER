## Why it matters

Onboarding is not a door you walk through once and never see again — it is the way every page gets made, the first time and every time after. One-time onboarding makes the originals; the Factory re-runs onboarding, one app at a time, for everything after. And the fix he named is the heart of it: mission, goal, vision are not one answer for everything — they are one answer per world, Personal and Space and Business, asked for each and guided, with nothing forced. Skip the business, skip the Space, use what you use — and the slots you didn't use are still yours, waiting in the Factory. Everything you make connects back to your profiles and shows up in your world — in your world tab. Around and around. That is the circle, and it is why the creation flow belongs inside the one system, not beside it.

## How to use it

- One-time onboarding creates the original pages. Every page after that comes from the Smart Factory: "create new smart app" → Personal, Business, or Space → onboarding runs again → page created.
- One at a time: Space, then Personal, then Business — never all at once.
- Mission / goal / vision are asked once per world — one for Personal, one for Space, one for Business — guided, and it fills out all three.
- Everything is optional: no business, skip it; no Space, skip it. Three to start, use as many as you want.
- Leftover slots stay yours: only a Personal? Two left — add another in the Factory any time (a private profile and a public profile, for example).
- Every new space or profile connects to your profiles and shows up in your menu, your profile, your business — in your world tab. It's a big circle.
- This is folded into the one-system merge spec (SN-2595) from the start.
