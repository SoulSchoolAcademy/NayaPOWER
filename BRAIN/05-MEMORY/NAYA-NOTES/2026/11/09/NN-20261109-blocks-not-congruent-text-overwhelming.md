## Why it matters

Congruence is what makes a page feel like one thing instead of a pile of things. When every board shares the same design, the same layout, the same spacing, the eye relaxes — the boards are parts of each other, and the page reads as one system. And text that overwhelms is text that is not read: short, clear, scannable words are the difference between a board that informs and a board that is a mess of text. Together the two rulings are one standard — the page must look like one thing, and read like one thing.
