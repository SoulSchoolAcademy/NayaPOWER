## Why it matters

This is the user-facing half of the whole picture: the last note said what the system IS; this one says what it FEELS like to use — push-button simple. Ask four questions, add your notes, boom, your world — with a Library. The Intelligent Library is the new piece: not a separate app, but what pinned smart notes become — the place that tells all about a Space, a Personal, a Business. Notes are not just feed content; they are the material the page is built from and the library the page carries. And everybody gets one — the Library is not a premium feature, it is part of having a world.
