## Why it matters

"Pro level" is the standard that replaces "good enough." Nothing out of line — no crooked board, no gray word, no purposeless orb, no number that disagrees with its gauge. And the second half is the method: wherever it can be cleaner, nicer, more beautiful to present, it is done that way — the work is not finished when it works, it is finished when it presents beautifully. That is the bar every board, every button, every number on the Dashboard is now judged by.

## How to use it

- Pro level everywhere — nothing out of line.
- Wherever it can be cleaner, nicer, more beautiful to present, it is done that way.
- Pass-3 carries the eight-times-amended brief; judged by eye on the rendered viewpoint, at real widths — if anything is out of line, it is not pro level, and it is not done.
