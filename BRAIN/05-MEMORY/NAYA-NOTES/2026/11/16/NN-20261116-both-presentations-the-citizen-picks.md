## Why it matters

The old instinct was to pick one presentation and argue for it. Shawn's ruling is the opposite: offer both and let the citizen choose. An unfold suits the reader who wants the story beat by beat; a visual presentation suits the one who wants to see it. Neither locks the other out, and the citizen who wants both gets both. The two rounds prove the pattern transfers — the same choice row, the same deck harvest, the same not-a-defect rule, from About to Skills with nothing re-argued.

## How to use it

Every section that presents a citizen's story offers Unfold / Presentation / Both on opening. The presentation deck is harvested from About, never rebuilt. A doubled heading across both presentations in Both mode is recorded as NOT a defect — never re-litigated, never "fixed". The citizen's pick is the decision.
