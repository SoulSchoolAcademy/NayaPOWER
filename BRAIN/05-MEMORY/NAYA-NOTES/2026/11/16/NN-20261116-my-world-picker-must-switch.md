The picker did everything except the one thing it exists for: it opened, it showed the worlds, it accepted the tap — and then nothing happened. The citizen did the right thing, the system did nothing, and the citizen is left wondering whether they did it wrong. This catch sits beside the earlier My World redesign (01:33Z) — the redesign fixed what the picker LOOKS like; this catch fixes what the picker DOES. A beautiful picker that switches nothing is still broken.

## How to use it

The picker is verified by the tap itself: pick a world, the feed is that world — proven on the page, at phone width. Any picker, anywhere in the system, is held to the same rule: a choice that does nothing is a dead end, and a dead end is a fail.
