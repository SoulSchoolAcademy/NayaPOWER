## What it is

People want the internet to automate it for them — most things done for them, push-button simple, beautiful, helpful, useful. The puzzle, understood, is to give them the ultimate interface of intelligence: the machine generates money for them, connects them automatically, organizes everything in the world for them, understands everything that matters to them.

## Why it matters

That is what we are building. Everything else is a step toward it or a step away from it.

## How to use it

Judge every build by the test — does it do it for them, or does it ask them to operate something? If it asks them to operate something, it is wrong. Build the interface, never the instruction manual.
