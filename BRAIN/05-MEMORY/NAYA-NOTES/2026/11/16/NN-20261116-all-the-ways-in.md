## Why it matters

A front door with one way in is a funnel; a front door with three ways in, each extraordinary, is a welcome. The three ways are not three tiers — the name-only entry is not a lesser path for people who don't care, and the visual is not a lesser path for people who don't read. Each is complete on its own terms. "We got you" is the sentence that holds it together — whatever the citizen wants, information or none, the system has a way in for them, and every way is done to the same standard.

## How to use it

The front door keeps all three ways in — name entry, text view, visual presentation — each complete, each extraordinary. The opening page offers the choice with two buttons; the login itself carries the direct entry. Both views end at the login page to put in the name — the agreement first, the name after. No way in is ever reduced to a shortcut or a test.
