## Why it matters

Points are authority — more points, more post weight — but a number alone tells the citizen nothing about where they stand. The level is the story of the number: what you are called, how far up the bar you are, what the next level gets you. The name changing with the level makes the progress visible in the one place the citizen always looks: under their own name. And the rewards, stated plainly, are what make the next level worth reaching for.

## How to use it

Under the profile, a spot says what level you're at. The name changes automatically with the level — five stars, a five star member; ten stars, a ten star member. A bar runs all the way up to 10 stars; click it and you see your level, the names of all the different levels, how the points work, and what you actually get at each amount of points.
