## Why it matters

A pop-up that uses a corner of the screen wastes the screen and insults the citizen's eyes: tiny text, hard to read, while the rest of the screen sits empty and black. The whole screen is there — use it. Max experience is not a luxury for big screens; it is the standard for every screen, regardless of how much information is on it. A small pop-up is a fail, the same as a flat button.

## How to use it

Every pop-up — the score breakdown, the composer, every dialog, every detail view — opens big, almost full screen, with an X in the corner to exit. The user gets the full-screen experience regardless of the information on the screen. Any round that builds or touches a pop-up builds it full screen.
