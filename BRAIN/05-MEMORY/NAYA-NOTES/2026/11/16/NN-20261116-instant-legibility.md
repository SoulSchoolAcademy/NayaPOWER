## Why it matters

"Looks cool" is the weakest reason to keep a picture. The test is one glance: can a stranger tell exactly what it is saying? The key says vault. Do-no-harm says the code. Math and chain fail — they are abstractions that need their own words to be understood, which means the words are doing the work and the picture is just standing there. The completeness audit is the other half: a page must say everything that matters AND nothing it cannot say clearly.
