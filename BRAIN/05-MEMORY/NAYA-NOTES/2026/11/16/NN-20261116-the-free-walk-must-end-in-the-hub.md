## Why it matters

Free forever is the promise — "free to join, forever" is in the definition itself. A walk that strands free citizens breaks the promise at the exact moment it should keep it: the end, when they have built their world and are ready to live in it. Hub-first also fixes the order of the whole business: the citizen is in first, and paying is an upgrade to a world they already inhabit, not a toll at the gate.

## How to use it

The end slide carries a Hub door on the free path, beside the paid path — Hub first, pricing after. The Hub greets them by name; the sidebar shows their pages; the bottom icons land on THEIR pages. Paying is an upgrade inside a world they already inhabit — the paid loop is tracked end to end, separately. A walk that strands a free citizen is named broken at its last step.
