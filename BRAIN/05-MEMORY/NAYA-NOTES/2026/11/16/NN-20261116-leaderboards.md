## Why it matters

He is not asking for a ranking table; he is asking for a mirror. A citizen should be able to go and see where they score in any area — their standing, made visible — and when they are at the top of an area, it shows. The aside matters: "a lot of times at the beginning they will" be at the top — early citizens top early boards, and the board showing that is what makes the beginning feel like winning instead of empty. The open questions (where it lives, which areas, what counts as scoring, how top displays) are the brief's job — this note records the decision and the shape so a future round does not have to rediscover a sentence in a chat log.
