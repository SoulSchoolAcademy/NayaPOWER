## Why it matters

Every system re-invents itself by accident: a new page needs a long text shown, so someone designs a new way to show long text, and now there are two ways, then five, and none of them is the one that worked. Mirror success is the rule against that drift. A success that stays on one page is a success wasted; a success mirrored everywhere is the system getting smarter in public.

## How to use it

Every proven tactic is captured and mirrored where it fits — the unfold, the three-bar stack, the clone trick, and the next one. New work starts from what already works — harvest first, never a fresh invention for a solved shape. The sense check governs the mirror — it must make sense on the receiving page.
