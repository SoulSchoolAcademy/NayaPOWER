## Why it matters

The composer is where the river starts — every post on every feed begins there. If the composer is confusing (which feed? which kind of post? who sees it?), the feed fills with mistakes. If the composer is beautiful and obvious, the feed fills with beautiful posts. And the bar is not "does it work" — it is "is it the most beautiful post anyone has seen." A button you don't notice is a button that isn't there: he didn't notice the Private button, so the eye that explains private/public is not decoration, it is the fix.

## How to use it

The composer opens full screen. At the top, choose the feed (dropdown). Each post type — post, Naya note, smart note — says what it IS, in plain words. Name, words (with speak), photo — up to nine as a block, size stated plainly. Preview as you type, exactly as it will post. Schedule it. Private or public, with an eye that explains who sees it. Then Post it. His question stands open: are the photos too big on a phone?
