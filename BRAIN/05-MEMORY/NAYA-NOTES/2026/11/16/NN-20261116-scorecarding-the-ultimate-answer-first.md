## Why it matters

Scorecarding is the method underneath the re-aim: before you judge a job, a round, or a lane, you name its ultimate answer — what it is ultimately trying to do — and then you score it against that answer and nothing else. Without the ultimate answer first, every score is arguable — a job can always defend itself with activity ("we woke 96 times, we dispatched 12 seats") and the human has no ground to say it failed. With it, the judgment is mechanical: did the page land proven right? Did the teaching reach the system? Did the work finish, done right, in optimal time? If not, it failed — whatever the count says.

## How to use it

Every job, round, and lane states its ultimate answer first — before any score, count, or verdict — then is judged finished / done right / optimal time against that answer. Activity without finishing is recorded as a fail, no matter how high the count. Wake-up and dispatch counts appear only as cost against value produced, never as the score. Sibling law, same pass: Optimize for Finishing and Producing Value.
