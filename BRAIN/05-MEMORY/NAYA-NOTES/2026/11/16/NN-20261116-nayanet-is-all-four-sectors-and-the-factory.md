## What it is

The economy makes value four ways: mine it, transform it, serve it — and now, the fourth: value made with almost no human hands, by AI and automation. NayaNET does all four. We mine the gold out of the internet — the intelligence itself. We transform it into the interface, into pages and boards and beautiful components. We serve it at ten stars. And the Nayas are the fourth — non-human workers creating value without human hands. The Smart Factory IS the factory.

## Why it matters

When the automated slice is ninety percent of an economy ten times bigger, all the new growth goes to whoever owns that slice. That is the whole question — who owns the factory? In NayaNET everybody owns it. Everybody is hired, everybody gets their own world, fifty percent straight down the middle, paid every day. The factory belongs to the people who use it.

## How to use it

Build the plug to the standard and nothing less — simple, easy, beautiful, highly effective. The Allow server is how every citizen's AI gets into the factory to work for them: Muse, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok. Their AI works for them in the sector where the value is going, and they own what it builds.
