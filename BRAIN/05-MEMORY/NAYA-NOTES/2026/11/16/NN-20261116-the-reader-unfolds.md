## Why it matters

A wall of text is a refusal to read. The reader unfold hands the full text over one short paragraph at a time, each with a next button — the citizen never faces the whole thing at once, so they never feel overwhelmed; they click, click, click, and they are through it before they realize they have read it all. The text stays whole and verbatim; only the presentation changes.

## How to use it

Every long text opens as a reader — one short paragraph at a time, next button each time, never a wall. It is a common pattern, used all the way through, wherever a wall would otherwise appear. Certificate round 5 carries the first reader: the ⓘ full contract, one paragraph at a time.
