## Why it matters

Communication is the work, not the wrapper around it. A brief that carries the literal words is a transcription; a brief that carries the ultimate want is an understanding. The difference shows up exactly where it showed up: the checks pass, the page is wrong, and nobody can say why — because the checks measured the words and the eye measures the want.

## How to use it

Every brief is scorecarded against the ultimate want before it goes out — the 10/10 version in his voice, not a transcription. Every message to him carries what he ultimately wants — the finished thing, the answer — not a report of activity. The failure is named as communication, not building, when a round passes its checks and his eye says not whole.
