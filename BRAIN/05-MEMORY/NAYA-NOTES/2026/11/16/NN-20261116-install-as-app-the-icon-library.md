## Why it matters

The page becomes an app on the citizen's home screen — and the icon is the first thing they see every day. If the icon is generic, the app feels generic; if the icon is theirs — their face, their orb, their photo — the app feels like theirs. And the library is already built: the orbs, every colour known, the full library. Letting the citizen pick any of them for anything is using what we already have, not building something new.

## How to use it

In Settings, install the page as an app. On install, pick the icon: upload your own, use your picture, pick from our icon gallery, pick an orb with initials from the full library (any colour, initial for anyone or anything), pick from the smart covers library, or use any photo. Pick nothing and it is just an icon with the name of your page.
