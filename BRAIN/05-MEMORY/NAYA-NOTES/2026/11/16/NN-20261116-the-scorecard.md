## What it is

Or 10 things you do 10 times better." His scorecard on the page, filed the same pass into the One List with his words: LOVES — the points (real, in tiles) and the name (the trophy paint). DON'T-LOVES — About has no beautiful unfold · Skills has no unfold/visual · My World is endless links nobody understands · Connections is not the proper system · Smart Mail is not pro-level · no Mail/IM toggle · membership still to do. His rulings inside the scorecard: About and Skills offer BOTH presentations — unfold style OR visual presentation style, their pick, or both · My World IS the calendar — the endless links go away when the calendar is built, the pieces go into the Dashboard and where they actually belong · Connections is a rectangle mini-messenger (initial on the side, scroll through, search — harvest live nayanet.app/rooms/connections) · Smart Mail pro-level with a Mail/IM toggle (reference live nayanet.app/rooms/mail) · OUR pages are the samples — on our pages they see all about Naya and the skills we have, the unfold and the visual presentation demonstrated with real content.

## Why it matters

A round that passes every check can still miss — the Personal round-4 story proves it three times over. The scorecard is the check the checks don't do: stand in front of the page as he sees it and name ten things you love, ten you don't, ten done ten times better. It forces the eye past the brief's diff and onto the whole page — and it forces the loves to be named too, so what works is kept, not rebuilt by accident.

## How to use it

Every page is judged by the scorecard before it is called whole. Every don't-love is filed in his words, with its One List item and its reference or choice named. Loves are named and protected. "Offer both" is the standing answer when a citizen might want either presentation — unfold or visual, their pick, or both.
