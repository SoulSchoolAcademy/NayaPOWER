## Why it matters

The pattern is iteration on a good thing until it stops being good: a round is asked to change one thing, and in the course of changing it, it rebuilds something that was already right — and the rebuild is worse. Every regression he has to catch by eye is a round spent twice — once to break the good thing, once to put it back — and his attention spent re-litigating a decision his eye already made. Restoration is faster, safer, and certain: the good version is already proven, already seen, already liked.

## How to use it

When his eye says something was better before, the good version is restored verbatim — harvested byte for byte from the build that had it right. No improving on, redesigning, or replacing the good version — restoration is the round. The five-icon bottom bar goes back on Hub and Personal; the nine area icons go in the sidebars in logical order, each with its area's count. A round whose change makes the page worse in his eye has one job: put back what worked.
