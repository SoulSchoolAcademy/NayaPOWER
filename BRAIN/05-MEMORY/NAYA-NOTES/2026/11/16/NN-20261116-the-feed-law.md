## Why it matters

Three things is a law, not a list: anything on a feed that is not a Naya Note, a Smart Note, or a post does not belong on the feed — it belongs somewhere else, or it does not exist. And a project is not a fourth thing and not a separate concept: a project IS a world you add, so the feed of a project is the feed of a world. The one row is the discipline that makes the law usable: kinds first, then topics, one row, never two — because two rows is where the "All" filter became a second control and the glow became a defect.

## How to use it

A feed holds exactly three things: Naya Notes, Smart Notes, posts. A project is a world you add — its feed is a world feed, never a separate project-feed concept. Under the tabs sits ONE filter row: type chips All / Smart Notes / Naya Notes / Posts first, then the topic chips. The composer actions (Message / Mail / Create world) are actions, not feed content.
