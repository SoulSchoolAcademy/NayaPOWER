## Why it matters

Two instincts collided, and both were his: minimalism (hide the name, let the orb speak) and scan-ability (the name is the thing you look for). The answer chose scan-ability, and the reason is a phone fact, not a taste: there is no hover on a phone. A hover-only name is a name that doesn't exist for half the citizens — and even on desktop, hiding the name adds a tap to learn who anyone is, on every card, every time. The rail is a list you scan; the name is the scan key.

## How to use it

Keep the name on the people-rail card, in the single row round 4.2 perfected. Make the ⓘ a real, beautiful "I". Add hover/tap on the orb as a bonus way to see the name, never the only way. If he picks option B, it lands as the stacked boxes — with the height cost stated plainly.
