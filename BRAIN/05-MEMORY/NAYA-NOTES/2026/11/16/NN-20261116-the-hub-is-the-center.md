## Why it matters

A citizen who can't find home is lost in their own world. The Hub as the visible center — top button on the left, the home, the main page, where they were welcomed in when they first joined — means nobody ever has to ask how to get back. The + buttons are the quick way everywhere, most-used on top, one click and you're there. The bottom-bar icons carry the notification counts for each area, so the citizen sees what is waiting before they choose where to go. And inside a page the flow is one motion: click your world, and you're in the scheduler — the day's mail, your messages with how many, the news, what's going on in any of the feeds for that day. Center, then worlds, then the day: that is the whole navigation, in that order.

## How to use it

Build navigation as a center with a flow: Hub top-left on every page; + buttons quick access, most-used first, the Hub's + navigating to the right places; bottom-bar Personal / Business / Space icons carrying each area's notification counts, tap to see the notifications then go to the page; inside a page, world → scheduler → the day's mail, messages, news, and feeds. The Hub round and the Personal hub-return round build to this law; the label Home vs Hub is still his call.
