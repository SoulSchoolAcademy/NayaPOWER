## Why it matters

A legal page is a promise written down — and a one-sentence summary is not a promise, it is a shrug. If somebody came to check our legality and found one sentence summing up what we're saying, they would read it as unprofessional, not airtight, not legit — "the answer is not a chance." Real legal agreements say everything and cover everything: full numbered sections, definitions, every case covered, airtight, formatted like real legal agreements, so anybody who reads them sees it as legit. And the presentation is half the verdict: the text sits off to the side on the left — not professionally done — and to a stranger it reads as a scam, "some bullshit, some not legit company," the opposite of what we want. The whole thing, content AND presentation, gets done professionally: centered, aligned, royal/elevated blocks, never plain text, never one-sentence summaries. Why they were like that, honestly: round 1 built summaries (58 boards) and the professional drafting was never done — the writer step was offered 16:30Z and untapped. And the four airtight parts — governing law, jurisdiction, dispute resolution, data controller — are HIS call, never invented: a made-up jurisdiction is worse than a summary.
