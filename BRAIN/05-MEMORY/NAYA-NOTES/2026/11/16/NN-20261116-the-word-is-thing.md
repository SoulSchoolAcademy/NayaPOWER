## Why it matters

Words are the interface before the interface. "Space" is our word — a room, a container. "Thing" is their word — the dog, the farm, the craft, the cause, the thing that makes up their world. When we say "space" where they mean "thing", we make them translate themselves into our language before they can even say what they love. The trio is Personal, Business, Thing — never labelled space.

## How to use it

The word is THING — in the trio, in the feeds, in the copy, everywhere a citizen's passion is named. Category feeds and auto-categorization are named apart, never blurred. Smart Lists organize it all; local / provincial / global curation syncs together.
