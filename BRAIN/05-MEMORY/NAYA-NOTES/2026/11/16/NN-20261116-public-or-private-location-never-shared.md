## Why it matters

Privacy is the promise the whole system stands on: NayaNET never shares your location, full stop. The location questions are not surveillance — they are the matching signal, and only the matching signal. The citizen tells the system where they are so it can find people like them, and the system never tells anyone else. Public or private is the same promise at the page level: the citizen chooses who the page is for.

## How to use it

In Settings, choose public or private for the page. Tell the system where you are — country, region, town, each level your choice — and it is used solely to connect you with like-minded people. NayaNET never shares your location.
