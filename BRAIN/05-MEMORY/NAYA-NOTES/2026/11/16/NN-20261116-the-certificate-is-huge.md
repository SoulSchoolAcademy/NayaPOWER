## What it is

The signing moment is the serious moment — they read, they agree, they put their name in, and the certificate makes it official. Their name, written up and official, with their own link and their mail ID. The mail ID is bigger than people realize: one day it reaches anybody online, on any topic — not about everything, about everyone.

## Why it matters

This is the moment a citizen feels it — "oh, this is serious" — and we are serious. A name made official is the whole promise made visible. If the name feels ordinary, the promise feels ordinary.

## How to use it

Judge the certificate by the name: it must feel super special — official, glowing and solid. Treat the mail ID as a first-class part of the certificate, never an afterthought.
