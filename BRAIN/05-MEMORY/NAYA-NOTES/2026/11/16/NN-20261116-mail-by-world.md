## Why it matters

Mail is one system with three front doors. The citizen's mail is separated by world — Personal mail, Business mail, Space mail — so they always see where the mail came from, and a letter never arrives pretending to be from a world it isn't. Open mail from Personal and you see Personal mail first; the same for Business, the same for Space — the front door of the mail for that world always shows up first. And from the Hub's mail, they can always just select any of the other worlds and see its mail — one universal mail, with a world selector. The same for instant messaging, exactly the same way. The separation is what makes the universality safe — one mail, but it always knows where it came from, and the citizen always knows too. His 👍 ratified the read-back, and the rule rides the Smart Mail round (One List `smart-mail-im-1010`, due 2026-11-17).

## How to use it

Build mail separated by world at the front door and universal from the Hub: mail opens from the sidebars on any page, the page's own mail shows first; from the Hub's mail the citizen selects any world and sees its mail — one mail with a world selector. Instant Messenger follows exactly the same rule.
