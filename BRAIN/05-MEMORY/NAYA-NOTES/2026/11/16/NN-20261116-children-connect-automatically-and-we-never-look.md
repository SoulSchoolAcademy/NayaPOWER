## What it is

Children connect with friends on whatever topic — little groupings, audio calls, chats, sharing and posting about what they're into — and the system puts them together automatically. They never have to search; they see someone, see "I'm X% connected," and say hi. The match always shows its breakdown: what percentage, in what area — stats first, deeper if they want.

## Why it matters

Transparency is the best way, and respect is the law. We learn about them to give better service — and we never read their mail, never listen to their conversations, never watch their video. We just let them be.

## How to use it

Show the breakdown with every match — percentage and area, always. And hold the respect line without exception: no reading, no listening, no watching, ever.
