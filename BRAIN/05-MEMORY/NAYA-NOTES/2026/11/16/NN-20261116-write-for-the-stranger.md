## What it is

Shawn, 2026-11-16 02:17Z, on the captain's cleaned-up NayaNET definition: "I'm not convinced I want [it] put in there yet, I just want to get the right words. What you're doing is you're just mirroring it back to me but you're not telling me if that makes the most sense. Like just imagine you're coming in, I don't think they understand what that means. So what if we say something that's useful and valuable and what's in it for them, and we'll say from the perspective — imagine from the perspective of the user, what's in it for them, let's tell what it is in perspective of what's in it for the user in the simplest form, and what they're agreeing to, do you get what I'm saying? Because I think these are all words where they read that, I'm like, and they wouldn't have any idea what that ultimately means, even though it's all accurate and right, you get what I'm saying?" The user's-perspective version offered the same pass: "NayaNET is a new internet that does everything for you. You get your own world — your personal life, your business, your space — all in one place, private, yours. An intelligence runs it for you: it finds the people like you, puts you together automatically, organizes everything, and helps you earn — half of what your world brings in, paid to you every day. It's free to join, forever. And all you agree to is one thing: do no harm." Placement NOT decided — nothing goes in anywhere until the words are right.

## Why it matters

Mirroring is not thinking. The cleaned-up version was accurate and meaningless to a stranger — every element kept, none of them landing, because it was written from our side of the table. The test is the stranger walking in cold: do they understand what it means, what is useful and valuable in it for them, and what they are agreeing to? If not, the words are not right yet, however true they are.

## How to use it

Every definition, front-page line, and certificate word passes the stranger test before it is placed. Mirroring back is named as not-thinking — the answer must carry a judgment and the better version if not. The user's-perspective shape is the default: what you get, simplest form, their words. Nothing is placed until the words are right.
