## Why it matters

This is Auto-Match as the citizen lives it: what you tell the system is how the system finds people like you. The more you tell it, the more it finds; if you don't set it, they can't find you; if you don't want to be found, don't set it. Every question is the citizen's choice — on or off, entered or skipped — and the feed follows: people into those things as connections, content about those things in the feed, people recommended. That is the customizable internet: it seeks to optimize for you.

## How to use it

In Settings, open the demographics menu and go through the questions: where you are, church or organization, work, female or male, language, interests, age, favourite brands, music, favourite people, favourite books. Enter what you want; turn each one on or off. Choose your content source: public content on a topic, setting or filter, just NayaNET content, or both. The feed, the connections and the recommendations follow what you entered.
