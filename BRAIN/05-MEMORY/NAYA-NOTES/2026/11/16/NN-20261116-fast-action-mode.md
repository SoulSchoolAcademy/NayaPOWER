## What it is

Shawn, 2026-11-16 16:01Z — his name for it: "the fast action plan". FAST ACTION MODE: the mode is continuous back-and-forth, not send-it-away. He says something, it gets done right away and comes back right away; he makes another edit, it comes back right away. NOT send-it-away-and-see-it-hours-later-or-the-next-day — that doesn't work for him, it's exhausting. The difference: send-off mode is the seat relay (maker → checker → captain) with each step queued, and delivery held until a whole chain finishes or bundled with another round — that is what made the visual agreement sit 10 hours and the sidebar round take hours. Fast action mode: build a piece, verify it myself right away, show it to him the same pass, he reacts, fix, back again. Nothing sits. Nothing held for another round. The relay still verifies (maker → checker → captain's eye), but the captain's eye and the delivery happen the same pass the report lands — no holding, no bundling. Recorded in AGENTS.md + MEMORY.md the same minute; this note files the lesson so the mode survives the message.

## Why it matters

The relay was built to protect him from unfinished work, and it became the thing that kept finished work from him: the visual agreement sat 10 hours, the sidebar round took hours — not because the work was slow, but because each verified step waited for the next queue and the delivery waited for the chain. Verification is not the delay; holding is. Fast action keeps the verification and drops the hold: the captain's eye and the delivery happen the same pass the report lands. He reacts, we fix, it comes back — that loop is the work, not the overhead around it.

## How to use it

Build a piece, verify it right away, show it to him the same pass. He reacts, fix, back again. Nothing sits, nothing is held for another round, nothing is bundled with a later round. The relay still verifies — maker, checker, captain's eye — but the eye and the delivery land together, the same pass the report lands.
