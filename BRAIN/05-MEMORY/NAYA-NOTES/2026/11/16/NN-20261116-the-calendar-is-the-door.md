## What it is

Click a day and boom, you're in that day's world: the dashboard for that day, the feed for that day, the news update for that day (local, provincial, global), the Smart Notes for that day — all the notes, all the events, all the posts. ## Why it matters

Every system has a front door, and most of them are menus. A calendar is different: it is time, and time is the one thing every citizen already understands. The visitor view is the same door from the other side: not your world, but a brief of it — book a time, see what's coming up, send a message. That is how the system spreads: "communicate with me here" replaces email and Facebook not by being better at messages, but by being a place with a door you can point someone to.

## How to use it

My World is the calendar — the build rides the Personal chain (Personal whole first, then the calendar through the My World doorway). The day view IS the dashboard for that day, not a second dashboard; visitors get the door, not the house. Each day lights up in its own color and feels alive; the day menu is an extraordinary presentation, or it is not done.
