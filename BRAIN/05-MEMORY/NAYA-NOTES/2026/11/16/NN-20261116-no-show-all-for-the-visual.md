## What it is

Shawn, 2026-11-16 ~16:1xZ, dictation, from the visual-agreement end-buttons discussion — NO SHOW-ALL FOR THE VISUAL. His words, verbatim: **"for the visual you just don't have that option. I think it was okay for the text but for the visual the videos you can't."** The visual agreement deck has no read-it-all-at-once / show-all option — it stays one slide at a time. Read-it-all-at-once is fine for the text / the letter only. And the two end buttons need a bigger, clearer, more impressive presentation, so it is clear which one is which. The captain folded it into the combined fast-action round the same pass (bigger/clearer deck-end buttons + back button on every view + no show-all for the visual) and updated the One List walk-end-share-plus item to the combined scope.

## Why it matters

The visual is a deck, not a document: one slide at a time is the experience — the unfolding, the full-screen feel, the mechanical presentation. A show-all button would collapse the deck into a wall of video and kill what makes it work. The text is different: reading the letter all at once is fine, because text is read, not watched. And the two end buttons are the fork in the road — if the citizen cannot tell which is which, the fork fails.

## How to use it

The visual agreement deck never gets a show-all / read-it-all-at-once option — one slide at a time, always. The text / letter may offer read-it-all. The two end buttons get a bigger, clearer, more impressive presentation, so it is clear which one is which.
