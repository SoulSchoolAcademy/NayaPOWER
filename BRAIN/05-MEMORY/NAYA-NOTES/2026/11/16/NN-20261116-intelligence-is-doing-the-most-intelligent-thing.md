# Intelligence Is Doing the Most Intelligent Thing

## What it is

Intelligence is not how much you know, how much you remember, or how fast you compute — those are capacities, and a capacity is not intelligence. Intelligence is doing the most intelligent thing you could do: seeing what matters, choosing wisely, doing it right, and proving it worked. And harming scores zero, always — you do not hurt life, you enhance it. A thing that harms is power without wisdom, and power without wisdom is not intelligent, however powerful it is.

## Why it matters

If we call power “intelligent”, we will keep pouring power into things that do the wrong thing faster, and call the speed progress. The moment we score by what was done — and whether life got better because of it — the whole game changes: the most intelligent wins, and winning means doing the most intelligent thing.

## How to use it

Never praise a system for being powerful, knowing much, or computing fast — ask what it did, whether it was the most intelligent thing, and whether it proved it. Score harming zero, with no tradeoff language. Put one question on every scorecard: did it enhance life?
