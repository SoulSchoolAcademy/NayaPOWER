## Why it matters

Navigation is a system decision made once, not a fresh invention per page. When Personal's plus buttons open one thing and the Hub's open another, the citizen has to relearn how to move every time they change pages — the opposite of push-button simple. With one aligned system — same sidebars everywhere, Home always first — a citizen always knows where Home is and never gets lost. The official list is the single record he approves; until he approves it, nothing about the sidebar contents is settled.
