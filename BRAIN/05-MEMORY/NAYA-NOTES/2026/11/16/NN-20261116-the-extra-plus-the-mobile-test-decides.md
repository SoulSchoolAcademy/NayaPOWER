## Why it matters

Two controls that look the same and sit together will be read as one control done twice — and a citizen who taps the wrong + gets the wrong menu, and learns not to trust either. The rule names the two jobs and the test that keeps them honest: LEFT + is CREATE (Make a post · Smart note · Naya note), RIGHT + is CONNECT (Message · Mail · Create world) — different jobs, different menus, never mixed up. And it is decided by measurement, not by taste — "it doesn't look bad" plus a checker measurement that all six fit on one line at phone width is the evidence, and the fallback is written before it is needed: if it ever wraps, the right + goes off to the side.

## How to use it

The two + buttons are two jobs — LEFT + CREATE, RIGHT + CONNECT — and are never mixed up or merged. The mobile fit decides: fits perfectly on one line at phone width → it stays; wraps to a second line → the right + moves to the side, in the corner, separate from the five. Any future change to the bottom bar re-runs the mobile-fit test — the test, not taste, decides.
