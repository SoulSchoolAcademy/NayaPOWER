## Why it matters

This is the charge that makes the Self-Check Law enforceable from his seat: he can SEE whether a round went through three cycles and a verification — by the difference. If the version he is shown looks hardly different from the one before, then no matter what score the round claims, it did not go through the cycles. Finish completely means all three together, in order: ALL the edits done, the pages aligned, the whole thing verified — and only then sent to him. A round that sends him a partial set of edits, or unaligned pages, or an unverified claim, has sent him unfinished work. The test is his eye, comparing versions: visible difference is the receipt for the cycles.

## How to use it

Send nothing to Shawn until all three are true: every edit done, pages aligned, verification complete. Check the visible difference against the last version before sending, not after. Treat a 9 or 10 score on a round with no visible difference as a false score — re-run the scorecard, don't defend it.
