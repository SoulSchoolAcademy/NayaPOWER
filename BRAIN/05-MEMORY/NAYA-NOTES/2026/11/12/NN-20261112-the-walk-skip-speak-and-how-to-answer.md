## What it is

Shawn's eye on the staged walk Q1 at 19:51Z Nov 12 — "Tell me who you are, in your own words" — three fixes, on ALL ten question pages, not just Q1.

(1) **A Skip at the bottom of every question** — tap it and move on with NO answer stored (a skipped question stores nothing; no invented answer).

(2) **The Speak button WORKS** — today it does nothing when tapped; it must listen, and the words appear in the box AS THEY SPEAK so they can see what it is saying; they edit after, then Enter, and Enter works.

(3) **The How-to-answer pop-up** — tap the "i" and it says, in plain words: one, type in the box; two, dictate — click Speak and it writes out what you say, edit after; three, ask Naya (your AI / Muse) to write it in your own words — share with them, copy it in if you want — copy-paste from your AI, dictate, or type it, up to you. Below the three ways, **EXAMPLES of a good answer — A, B, C** — and clicking one puts its text into the box, where they edit it and make it their own; the walk almost fills itself out for them. Each question gets its own A/B/C.

Folded into the S2 round as §1 G–I (addendum dea51194, 19:52Z) — one maker, one file, one redeploy. Honest boundary: the real-voice test (a real person speaking into a real mic) is Shawn's — the maker proves the wiring with a stub.

## Why it matters

Nobody should be stuck on a question they cannot answer yet — Skip keeps the walk moving and keeps the record honest, because a skipped question stores nothing and no answer is invented for them. Speak must actually listen, and they must SEE the words appear as they speak, or the button is a lie. And the "i" is the guide standing beside them: three ways in, in plain words, plus examples they can take and make their own — so the walk almost fills itself out. Three small fixes, on every question, and the walk stops being a test and starts being a conversation.

## How to use it
