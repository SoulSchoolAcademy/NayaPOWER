## What it is

Shawn 22:35Z Nov 12 — sharpens the One World Law; recorded in MEMORY.md as THE CLONE LAW. The Personal page becomes the Business and the Space by CLONE — exactly the same, same buttons, same features, everything, except the title. And the mission: where Personal says "my story", Space says "our story". The middle that changes is ONLY the title + the story/mission voice (my → our) + the world's own content; everything else is identical, byte for byte from the Personal template.

## Why it matters

Clone, don't rebuild — that is what makes the mirror round buildable and verifiable. Business and Space are the Personal page, exactly the same, with three swaps only: the title, the voice (my → our), and the world's own content. Alignment is then measured by diffing against the Personal template: any difference outside the three swaps is a defect, not a design choice. It kills the failure mode that produced the Space proof-host — a Space rebuilt by hand, with its own buttons, its own cover, its own top bar. Under the Clone Law there is no hand-built Space: there is the Personal template, cloned, with the title, the voice, and the content swapped. The mission voice is the one deliberate change of voice: "my story" becomes "our story" — a space is a shared story, not a solo one.

## How to use it

Build Space and Business by cloning the LIVE Personal page (v9, sha 992ab18c) — swap only the title, the voice (my → our), and the world's own content. Measure alignment by diffing the clone against the Personal template. Never hand-build a Space or Business page again.
