## Why it matters

A button in the wrong place is not a small defect — on the certificate page it steals the moment the citizen is claiming their identity, and it cuts off the very text that tells them what they just earned. On the main page its absence is the opposite failure: a returning citizen is forced through the whole process again when they should be one click from home. And the silent auto-jump is the worst of both — it takes the choice away entirely. The law puts the button where it belongs, gives the citizen the choice, and keeps the certificate page for claiming alone.

## How to use it

Any certificate page is checked for a "Jump back in" button — if it is there, it goes; the certificate page is for claiming, not returning. Any main page is checked for the button — a returning citizen we remember sees "Jump back in" + the small "New user? Create a new account — click here" line underneath, and nothing jumps them back in automatically: the choice is theirs. The fix round must also find and name where the silent auto-jump lives, since the live entrance file holds no auto-login logic.
