## What it is

Shawn extended the walk flow ~18:09Z Nov 12, asking — in his words — "doesn't that make sense, isn't that the right flow?" It is filed in MEMORY.md as THE END FLOW. After the ten questions there are TWO last pages.

First, the page-names page, where the citizen enters each page's title + URL — the chime for the system to create the pages, because all along, as they fill the walk out, the system is already creating.

Then the building page — "building it / wait a minute" — boom, done, "here's your pages", they click to view them.

Then it redirects to the PRICING page — the whole product shown before they have decided to pay, and they choose their package. Then back from pricing, right into the HUB, and the Hub has all their pages.

The data flow: the walk answers go into their Smart Box as they fill them out, and the system inputs that data into the pages and labels them — the pages are created from the walk, filled with their own words. The pages, especially where they get them, must be extraordinary.

The flow was confirmed back to him as the right flow, and an addendum was sent to the walk fix-round coordinator (637427b6) with interrupt the same pass.

## Why it matters

The walk does not end at "Congratulations, you're hired" — it ends when the citizen is standing inside their own world, holding pages built from their own words. The page-names page is the moment the system listens: their titles, their URLs, their names on their doors. The building page is the moment the system proves it: wait a minute, here they are. And pricing comes after the proof, not before it — they see the whole product first, then choose. Put pricing first and you are selling a promise; put it after the pages and you are selling something they already hold. The Hub at the end is the payoff: they arrive home and everything they just made is already there.

## How to use it

The walk fix round carries this flow: ten questions → page-names page (title + URL per page) → building page → "here's your pages" → pricing (whole product, choose a package) → Hub with all their pages. Answers land in the Smart Box as they are given, and the pages are created from the walk, labelled with the citizen's own words. A walk that ends at the end slide, or that shows pricing before the pages exist, is missing the end of the flow.
