## What it is

Shawn's charge ~18:16Z Nov 12 — in his words, "let's get this done right today, full speed ahead, into extraordinary" — filed in MEMORY.md as THE DREAM — THE AUTOMATED INTELLIGENT INTERNET. The product is an automated internet that is intelligent — it organizes your whole world and creates your world, all push-button simple. Instead of 99% of people failing, 100% success.

What everybody wants: a push-button simple system that makes them money, and to use the internet to make money and connect with people of like minds, into what they're into.

The connection: it auto-connects you with anybody in the world, on any topic, at any moment — search everybody in the network by username, search any of the pages, the results show up in Connections. Like Facebook's groups + pages structure, but more beautiful and intelligent — intelligence automation is the automatic product.

The lens, restated: always look from the user's point of view — how do I give them 10-star service, 10-star button, board, text, presentation? How do I make this automated and organized for them?

## Why it matters

This is the whole mission in one picture: the old internet made 99% of people fail because it was messy and manual — you had to do the organizing, the connecting, the building yourself, and most people never got there. The dream flips the number: the system does the organizing and the creating, push-button simple, so everybody succeeds. The search is the hinge — search everybody by username, search any page, and the results are people you can actually connect with, in Connections, on any topic, at any moment. That is not a feature; it is the product. Intelligence automation is the automatic product — the thing the system does for you without you asking, every time.

## How to use it

Every page, every build, every decision is checked against the dream: does this organize their world for them, or ask them to organize it? Does it create their world, or hand them the tools and walk away? Can anybody cold follow it — Step 1, Step 2, Step 3, the button, click here? And is it 10-star from their point of view — the button, the board, the text, the presentation? If the answer to any of those is no, the work is not the dream yet.
