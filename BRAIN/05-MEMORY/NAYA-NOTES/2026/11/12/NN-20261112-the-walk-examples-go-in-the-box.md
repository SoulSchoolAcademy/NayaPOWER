## Why it matters

The 19:51Z version still asked the citizen to reach for the examples — tap below the box, tap to fill. This ruling removes the reaching: the choice IS the box. Three real answers, written in Naya's own voice, sit there waiting to be picked and made theirs — and a fourth door, Full Custom, for anyone who wants their own words, with the three ways spelled out in its eye. No pop-up, no tap-to-fill, no hunting. The walk does the thinking; the citizen does the choosing.

## How to use it

Build the answer box as multiple choice — A, B, C written up, each editable in place, each with its own eye — plus Full Custom with its own eye naming the three ways. No examples below the box, no tap-to-fill, no separate How-to-answer pop-up. Every question step carries this shape; a skipped question still stores nothing. The example content is Naya's own canonical words — see SN-2709.
