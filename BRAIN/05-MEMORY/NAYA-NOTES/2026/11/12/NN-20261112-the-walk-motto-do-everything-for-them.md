## What it is

Shawn's motto for the walk, 20:15Z Nov 12: do everything for them. Give them an example. Allow them to take control and make it their own. But have it all laid out where they don't have to do any thinking — do them a solid, give them 10-star examples, and WE'LL BE the example. **And if they want to see example pages, show them NAYA'S pages** — Naya's own filled-out Personal, Business, Space as the living example of what a finished page looks like.

Recorded in MEMORY.md as HIS MOTTO FOR THE WALK, 20:15Z, and in `WALK-QUESTIONS-SPEC-20261112.md`. The pages-link rides with the S3 questions rebuild — it needs Naya's pages filled out first.

## Why it matters

This is the standard the whole walk is judged by: the citizen should never have to think. Not because they can't — because we already did it for them. Every question arrives with the answer half-written, in Naya's own voice, ready to pick and make theirs. And when they wonder what a finished page even looks like, we don't describe it — we show them Naya's own pages, filled out, living proof. The example is not a mock-up. We are the example.

## How to use it

Judge every walk step by the motto: if the citizen has to do thinking we could have done for them, the step fails. Fill out Naya's own Personal, Business, Space pages as the living example — a dependency on the S3 round — and link them from the walk so citizens can see a finished page.
