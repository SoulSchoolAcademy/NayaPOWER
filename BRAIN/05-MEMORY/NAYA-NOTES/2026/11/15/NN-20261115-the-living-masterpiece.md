## Why it matters

"Finishing touches" is the wrong phrase for what he means, and he knows it — that is why he corrects himself mid-sentence into "a living work of art." A finishing touch is the last 5% of a job; here the touch IS the job, because the product is the experience and the experience is made of touches. A button that is not beautiful is not a 95%-done button, it is a button that teaches the citizen this place is ordinary — and ordinary is the one thing the whole cannot afford to feel.

## How to use it

Every button carries a beautiful icon; beautiful icons + beautiful buttons are our thing. The plus button is white with the plus. Elevation is raised everywhere — inputs go in, buttons come out, icons inside buttons are elevated too. About bars become elevated boards; the high-level design is everywhere. The introduction board is extraordinary, not plain. Smart Tabs: one button, one thing; each its own colour; no double purple; premium, elite, high-tech, trillion-dollar. Every round is judged as a living masterpiece — "alright" is not a pass.
