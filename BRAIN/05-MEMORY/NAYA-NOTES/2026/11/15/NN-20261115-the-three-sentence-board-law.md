## Why it matters

A long board is not hard to read because the words are hard — it is hard because the eye has nowhere to rest and the thumb has nothing to do. "Unfold, unfold, next" is a rhythm for the hand as much as for the mind: each click is a small completion, and the reader keeps going because going is easy. A wall of text reverses that, and most people will leave — and what they skip is valuable information lost.

## How to use it

No board carries more than 4 sentences; the target is ~3. Counted sentence-by-sentence, on the served bytes — never paragraphs, never `<p>` counts. One button only — "Keep reading." White text throughout; no gray text anywhere it can be helped. The rhythm stays unfold: one board per click, in order, to the end.
