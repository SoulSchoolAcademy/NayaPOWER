## Why it matters

He asked the question and answered it in the same breath, and the answer is the more important half. The research exists to check the answer, not to replace it: it confirms that nothing out there is the whole we are building, and it returns three learnings that all pass his own test — each one removes a decision or a scroll rather than adding a feature. The Bugatti is the standard for the feeling, not the feature list: a Bugatti does not have more buttons than a family car, it has fewer, better ones.

## How to use it

Every new lesson from the outside is judged against simplify-don't-add: fewer decisions, quieter page, or it does not ride. A match arrives with a plan attached (time, call or place, one tap to say yes). One best match a day, with the reason attached — not an endless deck. Reactions attach to the thing (a note, a photo, a line) with a word, so the first message writes itself. The do-not-learn list stands: no endless scroll, no pay-to-be-seen, no surveillance, no mail-reading, no swipe grind.
