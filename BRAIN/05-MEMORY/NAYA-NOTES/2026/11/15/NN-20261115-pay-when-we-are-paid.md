# Pay When We're Paid

## What it is

Shawn settled the agreement's open payout-clock question (00:06Z, in chat): "when we get paid, they get paid." That replaces both fixed-clock candidates — neither applies. Keep it simple. The citizen's account shows pending → official/active → paid: how much they've made, how much is official, how much is pending, how much has been paid. Until we are paid it shows pending; when it's official it shows active. No heavy restrictions at the start — he is not putting too many restrictions on payouts until it runs into a major problem. In practice citizens often get paid around the tenth day, because that's when we get paid — do not stop that. If it ever has to change, he may add a stipulation later only if needed to protect the system, communicated to citizens as an update — "we had to evolve this, this is what we're doing now" — and that's it. Relayed into the agreement v4 build as addendum #3 (00:07Z); Step 8 of the merged manuscript carries it ("When we get paid, you get paid. Often around day 10.") with labels PENDING / OFFICIAL.

## Why it matters

A payout rule is a promise about money, and money promises are where trust is won or lost. A fixed clock would have made us promise a date we do not control; pay-when-paid promises only what is true — that the citizen is paid when we are — and the account proves it in three states a child can read. Starting light says we trust the citizen, and changing only on a real problem, with notice, says we will keep trusting them.

## How to use it

Step 8 states pay-when-paid with PENDING / OFFICIAL labels — never a fixed clock, never PENDING / PAID. The account shows pending, official, and paid as three visible states. No payout restriction is added at the start; any future stipulation arrives with a plain notification, never silently.
