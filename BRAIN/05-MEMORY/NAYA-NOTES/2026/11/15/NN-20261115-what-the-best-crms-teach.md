## Why it matters

The question he asked — "are we missing anything?" — is the question of a builder who knows the big shape is right and is hunting for the small thing that makes the big shape usable. A CRM's power is not its database, it is that one person, opened, shows everything: what you know about them, what you shared, what you owe them next, and when. Without that view, the pieces exist but the human has to assemble them every time — and assembly is friction, and friction is where relationships go quiet.

## How to use it

Notes on a person become a real capability: private dated notes on any connection, with the whole shared history in one place. Every connection and promise carries one dated next step; the day view shows what's due this morning. The relationship board shows where everything stands — talking, quiet, booked, owed — and the morning list is the whole system in one place. No surveillance logging is ever added to get there — no open-tracking, no mail-reading, ever.
