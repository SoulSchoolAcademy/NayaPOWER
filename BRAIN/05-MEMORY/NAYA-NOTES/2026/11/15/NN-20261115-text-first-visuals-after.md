## Why it matters

Pictures made before true words inherit the words' errors — v1 invented its own format, v2 showed two tiers where there are four, both beautiful, both wrong, both corrected by his eye at the cost of a round each. With the sequence, the expensive work (pictures, voice) happens once, on words already approved. It also protects Shawn's attention: he approves words — fast to read, fast to correct — instead of re-reading pictures that encode wrong words.

## How to use it

Every unfolding/visual round starts with approved words — no picture brief before them. Each block's words are sized to one picture, with the picture named in the block. The voice build waits until the words are final in a whole build. A visual that contradicts approved words is a defect of the visual, not a question for Shawn.
