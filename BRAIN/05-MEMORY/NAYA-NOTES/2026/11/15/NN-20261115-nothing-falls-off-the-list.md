## Why it matters

The Checklist Law governs items ON the list. An item that falls OFF the list escapes the law entirely — no timer fires, no floor-watch flags it, no self-audit names it. Four days of silence is the cost: the work was 137/137 + 21/21 + 20/20 on its items and NOT PASS on its page, and the machine reported nothing because there was nothing to report on. The machine's silence must mean done — because everything unfinished is named, timed, and visible.

## How to use it

No round closes its One List item on a NOT PASS — the item stays open with the named next step. An item moves to waiting-his-hand only in writing, with the holder named (his hand) and the ask attached. The floor-watch checks for finished work with no item, not only for quiet items. The inviter's server gap is tracked as his-hand work with the fold-or-separate question open — asked once, never re-asked.
