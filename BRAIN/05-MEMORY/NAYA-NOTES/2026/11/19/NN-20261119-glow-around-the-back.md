**Category:** Design — Messenger

## What it is

The active pill must NOT light up with a glow over the top of the button. The glow sits around the back of the button, like the others.

## Why it matters

Light over the top washes the button out and flattens it; light around the back lifts it and leaves the face clean. The glow is the rim, never the wash — the same law as the boards: light around the edge, never over the top.

## How to use it

Rides the Messenger next round (follow-on 5 dir exists). The eye proves the lit pill glowing around the back with a clean face.
