**Category:** Design — Calendar

## What it is

On the call he opened the Calendar fix-7 link and caught it live: every date number in the month grid sits in a GOLD tile, all 30 of them, every column — "instead of having all the numbers be like they are where it's color matched to what the colors the numbers are, you turn them all yellow." Screenshot 08:17 MST (`…/51/516bbf25…png`): headers right (Sun purple, Mon orange, Tue gold, Wed blue, Thu green, Fri red, Sat indigo), cell borders right, number tiles ALL gold. His ruling: the month grid's date-number digit tiles carry their weekday's colour — Sun purple, Mon orange, Tue gold, Wed blue, Thu green, Fri red, Sat indigo — NEVER all gold. The earlier "day-number tiles match the time digits / clock gold" ruling governs the DAY-page banner number ONLY. His refinement on the call (~08:18 MST): the numbers follow the DAY colour, NOT the gematria first-letter law — "all everything on Monday gets orange… everything on Sunday gets purples… I think that would present nicer. I never thought of that before. But yeah, you should do that." (By gematria first letter Monday would be red, Sunday indigo — not what he wants here.) And the clock: "Keep the time as it is. That color look is good." Fix-8 dispatched 08:18 MST to recolor the grid.

## Why it matters

A colour that belongs to another day breaks the signature piece — the day's frequency IS its colour, and the whole page speaks in that colour. All gold says nothing; per-day colour says which day you're looking at before you read a word. And the scope line matters as much as the colour: without it, the next seat re-applies the clock-gold ruling to the grid and turns it all yellow again.

## How to use it

Fix-8 recolors the month-grid number tiles to their weekday's colour — gold only in Tuesday's column — with the checker measuring tile colour per column at 1920 + 390. The clock and time digits stay EXACTLY as they are, approved as-is. The day-page banner number keeps its clock-gold treatment; the grid never takes it.
