## What it is

Shawn asked whether I fully understand the three things — smart note, Naya note, post — and dictated the full definitions. A smart note takes intelligence and distills it into its essence, says it in the most simple basic words, in a nutshell. In a nutshell IS the human level — literally what it's saying, the most simple words — and it sits at the top of the smart note. Then the child note, then the grandma note. Then the areas, on EVERY intelligent block: ultimately what it means · why it matters · how it connects · how to apply it · what's in it for you. That's what everybody wants to know. A smart note is about three to four sentences per section, roughly — a Naya note formatted down to about three or four sentences.

## Why it matters

The smart note is the form the whole network reads — shared intelligence, found by search engines, AIs and machines. If its shape is vague, the network's knowledge is vague. Fixing the shape — nutshell first because the nutshell is the human level, then child, then grandma, then the five areas on every block — means every note answers the five questions everybody actually asks, in words a child can understand, in three or four sentences a section. The definition refines the Nov 17 official format; it does not replace it — it makes the area list explicit and names the nutshell as the top.

## How to use it

Every smart note filed from here on carries this shape: in a nutshell at the top, child, grandma, then what it means · why it matters · how it connects · how to apply it · what's in it for you. The template and every future filing follow it. The Naya note keeps the same format at full length; the post takes the same framing as a third thing.
