*Paired Smart Note: SN-3874 — Frames and Feeds — the Two Kinds*

**Category:** Design — Presentation

## What it is

Shawn, on the live call, naming the two kinds the network is made of: "the network is made of frames and feeds. Everything is a frame — About, Skills, My World, the profile/contact card, membership, mail, messenger, the agreement — and they all get the same framed fill My World already has. The feeds themselves are different BECAUSE they are feeds — the Hub feed, the Personal/Business/World feeds flow as feeds, not frames. Frames and feeds, and that makes up our network."

## Why it matters

The frame law needs its exception stated, or the next builder will frame the feed and kill the flow. A feed is a river — it has to keep moving, newest first, never one-at-a-time. A frame is a room — you step into it and it holds you. Confusing the two breaks both.

## How to use it

Frame every page and area except the feeds: About, Skills, My World, the profile/contact card, membership, mail, messenger, the agreement — all with the same framed fill My World already has. Let the Hub feed and the Personal/Business/World feeds flow as feeds, not frames.
