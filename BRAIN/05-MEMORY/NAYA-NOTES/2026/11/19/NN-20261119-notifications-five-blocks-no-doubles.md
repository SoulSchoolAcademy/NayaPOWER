## What it is

The notifications slide holds five blocks: Personal · Business · Your Worlds · Smart Notes · Appointments. One label at the top of each block; the big number CENTRED in the middle of each block. No doubled labels — Sales and Appointments were each written twice, "Sales" above "Sales", "Appointments" above "Appointments" — and no doubled badges: a zero in the top-right corner AND a big zero in the box is two numbers for one thing; one number per block only. Naya Notes and Smart Notes combine under Personal as one total; Appointments and Sales each count as one notification. Numbers are real — 0 where empty, never invented.

## Why it matters

A notifications slide that doubles its labels and its numbers reads as a mistake, not a count. One block, one label, one number, centred — that is the slide a citizen trusts at a glance. And the number is always real, even when it is zero: an honest zero is intelligence, an invented number is not.

## How to use it

Rides the next calendar round after fix-11 lands: five blocks, one label at the top of each, one big number centred in the middle of each, no doubles anywhere. The eye proves one label and one centred number per block, on the page.
