*Paired Smart Note: SN-3771 — The Search Bar Sits With Your Contacts, and the Smart Tabs Jump to the List*

**Category:** Design — Messenger, contacts

## What it is

"And then the search search bar to search for all your contacts needs to be aligned with your contacts. But then you should also have the Smart Tab bars in here so that they can click any of the tabs and they'll go right to that contact list. So they can instant message with that contact list. So that's what's missing."

## Why it matters

Two missing pieces, both about getting to the right people without a dig. The search bar sits with the people it searches, on the same axis — a search bar floating away from its contacts is a search for something else. And the Smart Tab bars belong in the messenger too: one tap on a tab goes right to that contact list, so the citizen can instant message with that list directly. The tabs are the auto-organization, in the messenger.

## How to use it

Align the contact search bar with your contacts in the messenger. Put the Smart Tab bars in the messenger: click any tab to reach that contact list and instant message with it. Build it into the messenger frame, with the toggle and the expandable frame.
