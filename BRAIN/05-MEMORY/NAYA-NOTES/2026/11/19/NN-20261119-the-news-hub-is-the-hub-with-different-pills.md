*Paired Smart Note: SN-3737 — The News Hub Is the Hub With Different Pills*

**Category:** Design — news, the Hub

## What it is

The news hub's design, as he gave it on the call: not separate — news shows up in the Hub middle feed as intelligent blocks. The news hub looks exactly like the Hub except the pills read LOCAL / PROVINCIAL / GLOBAL instead of COLLECTIVE / PERSONAL / ACTIVITY; click between them to see what's going on.

## Why it matters

The citizen already knows the Hub — same feed, same blocks, same way of reading. Changing three pill words is all the learning a news hub needs. A separate news page would teach them a second system for the same job; the pills keep it one system with three scopes.

## How to use it

Build the news hub as the Hub, word for word, with the pills swapped: LOCAL / PROVINCIAL / GLOBAL in place of COLLECTIVE / PERSONAL / ACTIVITY. News lands in the middle feed as intelligent blocks; clicking a pill filters to that scope.
