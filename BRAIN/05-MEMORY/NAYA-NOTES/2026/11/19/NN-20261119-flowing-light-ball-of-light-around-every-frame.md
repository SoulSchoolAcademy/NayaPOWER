*Paired Smart Note: SN-3792 — The Flowing Light: A Ball of Light Around Every Frame*

**Category:** Design — Frame

## What it is

Around the framing of each frame — no matter the day, no matter the colours — a light goes around the outside of the frame, or around the light on the inside of the frame, just where it lights up: a ball of light flowing around the whole frame, so it always feels like it is moving, alive. And it extends: smart notes, notes, pages — everything always has this moving light going around it, always featuring it. The light travels the full perimeter and never stops.

## Why it matters

This extends the slow-circle law — the slow circle around orbs and blocks — to the frame itself and to every note and page. A still frame is a dead edge; a ball of light travelling the whole perimeter makes the frame feel alive, always moving, and it holds no matter the day and no matter the colours, because the light is the constant. It is rim light in motion, on the edge, never a wash over the board — the light features the thing it circles, it never floods it. When everything carries the moving light — smart notes, notes, pages — the whole system feels alive at once, not one lit object in a still room.

## How to use it

The frame build carries the flowing light as part of the frame itself, and the next rounds on notes and pages carry it onto every note and page: a ball of light travelling the full perimeter, never stopping, proved moving by the eye, never still.
