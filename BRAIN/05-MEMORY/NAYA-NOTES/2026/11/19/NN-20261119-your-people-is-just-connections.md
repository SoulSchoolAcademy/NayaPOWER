## What it is

Walking back up the Hub after the smart-bars dictation, he stopped on the label: "Okay, so the Your Worlds NayaNet your people." Then, confirming the name: "It says your people, it said just say connections, right?" The label reads "Your People" — it should just say "Connections".

## Why it matters

"Your People" is a sentence about people; "Connections" is the thing itself — the system's own word for the people you are connected to, the same word the connections page, the connect flow and the auto-match system already use. One name, everywhere, so the citizen never has to translate between two labels for the same door.

## How to use it

Everywhere the Hub (and the day page's worlds view) says "Your People", it reads "Connections" — the label, the heading, the tab. Proven on the page at 390 and 1920.
