## Why it matters

The anatomy is the shape every piece of intelligence in NayaNET takes — it is the format the citizen reads, the format the feed shows, the format the whole system distills into. Without a rule set, the nutshell bloats automatically: a paragraph where a sentence belongs, and the feature board stops being a feature. His order fixes the shape once — six boards, in this order, each lighting up in its own colour down the spectrum — so no builder has to guess the shape again, and no reader has to wade through a massive shell to find the nut. Taking the human note out is the same move as taking the keyword pills out of the messenger: one less thing between the citizen and the meaning. And the colours are not decoration — white for the feature, purple for the child, indigo for the grandmother's wisdom, emerald for what it means, gold for why it matters — each board announces itself by its light before a word is read.
