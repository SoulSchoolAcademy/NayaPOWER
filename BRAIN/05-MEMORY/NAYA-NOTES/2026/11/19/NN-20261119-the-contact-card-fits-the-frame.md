**Category:** Design — Messenger

## What it is

CONTACT CARD — DOES NOT FIT THE FRAME (his eye, second time he has said this style is out): when you click a contact to learn about them, the card sits out in the middle of the screen as a floating pop-up — not pro, not fit into the frame or the system. That style is OUT. THE FIX: the card must fit INTO the framing — inside the frame, not floating over the middle — and be easy to read. DYNAMIC: the numbers and the words must ALL be dynamic — living text, not flat. FOUR DIFFERENT COLOURS: the four action buttons — Smart Mail, Instant Message, Smart Meet, Call — must each be a DIFFERENT colour. Right now it is all one colour. PRO CONTACT CARD: it must feel like a pro contact card — quickly see all about them, make notes on them, message them, mail them, meet them, call them. SAME SYSTEM LANGUAGE: it must use the same language and the same buttons and icons as the rest of the system — which it does not today. The follow-on 4 eye PASS stands for size only; placement/style goes back.

## Why it matters

A floating pop-up is a visitor — it sits on top of the system instead of inside it. A contact card that fits the frame is part of the system: in the framing, easy to read, living text throughout, four actions in four colours so each is found at a glance, and the same buttons and icons as everywhere else. The eye passed the size; the style is what goes back — size was never the complaint.

## How to use it

Rides the Messenger next round (follow-on 5) as items 6–7. The eye proves the card in-frame, dynamic throughout, four colours, and the same system language.
