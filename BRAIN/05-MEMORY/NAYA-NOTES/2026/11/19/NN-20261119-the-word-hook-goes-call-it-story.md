## What it is

Still on the block's language, he named the real problem: "Then the hook would be basically the story story instead of hook. You just don't have the words hook. Is the problem. You need to change the word hook to story." The hook IS the story — the thing itself was never wrong. The word "hook" is the problem, so the word goes and "story" takes its place everywhere.

## Why it matters

A word is a promise about what a thing is. "Hook" promises a trick — something to catch you with. "Story" promises the thing itself: what happened, told straight. The block is a story, so it is called a story, and the citizen meets the real thing instead of a device. Words that misname the work make the work harder to trust.

## How to use it

Search every block, brief, template and label for the word "hook" and change it to "story" — the hook line, the hook field, the hook label, all of it. The story is the headline's companion: headline first, then the story.
