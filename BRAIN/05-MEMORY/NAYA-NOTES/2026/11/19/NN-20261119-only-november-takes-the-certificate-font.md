**Category:** Design — Calendar, day pages

## What it is

Walking the calendar day page, he gave the month header its treatment — then corrected himself in the same breath. "November 2026" gets the certificate treatment, the same as a citizen's name on the certificates: elite, matching the frame, looking special. Time sits in its own button, centered, directly underneath November 2026. On the left, sort of centered: "Thursday the 19th" — Thursday in the same certificate font as November, and the 19 in nice blocks, green. Then the correction: only the word "November" takes the certificate font. The "19" does not — it stays like the 19 on the day itself, dashboard-style real numbers with the green edge.

## Why it matters

The certificate font is for the word that names the month — the elite, special treatment belongs to November, and putting the date number in it too would spend that specialness twice and blur the two kinds of information. The 19 is a number, and numbers on this system are dashboard digit tiles with the day's colour around the edge — the same 19 the citizen already reads on the day itself. Same page, same number, same treatment; the month is the name, the number is the number.

## How to use it

Build the month header as: "November" in the certificate font; the "19" as dashboard digit tiles with the green edge, never in the certificate font. Time in its own button, centered under the month. "Thursday the 19th" to the left, Thursday in the certificate font. 2026 is unspecified — leave it as it is.
