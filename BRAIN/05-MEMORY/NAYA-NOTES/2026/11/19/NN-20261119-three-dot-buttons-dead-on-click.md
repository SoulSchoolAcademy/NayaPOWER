## What it is

On the live Personal walk, Shawn: "So I noticed when I click the three button the three little…" — the three little dots on each keyword pill show a hover hint ("menu sidebar smart messenger or neither") but clicking them does nothing at all — can't do anything with them. They must open that choice.

## Why it matters

A control that hints at a choice and then does nothing is worse than no control at all — it teaches the person that tapping here is pointless. Every dot that shows must open what it promises.

## How to use it

Rides the next Personal round with the keyword-bar fix. The eye proves the three dots open the choice they hint at — menu / sidebar / Smart Messenger / neither — on click.
