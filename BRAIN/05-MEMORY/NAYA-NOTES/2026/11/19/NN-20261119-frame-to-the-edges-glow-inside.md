## Why it matters

The frame is the presentation. A frame that does not hug the edges, a glow on the outside instead of the inside, one sentence per board with dots and icons scattered around — it doesn't present, it doesn't look formed, it reads as mess. Clean means the presentation fits its frame and feels part of it, the light lives inside, each board carries enough words to be a presentation of its own, and the next click is obvious and never blocking. The same frame discipline carries to About and Skills — one concept, reused, so the whole Personal area feels like parts of each other, one royal piece.

## How to use it

The walkthrough/agreement round rebuilds the visual — and the text unfold with it — inside the frame: frame to the screen edges, glow around the inside, Naya's photo in the top-right corner, three to five sentences per board, fewer boards, the Next button close to the bottom without blocking (or below the bottom frame), dots only at the bottom if at all. The calendar frame is an allowed alternative wherever it gives more spacing. Then the whole concept goes again into the Personal area — About and Skills, frame by frame, same frame, same discipline.
