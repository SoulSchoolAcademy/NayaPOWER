The check: every button on the page, clicked through, lands on the right page — current and relevant, not stale, not a dead end, not the wrong place. A page passes only when its buttons go where they should.

## Why it matters

A beautiful page with a button that goes to the wrong place is a broken promise — the citizen clicks in trust and lands somewhere they did not ask to go. Buttons are the page's word: where they go IS what the page says. Double-checking every destination, on every walk, is what keeps the word true. And it costs almost nothing to check while the eye is already walking the page — the expensive version is finding out from a citizen who already clicked.

## How to use it

On every eye walk and every checker, from now on: click through the buttons and verify each one lands on the right page — current and relevant, not stale, not a dead end, not the wrong place. A page passes only when its buttons go where they should. This rides every eye walk alongside the render checks, and it applies to rounds already running too — not only to rounds dispatched after the ruling.
