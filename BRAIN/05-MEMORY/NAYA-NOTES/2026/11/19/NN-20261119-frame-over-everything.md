**Category:** Design — Presentation, frame standard

## What it is

The frame goes over everything in the whole presentation, then each frame optimized. Frame = how max-level viewpoints are reached; text AND visual, About + Skills too — frame by frame, sideways or down. MEMORY (91).

## Why it matters

The frame stopped being one feature's presentation and became the presentation: frame the whole thing first, then perfect each frame — never perfect one corner while the rest stands unframed. A frame carries text AND visual together, in one viewpoint — that is how a max-level viewpoint is reached. About and Skills take the same treatment: the same kind of story, told frame by frame.

## How to use it

Frame the whole presentation first, then optimize each frame. Put text and visual together in every frame. Carry the same treatment to About and Skills — frame by frame, sideways or down.
