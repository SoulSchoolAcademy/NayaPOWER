## What it is

The wisdom slide redesign: the whole presentation scales up about 50% so it presents across the whole screen — nothing small, nothing lost in the middle. Order from the top: (1) "WISDOM OF THE DAY" at the very top · (2) the S orb/icon below it · (3) the greeting — "Good morning, Shawn" / "Good afternoon, Shawn", whichever the time of day actually is · (4) the quote below that. The slide should feel alive and moving, not static — the S orb flowing, the whole thing presenting, not sitting still. The ~50% is a guide, not a literal number — hit the sweet spot where it is the ideal size, centred on the page, and looks extraordinary. The maker LOOKS at it and makes sure it is right and looks right first, before calling it done. Don't build the number blind. This refines the morning ruling, which had the S orb, the greeting dead centre, WISDOM OF THE DAY below, and the quote centred — the label now leads at the top and everything is bigger.

## Why it matters

Wisdom is the first thing the citizen meets in the day — it should lead, fill the screen, and feel alive. A small static slide in the middle of the page loses the wisdom; a big presenting one, label first, orb flowing, greeting true to the hour, gives the day its feeling the moment it opens. And the size is judged by the eye, not the number — the maker looks first, so the slide is right when it lands.

## How to use it

Rides the next calendar round after fix-11 lands: label first, orb, greeting true to the time of day, quote, ~50% bigger, dynamic. The maker looks at it and makes sure it is right and looks right first — the eye proves the sweet spot.
