## What it is

When opened, the world clock fills the whole inner area below the clock — where the notifications sit — a full setting panel, not a small pop-up. Pick city or timezone from a drop-down, not a handful of city buttons — a citizen may not be from any of those cities; the clock then just adjusts. NO flat buttons anywhere in there — every button a real 3D object with depth; the Close button must POP — the flat green with white text barely reads and is not pro. Everything pro level.

## Why it matters

A setting squeezed into a small pop-up with a handful of city buttons leaves most citizens out — they are not from any of those cities. A full panel with a drop-down covers everybody, and buttons that are real 3D objects with depth are the pro level he expects everywhere. A flat button that barely reads is not a small miss — it is the panel failing to be pro.

## How to use it

Rides the next calendar round after fix-11 lands: full inner panel, drop-down city/timezone, no flat buttons, Close that pops. The eye proves the panel fills the inner area and every button reads as a real object.
