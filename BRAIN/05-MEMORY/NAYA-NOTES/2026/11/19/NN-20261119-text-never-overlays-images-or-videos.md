*Paired Smart Note: SN-3859 — Text Never Overlays Images or Videos*

**Category:** Design — Membership

## What it is

Shawn, on the live call, watching the membership eye walk inside the lesson view: "Okay, so the only thing is I can see is like obviously the text is overlaying the images. You can't have the text overlaying the images. Okay. Or the videos because that doesn't look good. Right?"

The eye's partial verdict the same hour confirms it at full size: the Academy headline "Five days. Five wins.", the Day 1 lesson title, and the FAC headline "Welcome in — you're hired" are page text sitting on top of images — the FAC one repeated as plain text below too. Text over a picture, text over a video — it doesn't look good.

## Why it matters

Text fighting the picture underneath reads as a mistake, not a design — the words are hard to read and the image is spoiled at the same time. A headline earns its own clean ground. The membership is the shop window for the whole system; a headline pasted over the art tells a cold person the work is sloppy before they read a word of it.

## How to use it

No headline, title, or label ever sits on top of an image or a video. Headlines go on plain ground, or they are baked into the artwork itself — one or the other, never page text over a picture. The membership updating pass moves every headline off its image (Academy, Day 1, FAC) and proves it at full size.
