**Category:** Design — Calendar

## What it is

The cover slide is OUT — "a useless page, all it is is a pretty picture on the page." "A lot of wisdom. Get rid of the cover one." No cover as its own slide. He will choose a specific cover for each day; that cover is the BACKGROUND behind the day's slides — notifications, journal, and the rest — but NOT the wisdom slide, which already has its own nice background. "We'll just have a natural background in the journal" — a natural background matching the colour of that day for each of these slides. The day opens on Wisdom of the Day (slide 1), then Notifications. This supersedes the 13:20 order — Cover 1 / Wisdom 2 / Notifications 3 — the cover is no longer a slide at all, and it answers his open question from 13:20, "does the cover even need to be its own slide — why is that page so valuable?" — it does not; its value is the day's feeling and identity, carried as the background.

## Why it matters

A slide you flip past adds a step and gives nothing back — the cover's value was never the slide, it was the feeling of the day the moment you open it. As the background, the cover gives that feeling to every slide at once, in the day's own colour, without costing a flip. And the day opens on Wisdom, where it should — the first thing the citizen meets is the wisdom of the day, not a picture.

## How to use it

Rides the next calendar round after fix-11 lands: cover slide removed, the day's cover behind the day's slides (not wisdom), day opening on Wisdom then Notifications. The eye proves no cover slide, the background behind the slides, and the new order.
