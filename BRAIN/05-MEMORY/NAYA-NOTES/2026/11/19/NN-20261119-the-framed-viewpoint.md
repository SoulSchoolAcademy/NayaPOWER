**Category:** Design — Presentation

## What it is

Shawn, on the live call, setting the law for how everything presents: "Instead of going scrolling down or scrolling across, or anything like that, we're just actually bringing people into the frame." Every area gets the same treatment — each step is its own frame: visual presentations framed as they go through, text presentations framed as they go through — so a person never scrolls down or across; the next frame comes INTO their viewpoint. It applies to the agreement (visual + text), the membership site, the mail, and the messenger — every lesson, every message, every page. One clean frame coming into view, no scrolling, no telling across from down. "Now we have our viewpoints. Our viewport."

## Why it matters

Scrolling is the old internet's way of making a person do the work of finding the next thing. A frame that comes into view does the work for them — one thing at a time, exactly where their eyes already are. That is the whole promise of the network in one gesture: the person never chases the content; the content arrives.

## How to use it

Every area presents frame by frame: the agreement as a framed presentation (walkthrough fix-9), the membership lessons frame by frame (membership rebuild), and the mail and the messenger the same way — each message, each piece, its own frame coming into the viewpoint. The mail framing round and the Messenger framing round follow; Messenger's waits for its one-item fix round to land (same file, one round at a time).
