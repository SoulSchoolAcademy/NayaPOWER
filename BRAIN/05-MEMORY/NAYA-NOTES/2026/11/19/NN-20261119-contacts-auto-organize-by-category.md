**Category:** System — Contacts, organization

## What it is

His suggestion, then confirmed and generalized: just as the Hub auto-organizes the feeds, it auto-organizes your contacts. Contacts are categorized, and you click the category to see the contacts in that category — not just all contacts all at once. The same auto-organization the feeds already have, now for the people you know.

## Why it matters

A contact list that shows everyone all at once is a pile, not a system — the citizen has to dig through everybody to find anybody. Categorized contacts turn the pile into shelves: family, friends, work, the people you actually need — one click and there they are. The Hub already proved the pattern on feeds; contacts are the same pattern on people. Findable in one click, or a search — never a scroll through everyone.

## How to use it

Categorize contacts automatically; the citizen clicks a category and sees the contacts in that category, not the whole list at once. The categories follow the same auto-organization the feeds use — no manual sorting for the citizen to do.
