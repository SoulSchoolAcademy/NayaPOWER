*Paired Smart Note: SN-3782 — The Post, A Third Thing*

**Category:** Design — Posts

## What it is

A post is a post, with or without images. You might add an image, then write whatever you want — or say "Naya, write this for a post" saying XYZ, and the post has a headline, a hook, in a nutshell what it is, and maybe an image. Or it's just your direct words — "share my direct words" rather than reformatting it. Or you tell Naya "make it sound engaging, funny, whatever" — your call. Posts are still formatted in the same intelligent block and framing — but a post is NOT a block of intelligence and NOT a full report. It's a post, with or without images.

## Why it matters

Three things now have names, and the names stop three kinds of confusion: a post treated as intelligence it isn't, a post graded as a report it isn't, and a citizen's direct words rewritten when they asked for them as-is. The post borrows the frame — headline, hook, in a nutshell — but its job is to be a post: an image if there is one, the citizen's words or Naya's, in whatever tone they ask for. The frame is shared; the job is different. And "hook" is a post word — news blocks keep HEADLINE → IN A NUTSHELL → STORY, never "hook".

## How to use it

When a citizen says "write this for a post", give them headline, hook, in a nutshell, maybe an image. When they say "share my direct words", share them as-is, no reformatting. When they ask for a tone — engaging, funny, whatever — that is their call. Never file a post as a smart note or grade it as a Naya note.
