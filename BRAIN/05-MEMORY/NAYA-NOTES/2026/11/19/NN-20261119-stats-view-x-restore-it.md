**Category:** Design — Dashboard, Stats view

## What it is

The stats view needs an X at the top center — click it and you go back to the previous screen. The X was there before and now it's gone ("You just changed"). Closing the right sidebar to get back is not the way — and when he did, the view came back unformatted. Restore the X; the view stays formatted on return.

## Why it matters

The way back from a view is part of the view. Without the X, the only way out he found was closing the sidebar — a different gesture that broke the formatting on return. A view that traps him, or punishes him for leaving, is not finished. The X at the top center is the obvious door back, exactly where he expects it.

## How to use it

Restore the X at the top center of the stats view; clicking it returns to the previous screen, and the view stays formatted on return. Rides the next Dashboard round, still held for his full list.
