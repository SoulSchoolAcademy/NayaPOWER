*Paired Smart Note: SN-3779 — Claim Your Smart Link, Not Your Name*

**Category:** Design — Dashboard

## What it is

The Dashboard feed says "claim your name" — it should say "claim your smart link", NOT your name, because the name is different from the smart link. You make a smart link for the system, then you can track any link and get the stats on it. The stats the Dashboard shows by default when you go in: how many clicks and how many sales you got to NayaNet. Business: clicks to the page, how many people joined / connected with you.

## Why it matters

The name is who you are; the smart link is what the system tracks. Calling the link a name confuses the two — and the whole point of the smart link is the tracking: make the link, track any link, get the stats on it. And the Dashboard opens on the numbers that matter by default: clicks and sales to NayaNet, clicks and joins for a business — the count of people who came and the count of people who connected, before anything else.

## How to use it

The Dashboard says "claim your smart link", never "claim your name". Its default stats are clicks and sales to NayaNet; a Business board's default stats are clicks to the page and joins. Rides the next Dashboard round, still held for his full list.
