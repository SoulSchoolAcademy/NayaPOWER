**Category:** Design — Naya Notes

## What it is

The Naya note is the full report, the full letter — not just child/grandma/in-a-nutshell, not just what it is and why it matters, but the FULL report of what it is. Same format, same section-by-section presentation, but the whole thing — sometimes things need to be explained in more than three or four sentences. Naya notes are the DEFAULT. A quick note on something is fine too — no extra effort, it just displays the whole thing there. And Naya reads the Naya notes — that's how she knows.

## Why it matters

The smart note is the essence; the Naya note is the record the essence comes from. If the record is cut short, the essence has nothing to distill — and Naya herself reads the Naya notes, so what is written there is literally what she knows. Naming Naya notes the default settles which form an ordinary note takes: the full thing, however long it needs to be, with a quick note allowed the same standing. The length is flexible; the format is not — the same section-by-section presentation carries both.

## How to use it

Write the note as a Naya note first, full length, in the Intelligent Block template — what it is → why it matters → how to use it — then distill the smart note from it. Never shorten the record to fit the essence; never promote the record to public without the citizen's choice. The Naya Note habit stands: the record and the lesson are two different files, filed in the same pass.
