*Paired Smart Note: SN-3842 — The Membership Is Not the Link*

**Category:** Design — Membership

## What it is

He clicked Membership and Academy on the link sent this morning — "this is not the membership and it's not Academy. That's not what we had before." The real membership system was started before — log in with ANY code to reach the login area — but he never saw it again. The order: complete the membership system — "really quite simple." The design: log in, another FEATURE COVER featuring that membership; scroll down, in the AREA BLOCK IN THE MIDDLE choose your lessons; each lesson a little THUMBNAIL and a BUTTON, IN A NUTSHELL; click in, ANOTHER COVER about the lesson, then the VIDEOS or the POWERCASTS right there — the cover, and below it FRAMED VIEWPORTS featuring the lesson or that video.

## Why it matters

A link called the membership when it is not the membership spends his trust and his time on the wrong thing. The real membership already exists in his memory — the job is to complete the one that was started, in the shape he already described, and never to send the other link as if it were the membership again.

## How to use it

Find the original any-code login build first and complete it: login → feature cover → area block in the middle → lessons (thumbnail + button, in a nutshell) → lesson cover → videos or powercasts in framed viewports. Keep the any-code login; don't rebuild auth. Never send the membership-personal link as the membership.
