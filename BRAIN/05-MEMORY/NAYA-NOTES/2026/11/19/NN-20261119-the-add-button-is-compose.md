## What it is

Its choices are the scopes Personal / Business / Worlds — NOT Mail / Activity / Worlds.

The plus was read as "add a contact" because nothing said otherwise — a button without a purpose gets guessed at, and the guess was wrong. Naming it compose settles it: the button creates another message, and its choices are the scopes of the citizen's worlds — Personal, Business, Worlds — not the app's bottom-bar destinations.
