*Paired Smart Note: SN-3750 — Socials Is Icons, Stats, and Re-Mappable Buttons*

**Category:** Design — Personal, Socials

## What it is

The Socials view is settled in three parts. Socials = icons for their socials — click an icon to access that site. The Socials page shows stats on their socials — clicks, or notifications that come through — so they know if there's any action there. Honest boundary recorded: clicks are our own tracked counts; platform notifications only if the platform sends them (no connection today). And the three cover buttons (Naya Academy / AI Masterclass / Fast Action Club) are re-mappable in Settings to their socials or any URL, if they choose; the default stays as built.

## Why it matters

Socials is a doorway, not a destination: an icon, one click, and the citizen is on that site. The stats tell them whether the doorway is busy — clicks we count ourselves, notifications only where the platform actually sends them, never invented. And the cover buttons are theirs to aim: the three defaults stay as built, but if the citizen wants a button to point at their socials or anywhere else, Settings lets them re-map it. Defaults that stay, choices that work — the same citizen-choice standard as the sidebar.

## How to use it

Build Socials as icons, one click to the site, with stats shown (clicks tracked by us; platform notifications only if the platform sends them). Make the three cover buttons re-mappable in Settings to socials or any URL, defaults unchanged. The About/Skills round proves the view, the stats, and the re-mapping on the page.
