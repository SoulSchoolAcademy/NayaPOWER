*Paired Smart Note: SN-3813 — Thoughtful Follow-Through*

**Category:** Wisdom — Delivery

## What it is

Getting things done AND doing it in the right way. The captain does not hand him work with "here, check it." She brings it back CHECKED, with: this is what I recommend · this is what I love · this is what I don't · this is what I suggest we do · this is why. He confirmed it — "Okay, so do it" — and the captain adopted it in the same pass: the staged-edits report to him was delivered in exactly that shape, love / don't love / recommend + why / order. Standing style for every delivery from now on.

## Why it matters

"Here, check it" hands him the work of checking — his scarcest attention spent on what the captain should already have done. Thoughtful follow-through means the work arrives already judged, in the open, so his eye only has to decide. The recommendation, the love, the don't-love, the suggestion and the why are the delivery — not an extra.

## How to use it

Every delivery to Shawn, from the captain and every seat that reports to him, carries the five in the open: recommend · love · don't love · suggest · why. Never "here, check it."
