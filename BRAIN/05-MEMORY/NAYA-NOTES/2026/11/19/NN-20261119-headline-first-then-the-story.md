**Category:** Design — intelligent blocks, the news block

## What it is

Refining the intelligent block's language on the live call, he settled the order of the block: "Yeah, I think it actually should be headline first and then the in a nutshell." Then the name, plain: "Headline. We call it headline." He had tried the alternatives out loud first — "Feature story. or feature." — and landed on headline. The block reads: headline first, then the quick story right underneath it, all inside the intelligent block.

## Why it matters

The block's top line is its name, and the name has to be the plainest word there is. Headline says what it is in one word — no "in a nutshell" label, no "feature story" dressing. The reader sees the headline, then reads the story under it, and the block speaks for itself.

## How to use it

Every intelligent block opens with a headline — the label is "Headline", never "in a nutshell", never "feature story" — and the story (the in-a-nutshell summary) sits right underneath it, inside the same block. Check every block language file for the old labels and swap them.
