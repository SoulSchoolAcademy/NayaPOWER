*Paired Smart Note: SN-3777 — The Info Card, Full Framed and Extraordinary*

**Category:** Design — Messenger, Connections

## What it is

The info card as it stands "sucks" — a small little pop-up. It must be full framed, beautiful, featuring that person: make notes on them; see the instant messages or mail to them; see the data — the percentage matched, in what areas, what the connection is, what's relevant; message them, mail them, schedule a Smart Meet, call, etc., all from the card. "The card itself needs to be extraordinary in its presentation."

## Why it matters

The info card is the whole person in one place — and right now it presents them as a small little pop-up, which says the person is small. A full framed card says the opposite: this person matters, here is everything about them, and everything you can do with them is right here. Notes, messages, mail, the match, the connection, the call — one card, one frame, extraordinary.

## How to use it

Rebuild the info card as a full framed presentation of the person: notes, instant messages and mail to them, the match data (percentage, areas, connection, relevance), and message / mail / Smart Meet / call, all from the card. Rides the Hub follow-up round (messenger lane), under the pop-up law — no more little tiny pop-ups.
