**Category:** Design — Personal, Hub

## What it is

The companion to the literal-copy ruling: a Personal / Business / Space page has its feature cover on top, with its menus and search and the standard menu bar Personal pages carry — and everything below that mirrors the Hub exactly. His framing: imagine the Hub with a cover feature and the standard Personal menu bar added on top; below it, the Hub. With that presentation in place, the Personal area jumps a lot further ahead.

## Why it matters

The copy ruling needs a boundary, or the maker will either copy the Hub whole and lose the Personal identity, or rebuild the Personal page from scratch and lose the Hub. The boundary is the top: the feature cover and the standard menu bar are what make it Personal — everything below is the Hub, untouched. One seam, in one place, and the citizen gets both: their own world on top, the whole system below.

## How to use it

Build the Personal page as the Hub below, with the feature cover + menus + search + standard menu bar on top. Nothing else differs. The mirror round proves the seam sits exactly at the top and nowhere else.
