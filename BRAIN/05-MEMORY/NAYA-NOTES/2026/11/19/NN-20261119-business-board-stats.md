**Category:** Design — Dashboard, Business boards

## What it is

The Business board shows the clicks to the page and how many people joined — "how many connected with you, how many clicks to your page. So connected with you, joins." Two numbers, both real: clicks in, joins out. That is what a business board's stats are.

## Why it matters

A business board that shows anything else is showing the wrong thing. Clicks tell him the page is being reached; joins tell him people connected. Those two numbers are the whole story of whether the business page is working — everything else is decoration around them.

## How to use it

Every Business board carries its stats as clicks to the page and joins. Rides the next Dashboard round, still held for his full list.
