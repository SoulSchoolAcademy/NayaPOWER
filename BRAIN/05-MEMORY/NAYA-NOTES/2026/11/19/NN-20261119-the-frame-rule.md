**Category:** Design — Standing Law

## What it is

On the live Personal walk, Shawn set the standing law, from now on: always try to take the apps and put them INSIDE the frame. Unless we're on a feed. The system is basically frames, feeds, covers, sidebars — that's how it works.

## Why it matters

This is the shape of the whole system in one sentence. An app outside its frame floats, drifts, and reads as a pop-up — the exact thing he has called out twice on the contact card. Inside the frame it belongs; the frame is the room, the feed is the one place that flows free, and everything else has its walls.

## How to use it

Standing law for every seat, every brief, every page from now on: apps go inside the frame; the feed is the exception. The eye proves it on the next Personal round and on every page after.
