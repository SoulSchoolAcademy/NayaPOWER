## What it is

On the live call, looking at the Personal mirror (screenshot `965d7d84…`), Shawn on the keyword/category bars: "The problem is is like you have all the keywords all bunched up and… And then they're supposed to be just running across essentially across the top. So that then you just click whatever one and then the. They'll show up, right? And then they should all be like beautiful nice elegant colors like the personal, the business, the world. And they should look like they're the same button set. Right? And the same type of glow. And but they shouldn't be in a whole bunch of buttons like was layered row one, two, three. There should just be one row and they just scroll across the the page. Right? Or they end up scrolling underneath. The page. Or those the sidebars. But they scroll across. And then you can just click to see whatever whatever category you want to see. And then it automatically creates categories when you add to the feed, right? To the personal business or world. So that it auto-organizes. But it's just one across and then it's not. multiple layered."

The fix: ONE row running across the top — the bars scroll across the page (underneath, under the sidebars). Click one and that category shows up. The look: beautiful, elegant colours like Personal / Business / World — the SAME button set, the SAME type of glow. Auto-organization: categories auto-create when you add to the feed (Personal / Business / World) — it auto-organizes.

## Why it matters

Bunched, layered rows read as clutter; one scrolling row reads as a single elegant control. The bars are doors into categories — one row, one gesture, and the system does the organizing itself instead of asking the person to.

## How to use it

Rides the next Personal round. The eye proves one row across the top, scrolling under the sidebars, same button set and glow as Personal / Business / World, categories auto-created from the feed.
