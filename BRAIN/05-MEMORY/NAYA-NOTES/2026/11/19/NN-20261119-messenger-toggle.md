## What it is

"What needs to be what needs to be there is a toggle to switch from instant message to Smart Mail. You just click the button or switch the toggle and it'll switch to mail and then that's what needs to be there."

## Why it matters

The messenger and the mail are one system with two speeds — instant and letter — and the citizen moves between them with one gesture, not by leaving the room. One click and the same frame that held the instant message now holds the mail: same people, same conversation, the other speed. Without the toggle, the messenger is a room with no door to the mail.

## How to use it

Put a toggle in the messenger frame: click it and instant message switches to Smart Mail. Naya's summary: instead of PowerCast as a button, the toggle flips between instant message and smart mail. Build it in the messenger lane, with the expandable frame.
