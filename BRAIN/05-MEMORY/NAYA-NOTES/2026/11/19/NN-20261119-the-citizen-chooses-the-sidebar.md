**Category:** Design — Hub, Personal, sidebar

## What it is

Walking the Hub inch by inch, his eye caught two defects: the sidebar won't reopen after closing, and Smart Messenger won't pull back up. The ruling that fixes them both: restore the right sidebar as the same beautiful sidebar as before, colour-aligned so it matches the left sidebar's sequence. And the citizen gets to choose — the IM bar, the DM bar, the sidebar, or neither — and whichever they choose has to work: it opens and closes reliably. The Messenger opening empty ("room capability is not exposed yet") is the current broken state this ruling fixes.

## Why it matters

A sidebar that won't reopen and a messenger that won't pull back up are doors that don't open — the citizen is locked out of their own world. Restoring the beautiful sidebar he already approved, instead of building a new one, keeps what was good. And the choice is the point: the citizen decides which bar they want, or none at all — but a choice that doesn't work is worse than no choice, because it promises a door and gives a wall. Every option has to open and close, every time.

## How to use it

Restore the right sidebar as the same beautiful sidebar as before, colour-aligned with the left sidebar's sequence. Offer the citizen the choice: IM bar, DM bar, sidebar, or neither — and prove each option opens and closes reliably. The Messenger opening empty is the defect to close, not a state to ship.
