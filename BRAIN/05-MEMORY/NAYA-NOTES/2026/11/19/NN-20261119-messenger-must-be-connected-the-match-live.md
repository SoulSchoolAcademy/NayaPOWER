## What it is

Shawn ordered "make sure that's working" — the IM / DM messaging on the sides — and restated the requirement in-call: the IM/DM messaging must show they are CONNECTED to all the feeds — Personal and the different feeds — with everything happening as a game of MATCH, matching the content with the related user.

The captain's check result, told to Shawn straight: it isn't working. Clicking DM on Sophia opens a messenger panel that does not know who Sophia is — no conversation, no messages, no box to type in, no feed, no matched content; it says the capability "is not exposed yet." Every tab in it is empty the same way, and her profile / about / mail buttons all open that same empty panel instead of what they promise. What IS working: percentages show on everyone, and the main feed does load (his "What is NayaNET, ultimately?" note was in it at check time).

Status: the messenger is a PLACEHOLDER, not connected to the feeds or the match. Fix round queued BEHIND the Hub sort round (same Hub file, one round at a time): wire the messenger to a real conversation per person, with their feed and matched content beside it. Not done.

## Why it matters

The messenger is where a citizen talks to a person the system matched them with — if it opens empty, the match is a number on a card and nothing more. "Connected to all the feeds" is the whole idea of the game of MATCH: the person, their content, and the conversation in one place, so talking to them is talking inside the world you share. A panel that does not know who Sophia is breaks that at the first click — and every button that opens the same empty panel teaches the citizen the buttons do not do what they say. The fix is not a nicer empty panel; it is a real conversation per person, with their feed and matched content beside it.

## How to use it

When the messenger round runs (behind the Hub sort round, same file, one round at a time): give every person a real conversation — opening DM on Sophia opens Sophia's conversation, with messages, a box to type in, her feed and her matched content beside it. No tab opens empty; no button opens a panel that does not know the person it was opened from. Verify by clicking DM on a real person and holding a real exchange — percentages showing is not the proof; a conversation happening is.
