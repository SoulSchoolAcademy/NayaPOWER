## Why it matters

The stars are the citizen's voice on a note's value — if lit and unlit read the same, the ranking says nothing and nobody bothers to rank. A star that reads at a glance, big and lit like a jewel, is an invitation: people want to touch it, want to rank it, and the feed learns what is valuable. The comment panel is where a citizen adds their own intelligence to a note — if it opens as a clutter of small text and buttons, the thought dies before it is typed. Input at the top, keywords underneath: the first thing you see is where to speak, and the second thing you see is what you might say. That is the whole panel doing its job — and doing it beautifully is what makes people come back to speak again.

## How to use it

When the note actions are next touched: make the lit star unmistakable at a glance — more contrast against unlit, bigger, more objectnality, a flow around the lit state — and check it by eye, lit vs unlit side by side, before calling it done. When the comment panel is next touched: input at the very top, lettering with depth, white at rest and purple on hover; strip the small text out; put the five keyword suggestions underneath — Ask a question, Build on this, Contribute intelligence, Report an outcome, Add evidence — and nothing else. Judge the panel by his bar: clean, elegant, beautiful.
