*Paired Smart Note: SN-3814 — Slides That Explain Themselves*

**Category:** Design — Calendar

## What it is

The Your Worlds slide gets a TITLE and an explanation of what each world is and what it does — icons alone told him nothing. The News slide is labelled as the news hub — Local / Province / Global — with SMALLER icons and a count of new stories on each icon. Every slide gets a natural background in the day's colour — "I'll have a natural background matching the colour of that day for each of these slides" — "Bear with me I'm picking out the covers." Dispatch holds until his covers arrive; no invented covers, no placeholders. (The background law itself rides chain (u) — the cover as the day's background, not a slide; this note carries the title-and-explanation law and the covers gate.)

## Why it matters

A slide that shows only icons asks him to guess what it is for, and a guess is work he should never have to do. A title and one line of explanation say what the slide is in a glance; a count on each news door says where the new stories are before a single tap. The slide explains itself, so the citizen never has to ask.

## How to use it

Rides fix-12 (item 11 and the news item): titles and explanations on the slides, smaller news icons with live counts, and the natural background once his covers arrive. The eye proves each on the page.
