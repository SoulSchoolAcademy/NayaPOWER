*Paired Smart Note: SN-3748 — About's Four Buttons and How They Behave*

**Category:** Design — Personal, About

## What it is

The About top buttons are settled: About (default, the text) / Photos / Videos / Socials across the top. And the behaviour is settled too: scroll down = unfold in learning about them; click slideshow = slideshow about them; Photos = all photos; Videos = all videos, link-only — YouTube URL entry or pasted links from Instagram/TikTok/anywhere, NO uploads (matches the membership media law).

## Why it matters

Four buttons, four views, and each one does exactly what it says — no guessing, no hidden behaviour. Scrolling unfolds the story; clicking plays it as a slideshow; Photos shows the photos; Videos shows the videos, and the videos arrive as links the citizen pastes, never as uploads the system has to store. The no-uploads rule is the membership media law applied here: the citizen's videos live where they already live, and NayaNET points at them.

## How to use it

Build the About top row as About / Photos / Videos / Socials, About the default. Scroll unfolds; the slideshow button slideshows. Photos shows all photos. Videos takes a YouTube URL or a pasted link from Instagram/TikTok/anywhere — no uploads. The About/Skills round proves each button and each behaviour on the page.
