*Paired Smart Note: SN-3827 — Settings Top-Right, Clear of Everything*

**Category:** Design — Dashboard

## What it is

The settings page overlays the X button. Settings belong in the top-right corner, clear of everything else. And the settings panel items — Preferences, app behavior, identity, install-as-app — sit at the bottom of the scroll today; they should show up when you click the Settings tab, inside the settings panel, inside the frame. Always put things inside the frame.

## Why it matters

The X is the way out — settings sitting on top of it takes the exit away. And items stranded at the bottom of a scroll are outside the thing they belong to; the panel is the frame for its own items, and they appear when Settings is clicked.

## How to use it

On the Dashboard, settings go top-right, clear of the X and of everything else. Preferences, app behavior, identity and install-as-app live inside the settings panel and appear when the Settings tab is clicked — never at the bottom of the scroll.
