*Paired Smart Note: SN-3716 — The Smart Bars Scroll and Auto-Categorize*

**Category:** Design — Hub smart bars / smart tabs

## What it is

The smart bars on the Hub, as Shawn walked them live: "They're actually supposed to scroll across right? And if I click any of them, it should pull up the section of them. But I noticed we had so many smart notes before we had it basically with the purpose of the bar is it plays auto-organization. So if you have a whole bunch of smart notes or posts or on the feed, it's just going to when you click the category it'll pull up just the smart notes related to that category. So all the tops are just auto-categorization that the system doesn't give you one feed of everything and looks at what the feed is, categorizes it, puts it into that category. And then so it shows up in that category. Will it show up in the all? Yes, but it'll show up in the the category. So our system by default always looks at oh what is this? Okay, what would be the right category for this? And then puts the category as a smart note or I mean as a a smart bar. Smart tab. And then they can just click to see all the notes within Naya or all the different categories. And they just slowly scroll across the top."

## Why it matters

The bar is the whole system in one gesture: not one feed of everything, but an automatic organizer. Every note and post is looked at, given its right category, and shown in All and in its category. Click a tab and you see only that category's notes. That is the difference between a feed and NayaNET — the system organizes itself, and the citizen just clicks and learns.

## How to use it

The Hub's smart tabs scroll slowly across the top; each tab opens its category's section — only that category's smart notes and posts — while All still shows everything. Every new note or post is auto-categorized on entry. Proven on the page at 390 and 1920.
