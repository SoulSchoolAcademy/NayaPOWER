**Category:** Design — Presentation

## What it is

The same beautiful format — section by section, each section a NEW COLOR and a NEW ELEVATION, so it breaks up intelligence into a valuable beautiful 10-star output people can take in and read faster. Most people just skim — they look, they see in a nutshell, why it matters, what's in it for them, how to apply it, how to connect it.

## Why it matters

Both kinds of note take the same format, and the format is doing the work. Intelligence broken into sections, each a new color and a new elevation, is intelligence a skimming reader can take in — the nutshell, why it matters, what's in it for them, how to apply it, how to connect it, all at a glance. A flat wall of text, however wise, fails the format: the reader never gets to the value. The color and the elevation are not decoration; they are what make a long piece readable in seconds. Ten stars is the presentation as much as the words.

## How to use it

Every note render — smart note, Naya note, post framing — presents section by section, each section a new color and a new elevation. A render that flattens the sections into one colour and one elevation is not the format, however accurate its words. The design laws and the brief template carry this as the presentation law.
