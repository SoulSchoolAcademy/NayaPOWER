## What it is

His defect call on the Dashboard, voice call ~21:03 MST: the orbs are aligned to the gauges — but sometimes when you switch in and out they don't stay aligned. His words: "It needs to stay aligned."

It went onto the Dashboard eye walk in progress as an observation check — and the eye confirmed it the same night (~21:13 MST): tapping an orb's "Show in gauge" shrinks the 9 orbs and crowds them tight against the gauge rim, unevenly; the selected orb enlarges, glows, overlaps the rim; tapping another orb back, or switching the window back to All, does NOT restore it — only a full reload does. The orb tap is the trigger, exactly the defect he named.

## Why it matters

The orbs are the face of the Dashboard — the living line between the person and their numbers. A line that holds on first sight and breaks on the second tap is a promise that doesn't keep. Alignment isn't a first impression; it's a state that has to survive every switch, every tap, every return. If it only holds until you touch it, it doesn't hold.

## How to use it

The orb-ring fix round carries this as its bullseye: tap any orb, switch in and out, switch windows, come back — the ring holds its alignment every time, with no reload to rescue it. The eye proves it by tapping, not by looking: the tap is the test, because the tap was the trigger.
