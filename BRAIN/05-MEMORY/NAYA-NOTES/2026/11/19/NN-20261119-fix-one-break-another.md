## What it is

His words, plain: "Really strange how I asked you to fix one thing you'll fix one thing and then you'll break other things." Fixing one thing must not break another — the whole page is checked as one system before it comes to him.

## Why it matters

A fix that breaks something else is not a fix, it is a trade — and he is the one who pays for it, finding the break with his own eye after the fix was called done. The page is one system, so the check cannot be scoped to the diff.

## How to use it

Before anything reaches him, check the whole page as one system — not just the item that was fixed. A round is not done when its item is fixed; it is done when the whole page still stands.
