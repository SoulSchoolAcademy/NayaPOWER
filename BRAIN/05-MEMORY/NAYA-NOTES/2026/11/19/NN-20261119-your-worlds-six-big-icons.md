**Category:** Design — Calendar day page

## What it is

The Your Worlds presentation is all wrong as a flat list of text rows — six beautiful big icons, the icons themselves; click an icon and see what went on in that world that day (screenshot 6f9c48c1…, 1,007,325 B). The list rows go entirely. And no label either (~08:45 MST): "I wouldn't call it your worlds rows one to six..." — the whole label block goes, "YOUR WORLDS" heading included; just the six icons, much simpler (confirmed when he asked "you think that's the way to do it?" — yes). PURPOSE (his explanation): the day lens — pick any day, click a world, see that day's feed, mail, messages, connections AND smart notes for that world; go back on any day and see what went on. The click carries the DAY filter — the back end knows from this screen they want only that day's content for that world, never everything in the feed or the mail. Queued as fix-9.

## Why it matters

A list of text rows is a directory; six big icons are a world you can enter. The day lens is the whole point of the day page — without the day filter the click just dumps the feed, and the page stops being a lens and becomes a door to nowhere in particular. Removing the label is the same instinct: the icons speak; a heading that names them adds nothing but clutter.

## How to use it

Fix-9 replaces the flat text-row list with six big icons (no label block at all) and wires each icon's click to the day-filtered view of that world — feed, mail, messages, connections, smart notes, all filtered to the shown day. The day filter is the back end's contract, not a front-end label. Proven on the page at 390 and 1920.
