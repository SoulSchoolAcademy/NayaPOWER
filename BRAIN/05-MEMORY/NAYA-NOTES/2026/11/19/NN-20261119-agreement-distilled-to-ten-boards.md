## Why it matters

Nobody joins a promise they cannot hold in their head. Ten boards, a few sentences each, in the voice of a friend — that is a promise a child and a grandma can both understand, and understanding is what makes them go "wow," respect it, and want to take part. The long version did the opposite: it asked for attention it had not earned, and attention not earned is attention lost. Keeping the full text in the governance library loses nothing — anyone who wants every word can have it — while the walkthrough finally carries the version people can actually receive. And the pictures are not decoration: a picture board tells the story the words point at, so the agreement is felt as well as read.
