## What it is

On the call he chose the boards' colour flow: each board lights up around the edge in the full spectrum — the sidebar sequence — as you scroll. NOT white/purple alternating. The captain had recommended the full flow, and Shawn chose it. A screenshot from the walk showed the mismatch still live: the left sidebar's colours do not match his sequence — Dashboard reads green not purple, Business reads gold not green.

## Why it matters

The colour flow is the system's signature — each board carries its own colour around its edge, in sequence, so the citizen can feel where they are as they scroll. White/purple alternating flattens that into a repeat; the full spectrum keeps every board distinct and the whole page alive. A board in the wrong colour is a board lying about where it is.

## How to use it

Every board lights up around its edge in the full spectrum, in the sidebar sequence, as the citizen scrolls — never white/purple alternating, never repeating. Check the sidebar sequence against the boards: Dashboard purple, Business green, and the rest in their own colours, all the way down.
