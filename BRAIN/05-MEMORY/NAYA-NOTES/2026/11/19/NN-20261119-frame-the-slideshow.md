## Why it matters

Both asks are the same instinct: make sure the thing he tests is the thing that is true. He remembers a glitch from yesterday's test — not exactly where — so the latest agreement goes back to him and gets checked against what he remembers, until it is aligned. And the slideshow gets a frame, because a slideshow in a frame is a presentation: the frame holds it, stages it, and tells the eye where the show begins and ends.
