## What it is

Shawn, on the live call, on the membership site: a topic may have a video, an audio, and a lesson — a little button switches between seeing just the video version, the audio version, or the lesson version (like the calendar thing), then click to the next one. And they can see all videos at once, all audios at once, all lessons at once — or the mix. (Rides the membership rebuild: the frame-per-topic switch + the All videos / All audios / All lessons / Mix views.)

## Why it matters

One topic, three shapes — the person chooses how they want to take it in, not the site. Video for the watchers, audio for the listeners, lesson for the readers — and the All views for the browsers who want one kind at a time. The switch is small and the freedom is large: the same teaching, however the person learns best.

## How to use it

Each membership topic gets a little switch button: video version, audio version, or lesson version — then click to the next one. And the views: all videos at once, all audios at once, all lessons at once — or the mix. It rides the membership rebuild.
