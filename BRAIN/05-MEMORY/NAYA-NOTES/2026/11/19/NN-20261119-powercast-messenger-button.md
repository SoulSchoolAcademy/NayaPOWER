**Category:** Design — Messenger

## What it is

"The current one should have it should have the where it can open the sidebar tab or open the instant message and whatever and then would just move over, right? And then PowerCast. I don't know if you need PowerCast in the messenger as a as a button. That doesn't really make sense."

## Why it matters

The messenger should open the sidebar tab or the instant message and just move over — beside what you are doing, never by taking the whole screen and making you lose your place. And a player button in a conversation is a button looking for a reason: he does not see the reason. This is his question, not a decided removal — Naya's summary proposes the toggle in its place instead.

## How to use it

Build the messenger to move over, opening the sidebar tab or the instant message beside the current view. Carry the toggle as the proposed replacement for PowerCast, and leave the PowerCast question open until his eye confirms it.
