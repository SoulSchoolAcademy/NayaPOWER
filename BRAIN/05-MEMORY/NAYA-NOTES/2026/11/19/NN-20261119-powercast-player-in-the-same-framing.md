**Category:** Design — Audio

## What it is

Shawn, on the live call: the Powercast player goes in the same master template framing as videos and lessons — the player in its frame, the little bit about it on the side. The membership rebuild brief already carries this (lesson, audio, videos within the frame); the anthem video frame on the agreement page is the same template.

## Why it matters

Audio gets the same shape as video — one template for everything that plays, so the ear learns the same layout the eye already knows. A player floating loose would break the one-template promise the moment sound starts; framed, with its little bit beside it, it belongs to the same family as every lesson and the anthem.

## How to use it

Every Powercast player displays in the master template: the player in its frame, a little bit about it on the side. The membership rebuild already carries it; the agreement-page anthem frame is the same template.
