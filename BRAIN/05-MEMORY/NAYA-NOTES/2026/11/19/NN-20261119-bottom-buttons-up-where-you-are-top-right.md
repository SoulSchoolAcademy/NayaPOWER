*Paired Smart Note: SN-3810 — Buttons Up, Where-You-Are Top-Right*

**Category:** Design — Calendar

## What it is

The day-nav buttons at the bottom — ‹ Wed · Keep reading ◆ · Fri › — go INTO the frame at the bottom, so they get cut off. Move them UP a little bit so they fit nice, clear of the frame. The where-you-are indicator — the dots 1–10 that tell you exactly where you are in the slideshow — belongs in the TOP-RIGHT corner, as he ruled before: Back top-left, where-you-are top-right, Keep reading dead centre. It does not sit at the bottom with the buttons. The same two fixes apply on the notifications slide too: the bottom buttons are just a little too low — lift them up, clear of the frame — and the where-you-are bar is missing from the top-right corner of that board. It goes in the top-right corner of that board, like he said.

## Why it matters

Buttons the frame cuts off cannot be pressed, and a where-you-are indicator sitting with the buttons tells the citizen nothing about where they are. The settled layout — Back top-left, where-you-are top-right, Keep reading dead centre — is the map of the day; it holds on every board, so the citizen always knows where they are and can always reach the next step.

## How to use it

Rides the next calendar round after fix-11 lands: buttons lifted clear of the frame, where-you-are top-right on the day slides and added top-right on the notifications board. The eye proves the buttons whole and the dots top-right on both boards.
