## What it is

No endless scroll — each question its own frame (like the calendar frames), multiple choice, click next through them all until answered. Harvest the walkthrough's frame-by-frame flow. Queue file updated.

## Why it matters

One question per frame is the walkthrough law applied to Settings: the citizen answers one thing, taps next, and is done — never a long form to scroll, never ten questions staring at once. The calendar and the walkthrough already proved the rhythm, so Set Your World takes it verbatim instead of inventing a new one. One rhythm everywhere is easier to learn than a new rhythm per page.

## How to use it

Build Set Your World as frames, one question each, multiple choice, with a next through them all until answered. Take the walkthrough's flow as it is — never an endless scroll, never a new form pattern.
