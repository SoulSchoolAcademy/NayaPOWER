## What it is

Shawn, on the live call, setting the law for how the whole network presents: "Instead of going scrolling down or scrolling across, or anything like that, we're just actually bringing people into the frame." Every area gets the same treatment: each step is its own frame — visual presentations framed as they go through, text presentations framed as they go through — so a person never scrolls down or across; the next frame comes INTO their viewpoint. It applies to the agreement (visual + text), the membership site, the mail, and the messenger — every lesson, every message, every page. One clean frame coming into view, no scrolling, no telling across from down. "Now we have our viewpoints. Our viewport." Carried already in the membership rebuild (frame-by-frame lessons) and walkthrough fix-9 (the agreement as a framed presentation); the mail framing round and the Messenger framing round follow — Messenger's waits for its one-item fix round to land (same file, one round at a time).

## Why it matters

Scrolling is the old internet asking the person to do the work. The frame coming into the viewpoint does the work for them — one thing in view, fully, and then the next. That is the whole promise of the network in one gesture: you never chase the content, it comes to you, clean.
