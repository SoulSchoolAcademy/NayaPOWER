*Paired Smart Note: SN-3739 — The Comment Area Puts the Entry First*

**Category:** Design — Hub, comments

## What it is

Walking the Hub inch by inch, he redesigned the comment area under a post: the heading comes out, the comment entry goes first at the top, the chips sit below as smaller recommendations, and the prompt is distilled to an elevated statement block at the bottom. The actions take their places too: COMMENT dead center, green (go); SHARE INTEL to the corner. And the 'Welcome' pill sitting there — why? That one is open, still to answer.

## Why it matters

The comment area's job is to get the citizen writing. A heading above the entry box is a fence between the reader and the writing; chips above the entry push the citizen toward somebody else's words first. Entry first means the first thing they see is where their own words go — then the recommendations, then the distilled prompt at the bottom to think with. COMMENT dead center in green is the go button you cannot miss; SHARE INTEL in the corner is the quiet second step.

## How to use it

Rebuild the comment area in this order: comment entry first at the top, chips below as smaller recommendations, the prompt distilled into an elevated statement block at the bottom, no heading. COMMENT sits dead center in green; SHARE INTEL sits in the corner. The stray 'Welcome' pill in the area is located and its purpose answered before it stays or goes.
