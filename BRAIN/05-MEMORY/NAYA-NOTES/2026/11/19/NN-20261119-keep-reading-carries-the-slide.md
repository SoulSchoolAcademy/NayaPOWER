*Paired Smart Note: SN-3805 — Keep Reading Carries the Slide*

**Category:** Design — Calendar

## What it is

Keep reading stays on the day's slides: when going through a day (Monday, Saturday, any day), Keep reading moves forward through that day's slides — it must NOT jump back to the beginning of that day's slides. Day-to-day lands on the same slide: going Monday → Tuesday (or any day to any day) lands on that SAME slide on the new day — the slide the citizen was looking for, on that day. Not the start of the day.

## Why it matters

The citizen is looking for a slide — Keep reading carries them forward through the day, and the day-to-day move carries them to the same slide on the new day. Jumping back to the beginning loses their place; landing on the start of the day loses what they were looking for. The slide is the place; the navigation keeps the place.

## How to use it

Rides the next calendar round after fix-11 lands: Keep reading moves forward through the day's slides, never back to the beginning; day-to-day lands on the same slide on the new day. The eye proves the place is kept.
