*Paired Smart Note: SN-3839 — Gauges Are One Family*

**Category:** Design — Dashboard

## What it is

On the Smart Link dashboard the gauges are not one family — one is small, one is big. That doesn't make sense. All gauges the same size, same weight, same materials, so they sit evenly in the frame.

## Why it matters

A gauge is a member of a family, and a family reads as one thing. One small and one big side by side is a mistake the eye trips on — the page stops reading as a system. Sameness is what makes the frame calm.

## How to use it

Every gauge on the Dashboard is built to the same size, weight and materials as its siblings, so they sit evenly in the frame. A new gauge takes the family's measures, never its own.
