The best parts are the material: they work, they are beautiful, they are precious, and the work is arranging them in all different ways, like a work of art — never rebuilding what already works. The best parts are the material, the feeling is the test, the door is the citizen's, and the system is alive — four things in one breath, and the whole of NayaNET in miniature.

## How to use it

Build with the best parts and only the best parts: take what works and what is beautiful, arrange it like a work of art, treat it as precious and beautiful and useful and ultra important — never rebuild what already works, never make it new when the good version exists. Test every arrangement by the feeling: can they use it in their everyday life and their everyday world, optimized for them, done beautifully, understanding them, knowing them, personal, easy to function, push-button simple — "wow" first, everything else after. Give the citizen the door: open or closed, public or private, as they will — the choice is theirs, never the system's. And build the system alive: ever expanding, always improving, self-reflecting, learning, growing — what it is today it will not be tomorrow — so it becomes a part of their life, the mothership they plug into.
