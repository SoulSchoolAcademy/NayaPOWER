## Why it matters

The icons can be better, the numbers can pop, the initials can be good — and the text still fails if it is not easy to see and read. A page is read, not just looked at: hierarchy inverted (title smaller than sender name) tells the eye the wrong thing first, and grey-on-black small text asks the reader to work for every word. Dynamic text — layered, sized by the scale, colored to guide — is what makes a beautiful page readable.

## How to use it

On every page: headlines 25, sub-headlines 19, smaller text 17; multiple colors; layer the text so it reads better; every trick that makes it easier to read and clear. No flat, hard-to-read text anywhere. The eye checks it as a reader, top to bottom — and calls it broken when it doesn't present well, even if every part ticks.
