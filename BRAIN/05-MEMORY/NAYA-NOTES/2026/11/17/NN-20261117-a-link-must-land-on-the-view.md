## Why it matters

A link is a promise: tap this and you are there. When seven links all land on the same page, the promise is broken seven times — and the person who tapped learns not to trust the next link either. The leaderboard is not the Hub feed; it is a view inside it, and the link has to open the view itself, not the front door and hope the person finds the room. One link, the current build, landing where it says it lands — that is the only honest way to send him anywhere.

## How to use it
