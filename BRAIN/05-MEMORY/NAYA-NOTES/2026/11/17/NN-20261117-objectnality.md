## Why it matters

Objectnality is the word for everything he has been teaching all evening — the ball not the circle, the 3D object, the real jewel, the tile you could pick up — given a name at last. It is the one test that settles every design question: could you pick it up? Does it feel like it exists in the physical world? If it reads as a picture of an object instead of the object itself, it is not done — however beautiful, however much glow, however many effects. And the little things are the hugest difference: the depth, the layers, the rim light, the press — each small, each decisive. Objectnality is the bar, and the bar is the object.
