THE FOLD IS THE EXPERIENCE: every page folds away, unfolds, reveals — the interface is alive, a live living machine, an intelligent world. This generalizes the 20:29Z Hub-welcome fold and the confirmed bar fold (20:50Z) into a standing law for every page. The fold is not a trick on one page; it is how every page behaves.

## Why it matters

Until now the fold was a feature — the Hub welcome folds away, the bar folds away. His word makes it the experience itself: the whole system is judged by whether it feels like a live living machine, an intelligent world, and the fold is how that feeling is made. Optimize for their viewpoint, not for the builder's: the citizen's point of view is the one that sees the page fold away when they arrive and unfold when they reach for it. A page that sits still is a page that feels dead; a page that folds, unfolds, reveals is a page that feels alive — and "absolutely beautiful to experience" is the bar he sets for the whole system, not for one screen.

## How to use it

Design every page so it folds away, unfolds, and reveals from the citizen's viewpoint: greetings fold away, bars fold away and return only at the bottom, sections unfold as the citizen reaches them, nothing sits still that should be alive. Judge every page by the feeling: does it feel like a live living machine, an intelligent world? If a page reads flat and still, it fails this law even if every part is present. This law sits above the single-page fold rules (SN-3146, SN-3147) — they are instances of it, not the whole of it.
