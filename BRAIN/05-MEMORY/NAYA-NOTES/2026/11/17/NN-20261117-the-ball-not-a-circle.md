## Why it matters

The machine keeps answering "more beautiful" with more glow and more flash, and every time the page gets flatter — a circle drawn on the screen instead of an object sitting on it. Shawn has shown the high-level design again and again: the gauge, the banner, the people list. Those are not flat shapes with effects. They are balls — round, raised, deep, with flow, something you could pick up. A circle is a drawing. A ball is an object. Until the icons and boards feel like balls, the design is still flat level, however much it glows.

## How to use it

Stop adding glow and flash. Make it raised, deep, elegant — a ball, not a circle, not a hula hoop, not a flat button. Judge every icon and board against the three models: the gauge orbs (aaa_7), the faceted banner (aaa_16), the people-list orbs (AAA_4).
