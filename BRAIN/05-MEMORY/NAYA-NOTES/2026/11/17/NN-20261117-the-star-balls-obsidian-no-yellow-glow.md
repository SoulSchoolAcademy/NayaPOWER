## What it is

Shawn, 2026-11-17 23:12Z, his words verbatim:

"Can you get rid of that like yellow glow that's on the the starballs? Like I just don't like that glow. Like maybe if you had the obsidian black, maybe those stars would look sharp. There's something with that that glow over top just doesn't look right."

THE LAW: no yellow glow ON the star balls — not a halo, not a wash, nothing over the top. The star balls are OBSIDIAN BLACK and the stars on them read SHARP. Any light lives behind or around the ball, never over it. This includes the clip fix's layered glyph halo on the lit star (0 0 14px rgba(212,160,23,.85)) — it goes. Sent to the Hub feed coordinator (renovation amendment) + the visual mockup build 23:12Z. (The eye walking the clip-fix build was already told to judge the halo against the hover law — this order supersedes: the halo goes regardless.)

## Why it matters

The yellow glow over the top is the same mistake as the purple glow over the board (SN-3180): light poured over the face of the object, washing out what sits on it. The stars cannot read sharp under a wash — the glow has to go so the black can be black and the star can be sharp. Obsidian black is not the absence of beauty; it is what makes the star readable. Any light lives behind or around the ball, never over it — the same rule, on the smallest object in the system.
