## How to use it

Never cite a voice ban as law — there is none. No voice on the intelligent boards for now, for his practical reasons: it can't play instantly, it takes up a lot of resources, and the boards are already beautiful and easy to read. Put pre-recorded Naya voice where it makes sense and the words don't change: on each page (a wave they can play, she tells them about that page), on cards that are there for good and not in the feed, on the whole walkthrough, each slide, and possibly the visual collective chain technology — as many places as possible. And put the voice playbacks at the end, once everything is done complete, so we never record voice we then have to re-record because the page changed. The instant question stays open until it is solved: playback must start the moment they click, which means pre-recorded audio made beforehand and served from the edge — a live board whose words change cannot be pre-recorded, and that is the part we haven't figured out yet.
