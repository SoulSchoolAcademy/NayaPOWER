## Why it matters

The bottom bar is the part people touch most — it is on every page, it is always under the thumb. Four things in one dictation, and each one is a law: it is the SAME bar everywhere (mirrored with Personal, not a different bar per page); it gets out of the way while you read (folds down on scroll); the notification is a light inside the icon itself, white in the middle, bright — one icon that lights up, never an icon beside its own light; and it is the way INTO the day — tap and you are in the Calendar for that day, where your stats and your notifications are waiting. The bar is not a row of shortcuts. It is the door to the day.

## How to use it

Build one bottom bar and mirror it everywhere — the same six tiles as Personal, same glyphs, same colors, same order, on every page. Make it fold down while the page scrolls and return when scrolling stops. Give every notification a white center inside the icon itself, bright, so it pops — the same treatment for each of them. And wire every tile to the Calendar, inside, for that day — tap MAIL and you are in the Calendar for today with your mail stats and notifications; tap ACTIVITY and you are in the Calendar for today with your activity stats and notifications. The day is the unit, and the bar is how you get there.
