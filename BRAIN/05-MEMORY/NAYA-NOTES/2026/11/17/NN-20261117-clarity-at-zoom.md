## What it is

Shawn, 2026-11-17 22:34Z, his words verbatim:

"if you zoom in and you can't see the clarity on it, it's just overlaid a circle and an icon that are running into each other. That's bad design. That's not quality."

The icon and its circle must stay crisp and separate at any zoom, with real space between them.

## Why it matters

A design that only looks right at one size is not finished — it is a picture that falls apart the moment his eye gets close. When the icon and the circle run into each other under zoom, the object stops being an object and becomes a smudge. Quality is not what it looks like from far away; quality is what survives being looked at closely. If it cannot be seen clearly zoomed in, it was never clear.

## How to use it

Zoom in on every icon-and-circle pair. The icon and the circle must stay crisp and separate at any zoom, with real space between them. If they run into each other, it is bad design — rebuild the pair until the space holds.
