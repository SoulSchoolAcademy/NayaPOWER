## Why it matters

The best interface is the one that folds up and hides away — his words, and the whole Hub in one sentence. The greeting is a welcome, not a page: it says hello and gets out of the way, and what is left is clean like software — search, three doors, categories, nothing explaining itself. The sidebar balance is the same idea at the edges: even on either side, nothing doubled, nothing the same colour twice in a row, the things you reach for first (My Worlds — create your world first) sitting where the hand falls. And the social-network feel is the shape he has been circling all day: Facebook's structure, but different, ours.

## How to use it

Build the Hub so the welcome greets and folds away, and the resting state is flush: search, Collective / Personal / Activity with no text below, then the categories. Balance the sidebars even: Settings right, Smart Factory right near the top, My Worlds just below it; no two neighbours the same colour. Left sidebar is where you go (Personal, Business, your world); right side toggles between sidebar and contacts/connections with X or the three-bars, instant chat switchable back on that side. Judge the result the way he does: does it read clean, like software, with the feel of a structured social network that is different from Facebook?
