## Why it matters

This is the shape of the day itself. Today the day's things live in separate places — the journal somewhere, the notes somewhere, the posts somewhere, the news somewhere, the work somewhere — and the citizen has to go find each one. Shawn's picture is one room per day: you go in and there is the visual timeline, and everything that day holds is already there — the journal, the Naya Notes, the posts, the smart notes, the intelligence, the news feeds, the world, the work, the personal life, all in one place. The + button stops being a generic add and becomes add-journal — write for that day, it is saved, everything you noted for that day stays, and you can go back on days. The calendar click already moves day to day; the viewer makes it a scroll, one day flowing into the next. And the feeling is the standard, not the decoration: high high level luxury spaces, absolutely epic worlds people walk into or click into — push-button simple, boom into the next one. A day that feels like a luxury world you walk into is a day a citizen wants to come back to. That is why the day is one place, and why the feeling is named in the same breath as the structure: the structure is where everything lives, the feeling is why anyone opens the door.

## How to use it

Build the day as one place: the visual timeline when you go in; the + button becomes add-journal — write for that day, saved automatically, everything noted for that day kept, go back on days; a viewer to scroll from one day to the next, the calendar click already moving day to day, the viewer making it a scroll. Put it all in that one place — journal, Naya Notes, posts, smart notes, intelligence, news feeds, world, work, personal. And hold the feeling: high high level luxury spaces, absolutely epic worlds people walk into or click into — push-button simple, boom into the next one. Never split the day back into separate rooms, and never let the day feel like a list when it should feel like a world you walk into.
