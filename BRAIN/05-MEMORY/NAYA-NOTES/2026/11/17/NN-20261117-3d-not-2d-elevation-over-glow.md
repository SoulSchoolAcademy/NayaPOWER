## What it is

## Why it matters

Beautiful is not the bar anymore — the icons are already beautiful, and they still read flat. Flat means 2D: sitting on the screen, not coming out of it. What he wants is the object itself: full, round, elevated, alive — press it and it goes in, let go and it comes out, something happening when you use it. Glow is not the effect; glow behind is only ever the frame. A page of beautiful flat things is still a flat page, and a flat page fails even when every part of it is pretty.

## How to use it

Nothing flat, anywhere. Every icon, button, and board is a full 3D object — full circle, not a hula hoop — with a ton of elevation, coming out of the page. 3D effect over glow every day of the week. Press in, come out, alive. If it still reads flat, it failed, however beautiful it is.
