## Why it matters

The control sat at the top-left because that is where menus usually sit — and that is exactly why it was wrong here. On the Hub, the left is where the world already lives. The menu control is the way in to the world, and the way in belongs on the right, where the choice it offers can open without covering what you came to see. One tap, two doors — the sidebar or the smart messenger — and whichever opens, the people are already there, the ones related to the feed topic you are looking at. The Hub behaving like Personal, Business, and World already do: one system, one gesture, everywhere.

## How to use it

The menu control goes to the right end of the top bar — not on the left. Tapping it offers open the sidebar, or open the smart messenger. The people related to the current feed topic show automatically on the side — collective people for COLLECTIVE, personal connections, world people — just like Personal/Business/World.
