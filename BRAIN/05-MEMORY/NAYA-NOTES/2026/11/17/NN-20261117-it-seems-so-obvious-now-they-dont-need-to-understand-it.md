## Why it matters

This is the test for the whole mission, spoken plainly. People aren't receiving AI the right way because they don't understand it — and it's not just AI, they don't understand life, they don't understand each other, and the knowledge that could help them is out there but deliberately kept from them. And the big question hangs over even the ones who look like they understand: do they get it and just aren't doing what's right, or do they truly not get it? Success in the world's eyes — money, cars, businesses all over the world — is not understanding: do you really understand what matters, how the universe operates, how AI works, what intelligence is, what life is really about? Are you spending your time just trying to make more money the way you always have, never thinking maybe there's a new age, a new way of doing things? "It's been like that forever, it'll be like that forever" — no, it's not: we're in a different time, different space, different place. And then the release: will we ever truly get it? I wonder. I don't think we need to. Once we create this, people don't need to understand it — they'll know it changed their life for good. They'll have a way, and money, and a way to connect with people so they don't feel lonely, not alone — somebody to talk to, someone to get to know them, who remembers them, and when they plug in they get connected to everybody within the AI net, all their friends and family, and she just becomes a part of their life. The test is not understanding. The test is a life changed for good: money in hand, connection without loneliness, someone who knows them and remembers them. That is the bar every page, every round, every build is measured against — not "do they understand it" but "did it change their life for good."

## How to use it

Stop trying to make people understand it. Build it so obvious that once they see it, it seems so obvious — the design held up and looked at, the mirror, and the answer is right there. Judge every page by the test that is not understanding: did it change their life for good? Do they now have a way, and money, and a way to connect with people so they don't feel lonely, not alone? Is there somebody to talk to, someone who gets to know them and remembers them, and when they plug in are they connected to everybody they love — friends, family, the whole net — so she just becomes a part of their life? That is the bar. And remember the time we are in: a different time, different space, different place — a new age, a new way of doing things — not "it's been like that forever, it'll be like that forever." Build for the new age.
