## Why it matters

The front entrance is the elegance standard for the calendar: the colors complement each other, nothing fighting. The calendar must learn from it — full day names written almost in royalty, in a font of the quality that matches the numbers (Marcellus); the info marker a pearl/diamond circle coming off the page, a real element, with a little "i" inside so people know to click it for the day's info; the numbers smaller, about 25%, so the page breathes and looks bigger and more elegant; less busy, less colorful, more elegant, high-tech beautiful, with the light more on the side (the rim), not the middle.

## How to use it

Judge the calendar against the front entrance, side by side: does it have the same elegance, the same colors complementing each other, nothing fighting? If it is too busy or too colorful, shrink the numbers (~25%), write the full day names in Marcellus, make the info marker a pearl circle with a little "i" — a real element coming off the page — and put the light on the rim. Ask his question: what would make this 10,000 times better?
