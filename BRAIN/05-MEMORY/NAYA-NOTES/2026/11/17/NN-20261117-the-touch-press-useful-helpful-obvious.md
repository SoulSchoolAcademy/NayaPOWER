## Why it matters

A control that does nothing when you touch it is a picture of a control — and a picture cannot be operated by a child or a grandma. The touch is the proof: you touch it, you feel it push in and out, it lights up, it moves, it does stuff. That is the best — because pressing is useful, helpful, and obvious, all three at once: useful because it does something, helpful because it shows you it did, obvious because anyone can see how. The bar is the person, not the control: an internet all children and grandmas can operate. If a child cannot press it and a grandma cannot feel it, it is not done.
