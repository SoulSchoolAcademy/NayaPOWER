THE LAW: JUST ENOUGH GLOW — underglow UNDERNEATH only. Anything more is not beautiful. The purple glow comes OFF the boards themselves — the boards are OBSIDIAN BLACK — and the board lights up AROUND THE EDGE, but not too much, just a PERFECT AMOUNT. A restrained rim light, nothing more. No wash over the board, no glow on the board, no purple haze. Sent to the Hub feed coordinator (renovation amendment) + the visual mockup build 23:13Z.

## Why it matters

Glow is the one thing the machine always overdoes — and "more glow" was the answer to everything until tonight. Just enough is the correction: the underglow underneath is beautiful, and anything more than that is not beautiful. The board itself is obsidian black — no purple on it, no wash over it, no haze — and the only light is the restrained rim around the edge, a perfect amount. Restraint is the beauty: the light frames the object instead of covering it, and the object gets to be black, real, and still.
