## Why it matters

The action row is the smallest thing on a feed post and the most-touched — it is where a citizen says what a post meant to them, and it is where the post's standing is counted. A flat LOVE / LIKE row with plain numbers undersells the thing it measures: ten stars is the language of the whole system — "we talk about 10 stars all the time" — so a five-star cap would make the feed speak a smaller language than the house speaks. Circles with a flow around them, stars that light up when you click — that is the orbit and the light-up he has already ruled for the bottom bar and the icons, brought to the feed. And the question underneath — one choice point or two standing buttons — is the push-button-simple question: one elegant place to say what you feel, or two buttons side by side. The comment box matters the same way: the "why" behind the stars is the intelligence, and a beautiful box is what invites it.
