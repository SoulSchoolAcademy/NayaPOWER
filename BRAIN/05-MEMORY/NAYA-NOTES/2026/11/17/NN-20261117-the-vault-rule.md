## Why it matters

The work has to go somewhere. A certificate made yesterday, liked, and then gone — the main site still serving the old plain walk while the layered one sits staged — is the system losing its own work. The Vault is the somewhere: saved, kept, findable, the moment it is made. And live-on-made is the rest of it: once a thing is made and proven, it goes live in the same pass, so finished work never sits staged and is never lost to waiting. The eye still verifies before live — the rule changes the waiting, not the verification. Waiting for a fix in flight is verification still running; sitting staged with nothing running is losing the work.
