## What it is

**STORY VOICE + WORD SPACING (his order).** The text must feel like a story being told — right grammar, written as if speaking it to them. His example of what's wrong: slide 1 says welcome, slide 2 is "hi I'm Naya" plus a whole bunch of text, slide 3 is one line "welcome to your world" — welcome appears TWICE and the spacing of the words across slides is uneven. Fix the pacing so no greeting repeats and the words spread like speech.

## Why it matters

He reads it as a listener, not a reviewer. A repeated welcome tells him the teller lost the thread; a crammed slide tells him the teller is rushing. The slides are beats of one telling: each slide carries its fair share of the words, in order, in spoken grammar.

## How to use it

Rewrite the slides as spoken beats: one greeting, once, at the start; each slide one thought, roughly even weight; read it aloud slide to slide and cut wherever it stumbles. No slide carries a whole bunch while the next carries one line.
