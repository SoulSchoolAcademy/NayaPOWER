## What it is

**HIS WORDS, ON THE BOARD LABELLED "IN A NUTSHELL":** "the in nutshell is not in a nutshell." The board carried more than a nutshell holds, so the name lied about the thing. Fix-1h carries the correction as item 4: the nutshell made a true nutshell, 3–4 sentences.

**FINAL BOARD FIT — ALMOST, THREE SMALL MOVES (his eye on the "in a nutshell" board):** it fits better now, almost — move the board UP just a tad, move it IN just a tad, and bring DOWN the brightness of the light on the "in a nutshell" board just a little (it is almost too much light). Small adjustments only, not a redesign.

## Why it matters

"Nutshell" is a promise to the reader: this is the whole thing, small enough to hold. A long board under that label breaks the promise and teaches the reader to distrust the labels.

## How to use it

When a board is called a nutshell, count the sentences: three or four, and the whole idea inside them. More than that — cut it until it fits, or rename the board to what it actually is.
