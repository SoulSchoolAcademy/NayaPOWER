## What it is

**THE RATE DOESN'T LOOK INTELLIGENT (his words):** "And what are you going to do with the stars?" and "And the rate doesn't intelligent." He was looking at the Hub's star ratings in his own screenshot: gold VALUE stars, 7/10, and blue INTELLIGENCE stars, 8/10 — neither one the right stars — with white slabs sitting over the Child Note and Grandma Note labels. The assistant's reply the same evening: "For the stars, they'll be purple and white with that same DM flow inside, no gold or blue, and they'll sit cleanly without colliding. And the orb at the top of the intelligent block will get its flow back around it, so it feels like one continuous, elegant system, just like you described."

## Why it matters

The rating is the measure of intelligence on the page; when the measure itself looks wrong, the page argues against itself. A visitor reads the stars before they read the words, and gold-and-blue stars tell them the intelligence here is decoration, not the real thing.

## How to use it

Stars purple and white with the DM flow inside — never gold and blue — for every star, every time. Prove a colour fix by reading the filed bytes and by rendering them, not by deduction. His screenshot is the latest bytes; his eye is the check.
