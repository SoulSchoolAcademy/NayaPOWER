A drift is how a design language dies — one gold star here, one blue label there, and a month later nothing on the page matches anything else.

## How to use it

When building or fixing the rating star, use purple and white only: one purple, one white, flow inside, INTELLIGENCE label purple. If a build shows gold or blue in the star or its label, that is the defect.
