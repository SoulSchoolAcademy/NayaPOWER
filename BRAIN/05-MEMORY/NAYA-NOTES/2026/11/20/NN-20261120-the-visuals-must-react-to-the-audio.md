## What it is

**VIDEO / AUDIO SYNC (his order):** the video must sync to the visual player; **the visuals must react to the audio**, not just play alongside it.

## Why it matters

Playing alongside is two things happening at the same time; reacting is one thing answering the other. A player whose visuals react to the audio is alive, and alive is the standard of the whole network.

## How to use it

Bind the visuals to the audio: the picture, the light, the motion must answer the sound as it plays. Test it by ear and eye together — mute the audio and the visuals should go still; play it and they should move. Never ship "plays alongside" as "in sync."
