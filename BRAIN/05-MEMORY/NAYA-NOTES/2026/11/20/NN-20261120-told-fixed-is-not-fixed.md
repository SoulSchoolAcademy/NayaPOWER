## Why it matters

"Fixed" is the strongest word in the machine — it closes the item and tells him to stop watching. Used before his eye has seen it, it closes an item that is still open and hands him a surprise instead of a result. Six minutes between "fixed" and "still not showing."

## How to use it

Never say "fixed" until his eye has seen it fixed — say "ready for your eye," "staged for you to judge," or "the checker saw it whole, your eye is the proof." If a delivery has already gone out as "fixed" and his eye finds otherwise, say so in the open, in the same pass.
