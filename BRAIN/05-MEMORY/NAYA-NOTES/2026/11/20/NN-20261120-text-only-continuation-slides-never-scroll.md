## What it is

**THE SLIDE PATTERN LAW (~16:27 MST).** "you shouldn't have to go image text image text image text. You can go image text so you have image and a little bit of text and you can have all text, no image, no video, just all text so it tells the rest of the story about that image it's continuation text. And then go back the next slide to image and then a little bit of text. You don't want to have them scroll down to read. If you're making them scroll down to read and then you've done then it's not right. I want them to every every frame so they stay in the frame. So just click in the button they never folding down."

## Why it matters

Slides do not have to alternate image-text rigidly. A text-only slide continues the story of the image before it — and no slide ever makes them scroll down to read. Every frame stays in the frame; they just click the button.

## How to use it

Image + a little text, then an all-text continuation slide where the story needs room, then image again. If text overflows a frame, it becomes the next slide — never a scroll. Applies to the agreement slides and every presentation after it.
