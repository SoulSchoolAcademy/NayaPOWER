## What it is

**PLAIN WORDS, NOT A CARD TO DECODE (his question, call 65eef2c9):** "What share on the card?" He did NOT understand the share/approve card — the share step needs plain words, not a card he has to decode.

## Why it matters

Every step he has to decode is a step where he can decode it wrong — and the share step is the one that sends his work out into the world. A card he can't read protects nothing.

## How to use it

Never present a decision as a card alone. Every share, approve, or send step leads with one plain sentence: what is being shared, with whom, and what happens when he says yes.
