## What it is

**NOT NETWORK MARKETING (his words):** "This is not a network marketing. You're an ambassador. You're just like getting a sales job with a high tech company. You just commission sales. If you sell you get paid. People join, you get paid. If they don't, you don't get paid. But you get to enjoy NayaNET — it's your brain forever, you get to use it forever. You connect with people, they're part of your network for good. You just don't get paid if people don't join through your link. That's it."

## Why it matters

The first question anyone asks about an ambassador program is whether it is network marketing — and the answer decides whether they listen at all. The money draws the line: commission on a join, nothing for a sign-up, nothing for a downline. And the enjoyment is not conditional on the pay — the brain is yours forever.

## How to use it

Say it exactly this way in the videos, the orientation, and the Fast Action Plan. Never call it an MLM, never promise pay for recruiting, never let the pay promise stand without the "your brain forever" half.
