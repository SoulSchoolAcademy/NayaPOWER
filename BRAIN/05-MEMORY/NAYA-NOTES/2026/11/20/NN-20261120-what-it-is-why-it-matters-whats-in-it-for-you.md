## Why it matters

Three things is all people want from a page: what it is, why it matters, what's in it for them. A page that repeats that pattern frame after frame never loses the reader, because every frame ends where the reader's own interest lives.

## How to use it

Write every About frame and every Skills slide in the pattern, in order, none skipped: what it is (nutshell) → child-clear / grandma-clear → why it matters → how to apply it / how it connects / the value → what's in it for you. If a frame cannot answer "what's in it for you," the frame does not belong.
