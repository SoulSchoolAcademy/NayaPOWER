## Why it matters

Source-verified is not screen-proven. A build can be right in its files and wrong on the screen, and the only thing that catches the gap is looking at the screen before shipping. A stopped deploy costs a round; a live page with the wrong logo costs his trust and another correction.

## How to use it

Every deploy with a visual claim carries a render gate: the claimed artwork must be seen resolving on screen before the deploy runs. If the render contradicts the source, the source loses and the deploy stops.
