## What it is

**A BILL OF RIGHTS, NOT A BILL OF RESTRICTIONS (his philosophy).** "If you want to create a great economy or a great nation, what you want is a bill of rights not a bill of restrictions. The Clarity Act was 630 pages and it was a bill of restrictions… probably 620 pages were restrictions." He wants a bill of rights, not a bill of restrictions.

## Why it matters

The Clarity Act is meant to be the founding law of NayaNET — the thing a citizen reads and feels safe inside. A 630-page bill of restrictions teaches a citizen that the system suspects them; a bill of rights teaches them that the system serves them.

## How to use it

Rewrite toward rights: every section states what is granted and protected first, in plain words, and any restriction that remains must name the right it protects. Measure the rewrite by counting pages of rights against pages of restrictions — rights winning by a wide margin. The rewrite itself is owed work; this note records the philosophy.
