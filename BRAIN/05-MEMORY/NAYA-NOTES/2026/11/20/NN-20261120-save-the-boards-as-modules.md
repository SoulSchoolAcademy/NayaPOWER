## What it is

**MODULES — SAVE AND REUSE (his order).** These board styles become the building blocks for memberships, about, skills, and the rest of the network — insert text / image / video and it presents at this level, no new pages built.

**WHAT IT MEANS.** The agreement's boards stop being one presentation's styling and become the system's modules: the same board, filled with whatever the area needs — text, an image, a video — presenting at this level every time. Memberships, About, Skills, and the rest of the network build from the saved boards instead of building new pages.

**WHAT RIDES WITH IT.** Members can swap the N for their own logo or put their face in the corners (see "The Format Is the Template"). The template carries the format; the modules carry the pieces.

## Why it matters

Every new page built from scratch is a new chance to get the presentation wrong. Saved modules mean the level is locked in once — insert the content, and it presents at this level, everywhere, without a new build.

## How to use it

Save the board styles as modules. When an area needs a presentation — memberships, about, skills, anything in the network — fill a saved board with its text, image, or video. Do not build a new page for it.
