## Why it matters

A single big board with text on it is a poster — it does not move, does not light up, does not answer a touch. Two or three inner boards inside one major board give the light somewhere to travel and the eye somewhere to land, and each board can light up on its own. And the board already exists in the Hub, already raised, already elevated — taking part of it is the work; building a board-shaped thing from scratch is the failure he keeps having to correct.

## How to use it

Build the showcase as one major board with two inner boards for the text (three on the odd slide). Light flows around the edge, it moves and breathes, on touch it feels active — press in and out, lights up, moves — exactly like the Hub's intelligent boards. Take the exact Hub board code, use part of it, never a new board. Beside the player for now; when the player is not there, the whole frame IS the intelligent board. No image, no video — the whole thing becomes one lit-up slide you read.
