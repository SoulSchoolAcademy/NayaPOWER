## What it is

**BAR RULE — NOT STICKY (2026-11-21 ~11:24 MST):** the bottom icon bar shows only while scrolling down. On most pages it isn't there at all — a small corner button pulls it up, or it can sit at the end of a page. (Supersedes the older "fades while scrolling, floats back" wording.)

## Why it matters

The bar is on every page — ADD · PERSONAL · BUSINESS · WORLDS · ECOSYSTEM · CONNECT — so a wrong bar rule is wrong everywhere at once. The sticky bar, the fading bar, the bar that floats back uninvited: each one takes space from the frame and attention from the thing he came to see. The not-sticky rule gives the frame the whole page and gives him the bar exactly when he is moving through it — and a small corner button for every other moment. The difference from the old wording is who decides: the old bar came back by itself after a pause; the new bar appears while he moves and waits to be called the rest of the time. One bar, the same everywhere, showing only while scrolling down — that is the bar he described, and it is the bar every page carries from here.

## How to use it

Build the bottom icon bar so it shows only while scrolling down. On most pages it is not there at all — a small corner button pulls it up, or it sits at the end of the page. One bar, the same on every page (ADD · PERSONAL · BUSINESS · WORLDS · ECOSYSTEM · CONNECT, with the plus button). Do not build the fade-and-float-back behaviour, do not claim it as a pass, do not cite the old wording. Render-proof a bar by scrolling: it shows while moving down, it is gone at rest, the corner button brings it back.
