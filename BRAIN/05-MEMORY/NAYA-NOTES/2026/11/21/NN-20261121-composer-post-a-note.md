## Why it matters

The citizen does not tap a button that says "Smart Note" — they tap ADD, and the action is "Post a note". "Smart Note" is what the system calls the thing behind the action, not what the citizen is asked to do. A button labelled "Smart Note" asks the citizen to know the system's vocabulary before they have done anything; a button that says "Post a note" asks them only to do the thing. And the note goes private by default, public only by the citizen's choice — the default protects them, the choice is theirs. A composer that says the plain thing and defaults private is a composer a child and a grandma can both use without a second thought.

## How to use it

Flow: ADD → Post a note → choose where it goes (feed picker; Space via plus button/dropdown when more than one Space) → private by default, public only by your choice. Audit every composer, every ADD flow, every button: any "Smart Note" on a button or label is out of alignment and gets renamed.
