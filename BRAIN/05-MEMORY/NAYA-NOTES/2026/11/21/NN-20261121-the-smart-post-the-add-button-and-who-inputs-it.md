## Why it matters

This is the difference between the three kinds of things in NayaNET, stated from the customer's point of view — and it is the difference that decides who does the work. A post is theirs: they write anything they want into the box, it formats beautifully, they add an image, they share it publicly or privately, and Naya does not rewrite it or input it for them. A smart note and a Naya note are NOT theirs to add: Naya, your AI, inputs them — the customer tells Naya ("smart note this" / "Naya note this") and Naya inputs the note. Posts are the customer's manual sharing; notes are Naya's input. And the smart post is not a mystery feature to find: it is the ADD button on the bottom bar — tap ADD, "Which feed?", and you are adding a post.
