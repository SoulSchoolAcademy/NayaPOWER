## Why it matters

This draws the line between the three things a person can put into NayaNET, so nobody has to guess which one they are making. A smart note is the public, auto-formatted, social intelligence — and it can never be unformatted, because the public standard holds. A Naya note is the private, auto-formatted full deal — and it can go plain in Settings, because it is yours. A post is the default, straight-up thing: here you are posting, it has the social share part, and if you wanted unformatted you would just post. Same formatting family, three different defaults — and images work on all of them.
