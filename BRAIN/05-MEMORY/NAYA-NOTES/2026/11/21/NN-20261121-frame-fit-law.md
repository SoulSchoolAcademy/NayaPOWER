## What it is

**FRAME FIT LAW (2026-11-21 ~11:24 MST):** everything fits INSIDE the frame — no scrolling inside a frame, aligned, same spacing and same look and feel as the screenshots. Overflow moves to the next frame.

## Why it matters

The About was rejected three times in one morning, and every rejection was a fit failure wearing a different costume: frames taller than the screen, words spilling past the bottom, scroll inside, density double the agreement's. Each round fixed the costume and kept the fit broken. The Frame Fit Law names the fit itself as the standard, for every page, so no round can pass a frame that scrolls inside it — and no round can call a split or a count a fix. A frame is whole or it is not; whole means everything inside it, aligned, same spacing, same look and feel as the screenshots. That is the bar the registry builds to, and the bar his eye holds. When the content is too much for one frame, it does not squeeze, shrink, or scroll — it moves to the next frame. That is the only overflow rule.

## How to use it

Build every frame so its content fits inside it whole — no scrolling inside a frame, ever. Align it, space it the same as its neighbours, match the look and feel of his screenshots. When content overflows, move it to the next frame — never squeeze it, never shrink it, never scroll it inside. Prove fit by render at phone width first: the frame whole, nothing cut, nothing scrolling inside it.
