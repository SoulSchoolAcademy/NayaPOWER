## What it is

**LAW A18 (his call, as recorded in MEMORY.md from the same call):** the block comes Naya framed, standard; a setting in the system lets the citizen choose framed or unframed. DEFAULT IS NAYA FRAMED. Separate from the unformat toggle (boards inside vs outer frame). Sent to both coordinators; law file A18 appended.

**HOW IT CONNECTS:** SN-4473 settled the NayaNET frame as the standard — every block wrapped in it, part of the anatomy. This note adds the citizen's choice: framed is the standard and the default, and a setting lets the citizen choose unframed if they want it. The frame is the default, not a forced lock.

**WHAT IT IS NOT:** it is not the unformat toggle (SN-4470). Unformat is a choice about the boards inside — verbatim, one elevated block, no auto-organization. Framed/unframed is a choice about the outer frame — the NayaNET frame around the whole block. Two settings, two different things; do not merge them.

## Why it matters

The frame is the signature — the NayaNET frame is what makes a block recognizably Naya's, and it is the default because the standard is framed. But a citizen who wants their block unframed can choose it, in a setting, without losing the intelligence inside. Default framed, choice unframed: the standard holds and the citizen is free.

## How to use it

Ship every block Naya framed, standard — that is the default. Put a setting in the system that lets the citizen choose framed or unframed. Keep it separate from the unformat toggle: unformat governs the boards inside, framed/unframed governs the outer frame. Never default a block to unframed. The framed/unframed setting is with both coordinators (Hub + Personal bar); render-verified, not claimed done here.
