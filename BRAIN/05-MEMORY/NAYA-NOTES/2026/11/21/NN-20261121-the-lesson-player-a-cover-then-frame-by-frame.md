## What it is

**THE LESSON PLAYER (settled design, as recorded in MEMORY.md from the same call, 2026-11-21 ~09:57 MST — part of the Academy flow):** click a lesson → another cover, looking like a Personal page, featuring THAT specific lesson (first cover was the course, then the lessons within the course). The cover introduces the lesson. Click the button → the lesson unfolds FRAME BY FRAME — images + video + text, you go through it.

**A TOGGLE lets the citizen view it however they want:** MIXED (images + videos + text — the full deal, the DEFAULT), or ALL VIDEO, or ALL AUDIO. No all-text view — "doing all text probably doesn't make sense" (his correction, same call).

## Why it matters

Two covers, two jobs: the first cover introduces the course, the second introduces the lesson — each one a Personal page in its own right, featuring that one thing. Then the lesson unfolds frame by frame, the way the agreement unfolds — you go through it, you do not scroll past it. And the toggle is the citizen's choice: we do them all, they choose how they view it — mixed by default, all video, or all audio. No all-text, because all text does not make sense for a lesson.
