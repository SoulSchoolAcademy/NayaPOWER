## Why it matters

The beautiful formatting is the product — but it is still the person's choice for their own things. A Naya note or a post can go plain: one elevated block, exactly as inputted, copy-paste or dictate in, share verbatim. What the person gives up is the auto-organization: formatted means we auto-organize and auto-format their content so it comes out beautifully, in boards; unformatted means one block, as inputted, no auto-organization. And the line that protects the public standard: a smart note is public, it carries the shared format the whole world and the search engines see, so it can never be unformatted. That is the difference, and it is the person's choice — except where the public standard holds.

## How to use it

Put two toggles in Settings: "unformat Naya notes" and "unformat posts". Turning one on stops the separation into elevated boards every three or four sentences — the content comes out as ONE elevated block, verbatim as inputted, still a block, just not as nice. Never offer the toggle for smart notes: a public smart note stays formatted, always.
