## What it is

**NO COUNTING LAW (2026-11-21 ~11:24 MST):** never count frames, never report "X of 98". Nobody sits through 98 slides. The 98-slide / deck-count approach is dead.

## Why it matters

The count is the defect: a deck that needs counting is a deck nobody will finish. Every About rebuild came back with a count — 42 frames, 98 frames, 51 of 98 zero scroll — and every count was the machine telling him the size of the thing instead of whether the thing was right. The counts made the work look measured while it was failing his eye three times in one morning. The No Counting Law kills the metric that let the failure hide: from here, a round reports frames whole, not frames counted. The fix is not a smaller number — the fix is no number at all. One headline-led frame per item replaces the counted deck: Motto, Vision, Goal, Mission — his list, in that order — and the frame is the unit, not the deck.

## How to use it

Never write a frame count in a report, a brief, a checker verdict, or a message to him — no "X of 98", no "42 frames", no "51/98". Build one headline-led frame per item and prove each frame whole: everything fits inside it, no scrolling inside it, aligned, same spacing and same look and feel as the screenshots. If a build needs more frames to fit, overflow moves to the next frame — and the count still goes unreported.
