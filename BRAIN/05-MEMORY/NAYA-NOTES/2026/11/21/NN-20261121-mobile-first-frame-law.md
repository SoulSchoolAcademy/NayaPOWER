## What it is

**MOBILE-FIRST FRAME LAW (voice, 2026-11-21 ~12:47 MST):** every frame is built mobile-first, then laptop. The 9:16 vertical frame gives more room to write and pairs as roughly a third/two-thirds with the text board beside it on a laptop; on mobile it folds to stacked blocks — video, then the next block. Every page must be easily toggled between mobile and laptop and look great on both. If a design does not convert well both ways, we do NOT build the page that way — only frames optimized for mobile first that still look great on laptop ship.

## Why it matters

He looks at the pages on his phone first — that is where his eye is, and that is where the citizens live. A frame designed laptop-first and squeezed onto a phone is a frame that scrolls inside itself, clips its own words, and hides its own buttons — every failure in the calendar walk is a laptop-first frame meeting a phone. Mobile-first is the order that makes the frame fit where it is actually seen, and the laptop is the second surface, not the first excuse.

## How to use it

Build every frame mobile-first, then laptop. Pair the 9:16 vertical frame roughly a third/two-thirds with the text board beside it on laptop; fold to stacked blocks on mobile. Make every page easily toggled between mobile and laptop, great on both. Do not ship a design that does not convert well both ways.
