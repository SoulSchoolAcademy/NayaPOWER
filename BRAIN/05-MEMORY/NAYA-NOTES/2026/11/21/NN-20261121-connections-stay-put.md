## What it is

**CONNECTIONS STAY PUT (voice, 2026-11-21 ~12:33 MST):** personal connections, business connections, world connections stay related to their own pages. Leave as is.

## Why it matters

Connections are relationships, and relationships live in a place. A business contact found on the business page is found; the same contact filed under a generic pile is lost. Staying put is what keeps the Personal page personal and the Business page business — the pages mean something because their people stay with them.

## How to use it

Do not move, merge, or re-file connections across pages. Personal, business, and world connections each stay related to their own page. Any round that touches connections checks this law first.
