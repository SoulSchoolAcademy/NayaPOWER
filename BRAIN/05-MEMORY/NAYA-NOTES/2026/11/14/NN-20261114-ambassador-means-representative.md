## Why it matters

A title without a meaning is an empty uniform — the citizen wears it, and the first person who asks "what does that mean?" finds nothing underneath. "Representative — part of the community, the kingdom, the world" gives them a sentence they can say with pride, and it tells them the truth about the role: belonging first, representing second. A civilization is not built by salespeople. It is built by representatives who are part of it.

## How to use it

Define the title the moment it is given — in the deed, Step 6, in his words. Belonging first, representing second: part of the community, the kingdom, the world. Never give a citizen a title the deed doesn't explain.
