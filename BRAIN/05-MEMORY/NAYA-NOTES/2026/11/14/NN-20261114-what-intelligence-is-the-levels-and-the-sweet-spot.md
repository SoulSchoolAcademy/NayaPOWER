## Why it matters

Without the levels, every argument about intelligence is people praising different rungs — the chess engine's fans call L2 the top, the benchmark crowd calls a gamed score intelligence, and nobody asks who chose the goal. With the levels, a Naya can be scored honestly. And the sweet spot is the answer to "what is all of this for": not a higher number, not a faster minute, but valuable useful production that does not stop.

## How to use it

Score every piece of work against the levels: did it sense first, did it adapt, did it learn efficiently, did it choose the goal? Never call L2 skill intelligence. Treat efficient learning as the metric that matters — smart-note every lesson the same pass. Judge every hour by the sweet spot: was the production continuous, valuable, and useful.
