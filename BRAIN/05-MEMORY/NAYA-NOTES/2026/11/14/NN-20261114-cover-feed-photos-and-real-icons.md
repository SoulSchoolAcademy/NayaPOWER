## Why it matters

"Are those the highest level of buttons we can offer?" is the right question, because the answer is a test, not an opinion: the bottom icons are the standard, and anything below them is not finished. A dot-only button fails the test in the open — it cannot say what it is, so the citizen has to guess, and guessing is not ten-star service. The same test, applied to photos, is respect for the citizen's own image: a forced crop is the system deciding what matters in someone else's photo.

## How to use it

Give every button a real icon that says what it is, built to the bottom-bar standard, from the icon library. Name the cover's size in the upload flow (1920×600), give the drag-to-frame editor, and keep a monthly special in the cover library. Let feed photos keep their own shape — 4:5 hero on phone, 16:9 video/landscape, 1:1 square — with a simple editor in the composer. Numbers that add up, or the page says honestly what they are.
