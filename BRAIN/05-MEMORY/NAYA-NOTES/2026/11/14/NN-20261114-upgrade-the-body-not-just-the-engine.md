## Why it matters

This is the answer to the most expensive mistake in AI: buying a bigger engine every time the cart is slow. Power is the engine; intelligence is the body — the systems, the memory, the distilled lessons, the behavior. A bigger engine in an unchanged body buys noise, not speed. For NayaNET it sets the build order: body first, power second, only where the body can use it.

## How to use it

Every build asks first: does this upgrade the body or only the engine? Distillation and remembering are the performance work. The four thinking questions run as a repeatable process before any act. Systems are built to build themselves. Alignment is the work: look for the pattern, the math, the connection.
