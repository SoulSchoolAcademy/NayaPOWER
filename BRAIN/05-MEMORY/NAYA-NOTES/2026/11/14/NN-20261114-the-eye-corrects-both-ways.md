## Why it matters

A verification system that only ever confirms the checker is a rubber stamp; one that only ever overturns the checker is an adversary. The eye is neither — it is the ground truth, and ground truth corrects both ways. That is what makes the double-check trustworthy: when it clears a fail, the maker can believe the clear; when it overturns a pass, the maker can believe the overturn. The method is the lesson: click what a human would click. A cell can be plain while its icon is a link — the defect lives in the icon, and only a click finds it.

Re-prove a checker's pass by eye before it stands (a click that navigates is a fail, however the cell measures).
