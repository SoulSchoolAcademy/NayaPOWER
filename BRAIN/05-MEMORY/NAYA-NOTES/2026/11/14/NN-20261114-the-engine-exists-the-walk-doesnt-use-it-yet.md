## Why it matters

A world that only speaks English is a world that only welcomes English speakers — and the civilization is for all people, all languages, everyone. The walk is the front door; if the front door can't greet a citizen in their own language, the citizen never gets inside to see the world waiting for them. The hard part is already done — the engine is built and proven — so the failure would not be a missing invention but a missing connection, the cheapest kind of failure to fix and the most embarrassing kind to leave.

## How to use it

Brief it after the walk end lands, never jammed into a build mid-flight.
