## Why it matters

A system that pays people is a system that will be gamed, and the first big run is not a hypothetical — it is the threat the whole protection exists for. One layer would fail: the cap alone is walked around, the hold alone punishes the honest, the score alone has to be defended. Three layers together mean the heist becomes a drip, the drip is visible, the money is cleared before it moves, and the gamers sort themselves out without anyone being accused. And the ladder matters just as much: a cap set too low tells the honest ambassador their best month is worth $3,000.
