## What it is

You go in and build without looking — you don't look at any of their pieces, you don't try to build the pieces like their pieces, you just build your own thing in the middle of their land — and yours is kind of flat and doesn't have a lot of style, and theirs all look extraordinary. The standard, not just the complaint: every button has to be the same level as the best button on the page; if one is elite and another is flat, the page isn't Naya. Look first, take the pieces verbatim, judge at rest by eye. And for the high end, right?" Filed in MEMORY.md the same pass. Durable rule (the Franchise Rule): build like the pieces already there — harvest the page's own good pieces verbatim, never build your own version in the middle of the brand's land.

## Why it matters

A brand is a promise the eye can check in two seconds, and one flat element breaks it for the whole page — the visitor does not think "one button is flat", they think "this page is not elite". The rule is cheap to follow and expensive to skip: looking first costs seconds, rebuilding a round costs hours, and his eye catching it costs trust. The telling protects the maker too: it separates a bad round from a bad builder.

## How to use it

Every build brief names the page's own good pieces to harvest before any new piece is drawn. No new button, board, or pill is invented on a page that already carries the pattern. The rest state is judged by eye before anything reaches his eyes. A round that fails the eye test is told plainly: what happened, the maker's normal standard, the standard to meet, and the gates that let it through.
