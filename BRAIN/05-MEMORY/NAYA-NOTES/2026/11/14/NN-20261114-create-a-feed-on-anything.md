## Why it matters

The step from "feeds for where you are from" to "feeds for anywhere, on anything" is the step from a product to a network: the first is a feature the citizen receives, the second is a power the citizen wields. And every created feed is a cluster the search engines and the AIs can find, so the network grows by what citizens care about instead of by what the system promotes. The auto/on-demand split is the privacy law made concrete.

## How to use it

Type any place — city, province, country — or any topic; the feed appears on the spot in the news-feeds format, own run three updates a day plus always-current. Every created feed is a mini social network (join, connect, instant-chat, invite). Every created feed gets its cluster page on the SEO rendered layer. The copy standard is baked in from birth. Auto stays only for where you're from.
