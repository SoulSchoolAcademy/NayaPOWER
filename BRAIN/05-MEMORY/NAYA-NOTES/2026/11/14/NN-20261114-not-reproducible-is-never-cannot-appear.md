## Why it matters

Every verification in the machine runs in some environment, and every environment has a blindness. If probes are allowed to turn their blindness into certainty, real defects get certified away — the banner would have stayed "cannot appear" while Shawn stared at it on his screen. A verdict's "cannot" must mean the world cannot, not that the probe could not see.

## How to use it

Never write "cannot appear" from a probe that could not reach the live data — write "not reproducible in the VM" and name the blindness first. Correct a wrong verdict in writing, own the miss, dispatch a fresh diagnosis. Keep settled items and open items separate.
