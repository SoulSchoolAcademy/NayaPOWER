## Why it matters

The entry dial is the first thing a citizen touches and the last thing a returning citizen sees — it is the door of the world. A gray, mushed-together dial tells them the world does not care; a jump that lands on the wrong page tells them the world does not know them. And the deeper lesson is in the comment: a wrong assumption written into code becomes a wrong behavior shipped to the citizen, and nobody notices because the button looks fine.
