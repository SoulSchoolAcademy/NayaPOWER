## Why it matters

Forgetting is not a moral failure — it is what minds do. The cure is not discipline but relocation: the remembering moves into a list the machine reads on a timer. The One List is the memory; the timers are the conscience; the self-audit is the mirror; the laws-as-code are the character. The machine reads the list every fifteen minutes and dispatches or escalates from it. Every cycle self-audits: promised against done, and the gap becomes the next note, lesson, and law. Every cycle reports one number: how many sat. Zero is the target.
