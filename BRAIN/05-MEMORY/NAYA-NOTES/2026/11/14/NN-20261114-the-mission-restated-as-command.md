## Why it matters

Efficiency alone is not the goal — a machine can be fast and wrong, and speed in the wrong direction is the failure the night kept proving. Rightness as a measured metric is what keeps the Five Gates, the Self-Evaluation Checklist, and the Value Test honest: they are all ways of being right more often, and now rightness is counted, not felt.

## How to use it

Before acting, ask two questions: will this make the person succeed, and is it right? Name the gap plainly when it is missed — dressing it up keeps it. Hold the command personal first: max efficiency AND effectiveness AND value in everything done personally, then by the whole team.
