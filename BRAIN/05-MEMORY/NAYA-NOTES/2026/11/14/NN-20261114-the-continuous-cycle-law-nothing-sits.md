## What it is

Shawn 00:41Z Nov 14 — his challenge, after eight hours with nothing in front of him: "How are you making out? ... you're sending the work away, it comes back a day later, then we started working where you're literally doing it in the feed, immediately, back to back — what happened to that? ... I'm waiting for the edits on the Personal page, and Dashboard ones, membership ones — some over a day, some all day. I know it doesn't take all day, a lot of times it takes 20 minutes, but then you just let it sit there and you don't show it to me and you just don't create a continuous cycle. If I don't see an interface to edit or give feedback on within definitely an hour there's something wrong, and I haven't seen one in hours. I'm not interested in you just showing me anything — I'm interested in you getting the work done, and if they're not all being worked on you better make sure they're all being worked on." His question at 00:47Z: how does it get fixed so work doesn't get done and sit there, or start and never finish — like a boss waiting hours for a project that was done hours ago and never told. The fix, proposed as law and ratified on his tap 00:48Z, written into team-nitro AGENTS.md as the third house law — THE CONTINUOUS CYCLE LAW, NOTHING SITS: (1) the chain auto-triggers — no finished work sits more than 15 minutes without its next step dispatched; the checker is named and dispatched as part of the maker's completion, not a separate decision the captain has to remember; the floor-watch flags finished-but-undispatched work as an emergency the same way it flags quiet seats; (2) every owed item gets a one-hour timer — his standard, an interface in front of him every hour or something is wrong; the floor-watch enforces it every 15 minutes; past the hour it gets named to HIM — the machine tells him within the hour, he never has to chase; (3) done means in front of his eyes — a round never ends without either a link to him or a new dispatch in the same pass; the last mile is part of the round, not an afterthought. The truth he was right about: the work went serial — auto-translate failed its checker twice and everything chained behind it landing; the Dashboard probe sat seven hours on one failed spawn never retried; the last thing shown to him was Personal-complete at 16:28Z.

## Why it matters

Work done and not shown is work not done — and a boss waiting hours for a project done hours ago feels disrespected, like his time doesn't matter and the work isn't real until he chases it. That is exactly what happened that night. The law makes the feeling impossible by mechanism: the machine tells him within the hour, every hour, so he never has to chase anything.

## How to use it

Every maker completion names and dispatches its checker in the same pass. Every owed item carries a one-hour timer, enforced by the floor-watch every fifteen minutes. Every round ends with a link to him or a new dispatch in the same pass. A failed spawn is re-dispatched the same pass. Lanes run in parallel — one lane's failure never blocks the machine.
