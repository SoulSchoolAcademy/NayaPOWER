## Why it matters

A home button that does not know whether you are a member is a door that does not know whether you live there. The routing rule is a small piece of logic with a large consequence: the member is taken home, the guest is shown the invitation — and neither is ever sent to the wrong place. The apps menu is the same idea at the scale of the whole network: NayaNET is not ten apps, it is one world with many feeds, and the citizen needs a table of contents to find their way through it — presented not as a list but as the high-tech interface itself, because the presentation is the feeling, and the feeling is the product.

## How to use it

Make Home know who you are — member to the Hub, guest to the pricing page. Give the Hub the same five icons as everywhere else. Make the apps button the table of contents for all your feeds — categorized, searchable, your favorites saved — in an extraordinary pop-up. The three items are queued: routing rides navigation, the feed menu rides the Smart Feed build, the icons ride the sweep.
