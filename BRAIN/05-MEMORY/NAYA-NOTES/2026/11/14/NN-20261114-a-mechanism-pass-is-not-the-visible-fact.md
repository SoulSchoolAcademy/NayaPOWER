## Why it matters

Every verification system drifts toward checking what is easy to check — the fixture, the programmatic fire, the byte count — and away from what matters, which is what the human sees. The drift is invisible because the checks keep passing. The only cure is a rule that cannot be gamed by a fixture: the eye reads the live page, adds the numbers, clicks the slot, and the page is whole only when the eye says so.
