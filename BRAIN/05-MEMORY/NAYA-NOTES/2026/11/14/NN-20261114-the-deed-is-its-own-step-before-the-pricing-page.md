## What it is

Shawn's 16:16Z clarification — the order, confirmed: the agreement is NOT on the pricing page. It is its OWN step before it — the contract, the deed: we give them the business, the brand, the land, the community, the user system; the agreement is what both sides collectively agree to. It unfolds block by block like an official letter; they click to agree at the bottom; THEN we give them their world (Personal · Business · Space · Dashboard); THEN they land on the pricing page and choose Free / Power / Pro / Max. Full order: sign the deed → receive your world → you're hired → pricing page. The build already had this order — confirmed, not changed. DEED LINE added to the manuscript cover (v2, in place): "This is the deed to your world — the land, the building, the business, the brand, and your permission to use them. It protects you, and it protects us, because now we both know how it works." Addendum 3b sent to the walk-end maker (re-read the cover; nothing agreement-worded goes on the pricing page itself).

## Why it matters

Order is meaning. Put the agreement on the pricing page and the citizen reads a promise through the lens of a price — every line becomes a justification for a number, and the promise shrinks to a sales pitch. Sign the deed first, receive the world second, and only then see the price — choosing from inside the kingdom, not from outside it. That order is the difference between a sale and a swindle in the citizen's own mind.

## How to use it

Keep the agreement its own step before the pricing page — never on it, never beside it. Order: deed → world → hired → pricing. Put the deed line on the manuscript cover, in his words. When the order is already right, confirm and hold it — don't redesign it.
