## Why it matters

Work has two halves: doing it, and proving it — and the second half is not paperwork, it is the work's existence. A file built but never pushed, proofs run but never recorded, a landing claimed but never written down: all of it is indistinguishable from work that never happened, because the next seat, the checker, the captain, and Shawn all arrive after the maker is gone, and the only thing they can see is the record. The finisher's reproof is the model — re-prove everything yourself, v2, and let v2 be the one that counts.

## How to use it

A maker that errors without a record leaves nothing behind — the finisher re-proves everything itself, and only the reproof banks. No proof ships without its record: the record file, the branch push, the staged bytes verified byte-identical. Scorecards name their next round. Pre-existing defects are named with their owner lane.
