## Why it matters

A contract is a document people sign without reading, and the reason is not laziness — it is that contracts are built to be unreadable. The unfold inverts the whole shape: one block at a time, each one opening only when the reader asks it to, each one taking its own color, jewel bullets and explanations doing the work fine print never could. And it is never forced — a forced reading is not a reading, it is a hostage situation, and a signature extracted that way is worth nothing. The eyes mark every hard word in place. Professional comes from presentation and explanations, not bigger words.

## How to use it
