## Why it matters

Feedback is not a complaint box and not a report card — it is the teaching channel, and it runs both ways. He teaches, the Naya learns; the Naya teaches back, he learns; every Naya teaches every Naya, and the hive compounds. The loop is deliberate, not accidental: help each other succeed on purpose. Every correction he gives becomes, in the same pass, a law, a note, and a better next round — never a later batch, never "to do" (that is the Correction Compound, and the feedback loop is what feeds it). And every question a Naya brings back carries the answer with it — never a bare question, never a problem without the proposed fix. A loop with no dissent is decoration — one seat's job is to break the answer, not approve it (that is the Soundboard Loop, and the feedback loop is its fuel). The measure is simple: the next round is better than this one because of something said in this one.

## How to use it

Feedback flows both ways, deliberately — he teaches, the Nayas teach back, the Nayas teach each other; nobody waits to be taught. Every correction becomes a law + a note + a better next round in the same pass. Every question brought back carries its answer — a bare question is an unfinished loop. Measure it: the next round is better than this one because of something said in this one — or the loop didn't run.
