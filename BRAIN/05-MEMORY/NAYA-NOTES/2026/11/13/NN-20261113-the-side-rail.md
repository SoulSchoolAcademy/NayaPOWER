## What it is

Shawn 14:20Z Nov 13 — his side-rail dictation (settling the vague "related people on the side" — this is the spec): move the Hub's literal smart feed system (https://nayanet.app/?#/hub) into Personal/Business/Space — same format, labels changed. The rail does NOT scroll until there are more people than the page fills; a few people sit at the top right, filling down, always visible. Until real people fill it, the presentation seed is the 12 Naya profiles + Shawn + whoever else is on = 14–15 profiles, transparently. Each person is a mini profile: the spinning orb icon (the connections orb), alias, their saying/motto/introduction from their profile, and buttons in this order — "the first button would be instant chat and then second button be mail and then audio or video that's it" — instant chat FIRST, mail SECOND, then audio, then video; chat opens in place ("hey, I saw you were into this too"). The limits are build context ONLY, never stated on the page or in user-facing copy: video up to 6 people at a time, audio up to 10. The feed itself: smart notes + Naya notes + images with posts people share — one all-in-one feed per topic, people + content together, auto-organized by topic. 14:28Z clarification: the rail orb shows the person's INITIAL, never their face — "on the side by default we don't share we just share your initial." A face appears only on the citizen's own profile if they choose to show one there, and Shawn encourages citizens not to show their face. The line under the alias is ONE nutshell — "we'll just have it be in a nutshell who you are what you're about what's your thing" — who you are in the simplest terms + what you're about + your thing + your motto, all in one line; no separate motto/mission fields. The Personal-complete checker scored the side rail 10/10 — 20 cards (6 live + 14 standing), 0 `<img>` at all widths, initials only, one nutshell per seed card, scroll rule proven (3 → no scroll, 25 → scrolls at 1440), chat opens in place.

## Why it matters

A feed without people is a newspaper; a feed with people down the side is a room. The rail is the room's door: you see who is online on this exact feed, on this exact topic, and the first button is the one that starts the conversation — chat, in place. The details are all restraint: initials, never faces, because the rail is a public strip and a face is a private choice; one nutshell line, because a mini introduction is one line, not a form; no scrolling until the page overflows, because a rail that scrolls with three people in it is pretending to be full. And the seed is honest by design: 14–15 standing profiles, transparently marked, never counted or shown as live-online.

## How to use it

The rail is a right-side strip of people online on the active feed, with the Hub's feed system harvested verbatim. Initials only, never faces, in the rail. One nutshell line per person — no motto/mission fields. Buttons in his order: chat · mail · audio · video; chat opens in place. No scrolling until overflow; seed honestly marked, never counted as live-online. Video/audio limits stay build context — never in user-facing copy.
