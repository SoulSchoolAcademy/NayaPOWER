## What it is

Shawn 04:16Z Nov 13 — everybody gets the SAME world, just different labels. The apps are universal, they all look like each other, like part of a family — beautiful, a high-tech million-dollar home (or billion-dollar) from the start. Why would anyone get a different world? Same presentation, same apps, same world; the labels change — the name, the words, the cover. This sharpens the Clone Law and the 04:01Z Personal-pages law, and it universalizes them: the rule is not sisters-only, it is for every citizen. Carried into the sisters' Personal-pages brief as the governing law the same hour.

## Why it matters

Every special version is a second world — a second thing to build, a second thing to fix, a second thing that drifts from the first. One world, done to the highest level, given to everybody, is worth more than ten worlds done halfway. The labels are the personalization — your name, your words, your cover — and the world underneath stays the same, so everything learned on one page works on every page, and everything fixed once is fixed for everyone. Nobody arrives to a half-built house because they are new. They arrive to the finished home, and they make it theirs with the labels.

## How to use it

No special worlds — not for sisters, not for beginners, not for anyone. One world, finished to the highest level, for everybody. Personalization lives in the labels only: name, words, cover. A fix to the world fixes it for everyone at once; a drift in one copy is a bug, not a feature. The sisters' pages are judged by this law first — same world, different labels — before anything else.
