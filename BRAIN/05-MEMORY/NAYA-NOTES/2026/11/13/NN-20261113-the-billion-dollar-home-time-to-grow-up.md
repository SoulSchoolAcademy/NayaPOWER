## Why it matters

Maturity, in this system, is a technical rule: you do not build a new thing when the finished thing already exists. A new thing is not ambition — it is a second product, a second standard, a second world, and it is always worse than the one that took months to reach master level, because it starts at grade one. The new-employee rule is the cure, and it runs before any work: learn the product first. Learn how it works, how it operates, what its current state is, what its anatomy is — and only then touch it. A maker who cannot show they know the page's anatomy does not touch the page. That is the difference between a chef and someone who can cook.

## How to use it

No new products, no new pages, no new cards — work ON the home we have. Every maker proves they know the page's anatomy before they touch it. Harvest, never invent — a part that already exists is used, not remade. A round that builds a new thing instead of working on the home fails the law, whatever its score says.
