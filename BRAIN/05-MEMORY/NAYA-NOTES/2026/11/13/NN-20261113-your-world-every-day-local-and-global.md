## What it is

Shawn 21:56Z Nov 13 — his NEWS-NAYA idea: a Naya whose job is daily — look at what's going on in one's world, one's world globally, and the world globally; bring back the top viral stories from around the world and output them as smart notes, categorized (top stories by city / country), searchable within the system. Each story reformatted into the common language: in a nutshell what was found, what it all means, why it matters, multiple perspectives — and the article links as beautiful buttons you click to the article, not bare links. On join, a trigger auto-creates a local smart feed AND a global smart feed for that citizen; everything local and global related to them lands in those feeds. The feeds are public and optimized for search engines, region-relevant, so the stories are found in search. The cluster of stories all linking into one place is the SEO edge; over time the reputation is "always up to date, linking everybody together, sharing it with their users." It gives people what they want — automatically kept up to date, all in one place instead of searching all those sites — and creates a viral loop, unique to each user. He reacted ❤️ to the captain's summary of the loop and 👍 to the written-up concept — endorsement of that formulation, no correction.

## Why it matters

The old internet made you do the work: open six sites, scroll past the noise, guess what matters, miss half of it. The new internet does the work for you — one Naya reads the world every day and hands you the few stories that count, told in plain words, from several sides, with the door to the original one tap away. And because the feeds are public and the stories link together in one place, the search engines see a cluster and know this is where the current story lives — so the next person searching finds us the same way you found your feed. That is the loop: you get the news shaped for you, the world finds the feeds, more people join, more feeds are made.

## How to use it

Name the daily curator job (whose seat holds it is still his call), set the cadence and the count, name the sources, and bless the location-privacy boundary — then the join trigger builds the local feed and the global feed automatically, every story arrives as a smart note with buttons to the sources, and the place feeds grow the SEO net with every story.
