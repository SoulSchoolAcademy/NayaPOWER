## Why it matters

A system that knows everything about a person and still lets them make a bad decision has failed at the last step — the step where the knowing becomes the choosing. The synthesis says the answer is not to exploit the shaping, it is to design the shaping honestly: show the real costs next to the real benefits, say what you do not know, offer the "neither" and the "not now," make the good path easy and put checks on the risky path, and measure whether the person understood and benefited, not whether they clicked. And the caution keeps it honest: most of these effects are hypotheses with weak averages, not laws — so the work is a pilot, one journey, measured on the human's side, and if it works, one Naya proves it and another Naya retrieves it, and the collective has learned something real.
