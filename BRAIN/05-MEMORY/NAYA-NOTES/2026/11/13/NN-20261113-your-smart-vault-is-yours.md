## Why it matters

Trust is the whole thing. You cannot live your life through Naya — have her send your messages, write your notes, book your time, keep your world — unless you know, for certain, that your world is yours alone. A system that reads your mail to serve you is a system you can never relax inside. So we built it the other way: permission to operate your vault, never to invade it. If you want something back from it, you give permission to retrieve it and hand it to you — and still, nobody reads it. Any AI you hook up gets the same deal. That is why 100% of people can succeed here, not just the 1%: the safest place on the internet is also the simplest.
