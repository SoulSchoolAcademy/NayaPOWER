## Why it matters

A score nobody can see is a score that changes nothing. Making the pill obsidian with a gold rim and an extraordinary star turns a number into something a person wants to watch climb — and once they want it to climb, they put more in, and the network gets more valuable for everyone. Contribution becomes authority, authority becomes weight, weight becomes visibility, visibility brings the next contribution. But the law has two halves and only one is built — a law announced as done when only its visible half exists is how the machine fools itself.
