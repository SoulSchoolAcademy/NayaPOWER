## What it is

## Why it matters

Speed is not a mood, it is a number: 1–1.5 hours for a full cycle, and the captain checks the moment a verdict lands. The 9+ rule is the filter that protects his eyes: only work that scores 9 or better reaches him, work below 9 goes back in, and the one case that does reach him without a score is the case the captain genuinely does not know. And the honest rate is part of the law: a law that pretends the machine is faster than it is cannot be used to plan.

## How to use it

No cycle runs past 1.5 hours. No verdict waits — the captain checks the moment it lands. Below 9 goes back in, always; 9+ goes to Shawn; uncertainty goes to Shawn too, by his own rule. The rate is counted honestly — seats + queues is the gap to close, not effort.
