## Why it matters

A factory is not a crowd of workers — it is stations, each with a named owner, each doing one job to a standard, back to back, with someone who knows the whole map and someone who verifies. Before this order the machine ran rounds: a round finished, the seat went quiet, and the next round had to be invented. Shawn's order replaces rounds with seats: a seat owns a page until it is 10/10, and when one round lands the next is already queued.

## How to use it

Seats, not rounds — a named owner per page until 10/10, back to back, hourly updates. The map-keeper holds the state of every page continuously and brings the gaps back for assignment. The verifier stands apart and never builds. Three major areas, each a full-time team, each owned end to end. No seat finishes and goes quiet — the next job is already queued.
