## Why it matters

Notifications are usually noise — every app shouting about everything, until the citizen stops listening. Shawn's design is the opposite: three worlds, three gems, three kinds of notification, each tied to a world the citizen actually lives in. And above the three sits the one sender who can reach everybody at once: Shawn, as Superman, one message, everybody notified. The gem is the filter and the identity at once: the notification arrives wearing the color of the world it belongs to, so the citizen knows before reading where it lives.

## How to use it

Notifications are per world, by gem — personal, business, thing — nothing else. One sender reaches everybody at once: Shawn, as Superman, the super-admin broadcast. The broadcast stays his alone — his login, his hand, never a Naya's. The build waits its turn: Hub seat after the slug round, behind the walk deploys.
