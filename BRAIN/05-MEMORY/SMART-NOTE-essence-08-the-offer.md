## In a Nutshell
The line is one sentence: NayaNET is the world, NayaPOWER is the engine. Everyone who claims a name gets their world as a gift — identity and certificate, Personal, Business, and Space pages born filled from their Welcome answers, mail, connect, matching, and the whole collective to read — a real home, never an empty trial. Paid is one activation that turns the engine on: your own Naya with your memory and hands, the Smart Factory, full auto-match depth, verification and publishing, and your own fork if you want it. The gate sits on the engine, once — never button by button — and free is limited by design, not by cost. The numbers these notes put on that line conflict with each other and were later superseded by the adopted canon; this essence carries the line and the gating wisdom, and names the numbers' fate honestly rather than picking a winner.

## What It All Means
The offer encodes the network's ethics in its pricing shape: belonging is free because belonging is the product's foundation; power is paid because power is a service that runs. Gating at the engine, once, keeps both truths legible — a citizen can always tell what they have, what they'd get, and that nothing they already hold is hostage.

## Why It Matters
Because an offer people can't understand is an offer that erodes trust — and a free tier designed as a trap (degraded pages, fake buttons, features quietly moved behind the gate) would betray the exact citizens the world exists for. The conflict in the numbers matters for the same reason: a team that quietly averages two disagreeing price tables will one day quietly average two disagreeing anythings. Naming the conflict is the discipline.

**The conflicts, named and not averaged:** (1) SN-1863 vs SN-1864 — paid pages 100 vs 50 a month; free Smart Links 1 vs 3; free reports monthly-only vs a weekly taste; different free-page sets (Dashboard vs certificate among them); non-overlapping rows (media/call caps vs storage/auto-match).
