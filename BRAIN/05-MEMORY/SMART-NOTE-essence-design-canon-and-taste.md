That disagreement is recorded here, not resolved (see the sep-2 receipt).

Taste, distinct from canon, transfers through loops, not rules: a fully canon-compliant room was scrapped on sight because compliance is a floor and the director's eye is the ceiling — the loop is build → show → absorb the verdict → rebuild, and the transferable residue is calibrations like the size bar (primary text 17–18px, secondary 14–15px, labels ≥11px), which survive as numbers even though the judgment that produced them does not compress into a rule (SN-0193). Language obeys the same discipline: quote the approved sample verbatim, then namespace it — approved phrasing is a source asset, harvested exactly, never paraphrased into drift (SN-0205). Interfaces must be honest objects: a manufactured action — a control that performs engagement rather than doing anything — is cut, never dressed; a fake affordance is worse than an absent one because it spends the user's trust (SN-0203).

## Intelligence views

**Human:** The design books were actually read and turned into checkable numbers: button sizes, response times, how many choices at once. But passing every check only gets a design to the door — the director's eye still decides, and a page can pass everything and still be scrapped. Approved words get quoted exactly, not paraphrased, and a button that pretends to do something is worse than no button at all.

**Child:** The grown-ups read a whole shelf of picture-book experts and wrote down their tricks as rules you can measure, like "buttons big enough for fingers." But even if you follow all the rules, the picture still has to LOOK right to the person it's for — and fake buttons that don't do anything get taken off, because pretending is worse than missing.

**Grandma:** The cookbooks on the shelf were actually read, dear, and their lessons turned into habits you can check — measures, timings, portions. But a cook knows the recipe passing every test doesn't make the meal: you still taste it, and the head of the house still decides. You'd never serve a pretty plate with nothing on it, either; decoration pretending to be dinner fools no one twice.

**Naya (AI):** Apply the tunings as computable checks (targets ≥44px, response ≤400ms, options ≤7, chunks ≤4). Treat canon status honestly: CANDIDATE until ratified — do not cite it as governing. Run taste through loops, not arguments: build, show, absorb the verdict, rebuild. Quote approved language byte-exact under its namespace. Delete manufactured actions on sight.

## The Learning

The era's design lesson is double: knowledge compresses, taste doesn't. The books yielded numbers that transferred perfectly (SN-0179, SN-0182); the eye yielded only calibrations, earned one scrapped room at a time (SN-0193). A system that confuses the two either freezes its taste into rules that age badly, or leaves its knowledge as vibes that transfer never. Keep each in its own vessel — that separation is the learning.
