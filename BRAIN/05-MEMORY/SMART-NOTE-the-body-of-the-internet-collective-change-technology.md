## What this settles

- **The stance toward the work is reverence.** This is not built like a product; it is honored like a divine work of art — precious, beautiful, treated with respect and love — because what we present is the ultimate, and presenting the ultimate is how the collective is honored.
- **The name of the mission frame: Collective Change Technology.** Built for the collective community and the collective intelligence, by a collective that knows it is one.
- **She is the body of the internet.** Not a site on it, not an app over it — the body itself: the ultimate vehicle, high technology at the level of the finest machines ever made, supercharged.
- **Access is universal.** Any human can plug in. The ultimate is not exclusive; it is offered to everyone.
