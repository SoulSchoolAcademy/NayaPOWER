## Reconciliation decisions

### R1 — Smart Note is not the permanent primitive
The corpus originally used Smart Note heavily. The reconciled architecture keeps Smart Note as a representation/capture concept and makes the durable architectural primitive the Intelligent Node/Object.

### R2 — Intelligent Block is retained by compatibility
Existing implementations and contracts may use “Intelligent Block.” It is treated as a governed durable intelligence object where required. The broader abstraction is Intelligent Node/Object.

### R3 — Concept #7 and #8 are one source
They have the same Git blob SHA. They must never produce two canonical concepts merely because two source filenames exist.

### R4 — Nine Nodes absorb the broad capability universe
The historical blueprint is not implemented as dozens of independent brains/modules. Durable responsibilities are assigned to the Nine Nodes and subordinate contracts.

### R5 — Hub is a projection
Feed, rooms, library, mail, reports and similar surfaces do not become competing intelligence stores.

### R6 — Channel neutrality
MCP, REST, GitHub, A2A, SDK, web, messaging and other channels are doors into one governed system.

### R7 — Proof outranks assertion
Repository presence, successful execution, HTTP success, receipts and agent claims do not alone prove desired outcomes.

### R8 — Learning requires future effect
A stored lesson is a candidate or record until later behavior demonstrates meaningful use.

### R9 — Self-building remains bounded
The system may propose and build within authorized scope, but cannot manufacture its own authority.

### R10 — Contextual graph is a capability, not a second brain
Concept #14 is compiled into KNOW/CONNECT/PROVE/VERIFY/LEARN and the existing graph layer. No independent Graph-RAG subsystem is created.

### R11 — Value is a measurement layer, not human worth
Concept #15's Value Calculus measures system/object/action contribution and resource efficiency. It must not become a measure of intrinsic human worth or an automatic monetary valuation.

This ledger is provenance metadata. It is not a substitute for source documents.
