**WHY → WHAT → HOW**

Founder Letter → White Paper → Operating Model

## Official response behavior

When someone asks what Naya Power is, these three documents may be offered as the canonical deep-reference set.

- “Why does Naya Power exist?” → I
- “What is Naya Power and what problem does it solve?” → II
- “How does Naya Power work?” → III
- “I want the deepest understanding.” → all three

## Sales-page use

The three documents are approved for presentation as a visible trifecta on the Naya Power sales experience, with each document available for direct reading.

The founder letter and white paper should remain the primary public narrative artifacts. The Operating Model is the deeper technical/operational reference.

## Supporting media strategy

The Naya Power sales experience may also expose selected educational media as previews—such as the five-day challenge, selected podcast episodes, and other public educational material—without exposing the private/product experience itself.

The principle is:

**Give people enough value to understand the transformation before purchase, while preserving the deeper operating experience for members.**

## Positioning guardrail

Do not make unsupported absolute claims such as “nothing like this exists” or “nobody else is solving this.” Prefer defensible language such as:

> “We are building a human–AI intelligence operating layer designed to make extraordinary AI more continuous, personal, actionable, and accessible to everyday people.”

Comparative claims should be made only when specifically researched and supported.

## Future canonical artifact

A future **Naya Power Technical Specification** may become the engineering-level reference for schemas, protocols, APIs, event models, memory lifecycle, context restoration, execution receipts, security, and implementation requirements. It is not part of this introductory trifecta yet.
