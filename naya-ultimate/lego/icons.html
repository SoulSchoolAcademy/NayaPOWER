Naya Lego · Icons

Icons are drawn, not shrunk.

Vector geometry, readable at 16px. One coherent visual family: 24×24 viewBox, 2px stroke, round caps and joins. White strokes on dark. If you can't make it good, omit it.

The law: A shrunken render is mud. Every icon is drawn for its size. An empty space is honest — a bad icon is a lie.
