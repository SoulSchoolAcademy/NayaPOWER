Naya Lego · Buttons

The Button Family

Black heart. White voice. Visible skin. Four systems, one DNA. Use heroes for the three flagship actions, the living system for spectrum-aware controls, the essential when simplicity wins, and primo for everyday actions.

The law: No flat buttons. Every button has depth, responds to hover, and ignites in its own color. Light up, don't glow out — depth through layers, not neon.

01 · Heroes — .living-btn

Why: Shawn-approved flagship buttons. Dark heart reads dimensional on black ground. The light sweep crosses on its own rhythm — something is always moving. Use for: the three most important actions on any page.

<button class="living-btn" data-spectrum="magenta">
  <span class="btn-glow"></span>
  <span class="btn-label">✦ ENTER THE STANDARD</span>
</button>
<!-- data-spectrum: magenta | purple | blue -->

02 · Living System — .lv-btn

Why: The most advanced button code. Seven shadow layers. Cursor sheen follows your pointer. Wakes at 160px proximity. Physical press with inner shadow deepening. Use for: any action that needs a spectrum identity.

Move your cursor near a button — it wakes before you touch it. The sheen follows your pointer. That's the living system.

<span class="lv-wrap">
  <span class="lv-aura"></span>
  <button class="lv-btn" style="--lv-accent:#e84fff;--lv-accent-rgb:232,79,255">
    <span class="lv-label">✦ Your Action</span>
  </button>
</span>
<!-- Requires living.js for proximity + cursor sheen. Sizes: lv-hero, lv-sm. Danger: lv-btn--danger -->

03 · Essential — .cx-btn

Why: Four shadows, perfectly chosen, beat seven layers of complexity when the job is simple. The simplicity benchmark. Use for: secondary actions, quiet contexts.

<button class="cx-btn">＋ CREATE</button>

04 · Standard — .primo

Why: The everyday workhorse. Themed glow variants per spectrum color. Use for: standard actions throughout any app.

<button class="primo theme-purple">Your Action</button>
