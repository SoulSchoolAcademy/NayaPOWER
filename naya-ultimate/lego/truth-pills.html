Naya Lego · Truth

Truth is part of the visual language.

A claim and a proof must never look the same. Scores move on evidence, never optimism. What is verified glows green; what is merely claimed stays quiet until it earns its light.

The law: Animated ≠ alive. Stored ≠ learned. Claimed ≠ proven. Every claim-bearing surface carries its truth state. No exceptions.

01 · Truth Cards — the three states

Why: Each card is a --tc2-driven truth statement: colored orb glyph, spaced cap label, plain-English evidence line. Hover ignites the card in its truth color.

✓
AUTH · VERIFIED

Independent evidence exists. The number survived contact with reality.

?
CLAIM · UNVERIFIED

Self-reported, awaiting its stamp. Shown honestly, never hidden.

◈
DEMONSTRATION

Specimen content. Beautiful on purpose, not pretending to be live.
