Install NayaNET

Put NayaNET on your home screen. One tap opens straight into your Hub — with the crystal N as your icon.

Your browser needs the manual way — seconds, I promise:

iPhone
  1. Tap Share — the square with the arrow pointing up.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Done.
Android
  1. Tap the three dots ⋮ up top.
  2. Tap Install app.
  3. Tap Install. Done.

Firefox or another browser? ⋮ → Share → Add to Home Screen.

Laptop / desktop
  1. Click the three dots ⋮ in the top-right corner.
  2. Click Install NayaNET….
  3. Click Install. Done.

On Edge: ⋮ → Apps → Install this site as an app.
