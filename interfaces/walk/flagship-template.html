✦ My Smart Link

A Smart Link is your own short NayaNET address — nayanet.live/your-name — that sends people to any link you choose and counts every click on the way through. Paste any link you want tracked — a video, a product page, your ambassador link — claim a name, then share the short address everywhere. Every click is counted, and when someone who arrived through it joins NayaNET, that join is credited to your link too. You choose where it sends people, and NayaNET never changes that. Pick your name carefully — a claimed name can’t be renamed later.

One short address for everything you share — and every click on it, counted. Paste the link you share today, pick the name you want instead, and your short link is live — with every click on it counted.
