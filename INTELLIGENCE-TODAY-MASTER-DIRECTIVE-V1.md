## 0. THE ONE SENTENCE

**Your Intelligence Today is the human's day, distilled — what happened,
what changed, what matters, what deserves to be remembered — so they never
have to replay their own life.**

It is not a dashboard. It is not a KPI grid (automatic rejection). It is
the **Daily Intelligence Cockpit**: the place a human opens every morning
and trusts by night.

## 1. THE JOB

- **Human job** (contract 14A): *"Know what matters right now."*
- **Primary action**: *"Act on top next move."* — one obvious next action,
  verb-first, unmistakable. If two actions compete for primacy, choose.
- **Fundamental question** (room table): *"What's important about my
  intelligence today?"*
- **The seven questions the room must answer**: What happened? What did I
  learn? What changed? What matters? What should I remember? What am I
  missing? What can I do about it?
- **Information hierarchy** (contract 14A, canonical order):
  **NOW → NEXT → WATCH → LEARNED → WAITING → RECENT PROOF.**
  NOW dominates.
- **The eight-step flow** (Smart Tab concept): ORIENT → SEE → UNDERSTAND →
  PRIORITIZE → REMEMBER → QUESTION → ACT → VERIFY.
- **The five-beat arc** (every surface): PRESENCE → RECOGNITION →
  DISTILLATION → INVITATION → FLOW.
- **Morning/evening aware** (contract 14): the room knows what time of the
  human's day it is and composes accordingly.

## 2. THE RUTHLESS FILTER

Every component on this page must do at least one of these, or it does
not belong: **CAPTURE · UNDERSTAND · REMEMBER · CONNECT · DECIDE · ACT ·
VERIFY** something valuable.

Before keeping any element, ask the three rejection questions (14D):
1. Does it help the human understand?
2. Does it help the human act or recover?
3. Does it communicate something true?
A "no" means REMOVE unless a stronger documented reason exists.

Usefulness and value are the keywords. "Sure, you put something there —
but what purpose does it serve? Would anyone actually need that?" If the
answer is vague, the component is decorative bullshit. Cut it.

## 3. THE KILLER FEATURES (buildable form)

**WHAT CHANGED** — the single most valuable thing this room can do. Not
"here are today's notes" but *"here is what is different from yesterday."*
Each change carries: what moved, **why it matters**, evidence, state.
This is intelligence, not inventory.

**WHAT I LEARNED** — Naya must distinguish the ladder:
INFORMATION ("I encountered this") → UNDERSTANDING ("I now understand
this") → CHANGE ("my understanding changed") → DECISION ("I decided
this") → KNOWLEDGE ("this is now durable intelligence"). The room shows
where today climbed the ladder.

**WHAT MATTERS NOW** — evidence-backed relevance, and crucially the WHY.
Never "You should do X." Always *"This appears important because X
changed, Y is unresolved, and Z depends on it."* Unresolved questions,
blocked work, new discoveries, awaiting actions, things whose consequences
matter.

**WHAT TO REMEMBER** — the deeply human one. *"These are the things from
today worth carrying forward."* The room must let the human mark what
survives into tomorrow — continuity they can touch. Tomorrow's briefing
starts where they left it.

**WHAT AM I MISSING** — stated as KNOWN / CHANGED / UNCERTAIN / MISSING.
*"You established X, but Y has not been verified."* This prevents false
completion. **Unknown ≠ success.** Never let the room imply a finished
day the evidence doesn't support.

**Progressive disclosure everywhere** (14A.7):
GLANCE → UNDERSTAND → INSPECT → PROVE. The first view gives distilled
meaning; deeper evidence is one tap away, never dumped.

## 4. VISUAL LAW FOR THIS ROOM

- **Theme: magenta #d86cff — human significance.** "Your Intelligence
  Today wears magenta because it is about the human." It *signs* the room
  (threshold jewel, header edge, primary accents) and *recedes* while the
  human works. It never floods. A room drowned in its theme is shouting
  its own name.
- **Color discipline**: every colored pixel has exactly one named job
  (emerald = alive/verified · gold #e8b64c = extraordinary consequence,
  rarest word · red = blocked/failed, never decorative · purple = Naya ·
  sapphire = knowledge). Point at any colored pixel and name its job; if
  you cannot, remove it. Per-play color may only encode the object's
  stable identity — never list position, never decoration.
- **Component physics**: DEPTH → LIGHT → STATE → RESPONSE → CONSEQUENCE.
  Boards are *destinations you can enter*, not cards with information.
  Buttons are physical objects: rest (quiet confidence) → hover ("I see
  you") → press (compresses, "I feel you") → processing (honest) →
  success (brief emerald) / failure (red + reason in words + recovery).
  Labels verb-first. No fake anything.
- **Type & space**: 16px floor, 17–18px target; labels never below 11px.
  Spacing on the 4/8/12/16/24/32/48/64/96 scale. Generosity is respect.
- **LAW ZERO (the veto)**: nothing — no glow, no depth, no color, no
  animation — may ever reduce readability. Beautiful but hard to read =
  wrong, overruling everything.
- **Motion**: only to communicate truthful state change. Three durations,
  one easing, transform + opacity. Reduced motion → instant, no argument.
- **The four questions, always answerable**: WHERE AM I? WHAT MATTERS
  NOW? WHAT CAN I DO? WHAT HAPPENS NEXT? (1s / 3s / 30s / return-visit.)

## 5. HONESTY LAW FOR THIS ROOM

- **Never manufacture**: intelligence, activity, counts, personalization,
  relationships, reports, receipts, completion, proof. (14A.5)
- **NOT_VERIFIED is a valid product state.** Honest absence beats
  fabricated richness. "A quiet day can stay quiet — no verified
  highlight was manufactured just to fill the page" — plus *one useful
  action*, never a dead end.
- **Never claim Naya remembered something she didn't.** Never show fake
  liveness, fake progress, fake personalization. If the interface says
  something happened, something happened.
- **The room is a projection, not a store.** Hub → runtime adapter →
  governed runtime → canonical intelligence. No duplicate intelligence
  stores merely for presentation. (Side Bar concept §14: semantic
  duplication is the biggest architectural risk.)
- **Claim only the rung you have evidence for**:
  IMPLEMENTED → INSPECTED → VERIFIED → LIVE VERIFIED → HUMAN APPROVED.
  "It renders" is not "it works."

## 6. COMPOSITION — THE BUILDABLE PAGE

```
ORIENT      Date · (true) greeting · the day at a glance (scoreline)
  ↓
NOW         What changed — ranked plays, biggest first.
            Each play: rank · time anchor · the announcer's call ·
            the nutshell. Tap → full depth (GLANCE→UNDERSTAND→INSPECT→PROVE).
            Turning points carry gold: consequence made visible.
  ↓
NEXT        The top next move. THE primary action. Verb-first.
  ↓
WATCH       What to keep an eye on.
  ↓
LEARNED     Where today climbed the ladder (info→understanding→decision→knowledge).
  ↓
WAITING     Open loops — honest, resumable.
  ↓
REMEMBER    What the human marks to carry forward → tomorrow's lineup.
  ↓
PROOF       Evidence one tap away, always. SOURCE→EVENT→STATE chain.
```

- One focal region; the squint test must preserve hierarchy.
- Full-bleed river feel; effortless scroll; never a dashboard grid.
- Mobile is first-class, not a shrunk desktop.
