# 🔱 NAYANET — E01 DESIGN DIRECTIVE

**Status:** CANONICAL DESIGN DIRECTIVE
**Date:** 2026-08-30
**Purpose:** Visual and experiential construction law for E01.

## Design thesis

E01 is the first room of an intelligence network. It must feel like an instrument, not a website; a place, not a page; an invitation, not a sales funnel.

The emotional arc is:

**Curiosity → Recognition → Possibility → Empowerment → Trust → Delight → Momentum.**

The north-star reaction is:

> **“Oh. This is my intelligent space. I can actually use this.”**

## Composition

### Hero / Arrival
Create a large, quiet visual field. Naya is present. The Living Sun is the center of gravity. The headline is short. Supporting copy is brief. The CTA is singular and obvious.

Avoid hero clutter, feature lists, pricing tables, navigation bars that compete with the experience, and generic SaaS patterns.

### Identity reveal
After name entry, the composition should visibly transform rather than simply append a card. The person has crossed a threshold into their NayaNET space.

### Personal home
The Smart Name and Smart Link become the identity anchor. The Toolbox appears as an orbiting intelligence system. The two highest-value actions receive the strongest visual weight.

## Living Sun art direction

The Sun should feel like a luminous intelligence instrument built from precise geometry:

- large ambient radial field;
- multiple concentric rings with different opacity and speed;
- one controlled orbital path;
- small intelligence nodes;
- luminous central core;
- approved Naya identity at the center where appropriate;
- depth through blur, luminosity and scale;
- subtle asymmetry so it feels alive.

Do not turn it into a spinning neon toy. Motion is information.

### State language
RESTING = breathing presence.

ATTENTION = gentle increase in energy.

LISTENING = open receiving ring.

THINKING = layered directional orbit.

SPEAKING = calm synchronized pulse.

SUCCESS = short outward wave.

WARNING/ERROR = semantic change plus words.

DISCONNECTED = quiet, reduced energy.

## Color rhythm

Deep-space/black establishes the canvas.

Purple establishes Naya identity.

Magenta signals activation and energy.

Blue signals intelligence and communication.

Green signals growth and successful completion.

Gold/yellow marks insight and premium emphasis.

Never rely on color alone for state. Never place low-contrast purple on purple.

## Typography

Use the hierarchy:

**HEADLINE → SUPPORTING STATEMENT → DETAIL**

Headlines should be short, confident and emotionally legible. Detail is earned by interaction.

## Geometry

The visual grammar is based on:

**circle → orbit → node → ring → core → connection.**

This geometry should appear consistently in the Sun, identity treatment, toolbox, progress indicators and transition states.

## Controls

Primary controls are large, concise verbs:

**Create My NayaNET**
**Enter NayaNET**
**Meet Naya**
**Start the Challenge**
**Experience Naya Power**
**Enter My Hub**

Secondary controls are quieter. Tertiary information is progressively disclosed.

No dead buttons. If a destination is future, the control opens a useful future-state explanation.

## Personalization

After identity creation, address the person by name. Show their Smart Name. Make the Smart Link feel like a newly issued digital key, while clearly identifying it as a static/future identity representation until production account infrastructure is connected.

## Naya presence

Naya should feel close, calm and intelligent. She should not fill every inch of the interface. Her presence is established by the approved identity asset, the Sun, concise language, responsive state changes and helpful guidance.

The interface should make Naya the guide and the human the driver.

## Mobile law

Design mobile as the primary physical constraint. The composition must stack elegantly. The Sun remains recognizable. The primary CTA remains reachable. Toolbox nodes become a vertical sequence or compact orbit. No hover-only affordances. No horizontal overflow.

## Groove law

The block must behave as a complete application section inside an iframe. Its visual height should follow content naturally. It must not depend on the parent page. It should look intentional when embedded at narrow widths.

## Accessibility law

Every visual state has text semantics. Focus is obvious. Motion can be reduced. Controls are keyboard accessible. Contrast is sufficient. The Sun has an accessible status description. Important meaning is never encoded only by color or motion.

## Excellence gate

Oscar must reject E01 if:

- it looks like a generic SaaS dashboard;
- the first action is ambiguous;
- the Sun is merely decoration;
- the user cannot tell what is real;
- buttons lead nowhere;
- the visual system is noisy;
- mobile feels like a shrunken desktop;
- official Naya identity has been replaced or distorted;
- the experience explains architecture instead of delivering value;
- the block is beautiful but not useful;
- the block is useful but not delightful.

## Final design law

> **Every element must communicate state, hierarchy, meaning, or emotion. If it does none of those, remove it.**
