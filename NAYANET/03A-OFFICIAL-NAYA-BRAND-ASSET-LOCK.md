## 3. Usage hierarchy

When representing Naya as a person/presence, prefer the approved profile images. When representing Naya as an identity, app icon, launcher, badge, or compact brand mark, prefer the official icon/logo assets.

Other existing Naya profile assets may be used when a particular experience requires them, but the four assets above are the default brand references and should dominate the product.

## 4. Do not

- Do not generate a visually different Naya portrait and present it as the official Naya.
- Do not substitute generic AI imagery.
- Do not distort, crop, recolor, or stylize the official assets in a way that changes identity without a documented design reason.
- Do not introduce competing Naya icons merely to decorate a screen.

## 5. Design principle

The asset is not the experience. The interface, Living Sun, motion, typography, spacing, interaction, and Naya behavior must create the extraordinary experience around the approved identity.

**Official Naya imagery establishes identity; product design establishes presence.**
