# 🔱 NAYANET — IDENTITY + INTELLIGENT CONNECTION BLUEPRINT

## 1. Identity

Every participant receives a NayaNET identity as early as possible.

The identity can become a durable network primitive supporting:

- personal portal;
- Smart Name;
- Smart Link;
- referral/badge link;
- Smart Mail;
- connection address;
- future AI identity.

## 2. Progressive security

The experience should allow low-friction entry while progressively encouraging account security.

Security-sensitive actions must require authenticated identity even if the initial onboarding experience begins with a simple name.

## 3. Smart Link

A Smart Link is a human-readable portal into a person's NayaNET presence.

Potential capabilities:

- profile;
- public artifacts;
- referral attribution;
- contact/connection request;
- AI-readable introduction where explicitly enabled.

## 4. Smart Mail

Smart Mail is the network's future intelligent messaging identity.

It should support:

- direct messages;
- notifications;
- introductions;
- daily reports;
- intelligent connection notices;
- system communications.

## 5. Intelligent discovery

Users should ask for outcomes, not search databases manually.

Example:

> "Find people in Kelowna interested in AI and entrepreneurship who are open to connecting."

Naya interprets intent, applies permission/privacy rules, and returns appropriate opportunities.

## 6. Matching

Matching signals may include:

- topic/interest;
- location where permitted;
- stated goals;
- availability;
- communication mode;
- shared projects;
- compatibility signals;
- opt-in connection status.

Do not infer sensitive traits without explicit product justification and governance.

## 7. Connection consent

A match is not a connection.

The system recommends; humans decide.

A connection request should make clear:

- who/what is requesting;
- why the match exists;
- what information is visible;
- what happens after acceptance.

## 8. Human + AI rooms

A room may contain:

- one human;
- multiple humans;
- one or more Nayas/AI agents;
- shared topic context;
- shared artifacts;
- optional aliases.

Naya may summarize, facilitate, translate, research, organize, and help execute agreed actions.

## 9. Alias / anonymous participation

Participants may eventually interact using an alias or anonymous-to-participants identity.

The platform must still maintain sufficient internal account integrity for safety, consent, abuse prevention, and moderation.

## 10. Trust controls

Required future controls:

- block;
- mute;
- report;
- leave room;
- revoke connection;
- visibility settings;
- contribution controls;
- identity mode controls.

## 11. Network law

> **Connect people by meaningful intent, not by extracting attention.**

The purpose of the network is useful communication, learning, opportunity, friendship, and intelligence—not engagement for engagement's sake.
