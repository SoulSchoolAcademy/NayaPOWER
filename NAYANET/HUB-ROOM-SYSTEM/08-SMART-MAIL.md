# 08 — Smart Mail

## Product job
Make communication an intelligence surface while keeping the human as final authority over sending.

## Core views
IMPORTANT, RESPOND, FOLLOW UP, DRAFTS, SENT.

## Message detail
Message content, sender/recipient context, related intelligence, relevant history, suggested response, consequences/considerations, and explicit send authority state.

## Rules
Reading is not sending. Drafting is not sending. Suggestion is not sending. Never send without explicit governed authority.

## Proof
Real mail data only; clear source and authorization state; no fabricated messages or delivery claims.
