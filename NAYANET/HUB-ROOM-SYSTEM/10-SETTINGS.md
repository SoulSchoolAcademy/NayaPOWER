# 10 — Settings

## Product job
Give the human control over their governed NayaNET environment.

## Sections
- Identity
- Privacy
- Authority
- Connections
- Notifications
- Data
- Security
- Appearance
- Your Intelligence

## Presentation
Settings should feel like a control environment, not an admin dump. High-impact controls are explicit about scope and consequences.

## Rules
Capability does not create authority. Destructive or privacy-affecting actions require clear confirmation and proof of resulting state.

## Proof
Changed settings produce observable state; reload preserves them where persistence exists; protected controls remain protected.
