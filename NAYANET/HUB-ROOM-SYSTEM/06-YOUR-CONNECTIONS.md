# 06 — Your Connections

## Product job
Turn relationships into useful intelligence rather than a passive address book.

## Presentation
A relationship graph/list with People, Agents, Systems, Organizations, and Projects. A selected connection opens a rich context view.

## Connection detail
- Identity
- Relationship/context
- Shared intelligence
- Recent interaction
- Common projects
- Last meaningful activity
- Relevant next action
- Privacy/visibility state

## Rules
Do not infer a relationship merely from a technical event. Show evidence/context for meaningful claims.

## Proof
Selecting a connection changes actual context; source and privacy state are visible; empty states are honest.
