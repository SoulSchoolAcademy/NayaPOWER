# 02 — Your Reports

## Product job
Turn accumulated intelligence into useful time-based understanding: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, and Yearly reports.

## Presentation
A DAY / WEEK / MONTH / YEAR selector, followed by evidence-backed synthesis.

## Required content
- Executive synthesis
- Discoveries
- Learning and changed understanding
- Decisions
- Creation/output
- Relationships/connections
- Activity and meaningful events
- Open questions / unresolved items
- Provenance and report period

Longer periods emphasize patterns and trajectory rather than repeating daily entries.

## Actions
Open source intelligence, inspect evidence, compare periods, capture a follow-up Smart Note.

## Rules
Reports summarize canonical intelligence; they do not become a second source of truth. Missing data is explicit. No invented trends, counts, or conclusions.

## Proof
Each period selector changes the actual report dataset; source period and retrieval/generation state are visible; reload remains coherent; existing rooms remain intact.
