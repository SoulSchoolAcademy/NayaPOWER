# 05 — Smart Ledger

## Product job
Provide durable accountability: what happened, under whose authority, what was executed, what was verified, and what remains unknown.

## Presentation
Truth-oriented event timeline/table with filters for time, type, actor, authority, outcome, and verification state. Important records may use large readable event cards.

## Record distinction
Clearly separate RECEIVED, AUTHORIZED, EXECUTED, VERIFIED, and PROJECTED. A transport response is not business proof.

## Actions
Inspect receipt, inspect evidence, filter, open related intelligence, trace correlation/idempotency identifiers where authorized.

## Rules
Unknown is not success. Blocked is not pass. Never synthesize receipts.

## Proof
Records survive reload and trace to canonical persistence/evidence. Unauthorized users cannot see protected records.
