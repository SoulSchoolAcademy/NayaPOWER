# 03 — Intelligent Library

## Product job
Make accumulated intelligence discoverable, understandable, and useful.

## Presentation
Search first, then featured intelligence and the canonical 58-domain universe. Preserve the established 58-domain model and its intended visual hierarchy.

## Domain room
A selected domain exposes:
- What I know
- What I learned
- What is uncertain
- Related intelligence
- Recent discoveries
- Smart Notes
- Questions worth answering
- Provenance/evidence

## Actions
Search, open domain, open intelligence item, capture note, ask Naya what is missing.

## Rules
Library content comes from canonical intelligence sources. Search results must not invent matches. Private intelligence stays private.

## Proof
Search materially changes results; domain selection changes the workspace; known/unknown states are honest; the 58-domain structure remains intact; reload/navigation do not erase completed rooms.
