# 09 — Smart Spaces

## Product job
Create contextual environments where intelligence, people, projects, notes, lists, activity, goals, rules, and connections come together.

## Presentation
A space selector followed by a rich workspace. Example contexts may include NayaNET, Human Maximus, projects, business, learning, or other explicitly created spaces.

## Rules
A space is a context boundary, not a new brain. Intelligence remains canonical and governed.

## Proof
Selecting a space changes actual context; membership and privacy are explicit; no fake spaces or fake activity are created.
