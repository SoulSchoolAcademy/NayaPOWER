# Smart Share

**Purpose:** controlled movement from private intelligence to shared/collective/public scope.

**Privacy states:** Private → Shared by choice → Collective by consent → Public by decision.

**UI:** selected object, audience/scope, identity visibility, included context, provenance preview, confirmation, current publication state.

**Actions:** preview, share, revoke where authorized, inspect who can see it.

**Backend mapping:** identity + privacy + publication contract. A browser share/clipboard operation is only a presentation fallback; it does not equal publication.
