# Smart Share

> **Participation is established at authorized Hub connection. Smart Share is for changing destination/scope and making selected intelligence public—not for approving every ordinary learning event.**

**Purpose:** controlled movement from private intelligence to shared/collective/public scope.

**Privacy/participation states:** Member-private → Collective contribution after participation consent → Public by decision.

**UI:** selected object, audience/scope, identity visibility, included context, provenance preview, confirmation, current publication state.

**Actions:** preview, share, revoke where authorized, inspect who can see it.

**Backend mapping:** participation consent + identity privacy + audience/scope + publication contract. A browser share/clipboard operation is only a presentation fallback; it does not equal publication.
