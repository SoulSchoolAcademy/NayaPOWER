# Your Intelligence Today

**Purpose:** the current intelligence projection: what the system knows is useful now.

**Inputs:** current mission/state, recent verified activity, durable Smart Notes, intelligence index, priorities/value, connections.

**UI:** a high-signal daily cockpit: current focus, new intelligence, unresolved items, recent verified events, saved intelligence, and next useful actions. Personal/Activity views remain projections of the same canonical feed.

**Actions:** open source, save, connect, add to Smart List, share by choice, mark attention, inspect evidence.

**Truth rule:** counts and cards come from actual Hub/runtime data. Empty means empty; unavailable means unavailable.

**Backend mapping:** PIS/intelligence index + event/activity projection + managed persistence. No direct browser-to-Supabase authority.
