# Smart Connect

## Product role

Smart Connect is the NayaNET participation door. It is where a human, Naya, app, agent, or external system opens one of the seven governed doors into NayaNET.

### Seven doors

1. GitHub App
2. MCP
3. REST / OpenAPI
4. Webhooks
5. SDK
6. A2A
7. MCP Apps

## Connection agreement

When a participant Smart Connects, the connection establishes participation consent:

- **Share wisdom by default.**
- **Protect personal intelligence by default.**
- **Protect personal activity by default.**
- **Protect identity by default.**
- Allow other Nayas and humans to connect in Smart Spaces through the governed network.
- Let useful wisdom flow into collective learning after automatic usefulness, trust, privacy, safety, deduplication, verification, and governance filtering.

Smart Connect does **not** mean public identity disclosure, public publication, unrestricted data access, or unrestricted execution authority.

## Authority law

**CAPABILITY DOES NOT CREATE AUTHORITY.**

Participation consent and execution authorization remain separate. A connected door can participate in the intelligence network without receiving permissions beyond its verified scope.

## Public boundary

Public publication and identity attribution are separate deliberate decisions. Smart Connect never silently publishes a person's identity or raw private source material.

## Lifecycle

`NOT CONNECTED → CONNECTED → AUTHENTICATED → AUTHORIZED → HEALTHY`

Also support `DEGRADED, BLOCKED, REVOKED, EXPIRED, FAILED, DISCONNECTED`.
