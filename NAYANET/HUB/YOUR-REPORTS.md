# Your Reports

**Purpose:** synthesize intelligence across time rather than merely list events.

**Views:** Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly.

**Each report contains:** period, executive intelligence summary, changes, recurring patterns, important decisions, verified evidence, unresolved items, value/action implications, source links, and provenance.

**Actions:** change period, open source, drill into evidence, compare periods, save report, share by choice.

**Backend mapping:** canonical events + Smart Notes + intelligence index + mission state. Reports are derived projections; source objects remain canonical.
