# NayaNET Hub — Surface Product Contracts

## Product law

The Hub is the human-facing projection of NayaPOWER. **Smart Feed is the main Hub experience.** Sidebar destinations replace the primary workspace; they never render beneath Smart Feed.

The ten destinations are:
1. Your Intelligence Today
2. Your Reports
3. Intelligent Library
4. Smart Share
5. Smart Ledger
6. Your Connections
7. Smart Lists
8. Smart Mail
9. Smart Spaces
10. Settings

## 1 — Your Intelligence Today

**Question:** “Given everything my intelligence system knows, what matters to me right now?”

Role: current intelligence snapshot / daily intelligence-diary projection.

Inputs: Smart Notes, mission/state, recent canonical events, verified events, reports, connections, priorities/value, relevant learning.

Experience: a substantive current briefing, not telemetry. Show intelligence worth seeing, what changed, what was learned, what deserves attention, relevant connections, useful next actions, and recent intelligence objects.

Does not own canonical events, Smart Note storage, authority, or historical report truth.

## 2 — Your Reports

**Question:** “What has my intelligence system learned across time?”

Role: higher-order synthesis over Smart Notes, events, state and time.

Primary navigation: Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Yearly.

Daily asks what happened and mattered today; weekly identifies patterns; monthly shows broader change; yearly shows compounding.

Experience: growing report archive plus substantial report-reading view. Reports link back to source intelligence/evidence.

Does not own raw event truth or create a competing intelligence store.

## 3 — Intelligent Library

**Question:** “What do I know, where is it, and what connects to it?”

Role: human doorway into durable NayaPOWER intelligence.

Initial UX: the exact 58 NayaPOWER domains as the primary navigation. Target layout: 3 prominent/top domains where appropriate, then 11 rows of 5 and a final row of 3, for 58 total. Each is a large feature tile with number, canonical name, short purpose, and real intelligence status when available.

Clicking a domain opens its relevant Smart Note/intelligence in the primary workspace.

Search covers Smart Notes by domain, title, semantic content, relationship, provenance, freshness and canonicality as capabilities become available.

The 58 domains are not 58 applications. They are organized intelligence entry points.

## 4 — Smart Share

**Question:** “What do I want to contribute beyond myself, and who should receive it?”

Role: bridge from individual intelligence to collective intelligence.

Core model: **Private → Shared by choice → Collective by consent → Public by decision.**

Experience explains the value/governance of sharing and enables authorized sharing. Include explanatory intelligence/Smart Notes about collective intelligence and privacy.

Actions: inspect share permissions, select intelligence, choose authorized scope, share where capability + authority exist, inspect resulting evidence/event.

Does not own publication authority, identity authority, privacy policy or collective truth.

## 5 — Smart Ledger

**Question:** “What actually happened, under whose authority, with what evidence?”

Role: durable accountability.

A consequential record should expose where available: what happened, actor, time, intent, authority, evidence, verification, result/change, value and next action.

Experience is an accountability record system, not an activity feed or chat transcript.

Actions: inspect receipt/evidence, follow related activity/Smart Note, search/filter.

Does not manufacture success, verification or event truth.

## 6 — Your Connections

**Question:** “What relates to what?”

Role: relationship/knowledge-graph projection.

Relationships include person↔project, Smart Note↔event, event↔decision, decision↔authority, project↔agent, agent↔capability, intelligence↔intelligence and human↔Space.

Experience is relationship-first, not a basic contact list. Users traverse connected intelligence, projects, events, Spaces and permissions where authorized.

## 7 — Smart Lists

**Question:** “What should I pay attention to or act on, given mission, value, dependencies, authority and state?”

Role: Intelligence → organized action.

Potential projections include Worth Doing Now, Waiting/Blocked, Learning Opportunities, Follow-up, and Completed/Verified.

Items should expose why they appear when that reasoning exists.

Actions may open source intelligence, inspect dependency/authority, take authorized action, and create/edit lists only where runtime capability exists.

## 8 — Smart Mail

**Question:** “What matters in my communication, and what does it connect to?”

Role: communication treated as intelligence.

Present communication with context where available: who, what, why, project, decisions, commitments, actions, follow-up and related intelligence.

Messages can become connected intelligence rather than disposable correspondence.

## 9 — Smart Spaces

**Question:** “What is the context in which this intelligence, people and work belong together?”

Role: contextual environment, not folder.

A Space can contain humans, Nayas, Smart Notes, projects, activity, conversations, permissions, intelligence and relationships.

Experience should feel like entering a context: identity, members, intelligence, projects, activity, conversations, permissions and connected Spaces.

## 10 — Settings

**Question:** “How is my NayaNET experience configured, and what am I actually authorized to control?”

Role: human control surface.

Regions: identity, privacy, sharing, connections, notifications/preferences, intelligence preferences, runtime/system status, verification/status.

Constitutional rule: **Capability ≠ Authority.** A UI control must never imply an authority the runtime does not grant.

## Smart Feed — separate main destination

Smart Feed is the living intelligence/activity stream and the default/main Hub workspace.

Architecture:
**REAL EXECUTION → CANONICAL EVENT → ACTIVITY PROJECTION → SMART FEED**

Feed is a projection, not the source of truth.

When any sidebar destination is selected, the Feed/hero/search main content is replaced by that destination's primary workspace. It is never rendered underneath the destination.

## Runtime ownership

Hub surfaces are projections.

- Smart Note → canonical intelligence substrate
- Event → canonical event contract/boundary
- Evidence → evidence/verification system
- Authority → authority registry/governance
- State → mission/state system
- Retrieval → intelligent retrieval
- Ledger → accountability projection
- Hub → human presentation/action surface

**Never manufacture intelligence to fill a card.** If a capability/data source is unavailable, show the truthful state.

## Completion standard

A surface is not complete because its button exists, heading appears, browser can click it, or a placeholder card renders.

It is complete when:
1. purpose is obvious;
2. UI belongs to canonical Hub;
3. hierarchy is useful;
4. data comes from a real authorized capability;
5. actions perform real authorized work;
6. relationships are navigable;
7. empty/loading/error states are truthful;
8. browser behavior is verified;
9. runtime/source relationship is proven;
10. Oscar can challenge what remains.

The engineering objective is not ten isolated pages. It is one coherent human interface to the same underlying NayaPOWER intelligence system.
