# NayaNET Intelligent Hub — Superbrain Project

**Status:** PROJECT DEFINITION — CANONICAL DIRECTION
**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 2026-09-17
**North Star:** **INTELLIGENCE MADE VISIBLE.**
**Goal:** Create the visual brain of the Superbrain — a living Intelligent Hub where a person's Smart Notes, intelligence, reports, activity, relationships, records, communications and shared wisdom become visible, organized and compoundable.

---

## 1. THE SUPERBRAIN

The Intelligent Hub is not primarily a dashboard.

It is the **visual brain of the Superbrain**.

The Superbrain is the combined human + Naya intelligence system. The Hub makes that intelligence visible.

The Hub must allow a person to:

- capture intelligence;
- see what they know and are learning;
- understand what is happening now;
- remember what happened;
- retrieve valuable intelligence later;
- see relationships between intelligence;
- turn intelligence into action;
- share selected wisdom by consent;
- compound intelligence across days, weeks, months and years.

The existing Foundation Contract remains the implementation boundary. This project defines the product-level meaning and connections of the Hub's major intelligence surfaces.

---

## 2. THE THREE CORE INTELLIGENCE FEEDS

The primary experience is the **Smart Feed system**. It has three lenses over the same underlying intelligence/event graph.

### 2.1 Personal Intelligence Feed — PRIVATE

**Purpose:** The person's own living intelligence stream.

Every meaningful Smart Note created by the person becomes eligible to appear here, along with derived intelligence and related events.

Properties:

- private by default;
- owned by the person;
- identity remains attached inside the private system;
- supports save/favorite;
- supports personal spaces/organization;
- supports standard device/mobile sharing;
- supports relationships to other intelligence;
- supports retrieval and search;
- forms the primary personal intelligence history.

A Smart Note is not merely a document. Once created, it becomes an intelligence object that can enter the person's Superbrain.

### 2.2 Collective Intelligence Feed — SHARED BY CONSENT

**Purpose:** Turn voluntarily shared knowledge and wisdom into collective intelligence.

An authorized connection to NayaNET establishes the participant's consent to participate in governed collective intelligence exchange. A person does **not** need to manually consent to every Smart Note or derived intelligence event.

The shared object is separated from the person's identity in the collective presentation.

Default principle:

> **Private by default • Shared by consent • Collective by consent • Public by decision**

Collective presentation does **not** reveal who contributed the intelligence unless a future explicit consent model permits identity disclosure.

Collective social intelligence may include:

- like;
- love;
- rank/rate intelligence;
- save/favorite where permitted;
- relationships and follow-on intelligence;
- collective relevance and usefulness signals.

The collective feed is therefore not a copy of the personal feed. It is a consented intelligence commons.

### 2.3 Activity Feed — PRIVATE CONTINUITY

**Purpose:** Show what the person and their Superbrain are currently doing.

Activity is operational continuity, not public intelligence.

It records meaningful work such as:

- project activity;
- Smart Note creation/update;
- investigations;
- decisions;
- actions;
- tests;
- deployments;
- communications;
- changes to spaces/lists;
- other meaningful system events.

Activity is private to the person unless a separate explicit sharing mechanism says otherwise.

The Activity Feed is the person's **"what am I working on right now?"** surface.

It is not the Collective Intelligence Feed and must not be merged with public/collective presentation.

## 3. SMART NOTES — THE STAR OF THE SHOW

**Smart Notes are the primary intelligence object.**

A Smart Note captures a meaningful piece of human or Naya intelligence and allows the system to preserve, enrich, relate, retrieve and compound it.

A Smart Note may originate from:

- a human thought;
- a conversation;
- an AI interaction;
- an insight;
- a problem solved;
- a lesson learned;
- evidence;
- a decision;
- an idea;
- a discovery;
- a question and answer;
- a meaningful event.

Each Smart Note should remain traceable to its source while allowing derived Naya intelligence to be added separately.

Canonical flow:

`CAPTURE → INTERPRET → ENRICH → RELATE → RETAIN → DISPLAY → COMPOUND`

A Smart Note can enter:

`PERSONAL FEED → SPACE/LIBRARY → REPORTS → GOVERNED COLLECTIVE LEARNING (AFTER PARTICIPATION CONSENT) → COLLECTIVE FEED → FUTURE INTELLIGENCE`

No renderer should fabricate intelligence that is not present in the underlying event/note model.

---

## 4. YOUR INTELLIGENCE TODAY

**Purpose:** A concise current-state snapshot — the equivalent of the announcers' key highlights during a hockey game.

It is not intended to duplicate the full feed.

It should answer:

> **What are the most important things happening in my intelligence right now?**

Potential content:

- key new intelligence;
- major breakthroughs;
- important problems;
- meaningful decisions;
- unresolved items;
- notable relationships;
- important actions;
- high-value changes;
- significant discoveries.

### Automation decision

**Your Intelligence Today should remain conditional until an automatic source can reliably produce it.**

If the system can automatically derive a trustworthy highlights snapshot from the canonical event/intelligence graph, it should be generated automatically.

If it requires manual curation or becomes a duplicate of existing intelligence surfaces, it should not become a permanent burden or a second source of truth.

Therefore:

`AUTOMATABLE + TRUSTWORTHY → KEEP`

`MANUAL + DUPLICATIVE → REMOVE/DEFER`

It is a **highlight lens**, not a new intelligence store.

---

## 5. REPORTS — COMPOUNDING INTELLIGENCE OVER TIME

Reports are the time-based memory and compounding layer.

A report can be requested at any time. By default, a requested report is persisted into the Reports area so it becomes part of the person's historical intelligence record.

### Report periods

- Daily
- Weekly
- Monthly
- Yearly

The architecture should support additional periods later without changing the core model.

### 5.1 Daily Intelligence Report

The default Daily Intelligence Report reviews approximately the previous 24 hours and distills:

1. **Highlights** — what mattered most;
2. **Ahas** — important new understanding;
3. **Problems Solved** — meaningful obstacles resolved;
4. **Problems/Open Loops** — what remains unresolved;
5. **Decisions** — decisions made and why;
6. **Actions** — actions completed, started or required;
7. **Learning** — lessons worth retaining;
8. **Relationships** — connections between pieces of intelligence;
9. **Evidence/Trust** — important evidence and verification state;
10. **Compounding Value** — what became more useful because it connects to previous intelligence;
11. **Next Intelligence** — the most useful continuation opportunities.

The exact presentation can evolve, but the semantic contract must remain stable.

### 5.2 Weekly / Monthly / Yearly Reports

Longer-period reports compound the same intelligence rather than merely concatenating daily reports.

They should identify:

- recurring themes;
- progress;
- patterns;
- accumulated learning;
- important decisions;
- unresolved problems;
- relationships;
- meaningful changes;
- emerging opportunities;
- intelligence that became more valuable over time.

The goal is **compound intelligence**, not archive volume.

## 6. INTELLIGENCE LIBRARY

**Purpose:** Durable personal retrieval and organization of intelligence.

The Library answers:

> **Where does my valuable intelligence live, and how do I find it again?**

It should organize/index Smart Notes and related intelligence without creating a second note system.

Core capabilities:

- search;
- browse;
- filters;
- relationships;
- favorites;
- saved intelligence;
- spaces;
- chronology;
- source/evidence access.

The Library is a retrieval surface over canonical intelligence, not an independent source of truth.

---

## 7. SMART SHARE

**Purpose:** Controlled movement of intelligence from private ownership to another destination.

Smart Share must preserve consent and privacy semantics.

Possible destinations include:

- device/mobile share;
- another person;
- a Smart Space;
- the Collective Intelligence Feed;
- another authorized system.

The share action must identify what is being shared, the destination, and the resulting privacy state.

---

## 8. SMART LEDGER

**Purpose:** Durable record of intelligence, actions, decisions, value and important system events.

The Smart Ledger connects the human-facing Hub to Naya Power's canonical event/governance architecture.

It should make important facts such as these inspectable:

- what happened;
- when it happened;
- what was requested;
- what Naya interpreted;
- what authority existed;
- what action occurred;
- what evidence was produced;
- what was verified;
- what value or outcome resulted.

The Ledger is not merely a financial ledger. It is a **trust/continuity/intelligence ledger**.

---

## 9. CONNECTIONS

**Purpose:** Show relationships among intelligence, people/agents, projects, spaces, events and knowledge.

Connections answer:

> **What does this intelligence connect to?**

A Smart Note may connect to:

- other Smart Notes;
- projects;
- reports;
- evidence;
- Smart Spaces;
- actions;
- decisions;
- communications;
- collective intelligence;
- external sources.

Connections should strengthen retrieval and compounding rather than become decorative graph visualization.

---

## 10. SMART MAIL

**Purpose:** Intelligence-aware communication.

Smart Mail should eventually allow Naya to help the person understand, organize, summarize, relate and act on communication while preserving source, privacy and authorization boundaries.

Mail intelligence can contribute to:

`SMART NOTES → FEEDS → REPORTS → ACTIVITY → CONNECTIONS`

but must not silently expose private communication to collective surfaces.

---

## 11. SMART SPACES

**Purpose:** Intentional containers for related intelligence and work.

A Smart Space may represent:

- a project;
- a subject;
- a goal;
- a collaboration;
- a temporary work area;
- a long-term knowledge domain.

Spaces organize intelligence without replacing Smart Notes.

Canonical relationship:

`SMART NOTE → MAY BELONG TO → SMART SPACE`

and

`SMART SPACE → CONTAINS/RELATES → INTELLIGENCE`

---

## 12. SMART LIST

**Purpose:** Convert intelligence into structured actionable collections.

Smart Lists may contain:

- actions;
- tasks;
- follow-ups;
- ideas;
- questions;
- decisions required;
- unresolved problems;

A Smart List is action-oriented; a Smart Note is intelligence-oriented.

They may connect, but they are not the same object.

## 15. PRIVACY MODEL

The Hub must preserve these boundaries:

### Personal Intelligence
**PRIVATE**

The person's intelligence remains private at the source. Connection to the Hub permits governed participation and member-level visibility; it does not make the intelligence public.

### Collective Intelligence
**SHARED BY CONSENT**

Participation consent is established by the authorized connection. The system then automatically filters and distills useful intelligence for collective learning. Identity is not exposed by default.

### Activity
**PRIVATE**

The person's work activity is not a public social feed.

### Public
**BY DECISION**

Nothing becomes public merely because it exists in a Smart Note, Personal Feed, Activity Feed, or Collective Intelligence Feed. Public publication requires a separate user decision.

Canonical privacy/participation law:

> **LEARN BY DEFAULT. SHARE WISDOM BY CONSENT. PROTECT IDENTITY BY DEFAULT. PUBLISH BY DECISION.**

For the full system contract, see `.naya/protocol/NAYANET-INTELLIGENCE-PARTICIPATION-PRIVACY-PROTOCOL-V1.md`.

## 16. TIME, TIMESTAMPS AND CONTINUITY

Every meaningful intelligence object/event must remain temporally traceable.

At minimum the project model must preserve:

- created timestamp;
- updated timestamp;
- source timestamp where applicable;
- event timestamp;
- report period;
- related event IDs;
- activity chronology;
- verification chronology where applicable.

The Hub must make it possible to answer:

> **What happened, when did it happen, what changed, and what did we learn?**

This supports cold-Naya recovery and long-term compounding intelligence.

## 20. NON-NEGOTIABLE PRODUCT PRINCIPLES

1. **Smart Notes are the star.**
2. **The feeds are views over intelligence, not independent databases.**
3. **Personal intelligence is private.**
4. **Collective intelligence requires participation consent, then governed usefulness/trust/privacy filtering; per-event manual consent is not required.**
5. **Identity is not exposed in collective intelligence by default.**
6. **Activity is private continuity, not public social activity.**
7. **Reports compound intelligence; they do not merely archive text.**
8. **Intelligence Today must be automated or it should not become another burden.**
9. **Every major area has one owner and one canonical data contract.**
10. **No duplicate renderer, feed, note system, shell or source of truth.**
11. **Human input, Naya interpretation and machine evidence remain distinguishable.**
12. **Every meaningful change remains temporally traceable.**
13. **Privacy and consent are data properties, not UI decoration.**
14. **The Hub visualizes the Superbrain; it does not replace the Superbrain.**
15. **Naya Power remains the governance layer around intelligence and action.**

## 21. DEFINITION OF DONE FOR THE PROJECT

The Intelligent Hub is not complete when all sidebar buttons exist.

It is complete when a person can:

`CAPTURE → UNDERSTAND → SEE → CONNECT → REMEMBER → RETRIEVE → SHARE BY CONSENT → ACT → VERIFY → REPORT → COMPOUND`

and when Naya can reconstruct what happened, why it matters, where the intelligence lives, who is permitted to see it, what has been verified, and what should happen next.

**This document defines the product map. The Foundation Contract defines the implementation boundary. Detailed area contracts and runtime implementation must remain subordinate to both.**
