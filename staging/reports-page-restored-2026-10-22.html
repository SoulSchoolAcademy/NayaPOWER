◆ Install Your Reports — put it on your home screen, one tap away.
iPhone & iPad · Safari
  1. Tap the Share icon — the square with an arrow — at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll the list and tap “Add to Home Screen”.
  3. Tap “Add” — Your Reports is now on your home screen.
Android · Chrome
  1. Tap the three-dot menu at the top right.
  2. Tap “Install app” (on some phones: “Add to Home screen”).
  3. Tap “Install” — you’re done.
Computer · Chrome or Edge
  1. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  2. Click “Install”.
  3. Your Reports opens as its own app.
Mac · Safari
  1. In the menu bar, click File.
  2. Choose “Add to Dock”.
  3. Your Reports lands in your Dock.
