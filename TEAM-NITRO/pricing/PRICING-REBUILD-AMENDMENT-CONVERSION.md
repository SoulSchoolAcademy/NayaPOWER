## 1. THE WHY — the science of a high converting page (his words, the law of this page)

- **Design quality IS trust.** The higher the quality of the design, the more they trust it. The more it feels real and active and high level, the more confidence they have in it. The more it feels push-button simple and easy to understand and logical, the more they appreciate it.
- **This is NOT a sales page.** It is: welcome to NayaNET. This is what it is. This is why it's awesome. This is what it does. Then the proof. Then the try.
- **The test for every section:** "is this the max level of experience I can give the user?" (the presentation rule, SN-2264). If it doesn't feel irresistible, it isn't done.
- The copy is written like a pro-level copywriter: deep, personal, real, totally valuable, almost literally irresistible. Like a viral marketing campaign that was irresistible — everybody's talking about it. Make that page.

A real object, elevated, defined, beautiful, like the icons on the pages he's approved. **No invented icon.**
- **Click the jewel → a detail view opens IN YOUR VIEWPOINT** (an overlay/panel, in the viewport — not a new page, not a scroll away). It tells you all about that feature, in this exact structure (the 7 parts, in order):
  1. **In a nutshell** — what it is, literally.
  2. **What's in it for you.**
  3. **How to use it.**

  6. **The pain point it solves** (in the world).
