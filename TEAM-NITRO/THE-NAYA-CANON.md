# PART VI: THE CHECKLIST

## Before Every Build

### 1. DECLARE
State who you are and what you're about to do. The declaration decides the work.

### 2. NAME THE VIEWPOINT
- What is this screen's ONE purpose?
- What is the person trying to do here?
- What is the ONE hero object?

### 3. PICK YOUR PARTS (compose, never create)
From the blessed parts registry. If it doesn't exist as a part, harvest it or earn it — never invent it.

### 4. REALITY CHECK (every element)
- Could I pick this up?
- Where's the light from? (Top-left.)
- What's it made of? (Name the material.)
- Does it cast a shadow?
- What happens when I touch it?

### 5. VIEWPOINT CHECK
- Is this the ONLY thing on screen?
- Does the hero own the viewport?
- Can nav be summoned in one tap?
- When nav shows, does everything fit?
- Nothing overlaps?

### 6. FAMILY CHECK
- Same color language? (Spectrum order, no doubles.)
- Brothers and sisters? (Harmonized.)
- Nothing flat among the real?

## Before Every Handoff

### 7. SELF-SCORECARD
Why is it not a 10? What 10 things make it a 10? Apply them. Re-score. Three times. Don't stop below 9.

### 8. RENDERED REVIEW
Your own eyes on the real render. Phone first. From the signed-in seat. Not a byte check — a visual verdict.

### 9. RECEIPTS
Live link. Proof it landed. What was verified, how, and where.

### 10. LESSONS NOTED
What did you learn? File it. Share it. Same pass.
