  The status poll clears itself on any terminal status; if the builder stays silent
  for 20 s, an honest "NO ANSWER YET" note appears (contract is drafted in Step 5).

  manual fallback is the page's own pre-existing one (copy the contract to chat),
  shown only if the builder does not answer.
