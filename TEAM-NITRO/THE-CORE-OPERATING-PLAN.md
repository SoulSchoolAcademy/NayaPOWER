# THE CORE OPERATING PLAN — The Solution, Adopted
**Written:** 2026-11-07 ~19:24Z, on Shawn's order: the audit named the problems; this is the solution, and it is adopted — not filed and forgotten, but run.

## The reframe — not 26 pages
It is not 26 pages. It is not really four pages either. It is four major components that make up the system to get the engine running — **the Hub, the Personal page, the Dashboard, and the Pricing page** — plus **the Walkthrough**, the onboarding flow that brings people in. The Personal page, full-featured, essentially IS the Business, the Spaces, and the Membership — one page carrying those worlds. The ambassador and affiliate tracking lives inside the Dashboard. Put those together and the car starts: it has a body and it has an engine, and we can get her started. After that, fine-tuning — and the other features. The app is essentially complete when the core is complete and synced, because the Personal page and the Hub carry the features with them: finish the areas and you finish the features connected to them — mail, lists, notes, links, memberships, ledger — all in sync. That is the goal. That is the plan.

## The system — how the work runs now
1. **Project Intelligence maps every part.** PI holds the map: every component, every feature connected to it, every piece that has to be done. No brief goes out without its parts named from PI. The map is the work list; the work list is the map.
2. **Every brief says what and how.** This is what needs to be done. This is how to do it. Do it. Then the next one. Look at the page, see what needs doing, say how to do it, do it, verify it, move to the next. Over and over again, without stopping.
3. **Parallel lanes, one per component.** One lane-lead per component, each with a builder and a verifier, running at the same time — Hub lane, Personal lane, Dashboard lane, Pricing lane, Walkthrough lane. I coordinate; the lanes do not wait on each other; each finishes its own component. The lanes are aligned: same brand, same bullseye, same three steps, same definition of done.
4. **Every fix starts with "should this exist at all?"** Before any button is changed, the question is asked: should this element be here at all — and is there a better way to do it? A terrible button does not get a prettier version of itself. It gets questioned first. Then it is fixed — or removed. This is the wise-one question, applied to every element, every time.
5. **Three-step verification per component.** Self-scorecard (the ten questions, written out) → a different seat (never the creator) → Shawn's eye. A component is done only when it is complete by his eye: every button works, every page is amazing, everything is in the right place, no obvious errors, no silly text, everything logical, sensible, beautiful, aligned with Naya's brand.
6. **All the tools, used.** Agents for the building. Smart Mail — as a list, one click — for the team. Project Intelligence for the map. Automated systems for the plumbing: the sync, the proof host, the inspector, the watchdog (deduped, one alert per fault). The machine runs the machine; the humans judge the result. Nothing is wasted: no time, no energy, no money spent on work that doesn't move the core forward.

## The core, component by component
- **Hub — the front door.** The feed, the notes, the way into everything. Done when the feed renders, the notes open inside the Hub, and every tab goes somewhere real.
- **Personal — the full-featured world.** Business, Spaces, and Membership in one page, with every feature connected to it working and synced: mail, lists, notes, links, memberships, notifications. Done when the world is whole.
- **Dashboard — the machine's face.** The speedometer, the ledger, the ambassador tracking, every number alive. Done when every number is real, every gauge moves, and every click lands.
- **Pricing — the demonstration.** The chart, the Top 10, the calculator, the close. Done when it is a masterpiece by his standard.
- **Walkthrough — the onboarding flow.** Ten questions, and a world appears. Done when a stranger can go from landing to their world without thinking.

## What "done" means now
The core is done when the car starts: all four components complete by his eye, all their features synced, the Walkthrough bringing people in. After that, fine-tuning and the other features — not before. Nothing new starts while the core is unfinished. Work-in-progress is one component per lane, and one core.

## The measurement
Three numbers, every week: rounds per page (target: one), defects found by the maker vs the seat vs Shawn (target: nearly all by the maker), time from start to his yes (target: falling). If they do not move, the plan is not working — and we say so, plainly, instead of announcing progress.
