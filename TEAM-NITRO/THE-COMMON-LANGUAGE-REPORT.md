### 2.1 BUTTONS — the standard is three pills

**The language:** An obsidian pill, bold white label you can read at a glance, ONE deep color per button as rim + glow. Rest: obsidian and white. Hover/tap: the whole pill lights up in its own color, lifts, depresses on press (scale 0.97, 0.1s). **LOCKED (Laws §5):** the Dashboard PTS / % / MONEY pills are the standard — harvested verbatim, never redesigned, never touched. **Evidence:** `the_best.png` shows it exactly; `aaa_7.png` shows the same three pills on the live Dashboard; `aaa_19.jpg` shows the same anatomy in "Enter NayaNET ◆" (green) and "Read the full agreement ◆" (purple). Sampled rim hues in `the_best.png`: purple / blue / green families, rendering darker than the locked hexes (still-image glow falloff).

**Measured values:** pill radius 999px **LOCKED** (rule book); rim 1.5–2px **LOCKED**; label bold white caps **PERCEIVED**; exact px height/width **UNMEASURED**.

**DO:** build every button to the PTS / % / MONEY anatomy; one deep color per button; bold white label; light up whole on hover; each button its own color.
**DO NOT:** ship a flat button (no depth, no rim, no glow); put two buttons of the same color next to each other; put gray text on a button; redesign the three standard pills.
