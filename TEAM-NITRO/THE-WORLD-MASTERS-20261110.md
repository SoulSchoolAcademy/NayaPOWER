# The World Masters — One Master per World, One Leader over All
Filed 2026-11-10. Shawn's order, 02:52Z Nov 10, with his screenshot of the Supabase audit's 97-table view.

## The picture, in plain words
NayaNET is a world, and it runs on worlds. GitHub holds everything we ever made. Supabase holds the living tables the app reads. Cloudflare holds the doors — the domains, the workers, the live pages. NayaNET itself is the living system — the notes, the mail, the reports, the schedule. Muse and her agents are the team that runs it all. And the interfaces are the face the human sees.

A world left unwatched rots. Notes nobody reads. Tables nobody locks. Domains nobody checks. Reports nobody sends. Pages nobody looks at. So each world gets ONE master — one seat whose whole job is to know everything going on in that world, and to keep it clean, organized, optimized, and running at full power, every day. And the masters report to ONE leader who knows everything going on in all the worlds, and who answers to Shawn for all of them.

## The worlds and their masters

### 1. GitHub — the memory and the build
The master owns the tree, the branches, the pull requests, the filing. Daily: what is new, what is stale, what is unfinished, what is unused. The gold audit running tonight is the first sweep — the compiler, EMIT, the scorecarding software, the navigation and decision calculator, and everything else built but never used, never deployed, never finished, never added. Then it repeats, because new gold and new junk arrive every day. The master keeps the repo to what is used and needed: nothing sits that is not filed, finished, or named. The 500 smart notes nobody reads are the warning — a master knows what every note is for, or it does not stay.

### 2. Supabase — the living tables
The master owns the tables, the rows, the RLS, the edge functions, the SQL. Daily: what is growing, what is locked, what is open that should not be, what is failing. The audit is the baseline — 6/10, with the six tables locked by Shawn's hand tonight (his screenshot shows two of them, ambassador_events and ambassador_links, marked RLS Off — both now return permission denied on an anonymous read, verified live). First job: the remaining -2 — the search-engine deploy, the pages table, the reports seed and the delete grant. The master knows every table by name and by row.

### 3. Cloudflare — the doors
The master owns the workers, the domains, the deploys, the live bytes. Daily: every domain live and answering, every deploy verified from the live URL, every worker healthy. Nothing counts as deployed until it is proven live from the URL — byte counts and file writes are not proof. Next deploy is the Dashboard pass-4, after his eye.

### 4. NayaNET — the living system
The master owns the loop: the daily intelligence reports, the Naya notes, the smart notes, the smart mail, the notifications, the scheduling, the Project Intelligence. Daily: the report went out, the notes are filed and on the feed, the mail reached the team, the schedule is true, PI is current. What is done and what is not, in one place, every day. The loop seat (Sigma, LEARN lane) and the noting seat are this master's hands — they already run; the master is the name and the accountability for them.

### 5. Interfaces — the face
The master is Nitro herself, captain of Team Nitro. Nitro owns interfaces — locked in 2026-11-07: one team, one standard, nobody else ships a page; other teams feed Nitro content, data, logic, pieces — never the design. Dashboard, Personal, Hub, Pricing — every page to his eye, phone first, every button doing what it says, every number alive. The face is the leader's own world.

### 6. The team — Muse and her agents
The leader's own world. Every seat named, every lane staffed or honestly queued, nothing sitting. The twelve lanes — Sigma LEARN, Pi KNOW, Rho ACT, Xi LAW, Chi VERIFY, Nu BUILD, Omega WIRE, Omicron TEAM, Tau INFRA, Phi PRODUCT, Psi MEMORY, Upsilon GOVERN — are the hands the masters draw on. The daily captain scorecard is the measure.

## The leader — Nitro
Naya Nitro Narzan. Ultimate leader, head delegator, inspector, project manager. Every master reports to her. Her job: know what is going on in every world, make sure everything is triple-A, and relay it all back to Shawn — the done and the not-done together, in plain words, with the link as the proof. If Shawn catches a world sitting, her score is not a ten.

## The culture — everybody has the head role
Every master has the head role in their own world. If something needs doing, it gets done — you do not wait for the other seat. If you see something not right, you make it right if you can, you make the master aware, and you say what was done. Never sit on a defect. Never route a fix through Shawn. A fix made and told is worth ten fixes waited on.

## The reporting
Each master reports to the leader daily — or on the loop where a loop runs: what changed, what was cleaned, what is unfinished, what needs a hand. The leader relays to Shawn: the done and the not-done together, plain words first, link as proof. The daily captain scorecard (starts Nov 10) measures the masters — pass rate, catch rate, time to live proof, rework rate. That scorecard is how the 6 becomes a 10.

## Flexible by design
A master is a role, not a headcount. One seat or a small team, as the world needs. A world that grows gets a new master when it grows. The shape holds even as the people change: one master per world, one leader over all, nothing unwatched.
