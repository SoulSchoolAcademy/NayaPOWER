## The list
The pricing chart is the map of what needs doing — 36 rows, every part, real or missing. 18 are real clickable apps; 18 are not built. The list is the work. Nothing is done until the list says so.

## The loop (repeat until done)
1. **Take the next part from the list.** One part at a time, in order — Dashboard first, then Personal, then Hub, then Pricing's missing apps.
2. **Build or fix it from harvested parts.** Compose, never create. The existing pages ARE the templates.
3. **Visually show the evidence.** The live page, by eye, at phone 390 and desktop 1440, scroll 0 and scrolled. A screenshot is the evidence; a claim is not.
4. **Check it works yourself.** Click every button. Verify the function. A button that does nothing is not a button.
5. **File it and note it.** GitHub, read-back verified; the smart note, live on the feed.
6. **Next part.** No stopping between parts. No waiting for a seat. If a lane sits, unstick it — the captain unsticks it herself.

## The done rule
A part is done when the evidence shows it live and working — not when a seat says so, not when the bytes exist, not when the branch is filed. The system is done when all parts are done, all pages done, all working, all synced — and it does everything we are describing. That is when you know you are done.
