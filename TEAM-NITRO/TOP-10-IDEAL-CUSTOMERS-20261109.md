# Our Top 10 Ideal Customers — So Everyone Sees Themselves in One

**Framing (Shawn's words, standing):** universal market, first customer first. We optimize for everybody in the foundation — and we optimize for #1 first in the launch sequence. One foundation, different starting experiences, not three products. This list is a building compass: if you understand who we're serving, that helps with building.

**The ranking (Tree, "NayaNET — The Top 10 Ideal Customers," relayed 2026-10-31, adopted with flags):** strategic ranking, not measured conversion data. Best payers: service owners + consultants. Best referrers: creators + community leaders. Best daily-life: families + hobbyists.

**What all humans want (his words, 2026-11-07):** to learn, to earn, to grow; a way to pay bills and rent and eat; an assistant to trust and believe in, truly solid, that helps and automates everything, push-button simple; to be acknowledged, loved, appreciated, valued, respected; to have valuable things and produce valuable things; a Naya that produces value and intelligence, creates beautiful things, and helps them succeed in life.

---

## 1. Solo service owners — the first customer

**Who they are:** one person, one skill, one business. The hairstylist. The cleaner. The landscaper. The photographer. The trainer. The baker. They are talented at the work — that was never the problem.

**What they're carrying:** everything around the work. The bookings. The messages. The no-shows. The "what time are you free next week?" asked for the hundredth time. The photos of their work scattered across their phone. The prices in their head. The customers they meant to follow up with and never did. They are overwhelmed by everything around the work, and the work itself is the only part they love.

**What Naya does for them:** she takes the repetitive work and runs it. A beautiful free Personal, Business, Space, and Dashboard — their whole world in one place. Bookings that book themselves. Their services, their prices, their photos, their contact — all in one beautiful place. One Smart Link they can share anywhere, and when someone taps it, the inquiry arrives organized, and the dashboard shows what happened and what to do next.

**The moment it clicks:** they see their own business running without them chasing it, and they say it out loud — *"where has Naya been all my life?"*

## 2. Coaches, consultants, and freelancers

**Who they are:** their knowledge is their product. They sell their time, their advice, their eye — one client at a time.

**What they're carrying:** getting found, getting booked, getting paid, and remembering who said what. The follow-up after the call. The notes from the last session. The invoice that went out late. The client who drifted away because nobody checked in. They know their craft; the business around it eats their hours.

**What Naya does for them:** she holds the whole practice. Sessions and bookings in one place. Notes that remember everything that matters — what was said, what was promised, what comes next. Smart Mail that reaches the right person at the right time. And when they're ready, a membership — their knowledge behind a pay door, earning while they sleep, 50% of the revenue theirs.

**The moment it clicks:** a client rebooks without a single message being chased, and the coach realizes the business is running while they were doing the work they love.

## 3. Creators, influencers, and affiliates

**Who they are:** they make things — videos, posts, lessons, recommendations — and people follow them for it. Their audience is their livelihood, and it lives on platforms they don't own.

**What they're carrying:** their work scattered across six platforms, their links in a bio that fits nothing, their best content buried under yesterday's feed, and no home of their own. They earn from other people's systems, on other people's terms.

**What Naya does for them:** she gives them a home. Their videos, their links, their memberships, their people — all in one place, all theirs. Smart Access: any video, audio, or package behind a pay door, the login arriving by Smart Mail, and they earn 50% of the revenue. And when they bring people into NayaNET, they earn half of everything those people bring — because they are the best referrers we have.

**The moment it clicks:** they share one link — one Smart Link — and watch a stranger become a member, a member become a payer, and the money arrive without a platform taking its cut first.

## 4. Home-based side-hustlers

**Who they are:** they have a day job and a dream on the side. The shop that opens after dinner. The craft, the course, the service they are building one evening at a time.

**What they're carrying:** no time, no budget, and a business that has to run while they are at work. They cannot afford a website agency, a booking system, and a marketing tool — and they cannot be in two places at once.

**What Naya does for them:** she runs the side hustle while they are at their job. Nine days free, zero cost — try the Bugatti before you buy it. Their page, their bookings, their links, their little shop, all in one place, working while they are not watching. And the moment someone joins through them, they earn — the side hustle starts paying for itself.

**The moment it clicks:** they check their phone at lunch and find a booking that came in while they were in a meeting. The business worked a shift they didn't have to.

## 5. Community builders

**Who they are:** the ones who gather people. The group chat that became a community. The club, the circle, the movement, the neighborhood, the cause. They hold people together — and they do it mostly by hand.

**What they're carrying:** a hundred conversations in ten places, nobody knowing where anything lives, the same question asked twenty times, the event nobody heard about, the new person who never got welcomed. The community grows, and the work of holding it grows faster.

**What Naya does for them:** she gives the community a home — a Space where everything lives: the people, the conversations, the events, the shared notes, the wisdom. New people get welcomed. Events get found. The community's intelligence compounds, day after day, because everybody's notes and learning feed the whole. And community leaders are among our best referrers — when they bring their people in, everybody earns.

**The moment it clicks:** a new member joins, finds everything without asking anyone, and says they feel like they've been here for months — on their first day.

## 6. Small businesses, 2–10 staff

**Who they are:** a small team with a real business — a salon with chairs, a café, a studio, a shop, a crew. More than one person, not yet a company. The owner still knows every customer by name.

**What they're carrying:** the team running on memory and group chats. Who is booked where. Who paid, who didn't. What the regulars like. The owner is the system — and the owner is exhausted.

**What Naya does for them:** she becomes the system the owner has been carrying in their head. Bookings, customers, the ledger — everything recorded, everything with a value, nothing entering the network unseen. The team sees the same world the owner sees. The dashboard shows what happened today and what to do next, and the owner finally gets to run the business instead of being run by it.

**The moment it clicks:** the owner opens the dashboard on a Monday morning and sees the whole week — bookings, money, people — without asking a single person a single question.

## 7. Relationship sales

**Who they are:** their business is people. The agent, the rep, the advisor, the broker — the one whose phone is their office and whose memory is their filing cabinet. They don't sell products; they sell trust, one relationship at a time.

**What they're carrying:** hundreds of people, and the fear of forgetting one. Who was looking for what. Who they promised to call back. Who went quiet and why. The follow-up that didn't happen is the sale that didn't happen — and they know it.

**What Naya does for them:** she remembers everything that matters. Every person, every conversation, every promise — in their dashboard, in their lists, in their journal. Smart Search finds anybody by topic or place. Smart Mail reaches exactly who should be reached, and nobody who shouldn't. The relationship stays warm because Naya never forgets, even when life gets busy.

**The moment it clicks:** they open a contact and see the whole history — the last conversation, the promise made, the birthday coming up — and the call they make lands like they never left.

## 8. Teachers and tutors

**Who they are:** they teach. In a classroom, online, one-to-one, after school, on weekends. Their knowledge is the product, and their students are the point.

**What they're carrying:** lessons scattered across drives and notebooks, students in five different chats, bookings by text message, payments by whatever arrives, and the same question answered twenty times because there is no one place where the answer lives.

**What Naya does for them:** she gives their teaching a home. Lessons, students, bookings, and payments in one place. A membership for their course — students join, the login arrives by Smart Mail, the teacher earns. The Academy idea, made theirs: their knowledge, their students, their world, organized. And what they teach compounds — their notes become the network's learning, and everybody gets more intelligent, day after day.

**The moment it clicks:** a student finds the answer themselves in the teacher's world — the lesson, the notes, the next step — without the teacher having to say a word.

## 9. Busy parents and families

**Who they are:** the household. Two jobs, school runs, practices, birthdays, bills, the calendar that lives in three places and none of them right. They are not building a business — they are holding a life together.

**What they're carrying:** the coordination load. Who needs to be where, when. The permission slip. The photo from three years ago that nobody can find. The memory of what happened last Tuesday. The feeling that the family is always one forgotten thing away from chaos.

**What Naya does for them:** she remembers everything that matters — the notes, the days, the daily, weekly, and monthly reports, the calendar, the booking, the journaling, all in the dashboard. Look back on any day and see what went on in your world. The family's world in one place: the people, the plans, the memories, the lists. Private by design — nobody reads their mail, and it is deleted by default, a day or a week or a month, their choice.

**The moment it clicks:** they look back on a day from months ago — the photos, the notes, the little things — and realize nothing precious was lost. Naya kept it all.

## 10. Hobbyists, learners, and retirees

**Who they are:** they have time, and they have curiosity. The gardener, the reader, the maker, the one learning a language, the one who retired and finally gets to do the thing they always wanted to do. They are not chasing money — they are chasing meaning.

**What they're carrying:** the desire to learn, to make, to share — and nowhere to put it. The knowledge in their head that nobody asks about. The hobby that deserves a home. The feeling that their best years of learning are still ahead of them, if only someone would teach them, or let them teach.

**What Naya does for them:** she gives their curiosity a world. Learn anything — the network's collective intelligence, everybody's notes, everybody's learning, compounding day after day. Share what they know — a note, a lesson, a space — and be acknowledged, loved, appreciated, valued, respected for it. Their knowledge becomes part of something bigger, and they earn points for sharing it, because sharing wisdom is gifting intelligence to the world.

**The moment it clicks:** they share one thing they know — one note, one lesson — and a stranger on the other side of the world says thank you, and means it.
