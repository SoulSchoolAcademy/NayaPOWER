What membership is

◆IN A NUTSHELL

Membership is smart access, just behind a paywall. You enter through the portal — and everything inside is yours to learn from, listen to, and watch.

◆WHAT IT’S NOT

It is not a separate app. Membership is an add-on to any page — Personal, Business, or Space — never a place of its own. And it is not a locked door with nothing behind it: the courses are there, in three formats, ready to open.

◆CHILD

Imagine a clubhouse with a door. You walk in through the front door, and inside there are lessons to watch, listen to, and read. That is membership: the clubhouse, and the door is the portal.

◆GRANDMA

Dear, it is simple. A membership is a room you belong to. You come in, you sit down, and everything you need to learn is laid out for you — in words you can read, voices you can hear, and pictures you can watch.

◆ULTIMATELY

Ultimately, membership is access with a welcome. The paywall is not a wall to keep people out — it is the door that says: this is yours now, come in and use it.

◆WHY IT MATTERS

It matters because learning should be one click away, not a search away. When the courses sit behind one portal, a citizen can begin in seconds — and beginning is the whole game.

◆HOW IT CONNECTS

Membership connects to the page it belongs to. It is an add-on to Personal, Business, or Space — the same world, the same citizen, with the courses sitting inside it.

◆HOW TO APPLY

Tap a course, and enter. Pick a lesson from the table of contents, and begin. Watch it, listen to it, or read it — the three formats are all there.

◆WHAT’S IN IT FOR YOU

Three courses, in three formats, behind one portal. Five Academy lessons, nine Fast Action Club lessons, and the Masterclass growing. And with Naya Max, you can build a membership of your own.
