THE BUTTONS ARE THE BEST

The buttons are the best.

Real objects. Rims in their own deep color. Light flowing around them.

PASTE ANYTHING

Paste anything. It comes out beautiful.

Plain text, a smart note, an image — the format does the work.

Type or paste anything in the box below — a paragraph, a smart note, a whole page — and press the button.

Text comes back distilled into the block's own voice: nutshell first, one idea per board, three sentences at most. An image comes back as the board itself — full-bleed, rimmed, lit.

The content never shows its seams. What you pasted stays in the box above; what comes out below is the beautiful version.
