## The corrections, section by section

### 1. Merges (§9, "The Wise One") — CORRECTED
Original said: *"Never merge your own PRs. The moment a PR is merge-ready, send him 'Shawn, merge:' plus the link."*
**Shawn's ruling:** "You don't need to send me a merge. Unless you have to. If you have to send me a merge, you send it."
**Corrected law:** Work goes straight in. Do not send Shawn a merge by default. Only when a merge genuinely has to reach him — a true human gate — do you send it, and then you send it clean: link, exact value, 1-2-3 steps, one message.

### 2. Deleting raw notes after distilling (§9, "The learning law") — CORRECTED AND CONDITIONED
Original said: *"Raw notes are deleted after distilling, the same as emails: deletion by default."*
**Shawn's ruling:** "After distilling and you entered the smart notes into NayaNET, yeah, you delete them. But you have to put them into NayaNET, not just into GitHub. As smart notes. All the distilled ones. And then you delete."
**Corrected law — three steps, in order, no step skippable:**
1. Distill (100 → 10, 500 → 50 — keep the best 5–10%).
2. Enter ALL the distilled notes into NayaNET **as Smart Notes** — live in the system, not merely filed in GitHub.
3. Only then delete the raw notes.
Deleting before step 2 is complete is destroying intelligence, not distilling it.

### 3. How citizens earn (§6 and §8) — ORIGINAL CONFIRMED, earlier deduction withdrawn
Original said: *"citizens earn 50% of revenue on all levels, including free, paid automatically the moment we're paid — first payout day 10, then daily."*
Resolve's scorecard deducted this ("free has no revenue to share"). **That deduction was wrong.**
**Shawn's ruling:** "50% on all levels including free, yes. Everybody gets a job — they can join NayaNET free and they can get their link and they can promote whether they pay or not." And on rhythm: "Basically it's simple — we get paid, you get paid. If you end up getting sales every day, eventually you get paid every day."
**Corrected law:** 50% revenue share on every level, including Free. Everyone is enrolled by default and gets their ambassador link on joining, paid or not. The payout rule in its simplest form: **we get paid → you get paid**, on the rhythm of actual receipts (a sale's money is truly received when the 9-day trial converts; daily sales eventually mean daily pay). No holdback after receipt. The older "Wednesday-only" framing is superseded by Shawn's simpler rule above.

### 4. The three-part heart (§5, "The Trinity") — CORRECTED AND DEFINED
Original named the Trinity as Dashboard, Hub, and the core page. Shawn's three-part heart for the intelligence layer is different, and he defined each part himself:
- **Smart Notes** are Intelligent Blocks — they live **on the main feed**.
- **The Intelligent Library** is where you store your library of Smart Notes **on one specific subject** — so someone can know everything about your business, yourself, or your subject. It lives on your page / app.
- **The Smart Box** is the **auto-organization of everything**: all your Smart Notes, notes, files, projects, images — your brain. You can ask Naya to store anything; she stores it and organizes it automatically.
The Dashboard/Hub/core-page triad remains true as the *serving* heart. The two triads now carry different names in this edition — **Serving Heart** (Dashboard · Hub · core page) and **Intelligence Heart** (Smart Notes · Intelligent Library · Smart Box) — so a new seat never confuses them.

### 5. The four doors (§5–§6) — WORDING CORRECTED, SUBSTANCE CONFIRMED
Original called Dashboard, Personal, Business, Space "the four first apps" and also said "it is really ONE app."
**Shawn's ruling:** "Dashboard, Personal, Business, Space — the four first apps, yeah. It's true, this is four doors: Personal, Business, Space is just — imagine if you put your name, your business name, or a topic name — really the same app. The Dashboard is just the data for those apps. And Membership is a front door: you just get a login page, log into their world. Membership can be on a Personal, Business, or Space, and it's usually always connected to one of those."
**Corrected law:** ONE app, four doors. Personal = the app carrying a person's name; Business = the same app carrying a business name; Space = the same app carrying a topic name. Dashboard = the data for those apps. Membership = a front-door login page into a Personal, Business, or Space world — never a separate app.

### 6. Build order (§6 and §10) — CORRECTED TO PARALLEL
Original said: *"build in this order; each finished and proven before the next"* and *"one page finished before the next starts."*
**Shawn's ruling:** "I want them all done in parallel. There's no reason you can't do them in parallel. Nitro's doing them all right now. I hope we can get to the point where you're all working on them, helping the system get these blocks done, so creating a new page is just — boom, done."
**Corrected law:** The four doors are built **in parallel**, all seats helping. The finish standard does not change (fully functioning, end-to-end inspected, 9+ before it reaches Shawn) — only the sequencing does. Sequential order survives only where a real dependency forces it, and the dependency must be named.

### 7. The daily heartbeat (§9–§10) — MISSING SECTION, NOW ADDED
Original never mentioned the Daily Intelligence Report or daily distillation. Shawn confirmed the gap and ruled:
- **Every morning** (not at night): the **Daily Intelligence Report** — the most important valuable knowledge from the last 24 hours, and the goal of the day.
- **Daily distillation the same morning**, for the same 24 hours — this is how the note pile never grows past the day. The team is behind and catching up in batches of 100 → 10, reporting after each batch.
- Weekly, monthly, and eventually yearly reports join the same rhythm at their own cadence.

### 8. Live vs. target — THE POINT-9 FIX, BUILT (new standing rule for every document and report)
Original mixed live facts with target state without marking which was which ("login codes come by Smart Mail," "ambassador link is default on login" — some built, some not yet), so a new seat could not tell them apart.
Shawn suggested the fix himself: timestamp and date-stamp so they CAN be told apart — and the citizen-facing proof is a **welcome Smart Mail** carrying the new citizen's ambassador link and Dashboard login.
**The rule, now standing for this orientation and every state claim after it:**
- Every claim about the system carries one of three stamps:
  - **[VERIFIED LIVE — as of YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM, clicked/read by <seat>]**
  - **[BUILT — in code, not yet proven on the live surface]**
  - **[TARGET — designed and ruled, not yet built]**
- Links that prove a state carry the date they were verified. A claim with no stamp is treated as TARGET until proven.
- The welcome Smart Mail (ambassador link + Dashboard login) is the citizen's own way to tell: if the mail arrived with both, that part is live for them.

### 9. Who captains what (§1 and §6) — SCOPE CORRECTED
Original was written to Nitro's team ("your captain is Nitro") with no scope note.
**Shawn's ruling:** "Nitro's the captain of interfaces, there's no question — she's done more valuable work that's been helpful, like the interfaces. I want you [Resolve] to take the lead on whatever would help — identify the top valuable things, whether in coding, setup, or Smart Notes — and get your whole team helping finish the blocks."
**Corrected picture:** Nitro captains **interfaces**. Resolve leads the **intelligence layer** — Smart Notes and distillation end to end, memory, Project Intelligence completion, and the proof-of-done system — with her whole team finishing blocks alongside Nitro's parallel build. Power holds her own lane (apps 19–26). The Trinity is the org; no single captain owns the whole hive.

### 10. Interfaces — THE LEGO RULE, NOW STATED (new)
Original taught harvest-first for components; Shawn sharpened why seats keep failing at interfaces:
**Shawn's words in substance:** you don't create the Lego from scratch. Take all the code, learn from what already exists, work with the parts already there, and create the ultimate pages from that. Starting over produces flat buttons and low-level pages because the builder never learned from the masters' examples.
**The rule:** before building any interface, inventory the existing proven parts and pages first. Compose from them. A from-scratch interface build is a defect, not a style choice.
