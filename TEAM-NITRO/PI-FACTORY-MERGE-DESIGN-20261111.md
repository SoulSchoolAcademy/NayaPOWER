# PI + FACTORY — ONE SYSTEM FOR CREATING IN NAYANET
Design note from Shawn's vision, 2026-11-11 14:32Z. Written in his meaning, never pasted verbatim.

## The vision
Project Intelligence and the Smart Factory merge into ONE system for creating inside
NayaNET. Not two tools — one system, the parts communicating together for the ultimate
goal: helping people build and organize their world.

## The shape
- **A project IS a page.** A project is an app: your Personal page, your Business, your
  Space, your Membership — or all of them. The ladder already counts projects (PI 3 / 10 /
  100 / unlimited). Now the project gets a brain and hands.
- **PI is the brain of the project.** Where you put everything in: notes, links, files —
  PDFs through your Smart Box. Like what we're doing right now: crafting and building,
  and PI is where all the intelligence about the project lives.
- **The Factory is the hands.** It builds the page from what PI knows. The intelligence
  you feed in becomes the page that comes out.
- **The loop closes:** the built page reports back to PI — the build, the receipt, the
  record — so PI always knows the true state of the project. One loop, not two apps.

## How the parts complement each other
- PI knows WHAT the project is (vision, goal, mission, about, notes, links, files).
- The Factory knows HOW to build it (parts, templates, the build queue, the deploy).
- The Smart Box holds WHAT the project is made of (PDFs, files, memory).
- A citizen never types the same thing twice: what they put in PI is what the Factory
  builds from. What the Factory builds is what PI records.

## What already exists (harvest, never remake)
- PI: the Living List, the board, the record of everything directed — the project's
  memory, already live at nayanet.app/pi.
- The Factory: the build queue, the contract, the templates, the processor — pages built
  from a contract, already proven (builds 0fe83325 + 49870fd2 DONE 13:4xZ).
- The Smart Box: the citizen's vault, files and memory, outside any one AI.
- The ladder: projects counted per level — the shape of the system is already in the
  pricing.

## What's new (the communication between them)
- The Factory's build READS the project's PI: the notes, the links, the files the citizen
  already fed in become the build's input.
- The build's receipt WRITES back to PI: the project always knows what was built, when,
  and what it looks like now.
- One project, one PI, one Factory build — the citizen sees one system, not three.

## The first step (smallest real move that proves the merge)
The flow's Step 1 fields pre-fill from the project's PI: vision, goal, mission, about —
pulled from the notes the citizen already put in, not typed again. If PI has nothing yet,
the fields are empty and the eyes walk them through it (fix-3). The build receipt lands
back in PI as the project's record. That one loop — in from PI, out to the page, back to
PI — is the merge, proven on one project, then it spreads to every project.

## The two missing layers (Shawn, 14:34Z — they belong inside the merged system)
- **THE TIME LAYER — calendar + journal + booking.** Where they book appointments, and
  see what's going on in their world. It lives INSIDE Personal / Business / Space — not a
  separate app (features, never separate apps, is the law) — and it syncs with all of
  this: PI, the Factory, the Box. Go back to the day before to see what happened (the
  journal), see what's upcoming (the calendar), make notes on things, book appointments
  with them — as a Personal, as a Business, as a Space. Your world's time, in your world.
  (Booking is already named MISSING in the system — needs building; Journal is one of the
  fulfillment clones. This is their home.)
- **THE LIVE LAYER — Smart Meet.** Video chat with four members, six members — or an
  audio conference. It works off the Personal / Business / Space page: click, boom, add
  the contacts, now let's video chat or audio conference. Or make an appointment for it:
  schedule a Smart Meet, schedule a video conference — and it lands on the calendar (the
  time layer above). (Smart Meet is already named one of the two genuinely new builds
  owed, with Smart Mail + Messenger-as-toggle. This is its home.)

## The standard
A child can build their world with it: feed PI what they know, the Factory builds it,
PI remembers it. Simple, easy, 10-star service — are we doing as much as we can for them?
