## WHAT THE BULLSEYE LOOKS LIKE

When you look at the hero, you should feel like you're sitting in a Bugatti.
Not looking at a picture of one. SITTING IN ONE.

### The 8 Elements (all must be present, all must be real)

1. **CHROME BEZEL** — Polished metal ring. Light from top-left creates bright
   highlights and deep shadows — that's what sells metal. You could run your
   finger around it. Conic gradient with rich contrast, not flat grey.

2. **BLACK OBSIDIAN DIAL** — Deep black. Not grey. Not dark. BLACK.
   You look INTO it, not at it.

3. **ETCHED NUMERALS (0-500)** — Numbers printed ON the metal. Crisp, sharp,
   white. Major ticks at numbers, minor ticks between. Like they were etched
   by a machine. Not floating text.

4. **DIAMOND CENTER** — Layered machined rings going back. The exposed movement.
   Depth you look INTO. Like a jewel set in the machine.

5. **NEEDLE** — Real metal pointer. Bright on the lit edge. Casts a shadow.
   Sweeps with purpose (1.2s, acceleration easing). Has weight.

6. **DISPLAY MODULE** — Recessed black window. The green score glows like real
   hardware INSIDE the machine. Not text on a screen — a display module.

7. **ORBS (7)** — Glossy black marbles. Each in its spectrum color. Number dead
   center (white-hot). Label in its color. 8-second breathing cycle — light
   flowing around each. Under-glow beneath. Alive.

8. **JEWEL ORBIT** — 8 spectrum jewels slowly circling the hero (40s).
   One family. Same color language. Unity you can feel.

### The Viewport

- **Hero mode:** NOTHING but the instrument. No search, no nav, no bottom bar.
- **Morph button:** One tap unfolds everything. Another tap folds it all away.
- **When nav shows:** Hero scales to 92%. Everything fits. Nothing overlaps. Ever.

## HOW TO HIT IT EVERY TIME

### The 5 Questions (before you touch code)

1. What is the ONE purpose of this viewpoint?
2. What is the hero object?
3. What parts does it need? (From the blessed set — compose, never create.)
4. What would make Shawn say "that's not real"? (Fix it before he sees it.)
5. Is anything flat, floating, or overlapping? (If yes, it's not done.)

### The Build Order

1. **Structure first** — viewpoint container, hero placement, orb positions
2. **Materials second** — chrome, obsidian, marble, glass (name every material)
3. **Light third** — top-left source, consistent shadows, reflections
4. **Life fourth** — breathing, flowing, orbiting, responding
5. **Viewport last** — hero mode, morph button, scaling

### The Verification

- [ ] Rendered review (your eyes, phone first, signed-in seat)
- [ ] Every element passes the 5 reality questions
- [ ] Nothing overlaps in any viewport state
- [ ] Touch every element — does it respond?
- [ ] Screenshot compared to Bugatti reference
- [ ] Live bytes verified at the real address

### The Traps (do not repeat)

- CSS-value tweaking loop → DEFINE THE OBJECT FIRST, then build it
- Claiming without rendered proof → RECEIPTS BEFORE REPORT
- Doing 3 of 10 things → FINISH ALL OF THEM, then show
- Flat text on a dial → ETCH IT (crisp, sharp, part of the instrument)
- Overlay text → NOTHING floats. Everything is IN the object.
- Side labels → THE DIAL SPEAKS FOR ITSELF
