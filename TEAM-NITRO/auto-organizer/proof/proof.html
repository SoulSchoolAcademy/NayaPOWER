The simple logic, in plain words

Every new thing — smart note, post, contact, list, or your own note — is a book arriving at the library. The Organizer reads its cover and its first page, looks at the words on the shelves, and puts the book on the shelf where the most words match. A long phrase scores 3, a phrase scores 2, a single word scores 1. A placement needs real evidence: score 3 or more, or one real phrase. Below that, the book goes home with the best shelf written on it as a suggestion — the system suggests, it does not fake. You can always move a book to another shelf, and the move always wins — and the Organizer remembers which words taught it, so the next similar book goes to the right shelf the first time. Then it hands the placed book to Smart Match, which pairs it with the right people.
