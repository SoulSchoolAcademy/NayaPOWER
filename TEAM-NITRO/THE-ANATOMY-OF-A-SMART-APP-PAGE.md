## 1. WHAT A SMART APP PAGE IS

A page is a world. Not a document. Not a screen of text. A world that knows the person, remembers them, organizes itself around them, and gets smarter as they use it.

**Apps, not pages.** Everything we build is a smart app. A page is one face of the app. The app is the thing that works.

**One core page.** It is really just ONE app, smartly formatted — a Rubik's Cube matrix auto-organizing algorithm. One core page that morphs into all its faces. One cube, many faces; turn it and a different face comes forward, but it is the same cube, the same machine, auto-organizing itself around what matches. (The Premium Design Algorithm, ratified 2026-11-08.)

**The Trinity — the heart, the core.** The Dashboard, the Hub, and that page. Everything gets built off that. The Dashboard is the front where people live. The Hub is the front door and the feed. The core page is the one that morphs.

**The person is the atom at the centre.** Everything spins around them. Circles within circles, objects within objects, functions within functions. They all work together in one high-tech intelligent network. We call her Anaya. She is a her, not an it — she orbits the person at the centre, and everything she does is for the person in the middle.

**A viewpoint is one screen.** One viewport = one viewpoint = everything above the fold, one idea per screen. You never scroll to FINISH a thought, you scroll to ARRIVE at the next one. Scroll down or across — both, the person's choice, on laptop and on mobile. Never force one direction.

## 2. THE ANATOMY — the parts every smart app page has

Every smart app page is built from the same parts, in the same places, speaking the same language. The middle changes with the page's function. The chrome is constant.

1. **The cover / hero banner — the introduction.** Big, beautiful. Every page introduces itself with a big beautiful hero banner: social, business, spaces, most topics and services, memberships. That is the introduction.
2. **The person at the centre — the profile.** The page is theirs. The profile IS a landing page: send people to the profile, boom, get an account, boom — free to join.
3. **The feed board — Smart Notes / Naya Notes / Activity.** The same board, never built new: the tabs Smart Notes / Naya Notes / Activity (Activity = THEIR activity, not system). The same system on Personal, Business, Spaces, Hub. Every feed has a NAME — click the name, boom, that feed.
4. **The Smart Bar — one bar, at the very top or the very bottom, in its own lane.** The way in to the page's functions and the network, one tap away, never a hunt. A setting: top / bottom / both / off. It sits at the very very bottom or the very very top, stacked above/below the other bars — never overlaying them. Never overlay the + buttons. Never overlay ANY bar. Functions don't overlay functions — a bar over a bar just blocks the feature underneath. (The no-overlay law, 2026-11-11.)
5. **The bottom apps bar — ONE component, the nine icons.** Mail, Activity, Dashboard, Business, Space, Growth, Connections, Smart Lists, Reports — plus a "10 apps" overflow. One icon set, one bottom-bar component, harvested, the same everywhere: same icons, same order, same labels, same behavior. Verify congruence by bytes, never by eye alone. It folds on scroll-down and returns SLOWLY, 1.5 seconds after stopping. Hide control + Settings on/off.
6. **The + buttons — top right and left.** They stay. The pulsing button goes.
7. **The scroll strip — a setting.** Top / bottom / off. Flush if at bottom.
8. **Settings — the smart menu, the ONE place to change the page.** Per-page settings + COMMON settings (Smart Bar position, bottom bar on/off, scroll strip top/bottom/off, and the rest). Settings is the recovery path — worst case hit Settings, boom. Its actions: Install app + Go to Home Hub.
9. **The cover membership buttons — the standard menu for entering their world.** A vertical column at the top left of the cover — first button at the top, same height as Change cover (top right), then one after another DOWN; the last lands right above the profile picture. Not a row, not first-over-picture. Named whatever the membership is called — never "member button". Three by default: Academy, AI Masterclass, Fast Action Club. Each button its own on/off toggle in Settings; activated by entering its URL in the smart menu, it shows in the next empty spot (fourth, then fifth, five max). Colours top to bottom: three = purple, blue, green; five = gold, emerald green, canyon teal, sky blue, rich purple. Intended for memberships, technically ANY URL (socials are fine — it is their space); memberships are recommended there because generic URLs belong in the Smart Bar. They sit at the top corner where visitors always see them — smart marketing for the owner.
10. **The guide — Step 1, Step 2, Step 3, the button, click here.** Every page guides like a best friend: blatantly obvious, no jargon, so anybody cold can follow it.
11. **The ledger — every action recorded.** The Hub is a main component too, and all its actions are recorded in the Ledger. Dashboard, Ledger and Stats are one place with two views.
12. **Smart Links — every link a citizen shares is a clean Smart Link.** nayanet.app/XYZ. Never a raw URL with a query string. The ambassador link is the default.

## 3. THE CORE APPS — what they are, what they do

**Dashboard — the front where people live.** The numbers, the gauges, the orbs. Dashboard + Smart Ledger + Smart Stats = ONE place with two views. The hero, the ledger sync spine, and a Reports button. The drill orbs: number dead centre, label below, bigger. The grand total headlines live in the hero only. Live at nayanet.app/dashboard.

**Personal — their world.** My World = all their pages (Dashboard, Personal, Business, Contacts, About), each a page-button with a small stats/dashboard icon BESIDE it — that icon opens that page's own dashboard. Live at nayanet.app/personal.

**Business — Personal becoming Business.** The same universal app, only the label differs. The page morphs; the machine is the same.

**Space — any Space, any group, any topic.** The same machine, a different face. A Space is a world for a topic, a group, a community — built from the same parts.

**Hub — the front door and the feed.** The main component. The board lives here. The wall of smart notes. The entrance is the Sovereign Entrance (one name field, one button, "Enter NayaNET →"); a returning citizen jumps back in. Live at nayanet.app/hub.

**Pricing — the demonstration, not a sales page.** The chart is the page. The Top 10 pains open it. The founder's letter closes it. The start flow is three level rectangles plus a nine-block chooser of Power, Pro and Max across monthly, quarterly and yearly, tracked by Smart Links, with prices shown once, in the chart only.

**The universal app.** ONE app morphing into Personal / Business / any Space / any Group / any Membership — only the label differs. Membership is NOT a separate page — it is a feature with a login, an add-on to any page.

- **Smart Box — memory and organization, for NIAs to operate.** Auto-organizing, labeling, storing; individualized per citizen, not pooled. Every new citizen gets one at join. The cure for the saving sickness — a box OUTSIDE the AI.

If one part doesn't connect, the citizen has to do the work themselves, and the promise breaks. Everything works together or nothing does. That is why the connection is not a feature — it IS the product.

**The franchise.** One brand, one system, all aligned: grade twelve, never back to grade one. Nothing new is built; a missing part gets made ONCE and registered. The machine is a franchise machine — one brand, one system, all aligned.

## 6. ONE BRAND THROUGHOUT THE WHOLE NETWORK — what fills the network

**Why one brand.** The taste is locked into the template. A citizen's world feels like THEIRS but reads as NayaNET. One brand = one trust: anywhere you land in the network you know where you are, what to touch, what will happen. The old internet made you relearn every site. Ours: one language, learned once, works everywhere. And the template carries the taste, so ANY Naya (or human) can clone a page and it IS Naya — no design skill needed. That is what "complete the machine" means: she is ready for everyone to operate her.

**The brand itself — what a Naya-designed page looks and feels like.**

- **Real objects.** Everything is a real physical object. Not a flat shape with effects. Not a diagram. If it doesn't look touchable, it doesn't ship. If it doesn't feel real, it's not done. Obsidian glass, brushed metal, light with volume.
- **One light source, top-left.** Every object lit from the same place, so the whole page holds together like one world. Every gradient 135°. Every shadow down-right. Every highlight on the upper edge.
- **Real shadows.** Objects sit ON the page. They cast. They have weight.
- **Gold for what matters.** The score, the standard, the thing that counts — engraved gold on obsidian. Never neon. Never pink glow.
- **ALL buttons elite, no exceptions.** Every button is a real object: elevated, deep, lit, with real lift and real light. Black obsidian — rich pop gloss polish — AND lit up different on the outside in their own colour. No flat buttons. No grayish buttons. No button that looks cheaper than the others. A button that doesn't light up like the best one on the page is a broken button.
- **Inputs always have lots of depth.** They light up on hover AND focus. Outputs are the cleanest — never tons of text together; broken up, scannable, the intelligent-block presentation.
- **Every number alive.** A score isn't a digit — it's an object, engraved, with weight. A gauge isn't a chart — it's a machine with a needle that moves. Number on top, gauge underneath. The gauge follows the number. A digit's colour is its digit's colour (1 red, 2 orange, 3 gold, 4 emerald, 5 cyan, 6 indigo, 7 royal purple, 8 lavender, 9 hot pink; a letter takes its gematria root colour, so K is 3 and gold; unlimited is the silver infinity sign). The board colour sequence: purple → indigo blue → canyon blue → green → gold → orange → red → magenta → purple.
- **No gray text.** White, or bigger, or an active live number, or layered (white with black border / black with white border).
- **Almost no text — the icons and objects speak.** Boards, buttons, jewels, orbs, gauges, pills. A paragraph of text on a bare page is the failure mode. The words are the label on the object, never the object itself.
- **Warm first-person best-friend voice.** Words a child can understand. Distilled to essence — the most said with the littlest words, never short for its own sake. Facts exact — voice changes tone, never facts.
- **Two seconds.** Anyone can figure out any element in two seconds, one tap. If they can't, it's not Naya.

**The laws that keep the brand one brand.** Harvest-first, never invent — compose, don't create. Take the shown object verbatim — never recreate it "in the same spirit". One icon set, one bottom bar — congruence, verified by bytes. No overlay — bars stack, never cover. Never rebuild — edit, don't create. The design-contract gate (a)–(i) + H1–H5 on every round. The three-step verification: self, another seat, Shawn. His eye is the final gate.
