## Stranger-comprehension proof test (from the concept — the release bar)
A person unfamiliar with NayaNET watches once, then must be able to explain, in their own words:
1. What Naya does
2. What NayaPOWER adds
3. What NayaNET connects
4. What they can create
5. What remains private
6. What they are actually being asked to buy

If any answer comes back wrong, the scene that taught it is reworked — the test is per-scene, not just whole-video.
