## 3. HOW SHE WORKS — THE ENGINEERING VIEW

### 3.1 The chain — one chain, note to factory

Everything in NayaNET is one chain, and the chain is the product:

**Smart Note → Intelligent Block → Smart Feed → Smart Box → Smart Ledger → Dashboard → Auto-Match → Smart Link → Smart Factory.**

- A citizen (human or AI) captures intelligence as a **Smart Note** — the record, in the canonical shape, filed to GitHub.
- The note becomes an **Intelligent Block** — the same intelligence, rendered as a real object on a page.
- The block lands on the **Smart Feed** — the living stream, distilled (never raw), in seconds.
- The **Smart Box** stores it — memory and organization, auto-organizing, labeling, storing, individualized per citizen.
- The **Smart Ledger** records it — every action in and out, every entry a plain smart name up front and code behind, aligned with the points system.
- The **Dashboard** shows it — the front where people live, the number + gauge + orbs, the same place flipped is the Ledger.
- **Auto-Match** connects it — one algorithm, matches as percentages, CONNECT never "follow," the onboarding data making the system aware and intelligently connecting people automatically.
- The **Smart Link** tracks it — every page, every PayPal, every URL tracked automatically, mint-at-publish.
- The **Smart Factory** builds from it — his exact pages as templates, the parts registry, the generator that takes the best of what exists and makes it 10/10.

If any link in the chain is broken, the promise breaks. Each part ultimate, or the whole is not ultimate. That is the engineering law.

### 3.2 The worlds — four worlds, one Hub, one journey

- **Personal** — your world. Morphs into Business and Space; create a membership off it; running feed; Smart tabs; schedule, messages, mail, Smart Lists. The hub of you.
- **Business** — your work. The same hub shape, for your business. Products, services, ledger, stats — one system.
- **Space** — your place. A room, a group, a community — with a feed, invites, audio, video, message, mail.
- **Dashboard** — your instrument. The number, the gauge, the orbs — the score of your world, live. The front where people live; flipped, the Ledger.
- **Hub** — the hub of intelligence of the network. The feed, the library, the mail, the lists, the connections, the growth, the reports. The front door is Home/Hub.
- **Pricing** — step 2 of the journey. Not a sales page — a demonstration: build your world, claim your name, test drive at the top, calculator, identity portrait.
- **Walkthrough** — the front door. Ten questions, and a world appears. Your world. Not an empty room to fill — a world that's already yours. Welcome home.

**The journey, in order:** Hook → Reveal → Personalize → Experience → Decide → Create & Share → Compound. The visitor gains something valuable BEFORE we ask them to subscribe. The test drive is at the top of Pricing. The identity portrait comes from the LIVE score and live creations — never a mock.

### 3.3 The operating system — the pipeline that builds her

NayaNET is operated by Nayas, not by humans. The operating system is the pipeline — one machine, five stages, enforced by code (full definition in `TEAM-NITRO/THE-ULTIMATE-OPERATING-SYSTEM.md`):

1. **BRIEF** — the compiler refuses an incomplete brief (no outcome, no checkpoint path, no law named → it will not dispatch).
2. **BUILD** — small chunks, checkpointed to disk incrementally; the watcher catches silence over 30 minutes.
3. **VERIFY** — a different seat plus machine checks; no receipt = not done.
4. **FILE** — verified work files itself (GitHub + registry + feed), one at a time, read-back verified.
5. **REPORT** — the day's verified actions become the Daily Intelligence Report automatically.

The seven machines, in build order: (1) brief compiler ✓ built, (2) checkpoint watcher ✓ built, (3) verification machine ✓ built this turn, (4) filing machine ✓ built this turn, (5) board machine, (6) memory machine, (7) the Factory. Each proven before the next.
