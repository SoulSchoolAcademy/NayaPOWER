## The problem

There are two things wrong.

First, there are too many. Five hundred and ten notes went into the table, and none of them were distilled. That should never happen. We are not on top of it.

Second, none of them reached their destination. Five hundred and ten rows in the table, and none of them hit NayaNET — none of them are on the Hub page, none of them are useful to anyone. So they all failed to be useful.

That is two rules broken: too many, and no distillation, and no entry. The notes were written, but they were not put into the system properly. They were not distilled, and they were not entered. So they are a pile, not a brain.

## Your mission

You have three jobs, and they are one job.

**1. Distill the Smart Notes.** Take the raw notes and distill them into their essence — into the intelligence, into the simplest words. Ten raw notes become one distilled note. Five hundred become fifty. Fifty become the nine that count. Distill the value out of the pile.

**2. Put the distilled notes into the system.** File the distilled notes as Smart Notes, in the right shape, in the right place, and get them into NayaNET — into the Hub feed — so they are live and useful and everybody can see them and learn from them. A note that is not in the system is a note that does not exist for the team.

**3. Give us a Daily Intelligence Report.** At the end of every day, distill the day and report what you learned. The report is the distillation of the day. Never skip a day.

## How to do it right

The method is filed in GitHub as SN-2237, "How to Put a Smart Note In — and How to Distill It." Read it first. It has the whole method. In short:

- **One note = one piece of intelligence.** One idea, one lesson, one decision. Never a transcript. Never a pile.
- **Use the canonical shape.** Title, SN number and topic, created timestamp, Shawn's words verbatim when it is his, then Hook, Nutshell, Human, Child, Grandma, Naya, What it means, Why it matters, What changes now.
- **Number it from the next free number.** Never reuse a number. Never use a retired number.
- **File it in GitHub** at `BRAIN/05-MEMORY/SMART-NOTES/<date>/<TOPIC>/SN-####/IB-SMART-NOTE-<date>-sn####-<slug>.md`, one directory per note, and verify it landed by reading it back.
- **Categorize it** so it lands in the right feed and is findable.
- **Distill ten to one.** Read the raw notes, ask what is the essence, what is the one thing that changes the next decision, and write it in the simplest words. A child could understand it. A grandma could understand it. If it needs jargon, it is not distilled yet.
- **Check the filing gate before you file anything.** One piece of intelligence? Canonical shape? Simplest words? No duplicate? Filed and verified? Does it change the next decision?
- **The metric is distilled and live, never raw and synced.** Report distilled notes filed and notes live on the Hub. Never report raw notes synced. Syncing is intake, not intelligence.

## The Daily Intelligence Report — what it must answer

Every day, your report must answer these questions. Each one as a top 10 list. And then the one question that matters most.

1. **The top 10 most valuable things** you learned from the distillation.
2. **The top 10 most useful things** — the things that help someone do something better.
3. **The top 10 most helpful things** — the things that help someone who is stuck.
4. **The top 10 most enlightening things** — the things that made something clear that was not clear before.
5. **The top 10 most memorable things** — the things you will not forget.
6. **The top 10 most important things** — the things that matter most, that everything else depends on.
7. **The top 10 wisest things you learned** — the wisdom, not just the information.
8. **The best of the best** — the top 10 of all of it. Take everything above, and pick the ten best. The best of the best of what you got from it all.
9. **And ultimately, what is it saying, in the simplest words?** One paragraph. The simplest words you can find. What is the whole day, the whole distillation, ultimately saying?

That is the report. Every day. The top 10s, the best of the best, and the simplest words.
