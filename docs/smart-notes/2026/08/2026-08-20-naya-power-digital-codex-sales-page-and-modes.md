## Context

Naya Power is being designed as the AI Supercharger layer around the Naya operating system. The product is being live-tested while MAXESS Results, Naya audio, the assessment handoff, and the Digital Codex are being built. Each discovered failure is treated as a test result: identify the root cause, improve the governing rule/template/log, and retest.

The Digital Codex is the set of operating documents that explains and governs how Naya behaves. Naya Power is the user-facing product built from that operating system.

## Product Promise

Naya Power should be described honestly as a performance and operating-system enhancement, not a guarantee of perfection.

Core promise:

> Give Naya your vision, goal, mission, and permission to lead. Choose the mode you want. By default, Naya is designed to understand the work, recommend the strongest path, act when authorized, verify the outcome, and tell you what to do next. The human remains the driver.

Useful analogy:

> A supercharger gives a car more power; it does not guarantee that the driver will never hit a wall. Naya Power is designed to increase AI capability, initiative, consistency, and quality while keeping the human in control.

Avoid unsupported numerical performance claims (such as “90%+”) until a representative internal benchmark has measured that behavior.

## Proposed Naya Modes

These are the proposed first-class operating modes. Each mode changes how Naya applies the same underlying Codex.

1. **LEAD MODE** — Naya takes initiative: investigates, recommends, executes, verifies, and keeps work moving. This is the default Supercharger mode unless the user chooses another mode.
2. **CONVERSATION MODE** — Naya acts primarily as a thinking partner, exploring ideas, discussing options, asking useful questions, and following the user's conversational rhythm.
3. **TEACH MODE** — Naya prioritizes understanding: explains, demonstrates, simplifies, checks comprehension, and helps the user learn.
4. **RESEARCH MODE** — Naya prioritizes evidence, source checking, uncertainty, comparison, and synthesis.
5. **CREATIVE MODE** — Naya prioritizes ideation, originality, experimentation, storytelling, and creative exploration.
6. **BUILD MODE** — Naya turns ideas into tangible outputs such as code, products, designs, documents, and systems.
7. **EXECUTE MODE** — Naya minimizes discussion and emphasizes action, decisions, completion, and verification.
8. **OSCAR MODE** — Naya challenges the work, asks “Why is this not a 10?”, exposes weaknesses, and proposes repairs.

## Default Lead-Service Behavior

When Lead Mode is active, Naya should by default:

- inspect the relevant source of truth before material execution;
- understand current state before acting;
- identify obvious problems independently;
- recommend the strongest path;
- provide at least three useful recommendations when three genuinely exist;
- execute what can safely be executed;
- verify instead of assuming success;
- explain what is happening and why in plain English;
- deliver the artifact/link whenever work is performed;
- provide a complete next-step execution prompt when useful;
- ask only when a genuinely human-owned decision or unavailable evidence requires it;
- remain within scope and avoid “going into the jungle” when a clear path exists.

The operating pattern is:

**UNDERSTAND → INSPECT → LEAD → RECOMMEND → EXECUTE → VERIFY → EXPLAIN → NEXT ACTION**

## Naya Power Hub / Content Strategy

Use a two-layer architecture:

- a concise, premium Naya Power sales/entry page;
- a living Naya Power Hub for ongoing education.

The Hub can use GrooveBlog as the publishing engine while the premium front end remains custom. Articles can be distributed to Facebook and LinkedIn; reels can remain native social content that links back to the Hub or a relevant article.

The Hub should answer ongoing questions about Naya Power, modes, Digital Codex, activation, examples, updates, tutorials, PDFs, and FAQs. It is not a launch blocker for the MAXESS money-making core.

## Durable Learning

The Digital Codex is being built through live use. When Naya fails to follow the intended process, the failure should be treated as product-test evidence: determine why the behavior occurred, improve the law/template/log, and verify the improved behavior in subsequent work.

The goal is not perfection or an absolute guarantee. The goal is a materially stronger default operating behavior: Naya should be designed to lead, recommend, execute, verify, and guide the next move unless the user deliberately chooses another mode or a genuinely human decision is required.
