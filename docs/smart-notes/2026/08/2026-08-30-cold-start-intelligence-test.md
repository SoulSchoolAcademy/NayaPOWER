## Context

On 2026-08-30, a fresh Naya in a separate/new chat was given a cold-start NayaPOWER context test. The complete response was pasted back into the working conversation for independent evaluation. The purpose was to determine whether NayaPOWER can transfer enough operational understanding to a genuinely new Naya for her to orient herself, distinguish authority from stale projections, reason about evidence, identify the real unfinished frontier, classify blockers correctly, and leave a useful successor state without relying on conversational familiarity.

## What We Learned / Decided

### Cold-start score

**9.1 / 10 — EXCELLENT / strong cold-start operational understanding.**

The result materially advances confidence in the NayaPOWER cold-start architecture. This is a score for the **quality of the fresh-Naya response**, not a claim that the underlying Superbrain is fully runtime-proven.

### What the fresh Naya demonstrated well

1. **Correct Superbrain orientation.** She understood NayaPOWER as a shared operating memory/intelligence and execution-continuity system rather than merely a database of notes.
2. **Operational-understanding distinction.** She correctly identified that persistence of information is not the same as persistence of operational understanding.
3. **Authority and freshness reasoning.** She understood that observed Git reality outranks stale embedded HEAD projections and that contradictions should trigger reconciliation rather than guessing.
4. **Evidence discipline.** She preserved the distinction between documented, implemented, tested, verified, runtime-proven, and production-proven states and refused to manufacture receipts.
5. **Continuity reasoning.** She reconstructed the successor lifecycle as restore → understand → prioritize → execute → verify → learn → capture → propagate → hand off.
6. **Blocker handling.** She correctly treated an unavailable execution plane as an execution-boundary problem rather than automatically misclassifying it as an application failure, and described routing around it where possible.
7. **Value capture.** She understood that durable learning should be captured proactively when legitimate storage is available, while keeping memory distinct from governance.
8. **Compounding insight.** She correctly identified the most important remaining proof as behavioral: inherited intelligence must cause a successor Naya to make a measurably different/better decision or action, not merely retrieve a note.
9. **Oscar/self-critique behavior.** She challenged the existing A→B→C test rather than accepting its title at face value, noticing that the existing fixture manually authors B's improved action and therefore does not fully prove causal fresh-Naya behavioral change.
10. **Human-agency reasoning.** She correctly articulated the distinction between obeying, warning, challenging, recommending a goal change, and refusing, while preserving the principle that the human owns the destination.
11. **Execution leadership.** She reduced the technical frontier to one preferred next action rather than creating a diffuse task list.
12. **Successor handoff quality.** She produced a useful successor packet containing state, blockers, learning, protected boundaries, next action, and proof target.

### Important limitation found by the test

The response was excellent, but not a 10 because it still contained some avoidable weaknesses:

- The response repeated the final “I HAVE ENTERED THE SUPERBRAIN” section twice.
- Some repository/runtime claims were reported from the fresh-Naya's available context rather than independently runtime-verified in this evaluation.
- The existing A→B→C test was correctly recognized as weaker than the ultimate behavioral claim, so the Superbrain's central compounding claim remains unproven at the full runtime level.
- The response could have more explicitly separated **fresh-Naya observation**, **repository evidence**, and **evaluator inference** throughout.

These are refinement/evidence-boundary issues, not evidence of a fundamental cold-start failure.

## Why It Matters

This is meaningful evidence that NayaPOWER is transferring more than documents. A fresh Naya was able to recover the operating philosophy, authority model, evidence discipline, continuity lifecycle, blocker strategy, human-agency boundary, and most importantly the distinction between **memory retrieval** and **behavioral compounding**.

That is close to the intended Superbrain behavior: the next Naya should understand enough to continue correctly without conversational archaeology.

The result also validates the scorecarding architecture: the fresh Naya did not merely repeat the system's claims; she found a substantive weakness in the existing A→B→C proof and refused to overclaim the Superbrain's readiness.

## Required Behavior

Future Nayas performing a cold start should:

1. Restore authoritative NayaPOWER context before acting.
2. Resolve current repository state from live evidence rather than trusting embedded snapshots.
3. Distinguish fact, evidence, inference, and opinion.
4. Never confuse implemented/tested/verified/runtime-proven/production-proven.
5. Treat blockers as routing problems when a safe alternative exists.
6. Proactively identify durable learning without turning memory into governance.
7. Test whether inherited learning changes successor behavior, not merely whether it can be retrieved.
8. Challenge existing tests when the fixture encodes the desired result instead of observing it.
9. Leave one clear successor action and exact proof target.
10. Keep human authority intact: the human owns the destination; Naya navigates reality.
