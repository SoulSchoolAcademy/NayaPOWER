# MAXESS AAA PAGE MAP

Reference architecture for the MAXESS Results page.

## Page story

1. Naya Arrival + Score
2. What Your Score Means
3. Your AI Fingerprint
4. Your Personalized Report
5. Your Pattern
6. Your Strength
7. Your Biggest Lever
8. Your Next Move
9. 18 Naya Masters
10. Naya / Playground / Practice
11. Final Personalized Continuation

## Section ownership

Each section owns its own content, visual design, interactions, responsive rules, accessibility behavior, and acceptance criteria.

## 1. Naya Arrival + Score

Purpose: immediate personal recognition and reveal.

Must contain:
- Naya identity;
- personalized greeting when available;
- primary MAXESS score;
- signature Orb;
- mastery stage;
- one primary Listen control;
- concise map framing.

## 2. What Your Score Means

Purpose: translate score into understanding.

Must contain:
- stage interpretation;
- plain-language meaning;
- how to use the score;
- Create → Score → Improve bridge.

## 3. Your AI Fingerprint

Purpose: show the shape of capability.

Must contain the five authoritative dimensions:
Direction, Communication, Evaluation, Iteration, Systems Thinking.

Must contain:
- radar/fingerprint visual;
- axis labels;
- real scores;
- dimension meaning;
- relationship explanation.

## 4. Personalized Report

Purpose: save-worthy synthesis.

Must contain:
- overall result;
- mastery stage;
- strongest signal;
- opportunity;
- plain-language synthesis;
- invitation to continue.

## 5. Pattern

Purpose: show relationships among dimensions.

Must contain:
- supporting relationship;
- leverage relationship;
- plain-language interpretation;
- Naya commentary.

## 6. Strength

Purpose: recognition.

Must contain:
- strongest dimension;
- why it matters;
- how to compound it;
- concrete next use.

## 7. Biggest Lever

Purpose: focus.

Must contain:
- highest-leverage improvement area;
- opportunity framing;
- one real task;
- one improvement;
- repeat loop.

## 8. Next Move

Purpose: action.

Must contain three linked actions:
1. protect strength;
2. build lever;
3. create → score → improve.

## 9. 18 Naya Masters

Purpose: continuation.

Must contain:
- all validated pathways;
- relevance/best-match cues;
- useful descriptions;
- usable links;
- relationship to the person's Strength and Lever.

## 10. Naya / Playground / Practice

Purpose: convert understanding into capability.

Must contain:
- Understand → Decide → Practice;
- preserved working media and controls;
- reliable lower-page rendering;
- mobile-safe interaction.

## 11. Final Personalized Continuation

Purpose: clear next step.

Must contain:
- personalized bridge;
- Naya voice;
- one clear continuation action;
- no generic hard-sell framing.

## Section completion standard

A section is COMPLETE only when:
- content is correct;
- design is intentional;
- real data is used where required;
- interactions work;
- responsive behavior works;
- accessibility is handled;
- performance is acceptable;
- regression is clean;
- human review finds no material defect.

## Global design rule

The page should feel like one premium narrative, not eleven unrelated modules.

Every section must answer one question and create the right emotional/cognitive transition into the next section.
