# SMART NOTE — 2026-08-18 — SECTION 01 VISUAL QA

Status: ACTIVE

## What happened

Section 01 rendered, but the first implementation was visually wrong despite being technically functional.

## What the human review identified

- The experience looked like a sales page instead of a personal results presentation.
- The composition did not use the full widescreen viewport effectively.
- There was far too much hero text.
- The hero should be dramatically simpler: **YOUR AI SCORE**, the real score, and the mastery stage.
- Naya should appear above or immediately around the score/orb as a compact conversational presence using the approved Naya image, not as a large side sales card.
- The Orb was acceptable as a starting point but was not yet extraordinary.
- The required small orbiting ball was missing.
- The small orbiting ball is a mandatory identity component: **MAXESS ORBITAL BEAD**.
- The Orb must continuously and visibly carry the bead around its outside edge.
- The Orb color must remain score-driven and transition smoothly through the approved MAXESS score palette.
- Copy must be tightly controlled. Do not invent generic marketing language.
- The section must be designed from the active MAXESS directives before implementation, not improvised from a partial prompt.

## Deeper lesson

Technically valid code is not evidence of a good visual experience.

The source must be derived from the complete product contract, visual system, section purpose, attention hierarchy, approved assets, and human outcome.

A small implementation is not automatically bad, but a suspiciously small implementation for a visually complex hero should trigger a completeness review. The target is not line count; the target is complete visual, interaction, data, responsive, accessibility, and performance behavior.

## New execution rule

For every major visual module:

**READ THE CONTRACT → MAP REQUIREMENTS → BUILD → RENDER → VISUALLY INSPECT → ASK “WHY ISN'T THIS A 10?” → FIX → RENDER AGAIN → HUMAN REVIEW.**

Never skip the render/inspection loop.

## New response rule

Every Naya Nitro execution must finish with:

- STATUS
- PROVEN
- REMAINING
- RECOMMENDATION
- NEXT PROMPT
- VIEWABLE SOURCE

The human must never be left wondering what to do next.

## Architectural lesson

Build MAXESS Results modularly, section by section, rather than attempting to repair the historical 8,000-line implementation. Preserve proven concepts and assets when useful, but do not preserve weak implementation merely because it exists.

## Current gate

Section 01 remains **NOT FROZEN** until the human visually reviews and approves the rendered experience.

Section 02 must not begin before that approval.
