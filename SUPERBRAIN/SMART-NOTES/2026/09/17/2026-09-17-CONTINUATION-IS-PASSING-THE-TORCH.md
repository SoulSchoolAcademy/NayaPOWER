## SITUATION

Repeated Naya sessions have demonstrated a continuity failure: a session can understand the project, perform repository work, and report a result while still leaving the next Naya without enough executable context to continue safely.

The missing concept is that **continuation is itself a required output of work**.

## LESSON

A Naya session has three major execution responsibilities:

1. **Prepare the next Naya continuously.** The continuation prompt is a complete mission packet that passes the torch.
2. **Execute and record truthfully in GitHub.** Before action, answer the relevant source/authority/state questions; after action, record what happened, what worked, what failed, what was verified, and what remains.
3. **Give Shawn a simple human handoff.** State what was done, why, what was verified, what remains, what Shawn needs to do, and what happens next, with clickable evidence.

These are not three separate chores. They are one continuity system.

## WHY IT MATTERS

The purpose of the Superbrain is to remove the need for Shawn to repeatedly reconstruct context for every new Naya.

Therefore the system must transform:

`MEMORY → CONTEXT → DECISION → ACTION → EVIDENCE → LEARNING → HANDOFF → FUTURE ACTION`

A continuation prompt carries executable context.

An Activity record carries operational evidence.

A Smart Note carries reusable intelligence.

The Shawn report carries human clarity.

Together they prevent the next Naya from starting cold in the wrong sense: without usable state.

## FUTURE NAYA BEHAVIOR

When doing substantive work, a Naya must ask:

- What am I doing?
- Why?
- What is authoritative?
- What is protected?
- What happened before?
- What evidence proves the current state?
- What exactly am I changing?
- How will I verify it?
- What must I record?
- What did I learn?
- What does Shawn need to know?
- What complete mission does the next Naya need?
- What is the one immediate next action?

The Naya must not leave the next Naya with “continue,” “keep working,” or “figure it out.”

## BEHAVIORAL CONSEQUENCE

Future Naya handoffs should be treated as incomplete when they lack the context, evidence, constraints, verification state, or executable next mission required for safe continuation.
