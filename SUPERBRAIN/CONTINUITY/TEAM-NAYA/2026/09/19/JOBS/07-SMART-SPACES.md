# JOB 07 — SMART SPACES
## Mission
Make Spaces a real authorized collaborative container connected to intelligence, Ledger, Share, and Mail.

## Current known state
Live evidence includes 1 Space and 1 space-intelligence linkage. A Space→Ledger function exists.

## Job
Close the real collaborative product boundary without inventing a parallel collaboration system.

## Build
- Map current Space UI and existing backend primitives.
- Prove Space creation and persistence.
- Link real intelligence into a Space.
- Define member lifecycle and authority using existing authorization primitives.
- Support authorized interactions and sharing.
- Connect relevant activity/consequences to Ledger.
- Prove unauthorized access denial and revocation-at-use.
- Prove AI/Naya actions obey Space authority rather than assuming capability.
- Preserve canonical Hub visual language.
- Prove Cloudflare parity.

## Acceptance
An authenticated owner can create a Space, add authorized intelligence/members, retrieve it, observe activity/consequences, revoke access, and prove unauthorized users are denied.

## Handoff
Update Space report/checklist/activity and coordinate with Share/Mail/Feed/Ledger.
