# JOB 09 — INTELLIGENT REPORTS
## Mission
Make Reports a trustworthy synthesis layer over canonical evidence, not a second evidence system.

## Current known state
`v7_intelligence_reports` contains 1 row and `nayanet_report_to_ledger` exists.

## Job
Connect report generation/retrieval to the canonical evidence spine and current Hub UI.

## Build
- Map current Reports UI/route.
- Define period and scope deterministically.
- Define report generation/storage ownership.
- Trace report claims back to source intelligence, cognition, Ledger, or other authorized evidence.
- Distinguish observed/verified facts from derived synthesis.
- Support regeneration without duplicate evidence.
- Enforce privacy and scope.
- Ensure stale reports are identified rather than presented as current.
- Connect appropriate report consequences to Ledger without treating the report itself as independent proof.
- Prove Cloudflare parity.

## Acceptance
A user can request/view a report for a defined scope and period, inspect supporting evidence, reload it, regenerate it safely, and never receive another user's protected material.

## Handoff
Update Reports report/checklist/activity and coordinate with Today/Feed/Ledger.
