# 3. THE MAIN SHOW — SMART FEED

**Smart Feed is the main show of the Intelligent Hub.**

It has three connected projections:

### Personal Intelligence
The user's private Smart Notes and intelligent blocks.

### Collective Intelligence
Wisdom the user intentionally chooses to share with the collective, subject to consent, privacy, identity, and authority rules.

### Activity
The private operational story of what actually happened in the user's Superbrain.

The simplest mental model is:

**Personal Intelligence = the wisdom.**

**Collective Intelligence = wisdom people choose to share.**

**Activity = what the Superbrain actually did.**

These are different views over the same governed intelligence/event ecosystem. They are not separate databases.

# 4. PERSONAL INTELLIGENCE

When useful intelligence is created:

**SMART NOTE → INTELLIGENT BLOCK → PERSONAL INTELLIGENCE**

Personal intelligence is private by default.

It can be:

- understood;
- searched;
- saved/favorited;
- organized into Smart Lists;
- connected to people/projects/spaces;
- revisited;
- applied;
- shared by explicit choice;
- retained as durable intelligence.

The Hub must preserve provenance, status, privacy, relationships, and evidence where applicable.

# 5. COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE

When a human intentionally chooses to share useful intelligence:

**PERSONAL INTELLIGENCE → SMART SHARE → CONSENT/PRIVACY/AUTHORITY → COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE**

The collective should receive the wisdom according to the sharing contract.

The system must not turn private intelligence into public intelligence merely because it exists.

**Privacy law:**

**Private by default • Shared by choice • Collective by consent • Public by decision.**

The collective projection can support discovery and social interaction around shared intelligence, while respecting identity and publication rules.

# 6. ACTIVITY FEED

Activity is the operational truth of what happened.

The intended path is:

**MEANINGFUL EVENT → CANONICAL EVENT → ACTIVITY PROJECTION → HUB ACTIVITY FEED**

The Activity Feed must never be fabricated simply to make the product look alive.

It should answer:

- What happened?
- When?
- What changed?
- Who/what acted?
- Was it verified?
- What evidence exists?
- What remains?
- What happens next?

Activity is different from a Smart Note:

**Smart Note = intelligence worth retaining.**

**Activity = meaningful operational history.**
