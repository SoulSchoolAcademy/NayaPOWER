THE TEN SIDEBAR AREAS\n\n## 4.1 YOUR INTELLIGENCE TODAY\nDefinition: the daily front door to the intelligence that matters most right now.\nQuestion answered: What do I need to know, understand, decide, or do today?\nHow it works: PIS assembles current intelligence from authorized sources, prioritizes meaningful items by relevance/consequence/freshness/verified state, connects current work to recent learning, and exposes evidence and next actions.\nInputs: Activity, Smart Notes, current mission/state, reports, connections, mail, lists, spaces, and approved external intelligence.\nOutput: human orientation, decisions, actions, saved intelligence, and next steps.\nBuild target: a living daily intelligence projection, not a manually maintained dashboard.\n\n## 4.2 SMART FEED\nDefinition: the stream of meaningful intelligence and operational events as they occur.\nQuestion answered: What happened, what changed, what matters, and what needs attention?\nHow it works: governed events enter a common contract, are normalized and visibility-classified, then appear with actor, time, status, provenance, evidence, and available actions.\nInputs: Naya events, human actions, Smart Notes, verified execution, connections, mail, spaces, lists, and system events.\nOutput: situational awareness, traceability, team communication, and navigation to the relevant Hub area.\nBuild target: one governed feed model with multiple projections, not many disconnected feeds.\nActivity is an operational projection of meaningful events. It is not noise and is not the upstream source of truth.\n\n## 4.3 YOUR REPORTS\nDefinition: evidence-backed synthesis over a defined time period, mission, topic, or outcome.\nQuestion answered: What actually happened and what does it mean?\nHow it works: select evidence window → retrieve PIS/CIS intelligence → separate observed fact from inference/unknown → synthesize patterns, changes, risks, decisions, learning, and next actions → preserve evidence links.\nBuild target: reports generated from evidence and intelligence, not invented narrative.\n\n## 4.4 INTELLIGENCE LIBRARY\nDefinition: the durable organized collection of reusable intelligence.\nPurpose: make important knowledge retrievable after the original conversation or event is gone.\nCore objects: Smart Notes, canonical knowledge, learning records, source references, evidence, relationships, and versions.\nHow it works: capture → classify → deduplicate → connect → preserve provenance/authority → retrieve later → feed relevant intelligence back into PIS/CIS.\nBuild target: governed intelligence retrieval, not merely a folder of Markdown.\nLearning law: storage is not learning; retrieval is not application; application is not verified improvement.\n\n## 4.5 SMART SHARE\nDefinition: controlled sharing of intelligence with another human, Naya, team, space, or collective.\nPurpose: cross trust boundaries without losing consent, provenance, ownership, or privacy.\nHow it works: select object → evaluate authority/privacy/consent/audience → create permitted shared projection/reference → retain identity/provenance → support change/revocation through the sharing contract.\nPrivacy law: Private by default • Shared by choice • Collective by consent • Public by decision.\nBuild target: permissioned intelligence transport, not a generic social share button.\n\n## 4.6 SMART LEDGER\nDefinition: evidence and value lineage for consequential intelligence, actions, transactions, and outcomes.\nQuestion answered: What happened, what was authorized, what value/cost/risk was involved, what evidence proves it, and what was learned?\nHow it works: bind authorization to execution; record event identity, evidence, provenance, relevant cost/risk/value, outcome, verification, and learning references.\nBuild target: append-oriented/evidence-preserving lineage where appropriate.\nImportant: this is not a cryptocurrency wallet or accounting replacement. It is the Superbrain's governed action/evidence/value history.\n\n## 4.7 YOUR CONNECTIONS\nDefinition: the governed relationship graph linking people, Nayas, organizations, systems, sources, projects, and intelligence.\nPurpose: answer who/what is connected to this intelligence and why the relationship matters.\nHow it works: identity + relationship objects with permission boundaries; connect people/systems to mail, spaces, projects, intelligence, and shared knowledge; support contextual retrieval.\nBuild target: permission-aware relationship intelligence, not simply an address book.\n\n## 4.8 SMART MAIL\nDefinition: communication intelligence that turns authorized messages into usable context and action.\nPurpose: make communication actionable without losing context.\nHow it works: ingest authorized messages → extract people/topics/commitments/decisions/dates/requests/evidence → connect to Connections, Spaces, Lists, Projects, Smart Notes, Reports → support governed drafting/actions → record meaningful outcomes.\nBuild target: communication intelligence layer, not another inbox skin.\nAuthority rule: reading/drafting is not sending; sending remains governed.\n\n## 4.9 SMART SPACES\nDefinition: governed contextual environments where humans and Nayas work around a mission, topic, project, community, or intelligence boundary.\nPurpose: give intelligence a place/context in which it can be organized and acted upon.\nHow it works: define members, permissions, purpose, context, and intelligence scope; aggregate relevant feed, notes, mail, lists, connections, reports, and actions without creating a duplicate truth store.\nBuild target: context + permission + intelligence projection.\n\n## 4.10 SMART LIST\nDefinition: an intelligence-aware collection of actionable items, commitments, decisions, follow-ups, or objects.\nPurpose: turn intelligence into organized action.\nHow it works: items may originate from mail, Smart Notes, reports, spaces, connections, or direct action; retain source, owner, status, priority, authority, evidence, and next action; changes emit governed events; completed work can produce outcomes and learning.\nBuild target: list engine connected to intelligence and execution, not a disconnected to-do list.\n\n# 5.
