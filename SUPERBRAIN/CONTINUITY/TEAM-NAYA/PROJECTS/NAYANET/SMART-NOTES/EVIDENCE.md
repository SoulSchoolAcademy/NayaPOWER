## Evidence law
- Evidence supports a specific claim; it does not make an unsupported claim true.
- Repository documentation is evidence of documentation, not proof of runtime behavior.
- Activity is a record/projection, not proof by itself.
- Fresh independent observation is required for runtime verification.
- Historical evidence cannot certify a newer current state.
