# 🔱 SMART NOTE — MAXIS FRONT DOOR UX FEEDBACK — 2026-08-27

**Status:** CANONICAL PRODUCT-LEARNING NOTE
**Origin:** Live user inspection of the current MAXIS front door.
**Project:** `SoulSchoolAcademy/Maxis`

## Durable lesson

The MAXIS front door must feel **warm, welcoming, dimensional, human, and alive** rather than like a conventional registration form.

The user specifically wants:

- **Enter your name or username** as the identity label.
- **Enter your name or username** as the placeholder.
- Naya's portrait cropped so the top of her head/face is visible.
- A tasteful Naya chat bubble with a warm welcome such as **Hi Naya, welcome to MAXIS.**
- A more visually compelling **WHAT'S MY AI SCORE?** presentation; the previous treatment felt flat and boring.
- Stronger warmth, glow, depth, and visual pop without clutter.
- Five authentication jewels with clearly visible distinct colors/illumination. The previous differences were technically present but visually too subtle.

## Product insight

The user is evaluating MAXIS by **felt experience**, not merely functional presence. A control can technically have hover/color behavior and still fail if the human can barely perceive it.

Therefore:

> **PERCEIVABLE > TECHNICALLY PRESENT**

This should be treated as an important UX verification principle for MAXIS.

## Design direction

The front-door sequence should feel like:

`NAYA → WELCOME → DISCOVER → IDENTIFY → ENTER`

not:

`FORM → AUTH → SUBMIT`

This is consistent with the existing MAXIS product law that the first screen should feel like entering an experience rather than filling out paperwork.

## AI Note / Future Naya instruction

When implementing visual feedback, do not stop at code-level existence. Inspect the rendered experience and ask whether the intended visual signal is unmistakable at normal viewing distance and on mobile.

If the user says an effect is **barely visible**, treat that as a real defect in the experience even if the CSS technically implements the effect.

Preserve the circular/jewel language, human warmth, accessibility, and low-friction identity flow.
