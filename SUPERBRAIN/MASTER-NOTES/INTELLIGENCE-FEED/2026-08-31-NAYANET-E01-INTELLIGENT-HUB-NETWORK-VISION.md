## What We Are Building

NayaNET is the intelligent network layer around Naya Power: a place where humans and AIs can find one another, communicate, collaborate, learn, and eventually compound intelligence through governed, permissioned sharing.

The launch does **not** lead with the global-network proposition. The front-door activation is practical AI empowerment: Naya Power, the 5-Day Challenge, AI Masterclass, and the broader Naya ecosystem. The deeper network capability is progressively revealed after entry.

## E-Series Construction

The product is built block-by-block / URL-by-URL:

**E01 → E02 → E03 → E04…**

Each approved E becomes a protected baseline. Later work must not silently redesign or regress an approved experience. Separate URLs/experiences may be independently deployed and tested while sharing a common identity/session contract and canonical design system. Use explicit URL transitions unless direct same-page communication is genuinely required.

## E01 — THE DOOR

E01 is intentionally simple and premium. Its job is to get the human into NayaNET with zero unnecessary friction.

Canonical direction:

> **Welcome to NayaNET**  
> **A new kind of intelligent network.**  
> **Your intelligence space to learn, connect and grow.**

The visitor supplies one name and enters. No email, password, last name, legal-name verification, or feature catalogue is required at the door.

The experience must feel elite, cinematic, spatial, restrained, intelligent, and unmistakably Naya—not like a SaaS dashboard or collection of cards.

## First Entry Value

Immediately after entry, reveal that the person received something valuable:

- public Smart Name / alias;
- internal Smart Mail identity;
- Smart Link;
- free NayaNET toolbox;
- path to Naya Power and activation experiences.

The human-facing Smart Name may change when available. The backend uses an immutable internal account identifier so changing a name never destroys history, messages, connections, or Naya Power state.

## Smart Mail

**Smart Mail is an internal NayaNET messaging identity, not an external email mailbox in V1.**

It supports the network inbox, sent messages, compose, replies, connection requests, notifications, and eventually intelligent spaces. Conventional SMTP/IMAP mailbox provisioning is deferred until it is strategically justified.

## Intelligent Hub

The Intelligent Hub is the premium cockpit into the Naya ecosystem and network. It should favor spatial/orbital/circular composition over generic rectangular card grids where that improves comprehension.

Initial destinations:

1. Naya Power
2. 5-Day Challenge
3. AI Masterclass
4. HMC Library
5. Power Seven Days a Week
6. MAXESS
7. Share / Invite

## Network Direction

The long-term network supports:

- Human ↔ Human
- Human ↔ Naya
- Human ↔ AI
- AI ↔ AI
- intelligent spaces
- text communication
- audio communication
- discovery by intent, interest, geography, presence, and availability
- Naya-assisted discovery

The network should become intelligent, not merely searchable.

## Permissioned Superbrain Interoperability

Connected Naya Power instances must be governed by explicit permission and scope. NayaNET must **not** require access to a person's entire private Superbrain.

Possible scopes include permission to:

- connect and communicate;
- participate in shared intelligent spaces;
- share selected wisdom/intelligence updates;
- contribute selected Smart Notes or intelligence reports to shared learning.

Private memory remains private unless deliberately authorized.

## Growth / Ambassador Direction

Each member can have a Smart Link / referral identity. Sharing should preserve attribution so later Naya Power activation or purchase can be attributed according to the Ambassador program rules.

Future external invitation campaigns may support:

**CSV upload → contacts → campaign → outbound invitation → attribution**

External email campaigns remain separate from Smart Mail and require appropriate email infrastructure and compliance.

**Build E01 first.** Do not build the full network or Hub before the entrance is excellent.

Acceptance requires technical, visual, interaction, emotional, brand, coherence, and human-review success. Technical compliance alone is not completion.
