# 🔱 SMART NOTE — LIVING SUN AS MAXIS INTERACTION LANGUAGE

**DATE:** 2026-08-28
**AUTHORITY:** NayaPOWER
**PRODUCT:** MAXIS
**TYPE:** Reusable design/interaction intelligence

## DISCOVERY

MAXIS should not feel like a questionnaire decorated with motion. Its assessment stage can become a recognizable interaction language: a living intelligence field surrounding the question that gives the human a sense of presence, progression, and agency without sacrificing clarity.

## CANONICAL CONCEPT

**NAYA LIVING SUN**

A central question is framed by 24 small orbs. The 24 positions represent a conceptual 24-hour cycle. Twelve colors repeat twice around the field. Only one orb is actively illuminated and travels slowly around the circumference like a comet or moving spotlight.

The visual metaphor is intentionally restrained: the question is the star; the field provides atmosphere and life.

## HUMAN-VALUE PRINCIPLE

The interaction must communicate:

> **This is the question that is on stage right now. Your choice matters.**

Selecting an answer may produce a subtle visual acknowledgement, but effects must never compete with reading, choosing, navigation, accessibility, or performance.

## NAYA ROLE

Naya should be present without interrupting. A compact control deck can combine:

- Naya profile image;
- concise text explaining what the question is really asking;
- optional Play Naya control;
- primary Let's Go action;
- Back/Next navigation.

Audio is enhancement, never a dependency.

## DESIGN LAWS

1. Question first.
2. Answers second.
3. Naya third.
4. Progress fourth.
5. Living field fifth.
6. One active energy orb by default.
7. Motion is slow and purposeful.
8. Reduced motion must preserve function.
9. Mobile is first-class.
10. Beauty must earn its cognitive cost.

## ARCHITECTURE LAW

The Living Sun is a presentation layer. It must consume the existing authoritative assessment configuration and scoring engine. It must not create a competing scorer, question source, identity system, result authority, or governance layer.

## PRODUCT VISION

The larger MAXIS loop is:

`DISCOVER → CHALLENGE → ANSWER → SCORE → UNDERSTAND → LEARN → ACT → IMPROVE → RETAKE → MASTER → SAVE → NEXT MAXIM`

The Living Sun is the interaction language for the **CHALLENGE → ANSWER** portion of that loop and should connect visually into the result/fingerprint experience over time.

## NEXT NAYA

Do not treat the first implementation as final. Run it with real questions, inspect mobile, test accessibility, measure performance, and perform an independent Oscar review. Preserve what works, repair what does not, and promote proven improvements into reusable system knowledge.
