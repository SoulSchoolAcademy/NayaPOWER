## AUTHORITY BOUNDARY

Automation may safely create/update approved non-governance artifacts according to explicit contracts.

Governance-sensitive changes — including constitutional law, high-authority rules, or protected mission decisions — must be surfaced as proposals and routed through the required authority/approval path rather than silently mutated by an automated promoter.
