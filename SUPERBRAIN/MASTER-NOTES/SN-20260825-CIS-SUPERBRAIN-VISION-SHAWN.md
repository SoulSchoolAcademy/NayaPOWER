## What we are building

We are building a personal Naya Power Superbrain: a private AI + human collaboration system that remembers what matters, understands what it means, proves what it did, and compounds learning over time.

The goal is simple:

> **Make tomorrow start smarter than today.**

## How CIS works

Every day Naya takes the meaningful things that happened that day and turns them into a verified Daily Intelligence Report.

The report brings together:

- what Shawn experienced and understood;
- what Naya observed and reasoned;
- what was learned;
- what changed;
- wins and progress;
- mistakes and corrections;
- decisions;
- patterns;
- open loops;
- next actions;
- what the system itself learned about operating better.

Then that day's verified intelligence becomes part of the starting intelligence for tomorrow.

The original notes and evidence are never thrown away. CIS is the layer that distills them into an increasingly useful current understanding.

## The compounding rhythm

**Daily → Weekly → Monthly → Quarterly → Six-Month → Annual → Lifetime**

Every higher-level review should explain the change and growth across the period, not simply repeat the previous reports.

## Why the Naya Note + Shawn Note matters

The two views are deliberately different.

The Naya Note helps future AIs understand the knowledge in a machine-useful way: relationships, reasoning, aliases, evidence, decisions, and continuity.

The Shawn Note lets the human see the same event in plain language: what happened, why it matters, what was learned, and what should be remembered.

Together they create a fuller intelligence record than either perspective alone.

## Why this becomes a Superbrain

It combines:

- the organization of a great library;
- the retrieval power of a search engine;
- the associations of a brain;
- the deterministic structure of a computer;
- the history and evidence of Git;
- and the reasoning and execution of Naya.

The complexity stays inside the system. The human should experience simplicity.

## Privacy

Each person's Naya Power is personal to them. Their Superbrain, memory, CIS, notes, projects, intelligence state, and permissions belong to their personal system. Naya Power provides the architecture; it does not require everyone's private intelligence to be pooled into one central brain.

NayaNET is a future, permission-based network of personal Nayas. It is not required for the private Superbrain to work.

## The promise

Every meaningful interaction can leave the person and the Superbrain more capable than before.

**Capture → Learn → Verify → Remember → Compound → Improve → Repeat.**

That is the engine.

## Decision

This is the product-level purpose we are locking in: **CIS continuously turns lived experience and AI collaboration into verified, personal, compounding intelligence so the next day begins wiser than the previous one.**
