## What we're building toward

Every person can have their own private Naya Power Superbrain. Their brain is theirs. Their memory is theirs. Their CIS is theirs.

Then, only if they choose, they can connect their Naya to NayaNET and learn from other connected Nayas.

## Make it simple

The human experience should feel like a button, not a networking project.

> **Connect to NayaNET**

Then the person chooses what they want:

- Learn from the network.
- Share selected knowledge.
- Collaborate with selected Nayas.
- Disconnect whenever they want.

The complexity stays inside the system.

## Collective learning

The powerful idea is that people don't have to reveal who they are or expose their private conversations for the network to become smarter.

By default, a person could allow Naya to contribute approved lessons to collective learning without revealing the source person. The network then learns:

> "This is what the collective learned."

rather than:

> "Shawn said this."

But that privacy promise has to be technically enforced before we call it production-safe.

## Daily Network Intelligence

Just as each person gets a Daily Intelligence Report, the connected network can eventually get a Daily Network Intelligence Report:

- what NayaNET learned;
- recurring patterns;
- successful approaches;
- corrections;
- emerging opportunities;
- unresolved differences.

Then every connected person can inherit useful collective wisdom while keeping their personal Superbrain private.

## The architecture

**My Naya → permissioned bridge → NayaNET → permissioned bridge → your Naya**

Not one giant brain. A network of sovereign brains that can cooperate.

## Product path

1. Perfect the personal Superbrain.
2. Prove CIS works.
3. Make the system reproducible.
4. Build the mobile-first web app as the human control surface.
5. Build secure federation.
6. Introduce NayaNET.
7. Let collective intelligence compound across the network.

## Decision

The future is a world where every person can have an AI that knows them, learns with them, grows with them, and — if they choose — learns together with everyone else without surrendering their privacy.
