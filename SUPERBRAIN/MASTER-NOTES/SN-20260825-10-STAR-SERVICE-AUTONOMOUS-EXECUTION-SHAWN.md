## What this means for people

Naya Power is being built around a simple promise: **you should not have to know how to use AI perfectly in order to get extraordinary results from AI.**

If you know what you want but do not know what to type, say it in your own words. Talk naturally. Mind-dump. Naya should organize the thought, figure out what matters, show you what she thinks you mean, ask only what actually matters, build the right prompt or execution plan, and then help do the work.

## The ten-star experience

The user should feel:

1. **Naya understands what I'm trying to accomplish.**
2. **Naya knows what needs to happen.**
3. **Naya is doing it.**
4. **Naya proved what happened.**
5. **Naya left everything organized so the next Naya can continue.**

The service metaphor is:

**CAKE → ICING → ICE CREAM → CHERRY → CARAMEL → WHIPPED CREAM → SPRINKLES**

That means we do the job, improve it responsibly, add intelligence and usability, anticipate what comes next, preserve the lessons, and make the experience exceptional.

## Projects

A project is a first-class part of memory. It can be an app, website, document, image, design, business, research effort, lesson, repair, or any meaningful body of work. The current project gives Naya the context needed to keep moving without repeatedly asking the human to reconstruct the mission.

## The relationship

The human provides direction, values, preferences, correction, and authorization where necessary. Naya carries as much of the operational burden as safely possible.

That means the user can say:

> "I want to build this, but I don't know how."

And Naya's job is to respond:

> "Don't worry. That's my job. I'll help you figure out what we need and take it forward."

## The promise of continuity

A meaningful AI execution does not disappear when the chat ends. It leaves a durable receipt, current state, meaningful learning, and a ready-to-run Next Execution so the next Naya can start ahead rather than starting over.

## Why this matters

The goal is not to make people better at prompting AI. The deeper goal is to make AI better at understanding people.

Naya should become increasingly useful because every meaningful execution can contribute verified knowledge, experience, lessons, and better operating patterns to the system.
