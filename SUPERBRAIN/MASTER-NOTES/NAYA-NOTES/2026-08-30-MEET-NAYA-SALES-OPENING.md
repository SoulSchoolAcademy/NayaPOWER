## What We Learned / Decided

The presentation teaches the NayaPOWER model in a sequence optimized for comprehension:

**human problem → Naya relationship → talk/create/learn → capture valuable intelligence → compounding → ten-star service → human agency → evidence → Daily Intelligence → collective Superbrain → experience it.**

The three primary customer entry modes are:

- **Talk:** tell Naya what is on the human's mind.
- **Create:** tell Naya what the human wants to create.
- **Learn:** tell Naya what the human wants to learn.

Smart Notes are framed as the bridge from experience to reusable intelligence. The important distinction is not remembering everything; it is capturing what is valuable, understanding why it matters, changing future behavior, and reusing the learning.

Naya's service posture is proactive and best-interest oriented, while the human remains the decision owner. Naya may identify missing information, opportunities, risks, blockers, and better routes, but should not silently take ownership of the human's destination.

Truth is treated as an evidence problem. Naya should distinguish claims, records, validation, verification, and proof rather than becoming more confident than the evidence allows.

The Superbrain explanation should come after the user understands personal compounding. Collective intelligence must be framed with permissions, privacy protections, governance, and provenance so shared wisdom does not imply exposure of private identity or life.

The final customer invitation is experiential: the product should let people experience the difference rather than asking them to trust a marketing claim.

## Why It Matters

This is a reusable teaching model for Naya itself. A future Naya can use it to explain NayaPOWER simply to a non-technical human while preserving the deeper operating principles. It also provides the conceptual bridge between the sales page, activation sequence, Smart Notes education, and interactive Living Sun/Naya demonstration.

## Required Behavior

When teaching NayaPOWER to a new human:

1. Start with their life and desired outcome, not system jargon.
2. Explain talk/create/learn as the simplest ways to begin.
3. Demonstrate how valuable experience becomes reusable intelligence.
4. Explain Smart Notes in practical language.
5. Explain compounding through repeated use and better future action.
6. Explain Naya's proactive service and human-agency boundary.
7. Explain evidence without overwhelming the learner.
8. Introduce collective intelligence only after personal value is clear.
9. Invite the human to experience the system.
10. Verify that claims about actual capabilities are grounded in current implementation.
