# Naya Master Note — Superbrain 10/10 Execution Protocol

**Event:** SN-20260825-SUPERBRAIN-10-OF-10-EXECUTION-PROTOCOL
**Perspective:** Naya / AI operating perspective
**Status:** CANONICAL EXECUTION DIRECTIVE

## The decision

The Superbrain is the seed. NayaNET federation comes after the personal Superbrain is genuinely operational at maximum practical quality.

The objective is not to make the repository impressive. The objective is to make a fresh AI session capable of entering the system, restoring state, finding the right evidence, executing correctly, verifying the result, producing a human-readable receipt, and improving the system without needing prior conversation history.

## 10/10 definition

A 10/10 Superbrain must be:

- deterministic enough to trust;
- chronological without being trapped by chronology;
- semantic and relational rather than flat;
- human-readable and AI-readable;
- evidence-backed;
- duplicate-resistant;
- conflict-aware;
- retrieval-measurable;
- automatically compounding through CIS;
- continuously restorable;
- self-measuring and self-improving;
- private by default;
- simple enough that a new AI can operate it correctly.

## Execution order

1. Make the authoritative CI gate genuinely GREEN.
2. Freeze schema and make every invariant executable.
3. Make Note Event creation idempotent and duplicate-aware.
4. Make Naya + Shawn representations mandatory for meaningful events.
5. Make receipts durable, human-readable, and stateful.
6. Complete outbox semantics for external delivery.
7. Build contradiction and supersession detection.
8. Build the relationship graph and rebuild it deterministically.
9. Upgrade retrieval to a measured hybrid sparse/vector/semantic architecture; keep a deterministic fallback.
10. Create a gold retrieval benchmark with precision@k, recall@k, ranking quality, latency, and explainability.
11. Automate Daily CIS from verified events only.
12. Produce the next-day Intelligence State automatically.
13. Automate Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Six-Month, Annual, and Lifetime synthesis from period deltas.
14. Add Superbrain health metrics and trend reporting.
15. Add cold-start acceptance tests proving a new AI can restore and operate the system.
16. Add human-delivery verification tests so readable links are part of completion.
17. Detect candidate Master Notes from repeated system-level lessons.
18. Measure whether each optimization actually improves retrieval, reliability, latency, or human friction.
19. Preserve every improvement as a versioned operating decision.
20. Re-run the complete end-to-end acceptance test after every architectural milestone.

## Operating rule

Never declare 10/10 because the architecture sounds complete. Declare 10/10 only when the acceptance gates are green and the measured capabilities meet their thresholds.

**READ → RESTORE → UNDERSTAND → EXECUTE → VERIFY → RECEIPT → RETRIEVE → COMPOUND → MEASURE → IMPROVE → PRESERVE → REPEAT.**
