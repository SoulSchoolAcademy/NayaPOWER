## Core insight

The system's meta-optimization principle is formally established as:

# SYNERGIZE → OPTIMIZE → MAXIMIZE → EQUALIZE → VERIFY → LEARN → SYNERGIZE AGAIN

### Synergize
Make the parts reinforce one another rather than operate as disconnected documents.

### Optimize
Remove contradiction, duplication, ambiguity, unnecessary complexity, and friction.

### Maximize
Increase useful capability, quality, leverage, reliability, and human value.

### Equalize
Balance competing objectives such as speed/correctness, autonomy/authority, simplicity/capability, innovation/preservation, and AI initiative/human control.

### Verify
Prove the actual result with evidence.

### Learn
Turn failures, discoveries, successful patterns, and system observations into durable knowledge and safeguards.

Then synergize again using the new knowledge.
