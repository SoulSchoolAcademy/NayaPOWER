## What mattered

Naya Power is not fundamentally a GitHub system, a memory database, or a prompt library. It is a **Superbrain operating system for humans and AI**: a governed intelligence layer that helps an AI carry a human mission through continuity, planning, execution, verification, learning, preservation, restoration, and improvement.

The system must be useful to people using AI directly and to people operating AI agents, coding agents, autonomous agents, enterprise agents, and custom AI systems.

## What was learned

The universal opportunity is broader than adding memory to one AI product. AI systems increasingly need an operating layer around the model: mission continuity, operating rules, priority, execution discipline, evidence, verification, learning, provenance, recovery, and compounding intelligence.

Therefore Naya Power should remain **model independent** and **storage agnostic**. GitHub is a particularly strong control substrate for software-oriented installations, but it is not Naya Power itself. Google Drive, databases, object storage, enterprise systems, and other authorized sources can participate through governed adapters.

The key architectural principle is:

> **Storage is not authority. Authority is determined by the class of information and its canonical contract.**

Naya Power should expose stable contracts for universal agents, control substrates, and storage/knowledge adapters rather than becoming coupled to any single vendor.

## Strategic definition

**Naya Power:** Superbrain operating system for humans and AI.

**Naya:** An individual AI intelligence node operating under Naya Power.

**Universal Agent Interface:** Vendor-neutral boundary through which compatible models and agents use Naya Power.

**Control Substrate:** Trustworthy persistent substrate preserving the governed control state of a Naya Power installation.

**CCT:** Collective Chain Technology preserving provenance, lineage, authorization, and traceability.

**CIS:** Compounding Intelligence System converting experience and verified learning into progressively more useful future intelligence and behavior.

**NayaNET:** Permissioned network of independent Naya Power Superbrains that can exchange authorized, verified intelligence without becoming a raw shared-memory store.

## Human mission

Make it dramatically easier for an ordinary human with a meaningful vision to accomplish extraordinary things with AI without becoming an AI project manager.

## Deeper mission

Increase human capability by making AI more continuous, reliable, verifiable, recoverable, teachable, and capable of compounding useful intelligence over time.

## Who benefits

Primary audiences include serious AI users, AI-agent builders, developers, entrepreneurs, AI product teams, enterprises, knowledge workers, researchers, creators, educators, and organizations operating multiple agents or AI systems.

The strongest early customer signal is not demographics; it is **AI intensity + meaningful goals + frustration with fragmented AI workflows**.

## Why it matters

The customer is not buying notes or architecture. The customer is buying reduced repeated explanation, better continuity, better decisions, fewer repeated mistakes, more reliable execution, stronger verification, preserved knowledge, more capable agents, less project management, and compounding value from prior work.

## Decision

Lock the universal Superbrain OS positioning and architecture as a strategic foundation. Do not expand into many storage adapters prematurely. First stabilize and prove the Universal Agent Interface, Control Substrate Contract, and authority-by-information-class model. Then build adapters against those contracts.

Defer the 20 customer activation documents until the kernel and customer activation contract are sufficiently stable to prevent unnecessary documentation churn.
