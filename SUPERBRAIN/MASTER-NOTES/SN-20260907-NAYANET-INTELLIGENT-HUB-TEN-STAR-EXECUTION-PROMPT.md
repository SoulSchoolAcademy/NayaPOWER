## MASTER PRODUCT LAWS

### 1. INTELLIGENCE OVER STORAGE

A Smart Note is not merely stored text. It is an intelligence event that can be understood, remembered, connected, retrieved, improved, and shared.

### 2. ONE INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM, THREE VIEWS

- **ACTIVITY** = what is happening now.
- **PERSONAL INTELLIGENCE** = what I have learned and retained.
- **COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE** = what people have intentionally contributed for others.

These are views over one intelligence system, not three disconnected products.

### 3. PROGRESSIVE DISCLOSURE

The first glance must be effortless. The deeper intelligence must be available without making the default experience dense.

### 4. AI ORGANIZES; HUMANS DO NOT DO ADMIN

The system should infer categories, relationships, themes, importance, recency, and useful connections wherever safely possible. Do not turn the user into a librarian.

### 5. RECOVERY VALUE IS THE NORTH STAR

The product succeeds when a user can recover a valuable thought months later, understand why it mattered, and apply it to the current moment.

### 6. USEFULNESS OVER VANITY

Collective intelligence should reward usefulness, clarity, trust, evidence, and practical value—not raw popularity.

### 7. PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

**Private by default. Shared by choice. Collective by consent. Public by decision.**

This is the canonical NayaNET trust statement and must be treated as product language, not decorative copy.

### 8. MEMORY MUST BECOME ACTIVE

The eventual system should not wait for the user to search. Naya should proactively surface relevant past intelligence when it can materially help.

### 9. RELATIONSHIPS OVER FOLDERS

Intelligence should connect through semantic relationships such as:

- Related to
- Builds on
- Contradicts
- Corrects
- Proves
- Inspired by
- Leads to
- Replaced by
- Applies to

### 10. EVERY IMPORTANT OUTPUT CAN BE HEARD

Introduce a premium **PLAY NAYA** control wherever an intelligence block or intelligence report is presented.

PLAY NAYA is not an afterthought. It is a first-class interaction and must feel native to NayaNET.

When activated, Naya should read the intelligence naturally and sequentially, including the perspectives available in the block:

**IN A NUTSHELL → HUMAN NOTE → CHILD NOTE → GRANDMA NOTE → NAYA NOTE → MACHINE NOTE → WHAT WE LEARNED · WHAT IT MEANS.**

The control must:

- look beautiful and unmistakably Naya;
- have a clear play/playing/pause state;
- provide visible feedback;
- allow replay;
- avoid overlapping or duplicate playback;
- be keyboard accessible;
- work on mobile and desktop;
- degrade gracefully if speech synthesis is unavailable;
- use the text actually displayed in the block, not hard-coded hidden content;
- be architected so it can later route through the Naya Voice API / cloned Naya voice without redesigning the interaction.

Label it **PLAY NAYA** or **READ NAYA** according to the strongest visual treatment, but the interaction should communicate “Naya will read this to you.”

## LOCKED INTELLIGENT FEED INTERACTION CONTRACT

This section is a **locked design contract** for the Personal Intelligence and Collective Intelligence feeds. Future Smart Notes and future feed iterations must inherit this interaction language unless a later explicit product decision supersedes it.

### A. PERSONAL AND COLLECTIVE MUST SHARE ONE INTERACTION LANGUAGE

Personal Intelligence and Collective Intelligence are different views of the same intelligence system. They must not look or behave like separate products.

The Collective Intelligence feed must **mirror the Personal Intelligence feed's full interaction architecture**, not use a shortened or stripped-down control set.

The distinction between Personal and Collective is primarily **intelligence ownership, provenance, and permission state**—not a reduced interaction vocabulary.

### B. INTELLIGENT BLOCK CONTROL PLACEMENT

The default block interaction architecture is:

- **TOP-RIGHT → ⭐ FAVORITE / STAR**
- **BOTTOM-LEFT → SAVE / KEEP**
- **BOTTOM-RIGHT → ＋ SHARE**
- **LOVE / RESONATE → independent control**
- **FIVE-STAR / USEFULNESS RATING → independent rating interaction where enabled**
- **PLAY NAYA → first-class playback control in the established block hierarchy**

Controls must remain visually compact and must not become a toolbar explosion.

### C. LOVE AND SHARE MUST NEVER BE COMBINED

**LOVE / RESONATE and SHARE are separate actions.**

Do not combine Love and Share into one button.

Love communicates appreciation/resonance/value.
Share initiates a sharing workflow.

These actions have different intent and must have different controls and state feedback.

### D. THE ＋ BUTTON IS THE SHARE GATEWAY

The **bottom-right ＋ button** is the primary sharing gateway and should mirror the proven mobile sharing interaction model.

Activating ＋ should expose the available sharing destinations/actions, which may include:

- social sharing;
- copy/share link;
- Smart Share;
- other legitimate sharing destinations available to the product.

Do not create a second competing share control merely to provide a different label.

### E. REMOVE UNEXPLAINED “SMART LINK” UI CONTROLS

Do not surface a **Smart Link** button inside an Intelligent Block unless it has a clearly defined, user-understandable purpose and is part of the approved interaction contract.

A control must never exist simply because the label sounds intelligent.

If link sharing is required, it belongs inside the **＋ SHARE** gateway unless a future product decision explicitly establishes a separate purpose.

### F. PRESERVE THE FULL VALUE INTERACTION SET

Do not shorten the interaction list simply to make Collective Intelligence look simpler.

The product should preserve the meaningful actions required to:

- appreciate/resonate;
- rate usefulness/value;
- save/keep;
- favorite;
- comment where appropriate;
- share;
- play/hear;
- connect or ask Naya where those actions are part of the approved view.

Progressive disclosure should reduce visual density without removing important capability.

### G. ACTION FEEDBACK

Every interactive state must be:

- obvious;
- immediate;
- reversible where appropriate;
- persistent within the current experience;
- accessible without relying on color alone.

The user should always understand what happened after tapping an action.
