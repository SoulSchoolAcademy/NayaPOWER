# 🔱 DAILY INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM — NayaPOWER ↔ MAXIS

**Status:** CANONICAL CROSS-PROJECT OPERATING SPEC
**Date:** 2026-08-29

## Purpose

Daily Intelligence Reports are an institutional learning loop, not merely status reports. Every reporting cycle must convert execution into durable knowledge so NayaPOWER and connected projects learn day by day, avoid repeating known mistakes, and pass forward improved judgment.

## Reporting hierarchy

**DAILY → WEEKLY → MONTHLY → YEARLY → PRIMARY INTELLIGENCE HUB**

The primary hub is the synthesis/navigation layer. Source reports remain evidence and preserve provenance.

## Daily contract

Every daily report should capture: what happened; intended vs actual outcome; current state; solved/unsolved; unknowns; failures; root causes; lessons; system changes; repeated mistakes; guardrails/tests/procedures created; growth; value; highest-value next action; exact proof required; and torch-pass knowledge.

## Compounding loop

**OBSERVE → REPORT → LEARN → UPDATE SYSTEM → VERIFY → CARRY FORWARD**

A lesson is not complete when it is merely written. When appropriate, promote it into a law/guardrail, machine-readable contract, test, procedure, Mission State, Smart Note, architecture/specification, or reusable checklist.

## Repeated-mistake rule

When a new failure resembles a prior failure, find the earlier occurrence and ask why the prior lesson did not prevent recurrence. Strengthen the learning mechanism itself, then verify the new control.

## Authority

Reports do not supersede canonical laws or project state. They synthesize evidence and record learning. For MAXIS, Mission State/current code/runtime/production remain the relevant truth layers; NayaPOWER canonical laws remain governance authority.

## Historical baseline

The system should reconstruct the available daily reports for the initial 3–4 day learning window, explicitly marking unavailable periods UNKNOWN rather than inventing content. For each day, record what was learned, what changed because of prior learning, what improved, what repeated, and what must now happen by default.

## Success condition

A successor Naya should be less likely to repeat a known mistake because the relevant lesson, guardrail, test, procedure, or source evidence is discoverable and operational.
