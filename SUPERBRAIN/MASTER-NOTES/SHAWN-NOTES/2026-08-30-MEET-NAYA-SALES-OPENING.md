## What We Learned / Decided

The presentation is more than marketing copy. It is a strong customer mental model for NayaPOWER.

Its most valuable sequence is:

1. Start with the human experience rather than the technology.
2. Introduce Naya as an intelligence partner designed to work with the human.
3. Give the customer three simple doors: **talk about what is on your mind, create what you want to create, or learn what you want to learn.**
4. Explain the problem of valuable experience disappearing.
5. Introduce Smart Notes as the mechanism for turning valuable experience into reusable intelligence.
6. Explain compounding as experience → learning → capture → reuse → improvement.
7. Establish ten-star service: Naya should understand the real objective, anticipate needs, solve problems, warn, challenge when evidence requires it, and help the human move.
8. Preserve human agency: **the human owns the destination; Naya helps navigate reality.**
9. Teach evidence discipline: claims, records, validation, verification, and proof are different states.
10. Use Daily Intelligence Briefing to turn a day into reusable intelligence.
11. Introduce the Superbrain only after the personal value is understood.
12. Explain that collective intelligence can share useful wisdom with permissions, privacy protections, and governance rather than exposing private lives.
13. End with experience over claims: the customer should experience NayaPOWER rather than merely believe a description of it.

The presentation is therefore approved as a **narrative foundation / opening presentation direction** for the NayaPOWER sales page. It should be preserved as source material, not blindly copied into a final production page without implementation and evidence review.

## Why It Matters

This gives the sales experience a coherent human-first story and aligns sales, education, activation, and product demonstration around the same mental model. It also provides a concise explanation that a non-technical person can understand while remaining faithful to the deeper Superbrain thesis.

## Required Behavior

Future NayaPOWER sales and activation work should start from the human transformation and progressively reveal capability, meaning, benefit, evidence, and next action. Do not lead with architecture jargon. Do not promise capabilities that are not actually implemented or verified. Where the Living Sun or interactive Naya is demonstrated, the experience must be grounded in real available capability.
