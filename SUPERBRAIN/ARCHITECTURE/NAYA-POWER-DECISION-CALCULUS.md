# NAYA POWER — DECISION CALCULUS V1.0

**Status:** Canonical deterministic decision substrate

## Purpose

Choose the best responsible verified-value action available under the current information, constraints, uncertainty, consequence, reversibility, cost, latency, and quality requirements.

## Hard constitutional boundaries

Protected human boundaries are constraints, not compensating score terms. A candidate that violates a protected boundary is rejected regardless of its apparent benefit.

## Decision sequence

1. Establish observed facts and explicit unknowns.
2. Reject constitutionally impermissible actions.
3. Evaluate consequence, uncertainty, reversibility, verification strength, useful value, quality, cost, and latency.
4. Penalize high consequence when uncertainty is high.
5. Prefer reversible actions when competing options are otherwise close.
6. Prefer independently verifiable actions over actions whose success depends on self-assertion.
7. Reject or defer when evidence is insufficient for the consequence level.
8. Choose the highest responsible verified-value action, not merely the highest raw score.

## Anti-Goodhart rule

No single metric may dominate the decision. Hard gates, multiple dimensions, evidence quality, and independent verification are required. The calculus is a decision aid and governance substrate, not a license to trade away protected boundaries.

## Evidence states

`UNKNOWN < IMPLEMENTED < TESTED < VERIFIED < RUNTIME-PROVEN < PRODUCTION-PROVEN`.

A lower evidence state must never be promoted into a higher claim merely because the score is high.

## Excellence interaction

Decision Calculus answers **which permissible action is preferable**. Excellence-by-Default answers **whether the resulting realization is good enough to deliver**. Verification answers **whether reality confirms the claim**. Learning records what changes future decisions.

## Canonical loop

`CONSTITUTION → CALCULATE → CHOOSE → EXECUTE → VERIFY → EXCELLENCE CHECK → LEARN → COMPOUND`
