# Intelligence Distillation & Comprehension Principle

## Purpose

NayaNET is an intelligence distillation and comprehension system. Its job is not merely to generate information; it is to help humans understand, remember, connect, and use intelligence with the least unnecessary cognitive effort while preserving access to the depth and evidence underneath.

## System model

- **NayaNET** — the human interface to intelligence.
- **The Feed** — the human-facing intelligence surface.
- **The Superbrain** — persistent intelligence infrastructure and memory.
- **Naya** — reasoning, synthesis, organization, and execution layer.
- **The human** — meaning, judgment, lived experience, values, and action.
- **The Intelligent Block** — the primary unit of human comprehension.

## Core loop

**RAW INTELLIGENCE → DISTILLATION → HUMAN COMPREHENSION → CONNECTION → ACTION → LEARNING → NEW INTELLIGENCE**

The objective is not information compression that destroys nuance. It is **meaning-preserving intelligence compression**: expose the essential shape quickly, then allow progressive disclosure into evidence, reasoning, sources, counterarguments, history, and full analysis.

## Intelligent Block standard

A person should be able to understand the essential intelligence of a primary block without reading the entire block. At minimum, the block should make it possible to perceive:

1. What is this?
2. What is the essential point?
3. Why does it matter?
4. What is known, uncertain, disputed, or unknown?
5. What evidence supports it?
6. What does it connect to?
7. What can the human do with it?
8. Where can the human go deeper?

## Interface law

Visual design is part of intelligence communication. Typography, color, spacing, elevation, position, motion, hierarchy, and interaction should communicate semantic meaning rather than merely decorate the interface.

- Important information should look important.
- Uncertainty should look uncertain.
- Evidence should be visually distinguishable from interpretation.
- Change should be visible.
- Relationships should be discoverable.
- Required actions should be obvious.
- Depth should be available without forcing depth on the user.

The interface should **communicate intelligence**, not merely contain it.

## Quality test

The relevant question is not simply whether an interface is beautiful, technically sophisticated, or information-dense. The higher-order test is:

> **How much accurate, meaningful intelligence can a human understand with the least unnecessary cognitive effort?**

This principle does not replace the evidence, provenance, safety, or verification laws of NayaPOWER. It operates above them as a human-comprehension objective.

## Product north star

> **NayaNET exists to make intelligence easier for humans to understand, remember, connect, and use. The system may contain extraordinary complexity underneath. The human should experience extraordinary clarity above.**

## Design consequence

Do not optimize for more output merely because more output is possible. Optimize for better understanding. Preserve the depth underneath while making the essential intelligence immediately legible.
