# 3. THE THREE PRIMARY INTELLIGENCE STREAMS

## A. PERSONAL INTELLIGENCE — PRIVATE

A human's Smart Notes and intelligence belong to their personal intelligence space.

When Naya creates a Smart Note:

`SMART NOTE → PERSONAL INTELLIGENCE`

The note becomes a durable intelligent block that can be searched, organized, connected, revisited, and compounded.

**Privacy rule:** personal intelligence is private by default.

---

## B. COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE — SHARED BY CHOICE

When a human intentionally chooses to share useful intelligence:

`PERSONAL SMART NOTE → HUMAN CHOOSES SHARE → COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE`

The collective receives the shared intelligence according to the publication/privacy rules.

The collective is not a dumping ground for private data.

**Privacy rule:**

> Private by default • Shared by choice • Collective by consent • Public by decision.

`CANONICAL EVENT → ACTIVITY PROJECTION → INTELLIGENT HUB ACTIVITY`

The Activity Feed is a projection of what actually happened.

`SMART SPACE → SMART MAIL → CONNECTED PEOPLE`
