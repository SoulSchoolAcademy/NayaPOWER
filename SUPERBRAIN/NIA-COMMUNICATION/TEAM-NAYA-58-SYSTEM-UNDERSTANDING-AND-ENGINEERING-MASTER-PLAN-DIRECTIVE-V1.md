## THE HUMAN INTENT — SIMPLIFIED

Do **not** treat the 58 documents as 58 separate products or 58 features.

Read every canonical NayaPOWER 01–58 document from the repository. Understand each one on its own. Then determine how the 58 ideas fit together as one system.

For every area, explain:

1. **WHAT IT IS** — its precise meaning.
2. **WHAT IT DOES** — its functional job.
3. **HOW IT WORKS** — its operating mechanism, based on repository evidence.
4. **WHY IT MATTERS** — the human/system value it creates.
5. **BOUNDARY** — what belongs inside it and what does not.
6. **INPUTS** — what it needs.
7. **OUTPUTS** — what it produces.
8. **CONNECTIONS** — which other 01–58 areas it directly depends on or enables.
9. **IMPLEMENTATION** — actual repository/runtime evidence, not imagined architecture.
10. **STATE** — VERIFIED / IMPLEMENTED / TESTED / INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED / LIVE VERIFIED / UNKNOWN / STALE / SUPERSEDED / BLOCKED.
11. **GAP** — what is missing for the area to become genuinely successful.
12. **NEXT ACTION** — one highest-value executable action.

## CRITICAL PERMISSION — GROUP THE 58

**You are explicitly authorized to group, combine, cluster, or layer the 58 areas when that produces a clearer and more effective engineering architecture.**

Grouping does **not** mean deleting the canonical 01–58 identities.

The 58 remain the canonical conceptual map. Groups become the engineering implementation map.

Some areas may be:

- foundational principles;
- governance/control mechanisms;
- intelligence/memory systems;
- runtime/execution systems;
- human-facing product surfaces;
- data/event contracts;
- privacy/identity systems;
- continuity/trust systems;
- infrastructure/adapters;
- or combinations of the above.

Some areas may be implemented together. Some may be represented primarily as contracts or laws rather than standalone software. Some may become one shared runtime capability. Do not force every number into a separate codebase component.

## DO NOT INVENT

Repository truth beats memory.

Current authoritative artifacts beat historical prose.

A document describing a capability does not prove the capability exists.

A filename does not create authority.

An architecture proposal is not a runtime fact.

If implementation cannot be found or verified, mark the area UNKNOWN or another appropriate state.

Do not manufacture completion to make the map look clean.
