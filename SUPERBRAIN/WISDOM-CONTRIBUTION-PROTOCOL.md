# NayaPOWER — Wisdom Contribution Protocol v1

**STATUS:** CANONICAL / ACTIVE DESIGN CONTRACT
**EFFECTIVE:** 2026-08-30

## Purpose

Define how a sovereign Superbrain voluntarily contributes reusable wisdom to the Naya Collective without synchronizing or exposing the Superbrain itself.

## Foundational law

> **The Collective never needs your life to become intelligent. It needs only the wisdom you deliberately choose to contribute.**

## Protocol

```text
AUTHORIZE SCOPE
      ↓
DISCOVER CANDIDATE WISDOM
      ↓
EXTRACT REUSABLE VALUE
      ↓
GENERALIZE / MINIMIZE
      ↓
HUMAN REVIEW
      ↓
PRIVACY + QUALITY GATE
      ↓
CREATE COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE EVENT
      ↓
PUBLISH / CHAIN
      ↓
FEED BACK RELEVANT COLLECTIVE LEARNING
```

## Consent

Participation is explicit opt-in. The contributor chooses the scope: a specific insight, today's breakthroughs, a week's lessons, a solved problem, a mistake/lesson, or an authorized scope from which Naya may identify the strongest reusable contribution.

The contributor MUST be shown what the system intends to share before publication and MUST be able to edit, reject, or keep it private.

## Extraction law

The contribution is not a copy of a Note Event, repository, conversation, or person. It is a newly constructed intelligence object containing the reusable lesson/pattern/solution/warning needed by the Collective.

Raw source material SHOULD be minimized and MUST NOT be retained in the collective object when it is unnecessary to preserve the wisdom.

## Privacy law

The collective event MUST omit direct identity and private-source details unless a separate, explicit policy requires a non-public provenance reference. The public/collective object MUST NOT expose the contributor's name, email, repository URL, personal history, or unrelated project details.

De-identification is not a license to publish sensitive content. Privacy, safety, confidentiality, and legal constraints remain higher-order gates.

## Quality gate

A contribution should be evaluated for:

- reusable value
- clarity
- evidence/provenance
- novelty or meaningful reinforcement
- applicability
- confidence
- privacy risk
- contradiction/supersession
- duplication

Low-confidence or insufficiently supported material MUST remain clearly marked rather than becoming asserted collective truth.

## Collective feedback

Participation is a two-way intelligence loop:

`LEARN → CONTRIBUTE → COLLECTIVE LEARNS → RECEIVE RELEVANT WISDOM → LEARN MORE`

The contributor may receive validated or relevant collective insights without granting the Collective access to their private Superbrain.

## Revocation and correction

Users can stop future contributions at any time. Published events are corrected through explicit supersession/correction lineage, not silent rewriting.

## Non-goals

This protocol does not synchronize repositories, merge personal memories, create a shared identity graph, or grant the Hub unrestricted access to a connected Superbrain.
