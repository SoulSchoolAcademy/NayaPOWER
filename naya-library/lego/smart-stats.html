Data Viz — Do / Don't

The laws of elite data presentation. Every rule paired: what to do, what never to do.

✓DO animate on load

Bars rise, lines draw themselves, donuts sweep in. Motion tells the viewer the data is alive and current.

✕NEVER show static dead charts

A chart that just sits there feels like a screenshot. If nothing moves, the viewer assumes nothing is live.

✓DO give every series one spectrum job

Magenta → purple → blue → green → gold, in order. Color means identity — the viewer learns what each color is.

✕NEVER rainbow without meaning

Random colors are decoration. If two series share a hue, the viewer can't tell them apart — that's a defect.

✓DO make every point hoverable

Hover reveals the exact value. The overview is calm; the detail is one hover away. Progressive disclosure.

✕NEVER hide the numbers

A chart without accessible values is a picture, not intelligence. Every visual must resolve to a number.

✓DO label truth on every stat

VERIFIED glows green. DEMONSTRATION glows gold. A number without a truth state is a claim, not a fact.

✕NEVER present estimates as facts

Simulated, projected, or estimated data must say so. A beautiful lie is still a lie — and it kills trust.

✓DO use tabular numerals

font-variant-numeric:tabular-nums — digits align in columns and don't jitter when live values update.

✕NEVER let live numbers jump layout

If updating a ticker shifts the whole row, the design is broken. Fixed-width numerals keep the page still.
