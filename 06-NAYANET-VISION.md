# NayaNET — Vision

## Vision
A CONNECTED HUMAN INTELLIGENCE NETWORK WHERE EVERY PERSON CAN DEVELOP A PERSONAL SUPERBRAIN AND, BY CHOICE AND CONSENT, CONTRIBUTE TO A LARGER COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE WITHOUT SURRENDERING OWNERSHIP, PRIVACY, OR AGENCY.

## The problem
Human intelligence is fragmented across people, chats, applications, documents, organizations, and temporary sessions.

NayaNET is designed to connect those islands while preserving their boundaries.

## Progression
PERSON → PERSONAL SUPERBRAIN → AUTHORIZED CONNECTION → SHARED INTELLIGENCE → COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE → GREATER HUMAN CAPABILITY.

## Required boundaries
OWNERSHIP • CONSENT • PRIVACY • PROVENANCE • SCOPE • REVOCATION • AUDITABILITY • HUMAN AGENCY.

## Vision test
Connection is progress only when it makes intelligence more useful without making humans less sovereign over their own intelligence.
