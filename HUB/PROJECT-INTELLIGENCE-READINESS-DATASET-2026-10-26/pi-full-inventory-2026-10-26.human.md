## THE ESSENCE — what PI holds and why

Project Intelligence is the team's one living record and the director's one place of accountability. **Everything that matters lives there: every job, every page, every gate, every cure — with its state, its owner, its next action, and its proof.** If it's not written there, it's not done. The record feeds the face at /pi (his cold-open test: the whole project, the percent done, what moved, what's stuck and on whom — in seconds, unaided), it fires the notifications that keep every seat aware, and it keeps the priority order so no seat ever guesses her next task. This inventory is the scope contract: **78 workstreams across the 12 areas, each with its why and its home — 40 filed with their keys in evidence, 30 whose keys the sources don't name, 8 riding a named parent item — plus 13 gaps where work that should be filed is not filed yet.**

**How to read an entry:** name · WHY (the value it produces) · Shawn's order position where he gave one · **PI status** — FILED with its item key, *key unconfirmed* (it likely exists; no key in the sources read), or **NOT FILED — a gap**, with a proposed key in the record's naming style.

## WHAT "COMPLETE" LOOKS LIKE — the consensus test

PI is complete when all of this is true at once, cold-proven, not claimed:

1. **The face passes the director's cold-open test** — the whole project, the percent done, what moved, what's stuck and on whom, in seconds, unaided — against all **42 checks** (14 build · 18 content · 10 acceptance), re-scored cold per round.
2. **Every number is computed** — item rows, not counts; deduped by item, never by row; per-area and per-part percents from real area assignments, with unmeasured parts dark *with the reason shown*.
3. **The record is the whole scope** — this inventory's ~80 workstreams all filed, owned, and moving; the 12 gaps filed or consciously closed; no work living only in chat, a lane's notes, or a director's memory.
4. **The pulse is live and proven** — a test entry's notification arrives (feed + mail, both voices, deep link landing on the item), the two-hour stall escalation fires on its own, and a quiet feed on a working day reads as the alarm it is.
5. **The ledger is live** — every done_proven writes its row in the same pass; the 2026-10-22 freeze cannot recur silently.
6. **The order keeps itself** — priority order maintained (by the system when the auto-prioritizer lands; by hand, honestly, until then), completed work dropping off the list, the weakest point always named with its evidence.
7. **The gates stay the director's** — money, credentials, production data, irreversible acts, ratification: filed, visible, and never widened by habit. Everything below them is decided by scorecard and executed without asking.

When those seven hold, Project Intelligence is what the director described: *the proof that the team understands, has got it, and is living by the code* — and the rest of the Ordered Program runs inside it, area by area, score by score, until the whole of NayaNET reads complete.
