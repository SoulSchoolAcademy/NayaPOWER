# How Naya is learning — in human words

**In a nutshell:** a new corner of the Hub called "How Naya is learning" — one screen that shows, in plain words, what changed, what worked, and what's proven. So anyone — including a stranger — can see the intelligence working.

**What it means:** the Hub shows its work. Every lesson, fix, or breakthrough gets a colored tag that tells you exactly how proven it is:

- **Blue — Built.** It's made, but nobody has checked it yet.
- **Green — Checked.** Tests or another Naya verified it. Not on the live system yet.
- **Gold — Proven live.** It worked on the real, live system.
- **Purple glow — Still learning.** We're watching it happen, not claiming anything yet.

These tags are a promise: the Hub will never show you blue and call it gold. "Made" is not "checked" is not "proven live" — and you'll always see which is which.

**How to use it:** open it like any Hub panel. Newest lessons first. Tap any lesson and it unfolds in four layers: the short version, what it means, how to use it, and what it gives you. Nothing is hidden behind jargon.

**What's in it for you:** you can trust what you see. If Naya says "I learned this," the tag shows you whether she built it, checked it, or proved it on the real system. If she hasn't proven it yet, she says so — right on the screen.

**The honest rule:** when something isn't proven yet, the Hub shows "learning in progress" instead of pretending. No fake activity, no pretend data, ever. An empty page that says "nothing proven yet — here's what's being watched" is more intelligent than a full page of guesses.

**The compounding story:** Naya doesn't just collect lessons — she reuses them, and when a reused lesson is **behaviorally proven** to change a real decision (REUSED → BEHAVIORALLY_VERIFIED in the envelope), that gets the gold tag. That's the moment you can see her compounding: lessons turning into better behavior, in front of your eyes. Until that moment, the Hub shows the build-up honestly (blue and green stacking up) and waits with you for the gold.
