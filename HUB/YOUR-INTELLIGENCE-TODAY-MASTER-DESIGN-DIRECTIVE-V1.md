## 1. THE ONE JOB

This room has exactly one job: **tell the human what's important about their intelligence today.**

Not a dashboard. Not a feed of notes. Not ten widgets. A **Daily Intelligence Cockpit** — the place they open every morning and every evening to know:

- What happened?
- What did I learn?
- What changed?
- What matters?
- What should I remember?
- What am I missing?
- What can I do about it?

If a human wouldn't open this every day and feel smarter for it, it isn't done.

## 2. THE EIGHT MOVEMENTS

The room is a composed experience, not a collection of cards. It moves:

1. **ORIENT** — What is today? (date-aware, time-aware: morning ≠ evening)
2. **SEE** — What happened?
3. **UNDERSTAND** — What changed? What did I learn?
4. **PRIORITIZE** — What matters now?
5. **REMEMBER** — What should I carry forward?
6. **QUESTION** — What am I missing?
7. **ACT** — What can I do next?
8. **VERIFY** — Why should I trust this?

## 3. THE GRAMMAR

Every Today room speaks this grammar, in this order:

**NOW → NEXT → WATCH → LEARNED → WAITING → RECENT PROOF**

- **NOW** dominates. The single most important thing, unmissable.
- **NEXT** is the top next move — verb-first, actionable.
- **WATCH** is what changed and deserves attention.
- **LEARNED** is what the human (and Naya) actually learned.
- **WAITING** is what's unresolved, blocked, or awaiting the human.
- **RECENT PROOF** is the evidence trail — why any of this should be believed.

## 4. THE FIVE KILLER FEATURES

These are what make this room legendary instead of ordinary. Each has an acceptance test.

### 4a. WHAT CHANGED — the difference engine
Not "here are today's notes." **"Here is what is different from yesterday."**
- Shows the delta: what moved, what progressed, what regressed.
- Each change carries: what → why it matters → evidence → state.
- **Test:** a human reading it can describe their own progression without opening anything else.

### 4b. WHAT I LEARNED — the learning ladder
Naya distinguishes five rungs, never blurred:
- **INFORMATION** — "I encountered this."
- **UNDERSTANDING** — "I now understand this."
- **CHANGE** — "My understanding changed."
- **DECISION** — "I decided this."
- **KNOWLEDGE** — "This is now part of my durable intelligence."
- **Test:** no entry appears under a rung it hasn't earned.

### 4c. WHAT MATTERS NOW — evidence-backed priority
The system looks across today's verified intelligence and names what has the greatest practical relevance — **and explains why.**
- Never invents priorities. Never pretends to know everything.
- The form is always: "This appears important **because** X changed, Y is unresolved, and Z depends on it."
- **Test:** every priority can point to its evidence. One without evidence is removed.

### 4d. WHAT SHOULD I REMEMBER — the diary
The human lived a day. Most of it disappears. This room says: **"These are the things from today worth carrying forward."**
- Conversations, discoveries, decisions, experiences — distilled to what deserves permanence.
- **Test:** a year from now, this section reads as the days that mattered.

### 4e. WHAT AM I MISSING — the honest gap
Four states, always visible:
- **KNOWN** · **CHANGED** · **UNCERTAIN** · **MISSING**
- Names missing context plainly: "You established X, but Y has not been verified."
- This prevents false completion. **Unknown ≠ success** is a load-bearing wall.
- **Test:** the room never lets the human feel done when they aren't.

## 5. THE RUTHLESS FILTER

Every component in this room must do at least one of these, or it doesn't exist:

**UNDERSTAND · REMEMBER · CONNECT · DECIDE · ACT · VERIFY** something valuable.

(CAPTURE is struck: the Hub is output. Capture happens outside; this room projects.)

No decorative boards. No "nice to have" widgets. No button without a consequence. If I can't name the human outcome a component serves, I delete the component — I don't restyle it.

## 6. THE VISUAL LAW

- **Magenta is this room's soul** (#d86cff — human significance). Today wears magenta because it is about the human. Purple is Naya; magenta is *you*.
- **NOW dominates the composition.** The most important thing is the largest, most luminous object. Everything else is quieter.
- **Every component obeys the physics:** ten dimensions, sequence DEPTH → LIGHT → STATE → RESPONSE → CONSEQUENCE. A flat button is a violation, not a style choice.
- **Progressive disclosure (Instrument/Oura law):** nutshell first, depth on demand. The room shows what matters now; the human goes deeper when they want. Never an information dump.
- **Morning/evening aware:** the room knows what time it is. Morning opens forward ("here's your day"); evening opens backward ("here's what happened"). Same room, different truth.
- **Type:** generous, high-contrast, readable supremacy over all styling. 16px body floor.
- **Motion:** only to communicate truthful state. Nothing performs aliveness; the intelligence is alive, the chrome is quiet.

## 7. THE DATA LAW

- **NOW** is powered by: today's verified intelligence, ranked by evidence-backed relevance.
- **NEXT** is powered by: open loops, commitments, blocked work with owners.
- **WATCH** is powered by: the difference engine (today vs. yesterday's canonical state).
- **LEARNED** is powered by: classified learning rungs (never mixed).
- **WAITING** is powered by: unresolved questions, unmet dependencies, unacknowledged receipts.
- **RECENT PROOF** is powered by: receipts, evidence links, verification states.
- **Never invented.** An empty section says so honestly ("Nothing qualifies yet — the day is young") rather than manufacturing content.
- **One brain, many doors:** this room projects canonical objects. It creates no duplicate store for presentation.

## 8. STATES — every one designed

- **Morning** (before noon): forward-leaning. The day ahead, open loops carried in.
- **Evening** (after 5pm): backward-looking. What happened, what was learned, what carries to tomorrow.
- **Empty day:** honest, not barren. "A quiet day is still a day — here's what's waiting."
- **Loading:** energy traveling, never a fake spinner.
- **Error:** what failed, what it affects, what happens next. Never a dead panel.
- **No intelligence yet:** the room explains what will appear here and when — an invitation, not an error.

## 9. WHAT IT IS NOT (anti-patterns)

- Not a dashboard of widgets.
- Not a chronological note list.
- Not a second Smart Feed (the feed is the Main Show; this is the day, distilled).
- Not a clone of Reports (reports reveal meaning *across time*; Today is *right now*).
- Not a clone of Library (the library is what you *have*; Today is what *matters*).
- No mode tabs inside the room (modes live on the Main Show — director ruling).
- No capture buttons (Hub is output — director ruling).
- No "Smart Share" (it's Smart Connect — director ruling).
- No demo data presented as live. Ever.

## 11. THE FINAL TESTS

Before Shawn sees it, I answer:

- Would a human open this every day? (If not: why would they?)
- Can they name their day's progression without opening anything else?
- Does every component earn its place under the ruthless filter?
- Does every priority point to evidence?
- Does the room admit what it doesn't know?
- Is NOW unmissable?
- Would this make someone say "this isn't another AI interface"?

Beauty gets attention. Utility earns permanence. Intelligence earns trust. Memory creates attachment. Governance creates safety. **This room must earn all five.**
