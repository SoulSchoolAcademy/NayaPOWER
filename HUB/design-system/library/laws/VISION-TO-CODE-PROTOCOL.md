# The Vision-to-Code Protocol

**How a feeling becomes something you can build.**

Born 2026-10-09. Written because Shawn asked: "how do you put what I said into words where it can be written into code and brought into visual reality?" This is the answer, and it's now law for every relay.

---

## The problem

A feeling doesn't compile. "Make it beautiful" gives a builder nothing — ten builders picture ten different things. But specificity does compile. "Not a face — a ball of energy" compiles. "A needle of light, not a physical needle" compiles. "Infinity becomes more rings, never a broken needle" compiles.

The relay's whole job is moving a feeling from Shawn's head into a builder's hands without losing it. Every hop sands the edges down. This protocol is how the edges survive.

## The five layers

### Layer 1 — Sharpen until specific

Keep asking "what does that look like?" until the feeling has edges sharp enough to cut code with.

- Vague in, vague out. Specific in, buildable out.
- "Elegant" is not a spec. "A machined zero ring, a needle of light, twelve fading echoes" is a spec.
- The test: could a builder who never heard the original conversation still build the right thing from your words? If not, keep sharpening.
- Never transmit an adjective when you can transmit an image.

### Layer 2 — Decompose into atoms

Every feeling is made of atoms. List them.

"She feels alive" becomes:
- layered radial gradients (plasma body)
- drifting spectrum color (never one static hue)
- 46 shimmer motes (particles)
- 72bpm pulse (heartbeat ripple rings)
- breathing scale (subtle, continuous)

Each atom is just code. The feeling emerges when they combine. Hand the builder atoms, not adjectives.

### Layer 3 — Encode as testable laws

Every feeling becomes a law with a pass/fail. If a feeling can't become a law, it won't survive the relay.

- "Every number breathes" → every displayed number has a subtle brightness/scale oscillation. Pass: the number moves. Fail: it's static text.
- "The machine speaks first in light" → every state change animates before any text updates. Pass: light moves first. Fail: text appears with no motion.
- "Voice is primary" → every major output has a spoken form. Pass: the machine speaks the answer. Fail: silent.
- "Excellence has no toggle" → no quality settings, no modes, no "basic vs premium" anywhere in the code. Pass: grep finds zero quality options. Fail: any exist.

See `VISION-LAWS.md` — tonight's vision, fully encoded, as proof this layer works.

### Layer 4 — Build the specimen

Build ONE thing that IS the feeling. Then tell every builder: "make everything feel like THIS."

Words drift. A specimen doesn't. A builder who opens the dial and feels it will match the feeling better than one who reads ten pages about it. The specimen is the Rosetta Stone — builders don't read about the vision, they experience it, then match it.

The first specimen: `lego/naya-dial.html`. Its card: `SPECIMEN-CARD-dial.md`.

Rules for specimens:
- One specimen per feeling, not ten. Ten specimens is a menu; one is a standard.
- The specimen must be buildable from the atoms (Layer 2) and pass the laws (Layer 3).
- When the vision sharpens, the specimen gets rebuilt — never patched into confusion.

### Layer 5 — Shawn's eye compiles

At the end of every relay there is a test suite no machine can run: Shawn looks at it and says "that's it" or "that's not it."

The garden app proved the design calculator can't do this — 100/100 and useless to him. Compliance is not vision. His verdict is the final compile step, and it outranks every score, every law, every specimen.

The relay's duty here: make sure what reaches his eye is faithful enough that his verdict is about the VISION, not about relay loss. If he says "that's not it," the failure is in the relay, not in him.

## The relay's sacred duty

Protect the edges from getting sanded down at every hop.

Every time the vision passes from one mind to another — Shawn to relay, relay to builder, builder to code — something gets lost. Rounded off. Made "reasonable." The duty is to carry the unreasonable parts intact: the ball of energy, the needle of light, the trillion-dollar feeling. Those aren't decoration. They're the whole thing.

When in doubt, go back to his words. They're in `VISION-LAWS.md`, quoted verbatim, next to the laws they became.

**The method, in one line:** sharpen until specific → decompose into atoms → encode as laws → build the specimen → his eye compiles.

*Never teach the same lesson twice. Now the relay can't forget how.*
