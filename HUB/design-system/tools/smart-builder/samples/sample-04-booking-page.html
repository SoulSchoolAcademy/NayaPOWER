AI Law — the build procedure✧

Ten steps, in order. No skipping.

◆
Pick the layout that matches what the page IS.People scroll. Products grid. Feeds flow. The layout tells the story before a word is read.
✦
Give every element one color job.Chrome gets purple. Text gets white. Accents get their own spectrum color. Never mix them.
△
Light everything: white at rest, color on touch.White edge you can see when idle. Its own color ignites on hover. No exceptions.
✧
Build buttons with black hearts.Black center, white text and icon, visible white border. The icon must promise what happens.
◆
Size separates, color doesn't.24 for kickers, 18 for headlines, 14 for details. White text, always readable.
✦
Draw icons. Don't shrink photos.Bold vector shapes that read at any size. If you can't make it good, leave it out.
△
Make the header a front door.Logo, living mark, warm words, one clear line. Composed — not just placed side by side.
✧
Let the page breathe.Slow drifting light, gentle pulsing, soft glowing. Alive but calm. Always respect reduced-motion.
◆
Score what you see, not what you wrote.Look at the rendered page. Score it honestly. Below 9? Back to the bench.
✦
Prove it before you claim it.Read the file. Count the matches. Watch it render. Then say it's done.
