§1 · The intake

A living form that holds the contract

Not a questionnaire. A form the human and the builder shape together, over the session, until the human says build. Pick a mode — the contract listens in all three.

%%INTAKE_MODES%%

Intake mode: questions — the contract is listening.

Two authors, one finalizer

The human and the builder both mold the contract. But only the human says “okay, build” — button or word. Finalize authority is human, always. And every intake element passes one bar: does it remove a step for the human, or add one? Anything that adds busywork fails.

§2 · The contract

What the builder is legally bound to

The contract holds everything the build needs — words, pictures, links, structure, address. Shown beautifully, in the design language. Never a raw form.

Mobile isn’t a version. Mobile is the app.
