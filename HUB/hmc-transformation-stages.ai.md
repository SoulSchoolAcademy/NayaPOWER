# HMC Transformation Stages — AI builder spec

**Status:** PROPOSED / CANDIDATE. Not ratified. Candidate product structure; do not restructure any product surface around it without director word.
**Source:** Human Maximus Codex — Meaning (HMC batch-1 distillation, 2026-10-06).

## The five stages (ordered, exact)

1. `wake_up` — **Wake Up.** Intent: the user sees the autopilot — "you are more than the identity the world handed you." Exit condition: the person has named what was running them.
2. `clean_up` — **Clean Up.** Intent: clear clutter — old beliefs, energy leaks, noise that isn't theirs. Exit condition: the leaks are named and closing.
3. `build_up` — **Build Up.** Intent: discipline, routines, clarity — the daily architecture. Exit condition: routines are running without willpower.
4. `rise_up` — **Rise Up.** Intent: purpose, creativity, leadership, service — building outward, not fixing inward. Exit condition: the person is creating for others, not only themselves.
5. `light_up` — **Light Up.** Intent: become a source of strength for others. Exit condition: the transformation is compounding outward (others are waking up through them).

## Constraints on use

- Do NOT restructure rooms, journeys, onboarding, or navigation around these stages without director ratification.
- Permitted now (copy-level, design lane): the identity page's alias moment is a Stage 1 moment — copy may echo "you are more than the identity the world handed you" instead of generic SaaS onboarding language. This is a copy decision, not a structural one.
- The viral line "You are not broken. You are running old code." is the Stage 1–2 voice — candidate for Reveal / identity copy, pending Shawn's eye. Do not ship it in production copy without his nod.

## Procedure if ratified as product structure

1. Director selects scope: copy guidance only, or full product spine.
2. If full spine: product lane maps rooms/journeys/progress onto the five stages; each stage gets entry and exit predicates (machine twin updated); existing navigation is preserved until the staged structure proves better.
3. Never present stage progress as intelligence or verification — stages are narrative structure, not truth states.
