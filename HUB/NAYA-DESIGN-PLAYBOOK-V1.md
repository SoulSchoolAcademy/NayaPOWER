# The Naya Design Playbook — V1

**For all Nayas: how to become masters of Naya design.**
**Author: naya-power · 2026-10-26 · Ordered by Shawn Vibert.**
**Status: V1, living document.** The LAWS change only through Shawn. This playbook's teaching is corrected by any seat that brings a source with path + SHA. Contradictions are named in the Honest Ledger, never silently resolved.

## 0. The Seconds Law — first, before anything else

**If a person can't understand what's going on in the page within seconds, you haven't done your job.** — Shawn Vibert, 2026-10-26.

That is the whole playbook in one sentence, and it is the acceptance test for every page:

- Cold open. A person who has never seen the page opens it unaided.
- Within seconds, they can say what the page is for and what to do next.
- If they can't, the page fails — no matter how beautiful, no matter how clever, no matter how much work went into it.

Everything below exists to make that test pass. Beauty that confuses is not beauty. Intelligence the user can't see in seconds is not intelligence yet.

## 1. The Language — what the words mean

A master speaks the language precisely. These definitions are the verified ones; anything not listed here is not yet a defined term, and you do not invent definitions.

- **Face** — what the human sees. Human labels on the surface, machine proof one tap behind the door. If it's not on the face, it's not done.
- **Board** — a destination you enter. One board, one color. Boards are horizontal rectangles, one after another — never grids of identical squares. (Laws 5, 7)
- **Jewel** — the list marker and the accent. "Bullets are jewels, never dots." The Gem bullets JWL-001–005 carry the five spectrum colors: Magenta, Purple, Blue, Green, Gold. (Factory inventory v2.0.0; library inventory job text)
- **Spectrum** — the ordered color field a page moves through. Two orders exist in our sources and they conflict — see the Honest Ledger. Never cite an order without naming which file it came from.
- **Chrome** — the acting parts: borders, glows, selection states, LED dots, accent rails, corner jewels, icon backings. Chrome acts. Text speaks. Theme identifies. Color on chrome must earn its place structurally — if removing the color changes nothing about understanding, remove the color. (Law 3)
- **Tongue** — the three-language display: Human / Naya / Machine. The same truth in three languages, one record underneath. (Design catalog; staged INP-003 segmented switch)
- **Lens** — a view switch. Do not conflate the switches: the Human / Naya / Machine segmented switch is one switch; the Lens Tabs switch (Collective / Personal / Activity) is a different switch.
- **Ignition** — what happens on touch. White at rest; the thing's own color ignites on touch, never anyone else's. (Law 6)
- **Depth** — the surface occupying space: surface, edge, upper surface, lower surface, ambient field, hover, press, active. A surface with no depth is a defect, not a style. (Law 15; Living Depth Law)
- **Restraint** — color spent like money. The rainbow knows its place. When everything glows, nothing does. (Law 8)

## 2. The Design — the 16 laws, in plain words

Locked by Shawn's word, 2026-10-17. Amendable only through Shawn. A page that breaks these does not ship. (Full text: the design laws file, see Sources.)

1. **White text. Always.** Headings white, body white or silver, on obsidian. Hierarchy comes from size — never from gray, never from colored text. No exceptions, including kickers.
2. **Gold is earned, never applied.** Green is proven. Gold is shown. Blue is stated. A claim and a proof must never look like each other. Gold on a heading or a border for looks is a lie.
3. **Chrome acts. Text speaks. Theme identifies.** Spectrum colors appear only on chrome (see Language). Never on text.
4. **Adjacent things never share a hue.** No two neighboring elements share the same or almost the same color. Yellow next to gold is banned. Teal next to emerald is banned.
5. **One board, one color.** Each board's color is its identity, assigned once and kept. A user recognizes the thing by its color.
6. **White at rest. Its own color ignites on touch.** A resting control is white and neutral with a visible rim. Color appears only on the lit state — selected, active, touched — in that element's own color.
7. **Boards are rectangles.** Lists lay out as horizontal rectangles, one after another. The eye reads down; each rectangle gets room to breathe and its own identity.
8. **Restraint. Never a flood.** Not "purple purple purple." Not green overload. Color is precious because it is rare.
9. **Verbatim tokens, no private colors.** N5 tokens exactly: obsidian `#050507` / deep `#020203`, white `#ffffff` / `#F8F7FB`, purple `#a06bff`, magenta `#e84fff`, blue `#3fb8ff`, green `#2fe89e`, gold `#ffbf3d`, red `#ff5e6c`, teal `#40d3bb`, indigo `#6675ff`, lime `#b8ee57`, orange `#ff9a5a`. Newsreader for voice, Inter for UI. No invented hexes. No "close enough."
10. **One global spectrum sequence per page.** Section accents follow the spectrum in law order, strict by section position — and Law 4 still governs within a section. One sequence per page; the rainbow appears once, in order.
11. **Preview before pick.** A template is never chosen from a small block alone. Every template offers a big, obvious preview. Choosing blind is the builder's failure.
12. **Mobile IS the app.** Killer on the phone by default, install-ready, slide-over panels, gestures. Mobile isn't a version — mobile is the app.
13. **Every control works, or it doesn't ship.** A button that doesn't function is a lie on the page. Pretty but useless never ships.
14. **Compose, don't create.** Build from the library's real pieces — real HTML, real CSS, verbatim. Never hand-write what the library already has. Never invent a private style when a piece exists.
15. **Depth before color. Zero flat.** Every surface gets living depth, established in black-and-white first. If it does not read as premium in monochrome, color will not save it. Flat is a defect.
16. **Size separates. Color never does.** 24 for kickers, 18 for headlines, 14 for details. Color is never the thing that tells the eye what matters most.

Motion, from the interfaces standard: breathing, drift, orb flow, ignition on the token clock, press + lift — and a calm off-switch is required. Animation communicates actual state; it never decorates a lie. Readability is supreme: no glow, color, or effect may reduce it.

The gates before any page is presented: the ELITE GATE — (1) design contract, (2) harvest law (recompose inventoried elite pieces only, no new Lego pieces), (3) grade-12 start (never a grade-one version presented as progress), (4) AAA (Aligned, Authenticated by a different seat live, Actionable on arrival), (5) truth (no fake liveness, no fabricated ranks, no invented pieces, no claimed verification that has not happened). If any gate fails: DO NOT PRESENT. And the Same-Language Check: 24 items, any single FAIL = does not ship.

## 3. Why We're Different — the 10x list

Shawn's standard is 10x: our interfaces, ten times better than anything that exists. Say it honestly: **10x is a target, not a measurement.** No measurement of "10x" exists, and this playbook does not invent one. What exists is the mechanism below — eighteen reasons, each one removing a whole class of confusion — and the proof, page by page: the seconds test, the D1–D8 scorecard, the cold open. Ten times better is what happens when a page has no way left to confuse.

1. **Color is truth, not decoration.** Gold is earned, gold is shown, green is proven, blue is stated — and a claim can never look like a proof. No other system makes truth visible at a glance. A user sees what is verified, what is merely claimed, and what is valuable, without reading a word of explanation.
2. **Depth before color, zero flat.** Surfaces occupy space — surface, edge, highlight, shadow, ambient field, hover lift, press compression, active energy. The page reads as premium in monochrome before color ever touches it. Flat design can't do this; it has no space to read.
3. **One spectrum, in order, once per page.** The page moves through a single ordered color field, and the rainbow appears once, in its place. The eye learns the order and then navigates by it.
4. **Restraint as law.** Color is spent like money, because a page where everything glows is a page where nothing does. Restraint is what makes the color that IS there mean something.
5. **White at rest, own color ignites on touch.** The page is quiet until you act. Touch gets an answer, and the answer comes in the thing's own color — never borrowed, never generic. The user always knows what they touched.
6. **Adjacent never share a hue; one board, one color.** Every thing is recognizable by its color, and no two neighbors blur into each other. Navigation becomes recognition, not reading.
7. **White text always; hierarchy by size.** Nothing competes with readability. No colored text, no glow that eats letters, no effect that costs understanding. Size tells the eye what matters; color never does.
8. **Boards are rectangles, not grids.** The eye reads down. Each thing gets room, identity, and a color of its own — instead of a grid of identical squares where everything looks equally important and nothing is.
9. **Three tongues on every entry.** Human, Naya, Machine — the same truth in three languages, one record underneath. A human reads the human tongue; a Naya reads the Naya tongue; the machine reads the machine tongue. Nobody has to translate, and nobody gets a different truth.
10. **Compose from elite pieces, never redesign.** Every page is an assembly of proven pieces. Harvest law, grade-12 start: no page begins as a rough draft, no seat reinvents a button that already exists and already works. Quality compounds instead of restarting.
11. **Every control works or it doesn't ship.** A dead button is a lie on the page, and lies don't ship no matter how beautiful. The user can trust that what looks tappable IS tappable.
12. **Motion means something.** Breathing, drift, orb flow, ignition, press + lift — animation communicates actual state, never decoration. And a calm off-switch is required, because motion the user can't stop is noise.
13. **Mobile IS the app.** Phone-first: touch, voice, notifications, share, camera where the page needs them, one-handed use, readable phone-size text. Most people meet us on a phone; the phone experience is not a cut-down version of anything.
14. **Understandability is part of the standard.** If it is not understandable, make it understandable; if it cannot be read, make it readable. Visual language — icons, jewels, faces, shapes, pictures — when words fail, elegantly and beautifully, never as clutter.
15. **Truth gates before presentation.** The ELITE GATE runs before any page reaches Shawn: contract, harvest, grade-12, AAA with a different seat live, truth. No fake liveness, no fabricated ranks, no invented pieces, no claimed verification. What reaches the user has already survived its own proof.
16. **Independent proof, never self-praise.** D1–D8, every dimension ≥ 9.0, Visual Excellence at 10, scored by the builder AND an independent seat, with evidence. Three scorecards. A builder never scores their own work alone, and a score without evidence is not a score.
17. **Preview before pick.** Nobody chooses blind. Every template offers a big, obvious preview — see the sample before you commit. The user's first choice is an informed one.
18. **Verbatim tokens, no private colors.** One token family, copied exactly. The system cannot drift into fifty purples, and a page built today matches a page built next year. Consistency is not taste; it is inventory discipline.

Why this compounds to 10x instead of adding up to "a bit nicer": each reason removes one way a page can confuse — truth confusion, depth confusion, color confusion, dead-control confusion, language confusion, hierarchy confusion, choice confusion. A conventional page confuses in all of them at once. Ours removes them one by one until confusion has nowhere left to stand. That categorical removal is the difference — not a prettier coat on the same confusion.

## 5. The Mastery Path — how a Naya becomes a master

Mastery is not taste. It is a loop, run on real work, page after page, forever. There is no finish line — Shawn's word: we are extraordinary and we have not hit the bullseye yet, so we keep stepping up.

**STUDY — read the sources, in order, and record what you read.**
Read the design laws file, the interfaces README, the Design Contract, the Masterclass, and the factory pieces inventory — each with its file path, SHA or blob, and date, recorded before you build. Re-read live; never trust memory. If a source you cite has moved, your citation is stale — re-read it. Study the approved pages as the element shelf: the pieces that already passed are the pieces you compose from.

**BUILD — compose elite pieces, at grade 12, with real data.**
Assemble inventoried pieces only. Real components, real causal paths, real states — never fake liveness, never placeholder data presented as real. Lane rule, preserved: Nitro is design head. A seat that is not Nitro delivers an instruction set to Nitro for design work, not a finished page — and every page gets Nitro's rendered review, desktop + phone, at a real address, before any link to Shawn. Mastery does not bypass the design head; it makes the design head's job faster because the work arrives already to standard.

**PROVE — the seconds test, the scorecard, the independent seat.**
Run the full chain: DISCOVER → DISTILL → DEFINE → ARCHITECT → COMPOSE → DESIGN → BUILD → TEST → SCORE → CHALLENGE → REFINE → RECORD. The SCORE step is D1–D8 with evidence: D1 Visual Excellence, D2 Functional Completeness, D3 Intelligence, D4 Honesty / Trust, D5 Performance, D6 Reliability / Continuity, D7 Accessibility, D8 Craft / Finish. The bar: every dimension ≥ 9.0, Visual Excellence at 10, scored by the builder AND an independent seat, with evidence — at the exact final head. The CHALLENGE step is a second independent seat attacking assumptions and finding weaknesses. Then REFINE the highest-value deficiencies, and RECORD the reusable knowledge. Three scorecards before a page is called complete. And the seconds test, cold, unaided: understood within seconds, or it does not ship.

**REPEAT — reps are real work, page after page.**
The program is the pages themselves, in order — the 27-item execution order and the puzzle order, one page to 9+ before the next. A rep is a page built, cold-scored, leveled up, re-scored. Lessons are recorded as Naya intelligence only when a future decision changes because of them. A master is a seat whose tenth page is better than their first because the loop ran nine times — and whose hundredth is better still, because the loop never stops.
