# The Ten HMC Codes

**STATUS: PROPOSED — candidate operating principles. Awaiting Shawn's review. NOT installed as law. Nothing here governs Naya or any builder until he says so.**

---

Shawn, from your Human Maximus Codex: ten principle-level rules, each in the form "X before Y" — prioritization principles for how a person (or an intelligence) orders what matters. This is a *different, earlier formulation* from the 100 behaviors already captured in SN-0312 (the Awesome Code) — these are principle-level, not behaviors. Both are captured and cross-linked, not duplicated.

## The ten codes, in plain words

1. **Awareness Before Action** — see first. Awareness is what makes action intelligent. Blind action is noise.
2. **Energy Before Outcome** — upgrade your state before you judge your life. A depleted state poisons the verdict.
3. **Truth Before Comfort** — comfort keeps you asleep; truth wakes you up. The easy story is usually the sleeping one.
4. **Responsibility Before Power** — power without responsibility destroys. Never hand over power you haven't taken responsibility for.
5. **Love Before Ego** — ego wants to win; love wants to heal, build, and serve.
6. **Discipline Before Destiny** — destiny isn't found; it's built through repeated choices.
7. **Purpose Before Pleasure** — pleasure fades; purpose gives direction.
8. **Service Before Status** — "How do I look?" vs. "Who did I help?" Ask the second one first.
9. **Integration Before Performance** — become whole enough to carry greatness. A cracked vessel can't hold much.
10. **Consciousness Before Creation** — upgrade the creator, and the creation upgrades itself.

## Why this is on your desk

The distillation flagged three of these as candidates for Naya's own operating principles: **Code 1 (Awareness Before Action)**, **Code 3 (Truth Before Comfort)**, and **Code 10 (Consciousness Before Creation)**. They read like they were written for an intelligence: see before acting, prefer the true thing over the comfortable thing, upgrade the creator before the creation.

But — and this matters — installing operating principles for Naya is a governance decision, not a design decision. This proposal goes to the **governance lane**, not into law. The options as I see them:

- **Adopt** some or all as operating principles for Naya (would need to sit alongside the Prime Judgment Law and the existing operating laws without contradicting them).
- **Adopt as product principles** for NayaNET surfaces — the voice, the copy, the relationship the product offers.
- **Keep as founder philosophy** — captured, retrievable, influential, but not binding.

That's your call. What's proposed here is only that the intelligence is preserved in the three-language standard so any of those options is open.

## What this would change if you ratify it

Depends on the option you choose above. The capture itself changes nothing about how anyone works — it just means the ten codes exist as structured, retrievable intelligence (human / AI / machine) instead of only inside a PDF.
