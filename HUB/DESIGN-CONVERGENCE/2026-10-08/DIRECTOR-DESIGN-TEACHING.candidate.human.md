## Fifteen Human Laws (Director 2026-10-08)
1. **Score the lived render** — browser/device reality outranks code self-score.
2. **Back it or bench it** — do not present below-nine work as ready; specific evidence required.
3. **Layout is meaning** — people: vertical relationship flow; browsing: suitable grid; reports: editorial sequence.
4. **Three color jobs** — chrome black/white/purple; primary text white; themed objects use stable spectrum.
5. **White idle rim, own color on hover/focus** — unmistakable separation; no blanket theme flood.
6. **Buttons are physical promises** — black #050505 core, white label/icon, visible rim, inset light, real shadow, truthful action.
7. **Typography by size** — candidate 24/18/14 roles; prose 18px proposal; readability wins over effects.
8. **Copy is design** — warm human invitation, concise payoff, not feature documentation.
9. **Native jewel icons** — distinct vector geometry, legible at small size; no shrunken image-as-icon.
10. **Maximum life, minimum noise** — breathing light, feedback; reduced-motion respected; no fake event animations.
11. **One system, not two** — one Hub chassis/socket, brain, mail composer, people record, canonical actions.
12. **Full usable canvas, no dead void** — responsive flow; top-left logo is a new candidate direction requiring shell reconciliation.
13. **Verify before claiming** — exact bytes, actual browser/use, independent test, source and receipt.
14. **Every affordance tells the truth** — + adds, chevron navigates, envelope opens mail; empty/error states honest.
15. **Header is a front door** — living identity mark, warm voice and one clear promise, not a cold feature label.

## Design anatomy
- Field: obsidian, raised black boards, primary white, purple signature (#9d75ff); legacy #050507 and #010103 are currently known variants.
- Spectrum: purple → indigo → blue → teal → green → lime → yellow → gold → orange → red → magenta; rich jewels in natural ordered flow, semantic rather than flood fills.
- Interaction: idle visible white rim; themed hover ignites *its own* color; primary chrome accent purple; focus visible; presses sink; real <button>/<a>; primary CTA proposed min 52px, general touch >=44px.
- Depth: field → board → nested panel → controls → overlay; facet jewel white-hot core, shadow and inner top light.
- Copy: invitation and user payoff. Intelligent Block: IN A NUTSHELL → WHAT IT MEANS → HOW TO USE IT → WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU; optional Human/Child/Grandma/Naya/Machine projections from the SAME intelligence object.
- Truth: LIVE requires receipt; SIMULATED/DEMO/LOCAL_ONLY/CANDIDATE/UNKNOWN/BLOCKED/VERIFIED are visibly distinct; no claim of production proof from animation.
