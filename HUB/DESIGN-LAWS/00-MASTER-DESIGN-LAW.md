# 🔱 NayaNET Master Interface Design Law V1

**Status:** Human-Director-directed normative design law  
**Parent authority:** `HUB/DESIGN-CONTRACT.md`  
**Purpose:** Decompose the Hub design contract into precise laws without creating a competing design system.

## 1. The standard

NayaNET must be designed as if it will be judged beside the best digital products, physical instruments, automotive interiors, operating systems, and luxury industrial-design objects in the world.

The ambition is not expensive-looking decoration.

The ambition is **world-champion interface craft**:

- unmistakably NayaNET;
- extraordinarily clear;
- visually blissful;
- physically present;
- intelligent in behavior;
- truthful in state;
- generous to human vision;
- fast;
- calm under complexity;
- memorable without becoming theatrical;
- beautiful enough that world-class design teams would study it;
- useful enough that the beauty disappears into effortless operation.

The interface should communicate exceptional value without ever requiring the user to admire the interface before they can use it.

## 2. The North Star

> **MAKE SOPHISTICATED INTELLIGENCE FEEL SIMPLE, BEAUTIFUL, TRUSTWORTHY, ALIVE AND USEFUL TO THE HUMAN.**

The experience should become so coherent that the human no longer feels like they are operating software.

They feel like they have entered an intelligent environment.

## 3. Design precedence

When laws compete, use this order:

1. **Human understanding and safety**
2. **Truth / state accuracy**
3. **Visual bliss: contrast, clarity, cleanliness, legibility**
4. **Purpose and information hierarchy**
5. **Accessibility and human comfort**
6. **Causal functionality**
7. **Performance and responsiveness**
8. **Material depth and physical presence**
9. **Semantic color**
10. **Motion / delight**
11. **Decoration**

No lower layer may damage a higher layer.

## 4. One intelligence, many representations

Human interface, Naya representation, machine representation, and proof representation are not separate products.

They are different expressions of the same governed intelligence.

The interface therefore must preserve:

`intent → purpose → intelligence → authority → capability → observation → truth → outcome → learning → continuity`

The human representation should compress this into understanding, not expose unnecessary machinery.

## 5. World-class craft test

Every major screen must answer:

- Where am I?
- What matters?
- What changed?
- What is true?
- What is uncertain?
- What can I do?
- What happens if I act?
- What happened after I acted?
- What did Naya learn?
- Can I continue later without rebuilding context?

If the human cannot answer these quickly, visual beauty does not rescue the design.

## 6. Preservation law

The current Hub concept is the visual floor, not the production architecture.

Every existing element must be classified:

**KEEP · IMPROVE · REPLACE · REMOVE**

Replacement requires evidence of superiority across purpose, clarity, craft, accessibility, performance, and system coherence.

"Cleaner code" is not sufficient evidence for a visually weaker interface.

## 7. Anti-fragmentation law

There is:

- one material language;
- one semantic spectrum;
- one icon family;
- one typography system;
- one depth system;
- one motion grammar;
- one state vocabulary;
- one component physics model;
- one acceptance system.

Rooms may have distinct themes. They may not invent independent design languages.

## 8. Minimum production standard

No visible production element is approved because it "looks good."

It must have:

**PURPOSE → ANATOMY → STATES → CAUSAL PATH → TRUTH BEHAVIOR → ACCESSIBILITY → RESPONSIVE BEHAVIOR → PERFORMANCE BUDGET → PROOF**

The builder must inspect the rendered artifact, not just the source.

## 9. Definition of elite

Elite means:

- exact geometry;
- generous readable typography;
- strong hierarchy;
- restrained light;
- meaningful color;
- believable depth;
- smooth physical response;
- clean spacing;
- consistent iconography;
- honest state;
- zero accidental-looking details.

Elite does **not** mean:

- excessive glow;
- tiny text;
- constant motion;
- glass everywhere;
- neon everywhere;
- random gradients;
- more cards;
- more effects;
- more density;
- decorative AI theater.

## 10. Success condition

NayaNET design reaches the intended standard when the human can say:

> "This is beautiful, but more importantly, everything makes sense. I know what is happening. I trust what I am seeing. I know what to do. It feels alive because it responds intelligently."

That is the target.
