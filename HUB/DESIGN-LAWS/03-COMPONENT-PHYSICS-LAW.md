# ◈ NayaNET Component Physics Law V1

## 1. Purpose

NayaNET components are treated as intelligent physical objects inside a digital environment.

The goal is not fake 3D.

The goal is **physical presence with semantic behavior**.

## 2. Five primary object classes

### POWER OBJECT
Buttons and consequential actions.

### INTELLIGENCE SURFACE
Boards, readers, reports, rooms, panels, destination objects.

### IDENTITY PORTAL
Inputs, forms, connection fields, activation and identity controls.

### ENERGY PATH
Progress, loading, synchronization, playback, transfer and process state.

### INTELLIGENCE GLYPH
Icons, symbols, state marks and identity emblems.

## 3. Ten dimensions of component physics

Every important interactive component must define:

1. **MATERIAL** — what does the surface feel like?
2. **FORM** — what geometry communicates purpose?
3. **DEPTH** — where does it sit relative to the screen?
4. **LIGHT** — how does illumination reveal hierarchy/state?
5. **COLOR** — what semantic meaning does color carry?
6. **STATE** — what is the object doing?
7. **MOTION** — how does it respond over time?
8. **SOUND/HAPTIC** — if used, what does it confirm?
9. **CONSEQUENCE** — what actually changes after interaction?
10. **MEMORY** — does the result persist/continue where appropriate?

## 4. Material stack

A premium dark object may use:

**ambient field → cast shadow → lower depth → main material → internal gradient → edge → top reflection → semantic light**

Not every component needs every layer.

Effects must remain subordinate to Visual Bliss.

## 5. Elevation tiers

Define consistent elevation tiers:
- **E0** background plane
- **E1** passive surface
- **E2** interactive surface
- **E3** selected/raised object
- **E4** temporary focus / inspector / portal
- **E5** critical modal/confirmation only

Do not invent arbitrary shadow depth per element.

## 6. Shape law

Shape may communicate purpose.

Use:
- rounded rectangle for dense functional objects;
- capsule for concise controls/status;
- jewel/faceted form for identity/state;
- radial/orbital form for central intelligence when semantically warranted.

Do not make every surface a rounded card.

## 7. State response

REST:
- quiet presence.

HOVER / APPROACH:
- recognition, slight lift, edge brightening.

FOCUS:
- unmistakable keyboard state.

ACTIVE / PRESS:
- physical compression.

LOADING / PROCESSING:
- state-driven energy, not fake busyness.

SUCCESS:
- brief resolved confirmation.

ERROR:
- immediate, readable recovery information.

DISABLED:
- visually non-live.

## 8. Environmental response

Major active boards may subtly influence their local environment:
- accent rail;
- nearby edge light;
- localized aura.

Do not tint the entire app every time a button is selected.

## 9. Physics test

A component is incomplete if it cannot answer:
- What material is this?
- Where does it sit?
- How do I know it can be touched?
- How do I know it noticed me?
- How do I know it acted?
- What changed?
- Will the result remain?

**DEPTH → LIGHT → STATE → RESPONSE → CONSEQUENCE → MEMORY**
