# 🥇 World-Champion Interface Review Law V1

## 1. Purpose

This is the final craft challenge.

It is deliberately severe.

The question is not:
"Does this look polished?"

The question is:
**"Would an elite product designer, industrial designer, accessibility expert, front-end engineer and ordinary human all find this unusually well made?"**

## 2. Five review lenses

### Human
Can I understand and use it without training?

### Artist
Is composition, rhythm, contrast, color and restraint exceptional?

### Industrial designer
Do objects feel physically coherent and intentionally engineered?

### Engineer
Can this quality remain consistent and maintainable?

### Proof reviewer
Does every claimed state correspond to observable truth?

## 3. The 50-point challenge

### Clarity
1. Is the purpose obvious?
2. Is current location obvious?
3. Is the next action obvious?
4. Is important text comfortably readable?
5. Is secondary text truly secondary?
6. Does the screen avoid unnecessary explanation?
7. Are technical details progressively disclosed?
8. Is uncertainty explicit?
9. Are privacy and authority states understandable?
10. Can a new user succeed without a manual?

### Composition
11. Is there a clear focal point?
12. Is alignment precise?
13. Is spacing intentional?
14. Does content breathe?
15. Is density appropriate to the room?
16. Are repeated structures rhythmically consistent?
17. Is anything awkwardly floating?
18. Is anything centered merely by habit?
19. Does the page feel balanced at multiple viewport sizes?
20. Would removing an element improve the composition?

### Craft
21. Do buttons feel engineered?
22. Do boards feel like destinations?
23. Are icons one family?
24. Are edges precise?
25. Are highlights restrained?
26. Are shadows believable?
27. Are gradients doing useful material work?
28. Is the spectrum semantic?
29. Are typography choices intentional?
30. Does every pixel look owned rather than defaulted?

### Interaction
31. Does hover acknowledge without distracting?
32. Does press feel physical?
33. Is keyboard focus excellent?
34. Does loading tell the truth?
35. Does success show the real consequence?
36. Can errors be recovered from?
37. Does back/reload preserve understandable state?
38. Are touch targets generous?
39. Does reduced motion retain meaning?
40. Is latency low enough that the system feels immediate?

### Distinction and trust
41. Could this interface belong to another company unchanged?
42. Does it avoid generic SaaS dashboard language?
43. Does Naya feel present without taking over?
44. Does the interface avoid fake AI theater?
45. Does it avoid vanity metrics?
46. Does it make evidence reachable?
47. Does it preserve privacy clarity?
48. Does it feel calm even when powerful?
49. Does it create at least one memorable "this is different" moment?
50. Is that memorable moment useful, not gimmicky?

## 4. Automatic rework triggers

Rework before approval if any of these occur:
- tiny text;
- low contrast;
- generic flat component replacement;
- excessive glow;
- random iconography;
- decorative animation;
- fake success;
- unclear active state;
- mobile visual collapse;
- keyboard invisibility;
- inaccessible color-only state;
- slow interaction;
- duplicated navigation;
- clutter added under the label "next level."

## 5. Competitive aspiration

The target is not to copy any admired company.

The target is to make NayaNET so coherent, distinctive and well-crafted that excellent design teams would recognize the care.

Borrow principles, not appearances.

The final product should look inevitable for NayaNET.

## 6. Final release question

> **Is this the strongest combination of clarity, beauty, intelligence, trust, physical presence and usefulness we know how to produce right now?**

If not, identify the highest-leverage gap and keep improving.
