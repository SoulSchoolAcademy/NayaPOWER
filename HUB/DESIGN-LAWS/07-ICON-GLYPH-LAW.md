# ◈ Intelligence Glyph & Icon Law V1

## 1. Prime law

NayaNET production icons are not emoji replacements.

They are **precision intelligence glyphs**.

## 2. One family

All primary production icons share:
- geometry logic;
- stroke philosophy;
- optical weight;
- facet language;
- perspective;
- corner treatment;
- depth behavior;
- highlight logic;
- semantic state behavior.

Do not mix:
- random emoji;
- unrelated icon packs;
- filled/outline styles arbitrarily;
- decorative illustrations as utility icons.

## 3. Visual character

Preferred character:
- faceted;
- jewel-like;
- technical;
- precise;
- modern;
- calm;
- luminous under interaction.

Not:
- cartoonish;
- clip-art;
- game-like;
- crypto-logo collage.

## 4. States

Every interactive glyph defines:
- rest;
- hover/aware;
- active/selected;
- focus;
- disabled;
- loading where applicable;
- success/error where applicable.

The icon itself should help communicate state.

## 5. Size system

Use a small standardized set, for example:
- 16px compact
- 20px utility
- 24px standard control
- 28–32px rail/board
- 40–64px identity/emblem

Optical size may vary slightly to equalize perceived weight.

## 6. Housing

Icons may sit inside:
- jewel housing;
- recessed well;
- illuminated node;
- plain inline area.

Housing should correspond to importance.

Not every icon needs a gemstone container.

## 7. Theme

A glyph may inherit a board/room theme.

At rest, keep energy controlled.

On selected/active state, the semantic light may intensify.

## 8. Accessibility

Icon-only controls require:
- accessible name;
- tooltip or equivalent when meaning is not obvious;
- clear focus state;
- sufficient target size.

Do not require users to memorize mystery symbols.

## 9. Production migration

Current unicode rail symbols are placeholders.

Replace them systematically, one family at a time, with visual comparison against the baseline.

Do not replace a distinctive placeholder with a generic icon that reduces character unless the new glyph is clearly superior.

## 10. Acceptance

An icon passes if:
- it looks like it belongs to NayaNET;
- its meaning is clear in context;
- its state is visible;
- it scales cleanly;
- it aligns optically;
- it does not depend on emoji rendering differences;
- it supports rather than competes with the label.
