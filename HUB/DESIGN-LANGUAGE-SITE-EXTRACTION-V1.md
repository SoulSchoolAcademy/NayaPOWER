## A1. The Serif Voice (new — not in the contract)

The corpus uses two typographic voices everywhere:

- **Authority voice:** extra-bold geometric sans, ALL CAPS for eyebrows and punch
  headlines, tight tracking, white.
- **Personal voice:** elegant serif italic, reserved for warmth — the "— Naya"
  signature, personal notes, emotional subheads.

**Law:** the serif italic voice is a first-class citizen. Every room that speaks *as
Naya* (reflections, personal notes, signatures) uses it. It never carries functional
labels, button text, or eyebrows — warmth, never chrome.

**Floor → ceiling:** floor = the two voices present and correctly assigned.
Ceiling = the serif voice given its own considered scale/color treatment per room,
never an afterthought.

## A2. The Eyebrow Component (new — named, not just observed)

Every site marks section identity the same way:

- Tiny uppercase sans, wide letter-spacing (~0.2em), gold (`--gold`) or dimmed white.
- "·" separators or dash prefixes (`— 14 · WHAT NAYA POWER TEACHES YOU`).
- Never a sentence, never body text (100-mistakes #21: eyebrows only).

**Law:** eyebrows are a locked component — `EyebrowLabel`: gold or muted, tracked-out,
one breath long. They answer "where am I" and nothing else.

## A3. The Two-Line CTA (new — specified)

Primary conversion buttons across the corpus share one pattern:

- Line 1: bold white promise ("START YOUR FREE TRIAL").
- Line 2: smaller, lighter supporting line ("ZERO COST TO START").
- Full-pill shape, dark fill, neon glowing border in the action's spectrum color.

**Law:** the two-line CTA is the standard for primary conversion actions. Line 1 =
the verb promise (≤5 words, 100-mistakes #13); line 2 = the risk remover. One
primary per view (100-mistakes #44).

## A4. Pill vs 13px Radius (director call needed)

- The contract's button recipe (§2.2) specifies `border-radius: 13px`.
- All six sites use full-pill (`999px`) for CTAs and interactive controls.

**Proposed resolution (CANDIDATE):** pills for CTAs and primary actions (the
corpus is unanimous); 13px retained for nav/rail buttons and dense controls
(the contract's recipe). One radius language per control class, never mixed in
one view (100-mistakes #84 covers icons; same discipline applies).

**Shawn decides.** This is the one visual call the corpus can't settle alone.

## A5. Floor-not-ceiling bindings (standing)

Every law above is a FLOOR. The ceiling checklist applies to all implementation:

1. Complete — zero stubs, placeholders, broken assets.
2. Bespoke icon art per room — one jewel family, eleven cuts.
3. Full state design per control — all 10 Button Law states real (the sites'
   hovers were barely perceptible; that is below the floor).
4. Hand-tuned glow — LAW ZERO: the moment glow touches legibility, the glow goes.
5. No template repetition across sections or rooms.
6. ~30 machine-checkable items from the 100 mistakes compiled into the rendered gate.
