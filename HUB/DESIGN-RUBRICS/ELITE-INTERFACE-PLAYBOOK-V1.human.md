# The Elite Interface Playbook — Screen-Scoring Rubric V1

**Status:** PROPOSED — CANDIDATE. **Awaiting Shawn Vibert's ratification before it becomes law.**

## What this is, in plain words

This is Shawn's own checklist — the 100-point rubric he wrote for designing, reviewing, and scoring every screen — turned into a living, machine-readable standard for NayaNET. It is the direct ancestor of the team's 9.0 auto-approval bar: 90 out of 100 here IS the 9.0 bar.

## What it would change

If ratified, every Hub screen gets scored against these 100 checkpoints before it ships. Nothing below 90/100 goes to Shawn. It gives every builder — human or Naya — the exact same rubric the director uses in his head.

## What changed from Shawn's original (and why)

Shawn wrote the Playbook for a bright-white interface. The team's standing color standard (2026-10-04) moved to a black canvas. So the **color specifics** from the original are updated, not the mechanics:

- **Canvas is black**, purple is accent and glow only (never solid fill), buttons are black with purple edge glow lighting up white.
- **Emerald for success** stands. Warning is **gold**; the error color stays open — it must come from the allowed palette (black, white, purple, blues, green, gold), and error states must always pair an icon + text, never color alone.
- The timeless parts — contrast, tokens, focus rings, spacing, motion budgets, accessibility, the one-click rule, consistency over novelty — are unchanged.

## The six conflict resolutions this file honors

1. Black canvas wins over the Playbook's white canvas — mechanics stand, color specifics superseded.
2. Amber and rose-pink state colors are banned — re-mapped inside the allowed palette (Emerald success stands, gold warns).
3. Mockups are legitimate exploration tools — but must be labeled as mockups and never presented as the real artifact.
4. The bias-to-shipped instinct applies *below* Shawn's human gates; production deploys, dispatches, and the database stay his explicit gates; nothing below 9.0 is shown to him.
5. Voice responses carry essence WITH story, metaphor, and action — compact, never walls of text.
6. Dated SmartNET-era items (old nav names, DID/soul-ID/SSO, SmartCoin, offline-by-design) are marked north-star, not current — the current identity page is device-local, never a verified credential.

## The final check

**90 out of 100 or rework.** That was Shawn's rule in the Reminders; it is the team's rule today. This file just writes it down in all three languages.
