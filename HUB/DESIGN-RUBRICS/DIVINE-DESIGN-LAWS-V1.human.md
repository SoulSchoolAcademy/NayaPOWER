# The 100 Divine Laws of Design — V1

**Status:** PROPOSED — CANDIDATE. **Awaiting Shawn Vibert's ratification before it becomes law.**

## What this is, in plain words

Shawn's 100 design commandments — the laws that make an app feel divine: awe, clarity, speed, trust, intelligence, community, emotion, and purpose. This file keeps the timeless ones as current design law and honestly marks the future ones as north-star, not current.

## The final law (the one that rules them all)

> **The app must feel like magic — but run like math.**

Everything below serves that sentence. If a law ever conflicts with it, the sentence wins.

## The ten sections, in plain words

1. **Visual laws (1–10)** — design for awe at first glance. Color is consciousness: purple = power, blue = trust, gold = divinity. Use real faces, real people — Naya's own picture welcomes the user by name. *(The white-canvas implication is superseded by the black-canvas standard; the color psychology stands.)*
2. **Intuitive UX laws (11–20)** — the user always knows three things: what is this, why does it matter, what do I do now. Three clicks or less. Onboarding is activation — the first moments are delight plus clarity.
3. **Performance laws (21–30)** — screens in under a second, zero lag, fail gracefully with love and a solution. *(Offline-by-design is a north-star aspiration, not current reality.)*
4. **Trust laws (31–40)** — the user's data belongs to the user. Transparent permissions, no silent tracking, everything undoable. *(DID, soul-ID, and SSO are north stars — the current identity page is a device-local profile, never a verified credential. Nothing here may imply otherwise.)*
5. **AI + intelligence laws (41–50)** — Naya reflects the user's tone and vibe, teaches as she serves, never answers generically. Voice is an interface. Responses carry story, metaphor, and action — distilled to essence, never walls of text.
6. **Backend laws (51–60)** — modular, realtime, transparently logged. Every backend action leaves a receipt — matching the team's evidence culture.
7. **Social + viral laws (61–70)** — everything shareable; engineer Magic Moments worth posting. The Reveal pop-up is the first-entry Magic Moment. **Demonstrate, don't promote** — the system markets itself when the results are real.
8. **Monetization laws (71–80)** — product-direction laws, not current build scope. SmartCoin mechanics are future.
9. **Emotional design laws (81–90)** — delight is the goal; speak like a friend; uplift, never shame. **Build intimacy, not addiction** — engagement mechanics must be intimacy-building, never addiction loops. This constrains all gamification.
10. **Cosmic purpose laws (91–100)** — every feature uplifts human potential. LOVE, TRUTH, PURPOSE, POWER.

## What it would change

If ratified, these commandments govern every design decision the way the constitution governs every agent decision. A design review would ask: does this feel like magic? Does it run like math? Does it answer the three clarity questions? Does it build intimacy, not addiction?

## What is honestly NOT law yet

DID, soul-ID, SSO, the SmartCoin economy, offline-by-design, and the SmartNET-era product surface are marked **north-star** in the machine twin. They are direction, not claims. The current identity page creates a device-local profile — this file never lets anyone imply otherwise.
