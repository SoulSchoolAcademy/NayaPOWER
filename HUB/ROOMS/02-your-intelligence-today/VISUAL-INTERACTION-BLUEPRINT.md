# Your Intelligence Today — Visual / Interaction Blueprint V1

**Purpose:** Explain the day in one beautiful, evidence-grounded intelligence story.

## First 3 seconds
The user sees:
- date + Space + freshness;
- **Today in a Nutshell**;
- a compact **Today Pulse**;
- the first major highlight.

## Desktop composition
```
[GLOBAL SEARCH]
[Your Intelligence Today] [Date] [Space] [Freshness]

[ TODAY IN A NUTSHELL — one sentence ]

[ TODAY PULSE ]
 events | discoveries | learning | decisions | reports

[ WHAT CHANGED ]
  Highlight Scene 1
  Highlight Scene 2
  Highlight Scene 3

[ NAYA'S REFLECTION — evidence-grounded ]

[ OPEN LOOPS / CARRY INTO TOMORROW ]

[ View this week in Reports ]
```

## Primary instrument
The **Daily Intelligence Story**.

## Controls + placement
Header:
- Today / Yesterday / date picker
- Space selector

Highlight:
- Open Origin
- Evidence
- Ask Naya
- Save/List
- Open in Feed
- Day in Ledger

End:
- View this week in Reports

No capture/follow-up Smart Note control.

## Data
Consumes same canonical objects projected from:
- Smart Notes;
- Activity;
- Reports;
- decisions/learning/evidence.

## Selection logic
Highlights must be explainable by:
importance, verified change, goals, decisions, learning, consequence, unresolved risk/opportunity.

## States
Quiet day is valid:
“No major changes today. Here are the two things worth remembering.”

## Mobile
Story becomes vertical full-screen sequence.
Sticky compact date/Space.
Evidence opens as sheet.

## Do not
- do not become Feed with bigger cards;
- do not manufacture highlights;
- do not use giant KPI tiles as decoration.

## Proof
User understands the day in ~10 seconds and can trace every highlight to source.
