# Your Intelligence Today — Human Spec

## What this room is

Your daily highlight reel — the fastest, most beautiful way to understand what actually mattered today.

## What you see

A day nutshell, real Today Pulse, the biggest changes, discoveries/learning/decisions, the underlying intelligence stream and Naya's evidence-based reflection.

## What you can do

Explore the day, ask Naya, open any highlight in its original room, inspect evidence, save/list it, capture a follow-up and inspect consequential activity in Ledger.

## How it connects

Today synthesizes Feed/Activity and can hand evidence to Reports, Library, Lists, Connections and Ledger. Reports turn many Todays into longer-term meaning.

## What it should feel like

Like opening a world-class highlight film of your own intelligence — clear, surprising, useful and deeply personal without being noisy.

**Current status:** proposed specification; not proof that runtime functionality is live.
