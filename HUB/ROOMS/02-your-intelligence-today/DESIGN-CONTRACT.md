# Your Intelligence Today — Design Contract V1

**Status:** PROPOSED · REVIEW OPEN
**Parent:** `HUB/DESIGN-CONTRACT.md`
**Metaphor:** **THE HIGHLIGHT REEL**
**Theme:** Magenta — human significance, daily synthesis, reflected meaning

## Creative intent

This should be the most beautiful room in NayaNET. It is not a dashboard; it is a premium daily intelligence story that lets the human understand the day in seconds, then descend into evidence.

## Center-workspace composition

Opening frame: date/context + one-sentence nutshell. Then Today Pulse as a sculpted metric ribbon, not KPI cards. Then a horizontal/stacked WHAT CHANGED narrative. Then the three-mode intelligence stream. End with a large Naya Reflection and carry-forward/open-loops section.

## Signature instrument

The daily highlight reel — a guided sequence of meaningful moments with reasons, evidence and next moves.

## Visual hierarchy

Nutshell first; meaningful change second; underlying stream third; Naya synthesis and tomorrow carry-forward close the story. Counts support the story, never dominate it.

## Living-depth expression

Magenta energy marks human significance; each highlight has its own semantic accent for discovery/learning/decision without breaking the room's magenta identity.

## Motion / liveness

Optional guided 'Explore Today' sequence may advance through highlights with cinematic but restrained scene continuity. Reduced-motion keeps the narrative intact.

## Naya presence

Naya synthesizes what matters most today, identifies missed signals/open loops and explains why each highlight was selected. No motivational filler.

## States

LOADING · EMPTY · READY · BLOCKED · UNAUTHORIZED · NOT_VERIFIED · VERIFIED · ERROR · OFFLINE · UNKNOWN · DISABLED.

Each state must preserve the room's identity; no generic error card replacing the room.

## Responsive

Full-screen story chapters with compact sticky date/Space. Swipe/scroll through highlights; evidence opens as a sheet; Naya Reflection remains readable and substantial.

## Must never become

- generic daily dashboard
- vanity pulse scores
- fabricated metrics
- canned inspirational reflection
- duplicating the whole Feed
- ten tiny KPI cards

## Non-regression anchors

- day-as-story model
- real canonical counts only
- Collective/Personal/Activity meaning
- highlight→origin/evidence links
- quiet days may stay quiet

## Design acceptance

A reviewer should be able to identify this room with the NayaNET logo removed, understand where they are, what matters, what action is available, and why the information can be trusted within seconds.
