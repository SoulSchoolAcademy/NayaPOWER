# 02 — Your Intelligence Today

**Metaphor:** THE HIGHLIGHT REEL  
**Route:** `/today`  
**Theme:** magenta  
**Human question:** **What mattered today?**

## Human promise

Give me the day in one beautiful intelligent presentation.

Not another feed. Not a pile of cards.

This room takes the living game occurring in Smart Feed and turns it into the **best highlight reel of the day**: what happened, what changed, what mattered, what I learned, what remains open and what Naya thinks deserves my attention.

## Signature visual

The room should feel like opening a premium **daily intelligence story**.

### Opening frame
- date + current Space;
- one-sentence “Today in a nutshell”;
- subtle day arc / timeline;
- current truth freshness (“updated 4 min ago”, “runtime offline”, etc.).

### Highlight reel
A sequence of the day's highest-value moments, each as a cinematic intelligence scene:
- **What happened**
- **Why it mattered**
- **What changed**
- **Source / evidence**
- **What it connects to**
- **Next useful action**

Highlights may be grouped naturally:
**Morning · Midday · Afternoon · Evening** or by meaningful chapters when time grouping adds little.

### End-of-day intelligence
- **Biggest change**
- **Best insight / learning**
- **Decision(s) made**
- **People / connections that mattered**
- **Open loops**
- **What Naya would carry into tomorrow**

The room should feel complete even if only one or two meaningful things happened.

## Highlight selection

A highlight is not simply “popular” or “recent.”

Candidate scoring should consider:
- human importance;
- verified change;
- active goals/context;
- decisions;
- learning;
- consequence;
- unresolved risk/opportunity;
- explicit human saves/flags.

Naya should be able to explain **why each item made the highlights**.

## Primary actions

- **Play / Explore Today** — step through highlights sequentially
- **Ask Naya about today**
- **Open in Feed**
- **Open Source / Evidence**
- **Save / Add to List**
- **Capture follow-up note**
- **View day in Ledger** for consequential activity

“Play” means guided presentation; it does not imply video.

## Time navigation

- Today
- Yesterday
- date picker
- “Previous meaningful day”
- optional compare with yesterday / last week only when useful

## Relationship to Reports

Today = one day's story.  
Reports = synthesis across many days/weeks/months.

A button at the end:
**View this week in Reports**

## Empty / quiet day

A quiet day can be beautiful:
“No major changes today. Here are the two things worth remembering.”

Do not manufacture highlights to fill space.

## Mobile

Full-screen story/chapters with swipe/scroll. Sticky compact date/context. Evidence opens as a sheet without breaking the story.

## Acceptance journey

A human can:
1. open Today and understand the day within ~10 seconds;
2. move through highlights;
3. understand why each matters;
4. jump to original Feed object;
5. inspect evidence;
6. ask Naya about the whole day;
7. leave with clear open loops / tomorrow carry-forward.

## Human Director source-note reconciliation

The Drive notes add required structure: a **TODAY PULSE** using only real canonical counts (intelligence events, discoveries, learning signals, decisions, creations); a large **WHAT CHANGED** narrative with Discovery / Learning / Decision highlights; **YOUR INTELLIGENCE STREAM** with COLLECTIVE / PERSONAL / ACTIVITY as genuine modes; and a large **NAYA'S REFLECTION — What matters most today** generated from evidence, never canned inspiration. The center workspace transforms into this room while the sidebar and Naya right rail remain.

## Shared five-layer mapping

- **ORIENTATION:** date, Space, freshness and 'your intelligence as it exists right now'
- **CURRENT STATE:** Today Pulse plus actual day activity
- **INTELLIGENCE:** What Changed highlights and evidence-grounded Naya Reflection
- **ACTION:** explore day, open origin, ask, save/list, follow-up note, day Ledger
- **PROOF:** every highlight and pulse number traces to canonical evidence
