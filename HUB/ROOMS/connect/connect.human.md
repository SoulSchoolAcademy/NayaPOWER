# Smart Connect — Design Contract (Human)

**Status:** PROPOSED — team review → scorecard → lock. **Build order:** 4.

## The room in one line

The atrium of doors: where your intelligence connects to the world — every crossing governed, every status honest. (Smart Share was too narrow a name; connection is the real feature.)

## Entering the room

The entrance atrium. Teal light flows like water toward a gallery of **doors** — nine portals, each a distinct jewel-object: GitHub, MCP, REST API, Webhooks, SDK, A2A, MCP Apps, AI Connect, Data Connect. Each shows its live truth: CONNECTED / AUTHENTICATED / AUTHORIZED / HEALTHY — or honestly shows what's missing.

## The five layers

**1. ORIENTATION.** "CONNECT YOUR INTELLIGENCE" + *"Your intelligence stays yours. Connect only what you choose."*

**2. CURRENT STATE.** The door gallery: nine registry doors as portal-objects with status seals. Below: the consent center — what of yours is shared, with whom, under what consent, each grant with its lineage.

**3. INTELLIGENCE.** Per-door: what this door can do, what it currently may do (LAW's verdict), relationship context (who/what is on the other side).

**4. ACTION.** Per-door CONNECT → CONFIGURE → VERIFY. Consent toggles with plain words. Share flows arrive here with previews.

**5. PROOF.** The four-light status per door (CONNECTED ✓ / AUTHENTICATED ✓ / AUTHORIZED ✓ / HEALTHY ✓ — or the honest gap, e.g. AUTHORIZATION — NOT GRANTED). Every grant shows lineage; every revocation is instant and recorded.

## Room grammar

- **HERO:** title + the ownership line.
- **INTELLIGENCE WALL:** door gallery (the room's signature visual).
- **ACTION DECK:** per-door Connect/Configure/Verify, consent toggles, share previews.
- **EVIDENCE LAYER:** four-light statuses, consent lineage, revocation receipts.
- **NAYA LAYER:** "Which door do I need?" — Naya recommends from the need, never upsells.

## Controls

- **Per-door CONNECT** (primo teal, on each portal): opens the real flow — CONNECT → CONFIGURE → VERIFY. No ambiguous "connected."
- **Four-light status** (per door): the governance philosophy as UX.
- **Consent toggles:** physical switches with plain words ("Naya may learn from my notes"), plus *Revoke* with instant effect.
- **"Share to collective"** (arrives from other rooms): always a preview — what, with whom, reversible how — before anything leaves.

**Open design decision for team review:** the atrium also proposed as a special feature block directly above Settings in the sidebar (slightly larger physical card announcing CONNECT YOUR INTELLIGENCE). Scorecard both placements.

## Truth rules

- **CONNECTED ≠ AUTHORIZED**, engraved and enforced.
- Doors mirror the canonical registry (`BRAIN/10-INTERFACES/0002-SMART-DOOR-REGISTRY-V1.json`) — never an app-invented list.
- Non-registry ideas live on an explicit roadmap board, never as READY doors.
- No control promises capability that isn't live.

## Connections

- **Powers:** Mail (channels), Spaces (membership), Connections (people). **Shares flow out through here** from every room. **Revocations ripple** everywhere. **Receipts to:** Ledger.
