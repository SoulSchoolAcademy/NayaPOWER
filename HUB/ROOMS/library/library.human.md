# Intelligent Library — Design Contract (Human)

**Status:** PROPOSED — team review → scorecard → lock. **Build order:** 3.

## The room in one line

Walking into your own mind: the canonical knowledge layer that answers what it is, why it matters, how it works, how to use it, where the source is, and what's related.

## Entering the room

The grand library. Dark obsidian shelves with lime edge-light. Search is the first thing you see — large, glowing subtly — because the Library answers questions. Quiet and certain: everything here is canonical, sourced, related.

## The five layers

**1. ORIENTATION.** "INTELLIGENT LIBRARY" + *"Everything you know. Everything you're learning. Everything still unresolved."* + hero search ("Search your intelligence…").

**2. CURRENT STATE — Featured Intelligence.** Three huge cards: MOST RELEVANT (what matters now), RECENTLY DEVELOPED (what changed recently), NEEDS ATTENTION (knowledge gaps / unresolved questions).

**3. INTELLIGENCE — Your Knowledge.** The domain collection (building on the 58-domain foundation — rich cards, not tiny tiles). Each domain card: name, intelligence-record count, developing count, unresolved count, "updated <time>." Clicking a domain turns the center into a domain-specific intelligence room: What I know · What I've learned · What I'm uncertain about · Related intelligence · Recent discoveries · Smart Notes · Questions — plus "ASK NAYA: What am I missing here?"

**4. ACTION.** Search, shelf filters, perspective tabs (Human / Simplified / Naya's take / Evidence), Save to list.

**5. PROOF.** Reading view = the full Smart Note board: identity jewel, title, "In a Nutshell," perspective layers, provenance footer. Every record sourced.

## Room grammar

- **HERO:** title + tagline + hero search.
- **INTELLIGENCE WALL:** featured cards + domain cards (rich, jewel-spined).
- **ACTION DECK:** filters, perspective tabs, Save to list, Ask Naya.
- **EVIDENCE LAYER:** provenance footers, source links, verification states.
- **NAYA LAYER:** "what am I missing here" per domain; Naya's take perspective.

## Controls

- **Search** (hero, the room's crown jewel): instant results with truth seals.
- **Domain cards:** rich cards with counts + freshness; click → domain room.
- **Perspective tabs** (reading view): Human · Simplified · Naya's take · Evidence.
- **"Save to list"** (primo lime): files by pointer, never copies.
- **"Ask Naya — what am I missing here?"** (domain view): gap analysis from real records.

## Truth rules

- Canonical only: every record has a source and verification state.
- Domains show honest counts; empty domains say so.
- "Needs attention" is computed from real gaps, never engagement bait.

## Connections

- **Reference for:** every room ("learn more" lands here). **Cited by:** Feed cards, Today highlights. **Collected by:** Lists (pointers). **Gaps feed:** Lists/Questions.
