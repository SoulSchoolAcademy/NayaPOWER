# Your Intelligence Today — Design Contract (Human)

**Status:** PROPOSED — team review → scorecard → lock. **Build order:** 1 (first).

## The room in one line

The most beautiful room in NayaNET: your daily intelligence cockpit — not a dashboard, the highlights reel of your intelligence as it exists right now.

## Entering the room

The briefing room. Sapphire calm on the obsidian foundation. Instead of a river of everything, there's a *reel* — today's story laid out like a highlights package: the moments that defined the day, each presented cinematically, each true. This is the room Shawn opens every morning, and it should feel like the day already makes sense.

## The five layers

**1. ORIENTATION.** Hero: "YOUR INTELLIGENCE TODAY" in huge type, the date beneath, and one sentence: *"Your intelligence as it exists right now. What you discovered. What you learned. What you created. What changed."*

**2. CURRENT STATE — the Today Pulse.** Not a generic score. Five large honest numbers, each from canonical data: INTELLIGENCE EVENTS · DISCOVERIES · LEARNING SIGNALS · DECISIONS · CREATIONS. If the data isn't there, the pulse says "quiet day" instead of inventing.

**3. INTELLIGENCE — What Changed.** A large horizontal intelligence narrative: *"Three things became clearer today."* Then three major cards — DISCOVERY ("You connected X with Y"), LEARNING ("You developed a stronger understanding of Z"), DECISION ("You chose to…"). Each card expands into its evidence.

**4. ACTION.** Your Intelligence Stream with three tabs — COLLECTIVE | PERSONAL | ACTIVITY — and they genuinely change the center content: Collective = network intelligence, Personal = private intelligence, Activity = operational history. Collective is the default. Plus per-card actions with real consequences (save, share via Connect, ask Naya).

**5. PROOF — Naya's Reflection.** A beautiful large panel at the bottom: "What matters most today," then Naya's actual synthesis — generated from evidence, never fake inspirational text. Every claim in it links to its source.

## Room grammar

- **HERO:** title + date + the one-sentence day + Today Pulse.
- **INTELLIGENCE WALL:** the "What Changed" narrative + three major cards.
- **ACTION DECK:** the stream tabs + per-card actions + "Catch me up."
- **EVIDENCE LAYER:** every number, card, and reflection claim carries a truth seal and expands to provenance.
- **NAYA LAYER:** Naya's Reflection panel — interpretation, clearly marked as hers.

## Controls

- **"Play the day"** (hero, primo sapphire button, 56px): a guided tour through the highlights, one beat at a time. The signature interaction of the room.
- **Mode tabs** (Collective | Personal | Activity): large segmented primo control; switching genuinely re-queries, not filters.
- **Per-card:** *Open evidence* (drill-down), *Save to list*, *Share* (→ Connect with consent preview), *Ask Naya*.
- **"What did I miss?"** (quiet, bottom): answers from evidence or honestly says "nothing found."
- **Day scrubber** (top): yesterday / today.

## Truth rules

- Never manufactures daily facts. A quiet day is presented as a quiet day.
- Every pulse number binds to a canonical count or is marked UNKNOWN.
- Naya's Reflection is labeled as synthesis; its claims link to evidence.
- Collective/Personal/Activity obey their privacy rules — no leakage between modes.

## Connections

- **Synthesizes from:** Smart Feed (all three modes).
- **Feeds into:** Reports (yesterday's Today becomes a Daily edition).
- **Drills into:** Library (concepts), Ledger (decision receipts), Lists (save a highlight).
- **Shares through:** Connect.
