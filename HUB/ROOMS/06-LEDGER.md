## 1. HUMAN JOB

**Inspect what happened and what proves it**

### The room must answer

- **WHERE AM I?** Smart Ledger
- **WHAT MATTERS?** Consequential activity, authority and evidence.
- **WHAT CAN I DO?** Inspect an event and its proof.
- **WHAT HAPPENS NEXT?** The visitor can understand what happened, under whose authority, and what proves it.

### Time-horizon test

**1 second:** The visitor immediately recognizes this as the evidence/flight-recorder room.  
**3 seconds:** They understand which events matter and can open one.  
**30 seconds:** They can trace an event from outcome to authority to proof.  
**Return visit:** Search/filter context and inspection position remain coherent.

## 7. UNIVERSAL STATE MATRIX

- keyboard-only navigation;
- assistive technology / screen readers;
- zoom up to 200%;
- reduced-motion preferences;
- high-contrast / accessibility settings;
