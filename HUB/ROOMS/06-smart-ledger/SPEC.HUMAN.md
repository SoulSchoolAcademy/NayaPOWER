# Smart Ledger — Human Spec

## What this room is
The place where NayaNET shows its work: what happened, who/what initiated it, what authority existed, what the result was and what evidence proves it.

## What you see
A live causal timeline. Each event can expand from a simple human explanation into authority, receipt, result and verification detail.

## What you can do
Filter activity, inspect receipts/evidence, open the related person/intelligence/Space, ask Naya to explain, export proof where supported or use a governed recovery action if one actually exists.

## How it connects
Every consequential room can link into Ledger. Ledger links back to the original intelligence, connection, message, Space or door instead of becoming a second data store.

## What it should feel like
Like entering a calm, beautiful black-box room where the truth is inspectable instead of hidden in backend logs.

**Current status:** proposed specification; not proof that runtime functionality is live.
