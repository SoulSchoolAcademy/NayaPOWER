# Smart Ledger — Design Contract (Human)

**Status:** PROPOSED — team review → scorecard → lock. **Build order:** 5.

## The room in one line

The truth room: the durable, human-readable record of what happened — not accounting, not logs.

## Entering the room

Down a level, the noise stops. The vault: quiet obsidian, gold used only as hairlines and wax seals — never washes, never text. Every consequential thing that ever happened in NayaNET is here as a receipt. A bank vault designed by a jeweler: absolute trust, zero theater.

## The five layers

**1. ORIENTATION.** "SMART LEDGER" + *"The durable record of what happened."*

**2. CURRENT STATE — Live Timeline.** Large chronological entries: time, event ("SMART NOTE CREATED — 'Why Naya should…'"), SOURCE (Shawn), AUTHORITY (Human), RESULT (Persisted), RECEIPT (SN-8F91…). Plus the standing strip: current balances/value positions in plain numbers.

**3. INTELLIGENCE.** Entries grouped by meaning, not just time: what this pattern of activity says. Every record distinguishes RECEIVED / AUTHORIZED / EXECUTED / VERIFIED — the NayaNET lifecycle as UX.

**4. ACTION.** Filter (ALL / ACTIONS / DECISIONS / SHARES / CONNECTIONS / SYSTEM). Per-receipt: expand proof, Verify (re-check live). Nothing here is authored — that absence is the point.

**5. PROOF.** The proof drawer per receipt: full evidence chain, authority, timestamps. "Verify" re-seals or honestly reports a gap.

## Room grammar

- **HERO:** title + tagline + standing strip.
- **INTELLIGENCE WALL:** the live timeline.
- **ACTION DECK:** filters + per-receipt Verify.
- **EVIDENCE LAYER:** proof drawers, lifecycle states.
- **NAYA LAYER:** "Explain this receipt" — Naya narrates what a receipt means in plain words.

## Controls

- **Receipt cards:** tap to expand the proof drawer.
- **Filter pills** (ALL / ACTIONS / DECISIONS / SHARES / CONNECTIONS / SYSTEM): slim.
- **"Verify"** (per receipt, quiet gold-seal button): re-checks evidence live, re-seals — or reports a gap.
- **"Explain"** (per receipt): Naya's plain-words narration.

## Truth rules

- Never fabricates a value, outcome, transaction, or receipt.
- If the runtime can't prove it, it isn't here.
- Lifecycle states (RECEIVED/AUTHORIZED/EXECUTED/VERIFIED) always visible; never implied.

## Connections

- **Records from:** every room (shares granted, reports generated, doors authorized, mail sent, notes created). **Cited by:** Reports (value claims). **Explains:** anything consequential anywhere.
