# Smart Mail — Design Contract (Human)

**Status:** PROPOSED — team review → scorecard → lock. **Build order:** 8.

## The room in one line

Communication intelligence — not an inbox: every thread with its context, and Naya drafts but never sends without your authority.

## Entering the room

The correspondence room in deep indigo. Threads lie on the desk like letters — each a real conversation with real provenance. The right panel holds the context: who this is, why it matters, what came before.

## The five layers

**1. ORIENTATION.** "SMART MAIL" + box tabs: IMPORTANT · RESPOND · FOLLOW UP · DRAFTS · SENT.

**2. CURRENT STATE.** Thread list with presence (who, last message, truth/authority state).

**3. INTELLIGENCE.** Per-thread context panel: who is this, why does this matter, previous relevant interaction, related intelligence, potential response.

**4. ACTION.** Reply, archive, "Ask Naya to draft" — drafts arrive clearly marked; **Naya never sends without the required authority.** Compose is a clean writing desk.

**5. PROOF.** Threads bound to real messages; authority-relevant threads show authorization state; sent items leave Ledger receipts.

## Room grammar

- **HERO:** title + box tabs.
- **INTELLIGENCE WALL:** thread list + reading pane.
- **ACTION DECK:** reply/archive/draft/compose/send.
- **EVIDENCE LAYER:** message provenance, authority states, sent receipts.
- **NAYA LAYER:** thread briefs ("what matters in this thread"), draft responses.

## Controls

- **Box tabs** (IMPORTANT · RESPOND · FOLLOW UP · DRAFTS · SENT): primo segmented.
- **Compose** (primo indigo): the writing desk.
- **Per-thread:** *Reply*, *Archive*, *Ask Naya to brief*.
- **"Ask Naya to draft"** (reading pane): draft appears marked DRAFT-BY-NAYA.
- **Send** (compose): always yours. No send happens without your press.

## Truth rules

- Real threads only; no demo inbox.
- Drafts vs. sent strictly distinguished.
- Authority-required threads show their state before you act.

## Connections

- **Channels from:** Connect doors. **Correspondents:** Connections. **Sent receipts to:** Ledger. **Related intelligence:** Library.
