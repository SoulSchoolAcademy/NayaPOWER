# Smart Feed — Human Spec

## What this room is

The live game of your intelligence — new understanding, activity, people, messages, decisions and connected intelligence as it happens.

## What you see

A living stream you can switch between Collective, Personal and Activity. Every object tells you why it is there and where it came from.

## What you can do

Open, ask Naya, save, add to a list, capture a follow-up, inspect evidence, or connect/share when authorized.

## How it connects

Feed objects can become Today highlights, report evidence, Library results, List items, Space activity, Mail context or Ledger proof without becoming copies.

## What it should feel like

Alive, relevant and calm — more like watching your intelligence organize itself than scrolling a social feed.

**Current status:** proposed specification; not proof that runtime functionality is live.
