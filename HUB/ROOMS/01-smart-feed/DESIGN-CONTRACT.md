# Smart Feed — Design Contract V1

**Status:** PROPOSED · REVIEW OPEN
**Parent:** `HUB/DESIGN-CONTRACT.md`
**Metaphor:** **THE GAME**
**Theme:** Emerald — live flow, healthy activity, current intelligence

## Creative intent

Make the current intelligence system feel alive without imitating social media. The human should feel motion, relevance and context, not algorithmic noise.

## Center-workspace composition

A slim orientation header and mode selector sit above one continuous vertical intelligence river. Objects vary in emphasis by meaning/relevance while maintaining calm reading rhythm. Filters stay progressive, not permanently spread across the canvas.

## Signature instrument

The living intelligence stream — PERSONAL / COLLECTIVE / ACTIVITY materially change the data and interpretation.

## Visual hierarchy

Current mode and new-since-last-visit are immediately legible. Intelligence objects dominate. Provenance/truth is always available but visually secondary until needed.

## Living-depth expression

Emerald semantic energy appears around active/live intelligence. Important objects gain stronger edge/elevation rather than larger decorative gradients.

## Motion / liveness

New verified intelligence may enter with restrained state-linked motion. No fake pulsing stream. Scroll restoration and return position matter.

## Naya presence

Naya explains 'why am I seeing this?', summarizes clusters, exposes uncertainty and suggests next useful actions.

## States

LOADING · EMPTY · READY · BLOCKED · UNAUTHORIZED · NOT_VERIFIED · VERIFIED · ERROR · OFFLINE · UNKNOWN · DISABLED.

Each state must preserve the room's identity; no generic error card replacing the room.

## Responsive

One full-width stream; mode control remains sticky/reachable; object actions open in an accessible bottom sheet; provenance never disappears.

## Must never become

- social-media clone
- engagement bait or vanity metrics
- mysterious ranking with no explanation
- fake live motion
- dense filter dashboard

## Non-regression anchors

- Personal / Collective / Activity distinction
- prominent intelligence provenance
- one canonical object identity
- same Hub shell/Naya

## Design acceptance

A reviewer should be able to identify this room with the NayaNET logo removed, understand where they are, what matters, what action is available, and why the information can be trusted within seconds.
