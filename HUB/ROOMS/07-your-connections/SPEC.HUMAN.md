# Your Connections — Human Spec

## What this room is
A living map of the people, Nayas, organizations and shared contexts that actually matter to your intelligence.

## What you see
A clear relationship map/list and a rich selected-connection view: what the relationship is, what you share, recent interaction, important context and the next relevant action.

## What you can do
Open a connection, message them, enter a shared Space, inspect shared intelligence, invite/connect, adjust sharing or revoke/disconnect when allowed.

## How it connects
Mail handles communication, Spaces hold shared contexts, Feed/Library show shared intelligence, Connect shows technical channels and Ledger proves changes.

## What it should feel like
Like seeing the meaningful constellation around you — not managing a contact database.

**Current status:** proposed specification; not proof that runtime functionality is live.
