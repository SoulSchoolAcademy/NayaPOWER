# Your Connections — Design Contract V1

**Status:** PROPOSED · REVIEW OPEN
**Parent:** `HUB/DESIGN-CONTRACT.md`
**Metaphor:** **THE CONSTELLATION**
**Theme:** Orange — relationship movement, collaboration, human/system connection

## Creative intent
Make relationships feel alive and intelligible without sacrificing clarity to graph spectacle.

## Center-workspace composition
A restrained constellation/list selector establishes context. The dominant experience is the selected connection intelligence panel with relationship, shared intelligence, projects/Spaces, recent interaction, context and next action.

## Signature instrument
Relationship constellation + selected-connection intelligence inspector.

## Visual hierarchy
Selected relationship meaning first; graph second. Consent/sharing state is always inspectable. Pending requests never blend into active connections.

## Living-depth expression
Orange energy can trace selected relationship paths; unselected nodes remain calm. Strong depth goes to the selected connection, not every node.

## Motion / liveness
Node/connection transitions should preserve mental map when changing categories or selection. No constant orbital spiderweb motion.

## Naya presence
Naya explains why a connection matters, summarizes shared context and suggests a next relevant action without inventing social closeness or permission.

## States
LOADING · EMPTY · READY · BLOCKED · UNAUTHORIZED · NOT_VERIFIED · VERIFIED · ERROR · OFFLINE · UNKNOWN · DISABLED.

## Responsive
List-first by default; constellation optional. Selected connection opens as a full intelligence profile with actions.

## Must never become
- contacts app clone
- giant useless spiderweb
- hidden consent scope
- suggested people with no legitimate basis
- connection == authority

## Non-regression anchors
- relationship intelligence over address-book data
- consent/sharing visibility
- selected connection context
- graph as supporting visualization
- cross-room relationship links

## Design acceptance
The room must be unmistakably NayaNET, make the signature instrument obvious, and preserve real state/proof without generic-dashboard drift.
