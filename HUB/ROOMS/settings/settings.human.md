# Settings — Design Contract (Human)

**Status:** PROPOSED — team review → scorecard → lock. **Build order:** 10 (hardened throughout).

## The room in one line

The quiet engine room: identity, privacy, authority, and your intelligence — elite, uncluttered, explaining rather than hiding.

## Entering the room

The engine room. No spectral color — white on obsidian, plain and honest like a well-made instrument panel. This room doesn't perform; it explains. Every switch tells you what it does before you touch it.

## The five layers

**1. ORIENTATION.** "SETTINGS" + section nav: IDENTITY · PRIVACY · AUTHORITY · CONNECTIONS · NOTIFICATIONS · DATA · SECURITY · APPEARANCE.

**2. CURRENT STATE.** Session truth cards: who Naya believes you are here; what's live, what's local, what's unknown; connection health per integration.

**3. INTELLIGENCE — Your Intelligence.** The special section: *"Your intelligence is yours."* Controls for privacy, sharing, collective participation, retention, export, deletion — each with plain-words consequences.

**4. ACTION.** Switches with explanations, "Test connection," type size / motion / density preferences, export/delete flows (with the gravity they deserve).

**5. PROOF.** Every state explains itself: why a connection shows what it shows, what a privacy setting actually changes, what export contains before you confirm.

## Room grammar

- **HERO:** title + section nav (quiet, elite).
- **INTELLIGENCE WALL:** section panels — identity, privacy, authority, your-intelligence.
- **ACTION DECK:** switches, test buttons, preference controls, export/delete.
- **EVIDENCE LAYER:** state explanations, connection health, export manifests.
- **NAYA LAYER:** "Explain this setting" — Naya in plain words, per control.

## Controls

- **Section nav** (IDENTITY · PRIVACY · AUTHORITY · CONNECTIONS · NOTIFICATIONS · DATA · SECURITY · APPEARANCE): quiet tabs.
- **Switches with consequences:** each states what changes before you touch it.
- **"Test connection"** (per integration): proves what's live, right now.
- **Type size / motion / density:** your readability law, in your hands.
- **Export / Delete** (YOUR INTELLIGENCE section): full manifest before confirm; delete with explicit typed confirmation.

## Truth rules

- Explains current state rather than hiding it.
- No dark patterns: the privacy-respecting choice is never harder to reach.
- Destructive actions show their full consequence first.

## Connections

- **Governs:** every room (identity, privacy, runtime truth flow outward). **Truth shown for:** Connect doors, session, integrations.
