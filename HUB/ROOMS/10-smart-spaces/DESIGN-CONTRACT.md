# Smart Spaces — Design Contract V1

**Status:** PROPOSED · REVIEW OPEN
**Parent:** `HUB/DESIGN-CONTRACT.md`
**Metaphor:** **THE WORLDS / CONTEXT ENVIRONMENTS**
**Theme:** Lime — context growth, active worlds, scoped intelligence

## Creative intent
Make Spaces feel like stepping into distinct worlds within one civilization. Context should be palpable without turning each Space into a separate app.

## Center-workspace composition
A gallery of substantial context portals/worlds leads into a Space home. Inside, purpose/current objective anchors the top, followed by key intelligence, people, activity, goals/rules and entry points to scoped views of the other rooms.

## Signature instrument
Context switcher + Space home that lenses all Hub intelligence.

## Visual hierarchy
Current Space is unmistakable. Purpose/objectives and key intelligence first, people/activity/goals next, secondary room projections after.

## Living-depth expression
Lime growth energy identifies active context. Each Space may have a derived secondary accent/identity while remaining within the NayaNET material system.

## Motion / liveness
Entering/switching Spaces should feel like moving into another context layer while keeping shell continuity. No fake world-transition spectacle that delays work.

## Naya presence
Naya's interpretation is explicitly Space-scoped and should tell the human when a conclusion comes from broader/global context rather than the active Space.

## States
LOADING · EMPTY · READY · BLOCKED · UNAUTHORIZED · NOT_VERIFIED · VERIFIED · ERROR · OFFLINE · UNKNOWN · DISABLED.

## Responsive
Space gallery → full Space home; persistent compact Space switcher survives across rooms; context scope remains obvious.

## Must never become
- folder-only metaphor
- separate database per Space
- duplicated rooms inside rooms
- hidden context switch
- privacy label without enforcement

## Non-regression anchors
- Space as context boundary
- private/shared/collective/public choices governed by actual policy
- same canonical intelligence substrate
- context persistence across rooms
- Naya context awareness

## Design acceptance
The room must remain unmistakably NayaNET, make its unique function obvious, and preserve real state/proof without generic settings/dashboard drift.
