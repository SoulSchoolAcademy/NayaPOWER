# Smart Spaces — Human Spec

## What this room is
The worlds of your life and work — projects, businesses, learning, home or any context where intelligence, people and activity belong together.

## What you see
Distinct Space portals and, inside each Space, its purpose, intelligence, people, projects, notes, lists, activity, goals, rules and connections.

## What you can do
Enter/create Spaces, invite/connect, set sharing/privacy, link intelligence, open that Space's Feed/Reports/Lists/Mail/Connections and ask Naya inside the context.

## How it connects
Spaces are a context lens over Feed, Today, Reports, Library, Lists, Mail, Connections and Ledger. They do not duplicate those systems.

## What it should feel like
Like stepping into a coherent world where everything relevant comes into focus and unrelated intelligence falls away.

**Current status:** proposed specification; not proof that runtime functionality is live.
