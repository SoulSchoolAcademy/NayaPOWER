# Smart Lists — Design Contract (Human)

**Status:** PROPOSED — team review → scorecard → lock. **Build order:** 7.

## The room in one line

Action intelligence — not Todoist, not a checklist: Now / Next / Waiting / Questions / Ideas / Opportunities / Completed, where every item tells you WHY it's here.

## Entering the room

The curator's gallery crossed with a war room. Your collections hang as framed exhibitions. Yellow appears only as tiny frame-ticks and markers (restrained, per law). Each list is intentional: things you chose.

## The five layers

**1. ORIENTATION.** "SMART LISTS" + list shelf.

**2. CURRENT STATE.** The seven action lanes: NOW (what matters immediately) · NEXT (what should happen next) · WAITING (what's blocked) · QUESTIONS (what needs an answer) · IDEAS (what deserves development) · OPPORTUNITIES (potential high-value moves) · COMPLETED (what actually happened).

**3. INTELLIGENCE.** Every item carries **WHY THIS IS HERE** — one line of real reasoning, e.g. "Connect GitHub — required to complete Smart Door verification." That's the killer feature: the list explains itself.

**4. ACTION.** New list, add/arrange/annotate, move items between lanes, share list via Connect.

**5. PROOF.** Items that are Smart Note pointers show truth seals; completed items link to their outcomes/receipts.

## Room grammar

- **HERO:** title + "New list" + lane overview counts.
- **INTELLIGENCE WALL:** the seven lanes (kanban-like, but calm and beautiful).
- **ACTION DECK:** per-item move/annotate/complete; "Save to list" lives on cards everywhere else.
- **EVIDENCE LAYER:** pointer seals, completion receipts.
- **NAYA LAYER:** "Why this is here" per item; suggested next moves.

## Controls

- **"New list"** (primo, top): name, cover note, start collecting.
- **Per-item:** *Move* (lane picker), *Annotate* (your note on why it's here), *Complete* (→ links outcome).
- **"Save to list"** (universal, on every card in every room): pick the list, done.
- **Share list** (list view): → Connect with preview (shares pointers + your annotations, with consent).

## Truth rules

- Pointers, never copies: one intelligence object in many lists, one truth.
- "Why this is here" is generated from real dependencies or written by you — never filler.
- Completed means outcome-linked, not just checked off.

## Connections

- **Collects from:** Feed, Today, Library, Reports (universal Save). **Shared via:** Connect. **Blocked items cite:** Connect doors, Ledger receipts.
