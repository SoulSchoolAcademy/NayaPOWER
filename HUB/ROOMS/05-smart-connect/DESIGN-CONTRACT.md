# Smart Connect — Design Contract V1

**Status:** PROPOSED · REVIEW OPEN
**Parent:** `HUB/DESIGN-CONTRACT.md`
**Metaphor:** **THE PORTAL BAY**
**Theme:** Teal / Emerald — connection, flow, health

## Creative intent
Make connection feel expansive and high-tech without obscuring governance. The room should visually express many doors into one intelligence core.

## Center-workspace composition
A central NayaPOWER core anchors the scene. Registered/live doors appear as dimensional portals/docking instruments around or beneath it with an equivalent accessible list. Roadmap doors live in a clearly separate horizon.

## Signature instrument
Governed Smart Door portal map + status/authority inspector.

## Visual hierarchy
Live/available registered doors first; current connection/health next; roadmap ideas clearly secondary. Connection and authority are visually separate.

## Living-depth expression
Teal/emerald flow energy travels only across real connected/healthy relationships. Contract-only doors stay dimensional but restrained.

## Motion / liveness
Connection/authentication/verification motion is state-bound. Never animate a handshake for a door that is not live.

## Naya presence
Naya can answer 'Which door should I use?' and explain capability vs permission, but cannot grant authority.

## States
LOADING · EMPTY · READY · BLOCKED · UNAUTHORIZED · NOT_VERIFIED · VERIFIED · ERROR · OFFLINE · UNKNOWN · DISABLED.

## Responsive
Portal composition becomes a vertical stack of door instruments while keeping status and privacy/authority detail.

## Must never become
- dead connect buttons
- roadmap door presented as live
- connected == authorized
- generic integrations marketplace
- fake success animation

## Non-regression anchors
- One brain many doors
- canonical registry truth
- CONNECT → CONFIGURE → VERIFY
- connection≠authority
- privacy/choice language

## Design acceptance
The room must remain unmistakably NayaNET, immediately orient the human, make its unique instrument obvious, and preserve real state/proof without generic-dashboard drift.
