# Smart Connect — Human Spec

## What this room is
The place where you connect NayaNET to the world — humans, agents, tools and systems — without surrendering control.

## What you see
A central intelligence core and beautiful connection doors with truthful status: connected, authenticated, authorized, healthy, blocked, contract-only or roadmap.

## What you can do
Choose a door, connect/authenticate where supported, configure it, test/verify it, see capabilities/authority and revoke/disconnect when permitted.

## How it connects
Connected identities appear in Connections, context can appear in Spaces, door activity proves itself in Ledger, and user controls live in Settings.

## What it should feel like
Like entering a precision portal bay — expansive and powerful, but governed and calm.

**Current status:** proposed specification; not proof that runtime functionality is live.
