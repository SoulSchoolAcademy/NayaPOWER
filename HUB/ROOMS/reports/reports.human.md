# Your Reports — Design Contract (Human)

**Status:** PROPOSED — team review → scorecard → lock. **Build order:** 2.

## The room in one line

A time machine for your intelligence: Day / Week / Month / Year synthesis where the entire center reorganizes per period — and every claim can prove itself.

## Entering the room

A press room crossed with an archive. Emerald light. Time-based volumes stand like bound editions on shelves — each with a cover: the period, the headline synthesis, the honest coverage note ("based on 41 verified notes; 3 gaps").

## The five layers

**1. ORIENTATION.** "YOUR REPORTS" + *"Understand what your intelligence became."* Giant period selector: DAY · WEEK · MONTH · YEAR.

**2. CURRENT STATE.** The edition shelf for the selected period — volumes newest first, each with its cover.

**3. INTELLIGENCE.** The open edition. Daily: Intelligence Summary, Discoveries, Learning, Decisions, Creation, Relationships, Activity, Open Questions, Naya's Synthesis. Weekly becomes a *story*: This Week's Story, Emerging Themes, Momentum, What Changed, What You Learned, What Remains Unresolved, Important Relationships, Next Opportunities. Monthly: Patterns, Trajectory, Knowledge Growth, Project Movement, Relationship Development. Yearly: *Your Year in Intelligence* — what you learned, created, what changed you, what you contributed, who you connected with, what you understand now that you didn't before.

**4. ACTION.** Generate (from real data only), drill down any claim to its evidence, share/export via Connect.

**5. PROOF.** Every section expandable; every claim links to underlying Smart Notes and evidence; coverage notes state what's missing.

## Room grammar

- **HERO:** title + tagline + giant period selector.
- **INTELLIGENCE WALL:** edition shelf → open edition with period-appropriate sections.
- **ACTION DECK:** Generate, drill-downs, Share/Export.
- **EVIDENCE LAYER:** claim-level provenance, coverage notes.
- **NAYA LAYER:** Naya's Synthesis section per edition.

## Controls

- **Period tabs** (DAY · WEEK · MONTH · YEAR): giant primo segmented control — the room's primary organ; the whole center reorganizes.
- **"Generate"** (per period, primo emerald): builds the edition only from real data; thin data → honest cover note, never padding.
- **Per-claim drill-down** (inline): opens the evidence chain.
- **Share / Export** (edition view): → Connect with consent preview.

## Truth rules

- Never synthesizes from absent data; coverage notes mandatory.
- Weekly is a story, not seven dailies stapled together.
- Every claim drillable to Smart Notes/evidence.

## Connections

- **Built from:** Today editions + Feed history. **Sources:** Library (concepts), Ledger (value claims). **Shared via:** Connect. **Saved to:** Lists.
