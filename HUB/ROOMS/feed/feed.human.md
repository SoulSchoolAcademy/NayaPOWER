# Smart Feed — Design Contract (Human)

**Status:** PROPOSED — team review → scorecard → lock. **Build order:** hardened throughout (the living hall).

## The room in one line

The game itself: the living presentation layer over everything Naya knows and everything happening — Collective, Personal, and Activity as three distinct intelligence modes, not filters.

## Entering the room

The central hall. A calm obsidian river flows down the center — living cards surfacing as things happen, gold-orange energy marking what's new since you last looked. Mission control at rest: everything alive, nothing shouting.

## The five layers

**1. ORIENTATION.** "SMART FEED" + live-state LED (LIVE / CATCHING UP / OFFLINE) + one line: *"Everything happening, as it happens."*

**2. CURRENT STATE.** The stream: full Smart Note boards in "In a Nutshell" form, newest first, each with a truth-state seal. Unread markers in gold-orange.

**3. INTELLIGENCE.** Cards aren't raw records — each shows Naya's one-line read ("why this matters") above the fold, with perspective layers one tap away.

**4. ACTION.** Mode tabs (Collective | Personal | Activity) genuinely re-query three different sources. Per-card: Love, Save to list, Share (→ Connect consent preview), Ask Naya. Filter pills. "Catch me up."

**5. PROOF.** Every card: source, timestamp, provenance, truth seal. Activity mode is the operational truth-log — RECEIVED / AUTHORIZED / EXECUTED / VERIFIED states visible.

## Room grammar

- **HERO:** title + LED + mode tabs (the tabs are hero-level; they're the room's primary organ).
- **INTELLIGENCE WALL:** the stream.
- **ACTION DECK:** filter pills + per-card jewel buttons + "Catch me up."
- **EVIDENCE LAYER:** truth seals, provenance footers, expandable evidence.
- **NAYA LAYER:** "Ask Naya about this feed" + per-card "why this matters."

## Controls

- **Mode tabs** (top, primo segmented, gold-orange active rail, 48px+): the most-touched control in the Hub.
- **Per-card jewel buttons:** *Love* (heart jewel), *Save* (bookmark → list picker), *Share* (→ Connect), *Ask Naya*.
- **Filter pills** (All, Mentions, Milestones, Naya insights, Unread): slim, single-select.
- **"Catch me up"** (header right): Naya narrates missed items from real evidence.

## Truth rules

- Three modes, three sources, three privacy rules — no leakage between them.
- No fake stream items. Empty = "nothing yet."
- Love/Save/Share have real causal paths (a Love is recorded; a Share goes through consent).

## Connections

- **Publishes from:** every room (Today distillations, Space activity, shares, receipts).
- **Distilled by:** Today. **Referenced by:** Library ("in the feed"). **Deep-links to:** every room ("open in Library/Ledger/…").
