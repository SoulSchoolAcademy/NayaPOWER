# Your Connections — Design Contract (Human)

**Status:** PROPOSED — team review → scorecard → lock. **Build order:** 6.

## The room in one line

Relationship intelligence — not contacts: people, agents, systems, organizations, and projects, with what you share and what that means.

## Entering the room

The constellation room. Lights down; people as stars, magenta threads for live relationships. Not a giant useless spiderweb — the map orients, the intelligence is the product.

## The five layers

**1. ORIENTATION.** "YOUR CONNECTIONS" + kind tabs: PEOPLE · AGENTS · SYSTEMS · ORGANIZATIONS · PROJECTS.

**2. CURRENT STATE.** The constellation map (supporting visualization) + selected-connection focus.

**3. INTELLIGENCE — Selected Connection.** NAME, relationship, shared intelligence, recent interaction, common projects, important context, last activity, next relevant action.

**4. ACTION.** Message (→ Mail), shared items, invite to space, connect/accept/decline requests.

**5. PROOF.** Every relationship shows its basis: governed connection records, permissions, what privacy allows you to see — never more.

## Room grammar

- **HERO:** title + kind tabs.
- **INTELLIGENCE WALL:** constellation (orientation) + focus card (the product).
- **ACTION DECK:** message, shared items, invite, requests.
- **EVIDENCE LAYER:** connection records, permission basis.
- **NAYA LAYER:** "Next relevant action" per connection — Naya's suggestion from real context.

## Controls

- **Kind tabs** (PEOPLE · AGENTS · SYSTEMS · ORGANIZATIONS · PROJECTS): primo segmented.
- **Person search** (top): find anyone instantly.
- **Focus card actions:** *Message*, *Shared items*, *Invite to space*.
- **Requests:** *Accept / Decline* with who-and-why context visible first.

## Truth rules

- Built only from governed connection records; permissions respected absolutely.
- Never exposes what privacy forbids. Suggested actions cite their basis.

## Connections

- **Targets for:** Connect sharing, Space membership, Mail correspondents. **Shared items link to:** Library, Lists.
