Huge title + one sentence explaining the room. 2. CURRENT STATE What's happening here right now? Real numbers, states, intelligence, relationships, events. 3. INTELLIGENCE What does Naya understand? Not merely raw records. 4. ACTION What can I actually do? Real buttons with real consequences. 5. PROOF Why should I trust this? Source, timestamp, provenance, status, evidence.
