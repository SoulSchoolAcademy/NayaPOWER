## 1. HUMAN JOB

**Enter or resume a durable context**

### The room must answer

- **WHERE AM I?** Smart Spaces
- **WHAT MATTERS?** The worlds/contexts that organize people, intelligence, work and scope.
- **WHAT CAN I DO?** Enter or resume the most relevant space.
- **WHAT HAPPENS NEXT?** The visitor feels context shift without losing NayaNET identity.

### Time-horizon test

**1 second:** The selected space feels unmistakably contextual.  
**3 seconds:** Purpose and scope are understandable before entry.  
**30 seconds:** The visitor can enter and resume work without relearning the space.  
**Return visit:** The space restores legitimate continuity.

## 3. VISUAL DIRECTION

### Material

**OBSIDIAN FOUNDATION → PRECISE EDGE → SUBTLE INSET HIGHLIGHT → REAL ELEVATION → CONTROLLED THEME ENERGY**

The room inherits the shared Naya material system. Theme energy appears at meaningful edges, jewels, focus states, active states and other semantic points — not as decorative wallpaper.

### Theme

**Lime** — `#b8ee57`

**Meaning:** Learning, growth, context.

**Current canonical status:** CANONICAL.

### Signature expression

**THE WORLDS.** Lime marks context and growth. Each Space is a coherent world, not a folder: identity, purpose, people, goals and intelligence form one environment. Entering a Space may subtly retint ambient shell energy while the navigation and interaction grammar remain stable. The human should *feel* the context change without having to relearn the product.

The room must feel like one member of the same living product, not an independent microsite.

## 5. INTELLIGENCE + DATA TRUTH

### Canonical objects

- Space definitions
- Membership/roles
- Scope/consent
- Space-linked intelligence

### Source of truth

**Canonical space/context records and governed membership data.**

The room is a projection of canonical intelligence. It must not create a shadow truth store merely to make the screen look complete.

### Truth rules

- **UNKNOWN ≠ PASS**
- **BLOCKED ≠ PASS**
- **IMPLEMENTED ≠ VERIFIED**
- **VERIFIED ≠ PRODUCTION-PROVEN**
- Never fabricate personalization, liveness, counts, relationships, messages, reports, receipts or connection state.
- Visual energy must follow actual state.

## 6. INTERACTION CONTRACT

Every consequential control follows:

**CONTROL → INTENT → SCOPE/AUTHORITY → CAPABILITY → OPERATION → OBSERVATION → RESULT → UI STATE → EVIDENCE WHERE REQUIRED**

### Primary interactions

- Enter/resume space.
- Inspect purpose/scope.
- Inspect members/roles.
- Open recent intelligence.
- Switch context → visible context transition, no identity recreation.

### Naya presence

Naya may explain, retrieve, compare, summarize, propose and — only when authorized — act in the context of this room.

Naya must know the active room, selected object/context, truth state and authority boundary before making a room-specific claim.

## 7. UNIVERSAL STATE MATRIX

The implementation must explicitly design applicable behavior for:

| State | Room requirement |
|---|---|
| **LOADING** | Retain world identity while content loads. |
| **EMPTY** | Explain space has no relevant intelligence yet. |
| **READY** | Current goal/state is prominent. |
| **BLOCKED** | Explain missing membership/dependency. |
| **UNAUTHORIZED** | No silent scope broadening. |
| **NOT_VERIFIED** | Space claims remain clearly bounded. |
| **VERIFIED** | Member/space status is evidence-backed. |
| **ERROR** | Preserve navigation identity and offer retry. |
| **OFFLINE** | Make stale context freshness explicit. |
| **DISABLED** | Unsupported local actions remain obvious. |
| **UNKNOWN** | Unknown member/state data stays unknown. |

## 9. ACCESSIBILITY

This room is not Signature unless its intelligence remains understandable and operable for people using:

- keyboard-only navigation;
- assistive technology / screen readers;
- zoom up to 200%;
- reduced-motion preferences;
- high-contrast / accessibility settings;
- touch input.

Focus order must follow cognitive order. Color may reinforce meaning but never be the sole carrier of meaning.

### Room-specific accessibility consideration

Context change must be announced; space identity and scope must be accessible before entering.

## 15A. SIGNATURE / BENCHMARK-TO-BEYOND

### Signature moment
Crossing into a Space changes what Naya retrieves, prioritizes and explains while preserving canonical object identity and scope — context switching feels like travel, not opening another folder.

### Best-of intelligence to synthesize
- **AKQA:** immersive digital environments.
- **Airbnb:** trust and context-rich decision making.
- **Spotify:** personalized content inside familiar grammar.
- **Huge / Work & Co:** coherence across a large ecosystem.

### Beyond-benchmark hypothesis
Spaces can exceed ordinary workspaces by becoming governed context lenses over one intelligence substrate: people, goals, retrieval, reports and Naya all adapt without copying data or broadening authority.

### Proof boundary
Test context-switch correctness, scope comprehension, cold return/resume time and cross-space leakage.
