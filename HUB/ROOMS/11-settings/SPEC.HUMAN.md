# Settings — Human Spec

## What this room is
Your control deck for how NayaNET relates to you — identity, privacy, authority, connections, notifications, your data and how the interface behaves.

## What you see
Clear human categories and a strong 'Your Intelligence' section explaining what is yours, what is shared and what each control actually changes.

## What you can do
Update preferences, manage consent/sharing, inspect or change connected channels, control notifications/accessibility, export or remove data where supported, sign out and open advanced System Health.

## How it connects
Settings routes technical connection work to Connect, relationship/privacy work to Connections/Spaces and activity/proof to Ledger.

## What it should feel like
Quiet, exact and safe — like a precision control deck rather than a settings junk drawer.

**Current status:** proposed specification; not proof that runtime functionality is live.
