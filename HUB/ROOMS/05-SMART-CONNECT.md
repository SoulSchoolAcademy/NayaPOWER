# 05 — Smart Connect / Smart Doors

**Metaphor:** THE PORTAL BAY  
**Route:** `/connect`  
**Theme:** teal / emerald  
**Human question:** **How can I or another system connect to this intelligence?**

## Human promise

Show every legitimate connection channel into NayaPOWER, what it can do, its real status and what authority is still required.

**One brain. Many doors.**

## Signature visual

A central **NayaPOWER core** with governed doors arranged like precision portals/docking points around it.

Each Door object is a dimensional instrument with:
- door identity/icon;
- provider/channel;
- who it is for;
- capability summary;
- current liveness;
- authentication/identity state;
- permission/authority state;
- health;
- connect/configure action;
- receipt/audit visibility.

The visual may feel expansive and spatial, but accessibility must provide an equivalent list.

## Canonical door truth

Door inventory/status comes from:
`BRAIN/10-INTERFACES/0002-SMART-DOOR-REGISTRY-V1.json`

Roadmap ideas are visually separated from registered/live doors.

**CONNECTED ≠ AUTHORIZED.**

## Primary actions

Per door:
- **Connect / Authenticate**
- **Configure**
- **View capabilities**
- **View required authority**
- **Test connection** when supported
- **Disconnect / revoke** when permitted
- **View receipts / activity**

Global:
- **Which door should I use? Ask Naya**
- **View connection health**
- **Request / prepare a new door** as a governed product workflow, not instant authority

## Door states

- LIVE
- CONNECTED
- AUTHENTICATING
- REGISTERED_CONTRACT_ONLY
- ROADMAP
- DEGRADED
- BLOCKED
- UNAUTHORIZED
- ERROR
- DISCONNECTED

Do not collapse these into “on/off”.

## Cross-room handoffs

- connected human/org → Connections;
- connected project context → Spaces;
- door activity/receipts → Ledger;
- mail door → Mail;
- privacy/scopes → Settings.

## Mobile

Portal view may reduce to vertical door instruments while preserving jewel identity and status.

## Acceptance journey

A human can identify the correct door, understand what it can and cannot do, see whether it is actually live, authenticate/connect where supported, and inspect the authority/proof boundary without confusing connection with permission.
