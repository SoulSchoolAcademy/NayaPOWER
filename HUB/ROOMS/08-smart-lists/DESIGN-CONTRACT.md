# Smart Lists — Design Contract V1

**Status:** PROPOSED · REVIEW OPEN
**Parent:** `HUB/DESIGN-CONTRACT.md`
**Metaphor:** **THE MISSION TABLE**
**Theme:** Purple — intelligence organized for action and development

## Creative intent
Make organizing intelligence feel like a mission table that explains itself. The room should reduce mental load, not recreate task-manager anxiety.

## Center-workspace composition
A concise purpose header above action lanes NOW/NEXT/WAITING/QUESTIONS/IDEAS/OPPORTUNITIES/COMPLETED. An active list can use one or more lanes depending on its purpose. WHY THIS IS HERE sits directly beneath each meaningful item.

## Signature instrument
Living intelligent lists + action lanes + explainable membership.

## Visual hierarchy
What needs attention now first, blockers/waiting second, development lanes next, completed as evidence/history. Avoid showing all lanes when empty and irrelevant.

## Living-depth expression
Purple intelligence energy emphasizes active/pinned items and smart-rule membership. Completed items settle rather than disappear.

## Motion / liveness
Moving an item between lanes should preserve identity and feel physical. Rule-driven movement should be explainable, not surprising.

## Naya presence
Naya can organize, explain membership, identify blockers/questions/opportunities and suggest changes, but should not silently reorder high-consequence work without user-visible reasoning.

## States
LOADING · EMPTY · READY · BLOCKED · UNAUTHORIZED · NOT_VERIFIED · VERIFIED · ERROR · OFFLINE · UNKNOWN · DISABLED.

## Responsive
One lane at a time with easy lane switcher; item actions in sheets; reorder has keyboard/button alternatives to drag.

## Must never become
- Todoist clone
- checklist with no intelligence
- mystery auto-prioritization
- copying canonical objects into list storage
- drag-only interaction

## Non-regression anchors
- human control
- manual/smart/hybrid behavior
- canonical object references
- WHY THIS IS HERE
- clear blocked/waiting distinction

## Design acceptance
The room must remain unmistakably NayaNET, make its unique function obvious, and preserve real causal/proof state.
