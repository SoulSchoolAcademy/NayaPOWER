# Smart Lists — Human Spec

## What this room is
Your action-intelligence room — what matters now, what should happen next, what is waiting, what needs an answer and what deserves development.

## What you see
Living lanes such as Now, Next, Waiting, Questions, Ideas, Opportunities and Completed, with a clear reason under each intelligent item.

## What you can do
Create lists, add or move intelligence, pin/reorder, set smart rules, ask Naya to organize/explain, open the source item and share when permitted.

## How it connects
Anything from Feed, Today, Reports, Library or Mail can be referenced in Lists. Lists can become inputs to Spaces and leave activity/proof in Ledger.

## What it should feel like
Like laying your intelligence on a calm mission table where every item has a reason and next relationship.

**Current status:** proposed specification; not proof that runtime functionality is live.
