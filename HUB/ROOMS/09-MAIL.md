## 1. HUMAN JOB

**Prioritize and respond to real communication**

### The room must answer

- **WHERE AM I?** Smart Mail
- **WHAT MATTERS?** Real communication that needs understanding or action.
- **WHAT CAN I DO?** Open/respond to the highest-value real conversation.
- **WHAT HAPPENS NEXT?** The person knows what matters, why, and what they can do.

### Time-horizon test

**1 second:** The highest-value communication signal is obvious.  
**3 seconds:** The visitor knows why it matters and what to do.  
**30 seconds:** They can inspect context and respond safely.  
**Return visit:** Unread/priority state remains truthful and provider-backed.

## 7. UNIVERSAL STATE MATRIX

The implementation must explicitly design applicable behavior for:

| State | Room requirement |
|---|---|
| **LOADING** | Do not flash fake messages. |
| **EMPTY** | Beautifully explain no priority signals; offer legitimate setup/search. |
| **READY** | Priority reason and top conversation are clear. |
| **BLOCKED** | Explain missing provider/permission/data. |
| **UNAUTHORIZED** | Provider and sharing scope explicit. |
| **NOT_VERIFIED** | AI-priority/synthesis not mistaken for provider fact. |
| **VERIFIED** | Provider-backed facts clearly differentiated. |
| **ERROR** | Keep draft safely if valid; expose send failure and retry. |
| **OFFLINE** | Read-only local cached state only if truth/freshness is explicit. |
| **DISABLED** | Send/action disabled when provider not connected. |
| **UNKNOWN** | Unknown sender/context stays unknown. |

This room is not Signature unless its intelligence remains understandable and operable for people using:

- keyboard-only navigation;
- assistive technology / screen readers;
- zoom up to 200%;
- reduced-motion preferences;
- high-contrast / accessibility settings;

## 15A. SIGNATURE / BENCHMARK-TO-BEYOND

### Signature moment
The highest-value conversation arrives with a one-line **why this needs you**, relevant context, commitments and a prepared next action — while the provider-backed message remains clearly distinct from Naya's synthesis.

### Best-of intelligence to synthesize
- **Slack / MetaLab:** conversational warmth and feedback.
- **Linear:** focus and low-noise density.
- **Things:** organization that reduces anxiety.
- **Stripe:** explicit action/consequence state.

### Beyond-benchmark hypothesis
Mail becomes meaningfully smarter when triage, context, commitments and drafting are intelligence layers over real provider truth, and the human sees signals rather than manually classifying noise.

### Proof boundary
Measure priority precision/recall, triage time, draft usefulness, send/recovery success and provider-state clarity.
