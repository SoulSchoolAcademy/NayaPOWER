# Intelligent Library — Human Spec

## What this room is
Your own intelligence vault — everything retained, what it means, what is changing and what you still do not know.

## What you see
A prominent intelligence search, featured intelligence, rich knowledge domains and domain-specific rooms that show knowledge, learning and uncertainty.

## What you can do
Search, browse, open domains/objects, ask Naya what is missing, save/list, inspect evidence and follow relationships.

## How it connects
Library objects are the durable versions of intelligence seen in Feed/Today/Reports and can be referenced by Lists, Spaces, Connections and Ledger.

## What it should feel like
Like walking into a beautifully organized version of your own mind rather than hunting through folders.

**Current status:** proposed specification; not proof that runtime functionality is live.
