# Intelligent Library — Design Contract V1

**Status:** PROPOSED · REVIEW OPEN
**Parent:** `HUB/DESIGN-CONTRACT.md`
**Metaphor:** **THE VAULT / WALKING INTO YOUR OWN MIND**
**Theme:** Sapphire — knowledge, retrieval, comprehension

## Creative intent
Make retained intelligence feel discoverable, rich and alive. The human should feel like their mind has structure without seeing a sterile folder tree.

## Center-workspace composition
Search is the hero instrument. Below it sit three large Featured Intelligence objects: Most Relevant, Recently Developed, Needs Attention. Then rich domain cards. Opening a domain replaces the center with a domain-specific intelligence environment.

## Signature instrument
Semantic retrieval + domain intelligence exploration.

## Visual hierarchy
Search first, featured meaning second, knowledge domains third, individual records only after intentional descent.

## Living-depth expression
Sapphire light and precise crystal-like domain identity. Domain cards feel like substantial knowledge objects, not tiny tiles.

## Motion / liveness
Search/retrieval transitions reveal relationships progressively; domain entry should feel like moving deeper into the same intelligence environment.

## Naya presence
Naya explains why results matter, highlights gaps/uncertainty and answers 'What am I missing here?' from actual retained intelligence.

## States
LOADING · EMPTY · READY · BLOCKED · UNAUTHORIZED · NOT_VERIFIED · VERIFIED · ERROR · OFFLINE · UNKNOWN · DISABLED.

## Responsive
Search-first. Featured intelligence stacks cleanly. Domains become large touch targets; domain detail is full-screen with evidence sheets.

## Must never become
- filesystem as default metaphor
- 58 tiny buttons
- search results with no why-match explanation
- fake knowledge counts
- hiding uncertainty

## Non-regression anchors
- semantic search
- provenance/truth
- domain foundation
- one canonical retained intelligence substrate
- Ask Naya contextual exploration

## Design acceptance
The room must remain unmistakably NayaNET, immediately orient the human, make its unique instrument obvious, and preserve real state/proof without generic-dashboard drift.
