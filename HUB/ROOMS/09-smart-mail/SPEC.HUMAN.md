# Smart Mail — Human Spec

## What this room is
Your communication-intelligence room — where messages are organized by meaning, attention and context rather than just time.

## What you see
Important, Respond, Follow Up, Drafts and Sent; then the real message with a context view explaining who, why it matters, previous interaction, related intelligence and possible response.

## What you can do
Read, reply/draft, compose, archive, prioritize, follow up, ask Naya, capture a Smart Note, add to a List, link to a Space or open the sender in Connections.

## How it connects
Connections provides relationship context, Spaces provide project/context scope, Library/Notes preserve useful intelligence, Lists track follow-up and Ledger proves governed message actions.

## What it should feel like
Like a quiet signal room that separates meaningful communication from noise.

**Current status:** proposed specification; not proof that runtime functionality is live.
