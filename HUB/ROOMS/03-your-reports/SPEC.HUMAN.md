# Your Reports — Human Spec

## What this room is

Your time machine for intelligence — understand what your days, weeks, months and years actually became.

## What you see

A report whose structure changes with the time scale: daily details, weekly story/momentum, monthly patterns/trajectory, yearly life-and-work intelligence.

## What you can do

Change period, compare time ranges, ask Naya, inspect evidence, save important conclusions, share/export when supported and open the source intelligence behind any conclusion.

## How it connects

Reports synthesize Today/Feed/Library/Space intelligence and can link decisions/actions to Ledger, people to Connections and projects to Spaces.

## What it should feel like

Reflective, deep and clarifying — like a film room where scattered events turn into a coherent story.

**Current status:** proposed specification; not proof that runtime functionality is live.
