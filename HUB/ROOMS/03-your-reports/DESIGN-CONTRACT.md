# Your Reports — Design Contract V1

**Status:** PROPOSED · REVIEW OPEN
**Parent:** `HUB/DESIGN-CONTRACT.md`
**Metaphor:** **THE FILM ROOM / TIME MACHINE**
**Theme:** Indigo — comprehension, depth, cross-time synthesis

## Creative intent

Entering Reports should feel like stepping back in time and seeing meaning emerge. Each time scale has a different narrative character.

## Center-workspace composition

Large period selector controls the whole scene. The center re-composes by time scale rather than merely swapping text. Executive story first, then meaningful sections, then evidence appendix. Charts only when they clarify.

## Signature instrument

Cross-time synthesis with period-specific narrative structures and evidence-backed comparison.

## Visual hierarchy

Meaning before metrics. Story before chart. Change before inventory. Evidence remains one layer away.

## Living-depth expression

Indigo depth grows with time scale; longer horizons may use slower, broader spatial composition rather than more cards.

## Motion / liveness

Changing periods can use a subtle temporal scene transition that preserves orientation. Never animate charts for spectacle.

## Naya presence

Naya explains patterns, contradictions, unresolved questions and opportunities and can compare periods without inventing causal claims.

## States

LOADING · EMPTY · READY · BLOCKED · UNAUTHORIZED · NOT_VERIFIED · VERIFIED · ERROR · OFFLINE · UNKNOWN · DISABLED.

Each state must preserve the room's identity; no generic error card replacing the room.

## Responsive

Report cover → narrative sections → evidence. Period selector remains easy to use. Visualizations collapse intelligently, not into unreadable miniature charts.

## Must never become

- same template for every period
- analytics dashboard full of ornamental charts
- record-count reports
- unsupported causal conclusions
- seven daily reports pasted into a week

## Non-regression anchors

- evidence-first synthesis
- DAY/WEEK/MONTH/YEAR distinction
- report conclusions traceable to canonical intelligence
- Naya interpretation separated from evidence

## Design acceptance

A reviewer should be able to identify this room with the NayaNET logo removed, understand where they are, what matters, what action is available, and why the information can be trusted within seconds.
