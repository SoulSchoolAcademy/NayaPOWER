# Smart Spaces — Design Contract (Human)

**Status:** PROPOSED — team review → scorecard → lock. **Build order:** 9.

## The room in one line

Private contextual chambers — a whole small Hub per purpose: NayaNET, Human Maximus, a project, home, business, learning. This is how one giant pile of memory is prevented.

## Entering the room

A corridor of private chambers, each behind its own door with a purple seal. Inside: the chamber's shared boards, members present, the activity of this collaboration. Private by default; every guest invited deliberately.

## The five layers

**1. ORIENTATION.** "SMART SPACES" + chamber switcher (your sealed doors).

**2. CURRENT STATE.** The open chamber: shared boards, members rail, activity stream.

**3. INTELLIGENCE.** Per chamber: Intelligence, People, Projects, Notes, Lists, Activity, Goals, Rules, Connections — the chamber's own memory, scoped and separate.

**4. ACTION.** New space, invite/manage members, chamber tabs (Boards · Members · Activity), space sharing rules.

**5. PROOF.** Membership records, sharing rules visible always, activity provenance; leaving revokes.

## Room grammar

- **HERO:** title + chamber switcher + "New space."
- **INTELLIGENCE WALL:** chamber boards.
- **ACTION DECK:** member manage, sharing rules, chamber tabs.
- **EVIDENCE LAYER:** membership records, rule visibility.
- **NAYA LAYER:** chamber briefs ("what's alive in this space").

## Controls

- **"New space"** (primo purple): name it, seal it, invite.
- **Chamber tabs:** Boards · Members · Activity.
- **Member manage:** invite, role, remove — each action states its consequence plainly.
- **Space sharing rules:** what the space may share outward — set once, visible always.

## Truth rules

- Private by default; sharing and membership require explicit governed state.
- Chamber memories never leak between chambers.
- Leaving a space revokes its view of you — enforced, not promised.

## Connections

- **Members from:** Connections. **Channels from:** Connect doors. **Lists hang here.** **Activity publishes to:** Feed (personal mode). **Receipts to:** Ledger.
