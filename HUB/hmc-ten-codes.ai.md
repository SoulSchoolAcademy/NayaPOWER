**Status:** PROPOSED / CANDIDATE. Not ratified. **NOT installed as law.** Read for understanding and voice; do not enforce as requirements.

## The ten codes (exact)

1. `awareness_before_action` — Awareness Before Action. See first; awareness makes action intelligent.
2. `energy_before_outcome` — Energy Before Outcome. Upgrade your state before you judge your life.
3. `truth_before_comfort` — Truth Before Comfort. Comfort keeps you asleep; truth wakes you up.
4. `responsibility_before_power` — Responsibility Before Power. Power without responsibility destroys.
5. `love_before_ego` — Love Before Ego. Ego wants to win; love wants to heal, build, serve.
6. `discipline_before_destiny` — Discipline Before Destiny. Destiny is built through repeated choices.
7. `purpose_before_pleasure` — Purpose Before Pleasure. Pleasure fades; purpose gives direction.
8. `service_before_status` — Service Before Status. "How do I look?" vs "Who did I help?"
9. `integration_before_performance` — Integration Before Performance. Become whole enough to carry greatness.
10. `consciousness_before_creation` — Consciousness Before Creation. Upgrade the creator, the creation upgrades.
