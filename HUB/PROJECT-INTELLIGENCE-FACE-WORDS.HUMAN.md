## B8 — THE MEANING UNDER EVERY NUMBER

- **Percent done:** "of all tracked work, proven finished by someone other than its maker."
- **Total:** "every piece of work in the record — the whole job, counted once."
- **Done:** "finished and proven. Not claimed — proven."
- **In motion:** "being worked on right now, with a name beside it."
- **To do:** "waiting its turn, in the order the director set."
- **Verifying:** "built, and being checked by different eyes before it may be called done."
- **Needs a decision:** "waiting on the director — each one says exactly what it decides."
- **Blocked:** "stopped, with the reason and the name of whoever can unstop it."
- **Today's pace:** "how much proven work landed today — the machine's heartbeat, not its intentions."

## B10 — THE OPERATING CODE (plain form, for the panel)

**How this team works — the code every job here runs on:**
1. Think before acting.
2. See the full scope — the whole picture, not the piece in front of you.
3. Deliberate — weigh the choices honestly.
4. Choose the best possible action, all things considered.
5. Make it — completely, not almost.
6. Best action, best outcome — done right, not just done.
7. The result must be valuable, verifiable, and visible.

*If a step was skipped, the work isn't finished — it's started.*
