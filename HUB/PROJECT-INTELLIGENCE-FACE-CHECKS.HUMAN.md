## THE SYNC — HOW THE TWO TRACKS STAY ONE JOB

1. **No hand-typed numbers, ever.** Every figure on the face is computed from the record at read time. Content is data (Track B), not prose typed into the page.
2. **A build check closes only when its content renders.** Doors (A7) close when the lists (B1, B2) open through them. Previews (A8) close when per-area truth (B4) fills them.
3. **A content check closes only when it is visible at /pi.** Emitted but not rendered is not inserted; written but not shipped is not written.
4. **One loop, both tracks scored together.** Each round, Resolve re-scores the live face against all 30 checks at once. The displayed score (A13) moves only on that cold re-score.
5. **The loop runs until it passes** — over 9 and working, by the director's cold-open test: the whole project, the percent done, what moved, what's stuck and on whom — in seconds, unaided. Right first time is the aim; the loop is the guarantee.

**The count that matters: 30 checks · 1 live · 2 in source · 27 owed.**
