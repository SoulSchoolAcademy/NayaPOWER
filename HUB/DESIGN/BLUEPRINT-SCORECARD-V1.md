## What the snapshot actually taught

The strongest cross-room findings were:

1. **Icon-family absence was systemic.** Unicode/placeholders could not create a recognizable Naya signature.
2. **Team-facing explanation leaked into the product.** Architecture/law paragraphs were being asked to do interface work.
3. **Microcopy repeated instead of being distilled.**
4. **Text density was being solved with smaller text instead of stronger hierarchy and disclosure.**
5. **Some action/color choices were arbitrary rather than semantic.**
6. **The room visuals were useful taste evidence but could not certify functionality, truth, responsive behavior, accessibility, performance, persistence, evidence, or runtime causality.**

Those findings are now compiled into the Protocol, Icon Grammar, Do/Don't Checklist, room freeze requirements, and hard gates.
