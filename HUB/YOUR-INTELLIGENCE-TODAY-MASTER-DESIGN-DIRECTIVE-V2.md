## I. THE JOB — ONE SENTENCE

Tell the human what matters about their intelligence today — what changed, what
they learned, what deserves them, what is unresolved, and what they are
missing — without making them reconstruct the day.

## II. THE STRUCTURAL TARGET

The 509 lab's structure is the target. Enhance it; do not redesign it.

- **Desktop:** fixed sidebar (rooms, jewel + label) · top area · main column that
  is the presentation. The sidebar is the 509's sidebar — it stays.
- **The ecosystem pill bar** (Home, Naya Power, 5-Day Challenge, Enter Free,
  Powercast, White Paper, About Us — the pills across the top): replace with
  **one plus button**; a **menu pops up**; the human clicks through. No pill nav
  across the top. *(Amends Contract V1 Vol 14B, 2026-10-02.)*
- **Mobile:** the sidebar becomes **a plus button on the left**; tap it, the
  rooms menu pops up, tap through. The menu is anchored, premium, thumb-sized
  targets, never a shrunken desktop drawer.
- **The presentation is the main feed/column and then you scroll down.** The
  feed/room IS the presentation. Nothing overlaps. Professional, well spaced,
  clean, premium, high level.

## III. SIDEBAR LAW

- The sidebar lists the rooms: jewel mark + plain label per entry, generous
  touch targets (48px minimum), the active room unmistakable (jewel lit, label
  bright, edge glow in the room's theme).
- **Smart Feed is never a sidebar entry.** The feed is the Main Show — the
  arrival surface — not a room among rooms.
- Order follows the human's mental model, not the system's: Today, Reports,
  Library, Connect, Ledger, Connections, Lists, Mail, Spaces, Settings.
- One nav surface at a time: sidebar, menu, or drawer — never two competing.
  Backdrop, Escape closes, focus trapped and restored, scroll locked behind.
- The sidebar never consumes the presentation on mobile — it collapses to the
  plus button (Section II).

## IV. THE TODAY ROOM — DAILY INTELLIGENCE COCKPIT

### What it is not
Not a dashboard. Not a grid of equal widgets. Not a note feed. Not an archive.

### What it is
A hockey-highlight reel of the human's intelligence: the day's most valuable
moments, distilled. The human opens it and within seconds knows what matters.

### The experience flow
ORIENT → SEE → UNDERSTAND → PRIORITIZE → REMEMBER → QUESTION → ACT → VERIFY.
Every section serves one step. A section that serves none is removed.

### The sections, in order
1. **NOW** — dominates the composition. The single most important thing about
   the human's intelligence right now. One focal region; the squint test must
   reveal it instantly.
2. **NEXT** — the obvious next move, evidence-backed, with the why: "this
   appears important because X changed, Y is unresolved, Z depends on it."
   Never "you should do X" without the reason.
3. **WATCH** — what changed. The killer feature: not "today's notes" but "what
   is different from yesterday," each change carrying why it matters and its
   evidence/state.
4. **LEARNED** — what the intelligence learned, distinguished honestly:
   INFORMATION (encountered) / UNDERSTANDING (now understood) / CHANGE
   (understanding changed) / DECISION (decided) / KNOWLEDGE (durable).
5. **WAITING** — unresolved: open questions, blocked work, commitments, things
   awaiting human action. With the reason each waits.
6. **RECENT PROOF** — receipts. What the system claims it did, proven — never
   asserted.

### Content laws
- **The ruthless filter:** every element must CAPTURE-value, UNDERSTAND,
  REMEMBER, CONNECT, DECIDE, ACT, or VERIFY something valuable — within an
  output-only room this means UNDERSTAND / REMEMBER / CONNECT / DECIDE-support /
  VERIFY. Anything else doesn't ship.
- **Distill before display.** Raw intelligence is not presentation. The system
  decides what matters; the surface shows the few things that matter now; depth
  is one tap away (GLANCE → UNDERSTAND → INSPECT → PROVE).
- **Never manufacture filler for empty categories.** An empty section is an
  honest invitation ("this is where today's changes will land"), never a fake
  card, never "nothing here."
- **Never fake intelligence.** Don't claim Naya remembered, learned, or decided
  unless she did. Don't show live dots on dead data. Don't personalize for a
  stranger.
- **Usefulness and truth, not clicks, engagement, or manufactured activity.**
- **Data flow:** today's events → Smart Notes / intelligent blocks → retrieval
  → classification → ranking → synthesis → briefing. The briefing is a derived
  projection — never the canonical source. No duplicate intelligence stores for
  presentation.
- **What am I missing** is a first-class section: KNOWN / CHANGED / UNCERTAIN /
  MISSING — it prevents false completion. Unknown ≠ success.
- **No capture, command, or invocation controls.** The Hub is output. Ask Naya
  = ask the brain (retrieval + synthesis), never hidden execution.

### Visual identity
- **Magenta (#d86cff) signs Today** — because it is about the human. It greets
  at the threshold (sidebar jewel, header edge, the primary action) and recedes
  while the human works. It never floods the room.
- The NOW region carries the strongest magenta energy on the surface; everything
  else is subordinate. Cinematic hierarchy, not inventory.

## V. DESIGN PHYSICS — THE EXECUTABLE ESSENCE

(Compressed from the contract. The full law lives in the Master Design
Contract V1; this is what my hands obey.)

- **MAKE DIGITAL OBJECTS FEEL PHYSICAL.** A button is a precision instrument
  with mass. A board is a destination you can enter. An icon is a glyph that
  communicates state. A progress bar is energy moving.
- **The sequence, every component:** DEPTH → LIGHT → STATE → RESPONSE →
  CONSEQUENCE. If I'm lost, I walk the sequence; the missing step is the work.
- **Depth is built:** material core → edge light → restrained highlight → cast
  shadow → semantic aura → state response. Flat + bordered is not depth.
- **One light source** (upper-left orb). Light is information, never decoration.
- **Color has exactly one job each:** emerald alive · gold extraordinary (rarest
  word) · red alarm (never decorative) · purple Naya · magenta the human ·
  sapphire knowledge. Point at any colored pixel and name its job, or remove it.
- **Type:** 16px floor, 17–18px target, labels never below 11px. One family.
  Hierarchy from size and weight before color.
- **Space:** 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 64 / 96. Generous negative space
  is a feature.
- **Motion:** three durations, one curve, transform + opacity only — and only to
  communicate truthful state change. Reduced-motion = instant state change.
- **LAW ZERO vetoes everything:** nothing may ever reduce readability.
- **No random decoration.** If removing it improves clarity, remove it.
- **The arc:** PRESENCE → RECOGNITION → DISTILLATION → INVITATION → FLOW.
- **One obvious next action** per surface. Verb-first buttons ("Verify now"),
  all seven states designed.

## VI. TRUTH STATES

The 11-state model, everywhere it applies: LOADING / EMPTY / READY / BLOCKED /
UNAUTHORIZED / NOT_VERIFIED / VERIFIED / ERROR / OFFLINE / DISABLED / UNKNOWN.
NOT_VERIFIED is a valid product state. The UI must never manufacture
intelligence, activity, counts, personalization, relationships, messages,
reports, receipts, connection state, completion, or proof.

## VII. RESPONSIVE LAW

Recompose, never shrink. Mobile keeps: hierarchy, 48px targets, readability,
the plus-button rooms menu, the full cockpit flow. The NOW region still
dominates. Nothing overlaps at 390px — verified, not assumed.

1. Render the **frozen bytes** full-screen, like he will see them.
2. Four tests: **1 second** (eye lands) · **3 seconds** (where am I, what
   matters, what can I do) · **30 seconds** (I can act) · **squint** (hierarchy
   survives).
3. Measure: type on scale, every color's job named, spacing on scale, depth per
   law, buttons verb-first with all states, motion tied to state.
4. Score D1–D8 with one line of evidence each. No averaging away a failed gate.
5. OSCAR: attack my own work like an enemy; fix what matters.
6. Name what I deliberately left out, and why.
7. Freeze the SHA. Producer self-score is diagnostic only — **Naya 1 judges the
   frozen SHA independently.**

**Automatic rejection** (fails on sight, no debate): KPI grid as first
