## 1. YOUR INTEL TODAY — SMART TAB CONCEPT, PART #25

A dialogue between Shawn (voice) and Naya, designing this exact room. What I took from it:

- **Lead mode:** treat the product as priceless. The keywords are *useful* and *valuable* — "What's the purpose? Where's the value? Would people actually need that?"
- **It is NOT a dashboard.** It is a Daily Intelligence Cockpit answering: what happened, what did I learn, what changed, what matters, what should I remember, what am I missing, what can I do.
- **The ruthless filter:** every component must CAPTURE / UNDERSTAND / REMEMBER / CONNECT / DECIDE / ACT / VERIFY something valuable, or it doesn't belong.
- **The eight movements:** ORIENT → SEE → UNDERSTAND → PRIORITIZE → REMEMBER → QUESTION → ACT → VERIFY.
- **The killer features:** What Changed (the difference engine — "here is what is different from yesterday"), What I Learned (the five-rung ladder: information / understanding / change / decision / knowledge), What Matters Now (evidence-backed priorities with WHY), What Should I Remember (the diary), What Am I Missing (known / changed / uncertain / missing).
- **Room jobs:** ten rooms, ten distinct questions. Today = "What's important about my intelligence today?" Never duplicated by Reports, Library, or Feed.
- **Contract-first:** full Product + UX + Intelligence Contract before code. Freeze ladder: IMPLEMENTED → DEPLOYED → PROVEN → FROZEN.
- **The line I kept:** "Beauty gets attention. Utility earns permanence. Intelligence earns trust. Memory creates attachment. Governance creates safety."
