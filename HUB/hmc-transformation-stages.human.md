# The Five Transformation Stages

**STATUS: PROPOSED — candidate product structure. Awaiting Shawn's review. Not law. Not a roadmap until he says so.**

---

Shawn, the Human Maximus Codex lays out human transformation as five ordered stages. The distillation flagged this as a **candidate product structure** for NayaNET — the staged path a person walks through the system. Proposed for your consideration, not installed anywhere.

## The five stages, in plain words

1. **Wake Up** — see the autopilot. "You are more than the identity the world handed you." The moment of noticing: the patterns were running you.
2. **Clean Up** — clear the clutter: old beliefs, energy leaks, the noise that isn't yours. What gets removed matters as much as what's added.
3. **Build Up** — discipline, routines, clarity. The daily architecture that holds the new self up.
4. **Rise Up** — purpose, creativity, leadership, service. You stop fixing yourself and start building something.
5. **Light Up** — become a source of strength for others. The transformation compounds outward.

## Why it's on your desk

Two reasons. First, the identity page's "type your alias → become your Smart Self" moment maps exactly to **Stage 1 (Wake Up)** — the distillation recommends the copy echo this ("you are more than the identity the world handed you") instead of generic onboarding language. That's a copy decision the design lane can take with your nod.

Second, the bigger question: is this the product's spine? A NayaNET journey structured as Wake Up → Clean Up → Build Up → Rise Up → Light Up would give the whole product a narrative arc your users already understand from the Codex work. That's a product decision — genuinely yours.

## What this would change if you ratify it

- As copy guidance: identity/onboarding language aligns to the stage language (smallest change, design lane).
- As product structure: rooms, journeys, and progress could be organized around the five stages (larger change, product lane).
- Either way, nothing is law — this is a candidate structure, proposed for your ruling.
