# NayaNET Smart Covers — the feature

**Every page is born beautiful. Its owner is always in control.**

## The three choices

When someone opens the cover control on any page — personal, business, space, membership — they get exactly three doors:

1. **The recommended cover.** Already in place. When a page is created, the system places the cover that fits it best — chosen from what the person said about themselves and what the page is for. Nobody ever starts from blank, and nobody has to decide anything to look beautiful.
2. **The library.** A searchable shelf of finished covers, organized by theme and findable by the plain words people actually type — *dog, gym, wedding, calm, office, prayer*. The recommended ones come first; the whole shelf is one search away.
3. **Your own photo.** Upload anything. The same gentle position adjuster works on uploads and library covers alike, so any picture can be made to sit right in the frame.

Changing your mind is free and instant. Nothing is ever locked.

## How the recommendation works

Every cover in the library carries a small card: its name, what it is, who it suits, and the words it answers to. Every page carries a card too: whose it is, what kind of page it is, what it's for — in the owner's own words where we have them.

The recommendation is a match between those two cards, made by the same matching intelligence that matches people to people and pages to people across NayaNET. It explains itself in one plain line — *"Picked because your page is about your dog grooming studio, and this cover is warm, golden and loved by pet pages."* A recommendation you can understand is a recommendation you can trust — and override.

## What done looks like

- A new page, opened cold on a phone, already wears a fitting cover.
- The cover control offers the three doors in one tap from any page.
- Searching the library by an ordinary word finds the right covers in the first screen.
- Picking a cover, or uploading a photo, saves — and the page shows it everywhere, immediately, at phone width and laptop width alike.
- The recommendation line says why, in words a person would use.
