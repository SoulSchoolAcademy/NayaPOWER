## Where we stand

**We are good.** The shelf holds 74 ready covers against a target of 60, every one of the twenty areas of life on the demand map has covers, and every cover carries its match card.

The honest shape of it:

- **Surplus — The Facets, 20.** The abstract language won, and we kept going after it had won. No more abstracts.
- **Deep — Kingdoms & Worlds, 13 · Sanctuaries, 9.** Choice without clutter.
- **Solid — Celebrations, Craft, Prosperity, 4 each.** Enough to choose from; more only when a real audience asks.
- **Thin — The Cosmos, 3 · Companions, 3.** Not gaps — every search word in these areas lands on a cover — but they are the shallow end. If creation ever resumes for a reason, it starts here, in that order, and nowhere else.
- **Unfinished, on purpose — 3 House area covers** (Booking, Templates, Intel Today) exist as designs. They belong to the born-with-cover wiring and ride the feature build, not another art round.

## The rule from here

Create against the inventory, never against enthusiasm: a new cover needs a named theme below solid, or a named audience with no cover at all. Otherwise the work is the feature — putting this shelf into citizens' hands.
