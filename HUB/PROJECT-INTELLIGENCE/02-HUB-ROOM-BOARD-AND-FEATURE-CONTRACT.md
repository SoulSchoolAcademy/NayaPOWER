# 02 — Hub Room, Board & Feature Contract

## 3. Search

Search is a first-class intelligence interaction.

Current language is strong:

> Search intelligence — what, why, source, learning, meaning…

The production search should search governed intelligence and preserve source/truth context. It should not be reduced to generic site search.

## 4. Feed modes

The Hub has three distinct modes:

### Personal
The human's private retained intelligence.

### Collective
Intelligence deliberately available to the collective layer under applicable consent/scope.

### Activity
A human-readable projection of meaningful system events and outcomes.

Changing a tab must change real query/state behavior, not merely color.

## 6. Intelligence-layer model

Where useful, one intelligence object may expose multiple human/machine views:

- **Human Note** — original human meaning/input;
- **Child / Simplified** — simplest clear explanation;
- **Grandma Note / Why Notice?** — human significance;
- **Naya Note** — Naya interpretation;
- **Machine Note** — evidence / machine boundary;
- **Adaptive Learning** — what the system learned or may learn;
- **What It Means** — significance;
- **What's In It For You?** — direct human value.

These are views over intelligence, not competing truth stores.

## 8. Room contracts

### Smart Feed
Primary living projection of useful intelligence and activity. Must preserve Personal / Collective / Activity boundaries.

### Your Intelligence Today
Answers:
- What happened?
- What changed?
- What did I learn?
- What matters now?
- What should I remember?
- What am I missing?
- What does Naya interpret?
- What can I do next?

If evidence is missing, show **UNKNOWN / NOT VERIFIED**, not invented daily intelligence.

### Your Reports
Synthesizes intelligence across time: daily → weekly → monthly → yearly → thematic/decision reporting, with drill-down to sources/evidence.

### Intelligent Library
Search, filter, inspect, connect and retrieve governed intelligence. It is not a second brain.

### Smart Connect
Human participation / governed connection surface. It covers:
- sharing choices;
- people and Naya connections;
- participation consent;
- collective-intelligence contribution;
- capability/channel discovery;
- explicit privacy and revocation state.

Smart Connect does **not** grant execution authority.

### Smart Ledger
Human-readable accountability surface:
- what happened;
- when;
- initiator;
- authority;
- receipt;
- evidence;
- outcome;
- verification.

Never fabricate a receipt/value/outcome.

### Your Connections
Governed relationships among people, Nayas, organizations, spaces and shared-intelligence contexts. Relationship != permission.

### Smart Lists
Human organization over canonical intelligence. Lists reference intelligence; they do not duplicate it.

### Smart Mail
Real governed communication only. Required states include:
**LOADING / NO MAIL / RUNTIME UNAVAILABLE / ACCESS BLOCKED / ERROR / READY**.

Never display sample mail as though it were live.

### Smart Spaces
Context boundaries for personal, project, business, research, community or collective work. Membership/sharing is governed.

### Settings
Human relationship with NayaNET:
- identity;
- authentication/session;
- privacy;
- connections;
- preferences;
- notifications;
- installed app;
- runtime/trust status.

Do not expose raw infrastructure as the primary UX.

## 9. Naya companion

The right-side Naya presence is for:

- interpretation;
- guidance;
- Ask Naya;
- What should I know?;
- context-sensitive help;
- trust/state explanation.

Naya should feel present without becoming a second dashboard.

## 10. Smart Connect + Smart Doors

The canonical Smart Door contract is owned by `BRAIN/10-INTERFACES/`.

Current registered Door families on main include:

- GitHub Connect;
- MCP Connect;
- AI Connect;
- Supabase / Data Connect;
- Email Connect;
- Calendar Connect;
- Voice Connect;
- Web Connect;
- Naya-to-Naya Connect.

Door law:

`NEED → CONNECT discovers Door → LAW evaluates permission → ACT invokes → OBSERVE → VERIFY`

The Door registry is discoverability metadata, not permission.

Future/historical channel concepts such as REST/OpenAPI, webhooks, SDK, A2A and MCP Apps should only appear as live when the current registry/runtime actually supports them.

## 11. Smart Door UI states

Every Door shown in Smart Connect should expose human-readable truth such as:

- CONNECTED;
- AVAILABLE;
- AUTHORIZATION REQUIRED;
- NEEDS ATTENTION;
- BLOCKED;
- DISCONNECTED;
- CONTRACT ONLY;
- UNAVAILABLE;
- UNKNOWN.

A Door may be technically connected while the requested operation remains unauthorized. The interface must make that understandable.
