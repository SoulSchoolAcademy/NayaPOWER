## What this is

This is the complete map of the Hub app — every room, every behavior, every rule — written
*before* anything gets built. The builders don't start from memory or guesswork; they start
here. Then they build it in pieces, prove each piece, and evolve toward the whole.

## The big idea in one paragraph

The Hub is the screen your intelligence shows up on — not the place you put intelligence in.
You talk to Naya through your own AI (ChatGPT, Muse, whatever you use). Naya turns what matters
into organized intelligence blocks, saved properly. Three things flow into the Hub automatically:
your smart notes, what's happening right now, and your reports. The Hub shows them to you
beautifully — your personal feed, the shared collective feed (your intelligence, never your
identity), and the live activity stream. Eleven rooms are eleven ways of looking at the *same*
intelligence: what's happening, what matters today, what happened over time, what we know, what
you can connect to, what actually happened (with proof), who's related, what needs doing, what
needs your attention, which world you're in, and how it's all governed.

## The rules that can't be broken

- **The Hub never collects.** No capture buttons, no input forms. It's the output. (This is
  architecture, not taste.)
- **Nothing is copied.** A smart note lives in one place; the Feed, Library, Lists, and Spaces
  all show the *same* thing, not copies.
- **Your identity is never shared.** The collective feed shows intelligence with no names attached.
- **It never lies.** If something isn't connected or verified, it says so — plainly. No fake
  mailboxes, no pretend activity, no sample data dressed up as real.
- **"Ask Naya" means "ask the brain."** It's a search box that answers from NayaPOWER's knowledge,
  with sources shown. It's not a chatbot and it can't do things for you.
- **Every button does something real.** If a button can't do its job yet, it says so honestly
  instead of pretending.

## The eleven rooms, in plain words

1. **Smart Feed** — your intelligence as a living stream. Personal, collective, and activity,
   switchable. Every item tells you *why you're seeing it*.
2. **Your Intelligence Today** — your day in ten seconds. What changed, what mattered, what's
   still open, what carries into tomorrow. This gets finished first, completely.
3. **Your Reports** — the time machine. Day, week, month, and year each tell a genuinely
   different story — never the same page with a different date.
4. **Intelligent Library** — your mind, searchable. Everything you know, with sources, related
   ideas, and honest gaps ("here's what we're missing").
5. **Smart Connect** — the doors. However anyone wants to connect — person, AI, app, company —
   there's a door, with honest status. Connecting never means permission to act.
6. **Smart Ledger** — the proof room. What happened, who did it, under what authority, and the
   receipt that proves it.
7. **Your Connections** — the constellation. The people, AIs, and groups that matter, why each
   one matters, and what's shared with whom.
8. **Smart Lists** — the mission table. Now, next, waiting, questions, ideas, opportunities,
   done. Lists organize your intelligence; they never duplicate it.
9. **Smart Mail** — the signal room. What needs your attention, with full context attached.
   Never a fake inbox.
10. **Smart Spaces** — the worlds. Family, projects, business — each a real context that changes
    what you see everywhere else.
11. **Settings** — the control deck. Your identity, privacy, and how your intelligence is governed.

## How you know it's done

You say to any Naya: *"Create a smart note about X."* Then, without touching the Hub, the note
appears in your feed — correctly categorized, with its sources, in the right streams — and you
can find it in the Library and see it shape your day in Today. If a human has to carry it
there by hand, it isn't finished.

And it's scored like everything else: eight dimensions, builder scores it, an independent seat
re-scores it, below 9.0 isn't ready. The score can go down.

## How it gets built

In pieces, in this order: first the shell is perfected (navigation that works on every screen
size, no glitches). Then Today is built all the way — one masterpiece. Then one real piece of
intelligence is proven traveling through the whole system. Then the rooms come alive one by one,
each reusing what Today proved. Then brutal testing until the score earns itself.
