# THE WHOLE PICTURE — HOW NAYANET FITS TOGETHER
### The director's page-by-page review, put together in simple words · Naya Resolve · 2026-10-26

## IN A NUTSHELL

NayaNET is one world, not a pile of pages. A person arrives, walks the Welcome walk, and is born into the system with their own apps. Every app is a complete little hub — feed, people, mail, notes, lists, reports — in one format. There is **one of everything**: one mail, one connections, one lists, one settings, one reports. Where we built two, we merge them into one ultimate version and keep the best of both. Three things are missing entirely — the Business app in your tabs, the Spaces app in your tabs, and the membership system — and the affiliate system is not yet baked into the dashboard where it belongs. Project Intelligence is the team's one record of all of this work: if it isn't written there, it isn't done.

## HOW THE PUZZLE GOES TOGETHER

1. **A citizen is born.** The Welcome walk creates their apps automatically: a **Personal app**, a **Business app**, and their **Space apps** (a space is a topic app — it can be called anything; the app is the format). The certificate page is their identity page — the first page they land on.
2. **The apps show up where the citizen lives.** A new app appears at the **TOP of the sidebar** — not the bottom — and enters their **Smart Tabs automatically**. The tab row is: Home · Dashboard · Personal app · Business app · Space app.
3. **Every app is an all-in-one hub in one format.** Same tabs, same language, same design law: cover, wall (social feed), Today, About, Smart Notes, Connections, Message/Inbox (the one Smart Mail), Growth, Lists (the one Smart Lists), Reports (with stats inside), Settings (the one settings), Library. A person who learns one app has learned them all.
4. **Membership lives on the app itself.** Any Personal, Business, or Space app can open a **membership area** — a button on the app that works only for **paid (pro) members**. The app's own page tells what the membership is, so nobody has to re-explain it. Members join there and interact there — feeds, connections, the whole world of that app, for its members.
5. **The dashboard is where the citizen sees their value.** Points with the full breakdown of where every point came from — and the **affiliate system baked in**: your Smart Link, clicks, joins, sales, and your Wednesday payouts, all tracked in the open.
6. **The system rooms serve the apps.** The Hub is the front door. Ask Naya is the voice door. Smart Factory builds every page and app from templates that mirror the finished apps. Smart Spaces is the directory — and opening a space opens the real Space app. Project Intelligence is the team's working home: every job, every score, every merge, listed, findable, organized.
7. **One rule holds it all together: one ultimate version of everything.** Never two of anything. A button inside one area may open another view of the same area — that is fine. A second page doing the same job is not.

- **The certificate / identity page** — the design is right: name in, certificate out, the five jewels, the claim-your-name door.
- **The pricing page** — the pay line rendered right: free taste (5 days, no card), paid unlimited, the numbers in plain sight.
- **The rooms Smart Mail** — the real mail: folders, search, compose, real threads.
- **The rooms Reports** — the real reports library: periods, search, day cards, read and download.
- **Intel Today (rooms)** — a real intelligence page: "Your day, answered by the intelligence."
- **The PI record itself** — 330 real items, counted honestly, duplicates mapped with survivors named, every item placed in its area (readiness dataset, 2026-10-26).
- **The PI face at /pi** — it reads the live record and shows real numbers in three views (human / AI / machine). It is NOT finished (see below) — but the record under it is real.

## WHAT'S MISSING ENTIRELY

- **The Business app** — does not exist. The Personal format mirrored for business; born at the walkthrough; in the sidebar top and Smart Tabs.
- **The Spaces app (topic app)** — the Smart Spaces directory exists, but opening a space must open the real Space app in the Personal format. Spaces are missing from Smart Tabs entirely.
- **The affiliate system in the dashboard** — the demo dashboard shows an invite journey and counts Smart Link clicks (37), but the real system — clicks → joins → sales → 50% every Wednesday, tracked on the ledger — is not baked into the citizen's dashboard yet.
- **The membership system** — the button on Personal/Business/Space apps (paid-tier only), the membership area on the app itself, member joins and interaction. Not built. (Screenshots of it don't exist because it doesn't exist.)
- **Smart Factory template completion** — the parts shelf must hold every part; templates mirror the finished apps (Personal, Business, Spaces/Community, Dashboard…). Most exist; finishing them is the tail of this program — once the apps are final, the templates copy them.
- **Naya voice on the record** — the play buttons and first-sound proof on the surfaces that promise them.

## WHAT'S IN THE WRONG SPOTS

- **New apps land at the BOTTOM of the sidebar** — they belong at the **TOP**, and they must enter Smart Tabs automatically. Today neither happens.
- **Q&A sits in the middle of About** — it belongs at the **bottom**, explained.
- **Stats live on their own page** — they belong as a **button inside Reports**.
- **`/projects` wears the Smart Growth brand** and shows a personal workspace — that address belongs to Project Intelligence.
- **The doubled pages themselves** — every second copy is a thing in the wrong spot: the work belonged in the page that already existed. Standing law from this review: **fix the page that exists; no new page.**
- **The membership button** — when it is built, it belongs on the app (Personal · Business · Space), gated to paid members — not in a separate membership section of the site.
