## 10. Refinement — feeds and memberships (Shawn, 2026-10-26 ~21:33Z — corrects and completes sections 4 and 5)

Yes, it makes sense, and here is the model back in plain words so the team builds the right thing:

**Three feeds, three different jobs.**
- **Personal feed:** your own feed of what is going on in your world. People connected to you can post on it, send you a message, and carry on a running conversation there — like your own personal feed, the way a personal feed works anywhere else.
- **Business feed:** the same thing, specifically for the business. People connected to the business post, message, and converse on the business's running feed. Personal and business are individual — one person, one business.
- **Spaces feed:** collective. A space is a group, for everybody — the running social conversation for the group. A space can also be the group for your personal network or for your business network. That is how the personal and business conversations scale into a group without losing the individual feeds.

**Memberships, corrected.**
- **Mostly in Spaces** — because that is where you want all your people to become members. A space membership can be public and open to everybody in the space, or paid.
- **Not limited to Spaces.** A Personal app or a Business app can have a membership area too — for example a private personal membership where only members get into that area and its content.
- **A membership area is similar to a Space, not identical.** Inside a membership area, members communicate the same way they do in a space — same concept, synced in spirit — but it works within the private membership area. So the communication pattern carries over; the boundary does not. Members are inside; everyone else is not.
- **Building memberships is for paid members only.** That gate holds on Personal, Business, and Spaces alike: paid members can build membership areas; everyone else cannot.
- **Not paid does not mean shut out.** If you are not a paid member, you can still join someone's Personal app, Business app, or Space, and you still get the running feed and the conversation there. What you do not get is a private membership area. Feed and joining stay open; the private membership area is the paid part.

This refinement stands alongside the earlier locked membership shape (owner chooses private, open-free, or paid access per area; content by link, not upload; one identity everywhere; the owner's own payment button in the first version, so NayaNET holds no owner money until the real checkout carries it). Where the earlier notes said "spaces + businesses," read "spaces + businesses + personal" from here on — personal membership areas are in, on the same paid-member gate.
