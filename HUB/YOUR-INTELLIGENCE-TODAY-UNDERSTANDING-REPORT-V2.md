## 2. What each document teaches — in my words

### Part #24 — Doors, not tabs
Despite the filename, this is not about a visual sidebar. It is the Smart Door
doctrine: MCP, API, GitHub, webhooks, SDK, A2A are *channels*, not architectures.
One brain, many doors. The sharpest warning in the whole corpus lives here:
**no duplicate intelligence stores merely for presentation.** Every projection —
Hub, feed, Today, reports — must remain a projection of canonical objects, never
become its own truth. For Today this means: the briefing is derived, never
canonical; the data flow is events → Smart Notes → retrieval → classification →
ranking → synthesis → briefing.

### Part #25a — Today is a cockpit, not a dashboard
The room has **one job**: tell the human what matters about their intelligence
today — what happened, what they learned, what changed, what matters, what they
should remember, what they are missing, what they can do about it. Every element
must earn its place against a ruthless filter: does it CAPTURE, UNDERSTAND,
REMEMBER, CONNECT, DECIDE, ACT, or VERIFY something valuable? If not, it does
not belong. The experience is a flow, not a grid: ORIENT → SEE → UNDERSTAND →
PRIORITIZE → REMEMBER → QUESTION → ACT → VERIFY. The killer feature is **"What
Changed"** — not "here are today's notes" but "here is what is different from
yesterday, why it matters, and the evidence." "What am I missing" exists to
prevent false completion: KNOWN / CHANGED / UNCERTAIN / MISSING. And the
contract comes before the code: make the product contract exceptional first,
then build — never polish a mediocre implementation into a beautiful mediocre
implementation. "Beauty gets attention. Utility earns permanence. Intelligence
earns trust. Memory creates attachment. Governance creates safety."

### Parts #27/#28 — The Living Interface Law
The physics of every component: **DEPTH → LIGHT → STATE → RESPONSE →
CONSEQUENCE**. The new design law in one sentence: **MAKE DIGITAL OBJECTS FEEL
PHYSICAL.** Buttons are precision instruments (beautiful at rest, "I know you're
here" on hover, physically depress on press, consequence on release). Cards are
spatial objects. Boards are destinations you enter. Icons are precision glyphs
that communicate state. Hard prohibition: **no random decoration** — remove
anything that exists only because "it looks cool." Motion communicates state,
depth, attention, life, or consequence — or it is deleted. Color has jobs:
magenta is one instrument, not the orchestra. "Alive" means responsive and
stateful — never constantly moving. "Premium is not glow. Premium is precision."
