Natural language is the interface. Users should not need to know the technical command.

## What a Smart Note is

A Smart Note is **one intelligence event / one Intelligent Block**, not multiple disconnected notes.

One canonical block contains/provides these perspectives:

1. **Human Note** — the human's experience, thought, feeling, learning, decision, discovery, question, or creation.
2. **Naya Note** — Naya's understanding, synthesis, interpretation, connections, and insight.
3. **Machine Note** — structured metadata, provenance, timestamp, classification, relationships, confidence, state, and machine-readable intelligence.
4. **Child Note** — the simplest explanation.
5. **Grammar Note** — the clearest formulation of the underlying lesson/principle.
6. **Nutshell** — the shortest useful expression.

Feed, Evidence, Connections, Collective, and Compounding are representations/projections of the same canonical block—not duplicate intelligence objects.

### Sovereign rule

> **Human intent above machine interpretation.**

Naya enriches the Human Note; it does not fabricate or silently rewrite what the human meant.

## Daily Intelligence

Smart Notes accumulate during the day. Naya distills them into a **Daily Intelligence Briefing** focused on what matters:

- what happened
- what was learned
- what changed
- decisions
- patterns
- what mattered most
- what should be remembered
- wisdom
- what carries forward

> **Distill the intelligence of the day into the wisdom worth keeping.**

The briefing is a higher-order projection of the day's notes, not a replacement for them.

## Collective intelligence decision

Do **both** real-time and distilled intelligence. They serve different purposes.

### 🌊 LIVE
Meaningful, consent-eligible intelligence can enter the Collective in near real time. This keeps the network alive and current.

### 💎 WISDOM
Naya continuously detects repetition, convergence, relationships, and higher-order patterns, producing Emerging Patterns, Collective Lessons, Principles, and Wisdom.

Therefore:

> **LIVE = what the network is learning now.**  
> **WISDOM = what the network has learned.**

The Collective must never simply receive raw personal notes by default.

**Personal Smart Note → Naya extraction → privacy/de-identification → consent gate → Collective Intelligence Event → Collective**

Core law:

> **Private by default. Shared by choice. Collective by consent. Public by decision.**

The Collective receives the lesson/insight/wisdom, not the person's private life or identity.

## Collective Feed architecture
