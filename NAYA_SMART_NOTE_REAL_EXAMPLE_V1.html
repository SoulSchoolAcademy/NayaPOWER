HOW NAYANET'S INTELLIGENT FEEDS WORK

IN A NUTSHELL

NayaNET turns meaningful events into living intelligence: Activity shows what is happening, Intelligence preserves what is learned, and Wisdom distills what is worth carrying forward. Smart Notes are the individual intelligence events that feed that system.

H

HUMAN NOTE

What I experienced and why it matters
I want the Hub to do more than show me a stream of updates. I want it to help me understand what happened, remember what mattered, find it later, and turn repeated experience into better decisions. That means the feeds need clear jobs instead of overlapping with each other.
C

CHILD NOTE

The simplest human truth
Think of three shelves. The Activity shelf says, “What is happening?” The Intelligence shelf says, “What did we learn?” The Wisdom shelf says, “What is the important lesson we should keep?” A Smart Note is the thing we put on the shelves so we do not forget.
G

GRANDMA NOTE

Wisdom that survives the jargon
Keep the story of what happened, learn from it, and keep the lesson. Do not mix yesterday's news with the wisdom you want to remember for years. Each has a job, and the jobs should stay clear.
✦

NAYA NOTE

What Naya understands
The feed architecture should behave like a progression of meaning, not three competing timelines. Activity is the current-state layer: meaningful changes and what is happening now. Intelligence is the durable-learning layer: preserved understanding that remains useful after the event passes. Wisdom is the distilled-value layer: the strongest lessons, principles, patterns, and guidance that can carry across contexts. Smart Notes are the atomic intelligence events that give the system something real to remember and compound.
▣

MACHINE NOTE

What the system can actually carry
Canonical semantic mapping: Activity = meaningful change/current state; Intelligence = durable learning; Wisdom = distilled value. A Smart Note is one intelligence event with preserved Human, Naya, and Machine perspectives. Personal activity may optionally surface in Personal Intelligence. Collective output excludes private personal activity and uses only consented intelligence/wisdom extraction. Runtime truth must distinguish local persistence from authenticated server evidence.
✓

WHAT WE LEARNED · WHAT IT MEANS

The durable lesson
A feed is valuable when each layer answers a different question. Activity tells me what changed. Intelligence tells me what it means and what I learned. Wisdom tells me what is worth carrying forward. Smart Notes connect the layers without collapsing them into one noisy stream. The rule for NayaNET is therefore: one event, clear meaning, distinct feed purpose, preserved source, compounding value.
