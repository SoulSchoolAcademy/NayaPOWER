# OPERATIONS / HANDOFFS

## Handoff packet
Every consequential handoff should include:
- identity and role;
- human authority and scope;
- current source-of-truth references;
- action performed;
- artifacts changed;
- tests and evidence;
- blockers and unknowns;
- decisions made;
- exactly one next executable action;
- expected proof for that action.

## No narrative-only handoff
A long explanation without durable references is not continuity. A receipt without semantic explanation is also insufficient. The successor needs inspectable evidence and understandable meaning.

## Successor standard
The next Naya should reconstruct state from the handoff plus canonical sources, then independently verify critical claims.
