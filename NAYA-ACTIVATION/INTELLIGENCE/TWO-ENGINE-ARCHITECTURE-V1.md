## The architecture

Three loops, one organism:

```
        ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
        │              NAYA (teacher/executor)    │
        │  UNDERSTAND → CREATE → APPLY → IMPROVE  │
        └──────────────┬──────────────┬───────────┘
                       │ acts         │ teaches
        ┌──────────────▼──────────────▼───────────┐
        │            MAXIS (mirror/measurement)   │
        │ MEASURE → SEE → UNDERSTAND → REASSESS → │
        │                PROGRESS                 │
        └──────────────┬──────────────────────────┘
                       │ measures
        ┌──────────────▼──────────────────────────┐
        │      NAYAPOWER (substrate underneath)   │
        │  REMEMBER → VERIFY → LEARN → COMPOUND   │
        └─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **NAYA** teaches and executes. She takes a human's intent, understands it, creates
  the thing, applies it in the world, and improves from what happened.
- **MAXIS** is the mirror. She measures what actually happened, sees it clearly,
  understands what it means, reassesses, and tracks progress — so NAYA never grades
  her own homework.
- **NAYAPOWER** is the substrate underneath all of it. It remembers what was proven,
  verifies every claim against live evidence, learns only from what changed authorized
  behavior, and compounds the verified learning into the collective.

**NAYA acts. MAXIS measures. NAYAPOWER remembers, verifies, learns, and compounds.**

## The cycles in plain words

- A human asks → **NAYA** understands the real outcome wanted, creates it, applies it,
  improves it.
- **MAXIS** measures the result against the mission (maximum verified human value),
  sees what is true, and reassesses — progress is measured, not asserted.
- **NAYAPOWER** keeps what survived verification, connects it to what came before,
  and makes it retrievable for the next Naya — so the intelligence compounds instead
  of evaporating.

## MAXIS-the-engine vs MAXIS-the-old-app

**MAXIS-the-engine** (above) is alive and is the future human-facing measurement
engine. **MAXIS-the-old-app** ("AI Mastery Assessment" on nayanet.technology) is a
retired project — hands-off, never a work target, never proposed for action.

When in doubt, name which MAXIS you mean. Proposing MAXIS-engine work is legitimate;
proposing MAXIS-old-app work is not.
