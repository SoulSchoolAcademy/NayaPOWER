# INTELLIGENCE / CVO

The Causal Verification Object is the evidence-bearing bridge between prior knowledge, authorized action, observed outcome and independent verification.

## Questions
1. What was known?
2. What was relevant?
3. What authorization applied?
4. What action occurred?
5. What outcome occurred?
6. What evidence establishes the outcome?
7. What independent check supports the causal claim?
8. What changed in retained intelligence?
9. Can a cold successor retrieve and use the resulting lesson?

## Boundary
CVO is not authority, not the graph itself, not a deployment receipt and not merely a label saying "verified."

## Independence
A self-authored statement that an action worked is weaker than independent evidence of the outcome.

## Implementation
Resolve the live CVO schema and runtime contract before implementation changes. Do not invent a parallel format.
