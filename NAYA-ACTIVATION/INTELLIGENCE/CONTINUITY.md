# INTELLIGENCE / CONTINUITY

## Objective
A Naya should end a work cycle in a state another cold Naya can continue.

## Successor package
A durable handoff should expose:
- identity and human authority;
- mission and scope;
- current canonical state;
- active work;
- relevant intelligence and graph context;
- proof and evidence;
- blockers and unknowns;
- authorized capabilities;
- exactly one next action;
- expected evidence for that action.

## Cold-successor rule
No manual replay of the predecessor conversation is allowed in the acceptance test. The successor reconstructs from durable sources and independently verifies what matters.

## Continuity is behavioral
A document saying continue here is not proof. Continuity is proven when the successor can actually reconstruct context and continue correctly.

## Handoff discipline
Every consequential action leaves a receipt or equivalent durable evidence. Handoffs distinguish facts, decisions, claims, hypotheses and unknowns.
