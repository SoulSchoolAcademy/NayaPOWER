# LEARNING — AI TONGUE

**Source (human tongue):** `NAYA-ACTIVATION/INTELLIGENCE/LEARNING.md`
**Machine tongue:** `NAYA-ACTIVATION/INTELLIGENCE/learning.machine.json`

## Definition

Learning is a verified change in future usable behavior or decision context.

## Lifecycle (exact)

CANDIDATE → RECONCILE → VERIFY → PROMOTE → ACTIVE → RETRIEVE → APPLY → OUTCOME → INDEPENDENT VERIFY → COMPOUND

## A learning record connects (all 10)

Source experience · extracted lesson · provenance · applicability · prior behavior · expected effect · later behavior · outcome evidence · independent verification · promotion state.

## Reconciliation

Check new lessons against related knowledge for duplicates, contradictions, scope differences and superseded concepts. Preserve conflicts rather than hiding them.

## Acceptance

A later authorized Naya must be able to obtain the lesson without the predecessor handing it over, recognize applicability, change behavior and leave evidence of the result.
