# NayaNET Intelligent Hub — Experience Contract V1

**STATUS:** HUMAN-DIRECTOR-DIRECTED DESIGN CONTRACT — 2026-10-01  
**AUTHORITY:** Human Director  
**SCOPE:** Welcome → Identity → Intelligent Hub visual language, interaction quality, layout, component behavior, and production-app conversion.  
**SOURCE BASELINE:** `HUB/NAYANET WELCOME PAGE.html`, `HUB/NAYANET INDENITY PAGE.html`, `HUB/NAYANET INTERFACE CONCEPT.html`, and the current governed NayaPOWER design/architecture contracts.

## 0. PURPOSE

Lock the design DNA before the static Hub concept is converted into a production application.

The existing Hub concept is a **minimum visual-quality reference**, not the production architecture and not a license to preserve dead controls or static mock behavior.

The production objective is:

> **The most premium, alive, dimensional, intelligent human-AI cockpit the user has experienced — while remaining fast, readable, governed, truthful, and connected to real capability.**

The Hub remains a projection/interface. It MUST NOT become a competing brain.

## 1. NON-NEGOTIABLE EXPERIENCE

The NayaNET interface MUST feel:

- premium;
- high-tech;
- dimensional;
- alive;
- electric;
- jewel-like;
- precise;
- coherent;
- responsive;
- calm enough to read;
- powerful without becoming visually chaotic.

Flat generic SaaS styling is a regression.

The visual system MUST preserve **living depth**: controls and boards should feel elevated out of the surface rather than painted onto it.

## 2. LIVING DEPTH

A premium interactive surface SHOULD use a disciplined stack of depth cues:

1. obsidian/near-black base;
2. local color aura;
3. elevated gradient body;
4. luminous edge/border;
5. restrained inner highlight/specular line;
6. lower physical-depth shadow or bevel;
7. cast shadow;
8. state glow;
9. short physically believable motion.

Depth MUST communicate hierarchy and interactivity. Glow alone is not depth.

Hover/active behavior MUST feel physical:
- hover: lift roughly 2–4 px, brighten edge/aura, strengthen icon;
- active/press: return toward the surface, reduce cast shadow, preserve legibility;
- selected: sustained local glow/state marker;
- disabled: visibly non-live and MUST NOT masquerade as active capability.

## 3. SPECTRAL INTELLIGENCE PALETTE

Foundation: obsidian / black / near-black.

The system MAY use the full elite spectral sequence, intentionally and with controlled saturation:

**MAGENTA → PURPLE → VIOLET → INDIGO → SAPPHIRE BLUE → CYAN/TEAL → EMERALD → LIME → YELLOW → GOLD → RICH ORANGE → RED → MAGENTA**

Representative current tokens include:
- magenta `#d86cff` / `#ff4fd8`;
- purple `#9d75ff`;
- indigo `#6675ff`;
- sapphire/blue `#55b9ee`;
- emerald `#55e39a`;
- lime `#b8ee57`;
- yellow `#f1d75a`;
- gold `#e8c766`;
- orange `#ff9a5a` / `#ff9b4a`;
- red `#ff5e6c`.

The earlier rule prohibiting yellow/gold accents is superseded for the Hub by this Human-Director direction.

Color MUST be thematic and semantic, not random decoration.

## 4. BOARD THEME LAW

Every major intelligence board is a distinct themed experience.

A board MUST:
- have one dominant spectral theme;
- carry that theme through border, local aura, accent rail, jewel/icon, section headers, active controls, and meaningful highlights;
- remain readable on the obsidian foundation;
- preserve a coherent family resemblance to every other board.

The current nine-board progression is a valid baseline:

1. What Is Naya Power? — magenta;
2. What Is Naya? — purple;
3. What Are Smart Notes? — indigo;
4. Your Intelligence Today — sapphire/blue;
5. Intelligence Reports — emerald;
6. Intelligent Library — lime;
7. Smart Lists — yellow;
8. Intelligent Feed / Smart Feed — gold/orange;
9. Smart Tabs — red.

Future boards MAY extend the full spectrum, but MUST NOT collapse into same-color repetition.

## 5. PREMIUM BOARD ANATOMY

A full Smart Note / Intelligent Block board SHOULD preserve this hierarchy:

1. identity/glyph jewel;
2. title;
3. intelligence/provenance metadata;
4. visible truth/source state;
5. “In a Nutshell” premium summary;
6. perspective/intelligence layers;
7. useful actions;
8. trust/provenance footer.

Current perspective vocabulary is valuable and SHOULD remain available where applicable:
- Human Note;
- Child / Simplified;
- Grandma Note / Why Notice?;
- Naya Note / Interpretation;
- Machine Note / Evidence Boundary;
- Adaptive Learning;
- What It Means / Significance;
- What’s In It For You? / Human Value.

The production app MAY progressively disclose these layers, but MUST NOT destroy the underlying intelligence model just to shorten the screen.

## 6. PRIMO BUTTON CONTRACT

Every primary NayaNET button MUST look intentionally engineered.

A premium button SHOULD include:
- strong but refined outline;
- raised gradient body;
- inset top highlight;
- physical lower edge/depth;
- cast shadow;
- local semantic color;
- icon with its own contained depth;
- hover lift;
- active press;
- visible keyboard focus;
- selected/on state;
- disabled/loading state.

Sidebar buttons MUST light up and elevate on hover/selection.

Board action buttons MUST remain tactile and stateful:
- Favorite;
- Save;
- Like;
- Love;
- Rank/Rate;
- Share;
- any future action with a real causal path.

No control may visually promise capability that does not exist.

## 7. DESKTOP LAYOUT CONTRACT

Preserve the current successful spatial model unless usability evidence proves a better arrangement:

### Left rail
Persistent navigation and identity orientation:
- NayaNET brand / Intelligent Hub;
- Smart Feed;
- Your Intelligence Today;
- Your Reports;
- Intelligent Library;
- Smart Connect;
- Smart Ledger;
- Your Connections;
- Smart Lists;
- Smart Mail;
- Smart Spaces;
- Settings;
- privacy statement at the bottom.

### Main intelligence column
Order:
1. top title + source/runtime state;
2. ecosystem/top action strip where still useful;
3. prominent intelligence search;
4. contextual greeting/hero;
5. Collective / Personal / Activity mode controls;
6. page/workspace content;
7. living Smart Note / Intelligent Block stream.

### Naya companion
Naya is the trusted thinking partner, not another dashboard widget.
The companion surface should expose useful actions such as Ask Naya / What Should I Know? only when connected to real behavior.

## 8. NAVIGATION / FEATURE MEANING

### Smart Feed
Living presentation layer over current intelligence/activity.
It MUST preserve separate:
- Collective;
- Personal;
- Activity
views with different privacy/provenance rules.

### Your Intelligence Today
Daily intelligence cockpit:
- what happened;
- what changed;
- what was learned;
- what matters now;
- what should be remembered;
- what may be missing;
- Naya’s interpreted view;
- high-value next actions.

It MUST NOT manufacture daily facts when retrieval/evidence is absent.

### Your Reports
Time-based intelligence synthesis:
- daily;
- weekly;
- monthly;
- yearly;
with drill-down to underlying Smart Notes/evidence.

### Intelligent Library
Search/browse/navigation layer for canonical intelligence.
It should answer “what is this, why does it matter, how does it work, how do I use it, where is the source, what is related?”

### Smart Connect
The human-facing participation, connection, sharing and consent surface.

Smart Connect SHOULD make it simple for the human to:
- understand what is private;
- choose what may be shared;
- inspect relevant people/Naya/space relationships;
- see which governed capabilities/Doors are available;
- understand whether a Door is connected, blocked, authorization-required, unavailable or unknown;
- deliberately contribute eligible intelligence to collective contexts where consent permits;
- revoke or change sharing/participation state where the governing runtime supports it.

Sharing MUST preserve ownership, provenance, privacy state, consent lineage and revocation semantics.

**Smart Connect does not grant execution authority.**

#### Smart Doors inside Smart Connect

Smart Connect is the human experience layer. **Smart Doors** are governed capability channels owned by `BRAIN/10-INTERFACES/`.

The governing law is:

> **Doors expose what Naya can do. LAW decides what Naya may do. ACT does it. VERIFY checks what happened.**

The current Smart Door contract/registry, not the visual Hub, determines which Doors exist and their truth state.

A connected or technically available Door MUST NOT be presented as authorization to perform a consequential operation.

Canonical Door flow:

`NEED → CONNECT discovers Door → LAW evaluates permission → ACT invokes Door → OBSERVE → VERIFY`

### Smart Ledger
Human-readable value/receipt/activity surface over governed records.
It MUST never fabricate value, outcome, transaction, receipt or economic claim.

### Your Connections
People/relationship surface using governed connection records and permissions.

### Smart Lists
Intentional collections of Smart Notes without duplicating canonical intelligence.
One intelligence object may belong to multiple lists.

### Smart Mail
Governed communication surface connected to real threads/messages and applicable authority.

### Smart Spaces
Focused collaboration/context areas.
Private by default; sharing and membership require explicit governed state.

### Settings
Identity, privacy, runtime/session truth, preferences, integrations and applicable controls.
Settings SHOULD explain current state rather than hide it.

## 9. WELCOME → IDENTITY → HUB FLOW

Canonical human journey:

**WELCOME → IDENTITY → INTELLIGENT HUB**

The Academy is not the default destination of this onboarding path.

### Welcome
Preserve most of the current premium entrance:
- black/obsidian canvas;
- orbital spectral jewel ring;
- premium portal/vault;
- restrained motion;
- name-first entry;
- About Us / White Paper / Powercasts access.

Improve only where it increases clarity, speed, accessibility or conversion without flattening the experience.

### Identity
Identity requires significant production work.

It MUST become the real activation/onboarding bridge, not merely a decorative localStorage alias screen.

It should establish or display, where applicable:
- human identity/session;
- NayaNET alias/namespace;
- Naya identity;
- privacy state;
- connected NayaPOWER state;
- GitHub/fork activation status when relevant;
- NayaNET membership/connection status;
- install-app option;
- exact next action.

The normal successful Identity action MUST enter the Intelligent Hub.

A Hub-side auth gate may exist as recovery/session-establishment behavior, but SHOULD NOT create a duplicate onboarding experience after Identity has already succeeded.

## 10. STATIC CONCEPT → PRODUCTION APP

The 800k+ single HTML concept is a visual/prototyping artifact.

Production implementation MUST be componentized.

Recommended boundaries:
- design tokens/theme;
- App Shell;
- Sidebar/NavButton;
- TopBar;
- Search;
- FeedModeTabs;
- IntelligenceBoard;
- IntelligenceLayer;
- ActionButton;
- NayaCompanion;
- workspace/page components;
- Welcome;
- Identity;
- runtime/data adapters.

Do not preserve repeated injected CSS/JS repair layers as the long-term application architecture.

The production app MUST consume governed runtime data and MUST NOT duplicate canonical intelligence into a second front-end database/model.

## 11. MOTION

Motion should make the interface feel alive, not noisy.

Use motion for:
- hover elevation;
- active press;
- board entrance;
- state transition;
- subtle spectral/aura movement;
- Naya presence;
- success/confirmation;
- controlled loading.

Avoid constant blinking, random particle noise, large layout shifts, or movement that competes with reading.

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 12. ICON QUALITY

Icons MUST look like part of a luxury intelligent instrument.

Prefer:
- coherent vector icon family;
- contained jewel/metal/energy treatment;
- consistent stroke/weight;
- semantic color;
- dimensional housing where appropriate.

Avoid a random mix of emoji, unrelated symbols and flat utility icons in the final production system.

Emoji MAY remain in concept/prototype content where useful, but production navigation/actions SHOULD converge on a coherent icon system.

## 13. TYPOGRAPHY / READABILITY

Visual spectacle MUST NOT reduce intelligence comprehension.

Requirements:
- high contrast;
- readable body sizes;
- short line lengths where appropriate;
- strong title hierarchy;
- consistent metadata scale;
- no pale decorative text for primary reading;
- progressive disclosure for very long intelligence;
- no information hidden only by color.

## 14. RESPONSIVE CONTRACT

Desktop, tablet and mobile MUST preserve:
- hierarchy;
- premium depth;
- tactile buttons;
- themed boards;
- navigation clarity;
- readable intelligence;
- real capability.

Mobile MUST NOT become a flat stripped-down afterthought.

Sidebar may become drawer/bottom navigation where needed, but feature meaning and semantic color remain consistent.

## 15. ACCESSIBILITY

Minimum requirements:
- keyboard operation;
- visible focus;
- semantic labels;
- sufficient contrast;
- reduced-motion support;
- useful screen-reader names;
- no color-only state;
- touch targets sized for reliable use.

Premium design includes accessibility.

## 16. PERFORMANCE

Luxury does not mean slow.

Production target:
- fast first useful paint;
- smooth interaction;
- no expensive uncontrolled animation loops;
- lazy-load noncritical imagery/data;
- avoid layout thrash;
- keep board rendering virtualized/paginated as feeds grow.

Perceived liveness MUST come from disciplined composition, not excessive DOM/CSS weight.

## 17. TRUTH / FUNCTIONALITY LAW

The interface may display:
- VERIFIED capability as live;
- unavailable capability as unavailable;
- unknown state as unknown;
- blocked state as blocked.

It MUST NOT display fake success.

A beautiful dead button is a defect.

A working control with generic/flat styling is also below the Hub quality bar.

The production goal is **visual excellence + causal functionality + truth + speed**.

## 18. PRESERVATION LAW

When rebuilding the app, preserve before replacing:
- left-rail placement and intelligence navigation logic;
- top search prominence;
- premium raised button feel;
- current board hierarchy;
- per-board spectral theming;
- living depth;
- source/truth visibility;
- Collective / Personal / Activity distinction;
- Naya as trusted thinking partner;
- obsidian foundation;
- privacy language;
- Smart Connect / Smart Door truth and capability≠authority separation.

A redesign MUST demonstrate a measurable UX/design improvement before discarding these characteristics.

## 19. ACCEPTANCE GATES

A production Hub screen is not complete until:

1. visual parity meets or exceeds the approved baseline;
2. every visible control has a real causal path or explicit unavailable state;
3. data/state comes from governed canonical seams;
4. navigation and browser history behave like an app;
5. loading/empty/error/blocked/unknown/success states are designed;
6. desktop/tablet/mobile pass;
7. keyboard/reduced-motion/basic accessibility pass;
8. performance remains responsive under realistic data volume;
9. no duplicate front-end brain/store is created;
10. Human Director visual review confirms the experience has not regressed.

## 20. NORTH STAR

> **OBSIDIAN FOUNDATION. SPECTRAL INTELLIGENCE. LIVING DEPTH. PRIMO CONTROLS. REAL CAPABILITY. ZERO VISUAL REGRESSION.**

The user should feel that the intelligence is alive, valuable, understandable and under their direction.
