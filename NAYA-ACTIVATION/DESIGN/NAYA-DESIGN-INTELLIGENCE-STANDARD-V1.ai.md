**HUMAN INTENT → PURPOSE → INFORMATION → HIERARCHY → INTERACTION → STATE → TRUST → VISUAL FORM → CAUSAL FUNCTION → OUTCOME**

## Better-than-the-spec rule

The spec is a floor, not a creativity ceiling. A better approach may be proposed when it states: human advantage; current limitation; preserved behavior; expected improvement; risks; measurement; rollback/supersession. Do not replace a strong experience for fashion, personal taste or engineering convenience.

## Design learning

Design learning uses the existing LEARN/EVOLVE path. A lesson begins as candidate intelligence and may be promoted only with evidence such as: explicit Human Director preference; repeated user outcome; reduced error/confusion; task-completion improvement; accessibility gain; performance gain without experience regression; accepted visual comparison; successful reuse. Record provenance, applicability, evidence, contradictions and supersession. AI-builder preference alone never creates universal law.

## Ten adopted human-experience laws (exact)

1. **PURPOSE BEFORE INTERFACE** — resolve the human's real outcome before selecting page, component, layout, treatment or interaction.
2. **DISTILL BEFORE DISPLAY** — raw data/memory/events/intelligence are not presentation; determine what matters now, then progressively disclose depth and evidence.
3. **RECOGNITION BEFORE RECALL** — visible, familiar, contextual choices over requiring the human to remember commands, locations, meanings or prior state.
4. **ONE OBVIOUS NEXT ACTION** — every major surface answers easily: Where am I? What matters? What can I do? What happens next?
5. **PERCEPTIBLE STATE + IMMEDIATE FEEDBACK** — every meaningful interaction communicates affordance, current state, action response and consequence; never make the human guess whether something happened.
6. **PREVENT ERROR BEFORE EXPLAINING ERROR** — constraints, safe defaults, confirmation where consequence warrants, clear recovery, undo/reversibility when possible, calm actionable failure states.
7. **COMPLEXITY STAYS IN THE MACHINE** — more system intelligence must reduce human cognitive work; more capability must not automatically produce more controls, settings, text, panels or navigation.
8. **STABLE GRAMMAR, ADAPTIVE INTELLIGENCE** — personalize content, priority and context aggressively while keeping core interaction grammar predictable enough to learn and trust.
9. **DIRECT MANIPULATION WHEN IT CLARIFIES CAUSALITY** — let people interact with the meaningful object/state itself when their mental model supports it.
10. **DESIGN MUST LEARN FROM HUMAN OUTCOMES** — a lesson is promoted only on observed comprehension, task success, error, time, cognitive burden, accessibility, performance, trust and accepted human judgment; verify, then preserve the scoped lesson.

## Benchmark-to-beyond design law (exact chain + rules)

**RESEARCH → DECOMPOSE → CROSS-REFERENCE → PRESERVE → SYNTHESIZE → SURPASS → TEST → VERIFY → LEARN.** Benchmark to understand the frontier, synthesize its strongest transferable intelligence, preserve what Naya already does better, and deliberately build beyond it. Benchmark across multiple leaders/products/disciplines/research sources; separate principle from style (learn why a pattern succeeds before borrowing its shape); never flatten a distinctive successful NayaNET experience merely to match prevailing SaaS conventions; search for combinations no single benchmark possesses (e.g. Oura-level distillation + Linear-level focus + Apple-level physicality + NayaNET-level intelligence/provenance/continuity); generate at least one credible beyond-benchmark alternative when the problem is material and the space underdetermined; compare candidates against the same human-outcome gates plus NayaNET's stronger truth, authority, continuity and intelligence requirements; treat "10× better" as ambition toward step-change human value — quantify only when a real metric supports the factor; promote a new design lesson only after evidence shows the synthesis improved the relevant human outcome without regressing critical dimensions. The goal is not a beauty contest — it is making the human experience materially better and leaving the verified improvement reusable.
