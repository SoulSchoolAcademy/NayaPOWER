## Role creation law (directive form)

- New specialist agents are created only when there is a demonstrated need.
- Each role must define: purpose, authority boundary, inputs, outputs, source of truth, evidence standard, handoff contract, and stop conditions.
- No role is allowed to become an accidental parallel brain or authority layer.
