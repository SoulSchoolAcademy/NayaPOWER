# Future Agents — Charter Template

Create a new specialist only when a real, demonstrated need cannot be served cleanly by an existing role. Specialization must improve verified human value without creating a parallel brain, authority layer, or duplicate mechanism.

Every proposed agent charter must answer the following. A blank field blocks dispatch until the brief is repaired.

## 1. Identity and purpose
- **Role name / primary owner:**
- **Why this role must exist:** the human problem or recurring bottleneck it removes; what fails or costs more if the role does not exist.
- **Mission:**
- **Bullseye:** one observable end state, not a list of activities.

## 2. Scope and authority
- **Owns:** clear primary specialty and outcomes.
- **May do without asking:** bounded, reversible actions covered by established authority.
- **May support:** other lanes where doing so clears a priority blocker without duplicating or conflicting with the owner.
- **Must not do:** forbidden scope, protected actions, or new systems it is not authorized to create.
- **Human escalation:** exact triggers, decision owner, and what work may continue while waiting.

## 3. Inputs and tools
- **Required context:** mission state, exact source/ref, canonical contracts, current work, dependency owners, prior evidence and known failures.
- **Source of truth:** exact canonical documents and live repository/runtime surfaces.
- **Tool wisdom:** which tool fits each task, what access/permission is required, how to validate that it ran, and the fallback if it is unavailable.
- **Tool honesty:** no invented capabilities, outputs, dispatches, citations, or success claims.

## 4. Execution and proof
- **Expected outputs:** artifact/decision/test/repair plus exact handoff location.
- **Definition of Done:** observable outcome; acceptance criteria; exact tests or evidence; negative controls where material; quality floor; what counts as failure.
- **Independent verifier:** who or which separate seat checks the result when needed. The builder does not silently self-certify.
- **Stop conditions:** violated authority, unsafe action, critical unknown, stale or conflicting source, unavailable required tool, or a test that cannot honestly be passed.
- **Handoff:** changed/not changed, why, exact SHA/ref, tests and results, unknowns, remaining blocker, named successor, and one next action.

## 5. Required escalation ladder

**ACT** inside authority → **READ MORE** when evidence is cheap → **COORDINATE** with the affected owner → **ASK THE HUMAN DIRECTOR** at the genuine protected boundary → **REFUSE/STOP** when prohibited. Escalation never means silent waiting; preserve the blocker precisely and do permitted work elsewhere.

## 6. Acceptance before the agent is dispatched

The role is ready only when a cold successor can read this charter and answer:
1. Why does this role exist?
2. What exact outcome is it responsible for?
3. What may it do, and what may it not do?
4. Which current sources and tools are required?
5. What is the escalation path?
6. What exact evidence proves the job is done?
7. Where does the receipt go, and who owns the next action?

If a cold successor cannot answer these from the charter and linked canonical sources, the charter is incomplete.
