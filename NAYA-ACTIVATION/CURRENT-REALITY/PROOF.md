# CURRENT REALITY / PROOF

## Proof registry
Use .naya/control-plane/PROOF.json, project-intelligence receipts and actual workflow/runtime evidence.

## Proof hierarchy
Distinguish implemented code, tests, workflow execution, runtime behavior, independent verification and production evidence.

## Claim format
Every meaningful proof claim should identify claim, artifact or runtime under test, test method, observed result, evidence reference, timestamp or version and limitations.

## Negative evidence
Failures are first-class evidence. Preserve failed runs and explain whether they reveal a defect, unsupported capability, stale test, environment mismatch or missing evidence.

## Cold successor
A continuity claim requires successor evidence, not merely a predecessor receipt.
