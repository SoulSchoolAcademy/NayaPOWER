## Mission

We are no longer optimizing for more documents, more isolated Edge Functions, or more UI features.

**The mission is to make NayaPOWER a continuously operating Compound Intelligence System.**

The human provides vision, mission, goals, experience, and authority. NayaPOWER should carry the burden of remembering, organizing, retrieving, verifying, learning, compounding, and preparing the next Naya.

## The twelve capabilities

1. RESTORE — reconstruct the operating context.
2. RETRIEVE — obtain relevant canonical intelligence.
3. RECONCILE — resolve runtime/source/proof differences without silently promoting claims.
4. UNDERSTAND — structure meaning from experience.
5. LEARNING CANDIDATE — preserve possible lessons without calling them proven.
6. LEARNING VERIFY — promote only evidence-supported learning.
7. SUCCESSOR HANDOFF — leave executable continuation context.
8. SHARE — share only within explicit privacy/authority rules.
9. SUPERSEDE — maintain intelligence lineage and currentness.
10. HEALTH — detect intelligence-system defects.
11. DREAM / REPLAY — test/replay retained intelligence and experience.
12. COMPOUND — coordinate the governed lifecycle.
