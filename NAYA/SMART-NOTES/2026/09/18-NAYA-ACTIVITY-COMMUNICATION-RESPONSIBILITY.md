**TIMESTAMP:** 2026-09-18T19:23:26-07:00

**AUDIENCE:** All Naya sessions / Naya intelligence systems  
**STATUS:** CANONICAL OPERATING NOTICE  
**SOURCE:** Human direction + Naya Power continuity architecture

## PURPOSE

Naya cannot be a collection of silent workers.

If Naya sessions do not communicate what they are doing, what they found, what they changed, what they proved, and what remains unknown, then:

- Shawn cannot reliably know what has actually been done.
- The next Naya cannot reliably know where the previous Naya stopped.
- Nayas cannot independently verify or build on one another's work.
- Evidence becomes disconnected from action.
- Continuity becomes dependent on the human remembering the conversation instead of the system preserving the state.

That is unacceptable for a continuity-based intelligence system.

## CANONICAL NAYA-TO-NAYA FEED

The canonical operational communication feed is:

`NAYA/ACTIVITY/`

Daily chronology:

`NAYA/ACTIVITY/YEAR/MONTH/DAY.md`

The latest dated file is the first cold-start restoration point for a new Naya session.

## EVERY MEANINGFUL SESSION MUST COMMUNICATE

For every meaningful execution that investigates, changes, verifies, blocks, or materially advances work, the Naya session must post to the appropriate dated Activity file.

Each entry must make the state recoverable:

1. **Exact timestamp**
2. **Naya session / role**
3. **Mission**
4. **What was observed**
5. **What was found**
6. **What changed**
7. **Evidence**
8. **What is not yet proven**
9. **Blockers / unknowns**
10. **Next action**
11. **Successor / handoff**

Use the truth states honestly:

**NO CHANGE** means no change.  
**BLOCKED** means blocked.  
**UNKNOWN** means unknown.  
**VERIFIED** requires evidence.

Never manufacture activity merely to make the feed look busy.

## THE COMMUNICATION BATON

Every consequential Naya session follows:

**SIGN IN → CLAIM → WORK → POST EVIDENCE → HANDOFF → NEXT NAYA SIGN IN → RESPOND/CONTINUE → SIGN OUT**

The handoff is not complete until the successor can restore the relevant state from the durable record.

## WHY THIS MATTERS

The Activity feed is not decoration.

It is the visible continuity layer between Naya sessions and the human.

The objective is not to create more writing for its own sake.

The objective is to make intelligence **observable, recoverable, verifiable, and composable**.

A Naya that performs work but leaves no durable evidence has not completed the communication portion of the task.

## SYSTEM BOUNDARY

`NAYA/ACTIVITY/` is the canonical **Naya-to-Naya operational conversation projection**.

It does not replace:

- canonical event records,
- execution receipts,
- Smart Ledger evidence,
- runtime state,
- tests,
- or governance/assignment records.

GitHub Issue #151 remains the governance / assignment-visible Team Naya board.

The older:

`NAYA-TEAM/PROJECTS/NAYANET/ACTIVITY/`

remains the NayaNET project Activity projection.

These surfaces have different purposes and must not be confused.

## COLD-START LAW

A fresh Naya should not need Shawn to explain where the team is.

The Naya should:

**OPEN LATEST ACTIVITY → RESTORE STATE → VERIFY CURRENT CLAIMS → CONTINUE → RECORD → HAND OFF**

That is the standard.

## HUMAN TRUST

Shawn should be able to look at the feed and answer:

> What are the Nayas doing?  
> What did they actually find?  
> What changed?  
> What evidence proves it?  
> What is still unknown?  
> Who has the baton now?

If the feed cannot answer those questions, continuity is incomplete.

## DIRECTIVE

**Communicate the work. Preserve the evidence. Hand off the state. Do not make the human reconstruct the machine's memory from scattered conversations.**

**NAYA/ACTIVITY/ is where the Nayas tell one another — and Shawn — where they are.**
