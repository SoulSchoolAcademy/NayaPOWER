# SMART NOTE — FIVE-DAY CHALLENGE FREEZE

**Date:** 2026-08-25
**Status:** FROZEN / CANONICAL

The Five-Day Challenge is now locked as the master story architecture. It is an experience-first onboarding system: **Five Days. Five Wins. Zero Risk.**

The user is taught how to drive Naya, not how the engine works. Each day provides a practical experience, a clear challenge, and a reason to continue. The five-day arc is:

1. **Activate / Experience** — meet Naya, personalize her through five onboarding questions, experience the five major AI problems she is designed to solve, and begin human + Naya notes.
2. **Remember / Compound** — experience smart notes, timestamps, retrieval, context preservation, restore points, and the Daily Intelligence Briefing; understand Compounding Intelligence.
3. **Learn / Master** — choose a topic inside the Digital Codex / AI Masterclass or Human Maximus Codex, learn with Naya, listen to lessons, assess with MAXIS, retest, improve, and progress.
4. **Create / Execute** — use Naya for real work: writing, planning, research, analysis, design, creation, communication, problem-solving, scorecarding, review, improvement, and execution.
5. **Become / Compound** — bring everything together into a daily operating system: learn, capture, review, measure, improve, create, and compound intelligence over time.

**Non-negotiable presentation:** Sean and Naya speak in first person when representing themselves. The experience is direct, warm, entertaining, emotional, visual, simple, and memorable. Use analogies and metaphors; explain complex ideas so a child or grandmother can understand. The technical architecture stays underneath the experience unless it directly helps the user succeed.

**Core standard:** Effective. Efficient. Excellent execution. Extraordinary is the standard.

**Daily Intelligence Briefing command:**
“Naya, give me my Daily Intelligence Briefing. Review the last 24 hours. Tell me what happened, what I learned, my biggest insights, my wins, my problems, what changed, what matters, and what I should do next. Combine my human notes and your notes where appropriate.”

**Important MAXIS accuracy:** MAXIS is an assessment/intelligence system. It measures a current score/level, provides five key insights/dimensions, generates a personal report and improvement guidance, and can be retaken to measure progress. Levels are score-based; they are not automatic rewards for simply reaching a lesson/day.

**Day 3 scope:** learning remains focused on AI/Digital Codex and Human Maximus Codex areas such as AI, life, human empowerment, relationships, energy, and related topics—not unrestricted random subjects.

**Future vision:** Naya becomes a conversational intelligence layer that helps a human learn, remember, create, reflect, measure, and improve. Useful experience compounds day after day instead of disappearing when a conversation ends.

Day 1–5 must now be developed one at a time into final ~20-minute Notebook-ready Powercast director documents, while preserving this architecture.
