## 3. THE MAIN SHOW: SMART FEED

The **Smart Feed is the central living intelligence experience of the Intelligent Hub.**

It is not simply a social feed and it is not simply an activity log. It is the human-readable projection of meaningful intelligence and meaningful operational events flowing through the Superbrain.

The Smart Feed has three connected projections:

1. **Personal Intelligence Feed** — private intelligence belonging to the individual.
2. **Collective Intelligence Feed** — intelligence voluntarily contributed to the collective, without exposing the contributor's identity by default.
3. **Activity Feed** — private operational visibility into meaningful activity happening in the person's Superbrain.

The three projections use related governed intelligence/event structures, but they have different purposes and visibility rules.

# 4. PERSONAL INTELLIGENCE FEED

## What it is

The Personal Intelligence Feed is the user's **private living stream of Smart Notes and intelligent blocks**.

When a user creates a Smart Note, the resulting intelligent block is private by default and appears in the user's Personal Intelligence Feed.

## What appears there

Examples include:

- a new Smart Note;
- an important insight captured during a conversation;
- a useful answer worth preserving;
- a decision or discovery;
- a distilled piece of intelligence;
- a personal Daily Intelligence item;
- relevant retained learning;
- an intelligence block returned by the Superbrain when it becomes useful now;
- other governed intelligence the user is authorized to see.

## What the block contains

An intelligent block should be able to carry or reference:

- what the intelligence is;
- where it came from;
- when it was created/observed;
- its source/actor;
- authority/scope;
- provenance;
- current status;
- relevant relationships;
- privacy/sharing state;
- actions such as save/favorite/share;
- evidence where applicable;
- version and learning/outcome references where applicable.

## How it operates

```
Human / Naya creates useful intelligence
        ↓
Smart Note / intelligent block
        ↓
Private by default
        ↓
Personal Intelligence Feed
        ↓
User can understand / save / organize / share
        ↓
Optional Smart Share
        ↓
Collective projection if authorized
```

The Personal Intelligence Feed is therefore the **private default home for the user's intelligence**.

## Privacy

**Private by default.** Nothing becomes collective merely because it exists in the Personal Intelligence Feed.

## User actions

The user can, subject to the governing contracts:

- open/read;
- save/favorite;
- organize into Smart Lists;
- connect related intelligence;
- share through Smart Share;
- use the intelligence in current work;
- retain it in the Intelligence Library;
- reference it in reports or other Hub areas.

# 5. COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE FEED

## What it is

The Collective Intelligence Feed is the **shared intelligence projection created from intelligence that people voluntarily agree to contribute to the collective**.

Its purpose is to let the network become smarter from the wisdom people choose to share.

## The critical rule

**The wisdom is shared; the contributor's identity is not revealed by default.**

The collective receives the intelligence block/wisdom according to the sharing contract, while contributor identity remains private unless the user explicitly chooses otherwise and the governing rules permit it.

## What appears there

The Collective Intelligence Feed can contain contributed:

- Smart Notes;
- distilled insights;
- Daily Intelligence wisdom;
- weekly/monthly report intelligence;
- lessons learned;
- useful discoveries;
- patterns;
- practical knowledge;
- other intelligence intentionally contributed through Smart Share.

## How it operates

```
Personal Intelligence
        ↓
User chooses Smart Share
        ↓
Authority + consent + privacy check
        ↓
Collective contribution
        ↓
Collective Intelligence Feed
        ↓
Collective discovers / evaluates / discusses intelligence
        ↓
Useful intelligence can be retained / connected / learned from
        ↓
Collective intelligence improves over time
```

## Social behavior

Unlike the private Personal Intelligence Feed, the Collective Intelligence Feed supports social interaction around shared intelligence, including:

- love;
- like;
- comment;
- save/favorite;
- sharing within permitted boundaries;
- intelligence ranking/evaluation.

These interactions are attached to the collective projection and do not change the user's private ownership/identity rules.

## The long-term purpose

The Collective Intelligence Feed is intended to create a **compounding intelligence commons**:

```
many people
   ↓
voluntarily shared wisdom
   ↓
distilled intelligence
   ↓
collective learning
   ↓
smarter future retrieval
   ↓
better intelligence for the network
   ↓
more useful contribution
   ↓
compounding intelligence
```

The system should learn from the **wisdom**, not require public exposure of the person who contributed it.

## Privacy law

**Private by default • Shared by choice • Collective by consent • Public by decision.**

The Collective Intelligence Feed is the shared projection. The Personal Intelligence Feed and Activity Feed remain private unless a future explicit contract authorizes another visibility state.

# 6. ACTIVITY FEED

## What it is

The Activity Feed is the user's **private operational record of meaningful activity in their Superbrain**.

It answers:

> **What has been happening in my Superbrain?**

It is different from the Personal Intelligence Feed because an activity event is about **something happening**, while a Smart Note/intelligent block is about **intelligence worth retaining or using**.

## What appears there

Examples:

- Naya signed in;
- Naya inspected a source;
- a decision was made;
- a governed execution started;
- an execution was verified;
- a Smart Note was created;
- an important intelligence item was saved;
- a report was generated;
- a connection was created;
- a list item changed;
- a mail event occurred;
- a space event occurred;
- a system/runtime event mattered;
- a blocker was discovered or recovered;
- a handoff was created;
- a consequential outcome was observed.

Noise should not become Activity merely because a system emitted an event. The Activity projection is for **meaningful, human-relevant operational truth**.

## How it operates

```
Something meaningful happens in the Superbrain
        ↓
Governed event
        ↓
Identity + timestamp + provenance + authority + status
        ↓
Evidence / outcome references where applicable
        ↓
Activity projection
        ↓
User can see what happened and follow the evidence
```

## Privacy

The Activity Feed is private by default because it exposes operational information about the user's Superbrain.

## Why it matters

The Activity Feed provides continuity and trust. It lets the user see that the Superbrain is actually doing things instead of merely claiming that it is.

It should make it possible to answer:

- What happened?
- When did it happen?
- What changed?
- Who/what acted?
- Was it verified?
- What evidence exists?
- What remains?
- What happens next?

## Relationship to the Personal Intelligence Feed

A Smart Note may create an Activity event because creating the note is meaningful activity.

But the two are not the same object or purpose:

**Personal Intelligence Feed = the intelligence.**

**Activity Feed = the meaningful event/history of what happened.**

# 8. THE SIDEBAR = MINI-PROJECTS

The sidebar is not the main intelligence stream. It is the set of focused workspaces built around the main Smart Feed.

## 8.1 YOUR INTELLIGENCE TODAY

**Purpose:** the highlights and snapshots of the current intelligence game/show.

It answers: **What matters most to me right now?**

It is a concise current projection assembled from the deeper intelligence system. It can surface important Smart Feed intelligence, current mission/state, recent learning, reports, relationships, and next actions.

It is not the complete feed.

## 8.2 SMART FEED

This is the **main show**.

It is the living stream containing the Personal Intelligence, Collective Intelligence, and Activity projections described above.

The sidebar entry is the door to the main feed experience.

## 8.3 YOUR REPORTS

Reports preserve synthesized intelligence so it does not disappear when the live feed moves on.

A Daily Intelligence Report can be generated from the day's evidence and intelligence and can appear in the feed as a current event/intelligence item, but the report itself is retained in Reports.

That means:

**Feed = what is happening now.**

**Reports = what was synthesized and saved so you can return to it later.**

Reports should support daily, weekly, monthly, mission, topic, and other governed report periods/views.

## 8.4 INTELLIGENCE LIBRARY

The Intelligence Library is the organized retrieval layer for durable intelligence.

The user's NayaNET intelligence model contains the broader NayaNET areas/knowledge domains. In simple Hub terms, these can be represented as searchable Smart Notes/intelligence entries that can be viewed from multiple perspectives.

The important distinction is:

**Library = organized durable intelligence.**

It is not merely a folder. It must preserve meaning, provenance, relationships, versions, evidence, and retrieval context.

## 8.5 SMART SHARE

Smart Share is the consent and permission interface for sharing intelligence.

A user can choose intelligence from their private system—such as Smart Notes or reports—and explicitly agree to contribute it to the collective or share it with an authorized person, Naya, team, or space.

Smart Share is where the system enforces:

- who can receive it;
- what is shared;
- what identity is revealed;
- authority;
- privacy;
- consent;
- provenance;
- revocation/change where supported.

## 8.6 SMART LIST

A Smart List is a user-created intelligent grouping of saved/favorited Smart Notes or intelligent blocks.

The user can group intelligence by any meaningful purpose:

- project;
- topic;
- research area;
- goal;
- client;
- idea collection;
- learning theme;
- anything else useful.

A Smart List is not restricted to one taxonomy. It is an actionable organization layer over existing intelligence objects.

It should retain the source of each item and preserve the relationship back to the original intelligence.

## 8.7 SMART MAIL

Smart Mail is the communication interface for sending/receiving messages within NayaNET.

It behaves like an inbox/message system, but its purpose is intelligence, not just mail delivery.

Messages can connect to:

- people/connections;
- Smart Spaces;
- Smart Lists;
- Smart Notes;
- reports;
- projects;
- decisions;
- commitments;
- evidence.

The user can communicate with people or spaces through Smart Mail subject to authority and privacy rules.

## 8.8 SMART SPACES

Smart Spaces are governed group environments—similar in social feel to a group, but designed around shared context and intelligence.

A Space provides the interface where members can:

- chat instantly;
- comment;
- contribute;
- like/love/share where permitted;
- discuss intelligence;
- interact with the group's context;
- communicate with the Space;
- access relevant feed, notes, lists, reports, mail, and connections.

A Space is a contextual environment, not a duplicate truth database.

## 8.9 YOUR CONNECTIONS

Connections are the user's governed relationship layer.

A person discovered in a Smart Space can be saved as a Connection. The user can then organize, understand, and communicate with that connection through the Hub.

Connections should preserve relevant context such as:

- where the relationship originated;
- associated Spaces;
- shared intelligence;
- communication history;
- relevant projects;
- permissions and relationship state.

## 8.10 SETTINGS

Settings control the user's preferences, privacy, notification, sharing, presentation, and operational choices.

The exact settings contract remains a mini-project to define before implementation is treated as complete.

Settings must never override higher-level NayaPOWER governance, authority, consent, or safety rules.
