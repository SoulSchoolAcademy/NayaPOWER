## THE PRODUCT LAYERS

**Naya Power** = operating architecture.

**Naya** = the intelligent partner operating through it.

**Naya Superbrain** = organized persistent intelligence/context/rules/tools/learning.

**NayaNET** = the network/ecosystem where people, Nayas, intelligence, communication, collaboration, and services connect.

**Intelligent Hub** = the human-facing cockpit/projection surface.

## THE INTELLIGENCE PIPELINE

### 1. Smart Note
One coherent intelligence event worth preserving.

### 2. PIS
Receives intelligence into the primary intelligence flow.

### 3. CIS
Checks, connects, learns, retains, applies, verifies, and compounds intelligence.

### 4. Adaptive Learning
Determines what should change because of the learning, subject to evidence, consequence, authority, and scope.

### 5. Smart Flow
Keeps useful intelligence moving across the system instead of allowing every interaction to become an isolated endpoint.

### 6. Superbrain
Makes retained intelligence available to future reasoning and action.

### 7. Outcome
The system observes what actually happened.

### 8. Verification
Evidence establishes whether the important claim/outcome is actually supported.

### 9. Learning update
The intelligence is reinforced, revised, superseded, rejected, or otherwise updated.

## THE THREE FEEDS

There is **one canonical event** and potentially several authorized projections:

**ACTIVITY FEED** = what happened operationally, what Naya/system did, what was verified, and what needs attention.

**PERSONAL INTELLIGENCE FEED** = private user intelligence and learning.

**COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE FEED** = intentionally published intelligence eligible for network use.

**Projection law:** ONE CANONICAL EVENT → MANY AUTHORIZED PROJECTIONS.

Never build three competing truths.

## THE CORE INFORMATION HIERARCHY

**CONTENT** = material that exists.

**INFORMATION** = organized facts/data.

**INTELLIGENCE** = useful understanding that can inform action.

**LEARNING** = retained intelligence that changes future capability/behavior.

**WISDOM** = reusable understanding strengthened through evidence, experience, context, and judgment.

The system is not trying to maximize content. It is trying to maximize useful, trustworthy intelligence and learning.

## THE MOST IMPORTANT SYSTEM RULE

**AUTOMATIC PROCESSING ≠ AUTOMATIC BELIEF.**

Naya can automatically inspect, classify, compare, deduplicate, retain candidates, and prepare actions. Trusted adoption still depends on authority, evidence, consequence, reversibility, and verification.

## THE SECOND MOST IMPORTANT RULE

**THE SUPERBRAIN IS A NETWORK, NOT A FILE.**

No Markdown file, Python script, database, GitHub repository, model, prompt, or UI is the Superbrain by itself.

The Superbrain is the connected system formed by their relationships, rules, state, retrieval, execution, evidence, learning, and continuity.
