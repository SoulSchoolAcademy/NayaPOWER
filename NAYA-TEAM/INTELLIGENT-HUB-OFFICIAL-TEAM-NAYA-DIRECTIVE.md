## 1. PURPOSE

This directive gives every Naya the same mental model for the Intelligent Hub: what NayaPOWER is, what the Hub is, what the main Smart Feed is, what its three feed projections do, what each sidebar mini-project does, and how all pieces connect.

This is an architecture/behavior directive. It does not claim that every feature is implemented or runtime-verified.

## 2. NAYAPOWER

NayaPOWER is the governed AI Superbrain system around intelligence and action. It supplies governance, reasoning, continuity, authorized execution, evidence, verification, learning, and recovery.

The human supplies vision, intent, values, priorities, and legitimate authority. Naya operates within that authority.

## 3. INTELLIGENT HUB

The Intelligent Hub is the human-facing projection and control surface for the Superbrain.

It is where intelligence becomes visible and usable: understand it, follow it, organize it, share it, communicate around it, act on it, and inspect what happened.

**The Hub is not the brain. It is not a second truth store.** Its areas are views/action surfaces over one governed intelligence system.

## 4. THE MAIN SHOW — SMART FEED

Smart Feed is the central living intelligence experience.

It has three projections:

### A. PERSONAL INTELLIGENCE FEED
**Purpose:** private living stream of the user's Smart Notes/intelligent blocks.

**Default:** PRIVATE.

**Input:** newly created or retrieved useful intelligence authorized for the user.

**Behavior:** show the intelligence with provenance/context; allow reading, saving/favoriting, organizing into Smart Lists, connecting, using, reporting, and optional Smart Share.

**Directive to Naya:** Never make a Smart Note collective merely because it exists. Treat private intelligence as private until an explicit authorized sharing action occurs.

### B. COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE FEED
**Purpose:** shared intelligence created from wisdom people deliberately contribute.

**Default:** SHARED BY CONSENT; contributor identity hidden by default.

**Input:** Personal intelligence intentionally passed through Smart Share and approved by the sharing contract.

**Behavior:** present the contributed wisdom to the collective; support love, like, comment, save/favorite, sharing where permitted, and intelligence evaluation/ranking.

**Directive to Naya:** Share the wisdom according to consent and privacy rules. Do not expose contributor identity by default. Preserve provenance internally even when identity is not displayed publicly.

**Long-term purpose:** collective intelligence compounds as useful contributed wisdom is distilled, connected, retrieved, evaluated, and learned from.

### C. ACTIVITY FEED
**Purpose:** private operational projection of meaningful activity in the user's Superbrain.

**Default:** PRIVATE.

**Input:** governed meaningful events such as sign-in, inspection, decisions, Smart Note creation, execution, verification, reports, connections, lists, mail, spaces, blockers, recovery, handoffs, and consequential outcomes.

**Behavior:** show what happened, when, what changed, actor/source, status, evidence, verification, remaining work, and next action where applicable.

**Directive to Naya:** Activity is not a dump of technical events. Only meaningful human-relevant operational truth belongs in the projection. Never confuse Activity with the intelligence itself.

## 5. THE CRITICAL DISTINCTION

**Personal Intelligence = the user's wisdom/intelligence.**

**Collective Intelligence = wisdom the user chose to contribute to the collective.**

**Activity = the meaningful story of what the Superbrain did.**

A Smart Note can create an Activity event, but the Smart Note and Activity event are different governed objects serving different purposes.

## 6. SIDEBAR MINI-PROJECTS

### Your Intelligence Today
Current highlights and snapshots. Answer: **What matters most right now?** Build from PIS/CIS and relevant feed/intelligence, not a manually disconnected dashboard.

### Smart Feed
The main show. Entry point to Personal Intelligence, Collective Intelligence, and Activity.

### Your Reports
Persistent evidence-backed synthesis. Daily/weekly/monthly and other governed reports remain accessible after live-feed items move on. Feed is live; Reports preserve the synthesis.

### Intelligence Library
Durable organized intelligence retrieval. It can expose NayaNET knowledge domains as searchable intelligence/Smart Note views and multiple perspectives. Preserve provenance, relationships, versions, evidence, and context. It is not merely a folder.

### Smart Share
The explicit consent/permission mechanism for sharing Smart Notes, reports, and other intelligence with the collective, people, Nayas, teams, or spaces. Enforce audience, authority, privacy, consent, identity visibility, provenance, and supported revocation/change.

### Smart List
User-created intelligent groupings of saved/favorited Smart Notes or intelligent blocks. Users may group by project, topic, goal, research, client, idea, learning theme, or any useful purpose. Preserve links to original intelligence.

### Smart Mail
Communication interface for messages within NayaNET. Inbox/message behavior plus intelligence context. Connect messages to people, spaces, lists, notes, reports, projects, decisions, commitments, and evidence. Reading/drafting does not equal authorization to send.

### Smart Spaces
Governed contextual environments for groups, projects, topics, communities, or missions. Provide chat, comments, contributions, reactions/sharing where permitted, and access to relevant intelligence. Do not create a duplicate truth store.

### Your Connections
Governed relationship layer. Users can save people encountered in Spaces or other authorized contexts. Preserve relationship origin, context, communication, shared intelligence, projects, and permission state.

### Settings
User controls for presentation, privacy, notifications, sharing, and operating preferences. Settings cannot override NayaPOWER governance, authority, safety, or consent.

## 7. UNIVERSAL CONNECTION

All areas participate in one flow:

**Source/event/object → identity/provenance/authority → normalize → intelligence → Smart Note/record → PIS/CIS → Smart Flow → Hub projection → human action → outcome → observe → verify → learn → adapt.**

Examples:

- Smart Note → Personal Feed → optional Smart Share → Collective Feed.
- Smart Note creation → Activity.
- Smart Note → Library.
- Smart Note → Today.
- Mail → Connection/List/Smart Note.
- Space → Feed.
- List → governed execution → Ledger.
- Ledger → Report → Library.
- Connections + Spaces + Library → PIS → Today.
- Verified outcome → CIS → future retrieval → better action.
