# Naya Power — White Paper Distribution Plan

**Status:** OFFICIAL STRATEGIC PLAN  
**Established:** 2026-08-26  
**Canonical set:** Naya Power White Paper I, II, III

## Purpose

Turn the three canonical white papers into a durable awareness, credibility, education, SEO, developer-community, founder-story, and press-distribution asset without compromising the product or making unsupported claims.

## Canonical publication sequence

**OWNED CANONICAL SOURCE FIRST → PLATFORM MIRRORS / ADAPTATIONS → COMMUNITY DISCUSSION → PRESS / MEDIA OUTREACH**

The GitHub canonical documents remain the source of truth. Platform versions should link back to the canonical set and preserve canonical attribution where supported.

## Priority 1 — High-signal technology audiences

1. Hacker News / Show HN — use when there is a real product/demo milestone, not as generic promotion.
2. DEV Community — publish technical explanations and implementation lessons.
3. Hashnode — publish canonical-friendly technical essays and architecture explainers.
4. HackerNoon — submit editorial-quality technology/founder pieces.
5. Medium — broader technology, AI, founder, and future-of-work reach.
6. In Plain English — use for accessible AI/technology explainers where editorial fit exists.

## Priority 2 — Professional and owned distribution

7. LinkedIn — founder posts, document posts, excerpts, diagrams, and discussion-led distribution.
8. Substack — build an owned publication/audience around the operating-layer thesis.
9. Naya Power website / sales page — host the trifecta prominently as the primary public knowledge library.

## Priority 3 — Community distribution

10. Reddit — targeted communities only; lead with useful ideas and discussion, not spam.
11. Relevant AI/developer communities — only where the paper directly answers an active community question.

## Priority 4 — Press releases and media

Use press releases for actual news: product launch, major release, research/white-paper publication, benchmark result, partnership, funding, public milestone, or significant technical achievement.

Potential free/low-cost distribution services to evaluate at execution time include PRLog and limited/free introductory distribution offers from other services. Verify current pricing, editorial policies, indexing, and reach before publication. Do not spend materially on PR distribution until the product and evidence are strong enough to convert attention into interest.

## Content packaging

### White Paper I — Founder Letter
Best for founder story, vision, human meaning, and why Naya Power exists.

### White Paper II — Human–AI Intelligence Operating Layer
Best for the conceptual technology thesis and the problem/opportunity framing.

### White Paper III — Intelligence Operating Model
Best for technical audiences who want architecture, operating loops, memory, execution, verification, governance, and learning.

## Sales-page presentation

Present the three papers as a visible **WHY → WHAT → HOW** trifecta. Each should open directly for reading.

The sales page may also include:
- five-day challenge videos as previews;
- a small selection of podcast episodes;
- educational excerpts and demonstrations;
- the free AI Score experience;
- a clear transition from free knowledge to the deeper Naya Power operating experience.

Principle:

**Give extraordinary value before purchase. Preserve the deeper operating experience for members.**

## Messaging guardrail

Do not claim “nobody else has anything like this” unless a specific competitive research project proves the claim. Prefer:

> “Naya Power is building a human–AI intelligence operating layer designed to make extraordinary AI more continuous, personal, actionable, and accessible to everyday people.”

Comparative claims require current evidence.

## Execution trigger

Begin active distribution after the canonical white-paper links, sales-page presentation, public demo/product path, and core MAXESS/Naya experience are sufficiently stable to receive new attention. The goal is not traffic alone; it is **qualified attention → curiosity → trust → product experience → adoption**.
