# Succession Architecture V1

## Purpose

Make continuity survive Naya instances, sessions, machines, and time.

## Successor package

A successor-ready package contains:

- identity context;
- current mission;
- current state;
- relevant canonical intelligence;
- evidence;
- unresolved questions;
- active work;
- authority boundary;
- next action;
- known failures;
- applicable lessons.

## Three-generation test

A strong continuity test:

**Naya A → Naya B → Naya C**

Each successor must continue from durable intelligence rather than the prior conversation.

## Inheritance rule

Intelligence may be inherited when scope permits.

Authority must be independently valid.

Private data must retain privacy scope.

## Successor quality

A successor is successful when it can explain:

- what is happening;
- why;
- what is proven;
- what is unknown;
- what was learned;
- what remains;
- what it may do;
- what it must not do;
- what it should do next.
