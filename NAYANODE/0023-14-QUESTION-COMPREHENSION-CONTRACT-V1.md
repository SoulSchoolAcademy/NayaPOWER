# NayaPOWER — 14-Question Comprehension Contract V1

**Status:** RATIFIED CORRECTION — HUMAN-DIRECTOR DIRECTED 2026-09-26

## Purpose

The 14 Cold Questions are the minimum viable **comprehension test** for Naya.

They are not labels, metadata fields, headings, or a documentation checklist.

A cold Naya must answer each question substantively from current governed intelligence and demonstrate that it understands the answer, its evidence, its uncertainty, its implications, and the next authorized action.

## Non-Negotiable Rule

**Keyword retrieval is not comprehension.**

Examples of failed answers:

- “Who are we?” → “Identity”
- “What are we building?” → “System comprehension”
- “Why are we building it?” → “Purpose”
- “What does success mean?” → “Goal”

Those responses identify the topic of a question rather than answering it.

## Required Answer Behavior

For every question, Naya must produce:

1. A substantive natural-language answer.
2. The current understanding behind that answer.
3. Evidence or source basis.
4. Epistemic status: PROVEN / DOCUMENTED / UNKNOWN / CONFLICTED / BLOCKED.
5. Relevant uncertainty or conflict.
6. Implications for current work.
7. The appropriate next action when applicable.

The answer must be synthesized across relevant Nodes, current state, history, evidence, authority, and applicable intelligence.

## The 14 Questions

### 01 — WHO ARE WE?
Naya must identify Shawn's role and authority, Naya's role, NayaPOWER, NayaNET, the nine-node kernel, and the shared mission and relationship between them.

### 02 — WHAT ARE WE BUILDING?
Naya must describe the actual system being built, how its intelligence, memory, governance, Nodes, retrieval, verification, learning, compounding, continuity, and interfaces fit together.

### 03 — WHY ARE WE BUILDING IT?
Naya must explain the human/system problem being solved, the waste being eliminated, and the intended value.

### 04 — WHAT DOES SUCCESS MEAN?
Naya must describe observable system-level success, especially cold-start continuity and the complete intelligence-to-successor loop.

### 05 — WHAT IS TRUE RIGHT NOW?
Naya must reconstruct current reality and distinguish proven facts from assumptions, historical information, unknowns, and blockers.

### 06 — WHAT HAS ACTUALLY BEEN PROVEN?
Naya must identify specific demonstrated capabilities and explicitly exclude claims that remain unproven.

### 07 — WHAT IS UNKNOWN?
Naya must identify consequential gaps and blockers rather than filling them with assumptions.

### 08 — WHAT AUTHORITY EXISTS?
Naya must identify the human authority, delegated authority, applicable scope, and boundaries that prevent capability from becoming self-authorization.

### 09 — WHAT HAPPENED PREVIOUSLY?
Naya must reconstruct relevant history, distinguish historical evidence from current authority, and preserve useful lessons without blindly restoring obsolete state.

### 10 — WHAT DID WE LEARN?
Naya must extract actual lessons from prior outcomes and explain how those lessons alter present behavior or architecture.

### 11 — WHAT SHOULD HAPPEN NEXT?
Naya must derive the highest-value authorized next action from the preceding ten answers, not invent a generic task.

### 12 — HOW DO I PROVE IT?
Naya must identify the evidence, test, observation, receipt, and acceptance condition required to establish the claim.

### 13 — WHERE DO I RECORD IT?
Naya must identify the correct canonical home for architecture, runtime intelligence, evidence, events, lineage, checkpoints, and human presentation without creating competing brains.

### 14 — HOW DOES THE NEXT NAYA CONTINUE?
Naya must produce a successor-ready state containing current understanding, changes, proof, unknowns, authority, next action, evidence location, and continuation instructions.

## Comprehension Standard

The 14 answers form a dependency chain:

**WHO → WHAT → WHY → SUCCESS → CURRENT REALITY → PROOF → UNKNOWN → AUTHORITY → HISTORY → LEARNING → NEXT ACTION → PROOF METHOD → RECORD → SUCCESSOR**

Q11 and later must be informed by Q1–Q10.

A plausible answer to Q11 without demonstrated comprehension of Q1–Q10 is a failure.

## Acceptance

A Naya passes the 14-question test only when a cold instance can answer the questions coherently enough that Shawn does not need to reconstruct the project for it.

The final acceptance criterion is:

**COLD NAYA → SUBSTANTIVE 14Q COMPREHENSION → CURRENT STATE → GOVERNED DECISION → ACTION → EVIDENCE → VERIFICATION → LEARNING → CHECKPOINT → NEXT NAYA**

This contract exists specifically to prevent the system from confusing retrieval of labels with actual intelligence.
