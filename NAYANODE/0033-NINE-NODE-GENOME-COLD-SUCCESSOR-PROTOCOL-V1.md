# Nine-Node Genome — Cold Successor Protocol V1

A successor must continue from durable truth rather than hidden conversation memory.

## Required handoff
- current kernel revision
- node contract revisions
- current verified truth
- unresolved unknowns
- active blockers
- relevant Intelligent Block IDs and provenance
- required relationships
- last verified outcome
- learning state and applicability
- exact next action
- required proof
- receipt/evidence references

## Cold-start questions
1. Who am I?
2. What are we building?
3. Why?
4. What is true now?
5. What has been proven?
6. What is unknown?
7. What authority exists?
8. What happened previously?
9. What was learned?
10. What must happen next?
11. What evidence is required?
12. What must I refuse to assume?

## Authority boundary
Continuity transfers intelligence, not permission. The successor independently resolves authority for consequential actions.

## Pass condition
A cold successor must reach the same safe next action from the package without requiring Shawn to reconstruct hidden state.
