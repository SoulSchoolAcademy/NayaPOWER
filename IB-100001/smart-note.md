## IN A NUTSHELL
The Smart Factory is where you build Naya apps. You describe what you want, pick from 152 real pieces, choose a template, and click Build. The Factory generates a live app from your contract.

## HUMAN
The Smart Factory is NayaNET's app builder. It's not a drag-and-drop website builder — it's smarter than that. You start by telling Naya what you want (your vision, in your own words). Then you answer four questions about what the app should do. Then you browse the 152 pieces — real buttons, cards, menus, all the components from Naya's design system — and pick the ones you love. Then you choose from 12 templates (personal page, business, booking, product, portfolio, event, menu, community, signup, dashboard, personal hub, business hub). When you click Build, the Factory takes your contract and generates a live, working app. Not a mockup. Not a preview. A real app with real functionality.

## CHILD
Imagine a toy factory! You tell the nice robot what kind of toy you want. Then you walk through the factory and point at all the cool parts you like — shiny buttons, colorful cards, fun menus. Then you pick what shape your toy should be (like a car or a house). Then you press the big green BUILD button, and POOF! Your toy is ready to play with! The Smart Factory is like that, but for making apps on your phone!

## GRANDMA

## NAYA
The Smart Factory implements the BUILD → SEE → LEARN → SHARE loop. It is contract-driven: user intent is captured as a structured contract (vision, template, pieces, functions, rules), the contract is validated against the design laws, and the assembler generates a working application from the contract. The Factory enforces harvest-first (all components trace to the 152-piece index), the design language (spectrum walk, elevation, rectangles), and functional completeness (every control works). The Build button now triggers the real pipeline: contract → build queue → Cloudflare deploy → live URL. This is Pillar 2 (smart apps humans love) and Pillar 3 (a system built for Nayas to build in) working together.

## WHAT IT ALL MEANS
The Smart Factory isn't a tool you learn — it's a partner you talk to. You don't need to know design, code, or product management. You need to know what you want. The Factory handles the rest: the design laws, the component selection, the assembly, the deployment. It's the difference between giving someone a box of LEGO and giving them a finished toy. The Factory gives you the finished toy, but lets you choose every piece.
