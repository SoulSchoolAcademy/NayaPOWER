# SEAN NOTE — DAILY INTELLIGENCE BRIEFING

**Status:** LOCKED / CANONICAL
**Date established:** 2026-08-24

## What I Want
I want a Daily Intelligence Briefing to happen as a permanent part of how I operate with Naya. Every day, I want to be able to ask Naya to review the previous 24 hours and give me the full intelligence picture.

## My Canonical Request
> “Naya, give me my Daily Intelligence Briefing. Review the last 24 hours. Tell me what happened, what I learned, my biggest insights, my wins, my problems, what changed, what matters, and what I should do next. Combine my human notes and your notes where appropriate.”

## Why It Matters
I do not want valuable experiences, lessons, discoveries, decisions, corrections, or wins to disappear into the past. I want the useful parts of each day to become intelligence I can call back later.

I want my human notes and Naya notes to work together. I want the record to be dated and time-stamped so I can retrieve the context later, understand what happened, and see how I have grown.

## Bigger Vision
The daily briefing should compound into weekly, monthly, quarterly, semiannual, and yearly intelligence reviews. Over time, this can become a meaningful record of my work, learning, decisions, growth, and journey.

The goal is not to document everything. The goal is to preserve what matters.

## Product Requirement
This must become part of the Naya Power / Digital Codex operating logic and be reflected in the canonical instruction set and future PDFs/protocols. It should be treated as a built-in behavior, not a temporary feature idea.

## Onboarding Requirement
Naya should interview a new user at activation so she can understand who they are, what matters to them, what they want to achieve, and how she can best help them. That initial context should become part of the foundation for the ongoing relationship.

## Core Principle
**Every day should leave something useful behind.**

The operating loop is:
**Experience → Note → Review → Learn → Compound → Act.**

## Additional Production Lesson
AI production instructions must explicitly specify voice identity and verify the result. Day 1 of the Five-Day Challenge was conceptually strong, but the generated video used a male voice despite the intended Naya female voice. Therefore, future Notebook/Powercast director documents must treat voice identity as a critical production constraint and include an explicit verification requirement: **female voice = Naya; first-person Naya delivery; do not substitute a male voice.**
