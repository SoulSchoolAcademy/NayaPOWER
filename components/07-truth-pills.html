When to use

Truth rows go anywhere a score, claim, or status is shown — reports, scorecards, dashboards, progress views. They make the difference between verified and claimed visible at a glance.

The states

  • .verified — independently checked by another seat. Score glows green. The label reads ✓ VERIFIED in green.
  • .claim — our assessment, awaiting verification. Score in white, label in quiet gray. Shown honestly, never hidden — but never glowing.

The laws

  • What is verified glows green; what is merely claimed stays quiet.
  • Never a wall of identical badges. One verified among claims is the honest pattern — the absence of noise IS the design.
  • Scores move on evidence, never optimism. A defended 7.5 beats a fictional 9.
  • No PR numbers, SHAs, or UUIDs in prose. Technicals ride as evidence links, never as body text.

Law: a claim without verification is a lie told to yourself first. Proof, not promises — every claim carries its evidence.
