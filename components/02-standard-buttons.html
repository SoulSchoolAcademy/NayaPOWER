Standard Buttons

Everyday actions. Primo for page actions, sn-btn for tight spaces. White at rest, theme-ignited on hover.

Primo buttons for page actions. Small sn-btn for dialogs and tight spaces. Hover to ignite.

When to use

  • .primo — the standard action button. Any action on any page that isn't a hero.
  • .sn-btn — the small button. Dialogs, popups, tight toolbars, inline actions.
  • .sn-btn.primary — the small primary. One per dialog.

Themes

Primo buttons take a theme class that sets their hover ignition color: .theme-magenta, .theme-purple (default), .theme-blue, .theme-green, .theme-gold. The theme is the button's color job — pick the one that matches what the button means, and never give adjacent buttons the same theme.

States

  • Rest: white pearl, 1.7px white rim, quiet spectrum halo
  • Hover: ignites in theme color, lifts 3px, glow blooms
  • Focus: 2px purple glow ring outside the element
  • Active: presses down slightly
  • Disabled: 60% opacity, no lift, honest about being unavailable

Law: one color job per thing. Adjacent buttons never share a hue. 44px touch floor — every button is touchable.
