When to use

Jewels are the visual markers of meaning — bullets, status indicators, section markers, list leaders. Wherever you'd use a dot, use a jewel. Wherever you'd use a colored badge, use a jewel in the right spectrum color.

The law

  • Jewels, not dots. Drawn, faceted, glowing — never plain circles.
  • Distinct cuts. Each spectrum color gets its own shape: magenta diamond, purple orb, blue triangle, green rotated square, gold dashed ring.
  • Spectrum order, always. Magenta → purple → blue → green → gold. Long sequences cycle back to magenta.
  • Never twice adjacent. Two neighboring jewels never share a color.
  • Color is identity. Each jewel color means something — see the meanings above.
