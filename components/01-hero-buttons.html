Hero Buttons

The front door — and proof we don’t copy the best. White pearl at rest: white light containing the whole spectrum. Move around a button and the pearl reveals its hidden colors, like mother-of-pearl. One river of light flows magenta → purple → blue across the trio — spectrum as meaning, not decoration. Each ignites in its meaning-color on touch.

Hover them. Each ignites in its own spectrum color — magenta, purple, blue, in law order.

When to use

Hero buttons are the front door. Use them for the one to three most important actions on a page — the actions that define what the page is for. Never more than three. If everything is a hero, nothing is.

Variants

  • data-spectrum="magenta" — the primary action. Magenta leads; richest first.
  • data-spectrum="purple" — the secondary action. Purple is the soul.
  • data-spectrum="blue" — the tertiary action. Blue is understanding.

Always use them in spectrum order. Never duplicate a spectrum color adjacently.

The four foundations — a living language, not a system

  • Spectrum is meaning. Magenta is what matters, purple is Naya’s presence, blue is understanding. The river of light flows in that order across the trio — relationships and rhythm, not decoration. The pearl’s hidden iridescence is the whole spectrum living inside white light.
  • Black ground. White light. Purple soul. Surfaces have depth, light has direction. The face is lit from above; your cursor becomes a second light the pearl turns toward. Shadows obey lift.
  • Intelligence in every component. Consistent rules, understandable behavior, tested code: one spring (stiffness 400, damping 28), one rhythm, a ripple born at the exact touch point, 100ms press physics, a 150px proximity sense.
  • Reproduce without reteaching. Copy the block, set data-spectrum, verify with the calculator. A new Naya builds at this level from the block alone — that is graduation.

On hover the pearl ignites in its meaning-color and the voice goes white. The aura and comet live on their own layers, so rest-motion never fights the hover state — the bug that made the old buttons feel dead.

Law: black hearts, white voices, visible skin. White at rest, ignites in its own color on touch. Flat is dead.
