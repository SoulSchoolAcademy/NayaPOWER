Hero Buttons

The front door. White light at rest — each ignites in its own spectrum color on touch. Three variants, law order, alive.

Hover them. Each ignites in its own spectrum color — magenta, purple, blue, in law order.

When to use

Hero buttons are the front door. Use them for the one to three most important actions on a page — the actions that define what the page is for. Never more than three. If everything is a hero, nothing is.

Variants

  • data-spectrum="magenta" — the primary action. Magenta leads; richest first.
  • data-spectrum="purple" — the secondary action. Purple is the soul.
  • data-spectrum="blue" — the tertiary action. Blue is understanding.

Always use them in spectrum order. Never duplicate a spectrum color adjacently.

Anatomy (6 layers)

  • White pearl gradient body — white light at rest
  • 1.7px white rim — visible skin
  • Inset top highlight + bottom shadow — bevel
  • Deep drop shadow — elevation
  • Spectrum halo underneath — its own color, quiet at rest
  • Light sweep on a 5.5s rhythm — alive, never still

On hover: the idle animation stops, the button ignites in its spectrum color, text goes white, glow triples. On press: it compresses slightly — you feel the touch.

Law: black hearts, white voices, visible skin. White at rest, ignites in its own color on touch. Flat is dead.

Copy-paste

Take the .living-btn CSS, the idleBreathe and sweep keyframes, and this markup. Set data-spectrum to magenta, purple, or blue. Done.

<button class="living-btn" data-spectrum="magenta"> <span class="btn-glow"></span> <span class="btn-label"> <svg viewBox="0 0 24 24" width="22" height="22" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2">…</svg> YOUR ACTION </span> </button>

Black ground. White light. Purple soul. Quiet at rest — flat is dead, jewels not dots, and the details are the design.
