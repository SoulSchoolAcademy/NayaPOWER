How to use

  1. Link living.css and living.js. One system, no forks.
  2. Copy any button's markup. Set --lv-accent and --lv-accent-rgb to its spectrum color.
  3. Add lv-hero for flagship size, lv-sm for compact, lv-btn--danger for destructive.
  4. Proximity, cursor light, and the ambient wash are automatic — living.js handles every .lv-btn and .lv-board on the page.
  5. Respects prefers-reduced-motion. Touch gets a brief wake flash.
