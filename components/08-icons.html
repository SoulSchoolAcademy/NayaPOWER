Five icons. Five distinct shapes. Each in its spectrum color — in law order, left to right.

When to use

Icons are the visual shorthand inside buttons, pills, and navigation. Each icon has one job and one shape.

The law

  • Craft distinct icons. At the highest level, draw a different icon for each meaning — never stamp the same icon repeatedly.
  • Stroke, not fill. Icons are 2px strokes in currentColor — they inherit their context's color.
  • Spectrum order. When icons appear in a row, they follow magenta → purple → blue → green → gold.
  • One icon, one meaning. The star always means achievement. The check always means verified. Never reuse a shape for a different meaning.

Law: distinct icons, not stamped duplicates. Each shape earns its meaning — and keeps it.

Copy-paste

Inline SVGs, 24×24 viewBox, 2px stroke, currentColor. Drop into any button label or pill.
