# NayaNET — What We Are Building

## The actual thing
NayaNET is the governed network and application environment through which NayaPOWER becomes human-operable and connected intelligence can be shared by permission.

NayaPOWER is the operating system and intelligence substrate.
NayaNET is the environment through which that system is experienced, connected, and eventually networked.

## Relationship
HUMAN → NAYANET → NAYA → NAYAPOWER → GOVERNED INTELLIGENCE → RUNTIME → VERIFIED HUMAN OUTCOME.

NayaNET is not a second brain. It is the governed environment through which the Superbrain becomes usable.

## Canonical Hub
NAYANET/HUB/index.html is the protected visual and structural North Star for the human operating surface.
The Hub is a projection and action surface, not a second intelligence authority.

## Network
PERSONAL SUPERBRAIN → PERMISSIONED NAYA NETWORK → COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE.

Collective intelligence requires consent, provenance, ownership, scope, authorization, revocation, auditability, and human control.

NayaNET should let a person enter, understand, operate, create, learn, connect, share, retrieve, verify, and continue without becoming the technical operator of the system.
