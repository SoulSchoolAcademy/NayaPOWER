## 01 — WHAT IS NAYA POWER?

Naya Power turns AI from a tool you repeatedly manage into an intelligent operating partner that preserves context, captures intelligence, takes responsible action, verifies results, learns from experience, and becomes more useful over time.

### HUMAN

It means less repetition, less lost knowledge, better continuity, faster action, and more useful help — while the human remains the director.

### CHILD

Imagine a really smart friend who remembers what you built, learns from what broke, and already knows what to do next instead of starting over every day.

### GRANDMA

It is like having a capable assistant who remembers what matters, learns how you work, checks the work, and helps keep things moving.

Naya Power gives Naya an operating discipline: restore context, understand the mission, act where authorized, verify what happened, capture what matters, learn, improve, and continue.

### MACHINE

It is an intelligence architecture connecting context, memory, structured intelligence, retrieval, action, evidence, verification, learning, authority, and continuity.

### LEARNING

AI becomes more valuable when useful intelligence can persist, connect, and change future behavior rather than disappearing with each conversation.

### ULTIMATE MEANING

Naya Power is about compounding human capability through a continuously improving human–AI intelligence relationship.

### HOW IT CONNECTS

**Human → Naya → Smart Notes → Compounding Intelligence → Intelligent Hub → Better context → Better reasoning → Better action → New intelligence.**

### HOW TO APPLY IT

Give Naya the mission, vision, relevant context, boundaries, and desired outcome. Capture important discoveries and decisions. Let Naya carry appropriate operational work, then verify the result.

### WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU?

**You get more continuity, more leverage, less repetition, and an AI partner that can become more useful because it can build on what matters.**

# PART IV — TRUST, EVIDENCE, AND VALUE

## 21 — WHAT IS VERIFICATION?

The process of checking what actually happened rather than accepting generated output or intention as proof.

## 22 — WHAT IS A SMART RECEIPT?

Human-readable evidence showing what intelligence or action was created, where it exists, what happened, and whether completion was actually verified.

## 23 — WHAT IS PROVENANCE?

The record of where intelligence or evidence came from and how it was transformed, so source and inference remain distinguishable.

## 24 — WHAT IS THE MAX VALUE PER ACTION (MVPA)?

A design principle for maximizing useful outcome from each meaningful action while accounting for truth, quality, effort, risk, and responsible constraints.

## 25 — WHAT IS RESPONSIBLE VERIFIED VALUE?

The principle of pursuing the highest useful outcome that is actually authorized, truthful, evidence-backed, safe, and aligned with the human mission.

## 26 — WHAT IS HUMAN AUTHORITY VS. AI INTELLIGENCE?

The distinction that being capable of doing something does not automatically mean Naya is authorized to do it. Intelligence informs action; human authority and higher-priority constraints govern permission.
