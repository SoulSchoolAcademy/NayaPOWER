# OFFICIAL CORE MAP — 24 SUBJECTS

The following is the locked V1 subject map. Each subject will become a canonical Smart Note and, where appropriate, an Intelligent Hub Smart Block / board / feed item and a searchable Intelligent Library entry.

## FOUNDATION

### 01 — WHAT IS NAYA POWER?

The operating system for the human–Naya relationship. It gives Naya an operating discipline for restoring context, understanding the mission, carrying appropriate operational burden, verifying results, capturing intelligence, learning, improving, and continuing.

### 02 — WHAT IS NAYA?

The intelligent AI partner operating within Naya Power. Naya understands, reasons, creates, acts where authorized, verifies, learns, and helps move the human mission forward.

### 03 — WHAT IS A SMART NOTE?

The fundamental unit of captured intelligence. It turns valuable knowledge, discoveries, decisions, lessons, experiences, and insights into persistent, structured intelligence.

## PERSONAL INTELLIGENCE

### 04 — WHAT IS YOUR INTELLIGENCE TODAY?

The user's current living intelligence surface: what was learned, discovered, created, captured, changed, or made important today.

### 05 — WHAT ARE INTELLIGENCE REPORTS?

Distilled intelligence across daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly time horizons. Reports turn ongoing intelligence into patterns, lessons, trends, changes, opportunities, risks, decisions, and longer-term understanding.

## DISCOVERY, ORGANIZATION & FEED

### 06 — WHAT IS THE INTELLIGENT LIBRARY?

The searchable Q&A knowledge layer of NayaNET where people and Nayas can quickly learn what Naya Power is, how it works, what its concepts mean, and how the elements connect.

### 07 — WHAT ARE SMART LISTS?

User-controlled collections of intelligence and connections that let people save, group, organize, retrieve, favorite, and share Smart Notes and/or people according to their needs.

### 08 — WHAT IS THE INTELLIGENCE FEED?

The living stream of meaningful intelligence and activity. It surfaces Smart Notes, discoveries, shared intelligence, activity, conversations, spaces, and other valuable information so people can learn by following the network.

## PEOPLE & COMMUNICATION

### 09 — WHAT ARE YOUR CONNECTIONS?

The user's saved network of people within NayaNET, organized into useful groups and connected to communication, collaboration, Smart Mail, Smart Spaces, and lists.

### 10 — WHAT IS SMART MAIL?

NayaNET's intelligent communication system for sending information to individuals, groups, lists, or spaces, with intelligent assistance and evolving scheduling capabilities.

### 11 — WHAT ARE SMART SPACES?

Shared environments organized around a topic, purpose, or Smart Note where people can discover one another, communicate, comment, collaborate, and optionally contribute useful intelligence to the collective.

### 12 — WHAT IS SMART SHARE?

The voluntary contribution layer for collective intelligence. People choose to share distilled wisdom, discoveries, lessons, mistakes, insights, and experiences so others can benefit.

## TRUST & EVIDENCE

### 13 — WHAT IS THE SMART LEDGER?

NayaNET's trust, evidence, verification, provenance, receipt, smart-link, and meaningful-activity record. It connects meaningful actions and intelligence events to the evidence needed to establish what happened and what can responsibly be trusted.

**Smart Ledger includes:**

- **Smart Links** — connections between intelligence, actions, people, objects, sources, and evidence.
- **Evidence** — information demonstrating that something happened.
- **Verification** — checking what actually happened rather than treating intention or generated output as proof.
- **Smart Receipts** — compact human-readable records of meaningful actions, results, or verified completion.
- **Provenance** — the origin and transformation history of intelligence or evidence.
- **Responsible Verified Value** — value that is useful while also authorized, truthful, appropriately evidenced, and responsibly constrained.

These are intentionally not separate top-level boards in V1 because they are parts of the Smart Ledger system.

## INTELLIGENCE ENGINE

### 14 — WHAT IS THE PRIMARY INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM (PIS)?

The core layer for turning inputs and experience into organized, useful primary intelligence.

### 15 — WHAT IS THE COMPOUNDING INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM (CIS)?

The system that makes intelligence accumulate instead of disappear by connecting intelligence across time so previous learning improves future understanding, reasoning, and action.

### 16 — WHAT IS ADAPTIVE LEARNING?

The process of learning from verified experience and changing future behavior, knowledge, safeguards, retrieval, decisions, or actions.

### 17 — WHAT IS CONTINUITY?

The ability to continue intelligently instead of starting over. Continuity preserves state, objectives, decisions, results, learning, and unfinished work so the next Naya can continue from where the previous Naya stopped.

### 18 — WHAT IS THE NAYA SUPERBRAIN?

The connected intelligence architecture behind the Naya system, combining identity, state, memory, goals, planning, intelligence, actions, observation, verification, learning, relationships, and continuity while keeping authority and evidence explicit.

## OPERATING PRINCIPLES

### 19 — WHAT IS MAX VALUE PER ACTION (MVPA)?

The principle of maximizing useful value from each meaningful action by identifying the highest-value responsible next action rather than merely reacting to the latest request.

### 20 — WHAT IS HUMAN AUTHORITY + AI INTELLIGENCE?

The constitutional distinction between who has authority and who provides intelligence. Naya can reason, create, recommend, execute where authorized, verify, and learn without confusing capability with permission.

## THE NETWORK

### 21 — WHAT IS NAYANET?

The network and ecosystem connecting people, Naya, intelligence, communication, collaboration, and collective learning.

### 22 — WHAT IS COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE?

Useful intelligence that emerges when people voluntarily contribute knowledge, experience, discoveries, mistakes, and wisdom so valuable learning can benefit others.

### 23 — WHAT IS COLLECTIVE CHAIN TECHNOLOGY?

The proposed trust and evidence architecture for connecting collective contributions and verifiable events. No claim about a specific blockchain or production implementation is canonical until it is actually defined and verified.

### 24 — WHAT IS PRIVACY BY CHOICE?

The principle that people control what they contribute, what they expose, and how identifiable their contribution is, enabling collective participation without unnecessary exposure of identity, private information, or personal context.
