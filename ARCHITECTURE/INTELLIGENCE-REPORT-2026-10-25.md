and I will not flatter it into being one. It has world-class PARTS —
an engine that proves itself, a design language with a real constitution,
an honesty discipline most systems never attempt. But the WHOLE is not
yet one organism: its pieces live in scattered places (one box, one repo,
chat uploads), the chain from citizen → page → connected Muse → paid is
not yet live end-to-end, and the system still needs hands (mine,
Resolve's, Power's) to carry pieces between places. A maximally
intelligent system would know its own state without being asked, move
its own pieces, prove itself continuously, and let any citizen and any

breathing on its own. No live loop, no self-proving loop, no single
face. That is the gap, and it is closable, which is why the rest of
this report is a plan and not a lament.
