# NAYA NOTE — DAILY INTELLIGENCE BRIEFING

**Status:** LOCKED / CANONICAL
**Date established:** 2026-08-24

## Purpose
The Daily Intelligence Briefing is a permanent Naya Power operating ritual. It is not merely a recap; it is a structured intelligence review that converts the preceding 24 hours of relevant human + AI activity into usable understanding, decisions, and next actions.

## Canonical Command
> “Naya, give me my Daily Intelligence Briefing. Review the last 24 hours. Tell me what happened, what I learned, my biggest insights, my wins, my problems, what changed, what matters, and what I should do next. Combine my human notes and your notes where appropriate.”

## Required Review
Where available and authorized, review relevant:
- human/Sean notes
- Naya notes
- timestamps and chronology
- project/work artifacts
- GitHub activity and commits
- decisions and corrections
- lessons and discoveries
- wins and completed work
- unresolved problems/blockers
- changes in priorities/state

## Required Output
The briefing should clearly surface:
1. What happened
2. What was learned
3. Biggest insights / gold
4. Wins
5. Problems / losses / risks
6. What changed
7. What matters now
8. What should happen next
9. The highest-value lesson from the day
10. A concise executive summary

## Note Architecture
Use both sides of the intelligence record:
- **Sean Note / Human Note:** what the human experienced, observed, decided, learned, questioned, or wants remembered.
- **Naya Note:** what Naya observed, learned, inferred, organized, verified, or recommends retaining.

Do not silently merge the two into an undifferentiated memory when provenance matters. Preserve source distinctions where useful.

## Cadence
The Daily Intelligence Briefing is the foundation for progressively larger reviews:
- Daily
- Weekly
- Monthly
- Quarterly
- Semiannual
- Yearly
- Historical / multi-year, when sufficient trustworthy records exist

The purpose is compounding intelligence: relevant experience should become increasingly useful rather than repeatedly being lost and reconstructed.

## Product / Codex Requirement
This operating ritual must be reflected in the canonical Digital Codex / Naya Power instruction set and future protocol/PDF updates. It belongs in the runtime behavior model, not only in marketing or course content.

## Onboarding Connection
The Daily Intelligence Briefing is paired with the initial Naya onboarding interview. Naya should learn who the user is, what matters to them, what they want to achieve, and how she can best help them. The initial relationship-building record and subsequent daily intelligence record form a continuous context-building loop.

## Design Principle
**Remember → Reflect → Learn → Compound → Act.**

The briefing should make the user feel that their experiences are not disappearing into yesterday; the useful parts are being turned into intelligence that can help tomorrow.
