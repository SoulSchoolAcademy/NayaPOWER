# NayaPOWER — Vision

## Vision
A WORLD WHERE HUMAN AND AI INTELLIGENCE IS PERSISTENT, CONNECTED, VERIFIED, DISTILLED, AND COMPOUNDED — SO PEOPLE BECOME MORE CAPABLE AND WISER RATHER THAN MERELY ACCUMULATING MORE INFORMATION.

## The future
A person should not repeatedly have to explain who they are, what they are building, what has been learned, what failed, what is true, what remains unknown, what decisions were made, or what should happen next.

Their intelligence environment should preserve relevant context and make it retrievable when it matters.

## Model independence
Underlying AI models may change. The human intelligence environment should not have to start over.

## Human capability
The vision is not permanent dependence on Naya. It is greater human capability, independence, creativity, effectiveness, understanding, and ability to teach others.

## From memory to intelligence
EXPERIENCE → MEMORY → CONNECTION → UNDERSTANDING → LEARNING → INTELLIGENCE → WISDOM → BETTER ACTION.

## Long-term network
PERSONAL SUPERBRAIN → PERMISSIONED INTELLIGENCE NETWORK → COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE.

Collective intelligence must increase human capability without turning private intelligence into public property.
