# NayaNET — Mission

## Mission
MAKE A GOVERNED HUMAN INTELLIGENCE NETWORK WHERE PEOPLE CAN USE THEIR PERSONAL SUPERBRAIN, CREATE REAL OUTCOMES, PRESERVE WHAT MATTERS, AND CONNECT INTELLIGENCE WITH OTHERS BY EXPLICIT CHOICE AND CONSENT.

## Mission pillars
1. HUMAN OPERATION — the person can use intelligence without becoming its technical operator.
2. CONTINUITY — meaningful work survives sessions, models, tools, and handoffs.
3. CREATION — intelligence moves from thought into useful reality.
4. LEARNING — the human becomes more capable, not merely more informed.
5. CONNECTION — intelligence can cross boundaries only by permission.
6. TRUST — consequential claims and actions have appropriate evidence.
7. AGENCY — the human remains the principal.

## Boundary
NayaNET must never improve usability by weakening governance. The interface connects to the real governed engine; it does not become a parallel intelligence system.
