## What Create Smart Note Means

A Smart Note is one intelligence event / one Intelligent Block.

It contains these six perspectives:

1. Human Note
2. Naya Note
3. Machine Note
4. Child Note
5. Grammar Note
6. Nutshell

These are perspectives/projections of ONE Smart Note, not separate intelligence events.

## Where It Goes

After creation, the Intelligent Block becomes part of the user's **Personal Intelligent Feed**, the user's living intelligence library. The system should automatically organize, connect, retrieve, and resurface intelligence without requiring manual database maintenance.

## Daily Intelligence

Smart Notes accumulate during the day. Naya can distill them into a **Daily Intelligence Briefing** focused on what happened, what was learned, what mattered, what changed, decisions, patterns, wisdom, what should be remembered, and what carries forward.

> **Distill the intelligence of the day into the wisdom worth keeping.**

## Collective Intelligence

The Collective learns continuously, but must not simply receive raw personal Smart Notes. The intended path is:

**Personal Intelligence → Intelligence Extraction → Privacy/Consent Gate → Collective Intelligence**

The Collective receives appropriate intelligence, not the person's private life or identity.

Core law:

> **Private by default. Shared by choice. Collective by consent. Public by decision.**

## Two Speeds of Collective Intelligence

### LIVE — Intelligence Stream

Meaningful, consent-eligible intelligence can enter the Collective in near real time.

### WISDOM — Distilled Intelligence

Naya detects repetition, convergence, relationships, patterns, lessons, principles, and higher-order wisdom over time.

> **LIVE = what the network is learning now.**
> **WISDOM = what the network has learned.**
