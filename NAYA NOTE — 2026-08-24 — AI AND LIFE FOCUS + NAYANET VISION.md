## MAXIS direction

MAXIS can ultimately help people learn and assess almost any subject, but our initial and intentional domain is **AI and Life**, grounded in the **Digital Codex** and **Human Maximus Codex**.

That focus is not a limitation. It is our identity and our expertise.

## Naya / NayaNet realization

The strategic breakthrough is that we do **not** need to create a new AI from scratch to begin.

Naya Power can be **AI-agnostic**: a high-level operating system, protocol layer, knowledge/memory structure, and way of working that can sit on top of capable existing AI systems.

Start with what people already know. If they already use ChatGPT, they do not need to learn a completely new AI. We can supercharge the experience with Naya Power.

Over time, NayaNet can evolve toward a broader native intelligence/network layer and potentially its own AI, but we do not need to wait for that future to create value today.

Naya represents a new interaction layer: **talk to the internet, work with it, learn with it, create with it, and let it become increasingly personal.**

## Why Naya is different

A general AI can be thought of as a vast library of knowledge.

Naya is tuned to the Codices and their operating principles. The Digital Codex governs how intelligence operates in the digital world; the Human Maximus Codex governs the pursuit of maximum human potential.

The goal is not merely more answers. The goal is a better **working relationship with intelligence** that remembers what matters, compounds useful experience, follows high-quality protocols, and helps the human move forward.

## Setup principle

A major onboarding requirement is that Naya should get to know the human immediately. The activation experience should include a short interview designed to understand the person’s identity, story, priorities, vision, goals, and desired outcomes.

The relationship begins with understanding the human, not dumping instructions on them.

## Personalization recommendation

As part of setup, recommend enabling personalization and voice, and selecting the desired Naya voice where supported.
