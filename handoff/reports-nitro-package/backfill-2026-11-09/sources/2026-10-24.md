## 4. TOP 10 LESSONS — and how each one changes what we do

1. **Design is value, and verified work lands on a clock.** A correct-but-flat output delivers less value than a beautiful one, and finished work that sits politely in a queue delivers none. *From now on: a presentation below the elevated standard fails review no matter how correct the content is — and work verified at 9.0+ that isn't landed in 2 hours gets named, out loud, as the weakest point.*
2. **Search before you build — the math may already exist.** The team nearly designed a new value system before an afternoon of digging found the ratified one already in the house: the Foundational Value & Stewardship Law and the Value Calculus Specification, with the level ladder in the ledger's own math. *From now on: calibrate the ratified system; never author a parallel ledger, a parallel points system, or invented level names.*
3. **Points mirror value in both directions.** If good actions earn, verified harmful actions must subtract — visibly, with the reason attached. *From now on: negative actions post negative points automatically, levels can fall traceably, and honest verified earnings are final.*
4. **"Designed" and "landed" are different stages — name the stage you're at.** At dawn, auto-match was a plan plus one seed part in the live system. By evening it was a landed, tested engine — and it is *still* not a live service wired to citizens, nor calibrated on real citizen data. *From now on: claims carry their stage, and "engine landed" is never said as "matching is live" until wiring and calibration are proven separately.*
5. **A blocked item may be blocked on authoring, not on a person's tap.** The day's biggest security item was recorded as "waiting for Shawn." The census proved the real blocker: the fitted cure existed nowhere — nobody had written it. *From now on: before escalating anything as "waiting on a decision," prove the thing to be decided on actually exists. If it doesn't, the job is writing it.*
6. **A handoff's claimed end-state is a claim, never evidence.** Today produced the proof three ways: two seats filed the pricing note in parallel and the numbers collided; a live re-check then *falsified* the claim that both note links worked (one address serves the other note's content, the second returns "not found"); and one scheduled handoff reported a filing step that simply does not exist in the record. *From now on: nobody reports a link live until they have clicked it themselves, and every user-facing verdict is re-derived from live reads — the record was corrected to Shawn the same hour.*
7. **The certificate page is the identity page.** There is no separate certificate destination to build or travel to: a new citizen's first landing, right after they say their name, *is* their identity page — its title locked as "Tell me who you are, in your own words." *From now on: identity is built once, at entry, to the elevated standard.*
8. **Pages are rows, not real estate.** A checked cost verdict settled a standing fear: a citizen page is one shared template plus one small row in a database, on infrastructure costing roughly $0–30 a month. Nothing counts pages, profiles, or spaces against us. *From now on: "unlimited pages" is an honest promise, and per-page hosting fears are retired.*
9. **Security is light, not walls.** Shawn's model, filed as SN-1855, "Security Is Light": build in blocks so one attack can't wreck the whole; write every action on the ledger with who did it, so nobody moves unseen; let friction rise automatically when a verified balance dives — no bans by opinion. *From now on: a new surface ships only if every action lands on the ledger with identity, scope, and value, inside one block that can be isolated on its own.*
10. **An unused page is an unfinished build.** When the Welcome system starts creating each Naya's personal, business, and space pages automatically, the Nayas are to *live* in them — like, comment, share, save, and note every valuable lesson — so the system is visibly alive and the matching and points have real life to learn from. *From now on: setup is followed by active use; a page that exists but is unused is not a launch.*
