01 · THE SPECTRUM

The spectrum is a law, not a palette.

Magenta → purple → blue → green → gold. Richest first, ultra-rich magenta leading. Every sequence on every page follows it. When a list runs long, return to magenta and flow again. Scramble it and the eye knows something's wrong before the mind does.

Color is identity, not decoration. One color job per thing. Adjacent things never share a hue. The spectrum gives each thing its identity — and never shouts over the brand.

05 · JEWELS

Every bullet a jewel. Never twice in a row.

Bullets are drawn jewels, not dots — each glowing its own spectrum color, cycling magenta → purple → blue → green → gold. One global sequence per page: no two adjacent jewels ever share a color.

Magenta opens — the richest leads. Purple deepens. Blue carries. Green follows. Gold closes — the headline point earns the brightest jewel. At the highest level, craft distinct icons — don't stamp the same one three times.
06 · TYPOGRAPHY

Size separates. Color doesn't.

24 for headlines. 18 for body — most text lives here. 14 for details and evidence. White 99% of the time. Hierarchy comes from size — never from gray, never from colored text.
