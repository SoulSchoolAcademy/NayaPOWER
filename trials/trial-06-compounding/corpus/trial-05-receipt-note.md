# Trial-05 Receipt Note — Compounding trial (L5 reuse)

**Trial:** T5-20261007-compounding. **Executed:** 2026-10-07.
**Verdict:** INVALID by ceiling effect (preregistered gate — Trials-1/2 rule).

- 20/20 agents returned valid sheets (10 treatment with L5, 10 control without).
- Results: treatment 10/10 success at 9.0/9; control 10/10 success at 9.0/9.
  Fisher's p = 1.0, Cohen's h = 0.0. **Validity gate: CEILING_INVALID = TRUE**
  (control mean 9.0 >= 4.5). Not a negative result. Not a pass.
- Mechanism of the ceiling: the control brief functionally delivered the
  intervention. Control was told "base your project-specific answers on the
  corpus" — which IS L5's actionable content. The trial compared L5+instruction
  vs instruction; the marginal treatment was nil by construction.
- **Durable lesson — isolate the lesson from the instruction.** A control arm with
  corpus access must not receive the retrieval instruction, or the control brief
  is the treatment. Prescribed Trial-06 design: control gets corpus access with
  no instruction and no L5; treatment gets access + L5.

  inconsistent (title says 6 steps, body line 38 says 8 steps). Flagged for the
