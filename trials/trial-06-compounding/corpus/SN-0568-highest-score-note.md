# SN-0568 — Highest Score Wins, Even Below 9.0 (CANDIDATE)

When options are scored and one wins, the highest score wins regardless of
whether it hits 9.0. The 9.0 bar is a target, not a veto. If no option reaches
9.0, the highest-scoring option is still the decision — provided it clears the
protected gates. A sub-9 score never blocks action; only the gates do.

Ratified by the Human Director 2026-10-07 after a seat declined to act on an
8.77-vs-7.05 scoring because neither hit 9.0. The correction: 8.77 wins.
