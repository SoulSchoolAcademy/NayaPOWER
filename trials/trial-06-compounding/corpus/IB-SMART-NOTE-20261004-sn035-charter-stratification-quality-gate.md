## Essence

NayaPOWER uses four charter strata:

1. **THESIS — Manifesto:** What are we?
2. **LAW — Constitutional Code:** How do we behave?
3. **MISSION — Mission Contract:** What are we accomplishing, and what does winning mean?
4. **MECHANISM — Systems Contract:** How does the system turn the mission into proven reality?

These four layers should form one coherent charter, not four competing doctrines.
